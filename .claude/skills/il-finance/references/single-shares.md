## Summary

- A share is a security: a certificate a company issues in a series that gives membership or participation in it (SHR-01).
- A saver who buys a listed share through a bank or broker is its holder in law, though the company's register shows the nominee company. Each holder may come to the general meeting and vote, and receives a dividend only when the company decides to pay one, which by default the board decides (SHR-01).

## Rules

### SHR-01 What a share is, and what holding one gives
- Rule: The Securities Law defines "ניירות־ערך" as certificates issued in series by a company, a cooperative society or another body "ומקנות זכות חברות או השתתפות בהם או תביעה מהם" (s.1). A share is the kind that gives membership or participation; a bond gives a claim (`corporate-bonds.md` CORP-01) (derived: the definition does not use the word "מניה").
  - Who is the holder: in a public company, a shareholder is anyone "מי שלזכותו רשומה אצל חבר בורסה מניה" whose share is among those registered in the company's register in the name of a nominee company ("חברה לרישומים"), or anyone registered in the register as a shareholder, or anyone holding a share warrant (Companies Law s.177). So a saver whose share is recorded at a bank or broker is a shareholder in law, though the register shows the nominee company.
  - The vote: "כל בעל מניה זכאי להשתתף באסיפה הכללית ולהצביע בה, כפוף להוראות התקנון" (s.188).
  - The dividend: "כל בעל מניה זכאי לקבל דיבידנד, בהתאם לזכויות הצמודות לכל מניה, אם הוחלט על חלוקת דיבידנד" (s.190). The board decides to distribute, unless the articles give the decision to the general meeting or set another way (s.307). So a dividend is never owed in advance.
  - If the company is run in a way that oppresses its shareholders, a shareholder may ask the court for orders to end it (s.191).
- Conditions / exceptions: The Companies Law was read as enacted in 1999 (Knesset site). Later amendments to ss.177, 188, 190, 191 and 307 were not checked against an official consolidated text. Shares of different classes may carry different rights under the articles (s.190, "בהתאם לזכויות הצמודות לכל מניה"). If the company fails, shareholders are paid after its creditors (REG-33).
