## Summary

A savings policy is a life-insurance policy with savings that is not approved as a provident fund: the investment-tracks circular (2015-9-7, issued by the Ministry of Finance's Capital Market Division; its 2022 amended text, circular 2022-9-30) calls the category a "פוליסה שאינה קופת ביטוח", and the Ministry of Finance says the Insurance Supervision Law and the Insurance Contract Law govern it. The saver carries the investment result, and an annual deposit ceiling does not apply. The State Comptroller describes it as a short- and medium-term investment tool rather than insurance. The contract-terms regulations cap the fee on the balance of a profit-sharing policy that is not an insurance fund at 1% a year of the portfolio, 2% with the Commissioner's approval, and above that only with approval and only as a percentage of value (reg. 6א, INS-25); the Ministry of Finance team writes that the cap is "כקבוע בפוליסה ולכל היותר עד 2% מהצבירה" (p.16). The 2012 provident-fund fee caps reach profit-sharing policies only in the lost-contact rule. Insurers' own terms set maximums that differ: Clal 6% of deposits plus 1.5% a year of the balance, and Phoenix 4% plus 1.7%; on which provision deposit fees rest is open (open question 16). The CMA average for 2024 was 0.78% of assets and 1.86% of deposits (life-insurance policies from 2004). By the Insurance Contract Law (s.46), after two years of premiums ("לאחר ששולמו דמי הביטוח בעד שנתיים") the holder may redeem all or part of the policy, and the insurer pays within 30 days of the demand; that s.46 reaches a savings policy is a reading (INS-07). The five insurers' terms read (Clal, Harel, The Phoenix, Migdal, Hachshara; of the eight in the regulator's Bituach-Net) allow withdrawal at any time and pay within 30 days of a written request with all documents, a clock that may start later than the law's; two of the five set a floor on a partial withdrawal and none names a surrender fee (INS-28); The Phoenix's FAQ for Phoenix Invest says 4 business days. No regulator rule on surrender fees or partial-withdrawal floors was found. Track switches take 3 business days. No route for moving a savings policy to another insurer without withdrawing was found.

Executive insurance is a life-insurance plan approved as a provident fund (קופת ביטוח), and most of the Provident Funds Law applies to it. Policies issued from 1 January 2013 fall under the 4% of deposits plus 1.05% of balance cap. Older policies keep their contractual fees: one 2009 Harel plan allows up to 1.7% of the balance. Some old policies carry a guaranteed annuity factor. A policy qualifies for mandatory pension if death and disability cover is held in it or elsewhere. Tax is in TXA and is open, so neither product can graduate yet.

## Rules

### Savings policy

### INS-01 A savings policy is a life-insurance policy with savings that is not an insurance fund; the holder's rights follow the portfolio's return less the fee (reg. 6א(ג))
- Rule: The investment-tracks circular, issued in February 2015 by the Ministry of Finance's Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Division ("אגף שוק ההון, ביטוח וחסכון", before the Authority existed) under both the Provident Funds Law and the Insurance Supervision Law, defines the category the product falls in: "פוליסה שאינה קופת ביטוח – פוליסת ביטוח שהוצאה על פי תכנית ביטוח משולבת בחיסכון שאינה קופת ביטוח" (circular 2015-9-7, s.2). The circular applies to every institutional body managing "פוליסות ביטוח חיים שאינן קופות ביטוח" (s.15). The Ministry of Finance's team on short- and medium-term savings writes: "פוליסת חיסכון היא מוצר חיסכון פיננסי, המנוהל על ידי חברות ביטוח. פוליסות אלו מפוקחות במסגרת חוק הפיקוח על הביטוח, חלות עליהן הוראות חוק חוזה ביטוח". It adds that the money is managed in a set number of tracks under the Commissioner's instructions (interim report, February 2025, pp.15 to 16). Since 2005 the Insurance Supervision Law defines "ביטוח תלוי תשואה" as "תכנית ביטוח שלפיה תגמולי הביטוח שלהם זכאי המוטב תלויים בתשואה שמניבות השקעות מסוימות של המבטח" (s.1). Section 36א applies sections 31 to 36 of the Provident Funds Law to such insurance and to the assets held against it. The State Comptroller writes that savings policies, "למרות שמן, אינן משמשות מכשיר ביטוחי אלא מכשיר השקעה לטווח הקצר והבינוני". For a profit-sharing life policy that is not an insurance fund, the contract-terms regulations tie the holder's rights to the portfolio's return: "התשואה נטו לצורך קביעת זכויות המוטב או המבוטח על פי הפוליסה תהיה בשיעור התשואה על תיק ההשקעות בניכוי דמי הניהול הקבועים" (reg. 6א(ג), as added in 1995, its tail deleted in 2004; INS-25).
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - **Reading, not a quoted rule:** the circular defines a category, "פוליסה שאינה קופת ביטוח", and the Ministry of Finance team speaks of "פוליסת חיסכון"; that the product sold as a savings policy is that category, and is the profit-sharing policy of reg. 6א, is a close reading of these texts. No official text found joins the two names in words.
  - **Inference, not a quoted rule:** a savings policy is "ביטוח תלוי תשואה" in the law's sense, because its balance follows the return of the track the holder chose. So s.36א reaches it. No official text found says this in words.
  - **The regulations' own term:** the fee regulations (reg. 8, INS-04) and the contract-terms regulations (reg. 6א, INS-25) use a different term, "ביטוח חיים משתתף ברווחים": the fee regulations add "למעט קופת ביטוח" (reg. 8(ו)), and reg. 6א says "שאינה קופת ביטוח". Its definition runs through a chain of references that ends in regulations repealed in 2023 (INS-25; open question 1).
  - **The Codex:** the Commissioner's codex, gate 1 (edition 3, 22.11.2018), defines "ביטוח תלוי תשואה" only "כהגדרתו בחוק הפיקוח על הביטוח" (p.3). It has no entry for a savings policy or for "משתתף ברווחים".
  - **Later wording of the circular:** the CMA's draft circular on managing investment tracks (19.5.2022) words the definition "פוליסת ביטוח חיים שהוצאה על פי תכנית ביטוח משולבת בחיסכון שאינה קופת ביטוח". The final text carries the same words: circular 2022-9-30 (20.12.2022) amends circular 2022-9-28 ("מסלולי השקעה בקופות גמל – תיקון", 28.9.2022), puts its start at 1 January 2024 (some provisions 1 January 2023), applies "על כל הגופים המוסדיים המנהלים קופות גמל ... או המנהלים פוליסות ביטוח חיים שאינן קופות ביטוח" (s.3, p.1), and its appendix, the text of circular 2016-9-13 ("מסלולי השקעה בקופות גמל – תיקון", 31.8.2016) with the edition changes, defines "פוליסה שאינה קופת ביטוח – פוליסת ביטוח חיים שהוצאה על פי תכנית ביטוח משולבת בחיסכון שאינה קופת ביטוח" (p.3). Circular 2022-9-28 itself was not opened (the independent check of 2026-11-06 named two trade-press leads, funder.co.il 141484 and bizportal 817236, not opened and not used); whether a later circular changed the definition again was not checked.
  - **Insurance cover:** some savings policies carry a risk or annuity component, "אם כי זה אינו תנאי להצעת המוצר" (Ministry of Finance team, p.16). Examples are Clal Safe (Clal's product page) and Phoenix plan 5552 (INS-03). The site decodes them as savings policies with insurance cover.
  - **How each insurer marks the product** on its own policy: INS-27. Executive insurance, by contrast, is approved as a provident fund (INS-13).
- Figures: about NIS 86 billion managed in savings policies, November 2024 (Ministry of Finance team, p.16). The same report gives about NIS 84 billion on pp.45 and 56; its own figures differ, and the date of the 84 is not given beside it. One-off figures, not updated.

### INS-27 How insurers mark a savings policy in their own terms (institution examples)
- Rule: Clal Insurance's "חיסכון פיננסי לעתיד" (plan 1418, 08/2024) says on its title page "הפוליסה אינה מוכרת כקופת ביטוח". Its balance ("חיסכון מצטבר") is the deposits revalued by the results of the chosen track, minus management fees and withdrawals (s.1 definitions, p.2). - Conditions / exceptions: These are the insurers' own wording on their own plans. The general definition is INS-01. A reader can use either phrase to tell a savings policy from an executive policy, which says it is approved as a provident fund (INS-13).

### INS-02 No annual deposit ceiling applies to a savings policy
- Rule: The State Comptroller found that savings policies are used "ללא הגבלה על היקף הכספים המופקד בו מדי שנה". It contrasts this with kupat gemel lehashkaa, whose annual ceiling puts it at a disadvantage against a savings policy for amounts above the ceiling.
- Conditions / exceptions: An insurer may still refuse or condition a deposit. In the Clal terms, one-time deposits and increases need the company's prior written consent (s. 7.1). The Comptroller summary does not cite the legal basis for the absence of a ceiling; no ceiling was found in any source read. For the kupat gemel lehashkaa ceiling, see `kupat-gemel-lehashkaa.md`.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [State Comptroller, January 2024](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/The-cost-of-living/2024-The-cost-of-living-104-Hon.pdf), summary pp. 336 to 337 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary; [Clal 1418](https://www.clalbit.co.il/media/yy5djhg3/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%93-1418.pdf), s. 7.1 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution
- Scope: general
- Dimensions: `deposit-ceiling`
- Likely to change: yes. The Comptroller raised it as a competition problem; any regulatory response would change it.

### INS-03 No regulatory management-fee cap for savings policies was found; insurers' terms set their own maximums
- Rule: The 2012 fee regulations cap fees for a "קופת גמל" (reg. 2 to 5). A savings policy is not a provident fund (INS-01). The only provision that extends the regulations to profit-sharing life policies is the lost-contact rule (reg. 8; INS-04). Reading: the 1.05% and 4% caps do not reach savings policies. The maximums are contractual and differ by insurer and plan:
  - Clal "חיסכון פיננסי לעתיד" (1418, 08/2024): "דמי-ניהול ההפקדה השוטפת בשיעור שלא יעלה על 6%" and "דמי ניהול החיסכון המצטבר בשיעור שנתי שלא יעלה על 1.5%" (s. 4.1). One-time deposits carry no deposit fee (s. 4.4). In the payout phase the fee is "שיעור שנתי קבוע שלא יעלה על 0.30%" (s. 4.3). The balance fee is collected daily at 1/365 of the annual rate (s. 4.5).
  - Phoenix "תכנית פרט-מסלול לחיים" (5552, November 2024 edition), a savings policy with life cover: up to 4% of the periodic payment and up to 1.7% a year of the balance; no fee from one-time deposits; 1% a year during annuity payment (s. 9.1, 9.2, 9.5).
  - Harel "הראל מגוון השקעות אישי" (1342, edition 05/20): a daily fee on the balance at an annual rate "שלא יעלה על 1.5%", 1% in the payment period, and no fee on deposits named in the fee clause (s.12.1, 12.2; added 2026-11-06, source in INS-28).
  - Hachshara "BEST INVEST אישי" (02.2022): a daily fee on the balance at an annual rate "שלא יעלה על 1.5%", 1% in the monthly payout period (s.12.1); a fee on regular deposits defined "כקבוע בדף פרטי הפוליסה" (s.1.5) with no rate or maximum stated in s.12; no deposit fee on a one-time deposit notified to the company (s.7.1) (added 2026-11-07, source in INS-28).
- Conditions / exceptions: The actual rate is in each policy's "דף פרטי ביטוח" and may vary by amount and track (Clal s. 4.2; Phoenix s. 9.3). On a track switch, Phoenix applies the new track's fee (Phoenix FAQ). Clal states that changes are notified by an updated policy page within 10 business days (s. 4.6). No CMA circular on savings-policy fees was found; the cap is in the regulations (INS-25). The CMA fee-discount circular 2017-9-15 (PF-13 to PF-16) does not reach savings policies, as a reading: it speaks of an "עמית", a provident-fund member, which a savings-policy holder is not, so a savings policy's fee discount has no five-year minimum and its rate may change on the policy's own terms.
- Figures: Clal 6% / 1.5% / 0.30%; Phoenix 4% / 1.7% / 1%; Harel no deposit fee / 1.5% / 1%; Hachshara deposit fee with no stated maximum / 1.5% / 1%. Contractual, per plan edition.

### INS-07 Getting the money out: after two years of premiums the law gives a right to full or partial redemption, paid within 30 days of the demand
- Rule: The Insurance Contract Law, chapter B "ביטוח חיים", s.46 "המרה ופדיון":
  - **The right:** "לאחר ששולמו דמי הביטוח בעד שנתיים רשאי המבוטח לדרוש, בהודעה בכתב למבטח, המרת הביטוח לביטוח מסולק או פדיון הפוליסה" (s.46(א)).
  - **The deadline:** on a redemption the insurer pays "את ערך הפדיון שנקבע לכך בפוליסה תוך 30 ימים מהיום שנמסרה לו הדרישה, או תוך זמן ארוך יותר שנקב המבוטח בדרישה ושלא יהיה יותר מ־90 ימים". Linkage differences are added from the day of the demand, and interest from the end of the 30 days (s.46(ג)).
  - **Part of the money:** "המבוטח רשאי להגביל את דרישתו לחלק מן הביטוח" (s.46(ד)).
  - **Irrevocable beneficiary:** a demand needs the written consent of a beneficiary named irrevocably (s.46(ה)).

  The insurer's cancelling the policy does not take away the right to redeem (s.47). An unpaid loan secured on the policy may be offset against the redemption value only after a written demand to repay it within the payout period (s.48). Sections 45 to 48 "אין להתנות עליהן אלא לטובת המבוטח או המוטב" (s.52(ב)). At the end of the insurance period, the contract-terms regulations require every life-insurance contract to say that the benefits are paid "תוך שבעה ימים מתום תקופת הביטוח או תוך שבעה ימים מיום שהוגשו למבטח המסמכים הנדרשים בפוליסה לענין זה, לפי המאוחר" (reg. 6(1)).
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - **Reading, not a quoted rule: it reaches savings policies.** The Ministry of Finance team says only that "חלות עליהן הוראות חוק חוזה ביטוח" (p.15; INS-01), and circular 2015-9-7 calls them "פוליסות ביטוח חיים שאינן קופות ביטוח" (s.15, p.7). That the law's chapter B, life insurance (s.41: "בביטוח חיים מקרה הביטוח הוא מותו של המבוטח או של זולתו או הגיעם לגיל מסויים"), and so s.46, reach a savings policy is this file's reading; no official text found says it in words.
  - **Reading, not a quoted rule:** s.46 can be varied only in the holder's favour. So a policy may let the holder withdraw sooner or be paid faster than the law requires: Clal, Harel, The Phoenix (plan 5552), Migdal (541104112) and Hachshara (BEST INVEST) allow withdrawal at any time (INS-28). It may not pay later than the law's 30 days, or refuse a partial redemption. The insurers' own 30 days run from receipt of a written request "וכל המסמכים" (Clal s.12.1.3, Harel s.16.9, Phoenix 5552 s.11.4, Migdal 541104112 s.16.9, Hachshara BEST INVEST s.14.4), while the law's run "מהיום שנמסרה לו הדרישה" (s.46(ג)); whether a clock that starts at the last document is compatible with s.46(ג) was not found (open question 17). Whether the insurers' floors on a partial withdrawal (90% of the balance, or a remainder under NIS 25,000; INS-28) are compatible with s.46(ד) was not found in any official text (open question 17).
  - **Exit fee:** the law pays the redemption value "שנקבע לכך בפוליסה", so the policy sets the amount. No regulation or circular that sets or bars a surrender charge on a savings policy was found. Where the Commissioner approves a balance fee above 2% a year, no fee of another kind may be charged (reg. 6א(א2), INS-25). None of the five insurers' binding terms read names a surrender fee: Clal 1418, Harel 1342, Phoenix 5552 (whose fee clause, s.9, and withdrawal clause, s.11, name none; s.11.3 deducts only debts and loans on the policy) and Migdal 541104112 (s.16.8 deducts only loans, debts and tax) and Hachshara BEST INVEST 02.2022 (fee clause s.12 and withdrawal clause s.14 name none) (INS-28). Open question 17.
  - **The two-year condition:** how s.46(א) applies to a policy funded by one deposit was not found (open question 17).
  - **Loans:** the Ministry of Finance team: "הגוף המוסדי רשאי להעניק הלוואה לחוסך כנגד החיסכון שנצבר בפוליסה" (p.16).
  - **No regulator circular** that binds every insurer on savings-policy payout times or partial withdrawals was found. The CMA plans a uniform withdrawal circular under both the Insurance and the Provident Funds laws (INS-24). Each insurer's own times and floors: INS-28. Tax on withdrawal: TXA.
  - **Amendments read:** s.46(ג)'s interest wording was changed in 2001 (Interest and Linkage Law amendment 8, s.7(2)) and in 2023 (amendment 9, s.40(3), in force from 1 October 2024). Neither touched the 30- and 90-day periods or s.46(ד). The Knesset's list of laws amending the Insurance Contract Law (Knesset OData, `KNS_IsraelLaw` 2000653) holds 13 amending laws; the independent check of 2026-11-06 read the published text of each and only these two touch s.46. The list is not complete (s.47 carries a 1982 amendment it does not show; that amendment does not touch s.46). The Wikisource consolidated copy of the law (a finding aid only, not a source) tags s.46 with only these two amendments.
- Figures: 30 days from the demand; up to 90 days if the holder asks; 2 years of premiums for the statutory right (fixed in law, s.46); 7 days at the end of the insurance period (fixed in regulation, reg. 6(1)).

### INS-28 Withdrawal at five insurers: any time, paid within 30 days of a written request with all documents, partial withdrawals with a floor at two (institution terms)
- Rule:
  - **Clal Insurance** ("חיסכון פיננסי לעתיד", plan 1418, 08/2024):
    - The holder may withdraw all or part of the balance at any time (s.12.1.1, 12.2.1). A full withdrawal is paid "בתוך 30 ימים" from receipt of a written request and all documents (s.12.1.3).
    - A partial withdrawal is taken pro rata from each track (s.12.2.2). If it is 90% or more of the balance, or would leave less than NIS 25,000 linked to the January 2008 CPI, Clal may treat it as a full withdrawal. It must first notify the holder, who may cut the amount within 7 business days (s.12.2.4).
    - A partial withdrawal can reduce the death cover where cover depends on the balance (s.12.2.3). Loans are available "לאחר ששולמו הפקדות שוטפות בעבור 3 שנים לפחות" (s.14.1). Clal's product page advertises withdrawal "ללא קנסות פדיון".
  - **The Phoenix** ("תכנית פרט-מסלול לחיים", plan 5552, November 2024, a savings policy with life cover):
    - The holder may withdraw the redemption value "כולו או חלקו, בכל עת קודם לתום תקופת הביטוח" (s.11.1). A partial withdrawal is taken pro rata from the tracks and reduces the basic risk cover pro rata (s.11.5, p.8).
    - A withdrawal of 90% or more of the redemption value counts as a full withdrawal for ending the insurance covers (s.11.2.2); no floor in shekels is set. The amount paid is net of any debt on the policy and of loans (s.11.3).
    - Payment is "בהתאם למועדים הקבועים בהסדר התחיקתי או בתוך 30 ימים מהיום שבו הגיעו למשרדי הפניקס בקשה כתובה וכל המסמכים הדרושים לתשלום ערך הפדיון, לפי המוקדם" (s.11.4); late payment carries late interest and linkage under the law.
    - The Phoenix's FAQ for "הפניקס אינווסט" (marketing text, not a binding document; whether Phoenix Invest is sold under plan 5552 was not checked) claims processing "עד - 4 ימי עסקים", up to 7 business days with the Apollo track, and fixed monthly withdrawals for a limited period or until the balance runs out. These are the FAQ's own claims, not terms.
  - **Migdal** ("מגדל קשת לפרט", "פוליסת ביטוח חיים לתשלום הוני (התוכנית אינה מאושרת כקופת גמל)", return-dependent, catalogue no. 541104112, edition 03.2025):
    - A withdrawal is "תשלום של ערך הפדיון, כולו או חלקו, לפני קרות מקרה הביטוח" (s.16.1); a request is handled from its arrival at Migdal, and a partial one names the tracks to draw from (s.16.2). No floor on a partial withdrawal is set in s.16.
    - Payment is "בהתאם להסדר התחיקתי, או בתוך 30 ימים מהיום שבו הגיעו למשרדי החברה בקשה בכתב למשיכה וכל המסמכים הדרושים לתשלום ערך הפדיון ... לפי המוקדם מבין שני מועדים אלה, ובכל מקרה לא יותר משני ימי עסקים לאחר יום משיכת הכספים ממסלולי ההשקעה" (s.16.9); late payment carries late interest and linkage under the law.
    - A full withdrawal ends the policy and its covers (s.16.7); the amount paid is net of loans, debts and tax (s.16.8). The balance fee is up to 2% a year as the policy-details page states (s.7.1). Where the policyholder is a company, withdrawal only on listed conditions, among them a move "לפוליסת חיסכון אחרת בעבור המבוטח" (s.16.5.3).
  - **Harel** ("הראל מגוון השקעות אישי", plan 1342, edition 05/20):
    - A holder who is an individual may withdraw all or part of the redemption value "בכל עת בטרם קרה מקרה הביטוח" (s.16.2). A partial withdrawal is taken pro rata from each track (s.16.5).
    - The same floor as Clal's applies, except "אלא אם הסכימה החברה אחרת": 90% or more, or a remainder under NIS 25,000 linked to the January 2013 CPI. Harel too notifies the holder and allows 7 business days to cut the amount (s.16.5).
    - The redemption value is paid within 30 days of the written request and the documents (s.16.9), valued at the business day before payment (s.16.10). Late payment carries late interest and linkage under the law (s.16.11).
    - A full withdrawal ends the policy and its covers (s.16.6). Harel may lend against the policy at the rate it sets (s.23). At the end of the insurance period the insured may take all or part of the redemption value as monthly payments for a chosen period of 1 to 30 years (s.22.1).
  - **Hachshara** ("BEST INVEST אישי", general terms, edition 02.2022; the cover page says "התכנית אינה מוכרת כקופת גמל"):
    - The redemption value is "החיסכון המצטבר בניכוי דמי ניהול" (s.1.25). The holder "רשאי, בכל עת לפני תום תקופת הפוליסה, למשוך את ערך הפדיון, כולו או חלקו" (s.14.2), under the legislative arrangement (s.14.1). A partial withdrawal is taken pro rata from each track unless the withdrawal form says otherwise (s.14.7). No floor on a partial withdrawal is set in s.14.
    - A request to withdraw a lump sum "תבוצע תוך 30 ימים מהיום שבו הגיעו למשרדי החברה בקשה כתובה וכל המסמכים הדרושים לתשלום ערך הפדיון" (s.14.4). The money leaves the tracks on the business day before payment, and Hachshara is not responsible for return changes between the request and payment (s.14.5). Late payment carries late interest and linkage under the legislative arrangement (s.14.6).
    - A full withdrawal, with regular payments stopped, cancels the policy from the payment date (s.14.8). Where the holder is a body of persons or a corporation, withdrawal only on listed conditions: a declared retirement grant with tax form 161, a move "לפוליסת חסכון אחרת" for the insured (s.14.2.1), or a court or labour-court order (s.14.2.2).
    - The fee clause charges a fee on the daily balance at an annual rate "שלא יעלה על 1.5%" (s.12.1.1), and up to 1% in the period when the redemption value is paid out in monthly payments under s.17 (s.12.1.2). A "דמי ניהול מהתשלום השוטף" is defined as a sum deducted from regular deposits "כאמור בסעיף 12 להלן וכקבוע בדף פרטי הפוליסה" (s.1.5) and is charged on regular deposits and agreed increases (s.6.3), but s.12 states no rate or maximum for it. A one-time deposit notified to Hachshara pays only the balance fee (s.7.1); one paid without notice is charged the deposit fee until identified, which is then returned to the balance (s.7.2). The fee and withdrawal clauses name no surrender fee.
    - At the end of the policy period the redemption value may be paid as a lump sum or in monthly payments (s.16.1); a monthly payment at the time of choice is at least NIS 250 and at most 1/12 of the redemption value (s.16.5).
- Conditions / exceptions: These are each company's own terms. They allow withdrawal at any time, where the law's right needs two years of premiums (INS-07). Migdal's plan, read 2026-11-07, sets no floor on a partial withdrawal, so the 90% or NIS 25,000 floor is Clal's and Harel's, not a market practice; that it is the product Migdal sells as its savings policy is read from its own title (a return-dependent life policy for a lump sum, not approved as a provident fund, INS-01), not from a Migdal page that calls it so. Their 30 days, though, run from receipt of the written request and all documents, while the law's run from the demand (s.46(ג)), so their clock can start later than the law's (open question 17). ### INS-10 Death benefit and optional insurance cover in a savings policy
- Rule: On the insured's death, payment depends on the insurance track the holder chose. Beneficiaries may take the death benefit as monthly payments by transferring it to an annuity policy, which is treated as a new insurance contract. The annuity management fee is not above 1.00% a year (s. 11.1.8).

### INS-11 Annuity option from a savings policy uses the factors in force at payout, not guaranteed ones
- Rule: At the end of the insurance period the holder may take the balance as a lump sum or, under conditions, as a lifelong monthly annuity (Clal 1418 s. 11.2). The conditions:
  - Age: at the start of payment the insured has reached early retirement age and is not over 80.
  - Size: the first monthly annuity is not below 5% of the minimum wage.

### INS-20 Withdrawal, partial withdrawal and transfer out of an executive policy
- Rule: Withdrawals from an executive policy follow the Provident Funds Law and its regulations (s. 23 applies via s. 56; Harel 1286 s. 13.2). For example, severance can be withdrawn on leaving a job, and a lump sum from age 60 only if the remaining annuity is not below the minimum annuity amount (s. 13.2.1, 13.2.2; PEN-28 to PEN-30). The holder may transfer all or part of the redemption value to another provident fund under the Transfer Regulations (s. 14.1; PF-17, PF-19). An outstanding loan must be repaid first (s. 14.2). A non-end-of-period payment is made within the times in the regulations under s. 23 or within 30 days, whichever is earlier; at the end of the insurance period, within 7 days (s. 13.9). After a full withdrawal the policy and its covers lapse (s. 13.4).

### INS-25 Regulatory cap on savings-policy management fees: 1% a year of the portfolio, 2% with the Commissioner's approval (reg. 6א)
- Rule: Regulation 6א(א) of the Insurance Contract Terms Regulations, its opening as replaced in 2012 (קובץ התקנות 7131, p.9) and the rest as set in 2004 (קובץ התקנות 6284, p.3): "מבטח המנפיק פוליסת ביטוח חיים משתתף ברווחים שאינה קופת ביטוח (בתקנה זו — פוליסה שאינה קופת ביטוח), רשאי לגבות דמי ניהול בעד ניהול תיק ההשקעות, באחת הדרכים האלה: (1) דמי ניהול קבועים בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1/12% לחודש מהשווי המשוערך של תיק ההשקעות; (2) דמי ניהול קבועים בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1/365% ליום מהשווי המשוערך של תיק ההשקעות". Reg. 6א(א1): "המפקח רשאי לאשר דמי ניהול בשיעור העולה על הקבוע בתקנת משנה (א), ובלבד שלא יעלה על שיעור של 2/12% לחודש או 2/365% ליום". Reg. 6א(א2): with the Commissioner's approval a higher rate may be charged, "ובלבד שלא יגבה דמי ניהול מכל סוג שהוא אלא כשיעור מהשווי המשוערך של תיק ההשקעות". Reg. 6א(ב): "שיעור דמי הניהול בפוליסת ביטוח חיים משתתף ברווחים ייקבע מראש בתנאי הפוליסה". Reg. 6א(ג): the net return that sets the holder's rights is the portfolio return less the management fee.
- Conditions / exceptions: Applies to a profit-sharing life policy that is not an insurance fund, which is the savings policy of INS-01; insurance funds (executive insurance) are under the 2012 provident fee regulations (INS-15). The cap is on the balance fee. Deposit fees (Clal 6% of regular deposits, INS-03) are not mentioned in (א) or (א1); under (א2) no fee of any other type may be charged. On which provision the insurers' deposit fees rest is open question 16. The 2012 wording (in force 1.1.2013) does not apply to insurance contracts made before then (7131 p.9, s.3). The regulation defines "ביטוח חיים משתתף ברווחים" only by reference, and the chain ends in repealed regulations: reg. 1 as added in 1995 (קובץ התקנות 5682, p.2) gives it the meaning in the insurer investment regulations of 1986; those were repealed and replaced in 2001 (קובץ התקנות 6094, p.22, reg. 66), and the 2001 regulations define the term by the 1998 report-details regulations (6094, p.8, reg. 1), whose definition read: "ביטוח חיים שבו זכויות המוטב והמבוטח תלויות בתשואה של השקעות מסוימות שהמבטח עושה באופן מיוחד לענין תכנית שאושרה כביטוח כזה בידי המפקח, או ברווחים אחרים הקשורים לאותה תכנית" (קובץ התקנות 5884, p.2). The 1998 report-details regulations were repealed by regulations signed on 20.6.2023 and published on 28.6.2023 (קובץ התקנות 10695, p.2). Where the definition lives now was not found (open question 1, narrowed). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the quote of 6א(א) used the plural "פוליסות" of the 2004 text with the 2012 ending; it now has the singular "פוליסת" of the current opening (7131 p.9). ### INS-26 Annual report to savings-policy holders: within three months of the insurer's year end, with set fields (reg. 6ב)
- Rule: A life-insurance contract must provide that the insurer "ישלח למבוטחים, מדי שנה, בתוך שלושה חודשים מתום שנת המאזן שלו, דין וחשבון לגבי השנה שנסתיימה" (reg. 6ב(א)), for every plan in the policy in force at year end. It must include (reg. 6ב(ב)): (1) identifying details including "מספר הפוליסה, סכום ביטוח למקרה הביטוח, סוג ההצמדה, ערך פדיון וערך פדיון מיוחד, ערך מסולק וערך מסולק מיוחד, יתרת חסכון, היחס בין מרכיב החסכון בפרמיה לסך כל הפרמיה וסך תשלומי הפרמיה בשנת הדיווח"; (2) the return the insurer earned on the participating portfolio in the year, "תוך ציון נפרד של התשואה הריאלית, התשואה הריאלית בניכוי דמי ניהול" and the four-year average real return before and after fees; (3) the portfolio composition by asset class (government bonds, other bonds, shares and other securities, loans, bank deposits, real estate, other).

## Left out and why

- All tax (capital gains on withdrawal, real vs nominal gain, notional redemption, section 125D, interest exemption for older holders, employer-contribution tax, severance reconciliation, Amendment 190): TXA owns it. Institution tax claims seen here are passed on below, not used.
- Pure risk insurance (life, disability, health, long-term care) as products in their own right: product.md section 4 excludes insurance other than savings policies. Risk pricing appears only as a component of executive insurance (INS-19).
- Annuity-only policies ("פוליסת קצבה" for beneficiaries or retirees, for example Clal's annuity policy at up to 1.00%): a payout product, mentioned only where a savings policy can convert into it.
- Old guaranteed-return policies and pension funds under special management: closed; flagged in open question 8.
- Insurance-agent commissions and the advice process: the site carries no agent links and does not recommend advisers; only the 2026 plan item on life-insurance discounts and commission clawback is recorded (INS-24).
