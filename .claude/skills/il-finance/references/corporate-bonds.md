## Summary

- A corporate bond is a certificate issued in a series by a company or other body that gives the holder a right to demand money from that body. The State does not stand behind it; the holder's claim is against the issuer (CORP-01, CORP-24). Commercial paper is a corporate promise to pay within one year of the offer, and cannot be converted. The trustee chapter of the Securities Law does not apply to it (CORP-02).
- Anyone offering bonds to the public needs an ISA-permitted prospectus and must appoint a trustee for the holders. The issuer then files periodic and immediate reports (CORP-03, CORP-04).
- By law the trustee must act for all holders, monitor the issuer and any collateral, report material breaches, and act when the issuer is in distress. If a payment is 45 days late, it must apply to court for insolvency proceedings unless holders of three-quarters of the unpaid amount object. Holders can accelerate on grounds listed in the law. Changing the terms needs a qualified majority at a holders' meeting (CORP-05 to CORP-09).
- Covenants, collateral, rating step-ups and issuer early redemption are set per series in the trust deed, so they differ from bond to bond (CORP-10 to CORP-12).
- Rating companies must register with the ISA under a 2014 law (CORP-13). Israeli ratings are on a local scale, relative to other Israeli issuers (CORP-14). Which agencies are registered today was not readable (open question 1).
- In 2025 Israeli companies raised about NIS 160 bn in bonds, plus NIS 24.4 bn in commercial paper. About 60% of the bond amount was shekel, 39% CPI-linked and 1% dollar-linked, and 81% was rated in the A group or above. Average daily turnover in corporate bonds was about a third of that in government bonds (CORP-16, CORP-17, CORP-19).
- A saver buys on TASE through a bank or broker (trading unit NIS 1 face value), in a public offering, or through bond funds and ETFs on TASE corporate-bond indices (Tel-Bond) (CORP-18, CORP-20 to CORP-22).

## Rules

### CORP-01 What a corporate bond is in law
- Rule: The Securities Law defines "תעודות התחייבות" as "תעודות המונפקות בסדרה על ידי חברה, אגודה שיתופית או כל תאגיד אחר והמקנות זכות לתבוע מתאגיד כאמור כסף, בתאריך קבוע או בהתקיים תנאי מסויים ואינן מקנות זכות חברות או השתתפות באותו תאגיד". The definition includes certificates convertible into shares ("וכן תעודות כאמור הניתנות להמרה במניות"). It excludes "תעודות שמנפיקה המדינה או המונפקות על פי חוק מיוחד וניירות ערך מסחריים".
- Conditions / exceptions: Section 35א1 takes debt certificates whose purpose is joint investment or joint profit out of most of the law and places them under the Joint Investment Trust Law. Government bonds are in `government-bonds-and-makam.md`.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [חוק ניירות ערך, תשכ"ח-1968 (Wikisource, revision 3088555, amendments to תשפ"ו)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), s. 35א (definition), s. 35א1 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `regulator`, `protection`
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-02 Commercial paper (ניירות ערך מסחריים)
- Rule: Section 1 defines "ניירות ערך מסחריים" as "ניירות ערך המונפקים בידי תאגיד, שהם התחייבות התאגיד לשלם למחזיק סכום כסף, במועד שאינו מאוחר מתום שנה מיום ההצעה ואשר אינם ניתנים למימוש או להמרה לניירות ערך אחרים". Commercial paper is excluded from "תעודות התחייבות" (CORP-01), so the trustee duty in s. 35ב (CORP-04) does not cover it. Commercial paper listed on TASE from 29 September 2016 trades in units of NIS 10 face value (NIS 1 if listed before that date). Its first-day base price is the tender price × 10, and its last trading day is three trading days before redemption.
- Conditions / exceptions: TASE describes the 2025 commercial-paper raising as variable-rate ("בריבית משתנה"). Whether a rating or any other protection is required for listed commercial paper was not found (open question 4).
- Figures: 2025: about NIS 24.4 bn raised through commercial paper, of which about NIS 18.1 bn by banks (2024: about NIS 10 bn, of which 6.2 bn by banks). These are calendar-year facts and change each year.
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), s. 1 "ניירות ערך מסחריים", s. 35א · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). ### CORP-03 Public offering needs a prospectus; the issuer then reports
- Rule: "לא יעשה אדם הצעה לציבור אלא על פי תשקיף שהרשות התירה את פרסומו" (s. 15(א)). A body whose securities were offered to the public under a prospectus must file reports with the ISA ("תאגיד שניירות ערך שלו הוצעו לציבור על פי תשקיף חייב להגיש לרשות דו״חות או הודעות", s. 36(א)). If its securities are listed, it files them with the exchange as well.
- Conditions / exceptions: The content of periodic and immediate reports is set in regulations that were not read (open question 13). Reports are published on the ISA's Magna and TASE's Maya systems (seen as the addressees of the issuer report in CORP-11; the systems themselves were not read).

### CORP-04 A trustee must be appointed for every public bond offering
- Rule: "לא יציע אדם תעודות התחייבות לציבור אלא אם כן מינה המנפיק נאמן למחזיקים בתעודות ההתחייבות" (s. 35ב(א)). The first trustee is appointed in the trust deed (שטר נאמנות) between the issuer and the trustee. Later trustees are appointed, and their term set, by a holders' meeting. After the second annual trust report, the trustee must convene a meeting for each series, no later than 60 days after the annual report is filed, at which the holders can decide on the trustee's continued term.
- Conditions / exceptions: Not required for commercial paper (CORP-02). The Trust Law applies unless the chapter provides otherwise, and some of its sections cannot be waived by contract (s. 35ב(ב)). Only the opening of s. 35ב(א) was read verbatim; the reappointment meeting is from a summarised reading of s. 35ב(א1).
- Figures: 60 days (fixed in law).
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), s. 35ב(א), (א1), (ב) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `protection`, `regulator`
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-05 Who may be a trustee, conflicts, and who pays
- Rule: A trustee must be a company registered in Israel whose main purpose is trusteeship ("הנאמן חייב להיות חברה רשומה בישראל שמטרתה העיקרית לעסוק בנאמנות", s. 35ג). It must be entered in the trustees register (מרשם הנאמנים) kept by the ISA (s. 35ג1). A person may not be trustee for a series where a controlling person, officer or relative holds office at the issuer, or where a conflict of interest with the holders is likely (s. 35ה). "המנפיק יישא בשכרו של הנאמן ובהוצאות הסבירות שהנאמן נשא או התחייב לשאת בהן" (s. 35ה1(א)). The trust deed sets the fee and how holders share any part of it (s. 35ו(א)(5)).
- Conditions / exceptions: If holders replace the trustee, they bear any unreasonable excess in the new trustee's fee. The Minister may add conditions such as a deposit, insurance and expertise (s. 35ג1(ה)). Apart from the s. 35ג and 35ה1(א) quotes, these points come from a summarised reading of the sections. The list of registered trustees was not read (open question 2).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), ss. 35ג, 35ג1, 35ג2, 35ה, 35ה1, 35ו · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `protection`, `regulator`, `fee-management-balance` (paid by the issuer, not the holder)
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-06 What the trustee must do
- Rule: "הנאמן חייב לפעול לטובת כלל המחזיקים בתעודות ההתחייבות והוא ינהג בזהירות, באמונה ובשקידה" (s. 35ח(א)). It must check any collateral before money is paid to the issuer and review it at least yearly, and monitor whether the issuer meets its obligations, including grounds for acceleration (s. 35ח(ב)). "נודע לנאמן על הפרה מהותית של שטר הנאמנות מצד המנפיק, יודיע למחזיקים בתעודות ההתחייבות על ההפרה" (s. 35ח(ג)). If it reasonably believes the issuer may not meet its obligations, it must examine why and act for the holders, including negotiating changes to the terms (s. 35ח(ד1)). "הנאמן חייב להגיש לרשות ולבורסה דוח שנתי על ענייני הנאמנות" (s. 35ח1(א)). On request of holders of at least 10%, it reports on its actions.
- Conditions / exceptions: Acting on a valid holders' resolution counts as meeting its duty (s. 35ח(ד2)). A court may direct the trustee on a holder's application (s. 35ח(ד3)). Signing the trust deed is not an opinion on the bonds (s. 35ט(ב)). Points not in quotation marks come from a summarised reading of the sections.
- Figures: 10% of holders for a report on request (fixed in law).
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), ss. 35ח, 35ח1, 35ט · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `protection`, `reporting-obligation`
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-07 When holders can demand immediate repayment
- Rule: The trustee or the holders may accelerate the bonds or enforce collateral on these grounds (s. 35ט1(א)): (1) "חלה הרעה מהותית בעסקי המנפיק לעומת מצבו במועד ההנפקה" and there is a real risk the issuer will not repay on time; (2) "תעודות ההתחייבות לא נפרעו במועדן או לא קוימה התחייבות מהותית אחרת שניתנה לטובת המחזיקים"; (3) "המנפיק לא פרסם דוח כספי שהוא חייב בפרסומו לפי כל דין, בתוך 30 ימים מהמועד האחרון שבו הוא חייב בפרסומו"; (4) "תעודות ההתחייבות נמחקו מהמסחר בבורסה". They must first notify the issuer ("אלא לאחר שמסרו למנפיק הודעה על כוונתם לעשות כן", s. 35ט1(ב)), unless notice would prejudice the remedy. If the trustee believes a ground exists, it must convene a holders' meeting with acceleration on the agenda "לא יאוחר מ־45 ימים לאחר המועד שבו התקיימה העילה האמורה" (s. 35יב1(א1)).
- Conditions / exceptions: The trust deed may add further grounds (s. 35ו(א) requires the deed to state them). Per a summarised reading, a holders' acceleration decision needs a 50% quorum and a majority (s. 35ט1(ד)); this was not read verbatim (open question 3).
- Figures: 30 days; 45 days (fixed in law).
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), ss. 35ו, 35ט1(א), (ב), (ד), 35יב1(א1) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `protection`, `liquidity-lockup`
- Likely to change: no
- *2026-10-24 R5 pass:* s.35ט1(ד) read verbatim on the Wikisource raw text: a holders' decision to accelerate or enforce collateral "תתקבל באסיפת מחזיקים שנכחו בה מחזיקים בחמישים אחוזים לפחות מיתרת הערך הנקוב של תעודות התחייבות מאותה סדרה, ברוב של המחזיקים ביתרת הערך הנקוב של תעודות ההתחייבות המיוצג בהצבעה או ברוב כאמור באסיפת מחזיקים נדחית שנכחו בה מחזיקים בעשרים אחוזים לפחות מהיתרה כאמור"; s.35ט1(ה): these provisions cannot be contracted out of except in the holders' favour; s.35ט1(ג): a cure period set in the deed must pass first, and the trustee may shorten it. Closes the quorum part of open question 3.

### CORP-08 Payment default: trustee must go to court after 45 days
- Rule: If 45 days have passed since a principal or interest payment date and the payment was not made ("חלפו 45 ימים מהמועד לתשלום קרן או ריבית בהתאם לתנאי תעודות ההתחייבות, ולא שולם תשלום כאמור"), the trustee must apply to open insolvency proceedings against the issuer. The exception is if the holders object, by a majority "המחזיקים יחד בשלושה רבעים לפחות מערך הקרן או הריבית שלא שולמה" (s. 35ח(ד2ב)). Per a summarised reading, the trustee must re-check the holders' position at least every 60 days.
- Conditions / exceptions: What happens next (creditors' arrangement, liquidation, recovery) is governed by the Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law 2018, which was not read (open question 12). A creditors' representative (נציג הנושים) may be appointed: in the trust deed with three-quarters approval (s. 35ז(3)), or under insolvency law by a majority across all series (s. 35יב17(ב)); both from a summarised reading.
- Figures: 45 days; three-quarters; 60 days (fixed in law).
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), ss. 35ח(ד2ב), 35ז(3), 35יב17(ב) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `protection`, `liquidity-lockup`
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-09 Holders' meetings and changing the terms
- Rule: The trustee convenes a holders' meeting when it sees a need, or when holders of at least 5% of a series' outstanding face value demand it ("שלו חמישה אחוזים לפחות מיתרת הערך הנקוב של תעודות ההתחייבות מאותה סדרה", s. 35יב1(א)). A change to the trust deed or the bond terms that the trustee does not find harmless needs a holders' resolution at a meeting attended by holders of at least 50% of the outstanding face value, "ברוב של המחזיקים בשני שלישים לפחות מיתרת הערך הנקוב של תעודות ההתחייבות המיוצג בהצבעה", or a higher majority set in the deed. An adjourned meeting needs 20% attendance (s. 35ז(2)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Per a summarised reading: the general quorum is two holders with at least 25% of voting rights (s. 35יב13); ordinary decisions pass by simple majority unless the law or deed says otherwise (ss. 35יב16, 35יב17); changes to collateral are further restricted (s. 35ז1). A debt settlement is in practice a change of terms; the ISA staff view on "material debt settlements" is in CORP-10.
- Figures: 5%; 50% and two-thirds; 20% (fixed in law).
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), ss. 35ז(2), 35ז1, 35יב1(א), 35יב13, 35יב16, 35יב17 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)
- Dimensions: `protection`
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-10 ISA staff position on the trustee's role (2024), as summarised by a law firm
- Rule: According to Meitar's client update of 29 October 2024, ISA staff published legal position 108-5, "תפקידי נאמן למחזיקים בתעודות התחייבות שהונפקו לציבור", on 24 September 2024. In it, staff take the view that the trustee must use independent judgement and may not rely only on the issuer's statements and confirmations; the trust deed may not exempt it from doing its own checks. The trustee must check covenant compliance at every test date set in the deed, and do its own calculation where needed. It must independently check any early-redemption amount the issuer computes. When collateral is replaced or released, it must check that holders' rights are not harmed. When an issuer asks to change the trust deed, it must check whether the change is a "material debt settlement" ("הסדר חוב מהותי") as defined in the Insolvency Law 2018. If so, the trustee must require the change to go through court, with an expert opinion and holders' approval.
- Conditions / exceptions: Secondary source only. The ISA's own text was not opened (open question 11). *2026-10-24 R5 pass:* a second law-firm update (Gornitzky, 25.9.2024) confirms the date (24.9.2024), the legal basis (s.35ב(א)) and that the position followed an audit by the ISA's enforcement department; the ISA's own PDF is still unread (the ISA site is JavaScript; browser to-do).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Meitar, "עמדת רשות ניירות ערך - תפקידי נאמן למחזיקים בתעודות התחייבות שהונפקו לציבור", 29 Oct 2024](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/The-Position-of-Israels-Securities-Authority-–-Trustee-Roles-of-Bondholders.pdf), pp. 1 to 3 · checked 2026-10-22 · secondary
- Dimensions: `protection`
- Likely to change: yes, staff positions can be revised

### CORP-11 Covenants and rating step-ups live in each trust deed (one issuer's example)
- Rule: Financial covenants (אמות מידה פיננסיות), collateral and coupon step-ups are not set by law. Each series' trust deed sets them. Example: Discount Investment Corporation reported on 6 November 2023 that S&P Maalot had lowered its series י and יא bonds to ilBB+ (negative outlook), two notches below the deed's base rating of ilBBB. Under s. 5.2 of the trust deeds, the coupon rises by 0.25% a year for each notch below the base rating, up to 1% (series י) or 0.75% (series יא) a year. The total of rating and covenant step-ups may not exceed 1.75% a year above the original coupon (4.8% and 3.4%). After the downgrade both coupons rose by 0.5%, to 6.05% and 4.65% a year. The higher rate applies until full repayment or until the rating is raised, when it falls back (deed s. 5.2.2).
- Conditions / exceptions: One issuer's terms only, shown to illustrate the mechanism. Other series may have no step-up, other triggers, or none of these terms. The prospectus and trust deed are the only source for a given bond.
- Figures: as in the example (issuer report of 6 Nov 2023; not a market norm).
- Source: [חברת השקעות דיסקונט בע"מ, "שינוי בשיעור הריבית עקב ירידה בדירוג...", 6.11.2023](https://www.dic.co.il/media/uohnqq1j/ירידה-בדירוג-ועליה-בשיעור-הריבית-סדרה-י-וסדרה-יא.pdf), pp. 1 to 2 and note 1 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution
- Dimensions: `protection`, `distribution-policy`, `rate-reset` (proposed in GOV)
- Likely to change: no (the example); terms differ per series

### CORP-12 Issuer early redemption (secondary)
- Rule: Meitar's summary of the ISA staff position says most trust deeds let the issuer redeem early. In such a case the deed usually requires the issuer to pay the highest of three amounts: market value; the liability value (principal plus accrued interest and linkage); and the remaining cash flows discounted at a government-bond yield plus a spread set in the deed. The trustee must check the issuer's calculation independently.
- Conditions / exceptions: Secondary, and stated as what "usually" happens, not a rule. Whether a given series can be called early, and on what formula, is in its trust deed.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Meitar client update, 29 Oct 2024](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/The-Position-of-Israels-Securities-Authority-–-Trustee-Roles-of-Bondholders.pdf), p. 2 · checked 2026-10-22 · secondary
- Dimensions: `liquidity-lockup`, `real-vs-nominal`
- Likely to change: no

### CORP-13 Rating companies must register with the ISA
- Rule: The law is "חוק להסדרת פעילות חברות דירוג האשראי, התשע״ד–2014", last amended in תשפ"ד. "לא יעסוק אדם בדירוג אלא אם כן נרשם כחברת דירוג" (s. 2(א)). The ISA chair, or an authorised ISA employee, registers rating companies (s. 4). A rating company "תהיה עצמאית בביצוע פעולותיה" (s. 16(א)). Being paid by the issuer or another offering participant is not in itself a breach of independence (s. 16(ג)), but for each rating the company must publish who paid for it (s. 16(ד)(1)). The ISA supervises rating companies and can impose monetary sanctions (ss. 21, 24 to 27, 39 to 41).
- Conditions / exceptions: The law does not apply to internal, unpublished ratings, or to an institutional investor's internal model approved by the Capital Market Commissioner. The Minister may grant exemptions (s. 54). Whether a rating is mandatory for a public bond offering or a TASE listing was not found in any source read (open question 4). Points not in quotation marks come from a summarised reading.
- Figures: maximum sanction NIS 5,000,000 for a rating company by the administrative enforcement committee (s. 40(3); fixed in law).
- Source: [חוק להסדרת פעילות חברות דירוג האשראי, התשע"ד-2014 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%A4%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%99), ss. 1, 2, 4, 16, 21, 40, 54 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror)

### CORP-14 Israeli ratings are on a local scale
- Rule: Midroog's ratings are "חוות דעת צופות פני עתיד בקשר לכושר החזר האשראי ביחס למנפיקים מקומיים אחרים". Its long-term local scale runs Aaa.il, Aa.il, A.il, Baa.il, Ba.il, B.il, Caa.il, Ca.il, C.il. Each category from Aa.il to Caa.il has modifiers 1, 2 and 3, where 1 is the top of the category and 3 the bottom. Baa.il means moderate relative credit strength, and such issuers "עלולים להיות בעלי מאפיינים ספקולטיביים מסוימים". Ba.il and below "בעלי מאפיינים ספקולטיביים", with risk rising down the scale. C.il issuers are "במצב של כשל פירעון עם סיכויים קלושים להחזר קרן וריבית". The short-term scale is P-1.il, P-2.il, P-3.il, NP.il. A rating review (בחינת דירוג) signals a possible change in the near term.

Midroog also rates hybrid instruments with a "hyb" indicator and structured finance with "sf".

### CORP-15 What a rating agency counts as default, including a debt settlement
- Rule: Midroog counts three kinds of event as a default on a debt: (1) non-payment or late payment of principal or interest under the financing documents, except a non-payment cured within a contractual grace period; (2) legal proceedings over the issuer's default (for example a liquidation request, temporary liquidator, receivership or stay of proceedings) where it is reasonable that planned debt payments will stop or not be made; (3) a debt settlement or restructuring in which the issuer offers holders new securities, cash or assets amounting to a reduced obligation compared with the original, and the settlement is meant to help the issuer avoid default. Technical defaults cured quickly (usually within 1 to 2 business days) are not counted.
- Conditions / exceptions: This is one agency's definition, used for its own statistics. It is not a legal definition. The legal triggers for holders are in CORP-07 and CORP-08.

### CORP-18 Trading on TASE: unit, hours and last trading day
- Rule: The trading unit for bonds is NIS 1 face value (USD 1 or EUR 1 for bonds denominated in those currencies). Bonds, commercial paper and makam share one session timetable. Each phase starts at a time the TASE board sets within these ranges: pre-opening 08:00 to 10:00; opening auction 09:30 to 11:30; continuous trading; pre-close 15:00 to 18:10 Monday to Thursday and 12:00 to 13:35 Friday; closing auction 15:10 to 18:30 (Friday 12:10 to 13:45); trading at the closing price (TAL) ending by 18:45 (Friday 13:50). A corporate bond's last trading day is three trading days before its original or early full-redemption date. If the prospectus sets a record date for final redemption, it is three trading days before that date, whichever is earlier. Corporate bonds subject to US tax law stop trading ten days before redemption. On a bond's first trading day, the base price is the price set in the issue tender.
- Conditions / exceptions: The exact times in force come from TASE board decisions, which were not read. TASE trades Monday to Friday since 5 January 2026 (BRK-29). How prices are quoted, the settlement cycle and the record date for coupons were not found (open question 6).
- Figures: unit NIS 1 face value (guide update 130, 5.2.24); time ranges (update 141, 4.1.26); last trading day (update 144, 3.3.26). Set by TASE rules; change by rule amendment.
- Source: [TASE trading guide 274698](https://content.tase.co.il/media/mwsduioc/274698.pdf), פרק א' s. 1(ב) and s. 6(ב); פרק ב' s. 1(ב); פרק ג' s. 1(א)(2) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (TASE rule guidelines)

### CORP-20 Buying in a public offering: early commitments, uniform price, pro-rata
- Rule: Under the Securities (Manner of Offering Securities to the Public) Regulations 2007, an issuer may take early commitments ("התחייבות מוקדמת") from classified investors ("משקיע מסווג") before the prospectus is published. Each commitment is at least NIS 800,000. Their total is capped by offering size: 0% up to NIS 25 m; 80% from NIS 25 m to 200 m; 90% from NIS 200 m to 600 m; 95% above that. Any commission paid to classified investors must be "סביר, מקובל וזהה לכל המשקיעים המסווגים". In a uniform offer, every bidder may submit orders at up to three different prices and may withdraw until the order period ends. Orders above the uniform price are filled in full. Orders at the uniform price are filled pro rata ("לפי חלקה היחסי"). If the offer is oversubscribed more than five times, classified investors get 50% of their commitment.
- Conditions / exceptions: Points not in quotation marks come from a summarised reading of the regulations. The Wikisource text shows amendments only up to תשע"ו (2016), so later changes may be missing (open question 5). The regulations read do not say how an individual's order reaches the tender (through a TASE member), any minimum, or whether bond tenders are run on price or on the interest rate (open question 5). Banks list "handling an order in an offering" as a separate fee line (BRK-16).
- Figures: NIS 800,000 minimum commitment (NIS 400,000 for an R&D company); caps 0/80/90/95%; up to 3 prices; extra allocations up to 15% (regs. 8, 9). Fixed in the regulations as read.
- Source: [תקנות ניירות ערך (אופן הצעת ניירות ערך לציבור), התשס"ז-2007 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_ניירות_ערך_(אופן_הצעת_ניירות_ערך_לציבור)), regs. 1, 2, 4(א), 5, 6, 6א, 7, 8, 9 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror; currency unconfirmed)
- Dimensions: `minimum`, `eligibility`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: yes, if amended after 2016

### CORP-23 Costs of buying and holding (pointer)
- Rule: Corporate bonds and commercial paper are bought and held in a brokerage account. ### CORP-24 What protects the holder, and what does not
- Rule: The law gives the holder a right to demand money from the issuing body (CORP-01). Nothing read says the State or anyone else pays if the issuer does not: the state-payment clauses that cover makam and government bonds (GOV-02) do not apply. Collateral exists only if the trust deed provides it (s. 35ו(א) requires the deed to state any collateral). The trustee's role is to monitor and enforce, not to pay (CORP-06 to CORP-08). Separately, safekeeping of the bonds at the bank or broker is covered in `brokerage-account.md` (BRK-07 to BRK-13) and `regulators-and-protection.md`.
- Conditions / exceptions: None of the Israeli sources read describes an investor-compensation scheme.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Securities Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך), ss. 35א, 35ו(א), 35ח · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror); GOV-02 for the contrast
- Dimensions: `protection`, `credit-risk` (proposed)
- Likely to change: no

## Left out and why

- **Tax** (coupon withholding, linkage, gains, losses on default, accrued interest, convertibles): owned by `tax-tier2-other.md`. TXS-03 in `tax-securities-israeli.md` already covers the general rule for gains on unlinked bonds and commercial paper.
- **Structured bonds (איגרות חוב מובנות)**: TASE counts them separately (NIS 27.0 bn raised in 2025). Not researched; whether they belong here or in `fx-and-structured-deposits.md` is a question for the knowledge-base owner.
- **Foreign corporate bonds bought abroad, Eurobonds of Israeli issuers, US-listed bonds**: not part of the TASE corporate-bond market described by the sources; also need foreign-tax work.
- **Private placements and qualified-investor-only issues**: not open to the general public.
- **Bank contingent capital (CoCo) and other hybrid instruments in detail**: only the rating indicator was recorded (CORP-14). Product terms not researched.
- **Default history from press and law-firm articles**: the brief allows only official summaries, and none was found (open question 10).
- **Institutional investors' bond rules (CMA investment regulations, the Hodak committee guidelines)**: they concern pension and provident managers, not the saver's own choice.
- **Bond analysis (duration, spreads, yield-to-maturity formulas for corporates)**: the shared yield convention is in GOV-06; no official corporate-specific source was read.

## Proposed dimensions

- `credit-risk`: the payer is a company, not the State. Missed or cut payments are possible, and protection depends on the trust deed and the trustee (CORP-01, CORP-07, CORP-08, CORP-24).
- `issuer-call`: the issuer may redeem early under its trust deed, at a formula set there (CORP-12).
- `rating-step-up`: the coupon changes with the rating or with covenant breaches (CORP-11).
- Also used here, proposed in `government-bonds-and-makam.md`: `market-price-exit`, `rate-reset`.

## Graduation status (D2)

Corporate bonds stay **tier 2**. Against the D2 test:

- **Sourced record:** partly met. Still missing for a full record: an official list of rating agencies and trustees (open questions 1 and 2), how a retail order enters a public tender (5), price quotation and settlement (6), the Tel-Bond methodology (8), and any official default history (10).

Coupons by linkage, accrued interest, losses on default or settlement, and convertibles are all open from this file's side. *2026-10-24:* `tax-tier2-other.md` exists: coupons and gains by linkage compute as for government bonds (TX2-06, TX2-07); the default case is still open (TX2 open question 6) and accrued interest is shared with tier 1 (G-10). Ranking and remaining steps in `scratchpad/reports/r5-tier2.md`.
