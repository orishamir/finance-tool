## Summary

- **The service.** Portfolio management is "ביצוע עסקאות, לפי שיקול דעת, לחשבונם של אחרים". It needs an ISA licence. A licensed portfolio manager may also do investment advice or investment marketing, but only one of the two. Every large group whose page was read says it does marketing, not advice. Under the law, such a firm may prefer financial assets it is affiliated with, provided it discloses this.
- **Duties.** The law requires:
  - loyalty and care;
  - fitting the service and the trades to each client's needs and instructions;
  - a written agreement before service starts, including a power of attorney, the fee and its calculation, and the client's right to cancel at any time;
  - a detailed report at least quarterly;
  - records kept for seven years.
- **Fees.** The fee may not depend on the client's profit or on the number of trades. The manager may take no other benefit, apart from listed exceptions that need the client's prior written consent.
- **Assets.** Client assets stay in the client's own account at a bank or TASE member, under a power of attorney, apart from the manager's own assets.
- **What it costs.** In 2025 the ISA reports a weighted average direct fee of 0.50%. By portfolio size, direct-fee income as a share of assets was:
  - 0.51% for portfolios under NIS 0.5m;
  - 0.66% for NIS 0.5m to 1.5m;
  - 0.61% for NIS 1.5m to 5m;
  - 0.43% above NIS 5m.

  Small portfolios hold 74% of their value in mutual funds, which carry their own fees. Related-party assets were 11% of managed assets.
- **Digital offerings found on the providers' own pages.**
  - Meitav Manager: from NIS 25,000. It holds Meitav's own mutual funds only and charges no direct fee.
  - Phoenix's digital portfolio: from NIS 50,000.
  - Altshuler Shaham: a digital joining route. Its minimum is not stated.

  The traditional minimums stated on own pages are NIS 300,000 to 400,000.
- **No full list.** The 131 licensed firms cannot be listed, because the ISA register needs JavaScript.

## Rules

### MGD-01 What portfolio management is, and the one-of-two rule
- Rule: "ניהול תיקי השקעות" means "ביצוע עסקאות, לפי שיקול דעת, לחשבונם של אחרים" (s.1). Managing portfolios needs a portfolio-manager licence from the ISA (s.2(ב); see REG-08). A portfolio-manager licensee "רשאי לעסוק גם בייעוץ השקעות או בשיווק השקעות, ובאחד מאלה בלבד" (s.2(ג)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Every large group read here states which of the two it chose, and each chose marketing:
  - Meitav: מיטב ניהול תיקים בע"מ is engaged in "ניהול תיקי השקעות ובשיווק השקעות" and "ואינה עוסקת בייעוץ השקעות".
  - Altshuler Shaham: it provides portfolio management and investment marketing, not advice.
  - Phoenix / Excellence: its site describes Excellence as an investment marketer, not an investment adviser.

  For the saver, a "marketer" may prefer its own group's products (MGD-12). In 2025, 2,647 clients (2.8%) of portfolio-management firms received only advice or marketing from them, with the investment decision left to the client.
- Figures: 131 firms, end-2025 (REG-08 has the count and its source).

### MGD-02 Loyalty and care
- Rule: "בעל רשיון יפעל לטובת לקוחותיו באמונה ובשקידה". He may not prefer his own interests, or one client over another (s.11). Section 20 adds a general duty of care: take reasonable steps to protect clients' interests.
- Conditions / exceptions: For an affiliated manager, the preference for affiliated assets in s.16א(ב) applies "notwithstanding" s.11(א) (MGD-12).

### MGD-03 Suitability: fitting the service and the trades to the client
- Rule: The licensee must fit the advice or marketing, and "את אופי העסקאות שהוא מבצע עבורם לצרכיו ולהנחיותיו של כל לקוח", "ככל האפשר" (s.12). This follows a discussion with the client of:
  - investment goals;
  - financial situation, including securities and financial assets held;
  - other relevant circumstances, to the extent the client agrees to disclose them.

  The client's needs and instructions are written into the agreement (s.13(ב)(2)). They must be updated whenever the client reports a change, and at intervals the ISA sets (s.13(ד), (ד1)).
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - The ISA's directive on clarifying client needs ("הוראה לבעלי רישיון בקשר לבירור צרכיו והנחיותיו של הלקוח", new version 2021) is named in an ISA 2023 document. Its text was not read. Only a secondary summary was read: Funder, 26 Jul 2021, written by a former ISA supervisor.
  - Per that summary, the directive requires clarifying at least the investment goals, in terms of horizon, and the financial situation. In portfolio management, the licensee must derive the investment policy or benchmarks from the client's risk level. Updates are due within one to two years, or on a material change.
  - Treat these details as secondary until the ISA text is read (open question 2).
- Figures: none fixed in the law.

### MGD-04 A written agreement before service, with set contents
- Rule: "בעל רשיון יערוך הסכם בכתב עם לקוח וימסור לו העתק ממנו, קודם לתחילת מתן השירות" (s.13(א)).
- What every agreement must contain (s.13(ב)):
  - (1) the client's identification details;
  - (2) the client's needs and instructions;
  - (3) "שכר והחזר הוצאות שיחוייב בהם הלקוח ודרך חישובם";
  - (4) "קביעה כי הלקוח רשאי לבטל בכל עת את ההתקשרות";
  - (5) whether advice or marketing may be given by phone;
  - (6) that confidentiality yields to duties of disclosure under law;
  - (7) for a TASE-member licensee, that the agreement is subject to the exchange rules.
- What a portfolio-management agreement adds (s.13(ג)):
  - (1) a power of attorney setting the scope of authority and discretion, including whether it is a blind trust ("נאמנות עיוורת");
  - (2) whether credit may be taken for the client, and on what terms;
  - (3) the permitted types of securities and financial assets and their proportions, or that these are at the manager's discretion;
  - (4) authority to buy or sell away from the exchange price;
  - (5) for an affiliated or marketing manager, the s.16א(א) disclosures.
- Conditions / exceptions: An agreement clause that exempts the licensee from liability under the law, or limits it, is void (s.13(ה)). Providers confirm the agreement step:
  - Meitav: "שירותי ניהול תיקי השקעות בכפוף לחתימה על הסכם על פי דין".
  - Altshuler: a management agreement plus a power of attorney "as is customary at your bank".
- Figures: none

### MGD-05 Exit: the client may cancel at any time; the assets are already in the client's account
- Rule: Every agreement must state that the client may cancel the engagement at any time (s.13(ב)(4)). The assets are held in the client's own account (MGD-14). Ending the service therefore means ending the agreement and the power of attorney; the assets stay where they are. Moving them to another custodian is a separate brokerage transfer: see BRK-18 for the supervised price at banks, and `switching-and-transfers.md`.
- What providers state:
  - Meitav, private clients: an existing securities portfolio can be brought in without selling it.
  - Meitav Manager: "ניתן לבקש משיכה מלאה / חלקית בכל עת". The money is paid "עד 7 ימי עסקים מקבלת הבקשה", and only to the bank account it came from. Moving the Manager holdings in kind is not addressed on the page (open question 4).
  - IBI: "תיק ההשקעות שלך נזיל וניתן למשוך ממנו כסף בכל עת".
- Conditions / exceptions: No provision on termination fees was found in the law sections read (open question 13).
- Figures: 7 business days (Meitav Manager, own terms).

### MGD-06 Quarterly report to the client, and records
- Rule: "מנהל תיקים ימציא ללקוח, אחת לשלושה חודשים לפחות, דין וחשבון מפורט על הרכב תיק ההשקעות שלו" (s.26(א)). The report covers:
  - the portfolio's composition;
  - the account balance;
  - fees and expenses charged, including payments to controlling or controlled persons;
  - every trade since the last report, marking special-risk trades and credit transactions.
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - Blind-trust clients are reported to as their agreement says (s.26(ב)).
  - More detail must be given on request unless unreasonable (s.26(ג)).
  - Reports may not mislead (s.26(ד)).
  - The manager must record every transaction for each client and keep the records for seven years (s.25).
- Providers confirm quarterly reports: Meitav ("דיווח רבעוני על מצב התיק"); Altshuler ("דיווח מידי רבעון"). Meitav Manager promises only "דיווחים תקופתיים".
- Figures: at least every 3 months; records kept 7 years. Fixed in law.

### MGD-08 Fee rules: no performance fee and no per-trade pay for the manager
- Rule: "לא יתנה מנהל תיקים את שכרו ברווח שהפיק הלקוח מעסקה, או במספר העסקאות שבוצעו עבור הלקוח" (s.24). The fee and expense reimbursement, and how they are calculated, must be in the agreement (s.13(ב)(3)).

### MGD-09 No other benefits to the manager, apart from consented commission rebates and listed exceptions
- Rule: A licensee, or anyone on its behalf, may not receive a benefit, directly or indirectly, in connection with advice, marketing or a transaction (s.17(א)). The only pay allowed is the fee and expenses in the agreement.
- Exceptions (s.17(ב)):
  - (3) a portfolio manager may receive a refund of buy or sell commissions "מאת חבר בורסה", for a specific trade or set of trades, if the client agreed in advance, in writing, to the refund and its rate;
  - (4) someone who is not a marketer may receive a distribution fee from a fund manager, with the same prior written consent to the fee and its rate, and within the Joint Investment Trust Law s.82(ג) rules;
  - (2) a marketer's benefit relating to assets it is affiliated with;
  - (1), (5) and (6) cover securities issued by the licensee, study-fund commissions for advisers, and analysis work.
- Conditions / exceptions: From the fund side, no distribution fee may be charged on units a portfolio manager buys for a client under a management agreement (MF-25).
- Figures:
  - The ISA reports NIS 1.6 m (0.13% of income) of commission rebates ("החזרי עמלות") to portfolio managers in 2025. It defines them as amounts a TASE member passes to the manager out of commissions clients paid it for trades the manager made.
  - "Other income" ("הכנסות אחרות") was NIS 112 m (9.4%). This is income from managing the portfolio that is not a management fee, for example "רכישת מוצרים מובנים עבור לקוחות; עמלות המרת מט"ח".

### MGD-10 What a managed portfolio costs: the fee layers
- Rule: A managed portfolio can carry up to four layers of cost. The ISA's income categories (p.17) and the providers' pages name them:
  - (a) **the direct management fee** ("דמי הניהול הישירים"). The ISA defines it as "דמי הניהול המתקבלים ישירות מהלקוח בהתאם להסכם ניהול התיקים". It is a percentage of the portfolio's value.
  - (b) **trading commissions and custody fees** charged by the custodian bank or TASE member under its own tariff (BRK-16, BRK-24).
  - (c) **management fees inside the mutual funds** the portfolio holds (MF-16, MF-19).
  - (d) **other manager income**: structured-product purchases, FX conversion, and commission rebates (MGD-09).
- What providers state about these layers:
  - Meitav, private clients: the bank's buy and sell commission falls as the portfolio grows. Bank custody fees are cut by 50% to 75% for Meitav clients. In portfolios built on Meitav mutual funds, "דמי הניהול נגבים רק דרך הקרנות" and there are no buy or sell commissions.
  - Meitav portfolio-management page, on its mutual-fund portfolio: "אין דמי ניהול ישירים".
  - Meitav Manager: "העלות היחידה ... היא דמי הניהול שנגבים בקרנות הנאמנות המוחזקות בתיק שלכם", "ללא דמי משמרת, ללא עמלת קנייה או מכירה וללא דמי ניהול".
  - IBI and Altshuler: the fee is set by portfolio size and nature and agreed in the agreement. Neither publishes rates.
- Conditions / exceptions: A "no direct fee" portfolio of the group's own funds is paid through layer (c). The group's fund manager earns that fee, which is the affiliation case in MGD-12. The fee rates of the funds used by Meitav Manager are not given on its page (open question 4).
- Figures: see MGD-11.

### MGD-12 Affiliated assets: preference allowed with disclosure, and not a "conflict of interest"
- Rule: As a base rule, in advice or portfolio management "לא יעדיף בעל רשיון ניירות ערך או נכסים פננסיים שלו", or those of a related company (s.16(א)). Section 16א applies to marketers, to portfolio managers that are related to an institutional body or a marketer, and to portfolio managers that market. These may prefer an affiliated asset over a comparable unaffiliated one, despite ss.11(א) and 16(א), "ובלבד שעמדו בכל דרישות הגילוי כלפי הלקוח" (s.16א(ב)). The disclosures are (s.16א(א)):
  - notice that the firm markets and does not advise;
  - which institutional bodies it is affiliated with;
  - the affiliation, its nature and the preference, given orally and in writing before engagement and published on its website.

  Affiliation is not treated as a conflict of interest for s.15 (s.16א(ג)). Under s.15, a licensee who learns of a conflict must tell the client and avoid acting in it unless the client consents in advance, in writing or by recorded call.
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - A portfolio manager may not buy for a client securities underwritten by a related company, or within set periods by an affiliate, without the client's prior written consent. It must report within 30 days and buy on the exchange or at no more than the exchange price (s.16(ב) to (ד)).
  - Meitav's disclosure page applies the rules above. Its portfolio manager has affiliations with assets issued or managed by group companies, including מיטב קרנות נאמנות בע"מ. "קיימת אפשרות שהיא תעדיף את הנכסים הפיננסיים שיש לה זיקה אליהן". Group companies "עשויות לקבל טובת הנאה". And "בהתאם להוראות החוק, זיקה של החברה לנכסים הפיננסים לא תיחשב כניגוד עניינים".
  - Meitav Manager is managed through Meitav mutual funds only ("מנהל הקרן הינו מיטב קרנות נאמנות בע"מ").

### MGD-15 If the manager fails: assets sit at the custodian; the manager must keep equity and security against negligence and embezzlement
- Rule: Client assets are in the client's account at the custodian (MGD-14, REG-09), so the manager's own failure does not put them in the manager's estate. Protection then depends on the custodian: a bank (REG-04 to REG-06; no deposit insurance), a non-bank TASE member (BRK-07, BRK-08), or a foreign custodian (BRK-14).
- What the licensee must maintain:
  - **Security for clients.** Under the Equity and Insurance regulations, a licensee must arrange or deposit security ("בטוחה") "לכיסוי חבותו בשל מעשה או מחדל רשלני כלפי לקוח". For a licensed corporation, the security must also cover employee "מעילה באמון" (reg. 3(א)).
  - **Its form.** Security may be insurance, a cash deposit, a securities deposit or a bank guarantee (reg. 1). Its scope is set by the licensee "considering its activity" (reg. 3(ב)); no minimum amount was found. The board approves it, and it is reviewed yearly (reg. 3(ג), (ד)). It stays valid for one year after the licensee stops operating (reg. 7(ב)).
  - **Equity.** A licensed corporation's equity may not fall "מסכום של מאתיים אלף שקלים חדשים", CPI-linked from November 1998 (regs. 2, 10).
  - **No trading below the floor.** A licensed corporation may not operate while below the insurance and equity conditions (law s.20ג).
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - No Israeli investor-compensation scheme was found (BRK summary).
  - The deposit-insurance bill would exclude portfolio managers as claimants (REG-05).
  - The equity figure is from the regulations as read on Wikisource through a summarising fetch, which reported no separate tier by assets managed. Verify this, and the indexed amount (open question 7). This partly answers REG open question 19.
- Figures: NIS 200,000 equity floor, CPI-linked from Nov 1998; the indexed amount was not computed. Security stays valid 1 year after the licensee stops operating.

### MGD-21 Online joining and algorithmic risk-setting (directive on technological means)
- Rule: The ISA's "הוראה לבעלי רישיון בקשר למתן שירותים תוך שימוש באמצעים טכנולוגיים", issued under s.28(ב) of the Advice Law, came into force in 2016 and sets how advice, marketing and portfolio-management services may be given through technological means. Its current text (file of December 2024, with the amendment in force from 21 August 2023) lets a licensee:
  - run the needs process and sign the agreement online (ch. ב', ss.11-13); the process cannot be completed until the client has answered every question, and if the client refuses a question or the answers contradict each other, it completes only after a conversation with a licensee (s.14);
  - set the client's risk level and investment policy with an algorithm (ch. ג'), provided the appointed licensee and the serving licensee understand its methodology and the client gets an explanation of it (ss.15-17); a client who wants a different policy than the algorithm set completes the process only after a conversation with a licensee (s.18);
  - run the ongoing service with an algorithm (ch. ד'); a portfolio managed by algorithm by a licensee related to a bank may hold only mutual-fund units (foreign funds permitted for offer in Israel included), ETFs, deposits and cash (s.21).
- Conditions / exceptions: A corporation's board must approve the technological means and its use, with the approval kept for 7 years (s.6); a licensee keeps a retrievable record of each process for seven years (s.8); the licensee is liable to the client for damage caused by the technological means and its insurance must cover it (ss.4-5). Section 9 is marked cancelled from 7 August 2022; the "update at least every 12 months" that a secondary summary (Funder, 27/07/2021) described is not in the current text. The ISA staff's March 2023 proposal amended chapter ה' (signals services) and chapter ו' (social trading); the amendment's start is 21 August 2023. Re-sourced 2026-10-26 (open-questions audit): the record rested on the 2021 secondary summary and the 2023 draft until the ISA's own file was read; the address came from the glossary research of the same day.
- Figures: in force 2016; amendment in force 21.8.2023; records kept 7 years.
- Source: [רשות ניירות ערך: הוראה לבעלי רישיון בקשר למתן שירותים תוך שימוש באמצעים טכנולוגיים](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-6283705a39f5d/horaa7822.pdf), p.8, ss.6, 8, 11-13; p.9, s.14 "ההליך יתבצע כך שלא ניתן יהיה להשלימו מבלי שהלקוח השיב לכל השאלות הנכללות בשאלון בירור הצרכים והמטרות של הלקוח", ch. ג' ss.15-18; p.9, s.21; p.4, note 1 (start of the 2023 amendment) · checked 2026-10-26 · primary

### MGD-22 Qualified clients (לקוח כשיר): an individual can opt into a lighter-protection category
- Rule: A "לקוח כשיר" is a client listed in the First Schedule at the time of engagement (s.1). An individual qualifies only with prior written consent to be treated as one, and if two of three tests are met:
  - "השווי הכולל של מזומנים, פיקדונות, נכסים פיננסיים וניירות ערך" above NIS 12 million;
  - capital-market expertise, or at least one year in a professional role that needs it;
  - at least 30 trades a quarter on average over the previous four quarters, not counting trades a portfolio manager made for them.
- Conditions / exceptions: Which duties do not apply to a qualified client was not established in this pass. One example elsewhere is BRK-07, where the foreign-custody warning does not apply (open question 11). Not relevant to the founder's profile; recorded because the term appears in agreements.
- Figures: NIS 12 million and 30 trades a quarter. The NIS amount is as shown on Wikisource, which carries an amounts update (י"פ תשפ"ו, 2532). The update mechanism for this schedule was not established.

## Left out and why

- **Tax treatment of managed portfolios** (capital gains inside the client's account, any VAT on fees, Meitav Manager's statement that no tax is paid on trades inside funds): `tax-tier2-other.md`. Never stated from memory.
- **Investment advice (ייעוץ השקעות) by banks and advisers, signals services, social trading**: not portfolio management. The 2023 ISA draft on signals was used only for the directive's in-force date.
- **Hedge funds in trust, structured products and private funds offered inside managed portfolios**: MF-53 and `fx-and-structured-deposits.md`. They are named here only where they set a fee or minimum on a managed-portfolio product.
- **Corporate, institutional and US-person portfolios**: not for the site's audience.
- **Board and governance rules for large portfolio managers (ss.24א to 24יא) and the sanctions schedules**: internal governance and enforcement, which change nothing a saver chooses.
- **Foreign robo-advisers used directly from Israel**: no Israeli register covers them; see BRK-06 for the section 49A permit route.
- **Per-firm fee comparisons from press or comparison sites**: secondary, not tariffs (D4). Supermarker is used only as a lead list.
- **Search budget**: 7 web searches were used. The remaining gaps are recorded above, not filled.

## Proposed dimensions

- `conflict-of-interest`: whether the provider is paid by, or belongs to the group of, the products it puts the saver into. Examples are affiliated funds and hosting (MGD-12, MGD-13).
- `fee-fund-underlying`: fees charged inside funds held by a wrapper or a managed portfolio, on top of the wrapper's own fee (MGD-10, MGD-17). This can be read as an instance of `fee-<type>`.

## Graduation status

- **Known (primary or institution):**
  - the legal basis and the manager's duties (MGD-01 to MGD-09, MGD-14, MGD-15);
  - ISA fee averages by portfolio size, which give the D4 regulator reference level for this provider class (MGD-11);
  - fee layers and fund share by size (MGD-10, MGD-17);
  - affiliation and hosting scale and fees (MGD-12, MGD-13);
  - custody and protection, which follow the custodian (MGD-14, MGD-15, REG-09);
  - one digital product with full public terms, Meitav Manager (MGD-19).
- **Open, and blocking tier 1:**
  - (a) ~~tax treatment in `tax-tier2-other.md`, including VAT on fees~~ closed 2026-10-24: the portfolio is taxed as the securities it holds (TX2-15) and VAT on the fee is sourced (TX2-17); only TX2 open question 11 (fees in a year with no gains) and TXS open question 12 remain, neither a block;
  - (b) no enumerable provider list (ISA register);
  - (c) no published per-firm tariffs for traditional portfolios, so the workbench (3.4) could show only the ISA average and "entered by you";
  - (d) the ISA suitability and technology directives have not been read in the original. *2026-10-26 (open-questions audit):* the technology directive is now read in the ISA's own file (MGD-21); the needs-clarification directive is still unread (open question 2, narrowed).
- **Recommendation:** stays tier 2. The "not covered yet" page can state the duties, the custody model, the ISA averages by size, the fund-fee layer, the affiliation rules, and Meitav Manager's terms, with the open items above. *2026-10-24:* closest of the nine tier-2 instruments to graduation (ranking in `scratchpad/reports/r5-tier2.md`): the tax side computes, the provider list exists (MGD-23), and the record can be drafted as a brokerage account plus a direct fee layer (`scratchpad/reports/tier2-drafts/managed-portfolio.json`). Remaining: (b) which of the 129 licensees take retail clients and at what minimum (browser pass over their sites), (c) fees entered by the person or the ISA average (MGD-11) under D4, (d) the ISA directives in the original, and two method formulas (the direct fee with VAT; the fee-as-loss case) that `docs/method.md` does not have yet.
- **Fit with D11:** robo products set a risk level by questionnaire. The site does not adopt such a score; per D11 the person sets their own loss filter. The UX report (`research/discovery/ux-landscape.md`, rows "Robo-advisor questionnaires" and "Questionnaire / robo") records why.
