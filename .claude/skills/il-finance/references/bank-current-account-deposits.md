## Summary

Bank deposits and current accounts are supervised by the BoI Banking Supervision Department. Israel has no deposit insurance law; a private bill to create a state guarantee for deposits at small banks was tabled on 2 Feb 2026 and has not moved past "placed on the table for preliminary discussion" (BANK-23).
What a bank must tell you about a deposit is set by the 1992 disclosure rules (agreement contents, renewal and maturity notices, break forms, statements, the deposit rate board, advertising) and, since 1 April 2025, by directive 447: a uniform "concentrated publication" of rates for eight standard shekel deposit types and for current-account credit balances, on the website, in the app and in branches, with a search tool (BANK-02 to BANK-14).
Directive 447A (published 9 Apr 2025, in force from October 2025) makes banks that sell money-market funds or makam show them next to deposits under the heading "מוצרי שוק הכסף", with no product favoured, and show each customer all such holdings in one place (BANK-15).
Since 1 Dec 2025 every bank other than a small one must let an individual open a shekel deposit without a current account ("closed system"): money comes only from, and returns only to, the person's own account at another bank (BANK-21). Since late 2025 a bank must notify an individual whose current-account credit balance stays above NIS 15,000 for a quarter, and point to alternatives (BANK-20).
Deposit terms that change the outcome: fixed vs variable (prime-based) rate, CPI linkage, period, exit points, whether and at what cost a deposit can be broken, auto-renewal, amount tiers. These are product terms, not law; banks publish them in their 447 tables (BANK-25 to BANK-28).
A competition-authority declaration of the five large banking groups as a "concentration group", with deposit directives, was made in May 2026 and is under appeal (BANK-24).

## Rules

### BANK-01 A bank may not unreasonably refuse a deposit or a current account
- Rule: A banking corporation may not unreasonably refuse to accept a monetary deposit in Israeli or foreign currency, or to open and run a current account in Israeli currency as long as the account is in credit and the customer meets the account agreement. There is no duty to give credit.
- Conditions / exceptions: Setting unreasonable conditions counts as refusal (s.2(b)). Anti-money-laundering identification rules can still be a barrier, which is why the closed system exists (BANK-21).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Banking (Customer Service) Law 1981, consolidated text on BoI site (version 10/11, marked unofficial)](https://boi.org.il/media/y3akar3j/חוק-הבנקאות-שירות-ללקוח-התשמא-1981.pdf), s.2(a)(1)-(2), 2(b) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (unofficial consolidation). Restated in [BoI directive 417, version 4 (5/24)](https://boi.org.il/media/d10jeelv/h2782.pdf), s.1(a) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: eligibility, regulator
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-02 What a deposit agreement must state
- Rule: An agreement to deposit money, or the documents completing the deposit, must state: the amount; the period and repayment terms including repayment dates and renewal terms (if renewal is for a different period, that period); the interest rate, its calculation periods and payment date, and, if the bank may change it, the conditions for change; for a linked deposit, the linkage type and base, the linkage rate, whether linkage also applies to the interest, and any minimum or maximum time on which linkage depends.
- Conditions / exceptions: For deposits longer than one year the agreement must also state: restrictions on transferring the deposit; other benefits and their conditions; for deposits under the Savings Encouragement Law 1956, the tax-exemption terms; for deposits made from time to time (savings plans), the deposit schedule and limits on amounts and dates; for deposits that allow early repayment, the early-repayment terms.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Banking Rules (Customer Service) (Fair Disclosure and Delivery of Documents) 1992, consolidated (amendments to 30.12.2014), BoI copy](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.7(a), 7(b) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: liquidity-lockup, real-vs-nominal, regulator, reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-03 Written agreement for deposits over one year; copy to the customer
- Rule: An agreement for a fixed-term deposit for a period over one year, and an agreement to open and run a current account, must be drawn up in writing and the customer must be able to read it before signing. A copy must be given to the customer (or made available in the online account if the customer agreed).
- Conditions / exceptions: The supervisor may exempt certain account types from the signature requirement (s.3(a1)).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.3(a)(2)-(3), 4 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-04 Interest is quoted as an annual rate; effective annual rate for unlinked shekel deposits
- Rule: Wherever the rules require an interest rate it means an annual tariff rate unless stated otherwise. For unlinked credit and shekel deposits, the effective annual rate (ריבית מתואמת שנתית) must also be stated, unless interest payment dates are not fixed in advance. For linked deposits the bank must say whether linkage applies only when the index rises or also when it falls.
- Conditions / exceptions: The effective annual rate formula is in Schedule A of the rules (compounding by the number of interest calculation periods per year, or by the deposit length in days on a 365-day year).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.1 (definition "ריבית מתואמת שנתית"), s.2(a), (b), (d), Schedule A · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: real-vs-nominal, reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-05 Auto-renewing deposits and the renewal notice
- Rule: A deposit renewed automatically under the customer's standing instructions is not treated as a new deposit for the agreement-content rule (BANK-02). For deposits of one year or more, the bank must notify the renewal date no earlier than 45 and no later than 21 days before it, and say that the rate for the renewal is on the branch board from the renewal day.
- Conditions / exceptions: Directive 447 Annex A shows, for deposits under one year, whether automatic renewal exists and how many renewal periods are possible (BANK-14). Institution practice seen: Yahav's 447 table lists renewal limits per product (for example "עד 180 יום" for the daily deposit); Mercantile's deposits for non-customers are not auto-renewed.
- Figures: 21 to 45 days before renewal; fixed in the rules.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.9 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Yahav rate board 13_26](https://www.bank-yahav.co.il/media/zidh1r2r/deposit-interest.pdf), p.6 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution. [Mercantile, deposits without account](https://www.mercantile.co.il/private/investments-savings/deposit-savings/deposits-without-account/) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution
- Dimensions: liquidity-lockup, reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-06 Breaking a deposit: the bank's consent, the form, and the cost
- Rule: If the agreement lets the depositor withdraw outside the agreed dates, the request is made on a dedicated form that shows the nearest date on which the money can be withdrawn without changing the original terms, or the principles for breaking that deposit type. If early withdrawal was not agreed in advance and the bank agrees to it, the form must detail the changes to the original terms or the exact consequences for that deposit. Directive 447 requires each standard deposit type to show one of: breakable without penalty; breakable with penalty, with the penalty mechanism described; not breakable.
- Conditions / exceptions: Whether a deposit can be broken at all, and the penalty, are product terms set by the bank. Institution example (Discount): a break needs the bank's approval; the penalty is set on the day of the break from a daily-updated "borrowers' rate table" according to deposit type and remaining period to the next exit point or maturity; it is never less than the deposit rate plus a break margin; the customer gets the lower of the principal and the discounted break value, so a break can mean no interest and a loss of principal; deposits of one year or more cannot be partly broken in the first year. Yahav's 447 table shows "אין אפשרות לשבירה" for all its standard deposits.
- Figures: none in law.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.10 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Directive 447 (5/24)](https://www.boi.org.il/media/orgfwntb/447.pdf), Annex A, column "אפשרויות לשבירת פיקדון" · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Discount, money-market products page, "הבהרות"](https://www.discountbank.co.il/private/savings-investments/savings-interest/) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution. [Yahav rate board 13_26](https://www.bank-yahav.co.il/media/zidh1r2r/deposit-interest.pdf), p.6 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution
- Dimensions: liquidity-lockup, fee-exit, protection
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-07 Notice before a deposit of one year or more matures
- Rule: Between 45 and 21 days before a fixed-term deposit of one year or more is repaid (if it is not renewing), the bank must notify the amount standing to the depositor's credit (with its components if not final), the date the account will be credited, and the consequences of not withdrawing on time.
- Conditions / exceptions: Does not apply to deposits of a year or more repaid in instalments no more than three months apart. Schedule E exempts notices under s.11 for amounts up to the exempt sum.
- Figures: exempt sum NIS 1,000 (Schedule E, s.34); the supervisor may change Schedule E amounts by notice in Reshumot (s.35). Date of the figure: as in the consolidated text to 2014; later change not checked (open question 3).
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.11, 34, 35, Schedule E · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: liquidity-lockup, reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-08 Notices at interest crediting and after repayment; statements
- Rule: Near each interest payment or crediting on a deposit the bank notifies the rate, the period and the amount (at most monthly if interest is credited more often). Within 30 days after each fixed-term deposit is repaid it notifies the amount, interest amount, rate, linkage differentials, the indices or rates used, and the period. For fixed-term deposits the bank sends a notice at the end of the deposit period or a statement at least once a year, showing amount, interest and its period, rate (per amount if several), whether fixed or variable (if variable, how and to what it changed), linkage differentials and indices.
- Conditions / exceptions: Post-repayment notice is not needed for deposits repaid within three months if the details were in the deposit document, or for unlinked deposits if they were in the pre-maturity notice. Schedule E exempts the s.8 interest-crediting notice up to NIS 75.
- Figures: NIS 75 exempt sum (Schedule E); 30 days.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.8, 12, 30(c), 30(e), Schedule E · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-09 Current-account credit interest: what must be displayed
- Rule: A bank that pays interest on current-account credit balances must display in branches and on its website: whether joining is automatic or needs a separate signature; the minimum and maximum amounts on which interest is paid, if any; the rate; and the crediting dates. On request it must tell the customer the rate, period and amount credited.
- Conditions / exceptions: Under directive 447 the current-account row must show rate type (variable or fixed), "how to join and conditions for receiving the interest", and the offered nominal annual rate; a bank that pays none must show "לא מוצעת ריבית על יתרת זכות בחשבון". Institution example (Yahav, board effective 06/10/2026): credit interest is a time-limited conditional benefit (salary or allowance deposit, card spending, daily balance cap, paid quarterly after tax, granted automatically).
- Figures: none in law.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.13 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Directive 447](https://www.boi.org.il/media/orgfwntb/447.pdf), s.4(a), Annex A · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Yahav rate board 13_26](https://www.bank-yahav.co.il/media/zidh1r2r/deposit-interest.pdf), p.7 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution
- Dimensions: eligibility, reporting-obligation, liquidity-lockup
- Likely to change: yes: several private bills on credit-balance interest exist in the 25th Knesset (BANK-29); the competition-authority directives (BANK-24) target deposit pricing.

### BANK-10 The deposit rate board in branches and online
- Rule: Every branch and the bank's website must show a board titled "שיעורי ריבית על פקדונות" listing the minimum nominal rates the bank pays on every deposit type with a term up to one year. Where the minimum rate applies only to a few deposits, the rate for a "common deposit" (פיקדון שכיח) must also be shown. For each type the tariff rate and the period it applies to are shown.
- Conditions / exceptions: Directive 447 builds the uniform format on top of this (BANK-13).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.14 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-11 Advertising deposits
- Rule: A bank that advertises an offer to accept deposits must include the deposit terms, including the offered effective annual rate, the linkage type and base, and the deposit period.
- Conditions / exceptions: Directive 447 and 447A add display rules for the bank's own channels (BANK-13, BANK-15). Institution disclaimers seen: rate tables are "not an offer", terms in the deposit agreement bind (Leumi, Mizrahi); examples in ads are theoretical (FIBI).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.33B (33ב) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-12 Advance notice of changes to account terms and tariff
- Rule: The bank must notify all customers of changes to account terms, including changes to fee tariffs published under the 2008 fee rules, at least two weeks before the change. Changes to the base rate, the maximum risk premium, the maximum excess-credit rate and the credit allocation fee must be notified at least three business days before the change (if such a change stems from law or a competent authority's directive, on the date of the change, if possible). A change in the customers' favour may be notified up to three business days after it.
- Conditions / exceptions: Does not apply to interest types whose rates change daily. For individual customers with benefits granted for over three months, the end of the benefit must be notified at least two weeks ahead under these rules; the Banking (Customer Service) Law now requires 21 days for banking benefits (BANK-19).
- Figures: two weeks; three business days.
- Source: [Disclosure rules 1992 (BoI copy)](https://boi.org.il/media/rvzi1ngd/151.pdf), s.5(a)(1)-(3), 5(a)(6), 5(b)(1) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: fee-change-over-time, reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: no

### BANK-13 Directive 447: concentrated publication of deposit and credit-balance rates (since 1 April 2025)
- Rule: Every banking corporation must publish, as a concentrated publication, the minimum rates it is bound to pay on deposits and current-account credit balances. The standard shekel deposit types come first, in the wording and format of Annex A, under "שיעורי ריבית על סוגי הפיקדונות השקליים המקובלים ועל יתרות זכות בחשבון"; any other deposit types the bank offers follow under "שיעורי ריבית על סוגי פיקדונות נוספים", showing at least the Annex A characteristics with adjustments. Rates are annual nominal percentages, correct at display time and before capital-gains tax. For variable-rate deposits the rate column shows both the anchor rate and the spread and their result. Different amount ranges with different rates go on separate rows. The publication must carry a prominent link to the BoI comparative interest information, explain the deposit process clearly, and be timely, accessible and prominent. It must appear at least in every branch, on the website (public site and personal area) and in the app (layout may be adapted for the app). The bank must also offer, at least on the website and in the app, a search tool that lets customers filter deposit types by their own parameters; search results follow the same display rules.
- Conditions / exceptions: Applies to banking corporations under the Banking (Customer Service) Law. A bank that offers only one deposit type, or only credit-balance interest, is bound only by the display-format section (s.3(b)). A bank need not list Annex A types it does not offer. The circular says the list of standard types came from a supervision mapping and will be updated as needed.
- Figures: effective 1 April 2025 (circular s.11). Version 1, circular 2781-06, 20 May 2024.
- Source: [Directive 447 "פרסום שיעורי ריבית על פיקדונות ועל יתרות זכות בחשבון" (5/24)](https://www.boi.org.il/media/orgfwntb/447.pdf), s.1-8 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Circular 2781-06, 20 May 2024](https://boi.org.il/media/nxzbaarp/h2781.pdf), s.7-11 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. In force and "implemented in practice" per [BoI press statement, 6 May 2026](https://www.boi.org.il/media/pcuny0hd/עמדת-בנק-ישראל-בנוגע-להכרזת-הממונה-על-התחרות-על-חמש-הקבוצות-הבנקאיות-כקבוצת-ריכוז-ומתן-הוראות-בתחום-הפיקדונות.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: reporting-obligation, regulator
- Likely to change: yes: the circular says the standard list may be updated; whether 447 has been amended since 5/24 is open (open question 2).

### BANK-14 Directive 447 Annex A: the standard deposit taxonomy
- Rule: Annex A fixes eight standard shekel deposit types by period: פיקדון יומי, פיקדון שבועי, פיקדון חודשי, פיקדון ל-3 חודשים, פיקדון ל-6 חודשים, פיקדון לשנה, פיקדון לשנתיים, פיקדון ל-3 שנים. For each the bank shows: rate type (משתנה/קבועה); amount range (טווח סכום להפקדה); break options (קיימת - ללא קנס שבירה / קיימת - עם קנס שבירה + פירוט מנגנון הקנס / אין אפשרות שבירה); offered nominal annual rate (שיעור הריבית המוצעת), which is the minimum rate the bank pays for that type; and the common-deposit rate where the minimum applies only to a few deposits. Types up to six months also show renewal terms (automatic renewal at end of term: yes/no; number of possible renewal periods). Types of one year and over also show linkage (לא צמוד / צמוד למדד המחירים לצרכן) and exit points (whether they exist; their frequency). The current-account row shows rate type, how to join and conditions, and the offered rate.
- Conditions / exceptions: Linkage is a column only for one-year-and-over types; the shorter types are unlinked in the template. Institution example of a full Annex A table: Yahav board 11_26 (24/09/2026), which also lists CPI-linked savings ("חסכון אודם") with "+ הצמדה למדד".
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Directive 447](https://www.boi.org.il/media/orgfwntb/447.pdf), Annex A and notes 1-2 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Circular 2781-06](https://boi.org.il/media/nxzbaarp/h2781.pdf), explanation to s.4 and Annex A (exit points for one year and over, renewal for short deposits) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. - Dimensions: liquidity-lockup, real-vs-nominal, minimum, reporting-obligation
- Likely to change: yes (list may be updated by the supervisor)

### BANK-15 Directive 447A: money-market products shown together (deposits, money-market funds, makam)
- Rule: A bank through which customers can order money-market fund units or buy makam must publish concentrated information on deposits (per 447), money-market funds and makam under the heading "מוצרי שוק הכסף". The first filter is the desired investment period and currency, and the customer can choose among deposits, money-market funds and makam. The top of the publication must explain that its purpose is to put a range of products in front of the customer and that the products differ. No product may be favoured (for example by text size or emphasis). The bank must explain clearly how to buy funds and makam. Shekel and FX money-market funds are shown in the Annex A format, by period in ascending order of the next investment date; fixed-dates (renewing) funds are shown only if the manager reported an expected yield. Makam series are shown in the Annex B format in ascending order of days to redemption. Fund and makam data are updated at least daily. In the personal area on the website and in the app the bank must show each customer up-to-date consolidated information on all their holdings in deposits, money-market funds and makam, without favouring any product. The bank must link to the Israel Securities Authority's general information on money-market funds.
- Conditions / exceptions: Applies only to banks where units can be ordered or makam bought. Fund data are as reported by the manager; the bank acts as a conduit. Expected net yield is the manager's estimate, not guaranteed, before capital-gains tax and bank fees, after management and trustee fees. Makam yield shown is gross, without buy/sell fees and tax. Choosing a period over one year shows no fund or makam data (circular explanation). The regulatory impact report says the directive was prepared in cooperation with the ISA.
- Figures: published 9 April 2025 (circular 2813-06). Effective six months after publication; sections 19-20 (personal holdings view) and the public-site display requirement ten months after publication (circular s.12). The regulatory impact report gives "החל מ02/10/25 (כניסה מדורגת)". A press report says the start of most sections was deferred to 19.10.2025 (secondary; BoI deferral circular not read, open question 1). BoI said on 6 May 2026 that 447A is "implemented in practice".
- Source: [Directive 447A "פרסום מידע מרוכז על קרנות כספיות ומק"מ" (04/25)](https://boi.org.il/media/gcmk52bw/447a.pdf), s.1-20, Annexes A-B · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Circular 2813-06, 9 April 2025](https://boi.org.il/media/rall4hl2/h2813.pdf), s.5-12 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Regulatory impact report for 447A, 7 April 2025](https://boi.org.il/media/1gmhymzy/202511.pdf), pp.1-7 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. ### BANK-16 Fee tariff: closed list, full and reduced tariffs, where shown
- Rule: A bank may charge fees only for services in the full tariff set by the Governor (or services added with the supervisor's approval), and for customer types with a reduced tariff only for services in that reduced tariff. The full tariff (First Schedule of the 2008 fee rules) is divided into parts: 1 current account, 2 information and alerts, 3 credit, 4 securities, 5 foreign currency, 6 debit cards, 7 foreign trade, 8 futures and options, 9 special services, 10 diamonds, 11 third-party expenses, 12 card acquiring, 13 clearing services, 14 payment apps, 15 financial information aggregation. Reduced tariffs exist for current account, housing loans, debit cards and card acquiring. The bank must display the full tariff and reduced tariffs in branches, deliver them on request in branches, on its website and by direct channels, and give the customer the relevant reduced tariff when service starts.
- Conditions / exceptions: The full tariff has no part for deposits or savings plans; the cost of breaking a deposit is a deposit term (BANK-06), not a tariff fee. Items marked "(בפיקוח)" in the schedule (including the basic track price and single teller and direct-channel actions) are price-controlled; the figures are a data-track item. Per-bank publication locations are listed under Institutions.
- Figures: none here (tariff figures go to `data/`).
- Source: [Banking Rules (Customer Service) (Fees) 2008, consolidated to amendment of 9.4.2025, BoI copy](https://boi.org.il/media/jbwnaivp/159a.pdf), s.2-4, First and Second Schedules · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Banking (Customer Service) Law, BoI consolidation 10/11](https://boi.org.il/media/y3akar3j/חוק-הבנקאות-שירות-ללקוח-התשמא-1981.pdf), s.9T (9ט), 9Y (9י) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (unofficial consolidation)
- Dimensions: fee-deposit, fee-custody, regulator
- Likely to change: yes: the fee rules have been amended repeatedly (last amendment listed 9.4.2025).

### BANK-17 Current-account fee tracks: basic, extended, extended plus
- Rule: Every bank must include the basic track (מסלול בסיסי) and the extended track (מסלול מורחב) among its services and let any customer join. Basic: up to one teller action and up to ten direct-channel actions a month. Extended: up to ten teller actions and up to fifty direct-channel actions a month. Extended plus (מסלול מורחב פלוס): the extended track's actions plus further services decided by the bank. Joining takes effect on the first of the month after notice; cancelling likewise. Actions beyond the track are charged per the full tariff. For a senior citizen or a person with disabilities, and separately for a small business or exempt dealer, the bank must each calendar year compare what the customer paid with what they would have paid in the basic or extended track and, if they would have paid less in every month, enrol them in that track by 1 March of the following year and notify them.

### BANK-21 Deposit without a current account ("closed system")
- Rule: Every bank that is not a "bank with a small scope of activity" must let an individual deposit money in a shekel deposit through a closed-system account, simply and conveniently, including online. A closed-system account holds and manages the individual's deposits; the money comes only from an account of the same individual at another bank or at the Postal Bank and is returned only to the account it came from. The customer may authorise the receiving bank in writing to handle all the steps. The source bank may not exert unfair influence on the customer about it. The receiving bank must publish information about the service. Under directive 417, the funds may on the customer's request stay at the receiving bank for a further deposit period instead of returning; money in a closed-system account cannot be pledged or set off; transfer fees may not be higher than for a transfer to a non-closed account.
- Conditions / exceptions: Shekel deposits only; the supervisor may extend it to FX deposits by directive. A bank that stops being "small" becomes bound six months later. "Small" is defined in s.5B1(a) of the law, not read (open question 5); the bill's explanatory note describes it as a bank whose assets are small relative to all banks. Institution practice: Leumi "פיקדון ללא חשבון עו"ש" (private customers over 16 with no Leumi account; signed account-ownership confirmation from own bank; 7 days to transfer; no fee; money returns to source account at maturity); Mercantile "פיקדונות ללקוחות כל הבנקים" (shekel 3-month fixed, 1-year fixed, 1-year prime-based; minimum NIS 250; no auto-renewal; no exit points; cannot be pledged or offset; offer valid 3 business days); Massad "פיקדון ללקוחות כל הבנקים" ("ללא עמלות"); FIBI "פיקדון ללקוחות כל הבנקים" (page not opened). ### BANK-32 The bank's annual reports to the customer (directive 425): what the deposit, securities and fee tables show
- Rule: Proper Conduct of Banking Business directive 425 "דוחות שנתיים ללקוחות התאגידים הבנקאיים" (version 6, 21.06.2026, circular 2851) requires every bank to show, in a separate tab of the customer's online account, a shortened report and a detailed report in the formats of its first and second schedules, "עד לתאריך 28 בפברואר" for the previous calendar year (s. 3), to notify the customer when they are posted (s. 4), to hand a customer without an online account the shortened report by that date and the detailed one within 7 business days of a request (s. 5), and to keep at least the last three years' reports accessible online (s. 7). Shortened report (first schedule): part א account details (owners, account number, type, "מועד פתיחת החשבון", "מעביר משכורת (כן/לא)"); part ב balances at 31.12, assets side "יתרת עו"ש", "פיקדונות וחסכונות", "תיק ני"ע*" split "מניות", "אג"ח", "מק"מ", "אחרים" (at the last trading day's closing price), liabilities side; part ג activity: for the current account, used credit lines, "סך פיקדונות/חסכונות" and loans, each with "יתרה חודשית ממוצעת* (קרן בלבד)", "שיעור ריבית שנתית ממוצעת" and "סך הכנסות/הוצאות מריבית והצמדה"; for "תיק ני"ע", "יתרה חודשית ממוצעת" and "שיעור תשואה שנתי**" (TWR, before tax, after fees; not shown for a managed portfolio); table 2 "פיקדונות וחסכונות", one row per deposit with "מספר פיקדון", "סכום הקרן המצטבר ליום הדוח", "מועד הפקדה ראשונה בפיקדון", "תחנת יציאה קרובה", "מועד פירעון", "מטבע", "הצמדה", "סוג ריבית", "אופן חישוב ריבית", "שיעור ריבית נומינלית", "שיעור ריבית מתואמת" and "שווי* ליום" (principal, interest and linkage); table 4 "עמלות" by group ("עמלות עובר ושב", "עמלות אשראי", "עמלות ניירות ערך", "עמלות אחרות") with "שם העמלה", "סך הסכום השנתי ששולם בגין העמלה", "מס' פעולות שבוצעו/סכום עסקה" and "ממוצע שנתי של העמלה (סכום/שיעור)"; table 5 "ריבית על יתרות זכות בחשבון" with "סוג ריבית", "עד סכום", nominal and adjusted rates.
