# Tax on Israeli securities and Israeli funds (shares, bonds, mutual funds, money-market funds, ETFs)

Checked: 2026-10-22 · Owner: tax-specialist
Serves: product.md 3.1 (instrument guide: money-market fund, ETF and mutual-fund pages), 3.2 (wrapper mode and the idle-cash preset), 3.3 (dimension checklist), 3.4 (fund variants), 3.8 (rule base). Scope: what an individual Israeli resident pays on Israeli-listed shares and bonds and on Israeli mutual funds, money-market funds, tracking funds and ETFs (קרנות סל). Covers capital gains, distributions, dividends, losses, withholding, switching, and the currency component of shekel funds that hold foreign assets. ## Summary

- Shares and most securities: 25% of the **real** gain (the gain above CPI), withheld by the bank or TASE member when you sell. 30% for a substantial shareholder. Unlinked bonds: 15% of the **whole (nominal)** gain.
- An Israeli mutual fund, including a money-market fund and an ETF (קרן סל), is either an **exempt fund** (קרן נאמנות פטורה) or a **taxable fund** (קרן נאמנות חייבת). Each fund's agreement or prospectus states which (s.88), and TASE's Maya publishes it per fund (TXS-24).
- Exempt fund: the fund pays nothing. You pay 25% of your real gain only when you sell or redeem, and 25% on any distribution. Taxable fund: the fund pays tax on its income at the rates an individual would pay. You pay nothing on redemption or on distributions, but a loss on its units cannot be offset.
- **Money-market funds (Q15):** most are exempt funds; on 2026-10-22 Maya listed 2 of 73 as taxable (TXS-24; named in TXS-26). In an exempt one a saver pays nothing while holding, and on redemption the bank withholds 25% of the real (CPI-adjusted) gain. The 15%-of-nominal rate that applies to a shekel deposit does **not** apply to the saver in an exempt money-market fund. In a taxable one the fund pays at an individual's rates and the saver pays nothing (TXS-11).
- Losses: in the same year they offset capital gains, and also interest and dividends from securities taxed at up to 25%. Unused losses carry forward without a time limit, but only against capital gains, and only if a return was filed for the year of the loss. The bank nets gains and losses only inside itself; netting across banks needs an annual return.
- Moving from one fund or security to another is a sale and is taxed. There is no tax-free switch in a brokerage account.

## Rules

### TXS-01 Capital gain on Israeli-listed shares: rate
- Rule: An individual pays tax on real capital gain at the s.121 rates, but at no more than 25%. The gain is treated as the top slice of their taxable income. In the Ordinance's words: "בשיעור שלא יעלה על 25%" and "ויראו את רווח ההון כשלב הגבוה ביותר בסולם הכנסתו החייבת". A substantial shareholder (בעל מניות מהותי) who sells securities of that company pays up to 30%.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 30% case is s.91(b)(2): a seller who is a substantial shareholder at the time of sale or at any time in the 12 months before it (`במועד מכירתו של נייר הערך או במועד כלשהו ב-12 החודשים שקדמו למכירה`). The Tax Authority guide defines a substantial shareholder as someone holding, directly or indirectly, alone or with a relative or another (as defined in s.88), at least 10% of any kind of means of control in a body of persons, and applies the 30% to a seller who was one "בעת המכירה או בשנה שקדמה לה". Assets bought before 2012 have a linear split with older rates. That is not relevant to a new saver and is left out. Age 60+: see TXS-23. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* printed guide page numbers removed from the text (the Source line carries the file pages); the 12-month test is quoted from s.91(ב)(2), and the guide's wording corrected ("בשנה שקדמה לה", not "בשנים").
- Figures: 25%; 30% (in force 2026; fixed in law).

### TXS-02 Real vs nominal: the inflationary amount
- Rule: Capital gain splits into real gain and the inflationary amount (סכום אינפלציוני). The inflationary amount is the part of the gain equal to the excess of the CPI-adjusted original price over the original price. Real gain is "ריווח ההון פחות הסכום האינפלציוני". Only the "taxable inflationary amount" (סכום אינפלציוני חייב), meaning the amount accrued up to 31.12.1993, is taxed, at 10%. The rest of the inflationary amount is exempt. When the index is lower on the sale day than on the purchase day, the inflationary amount is zero, not negative, and the real gain equals the nominal gain: "מכיוון שהסכום האינפלציוני הינו סכום שלילי, במקרה כאמור אנו נראה בסכום האינפלציוני 0 וכל רווח ההון יהיה רווח הון ריאלי" (circular 10/2025, appendix, example 3), so tax never exceeds the rate times the nominal gain. When the inflationary amount is larger than the nominal gain, "כל רווח ההון הנומינלי ייחשב כסכום אינפלציוני פטור ממס" (example 2): the real gain is zero and no capital loss arises, because "הפסד הון בר קיזוז נמדד בערכים נומינאליים בלבד" (s.2.1) and a capital loss is the excess of the remaining original cost over the consideration (s.88). If a sale ends in a nominal loss while the index fell, the part of the loss equal to the fall is not allowed and only the rest can be offset (example 4; the Supreme Court in ע"א 3555/15 מוזס, para 26, held that a negative inflationary amount is not part of the tax base). The circular's examples use an exchange rate; the rule it quotes from the ruling covers both: a "סכום אינפלציוני", "בין אם מקורו בשינוי ערך מדד או בשינוי שער חליפין", is not offsettable as a loss.
- Conditions / exceptions: The index (מדד) is the CPI as last published before the relevant day. For a security held by an individual that is denominated in or linked to foreign currency, the exchange rate is the index (TXS-04). The nominal-loss rule does not apply to holders under the Inflation Adjustments Law (circular 10/2025, note 1). The 2025 form 1325 writes a negative nominal loss line as 0 ("אם התוצאה שלילית יש לרשום 0", note 6). Open question 15 (and `tax-general.md` open question 13) closed 2026-10-26.
- Figures: 10% on the pre-1994 inflationary amount. Nothing on the inflationary amount accrued after 1993.

### TXS-03 Unlinked bonds and other unlinked debt: 15% of the whole gain
- Rule: The capital gain on selling a bond, commercial paper, loan or "מילווה" that is not linked to the CPI is taxed at no more than 15% (20% for a substantial shareholder). "ויראו את כל רווח ההון כרווח הון ריאלי": the whole nominal gain is taxed and there is no inflation relief. "Not linked" also covers partial linkage: "או שהוא צמוד בחלקו לשיעור עליית המדד, כולו או חלקו".
- Conditions / exceptions: s.91(b)(3)(b): "שר האוצר רשאי לשנות בצו את שיעור המס ... בהתאם לשינוי במדד". Whether such an order was ever made was not checked (open question 10). This covers capital gains on a bond. Interest and discount income on bonds and makam are in TXI. s.97(a)(1) exempts gains on certain listed non-convertible bonds issued before 8.5.2000; that is legacy only.
- Figures: 15% / 20% (in force 2026).
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), p.151, s.91(ב)(3)(א)-(ג) ("ויראו את כל רווח ההון כרווח הון ריאלי") · checked 2026-10-25 · primary
- Scope: general

### TXS-04 Securities in foreign currency: the exchange rate is the index
- Rule: For a security held by an individual that is denominated in or linked to foreign currency, the exchange rate is the index: "לענין נייר ערך בידי יחיד, הנקוב במטבע חוץ או שערכו צמוד למטבע חוץ, יראו את שער המטבע כמדד". The real gain is therefore the gain above the change in that currency, not above CPI.
- Conditions / exceptions: This applies to Israeli-listed securities and fund units that are denominated in or linked to foreign currency (for example dollar-denominated units, TXS-14). Foreign securities: see TXF.
- Figures: none
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), p.146, s.88, definition מדד ("יראו את שער המטבע כמדד") · checked 2026-10-25 · primary

### TXS-05 Withholding by banks and TASE members on securities sales
- Rule: A "financial institution" (מוסד כספי, defined as "תאגיד בנקאי או חבר בורסה") that pays sale proceeds to an individual withholds 25% of the real capital gain. For an unlinked security it withholds 15% of the (nominal) capital gain.
- Conditions / exceptions: These regulations set no lower rate for age or low income (TXS-23). For a sale of traded securities, no 30-day report is needed if the tax was withheld in full: "במכירת ניירות ערך סחירים אין להגיש הודעה כאמור אם נוכה מלוא המס כחוק". If it was not withheld (for example at a broker that does not withhold), the saver must file an annual return and report twice a year, by 31 July (January-June) and by 31 January (July-December), paying an advance with each report. Filing thresholds are in TXG. The text read is National Insurance's consolidated text, which lists amendments up to ק"ת 7907 (27.12.2017); later amendments were not checked (open question 3). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* guide quote made verbatim ("אם נוכה מלוא המס כחוק"); the regulations are now read in an official consolidated text, not a copying site.
- Figures: 25% real; 15% nominal (unlinked).

### TXS-06 Which lot is sold, and which fees reduce the gain
- Rule: When identical securities bought at different times are sold, they are treated as sold in order of purchase (first in, first out) unless the seller proves to the assessing officer the purchase date and price of a specific security. "Identical securities" includes units in the same mutual fund, if they are managed by one portfolio manager. Also: "בחישוב רווח ההון במכירת ניירות ערך יותרו בניכוי מהתמורה ההוצאות שהוצאו באותה שנת מס". "Expenses" means management and custody fees for securities transactions ("דמי ניהול ודמי משמורת ... בעד פעולות בניירות ערך").
- Conditions / exceptions: Only expenses from the same tax year count. The regulations count fees "בשנות המס החל במועד הקובע ואילך", and s.88 sets that date at 1 January 2003 (added 2026-10-26, open question 14). Fees of the year that were not deducted in that year count as a capital loss from securities: "הוצאות בשל ניירות ערך, כפי שקבע שר האוצר באישור ועדת הכספים של הכנסת, שלא נוכו בשנת המס, ייחשבו לענין סעיף זה כהפסד הון מניירות ערך" (s.92(א)(5)), so they offset as such a loss, and carry forward only if returns are filed (circular 10/2025 s.4.3). At withholding the bank or TASE member deducts them only "אם אישר זאת המנהל, בהתאם לכללים שקבע" (reg. 8 of the 2002 withholding regulations); that approval or those rules were not found, and neither was a bank text showing the deduction. The Tax Authority's 2025 form 1325 puts only transaction commissions into cost and proceeds ("לא כולל עמלות תקופתיות"). Open question 12 narrowed 2026-10-26.

### TXS-07 Two tax tracks for an Israeli mutual fund, fixed in its prospectus
- Rule: The Ordinance has two kinds of fund. A "taxable mutual fund" is one whose fund agreement or prospectus says it will be taxable. An "exempt mutual fund" is one whose agreement or prospectus says so "בקביעה שאינה ניתנת לשינוי" (irrevocably), or a taxable fund whose manager notified the Tax Authority at least 30 days before a tax year that it becomes exempt from the first day of that year. On such a switch, all the fund's assets are treated as sold on the last day of the year of the notice, and the fund pays the tax within 30 days. For unit holders whose gain is exempt, that day becomes the purchase date and the value on that day becomes the original price. There is no path from exempt to taxable.
- Conditions / exceptions: "Mutual fund", "unit", "unit holder" and "prospectus" take their meaning from the Joint Investment Trust Law (חוק השקעות משותפות בנאמנות). Each fund's agreement or prospectus states its track (s.88), and TASE's Maya publishes it per fund (TXS-24). 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the sentence that prospectuses show the track in a field "מסלול המס" generalised one company's prospectus (Migdal Mutual Funds); the field and examples are in TXS-26, and the Migdal prospectus, which only restated s.88, was dropped as a source.
- Figures: notice at least 30 days before the tax year.

### TXS-09 Exempt fund: what the saver pays on sale or redemption
- Rule: An individual's real capital gain on selling or redeeming a unit in an exempt fund is taxed under s.91(b)(1): at most 25%, as the top slice of income. The bank or TASE member withholds 25% of the real gain: "חייב המשלם למוכר שהוא יחיד בעל יחידה בקרן נאמנות פטורה, רווח הון ריאלי בשל מכירה או פדיון של יחידה, ינכה ממנו מס בשיעור של 25%".
- Conditions / exceptions: Real gain is measured against CPI for shekel units (TXS-02), against the exchange rate for units denominated in or linked to foreign currency, and against the fund's "principal currency" for a foreign-investment fund (TXS-14). The rule is for a resident individual who is not a substantial shareholder, has no business or bookkept securities income and claimed no financing expenses; surtax is apart. It applies to units bought from 1.1.2006. 2026-10-26 (scope settling): Migdal Mutual Funds' prospectus (p.72, s.25.2.2.4), which restated the rate, was dropped as a source. Surtax: see TXG. Age 60+: see TXS-23.

### TXS-11 Taxable fund: the fund pays, the saver does not, and losses are lost
- Rule: The taxable income of a taxable fund is taxed at the rates that would apply if an individual (not in business) had received it, "אלא אם כן נקבע במפורש אחרת". Where no special rate exists, the top s.121 rate applies. The individual unit holder is exempt on sale or redemption (s.97(a)(7): "רווח הון שהיה ליחיד במכירה או בפדיון של יחידה בקרן נאמנות חייבת") and on distributions (s.125B1(a)). A loss on units of a taxable fund cannot be offset against gains or income of any kind.
- Conditions / exceptions: Why losses are lost: s.92(a)(1) offsets only a loss that "אילו היה ריווח הון היה מתחייב עליו במס". The Tax Authority guide says "הפסד ממכירת נכס שאילו היה רווח היה פטור ממס, אינו בגדר הפסד הון". 2026-10-26 (scope settling): Migdal Mutual Funds' prospectus (s.25.2.1), which restated these rules and added that a taxable fund's inflationary amount on securities is measured by CPI (s.25.2.1.3), was dropped as a source; the CPI point follows from s.88's "מדד" (TXS-14). Derived, not stated in one place: by s.129C(a)(2) read with s.125C(c) and s.91(b)(3), a taxable fund holding unlinked shekel interest instruments bears 15% of the nominal interest and gains at fund level, and the saver pays nothing more. The bizportal secondary source (S10) says the same. The Tax Authority states the principle in circular 7/2003 (s.2.1.1): "שיעורי המס החלים על קרן חייבת זהים לשיעורי המס החלים על יחיד שהיחידה אינה בעסק ואינה מהווה הכנסה מעסק בידו", with the 2003 rates on linked and unlinked assets as its example; the individual's rate on unlinked interest today is 15% (s.125ג(ג)(1)) and on gains from unlinked bonds and loans at most 15% of the whole gain (s.91(ב)(3)(א)). *2026-10-26 open-questions audit:* open question 11 closed by the circular; its figures are the 2003 rates, the current ones are the Ordinance's.
- Figures: individual rates applied at fund level (for example 15% unlinked interest, 25% real gains); 0% to the unit holder.

### TXS-12 Money-market funds: what a saver pays, and on what base (closes Q15)
- Rule: No tax rule specific to money-market funds was found. The Ordinance sections that deal with funds (s.88, 88A, 91, 94, 97, 101A, 125B1, 129C, 129D) use only "exempt" and "taxable" fund, never "קרן כספית". A money-market fund is taxed under its tax track (TXS-07 to TXS-11), set in its agreement or prospectus (s.88) and published per fund on Maya (TXS-24). Most money-market funds are exempt: on 2026-10-22 Maya's export listed 71 of 73 as exempt and 2 as taxable (TXS-24; the named funds and prospectuses are in TXS-26).

  For an exempt money-market fund, a saver pays:
  - Nothing while holding. The fund's shekel interest and discount income is exempt inside the fund (TXS-08).
  - 25% of the real gain on redemption or sale: the gain above CPI over the holding period. The bank or TASE member withholds it (TXS-09).
  - 25% on any distribution (TXS-10).

  The 15%-of-nominal rate that applies to a shekel deposit or a makam held directly (TXI) does not apply to the saver in an exempt money-market fund. A unit is not a bond, and reg.2(b) (15%) covers unlinked securities, while reg.3A sets 25% of real gain for exempt-fund units.
- Conditions / exceptions: (a) A loss on redemption can be offset (TXS-17 to TXS-19), unlike a loss on a deposit. (b) In a taxable money-market fund the fund bears tax at the rates an individual would pay (circular 7/2003 s.2.1.1, TXS-11), today 15% of nominal interest on unlinked shekel instruments, and the saver pays nothing and cannot offset a loss. Maya lists 2 of 73 money-market funds as taxable (TXS-24). (c) An exempt fund may still bear foreign tax on income from securities traded abroad or dual-listed (TXS-15; the prospectuses' wording, TXS-25). (d) The tax track is per fund: it is read from each fund's agreement or prospectus, or Maya's per-fund field (TXS-24); `data/funds/` needs a tax-track field. (e) CPI deflation over the holding period: the inflationary amount stops at zero, not below, so the base is the whole nominal gain and never more, and on a nominal loss the part equal to the CPI fall is not allowable (TXS-02: circular 10/2025, appendix, examples 3 and 4; open question 15 closed 2026-10-26; the record's Hebrew said so from that day, this line on 2026-10-26, follow-ups brief item 8). 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the two prospectuses read (Yalin Lapidot, Migdal Mutual Funds) and the Yalin Lapidot fund's 10% foreign-asset cap moved to TXS-26; the sentence that no taxable money-market fund was found was stale against TXS-24.
- Figures: 0% during; 25% of real gain at exit; 25% of distributions (2026).

### TXS-14 Currency component of a shekel fund that holds foreign assets
- Rule: Which index measures a fund unit's real gain depends on how the unit is denominated and on the fund's status:
  - Shekel units of an ordinary exempt fund: CPI (s.88 "מדד").
  - Units denominated in or linked to foreign currency: that exchange rate (s.88, "מדד": "לענין נייר ערך בידי יחיד, הנקוב במטבע חוץ או שערכו צמוד למטבע חוץ, יראו את שער המטבע כמדד"; a unit is a "נייר ערך" under s.88).
  - A "foreign-investment fund" (קרן נאמנות להשקעות חוץ): the inflationary amount uses the "principal currency" exchange rate chosen in its prospectus. This is an exempt fund with at least 90% of its investments in each tax year in assets whose interest is paid by, or whose inflationary amount is computed by, that currency's exchange rate.

  Derived: for a shekel-denominated fund holding foreign assets that is not a foreign-investment fund, the inflationary amount follows CPI. Any part of the gain that comes from the shekel weakening faster than CPI is therefore taxed as real gain, and a stronger shekel reduces the gain.
- Conditions / exceptions: A deviation from the 90% test for a cumulative period of up to 10 days in a tax year does not count (reg. 1). Longer deviations: Income Tax circular 7/2003 (22.5.2003, section 4.1) says a foreign-investment fund must meet the conditions from its start to its end; once it deviates it stops being one and cannot become one again, and from then the inflationary amount follows the CPI from purchase to sale; a unit holder who redeemed before the deviation is taxed as in a foreign-investment fund, one who redeemed after it as in a fund that is not one. Open: whether the 2003 regulations still operate. They define their fund as an exempt or mixed fund "as defined in s.105יא" and were made under s.105יט. Amendment 147 (2005, in force 1.1.2006) repealed Part ה3 of the Ordinance, which held both sections; its transitional section keeps regulations made under s.105יד and s.105טו in force as if made under the new sections, and no such clause for s.105יט was found in its text. No amendment or repeal of the 2003 regulations was found (the Reshumot booklet has no consolidated version; a web search found none). *2026-10-26 (scope settling):* Migdal Mutual Funds' prospectus (ss.25.3, 25.4), cited for the currency-unit rule, the circular's position and the regulations' still applying, was dropped as a source: the first two are in s.88 and the circular, and a company's description of the law does not settle the open point. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the long-deviation sub-rule now rests on the circular itself, read on gov.il (it was institution strength through the prospectus); the question whether the regulations still operate is stated plainly.
- Figures: 90% test; 10-day tolerance.

### TXS-16 Dividends from Israeli companies
- Rule: A dividend received by an individual is taxed at 25%, or 30% if the individual is a substantial shareholder at receipt or at any time in the previous 12 months. The guide says the return is filled from the payer's annual certificate, which states each income type, the rate and the tax withheld. National Insurance and health tax on dividends: see TXG. ### TXS-17 Loss offset in the same year
- Rule: A capital loss is offset first against real capital gain. Each remaining shekel of loss offsets 3.5 shekels of taxable inflationary amount. Capital losses offset only capital gains (including on traded securities) and real-estate betterment. In addition, a loss on a security in the same tax year can offset interest or dividends paid on that security, and interest or dividends on other securities, provided the tax rate on that interest or dividend does not exceed 25%. A loss on an asset whose gain would have been exempt is not a capital loss. A loss on an asset abroad is offset first against foreign gains (detail in TXF).
- Conditions / exceptions: The 25% condition excludes, for example, dividends taxed at 30% to a substantial shareholder. The offset against interest and dividends is same-year only (TXS-18). Losses on taxable-fund units cannot be offset (TXS-11). s.92(a)(5): securities expenses set by the Minister and not deducted in the year count as a securities loss.
- Figures: 1 : 1 against real gain; 1 : 3.5 against taxable inflationary amount; 25% rate ceiling for interest and dividend offset.
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), p.155, s.92(א)(1), (3), (4), (5) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. ### TXS-18 Carrying losses forward
- Rule: A loss not offset in its year is carried to the following years, one after another, without a time limit. It can be used only against capital gains (and betterment), not against interest or dividends. The condition is that a return was filed for the year of the loss: "ובלבד שהוגש לפקיד השומה דוח לשנת המס שבה היה ההפסד". The guide: "הפסד מועבר מני"ע סחירים שנוצר בשנת המס 2006 ואילך, ניתן לקיזוז בשנות המס הבאות כנגד כל רווח הון ..., אך לא כנגד ריבית ודיבידנד". A carried loss is entered in field 48 of the return.
- Conditions / exceptions: A securities loss from up to 31.12.2005 can still offset interest and dividends taxed at no more than 20%. That is legacy. A foreign carried loss goes first against foreign gains (TXF).
- Figures: no time limit.

### TXS-19 Netting at the bank vs across institutions
- Rule: When computing the gain for withholding, the financial institution offsets capital losses from securities and future transactions under s.92 only if (1) the loss came from a security it manages, and (2) the gain arose in the same tax year, before or after the loss. If a portfolio moves from one manager to another, the assessing officer may allow offset of losses incurred at the previous manager, on conditions he sets. Offsetting across banks or brokers needs an annual return: the withholding agent offsets only losses on securities it manages (reg. 9(א)(1)). In the return, traded-securities gains and losses go on form 1322 (appendix C), based on the institutions' annual certificates (form 867).
- Conditions / exceptions: The Tax Authority guide says that the interest payer (for example the bank) may already have made the offset of a securities loss against interest at withholding ("ייתכן מצב שמשלם הריבית (לדוגמה הבנק) עשה את פעולת הקיזוז כבר בשלב ניכוי המס במקור"). The regulation that provides for this was not found in the two withholding regulations read (open question 5). Joint account: payments to partners in an account are treated as equal shares unless the assessing officer is shown otherwise (reg.10). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the printed guide page was removed from the text and the guide sentence quoted (file page in the Source line).

### TXS-20 Spouses: whose gain it is
- Rule: Capital gain, like other income that is not from personal effort, is attributed to the spouse with the higher personal-effort income. ### TXS-21 Switching between funds or securities is a sale
- Rule: "Sale" includes "חליפין, ויתור, הסבה, העברה, הענקה, מתנה, פדיון" and any other event by which an asset leaves a person's possession, except inheritance. Redeeming one mutual fund to buy another, or selling one ETF or share to buy another, is therefore a sale. Tax is due (withheld, TXS-05 and TXS-09) on any real gain, even if the money never leaves the account.
- Conditions / exceptions: s.101A(a)(12) lets the Finance Minister set "מקרים שבהם יראו יחידה בקרן נאמנות פטורה כנמכרת וכנרכשת מחדש". No order or regulation under it was found (open question 9). The contrast is the provident wrappers, where moving between tracks or funds is not a tax event: see TXK (kupat gemel lehashkaa, IRA), TXH (keren hishtalmut), TXP (pension). In a brokerage account, the deferral that tax-free switching gives in those wrappers is lost at every switch.

### TXS-22 Exempt fund vs taxable fund: what changes for the saver
- Rule: This summarizes TXS-08 to TXS-11; it adds no new law.

### TXS-23 Age 60 and over

## Left out and why

- Pre-2012 and pre-2003 linear-split computations, bonds issued before 8.5.2000 (s.97(a)(1)), and units bought before 1.1.2006: only very old holdings are affected.
- Securities-lending fees in a taxable fund (the prospectus reports 15%): a fund-level detail with no saver choice attached.
- Bonus shares and bonus units (s.94(a)), options, futures (reg.3): not a saver's core path.
- Foreign residents' exemptions (s.97(b2), s.101A(a)(8)): the audience is Israeli residents.
- Companies as unit holders (reg.3B, withholding at the s.126 rate): the audience is individuals.
- REIT taxation: TX2. Surtax, filing thresholds, National Insurance: TXG.

## Proposed dimensions

- `fund-tax-track`: exempt vs taxable mutual fund (TXS-07). It changes when tax is paid, on what base, and whether losses can be used. It applies to every Israeli fund variant in 3.4 and must be read per fund from the prospectus.
