# Tax on Israeli securities and Israeli funds (shares, bonds, mutual funds, money-market funds, ETFs)

Checked: 2026-11-12 · Owner: tax-specialist
Serves: product.md 3.1 (instrument guide: money-market fund, ETF and mutual-fund pages), 3.2 (wrapper mode and the idle-cash preset), 3.3 (dimension checklist), 3.4 (fund variants), 3.8 (rule base). Roadmap release 1 (instrument guide) and release 2 (closes tax report Q15, which blocks the idle-cash preset).
Scope: what an individual Israeli resident pays on Israeli-listed shares and bonds and on Israeli mutual funds, money-market funds, tracking funds and ETFs (קרנות סל). Covers capital gains, distributions, dividends, losses, withholding, switching, and the currency component of shekel funds that hold foreign assets. Out of scope: foreign-domiciled funds and foreign securities (`tax-securities-foreign.md`, TXF); interest on deposits, makam and bonds (`tax-interest-deposits-bonds.md`, TXI); brackets, surtax, filing, National Insurance on passive income (`tax-general.md`, TXG); REITs (`tax-tier2-other.md`, TX2); switching inside provident wrappers (`tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md` TXK, `tax-hishtalmut.md` TXH, `tax-pension-gemel-lekitzba.md` TXP). None of those files existed on 2026-10-22, so pointers are by file name only.

How the law was read: the Income Tax Ordinance and the regulations were read on Hebrew Wikisource (a mirror of the official text, same status as Nevo in the README). The fetch tool shows at most 125 characters per quote, so long provisions were read in pieces. The Tax Authority guide and the prospectuses were read as page images. Page numbers for the Tax Authority guide (S5) are the printed numbers; the PDF page is the printed number plus 2.

## Summary

- Shares and most securities: 25% of the **real** gain (the gain above CPI), withheld by the bank or TASE member when you sell; an exemption from that withholding goes to the bodies the regulations list and to companies, as a rule not to an individual (TXS-42). 30% for a substantial shareholder. Unlinked bonds: 15% of the **whole (nominal)** gain.
- An Israeli mutual fund, including a money-market fund and an ETF (קרן סל), is either an **exempt fund** (קרן נאמנות פטורה) or a **taxable fund** (קרן נאמנות חייבת). Each fund's agreement or prospectus states which (s.88), and TASE's Maya publishes it per fund (TXS-24).
- Exempt fund: the fund pays nothing. You pay 25% of your real gain only when you sell or redeem, and 25% on any distribution. Taxable fund: the fund pays tax on its income at the rates an individual would pay. You pay nothing on redemption or on distributions, but a loss on its units cannot be offset.
- **Money-market funds (Q15):** most are exempt funds; on 2026-10-22 Maya listed 2 of 73 as taxable (TXS-24; named in TXS-26). In an exempt one a saver pays nothing while holding, and on redemption the bank withholds 25% of the real (CPI-adjusted) gain. The 15%-of-nominal rate that applies to a shekel deposit does **not** apply to the saver in an exempt money-market fund. In a taxable one the fund pays at an individual's rates and the saver pays nothing (TXS-11).
- Losses: in the same year they offset capital gains, and also interest and dividends from securities taxed at up to 25%. Unused losses carry forward without a time limit, but only against capital gains, and only if a return was filed for the year of the loss. The bank nets gains and losses only inside itself; netting across banks needs an annual return.
- Moving from one fund or security to another is a sale and is taxed. There is no tax-free switch in a brokerage account.
- An Israeli fund on a foreign index: no Israeli law read makes the holder's tax differ between a fund that holds the shares and one that holds a swap, since an exempt fund's income is exempt whatever its source; that the two are taxed the same for you is inferred, and no official text says it. Read: they differ in foreign tax inside the fund: US tax on the dividends of shares it holds (the rate for an Israeli fund is not settled), and no US dividend-equivalent tax on a swap whose index is a US "qualified index" when the swap is priced or issued (TXS-41).
- Moving your own securities to another bank or broker: the law ties the cost and purchase day to the holder, not to the account, and after the move the institution that pays the sale proceeds withholds from the cost it holds; a missing cost is declared on form 747 or 746 or recovered through the return. Whether the move itself is a sale is not said by any official text. Bank Leumi and Bank Massad say a move to the same holder is no tax event; Discount Bank lists it apart from other transfers but states withholding as the rule for every kind it lists; Bank Yahav calls any transfer a potential tax event (TXS-38 to TXS-40, open question 17).
- A fund with a limited period ends in a wind-up: the liquidator redeems the units, so an exempt fund's holder is taxed and withheld as on any redemption, and a loss is an ordinary capital loss. This is derived from the texts; no Tax Authority sentence names a wind-up, and the tax year of the sale is open (TXS-33 to TXS-37).
- All of this assumes the gain is capital. Trading that amounts to a business is taxed at ordinary rates; the Tax Authority weighs nine helper tests with no formula (TXS-32).

## Rules

### TXS-01 Capital gain on Israeli-listed shares: rate
- Rule: An individual pays tax on real capital gain at the s.121 rates, but at no more than 25%. The gain is treated as the top slice of their taxable income. In the Ordinance's words: "בשיעור שלא יעלה על 25%" and "ויראו את רווח ההון כשלב הגבוה ביותר בסולם הכנסתו החייבת". A substantial shareholder (בעל מניות מהותי) who sells securities of that company pays up to 30%.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 30% case is s.91(b)(2): a seller who is a substantial shareholder at the time of sale or at any time in the 12 months before it (`במועד מכירתו של נייר הערך או במועד כלשהו ב-12 החודשים שקדמו למכירה`). The Tax Authority guide defines a substantial shareholder as someone holding, directly or indirectly, alone or with a relative or another (as defined in s.88), at least 10% of any kind of means of control in a body of persons, and applies the 30% to a seller who was one "בעת המכירה או בשנה שקדמה לה". Assets bought before 2012 have a linear split with older rates. That is not relevant to a new saver and is left out. Age 60+: see TXS-23. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* printed guide page numbers removed from the text (the Source line carries the file pages); the 12-month test is quoted from s.91(ב)(2), and the guide's wording corrected ("בשנה שקדמה לה", not "בשנים").
- Figures: 25%; 30% (in force 2026; fixed in law).

### TXS-02 Real vs nominal: the inflationary amount
- Rule: Capital gain splits into real gain and the inflationary amount (סכום אינפלציוני). The inflationary amount is the part of the gain equal to the excess of the CPI-adjusted original price over the original price. Real gain is "ריווח ההון פחות הסכום האינפלציוני". Only the "taxable inflationary amount" (סכום אינפלציוני חייב), meaning the amount accrued up to 31.12.1993, is taxed, at 10%. The rest of the inflationary amount is exempt. When the index is lower on the sale day than on the purchase day, the inflationary amount is zero, not negative, and the real gain equals the nominal gain: "מכיוון שהסכום האינפלציוני הינו סכום שלילי, במקרה כאמור אנו נראה בסכום האינפלציוני 0 וכל רווח ההון יהיה רווח הון ריאלי" (circular 10/2025, appendix, example 3), so tax never exceeds the rate times the nominal gain. When the inflationary amount is larger than the nominal gain, "כל רווח ההון הנומינלי ייחשב כסכום אינפלציוני פטור ממס" (example 2): the real gain is zero and no capital loss arises, because "הפסד הון בר קיזוז נמדד בערכים נומינאליים בלבד" (s.2.1) and a capital loss is the excess of the remaining original cost over the consideration (s.88). If a sale ends in a nominal loss while the index fell, the part of the loss equal to the fall is not allowed and only the rest can be offset (example 4; the circular states the Supreme Court's holding in ע"א 3555/15 מוזס that the assessing officer's position, to separate the inflationary amount and not allow it as an offset against the gain, "היא בדין ותואמת את הוראות הפקודה ותכליתה", appendix p.9). The circular's examples use an exchange rate; the rule it quotes from the ruling covers both: a "סכום אינפלציוני", "בין אם מקורו בשינוי ערך מדד או בשינוי שער חליפין", is not offsettable as a loss.
- Conditions / exceptions: The index (מדד) is the CPI as last published before the relevant day. For a security held by an individual that is denominated in or linked to foreign currency, the exchange rate is the index (TXS-04). The nominal-loss rule does not apply to holders under the Inflation Adjustments Law (circular 10/2025, note 1). The 2025 form 1325 writes a negative nominal loss line as 0 ("אם התוצאה שלילית יש לרשום 0", note 6).
- Figures: 10% on the pre-1994 inflationary amount. Nothing on the inflationary amount accrued after 1993.

### TXS-03 Unlinked bonds and other unlinked debt: 15% of the whole gain
- Rule: The capital gain on selling a bond, commercial paper, loan or "מילווה" that is not linked to the CPI is taxed at no more than 15% (20% for a substantial shareholder). "ויראו את כל רווח ההון כרווח הון ריאלי": the whole nominal gain is taxed and there is no inflation relief. "Not linked" also covers partial linkage: "או שהוא צמוד בחלקו לשיעור עליית המדד, כולו או חלקו".
- Conditions / exceptions: s.91(b)(3)(b): "שר האוצר רשאי לשנות בצו את שיעור המס ... בהתאם לשינוי במדד". Whether such an order was ever made was not checked (open question 10). This covers capital gains on a bond. Interest and discount income on bonds and makam are in TXI. s.97(a)(1) exempts gains on certain listed non-convertible bonds issued before 8.5.2000; that is legacy only.
- Figures: 15% / 20% (in force 2026).
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), p.151, s.91(ב)(3)(א)-(ג) ("ויראו את כל רווח ההון כרווח הון ריאלי") · checked 2026-10-25 · primary
- Scope: general
- Dimensions: `real-vs-nominal`, `tax-at-exit`
- Likely to change: yes (Minister's order power)

### TXS-04 Securities in foreign currency: the exchange rate is the index
- Rule: For a security held by an individual that is denominated in or linked to foreign currency, the exchange rate is the index: "לענין נייר ערך בידי יחיד, הנקוב במטבע חוץ או שערכו צמוד למטבע חוץ, יראו את שער המטבע כמדד". The real gain is therefore the gain above the change in that currency, not above CPI.
- Conditions / exceptions: This applies to Israeli-listed securities and fund units that are denominated in or linked to foreign currency (for example dollar-denominated units, TXS-14). Foreign securities: see TXF.
- Figures: none
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), p.146, s.88, definition מדד ("יראו את שער המטבע כמדד") · checked 2026-10-25 · primary

### TXS-05 Withholding by banks and TASE members on securities sales
- Rule: A "financial institution" (מוסד כספי, defined as "תאגיד בנקאי או חבר בורסה") that pays sale proceeds to an individual withholds 25% of the real capital gain. For an unlinked security it withholds 15% of the (nominal) capital gain.
- Conditions / exceptions: These regulations set no lower rate for age or low income (TXS-23). For a sale of traded securities, no 30-day report is needed if the tax was withheld in full: "במכירת ניירות ערך סחירים אין להגיש הודעה כאמור אם נוכה מלוא המס כחוק". If it was not withheld (for example at a broker that does not withhold), the saver must file an annual return and report twice a year, by 31 July (January-June) and by 31 January (July-December), paying an advance with each report. Filing thresholds are in TXG. The text read is National Insurance's consolidated text, which lists amendments up to ק"ת 7907 (27.12.2017); later amendments were not checked (open question 3). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* guide quote made verbatim ("אם נוכה מלוא המס כחוק"); the regulations are now read in an official consolidated text, not a copying site.
- Figures: 25% real; 15% nominal (unlinked). Where not withheld: two reports a year with an advance each, by 31 July (January to June) and by 31 January after the tax year (July to December) (guide 2025, printed p.46: "עד ה-31 ביולי של שנת המס וב-31 בינואר שלאחר שנת המס").

### TXS-06 Which lot is sold, and which fees reduce the gain
- Rule: When identical securities bought at different times are sold, they are treated as sold in order of purchase (first in, first out) unless the seller proves to the assessing officer the purchase date and price of a specific security. "Identical securities" includes units in the same mutual fund, if they are managed by one portfolio manager. Also: "בחישוב רווח ההון במכירת ניירות ערך יותרו בניכוי מהתמורה ההוצאות שהוצאו באותה שנת מס". "Expenses" means management and custody fees for securities transactions ("דמי ניהול ודמי משמורת ... בעד פעולות בניירות ערך").
- Conditions / exceptions: Only expenses from the same tax year count. The regulations count fees "בשנות המס החל במועד הקובע ואילך", and s.88 sets that date at 1 January 2003 (added 2026-10-26). Fees of the year that were not deducted in that year: s.92(א)(5) says "הוצאות בשל ניירות ערך, כפי שקבע שר האוצר באישור ועדת הכספים של הכנסת, שלא נוכו בשנת המס, ייחשבו לענין סעיף זה כהפסד הון מניירות ערך". Whether that reaches management and custody fees today is open (`tax-tier2-other.md` open question 11, narrowed 2026-11-07): it covers only expenses the Minister determined with the Finance Committee's approval, and no determination under s.92(א)(5) (added by Amendment 147 in 2005) was found; the definition of "הוצאות" above is in regulations of 2002 made "לפי סעיפים 98, 105יד, 105יט ו-243", not under s.92. If it applies, the loss offsets in its year against capital gains and against interest on securities taxed at no more than 25%, a makam's discount included (TXI-17), and carries forward against capital gains only, if returns are filed (circular 10/2025 s.4.3). Corrected 2026-11-07: until then this sentence said the fees "count as" such a loss, without the condition. At withholding the bank or TASE member deducts them only "אם אישר זאת המנהל, בהתאם לכללים שקבע" (reg. 8 of the 2002 withholding regulations); that approval or those rules were not found, and neither was a bank text showing the deduction. The Tax Authority's 2025 form 1325 puts only transaction commissions into cost and proceeds ("לא כולל עמלות תקופתיות"). Open question 12 narrowed 2026-10-26. Income Tax circular 22/2002 (s.1.5.1, written for the 2003 reform) states the same order and ties the exception to a separate account: first in, first out "אלא אם ניתן לערוך יחוס ספציפי של עלות הרכישה במקרה בו נייר הערך הנמכר, נרכש בחשבון בנק נפרד ומוחזק באותו חשבון". It says nothing about a security moved to another account after purchase.

### TXS-07 Two tax tracks for an Israeli mutual fund, fixed in its prospectus
- Rule: The Ordinance has two kinds of fund. A "taxable mutual fund" is one whose fund agreement or prospectus says it will be taxable. An "exempt mutual fund" is one whose agreement or prospectus says so "בקביעה שאינה ניתנת לשינוי" (irrevocably), or a taxable fund whose manager notified the Tax Authority at least 30 days before a tax year that it becomes exempt from the first day of that year. On such a switch, all the fund's assets are treated as sold on the last day of the year of the notice, and the fund pays the tax within 30 days. For unit holders whose gain is exempt, that day becomes the purchase date and the value on that day becomes the original price. There is no path from exempt to taxable.
- Conditions / exceptions: "Mutual fund", "unit", "unit holder" and "prospectus" take their meaning from the Joint Investment Trust Law (חוק השקעות משותפות בנאמנות). Each fund's agreement or prospectus states its track (s.88), and TASE's Maya publishes it per fund (TXS-24). 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the sentence that prospectuses show the track in a field "מסלול המס" generalised one company's prospectus (Migdal Mutual Funds); the field and examples are in TXS-26, and the Migdal prospectus, which only restated s.88, was dropped as a source.
- Figures: notice at least 30 days before the tax year.

### TXS-11 Taxable fund: the fund pays, the saver does not, and losses are lost
- Rule: The taxable income of a taxable fund is taxed at the rates that would apply if an individual (not in business) had received it, "אלא אם כן נקבע במפורש אחרת". Where no special rate exists, the top s.121 rate applies. The individual unit holder is exempt on sale or redemption (s.97(a)(7): "רווח הון שהיה ליחיד במכירה או בפדיון של יחידה בקרן נאמנות חייבת") and on distributions (s.125B1(a)). A loss on units of a taxable fund cannot be offset against gains or income of any kind.
- Conditions / exceptions: Why losses are lost: s.92(a)(1) offsets only a loss that "אילו היה ריווח הון היה מתחייב עליו במס". The Tax Authority guide says "הפסד ממכירת נכס שאילו היה רווח היה פטור ממס, אינו בגדר הפסד הון". 2026-10-26 (scope settling): Migdal Mutual Funds' prospectus (s.25.2.1), which restated these rules and added that a taxable fund's inflationary amount on securities is measured by CPI (s.25.2.1.3), was dropped as a source; the CPI point follows from s.88's "מדד" (TXS-14). Derived, not stated in one place: by s.129C(a)(2) read with s.125C(c) and s.91(b)(3), a taxable fund holding unlinked shekel interest instruments bears 15% of the nominal interest and gains at fund level, and the saver pays nothing more. The bizportal secondary source (S10) says the same. The Tax Authority states the principle in circular 7/2003 (s.2.1.1): "שיעורי המס החלים על קרן חייבת זהים לשיעורי המס החלים על יחיד שהיחידה אינה בעסק ואינה מהווה הכנסה מעסק בידו", with the 2003 rates on linked and unlinked assets as its example; the individual's rate on unlinked interest today is 15% (s.125ג(ג)(1)) and on gains from unlinked bonds and loans at most 15% of the whole gain (s.91(ב)(3)(א)). **2026-10-26 open-questions audit:* the circular's figures are the 2003 rates, the current ones are the Ordinance's.
- Figures: individual rates applied at fund level (for example 15% unlinked interest, 25% real gains); 0% to the unit holder.

### TXS-12 Money-market funds: what a saver pays, and on what base (closes Q15)
- Rule: No tax rule specific to money-market funds was found. The Ordinance sections that deal with funds (s.88, 88A, 91, 94, 97, 101A, 125B1, 129C, 129D) use only "exempt" and "taxable" fund, never "קרן כספית". A money-market fund is taxed under its tax track (TXS-07 to TXS-11), set in its agreement or prospectus (s.88) and published per fund on Maya (TXS-24). Most money-market funds are exempt: on 2026-10-22 Maya's export listed 71 of 73 as exempt and 2 as taxable (TXS-24; the named funds and prospectuses are in TXS-26).

  For an exempt money-market fund, a saver pays:
  - Nothing while holding. The fund's shekel interest and discount income is exempt inside the fund (TXS-08).
  - 25% of the real gain on redemption or sale: the gain above CPI over the holding period. The bank or TASE member withholds it (TXS-09).
  - 25% on any distribution (TXS-10).

  The 15%-of-nominal rate that applies to a shekel deposit or a makam held directly (TXI) does not apply to the saver in an exempt money-market fund. A unit is not a bond, and reg.2(b) (15%) covers unlinked securities, while reg.3A sets 25% of real gain for exempt-fund units.
- Conditions / exceptions: (a) A loss on redemption can be offset (TXS-17 to TXS-19), unlike a loss on a deposit. (b) In a taxable money-market fund the fund bears tax at the rates an individual would pay (circular 7/2003 s.2.1.1, TXS-11), today 15% of nominal interest on unlinked shekel instruments, and the saver pays nothing and cannot offset a loss. Maya lists 2 of 73 money-market funds as taxable (TXS-24). (c) An exempt fund may still bear foreign tax on income from securities traded abroad or dual-listed (TXS-15; the prospectuses' wording, TXS-25). (d) The tax track is per fund: it is read from each fund's agreement or prospectus, or Maya's per-fund field (TXS-24); `data/funds/` needs a tax-track field. (e) CPI deflation over the holding period: the inflationary amount stops at zero, not below, so the base is the whole nominal gain and never more, and on a nominal loss the part equal to the CPI fall is not allowable (TXS-02: circular 10/2025, appendix, examples 3 and 4; the record's Hebrew said so from 2026-10-26, this line on 2026-10-26, follow-ups brief item 8). 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the two prospectuses read (Yalin Lapidot, Migdal Mutual Funds) and the Yalin Lapidot fund's 10% foreign-asset cap moved to TXS-26; the sentence that no taxable money-market fund was found was stale against TXS-24.
- Figures: 0% during; 25% of real gain at exit; 25% of distributions (2026).

### TXS-13 Israeli tracking funds and ETFs (קרנות מחקות, קרנות סל): tax status is set fund by fund
- Rule: Israeli tracking funds and ETFs are mutual funds, so TXS-07 to TXS-11 apply to them. The Ordinance gives no tax status to a type of fund: a fund is exempt if its fund agreement or prospectus says so "בקביעה שאינה ניתנת לשינוי", or if a taxable fund notified the Director at least 30 days before a tax year and paid the tax of s.88א; it is taxable if its agreement or prospectus says so (s.88, definitions `קרן נאמנות פטורה`, `קרן נאמנות חייבת`). So each tracking fund's or ETF's status is read per fund: in its agreement or prospectus, or in Maya's tax-status field (TXS-24).
- Conditions / exceptions: No official text found says that all tracking funds or ETFs are exempt. A fund's status can change, from taxable to exempt only (s.88, TXS-07). Migdal Mutual Funds' 2026 fund tables (all its ETFs exempt; one fund taxable until 31.1.2026 and exempt from 1.2.2026) moved to TXS-27 on 2026-10-26 (scope settling). ### TXS-14 Currency component of a shekel fund that holds foreign assets
- Rule: Which index measures a fund unit's real gain depends on how the unit is denominated and on the fund's status:
  - Shekel units of an ordinary exempt fund: CPI (s.88 "מדד").
  - Units denominated in or linked to foreign currency: that exchange rate (s.88, "מדד": "לענין נייר ערך בידי יחיד, הנקוב במטבע חוץ או שערכו צמוד למטבע חוץ, יראו את שער המטבע כמדד"; a unit is a "נייר ערך" under s.88).
  - A "foreign-investment fund" (קרן נאמנות להשקעות חוץ): the inflationary amount uses the "principal currency" exchange rate chosen in its prospectus. This is an exempt fund with at least 90% of its investments in each tax year in assets whose interest is paid by, or whose inflationary amount is computed by, that currency's exchange rate.

  Derived: for a shekel-denominated fund holding foreign assets that is not a foreign-investment fund, the inflationary amount follows CPI. Any part of the gain that comes from the shekel weakening faster than CPI is therefore taxed as real gain, and a stronger shekel reduces the gain.

### TXS-17 Loss offset in the same year
- Rule: A capital loss is offset first against real capital gain. Each remaining shekel of loss offsets 3.5 shekels of taxable inflationary amount. Capital losses offset only capital gains (including on traded securities) and real-estate betterment. In addition, a loss on a security in the same tax year can offset interest or dividends paid on that security, and interest or dividends on other securities, provided the tax rate on that interest or dividend does not exceed 25%. A loss on an asset whose gain would have been exempt is not a capital loss. A loss on an asset abroad is offset first against foreign gains (detail in TXF).
- Conditions / exceptions: The 25% condition excludes, for example, dividends taxed at 30% to a substantial shareholder. The offset against interest and dividends is same-year only (TXS-18). Losses on taxable-fund units cannot be offset (TXS-11). s.92(a)(5): securities expenses set by the Minister with the Finance Committee's approval and not deducted in the year count as a securities loss; no such determination was found, so whether management and custody fees count is open (`tax-tier2-other.md` open question 11, narrowed 2026-11-07; TXS-06). Circular 10/2025 adds: interest or a dividend on the security sold counts only if actually paid in the year of the loss (s.3.3.1); the 25% test is read before surtax, so an individual liable for surtax can still offset if the rate before surtax meets it (s.3.3.2); interest or a dividend that is business income cannot be offset (s.3.5); a securities loss cannot be offset against interest on a savings plan, on deposits including structured deposits, on a loan that is not a security, or against profits a mutual fund paid (s.3.7). The order of use and what must be used first: TXS-30.
- Figures: 1 : 1 against real gain; 1 : 3.5 against taxable inflationary amount; 25% rate ceiling for interest and dividend offset.

### TXS-19 Netting at the bank vs across institutions
- Rule: When computing the gain for withholding, the financial institution offsets capital losses from securities and future transactions under s.92 only if (1) the loss came from a security it manages, and (2) the gain arose in the same tax year, before or after the loss. If a portfolio moves from one manager to another, the assessing officer may allow offset of losses incurred at the previous manager, on conditions he sets. Offsetting across banks or brokers needs an annual return: the withholding agent offsets only losses on securities it manages (reg. 9(א)(1)). In the return, traded-securities gains and losses go on form 1322 (appendix C), based on the institutions' annual certificates (form 867).
- Conditions / exceptions: The Tax Authority guide says that the interest payer (for example the bank) may already have made the offset of a securities loss against interest at withholding ("ייתכן מצב שמשלם הריבית (לדוגמה הבנק) עשה את פעולת הקיזוז כבר בשלב ניכוי המס במקור"). The regulation that provides for this was not found in the two withholding regulations read (open question 5). Joint account: payments to partners in an account are treated as equal shares unless the assessing officer is shown otherwise (reg.10). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the printed guide page was removed from the text and the guide sentence quoted (file page in the Source line).

### TXS-20 Spouses: whose gain it is
- Rule: Capital gain, like other income that is not from personal effort, is attributed to the spouse with the higher personal-effort income. ### TXS-21 Switching between funds or securities is a sale
- Rule: "Sale" includes "חליפין, ויתור, הסבה, העברה, הענקה, מתנה, פדיון" and any other event by which an asset leaves a person's possession, except inheritance. Redeeming one mutual fund to buy another, or selling one ETF or share to buy another, is therefore a sale. Tax is due (withheld, TXS-05 and TXS-09) on any real gain, even if the money never leaves the account.
- Conditions / exceptions: s.101A(a)(12) lets the Finance Minister set "מקרים שבהם יראו יחידה בקרן נאמנות פטורה כנמכרת וכנרכשת מחדש". No order or regulation under it was found (open question 9). The contrast is the provident wrappers, where moving between tracks or funds is not a tax event: see TXK (kupat gemel lehashkaa, IRA), TXH (keren hishtalmut), TXP (pension). In a brokerage account, the deferral that tax-free switching gives in those wrappers is lost at every switch. A fund's wind-up is a redemption too: TXS-33.

### TXS-22 Exempt fund vs taxable fund: what changes for the saver
- Rule: This summarizes TXS-08 to TXS-11; it adds no new law.
  - Exempt fund: tax is deferred to exit, charged on real gain, at the saver's rate (top slice, up to 25%, and possibly lower at age 60+). Losses can be offset.

### TXS-28 Joint account: the regulations split the payment equally; two banks say they withhold at the highest holder's rate
- Rule: The withholding regulations treat a payment to partners in an account at a financial institution as paid equally to each of them, unless the assessing officer is satisfied otherwise and directs each partner's share in writing: reg. 10 of the 2002 regulations for consideration, capital gain, real capital gain and payment, also for partners in a security ("כמשתלם באופן שווה לכל אחד מהשותפים"), and reg. 9 of the 2005 regulations for interest under regs. 4 to 8 ("כמשתלמת באופן שווה לכל אחד מהשותפים כולם"). Neither regulation says that the highest rate of any partner applies to the whole account. The word "הגבוה" appears in neither regulation in that sense.
- Conditions / exceptions: Discount and Mercantile write in their general terms that "בהתאם להנחיות רשות המסים", as long as no Tax Authority approval is presented or the law provides otherwise, the bank withholds in the account at the highest rate that applies to any of the account's owners; a Mercantile tax page says the same of a deposit owned by several individuals of different tax class, for example an Israeli resident and a foreign resident. These are the banks' own terms. The Tax Authority's direction they cite was not read: it is not in the two regulations, and a search of the library's text found the wording only in those banks' documents. So the highest-rate practice is established only as those banks' term; the law read gives equal shares. Withheld tax is credited against the certificate (TXI-08, reg. 17 of the 2005 regulations); how a partner recovers withholding above his own rate was not read.

### TXS-29 Forms 747 and 746: an individual declares a traded security's cost to the TASE member, for withholding only
- Rule: Since the Tax Authority's notice of 19 May 2025, forms 746 and 747 replace form 938 ("בקשה לקביעת עלות לנייר ערך נסחר לצורך ניכוי המס במקור"). ### TXS-30 Using a capital loss: no carry-back, only its owner, the taxpayer's order, and earlier offsets come before carrying forward
- Rule: The Tax Authority's circular 10/2025 sets these limits on using a capital loss. (1) No carry-back: a loss offsets gains of its own year and later years, never an earlier year ("אינם מאפשרים קיזוז הפסדים כנגד רווחים שנוצרו עובר לשנת המס שבה נוצר ההפסד", s.2.2). (2) It belongs only to the person who made it and cannot pass to anyone else "בדרך של מכירה, ויתור, מתנה, הקנייה לנאמנות, הורשה או בכל דרך אחרת" (s.2.4). (3) The taxpayer chooses the order among the offsets the law allows, because the Minister has made no regulations under s.92(ג) ("הנישום רשאי לבחור את סדר קיזוז הפסדיו", s.6.1); the exception is a foreign loss, which goes first against foreign gains and, for a securities loss, foreign interest or dividends (ss.3.8, 6.1; TXF-23). (4) A loss carries forward only if every offset open in earlier years was used, including same-year interest and dividends on securities ("מיצה את כל אפשרויות הקיזוז שהיו לו", s.4.2). Once real gain is used up, a loss must also be set against that gain's taxable inflationary amount before any other gain; a taxpayer who skips this cannot carry that loss forward (s.3.2). (5) There is no duty to offset against exempt income, such as an exempt inflationary amount, and not doing so does not cost the carry-forward (s.6.2). (6) A loss is recognised on the sale date, but the assessing officer may decline to recognise it then, "מקום בו מכירת הנכס נועדה להביא להפחתת מס בלתי נאותה" (s.5).
- Conditions / exceptions: The circular does not cover a loss whose source is income taxed under Part ב of the Ordinance (s.1.4). A loss on an asset whose gain would have been exempt is not offsettable (s.2.5, TXS-17), and that includes a loss from an exchange-rate move for an individual (s.2.5; TXS-02). Not established: what counts as an improper tax reduction for an ordinary investor. Section 5 gives no test and its note 7 points to ע"א 7481/17 (Segal), not read; the circular does not say whether selling at a loss and buying the same security back is caught. A circular is the Authority's reading of the law, not the law; the case law it names in ss.2.2, 2.4, 6.2 was not read.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [חוזר מס הכנסה 10/2025: קיזוז הפסדי הון (רשות המסים, 27.11.2025)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/professional-directives-271125-1/he/IncomeTax_professional-directives-271125-1.pdf), p.2, s.2.2 ("אינם מאפשרים קיזוז הפסדים כנגד רווחים שנוצרו"); p.3, s.2.4 ("היא של הישות המשפטית שלה נוצר ההפסד בלבד"); p.4, s.3.2 ("יהיה מנוע מלהעביר את ההפסד לשנים הבאות"); p.6, s.4.2, s.5 ("רשאי פקיד השומה שלא להכיר בהפסד"), s.6.1 ("הנישום רשאי לבחור את סדר קיזוז הפסדיו"); p.7, s.6.2 ("לא קיימת חובה לקזז הפסדים") · checked 2026-10-31 · primary.

### TXS-32 A person who trades can be taxed as a business instead of at the capital-gain rate: the Tax Authority's helper tests
- Rule: Income from selling listed securities and futures is either capital, taxed at the capital-gain rates, or business income ("פירותית (עסקית)"), taxed at the person's ordinary rates under s.121 or s.126 (circular 13/2004, s.1.3). The classification rests on weighing nine helper tests together ("מבחני עזר"; "לא די בתוצאת יישומו של מבחן אחד בלבד"), with no formula (s.2.2): frequency of transactions, holding period, expertise, size of the investment, financing, organisation, ripening and improving the asset, the nature of the asset, and special circumstances, the last able to overturn the rest (s.2.3).
- Conditions / exceptions: The circular says the tests read differently for securities than for other assets: many more transactions are needed than for real estate, and buying and selling within one month can still be capital (s.2.3(א), (ב)). Investing through a bank, an adviser or a broker does not by that fact alone make a gain business income, while activity in the personal portfolio of someone whose profession is this field can point that way (s.2.3(ג)). Bank credit to finance the activity may point to business income (s.2.3(ה)). A future is not business activity in itself, and one that hedges a capital investment is not business income (s.3). Cases the assessing officer is directed to look at: share manipulation, brokers trading their own portfolio, those whose main occupation is the capital market, and a very high number of transactions with short holding and considerable sums (s.4). Clear capital examples, for someone not an interested party or controlling holder and whose securities activity is not his only activity: an inexperienced saver holding shares two months on average with some tens of transactions a month, or more through a portfolio manager without his involvement; and put options protecting a held share investment (s.5). The circular gives examples, not a threshold; it is from 2004, its rates in s.1.2 are out of date, and whether later rulings changed the tests was not read. The cases it names (s.2.1) were not read. It does not mention a provident fund (TXK open question 8).

### TXS-33 A fund's wind-up: the payment for the units is a redemption, so an exempt fund's holder is taxed as on any redemption
- Rule: Derived: no Tax Authority sentence names a wind-up, and three texts join with no step between them. (1) The fund law, for every wind-up, the end of a fund's stated period included (s.103, s.107(ב)(1)): "מפרק קרן יפדה את יחידות הקרן במועד הפירוק" (s.108(ה)). (2) The Ordinance takes its words from that law: "קרן נאמנות", "יחידה" and "בעל יחידה" are "כמשמעותם בחוק השקעות משותפות בנאמנות" (s.88). (3) "מכירה" is "לרבות ... פדיון, וכן כל פעולה או אירוע אחרים שבעקבותם יצא נכס בדרך כל שהיא מרשותו של אדם" (s.88), and reg. 3א withholds on "מכירה או פדיון של יחידה". So an individual's real gain at the wind-up of an exempt fund is taxed and withheld as in TXS-09: up to 25%, with 25% withheld. The fund keeps its tax track to the end: the choice "מחייב את הקרן עד לפירוקה" (circular 7/2003, p.4).
- Conditions / exceptions: A taxable fund's holder is exempt on "במכירה או בפדיון של יחידה" and his loss is lost (TXS-11). Not a distribution on these texts: s.125ב1 speaks of "רווחים שחילק מנהל קרן", while the fund law's word at wind-up is redeem. A distribution a manager declares before the wind-up would fall under TXS-10; no fund document read says one is made. s.93 (the wind-up of a "חבר בני אדם") names no fund, and whether a fund is one under the s.1 definition is not stated; if it were, s.93(א)(3) also treats the holding as sold, so the event is a sale on either route and only the moment of computing it (s.93(א)(4)) would differ. Who withholds: TXS-34. A loss: TXS-35. Currency: TXS-36. The date of the sale, and so its tax year: open question 21. The rule was read from the law and has not been checked against a real wind-up and its form 867 (open question 22). The surtax and age 60 rules follow TXS-09.

### TXS-34 Who withholds when a unit is redeemed, a wind-up included
- Rule: The payer, at the time of payment (s.164, "בשעת התשלום"). Reg. 1's "חייב" reaches an "מוסד כספי ... שבאמצעותו משתלמים התמורה או התשלום, הכל במישרין או בעקיפין" and also a "חייב כהגדרתו בצו קרנות הנאמנות". That order's payer is a person who pays a unit holder on the sale of a unit, including a financial institution through which the income is paid, "וכן קרן נאמנות המשלמת לבעל יחידה תשלומים". The Tax Authority's circular 7/2003 s.4.6 says which of them: on a gain from selling a unit of an exempt fund the duty "חלה על המפיץ (חבר הבורסה דרכו נמכרת היחידה)", and on a payment to the holder such as distributed profits it "תחול על הקרן". Inferred, no text says it of a wind-up: since a wind-up payment is a redemption (TXS-33), the withholder is the distributor, the bank or TASE member that holds the units for the person.
- Conditions / exceptions: Limits of the texts: circular s.4.6 cites the 2002 regulations on payments to unit holders, repealed from 1.1.2006, when the same withholding moved to reg. 3א and reg. 1 gained the reference to the order (ק"ת 6449); no later text restates s.4.6. The order was read only as enacted in 2002, in a scan's text layer (two misread letters read by sense), and its definitions point to the repealed s.105יא; reg. 1 of the consolidated regulations still refers to it. The holder's side is as for any withheld sale: an annual form 0867, no half-yearly report (TXF-32, TXS-19). ### TXS-35 A loss at the wind-up of an exempt fund is a capital loss and is offset like any other
- Rule: Derived. A wind-up payment is a sale (TXS-33), so a shortfall on an exempt-fund unit is a capital loss: s.92(א)(1) covers a loss that "אילו היה ריווח הון היה מתחייב עליו במס", and an exempt fund's gain is taxed (TXS-09). It is offset as in TXS-17 and TXS-30: first against real capital gains; in the same year also against interest or dividends on securities taxed at up to 25%, though never against profits a fund paid (TXS-10). What is left carries forward against capital gains only, given a return for the loss year and the offset year (TXS-18). At withholding the bank nets only losses on securities it manages, in the same tax year (reg. 9(א), TXS-19); across institutions it takes a return.
- Conditions / exceptions: The circular says of a unit "הפסד שנוצר לבעל יחידה ממכירת יחידה בקרן נאמנות פטורה יותר בקיזוז" (7/2003, p.12); its "מס כנגד מס" mechanism rests on a repealed section, so the mechanism here is s.92's. A taxable fund's unit gives no loss (TXS-11). The part of a nominal loss caused by a fall in the index is not allowable (TXS-02, TXF-03). No text says anything special about a loss on a wind-up. Inferred: the holder cannot choose the moment, and any unit still held on the date is redeemed with the rest. Which tax year a loss falls in when the fund ends in one year and pays in the next: open question 21.

### TXS-36 Foreign currency at wind-up: the wind-up changes no index rule
- Rule: Derived. s.88 gives no exception for a redemption at wind-up, so TXS-14 applies as at any sale. A unit denominated in or linked to a foreign currency is measured by that rate ("יראו את שער המטבע כמדד"). A shekel unit of a fund holding foreign assets is measured by CPI, unless the fund is a foreign-investment fund, measured by its principal currency. Both run from the day of purchase to the day of sale ("כפול במדד ביום המכירה ומחולק במדד ביום הרכישה").
- Conditions / exceptions: A foreign-investment fund must meet the 90% test throughout: "מרגע הקמתה ועד סוף ימיה" (circular 7/2003, p.18), with up to 10 days a tax year tolerated (the 2003 regulations, reg. 1). No text exempts a fund's last stretch. A fund that moves into shekel cash or makam ahead of its wind-up would fail the test on these words, and a holder who redeems after that is taxed on CPI for the whole period (circular s.4.1, p.18). Inferred: no text speaks of wind-up here. It matters only to shekel units: a unit denominated in foreign currency keeps the rate under s.88 whatever the fund's status. Whether the 2003 regulations still operate is TXS-14's open point. A currency fall: TXF-03. The date the rate is taken, and the currency of the payment: open question 21.
- Figures: 90% test; 10-day tolerance (TXS-14).

### TXS-37 A corporate-bond default inside an exempt fund is not a tax event for the holder
- Rule: Derived, and no text says it in words. The fund's "הכנסותיה ורווחיה ... פטורים ממס" (s.129ג(א)(1)), and the holder is taxed on selling or redeeming the unit and on distributions (TXS-09, TXS-10). So a default inside the fund reaches the holder only through the unit's price. It makes no sale of his and no loss of his until the unit is sold or redeemed, the wind-up included (TXS-33, TXS-35), and then the result is one figure for the unit, across all that the fund held.

No provision read passes a fund's loss to its holders; s.129ד is a taxable fund's rule. Money recovered from a defaulted bond after the units were redeemed: open question 23.

### TXS-38 Moving your own securities to another account: the cost and purchase day are the holder's, and no text says whether the move is a sale
- Rule: The Ordinance defines a sale as "לרבות חליפין, ויתור, הסבה, העברה, הענקה, מתנה, פדיון, וכן כל פעולה או אירוע אחרים שבעקבותם יצא נכס בדרך כל שהיא מרשותו של אדם" (s.88). No law, regulation, circular, form or other Tax Authority text read says in words whether moving one's own securities from one bank or broker to another, in Israel or abroad, is a sale (open question 17). What the texts do fix is that the tax cost belongs to the holder, not to the account: the original price ("מחיר מקורי") is "סכום שהוציא הנישום לרכישתו של אותו נכס", and the purchase day ("יום רכישה") is "היום שבו, בדרך כלשהי, הגיע הנכס לידי הנישום" (s.88); neither turns on where the security is kept. The 2002 withholding regulations foresee a portfolio moving between managers without calling it a sale: the payer offsets only losses on securities it managed, and "פקיד השומה רשאי להתיר קיזוז בשל הפסדים מניירות ערך שנוצרו אצל מנהל תיק ניירות ערך אחר ... אם הועבר תיק ניירות ערך מאותו מנהל למנהל אחר, בתנאים שיקבע" (reg. 9). The Tax Authority's forms 746 and 747, which let a holder declare a cost the TASE member has wrong, are "רק למצב בו אין שינוי בזהות המחזיק" (TXS-29), and form 747's annex defines the cost to declare as "עלות ני"ע לצורכי מס הנומינלית שנקבע למעביר".
- Conditions / exceptions: Read versus inferred. Read: the definitions, reg. 9 and the forms' words. Inferred, and not said by any official text: that a move between one's own accounts is not a sale, because the security does not leave the holder's possession (the closing words of the s.88 definition), and that the original price and purchase day therefore carry on unchanged; the word "העברה" in the same list could be read the other way (open question 17). Form 747's annex says "the transferor" ("המעביר") without saying whose transfer it means. Which lots a partial move carries is not stated: the first-in-first-out order is written for a sale, and circular 22/2002 allows specific identification only for a security "נרכש בחשבון בנק נפרד ומוחזק באותו חשבון" (TXS-06); banks' practice is TXS-40. A move to another person (a gift, an off-exchange sale) is a sale, and a gift to a relative keeps the giver's cost through the Tax Authority's route (SW-36 in `switching-and-transfers.md`). The later sale, and what to do when the new institution lacks the cost: TXS-39. Searched for a Tax Authority sentence (2026-11-06): the 2002 withholding and computation regulations; circular 22/2002; the notice of 19.5.2025 and forms 746 and 747; the Tax Authority's professional-division summaries for 2018 and 2019 (both cover only gifts between relatives); the State Comptroller's 2022 report on the Tax Authority; form 1325's notes; the 2025 annual guide; Bank of Israel directive 432 (no duty to pass tax data); web searches on gov.il and tase.co.il for a rule that makes the transferring member send the cost. None says more.

### TXS-39 After a move, the institution that pays the sale proceeds withholds from the cost it holds; a missing cost is declared to it or recovered through the return
- Rule: Withholding on a sale falls on the "חייב", a person paying the seller proceeds, "לרבות מוסד כספי ... שבאמצעותו משתלמים התמורה או התשלום" (2002 withholding regulations, reg. 1). It withholds 25% of the real gain, or 15% of the gain on an unlinked security (reg. 2; TXS-05), and reports for each seller "את המחיר המקורי, יום הרכישה ויום המכירה שנקבעו" (reg. 13). Where the cost it holds does not match what was paid, the texts give three routes:
  - (1) Form 747 (declared cost up to 500,000 NIS in a tax year) or form 746 (above that, valid once the assessing officer signs it), handed to the TASE member, for withholding only (TXS-29). On form 747 the declarer states an active tax file and "מתחייב להגיש דוח מס עבור שנת המס בה ימכרו ניירות הערך ולצרף לדוח העתק מטופס זה", so this route brings an annual return for the year of the sale with it.
  - (2) The assessing officer may allow lower withholding in writing, "אם היה סבור, שעלול להתהוות עודף מניכוי המס" (reg. 11).
  - (3) Withholding does not settle the tax: the regulations do not free the seller from reporting the gain (reg. 16); the withheld tax is credited under s.165 against the 867 certificate (reg. 15); and in the return the gain is computed from the original price, "סכום שהוצא לרכישת נייר הערך" (form 1325, note 3). Tax withheld on a missing or wrong cost therefore comes back through the return (refunds: TXG-25).

  Losses realised at the old institution are not offset by the new one unless the assessing officer allows it (reg. 9(ב)); otherwise they are offset in the return (TXS-19, TXS-18).
- Conditions / exceptions: Read versus inferred. Read: who withholds, the rates, the three routes and the return's cost. Inferred: that after a move the new institution is the withholder (it is the one through which the proceeds are paid, reg. 1). Forms 746 and 747 serve only individuals with an active tax file, and only for securities "המוחזקים בידי חבר בורסה ישראלי"; a holder without a file has routes (2) and (3). Moved to a foreign broker that does not withhold: no Israeli payer withholds at the later sale, so the seller reports twice a year with an advance and files a return (TXG-20; foreign brokers' withholding is BRK-27 in `brokerage-account.md`), computing the gain from his own original price. Moved from a foreign broker to an Israeli bank or member: the member has only the cost that reaches it, and the holder can declare it on form 747 or 746 (inferred from the forms' purpose; no text names this case). The notice says the forms "נועדו לקביעת עלות לנייר ערך נסחר בידי יחיד" and names a security "נסחר בבורסה" without saying which exchange; form 747 covers one held "באמצעות חבר בורסה ישראלי". Whether a security listed only on a foreign exchange qualifies is not stated (open question 17). What the receiving institution records when no cost reaches it is not stated by any general text read: the Tax Authority wrote that a gift between relatives outside its route "הייתה גוררת ניכוי מס במקור בגינה וכן רישום עלות אפס אצל הקרוב מקבל ניירות הערך" (2019 summary, SW-36), which is a move to another owner; for a move between one's own accounts only banks' statements were found (TXS-40).
- Figures: 25% of the real gain, 15% of the gain on an unlinked security (TXS-05); 500,000 NIS a year on form 747 (TXS-29).

### TXS-40 Banks and a broker on moving securities between accounts: Leumi and Massad say the same holder pays no tax and keeps the cost and another holder is taxed at the closing price and starts from zero; Discount lists the same holder apart without saying it pays no tax
- Rule: Three banks' and one broker's own pages and forms:
  - Bank Leumi: "ככלל, כל פעולה של העברת נייר ערך מחשבון אחד לחשבון אחר (לרבות העברה לחשבון בבנק, ברוקר או חבר בורסה אחר בארץ או בחו"ל), הינה אירוע מס". Its exceptions, "מקרים שאינם אירוע מס", start with "העברות בישות משפטית זהה (העברה לחשבון בעל אותו מספר תעודת זהות ...)", then spouses, parents and children under 18, inheritance, divorce and relatives who sign the Tax Authority's declaration. Such transfers move first in, first out ("החוק קובע כי יש להתייחס להעברות זהות בהתאם לשיטת FIFO"; the page names no law), except divorce, inheritance and a relative's declaration, which move a share of every layer. Between deposits not under the same client number the accumulated gains and losses do not move, and unused losses need a return. For a transfer outside the exceptions it says the Tax Authority set guidelines: the transferring deposit is taxed at the closing price on the transfer day, and "בפיקדון המקבל - שער העלות של הנייר יהיה 0".
  - Bank Massad's transfer form: "בביצוע העברה בזהות מלאה אין אירוע מס" (p.1); a move in a different identity "ייחשב אירוע מס בחשבון המעביר ולא יועברו נתוני העלויות לצרכי מס לחשבון המקבל" (p.1), the tax on a transfer not exempt is computed at the closing price on the transfer day, and a receiver in a different identity without payment is recorded at "שער עלות אפס" (p.2, item 7). On a move to another TASE member the client agrees that the securities' details, "לרבות השערים בהם נרכשו ניירות הערך על ידינו", "יועברו למסלקת הבורסה, ויימסרו באמצעותה לחבר הבורסה האחר" (p.2, item 1): this is how, at Massad, the purchase prices reach the receiving member.
  - Discount Bank: its form 03-007 lists "העברה לחשבון על שם אותם בעלים (זהות מלאה), לרבות העברה בין בני זוג נשואים או ילדיהם עד גיל 18" apart from transfers in a different identity, moves securities "לפי סדר רכישתם (FIFO), כל עוד אין הוראה מפורשת אחרת של רשות המיסים", and states withholding as the rule for every kind it lists unless exempt by law or approved by the assessing officer (open question 17). Its tax page says a relatives' transfer made after 1.1.2020 moves a share of every layer, with cost and purchase day recorded as the transferor's "(שמירה על עיקרון רצף המס)".
  - Interactive Israel's securities-transfer page, to the question whether purchase dates, purchase prices and tax layers update by themselves: "לא בהכרח. בעת העברת ניירות ערך, ייתכן שנתוני העלות לא יועברו או ייקלטו באופן אוטומטי ומלא"; it asks the holder to check with the transferring bank or broker that they arrived in full.

None cites a law or a published Tax Authority text for a move between one's own accounts: Leumi asserts that "the law" sets the first-in-first-out order of identical transfers and names none; Leumi and Discount refer to Tax Authority guidelines for other kinds of transfer that were not found published (open question 17). ### TXS-41 An Israeli fund on a foreign index, holding the shares or a swap: what decides the tax
- Rule: Read: an exempt fund's own income is exempt whatever its source, "הכנסותיה ורווחיה של קרן נאמנות פטורה פטורים ממס" (s.129ג(א)(1)), and a taxable fund pays at an individual's rates (s.129ג(א)(2), TXS-11). Inferred, no official text says it: for the holder, Israeli tax on an Israeli tracking fund or ETF does not turn on how the fund tracks its index, because the same three things decide it whether the fund holds the index's shares or a swap or futures on the index. (1) The fund's tax track, set fund by fund (TXS-07, TXS-13). Since s.129ג(א)(1) names no source, dividends an exempt fund receives and payments it receives under a swap are not taxed in Israel inside it, and the holder pays 25% of the real gain on a sale and 25% on a distribution (TXS-09, TXS-10). (2) The index that measures the holder's real gain (TXS-14). For shekel units it is the CPI, unless the fund is a "קרן נאמנות להשקעות חוץ" under the 2003 regulations, whose test looks at what the fund's assets are in each tax year, not at the tracking method; whether those regulations still operate is open (TXS-14). (3) Foreign tax paid inside the fund. It is a cost of the fund; that the holder cannot credit it is a reading which no source states in words (TXS-15, TXF-16, open question 8). On the texts read, only this third thing differs between the two methods:
  - **Holding US shares.** US tax is withheld from the dividends the fund receives, and the rate is not settled. The US-Israel treaty's 25% (TXF-12) is for a "resident of Israel". That is either an "Israeli corporation", defined as "any body of persons taxed as a body of persons resident in Israel under the income tax ordinance" (Art. 2(1)(f)(ii)), or another person resident in Israel for tax, "but in the case of a partnership, estate, or trust only to the extent that the income derived by such partnership, estate, or trust is subject to Israeli tax as the income of a resident either in the hands of the respective entity or of its partners or beneficiaries" (Art. 3(1)(a)(ii)). No official text read says how an exempt fund, whose income s.129ג(א)(1) exempts, meets either test. So whether its US dividends bear 25% or the 30% no-treaty rate is open (open question 24).
  - **Through a swap.** The fund receives no dividend. US rules treat a payment under a swap that references a US dividend as a "dividend equivalent", which is a US-source dividend: "Dividend equivalent payments are treated as U.S. source dividends such that withholding under chapter 3 may apply" (IRS Pub. 515). The regulation's rules for such swaps (paras. (d)(2), (e)) apply to payments from 1 January 2017 on a transaction "with a delta of one issued on or after January 1, 2017" (26 CFR 1.871-15(r)(2)). But "a qualified index is treated as a single security that is not an underlying security" (1.871-15(l)(2)(i)), so a swap on a qualified index carries no dividend equivalent. The test is made once for each swap, at its own calculation time, not again each year: it "is made at the calculation time for the transaction based on whether the index is a qualified index on the first business day of the calendar year containing the calculation time" ((l)(2)(i)). The calculation time is the earlier of when the swap is priced and when it is issued ((g)(2)(ii)). An index created later in that year is tested on the day it is created ((l)(2)(ii)). The index qualifies ((l)(3)) if it references 25 or more component securities and only long positions; no "component underlying security" (a component interest that "could give rise to a U.S. source dividend", (c)(15)) weighs more than 15%, and no five or fewer of them together weigh more than 40%; it is rebalanced only by publicly stated, predefined criteria; the dividend yield from its component underlying securities in the year before was not above 1.5 times the S&P 500's; and futures or options on it trade on a US-registered exchange or board of trade, or on a qualifying foreign one, in which case the component underlying securities must together weigh less than 50% ((l)(3)(vii)(B)). An index that is widely traded, has 10% or less in component underlying securities and was not formed or availed of mainly to avoid US withholding is qualified regardless ((l)(4)). Two overrides: an index is not qualified if treating it so would be contrary to the rule's purpose, a safe harbor for passive, diverse, widely used indices ((l)(1)); and a swap that also references a short position in a component, other than on the whole index or a short of 5% or less, or that the holder or a related person hedges by reducing exposure to a component, is not treated as referencing a qualified index ((l)(6)). Whether a given index (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, MSCI ACWI) qualified on a given year's first business day was not checked. What the fund then receives is set by the swap's own terms with its counterparty, for example a net index that deducts withholding. Those terms are each fund's facts (`etfs-and-index-funds.md`, ETF-53, ETF-54, ETF open question 28).
- Conditions / exceptions: Examples, from each fund's own report: two Israeli funds on foreign indices report themselves exempt, with shekel units and the US dollar as their main currency under the 2003 regulations, and get their exposure mainly through unfunded swaps or futures. They are Harel's ETF on the S&P 500 (Harel Sal (4D) S&P 500, TASE 1149020) and Migdal's ETF on MSCI ACWI (MTF Sal (4D) MSCI AC World, TASE 1201003). So these two funds report the dollar as their index while tracking through a swap. Whether the 2003 regulations still operate is open (TXS-14). Inferred, no official text says it: s.129ג(א)(1) exempts an exempt fund's income without naming its source, so nothing in Israeli law read makes a swap fund and a fund that holds the shares differ for the holder. They differ in the foreign tax inside the fund, as above, and in costs (the swap's cost, ETF open question 22). The US rules were read in their own words; how a US withholding agent or a swap counterparty applies them to an Israeli fund was not read. No official text compares the two methods, or an Israeli fund with a foreign fund on the same index (TXF open question 8).
- Figures: 25 or more components; 15% for one component underlying security; 40% for five or fewer of them; 1.5 times the S&P 500's dividend yield; under 50% in component underlying securities for an index traded only on a foreign exchange; 10% for the (l)(4) safe harbor; 5% de minimis short; delta-one transactions issued on or after 1.1.2017 (26 CFR 1.871-15 as of 1.10.2026). ### TXS-42 Exemption from withholding on securities (פטור מניכוי מס במקור): who can have it, and an ordinary individual, as a rule, cannot
- Rule: The duty to withhold comes from s.164 of the Ordinance: whoever pays "תמורה כמשמעותה בסעיף 88", interest or dividend withholds "בשעת התשלום ... באופן ובשיעורים שנקבעו". For a sale of securities, fund units or futures the rates and the payer are set by the 2002 regulations: a bank or TASE member paying an individual withholds 25% of the real gain, 15% for an unlinked security, and a body of persons is withheld at the s.126(א) rate (regs. 2, 3ב; TXS-05). The duty falls away for a holder in three ways, each in the regulations' own text. (1) **The list.** The regulations' seller ("מוכר") excludes "אדם מן המפורטים בתוספת" to the 2005 regulations on withholding from interest, dividend and certain gains, so no withholding applies to them under the 2002 regulations at all. The list has 13 items: the State and public bodies taxed like it; a local authority; the Jewish Agency and the World Zionist Organization; Keren Kayemeth; Keren Hayesod; a public institution under s.9(2) "שאישר המנהל לענין זה"; a provident fund; a banking corporation; an insurance company; a city association; a religious council; a mutual fund; and item 13, "כל אדם אחר שאושר לענין זה בידי פקיד השומה לאותה שנת מס". (2) **A written reduction.** "פקיד השומה רשאי להתיר בכתב, כי ניכוי המס לפי תקנות אלה יוקטן", if he thinks excess withholding could arise (reg. 11). The Tax Authority's 2026 execution instruction describes this power as reducing the rate "ואף לאפסו אם שוכנע כי יוותר עודף מס". (3) **A foreign resident** whose gain is exempt is not withheld if he files a declaration on form 2402 with the bank or member, within 14 days of opening the account and once every three years when in Israel (reg. 5). That route is not an Israeli resident's.
- Conditions / exceptions: **An Israeli resident individual.** The law's words do not shut him out: in the Ordinance "אדם" is "לרבות חברה וחבר בני-אדם" (s.1), so item 13 and reg. 11 are not limited to bodies. The Tax Authority's practice does. Its execution instruction 02/2026 (19.1.2026) gives approvals a "capital-market section" ("סעיף שוק ההון") covering interest and all capital-market income, futures and derivatives included (s.18). Companies and cooperative societies are entitled to it after filing the report under s.91(ד)(2ג)(ב) and passing the usual check (s.18.1); so is a partnership made only of companies (s.18.2). For individuals: "ככלל, יחידים לא יהיו זכאים לאישורי ניכוי מס במקור הכוללים סעיף שוק ההון", and the assessing offices are blocked from transmitting it for an individual (s.18.3). Only "במקרים חריגים בלבד", or where an individual "הכנסות מעסק בשוק ההון והוא פתח תיק מוסד כספי במע"מ", is the request examined, by the Assessment Department of the Assessment and Audit Division, not by the assessing office (s.18.3, s.20). The instruction does not say what makes a case exceptional. A one-off approval, for example for a capital-gain transaction, lasts three months and "אינו תקף לחבר בורסה לניכוי משוק ההון" (s.12.1). **How the payer is shown it.** Approvals are electronic only, not printed or mailed; each payer checks the payee's approval online before paying, on the Tax Authority's site or systems (s.1, s.6.3). The 2026 approvals run from 1.1.2026 to 31.3.2027 (s.1); item 13 approves a person "לאותה שנת מס". **Other routes read.** The Tax Authority's guide says in general that whoever expects his tax to be lower than what will be withheld from non-salary income may ask the assessing officer, who "יקבע לו שיעורים מוקטנים או יפטור אותו לגמרי מהניכוי", and that exemption approvals are refused or withdrawn where there are collection defects (guide 2025, printed p.118, s.9(ג), (ד)). It does not mention securities, and the 2026 instruction is the narrower text for the capital-market section. Form 2542, which the instruction names for an individual's request for exemption (s.8.6), lists business payment types only (construction, services, insurance commissions and the like) and no securities income (form of 5.2002). Its service page says "שירות זה מיועד לבעל עסק שמעונין לקבל פטור מניכוי מס במקור או להקטינו" and nothing about securities ([itc2542](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc2542), updated 15.11.2020, read in a browser 2026-11-12). Form 748 gives an individual with an active file an exemption from withholding only for traded securities given as a donation, shown to the TASE member for that transfer (TXS-29; notice of 19.5.2025, s.א.2, s.א.3). **So, for the site's reader:** a salaried saver with an ordinary account will, as a rule, not hold an exemption from withholding on securities. An execution instruction is the Authority's direction to its offices, not law; it is reissued every year. **Not read:** amendments to either regulation after 27.12.2017 (open question 3; the gazettes of 2011, 2012, 2014 and 2017 change neither the seller's definition, nor reg. 11, nor the list); what Interactive Israel means by "פרטים עם פטור מתשלום מס בניירות ערך במקור" (BRK-65, a company's own words); a new immigrant's or veteran returning resident's exemption under s.14 of the Ordinance, and whether it reaches any security traded in Tel Aviv.

## Left out and why

- Pre-2012 and pre-2003 linear-split computations, bonds issued before 8.5.2000 (s.97(a)(1)), and units bought before 1.1.2006: only very old holdings are affected.
- Securities-lending fees in a taxable fund (the prospectus reports 15%): a fund-level detail with no saver choice attached.
- Bonus shares and bonus units (s.94(a)), options, futures (reg.3): not a saver's core path.
- Foreign residents' exemptions (s.97(b2), s.101A(a)(8)): the audience is Israeli residents.
- Companies as unit holders (reg.3B, withholding at the s.126 rate): the audience is individuals.
- REIT taxation: TX2. Surtax, filing thresholds, National Insurance: TXG.

## Proposed dimensions

- `fund-tax-track`: exempt vs taxable mutual fund (TXS-07). It changes when tax is paid, on what base, and whether losses can be used. It applies to every Israeli fund variant in 3.4 and must be read per fund from the prospectus.
