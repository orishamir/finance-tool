## Summary

An Israeli mutual fund (קרן נאמנות) is a trust: a trustee holds the assets as separate property for the unit holders and a licensed fund manager invests them under a prospectus the ISA allowed (MF-01 to MF-04). Open funds are bought and redeemed through a bank or TASE member at a price the manager calculates once per pricing day; an order before the fund's designated hour gets that day's price, a later one the next day's (MF-09 to MF-12). Money-market funds hold short, highly rated shekel or FX assets with an average life of at most 90 days (MF-29 to MF-33). Since 3 October 2024 they come in two kinds: daily funds (redeemable any trading day, no expected yield) and fixed-date "renewable" funds that lock money until a fixed date, may publish an expected yield net of fees, and renew automatically (MF-34 to MF-38). Fees: an annual management fee and trustee fee inside the price; a possible load or exit deduction; a distribution fee the manager pays the bank (capped at 0.1% a year for money-market funds); and bank or broker commissions and custody fees that differ by institution (MF-16 to MF-28). The ISA's 2025 average management fee was 0.17% for money-market funds, 1.01% for active funds and 0.31% for open tracking funds (MF-18). Every fund name carries a two-character code for maximum equity and FX exposure, for example (4A) or (00) (MF-43).

## Rules

### MF-01 A mutual fund is a trust between a trustee and a fund manager
- Rule: A fund can be set up only under a fund agreement between a trustee approved under section 9 and a fund manager approved under section 13. The trustee receives the fund's assets, and the manager runs them. A fund consists of units, "שכל אחת מהן מקנה זכות שווה בקרן" (each giving an equal right in the fund).
- Conditions / exceptions: The fund agreement must state, among other things, the fund name, whether it is open or closed, its pricing dates, the maximum load or redemption deduction, the investment policy, the trustee's and manager's pay, and how the agreement may be changed (section 5(א)(1)-(14)). Changes to the agreement are filed with the ISA before they take effect (section 8(א)).

### MF-02 Fund assets belong to the trustee for the unit holders, are separate, and cannot be attached
- Rule: "נכסי הקרן יהיו מוקנים לנאמן לטובת בעלי היחידות, ויהיו רכוש נפרד מרכושו האחר" (section 76(א)). "נכסי קרן אינם ניתנים לעיקול" (section 79). The manager may not pledge fund assets except to carry out a transaction for the fund (section 79).
- Conditions / exceptions: The trustee gives the manager a power of attorney to manage the assets (section 76(ב)). The ISA's investor guide says the trustee holds the assets in a bank account in trust for the holders and supervises the manager. What happens to holders if the manager or the trustee becomes insolvent is not spelled out in the sections read (see open question 12 and `regulators-and-protection.md`).

### MF-03 The trustee supervises the manager
- Rule: The trustee supervises compliance with the law, the fund agreement and the prospectus, and checks internal controls (section 78(א)). It can order the manager to fix deficiencies within a set time (section 78(ד)) and reports to the ISA and TASE quarterly (section 78(ה)). The ISA guide adds that the trustee reports material deficiencies, and those not corrected, to the ISA.

### MF-04 Units are offered to the public only under an ISA-permitted prospectus, for up to 12 months at a time
- Rule: "הצעה לציבור של יחידות, תיעשה על פי תשקיף שהרשות התירה פרסומו" (section 25(א)). For an open fund whose units are already held by the public, the offering period may start on the prospectus date and must end no later than 12 months after it (section 32(א)(2)). The ISA guide says all funds of a manager are offered through one main prospectus (תשקיף עיקרי) published once a year; a new fund also has its own prospectus; later information is in the annual report, monthly reports and immediate reports.
- Conditions / exceptions: The prospectus date must be no later than 15 days after the ISA's permit unless the ISA extends it, and the prospectus is published by the first business day after that date (section 31). A renewable money-market fund is offered under one prospectus for all its series (MF-35).

### MF-05 Open and closed funds
- Rule: An open fund (קרן פתוחה) and a closed fund (קרן סגורה) are whatever the fund agreement designates (section 1). The ISA guide describes an open fund as one that sells and redeems units on demand at a price based on the fund's net asset value at the end of each trading day, with no limit on the number of units, and whose units are not traded on the exchange. A closed fund issues a limited number of units under the prospectus, which trade on the exchange at supply-and-demand prices.
- Conditions / exceptions: An ETF (קרן סל) is a closed tracking fund designated as an ETF in its fund agreement (section 1); ETFs are covered in `etfs-and-index-funds.md`.

### MF-06 Open tracking funds (קרנות מחקות) and active funds
- Rule: A tracking fund (קרן מחקה) is one whose investment policy aims to achieve results derived from the rate of change in the price of an index or commodity (section 1). An open tracking fund is bought and redeemed like any open fund, at the end-of-day price. An active fund is one in which the manager makes investment decisions to beat its reference, within its stated investment policy.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ISA guide says a tracking fund aims for the index return but does not commit to it, and tracking funds generally charge lower management fees than active funds. Meitav's site describes the open tracking fund the same way ("אינה מתחייבת לכך").
- Figures: End-2025: open tracking funds 799 funds, NIS 128.9 bn; active funds (excluding alternative funds) 1,067 funds, NIS 228.2 bn (ISA 2025 report p. 3).

### MF-07 Legal definition of a money-market fund
- Rule: A money-market fund (קרן כספית) is a fund in which both hold: (1) the assets it holds are characterised by low credit risk, where credit risk means the risk of loss from the default of a body holding the fund's cash and deposits or of another body with a financial obligation to the fund; and (2) the fund agreement says it is a money-market fund. The ISA may set rules and directives for money-market funds on prices, publication, permitted assets, limits, reports and disclosure (section 129ג).
- Conditions / exceptions: Added by Amendment 31 (passed 1 July 2024, published 3 July 2024, in force three months after publication). The ISA's directive gives the in-force date as 3.10.24.

### MF-08 Fixed-date funds (קרן מועדים קבועים): the legal basis for renewable money-market funds
- Rule: The manager of an open fund may set fixed dates in the fund agreement for offering and redeeming units. The interval between adjacent fixed dates may not exceed 12 months, or a longer period the ISA sets by fund type, but never more than 36 months. The manager may make redemption conditional on advance notice from holders, subject to ISA preconditions (section 47 as replaced by Amendment 31).
- Conditions / exceptions: Offering and redemption dates must fall on price-calculation days (section 47(א)(2)). Transitional rules limit changes to fixed dates of funds offered before the amendment (Amendment 31, section 20(ב)-(ג)).
- Figures: 12 months default maximum interval, 36 months absolute maximum; fixed in law, effective 3 October 2024.
- Source: [חוק השקעות משותפות בנאמנות (תיקון מס' 31), התשפ"ד-2024 (ספר החוקים 3232)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lsr_4619060.pdf), p.2 (gazette p.990), s.7 (new s.47, continues p.3) · primary · checked 2026-10-25. ### MF-09 Unit prices: computed by the manager from net asset value, on pricing days only
- Rule: The unit price on any day after the first offering day equals the purchase price of the fund's assets on that day divided by the number of units (section 42(א)(2)); the redemption price of an open fund equals the sale price of its assets (section 42(ב)); the manager computes both from the net asset value (section 43(א)). An open fund sells and redeems units only on days for which it must compute prices (section 46(א)); the Finance Minister sets those days by regulation (section 46(ד)-(ה)). The ISA guide says the price is set at the end of each trading day, after deducting the management fee, trustee fee and other expenses.
- Conditions / exceptions: For fixed-date money-market funds the manager publishes prices for the fixed date only (ISA directive section 2.3). The ISA may set how and when money-market fund prices are published (section 129ג(2)).

### MF-10 Forward pricing and the designated hour (השעה היעודה)
- Rule: Each fund's designated hour is set in its prospectus under rules the TASE clearing house sets after consulting the ISA. An order or redemption instruction that reaches the clearing house before that hour is executed at that day's price; one received on another day or after the hour is treated as submitted on the next price-calculation day (section 46(ג)). So the investor does not know the price when placing the order.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 2017 ISA guide says the designated hour is not the same for all funds, that some funds have more than one designated hour per day, and that the manager reports any change in an immediate report. Meitav's site says orders before the designated hour execute the same day and later orders the next trading day. The actual hours in force were not found (open question 3).

### MF-11 Orders go through a distributor (bank or TASE member), in cash, through the clearing house
- Rule: A distributor (מפיץ) is a TASE member with which orders for units can be placed (section 1). Orders and redemption instructions are submitted to the distributor (section 44(א)) and passed to the manager through the clearing house, in the way and at the time the ISA sets in rules ("בכללים"; section 44(ב) as amended in 2024); from 2.2.2027 the words become "בהוראות" (ISA directives). The manager accepts payment for units in cash only (section 44(ג)). On a sale the distributor transfers the price through the clearing house to the fund (section 45). On redemption the fund pays through the distributor, and "המפיץ יעביר את הסכום האמור לבעל היחידה בו ביום" (section 46(ב)).
- Conditions / exceptions: A distributor may not refuse to distribute units offered under a prospectus the ISA permitted "for reasons related to the fund or the fund manager" ("מטעמים הקשורים בקרן או במנהל הקרן", section 44(א), ISA consolidated text p.36). Amendment 33 of the law (חוק לעידוד פעילות בשוק ההון (תיקוני חקיקה), התשפ"ו-2026, s.3(5), p.3) deletes those words, so from 2.2.2027 the ban on refusing has no such limit, and in s.44(ב) "בכללים" becomes "בהוראות"; s.3 of that law takes effect six months after its publication on 2.8.2026 (s.4, p.8). The same amendment changes s.49(ד) (MF-16), not this rule. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the rule stated the distributor's duty without the limiting words of the current s.44(א) and called the 2027 change an exception marked on the mirror; it now gives the current text and what Amendment 33 changes, with its start date. The ISA guide (2017) says orders can be given in a branch, by phone, on the distributor's website or through an investment adviser, and that the investor buys by amount and receives units. The number of business days until redemption cash is usable was not found (open question 4).

### MF-12 The manager may stop offering units or fill orders only in part
- Rule: The manager may stop and restart public offerings with a newspaper notice (section 46(ו)). The ISA guide says that when a manager decides to create fewer units than ordered, or none, it may reject orders in full or in part, and if it does not fill all orders it must fill them at a uniform rate.
- Conditions / exceptions: If net assets or holder numbers fall below set minimums, the manager must keep offering units, subject to exceptions (section 46(ז)).
- Figures: none

### MF-13 Redemptions can be suspended
- Rule: The ISA chair may suspend offering or redemption for up to three trading days after consulting the TASE chair; the manager, with trustee approval, may suspend redemptions for up to three trading days because of a serious operational disruption; the ISA chair may suspend for up to seven trading days where holders' interests are at risk. Extensions need ministerial approval, up to 60 consecutive days in total. Suspensions are published in a newspaper (section 48).
- Conditions / exceptions: Read through a summarising fetch of the mirror with short quotes; the exact conditions were not checked word for word (open question 12).

### MF-24 Distribution fee (עמלת הפצה): caps by fund type

### MF-26 What a distributor may charge the unit holder directly

### MF-29 Money-market fund: permitted assets
- Rule: A money-market fund may hold only: (א) bonds listed on an exchange or regulated market, not linked except to an exchange rate, with at most one year to maturity, and, if not issued by the State, rated per appendix A; (ב) such bonds with a floating rate reset every six months or less, rated as required; (ג) State-issued makam listed on TASE with at most 13 months to maturity; (ד) debt certificates (תעודת חוב) not linked except to an exchange rate, meeting regulation 2(8)(א) of the assets regulations and rated short-term per appendix A; (ה) such bonds with more than one year to maturity if redeemable at least once every six months, with volatility similar to (א), rated long-term; (ו) for a daily fund, fixed-term deposits (פיקדון לזמן קצוב); (ז) for a fixed-date fund, a bank deposit for a pre-set period of more than one day and no longer than the days remaining to the next fixed date; (ח) cash.
- Conditions / exceptions: The directive replaces the money-market references in the assets regulations; listed regulations of the assets regulations still apply unless the directive says otherwise (directive 1(א)-(ב)). ### MF-31 Money-market fund: concentration and liquidity limits

### MF-33 Money-market fund: repo and reverse repo
- Rule: A money-market fund may do repo (sell bonds and commit to buy back) and reverse repo (buy bonds and commit to sell back) only with the Bank of Israel or with a bank in the first or second credit-risk group under the ISA custodian and credit-risk directive. Repo: commitment at most 30 days (or the next fixed date, if later, for fixed-date funds), cleared through the TASE clearing house, bonds committed at most 10% of fund assets. Reverse repo: bonds received at most 15% of fund assets, market value at least the cash paid at all times, cancellable at up to two business days' notice for daily funds, counterparty and bond issuer not in the same group.
- Conditions / exceptions: Credit regulations apply only so that the fund's credit leverage is zero except through repo.
- Figures: 30 days, 10%, 15%, 2 business days; set by directive.

### MF-35 Renewable (fixed-date) money-market funds (קרן כספית מתחדשת / קרנות כספיות מועדים קבועים)
- Rule: The investor chooses a period (the ISA's examples: a week, a month, a quarter) and may know in advance the expected yield. Liquidity is only at fixed dates known in advance: "המשקיעים אינם יכולים למשוך את כספם לפני תום התקופה". If the investor does not redeem at the end of the period, the money is reinvested automatically for the next period, and so on until they redeem. The expected yield may change from period to period with interest rates. Funds of the same period with different fixed dates are offered as "series" of one fund under one prospectus, differing only in fixed date, expected yield, the date in the series name, the account number and the TASE number.
- Conditions / exceptions: Units of a new series are offered from seven days after the series date (staff position item 4). The ISA example series names: "קרן כספית חודשית 1/MM/2025 מתחדשת", "10/MM/2025", "20/MM/2025".

### MF-36 Expected yield (תשואה צפויה): when and how a renewable fund may publish it
- Rule: A manager may show an expected yield in a fixed-date money-market fund only if assets whose remaining term goes beyond the next fixed date are at most 25% of net assets (that is, at least 75% mature before it). Once it chooses to show one, it must keep showing it for every future fixed date. The yield is shown in annual terms, through a dedicated reporting form, from when the manager decides it can estimate it properly; the report also gives the share of assets maturing before the fixed date and the expected and actual yield for the last known fixed date. After the first publication the manager recomputes it daily up to the designated hour of the fixed date and reports immediately to the ISA and TASE any change of at least 0.1 percentage point. Within five trading days after each fixed date it reports actual against expected yield, including 12-month comparisons. For funds with periods of six months or more it reports an update at least five trading days before the end of the period.
- Conditions / exceptions: Showing an expected yield is optional; a fixed-date fund that never shows one need not meet the 75% condition (directive notes p. 24). Breaching the 25% limit requires an immediate report (directive 7.3).
- Figures: 75% / 25%, 0.1 percentage point, 5 trading days; set by directive.
- Source: [הוראה למנהלי קרנות ונאמנים בדבר תנאים שיחולו לגבי קרן כספית (2024)](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-62bd445f910eb/MoneyMarketFundReg24.pdf), pp.31-34, ss.3.1, 7.3, 9.2-9.3, notes pp.9-11, 24 · primary · checked 2026-10-25.

### MF-44 The "(!)" mark for credit risk
- Rule: The ISA guide (2017) says a manager whose fund may have credit-risk exposure (bonds not rated investment grade, or cash and deposits at banks outside the first rating group on ISA criteria) above its maximum equity exposure must add "(!)" after the exposure profile in the name, for example "קרנית (2A)(!)". Without the mark, the fund commits not to take such exposure above its equity ceiling. For cash and deposits only, the mark relates to banks in the highest-risk group.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ISA directive behind the mark was not read; only the 2017 guide (open question 9). Not in the classification regulations.
- Figures: none

- Dimensions: protection
- Proposed dimension: product-marking
- Likely to change: unknown (text may have changed since 2017)

### MF-45 Classification headings (כותרות מאפיינות)
- Rule: Funds are grouped by characterising headings in three levels, top (כותרת על), main (כותרת ראשית) and sub (כותרת משנית), from a list the ISA publishes on its website and may change with 14 days' notice to managers. The manager picks the heading that best fits the fund's investment policy and reports it within two days after the prospectus date; a change is reported at least two days before it takes effect. Within a heading, funds are ordered by ascending maximum equity grade and then alphabetically.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ISA guide's examples: "(4B) ת"א 125" = מניות בארץ / מדד מניות / ת"א 125; "(00) שקל קצר" = אג"ח בארץ - שקליות / אג"ח שקליות בלבד / שקליות לטווח קצר (עד שנה); "(00) כספית" = קרן כספית / כספית שקלית. ### MF-46 What every fund publication must show, and the special marks
- Rule: A publication classifying funds must show: fund name and TASE number; manager name; whether it is an ETF; its heading; its exposure profile with explanation; shekel return over uniform periods; standard deviation; the manager's annual fee rate; the load rate if a load is charged; and for a קפ"מ an estimated annual return labelled as an estimate with its update frequency (regulation 3(א)). It must also say whether the fund is: unrestricted in foreign securities; a tracking fund; leveraged; inverse-exposure; a fixed-date fund (section 47(א)); denominated in US dollars; a foreign-investment fund (with its principal currency rate); or a קפ"מ (regulation 3(ב)).

### MF-47 Fund naming principles
- Rule: Per the ISA guide (2017): a fund's name may not mislead; should reflect who most influences its management; a name that includes an asset class, exposure level or management style commits the fund to that exposure or style; the name starts with the manager's name or the external investment manager's name; and no word unrelated to the fund's investment policy may appear. Examples of binding meanings: "מניות ת"א 35" at least 50% exposure to that index; "אג"ח מדינה" at least 75% in government bonds; "שקלית" at least 75% in shekel assets and at most 10% non-shekel; "מק"מ ואג"ח בנקים" at least 75% in makam and a commitment to invest in bank bonds; "20/80" at least 80% in bonds and deposits and up to 20% equity.
- Conditions / exceptions: The name is only partial information; the prospectus and immediate reports govern. The full ISA circular on naming was not read (open question 9).
- Figures: as listed (2017)
- Source: [ISA investor guide (2017)](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-64186b589b352/080217.pdf), pp. 14-15 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (dated)
- Dimensions: reporting-obligation
- Proposed dimension: product-marking
- Likely to change: unknown

### MF-48 No share classes: many funds share one policy

### MF-49 Distribution policy: most Israeli funds accumulate
- Rule: The ISA guide distinguishes a distributing fund (קרן מחלקת), which distributes payments to holders once or more a year at the manager's discretion and without link to profits actually made, from a non-distributing fund (קרן שאינה מחלקת), which reinvests income such as dividends and interest. "בפועל, רוב קרנות הנאמנות בארץ הן קרנות שאינן מחלקות תשלומים." The law also lets a manager allot bonus units (יחידות הטבה) to holders without consideration (section 58(ב)).
- Conditions / exceptions: No section on cash distributions was found in the law text read; the rule for a given fund is in its prospectus (open question 16). Tax on distributions belongs to `tax-securities-israeli.md`.
- Figures: none current

### MF-53 Hedge funds in trust, high-tech funds and private investment trust funds: out of tier 1
- Rule: Hedge funds in trust (קרן גידור בנאמנות) are ISA-supervised funds under a prospectus that may use leverage, short selling and derivatives; permanent regulation was completed in 2025. ### MF-54 The exposure-profile code in a fund's name
- Rule: Each Israeli mutual fund carries a two-character exposure profile: a digit for its maximum equity exposure and a letter for its maximum foreign-currency exposure, both as a share of net asset value. Scale: 0 = none; 1/A = up to 10%; 2/B = up to 30%; 3/C = up to 50%; 4/D = up to 120%; 5/E = up to 200%; 6/F = over 200%. A "(!)" in the name flags possible credit-risk exposure (non-investment-grade bonds, bonds of issuers with no Israel nexus, or second-group banks and brokers) above set thresholds.
- Conditions / exceptions: Maya's own tooltips confirm 0, 1, 2, 4 and A, B, D in TASE's wording ("מניות: 4 - עד 120% מהשווי הנקי של נכסי הקרן"). The 3/C, 5/E and 6/F steps and the "(!)" definition come from a fund manager's page, which cites the ISA directive on adding "!" to fund names (new version, 2026); the directive itself was not read.
- Figures: as above.
- Source: [Maya fund list, exposure tooltips](https://maya.tase.co.il/he/funds/mutual-funds) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary; [Migdal Capital Markets, פרופיל חשיפה](https://www.msh.co.il/migdal-capital-markets/mutual-funds/exposure-profile/) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution
- Dimensions: `product-marking`, `currency`
- Likely to change: no.
