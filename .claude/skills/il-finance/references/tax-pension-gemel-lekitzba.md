## Summary

Deposits: an employee's own pension deposit earns a **35% tax credit** (`זיכוי ממס`), not a deduction. The credit is counted on deposits up to 7% of "eligible income" (`הכנסה מזכה`), which for salary is capped at 116,400 NIS a year (9,700 a month). That cap is **CPI-linked and frozen at its 2024 value for 2025 to 2027**. It is **not** tied to the average wage. A self-employed person, or anyone depositing on income without employer deposits, gets a mix of a **deduction** (`ניכוי`, s.47, worth the marginal rate) and the credit. The "beneficiary member" status (`עמית מוטב`, deposits of at least 16% of the average wage, 26,436 NIS in 2026, employer deposits included) changes which caps apply. **Employer deposits** are not taxed when deposited up to 7.5% of salary capped at 2.5 times the average wage (34,423 NIS a month in 2026). Severance deposits are not taxed up to the severance ceiling (45,600 NIS in 2026). These employer ceilings, the 16% test and the continuity ceiling **do** move with the average wage every year. Anything above them is taxed as salary when deposited. **During:** the fund's income is exempt at fund level. Switching tracks or moving the money to another pension fund or provident fund for annuity is not taxed; the money is taxed only when paid out (TXP-32). The exception is severance money moved to a "new account", which has income tax withheld on its non-exempt part before it moves (TXP-32). **Exit:** an annuity from retirement age is partly exempt: 57.5% of a 9,430 NIS a month ceiling in 2026 (5,422 NIS), rising by law to 62.5% in 2027 and 67% from 2028. Exempt severance taken on leaving earlier jobs (within 32 years before entitlement age) reduces that exemption at a 1.35 weighting. A withdrawal that the law does not allow is taxed at the higher of 35% and the marginal rate, with exceptions for low income, medical costs, severe disability and death.

## Rules

### TXP-01 Credit versus deduction: what each is worth
- Rule: s.45A gives a credit of 35% of qualifying amounts paid to a provident fund for annuity (`קופת גמל לקיצבה`), regardless of the person's bracket. s.47 gives a deduction from taxable income, so its value is the person's marginal rate. The Tax Authority guide: "הניכוי משפיע על המס על-פי שיעור המס השולי של הכנסתך. הזיכוי הוא בשיעור של 35% מתשלומיך".
- Conditions / exceptions: A payment deducted under s.47 is not counted again for the s.45A credit (s.47(c), `סכום שנוכה לפי סעיף קטן (ב) או (ב1) לא יובא בחשבון לצורך סעיף 45א`). Amounts paid by the person or the spouse count (`ששילמו הוא או בן זוגו`). The credit also covers payments to the state, a local authority or another body set by the Minister to preserve pension rights, and survivors'-annuity insurance (`ביטוח קצבת שאירים`). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the guide quote is now verbatim ("על-פי", not "לפי"); the Ordinance is read in the Tax Authority's consolidated text, not Wikisource.
- Figures: 35% (fixed in law, s.45A(b)).

### TXP-02 Employee's own deposit: s.45A credit and its ceiling (not a beneficiary member)
- Rule: For an individual who is not a beneficiary member, the total amount on which the credit is given is the **higher** of (1) a fixed floor and (2) the **lower** of the amounts actually paid and, for someone with work income in the year, **7% of eligible income** (`7% מהכנסתו המזכה`). For someone with no work income it is 5%. Eligible income (`הכנסה מזכה`) for someone with only work income is total taxable income up to 116,400 NIS a year.
- Conditions / exceptions: Within the 7%, survivors'-annuity insurance counts for at most 1.5% of eligible income, and life insurance, survivors' insurance and payments on non-work income together for at most 5%. Eligible income for mixed income is the work-income cap plus the non-work cap less work income (s.47(a)). The guide's worked example: salary 80,000 NIS (below the cap) gives a credit of 80,000 × 7% × 35% = 1,960 NIS. "בכל מקרה יינתן לך זיכוי בשל הפקדה מינימאלית של 2,268 ש"ח ששולמו לקופה, ללא קשר לתקרת ההכנסה". The employee's deposit is reported on Form 106 field 045/086 and the credit is computed in annual-return sections 73 to 76; when a deduction or credit is claimed for payments to a provident fund that are not on Form 106, the guide asks for the payment certificates; its form 158 is a declaration only for payments for a child over 18 who is the beneficiary. Whether payroll grants the credit monthly is open question 6. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the form 158 sentence was wrong (form 158 is for a child over 18) and now names the certificates the guide asks for; the "higher of" sentence was re-read in s.45א(ד) of the consolidated Ordinance ("לא יעלה על הגבוה מבין הסכומים המפורטים להלן") and stands, so the 2026-10-23 review annotation is resolved.
- Figures: 35%; 7% of eligible income; eligible-income cap 116,400 NIS/year (9,700/month, booklet row "תקרת הכנסה מזכה 47(א)(1)(1) - רק מהכנסת עבודה"); floor 2,268 NIS/year (189/month, booklet row "45א(ד)"). Tax years 2024 to 2027. Base values in the Ordinance's text: 84,000 (s.47(א)(1)) and 1,632 (s.45א(ד)); updated each 1 January by CPI under s.120B, frozen at the 1.1.2024 values for 2025 to 2027 (TXP-27).

### TXP-03 Beneficiary member (`עמית מוטב`): definition and test
- Rule: A beneficiary member is an individual for whom, because of their income, amounts of at least 16% of the average wage for the year were paid to a provident fund for annuity in the tax year. Being one switches the credit to s.45A(e) (TXP-04) and opens the s.47(b1) deduction (TXP-06).
- Conditions / exceptions: The guide: for an employee, the employer's deposits count toward the 16% ("אצל שכיר יילקחו בחשבון לעניין זה גם הפקדות המעביד לטובתו כ'עמית שכיר'"). The employer deposits are entered in the return (field 248/249) for this test. The guide also says that a beneficiary member may be better off computed as an ordinary individual (track A, `מסלול א'`), and the online system computes both. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the 2024 and 2025 values no longer rest on "the s.47 footnote" (a Wikisource editor note) but on the Tax Authority's 2024 and 2025 booklets, whose 2025 edition explains which average wage applied to which provision.
- Figures: 2026: average wage 13,769 NIS/month; 16% threshold 26,436 NIS/year (deductions booklet 2026). 2025: average wage 12,536, threshold 24,069. In 2025 National Insurance published two average wages; the Tax Authority applied 12,536 (the contributions figure) to the beneficiary-member test and 13,316 (the benefits figure) to the employer ceilings (deductions booklet 2025, chapter א section 5). 2024: 12,536 and 24,069 (deductions booklet 2024). The 16% is the Ordinance's (s.47(א)(7)); the average wage is the one National Insurance publishes, as defined in s.3(ה3)(2). Updated yearly with the average wage; not frozen (the 2026 amount moved during the 2025-2027 freeze).

### TXP-04 Beneficiary member: s.45A(e) credit ceiling
- Rule: For a beneficiary member the credited amount is the higher of the 2,268 NIS floor and the lower of the amounts paid and: (a) with no insured income (`הכנסה מבוטחת`), 5% of taxable income up to twice the s.47(a)(1) amount; (b) with insured income, 7% of eligible income that is insured income, **plus** 5% of taxable income that is not insured income, up to twice the s.47(a)(1) amount less the lower of that amount and the insured income.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 1.5% (survivors' insurance) and 5% (life, survivors, non-work) sub-limits apply as in TXP-02. A beneficiary member may also claim the credit for payments for a child aged 18 or over (s.45A(b1)). "Insured income" is work income on which an employer paid amounts to a provident fund for annuity in the year, plus work income that carries a pension right by law or contract.
- Figures: booklet 2026: "45א(ה)(1)" 189/month; "45א(ה)(2)" 19,400/month (= 2 × 9,700). Tax years 2024 to 2027, frozen (TXP-27).

### TXP-05 s.47 deduction for someone who is not a beneficiary member
- Rule: An individual who is not a beneficiary member and who (or whose spouse) paid amounts to a provident fund for annuity may deduct them up to: (1) 7% of eligible income that is not work income, plus up to 4% more on the part paid above 12% of that income; and (2) the lower of 5% of eligible work income that is not insured income, and 5% of taxable work income up to 2.5 times the s.47(a)(1) amount less insured income.
- Conditions / exceptions: Applies only to payments to a provident fund for annuity (`לקופת גמל לקצבה בלבד`) or to preserve pension rights. Eligible income without work income is capped at 164,400 NIS/year. The guide adds an enlarged rate for those aged 50 or more (TXP-08). The deducted amount does not also earn a credit (TXP-01).
- Figures: 7% (+4% above 12%); 5%; caps 116,400 NIS/year (work) and 164,400 NIS/year (13,700/month, booklet row "47(א)(1)(2) - לא מהכנסת עבודה"), 2024 to 2027, frozen. Base values 84,000 and 118,800 (2004).

### TXP-06 s.47(b1) deduction for a beneficiary member (the self-employed case)
- Rule: A beneficiary member may deduct payments to a provident fund for annuity up to (1) 11% of "income for a self-employed member" (`הכנסה לעמית עצמאי`: taxable income up to the s.47(a)(1) amount, less insured income), and (2) for amounts not deducted under (1), 7% of "additional income" (`הכנסה נוספת`), plus up to 4% more for amounts above 12% of it. The guide calls these the first and second tiers (`רובד ראשון`, `רובד שני`).
- Conditions / exceptions: Additional income is the lower of (1) taxable income that is not insured income, up to the s.47(a)(1) amount, and (2) taxable income up to 2.5 times that amount, less the higher of insured income and that amount. In the second tier, the deposits that made the person a beneficiary member (up to 16% of the average wage) are not deducted again (s.47(b1)(2) and the guide's examples). Amounts deducted under s.47(b) cannot be deducted under (b1) and vice versa (s.47(b2)). A beneficiary member can also deduct for a child aged 18 or over. The guide says deposits cannot be moved between spouses for beneficiary-member purposes. **[2026-10-22 browser pass 3: the guide's worked example of the split is TXP-31.]**
- Figures: 11%; 7% (+4%); booklet 2026: "תקרת הכנסה לעמית עצמאי 47(א)" 9,700/month; "תקרת הכנסה נוספת 47(א)(5)" 24,250/month (= 2.5 × 9,700). Frozen 2024 to 2027.

### TXP-10 Employer pension deposit (employer-contribution component): not taxed at deposit up to the ceiling

### TXP-11 Employer severance deposit: not taxed at deposit up to the severance ceiling
- Rule: Amounts all employers paid to provident funds for annuity on the severance component (`מרכיב הפיצויים`) above the severance ceiling (`תקרת הפיצויים`) are work income when paid to the fund; amounts up to it are income when the employee receives them. The severance ceiling is "the employee's salary on the deposit date or 32,000 NIS, whichever is lower" (Ordinance s.3(ה3), definition `תקרת הפיצויים`); from tax year 2018 the 32,000 is adjusted on 1 January each year by the rise in the average wage, rounded to 100 NIS (s.3(ה3)(1א)(ג)). Severance top-up deposits (`השלמה לפיצויים`) have their own ceiling: the severance ceiling times the years worked for the same employer, less taxable accumulated amounts paid for those years (s.3(e3)(1b)).
- Conditions / exceptions: The severance ceiling and its terms are Ordinance s.3(ה3) terms; the regulations only cap the employer's severance deposit at the rate needed to cover the employer's obligation (with 8⅓% stated, reg. 19(a)(1)(b); the 1964 text prints "8⅓%", and the subdivision (1) appears only in National Insurance's copy). Withdrawal of severance on leaving: TXP-19 to TXP-21. *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the 32,000 and the indexation are attributed to the Ordinance, not the regulations; "2017 base" replaced by the Ordinance's own words (adjusted from 2018); the yearly amounts no longer rest on a Wikisource footnote.
- Figures: 45,600 NIS (2026, deductions booklet 2026); 44,100 (2025, deductions booklet 2025). Indexed on 1 January each year by the rise in the average wage, rounded to 100 NIS (s.3(ה3)(1א)(ג)); not frozen.

### TXP-12 What happens later to employer amounts that were taxed at deposit

The recognized annuity applies only in a fund that is not an "old fund" (`קרן ותיקה`). The s.125C(c) rate is in `tax-interest-deposits-bonds.md`.

### TXP-14 Qualifying annuity (`קצבה מזכה`): partial exemption from retirement age
- Rule: A qualifying annuity received by an individual who has reached retirement age is exempt up to a percentage of the qualifying-annuity ceiling (`תקרת הקצבה המזכה`). A qualifying annuity is all annuities the person receives from an employer, a provident fund or disability insurance, excluding the recognized annuity; survivors' annuities are excluded from s.9A (TXP-23). The exempt amount is the same for everyone who qualifies and is not a share of each person's annuity; it never exceeds the annuity itself (circular 2/2013 s.3.3).
- Conditions / exceptions: Retirement age (`גיל פרישה`) is as defined in s.1 (the Retirement Age Law), and someone who retired because of a permanent disability of 75% or more counts as having reached it. The 2025 guide gives 67 for men; the booklet gives the women's table (62 for those born up to December 1959, rising by steps to 65 for those born from January 1970). Entitlement age (`גיל הזכאות`) is the later of retirement age and the age the person starts receiving the qualifying annuity. For a part year, the ceiling is the sum for the months the annuity was received (guide). The taxable rest is reported in field 258/272; Form 106 shows "קיצבה - שיעור הפטור" and the exempt amount (field 209). Exempt grants reduce this exemption (TXP-15).
- Figures: ceiling 9,430 NIS/month (2024 to 2027; 8,190 in 2012; 9,120 in 2023), CPI-indexed under s.120B and frozen 2025 to 2027. Exempt share by tax year (s.9A(g), Amendment 275): 2012-2015 43.5%; 2016-2019 49%; 2020-2024 52%; 2025 57%; **2026 57.5%** (5,422 NIS/month, booklet); 2027 62.5%; from 2028 67%. Circular 2/2013 says 67% "from 2025"; that was changed by Amendment 275.

### TXP-15 Offset formula (`נוסחת הקיזוז`): exempt severance reduces the annuity exemption
- Rule: For someone who retired from 1.1.2012, exempt retirement grants (`מענקים פטורים`) received for up to 32 years of work before entitlement age, indexed to the CPI and multiplied by a factor (`מקדם`, about 1.35), are subtracted from the exempt capital. Exempt capital (`ההון הפטור`) = exempt share × qualifying-annuity ceiling × the conversion multiplier (`מכפיל ההמרה להון`, 180). What is left (`יתרת ההון הפטורה`), divided by 180, is the monthly exempt annuity; this amount, CPI-indexed, stays with the person for all the years the annuity is paid.
- Conditions / exceptions: The factor is (180 × ceiling × 35%) divided by (32 × the s.9(7a) annual cap on the exempt retirement grant); the circular gives about 1.35 for 2012 data and says it is not expected to change from year to year because both amounts are CPI-indexed. Retirees up to 31.12.2011 use the older combination formula (`נוסחת השילוב`). The guide: "משיכה של מענקי פרישה בפטור לפי סעיף 9(7א) מפחיתה את הפטור על הקצבה". Grants received for years after entitlement age do not reduce the annuity exemption (circular 7.6.3). Someone who will receive grants for pre-entitlement years after reaching entitlement age can tell the assessing officer, and the expected exempt grants are deducted in advance at 1.35 (circular 7.6.1, 7.6.2).
- Figures: 180 (fixed in law, changeable by the Minister with Knesset approval); 32 years; factor about 1.35 (circular).

### TXP-16 Fixing the exemption with the assessing officer (process)
- Rule: Circular 2/2013, "until a new instruction is given": every individual who reaches entitlement age and wants the qualifying-annuity exemption applies to the assessing officer (`פקיד השומה`), who sets the remaining exempt capital after the offset formula. The approval is an amount approval, issued in the year it is produced, with two figures: the exempt annuity for that year, and the exempt share of the qualifying-annuity ceiling. The share stays fixed for the person's life and changes during the phase-in years with each change in the exempt percentage.
- Conditions / exceptions: Choices: keep all of the remaining exempt capital for the monthly exemption, or set part of it aside for tax-free commutation (TXP-17). Someone who chose an exempt annuity and changes their mind within 90 days can change the choice by a reasoned notice to the assessing officer. After five months from the end of the month of the first choice, or after three payslips that include the annuity exemption, whichever is earlier, an exempt annuity can no longer be turned into a commutation reserve; a commutation reserve can still be turned into an exempt annuity (circular 5.6.2). The remaining exempt capital and the commutation reserve are fixed when the exemption is first received (circular 7.6). Someone choosing a different exempt percentage gets a new approval (circular 7.3). Today the step is the Tax Authority's service "בקשה לקיבוע זכויות" on form 161ד, filed with the assessing officer through the personal area (TXP-28; this sentence updated 2026-10-26, scope settling).
- Figures: 90 days; 5 months or 3 payslips.

### TXP-17 Commutation of an annuity (`היוון קצבה`)
- Rule: A lump sum from commuting annuities is exempt up to the year's exempt share × ceiling × 180, or up to the remaining exempt capital if exempt grants were received, provided the person filed a request with the assessing officer, on the form set by the Director, within 90 days from the first time an exemption was given for a qualifying annuity. The monthly exemption is then reduced by the exempt commutation divided by 180. A commutation that is not exempt is taxed at marginal rates and may be spread back over up to six tax years (s.8(c)(3)); the circular adds that from age 70 the spread shortens by one year for every four years of age (from 94 no spreading).
- Conditions / exceptions: Commutation is allowed only within s.23 of the Provident Funds Law; the circular describes it as: the money is in a paying annuity fund (not an old fund, where commutation is not possible), done under the fund's commutation method, and the annuity left after commutation is not below the minimum annuity amount (`סכום קצבה מזערי`), or up to 25% of the annuity for no more than five years. A commutation not in line with s.23 is taxed as a non-compliant withdrawal (TXP-18). To commute, the person shows the assessing officer annuity payslips proving the minimum annuity exists. Commuting severance-component money counts as revoking the notice to keep it for an annuity (s.9A(e)(4), TXP-21). Product limits belong in `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`.
- Figures: 180; 90 days; minimum annuity 5,306 NIS/month (2026, booklet row "מרכיב התגמולים - סכום קצבה מזערי"); 6 years back.

### TXP-18 Early or non-compliant withdrawal (`משיכה שלא כדין`)
- Rule: Money a provident fund pays its member contrary to the provisions under s.23 of the Provident Funds Law is a non-compliant payment under s.87(א)(2) and the regulations under it: "כספים ששולמו על ידי קופת גמל לחבריה בניגוד להוראות לפי סעיף 23 לחוק הפיקוח על קופות גמל". In practice that is a lump sum from a provident fund for annuity that s.23 does not allow, or money taken from a provident fund before the time it may be withdrawn. A payment s.23 allows is not one: an annuity (TXP-14), a commutation of the annuity, or the withdrawal of a "minimal accumulation sum" (`סכום צבירה מזערי`), which circular 2/2013 s.5.2 lists as the lawful lump sums from a provident fund for annuity and taxes as a commutation (TXP-17); how other lawful lump sums are taxed is open question 9. A non-compliant payment is taxed at the **higher of a fixed 35% and the marginal rate**. The fund withholds 35% (reg. 3 of the Income Tax (Rules on Taxing Payments to an Unapproved Fund and Non-Compliant Payments) Regulations 1962, as cited by the circular). *2026-11-12 wording pass:* the rule read, after the guide's sentence ("משיכה מקופת גמל לקצבה, או משיכה מקופת גמל טרם מועד משיכתה, מהווה משיכה שלא כדין", p.45 s.29), as if every withdrawal from a provident fund for annuity were non-compliant; the guide's sentence sits under the heading "משיכות שלא כדין", and the Ordinance and the circular limit it to payments contrary to s.23: "המס על היוון קצבה שלא לפי הוראות סעיף 23 לחוק הפקוח על קופות הגמל, יהיה כשיעור המס המוטל על כל משיכה שלא כדין מקופת גמל" (circular 2/2013 s.5.1).
- Conditions / exceptions: In the 35% computation only basic credit points are given (resident and travel: 2.25 for a man, 2.75 for a woman); in the marginal computation, credits, deductions and offsets are given as for personal-exertion income. The guide reports the income in field 213 (registered spouse's fund) or 313 (the other spouse's fund). Not taxed at that rate when: (a) low household income (regs 34(b)(2) for an employee and 38 for a self-employed member of the 1964 provident fund regulations): the withdrawal up to the gap between the income of the member and spouse and the monthly minimum wage (doubled for a single person or a couple with a child under 18), via form 159א to the fund, which handles it itself; all income from every source counts, exempt income too; the member declares one of the two months before the request, the declaration holds for that month and the next two, and for that period the fund pays the gap without withholding (Tax Authority letter of 26.10.2025); (b) medical expenses (not dental) above half the annual income of the member, spouse and the relative, up to the expense; (c) the member or a relative was found to have a permanent disability of 75% or more after deposits to that account began; (b) and (c) by application on form 159 to the assessing officer; (d) on the member's death, heirs are exempt. The withdrawal is personal income of the account holder. The taxable base is the whole amount paid: reg. 3(1) of the 1962 regulations taxes the member "על אותם כספים" at 35% "גם אם הוא פטור ממס או ששיעור המס שהוא חייב בו נמוך מ־35%", and s.87(ד) sets the regulations aside where the ordinary tax on the same money is higher, which is the "higher of" rule. The Provident Funds Law limits when such money can be withdrawn at all (product rule, `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`).
- Figures: child's age for the doubled minimum income: under 18 (letter of 26.10.2025, p.1 s.ב.3, table: "יחיד עם ילד אחד לפחות שטרם מלאו לו גיל 18"). 35% (2026 booklet: "תשלום שלא כדין מקופת גמל לקצבה – הגבוה מבין 35% ושיעור המס"; for a provident fund for benefits, 35%); minimum wage for the low-income test 6,248 NIS/month, 6,247.67 and doubled 12,495.34 in the letter of 26.10.2025, as of 4/2025 (booklet row "'שכר מינימום' - לצורך משיכה בפטור מקופ"ג"; 2025 guide "נכון ל 12/2025").

### TXP-19 Severance grant on leaving a job: exemption per year of work
- Rule: A capital grant received because of retirement (`מענק הון שנתקבל עקב פרישה`) is exempt up to one month's last salary for each year of work, but never above a fixed amount per year of work (pro rata for part years). Severance-component money in a provident fund for annuity counts as a grant received, even if left in the fund, unless the person used continuity (TXP-20, TXP-21).
- Conditions / exceptions: The Director may exempt more in some cases; salary raised shortly before retirement unreasonably can be ignored; part-time last salary uses the last full-time salary for full-time years. The taxable part is personal-exertion income that may be spread in equal parts over the work years, up to six tax years ending in the year received, or by request over another period including later years (s.8(c)(3)). Taking it exempt reduces the later annuity exemption (TXP-15). The employer attaches grant details to its report for the month of payment; Form 106 shows "מענקים עקב פרישה/מוות ששולמו - סכום המענק הפטור ממס" (field 209) and the taxable part (field 258/272). The employer's notice and the employee's choices on leaving are on the Tax Authority's form 161, "הודעה על פרישה מעבודה" (TXP-29), whose part B splits each grant between annuity continuity, severance continuity, an exempt grant and a taxable grant (TXP-30; this sentence updated 2026-10-26, scope settling). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the yearly amounts (13,310 in 2023; 3,900 in a 1990 base) came from Wikisource notes and were removed; the indexation and freeze are attributed to s.120א-120ב and the 2024 freeze law, and the 2026 amount to the booklet.
- Figures: 13,750 NIS per year of work in 2026 (deductions booklet 2026). The Tax Authority's consolidated text of s.9(7א)(א)(2) still prints the cap in old currency (25,000 לירות). The s.9(7א) amounts are "social reliefs" of s.120א, updated each 1 January by the CPI (s.120ב(א)), with no update on 1 January of 2025 to 2027 (s.120ב(ה)).

### TXP-20 Severance continuity to a new employer (s.9(7a)(a)(4))
- Rule: An employee who, on leaving, had severance money in a provident fund (or received a grant and moved it into the severance component) and elected in writing to the Director to apply this rule is not treated as having received it, provided that within one year they start work with another employer who pays severance into that component, up to a ceiling. If they later leave again without continuing, the periods are joined as if all the years were worked for the last employer, and the earlier grants with their returns count as part of the last employer's grant.
- Conditions / exceptions: Ceiling (`סכום התקרה` for this rule): four times the average wage times the years worked for that employer, plus what is needed to cover that employer's obligations under the Severance Pay Law. Receiving part of the grant taxes the part received. The election can be revoked within two years; then the exemption uses the CPI-indexed salary at retirement and the ceiling at revocation, without the later work period. Any withdrawal of such money before leaving the last employer is taxable. The same rule applies on death.
- Figures: 1 year; 2 years; 4 × average wage per year of work (average-wage-linked, not frozen).
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), pp.38-39, s.9(7a)(a)(4)(a)-(d) · checked 2026-11-07 · primary.

### TXP-21 Leaving severance in the fund for an annuity (s.9(7a)(g), (g1); booklet: "רצף קצבה")
- Rule: Severance money left in a provident fund for annuity is not a grant received if, on leaving, the employee notified the Director that they want to keep it for an annuity. An employee whose severance-component balances in all annuity funds for that employer do not exceed a set amount, or the severance ceiling (`תקרת הפיצויים`) for each year of work, whichever is higher, is **deemed** to have given that notice unless they notified otherwise.
- Conditions / exceptions: The employee can revoke by notice; then the whole amount with its returns is a retirement grant, with the exemption ceiling at the time of revocation, reduced if a grant was received on leaving. Withdrawing severance money as a commutation counts as revoking (s.9A(e)(4)). Amounts split to a former spouse under the Pension Savings Division Law are outside this rule (s.9(7a)(h)). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the yearly amounts (392,270 in 2023; "360,000 in 2017 base") came from Wikisource notes; the Ordinance's 360,000, its indexation and the freeze are now attributed to the law, and the 2026 amount to the booklet.

### TXP-23 Survivors and death before taking the money

### TXP-24 Provident fund for annuity versus pension fund: one tax category

### TXP-27 Which figures are frozen for 2025 to 2027 and which move with the average wage
- Rule: s.120B: on 1 January of each tax year the "social reliefs" (`הנחות סוציאליות`) are indexed by the CPI rise of the previous year. The list in s.120A includes ss.9(7a), 9A, 45A and 47. For tax years 2025 to 2027 they are **not** indexed and stay at their 1 January 2024 values (after rounding). On 1 January 2028 they are indexed from the unrounded 1.1.2024 values using the CPI of tax year 2027. s.3(e3) is not in that list: its amounts are defined by the average wage.
- Conditions / exceptions: Frozen (CPI-linked, 2024 values for 2025 to 2027): eligible-income caps 116,400 / 164,400; credit floor 2,268; qualifying-annuity ceiling 9,430; retirement-grant cap 13,750 and death-grant cap 27,520 per year of work; continuity amount 405,900. Average-wage-linked (change every year, not frozen): employer-component ceiling 2.5 × average wage (34,423 in 2026); severance ceiling (45,600 in 2026); beneficiary-member 16% test (26,436 in 2026); severance-continuity ceiling 4 × average wage per year (TXP-20); the self-employed deposit thresholds (TXP-09). ### TXP-29 Leaving a job: the new form 161 (employer notice and employee choices)
- Rule: From 1 January 2024 employers must report an employee's final retirement on the new form 161, "הודעה על פרישה מעבודה". It covers every amount and right the employer made available, whether paid directly or through provident funds. Part A is the employer's notice. Part B is where the employee records their choices for the severance grants made available to them. Part C is the employer's calculation and instructions to the provident funds on how to characterise the money and what tax to withhold.
- Conditions / exceptions: Retirements before 1.1.2024 reported after that date also use the new form. The employer gives the completed form to the assessing officer that holds its withholding file, and to the employee (or survivors or representatives). Employers file online (CRM or the representatives' system), in person or by post; there is also a digital 161 reporting system. If the employer filled part C2, the employee must ask the assessing officer for instructions to the employer and funds according to their part B choice, then pass those instructions on. Attach the last three payslips, plus a current balance confirmation from the funds if more than three months have passed since retirement. The employee can reach the assessing officer through the online enquiries system, the personal area, or a booked visit. The choices offered in part B were not read (they are in the form PDF).

### TXP-30 Form 161 part B: the employee's four choices for each severance grant
- Rule: For each grant the employer reported (part A.9), the employee splits the amount between: (א) "רצף קצבה", leaving the severance money in the provident fund for annuity so it becomes annuity; (ב) "רצף פיצויים", deferring the tax event to retirement from the next employer, up to the severance-continuity ceiling; (ג) a tax-exempt grant under s.9(7א), up to the exempt-grant ceiling; (ד) a taxable grant (anything above the exempt ceiling, or what the employee takes without using the exemption). Part B.2 lets the employee ask to spread the taxable grant under s.8(ג)(3) (request to the assessing officer on form 116ג) and to direct severance from the employer or a central severance fund to continuity. Part B.3 holds declarations (no other exempt grants for this period, no continuity request from other employers, not yet at eligibility age).
- Conditions / exceptions: Default: the employee may leave part B blank and is treated as asking for annuity continuity, if the employer marked in A.12 that the total does not exceed the default-continuity ceiling and that no grants come from payers other than an annuity fund. Annuity continuity can be reversed at any time on form 161ג, keeping any unused s.9(7א) exemption; reversal covers all amounts designated from that employer (except old pension funds, payer code 5). Severance continuity requires a new employer depositing to a severance component within one year, otherwise the employee reports to the assessing officer within 13 months; it can be reversed within two years; after two years outside a new retirement it loses the exempt grant, and choosing it means no exempt grant now. Severance deposits that were taxed when deposited and reported as "תשלומים פטורים" (part A.10) can be withdrawn from retirement without tax, but their gains are taxed at the s.125ג(ג) rate (the form says 15% for 2023), or they can be taken later as a tax-exempt annuity. The form warns that withdrawing grants reduces the future pension and may reduce the monthly annuity exemption. Ceilings are in footnotes 26 to 28 of the form, not recorded here.
- Figures: none recorded (ceilings in the form's footnotes; see TXP-19 to TXP-21).

### TXP-31 Beneficiary member with income that is not work income: how one year's deposits split between deduction and credit
- Rule: The guide works this through for a person whose taxable income is not from work (240,000 NIS) and who deposits 40,000 NIS to a provident fund for annuity as a self-employed member. It is a beneficiary member (TXP-03).
  - First tier: 11% of "income for a self-employed member" (taxable income up to 116,400, less insured income) is deducted: 11% × 116,400.
  - Second tier: amounts deducted in the first tier, and the deposits that made the person a beneficiary member, are left out. The base is the year's deposits minus the beneficiary threshold: 40,000 − 24,069 = 15,931 (2025 threshold). "Additional income" is 116,400. Of that base, up to 7% of additional income is deducted, plus the part above 12% of additional income, up to 4% more. In the example 15,931 / 116,400 = 13.7%, so 7% + 1.7% = 8.7% × 116,400 = 10,127.
  - Total deduction 12,804 + 10,127 = 22,931. The rest of the deposit, 40,000 − 22,931 = 17,069, "תילקח בחשבון לחישוב הזיכוי": it is credited under s.45A(e) (TXP-04).
- Conditions / exceptions: The guide prints the first-tier figure as "12,408", but 11% × 116,400 = 12,804, the figure its own total uses. The 13.7% is rounded in the guide; computed exactly, the second tier is 8,148 + (15,931 − 13,968) = 10,111. For 2026 the threshold is 26,436 (TXP-03). The guide also says the online system computes both this route and the ordinary one (track A) and the person may be better off under track A (TXP-03).
- Figures: 11%, 7%, 4%, 12%; 116,400 (frozen 2024-2027); threshold 24,069 (2025) / 26,436 (2026).
- Source: [Tax Authority, "דע זכויותיך וחובותיך 2025"](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf), pp.63-64, "הניכוי לפי מסלול ב' ל'עמית מוטב'", example ב "הכנסה שאינה הכנסת עבודה בלבד" · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (page image; downloaded and read, browser pass 3)

### TXP-32 A member's transfer between pension funds or provident funds for annuity is not taxed, except severance money moved to a "new account"
- Rule: Moving money, whole or part, from one pension fund or provident fund for annuity to another annuity fund under the transfer regulations (and a track switch inside the fund, PF-23) is not a payment to the member, so nothing is taxed when it moves; the money keeps its tax history and is taxed only when it is paid out, by TXP-14 to TXP-23. One exception: severance money moved to a "new account" (`חשבון חדש`) has income tax withheld before it moves.
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - **The exception: severance money moved to a new account.** The Provident Funds Law s.23(א)(2א) allows "העברה של כספים ממרכיב הפיצויים בקופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קופת גמל מרכזית לקצבה או מקופת גמל אישית לפיצויים, לחשבון חדש בקופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קרן פנסיה או קופת גמל מרכזית לקצבה, שלא יהיה ניתן להפקיד אליו תשלומי כספים נוספים, וזאת לאחר שנוכה מהסכום האמור מס בהתאם להוראות סעיף 164 לפקודת מס הכנסה לגבי החלק שאינו פטור לפי סעיף 9(7א)". So: the money may come from the severance component of any annuity fund that is not a central fund, a pension fund included, or from an individual severance fund; the new account is never in a pension fund or a central fund; and income tax is withheld (s.164) on the part of the severance money that the severance exemption (s.9(7א)) does not cover, before the move. Money moved this way no longer counts as severance component. Paragraph (2א) was added by Amendment 190 to the Ordinance (s.11(1)(ג), 2012), which then spoke of a "קופת גמל לא משלמת לקצבה" on both sides; Provident Funds Law Amendment 13 (s.10(1)(ב), 2015) gave it the wording above. Its twin, (2ב), moves a beneficiary's money after the member's death to a new account and names no tax withholding; it is not a member's own transfer.
  - **Taxed but not moved.** Amendment 13 also added s.23(ב2)(1) (s.10(4)): "לעניין דיני המס בלבד, יראו כספים מקופת גמל אישית לפיצויים או ממרכיב הפיצויים בקופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קופת גמל מרכזית לקצבה, שנוכה מהם מס כאמור בסעיף קטן (א)(2א) ולא נמשכו בידי העמית ולא הועברו לחשבון חדש כאמור באותו סעיף קטן, ככספים שהועברו לחשבון חדש כאמור, בתום שלושה חודשים מיום ניכוי המס כאמור". Severance money taxed this way that the member neither withdraws nor moves is treated, for tax only, as new-account money three months after the withholding.
  - **What the Ordinance does with new-account money afterwards.** It is "exempt payments" (s.9א(א), "תשלומים פטורים" para (2), "כספים שהועברו לחשבון חדש כאמור בסעיף 23(א)(2א) או (2ב)", p.49; TXA-17), so an annuity from it is a recognised annuity (TXA-18). Its gains are taxed apart: "ריבית ורווחים אחרים שמשך יחיד מקופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קרן פנסיה או קופת גמל מרכזית לקצבה והנובעים מכספים שהועברו לחשבון חדש" are income from interest, the linkage part income from linkage differences, and a partial withdrawal is split pro rata between principal, interest and linkage (s.3(ה4)(3), p.21); on commuting a recognised annuity from new-account money "יחול האמור בסעיף 3(ה4)(3)" (s.9א(ה1), p.51). The new account may be withdrawn as a lump sum (s.23(א)(1)(ב)(2); PEN-30).
  - **Everything else is not taxed: the reading.** No text says "not a tax event" in these words for annuity funds; the rule is a reading of five texts, the same kind of reading as TXK-04. (1) The Provident Funds Law treats a withdrawal and a transfer as different acts: s.23(א) governs "משיכת כספים מקופת גמל, לרבות קבלת קצבה מקופת גמל לקצבה, והעברת כספים מקופת גמל אחת לקופת גמל אחרת", with "(1) משיכה בידי עמית" and "(2) העברה בידי עמית" as separate paragraphs; the transfer regulations are made under s.23 (SW-40). (2) The Ordinance's charge on provident money paid out against the rules reaches only "כספים ששולמו על ידי קופת גמל לחבריה בניגוד להוראות לפי סעיף 23 לחוק הפיקוח על קופות גמל" (s.87(א)(2), TXP-18); a transfer the regulations allow is neither paid to the member nor against them. (3) The transfer regulations carry the payment dates over: on a transfer to an annuity fund "יראו את כל מועדי התשלום לקופה המעבירה כמועדי התשלום לקופה המקבלת", and only where the receiving fund is an insurer's fund are they carried over "רק לעניין דיני המס" (reg. 2(ב)); for tax law the money is treated as having been in the receiving fund all along either way. (4) The one other transfer between annuity funds that the Ordinance makes income is named in terms: sums moved to a new account in the name of a former spouse under the Pension Savings Division Law "יראו אותם כהכנסה שהתקבלה בידי העמית" (s.3(ה7)); no provision does that for a member's own transfer, and (2א) above names its own withholding. (5) The Ordinance does give the Minister of Finance a power to exempt transfers: "שר האוצר, באישור ועדת הכספים של הכנסת, רשאי לקבוע כללים לענין מתן פטור ממס בשל סכומים שהועברו מקופת גמל לקופת גמל אחרת, או בשל סכומים ששונה ייעודם באותה קופה" (s.3(ד)(2), p.19). That power sits in s.3(ד), whose para (1) makes sums a provident fund pays back to an employer the employer's income, and the rules made under it are the 1990 employer rules below; read with (1) to (4), it does not create a charge on a member's transfer, since no charging provision names one (a reading; no text says so).
  - **Supporting evidence.** The Minister of Finance, presenting the child-savings regulations to the Finance Committee, wrote in general terms that "העברת כספים בין קופות גמל נהנית מהטבה של דחיית מס" (letter of 10.8.2016, p.2); the letter is about a kupat gemel lehashkaa and is not a provision.
  - **The employer's side** is exempt in terms, within limits: the 1990 rules, made under s.3(ד)(2), exempt "סכומים שהועברו על ידי מעביד מקופת גמל אחת לקופת גמל אחרת" in listed cases (s.2(א)), among them "(1) העברה מקופת גמל לקיצבה שאינה קופת ביטוח לקופת גמל אחרת לקיצבה שאינה קופת ביטוח" and "(2) העברה מקופת ביטוח לקופה אחרת המשמשת לאותה מטרה, למעט העברה לקופת גמל לקיצבה שאינה קופת ביטוח". So an employer's transfer between annuity funds is exempt only between funds that are not insurer's funds, or from an insurer's fund to another insurer's fund; an employer's transfer from an insurer's fund to a non-insurer annuity fund is excluded. The exemption applies only "אם המעביר לא תבע בניכוי כל סכום בשל ההעברה" (s.2(ב), printed p.1004). See SW-40.
  - Which moves are allowed, between which funds and up to what amount, is a product rule (SW-40, PEN-61). Not covered here: a transfer to a former spouse (s.3(ה7), taxed as above), and money leaving the provident system, which is a withdrawal (TXP-18).
- Figures: three months (s.23(ב2)(1)). Fixed in law.

### TXP-33 When an annuity from a provident fund for annuity counts as personal-effort income
- Rule: The s.1 definition of personal-effort income (`הכנסה מיגיעה אישית`) opens "לרבות" and lists: (1) an annuity paid by a former employer; (2) "קיצבה המשתלמת מאת קופת גמל לקיצבה בשל עבודה, או מכוח חברות בה במשך חמש שנים לפחות, לאדם שבחמש השנים שקדמו לתחילת תשלום הקיצבה היתה מרבית הכנסתו החייבת מיגיעה אישית"; (3) a survivors' annuity paid on rights under (1) or (2); (3א) a disability annuity from an annuity or benefits fund; (4) an annuity from the National Insurance Institute; (5) a grant on retirement or death; (6) a sum received by commuting an annuity under (1) to (4); (7) certain rent.
- Conditions / exceptions:
  - The text, read: an annuity from a provident fund for annuity is personal-effort income if it is paid "בשל עבודה", or "מכוח חברות בה במשך חמש שנים לפחות", with the trailing condition that most of the person's taxable income in the five years before the annuity began was personal-effort income.
  - Two points are readings, not text, and are open question 17. (a) The trailing words "לאדם שבחמש השנים שקדמו … מיגיעה אישית" follow the second half after a comma; they may govern only the five-year membership half (so an annuity "בשל עבודה" qualifies with no further test) or both halves (so even an annuity for work needs the income test). (b) That an annuity outside para (2) is income that is not from personal effort is an inference: the definition opens with "לרבות" (includes), so its list extends the term and does not in terms close it. The Ordinance also does not say which annuities are "בשל עבודה"; whether that covers, for example, an annuity bought with money moved in from a kupat gemel lehashkaa (TXK-10) is the same open question.
  - The difference matters for the brackets before 60 (TXG-02, TXG-03); from 60 the personal-effort brackets apply to all taxable income (TXG-04). The s.9A exemption (TXP-14) is a separate test.
- Figures: five years; "מרבית" (most). Fixed in law.
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf#page=14), p.14, s.1 definition "הכנסה מיגיעה אישית" (head and para (1)) "קיצבה המשתלמת מאת מעביד לשעבר" · checked 2026-11-07 · primary. [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf#page=15), p.15, same definition, paras (2) to (7), "קיצבה המשתלמת מאת קופת גמל לקיצבה בשל עבודה" · checked 2026-11-07 · primary.

- Disability insurance (`אובדן כושר עבודה`) deductions (s.32(14)): not a savings rule. Noted only for TXP-07. The guide adds that employer deposits above 7.5% remove the employee's disability-insurance deduction (printed p.59); left for `tax-general.md` if needed.
- Pension Savings Division Law (divorce) transfers: rare; mentioned only as an exception in TXP-21.
- Budgetary (state) pensions, kibbutz members, foreign pensions (s.9C): not tier-1 savings products.
- Life-insurance credit (25%, s.45A(a)): insurance, not savings.
- National Insurance and health tax on deposits and annuities: `tax-general.md`.
- No computed 2026 shekel amounts (for example the maximum exempt employer deposit or the 2026 exempt capital) are given; they are computations for `docs/method.md`.

## Proposed dimensions

- `employment-status`: whether the person is salaried, self-employed or both changes which deposit benefit applies (TXP-02 to TXP-09).
- `death-and-survivors`: what heirs and survivors receive and how it is taxed (TXP-22, TXP-23).
