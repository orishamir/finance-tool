## Summary

Deposits: an employee's own pension deposit earns a **35% tax credit** (`זיכוי ממס`), not a deduction. The credit is counted on deposits up to 7% of "eligible income" (`הכנסה מזכה`), which for salary is capped at 116,400 NIS a year (9,700 a month). That cap is **CPI-linked and frozen at its 2024 value for 2025 to 2027**. It is **not** tied to the average wage. A self-employed person, or anyone depositing on income without employer deposits, gets a mix of a **deduction** (`ניכוי`, s.47, worth the marginal rate) and the credit. The "beneficiary member" status (`עמית מוטב`, deposits of at least 16% of the average wage, 26,436 NIS in 2026, employer deposits included) changes which caps apply. **Employer deposits** are not taxed when deposited up to 7.5% of salary capped at 2.5 times the average wage (34,423 NIS a month in 2026). Severance deposits are not taxed up to the severance ceiling (45,600 NIS in 2026). These employer ceilings, the 16% test and the continuity ceiling **do** move with the average wage every year. Anything above them is taxed as salary when deposited. **During:** the fund's income is exempt at fund level. **Exit:** an annuity from retirement age is partly exempt: 57.5% of a 9,430 NIS a month ceiling in 2026 (5,422 NIS), rising by law to 62.5% in 2027 and 67% from 2028. Exempt severance taken on leaving earlier jobs (within 32 years before entitlement age) reduces that exemption at a 1.35 weighting. A withdrawal that the law does not allow is taxed at the higher of 35% and the marginal rate, with exceptions for low income, medical costs, severe disability and death.

## Rules

### TXP-01 Credit versus deduction: what each is worth
- Rule: s.45A gives a credit of 35% of qualifying amounts paid to a provident fund for annuity (`קופת גמל לקיצבה`), regardless of the person's bracket. s.47 gives a deduction from taxable income, so its value is the person's marginal rate. The Tax Authority guide: "הניכוי משפיע על המס לפי שיעור המס השולי של הכנסתך. הזיכוי הוא בשיעור של 35% מתשלומיך".
- Conditions / exceptions: A payment deducted under s.47 is not counted again for the s.45A credit (s.47(c), `סכום שנוכה לפי סעיף קטן (ב) או (ב1) לא יובא בחשבון לצורך סעיף 45א`). Amounts paid by the person or the spouse count (`ששילמו הוא או בן זוגו`). The credit also covers payments to the state, a local authority or another body set by the Minister to preserve pension rights, and survivors'-annuity insurance (`ביטוח קצבת שאירים`).
- Figures: 35% (fixed in law, s.45A(b)).
- Source: [Income Tax Ordinance s.45A(b), s.47(c), Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Tax Authority, "דע זכויותיך וחובותיך 2025", printed p.60 and p.68](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`
- Likely to change: no

### TXP-02 Employee's own deposit: s.45A credit and its ceiling (not a beneficiary member)
- Rule: For an individual who is not a beneficiary member, the total amount on which the credit is given is the **higher** of (1) a fixed floor and (2) the **lower** of the amounts actually paid and, for someone with work income in the year, **7% of eligible income** (`7% מהכנסתו המזכה`). For someone with no work income it is 5%. Eligible income (`הכנסה מזכה`) for someone with only work income is total taxable income up to 116,400 NIS a year.
- Conditions / exceptions: Within the 7%, survivors'-annuity insurance counts for at most 1.5% of eligible income, and life insurance, survivors' insurance and payments on non-work income together for at most 5%. Eligible income for mixed income is the work-income cap plus the non-work cap less work income (s.47(a)). The guide's worked example: salary 80,000 NIS (below the cap) gives a credit of 80,000 × 7% × 35% = 1,960 NIS. "בכל מקרה יינתן לך זיכוי בשל הפקדה מינימאלית של 2,268 ש"ח ששולמו לקופה, ללא קשר לתקרת ההכנסה". The employee's deposit is reported on Form 106 field 045/086 and the credit is computed in annual-return sections 73 to 76; the guide asks for a declaration on form 158 for credits claimed in the return. Whether payroll grants the credit monthly is open question 6.
- Figures: 35%; 7% of eligible income; eligible-income cap 116,400 NIS/year (9,700/month, booklet row "תקרת הכנסה מזכה 47(א)(1)(1) - רק מהכנסת עבודה"); floor 2,268 NIS/year (189/month, booklet row "45א(ד)"). Tax years 2024 to 2027. Base values 84,000 and 1,632 (2004), updated each 1 January by CPI under s.120B, frozen at the 1.1.2024 values for 2025 to 2027 (TXP-27).
- Source: Ordinance s.45A(d), s.47(a) (link above) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Tax Authority, deductions booklet from January 2026, updated April 2026, p.12](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. Guide 2025, printed pp.68-69 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `deposit-ceiling`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: yes. CPI indexation resumes on 1.1.2028 (TXP-27).

### TXP-03 Beneficiary member (`עמית מוטב`): definition and test
- Rule: A beneficiary member is an individual for whom, because of their income, amounts of at least 16% of the average wage for the year were paid to a provident fund for annuity in the tax year. Being one switches the credit to s.45A(e) (TXP-04) and opens the s.47(b1) deduction (TXP-06).
- Conditions / exceptions: The guide: for an employee, the employer's deposits count toward the 16% ("אצל שכיר יילקחו בחשבון לעניין זה גם הפקדות המעביד לטובתו כ'עמית שכיר'"). The employer deposits are entered in the return (field 248/249) for this test. The guide also says that a beneficiary member may be better off computed as an ordinary individual (track A, `מסלול א'`), and the online system computes both.
- Figures: 2026: average wage 13,769 NIS/month; 16% threshold 26,436 NIS/year. 2024 and 2025: 12,536 and 24,069 (as the s.47 footnote and the 2025 guide state; see open question 4). Updated yearly with the average wage as published by National Insurance (s.3(e3)(2) definition, referenced by s.47(a)). Not frozen.
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.47(a) definitions `עמית מוטב`, `השכר הממוצע במשק` · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Booklet 2026](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf>) p.12 rows "שכר ממוצע במשק 13,769" and "עמית מוטב 47(א)(7) 26,436 לשנה" · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Guide 2025](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf>) printed pp.60-61, p.64 (section 59) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: `eligibility`, `deposit-ceiling`, `tax-at-deposit`
- Likely to change: yes, every year with the average wage.

### TXP-04 Beneficiary member: s.45A(e) credit ceiling
- Rule: For a beneficiary member the credited amount is the higher of the 2,268 NIS floor and the lower of the amounts paid and: (a) with no insured income (`הכנסה מבוטחת`), 5% of taxable income up to twice the s.47(a)(1) amount; (b) with insured income, 7% of eligible income that is insured income, **plus** 5% of taxable income that is not insured income, up to twice the s.47(a)(1) amount less the lower of that amount and the insured income.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 1.5% (survivors' insurance) and 5% (life, survivors, non-work) sub-limits apply as in TXP-02. A beneficiary member may also claim the credit for payments for a child aged 18 or over (s.45A(b1)). "Insured income" is work income on which an employer paid amounts to a provident fund for annuity in the year, plus work income that carries a pension right by law or contract.
- Figures: booklet 2026: "45א(ה)(1)" 189/month; "45א(ה)(2)" 19,400/month (= 2 × 9,700). Tax years 2024 to 2027, frozen (TXP-27).
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.45A(e), s.47(a) `הכנסה מבוטחת` · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Booklet 2026](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf>) p.12 · primary. ### TXP-05 s.47 deduction for someone who is not a beneficiary member
- Rule: An individual who is not a beneficiary member and who (or whose spouse) paid amounts to a provident fund for annuity may deduct them up to: (1) 7% of eligible income that is not work income, plus up to 4% more on the part paid above 12% of that income; and (2) the lower of 5% of eligible work income that is not insured income, and 5% of taxable work income up to 2.5 times the s.47(a)(1) amount less insured income.
- Conditions / exceptions: Applies only to payments to a provident fund for annuity (`לקופת גמל לקצבה בלבד`) or to preserve pension rights. Eligible income without work income is capped at 164,400 NIS/year. The guide adds an enlarged rate for those aged 50 or more (TXP-08). The deducted amount does not also earn a credit (TXP-01).
- Figures: 7% (+4% above 12%); 5%; caps 116,400 NIS/year (work) and 164,400 NIS/year (13,700/month, booklet row "47(א)(1)(2) - לא מהכנסת עבודה"), 2024 to 2027, frozen. Base values 84,000 and 118,800 (2004).
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.47(a), (b) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Booklet 2026](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf>) p.12 · primary. [Guide 2025](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf>) printed pp.60-61 (worked examples) · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `deposit-ceiling`
- Likely to change: yes (indexation resumes 2028).

### TXP-06 s.47(b1) deduction for a beneficiary member (the self-employed case)
- Rule: A beneficiary member may deduct payments to a provident fund for annuity up to (1) 11% of "income for a self-employed member" (`הכנסה לעמית עצמאי`: taxable income up to the s.47(a)(1) amount, less insured income), and (2) for amounts not deducted under (1), 7% of "additional income" (`הכנסה נוספת`), plus up to 4% more for amounts above 12% of it. The guide calls these the first and second tiers (`רובד ראשון`, `רובד שני`).
- Conditions / exceptions: Additional income is the lower of (1) taxable income that is not insured income, up to the s.47(a)(1) amount, and (2) taxable income up to 2.5 times that amount, less the higher of insured income and that amount. In the second tier, the deposits that made the person a beneficiary member (up to 16% of the average wage) are not deducted again (s.47(b1)(2) and the guide's examples). Amounts deducted under s.47(b) cannot be deducted under (b1) and vice versa (s.47(b2)). A beneficiary member can also deduct for a child aged 18 or over. The guide says deposits cannot be moved between spouses for beneficiary-member purposes.
- Figures: 11%; 7% (+4%); booklet 2026: "תקרת הכנסה לעמית עצמאי 47(א)" 9,700/month; "תקרת הכנסה נוספת 47(א)(5)" 24,250/month (= 2.5 × 9,700). Frozen 2024 to 2027.
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.47(a), (b1), (b2) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Booklet 2026](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf>) p.12 · primary. [Guide 2025](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf>) printed pp.60-62 · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `deposit-ceiling`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: yes (indexation resumes 2028).

### TXP-07 Extra 0.5% credit for a self-employed person without a disability-insurance deduction
- Rule: An individual who was not allowed a deduction for preferred disability insurance under s.32(14)(b) (`ביטוח מועדף מפני אבדן כושר עבודה`) gets a 35% credit on deposits as a self-employed member, up to 0.5% of business or professional income, capped at twice the s.47(a)(1) amount less insured income.
- Conditions / exceptions: Only for deposits "as a self-employed member". The 5.5% figure seen in secondary sources appears to be 5% (s.45A(e)) plus this 0.5%; the law text has no 5.5% rate.
- Figures: 35% on up to 0.5%; cap 232,800 NIS/year (2 × 116,400) less insured income, 2024 to 2027.
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.45A(f) (`סעיף 45א(ו)`) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror).
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: yes (indexation resumes 2028).

### TXP-08 Enlarged deduction from age 50 (2025 guide)
- Rule: The 2025 guide: someone who was 50 or older on 1.1.2025 is entitled, for income that is not salary, to higher deduction rates: up to 10.5% instead of 7%, 6% instead of 4%, and 16.5% instead of 11%. For "salary that is not insured", 7.5% instead of 5%.
- Conditions / exceptions: Subject to maximum amounts 11,508 or 18,084 NIS (non-salary) and 5,820 NIS (non-insured salary), tax year 2025. The guide cites the Income Tax (Deduction of Payments for Benefits or Annuity) Regulations 1980 (`תקנות מס הכנסה (ניכוי תשלומים בעד תגמולים או קצבה) התש"ם-1980`), which were not read.
- Figures: 2025 only. 2026 amounts not found (open question 7).

### TXP-09 Self-employed: the duty to deposit and how it meets the benefits
- Rule: The 2025 guide: since January 2017 every self-employed person aged over 21 and up to early-retirement age must deposit to a provident fund for annuity: 4.45% on income up to half the average wage and 12.55% on the part between half and the full average wage. Deposits made as an employee on insured income are subtracted. Not depositing brings a 500 NIS fine. Someone aged 55 or older on 1.1.2017 is exempt from the duty.
- Conditions / exceptions: The deposit earns the s.47 deduction and s.45A credit as in TXP-05 to TXP-07. The duty itself is a product rule and belongs in `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`; the governing law section was not read (open question 8).
- Figures: 2025 (guide): half average wage 75,216 NIS/year, average wage 150,432 NIS/year; worked example income 150,432 gives 12,787 NIS. 2026 thresholds not published in the sources read; the 2026 average wage is 13,769 NIS/month (booklet p.12). Average-wage-linked.
- Source: [Guide 2025](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf>) printed pp.59-60 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: `eligibility`, `deposit-ceiling`
- Likely to change: yes, yearly with the average wage.

### TXP-10 Employer pension deposit (employer-contribution component): not taxed at deposit up to the ceiling
- Rule: Amounts that all of an employee's employers paid for the employee to provident funds for annuity on the employer-contribution component (`מרכיב תגמולי המעביד`), **above** "the deposit rate multiplied by the employee's salary or by the ceiling amount, whichever is lower", are the employee's work income **when paid to the fund**. Amounts up to that level are work income only when the employee receives them.
- Conditions / exceptions: The deposit rate (`השיעור להפקדה`) is the lower of the rate set under s.22 of the Provident Funds Law for the employer component and the rate the employer actually deposited. The regulations set the employer-component cap at 7.5% of salary for a fund for annuity (reg. 19(a)(1)(c)(1), `שיעור שלא יעלה על 7.5% מן המשכורת`). The ceiling amount (`סכום התקרה`) is 2.5 times the monthly average wage. "Salary" is the salary on which the employer paid the fund. The ceilings apply to payments and salaries from all employers together; a salaried person with several employers whose deposits exceeded the ceilings attaches a computation on form 134 to the return. Above-ceiling amounts appear in Form 106 as "סה"כ ההפרשה שהעובד חוייב במס בגינה" and are included in salary field 158/172. What happens to the taxed amount later: TXP-12.
- Figures: 7.5% (regulations); ceiling 34,423 NIS/month in 2026 (booklet row "תקרה להפרשות המעביד לקופת גמל לקצבה 3(ה3)"); 33,290 (2025), 31,340 (2024), 29,675 (2023). Updated every year with the average wage (2.5 × average wage); not frozen.
- Source: Ordinance s.3(e3)(1), (2) (`סעיף 3(ה3)`) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Income Tax (Rules for Approval and Management of Provident Funds) Regulations 1964, reg. 19, Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_מס_הכנסה_(כללים_לאישור_ולניהול_קופות_גמל)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror; version currency unconfirmed, open question 12). Booklet 2026 p.12 · primary. Guide 2025 printed p.29 section "ו" and p.20 item 16 · primary. [Form 106, updated 1.2026](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/itc-106/en/Service_Pages_Income_tax_itc106-acc.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `deposit-ceiling`
- Likely to change: yes, every year with the average wage.

### TXP-11 Employer severance deposit: not taxed at deposit up to the severance ceiling
- Rule: Amounts all employers paid to provident funds for annuity on the severance component (`מרכיב הפיצויים`) above the severance ceiling (`תקרת הפיצויים`) are work income when paid to the fund; amounts up to it are income when the employee receives them. The severance ceiling is "the employee's salary on the deposit date or 32,000 NIS (2017 base), whichever is lower". Severance top-up deposits (`השלמה לפיצויים`) have their own ceiling: the severance ceiling times the years worked for the same employer, less taxable accumulated amounts paid for those years (s.3(e3)(1b)).
- Conditions / exceptions: The regulations cap the employer's severance deposit at the rate needed to cover the employer's obligation (with 8 1/3% stated, reg. 19(a)(1)(b)(1)). The booklet labels the 45,600 figure "לשנה" (per year); how the "salary on the deposit date" limb is applied over a year is open question 5. Withdrawal of severance on leaving: TXP-19 to TXP-21.
- Figures: 45,600 NIS (2026), 44,100 (2025), 41,500 (2024), 39,300 (2023). Indexed on 1 January each year by the rise in the average wage, rounded to 100 NIS (s.3(e3)(1a)(c)); not frozen.
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.3(e3)(1a), (1b), (2) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Booklet 2026](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf>) p.12 row "תקרת סכום הפיצויים להפקדה 3(ה3) 45,600 לשנה" · primary. - Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `deposit-ceiling`
- Likely to change: yes, yearly.

### TXP-12 What happens later to employer amounts that were taxed at deposit
- Rule: Employer amounts taxed at deposit become "exempt payments" (`תשלומים פטורים`). The part of an annuity that comes from exempt payments is a "recognized annuity" (`קצבה מוכרת`), exempt with no ceiling from age 60, or from early retirement for disability. Severance amounts taxed at deposit (s.3(e3)(1a), (1b)) and later received as a grant: their interest and other gains are taxed at the rate in s.125C(c) when withdrawn (s.9(7a)(a1)(1)); a partial withdrawal is treated as including gains pro rata.
- Conditions / exceptions: "Exempt payments" also include employee payments above the statutory maximum and self-paid amounts that got no s.45A credit or s.47 deduction; those routes, and lump-sum commutation of a recognized annuity (only the "relative profit component" taxed, at 15%, s.9A(e)(3)), are covered in `tax-insurance-and-amendment-190.md`. The recognized annuity applies only in a fund that is not an "old fund" (`קרן ותיקה`). The s.125C(c) rate is in `tax-interest-deposits-bonds.md`.
- Figures: age 60; 15% on the relative profit component.
- Source: Ordinance s.9A(a) definitions, (b1), (e)(3); s.9(7a)(a1) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Tax Authority, Income Tax circular 2/2013 (27.5.2013), s.2.2.1](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/income-tax-professional-inst-02-2013/he/Policy_IncomeTaxInst_hoz02-2013.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-at-deposit`, `tax-at-exit`, `age-or-use-exemption`
- Likely to change: no

### TXP-13 Growth inside the fund: the fund's income is exempt; exceptions
- Rule: The income of a provident fund is exempt (`הכנסתה של קופת גמל אם לא הושגה מעסק שקופת הגמל עוסקת בו`). No annual tax on the member for the fund's gains was found in the sources read; the member is taxed only at exit, by the rules in TXP-14 to TXP-23.
- Conditions / exceptions: Not exempt: income from a business the fund itself carries on, and income paid by a company in a business the fund controls, or in which it has a "material holding" (`החזקה מהותית`, above the holding limits in ss.26 and 39(b) of the Provident Funds Law, as in force when the Economic Efficiency Law 2026 took effect). These are fund-level exceptions; whether they reduce member returns is not quantified. s.9(18) separately exempts interest, linkage and gains that an individual receives "from a provident fund for benefits" (`מקופת גמל לתגמולים`) from their own or employer deposits; its wording names the benefits type, not the annuity type (open question 9).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.9(2), s.9(18) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). [Guide 2025](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf>) printed p.87 (s.9(18) row) · primary.
- Dimensions: `tax-during`
- Likely to change: no (the material-holding rule is new in 2026).

### TXP-14 Qualifying annuity (`קצבה מזכה`): partial exemption from retirement age
- Rule: A qualifying annuity received by an individual who has reached retirement age is exempt up to a percentage of the qualifying-annuity ceiling (`תקרת הקצבה המזכה`). A qualifying annuity is all annuities the person receives from an employer, a provident fund or disability insurance, excluding the recognized annuity; survivors' annuities are excluded from s.9A (TXP-23). The exempt amount is the same for everyone who qualifies and is not a share of each person's annuity; it never exceeds the annuity itself (circular 2/2013 s.3.3).
- Conditions / exceptions: Retirement age (`גיל פרישה`) is as defined in s.1 (the Retirement Age Law), and someone who retired because of a permanent disability of 75% or more counts as having reached it. The 2025 guide gives 67 for men; the booklet gives the women's table (62 for those born up to December 1959, rising by steps to 65 for those born from January 1970). Entitlement age (`גיל הזכאות`) is the later of retirement age and the age the person starts receiving the qualifying annuity. For a part year, the ceiling is the sum for the months the annuity was received (guide). The taxable rest is reported in field 258/272; Form 106 shows "קיצבה - שיעור הפטור" and the exempt amount (field 209). Exempt grants reduce this exemption (TXP-15).
- Figures: ceiling 9,430 NIS/month (2024 to 2027; 8,190 in 2012; 9,120 in 2023), CPI-indexed under s.120B and frozen 2025 to 2027. Exempt share by tax year (s.9A(g), Amendment 275): 2012-2015 43.5%; 2016-2019 49%; 2020-2024 52%; 2025 57%; **2026 57.5%** (5,422 NIS/month, booklet); 2027 62.5%; from 2028 67%. Circular 2/2013 says 67% "from 2025"; that was changed by Amendment 275.
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.9A(a), (b), (g) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). ### TXP-15 Offset formula (`נוסחת הקיזוז`): exempt severance reduces the annuity exemption
- Rule: For someone who retired from 1.1.2012, exempt retirement grants (`מענקים פטורים`) received for up to 32 years of work before entitlement age, indexed to the CPI and multiplied by a factor (`מקדם`, about 1.35), are subtracted from the exempt capital. Exempt capital (`ההון הפטור`) = exempt share × qualifying-annuity ceiling × the conversion multiplier (`מכפיל ההמרה להון`, 180). What is left (`יתרת ההון הפטורה`), divided by 180, is the monthly exempt annuity; this amount, CPI-indexed, stays with the person for all the years the annuity is paid.
- Conditions / exceptions: The factor is (180 × ceiling × 35%) divided by (32 × the s.9(7a) annual cap on the exempt retirement grant); the circular gives about 1.35 for 2012 data and says it is not expected to change from year to year because both amounts are CPI-indexed. Retirees up to 31.12.2011 use the older combination formula (`נוסחת השילוב`). The guide: "משיכה של מענקי פרישה בפטור לפי סעיף 9(7א) מפחיתה את הפטור על הקצבה". Grants received for years after entitlement age do not reduce the annuity exemption (circular 7.6.3). Someone who will receive grants for pre-entitlement years after reaching entitlement age can tell the assessing officer, and the expected exempt grants are deducted in advance at 1.35 (circular 7.6.1, 7.6.2).
- Figures: 180 (fixed in law, changeable by the Minister with Knesset approval); 32 years; factor about 1.35 (circular).
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.9A(a) `מכפיל ההמרה להון`, s.9A(c) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). ### TXP-16 Fixing the exemption with the assessing officer (process)
- Rule: Circular 2/2013, "until a new instruction is given": every individual who reaches entitlement age and wants the qualifying-annuity exemption applies to the assessing officer (`פקיד השומה`), who sets the remaining exempt capital after the offset formula. The approval is an amount approval, issued in the year it is produced, with two figures: the exempt annuity for that year, and the exempt share of the qualifying-annuity ceiling. The share stays fixed for the person's life and changes during the phase-in years with each change in the exempt percentage.
- Conditions / exceptions: Choices: keep all of the remaining exempt capital for the monthly exemption, or set part of it aside for tax-free commutation (TXP-17). Someone who chose an exempt annuity and changes their mind within 90 days can change the choice by a reasoned notice to the assessing officer. After five months from the end of the month of the first choice, or after three payslips that include the annuity exemption, whichever is earlier, an exempt annuity can no longer be turned into a commutation reserve; a commutation reserve can still be turned into an exempt annuity (circular 5.6.2). The remaining exempt capital and the commutation reserve are fixed when the exemption is first received (circular 7.6). Someone choosing a different exempt percentage gets a new approval (circular 7.3). The brief's name for this step, "fixing rights" (`קיבוע זכויות`), and its form number were not found in the sources read (open question 1).
- Figures: 90 days; 5 months or 3 payslips.
- Source: [Circular 2/2013](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/income-tax-professional-inst-02-2013/he/Policy_IncomeTaxInst_hoz02-2013.pdf>) ss.5.6, 7.1-7.3 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.9A(e)(1) (request "בטופס שקבע המנהל" within 90 days) · primary (mirror).
- Dimensions: `tax-at-exit`, `reporting-obligation`
- Likely to change: yes. The circular itself says it applies until a new instruction; a later procedure or online service may exist (open question 1).

### TXP-17 Commutation of an annuity (`היוון קצבה`)
- Rule: A lump sum from commuting annuities is exempt up to the year's exempt share × ceiling × 180, or up to the remaining exempt capital if exempt grants were received, provided the person filed a request with the assessing officer, on the form set by the Director, within 90 days from the first time an exemption was given for a qualifying annuity. The monthly exemption is then reduced by the exempt commutation divided by 180. A commutation that is not exempt is taxed at marginal rates and may be spread back over up to six tax years (s.8(c)(3)); the circular adds that from age 70 the spread shortens by one year for every four years of age (from 94 no spreading).
- Conditions / exceptions: Commutation is allowed only within s.23 of the Provident Funds Law; the circular describes it as: the money is in a paying annuity fund (not an old fund, where commutation is not possible), done under the fund's commutation method, and the annuity left after commutation is not below the minimum annuity amount (`סכום קצבה מזערי`), or up to 25% of the annuity for no more than five years. A commutation not in line with s.23 is taxed as a non-compliant withdrawal (TXP-18). To commute, the person shows the assessing officer annuity payslips proving the minimum annuity exists. Commuting severance-component money counts as revoking the notice to keep it for an annuity (s.9A(e)(4), TXP-21). Product limits belong in `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`.
- Figures: 180; 90 days; minimum annuity 5,306 NIS/month (2026, booklet row "מרכיב התגמולים - סכום קצבה מזערי"); 6 years back.
- Source: Ordinance s.9A(e)(1), (2), (4); s.8(c)(3) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). Circular 2/2013 ss.5.1-5.3, 7.4, 7.5 · primary. Booklet 2026 p.12 · primary. [Provident Funds Law s.23, Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror).

### TXP-18 Early or non-compliant withdrawal (`משיכה שלא כדין`)
- Rule: A withdrawal from a provident fund for annuity, or a withdrawal from a provident fund before its time, is a non-compliant withdrawal under s.87 and the regulations under it. It is taxed at the **higher of a fixed 35% and the marginal rate**. The fund withholds 35% (reg. 3 of the Income Tax (Rules on Taxing Payments to an Unapproved Fund and Non-Compliant Payments) Regulations 1962, as cited by the circular).
- Conditions / exceptions: In the 35% computation only basic credit points are given (resident and travel: 2.25 for a man, 2.75 for a woman); in the marginal computation, credits, deductions and offsets are given as for personal-exertion income. The guide reports the income in field 213 (registered spouse's fund) or 313 (the other spouse's fund). Not taxed at that rate when: (a) low household income: the withdrawal up to the gap between the couple's income and the monthly minimum wage (doubled if either has a child under 18), via form 159א to the fund; (b) medical expenses (not dental) above half the annual income of the member, spouse and the relative, up to the expense; (c) the member or a relative was found to have a permanent disability of 75% or more after deposits to that account began; (b) and (c) by application on form 159 to the assessing officer; (d) on the member's death, heirs are exempt. The withdrawal is personal income of the account holder. Which amount is the taxable base (the whole withdrawal, or only benefited deposits and gains) was not stated in the sources read (open question 3). The Provident Funds Law limits when such money can be withdrawn at all (product rule, `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`).
- Figures: 35% (2026 booklet: "תשלום שלא כדין מקופת גמל לקצבה – הגבוה מבין 35% ושיעור המס"; for a provident fund for benefits, 35%); minimum wage for the low-income test 6,248 NIS/month (booklet row "'שכר מינימום' - לצורך משיכה בפטור מקופ"ג"; 2025 guide "נכון ל 12/2025").

### TXP-19 Severance grant on leaving a job: exemption per year of work
- Rule: A capital grant received because of retirement (`מענק הון שנתקבל עקב פרישה`) is exempt up to one month's last salary for each year of work, but never above a fixed amount per year of work (pro rata for part years). Severance-component money in a provident fund for annuity counts as a grant received, even if left in the fund, unless the person used continuity (TXP-20, TXP-21).
- Conditions / exceptions: The Director may exempt more in some cases; salary raised shortly before retirement unreasonably can be ignored; part-time last salary uses the last full-time salary for full-time years. The taxable part is personal-exertion income that may be spread in equal parts over the work years, up to six tax years ending in the year received, or by request over another period including later years (s.8(c)(3)). Taking it exempt reduces the later annuity exemption (TXP-15). The employer attaches grant details to its report for the month of payment; Form 106 shows "מענקים עקב פרישה/מוות ששולמו - סכום המענק הפטור ממס" (field 209) and the taxable part (field 258/272). The employee notice and choice forms used on leaving were not read (open question 2).
- Figures: 13,750 NIS per year of work (2024 to 2027; 13,310 in 2023; 3,900 in 1990 base), CPI-linked under s.120B and frozen 2025 to 2027.

### TXP-20 Severance continuity to a new employer (s.9(7a)(a)(4))
- Rule: An employee who, on leaving, had severance money in a provident fund (or received a grant and moved it into the severance component) and elected in writing to the Director to apply this rule is not treated as having received it, provided that within one year they start work with another employer who pays severance into that component, up to a ceiling. If they later leave again without continuing, the periods are joined as if all the years were worked for the last employer, and the earlier grants with their returns count as part of the last employer's grant.
- Conditions / exceptions: Ceiling (`סכום התקרה` for this rule): four times the average wage times the years worked for that employer, plus what is needed to cover that employer's obligations under the Severance Pay Law. Receiving part of the grant taxes the part received. The election can be revoked within two years; then the exemption uses the CPI-indexed salary at retirement and the ceiling at revocation, without the later work period. Any withdrawal of such money before leaving the last employer is taxable. The same rule applies on death.
- Figures: 1 year; 2 years; 4 × average wage per year of work (average-wage-linked, not frozen).
- Source: [Ordinance](<https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה>) s.9(7a)(a)(4)(a)-(d) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror).
- Dimensions: `tax-at-exit`, `switching-tax-event`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: yes (the ceiling moves with the average wage).

### TXP-21 Leaving severance in the fund for an annuity (s.9(7a)(g), (g1); booklet: "רצף קצבה")
- Rule: Severance money left in a provident fund for annuity is not a grant received if, on leaving, the employee notified the Director that they want to keep it for an annuity. An employee whose severance-component balances in all annuity funds for that employer do not exceed a set amount, or the severance ceiling (`תקרת הפיצויים`) for each year of work, whichever is higher, is **deemed** to have given that notice unless they notified otherwise.
- Conditions / exceptions: The employee can revoke by notice; then the whole amount with its returns is a retirement grant, with the exemption ceiling at the time of revocation, reduced if a grant was received on leaving. Withdrawing severance money as a commutation counts as revoking (s.9A(e)(4)). Amounts split to a former spouse under the Pension Savings Division Law are outside this rule (s.9(7a)(h)).
- Figures: 405,900 NIS (2024 to 2027; 392,270 in 2023; 360,000 in 2017 base) "לתקופת הצבירה" (booklet), CPI-linked and frozen 2025 to 2027; alternative: severance ceiling 45,600 (2026) × years of work, average-wage-linked.

### TXP-23 Survivors and death before taking the money
- Rule: A survivors' annuity (`קצבת שאירים`) arising from exempt payments is exempt. Other survivors' annuities paid from a provident fund for annuity, an insurance plan or a budgetary pension are exempt up to the qualifying-annuity ceiling (s.9(6f)). Heirs withdrawing a member's provident fund money are exempt from the non-compliant-withdrawal tax (TXP-18). Form 106 line "קיצבת שאירים - הסכום הפטור ממס".

### TXP-27 Which figures are frozen for 2025 to 2027 and which move with the average wage
- Rule: s.120B: on 1 January of each tax year the "social reliefs" (`הנחות סוציאליות`) are indexed by the CPI rise of the previous year. The list in s.120A includes ss.9(7a), 9A, 45A and 47. For tax years 2025 to 2027 they are **not** indexed and stay at their 1 January 2024 values (after rounding). On 1 January 2028 they are indexed from the unrounded 1.1.2024 values using the CPI of tax year 2027. s.3(e3) is not in that list: its amounts are defined by the average wage.
- Conditions / exceptions: Frozen (CPI-linked, 2024 values for 2025 to 2027): eligible-income caps 116,400 / 164,400; credit floor 2,268; qualifying-annuity ceiling 9,430; retirement-grant cap 13,750 and death-grant cap 27,520 per year of work; continuity amount 405,900. ### TXP-29 Leaving a job: the new form 161 (employer notice and employee choices)
- Rule: From 1 January 2024 employers must report an employee's final retirement on the new form 161, "הודעה על פרישה מעבודה". It covers every amount and right the employer made available, whether paid directly or through provident funds. Part A is the employer's notice. Part B is where the employee records their choices for the severance grants made available to them. Part C is the employer's calculation and instructions to the provident funds on how to characterise the money and what tax to withhold.
- Conditions / exceptions: Retirements before 1.1.2024 reported after that date also use the new form. The employer gives the completed form to the assessing officer that holds its withholding file, and to the employee (or survivors or representatives). Employers file online (CRM or the representatives' system), in person or by post; there is also a digital 161 reporting system. If the employer filled part C2, the employee must ask the assessing officer for instructions to the employer and funds according to their part B choice, then pass those instructions on. Attach the last three payslips, plus a current balance confirmation from the funds if more than three months have passed since retirement. The employee can reach the assessing officer through the online enquiries system, the personal area, or a booked visit. The choices offered in part B were not read (they are in the form PDF).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Tax Authority, הודעה על פרישה מעבודה (טופס 161 חדש)](https://www.gov.il/he/service/notice-of-retirement) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: `tax-at-exit`, `transfer-switch-process`

### TXP-30 Form 161 part B: the employee's four choices for each severance grant
- Rule: For each grant the employer reported (part A.9), the employee splits the amount between: (א) "רצף קצבה", leaving the severance money in the provident fund for annuity so it becomes annuity; (ב) "רצף פיצויים", deferring the tax event to retirement from the next employer, up to the severance-continuity ceiling; (ג) a tax-exempt grant under s.9(7א), up to the exempt-grant ceiling; (ד) a taxable grant (anything above the exempt ceiling, or what the employee takes without using the exemption). Part B.2 lets the employee ask to spread the taxable grant under s.8(ג)(3) (request to the assessing officer on form 116ג) and to direct severance from the employer or a central severance fund to continuity. Part B.3 holds declarations (no other exempt grants for this period, no continuity request from other employers, not yet at eligibility age).
- Conditions / exceptions: Default: the employee may leave part B blank and is treated as asking for annuity continuity, if the employer marked in A.12 that the total does not exceed the default-continuity ceiling and that no grants come from payers other than an annuity fund. Annuity continuity can be reversed at any time on form 161ג, keeping any unused s.9(7א) exemption; reversal covers all amounts designated from that employer (except old pension funds, payer code 5). Severance continuity requires a new employer depositing to a severance component within one year, otherwise the employee reports to the assessing officer within 13 months; it can be reversed within two years; after two years outside a new retirement it loses the exempt grant, and choosing it means no exempt grant now. Severance deposits that were taxed when deposited and reported as "תשלומים פטורים" (part A.10) can be withdrawn from retirement without tax, but their gains are taxed at the s.125ג(ג) rate (the form says 15% for 2023), or they can be taken later as a tax-exempt annuity. The form warns that withdrawing grants reduces the future pension and may reduce the monthly annuity exemption. Ceilings are in footnotes 26 to 28 of the form, not recorded here.
- Figures: none recorded (ceilings in the form's footnotes; see TXP-19 to TXP-21).
- Source: [Tax Authority, form 161 (accessible .docx), part B](<https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/notice-of-retirement/he/161-2023.%20docx.docx>), via the [service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/notice-of-retirement) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: `tax-at-exit`, `annuity-vs-lump-sum`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: no.
