## Summary

- A kupat gemel lehashkaa is a provident fund for paying a lump sum to a self-paying member or the member's beneficiaries. It was created by Amendment 15 to the Provident Funds Law in June 2016 (KGL-01). The first tracks opened on 2016-11-10 (KGL-22).
- One annual deposit ceiling applies per person across all of that person's gemel lehashkaa accounts at every manager. It is fixed in the law at NIS 70,000 and CPI-linked every 1 January. For 2026 the CBS linkage calculator gives NIS 83,641.09 (KGL-03, KGL-24); no regulator notice was found, and the law does not require one.
- The home-sale exception in the discovery report is not a current rule. The bylaws describe it as a window that closed on 31 Dec 2017, capped at NIS 2,500,000 (not 2,000,000), and the current law text marks section 22(א1) as repealed (KGL-05).
- Money can be withdrawn at any time, in full or in part. Payment is due within 4 business days, under rule 41יא(א) of the 1964 provident-fund rules (KGL-08). At early retirement age (60) the balance can move to an annuity provident fund, into a track for annuity recipients, for an annuity only (KGL-09). Transfers out go to another gemel lehashkaa. No route into a gemel lehashkaa from another product type was found (KGL-10).
- Every fund's bylaws must set a default track, and a member who does not choose joins it (KGL-11). In every bylaws document read, the manager chose its "general" (כללי) track (KGL-29). The age-based default model does not apply to this product.
- Management fees may be charged on the balance (at most 1.05% a year) and on deposits (at most 4%), under the 2012 fee regulations. The text was read through a mirror (KGL-14). An offered discount must last at least five years under CMA circular 2017-9-15 (KGL-16); some bylaws still say two (KGL-30).
- 12 managing companies offer it to the general public and 6 sector funds offer it to defined groups (CMA Gemel-Net, period 202609).

## Rules

### KGL-01 What it is and its legal basis
- Rule: A "קופת גמל להשקעה" is defined in section 1 of the Provident Funds Law as "קופת גמל המיועדת לתשלום סכום הון לעמית עצמאי או למוטביו". The definition was added by Amendment 15, passed on 30 May 2016 and published on 7 June 2016. The same amendment added "להשקעה" to the fund types in section 13(א), set the deposit ceiling in section 22(א) (KGL-02), allowed pledging in section 25(א)(1) (KGL-18), and amended section 27(ג). It also amended the Income Tax Ordinance (sections 1, 3(ה4)(3), 3(ה7)(2), 9(17א)(ד) and 9א(א)); those are for the tax file. Amendment 15 also added "קופת גמל להשקעה" as a "סוג מוצר פנסיוני" in the advice, marketing and clearing law, and as a form of pension saving in the law on dividing pension savings between separated spouses.
- Conditions / exceptions: The definition says "עמית עצמאי", meaning a member paying for themselves. The wording on Wikisource is "עמית יחיד המשלם כספים לקופת גמל בעד עצמו" (read through the mirror only). For employer deposits, see open question 5.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf) pp.2-3 (gazette pp.885-886, Sefer HaChukim 2554, 7.6.2016), ss.1-9 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. Context: the explanatory notes of [הצעת חוק לתיקון פקודת מס הכנסה (חשבון השקעה), התשפ"ג-2023 (פ/3539/25)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lst_2708087.pdf) p.3 describe Amendment 15 as the law "שאפשר לחברות מנהלות להקים מוצר חיסכון שנקרא" a gemel lehashkaa · checked 2026-10-25 · primary (Knesset document, descriptive).

### KGL-02 Annual deposit ceiling: one ceiling per person across all managers, CPI-linked
- Rule: The total a member may deposit "בכלל חשבונותיו בכל קופות הגמל להשקעה" may not exceed NIS 70,000 "בכל שנת כספים". The amount is updated every 1 January by the rise in the CPI known on that date. For the first update, the comparison is with the CPI known on 1 July 2016.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ceiling is per member (per ID number, as Altshuler words it), not per account and not per manager. A child's account counts against the child's own ceiling (KGL-21). For the expired home-sale window, see KGL-05.
- Figures: NIS 70,000 per tax year (שנת כספים), base CPI known on 1 July 2016. Indexed every 1 January. The base figure is fixed in law.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf) p.2 (gazette p.885), s.3 (adding to section 22(א)) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. The bylaws repeat the rule: [Altshuler Shaham "חיסכון פלוס" bylaws, March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clause 8.1 · checked 2026-10-25 · institution.
- Dimensions: `deposit-ceiling`, `real-vs-nominal`
- Likely to change: yes. A private bill to raise the ceiling to NIS 300,000 a year passed a preliminary reading (17 for, none against), as reported on 20 Mar 2024 by [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/consumerism/news/article/1005636) (secondary). Its current status is open question 12. The current law text still shows the 70,000 base.

### KGL-05 Home-sale exception: a window that closed on 31 Dec 2017, not a current rule
- Rule: Notwithstanding the annual ceiling, a member could deposit "עד ליום י"ג בטבת התשע"ח (31 בדצמבר 2017)" an amount up to NIS 2,500,000 ("שני מליון וחמש מאות אלף") or the proceeds from selling dwellings, whichever was lower. ### KGL-06 Who may open one
- Rule: A kupat gemel lehashkaa pays a lump sum "לעמית עצמאי או למוטביו" (law s.1 as amended by Amendment 15, KGL-01). The 1964 rules define that member as "עמית יחיד, לרבות עובד בשל תשלום שהוא משלם לקופת גמל בלא תשלום מקביל של מעביד" (rule 1). The ceiling is per member across all of that member's accounts (s.22(א), KGL-02).
- Conditions / exceptions: Neither the law nor the rules set an age or a residence condition for opening one. That is a reading of their silence, not a stated rule (open question 6). No limit on the number of accounts per person was found; the ceiling is worded across all of them. What the companies say about opening in another person's name and about who may join is KGL-26; who manages and receives a minor's money is unsettled, KGL-27. Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass).
- Figures: none.

### KGL-08 Withdrawal at any time, in full or in part; paid within 4 business days
- Rule: A member who asks for all or part of the money "יקבל את הכספים כאמור בתוך 4 ימי עסקים מהיום שהגיעה בקשתו לקופה". If that date falls in one of the first three business days of a month, the fund may defer payment to the fourth business day of that month. A member who asks for consecutive monthly payments is paid on a fixed day set in the bylaws, or on the first business day of each month (Income Tax Rules (Approval and Management of Provident Funds), 1964, rule 41יא(א)). If payment is late, the fund pays late-payment interest "בשיעור הריבית בשל איחור בהעברת כספים מהמערכת הבנקאית שקובע החשב הכללי", deducted from its management fees (rule 41יז1). The CMA standard bylaw for kupat gemel lehashkaa repeats the four business days and the deferral (ss.12.1-12.2). No exit fee: PF-01 (law s.32(א)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Applicability: rule 41יא covers every member "הזכאי לכספים על פי הוראות תקנות אלה". The rules define "קופת גמל" as "כמשמעותה בסעיף 47(א)(2) לפקודה", and the Ordinance's s.1 (p.16) defines "קופת גמל" and "קופת גמל להשקעה" "כהגדרתן בחוק הפיקוח על קופות גמל". No text names kupat gemel lehashkaa in rule 41יא itself; the application is a strong reading confirmed by the CMA standard bylaw. Re-check s.47(א)(2) before encoding more on this chain. Tax is withheld at payment: `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. The withdrawal report within 10 business days and the higher-of-two clause are KGL-28 (unsettled). Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass); Meitav's product page was dropped as a source.
- Figures: 4 business days from the day the request reaches the fund; deferral to the 4th business day when the date falls in the month's first 3 business days (fixed in regulation).
- Source: [תקנות מס הכנסה (כללים לאישור ולניהול קופות גמל), התשכ"ד-1964](https://www.btl.gov.il/Laws1/02_0103_100022.pdf) p.16, reg. 41יא(א) "יקבל את הכספים כאמור בתוך 4 ימי עסקים מהיום שהגיעה בקשתו לקופה" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [תקנות מס הכנסה (כללים לאישור ולניהול קופות גמל), התשכ"ד-1964](https://www.btl.gov.il/Laws1/02_0103_100022.pdf) p.17, reg. 41יז1 "תשלם לו הקופה או תזכה את חשבונו, לפי הענין, בריבית פיגורים" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [CMA standard bylaw for kupat gemel lehashkaa (2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-2829/he/regulation_hm_takanongemel.pdf) ss.12.1-12.2 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. No exit fee: PF-01.

### KGL-10 Transfers between funds

### KGL-12 What kinds of tracks a gemel lehashkaa may offer (regulator's categories)
- Rule: Under circular 2020-9-18, annex section 7, a managing company may run the following tracks in study funds, gemel lehashkaa and non-insurance-fund policies:
  - (א) "שני מסלולים כלליים ובלבד שאחד ממסלולי ההשקעה יהיה מסלול פאסיבי-כללי" (two general tracks, one of which is passive-general), as defined in the CMA track list;
  - (א1) further general tracks, closed to new members;
  - (ב) specialised tracks by asset class, by investment field, by investment instrument, or combined;
  - (ג) tracks for a defined target population;
  - (ד) other specialised tracks with the Commissioner's approval;
  - (ה) "לא יותר מעשרה מסלולי השקעה מתמחים" (no more than ten specialised tracks).

  Further rules in the same circular:
  - Section 8 bans track names that promise quality or risk, giving as examples "בטוחה, רווחית, סולידית, יציבה, איתנה, אגרסיבית".
  - Section 9 requires a track's name to match its investment policy as set out in the CMA track list (רשימת מסלולי ההשקעה, section 10).
  - Section 12(ג) requires at least 45 days' notice, and no more than three months, before a track's policy changes.
  - Section 14 allows a track to be closed only if it is empty, or through a merger.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ten-track limit in 7(ה) conflicts with the 202609 data: Meitav reports 16 gemel lehashkaa tracks, 15 of them not "general". ### KGL-14 Fee bases and the regulated maximum (domain open question 8, for this product)
- Rule: A managing company may charge management fees from two bases:
  - the accumulated balance (יתרה צבורה), at most 1.05% a year ("מן היתרה הצבורה בחשבונו של כל עמית בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1.05% לשנה");
  - the payments transferred to it for each member, at most 4% ("מתוך התשלומים המועברים אליה בשל כל עמית – בשיעור שלא יעלה על 4%").

  Regulation 2 applies to every provident fund except those listed in regulation 2(ב). Gemel lehashkaa is not on that list. The listed exclusions include study funds, "קופת גמל בניהול אישי" and "קופת גמל ענפית"; regulation 4 caps those at 2% a year of the balance, and regulation 5 has sector funds charge actual expenses within the regulation 4 cap. The bylaws repeat both bases and point to "שיעור דמי הניהול המרבי הקבוע בהסדר התחיקתי".
- Conditions / exceptions: Each track counts as a separate fund for fee purposes (regulation 6). The fee regulations were read only through the Wikisource mirror (open question 3). Kol Zchut, which cites "סעיף 2(א)", returned 403. The self-directed variant falls under regulation 4; see `ira-self-directed.md`. Sector funds charge "לפי הוצאות שהוצאו בפועל" up to the regulatory maximum (Yachad Rofim clause 35).
- Figures: balance up to 1.05% a year; deposits up to 4% of each payment. Fixed in the regulations of 2012 (effective 1 January 2013; amended 2014 and 2022). Not indexed.

### KGL-16 Fee discounts: minimum duration and when they can end early
- Rule: A managing company that offers a member fees below the legal maximum must keep the offer valid for "תקופה של חמש שנים או יותר" from the day the lower fee starts (CMA circular 2017-9-15, s.3(א)). It may raise the fee before the discount period ends only in the cases of s.3(ב): six months after deposits stop, if not because of the employer; a full or partial withdrawal, or a full or partial transfer to another fund or to another track in the same fund; or a mechanism in the agreement that grades the fee by balance or by another variable the Commissioner approved, other than membership length. Each early raise needs a notice in a separate document; the raise takes effect two months after the notice, or at a later date it states, and the notice may not be sent more than four months ahead (s.4(א)). The circular applies to every institutional body except sector funds and guaranteed-return funds (s.5(א)), so it covers kupat gemel lehashkaa managers. The same circular is PF-13, PF-14 and PF-16.
- Conditions / exceptions: Some kupat gemel lehashkaa bylaws still say two years, the period of the replaced circular 2012-9-22, and add an exception the circular does not have; the companies' wording is KGL-30. The circular governs. Corrected 2026-10-25 (scope pass): the record said the discount must last at least two years, from the bylaws. Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass): the companies' part is KGL-30.
- Figures: discount valid at least 5 years; a raise takes effect 2 months after notice, notice not more than 4 months ahead (circular).
- Source: [חוזר גופים מוסדיים 2017-9-15 – דמי ניהול במכשירי החיסכון הפנסיוני – תיקון](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-989/he/regulation_h_2017-9-15.pdf) p.1, s.3(א) "תקפה במהלך תקופה של חמש שנים או יותר"; p.2, s.3(ב); p.3, s.4(א), 5(א) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. ### KGL-17 Beneficiaries and death
- Rule: A member may name beneficiaries for the balance on death, through an original signed form, through a licensee, or digitally under the Commissioner's rules. Without shares stated, beneficiaries split equally. A later will that deals with the fund money counts as the beneficiary instruction if it is dated after the last instruction and was delivered before payout. Without beneficiaries, the money goes to heirs under a will-probate or inheritance order. The manager may pay a small balance without an order when all of the following hold:
  - the balance is at most NIS 5,000 when the request is made, indexed under the regulatory arrangement;
  - three years have passed since the death;
  - the claimants sign an indemnity;
  - the claimants are the spouse, parent or child;
  - no order appears in the inheritance registry.
- Conditions / exceptions: none beyond the above.
- Figures: NIS 5,000 (indexed, base date not stated); 3 years.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 22–26 (same in Infinity) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.
- Dimensions: `eligibility`
- Proposed dimension: `beneficiaries-on-death`.
- Likely to change: the CMA 2026 plan (p. 20) updates the circular on locating members and beneficiaries.

### KGL-18 Pledging rights, loans and attachment
- Rule: Amendment 15 added gemel lehashkaa to section 25(א)(1), which allows a member's rights to be pledged. The bylaws say rights "ניתנות לשעבוד" and are otherwise not transferable to another person, except through enforcement of a pledge (treated as a withdrawal under section 23) or under the 2014 law on dividing pension savings between separated spouses. A transfer is blocked while an attachment (עיקול) or pledge covers the whole balance. Some funds lend to members. Infinity's bylaws allow loans:
  - total loans up to 10% of the fund's assets;
  - each loan up to 80% of the member's balance;
  - a term of no more than 7 years;
  - repayment starting no later than one year after the loan.

  An outstanding loan is deducted on withdrawal. The Yachad Rofim template note says the clause is included only where the fund offers loans.
- Conditions / exceptions: Whether, and how far, creditors can attach a member's gemel lehashkaa balance was not researched; see `regulators-and-protection.md`.
- Figures: Infinity loans as listed above.
- Source: [Sefer HaChukim 2554, p. 885](https://fs.knesset.gov.il//20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf), Amendment 15 section 4 · primary; [Altshuler bylaws](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 17.1, 28; [Infinity bylaws](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf), clause 38; [Yachad Rofim bylaws](http://www.krn-rofim.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/%d7%aa%d7%a7%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%9f_%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a4%d7%aa_%d7%92%d7%9e%d7%9c_%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%a9%d7%a7%d7%a2%d7%94__25.7.2024-1.pdf), clause 36 footnote 2 · institution · checked 2026-10-22.

## Left out and why

- Tax at deposit, during and at exit, the annuity exemption, and the claims on Meitav's page about tax-free annuities after 60 and 25% tax on lump sums: these belong in `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. The Amendment 15 Income Tax Ordinance hooks are listed in KGL-01 for that file.
- The self-directed (IRA) form of gemel lehashkaa: belongs in `ira-self-directed.md`. Fee regulation 4 (2% a year of the balance) applies to "קופת גמל בניהול אישי".
- Transfer timelines, statement rules and fee-regulation structure shared by all provident products: these belong in `provident-family-common.md`. They are quoted here only where a saver needs them for this product.
- The per-manager track list, per-track fees and returns, and the Meitav app walk-through: these belong in `tracks-catalogue-and-decoder.md`. Only the categories and counts are given here.
- Fee ranges observed in Gemel-Net: left out because the dataset's units are undefined (open question 14), and the full 160-row listing was read through a summarising fetch that may have dropped rows. Counts were confirmed with filtered queries instead.
- Historical rules before 2016 (the old capital "קופת גמל לתגמולים", closed to new deposits in 2008, per the Knesset bill notes): these do not change the outcome for a saver opening a fund today.
- Kol Zchut, Hamichlol, igemel-net and other secondary explainers: not used, because the regulation text, the bylaws or the regulator's own data were reachable for the same points.
