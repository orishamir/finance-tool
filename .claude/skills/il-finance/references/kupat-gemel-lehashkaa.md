## Summary

- A kupat gemel lehashkaa is a provident fund for paying a lump sum to a self-paying member or the member's beneficiaries. It was created by Amendment 15 to the Provident Funds Law in June 2016 (KGL-01). The first tracks opened on 2016-11-10 (KGL-22).
- One annual deposit ceiling applies per person across all of that person's gemel lehashkaa accounts at every manager. It is fixed in the law at NIS 70,000 and CPI-linked every 1 January. For 2026 the CBS linkage calculator gives NIS 83,641.09 (KGL-03, KGL-24); no regulator notice was found, and the law does not require one.
- The home-sale exception in the discovery report is not a current rule. The bylaws describe it as a window that closed on 31 Dec 2017, capped at NIS 2,500,000 (not 2,000,000), and the current law text marks section 22(א1) as repealed (KGL-05).
- Money can be withdrawn at any time, in full or in part. Payment is due within 4 business days, under rule 41יא(א) of the 1964 provident-fund rules (KGL-08). At early retirement age (60) the balance can move to an annuity provident fund, into a track for annuity recipients, for an annuity only (KGL-09). Transfers out go to another gemel lehashkaa. No route into a gemel lehashkaa from another product type was found (KGL-10).
- Every fund's bylaws must set a default track, and a member who does not choose joins it (KGL-11). In every bylaws document read, the manager chose its "general" (כללי) track (KGL-29). The age-based default model does not apply to this product.
- Management fees may be charged on the balance (at most 1.05% a year) and on deposits (at most 4%), under the 2012 fee regulations (KGL-14). An offered discount must last at least five years under CMA circular 2017-9-15 (KGL-16); some bylaws still say two (KGL-30).
- 12 managing companies offer it to the general public and 6 sector funds offer it to defined groups (CMA Gemel-Net, period 202609).

## Rules

### KGL-01 What it is and its legal basis
- Rule: A "קופת גמל להשקעה" is defined in section 1 of the Provident Funds Law as "קופת גמל המיועדת לתשלום סכום הון לעמית עצמאי או למוטביו". The definition was added by Amendment 15, passed on 30 May 2016 and published on 7 June 2016. The same amendment added "להשקעה" to the fund types in section 13(א), set the deposit ceiling in section 22(א) (KGL-02), allowed pledging in section 25(א)(1) (KGL-18), and amended section 27(ג). It also amended the Income Tax Ordinance (sections 1, 3(ה4)(3), 3(ה7)(2), 9(17א)(ד) and 9א(א)); those are for the tax file. Amendment 15 also added "קופת גמל להשקעה" as a "סוג מוצר פנסיוני" in the advice, marketing and clearing law, and as a form of pension saving in the law on dividing pension savings between separated spouses.
- Conditions / exceptions: The definition says "עמית עצמאי", meaning a member paying for themselves. The wording in the 2005 gazette (file p.2, [ספר החוקים](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/16/law/16_lsr_299984.pdf#page=2), checked 2026-10-31) is "עמית יחיד המשלם כספים לקופת גמל בעד עצמו". For employer deposits, see open question 5.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf) pp.2-3 (gazette pp.885-886, Sefer HaChukim 2554, 7.6.2016), ss.1-9 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. Context: the explanatory notes of [הצעת חוק לתיקון פקודת מס הכנסה (חשבון השקעה), התשפ"ג-2023 (פ/3539/25)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lst_2708087.pdf) p.3 describe Amendment 15 as the law "שאפשר לחברות מנהלות להקים מוצר חיסכון שנקרא" a gemel lehashkaa · checked 2026-10-25 · primary (Knesset document, descriptive).
- Scope: general
- Dimensions: `regulator`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: no.

### KGL-02 Annual deposit ceiling: one ceiling per person across all managers, CPI-linked
- Rule: The total a member may deposit "בכלל חשבונותיו בכל קופות הגמל להשקעה" may not exceed NIS 70,000 "בכל שנת כספים". The amount is updated every 1 January by the rise in the CPI known on that date. For the first update, the comparison is with the CPI known on 1 July 2016.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ceiling is per member, not per account and not per manager. A child's account counts against the child's own ceiling (KGL-21). For the expired home-sale window, see KGL-05.
- Figures: NIS 70,000 per tax year (שנת כספים), base CPI known on 1 July 2016. Indexed every 1 January. The base figure is fixed in law.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf) p.2 (gazette p.885), s.3 (adding to section 22(א)) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary. ### KGL-06 Who may open one
- Rule: A kupat gemel lehashkaa pays a lump sum "לעמית עצמאי או למוטביו" (law s.1 as amended by Amendment 15, KGL-01). The 1964 rules define that member as "עמית יחיד, לרבות עובד בשל תשלום שהוא משלם לקופת גמל בלא תשלום מקביל של מעביד" (rule 1). The ceiling is per member across all of that member's accounts (s.22(א), KGL-02). The CMA standard bylaw lets the company accept or refuse a joining request: "החברה המנהלת רשאית לאשר הצטרפותו של עמית או לסרב לקבלו כעמית וזאת על פי שיקול דעתה הבלעדי ובכפוף לכל דין" (s.7.1), and bars it from closing a member's account against the member's will except in the cases of its s.33 (s.7.2).
- Conditions / exceptions: Neither the law nor the rules set an age or a residence condition for opening one. That is a reading of their silence, not a stated rule (open question 6). No limit on the number of accounts per person was found; the ceiling is worded across all of them. What the companies say about opening in another person's name and about who may join is KGL-26; a minor's withdrawal is paid to the guardian parent, KGL-27. Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass). 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the joining clauses (standard bylaw ss.7.1-7.2) moved here from KGL-26, where they were given as Altshuler Shaham's cl. 7.1-7.2, which copy them.
- Figures: none.

### KGL-07 How money goes in, and minimums
- Rule: A managing company may accept deposits only by credit card, bank transfer, standing order (direct-debit authorisation), or cheque. A member may deposit into, or move money to, any track listed in the bylaws annex.

### KGL-12 What kinds of tracks a gemel lehashkaa may offer (regulator's categories)
- Rule: The CMA's circular on investment tracks in provident funds ("מסלולי השקעה בקופות גמל", the edition of circular 2016-9-13 as amended by circular 2022-9-28 of 28.9.2022 and circular 2022-9-30 of 20.12.2022, whose annex carries the amended text; in force from 1 January 2024, with s.6(ב), the sustainability and environmental tracks and s.17 from 1 January 2023, circular 2022-9-30 s.4) lets an institutional body run, in study funds, kupat gemel lehashkaa and policies that are not insurance funds, "מסלול השקעה כללי ומסלולי השקעה כמפורט בסעיף 6", without the duty of s.6(ב) to run a combined-tradable and a flexible index-tracker track (s.7(א)). The specialised tracks of s.6(א) follow the five clusters of the CMA's list of investment tracks:
  - cluster 1, actively managed tracks;
  - cluster 2, actively managed tracks with variable fees, allowed only if the body also runs the same specialisation in cluster 1 (s.6(ג));
  - cluster 3, tracks specialising in tradable assets;
  - cluster 4, index-tracking tracks;
  - cluster 5, faith and sustainability tracks, one of each kind only (s.6(ד)); the environmental track may be run only in a study fund or a kupat gemel lehashkaa (s.6(א)(5)).

  The board must approve, with reasons, that each specialised track's investment policy differs markedly from every other specialised track in the same fund (s.6(ד)), except cluster 2 against cluster 1 (s.6(ה)). The bylaws of a kupat gemel lehashkaa must set a default track (s.7(ב), the second subsection so lettered). A member who picks a money-market track chooses when joining it a period of six, twelve or twenty-four months and the track the money moves to at its end, and gets a notice one to two months before the end (s.6א). Further rules in the same text:
  - s.8 bans misleading track names, or names promising a quality or quantity or implying how the money is managed, with the examples "בטוחה, רווחית, סולידית, יציבה, איתנה, אגרסיבית";
  - s.9 requires a track's name to match its investment policy as set out in the CMA's list of investment tracks, which the CMA publishes on its site; bodies adopt a change in the list within three months unless the Commissioner says otherwise (s.10);

### KGL-14 Fee bases and the regulated maximum (domain open question 8, for this product)
- Rule: A managing company may charge management fees from two bases:
  - the accumulated balance (יתרה צבורה), at most 1.05% a year ("מן היתרה הצבורה בחשבונו של כל עמית בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1.05% לשנה");
  - the payments transferred to it for each member, at most 4% ("מתוך התשלומים המועברים אליה בשל כל עמית – בשיעור שלא יעלה על 4%").

  Regulation 2 applies to every provident fund except those listed in regulation 2(ב). Gemel lehashkaa is not on that list. The listed exclusions include study funds, "קופת גמל בניהול אישי" and "קופת גמל ענפית"; regulation 4 caps those at 2% a year of the balance, and regulation 5 has sector funds charge actual expenses within the regulation 4 cap. The CMA standard bylaw repeats both bases and points to "שיעור דמי הניהול המרבי הקבוע בהסדר התחיקתי" (s.35.1).

The self-directed variant falls under regulation 4; see `ira-self-directed.md`. A sector fund charges "לפי הוצאות שהוציאה בפועל, בכפוף לשיעור המרבי הקבוע בתקנה 4" (regulation 5). ### KGL-16 Fee discounts: minimum duration and when they can end early
- Rule: A managing company that offers a member fees below the legal maximum must keep the offer valid for "תקופה של חמש שנים או יותר" from the day the lower fee starts (CMA circular 2017-9-15, s.3(א)). It may raise the fee before the discount period ends only in the cases of s.3(ב): six months after deposits stop, if not because of the employer; a full or partial withdrawal, or a full or partial transfer to another fund or to another track in the same fund; or a mechanism in the agreement that grades the fee by balance or by another variable the Commissioner approved, other than membership length. ### KGL-17 Beneficiaries and death
- Rule: A member may name beneficiaries for the balance on death, through an original signed form, through a licensee, or digitally under the Commissioner's rules. Without shares stated, beneficiaries split equally. A later will that deals with the fund money counts as the beneficiary instruction if it is dated after the last instruction and was delivered before payout. Without beneficiaries, the money goes to heirs under a will-probate or inheritance order. The manager may pay a small balance without an order when all of the following hold:
  - the balance is at most NIS 5,000 when the request is made, indexed under the regulatory arrangement;
  - three years have passed since the death;
  - the claimants sign an indemnity;
  - the claimants are the spouse, parent or child;
  - no order appears in the inheritance registry.
- Conditions / exceptions: none beyond the above.
- Figures: NIS 5,000 (indexed, base date not stated); 3 years.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 22–26 (same in Infinity) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.

### KGL-18 Pledging rights, loans and attachment
- Rule: Amendment 15 added kupat gemel lehashkaa to section 25(א)(1) of the Provident Funds Law, which allows a member's rights to be pledged. The CMA's standard bylaw for kupat gemel lehashkaa says the rights "ניתנות לשעבוד" and are otherwise not transferable to another person, except to a creditor enforcing a pledge (treated as a withdrawal by the member under section 23 of the law) or under the 2014 law on dividing pension savings between separated spouses (s.28). Money may not be moved to another provident fund while an attachment or a lawful pledge covers the whole balance, or while a loan balance is unpaid (s.17.1.1, 17.1.2); when only part is attached or pledged, that part stays (s.18.2). The fund may lend to members at its discretion, subject to the investment rules regulations and circular 2016-9-5, when all of these hold (s.38.1):
  - total loans up to 10% of the fund's assets at their estimated value;
  - each loan up to 80% of the member's balance when it is given;
  - a term of no more than 7 years;
  - the member undertakes in writing to repay;
  - the member may repay early on terms like those for similar loans in the market;
  - repayment starts no later than the end of the first year after the loan.

  When a member asks to withdraw all or part of the balance so that what remains would be lower than the loans, the balance is taken net of the loans and the sum deducted repays them; for the tax liability, the balance is counted with the loans and any other sum owed for them added back (s.38.2). "הלוואות" there means the member's outstanding debt to the company for the loans given.
- Conditions / exceptions: Whether, and how far, creditors can attach a member's gemel lehashkaa balance was not researched; see `regulators-and-protection.md`. Whether a given fund lends at all is its own decision ("רשאית על פי שיקול דעתה"). Altshuler Shaham's bylaws (clauses 17.1, 28) and Infinity's (clause 38) carry the standard text; they were the sources until 2026-11-12, when the rule was re-sourced to the standard bylaw and became general, and later that day, after review, they left the Source line as company copies of a general rule. The same review found the card said loans are always deducted on a withdrawal; s.38.2 deducts them only when the remaining balance would be lower than the loans.
- Figures: 10% of assets; 80% of the balance; 7 years; 1 year to the first repayment (standard bylaw s.38.1).
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf#page=2) p.2, s.4 (amends s.25(א)(1)) · checked 2026-11-12 · primary; [תקנון תקני לקופת גמל להשקעה (רשות שוק ההון, נשלח לחברות המנהלות במכתב 2016-19364 מ־29.9.2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-2829/he/regulation_hm_takanongemel.pdf#page=9) p.9, s.28 "ניתנות לשעבוד" · checked 2026-11-12 · primary; [תקנון תקני לקופת גמל להשקעה (רשות שוק ההון, נשלח לחברות המנהלות במכתב 2016-19364 מ־29.9.2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-2829/he/regulation_hm_takanongemel.pdf#page=6) p.6, ss.17.1, 18.2 "מוטל עיקול או שעבוד שנעשה כדין" · checked 2026-11-12 · primary; [תקנון תקני לקופת גמל להשקעה (רשות שוק ההון, נשלח לחברות המנהלות במכתב 2016-19364 מ־29.9.2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-2829/he/regulation_hm_takanongemel.pdf#page=11) p.11, s.38.1 "קופת הגמל רשאית על פי שיקול"; p.11, s.38.2 "שהיתרה לאחר המשיכה תהיה נמוכה מההלוואות" · checked 2026-11-12 · primary

### KGL-21 Children: a gemel lehashkaa in a child's name is the same wrapper, with the child's own ceiling
- Rule: The deposit ceiling of a kupat gemel lehashkaa is per member, across "בכלל חשבונותיו בכל קופות הגמל להשקעה" (law s.22(א), copied in the CMA standard bylaw s.8.1; KGL-02). So an account whose member is a child counts against the child's own ceiling, not a parent's. The government child-savings programme (חיסכון לכל ילד, `child-savings.md`) is a different product: the CMA's Gemel-Net dictionary lists "קופת גמל להשקעה חיסכון לכל ילד" as its own fund type, beside "קופת גמל להשקעה" (row 5).
- Conditions / exceptions: That a child's account counts against the child's own ceiling is a reading of "per member", not a sentence of the law. Neither the law nor the rules set an age condition for joining (KGL-06, open question 6). What the companies say about opening an account in another person's name is KGL-26; a minor's withdrawal is paid to the guardian parent, KGL-27. One company's children's product is KGL-31. For tax on a child's account, see `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. Split on 2026-11-12: the record joined Meitav's own product page (now KGL-31) with these general points, and its "irrevocable gift" sentence pointed to KGL-06, which does not say it (the companies' statements are KGL-26).
- Figures: per-person ceiling as in KGL-03.
- Source: [תקנון תקני לקופת גמל להשקעה (רשות שוק ההון, נשלח לחברות המנהלות במכתב 2016-19364 מ־29.9.2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-2829/he/regulation_hm_takanongemel.pdf#page=5), p.5, s.8.1 "בכלל חשבונותיו בכל קופות הגמל להשקעה" · checked 2026-11-12 · primary; [גמל-נט: מילון השדות](https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=76ad9eed-d076-46d6-96e2-a4eb8e045444&limit=100), row 5 (`FUND_CLASSIFICATION`) "קופת גמל להשקעה חיסכון לכל ילד" · checked 2026-11-12 · primary

## Open questions

4. **Deposits over the ceiling across managers.** How is a breach that spans two managers detected, and what happens to the excess: refund, refund with returns, or tax? The bylaws only say each manager checks at deposit time. **[2026-10-25 scope pass]** Partly answered: the check at deposit is the CMA standard bylaw s.8.2, KGL-04; detection and refund across managers are still open.
   - Where the answer probably is: the CMA payments circular ("חוזר אופן הפקדת תשלומים", listed in the 2026 plan p. 18), the pension clearing house (מסלקה פנסיונית) rules, or the 2014 payments regulations. Those regulations' regulation 12 covers only employer over-deposits.
5. **Employer deposits.** The law's definition points to a self-paying member ("עמית עצמאי"). No source found says whether an employer may deposit into an employee's gemel lehashkaa, or how such a deposit would count.
   - Where the answer probably is: the payments circular, or the 2014 payments regulations in their official text.
6. **Residency and age.** Can a non-resident open or keep an account? From what age can a minor act without a guardian? **[2026-10-25 scope pass]** Partly answered: the law and the 1964 rules set no age or residence condition (a reading of their silence), KGL-06; who manages and receives a minor's money stays unsettled, KGL-27.
   - Found so far: the 2026 plan (p. 21) implies that Israelis living abroad hold provident accounts.
   - Where the answer probably is: the joining circular ("חוזר הצטרפות לפנסיה וגמל"), which is being revised (2026 plan p. 19).
7. **Minimum deposits** per manager. Only Altshuler was found (NIS 100 a month by standing order). Other managers' joining pages or forms have not been collected.
9. **Transfers into a gemel lehashkaa from other products** (study fund, tagmulim, policies), and from a gemel lehashkaa to products other than an annuity fund.
   - The mirror's regulation 3(א) lists only gemel lehashkaa to gemel lehashkaa. The תשפ"ג amendment (ק"ת 2099) was not checked in its official text.
   - Where the answer probably is: the official transfer regulations and the CMA transfer circular.
12. **Status of the bill to raise the ceiling to NIS 300,000** (preliminary reading reported 20 Mar 2024; secondary source). Where the answer probably is: the Knesset bills database.

## Left out and why

- Tax at deposit, during and at exit, the annuity exemption, and the claims on Meitav's page about tax-free annuities after 60 and 25% tax on lump sums: these belong in `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. The Amendment 15 Income Tax Ordinance hooks are listed in KGL-01 for that file.
- The self-directed (IRA) form of gemel lehashkaa: belongs in `ira-self-directed.md`. Fee regulation 4 (2% a year of the balance) applies to "קופת גמל בניהול אישי".
- Transfer timelines, statement rules and fee-regulation structure shared by all provident products: these belong in `provident-family-common.md`. They are quoted here only where a saver needs them for this product.
- The per-manager track list, per-track fees and returns, and the Meitav app walk-through: these belong in `tracks-catalogue-and-decoder.md`. Only the categories and counts are given here.
- Fee ranges observed in Gemel-Net: left out because the full 160-row listing was read through a summarising fetch that may have dropped rows (the fields' units, percent, are CAT-35 and CAT-36). Counts were confirmed with filtered queries instead.
- Historical rules before 2016 (the old capital "קופת גמל לתגמולים", closed to new deposits in 2008, per the Knesset bill notes): these do not change the outcome for a saver opening a fund today.
- Kol Zchut, Hamichlol, igemel-net and other secondary explainers: not used, because the regulation text, the bylaws or the regulator's own data were reachable for the same points.
