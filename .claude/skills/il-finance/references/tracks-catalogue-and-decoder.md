## Summary

- The CMA publishes one row per **track** per month for every provident, study, investment-provident, child-savings, pension and insurance savings track, on data.gov.il, history from 1999. Each dataset ships a CMA field dictionary (resource "פירוט שינויים פורמט חדש ..."), which states that amounts are in NIS millions and yields are **nominal gross** (CAT-02 to CAT-04). This closes part of discovery O11; fee averaging and risk-measure method remain open, exposure units inferred (CAT-06, CAT-07, CAT-03). The exposure fields are the regulator's "total exposure": holdings plus derivatives in delta terms, so they can pass 100% of assets and overlap (CAT-44).
- **Fee fields (2026-10-23).** `AVG_ANNUAL_MANAGEMENT_FEE` and `AVG_DEPOSIT_FEE` are percent values (of assets per year; of deposits) and hold the **average actually charged over the latest completed calendar year**: in October 2026 both portals label them "לשנת 2025" / "בשנת 2025" (CAT-35, CAT-36). The CMA defines the same-named member-report figure as the rate "שגבתה הקופה בפועל מכלל העמיתים" (after discounts, CAT-37); the identity with the dataset field is inferred. Pension fees are per fund, not per track (CAT-39). Empty means not reported, not zero (CAT-40). The weighting of the average is still not stated by the CMA (OQ2).
- In Gemel-Net, `FUND_ID` is the **track's** income-tax number. The fund (קופה) number, for example Meitav's 7857 "מיטב גמל להשקעה", is not in Gemel-Net. It is in the `transfer_bank` dataset, which maps fund number to track number (CAT-09, CAT-30).
- Since the CMA circular on investment tracks (2022-9-28 as amended by 2022-9-30), a track's name must match the CMA's list of tracks and its policy. Misleading words such as "בטוחה" or "סולידית" are banned. Specialized tracks fall into five clusters (CAT-14 to CAT-21). Institutions implemented the new names on 1.7.2024, the day the list's explanatory notes give for its entry into force. The CMA's list ("רשימת מסלולי השקעה – תיקון", ref. 2024-1471, 17.4.2024, on gov.il; no later amendment found on 2026-11-07) sets every standard name's policy and the products that may use it: CAT-23 (general, age, money-market, credit-and-bonds and flexible tracks, and a table of where every other name is), CAT-47 to CAT-52 and CAT-45.
- Meitav: in report period 202609, kupat gemel lehashkaa fund 7857 has **16** public tracks and exactly **one** named "כללי" (7978), which Meitav marks as the default track at joining. The data rules out two distinct "general" investment-provident tracks at Meitav. What the app's duplicate is could not be seen without the app (CAT-25 to CAT-28; not needed for the site, product.md D17).
- The same standard track name can hold quite different mixes at different managers, and the actual mix can change without a rename (CAT-12, CAT-24). The catalogue must show the CMA record (classification, fee, exposures with date), not infer content from the name.

### CAT-03 Units: amounts in NIS millions; rates in percent
- Rule: The dictionaries' general note 1 says "סכומים במיליוני ₪" (gemel, insurance) and "כלל הסכומים הנקובים בשורה הינם במיליוני שקלים" (pension). So `DEPOSITS`, `WITHDRAWLS`, `INTERNAL_TRANSFERS`, `NET_MONTHLY_DEPOSITS` (pension: `MONTHLY_DEPOSITS`) and `TOTAL_ASSETS` are NIS millions at the period end. Fee and yield fields are **percent values** (0.61 means 0.61%), not fractions. `LIQUID_ASSETS_PERCENT` is a percent of the track's assets ("אחוז הנזילות של נכסי הקופה").
- Conditions / exceptions: Percent units are confirmed by the institution's own display of the same numbers with a % sign. Meitav shows track 7978's fee as 0.61% and the CMA field shows 0.61. Meitav also shows assets "באלפי ש"ח" equal to the CMA value times 1,000, for example 3,884,432 against 3884.43. Infinity's monthly report for June 2019 gives track 7232 "סך נכסים ליום 30/06/2019 בש"ח" 37,660,994 against the CMA's 37.64 for 201906, and track 1084 234,755,283 against 234.59 (checked 2026-11-03). **Inferred, not stated:** `STOCK_MARKET_EXPOSURE`, `FOREIGN_EXPOSURE` and `FOREIGN_CURRENCY_EXPOSURE` are NIS millions, not percent. In 202609, track 13259 (S&P 500) shows 1284.46 against total assets of 1291.02, and track 103 shows 12378.07 against 25706.14. Their share of assets is a computation (exposure ÷ `TOTAL_ASSETS`). The regulator's own Gemel-Net track page shows the same three exposures both in percent and "במיליוני ש\"ח" (read in a browser 2026-11-03, CAT-44), which fits the unit but does not state the dataset's. What the exposure figures count, and why they can pass 100% of assets, is CAT-44. Note that the dictionary types several percent fields as `int(10)`, while the live datastore types them numeric with decimals. The exposure units are inferred, not stated. 2026-11-12: the Source line names each dictionary and its rows ahead of Meitav's page, which now carries its own institution label; it only shows the same values with a % sign.

### CAT-04 Yields are nominal and gross of management fees
- Rule: All yield fields in the CMA's datasets are nominal and gross: "תשואות נומינליות ברוטו" (Gemel-Net, Bituach-Net); "כלל התשואות הפיננסיות הינן נומינליות ברוטו" (Pension-Net). A saver's net return therefore needs the fee the saver actually pays subtracted, and that fee is not in the data (data-landscape 6.3 item 8).

### CAT-05 Yield period conventions
- Rule: `REPORT_PERIOD` is yyyymm. `MONTHLY_YIELD` is the yield for that month. `YEAR_TO_DATE_YIELD` is cumulative from the start of the calendar year to the report month; before the format change it was a 12-month cumulative figure (dictionary note "היה מצטברת ל12 חודשים, הפך למתחילת שנה"). `YIELD_TRAILING_3_YRS` and `_5_YRS` are cumulative over 3 and 5 consecutive years (old names `..._36_HODASHIM`, `..._60_HODASHIM`). `AVG_ANNUAL_YIELD_TRAILING_3YRS` and `_5YRS` are average annual yields over the same windows.
- Conditions / exceptions: **Inferred from the data, not stated by the CMA:** the average annual figures are geometric (compound) annualisations of the cumulative ones. 7978 in 202609: (1 + 45.15%)^(1/3) − 1 = 13.22%, matching the field; 5 years: (1 + 47.10%)^(1/5) − 1 = 8.02%, matching. 7860: (1 + 82.45%)^(1/3) − 1 = 22.19%, matching. It is also inferred that the 36- and 60-month windows end at the report month. Trailing fields are null when the track is younger than the window (Meitav 15347 to 15351 in 202609). In pension, yields were published at both fund and track level until 12/2016 and at track level only from 01/2017. In 2023 rows, `SPECIALIZATION` was null for many tracks (CAT-10). 2026-11-12: the Source line links the three dictionaries ahead of the worked example (the pension dictionary's rows corrected to 11 and 20 to 26).

### CAT-06 Fee fields: average rates, apparently for the previous year
- Rule: `AVG_ANNUAL_MANAGEMENT_FEE` is "שיעור דמי ניהול ממוצעת שנתי – נכסים", the average annual management-fee rate on assets. `AVG_DEPOSIT_FEE` (pension: `AVG_FEE_ON_DEPOSITS`) is "שיעור דמי ניהול ממוצעת- הפקדות", the average rate on deposits. Both are percent (CAT-03). They are averages across the track's members, not the price a given saver pays and not a list price.
- Conditions / exceptions: The CMA does not define the averaging (asset-weighted or member-weighted) or the period. Two sourced facts point to "average actually charged in the previous calendar year". (1) The CMA portals label the pair with the latest completed year ("לשנת 2025", CAT-36); as a named example, Meitav labels the same numbers "דמי ניהול ממוצעים מצבירה בשנה קודמת" and "דמי ניהול ממוצעים מהפקדה משנה קודמת" ([Meitav reports page](https://www.meitav.co.il/provident_pension/kupa_gemel_invest_reports/), checked 2026-10-25, institution). (2) In the data the value changes only in January: 7978 was 0.57 (2024), 0.60 (2025), 0.61 (2026); 7860 was 0.54, 0.58, 0.59. A track with no prior year has null fees: 15472 in 202609; 14270 in 2023, inception 2023-01-01. In Pension-Net, every Meitav comprehensive track in 202609 shows the same fees (0.11 and 1.25), which suggests the fee is reported at fund level (an inference then; CAT-39 confirms it). ### CAT-07 Risk and liquidity fields
- Rule: `STANDARD_DEVIATION` is "סטית תקן – 5 שנים עוקבות" (old name `STIAT_TEKEN_60_HODASHIM`). `ALPHA` is "אלפא שנתית": "חישוב אלפא שנתית של תשואות הקופה על סמך התשואה החודשית של 60 החודשים האחרונים", an annual alpha computed from the last 60 monthly yields. `SHARPE_RATIO` is "שארפ ריבית חסרת סיכון": "מדד שנתי של תשואות ... ביחס למדד ריבית ללא סיכון, על סמך התשואה החודשית של 60 החודשים האחרונים", an annual measure against a risk-free interest index over 60 months. `LIQUID_ASSETS_PERCENT` is the liquidity percentage of the track's assets.
- Conditions / exceptions: The following are not defined in any source read: the alpha benchmark, the risk-free index, whether the standard deviation is annualised, and what counts as "liquid" (open question 3). Meitav shows the standard deviation without a % sign (for example 5.99 for 7978). Per product.md section 4, the site does not rank by past return; these fields are history only. 2026-11-12: the Source line's four links, titled "1" to "4", now carry each dictionary's name and its rows.

### CAT-08 Flow fields, and how mergers show in the flows
- Rule: `DEPOSITS` are deposits by salaried and self-employed members, excluding transfers. `WITHDRAWLS` (sic) are payments to members, excluding transfers. `INTERNAL_TRANSFERS` is net transfers: in from other provident funds minus out to other funds (gemel); pension's description reads "העברות בין המסלולים". `NET_MONTHLY_DEPOSITS` is deposits − withdrawals ± transfers.
- Conditions / exceptions: The pension dictionary describes the net field as "(משמאל לימין) העברות + משיכות - הפקדות", which contradicts the data. Data check: Meitav pension track 1589, 202609: 41.29 − 3.61 + 143.12 = 180.8, matching the field. So use deposits − withdrawals + transfers for both datasets (my computation). **Inferred from one case:** a track merger is not recorded as a transfer. In 202407 Meitav track 7981 went to 0 assets and track 7862's assets rose from 63.31 to 125.92 while 7862's `INTERNAL_TRANSFERS` was −2.41. ### CAT-11 How a saver-facing name maps to the data
- Rule: A saver-facing label combines up to six layers. The data keeps them in separate places:
  1. the controlling group (`CONTROLLING_CORPORATION`, for example "מיטב דש השקעות בע"מ");
  2. the managing company (`MANAGING_CORPORATION`, for example "מיטב גמל ופנסיה בע"מ");
  3. the product type (`FUND_CLASSIFICATION`, for example "קופת גמל להשקעה");
  4. the fund (קופה), with its own income-tax number and name ("מיטב גמל להשקעה", 7857), found only in `transfer_bank` and in the institution's documents;
  5. the track (מסלול), with its own income-tax number = Gemel-Net `FUND_ID`;
  6. the track name (`FUND_NAME`), which in Gemel-Net is the full string brand + product + standard track name (for example "מיטב גמל להשקעה כללי").
  A marketing name can hide the product type: in 202609 the general investment-provident tracks include "אלטשולר שחם חיסכון פלוס כללי", "אנליסט מסלולית - קופת גמל להשקעה כללי" and "כלל גמל לעתיד כללי", and Meitav markets "מיטב קידס" as a children's plan "המבוצעת באמצעות קופת גמל להשקעה".
- Conditions / exceptions: Names in `transfer_bank` ("שם מסלול ארוך", "שם מסלול קצר") can differ from Gemel-Net `FUND_NAME`, and from institution pages. For example Meitav writes "כספי (שיקלי)" in its flyer, while the data has "כספי (שקלי)". Match on track number, not name. The Meitav names are examples of how one company labels its tracks, from its own pages. 2026-11-12: Meitav's two pages carry their own institution label on the Source line, after the regulator's data.

### CAT-13 Merged, renamed and legacy tracks: how they appear
- Rule: The CMA's investment-tracks circular lets an institution close an investment track only if it has no members or managed assets, or through a merger "בהתאם להוראות הדין" (circular 2022-9-30, s.14). ### CAT-15 A track's name must match its regulatory policy as set in the CMA list
- Rule: A track's name must fit its regulatory investment policy as set out in the list of investment tracks (s.9(א)). ### CAT-16 Misleading track and fund names are prohibited

### CAT-17 Track categories: general, default, specialized, and the five clusters
- Rule: The circular defines these categories.
  - **General track** ("מסלול כללי"): a track that is not specialized, not a default track and not an annuitants' track.
  - **Specialized track** ("מסלול השקעה מתמחה"): a track in the list under one of five clusters ("אשכולות"):
    - אשכול 1, active management (specialized tracks characterised by direct investment alongside index-tracking assets);
    - אשכול 2, active management with variable fees ("דמי ניהול משתנים"), charged according to the yield achieved;
    - אשכול 3, direct investment in tradable assets ("נכסים סחירים");
    - אשכול 4, index-tracking ("מכשירים עוקבי מדדים");
    - אשכול 5, "אמונה וקיימות": the site keeps its tracks run by a sustainability or environmental view and leaves out its religious-law tracks (D113).
  - Also defined: the **age-dependent model** ("מודל השקעות תלוי גיל"); the **hosted track** ("מסלול אירוח"), in which an external investment manager manages members' money; the **index-tracking track** ("מסלול השקעה עוקב מדדים"), which meets s.6 of chapter 4, part 2, title 5 of the consolidated circular.
- Conditions / exceptions: Category names as used in the data (CAT-10): "מתמחים בניהול אקטיבי", "מתמחים באפיקי השקעה סחירים", "עוקבי מדדים", "קיימות". The CMA's list of investment tracks (2024 amendment) names the standard tracks of each cluster:

### CAT-18 What a kupat gemel lehashkaa (and keren hishtalmut) may offer, and its default track
- Rule: In study funds, investment provident funds and non-insurance-fund policies, an institution may manage a **general track** and the specialized tracks of s.6, without the s.6(ב) obligation to offer "משולב סחיר" and "עוקב מדדים גמיש" (s.7(א)). ### CAT-19 Age-dependent and general tracks in pension and tagmulim-type funds (decoding names like "לבני 50 ומטה"): a general track is closed to new joiners
- Rule: In provident funds that are not study funds and not investment provident funds, a new member who did not choose a track joins the default track that matches their age (s.4(ב)). The model is either three tracks (age up to 50; 50 to 60; 60 and over, s.4(א)(1)) or tracks for birth-date cohorts spanning at least 5 and at most 10 years (s.4(א)(2)). Such a fund may keep a general investment track only closed to new joiners: s.3(ד), as amended by circular 2022-9-30 (in force 1.1.2024), reads "מסלול השקעה כללי ובלבד שמסלול כאמור יהיה סגור להצטרפות של עמיתים שלא היו באותו מסלול ביום התחילה". A "מסלול כללי" is a track that is not specialised, not a default track and not for annuitants (s.2). The circular's "קופת גמל" leaves out central funds, old pension funds (קרן ותיקה), sick-pay, self-directed, holiday and other-purpose funds, old guaranteed-yield insurance funds, the five guaranteed-yield provident funds the fee regulations name and the two Bank Leumi employees' funds (s.2; CAT-53), so it covers new pension funds, comprehensive and general: a pension fund's general track is closed to new joiners. The amendment's explanatory note and the CMA's 2024 list amendment say the same of annuity provident funds ("המסלולים הכלליים (שסגורים למצטרפים חדשים בקופות גמל לקצבה)").
- Conditions / exceptions: "יום התחילה" is the start of the tracks circular (s.2, s.16); the 2020 amendment's opening says the base circular 2015-9-29 came into force on 1 January 2016. Example (named, not the rule): Clal's comprehensive pension bylaws of May 2026 list "מסלול כללי (שההצטרפות אליו נסגרה ביום 1 בינואר 2016)", "סגור להצטרפות של עמיתים, שלא היו עמיתים במסלול זה ביום 01.01.2016" ([כלל פנסיה מקיפה – תקנון, מאי 2026](https://www.clalbit.co.il/media/1r3nyivr/תקנון-כלל-פנסיה-מקיפה-y703-5-2026.pdf), p.21, read in the library copy, checked 2026-11-06, institution). ### CAT-21 Changes to a track's specialization, deviations, and notice to members
- Rule: An institution may change the specialization of a specialized track (as set out in the list) only if its board approves that the change better reflects the track's investment mix as actually managed (s.12(א)). In a track's first year the institution may deviate from the regulatory policy while the track's assets are too small to follow it (s.13(א)).

### CAT-23 What specific standard tracks hold, as the CMA's list of investment tracks sets them

### CAT-29 Refresh: cadence, new-period detection, and API behaviour
- Rule: All three CMA datasets are tagged frequency "Day", update "Automat". The 2024+ resources were last modified 2026-10-22 around 04:00. Data is monthly by `REPORT_PERIOD`. ### CAT-44 The exposure fields are total exposure: what is held plus exposure through derivatives, so they can pass 100% of assets
- Rule: The CMA's monthly-report circular defines the three totals an institution reports for each fund or track, "חשיפה כוללת למניות (קוד דיווח KT761), חשיפה כוללת למט"ח (קוד דיווח KT762) וחשיפה כוללת לחו"ל (קוד דיווח KT763)": "חשיפה כוללת הינה סך החשיפה לאפיק על ידי אחזקה ישירה (שווי נכס המוחזק), בצירוף החשיפה בגין הפעילות בנגזרים על אותו נכס הנקבעת בדרך המקובלת למדידתה". ### CAT-53 Guaranteed-yield provident funds: five funds named in the fee regulations, outside the tracks circular, and the four general tracks inside them
- Rule: "קופת גמל מבטיחת תשואה" is a closed list of five funds named in the management-fee regulations: "(1) מנורה מבטחים יתר; (2) ישיר ביטחון; (3) אקסלנס גמולה; (4) מנורה מבטחים אמיר; (5) בר קרן גמולים" (reg. 1). Reading, not a quoted rule: the regulations name funds, not tracks, so the whole fund is outside the circular, its non-guaranteed track included.
- Conditions / exceptions:
