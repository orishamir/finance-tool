## Summary

Every employee in Israel aged 21 (men) or 20 (women) and up must be insured in a pension product after a six-month waiting period (or from day one, paid retroactively, if they already had a pension). The minimum rates since 1 January 2017 are 6% employee, 6.5% employer, 6% severance, on wages up to the national average wage. Self-employed people must deposit 4.45% of income up to half the average wage and 12.55% from half to the full average wage into an annuity-type fund. The default product is a new comprehensive pension fund (קרן פנסיה חדשה מקיפה): savings plus pooled disability and survivors' insurance, with 30% of assets covered by a state guaranteed-return mechanism and actuarial balancing that can move everyone's balance. Deposits above a ceiling (20.5% of twice the average wage a month) go to a general pension fund (קרן פנסיה כללית). If an employee does not choose, the employer must use one of four "selected" funds (Infinity, Altshuler Shaham, Mor, Meitav, from 1 Nov 2024 for four years) at 1% of deposits plus 0.22% of balance, fixed for at least 10 years. The legal maximum is 6% of deposits plus 0.5% a year of the balance. Money is locked until retirement (old-age pension from 60 at the earliest) except the severance component and a few narrow cases. A provident fund for annuity (קופת גמל לתגמולים ואישית לפיצויים) holds the same kind of money without insurance cover and without the guaranteed-return mechanism, and pays an annuity only by transferring the money to a pension fund or insurer.

## Rules

### Mandatory pension for employees

### PEN-01 Who the mandatory-pension extension order covers
- Rule: The consolidated extension order for mandatory pension (צו הרחבה [נוסח משולב] לפנסיה חובה, 2011) applies to every employee employed or to be employed in any workplace in Israel, except employees who already have a pension arrangement at least as good (from 1.1.2014, an arrangement at 17.5% of wages or more).
- Conditions / exceptions: Does not apply below age 20 for women and 21 for men ("מי שטרם מלאו לו - באישה 20 שנים ובגבר 21 שנים"); on reaching that age, earlier employment counts toward the waiting period. Excludes a person who retired at mandatory retirement age and receives a pension (National Insurance benefits are not a pension for this purpose). Existing better arrangements (collective agreements, older pension funds) are preserved (section 5).
- Figures: ages 20 and 21; threshold 17.5% of wages for "better arrangement" from 1.1.2014. Fixed in the order.

### PEN-02 Waiting period
- Rule: An employee is entitled to pension insurance and contributions immediately after six months from the start of work ("מיד בתום 6 חודשים מתחילת העבודה", the תקופת המתנה). An employee who starts work while already insured in any pension insurance is entitled to contributions from the first day of work; the contributions are paid after 3 months of work or at the end of the tax year, whichever is earlier, retroactively to the first day, and the waiting period does not apply.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 2008 transitional periods (9 months) are spent. Applies to employees covered by PEN-01.
- Figures: 6 months; 3 months for retroactive payment. Fixed in the order.

### PEN-05 Products that satisfy the order, and severance in place of severance pay
- Rule: The employee may choose, in writing to the employer within 60 days of starting work, comprehensive pension including a provident fund for annuity approved by the Commissioner, provided it includes death and disability cover, in the same fund or another. If the employee does not choose, the employer insures them in a new comprehensive pension fund. Employer severance payments under the order replace statutory severance pay for the wage and periods they cover (section 14 of the Severance Pay Law). Severance deposits are not returned to the employer except where the employee withdrew money before a qualifying event (death, disability, retirement at 60 or older) or where severance rights were removed under sections 16-17 of the Severance Pay Law.
- Conditions / exceptions: Section 3(ג) preserves the employee's right to move to another fund. An employer may top up severance to 8.33% (section 9). Pension, disability and survivors rights follow the fund's bylaws (section 10).
- Figures: 60 days to choose. Fixed.

### PEN-06 Employee choice of fund and the employer's default
- Rule: An employee entitled to join a provident fund may choose any fund intended for the purpose set by law or agreement, and the employer may not condition payment on a particular fund (Provident Funds Law section 20(א)). If the employee did not choose after being given the opportunity, the employer may deposit in a default fund (section 20(ב)). Under the CMA circular, a managing company may accept such an employee only if its fund is a selected pension fund (קרן נבחרת) under the distribution mechanism, or a default fund chosen by the employer through a competitive process. From 1 June 2025 employers must place non-choosing employees in one of the selected funds by an automatic distribution mechanism; employers with fewer than 50 employees may choose among the selected funds at their discretion.
- Conditions / exceptions: An employer that runs its own competitive process for a default fund may sign for up to 5 years; the employees join that fund's default investment and insurance tracks, and its default insurance cover must include at least disability cover for a 25% loss of work capacity and death cover that includes a lifetime spouse pension. The managing company of a default fund may not condition joining or its fee on buying other products it or a related party markets.
- Figures: 50 employees; 5-year limit; 25% disability threshold. Set by circular.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל), התשס"ה-2005](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/16/law/16_lsr_299984.pdf) pp.9-10, s.20 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 16), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343666.pdf) p.2, s.1 (latest change to section 20) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוזר גופים מוסדיים 2024-9-6 – הוראות לעניין בחירת קופת גמל – עדכון](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/notice-2024-097/he/Regulations_regarding_the_selection_of_%20a_provident_fund-updating%20_(pdf).pdf) pp.4-9, annex ss.3, 7, 11-12 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [דוח הממונה על שוק ההון, החיסכון והביטוח לשנת 2024 – פרק 2: סקירת פעילות הרשות](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/press_0083/he/Commisioner%20Report%20Design%20B_chapter2%20ver6_digital.pdf) p.2 "השלימה הרשות את ההליך הרביעי לקביעת קרנות פנסיה נבחרות" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [דוח הממונה על שוק ההון, ביטוח וחיסכון לשנת 2025 – פרק 2: סקירת פעילות הרשות](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/tender-0102/he/Commissioner%20Report%202025%20chapter%202_Digital%20-%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%A9%20%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99.pdf) p.4 "נכנס לתוקף מנגנון חלוקה חדש בין קרנות הפנסיה הנבחרות" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

### PEN-07 Mandatory pension deposits for the self-employed
- Rule: A self-employed person must deposit into a provident fund for annuity, by the end of the tax year, 4.45% of the part of "income liable for deposit" (הכנסה חייבת בהפקדה) up to half the average wage, and 12.55% of the part above half and up to the average wage. Deposits made by or for them on insured employee income are deducted from the amount due.
- Conditions / exceptions: Not liable at the end of the tax year if under 21, if they have reached early retirement age under the Retirement Age Law, or if fewer than six months have passed since first VAT registration as a dealer; also not liable if aged 55 or more on the law's effective date. Part of each year's deposits is assigned to an unemployment-savings component (מרכיב חיסכון למצב אבטלה): the lower of one-third of the deposits or an annual amount set by reference to section 9(7א)(א)(2) of the Income Tax Ordinance. Enforcement: the Fines Collection Center sends a notice to a liable person who earned above 12 times the minimum wage and did not deposit; 90 days to pay; failure is an offence with a NIS 500 fine, indexed. The offence applies to deposits for tax year 2018 onward. Re-sourced 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the 2026 amounts are computed from the NII average wage (PEN-04); the Harel 2026 table, which printed NIS 1,170.24, is no longer cited.

Amounts follow the average wage.

### Fund types, ceilings and insured salary

### PEN-08 New comprehensive pension fund and its monthly deposit ceiling
- Rule: A new comprehensive pension fund (קרן פנסיה מקיפה חדשה) is a pension fund approved by the Commissioner and by the Tax Authority as a provident fund for annuity, approved after 1 January 1995, whose bylaws give the member old-age pension, survivors' pension and disability pension rights. Employer and employee deposits to it "לא יעלו על תקרת ההפקדה החודשית" (Income Tax Rules (Approval and Management of Provident Funds), 1964, reg. 19(ב1)(1)). The regulations define the monthly deposit ceiling (תקרת הפקדה חודשית) as "20.5% מהתקרה החודשית של השכר המבוטח", and the monthly insured-salary ceiling as twice the average monthly wage of a salaried employee (the National Insurance s.2 figure), computed by the formula of the seventh schedule (reg. 1). The CMA standard pension bylaw adds a second limb: the monthly deposit ceiling is the lower of that amount and 22.83% of all the salary components the employer reports (s.1, "תקרת הפקדה חודשית" (b)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Deposits above the ceiling, and deposits for a disabled member above the partial-disability deposit ceiling, are moved to the member's account in the general pension fund of the same manager (PEN-10). The seventh-schedule formula (L_t = Σ 2X_n − Σ Z_n over the year to date) lets unused ceiling roll forward within the year; for a single month with no carry the ceiling is 2 × X × 20.5%. The 2026 value is a computation over primary inputs: 2 × 13,769 × 20.5% = NIS 5,645.29 (the NII average wage from 1.1.2026, 13,769, is the same figure in the Tax Authority's 2026 deductions booklet). *2026-10-25 scope pass:* re-sourced from the regulation, NII and the CMA standard bylaw; the Harel table and the Mor bylaws, which print the same figure, are no longer cited.

### PEN-09 Insured salary and determining salary
- Rule: The comprehensive fund infers the insured salary (שכר מבוטח) from contributions: for an employee member, the month's tagmulim contributions divided by the tagmulim deposit rate of that month ("בשיעור ההפקדות למרכיב התגמולים באותו החודש"); for a self-employed member, contributions divided by 16% unless the member proves a different rate. Several sources of deposits are added up. Insurance benefits use the determining salary (שכר קובע): the highest of the average updated insured salary over the last 12 months, the last 3 months, or the 12 months before the last 12, "אך לא יותר מסכום ששווה ל-3 פעמים השכר הממוצע במשק" (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.1). For a month without deposits while still insured, the last determining salary computed applies.

### PEN-12 Disability cover
- Rule: A disabled member (נכה) is an insured member who lost at least 25% of the capacity to work in their own job and in any other suitable job, for more than 90 consecutive days; full disability (נכה מלא) is at least 75% (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.1). The disability pension equals the insured disability rate times the determining salary for full disability, times the degree of disability for partial disability, and 133% for a dependent ("סיעודי") full disability, provided the pension does not exceed the determining salary (s.69). Payment starts "החל ביום ה-61 שלאחר מועד האירוע המזכה בקצבת נכות" (s.72(א)) and continues until the earliest of the insurance end age, the end of the disability, or death (s.72(ג)). While disabled, the fund credits monthly contributions to the disabled member's account (זקיפת דמי גמולים לעמית נכה), so old-age savings continue (s.74).
- Conditions / exceptions: Progressive disability cover (קצבת נכות מתפתחת) raises the pension by 2% a year if bought (ss.26, 70). Double disability pension (קצבת נכות כפולה) in the first two months (ss.27, 71); whether it is default cover is an optional provision (PEN-11). A partial-disability member may keep insurance for the remaining salary by paying insurance costs. Medical decisions follow the fund doctor, medical committee and appeals committee (ss.62-67). ### PEN-13 Survivors cover

### PEN-18 Guaranteed-return mechanism (replacing new designated bonds)
- Rule: The state guarantees a return top-up to eligible pension funds: old (ותיקה) and new comprehensive (חדשה מקיפה) funds. For 30% of eligible assets (70% for "existing entitled" members, those entitled before 1 January 2004) the target return is an annual 5.15% ("ריבית שנתית בשיעור 5.15%"), and the cumulative target over 60 months is that rate compounded "בתוספת שיעור עליית מדד החודש שפורסם לאחרונה לפני חודש הבדיקה לעומת מדד חודש הבסיס" (s.34ב), that is, CPI plus 5.15%. From the determining date (in force on 1 October 2022) the covered share is computed monthly, net of designated bonds; in a new comprehensive or multi-track fund those assets are invested in a guaranteed-return track that mirrors the largest track. After each 60-month period, a return above the cumulative target goes to a designated fund; a shortfall is paid by the state, first from that fund and then from the budget. The guarantee for a given asset holds for 60 months plus two further 60-month periods even if the rules change. The designated fund's money cannot be attached.
- Conditions / exceptions: Designated bonds (איגרות חוב מיועדות) are the non-tradable "ערד" and "מירון" series issued to pension funds; bonds already issued remain until redeemed. ### PEN-19 Actuarial balancing
- Rule: Members' and pensioners' rights are updated by the actuarial surplus or deficit (עודף או גירעון אקטוארי). "שיעור העודף או הגירעון האקטוארי הנובע מגורמים דמוגרפים ... ייזקף בכל רבעון": to all members by updating their balances, and to pensioners by updating their pensions. Surplus or deficit from returns and the discount rates goes "למקבלי הקצבאות בקרן בלבד", through a pensioners' reserve (עתודה למקבלי קצבה) "בשיעור שלא עולה על 1% מהתחייבויות הקרן למקבלי קצבה", with a separate reserve per annuitant track; returns on members' balances create no surplus or deficit (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.109(א)-(ב); Codex part 6, s.4.3.3 "חלוקת עודף או גירעון אקטוארי", from circular 2014-3-1).
- Conditions / exceptions: Every comprehensive fund writes its bylaws on the standard text (circular 2016-3-4, s.2(א)). *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the fund-by-fund figures moved from the 2024 values (2024 report, table ד-13) to the 2025 values of the CMA report for 2025, chapter 4, table ד-14, file p.12; the table also repeats 2022 to 2024, and the 2024 values there are the ones the rule gave before. *2026-10-25 scope pass:* re-sourced from the CMA standard bylaw and the Codex; the Mor bylaws are no longer cited. ### PEN-27 Old-age pension: when and how it is computed
- Rule: A member may start an old-age pension (קצבת זקנה) from the minimum eligibility age or any later date they choose, provided they do not receive a disability pension from the fund (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.42). The fund acknowledges a request within 7 days and approves it within 10 days, and the choices cannot be changed after the first payment (s.43); the first eligibility date is in s.44. The pension equals the balance divided by the conversion factor (מקדם המרה) "המפורט בנספחים [נספח שתקבע החברה המנהלת, בכפוף לאישור הממונה]", by the member's age, spouse's age, children, guaranteed-payment period and survivors' rates chosen (s.45). The member chooses a spouse survivors' rate of at least 30% of the old-age pension and total survivors' rates up to 100% (s.48(ג)-(ד)), and optionally a guaranteed-payment period (תקופת תשלומים מובטחים) of 60, 120, 180 or 240 months, ending no later than age 87, available only to a member who received no disability pension in the 24 months before (s.50). A member may convert only part of the balance (s.47) or commute part (s.51).
- Conditions / exceptions: A notice is sent six months before insurance end age or mandatory retirement age. The pension is paid "עד ליום השלישי של כל חודש, בשל החודש הקודם" and is updated by the CPI (s.46) and by actuarial balancing (PEN-19). The member may ask for up to three months' pension retroactively, if they were of eligible age. Deposits may continue after the insurance end age, mandatory retirement age, and after the pension started (s.53). Each fund's conversion factors are its own annex, approved by the Commissioner; how they are set is PEN-48, the nine funds' tables PEN-51 to PEN-60, and the expected pension the yearly statement prints PEN-50. Every comprehensive fund writes its bylaws on the standard text (circular 2016-3-4, s.2(א)). *2026-10-25 scope pass:* re-sourced from the CMA standard bylaw (the 7-day and 10-day times and the 24-month condition added from it); the Mor bylaws are no longer cited.
- Figures: 7 and 10 days; 30%, 100%, 60/120/180/240 months, age 87, 24 months. Fixed in the CMA standard bylaw.

### PEN-28 Commutation and the minimum annuity amount
- Rule: At the pension request, a member may commute (היוון) the part of the pension above the minimum annuity amount (סכום קצבה מזערי) into a lump sum (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.51(א)(1)). ### PEN-32 Death
- Rule: On the death of a member or a pensioner, money due to survivors is paid as survivors' pensions under the bylaws. If there are no survivors, the balance is paid to the beneficiaries (מוטבים) named in writing, by the rates the member set or in equal shares; if there are no beneficiaries, to the heirs. A pensioner who chose a guaranteed-payment period and has no survivors: beneficiaries get the commuted value of the remaining guaranteed payments.
- Conditions / exceptions: The pension part is the CMA standard text every comprehensive fund uses (circular 2016-3-4, PEN-09): "מוטב" is whoever the member or pensioner named in the last written beneficiary instruction, and a later will that expressly divides the fund money counts as the beneficiary instruction (s.1); with no survivors the balance goes to the beneficiaries at the member's rates or in equal shares, and with no beneficiaries to the heirs (s.115); the guaranteed-payment rule is s.50(ג). In Mor's provident-fund bylaws, a small balance (up to NIS 5,000, indexed) may be paid without a probate order to a spouse, parent or child, after three years and with conditions, and a beneficiary may move the money to a new account in their own name (section 23(א)(2ב)); the provident-fund standard text was not read for this. ### PEN-48 How a new pension fund's conversion factors are set
- Rule: A new pension fund's bylaws carry annexes of factors that convert a member's balance into an old-age pension, by the member's age when the pension starts, sex, birth year, the spouse's age, the guaranteed-payment period chosen and the survivors' pension rate chosen (Codex, pension part, s.(ד)(2)(א)). The factors are computed at an annual discount rate ("ריבית שנתית להיוון") less the maximum management-fee rate, or the maximum rate the pension fund's bylaws allow to be charged on the fund's assets, whichever is lower, and on the actuarial assumptions (s.(ד)(2)(ב)); they are updated when the discount rate or the actuarial assumptions change, and as the Commissioner directs (s.(ד)(2)(ג)). The annual discount rate is defined in the consolidated circular: for a comprehensive pension fund "ריבית שנתית אפקטיבית בשיעור של 4.38% או בשיעור אחר כפי שיקבע הממונה מזמן לזמן", and 4.26% for a pensioner paid for a qualifying event before 1 January 2023; for a general pension fund 4%. A manager computes the actuarial balance and the factors on the Commissioner's default assumptions, or on other assumptions only after testing them, filing a reasoned request with its appointed actuary's statement, and getting the Commissioner's prior written approval; from January 2019 it re-tests every assumption once in three years.
- Conditions / exceptions: The maximum fee from old-age pensioners whose pension started on or after 1 February 2022 is 0.3% a year (PEN-21). Of the nine funds' current bylaws, seven state on their factor tables that the factors are computed at a 0.3% fee; Clal's tables do not restate the fee, and its fee-sensitivity annex puts the zero change at 0.30%; Infinity's annex is ambiguous (PEN-51, PEN-55). A pensioner charged less than the maximum fee does not get a different factor: the Codex computes the factors at the discount rate "בניכוי שיעור דמי ניהול מרבי או השיעור המרבי שניתן לגבות מנכסי הקרן בהתאם לתקנון קרן הפנסיה, לפי הנמוך מבניהם" (s.(ד)(2)(ב), p.10), a maximum and not the fee charged, and in a pension fund the pensioners' fee is one rate on the pensioners' pool (below). So a fund that writes a lower pensioner maximum into its bylaws computes all its factors at that lower rate, and a fee charged below the maximum leaves the factor as it is. *2026-11-06:* the fee the Codex takes off is a fund-wide maximum: it defines "שיעור דמי ניהול מרבי" as "שיעור דמי הניהול המרבי שחברה מנהלת רשאית לגבות מתוך סך כל הנכסים העומדים כנגד התחייבויות הקרן לכלל מקבלי הקצבה לפי תקנות דמי ניהול" (pp.2-3); the regulation caps the pensioners' fee on "סך כל הנכסים העומדים כנגד התחייבויות הקרן לכלל מקבלי קצבת הזקנה" who started from 1.2.2022 (reg. 3(א)(1א)), where for an insurance policy it caps it per annuitant ("התחייבויות הקופה למקבל קצבת זקנה", reg. 2(א)(4)); and the standard bylaw charges pensioners "מתוך סך הנכסים העומדים כנגד סך ההתחייבויות למקבלי קצבאות בקרן" (s.8(א)(1)). Inferred, not stated: what a fund leaves uncharged below the maximum stays in the pensioners' pool and reaches pensioners through actuarial balancing (PEN-19); no text read says this. Six funds' bylaws (Harel, Meitav, Clal, Menora Mivtachim, Migdal, Infinity) print a table of how the factor changes with the fee, each that fund's own term; none read says the table is applied to a pensioner's actual fee. *2026-11-06, after an independent check:* the earlier wording here ("the factors the nine funds print are stated at 0.3%"; "a pensioner charged a different fee has the factor adjusted") overstated what was read and was replaced. The 4.38% definition was set by circular 2022-9-20, from the financial statements of the second quarter of 2022 (the consolidated text's footnote 68). The 2018 standard bylaw text printed 3.74% net (PEN-20); the consolidated circular's later definition governs. The Codex pension part (file codex6.6, current Codex numbering gate 6 part 6; the file's own header reads "חלק 3 – הוראות ספציפיות לקרן פנסיה") states no version date and its footnotes cite circulars up to 2014; its text layer garbles digits, so s.(ד)(2) was read on the rendered page. The consolidated circular's gate 5 text read is edition 9 of 27.7.2022; it does not yet carry circular 2024-9-8 (PEN-49).

### PEN-34 What a provident fund for annuity is since 2008
- Rule: Approval of a fund as a provident fund for tagmulim (קופת גמל לתגמולים) or a personal severance fund (קופת גמל אישית לפיצויים) covers money deposited for tax years before 2008 only. The CMA's standard bylaws for the current product are "התקנון התקני של קופת גמל לא משלמת לקצבה, קופת גמל לתגמולים וקופת גמל אישית לפיצויים" (circular גמל 2021-2-1): new money is pension savings for retirement; Gemel-Net classifies it as "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים". The fund does not pay annuities itself ("קופת גמל לא משלמת לקצבה"): withdrawal of tagmulim as an annuity is done by transferring the money to a pension fund or an insurance fund (קופת ביטוח). A non-annuity withdrawal of tagmulim is allowed only at mandatory retirement age with total balances up to the minimum accumulation amount and either no annuity or annuities above the minimum annuity amount ("ולא משולמת לו קצבה או משולמת לו קצבה אשר עולה על סכום הקצבה המזערי"), from a new account (severance or beneficiary money), or under the income, medical or disability conditions (circular s.14.1-14.2).
- Conditions / exceptions: Money deposited up to the end of tax year 2007 (employee) can be withdrawn at 60, or under older conditions (for money before 2 January 2005: at 60 after retiring or cutting work by 50%, or after leaving a job and not starting another within six months, or 13 months at an employer that does not pay to an annuity fund). Self-employed: money to 31 December 2005 after 15 years from first deposit or at 60; from 1 January 2006 at 60. Tax treatment of each is in TXP. Since 21.10.2021 a salaried member may make a tagmulim withdrawal outside these conditions (taxed as a non-compliant withdrawal) only after the employment with that employer ended or at the minimum old-age eligibility age (circular גמל 2021-2-1, s.14.3). Corrected 2026-10-26 (scope settling): the pre-2008 limit is amendment 3's s.13(א)(4), now cited; the annuity condition was inverted ("no annuity above the minimum"); the CMA's circular replaces Mor's copy of the standard bylaws as the source. Where the money may move for an annuity and the caps on the move: PEN-61 (2026-11-06).

### PEN-35 Provident fund for annuity versus pension fund
- Rule: A provident fund for annuity is not an eligible fund for the guaranteed-return mechanism (law s.34ב, PEN-18), and its annuity terms are set by the pension fund or insurer the money is moved to at retirement (PEN-34). It satisfies mandatory pension only if death and disability cover is held elsewhere (PEN-05). Fee caps are 4% of deposits and 1.05% of balance (PEN-22), against 6% and 0.5% in a comprehensive pension fund (PEN-21). Deposits to it are limited by the 1964 regulations: employer tagmulim "שיעור שלא יעלה על 7.5% מן המשכורת" (reg. 19(א)(1)(א), and 19(א)(1)(ג)(1) for an annuity fund), severance "לא יעלה על השיעור של 8 אחוזים ושליש" (reg. 19(א)(1)(ב)(1)), employee tagmulim "אינו עולה על 7% ממשכורתו" (reg. 19(ב)(2)).

### PEN-61 Getting an annuity from a provident fund for annuity: where the money may move, and the caps on the move
- Rule: A provident fund for annuity (in the law since 2015 "קופת גמל לחיסכון": "קופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קופת ביטוח, קרן פנסיה או קופת גמל מרכזית לקצבה") pays no annuity itself: "משיכת כספים בידי עמית ממרכיב התגמולים על דרך של קצבה תיעשה באמצעות העברתם לקרן פנסיה או לקופת ביטוח" (CMA standard provident bylaw, circular גמל 2021-2-1, s.14.1; PEN-34). The transfer regulations let money into an annuity fund from a tagmulim fund (reg. 2(א)(2)) or a personal severance fund (2(א)(3)) "ובלבד שהקופה המקבלת היא קופת גמל משלמת לקצבה", into the member's account by the components it had in the transferring fund (2(ב)). A receiving fund "לא יהיה רשאי לקבל כספים שלא ניתן להעביר" (2(ד), as replaced in 2023): into a new comprehensive pension fund, no more than the monthly maximum deposit to that fund times the months since "מועד התשלום הראשון לקופה המעבירה", less the balance of money deposited for the member to that or another comprehensive fund for the same months (2(ד)(1)). Since 2023 that date is defined as "מועד התשלום הראשון לקופה המעבירה שאליה שולמו לראשונה כספים או לקופת גמל שאינה קרן חדשה מקיפה שאליה שולמו לראשונה כספים שהועברו לקופה המעבירה, לפי העניין" (2(ד)(1) as amended in 2023): read as: for money that came into the transferring fund from an earlier fund that is not a comprehensive fund, the months count from the first payment to that earlier fund, so the cap is larger than if they counted from the first payment to the transferring fund. The 2008 text names the receiving fund "קרן חדשה זכאית"; a 2022 amendment put "קרן חדשה מקיפה" in its place throughout the regulations (s.2, in force from "המועד הקובע כהגדרתו בסעיף 34ב לחוק קופות גמל", s.4). From a non-insurance annuity fund into an insurance fund, nothing that would leave the member with less than the monthly deposit ceiling times the months since the first payment, in non-insurance annuity funds; here the date is that of the first payment to the transferring fund or "לקופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קופת ביטוח שאליה שולמו לראשונה כספים שהועברו לקופה המעבירה" (2(ד)(1א), from 1.9.2023, not for policies issued before then or money moved into them).
- Conditions / exceptions: Read: the 2008 regulations allowed tagmulim money in only if it could not be commuted until five years after the transfer or the eligibility age, whichever came first; the 2014 amendment replaced 2(א)(2) with the condition on the receiving fund alone. Amendment 3 (2008) had defined a "קופת גמל לא משלמת לקצבה" as one "שלא ניתן למשוך ממנה כספים, למעט כספים ממרכיב הפיצויים, אלא באמצעות העברתם לקופת גמל משלמת לקצבה"; Amendment 13 (2015) deleted both definitions while the transfer regulations still use the term, and gave "קצבה" a second form: payments "מקופת גמל, לפי תקנונה, במשך תקופה מוגדרת", then lifelong payments from an insurer under a policy bought under s.16(ד)(6) or from another fund. No CMA provision putting that second form into a provident fund's bylaws was found; the standard bylaw as amended in 2021 names only the transfer. Severance money (2(ד)(2), as amended in 2014): for a member under 60, severance money for any year may not move into any annuity fund, a general fund or an insurer included, unless for that year employer's and employee's tagmulim were deposited at the rate reg. 19 of the 1964 Income Tax regulations would have required had the severance been in an annuity fund, and that tagmulim money moves with it or the receiving fund is the one it was deposited in (2(ד) as a whole does not apply to transfers under reg. 34א of those regulations). Inferred, not stated: a balance above the comprehensive-fund cap can go to a new general pension fund, on which reg. 2(ד)(1) sets no amount cap; 2(ד)(2) still applies to severance money. In the receiving pension fund the money joins the member's balance (standard bylaw s.9(7), PEN-63), so the pension is the balance divided by that fund's conversion factor (PEN-27, PEN-48; the nine funds' factors PEN-51), and a lump sum comes out only by commuting what is above the minimum annuity amount (PEN-28) or in the law's withdrawal cases (PEN-30); no exception for transferred money was found in the law, the regulations or the standard bylaw. Not found: any text that obliges a pension fund or insurer to accept such a transfer (the standard pension bylaw lets the manager approve or reject a joining request within 14 days, s.13(ג), and the joining circular provides for rejecting one with written reasons, PEN-63), and an insurer's factors for money moved in at retirement (each policy's terms, INS-17). Starting a pension on part of the balance while still insured changes the cover (PEN-62). Tax on the annuity and on commuting it: TXP.
- Figures: the monthly deposit ceiling of a comprehensive fund (PEN-08); 1.9.2023. Fixed in regulation.

### PEN-62 Starting a pension on part of a pension-fund balance changes the insurance; money moved in from a kupat gemel lehashkaa is the exception at eight of nine funds

### PEN-63 Money moved into a pension fund for an annuity: it joins the balance, the fund may refuse a joining request, and money from a kupat gemel lehashkaa cannot come back out as a lump sum
- Rule: In the CMA's standard pension bylaw, a member's accumulated balance is updated with, among other things, "כספים שהועברו אל הקרן או ממנה בעקבות בקשת עמית להעברת כספים בין קופות גמל" (s.9(7)); the old-age pension is computed on the accumulated balance at the fund's conversion factor (PEN-27, PEN-48), so money moved in from a provident fund for annuity or a kupat gemel lehashkaa is converted like the rest of the balance. No text was found that obliges a pension fund or an insurer to take a member in: the CMA's joining circular provides for a managing company's rejecting a request to join, "דחתה חברה מנהלת של קרן פנסיה או קופת גמל בקשת הצטרפות, למעט דחיה בשל חיתום, תשלח לעמית הודעה בכתב על כך בתוך 14 ימי עסקים ... ותנמק החלטתה" (s.14). Money moved from a kupat gemel lehashkaa (allowed from early retirement age, KGL-09) is credited "לשם קבלת קצבה בלבד" (transfer regulations reg. 2(ב)(2)), and the Commissioner's clarification, published as a draft on 13.10.2020, reads that as barring a member from going back on the choice once the money has moved, "כגון: באמצעות הותרת הכספים בקופה לקצבה לצורך הורשה או משיכת הכספים בסכום הוני": from receipt the receiving fund treats the money as in its annuity-recipients' track, "החברה המנהלת של הקופה המקבלת לא תאפשר לעמית לחזור בו מבחירתו לקבלת קצבה" (s.1), and on death the survivors' or heirs' rights follow the terms of the annuity track the member chose (s.2).
- Conditions / exceptions: The clarification is headed "טיוטה" and no final version was found (gov.il's file is the draft; one web search for its title on 2026-11-07 returned only that file and secondary pages); it ties the bar to the tax benefit the move carries ("גלומה בפעולה זו הטבת מס"; the tax is TXK, TXK OQ4). It names a lump-sum withdrawal and leaving the money for inheritance; it does not say in terms whether commuting part of the pension above the minimum annuity amount (PEN-28) counts as going back on the choice, and no fund's bylaws were found to say (the eight funds' clauses on such money concern only the insurance, PEN-62). It also allows the money to sit in the default age track between its arrival and its absorption into the pensioners' track at the start of the next month without breaching the regulation. Money moved from a provident fund for annuity carries no such words since 2014 (PEN-61): it is commuted like any balance, only above the minimum annuity amount (PEN-28) or in the law's withdrawal cases (PEN-30). The joining circular's s.14 is a duty to explain a rejection, not a right to be admitted; the standard bylaw likewise lets the manager decide on a joining request within 14 days (s.13(ג), PEN-61). An insurer's factors for money moved in at retirement are each policy's terms (INS-17); none was read for this route. Leads: pensuni.com and prisha.co.il pages on the 2020 route (not cited).
- Figures: 14 business days (joining circular s.14)

### Payslip

### PEN-36 What a payslip must show about pension deposits
- Rule: Under the Wage Protection Law, every employee must get a written payslip that includes: the total pay counted for pension or other social rights, broken down by the right it counts toward (תוספת item 5(6), "המובאים בחשבון לעניין זכויות פנסיוניות"); each deduction to a provident fund, by fund and amount (item 6(4), "ניכוי לקופת גמל, שיפורט לפי הקופה שאליה הניכוי מיועד וסכומו"); and the employer's payments for social benefits not paid to the employee, including employer contributions to a provident fund (item 7; the 2008 text reads "לרבות הפרשות המעביד לקופת גמל", and since the 2014 term-change law, which lists the Wage Protection Law in its schedule, "המעביד" reads "המעסיק").
- Conditions / exceptions: *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the item 7 quote was the post-2014 wording attributed to the 2008 text; it now quotes the 2008 text and names the 2014 change (חוק להחלפת המונח מעביד, p.2 s.1 and p.5 schedule item 11). The law does not require the payslip to show the contribution rate (percent) or to name the severance part separately. A person can derive their rates as: employee rate = employee pension deduction ÷ pensionable pay; employer rate = employer tagmulim payment ÷ pensionable pay; severance rate = employer severance payment ÷ pensionable pay (a computation for `docs/method.md`, not a rule). The comprehensive fund infers the insured salary from tagmulim deposits (PEN-09), so the annual fund statement's insured-salary figure is a second place to check. The exact line labels on payslips were not sourced (open question 11).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [חוק הגנת השכר (תיקון מס' 24), התשס"ח-2008](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_300652.pdf) p.2, s.24 "מעביד חייב לנהל פנקס שכר ולמסור לכל עובד" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק הגנת השכר (תיקון מס' 24), התשס"ח-2008](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_300652.pdf) p.6, schedule items 5(6), 6(4), 7 "ניכוי לקופת גמל, שיפורט לפי הקופה שאליה הניכוי מיועד וסכומו" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק להחלפת המונח מעביד (תיקוני חקיקה), התשע"ד-2014](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/19/law/19_lsr_306572.pdf) p.2, s.1; p.5, the schedule (Wage Protection Law listed; "המעביד" becomes "המעסיק") · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

## Proposed dimensions

- `actuarial-adjustment`: the balance or pension can change by an actuarial surplus or deficit unrelated to the person's own investment return (PEN-19). Applies to pension funds only.
- `default-assignment`: what a person gets if they never choose (default fund, default investment track, default insurance track) and which fee is locked for how long (PEN-06, PEN-11, PEN-17, PEN-23).
- `employer-dependence`: rights or fees that depend on the current employer (severance release, fee discounts given through the workplace, employer's fund choice) (PEN-05, PEN-25, PEN-31).
