## Summary

Every employee in Israel aged 21 (men) or 20 (women) and up must be insured in a pension product after a six-month waiting period (or from day one, paid retroactively, if they already had a pension). The minimum rates since 1 January 2017 are 6% employee, 6.5% employer, 6% severance, on wages up to the national average wage. Self-employed people must deposit 4.45% of income up to half the average wage and 12.55% from half to the full average wage into an annuity-type fund. The default product is a new comprehensive pension fund (קרן פנסיה חדשה מקיפה): savings plus pooled disability and survivors' insurance, with 30% of assets covered by a state guaranteed-return mechanism and actuarial balancing that can move everyone's balance. Deposits above a ceiling (20.5% of twice the average wage a month) go to a general pension fund (קרן פנסיה כללית). If an employee does not choose, the employer must use one of four "selected" funds (Infinity, Altshuler Shaham, Mor, Meitav, from 1 Nov 2024 for four years) at 1% of deposits plus 0.22% of balance, fixed for at least 10 years. The legal maximum is 6% of deposits plus 0.5% a year of the balance. Money is locked until retirement (old-age pension from 60 at the earliest) except the severance component and a few narrow cases. A provident fund for annuity (קופת גמל לתגמולים ואישית לפיצויים) holds the same kind of money without insurance cover and without the guaranteed-return mechanism, and pays an annuity only by transferring the money to a pension fund or insurer.

## Rules

### Mandatory pension for employees

### PEN-01 Who the mandatory-pension extension order covers
- Rule: The consolidated extension order for mandatory pension (צו הרחבה [נוסח משולב] לפנסיה חובה, 2011) applies to every employee employed or to be employed in any workplace in Israel, except employees who already have a pension arrangement at least as good (from 1.1.2014, an arrangement at 17.5% of wages or more).
- Conditions / exceptions: Does not apply below age 20 for women and 21 for men ("מי שטרם מלאו לו - באישה 20 שנים ובגבר 21 שנים"); on reaching that age, earlier employment counts toward the waiting period. Excludes a person who retired at mandatory retirement age and receives a pension (National Insurance benefits are not a pension for this purpose). Existing better arrangements (collective agreements, older pension funds) are preserved (section 5).
- Figures: ages 20 and 21; threshold 17.5% of wages for "better arrangement" from 1.1.2014. Fixed in the order.
- Source: [צו הרחבה (נוסח משולב) לפנסיה חובה, ילקוט הפרסומים 6302, 27.9.2011](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/labor-wage/he/workers-rights_working-conditions_pension_H096.pdf) pp.2-4 (gazette pp.6939-6941), ss.3, 4 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.
- Dimensions: eligibility, regulator
- Likely to change: no

### PEN-02 Waiting period
- Rule: An employee is entitled to pension insurance and contributions immediately after six months from the start of work ("מיד בתום 6 חודשים מתחילת העבודה", the תקופת המתנה). An employee who starts work while already insured in any pension insurance is entitled to contributions from the first day of work; the contributions are paid after 3 months of work or at the end of the tax year, whichever is earlier, retroactively to the first day, and the waiting period does not apply.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 2008 transitional periods (9 months) are spent. Applies to employees covered by PEN-01.
- Figures: 6 months; 3 months for retroactive payment. Fixed in the order.
- Source: [צו הרחבה (נוסח משולב) לפנסיה חובה, ילקוט הפרסומים 6302, 27.9.2011](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/labor-wage/he/workers-rights_working-conditions_pension_H096.pdf) p.4 (gazette p.6941), s.6(ה)-(ו) "מיד בתום 6 חודשים מתחילת העבודה" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.
- Dimensions: eligibility, liquidity-lockup
- Likely to change: no

### PEN-03 Minimum contribution rates
- Rule: From 1 January 2017 the minimum contributions are 6% employee (תגמולי עובד), 6.5% employer (תגמולי מעסיק) and 6% employer for severance (פיצויים), 18.5% in total, of the wage on which the obligation applies (PEN-04). These are minimums; an employment agreement may set more.
- Conditions / exceptions: In an insurance fund (executive insurance) or a provident fund that is not a pension fund, the employer's tagmulim payment may include the cost of loss-of-work-capacity insurance needed to secure 75% of the wage, provided the employer's tagmulim share alone is not below 5% and the employer total for tagmulim plus that insurance does not exceed 7.5%. An employer with a section-14 arrangement at 8.33% continues it when the employee moves to a pension fund.
- Figures: history: 17.5% (5.5% employee, 6% employer, 6% severance) from 1.1.2014 under the 2011 order table; 18% (5.75%, 6.25%, 6%) from 1.7.2016; 18.5% (6%, 6.5%, 6%) from 1.1.2017. Fixed in the agreement; no later change found (open question 2).

### PEN-04 Wage base for mandatory contributions
- Rule: The insured wage is the employee's wage and its components as defined in the Severance Pay Law and its regulations ("שכר העובד ורכיביו כמשמעם בחוק ובתקנות פיצויי פיטורים"), and the obligation applies to the wage paid or to the national average wage as updated from time to time, whichever is lower ("הנמוך מבין השניים").
- Conditions / exceptions: The order does not require contributions on wages above the average wage; arrangements above it come from the employment agreement. The 2016 agreement defines its wage base ("השכר הקובע") as the wage under the agreement that applies to the employee, and not less than the insured wage under the mandatory-pension agreement. The average wage is the National Insurance figure under section 2 of the National Insurance Law.
- Figures: average wage 2026: NIS 13,769 a month (Harel 2026 table, institution; NII publication not reached, open question 5). Updated by NII.
- Source: [צו הרחבה (נוסח משולב) לפנסיה חובה, ילקוט הפרסומים 6302, 27.9.2011](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/labor-wage/he/workers-rights_working-conditions_pension_H096.pdf) p.2, s.2(ב); p.4, s.6(ב)-(ג) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [צו הרחבה בדבר הגדלת ההפרשות לביטוח פנסיוני במשק (2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/labor-wage/he/workers-rights_working-conditions_pension_Pension2016.pdf) p.1, s.2(א) "השכר הקובע" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [Harel, תקרות וסכומים לשנת 2026](https://media.harel-group.co.il/media/eh3fyryd/תקרות-הפקדה-בקופות-הגמל-ובקרנות-השתלמות-2026.pdf) p.1 "שכר ממוצע במשק" · checked 2026-10-25 · institution.
- Dimensions: deposit-ceiling, eligibility
- Likely to change: yes, the average wage is updated by NII each year

### PEN-05 Products that satisfy the order, and severance in place of severance pay
- Rule: The employee may choose, in writing to the employer within 60 days of starting work, comprehensive pension including a provident fund for annuity approved by the Commissioner, provided it includes death and disability cover, in the same fund or another. If the employee does not choose, the employer insures them in a new comprehensive pension fund. Employer severance payments under the order replace statutory severance pay for the wage and periods they cover (section 14 of the Severance Pay Law). Severance deposits are not returned to the employer except where the employee withdrew money before a qualifying event (death, disability, retirement at 60 or older) or where severance rights were removed under sections 16-17 of the Severance Pay Law.
- Conditions / exceptions: Section 3(ג) preserves the employee's right to move to another fund. An employer may top up severance to 8.33% (section 9). Pension, disability and survivors rights follow the fund's bylaws (section 10).
- Figures: 60 days to choose. Fixed.
- Source: [צו הרחבה (נוסח משולב) לפנסיה חובה, ילקוט הפרסומים 6302, 27.9.2011](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/labor-wage/he/workers-rights_working-conditions_pension_H096.pdf) pp.2-5 (gazette pp.6939-6942), ss.3, 7-10 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.
- Dimensions: insurance-cover, eligibility, liquidity-lockup
- Likely to change: no

### PEN-06 Employee choice of fund and the employer's default
- Rule: An employee entitled to join a provident fund may choose any fund intended for the purpose set by law or agreement, and the employer may not condition payment on a particular fund (Provident Funds Law section 20(א)). If the employee did not choose after being given the opportunity, the employer may deposit in a default fund (section 20(ב)). Under the CMA circular, a managing company may accept such an employee only if its fund is a selected pension fund (קרן נבחרת) under the distribution mechanism, or a default fund chosen by the employer through a competitive process. From 1 June 2025 employers must place non-choosing employees in one of the selected funds by an automatic distribution mechanism; employers with fewer than 50 employees may choose among the selected funds at their discretion.
- Conditions / exceptions: An employer that runs its own competitive process for a default fund may sign for up to 5 years; the employees join that fund's default investment and insurance tracks, and its default insurance cover must include at least disability cover for a 25% loss of work capacity and death cover that includes a lifetime spouse pension. The managing company of a default fund may not condition joining or its fee on buying other products it or a related party markets.
- Figures: 50 employees; 5-year limit; 25% disability threshold. Set by circular.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל), התשס"ה-2005](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/16/law/16_lsr_299984.pdf) pp.9-10, s.20 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 16), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343666.pdf) p.2, s.1 (latest change to section 20) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוזר גופים מוסדיים 2024-9-6 – הוראות לעניין בחירת קופת גמל – עדכון](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/notice-2024-097/he/Regulations_regarding_the_selection_of_%20a_provident_fund-updating%20_(pdf).pdf) pp.4-9, annex ss.3, 7, 11-12 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [דוח הממונה על שוק ההון, החיסכון והביטוח לשנת 2024 – פרק 2: סקירת פעילות הרשות](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/press_0083/he/Commisioner%20Report%20Design%20B_chapter2%20ver6_digital.pdf) p.2 "השלימה הרשות את ההליך הרביעי לקביעת קרנות פנסיה נבחרות" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [דוח הממונה על שוק ההון, ביטוח וחיסכון לשנת 2025 – פרק 2: סקירת פעילות הרשות](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/tender-0102/he/Commissioner%20Report%202025%20chapter%202_Digital%20-%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%A9%20%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99.pdf) p.4 "נכנס לתוקף מנגנון חלוקה חדש בין קרנות הפנסיה הנבחרות" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

### PEN-07 Mandatory pension deposits for the self-employed
- Rule: A self-employed person must deposit into a provident fund for annuity, by the end of the tax year, 4.45% of the part of "income liable for deposit" (הכנסה חייבת בהפקדה) up to half the average wage, and 12.55% of the part above half and up to the average wage. Deposits made by or for them on insured employee income are deducted from the amount due.
- Conditions / exceptions: Not liable at the end of the tax year if under 21, if they have reached early retirement age under the Retirement Age Law, or if fewer than six months have passed since first VAT registration as a dealer; also not liable if aged 55 or more on the law's effective date. Part of each year's deposits is assigned to an unemployment-savings component (מרכיב חיסכון למצב אבטלה): the lower of one-third of the deposits or an annual amount set by reference to section 9(7א)(א)(2) of the Income Tax Ordinance. Enforcement: the Fines Collection Center sends a notice to a liable person who earned above 12 times the minimum wage and did not deposit; 90 days to pay; failure is an offence with a NIS 500 fine, indexed. The offence applies to deposits for tax year 2018 onward.
- Figures: 4.45% and 12.55%. 2026 (Harel, institution): half average wage NIS 6,884.5; average wage NIS 13,769; maximum required NIS 1,170.24 a month. Amounts follow the average wage.
- Source: [חוק ההתייעלות הכלכלית (תיקוני חקיקה להשגת יעדי התקציב לשנות התקציב 2017 ו-2018), התשע"ז-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_366872.pdf) p.4 (gazette p.218), chapter ב', s.3(א) "על חלק ההכנסה החייבת בהפקדה שאינו עולה על מחצית השכר הממוצע במשק" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק ההתייעלות הכלכלית (תיקוני חקיקה להשגת יעדי התקציב לשנות התקציב 2017 ו-2018), התשע"ז-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_366872.pdf) pp.3-9, chapter ב', ss.2-11, 18 · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [Harel 2026 table](https://media.harel-group.co.il/media/eh3fyryd/תקרות-הפקדה-בקופות-הגמל-ובקרנות-השתלמות-2026.pdf) p.1 "פנסיית חובה לעצמאים" · checked 2026-10-25 · institution.

### Fund types, ceilings and insured salary

### PEN-08 New comprehensive pension fund and its monthly deposit ceiling
- Rule: A new comprehensive pension fund (קרן פנסיה מקיפה חדשה) is a pension fund approved by the Commissioner and by the Tax Authority as a provident fund for annuity, approved after 1 January 1995, whose bylaws give the member old-age pension, survivors' pension and disability pension rights. Employer and employee deposits to it "לא יעלו על תקרת ההפקדה החודשית" (Income Tax Rules (Approval and Management of Provident Funds), 1964, reg. 19(ב1)(1)). The regulations define the monthly deposit ceiling (תקרת הפקדה חודשית) as "20.5% מהתקרה החודשית של השכר המבוטח", and the monthly insured-salary ceiling as twice the average monthly wage of a salaried employee (the National Insurance s.2 figure), computed by the formula of the seventh schedule (reg. 1). The CMA standard pension bylaw adds a second limb: the monthly deposit ceiling is the lower of that amount and 22.83% of all the salary components the employer reports (s.1, "תקרת הפקדה חודשית" (b)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Deposits above the ceiling, and deposits for a disabled member above the partial-disability deposit ceiling, are moved to the member's account in the general pension fund of the same manager (PEN-10). The seventh-schedule formula (L_t = Σ 2X_n − Σ Z_n over the year to date) lets unused ceiling roll forward within the year; for a single month with no carry the ceiling is 2 × X × 20.5%. The 2026 value is a computation over primary inputs: 2 × 13,769 × 20.5% = NIS 5,645.29 (the NII average wage from 1.1.2026, 13,769, is the same figure in the Tax Authority's 2026 deductions booklet). *2026-10-25 scope pass:* re-sourced from the regulation, NII and the CMA standard bylaw; the Harel table and the Mor bylaws, which print the same figure, are no longer cited. Open questions 5 and 6 are answered.
- Figures: comprehensive-fund monthly ceiling 2026: NIS 5,645.29 (computed: 2 × NIS 13,769 × 20.5%). 22.83% factor in the CMA standard bylaw. Updated with the average wage.

### PEN-09 Insured salary and determining salary
- Rule: The comprehensive fund infers the insured salary (שכר מבוטח) from contributions: for an employee member, the month's tagmulim contributions divided by the tagmulim deposit rate of that month ("בשיעור ההפקדות למרכיב התגמולים באותו החודש"); for a self-employed member, contributions divided by 16% unless the member proves a different rate. Several sources of deposits are added up. Insurance benefits use the determining salary (שכר קובע): the highest of the average updated insured salary over the last 12 months, the last 3 months, or the 12 months before the last 12, "אך לא יותר מסכום ששווה ל-3 פעמים השכר הממוצע במשק" (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.1). For a month without deposits while still insured, the last determining salary computed applies.
- Conditions / exceptions: The 2026 cap is a computation over primary inputs: 3 × NIS 13,769 (NII average wage from 1.1.2026) = NIS 41,307. Every comprehensive fund writes its bylaws on the standard text (circular 2016-3-4, s.2(א)). Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass): the 12.5% floor that current bylaws add to the employee and self-employed divisors is an unsettled claim, PEN-37; the general fund's cap of four times the average wage is Mor's bylaw, PEN-38. ### PEN-12 Disability cover
- Rule: A disabled member (נכה) is an insured member who lost at least 25% of the capacity to work in their own job and in any other suitable job, for more than 90 consecutive days; full disability (נכה מלא) is at least 75% (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.1). The disability pension equals the insured disability rate times the determining salary for full disability, times the degree of disability for partial disability, and 133% for a dependent ("סיעודי") full disability, provided the pension does not exceed the determining salary (s.69). Payment starts "החל ביום ה-61 שלאחר מועד האירוע המזכה בקצבת נכות" (s.72(א)) and continues until the earliest of the insurance end age, the end of the disability, or death (s.72(ג)). While disabled, the fund credits monthly contributions to the disabled member's account (זקיפת דמי גמולים לעמית נכה), so old-age savings continue (s.74).
- Conditions / exceptions: Progressive disability cover (קצבת נכות מתפתחת) raises the pension by 2% a year if bought (ss.26, 70). Double disability pension (קצבת נכות כפולה) in the first two months (ss.27, 71); whether it is default cover is an optional provision (PEN-11). A partial-disability member may keep insurance for the remaining salary by paying insurance costs. Medical decisions follow the fund doctor, medical committee and appeals committee (ss.62-67). ### Investment tracks, guaranteed return and actuarial balancing

### PEN-17 Investment tracks and the age-dependent default
- Rule: The default investment track is an age-dependent track (מסלול השקעות תלוי גיל), in three bands: up to 50, 50 to 60, and 60 and over; a member is moved automatically to the track for their age, unless they chose otherwise (CMA tracks circular 2022-9-30, annex s.4(א); list 2024-1471; CMA standard pension bylaw s.19(א)(1)). Annuitants have their own default track (s.20(א)(1)). A member who joins without choosing goes to the default track; a member renewing within 12 months keeps the old track, with new severance deposits going to the default (s.21). A member may move all or part of the balance or of future deposits between tracks; severance money from a specific employer can go to a non-default track only with the employer's approval, the member's entitlement, or an unconditional-entitlement arrangement (s.22(א)-(ב)). The move is made "בתוך שלושה ימי עסקים, אלא אם כן נקבעה בתקנון הקופה תקופה קצרה יותר" (Income Tax Rules (Approval and Management of Provident Funds), 1964, reg. 33ב(ב), which applies to new pension funds by 33ב(א)(2); the standard bylaw s.22(ד) says "בתוך 3 ימים").
- Conditions / exceptions: Which specialised tracks a fund offers is each manager's menu; Mor's is PEN-41. Every comprehensive fund writes its bylaws on the standard text (circular 2016-3-4, s.2(א)). Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass): Mor's track list, its halacha track and its statement on designated-bond assets are PEN-41.
- Figures: age bands 50 and 60; 3 business days (fixed in regulation).

### PEN-18 Guaranteed-return mechanism (replacing new designated bonds)
- Rule: The state guarantees a return top-up to eligible pension funds: old (ותיקה) and new comprehensive (חדשה מקיפה) funds. For 30% of eligible assets (70% for "existing entitled" members, those entitled before 1 January 2004) the target return is an annual 5.15% ("ריבית שנתית בשיעור 5.15%"). From the determining date (1 October 2022) the covered share is computed monthly, net of designated bonds; in a new comprehensive or multi-track fund those assets are invested in a guaranteed-return track that mirrors the largest track. After each 60-month period, a return above the cumulative target goes to a designated fund; a shortfall is paid by the state, first from that fund and then from the budget. The guarantee for a given asset holds for 60 months plus two further 60-month periods even if the rules change. The designated fund's money cannot be attached.
- Conditions / exceptions: Designated bonds (איגרות חוב מיועדות) are the non-tradable "ערד" and "מירון" series issued to pension funds; bonds already issued remain until redeemed. The minister reports each year by 30 May. The general fund and provident funds for annuity are not eligible funds.
- Figures: 30%, 70%, 5.15%, 60 months, from 1.10.2022. Fixed in law; whether 5.15% is CPI-linked is not stated in the text read (open question 8).

### PEN-19 Actuarial balancing

### PEN-25 Fee discounts and what can end them (provident fund bylaws)

### PEN-26 Retirement ages

### PEN-27 Old-age pension: when and how it is computed
- Rule: A member may start an old-age pension (קצבת זקנה) from the minimum eligibility age or any later date they choose, provided they do not receive a disability pension from the fund (CMA standard pension bylaw, s.42). The fund acknowledges a request within 7 days and approves it within 10 days, and the choices cannot be changed after the first payment (s.43); the first eligibility date is in s.44. The pension equals the balance divided by the conversion factor (מקדם המרה) "המפורט בנספחים [נספח שתקבע החברה המנהלת, בכפוף לאישור הממונה]", by the member's age, spouse's age, children, guaranteed-payment period and survivors' rates chosen (s.45). The member chooses a spouse survivors' rate of at least 30% of the old-age pension and total survivors' rates up to 100% (s.48(ג)-(ד)), and optionally a guaranteed-payment period (תקופת תשלומים מובטחים) of 60, 120, 180 or 240 months, ending no later than age 87, available only to a member who received no disability pension in the 24 months before (s.50). A member may convert only part of the balance (s.47) or commute part (s.51).
- Conditions / exceptions: A notice is sent six months before insurance end age or mandatory retirement age. The pension is paid "עד ליום השלישי של כל חודש, בשל החודש הקודם" and is updated by the CPI (s.46) and by actuarial balancing (PEN-19). The member may ask for up to three months' pension retroactively, if they were of eligible age. Deposits may continue after the insurance end age, mandatory retirement age, and after the pension started (s.53). Each fund's conversion factors are its own annex, approved by the Commissioner. Every comprehensive fund writes its bylaws on the standard text (circular 2016-3-4, s.2(א)). *2026-10-25 scope pass:* re-sourced from the CMA standard bylaw (the 7-day and 10-day times and the 24-month condition added from it); the Mor bylaws are no longer cited.
- Figures: 7 and 10 days; 30%, 100%, 60/120/180/240 months, age 87, 24 months. Fixed in the CMA standard bylaw.

### PEN-28 Commutation and the minimum annuity amount

### PEN-31 Severance component when leaving a job

### PEN-32 Death
- Rule: On the death of a member or a pensioner, money due to survivors is paid as survivors' pensions under the bylaws. If there are no survivors, the balance is paid to the beneficiaries (מוטבים) named in writing, by the rates the member set or in equal shares; if there are no beneficiaries, to the heirs. A pensioner who chose a guaranteed-payment period and has no survivors: beneficiaries get the commuted value of the remaining guaranteed payments.
- Conditions / exceptions: In the provident fund bylaws, a small balance (up to NIS 5,000, indexed) may be paid without a probate order to a spouse, parent or child, after three years and with conditions. A beneficiary may move the money to a new account in their own name (section 23(א)(2ב)).

### PEN-33 Processing times; a withdrawal is paid within 4 business days
- Rule: A member who asks for all or part of the money "יקבל את הכספים כאמור בתוך 4 ימי עסקים מהיום שהגיעה בקשתו לקופה"; if that date falls in one of the first three business days of a month, the fund may defer payment to the fourth business day of that month (Income Tax Rules (Approval and Management of Provident Funds), 1964, reg. 41יא(א)). This binds pension funds and provident funds alike. The CMA standard pension bylaw sets the other service times: joining decided "בתוך 14 ימים" and notice of joining within 10 days (s.13(ג), (ה)); excess deposits moved to the general fund "עד ליום החמישי בחודש העוקב" (s.18(א)); a track switch within 3 days (s.22(ד); three business days by reg. 33ב(ב), PEN-17); an old-age pension request acknowledged within 7 days and approved within 10 days (s.43(ג)-(ד)); the pension paid by the third of each month (s.46(א)); a withdrawal request answered "בתוך 2 ימים" (s.108(ב)) and paid "בתוך 4 ימים ממועד קבלת הבקשה" (s.108(ז)).

### Provident fund for annuity

### PEN-34 What a provident fund for annuity is since 2008
- Rule: Approval of a fund as a provident fund for tagmulim (קופת גמל לתגמולים) or a personal severance fund (קופת גמל אישית לפיצויים) covers money deposited for tax years before 2008 only. The current product is run under standard bylaws as "קופת גמל לחיסכון, קופת גמל לתגמולים וקופת גמל אישית לפיצויים": new money is pension savings for retirement; Gemel-Net classifies it as "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים". The fund does not pay annuities itself ("קופת גמל לא משלמת לקצבה"): withdrawal of tagmulim as an annuity is done by transferring the money to a pension fund or an insurance fund (קופת ביטוח). A non-annuity withdrawal of tagmulim is allowed only at mandatory retirement age with total balances up to the minimum accumulation amount and no annuity above the minimum, from a new account (severance or beneficiary money), or under health and economic conditions.
- Conditions / exceptions: Money deposited up to the end of tax year 2007 (employee) can be withdrawn at 60, or under older conditions (for money before 2 January 2005: at 60 after retiring or cutting work by 50%, or after leaving a job and not starting another within six months, or 13 months at an employer that does not pay to an annuity fund). Self-employed: money to 31 December 2005 after 15 years from first deposit or at 60; from 1 January 2006 at 60. Tax treatment of each is in TXP.
- Figures: as stated. Fixed in law and bylaws.
- Source: [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל), התשס"ה-2005](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/16/law/16_lsr_299984.pdf) p.8, s.13(א) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf) p.2, s.2 (latest change to section 13(א)) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [Mor gemel standard bylaws 18.02.2026](https://www.moreinvest.co.il/media/5z0eicf4/res_גמל-לחיסכון-פברואר-2026-נקי-הוספת-מסלול-השקעה.pdf), sections 13-15 and the title page · checked 2026-10-25 · institution; [גמל-נט – שאילתת סיווג קופות ואוכלוסיית יעד, תקופה 202609](https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=a30dcbea-a1d2-482c-ae29-8f781f5025fb&filters=%7B%22REPORT_PERIOD%22%3A202609%7D&fields=FUND_CLASSIFICATION,TARGET_POPULATION&distinct=true&limit=200) · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

### PEN-35 Provident fund for annuity versus pension fund
- Rule: A provident fund for annuity is not an eligible fund for the guaranteed-return mechanism (law s.34ב, PEN-18), and its annuity terms are set by the pension fund or insurer the money is moved to at retirement (PEN-34). It satisfies mandatory pension only if death and disability cover is held elsewhere (PEN-05). Fee caps are 4% of deposits and 1.05% of balance (PEN-22), against 6% and 0.5% in a comprehensive pension fund (PEN-21). Deposits to it are limited by the 1964 regulations: employer tagmulim "שיעור שלא יעלה על 7.5% מן המשכורת" (reg. 19(א)(1)(א), and 19(א)(1)(ג)(1) for an annuity fund), severance "לא יעלה על השיעור של 8 אחוזים ושליש" (reg. 19(א)(1)(ב)(1)), employee tagmulim "אינו עולה על 7% ממשכורתו" (reg. 19(ב)(2)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Whether a provident fund for annuity carries insurance cover depends on whether its manager buys it for members; the CMA regulation on that, תקנות הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (כיסויים ביטוחיים בקופות גמל), התשע"ג-2013, was not read. Split on 2026-10-25 (scope pass): Mor's statement that its fund has no insurance cover is PEN-43; the loan limits seen in Mor's bylaws are an unsettled claim, PEN-44.
- Figures: deposit limits 7.5% employer tagmulim, 7% employee tagmulim, 8⅓% severance (fixed in regulation); fee caps as in PEN-21 and PEN-22.
- Source: [חוק ההתייעלות הכלכלית (תיקוני חקיקה להשגת יעדי התקציב לשנות התקציב 2021 ו-2022), התשפ"ב-2021](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/24/law/24_lsr_611567.pdf) p.34, s.34ב · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [תקנות מס הכנסה (כללים לאישור ולניהול קופות גמל), התשכ"ד-1964](https://www.btl.gov.il/Laws1/02_0103_100022.pdf), pp.6-7, reg. 19(א)(1)(א), (ב)(1), (ג)(1); 19(ב)(2) "שיעור שלא יעלה על 7.5% מן המשכורת" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary

### Payslip

### PEN-36 What a payslip must show about pension deposits
- Rule: Under the Wage Protection Law, every employee must get a written payslip that includes: the total pay counted for pension or other social rights, broken down by the right it counts toward (תוספת item 5(6), "המובאים בחשבון לעניין זכויות פנסיוניות"); each deduction to a provident fund, by fund and amount (item 6(4), "ניכוי לקופת גמל, שיפורט לפי הקופה שאליה הניכוי מיועד וסכומו"); and the employer's payments for social benefits not paid to the employee, including employer contributions to a provident fund (item 7; the 2008 text reads "לרבות הפרשות המעביד לקופת גמל", and since the 2014 term-change law, which lists the Wage Protection Law in its schedule, "המעביד" reads "המעסיק").
- Conditions / exceptions: *2026-10-25 rule-text pass:* the item 7 quote was the post-2014 wording attributed to the 2008 text; it now quotes the 2008 text and names the 2014 change (חוק להחלפת המונח מעביד, p.2 s.1 and p.5 schedule item 11). The law does not require the payslip to show the contribution rate (percent) or to name the severance part separately. A person can derive their rates as: employee rate = employee pension deduction ÷ pensionable pay; employer rate = employer tagmulim payment ÷ pensionable pay; severance rate = employer severance payment ÷ pensionable pay (a computation for `docs/method.md`, not a rule). The comprehensive fund infers the insured salary from tagmulim deposits (PEN-09), so the annual fund statement's insured-salary figure is a second place to check. The exact line labels on payslips were not sourced (open question 11).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [חוק הגנת השכר (תיקון מס' 24), התשס"ח-2008](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_300652.pdf) p.2, s.24 "מעביד חייב לנהל פנקס שכר ולמסור לכל עובד" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק הגנת השכר (תיקון מס' 24), התשס"ח-2008](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_300652.pdf) p.6, schedule items 5(6), 6(4), 7 "ניכוי לקופת גמל, שיפורט לפי הקופה שאליה הניכוי מיועד וסכומו" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary; [חוק להחלפת המונח מעביד (תיקוני חקיקה), התשע"ד-2014](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/19/law/19_lsr_306572.pdf) p.2, s.1; p.5, the schedule (Wage Protection Law listed; "המעביד" becomes "המעסיק") · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

- Pension splitting on divorce (חוק לחלוקת חיסכון פנסיוני בין בני זוג שנפרדו): mentioned only where bylaws refer to it; not a saving decision.
- Loans from pension funds: bylaws allow them (section 23) but terms are in a separate document not read.
- National Insurance old-age pension: a state benefit, not a product.
- The 2005 industry-sector extension order (prisha.co.il, zav2006.pdf): opened, but superseded by the general order and not used.
- Disability claim procedure in detail (doctors, committees, appeals): process, not product choice; times summarised in PEN-33 only.

## Proposed dimensions

- `actuarial-adjustment`: the balance or pension can change by an actuarial surplus or deficit unrelated to the person's own investment return (PEN-19). Applies to pension funds only.
- `default-assignment`: what a person gets if they never choose (default fund, default investment track, default insurance track) and which fee is locked for how long (PEN-06, PEN-11, PEN-17, PEN-23).
- `employer-dependence`: rights or fees that depend on the current employer (severance release, fee discounts given through the workplace, employer's fund choice) (PEN-05, PEN-25, PEN-31).
