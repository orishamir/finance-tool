## Summary

- Kupat gemel lehashkaa: no deduction or credit on deposits; gains inside the fund are not taxed; switching tracks or moving to another kupat gemel lehashkaa is not a withdrawal.
- A lump-sum withdrawal at any age is taxed: the law treats the gains withdrawn as interest income, taxed at up to 25% (s.3(ה4)(3), s.125C). The manager withholds; managers say 25% of the real gain. The exact legal route that removes the inflation part was not confirmed (open question).
- What changes at 60: from early retirement age (60) the balance may be moved to a provident fund for annuity, only to receive an annuity. Because the person's own deposits count as "exempt payments", the resulting "recognized annuity" is fully exempt from 60 (s.9A(b1)). A lump sum taken straight from the kupat gemel lehashkaa after 60 is taxed the same as before 60.
- A special exemption in s.9(18ב) (5 years, age 60, no apartment purchase) is not the general age-60 rule. It belonged to the 2016 multiple-apartments chapter and its status is unresolved.
- IRA: in law an IRA is a provident fund "in personal management". No IRA-specific tax rule was found, so it follows the wrapper it sits in.
- Child savings: the deposits, the parental addition and the grants are not income. Gains are taxed on withdrawal (National Insurance says "capital gains tax"), but no official source gives the rate. The tax on the annuity route has no clear statutory basis.

## Rules

### TXK-01 No deduction or credit on deposits to a kupat gemel lehashkaa
- Rule: The pension credit (s.45A(b), 35%) and deduction (s.47(b)) apply only to sums paid "לקופת גמל לקצבה". No provision gives a credit or deduction for deposits to a kupat gemel lehashkaa. The Ordinance lists sums an individual pays for himself into a kupat gemel lehashkaa as "תשלומים פטורים" (non-benefited "exempt payments", s.9A(a) para (4)), with no condition about credits. Para (3), for annuity funds, carries that condition.
- Conditions / exceptions: Deposits are made from income already taxed. The study-fund deduction for the self-employed (s.17(5A)) is a separate wrapper (`tax-hishtalmut.md`). A Knesset private bill's explanatory notes (2023) describe the product as savings "בלא הטבות מס בהפקדה". That is not law, but it agrees.

### TXK-02 Annual deposit ceiling (tax-relevant limit; the product rule is in `kupat-gemel-lehashkaa.md`)
- Rule: A member may deposit at most 70,000 NIS in each financial year ("בכל שנת כספים"), in total across all his accounts in all kupot gemel lehashkaa (Provident Funds Law s.22(a)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Per member, across every manager. Deposits under the 2016 special s.22(א1) window (TXK-15) were allowed above the ceiling, through 31.12.2017. That subsection is now marked repealed.
- Figures: 70,000 NIS base (adjusted to 2016); **83,641 NIS for 2026**. Updated every 1 January by the CPI rise; the first update was against the CPI known on 1 July 2016. The statute fixes the indexation rule. The 2026 figure appears as an editor's note in the Wikisource text, and in Harel's 2026 table ("תקרת הפקדה שנתית לגמל להשקעה ... 83,641 ₪"). It was not found in a CMA publication. Whether the 2025-2027 freeze of tax amounts touches this figure was not checked; it is in the Provident Funds Law, not the Ordinance.
- Source: [Provident Funds Law, Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)), s.22(a) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (base, indexation); [Harel, "תקרות וסכומים לשנת 2026"](https://media.harel-group.co.il/media/eh3fyryd/תקרות-הפקדה-בקופות-הגמל-ובקרנות-השתלמות-2026.pdf), p.1 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution (2026 figure)

### TXK-03 No tax on growth inside the fund
- Rule: The income of a provident fund is exempt unless it comes from a business the fund carries on, or from a company in a business the fund controls or holds a material holding in (s.9(2)(a)). "קופת גמל" here excludes an insurance fund (s.9(2)(b)). A kupat gemel lehashkaa is a provident fund (Provident Funds Law s.1, s.13(a)).
- Conditions / exceptions: The business-income exception exists; how it would apply to a fund, or to an IRA (TXK-17), was not researched. Tax arises only when the member withdraws (TXK-05).

### TXK-04 Track switches and transfers between kupot gemel lehashkaa are not taxed
- Rule: The tax charge in s.3(ה4)(3) applies to sums an individual "משך" (withdrew) from a kupat gemel lehashkaa. Switching tracks inside the fund, or transferring the balance "מקופת גמל להשקעה לקופת גמל להשקעה אחרת", is not a withdrawal. The fund's own sales are exempt fund income (TXK-03). On a transfer, the tax law treats all payment dates into the transferring fund as payment dates into the receiving fund (transfer regulations, reg. 3(ב)(1)).
- Conditions / exceptions: The legal text has no explicit sentence that says "no tax event" for a kupat gemel lehashkaa switch. That conclusion rests on the withdrawal wording plus the fund-level exemption. A manager states it explicitly: "ניתן לשלב בין המסלולים או לשנות אותם מעת לעת ללא אירוע מס". A transfer to an annuity fund is covered in TXK-10. A transfer out to a different wrapper (for example a brokerage account) is a withdrawal.

### TXK-05 Lump-sum withdrawal: gains are treated as interest income
- Rule: "ריבית ורווחים אחרים ... שמשך יחיד מקופת גמל להשקעה, יראו אותם כהכנסה מריבית". Gains equal to the linkage differentials computed on the money are treated as income from linkage differentials. A partial withdrawal is treated as coming proportionally from principal, interest and linkage ("באופן יחסי").
- Conditions / exceptions: No age, holding-period or purpose condition appears in s.3(ה4)(3). A withdrawal at 30 and a withdrawal at 65 are classified the same way. Only the deposited principal comes out untaxed. The provision says "יחיד" (an individual) and does not distinguish who deposited. This is relevant to child savings (TXK-23).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), p.21, s.3(ה4)(3) "יראו אותם כהכנסה מריבית" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

### TXK-06 Rate on that interest: up to 25%, as the top slice of income
- Rule: s.125C applies to interest deemed under s.3(ה4)(3), even though it otherwise excludes payments from provident funds (s.125C(e)). Under s.125C(b) an individual pays "בשיעור שלא יעלה על 25%" on interest income, and that income is treated as the highest step of his taxable income. s.125C(c)(1) sets 15% where the interest is paid on an asset that is not CPI-linked, or only partly linked.
- Conditions / exceptions: s.125C(d) moves interest to marginal rates in listed cases (business income, interest expense claimed, substantial shareholder, and others). None names a kupat gemel lehashkaa. No official source states whether a kupat gemel lehashkaa balance is an "asset linked to the index" (25% on real) or not (15% on nominal). Managers apply 25% on real (TXK-07). Surtax (s.121B) may apply on top (`tax-general.md`). For a person aged 60 or older with low income, whether the lower brackets can replace the 25% cap is open (tax report Q16, `tax-interest-deposits-bonds.md`).
- Figures: 25% maximum; 15% for non-linked assets. In force 2026. Fixed in law; the Minister may change the 15% by order (s.125C(c)(2)).
- Source: [פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa/he/LegalInformation_kesher_%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%20%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94%20%5B%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%5D%20-%20%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93.pdf), pp.203-204, s.125ג(א)-(ה) "בשיעור שלא יעלה על 25%" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary.

- Likely to change: see TXK-21 (proposed uniform 25% on real gains across products)

### TXK-07 Base is the real gain; the manager withholds 25%
- Rule: Managers describe a lump-sum withdrawal as "בניכוי 25% מס רווחי הון על הרווח הריאלי בלבד" (withheld at source, real gain only). A press article says "עד 25% על הרווח הריאלי".
- Conditions / exceptions: The statute (TXK-05) separates the linkage-differentials part from the interest part. s.9(13) exempts linkage differentials an individual receives on an asset, subject to conditions (not partial linkage differentials, no interest expense claimed, not business income). However, the Tax Authority's 2025 guide adds to its s.9(13) row: "הוראות אלה לא יחולו על חשבון בקופת גמל". So the provision that actually exempts the inflation part of a kupat gemel lehashkaa withdrawal was not found (open question 2). The withholding regulation that sets the 25% for this payment was not found either: the Wikisource text of the 2005 withholding regulations shows 20% for interest paid by a provident fund, which looks out of date (open question 3). Treat "25% of the real gain" as institution-strength until a Tax Authority source is found.
- Figures: 25% (2023 manager document; 2026 article).

### TXK-08 The person's own deposits count as "exempt payments"
- Rule: "תשלומים פטורים" include "(4) סכומים ששילם יחיד בעד עצמו לקופת גמל להשקעה". "קצבה מוכרת" is "חלק מקצבה המשולמת על ידי קופת גמל לקצבה שאינה קרן ותיקה, הנובע מתשלומים פטורים".
- Conditions / exceptions: Only sums the individual paid for himself. Deposits made by others into the person's account (for example National Insurance deposits into a child-savings kupat gemel lehashkaa) are not named (TXK-25). The recognized annuity must be paid by a provident fund for annuity that is not a veteran fund ("קרן ותיקה"). A kupat gemel lehashkaa does not pay annuities itself; see TXK-10.
- Figures: none.

### TXK-09 Recognized annuity is exempt from age 60
- Rule: "(ב1) הקצבה המוכרת שמקבל אחד מאלה פטורה ממס: (1) יחיד שמלאו לו 60 שנים; (2) יחיד שפרש פרישה מוקדמת כאמור בפסקה (2) להגדרה 'גיל פרישה'". The definition covers early retirement because of a stable disability of 75% or more.
- Conditions / exceptions: s.9A(b1) states no ceiling and no income test, unlike the qualifying-annuity exemption in s.9A(b) (`tax-pension-gemel-lekitzba.md`). The exemption covers the whole recognized annuity, principal and gains. NII and health contributions on such an annuity were not researched (open question 9).

### TXK-10 The route to the exemption: transfer to a provident fund for annuity from age 60

### TXK-11 Capitalizing a recognized annuity: 15% on the "relative profit component"
- Rule: "על סכום שהתקבל עקב היוון קצבה מוכרת, אשר אינו פטור ממס לפי פסקה (1), יוטל מס רק בשל מרכיב הרווח היחסי, בשיעור של 15%" (s.9A(e)(3)). ### TXK-12 The founder's age-60 example: what changes at 60 and what does not
- Rule (composite of TXK-01 to TXK-11; it adds no new law): The claim "a provident fund doesn't pay the 25% tax if you withdraw after 60" is true only for one route.
  - **Unchanged at 60:** A lump sum taken straight from the kupat gemel lehashkaa is still deemed interest and taxed at up to 25% (TXK-05, TXK-06; managers apply 25% on the real gain, TXK-07). Before and after 60, growth is untaxed and track switches and moves between kupot gemel lehashkaa are not taxed (TXK-03, TXK-04). Deposits never got a deduction (TXK-01).
  - **New at 60:** The balance may be moved to a provident fund for annuity, into an annuitants' track, for an annuity only (TXK-10). The person's own deposits are exempt payments (TXK-08), so the annuity they produce is a recognized annuity, fully exempt from 60 (TXK-09), principal and gains, with no ceiling in the statute. If such an annuity is capitalized (permission open), 15% applies to the nominal profit share (TXK-11).
  - **Not the general rule:** s.9(18ב) (five years, age 60, no apartment bought) covered only a closed 2016-2017 deposit window and its status is unresolved (TXK-15).
- Conditions / exceptions: The age-60 benefit means trading a lump sum for an annuity. The comparison with an ETF in a brokerage account therefore depends on whether the person wants an annuity. The exemption may be narrowed (TXK-21).
- Figures: 25% (lump sum), 0% (recognized annuity from 60), 15% (capitalized recognized annuity). 2026, fixed in law.

### TXK-22 Child savings: the provident track is a kupat gemel lehashkaa
- Rule: A parent may choose to have the savings held "בקופת גמל להשקעה" (s.74B(a)(2a), "74ב(א)(2א)"), once only. If no choice is made within six months of birth, the money goes to a kupat gemel lehashkaa by default (s.74B(d)(1a), "74ב(ד)(1א)"). After 18 the child may move between kupot gemel lehashkaa and tracks under the Provident Funds Law (regulations, reg. 11(b)). Before 18 a parent may move to another listed kupat gemel lehashkaa (reg. 7(a)).
- Conditions / exceptions: The bank alternative is a savings account at a bank; its tax treatment is not sourced (open question 1). Whether moves before or after 18 are tax-free is not stated in these texts. By analogy with TXK-04 they are not withdrawals, but this is unconfirmed for child savings.

### TXK-23 Child savings, provident track: the kupat gemel lehashkaa lump-sum rule appears to apply (inference)
- Rule (inference, labelled): The provident-track money sits in a kupat gemel lehashkaa (TXK-22). s.3(ה4)(3) taxes gains that any individual withdraws from a kupat gemel lehashkaa, with no condition on who deposited (TXK-05). Read together with the BTL statement (TXK-20), a child's withdrawal of gains would be deemed interest at up to 25% (TXK-06), with managers applying 25% on the real gain (TXK-07).
- Conditions / exceptions: No official text says this for child savings. Under D10 it should not compute until confirmed (open question 1). Age changes nothing in s.3(ה4)(3): a withdrawal at 18, at 21 or later is classified the same. The age-21 grant is added only if nothing was withdrawn before 21 (s.74C(b)). Money not withdrawn at 18 keeps being managed in the same fund or bank (reg. 11(a)). There is no tax event at 18 or 21 itself; tax arises on withdrawal (TXK-20). **[2026-10-22 browser pass 3: a dated press guide states the rates for both routes: kupat gemel lehashkaa 25% of the real gain; CPI-linked bank deposit 25% of the real gain; unlinked shekel bank deposit 15% of the nominal gain; "המס מנוכה במקור על ידי הקופה או הבנק". ### TXK-24 Child savings: early withdrawal and death

### TXK-25 Child savings: the annuity route, stated by NII but without a clear statutory basis
- Rule: National Insurance says that if the savings stay in the kupat gemel lehashkaa until retirement, the child can take them as an annuity after retirement without capital-gains tax ("הילד יוכל לבחור לקבל את כספי החיסכון לאחר הפרישה כקצבה").
- Conditions / exceptions: The recognized-annuity exemption rests on "exempt payments", defined as sums an individual paid "בעד עצמו" into a kupat gemel lehashkaa (TXK-08). National Insurance deposits and the parental addition are not paid by the child for himself, so the statutory route is unclear (open question 1). A deposit the adult child adds personally would fall under TXK-08. Whether such deposits are allowed is a product question (`child-savings.md`).

## Open questions

1. **Child savings (tax report Q4, partly closed).** Closed: deposits, the parental addition and grants are not income (TXK-19); gains are taxed on withdrawal (TXK-20); the provident track is a kupat gemel lehashkaa (TXK-22). Still open: **[2026-10-22 browser pass 3: rates for both routes now have a secondary source (see TXK-23); still no official text.]**
   - the rate and base on gains for the provident track (TXK-23 is an inference only) and for the bank track;
   - who withholds;
   - tax on medical-early and death payments;
   - the statutory basis for the annuity-route exemption that NII states (TXK-25).
   Tried: BTL FAQ pages, NII Law ch. D sign E and its 2015 enactment, the 2016 regulations, and the Ordinance text searched for "חיסכון ארוך טווח". Kol Zchut returned 403. Likely answer: a Tax Authority circular or ruling on child savings, the Ministry of Finance child-savings site, or the CMA.
2. **Inflation part of a kupat gemel lehashkaa withdrawal.** s.3(ה4)(3) separates linkage differentials. s.9(13) would exempt them, but the 2025 Tax Authority guide says its s.9(13) conditions "לא יחולו על חשבון בקופת גמל". Which provision exempts the CPI part, and whether 25% real (s.125C(b)) or 15% nominal (s.125C(c)) is the legal rate? Managers say 25% real. Likely in: a Tax Authority circular on Amendment 15 to the Provident Funds Law (2016), or the withholding regulations.
3. **Withholding regulation for kupat gemel lehashkaa withdrawals.** The Wikisource text of the 2005 withholding regulations shows 20% (15% for older deposits) for interest paid by a provident fund. That looks pre-2012 and not updated. The current regulation and rate were not found. Likely in: the current consolidated regulations (Nevo or the Tax Authority) or the withholding tables on gov.il.
4. **Capitalizing money moved from a kupat gemel lehashkaa to an annuity fund.** Reg. 2(ב)(2) says "לשם קבלת קצבה בלבד". Can it be capitalized, and if so does s.9A(e)(3) (15% of the relative profit) apply, and how does s.9A(e)(1) interact? Likely in: CMA guidance on transfers and fund bylaws. Kol Zchut "פטור ממס רווחי הון על קופת גמל להשקעה המשולמת כקצבה לאחר גיל 60" (https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/פטור_ממס_רווחי_הון_על_קופת_גמל_להשקעה_המשולמת_כקצבה_לאחר_גיל_60) returned 403.
5. **Death before transfer.** What tax applies when beneficiaries or heirs withdraw a kupat gemel lehashkaa balance as a lump sum, and does s.9A(f)(2) cover a balance still in the kupat gemel lehashkaa? Likely in: a Tax Authority circular, or fund bylaws (institution).
6. **Status of s.9(18ב) and Provident Funds Law s.22(א1)** after HCJ 10042/16 (Wikisource marks both repealed; Nevo shows text; the 2025 guide lists it as 9(18ג)). The decision itself was not opened: https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts/16/420/100/o23&fileName=16100420_o23.pdf&type=4. Low impact (closed window).
7. **IRA (tax report Q13, partly closed).** Closed: an IRA is a provident fund in law, and no IRA-specific provision exists in the Ordinance by text search. Open:
   - a CMA or Tax Authority statement confirming this;
   - foreign withholding on dividends and interest received by an Israeli provident fund or IRA (treaty relief for pension funds?);
   - any withholding the manager applies inside the account.
   The web search budget ran out during this session, so CMA circulars on IRA were not searched. Retry in a new session.
8. **s.9(2)(a) business-income exception** for an actively traded IRA: any ruling or circular?
9. **NII and health contributions** on a recognized annuity that came from a kupat gemel lehashkaa, and on the deemed interest from a lump sum (link to tax report Q10, `tax-general.md`).
10. **Losses.** If a kupat gemel lehashkaa is withdrawn at a loss (less than deposits), can the loss offset other interest or capital gains (s.92)? No source found.
11. **Ceiling source (tax report Q12, partly closed).** No deduction or credit on deposits is closed (TXK-01). The ceiling rule is primary (s.22(a)). The 2026 figure 83,641 comes from Harel and a Wikisource editor's note; a CMA publication was not opened (gov.il was not searched this session).
