## Summary

- **Savings policy.** In tax law the savings component of a life policy is a "savings plan" (`תכנית חיסכון`). Deposits get no credit or deduction (the 25% life-insurance credit excludes the savings component). Gains the individual receives are **interest income**, and the part equal to linkage on the payments is linkage income (s.3(ה5)). The insurer withholds: 25%, and a profit-sharing policy is withheld at 25% even when not CPI-linked. That this means "25% of the real gain" is stated only by secondary sources; the statute does not say whether a policy is a linked asset (25%, real) or an unlinked one (15%, nominal). Track switches are "not a tax event" in secondary sources only. Moving to another insurer: not found. On death, the death-risk amount is exempt; the savings component is not.
- **Executive insurance** is an insurance plan approved as a provident fund (`קופת ביטוח`). Its annuity type is a provident fund for annuity, so the TXP rules apply. An insurance fund is never an "old fund", so the recognized annuity and commutation are open to it. Unlike other funds, the Ordinance's fund-level income exemption excludes insurance funds; how growth inside executive insurance is taxed was not found.
- **Amendment 190** (in force 1.1.2012). Money deposited without a tax benefit ("exempt payments") produces a "recognized annuity", fully exempt from age 60. If commuted, only the "relative profit component" is taxed, at **15%, nominal** (s.9A(e)(3)): this closes the rate part of tax report Q6. Commutation needs the remaining annuity, counting all annuities, to be at least the minimum annuity (5,306 NIS/month, 2026). Funds add: age 60, three annuity payslips, and age-based ceilings (11,951,492 NIS at 60 or under in 2026) for withdrawal without a Tax Authority approval. These are the conditions of the Tax Authority's ruling 6849/15 for withholding without an approval (TXA-30; found 2026-10-26).

## Rules

### Savings policies (`פוליסת חיסכון`)

### TXA-01 The savings component of a life policy is a "savings plan" in tax law
- Rule: s.1 defines "תכנית חיסכון" as a savings plan at a banking corporation, "וכן מרכיב החיסכון בפוליסה לביטוח חיים", "או תכנית חיסכון שצורפה לפוליסה כאמור ושאושרה על ידי הממונה" (the Commissioner under the Insurance Supervision Law). Every Ordinance rule that names a "savings plan" therefore reaches the savings component of a life-insurance policy unless it says otherwise.
- Conditions / exceptions: "מרכיב החיסכון" itself is not defined in s.1 or s.3 (as far as the fetch tool could see). Whether every product sold as "פוליסת חיסכון" is "the savings component of a life policy" is a product question (open question 1).

### TXA-02 No credit or deduction on deposits to the savings component
- Rule: The 25% credit for life insurance (s.45A(a)(1)) is for premiums an Israeli resident pays "לביטוח חייו או חיי בן זוגו בחברת ביטוח", and s.45A(c) defines life insurance as death-risk cover "בלא מרכיב החיסכון". The 2025 guide: the 25% credit is given "בעד תשלומי פרמיות (ללא התשלום עבור מרכיב החיסכון)". The 35% credit and the s.47 deduction are for payments to a provident fund for annuity (TXP-01), which a savings policy is not. No provision giving a benefit on savings-policy deposits was found.
- Conditions / exceptions: The risk part of a policy, if any, can earn the 25% credit (TXA-10). The "not found" for the savings part rests on the Ordinance sweep in the method note.
- Figures: 25% (fixed in law).

### TXA-03 Gains received from a policy savings plan are interest income; linkage on the payments is linkage income
- Rule: s.3(ה5): "רווחים שקיבל יחיד מתכנית חיסכון בפוליסת ביטוח חיים, יראו אותם כהכנסה מריבית", but gains equal to "הפרשי הצמדה שחושבו על תשלומים ששולמו לתכנית החיסכון" are "הכנסה מהפרשי הצמדה". So the tax is on gains when the individual receives them, not while they build up (tax report 3.3 secondary claim "on withdrawal" is consistent with this; TXI-07 says the same for savings plans in general).
- Conditions / exceptions: s.3(ה5) names no index and has no pro-rata rule for partial withdrawals (unlike s.3(ה4)(3) for provident funds, TXK-13) and no death rule (open questions 4, 5). Linkage differentials are exempt under s.9(13) when its conditions hold (TXI-05). The 2005 sentence that kept provident-fund accounts out of s.9(13) never reached a policy, which is not a provident fund, and Amendment 190 deleted it from 1.1.2012 (TXK-07); the 2025 guide's s.9(13) row still prints it and is not cited. The 2005 bill's notes state the mechanism for policy gains (TXA-28). Amendment tags on s.3(ה5): תשס"ג (the first half, 2002), תשס"ה (the linkage clause, Amendment 147 s.2(4)). *2026-10-25 tax-bases research:* re-sourced to the Tax Authority's consolidated text.

### TXA-04 The insurer withholds; a profit-sharing policy is withheld at 25% even if not linked
- Rule: A payer other than a bank that pays interest to an individual withholds 25%; on an asset not linked to the index it withholds 15%, "למעט על תכנית חיסכון בפוליסה משתתפת ברווחים" (reg. 5(a)). "פוליסה משתתפת ברווחים" is "תכנית חיסכון בפוליסת ביטוח חיים שאישר המפקח על הביטוח כפוליסה משתתפת ברווחים, או המצורפת לפוליסה כאמור" (reg. 1). The withholding base "ריבית" excludes linkage differentials exempt by law but includes partial linkage differentials (reg. 1).
- Conditions / exceptions: Insurance companies are on the list of recipients not withheld from (addendum item 9); this matters only for corporate holders. Whether today's savings policies are approved as profit-sharing policies was not confirmed (open question 1). The text read is consolidated to the amendment of 27.12.2017 (TXI open question 18). Annual certificate form 0867 and crediting of withheld tax: TXI-08.
- Figures: 25% / 15%; in force from 1.1.2006.
- Source: [Income Tax (Withholding from Interest, Dividends and Certain Profits) Regulations 2005, consolidated copy on btl.gov.il](https://www.btl.gov.il/Laws1/02_0103_100269.pdf), regs. 1, 5(a), addendum · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: `reporting-obligation`, `tax-at-exit`
- Likely to change: no
- *2026-10-24 R5 pass:* reg. 1 re-read on the Wikisource raw text: "נכס שאינו צמוד למדד" – "לרבות נכס שצמוד בחלקו לשיעור עליית המדד, כולו או חלקו, או שאינו צמוד למדד עד לפדיון או עד להחזר; לענין זה, "מדד" – כהגדרתו בסעיף 88 לפקודה"; "פוליסה משתתפת ברווחים" – "תכנית חיסכון בפוליסת ביטוח חיים שאישר המפקח על הביטוח כפוליסה משתתפת ברווחים, או המצורפת לפוליסה כאמור". ### TXA-05 Rate and base on policy gains: the base is the real gain by law; up to 25%, with 15% open only for a policy that is not profit-sharing
- Rule: Interest of an individual is taxed at up to 25%, and at 15% when paid on an asset "שאינו צמוד למדד או שהוא צמוד בחלקו" (s.125C(b), (c)(1)); s.125C has no exception for policies. The 2025 guide says a deposit or savings plan counts as "not linked" unless fully linked, and "ריבית ורווחים שמקורם בפיקדון או בתוכנית חיסכון 'צמודים למדד', הרווח עליהם מעל עליית המדד ממוסה במס בשיעור שלא יעלה על 25%". The base of a policy's gains is the gain above linkage on the payments (s.3(ה5) with s.9(13); TXA-28). No official text says in words whether s.125C(c)(1)'s 15% can reach a policy that is not profit-sharing.
- Conditions / exceptions: **2026-10-25 tax-bases research:** the base is settled: s.3(ה5) carves the linkage on the payments out as linkage income, exempt under s.9(13), and the 2005 bill's notes say so for policy gains (TXA-28). Two texts point to 25%: withholding reg. 5(א) withholds 25% on a profit-sharing policy even when it is not linked (TXA-04), and before 2006 the Ordinance itself excluded profit-sharing policies from the lower rate for unlinked plans (the old s.125C(c), quoted in the Amendment 147 bill's notes, p.958). Whether s.125C(c)(1)'s 15% can reach a policy that is not profit-sharing is a reading question only (open question 1, narrowed to the rate), and whether today's savings policies are approved as profit-sharing was not found. The investment house's article is no longer cited. Before: under D10 the 25%-real figure computed only labelled "secondary source", and the 15%-nominal alternative stayed open (open question 1). The withholding exception in TXA-04 points to 25% for profit-sharing policies but is a withholding rule, not the final rate. People aged 60 or more: TXI-19. Surtax: `tax-general.md` TXG-09, TXG-10.
- Figures: 25% / 15% (fixed in law; the Minister may change the 15% by order, s.125C(c)(2)).
- Source: Ordinance s.125C(b), (c) (link above) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (mirror). Guide 2025 printed p.40, "אופן הצמדה" and the two paragraphs after it · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. ### TXA-06 Older policies: which deposits the regulations cover, and the pre-2012 rates
- Rule: Reg. 20(a)(4): for savings plans in a life policy, on interest and gains from deposits made "ביום כ"ו בכסלו התשס"ג (1 בדצמבר 2002) ואילך", the payer withholds 15% on the "interest part" ("חלק הריבית") and 20% on the "additional interest part" ("חלק הריבית הנוסף"), instead of reg. 5. The parts are a linear split of the interest by period (reg. 20(b), (c)): the part accrued before the regulations' start (1.1.2006) and the part from then until the day before the "change date". The guide gives the same eras for savings plans opened before 1.1.2012: from 1.1.2012, 25% or 15%; 2006-2011, 20% or 15%; before 2006, a further split.
- Conditions / exceptions: Gains on policy deposits made **before 1.12.2002** are not covered by reg. 20(a)(4); their tax was not found (open question 2). "מועד השינוי" is defined by reference to s.88 (reg. 1); its date was not re-read here (the guide's era boundary is 1.1.2012). For new money this rule does not apply.

### TXA-08 Switching tracks inside a policy; moving to another insurer

### TXA-10 Life-insurance (risk) premiums: 25% credit
- Rule: A resident individual gets a 25% credit on premiums he or his spouse pays for life insurance on his or the spouse's life with an insurer, excluding the savings component (TXA-02). A beneficiary member may also claim it for a child aged 18 or over (s.45A(b1)).

### TXA-19 Commuting a recognized annuity: 15% on the relative profit component, nominal (closes the rate in Q6)
- Rule: "על סכום שהתקבל עקב היוון קצבה מוכרת, אשר אינו פטור ממס לפי פסקה (1), יוטל מס רק בשל מרכיב הרווח היחסי, בשיעור של 15%" (s.9A(e)(3)). Relative profit component = commuted amount × (accumulated balance − exempt payments) ÷ accumulated balance. "היתרה הצבורה" is the money credited in the person's account in a provident fund for annuity that is not an old fund, "עד למועד חישוב רווחי הקופה שקדם לגיל הזכאות של היחיד, והנובעים מתשלומים פטורים". - Conditions / exceptions: Nominal: the formula has no inflation adjustment (TXK-11); funds call it "15% ממרכיב הרווח הנומינלי". The s.9A(e)(1) exempt commutation and (e)(3) work together as circular 2/2013 s.5.2.5 says: the exempt-commutation ceiling covers all commutations, of a qualifying and of a recognized annuity; an exempt commutation of a recognized annuity reduces the balance left for exempt commutation by the whole amount commuted; above the ceiling a recognized-annuity commutation pays 15% of the profit component (open question 11, closed 2026-10-26). It is not income from a capital source for the further 2% (s.121ב(א1)) when the annuity is one of paras (1) to (4) of "הכנסה מיגיעה אישית" in s.1, which counts "סכום המתקבל עקב היוון קיצבה" of such an annuity as personal-exertion income; para (2) is an annuity from a provident fund for annuity "בשל עבודה, או מכוח חברות בה במשך חמש שנים לפחות". ### TXA-22 How funds run the 15% withdrawal (fund conditions and withholding)
- Rule: The conditions under which a fund withholds 15% of the nominal profit component on commuting a recognized annuity without an assessing officer's approval are the Tax Authority's ruling 6849/15 (TXA-30). Fund documents state them for withdrawing Amendment 190 money "בדרך של היוון קצבה מוכרת", all at the withdrawal date: age 60 or more; receiving an annuity above the minimum annuity; showing three payslips in the member's name proving it; the exempt-payment balance at the deposit date was within the ceiling (TXA-24); a declaration about exempt payments in other funds. Altshuler: the fund withholds "וללא צורך בהצגת אישור מרשות המיסים לקביעת שיעור ניכוי המס"; for amounts up to the benefited ceiling (TXA-23) and their gains, an approval from the Tax Authority addressed to the fund is needed. If a condition fails, the withdrawal is non-compliant and taxed accordingly (Analyst).

### TXA-28 Savings-policy gains: taxed when received, on the real gain; a track switch inside the insurer is not taxed, moving insurer is a taxable withdrawal
- Rule: "רווחים שקיבל יחיד מתכנית חיסכון בפוליסת ביטוח חיים, יראו אותם כהכנסה מריבית, ואולם רווחים שקיבל כאמור בסכום הפרשי הצמדה שחושבו על תשלומים ששולמו לתכנית החיסכון, יראו אותם כהכנסה מהפרשי הצמדה" (Ordinance s.3(ה5); TXA-03). The linkage part is exempt under s.9(13), on its three conditions (not partial linkage differentials, no interest or linkage expense claimed, not business income); the 2005 sentence that kept provident-fund accounts out of s.9(13) never reached a policy, which is not a provident fund, and was deleted from 1.1.2012 in any case (TXK-07). The 2005 bill's notes say it for policy gains in particular: "ורווחים שקיבל מתכנית חיסכון בפוליסת ביטוח חיים, רואים אותם כריבית. מרכיב הפרשי ההצמדה ברווחים אלה זכאי לפטור ממס בידי יחיד, לפי סעיף 9(13) לפקודה". So tax falls only when the individual receives gains, and only on the gain above linkage on the payments. The Ministry of Finance team on short- and medium-term savings instruments (February 2025; its members include the Tax Authority's director) describes current law: on withdrawal the policy's gains are taxed as interest income; "מעבר בין מסלולי השקעה שונים בתוך חברת הביטוח לא נחשבת "אירוע מס""; moving to another insurer can be done only by withdrawing and cashing the money and is a taxable event.
- Conditions / exceptions: The rate is up to 25% as the top slice (s.125C(b)); the insurer withholds 25% on a profit-sharing policy even when it is not linked (withholding regulations 2005 reg. 5(א), TXA-04). Whether s.125C(c)(1)'s 15% can reach a policy that is not profit-sharing stays open (open question 1, narrowed to the rate; the base is settled), and whether today's savings policies are approved as profit-sharing policies was not found. The s.125D reliefs reach policy gains (a reading of two definitions, TXA-07); the retirement-age relief for people who were at least 55 on 1.1.2003 is TXI-21; the statutory s.125D amounts are TXI-20 and TXI-21. The payer issues the withholding certificate (TXI-08). The team report is an official description of current law, not law. What insurers state on top of this (The Phoenix's year-end notional redemption, its March certificate, the s.125D figures it prints) is TXA-29. Split on 2026-10-25: the companies' part is TXA-29.

### TXA-30 The Tax Authority's ruling 6849/15: when a fund withholds 15% on commuting a recognized annuity without an approval (age 60, minimum annuity and three payslips, age ceilings, the benefited layer)
- Rule: The Tax Authority's professional division published ruling 6849/15, "הסדר ניכוי מס במקור בעת היוון קצבה מוכרת – החלטת מיסוי בהסכם" (5.5.2015). A managing company may withhold tax under reg. 6(י) of the Income Tax (Deduction from Salary and Wages) Regulations 1993 when it commutes a recognized annuity, "וללא צורך באישור פקיד השומה", provided all of these hold (facts 1.1 to 1.7, s.3): the company marks as exempt payments the amounts deposited after 1.1.2012 that are employer deposits above the s.3(ה3) ceilings, and employee and self-employed deposits "העולות על התקרות המרביות למתן הטבות מס לפי סעיפים 45א ו- 47 לפקודה (ללא תלות בחבות המס)"; it keeps recognized-annuity and qualifying-annuity money apart; the member is 60 or older; the member receives the minimum annuity and has shown three payslips; and the member's exempt payments, together with the recognized-annuity balances in all accounts (other managers included) and amounts already drawn as or commuted from a recognized annuity, do not exceed the ceiling: up to age 60, the sum needed for an annuity of 4 times the average wage (as defined in s.3(ה3)) from age 60; up to 67, for an annuity of 4 times the average wage from the deposit date; after 67, 5 times, "והכל על פי טבלת מקדמים שנקבעה לעניין זה בלבד" (s.3.5). Balances at other funds are checked on the member's declaration or a clearing-house report (fact 1.7).
- Conditions / exceptions: The factor table is the same for men and women and settles nothing outside this arrangement (s.3.1). Depositing above the ceiling is allowed, but a commutation of such money is examined by the assessing officer (s.3.4); the assessing officer may approve marking other sums as exempt payments (s.3.2); the legislator or the Capital Market Division may set stricter ceilings (s.3.3). Not binding: it is a ruling made in agreement with one unnamed managing company and binds no one else; the Tax Authority's summary search says the public can expect it to act the same way in similar circumstances, "כך שמוצגת למעשה עמדתה הרשמית של רשות המסים בסוגיות העקרוניות המתוארות". The funds' forms and tables apply it: their conditions are TXA-22, their benefited layer TXA-23, and their 2026 ceilings in TXA-24 equal these factors times the 2026 average wage (4 × 13,769 × 217 = 11,951,492 at 60; 5 × 13,769 × 180 = 12,392,100 at 67, computed). The tax itself (15% of the relative profit component) is s.9A(e)(3) (TXA-19). The page opens from the Tax Authority's summary search (https://www.misim.gov.il/tmmisuyweb/frmAbsSearch.aspx?cur=4, area "קופות גמל ומיסוי יחיד", subject "קצבה מוכרת"), which sets the session the link needs. Closes open questions 9 and 10 (2026-10-26).
- Figures: multipliers 4 (to age 67) and 5 (after 67) times the average wage, 13,769 NIS a month in 2026 (TXP-03). Factors by age at deposit (s.3.5): 60 or under 217; 61 212; 62 207; 63 202; 64 197; 65 191; 66 186; 67 180; 68 172; 69 167; 70 162; 71 156; 72 151; 73 146; 74 140; 75 134; 76 129; 77 123; 78 117; 79 112; 80 106; 81 100; 82 94; 83 89; 84 83; 85 79; 86 73; 87 68; 88 64; 89 60; 90 56; 91 51; 92 48; 93 45; 94 43; 95 40; 96 38; 97 37; 98 35; 99 33. The factors are fixed in the ruling; the ceilings move with the average wage.
