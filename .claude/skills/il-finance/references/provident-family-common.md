## Summary

The caps on what a manager may charge are in one regulation, the Provident Funds (Management Fees) Regulations 2012. The caps are 1.05% a year of the balance plus 4% of each deposit for provident funds, which by the regulation's own exclusion list includes kupat gemel lehashkaa. For a comprehensive pension fund the caps are 0.5% plus 6%, for a general pension fund 1.05% plus 4%, and for keren hishtalmut and IRA 2% of the balance, with no deposit-fee item. The caps apply per track. On top of the management fee the fund pays a closed list of direct investment-management expenses (הוצאות ניהול השקעות). For the largest of these, external management, each manager publishes a yearly cap per track. Any discount below the cap must last at least five years under the CMA circular, which covers kupat gemel lehashkaa too; the two years in some gemel lehashkaa bylaws is the replaced circular's (PF-13, KGL-30). A manager can raise the fee earlier in listed cases, such as six months after deposits stop or after a transfer out, and must send two months' notice in a set format. A saver may move money between funds of the same kind. The transferring fund has 10 business days and pays late-transfer compensation out of its own fees. The law lists no transfer fee among the permitted charges. A keren hishtalmut cannot be transferred in part. A saver can deal directly with the manager without an agent. Fund assets are held in trust, separately from the manager's own property, and cannot be attached.

## Rules

### PF-01 Only a closed list of charges may come out of a provident-family account
- Rule: A managing company may charge the fund's assets, members' accounts or deposits only for (1) management fees, (2) direct expenses of transactions in fund assets "כפי שקבע השר", and (3) insurance premiums under section 16(ד)(6). It and its related parties may receive no other benefit in connection with managing the fund.
- Conditions / exceptions: A sector fund (קופת גמל ענפית) charges management fees according to the expenses it actually incurred (s. 32(ג)). The Minister sets the maximum amounts or rates, with Finance Committee approval (s. 32(ב)); see PF-02 to PF-08. Reading, not a quoted rule: the list contains no transfer fee, exit fee or track-switch fee. No regulation found authorises one (PF-19, PF-23).

### PF-02 Cap for provident funds (regulation 2): 1.05% a year of balance and 4% of deposits
- Rule: A managing company of a "קופת גמל" may charge up to 1.05% a year of each member's accumulated balance ("מן היתרה הצבורה בחשבונו של כל עמית בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1.05% לשנה") and up to 4% of the payments transferred for each member ("מתוך התשלומים המועברים אליה בשל כל עמית – בשיעור שלא יעלה על 4%").
- Conditions / exceptions: For regulation 2, "קופת גמל" excludes these fund types, which have their own caps (PF-04 to PF-06): guaranteed-return insurance and provident funds, old pension funds (קרן ותיקה), new comprehensive and new general pension funds, keren hishtalmut, IRA (קופת גמל בניהול אישי), central funds, sector funds, sick-pay, holiday and other-purpose funds (reg. 2(ב)). Some members have money in an account at a non-insurance provident fund that came from employee or employer contributions for tax years before 2008. For them the fund may charge the higher of the standard caps above or, instead, up to NIS 6 a month on the member's total balance across all their accounts in that fund (reg. 2(א)(2א)). Annuitants in the payout phase have lower caps: the Wikisource text was read only in summary, with 0.6% a year and 0.3% for old-age annuities from 1 Feb 2022, and the exact wording was not quoted. That phase belongs in `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`.
- Figures: 1.05% a year of balance; 4% of each deposit. In force since 2014 (a transitional 1.1% cap on balance applied until 31 Dec 2013, reg. 7). Fixed in the regulation. The NIS 6 a month alternative is CPI-linked, updated each year (reg. 10א). The current amount was not read.

### PF-04 Cap for a new comprehensive pension fund: 0.5% a year of balance and 6% of deposits

### PF-08 Lower fee cap on lost-contact and deceased members' accounts
- Rule: For a member whose contact was lost (one year after they became one) or who died, where the manager did not complete the search steps required by the locating regulations within the required period, the fee may not exceed 0.3% a year of the balance or the rate last charged, whichever is lower (reg. 8(א)).

### PF-10 Direct investment-management expenses are charged on top of the management fee, from a closed list
- Rule: Direct expenses of transactions in an institutional investor's assets "יהיו אלה בלבד": securities trading commissions; custody fees; costs of non-traded investments and loans and of real-estate rights, paid to non-related parties; taxes; reinsurance for certain pension funds; litigation costs over investments; external management fees (עמלת ניהול חיצוני, defined by six situations such as investing in unrelated funds or paying foreign or licensed portfolio managers); and mortgage-origination costs within limits set by the Commissioner (reg. 2(א)).

### PF-11 External management fee cap: each manager sets it per track per year and must publish it
- Rule: Each institutional investor sets, each year, a maximum external-management-fee rate per fund or track ("מגבלת עמלת ניהול חיצוני"). It is calculated on estimated total assets at the end of the prior year, excludes variable fees, and is stated on an annual basis. ### PF-15 Changing employer: the better group arrangement applies
- Rule: If a member leaves a workplace that has a fee arrangement with an institutional body (the "previous workplace arrangement") and starts at a workplace that has a different arrangement with the same body, the more favourable arrangement ("הסדר דמי ניהול מיטיב") applies. ### PF-16 Notice before a fee increase, and notice of a discount
- Rule (increase): A body that wants to raise the fee sends the member, and any licensee giving them ongoing advice or marketing, a notice in a separate document ("הודעה במסמך נפרד"). The increase takes effect at the end of two months from sending, or at a later date stated in the notice. The notice may not be sent more than four months before the increase (s. 4(א)). It uses the appendix-A template: a table of current and new rates "מהפקדה" and "מחיסכון", and the shekel amount charged over the past year against what the new rate would have charged. It must tell the member they can compare offers with the CMA "מחשבון דמי ניהול" (s. 4(ג), appendix A). If the body holds the member's email, the notice goes by email with the subject line "לתשומת לבך – דמי הניהול בחיסכון הפנסיוני שלך ב___ משתנים". 30 days before the increase, an SMS goes to the member's mobile (appendix B) (s. 4(ג), (ד)).
- Rule (discount): When a discount is given, the body shows it in the member's personal online account as soon as possible. The display includes the new rate, the start and end of the discount period, and the cases in which the fee may be raised. If the member's email is known, a copy is emailed within 10 business days (s. 3א).
- Conditions / exceptions: The gemel lehashkaa bylaws repeat the two-month and four-month rule (s. 37.1, 37.2) and add the exception in PF-14.
- Figures: 2 months; not more than 4 months ahead; SMS 30 days ahead; discount email within 10 business days.

### PF-17 A member may transfer money between provident funds; which routes are allowed
- Rule: Withdrawal and transfer from a provident fund are allowed only as the law lists ("תהיה כמפורט להלן, בלבד"), including transfer by the member of money in their account (law s. 23(א)(2)). The Minister sets the conditions (s. 23(ב)). The Transfer Regulations list the permitted routes. Into an annuity fund (reg. 2(א)): from another annuity fund; from a tagmulim fund or a personal severance fund, if the receiving fund pays annuities; from a keren hishtalmut, if the member could withdraw it tax-exempt; from a gemel lehashkaa, "ובלבד שהעמית הגיע לגיל הפרישה המוקדמת". ### PF-20 Transfer process: the request goes to the receiving fund; the member can cancel until the money moves
- Rule: The member signs a transfer request with the receiving fund. The receiving fund checks eligibility and forwards the request to the transferring fund electronically within two business days (circular 2017-9-17 s. 3(א) to (ו)). If the transferring fund cannot transfer, it notifies the receiving fund within three business days with the reasons from a set list (s. 3(ז)). The member may cancel by signing a cancellation notice before the transfer date. The form states "בקשת העברה זו ניתנת לביטול ובלבד שהכספים טרם הועברו" (s. 3(ט); appendix A1). Each fund sends the member a confirmation of the transfer within 15 business days (s. 6). If an active member's request is not transferred within 120 days of signing it lapses, unless the member stated that no further deposits will be made (s. 3(יב), 3(יג)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Insurance-cover continuity rules apply on transfer into a new pension fund (s. 4) and belong in `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`. The steps for the playbook belong in `switching-and-transfers.md`.
- Figures: 2, 3 and 15 business days; 120 and 60 days.

### PF-23 Track switching: within three business days; no fee or frequency limit in the regulation
- Rule: Money moves from one investment track to another in the same fund "בתוך שלושה ימי עסקים, אלא אם כן נקבעה בתקנון הקופה תקופה קצרה יותר" (Income Tax Regulations (Rules for Approval and Management of Provident Funds), reg. 33ב(ב)). ### PF-27 Lost contact and dormant accounts: definitions and the manager's search duty
- Rule: A member is "עמית שהקשר עמו נותק" if two periodic reports sent by post (not email) were returned within three years ("דואר חוזר"), or if their account is a dormant account ("חשבון רדום"). For a non-pension account, an account is dormant when no instruction was given for 10 years, or 5 years if the manager knows the money is withdrawable, whichever is earlier; pension accounts follow rules tied to retirement age. The manager must check identity details against the Population Registry, and for lost-contact members act "בשקידה סבירה" to locate them through contacts, employer and data sources within one year, repeating every three years. For dormant accounts it repeats the search only if the balance exceeds NIS 5,000 (Locating Regulations, s. 1 to 3). Beneficiaries of deceased members are searched on a set timetable (s. 4).
- Conditions / exceptions: The law empowers the Commissioner to set search duties (law s. 24). A lower fee cap applies where search duties were not met (PF-08). No public lookup database is required by these regulations. Whether Har HaKesef (the CMA's unclaimed-money lookup) is fed by them was not verified (open question 7).
- Figures: NIS 5,000, CPI-linked each 1 March since 2014; 10 years and 5 years; 3 years.

### PF-28 Pension: inactive accounts are merged into the new fund when a member joins, unless they object
- Rule: When a member joins a pension fund, the receiving fund may check, using ID and date of birth, whether money is registered in their name in other pension funds. ### PF-30 Merger, split and change of manager need CMA approval and advance notice; accounts are not merged
- Rule: A managing company may merge provident funds only if the Commissioner approved the plan in advance and members were notified in advance. The merger does not merge members' accounts ("אין במיזוג כאמור… כדי להביא למיזוג של חשבונות עמיתים") (law s. 37). The same protection applies to a split (s. 38). A voluntary transfer of management to another company also needs prior approval and advance notice to members (s. 41). If a receiver or liquidator is appointed to the managing company and not removed within 60 days, it must transfer management of its funds to another managing company (s. 42).
- Conditions / exceptions: The law gives members no specific right to object to a merger, and no fee condition attached to one, in the text read. A member can still transfer out under PF-17. A merger requires the external-management cap to be published within 30 days of approval (PF-11).

### PF-35 How the statement's fee figures are defined: the rates, the shekel line, the fund average and the direct-expense ratio
- Rule (deposit-fee rate): "דמי ניהול מהפקדה – יש להציג את שיעור דמי הניהול מהפקדה שוטפת שנגבו בפועל מהחשבון בתקופת הדוח", shown as "0%" when none is charged, "למעט במקרים בהם לא ניתן לגבות דמי ניהול מהפקדה כגון: קופה בניהול אישי, קרן השתלמות, קופה לפי הוצאות בפועל"; computed as D/H, D the deposit fees actually charged in the period and H the deposits actually paid in it (sign ב, ג.1).
- Rule (balance-fee rate): the rate actually charged in the year, on an annual basis, D = ∏(1 + P_t) − 1 over the T days (daily charging) or 12 months (monthly charging), where P_t is the rate charged on day or month t; for an account in several tracks it is the weighted average across the tracks, with the note "דמי ניהול משוקללים בכל המסלולים בחשבונך" (ג.2). A portfolio-manager fee in an IRA is a separate line and is not part of this rate (ג.3).
- Rule (shekel line): "דמי ניהול שנגבו בשנה זו" is the total actually charged from the account, one line, identical to the "סך דמי ניהול ששולמו" total of the detailed report's table ב.5 (ב.7).
- Rule (fund average): the balloon "לידיעתך, ממוצע דמי ניהול בקופה: מהפקדה ____% מחיסכון ____%" is filled with "שיעור דמי הניהול (מחיסכון ומהפקדה, בהתאמה) הממוצעים שגבתה הקופה בפועל מכלל העמיתים בקופת הגמל" (ג.5): the same definition as the Gemel-Net field (CAT-37). The detailed report adds the average per track (PF-36).
- Rule (direct expenses): "הוצאות ניהול השקעות" is the weighted average over the member's tracks at year end of each track's ratio: direct expenses under reg. 2א sub-items (1) to (4) and (8) to (10) of the 2008 direct-expenses regulations, divided by the average of the track's asset value at the end of the previous year and at the end of the report year (a track approved during the year: its first quarter-end value instead of the previous year end) (ג.4, with a worked example). Returns in table ד are after these expenses and before management fees.
- Rule (quarterly): the quarterly report shows the fee rates "על בסיס שנתי" with the note "דמי הניהול מוצגים על בסיס שנתי", and omits the "הוצאות ניהול השקעות" line (chapter 3, s. 2(ד)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Draft 2025-26 renames table ג "אחוז דמי ניהול והוצאות בחשבון בשנת XXXX ועלות שנתית צפויה לשנת XXXX+1", adds the line "עלות שנתית צפויה*" computed under circular 2023-9-6 with the footnote "העלות השנתית הצפויה מהווה תחזית משוערת בלבד" (the full notice is attached to the detailed report only), and adds it to the quarterly report too (draft pp. 38 to 40, 158). - `fee-direct-expenses`: investment-management expenses charged to the fund on top of the management fee (PF-10, PF-11). It fits the `fee-<type>` pattern but should be listed so 3.5 treats it as its own line.

## Left out and why

- Tax treatment of transfers and track switches (for example, Phoenix states a transfer "אינו אירוע מס"): tax files own it.
- Insurance-cover continuity on transfer into a pension fund (Transfer Regs reg. 8; circular 2017-9-17 s. 4): pension-specific, belongs to `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`.
- Withdrawal deadlines, deposit ceilings and payment methods (for example, gemel lehashkaa withdrawals within four business days, bylaws s. 12.1; the s. 22 deposit ceiling): wrapper files.
- Beneficiary designation and inheritance (bylaws s. 22 to 26): changes no saving decision in scope.
- Savings-policy fee caps (domain open question 8, second half): `savings-policies-and-executive-insurance.md`. Lead: reg. 2(ב) excludes only guaranteed-return insurance funds, so insurance provident funds (קופות ביטוח) appear to fall under reg. 2.
- Distribution-fee caps for advisers (s. 28(ג) points to law s. 32 rules not read): matters only if the site ever discusses advisers, which it does not recommend.
- Old pension funds (קרנות ותיקות), sector funds, central severance funds and other-purpose funds: not choosable by an individual (domain report section 7).
- Secondary sources seen in search (hon.co.il summary of a Harel report, Kol Zchut, Ynet, comparison sites): not used for any rule. Kol Zchut returned 403.
