## Summary

The caps on what a manager may charge are in one regulation, the Provident Funds (Management Fees) Regulations 2012. The caps are 1.05% a year of the balance plus 4% of each deposit for provident funds, which by the regulation's own exclusion list includes kupat gemel lehashkaa. For a comprehensive pension fund the caps are 0.5% plus 6%, for a general pension fund 1.05% plus 4%, and for keren hishtalmut and IRA 2% of the balance, with no deposit-fee item. The caps apply per track. On top of the management fee the fund pays a closed list of direct investment-management expenses (הוצאות ניהול השקעות). For the largest of these, external management, each manager publishes a yearly cap per track. Any discount below the cap must last for a minimum period, which the CMA circular sets at five years but one gemel lehashkaa bylaw sets at two (open question 2). A manager can raise the fee earlier in listed cases, such as six months after deposits stop or after a transfer out, and must send two months' notice in a set format. A saver may move money between funds of the same kind. The transferring fund has 10 business days and pays late-transfer compensation out of its own fees. The law lists no transfer fee among the permitted charges. A keren hishtalmut cannot be transferred in part. A saver can deal directly with the manager without an agent. Fund assets are held in trust, separately from the manager's own property, and cannot be attached.

## Rules

### PF-01 Only a closed list of charges may come out of a provident-family account
- Rule: A managing company may charge the fund's assets, members' accounts or deposits only for (1) management fees, (2) direct expenses of transactions in fund assets "כפי שקבע השר", and (3) insurance premiums under section 16(ד)(6). It and its related parties may receive no other benefit in connection with managing the fund.
- Conditions / exceptions: A sector fund (קופת גמל ענפית) charges management fees according to the expenses it actually incurred (s. 32(ג)). The Minister sets the maximum amounts or rates, with Finance Committee approval (s. 32(ב)); see PF-02 to PF-08. Reading, not a quoted rule: the list contains no transfer fee, exit fee or track-switch fee. No regulation found authorises one (PF-19, PF-23).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Supervision of Financial Services (Provident Funds) Law 2005, s. 32 "הוצאות, דמי ניהול ועמלת הפצה" (Hebrew Wikisource mirror)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (law text read through a mirror)
- Dimensions: `fee-management-balance`, `fee-deposit`, `fee-exit`, `regulator`
- Likely to change: no

### PF-02 Cap for provident funds (regulation 2): 1.05% a year of balance and 4% of deposits
- Rule: A managing company of a "קופת גמל" may charge up to 1.05% a year of each member's accumulated balance ("מן היתרה הצבורה בחשבונו של כל עמית בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1.05% לשנה") and up to 4% of the payments transferred for each member ("מתוך התשלומים המועברים אליה בשל כל עמית – בשיעור שלא יעלה על 4%").
- Conditions / exceptions: For regulation 2, "קופת גמל" excludes these fund types, which have their own caps (PF-04 to PF-06): guaranteed-return insurance and provident funds, old pension funds (קרן ותיקה), new comprehensive and new general pension funds, keren hishtalmut, IRA (קופת גמל בניהול אישי), central funds, sector funds, sick-pay, holiday and other-purpose funds (reg. 2(ב)). Some members have money in an account at a non-insurance provident fund that came from employee or employer contributions for tax years before 2008. For them the fund may charge the higher of the standard caps above or, instead, up to NIS 6 a month on the member's total balance across all their accounts in that fund (reg. 2(א)(2א)). Annuitants in the payout phase have lower caps: the Wikisource text was read only in summary, with 0.6% a year and 0.3% for old-age annuities from 1 Feb 2022, and the exact wording was not quoted. That phase belongs in `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`.
- Figures: 1.05% a year of balance; 4% of each deposit. In force since 2014 (a transitional 1.1% cap on balance applied until 31 Dec 2013, reg. 7). Fixed in the regulation. The NIS 6 a month alternative is CPI-linked, updated each year (reg. 10א). The current amount was not read.
- Source: [Provident Funds (Management Fees) Regulations 2012, reg. 2(א)(1), (2), (2א), 2(ב), 7, 10א (Hebrew Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(דמי_ניהול)); cross-read on [Nevo mirror](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/500_714.htm), which shows the last amendment as ק"ת 9942, 24 Jan 2022, in force 1 Feb 2022 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (regulation text read through two mirrors)
- Dimensions: `fee-management-balance`, `fee-deposit`
- Likely to change: no amendment is listed in the CMA 2026 regulation plan (PF-31). It can change only by regulation with Finance Committee approval.

### PF-03 Kupat gemel lehashkaa falls under the regulation-2 cap (4% of deposits, 1.05% of balance)
- Rule: Kupat gemel lehashkaa is not on the regulation-2(ב) exclusion list, so the regulation-2 caps (PF-02) apply to it. The regulation does not name the product. That it applies is my reading of the exclusion list, and a managing company confirms it.
- Conditions / exceptions: The Altshuler Shaham gemel lehashkaa bylaws do not state a figure. They say fees from balance and from deposits may not exceed "שיעור דמי הניהול המרבי הקבוע בהסדר התחיקתי" (bylaws s. 35.1). The Phoenix gemel lehashkaa FAQ states the deposit fee is up to "תקרה חוקית של 4%" and the balance fee is capped at 1.05% a year. This closes domain open question 8 for gemel lehashkaa at primary strength for the cap itself; the mapping of the product to regulation 2 rests on the exclusion list plus one institution confirmation.
- Figures: as PF-02.
- Source: [Management Fees Regulations, reg. 2(ב) (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(דמי_ניהול)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary; [Altshuler Shaham "חיסכון פלוס" gemel lehashkaa bylaws, March 2025 edition, s. 35](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution; [Phoenix gemel lehashkaa FAQ](https://www.fnx.co.il/gemel/gemelhashkaa/faq/) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution

### PF-04 Cap for a new comprehensive pension fund: 0.5% a year of balance and 6% of deposits
- Rule: A managing company of a קרן חדשה מקיפה may charge up to 0.5% a year of each member's balance and up to 6% of payments transferred to the fund for each member ("מתוך התשלומים המועברים לקרן בשל כל עמית – בשיעור שלא יעלה על 6%").
- Conditions / exceptions: Annuitants: old-age annuitants from 1 Feb 2022, up to 0.3% a year (reg. 3(א)(1א)). There is a rule on annuities below the minimum annuity (reg. 3(א)(3), "6% מההפרש שבין קצבת מינימום…"). In a pension fund, a discount applies to the whole balance. If a member is entitled to different discounts on different balances in the same fund, the highest discount applies to the whole balance (PF-13).
- Figures: 0.5% a year; 6% of deposits. Fixed in the regulation.
- Source: [Management Fees Regulations, reg. 3(א) (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(דמי_ניהול)); [Nevo mirror](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/500_714.htm) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. Corroborated: Menora Mivtachim Pension publishes "up to 0.50% of total assets" and "up to 6.00% of contributions" under these regulations for Jan to Sep 2026 ([Menora fees page](https://www.menoramivt.co.il/general/investments-management-fee), checked 2026-10-22, institution).
- Dimensions: `fee-management-balance`, `fee-deposit`
- Likely to change: no

### PF-05 Cap for a new general pension fund: 1.05% a year of balance and 4% of deposits

- Figures: 1.05% a year; 4% of deposits. Fixed.

### PF-06 Cap for keren hishtalmut and IRA (regulation 4): 2% a year of balance, no deposit-fee item
- Rule: A managing company of a keren hishtalmut, an IRA (קופת גמל בניהול אישי), a central fund, or a sick-pay, holiday or other-purpose fund "רשאית לגבות דמי ניהול מיתרת הכספים הצבורה בחשבונו של כל עמית, בשיעור שלא יעלה על 2% לשנה".
- Conditions / exceptions: Regulation 4 authorises a fee from the balance only and contains no fee-from-deposits item. Reading: a deposit fee on a keren hishtalmut or IRA has no basis in this regulation. Confirm against `keren-hishtalmut.md` data. Sector funds charge actual expenses within this cap (reg. 5).

### PF-08 Lower fee cap on lost-contact and deceased members' accounts
- Rule: For a member whose contact was lost (one year after they became one) or who died, where the manager did not complete the search steps required by the locating regulations within the required period, the fee may not exceed 0.3% a year of the balance or the rate last charged, whichever is lower (reg. 8(א)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Not applicable to sector funds, most old pension funds, and certain deceased annuitants (reg. 8(ד)). If contact is restored, the manager may return to the prior rate and collect the difference for the gap period, capped at 0.5% a year. That detail comes from a summary of reg. 8(ב); the exact wording was not quoted. For what counts as lost contact, see PF-27.
- Figures: 0.3% a year. Fixed.

### PF-09 How the balance fee is collected and prorated
- Rule: The balance-based fee is collected in equal parts either on the last business day of each month or at the end of each business day (reg. 9(א)). For money deposited, withdrawn or transferred during a month, the balance fee is prorated to the days the money was in the account (reg. 10). The NIS-per-month alternative for pre-2008 money is collected on the last business day of the year or at withdrawal or transfer (reg. 9(ב)).
- Conditions / exceptions: none found.
- Figures: none.

### PF-10 Direct investment-management expenses are charged on top of the management fee, from a closed list
- Rule: Direct expenses of transactions in an institutional investor's assets "יהיו אלה בלבד": securities trading commissions; custody fees; costs of non-traded investments and loans and of real-estate rights, paid to non-related parties; taxes; reinsurance for certain pension funds; litigation costs over investments; external management fees (עמלת ניהול חיצוני, defined by six situations such as investing in unrelated funds or paying foreign or licensed portfolio managers); and mortgage-origination costs within limits set by the Commissioner (reg. 2(א)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Specialised tracks mainly invested in tradable assets, and index-tracking specialised tracks, established after 1 Jan 2023 may charge only commissions, custody, taxes, litigation and external management. Their external management is limited to two of the six situations and to tradable securities. Tracks with a variable (performance) management fee established after 1 Jan 2023 may charge only taxes (reg. 2(ב)). These expenses are deducted from the fund, so they show in returns, not as a line deducted from the member's account (Phoenix FAQ: "אינן חלק מדמי הניהול, אך מקוזזות מהתשואה בפועל", institution).
- Figures: no overall percentage cap on direct expenses was found in the regulation (see PF-11 for the external-management cap). Example of published actual rates, not caps: Menora Mivtachim Pension 2025 investment-management expenses 0.15%, of which external management 0.14% ([Menora](https://www.menoramivt.co.il/general/investments-management-fee), institution). Altshuler Shaham hishtalmut "הלכה" 2025 0.11%, of which external 0.08% ([Altshuler](https://www.as-invest.co.il/interstedin/קרנות-השתלמות/הוצאות-לניהול-השקעות-ודמי-ניהול-ממוצעים-השתלמות-הלכה/), institution).
- Source: [Provident Funds (Direct Expenses for Executing Transactions) Regulations 2008, reg. 1, 2 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(הוצאות_ישירות_בשל_ביצוע_עסקאות)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary; law s. 32(א)(2) (PF-01)
- Dimensions: `fee-direct-expenses` (proposed), `fee-trade`, `fee-custody`
- Likely to change: yes. The regulation was amended in 2022 and 2023. The direct-expenses rules interact with the 2026 investment-tracks circular (PF-31).

### PF-11 External management fee cap: each manager sets it per track per year and must publish it
- Rule: Each institutional investor sets, each year, a maximum external-management-fee rate per fund or track ("מגבלת עמלת ניהול חיצוני"). It is calculated on estimated total assets at the end of the prior year, excludes variable fees, and is stated on an annual basis. The investor must publish it on its website by 31 December of the preceding year (later for new funds and mergers; within 30 days of approval for a merger) and "לא יגבה הוצאות ישירות מסוג עמלת ניהול חיצוני, מעבר למגבלת עמלת הניהול החיצוני" (reg. 2א).
- Conditions / exceptions: Guaranteed-return channel investments are excluded from the calculation.
- Figures: none in the regulation. Per-manager figures were not collected (open question 3).
- Source: [Direct Expenses Regulations, reg. 2א (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(הוצאות_ישירות_בשל_ביצוע_עסקאות)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: `fee-direct-expenses` (proposed), `fee-change-over-time`
- Likely to change: yes, the cap is reset by each manager every year.

### PF-12 Disclosure of direct expenses and of the "expected annual cost"

Wherever management fees must be shown, the expected annual cost must be shown as well (reg. 3ב(ג)).
- Conditions / exceptions: Expenses of guaranteed-return investments are excluded from member reports (reg. 3ב(ד)). The rule was read in summary from the Wikisource text. The term "עלות שנתית צפויה" appears in the CMA 2026 plan item on presenting the expected annual cost in the advice and marketing process (PF-31).

In a pension fund the discount applies to the whole balance, and if different discounts apply to different balances, the highest applies to all of it (s. 3(ה)).

### PF-14 When a manager may raise the fee before the discount period ends
- Rule: A manager may raise the fee before the end of the discount period, after notice under PF-16, in these cases (circular s. 3(ב)): (1) "שישה חודשים אחרי הפסקת תשלומים לחיסכון המועברים לקופת הגמל עבור העמית שלא אירעה בשל המעביד"; (2) a full or partial withdrawal, or a full or partial transfer of the balance to another provident fund or to another investment track in the same fund; (3) cancelled; (4) a raise under a mechanism in the agreement or plan terms that grades the fee by balance or by another variable the Commissioner approved, except a mechanism based on the member's seniority.
- Conditions / exceptions: The gemel lehashkaa bylaws repeat cases (2) and (4) (s. 36.2). They also allow a raise without the PF-16 notice in those cases if the member and the licensee were told in a separate document, within three months of the discount, of the right to cancel it (s. 37.3). A track switch is a permitted trigger for a raise, but it does not raise the fee automatically: Phoenix states fees "אינם משתנים אוטומטית במעבר בין מסלולים בתוך אותה קופה" (institution).
- Figures: six months after deposits stop.
- Source: [CMA circular 2017-9-15, s. 3(ב)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-989/he/regulation_h_2017-9-15.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary; [Altshuler bylaws s. 36.2, 37.3](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf) · institution; [Phoenix FAQ](https://www.fnx.co.il/gemel/gemelhashkaa/faq/) · institution
- Dimensions: `fee-change-over-time`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: see open question 1.

### PF-15 Changing employer: the better group arrangement applies
- Rule: If a member leaves a workplace that has a fee arrangement with an institutional body (the "previous workplace arrangement") and starts at a workplace that has a different arrangement with the same body, the more favourable arrangement ("הסדר דמי ניהול מיטיב") applies. That is the one with the lower total fee from monthly deposits and balance, computed by the appendix-A formula. If the previous arrangement was better, the new one applies once the previous arrangement's discount period ends. During the discount period the member may ask to move to the arrangement that would apply under this rule (s. 3(ד)).
- Conditions / exceptions: This covers only a move between two arrangements at the same body. Leaving an employer without a new arrangement falls under PF-14 case (1): six months after deposits stop.
- Figures: none.

### PF-16 Notice before a fee increase, and notice of a discount
- Rule (increase): A body that wants to raise the fee sends the member, and any licensee giving them ongoing advice or marketing, a notice in a separate document ("הודעה במסמך נפרד"). The increase takes effect at the end of two months from sending, or at a later date stated in the notice. The notice may not be sent more than four months before the increase (s. 4(א)). It uses the appendix-A template: a table of current and new rates "מהפקדה" and "מחיסכון", and the shekel amount charged over the past year against what the new rate would have charged. It must tell the member they can compare offers with the CMA "מחשבון דמי ניהול" (s. 4(ג), appendix A). If the body holds the member's email, the notice goes by email with the subject line "לתשומת לבך – דמי הניהול בחיסכון הפנסיוני שלך ב___ משתנים". 30 days before the increase, an SMS goes to the member's mobile (appendix B) (s. 4(ג), (ד)).
- Rule (discount): When a discount is given, the body shows it in the member's personal online account as soon as possible. The display includes the new rate, the start and end of the discount period, and the cases in which the fee may be raised. If the member's email is known, a copy is emailed within 10 business days (s. 3א).
- Conditions / exceptions: The gemel lehashkaa bylaws repeat the two-month and four-month rule (s. 37.1, 37.2) and add the exception in PF-14.
- Figures: 2 months; not more than 4 months ahead; SMS 30 days ahead; discount email within 10 business days.
- Source: [CMA circular 2017-9-15, s. 3א, 4, appendices A and B (Prisha copy, pp. 2 to 7)](https://www.prisha.co.il/UserFiles/File/pdf/hoz/hoz2017915.pdf) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (official circular on a third-party host; pp. 1 to 2 match the gov.il copy)
- Dimensions: `fee-change-over-time`, `statement-disclosure` (proposed)

### PF-17 A member may transfer money between provident funds; which routes are allowed
- Rule: Withdrawal and transfer from a provident fund are allowed only as the law lists ("תהיה כמפורט להלן, בלבד"), including transfer by the member of money in their account (law s. 23(א)(2)). The Minister sets the conditions (s. 23(ב)). The Transfer Regulations list the permitted routes. Into an annuity fund (reg. 2(א)): from another annuity fund; from a tagmulim fund or a personal severance fund, if the receiving fund pays annuities; from a keren hishtalmut, if the member could withdraw it tax-exempt; from a gemel lehashkaa, "ובלבד שהעמית הגיע לגיל הפרישה המוקדמת". Into a non-annuity fund (reg. 3(א)): tagmulim to tagmulim; personal severance to personal severance, or to tagmulim under the income-tax rules; keren hishtalmut to keren hishtalmut; "מקופת גמל להשקעה לקופת גמל להשקעה אחרת"; other routes only with the Commissioner's approval and the Tax Commissioner's consent.
- Conditions / exceptions: No route was found from keren hishtalmut or tagmulim into gemel lehashkaa, or from gemel lehashkaa into keren hishtalmut. An annuity fund is not a source in reg. 3. Transfer is barred when the whole balance is attached or pledged, when a loan is outstanding, when an old-age annuity has started, when a disability annuity is claimed or approved, when the member died before the determining date, when the receiving fund is closed, or when the member applied to a comprehensive fund while still active in the transferring fund (reg. 4).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Provident Funds Law s. 23 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)); [Provident Funds (Transfer of Money between Provident Funds) Regulations 2008, reg. 2, 3, 4 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(העברת_כספים_בין_קופות_גמל)); cross-read on [Nevo mirror](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_929.htm), which shows the last amendment as ק"ת 10716, 12 Jul 2023, in force 1 Sep 2023 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary

### PF-19 Transfer deadline 10 business days; late-transfer compensation from the manager's fees; no transfer fee
- Rule: The transferring fund's managing body transfers the balance "בתוך עשרה ימי עסקים מהיום שבו הועברה לו בקשת העברה מפורטת, חתומה ומלאה", unless the request was cancelled. If the receiving body is late, it credits the member the difference out of its own fees (reg. 6(ג1)). Transfers go directly between the managing bodies "ולא באמצעות העמית" and in cash unless the bodies agree otherwise (reg. 7).

### PF-20 Transfer process: the request goes to the receiving fund; the member can cancel until the money moves
- Rule: The member signs a transfer request with the receiving fund. The receiving fund checks eligibility and forwards the request to the transferring fund electronically within two business days (circular 2017-9-17 s. 3(א) to (ו)). ### PF-21 No agent is needed: a saver can transact directly with the manager

### PF-22 The pension clearing house: what it does and what a saver can get from it
- Rule: The central pension clearing system ("מערכת סליקה פנסיונית מרכזית") is a central system for transferring customer information or money. ### PF-23 Track switching: within three business days; no fee or frequency limit in the regulation

### PF-26 Statements: annual report within three months, quarterly report within 60 days

### PF-27 Lost contact and dormant accounts: definitions and the manager's search duty
- Rule: A member is "עמית שהקשר עמו נותק" if two periodic reports sent by post (not email) were returned within three years ("דואר חוזר"), or if their account is a dormant account ("חשבון רדום"). For a non-pension account, an account is dormant when no instruction was given for 10 years, or 5 years if the manager knows the money is withdrawable, whichever is earlier; pension accounts follow rules tied to retirement age. The manager must check identity details against the Population Registry, and for lost-contact members act "בשקידה סבירה" to locate them through contacts, employer and data sources within one year, repeating every three years. For dormant accounts it repeats the search only if the balance exceeds NIS 5,000 (Locating Regulations, s. 1 to 3). Beneficiaries of deceased members are searched on a set timetable (s. 4).
- Conditions / exceptions: The law empowers the Commissioner to set search duties (law s. 24). A lower fee cap applies where search duties were not met (PF-08). No public lookup database is required by these regulations. Whether Har HaKesef (the CMA's unclaimed-money lookup) is fed by them was not verified (open question 7).

### PF-28 Pension: inactive accounts are merged into the new fund when a member joins, unless they object

### PF-30 Merger, split and change of manager need CMA approval and advance notice; accounts are not merged
- Rule: A managing company may merge provident funds only if the Commissioner approved the plan in advance and members were notified in advance. The merger does not merge members' accounts ("אין במיזוג כאמור… כדי להביא למיזוג של חשבונות עמיתים") (law s. 37). The same protection applies to a split (s. 38). A voluntary transfer of management to another company also needs prior approval and advance notice to members (s. 41). If a receiver or liquidator is appointed to the managing company and not removed within 60 days, it must transfer management of its funds to another managing company (s. 42).
- Conditions / exceptions: The law gives members no specific right to object to a merger, and no fee condition attached to one, in the text read. A member can still transfer out under PF-17. A merger requires the external-management cap to be published within 30 days of approval (PF-11).
- Figures: 60 days.
- Source: [Provident Funds Law s. 37, 38, 41, 42 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary; bylaws s. 34 repeats this (institution)
- Dimensions: `protection`, `regulator`
- Likely to change: no

### PF-31 Planned changes in the CMA 2026 regulation plan that touch these products
- Rule: The plan, published 31 Dec 2025 and updated 14 Jan 2026, lists these items for the long-term savings division and others:
  - Clearing house: an update to the mandatory-use circular, adding a future action, and an update to the payment circular for using the clearing house (p. 18).
  - Advice and marketing: the advice-document circular, including how the expected annual cost (עלות שנתית צפויה) is presented (p. 18). A power-of-attorney circular for licensees (p. 19). An update to the uniform-structure circular for information transfer (p. 19).
  - Customer service: a non-statutory withdrawal on the customer's own initiative may be executed only after advice or marketing (p. 19).
  - Joining: a review of the joining circular for pension and gemel funds (p. 19).
  - Locating: an update of the locating circular, replacing the regulations under the Commissioner's authority (p. 20).
  - Investment tracks: an investment-tracks circular updating track policies after the reform (p. 20).
  - Withdrawal and retirement: a withdrawal circular with uniform rules, and a retirement circular (p. 20).
  - Returns publication: rules for publishing returns and investment tracks (p. 21).
  - Transfers: an amendment to the transfer circular allowing identification other than an Israeli ID for residents abroad (p. 21).
  - IRA: an amendment to the IRA regulations (p. 16).
- Conditions / exceptions: No item in the plan changes the management-fee caps, the fee-discount circular, or the transfer regulations. The plan says some items may not be completed within the year (p. 3). No source was found for an "arbitrage reform" that merges wrappers (open question 10).
- Figures: none.

- Dimensions: `regulator`, `fee-change-over-time`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: yes, by definition. Re-check each item's status at the next plan publication (expected around Dec 2026).

### PF-32 Finding lost accounts: the CMA pension-savings search ("הר הכסף" route)
- Rule: The CMA service locates inactive accounts across provident funds, pension funds, life insurance with a savings component, study funds and death-insurance policies without savings. The saver needs their ID number and ID issue date; the answer is immediate and free.
- Conditions / exceptions: It shows only that an account may exist; balances and rights are with the institutions. Querying someone else's ID without permission may be a criminal offence. CMA public enquiries: *3002 or 1222-3002, Sunday to Thursday 9:00 to 16:00. The page was machine-translated by the browser; facts above are from that rendering.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [CMA, קבלת מידע על חסכונות פנסיוניים](https://www.gov.il/he/service/pension_savings_search) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary
- Dimensions: `dispute-channel`
- Likely to change: no.

## Proposed dimensions

- `fee-direct-expenses`: investment-management expenses charged to the fund on top of the management fee (PF-10, PF-11). It fits the `fee-<type>` pattern but should be listed so 3.5 treats it as its own line.
- `statement-disclosure`: what the institution must tell the saver, where, and when (statements, fee-change notices, expected annual cost). The existing `reporting-obligation` reads as the saver's own duty (for example tax filing), so it does not cover this.
