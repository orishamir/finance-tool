The exchange's sheet still shows that **no custody fee was collected from individuals in H1 2026, in any portfolio band**. That is a fact, not a price.

Under D56 the two grey `availability` entries go. The house is now priced at the list:
- at least 1,860 ₪ a year before any trade, below 600,000 ₪;

- Custody of at least 75 ₪ a month, plus a periodic fee of up to 80 ₪ a month: 1,860 ₪ a year together, whatever the portfolio, below 600,000 ₪.

## 3. The ordinary customer's price (the most the documents allow)

**Case 1: an S&P 500 tracking fund or ETF in Tel Aviv, in shekels**

- A tracking mutual fund: **0.15%, at least 16 ₪ a trade** (4(א)5).
- An ETF in continuous trading: 0.15%, at least 3.5 ₪.
- Custody: **0.15% a year, at least 75 ₪ a month**, collected monthly. Read alone, the price list prints no period for the 0.15%; the exchange's sheet says "לשנה". Below 600,000 ₪ the minimum governs: 900 ₪ a year.
- Periodic fee: **up to 80 ₪ a month**, priced at 80, so 960 ₪ a year. Business accounts pay up to 10,000 ₪ instead; that is not this reader.
- Standing order: no fee line. The monthly-investment page lets any security be picked, and each buy is a trade at the minimum above. The older direct-deposit page buys only Altshuler's own funds.
- Dividend or interest handling: **0.3% of each payment** (4(ב)5). An accumulating fund pays none.
- Inactivity: none printed.
- Transfer out: **20 ₪ per security, at most 99 ₪** a day.
- Data: advanced systems and quotes cost up to 3,000 ₪ a month, by the client's choice. Whether the basic platform is free is **not stated** in any binding document.
- Opening: at least **5,000 ₪**.

An illustration, inferred and not printed anywhere: 67,000 ₪ in a tracking fund pays 900 ₪ custody plus 960 ₪ periodic fee, 1,860 ₪ a year (2.8%), before any trade.

**Case 2: an ETF in the US**

- Trading, the priced track (3): **0.15%, at least 9$**. The other tracks are (1) 1 cent a share, at least 9$, and (2) 11$ fixed, "על פי אחד מהמסלולים". Which track applies is not stated.
- On top of every track, "ECN, SEC, TAF, ORF, TAX ועוד" "וייגבו בנוסף". These are **not limited**.
- Conversion: **no fee; a spread of up to 0.7%**, limited by the document.
- Custody: the same line (0.15% a year, at least 75 ₪ a month), plus the same 80 ₪.
- Dividends: 0.3% of each.
- Transfer out: **10$ per security**.
- FX transfer: 36$, or up to 450$ to a third party.
- Europe: the price list names no exchange outside the US, and the exchange's sheet leaves "מחוץ לארה"ב" blank.
