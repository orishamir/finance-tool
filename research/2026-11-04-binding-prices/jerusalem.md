# Bank of Jerusalem (`jerusalem`): binding prices, 2026-11-04

Read on 2026-11-04 under D56. The new snapshot is `data/fees/jerusalem@2026-11-04.json`, copied from `jerusalem@2026-10-31.json`.

**In one line.** Bank of Jerusalem advertises no securities price at all, only "עמלות נמוכות", "מינימום עמלות" and "עמלות תחרותיות". The price list prints custody of **0.15% a quarter (0.60% a year)** on every holding in Israel and abroad, with no waiver for anyone. The online price is **0.50% with a 20 ₪ minimum** in Tel Aviv.

**No price value moves.** The changes:
- The two conversion lines gain a spread of "not limited". The general terms set the rate as the bank's own highest cheque-selling rate. The calculation does not read the spread.
- The custody ceiling's note now says "per deposit", from the exchange's sheet.
- The online lines' minimum and maximum now cite the newest annex E (08/06/2026), with the same figures.
- `left_out` goes from 0 entries to 6: third-party costs abroad (a broker up to 3% of the trade since 16.9.2026), the custodian fee, the exchange's data packages, the dropped online conversion discount, the current account and the averages.

**The standing order.**
- The general terms (January 2024, s.138.3.2 and s.151.2) know a standing order to buy securities and fund units. No price list prints a price for one.
- Read the same way as FIBI and Mizrahi-Tefahot, the most the documents allow is the clerk price: **0.55% with a 20 ₪ minimum**. That is inferred.
- The record prices a purchase the reader places online each month: 0.50%, minimum 20 ₪. Below about 3,600 ₪ a month both pay the 20 ₪ minimum, so the two readings differ only above that.

**No offers, no groups.** Annex A lists only prepaid-card holders. No campaign, no young-customer price and no promotion terms exist.

- Abroad: 0.04% to 0.19%.

Customers on average pay about a fifth of the list custody. That is negotiated, not printed.

## 5. Still open

- **The standing order's price.** Does the bank charge a monthly purchase by standing order at 0.50% or at 0.55%? It moves nothing below about 3,600 ₪ a month, because the 20 ₪ minimum binds either way. A written answer from the bank (*5727, trading room *5827) or a customer's statement would settle it.
- **The app.** Note 4 and annex E price "אינטרנט" only. The banking-by-communication agreement counts an app among the internet channels, so the record treats the "ירושלים טרייד" app as internet (inferred). The same answer would settle it.
- **The dropped online conversion discount.** The combined file still prints 0.18% "אינטרנט / אפליקציה". The newer annex E omits it, with no change notice. The record prices 0.20%, the most either document allows.
- **The 0.50% custodian fee (4(ב)(4))**: what it is charged on is not printed. Not priced.
- **Third-party costs abroad (decision for the lead or founder):**
  - the new broker ceiling of 3% of the trade;
  - the correspondent bank, up to 100$;
  - the agents' corporate-event fees, without a ceiling.
  All are kept out of the price, as at FIBI, Hapoalim and Mizrahi-Tefahot. For a US ETF at this bank the 3% ceiling is the largest unpriced cost.
- **The current account (decision for all banks):** 9.5 ₪ a month, flat.
- **For other houses:** nothing found. The bank's foreign custodian and platform is Interactive Brokers (/capital-market/custodian), which may matter for the `interactive-israel` job.
- **Tabs.** One Chrome tab was opened and closed: the bank's pages, which sit behind a Radware bot check for curl, and the exchange's sheet. The group is gone. curl fetched the tariff files at five-second intervals with no block.
