# Excellence Trade (`excellence`): binding prices, 2026-11-04

Read 2026-11-04 under D56. Record: `data/fees/excellence@2026-11-04.json` (new snapshot, copied from `excellence@2026-10-31.json`).

**In one line.** No binding document waives custody, the conversion fee or the handling fee for an ordinary customer. The only price list (PDF of 15.7.2026, unchanged today) prints:
- custody of **0.6% a quarter, at least 40 ₪ a month**: up to 2.4% a year;
- a handling fee of **up to 99 ₪ a month**;
- a conversion fee of **0.1%**, plus a spread "הנקבע בהסכם מול הלקוח", which no published document limits.

The joining page's "ללא עמלת אי-ביצוע, ללא עמלת מט"ח וללא דמי משמרת" is contradicted line by line.

The two handling-fee exemptions are offers: 24 months, and "לכל חיי החשבון" for accounts opened by 31.12.2026. Each needs 10,000 ₪, and each says in its own terms that it does not cover "דמי משמרת" or "עלויות המרה". A third offer, no buy fee on Kesem's funds (at most 40 buys in 2026), touches the reader's case directly, because Kesem runs an S&P 500 tracking fund.

The exchange's sheet shows what customers actually paid in H1 2026: custody of about 0.05% a year at most, and trades at about a fifth of the list. That is a fact, not a price. **No line's value changed**, and Excellence stays last.

**The catch, for an ordinary customer:**
- Custody of 0.6% a quarter (2.4% a year), at least 480 ₪ a year.
- Up to 99 ₪ a month of handling fee.
- A 10 ₪ minimum on every buy, which is 2% of a 500 ₪ monthly buy.
- A conversion spread that no published document limits.

The handling-fee offers remove only the 99 ₪, and only for those who deposit 10,000 ₪. The dealer-information page explains how the real price is set: "עמלות ששיעורן נקבע באופן ספציפי במסגרת חוברת פתיחת החשבון ... תיוותרנה ללא שינוי. כל עמלה ששיעורה לא נקבע כאמור ... תהיה בשיעור הקבוע בתעריפון". So each customer's price sits in an unpublished booklet. The list is the most it may charge.

**What customers paid (a fact, not a price).** From the exchange's calculator, Excellence's sheet, "עמלות ממוצעות בפועל לפי שווי תיק 01/01/26 - 30/06/26":
- "דמי ניהול - ני"ע ישראלים" (an annual rate): 0.0492% up to 25 thousand ₪, then 0.0402%, 0.0235%, 0.0171%, 0.0109%, 0.0054%, 0.0029%, 0.0019%, and 0.0005% above 1 million.
- Israeli shares and bonds: 0.0859% to 0.0600%.
- Makam: about 0.05%.
- Foreign securities: 0.2818% to 0.0693%.

Our inference, not read anywhere: most customers pay custody far below the list, presumably through the booklet. Nothing published binds the house to that for a new customer.

## 3. The ordinary customer's price (the most the documents allow)

**Case 1: an S&P 500 tracking fund or ETF in Tel Aviv, in shekels**
- Trade: **0.4%, at least 10 ₪**. Both tracking funds and ETFs are on line 4(א)1, and so is an unexecuted order. The 16 ₪ line is for traditional funds only.
- Custody: **0.6% a quarter, at least 40 ₪ a month**, collected "חודשי/רבעוני". Up to 2.4% a year.
- Section 4(ב)2 adds "דמי משמרת 0.1%", collected quarterly, "לחשבונות ... שמהות פעילותם היא אחזקת נכסים ולא מסחר פעיל". The price list does not say whether it replaces item 8 or comes on top of it. At most it adds 0.4% a year. It is not priced (see 5).
- Handling fee: **up to 99 ₪ a month**, priced at 99, so 1,188 ₪ a year.
- Standing order: no fee line. Each monthly buy is a trade at the 10 ₪ minimum.
- Dividends: no line prices receiving one. An accumulating fund pays none.
- Inactivity: none printed.
- Transfer out: **0.1% of the value, at least 99 ₪**, with no maximum.
- Data: "מערכות מתקדמות/ציטוטים עד 3,000 ₪" a month. Whether the basic system is free is printed in no binding document.
- Opening: 4,500 ₪, until 31.12.2026.

An illustration, our arithmetic and not printed anywhere: 67,000 ₪ in a tracking fund pays 1,608 ₪ custody and 1,188 ₪ handling, **2,796 ₪ a year (4.2%)**, plus 268 ₪ to buy.

**Case 2: an ETF in the US**
- Trading: the client's choice of track: "3 סנט למניה מינימום 8$", "11$ פיקס לעסקה", or "0.3% + עמלת ברוקר". On every track, "עמלת מקסימום 2% משווי העסקה".
- The record prices 0.3% with the 8$ minimum and the 2% cap. The broker fee of that track is not quantified, so it runs up to the cap.
- Outside the US: 0.4%, at least 35 in the trade's currency, and "ייתכנו עמלות צדדים שלישיים", **not limited**.
- Conversion: **0.1% plus a spread not limited by the documents**.
- Custody: the same line, plus the same handling fee.
- Transfer out: 0.1%, at least 10$.
- Dollars left uninvested earn no interest (part 1, item 5; the settlement's arrangement).

**Current account.** A non-bank house: none is needed. Money is deposited into Excellence's trust accounts at Hapoalim and Leumi.
