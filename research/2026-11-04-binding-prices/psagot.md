# Psagot Trade (`psagot`): binding prices, 2026-11-04

Read 2026-11-04 under D56. Record: `data/fees/psagot@2026-11-04.json` (new snapshot, copied from `psagot@2026-10-23.json`).

**In one line.** No binding document waives custody. The price list, read again today and unchanged since January 2026, prints custody at **0.12% a year, collected 0.03% a quarter**. The advert behind the founder's impression says **"פטור מלא מדמי ניהול ללא הגבלת זמן"**. It never says "דמי משמרת", and the price list has no line called "דמי ניהול". Even so, the exchange's calculator shows that Psagot's customers **actually paid 0% custody on average in the first half of 2026**, in every portfolio size. So in practice Psagot does not collect the fee, but no published document says so. The ranked price stays at 0.12% (D56).

## 3. The ordinary customer's price (the most the documents allow)

**Case 1: an S&P 500 tracking fund or ETF in Tel Aviv, in shekels**

- Buying and selling: **0.07%, at least 2 ₪ a trade**. This holds for both a mutual fund ("קניה, מכירה, ופדיון של קרנות נאמנות", 0.07%, min 2 ₪) and a share-rules ETF.
- Custody: **0.12% a year, collected at 0.03% each quarter**, with no minimum or maximum printed.
- Account or handling fee: none ("ללא עלות").
- Standing-order purchase: not offered in any document read, and no fee printed.
- Dividends on Israeli securities: no fee printed.
- Inactivity: none printed.
- Transfer out: 0.07%, at most **70 ₪ per instruction**.
- Data: delayed TASE data is free, so the account works without paying. Real-time data is optional: L1 4.60 ₪ and L2 40.32 ₪ a month.
- Opening: at least **10,000 ₪** (landing page).

**Case 2: an ETF abroad**

- US trades: **$0.01 a share, at least $6.5**. On top come "עמלת הקורספונדנט ו/או עמלות צד שלישי" (also "בעת חלוקת דיבידנד") and SEC/ORF/ECN/TAF. These are **not limited**.
- Dollar conversion: **1.5 agorot "עבור כל דולר/שקל"**, which can be read two ways: about 0.48% per dollar at 3.12, or 1.5% per shekel. Spread: **not limited**.
- Custody: 0.12% a year (the line covers foreign securities).
- Transfer out: **$10 per security**.
- Europe: the tariff prints 0.15%, at least 15 €, "עשויה להשתנות"; the exchange's sheet prints 0.25%, at least 50 in the trade currency. Not settled, so it stays grey.
