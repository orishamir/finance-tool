# One Zero Digital Bank (`one-zero`): binding prices, 2026-11-04

Read on 2026-11-04 under D56. Record: `data/fees/one-zero@2026-11-04.json`, a new snapshot copied from `one-zero@2026-10-31.json`.

**In one line.** The zero custody holds. The price list in force since July 2026 prints custody ("דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך") at **"0 אחוז"** for securities traded in Israel, abroad and off-exchange. The zero comes with no plan, no minimum, no end date and no condition. A customer with no plan (ZERO) gets it, and no document requires a plan to trade. The catch is abroad, not in Tel Aviv. Note (3) and part 11 pass on **third-party custodian costs abroad**: "עד 5,000 דולר ארה"ב לחודש" (not stated per client) and "עד 50 דולר ארה"ב ללקוח לכל אירוע חברה". Part 11 has no custodian item for Israeli securities. The real cost for the Tel Aviv reader is elsewhere: **0.3% a trade with a 30 ₪ minimum**, and no standing order on TASE.

## 3. The ordinary customer's price (ZERO, no plan; the most the documents allow)

**The current account itself:** **0 ₪ a month.** The tariff prints these at 0 ₪:
- "פעולה על ידי פקיד" and "פעולה בערוץ ישיר".
- "מסלול בסיסי" ("בפיקוח") and "מסלול מורחב".

ZERO is "ללא מסלול, חיוב בהתאם לתעריפון". The debit card (direct Mastercard) costs **7 ₪ a month** (annex ד', p.19). The Platinum credit card costs 0 ₪, subject to the bank's credit approval. No document read says a card is required. The home page FAQ says a card arrives within about 7 business days of opening, and it does not say which card.

**Case 1: an S&P 500 tracking fund on TASE, in shekels**
- Trading: **0.3%, at least 30 ₪, at most 6,000 ₪ a trade** (4(א)(1)). Footnote 9 includes a "קרן מחקה". Direct and clerk orders cost the same.
- Custody: **0**, guaranteed, with no plan or condition. Part 11 has no Israeli custodian item. Note (3) passes on TASE's own costs at TASE's tariff: data (item 10), and deposit, withdrawal or transfer (item 18). The documents do not say whether every customer pays the data cost.
- Standing order: **none on TASE**. The service covers only US ETFs, so a monthly buy is a separate trade at the 30 ₪ minimum.
- Dividends and distributions: no fee printed for Israeli securities.
- Inactivity: none printed.
- Transfer out: 5 ₪ per transfer to the customer's own account elsewhere ("בפיקוח"). On closing, at most 10 ₪, and all closing fees together at most 40 ₪.
- Account and plan: 0 ₪ with no plan.
- An ETF (קרן סל) on TASE: availability stays **unknown**. Both the bank and the exchange sheet name only TA-125 shares, Israeli mutual funds and US securities.

**Case 2: an ETF in the US**
- Trading: **0.3%, at least 24 $, at most 1,500 $** (4(א)(4)). On top, at actual cost: broker "עד 1 דולר לעסקה", correspondent "עד 100 יורו לעסקה", and US taxes and SEC "עד 30 דולר … לכל 1 מיליון דולר" on sales.
- Conversion: **0.15%, at least 5 $, at most 3,000 $**. The spread is **not limited by the documents**: the bank sets its own rate.
- Custody: 0 for the bank's own fee. Foreign custodian costs are passed on: **up to 50 $ per corporate event per client**, and up to 5,000 $ a month (not stated per client). A US ETF paying quarterly dividends could, read at the ceiling, carry up to 200 $ a year. This is inferred: no document defines a "corporate event", and no customer report was found.
- Market data abroad: up to 10 $ per client a month (item 19).
- Transfer out: up to 2 $ per symbol (item 20), plus the 5 ₪ line.
- Standing order: available for US ETFs, at the same commission and conversion.
- Europe: not offered.
