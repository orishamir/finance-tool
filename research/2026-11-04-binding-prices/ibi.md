# IBI Trade (`ibi`): binding prices, 2026-11-04

Read 2026-11-04 under D56. Record: `data/fees/ibi@2026-11-04.json` (new snapshot, copied from `ibi@2026-10-23.json`).

**In one line.** IBI's adverts say "ללא דמי משמרת" and "ללא דמי שמירה על כלל הקרנות במערכת, כולל קרנות מחקות". Its only price list, "נכון לתאריך: 1.7.2026", prints custody at **"0.1% משווי החשבון לרבעון"**: 0.4% a year, with no cap (the January 2025 list capped it at 5,000 ₪ a quarter). It also prints a usage fee of **"עד ₪ 50 אחת לחודש"**. So the claim is contradicted and both costs stay in the ranked price. Two facts point the other way, though:
- The exchange's sheet, which IBI itself submits, lists the account fee as "דמי ניהול/טיפול פקדון ... 50.00 ₪ לחודש" and shows no 0.1%.
- What customers actually paid in "דמי ניהול" in H1 2026 was 0.0673% to 0.0012% of the portfolio, far below either fee.

The waivers are all offers: a one-off 300 ₪ for new customers, and two years without the basic usage fee only for those who join through an approved distributor. No price moves.

**The catch, for an ordinary customer.** Custody of 0.1% of the whole account each quarter (0.4% a year), with no cap since July 2026. On top come a usage fee of up to 50 ₪ a month, a 15,000 ₪ minimum to open, and no standing order on TASE. The company may change the minimum ("או סכום שתקבע החברה מעת לעת"). Every Tel Aviv trading price is printed as a ceiling ("עד"), so the actual rate sits in the signed agreement, which is not published.

**What customers paid (a fact, not a price).** The exchange's calculator, IBI's sheet, "עמלות ממוצעות בפועל לפי שווי תיק 01/01/26 - 30/06/26" (individuals and small businesses):

- "דמי ניהול - ני"ע ישראלים" and "ני"ע זרים" (identical in every band): **0.0673%** up to 25 thousand ₪, then 0.0478%, 0.0278%, 0.0203%, 0.0132%, 0.0065%, 0.0037%, 0.0028%, and **0.0012%** above 1,000 thousand ₪.
- Trading in Israeli shares and bonds: 0.0451% to 0.0955%.
- Makam: 0.0379% to 0.0602%.
- Foreign securities: 0.0939% to 0.3614%.

The sheet's list rates ("עדכון אחרון: 06/07/26") match the tariff on trading: 0.15% with a 3.50 ₪ minimum; makam 0.1% with 3.50 ₪; US $0.01 a share with a $10 minimum; outside the US 0.3% with 30. Its only custody row is **"דמי ניהול/טיפול פקדון ני"ע כללי - לפקדון 50.00 ₪ לחודש"**. It has no 0.1% row.

Our inference, not read anywhere: a falling average like this fits a small fixed monthly fee that is often waived or offset, and does not fit 0.1% of value a quarter. One customer reports (hasolidit, 2024-11-27, a report and not a document) "דמי שימוש הם 15 שקל אבל הם מתקזזים מפעולות שאני עושה ולכן כמעט כל חודש אני לא משלם אותם".

## 3. The ordinary customer's price (the most the documents allow)

**Case 1: an S&P 500 tracking fund or ETF in Tel Aviv, in shekels**

- A tracking fund (קרן נאמנות): **0.08%, at least 5 ₪ a trade**. Printed "עד", a ceiling.
- An ETF (קרן סל), under "מניות, אגרות חוב וקרנות סל": **0.15%, at least 3.5 ₪**. Also "עד".
- Custody: **0.1% of the account's value a quarter (0.4% a year)**, with no minimum or maximum.
- Usage fee: **up to 50 ₪ a month**, priced at 50. A second account costs up to 100 ₪.
- Standing order: **none on TASE**. A monthly buy is a separate trade at the minimum.
- Dividends on Israeli securities: no fee printed.
- Inactivity: none printed.
- Transfer out: **20 ₪ per security, at most 150 ₪ per transfer** (same owner). A different owner pays 35 ₪ per security.
- Data: real-time quotes and advanced systems cost up to 3,000 ₪ a month, by the client's choice. Whether the basic platform needs paid data is not stated.
- Opening: at least **15,000 ₪**, and the company may change it.

An illustration, inferred and not printed anywhere: a 67,000 ₪ account at these ceilings pays 268 ₪ custody plus 600 ₪ usage fee, about 868 ₪ a year (1.3%), before any trade.

**Case 2: an ETF in the US**

- Trading, the priced track (3): **0.15%, at least $10**. The other tracks are:
  - (1) $0.01 a share, at least $10;
  - (2) $14 a trade;
  - (4) 0.15% plus $0.01 a share, at least $6.
- On top of any track come "SEC, TAF, ECN, ORF, TAX ועוד", which "ייגבו בנוסף". These are **not limited**.
- Conversion: **spread up to 0.7% and no fee**. This is limited by the document.
- Custody: the same 0.1% a quarter, on the whole account.
- Corporate events "שאינם בגדר חובת היענות": $50, charged only "במידה שישנו מענה מצד הלקוח".
- Transfer out: **$10 per security**.
- Withdrawing foreign currency abroad: $35, plus a correspondent bank's fee that is "not limited".
- Standing order: reminders only.
- Europe: 0.3%, at least 30 in the trade currency, "עשויה להשתנות".

## 5. Still open

- **Is the 0.1% a quarter collected at all?** IBI's own filing to the exchange omits it and shows 50 ₪ a month instead, and the averages are far below it. Any of these would settle it: a written answer from IBI (6212*), a customer's quarterly statement, or the signed fee annex. Until then D56 prices 0.1%. **Decision for the lead or founder:** does the exchange sheet count as a binding document? IBI submits it "בהתאם לחוק ניירות ערך", and the exchange's own note says the full tariff governs. If it counts, custody would read 50 ₪ a month and no 0.1%.
- **Which US track is the ordinary customer's.** The tariff leaves it to the client's contract. The lead decides between pricing track 3 (now), the dearest track for a given trade, or grey.
- **The plans behind the usage fee** ("מסלול בסיסי", "מסלול מורחב") and the reported 15 ₪ offset against commissions. A copy of the account agreement would settle both.
- **The "0 ש"ח דמי טיפול לשנה" on the IBI SMART page.** It is in no terms read. IBI could say which terms govern it.
- **For other houses:** nothing found.
- **Tabs.** The one Chrome tab opened (the exchange calculator) was closed, and no tab fell out of the group.
