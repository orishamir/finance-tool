## 3. The ordinary customer's price (the most the documents allow)

**Case 1: an S&P 500 tracking fund or ETF in Tel Aviv, in shekels (online)**
- Buying and selling: **0.4%, at least 26 ₪, at most 6,300 ₪**. The minimum is capped at 27% of the payment (annex E p.13; 4(א)(1) note 3). Footnote {4} takes in "קרן מחקה" and ETFs.
- Monthly standing order (tracking fund, buying only): **0.225%, at least 5 ₪**. No channel is named.
- Custody: **0.15% a quarter (0.6% a year)**, collected the month after each quarter.
  - Ceilings: 3,700 ₪ per security, 11,500 ₪ per deposit.
  - Not charged on makam or a money-market fund.
- Dividends on Israeli securities: no fee printed. Inactivity: none printed.
- Transfer out: **5 ₪ per transfer** to one's own account at another regulated body, plus TASE costs. On closing, at most 10 ₪, and all closing fees together at most 40 ₪.
- Data or platform: nothing required.
- Current account: **up to 5.50 ₪ a month** as a minimum, or 1.65 ₪ per direct action, or a track of 10 or 25 ₪.

Illustration, inferred: a 67,000 ₪ account pays 402 ₪ a year in custody (0.6%).

**Case 2: an ETF in the US**
- Trading: **0.3%, at least 24 $, at most 6,750 $** (p.14).
- On top come part 11's costs:
  - broker "עד 2 סנט לכל ע.נ.";
  - custodian in the US "עד 3 דולר לנייר", but "פעילות בקרנות – לפי חיוב בפועל";
  - "מיסים והיטלים". These and the fund custodian costs are **not limited**.
- Conversion: **0.16%, at least 5.76 $, at most 2,400 $** (direct). Spread: **not limited by the documents**.
- Custody: **0.2% a quarter (0.8% a year)**, at most 7,400 ₪ per security and 23,000 ₪ per deposit.
- Transfer out: 5 ₪, plus custodian costs.
