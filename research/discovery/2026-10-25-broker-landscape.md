# Broker landscape: where an Israeli private person can buy funds and ETFs

Research note, 2026-10-25. Asked by the founder: "any more brokers we should check? like leumi invest, meitav trade, altshuller trade?" Scope: every place an Israeli resident can open an account to buy (a) Israeli mutual funds (קרנות נאמנות, including tracking funds), (b) ETFs on the Tel Aviv exchange (קרנות סל), (c) foreign ETFs (US or London). Banks and their trading apps or sub-brands, non-bank TASE members, and foreign brokers that take Israeli residents.

Method: the TASE member list (il-finance BRK-32, read in Chrome, checked 2026-10-25) is the register for Israeli brokers; each provider's own site was then opened with curl on 2026-10-25. Aggregators (TheMarker/Supermarker, Funder, review sites) were used only to learn that a brand exists, never for a figure; every row below cites the provider's own page or the TASE list. Anything that could not be read on an official page is marked **unconfirmed**.

Legend for "Sells": **a** Israeli mutual funds, **b** TASE ETFs, **c** foreign ETFs. "Held" means a snapshot under `data/fees/`.

## 4. Answers to the founder's examples

- **Leumi Invest**: no such brand was found. Leumi sells through Leumi Trade (same tariff we hold) and through **Pepper**, whose own securities prices sit in an annex of the same Leumi tariff and are not in our data. Pepper is the Leumi brand worth adding.
- **Meitav Trade**: its tariff exists after all, linked as "תעריפון" from the Meitav Trade about page ("גרסת 04/2026"). Collected into `data/fees/meitav-trade@2026-10-25.json`. Its list prices are high (0.3% on TASE trades, custody 0.15% a quarter with a 270 NIS minimum); the low prices it advertises are promotions, not tariff.
- **Altshuler Trade**: already held; the file we read is still the one served (last modified May 2025).

## 5. What to collect next, by likely impact

Ranked by how many people use it and whether its pricing could beat what we hold for a buy-and-hold saver (a monthly purchase of a tracking fund, a TASE ETF or a US/UCITS ETF).

1. **Fair** (a, including tracking funds): 0 NIS a trade on open funds and 0.08% (min 3 NIS) on tracking funds, no monthly fee and no custody line. For the site's core case (an S&P 500 tracking fund bought monthly) this could be the cheapest route we know of, and we hold nothing for it. One HTML page, server-rendered, readable by curl.
2. **Pepper (Leumi annex א')**: a flat 4 NIS per TASE order up to 30,000 NIS, with third-party costs absorbed, beats every percentage tariff for small monthly orders. Leumi is the largest or second-largest bank, so many readers can reach it. The lines are in the Leumi PDF we already cite; they need a new Leumi snapshot that adds the Pepper lines as a `band` ("לקוחות PEPPER"). Open point: whether TASE ETFs count as "מניות" under this line.
3. **Blink** (a, c): up to 10 trades a month free, then 1 cent a share with a 1.5 USD minimum, no custody fee. Strong for small US-ETF buyers, but the FX conversion rate is set by Blink with no spread printed, and TASE ETFs and tracking funds are not offered. Needs the per-share unit (already in the schema) and an `availability` entry for `etf_units: false`.
4. **Meitav Trade**: done today (list tariff 04/2026). Open: whether its TASE line covers open mutual-fund units (the tariff has no mutual-fund line, so the site will treat mutual funds as unpriced there), and the promotional tracks its site describes without figures.
5. **Firstrade**: $0 commissions on US ETFs for Israeli residents. It matters only for readers willing to hold US-domiciled ETFs and wire USD abroad; the tax and estate effects of US-domiciled funds are rule questions for il-finance, not stated here.
6. **Other banks' population annexes (annex א')**: the same gap as Pepper may exist at other banks (young-customer or digital tracks). Not checked; worth one pass over the PDFs we already cite.
7. **FIBI HighTech 10 and similar club promotions**: time-limited (to 31.12.2026) and at the bank's discretion; not list prices, so they belong in "offers you can ask for", not in the tariff data.
8. **eToro, Schwab, Trading 212, Saxo**: eligibility for Israelis unconfirmed on official pages; needs a person in a browser.
