# Bullet bond funds: the official term, and whether they can be bought in Israel

Issue #159, research half. Checked 2026-11-01 unless a line says otherwise. In this report "read" means I read the words in the document cited. "Inferred" means I concluded it from what I read. Nothing here was committed.

## 1. The official term

**No authority defines a product called "אג"ח בולט", as a fund or as a bond.** What exists is a concept in the law, and an index name at the exchange.

**Recommended term for the fund: קרן אג"ח שתקופת קיומה מוגבלת** (a bond fund whose period of existence is limited).

**How strong the basis is:**

| Part of the term | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| "תקופת הקיום מוגבלת" as what the fund is and what happens at the end | Law and regulation, quoted below | Strong for the meaning |
| The whole phrase as the name of a class of fund | None. No document uses it | None. It describes; no authority gave it as a name |
| "אג"ח" | Classification headings seen in prospectuses ("אג"ח בארץ - חברות והמרה") | Description only |

The founder should know that I did not find an official name to hand over. This is the regulation's own condition, written about a bond fund. The word "בולט" cannot vanish from the site altogether, because fund names contain it ("אינדקס Bullet", "תל-בונד בולט").

**The alternative, if the founder prefers the market's word:** "קרן מחקה על מדד בולט". "מדד בולט" is the exchange's word, read only in reprints of one notice, and "קרן מחקה" is the law's. It would not cover Yalin's funds by their own words, since they say "אג"ח לפדיון", though their index editor's text was not checked for "בולט". All funds found that wind up are tracking funds.

### The candidates, by authority

**Law. A concept, not a class.** [Joint Investment Trust Law, ISA consolidated copy](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-628ccfb552dac/Hashka01.pdf):
- File p.6, s.5(א)(10): "תקופת קיומה של הקרן, אם הוגבלה התקופה".
- File p.67, s.103(1): "נסתיימה תקופת קיומה של הקרן כפי שנקבע בהסכם הקרן (להלן - תום תקופת הקרן)".

**Regulation. A required heading.** [קובץ התקנות 7646, 12.4.2016](https://olaw.org.il/takanot/takanot-7646.pdf#page=32) (third-party host of the gazette), file p.32 item (12): "אם תקופת קיום הקרן מוגבלת - תחת הכותרת "תקופת קיום הקרן", יצוין הדבר, וכן תקופת קיום הקרן והודעה כי עם סיום התקופה תפורק הקרן". The page falls in the prospectus-particulars amendment (gazette pp.1022 to 1034, from the issue's contents).

**ISA. Nothing found.** No fund use of "בולט" in the 57 ISA documents in the library, including the 2024 and 2025 fund reports. The classification headings seen have no such heading. One search limited to isa.gov.il and gov.il found no staff position. The ISA's full heading list and investor pages could not be read by script. The tracking directive only treats a bond index's exposure to bonds going to redemption ([mutualfund091024.pdf](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-62bd445f910eb/mutualfund091024.pdf#page=5), p.5 s.9).

**TASE. An index name, not a defined fund or bond.** TASE notice of 18.3.2025, read in reprints ([Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/178662), [Mondo Visione](https://mondovisione.com/news/tel-aviv-stock-exchange-launches-3-new-bullet-indices-tel-bond-bullet-2029-ils-2025318)). TASE's own copy and its index rules were not reachable.
- "מדדי בולט נועדו לאפשר למשקיעים לקבע תשואה לפדיון של תיק אג"ח בעל מאפיינים מוגדרים. בשונה מרוב המדדים, למדדי בולט יש מועד סופי וכאשר נפדית האג"ח האחרונה הנכללת בו, המדד מפסיק להתקיים".
- "קרנות הבולט הפכו לאחד ממוצרי ההשקעה הפופולאריים", in passing, with no definition.
- TASE's 2025 periodic report and 2024 annual review have no fund-sense use.

**Rejected: "קרן מועדים קבועים"** (s.47, MF-08). It means fixed offer and redemption dates. The funds here are open funds with daily orders (Yalin: "סוג הקרן: קרן פתוחה"; Kesem's table: ב'-ה' 16:00). Funder's tag "מועדים קבועים" on such funds is unreliable.

### The single bond: a separate concept, with no name

The issue's other meaning. No authority read has a word for a bond that repays all its principal at the end. So there is no term to recommend, only plain wording, for one line in the bond guides and not a product name:
- Government bonds, [תקנות מילווה המדינה, קובץ התקנות 6457, 2006](https://free-justice.openapi.gov.il/free/moj/portal/rest/searchpredefinedapi/v1/SearchPredefinedApi/Documents/yC2TN9CtZzPO8Yj5JpRxmWhyAjAO9sieYV7GsTkIsdY=#page=3), file p.3, reg.6: "וייפדו במלואן ביום תשלום הקרן".
- A company's own series, Migdal Capital Raising, in Migdal's 2024 financial report p.249: "קרן אגרות החוב תעמודנה לפירעון בתשלום אחד אשר ישולם ביום 31 בדצמבר 2037". It has no public link in the library, so it is usage only and not in the rule base.

A note on the wording (mine): in Hebrew "קרן" means both fund and principal, so a sentence about the bond should say "קרן האג"ח".

The ISA's bonds page and TASE's knowledge pages could not be read (section 8).

**So which does the site mean?** The fund, as the issue guessed, but narrower than the issue's wording: **a fund with a stated end that winds up**, set apart from funds with a year in the name that roll on. Both look alike by name. The single bond gets a line, not a concept of its own.

## 4. What happens at the date

### Israeli funds

**The law, for any fund with a limited period** (MF-60; [file pp.69-71](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-628ccfb552dac/Hashka01.pdf#page=69)):
- Wind-up date is "תום תקופת קיום הקרן כפי שנקבע בהסכם הקרן" (s.107(ב)(1)).
- The manager serves as liquidator (s.105(א)).
- Notice comes 60 to 30 days before (s.106(א)), and offering stops from the first pricing day after it (s.106(א1)).
- The manager may depart from the policy while selling (s.106(א2)).
- "מפרק קרן יפדה את יחידות הקרן במועד הפירוק" (s.108(ה)).
- Wind-up expenses come out of the liquidator's capped pay, and sale commissions out of the fund (s.105(ג)).

**What one fund's own document says**: [Yalin 5137492, annual report](https://mayafiles.tase.co.il/rpdf/1629001-1630000/P1629790-00.pdf#page=1), pp.1-2:
1. "בשנת 2026, אשר הינה שנת פדיון הקרן, האג"ח שבמדד יפדו בהתאם למתודולוגית המדד וכך תבוא הקרן לפירוקה".
2. As each series leaves the index, a makam series nearest year end replaces it at equal weight.
3. On the last trading day all makam leave.
4. After the year's end the manager sells what remains and holds shekel cash.
5. Payment "יהיה לא יאוחר מתאריך ה-15/01/2027".

TASE describes the same step as a "filler" for its indices. Kesem's document says only that the fund winds up. Other funds' payment dates were read on Funder's copies (5136882, 15.1.2028; 5138276, 15.1.2029).

**Inferred:** the money arrives as cash in the securities account, and the reader decides what to do next. No real wind-up was read, so this and the timing are unconfirmed (section 8).
