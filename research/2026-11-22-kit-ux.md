# The kit: less clutter, easier to use (research, 22.11.2026)

## Summary

The slider field is cluttered mainly because the end values patch a deeper problem. The slider runs right to left, and Hebrew readers expect sliders to run left to right. In the one study of Hebrew speakers, 90% preferred a left-to-right slider. The chart under the sliders also runs left to right. Run the slider left to right and drop the end values. Show each figure once, with no number in the reset. Put money on a stepped (non-linear) scale, so that 10,000 ₪ isn't stuck at the track's edge. That takes the index page's four sliders from 12–16 numbers down to 4. Across the kit the same habits repeat: a figure or unit said twice, many colour roles, and extras shown at rest. Each of these is small, but together they make the kit read like a form rather than a tool. The evidence is strong that sliders suit rough values and typed boxes suit exact ones. It is moderate on direction (one preference study) and on non-linear scales (usability testing, not trials).

**How I worked.** I read the guidelines, design-system §3.3 and §11, and the kit code (`fields.tsx`, `knobs.tsx`, `callout.tsx`, `help-mark.tsx`). I searched the user-lab reports for "slider". On the web I looked at, in order: NN/g, Baymard, CHI papers, Apple, Material and GOV.UK. I could not run `shot.mjs` or FlareSolverr, because this session had no shell. What I *observed* comes from the existing 390 px shot of the index sliders (a 390 px screenshot of the index page's sliders), older gallery shots, and the code. The Material 2 and 3 pages need scripts and didn't load, so Material 1's text stands in for them. Lines marked **(inference)** are my own reasoning.

## 1. The slider field (`RangeField`, `KnobStrip`)

**Observed.** Each field shows a label, a value, a unit, and the value at each end. Once a value changes, the reset adds a fifth figure ("↺ חזרה ל־10,000 ₪"). So four fields show 12 numbers, or 16 once they are changed. "שנים" appears both as the label and as the unit. The 10,000 ₪ default sits 2% along the 0–500,000 track, against its edge. One simulated reader wrote: "the first knob is already at the far right, so I doubt it goes up to my 30,000". That reader took the right end as full ([lab report][lab1]). Another lab run found the slider showing 800 for a typed 750 ([lessons][lab2]). Simulated readers on phones left the sliders alone ("I wasn't going to fiddle with them on my phone", [lab][lab3]). Simulated readers are weak evidence.

**What the evidence says.**
- **Sliders suit rough values; exact values are typed.** "Whenever the exact value matters, sliders are not okay." A precise value is hard to hit on touch ([NN/g 2015][nn-slider]; expert guidance, strong as convention). The amount today is a figure people know exactly. The years and the return are what-ifs.
- **The slider and the box always show the same value,** within 0.1 s. A mark at the default and a reset are worth having ([NN/g 2017][nn-knobs]). The 750/800 mismatch breaks this.
- **Price-like ranges need a non-linear scale,** and the live value must show while dragging ([Baymard 2015][baymard]; testing on 18 sites, moderate, old). Apple: when a slider is non-linear, labels along it give context ([Apple HIG][hig]).
- **Marks along a slider pull answers toward them** ([Matejka et al., CHI 2016][matejka]; 2,000 people, strong for ticks, weaker for end labels).
- **Labels under the track get hidden by the finger** ([NN/g 2015][nn-slider]).
- **Direction.** Apple, Material and Microsoft all put the minimum on the leading side, which is the right in Hebrew ([HIG][hig]; [Material 1][m1-bidi]). But users disagree. In a survey of 234 Hebrew speakers, **90% preferred the left-to-right slider**. 91% preferred a left-to-right trend graph and 87% a left-to-right year range ([Goldenberg & Tractinsky, CHI 2021][gt2021]). That is one exploratory survey of preference, not performance. The sample was self-selected and nearly all fluent in English. Still, the effect is large and it is the only direct evidence. Hebrew readers' sense of which way numbers grow is weak and mixed ([Shaki & Fischer 2008][shaki]; [Zohar-Shai et al. 2017][zohar]).

**Weighing the guideline.** Its worry is real: an RTL slider above an LTR chart is ambiguous. But the end values treat the symptom and cost 8 numbers. Running the slider left to right removes the cause. The fill then grows the same way as the chart. **(inference)**

**Three low-clutter designs** (each shows 4 numbers at rest):

- **A. Quiet slider** (recommended).
  - Label and value on one line, with the slider under them. No end values.
  - The slider runs left to right (`dir="ltr"` on the input).
  - Money moves on a stepped scale. For example, 0–20,000 in steps of 1,000, 20,000–100,000 in steps of 5,000, and 100,000–500,000 in steps of 25,000, each a third of the track. 10,000 ₪ then sits about a sixth of the way along.
  - The thumb follows a typed 750 exactly.
  - The reset is "↺" plus a word, with no figure.
  - *Loses:* the ceiling isn't shown (the box takes more anyway), and the reset doesn't show what it returns to.
  - *Sure:* moderate.
- **B. Type what you know, drag what you explore.**
  - The amount today and the monthly deposit become plain typed boxes. The years and the return keep a quiet slider.
  - *Loses:* dragging the deposit to see "what if I put in more", and the four fields no longer look alike.
  - *Sure:* moderate. Fits the evidence best, costs play.
- **C. Numbers in a sentence.**
  - "10,000 ₪ היום, 500 ₪ בחודש, 10 שנים, 7% בשנה", where each figure is a button that opens its own box and slider (the kit's `InputSlot` grown into a control).
  - *Loses:* an extra tap, and the figures must clearly look tappable: weak signals cost 22% more time ([NN/g 2017][nn-flat]).
  - *Sure:* low. Untested here.

A value shown only while dragging (Material's bubble) adds nothing here: the box already shows it.

## 2. Other pieces

**Reset on every field (`ResetToStart`).** "חזרה ל־12,000 ₪" puts a second number on the field. *Change:* "↺ איפוס", with the figure in its spoken name. On a slider, a small mark at the default ([NN/g 2017][nn-knobs]). *Cost:* the default is seen only on use. *Sure:* moderate.

**A unit that repeats the label.** "שנים … 10 שנים" (observed). *Change:* drop the unit where the label names it, as wording.md already asks of chips. *Cost:* none. *Sure:* high.

**`Callout`.** Each note shows four signals: fill, frame, a 4 px edge and an icon. GOV.UK: use such boxes "very sparingly", because on busy pages people miss them ([GOV.UK inset text][govuk-inset]; guidance, no research cited). *Change:* keep the fill for caution and blocked only. An info note gets the edge and icon alone. One box per block. *Cost:* info notes stand out less. *Sure:* low to moderate.

**`HelpMark` "?".** Most people never open a tip. A tip is short, and it doesn't open an overlay ([NN/g 2026][nn-tips]; expert review). Today every "?" opens the full sheet. *Change:* a one-sentence explanation opens as a small popover beside the mark. The sheet stays for terms that carry a source or rules. *Cost:* "?" has two behaviours. *Sure:* moderate.

**`Tag`, `LabelChip`, `PartChip`, chips.** All of these are pills, but some respond to a tap and some don't. Weak signals of what can be tapped cost 22% more time and 25% more fixations ([NN/g 2017][nn-flat]; eyetracking, n=71). *Change:* keep the pill shape for things that respond to a tap. Show a kind that doesn't respond as an icon and words, with no pill. *Cost:* restyling tags. *Sure:* moderate **(inference from the study)**.

**Not changed.** I found nothing worth changing in the sheets (X, Back, one at a time: they already match [NN/g][nn-sheet]), in the folds and `PhoneFold` (the summary line matches [NN/g][nn-acc]), or in the tables and phone rows. I didn't see the chart controls, `/deal` or the guide in use, so I have nothing on them.

## 3. Patterns across pieces

- **Every number once.** End values, reset figures, and a sentence that echoes the box beside it (`InputSlot`) each repeat a figure. Each figure should have one place.
- **One unit, one label.** Say it in the label or in the unit, not both.
- **Underlines everywhere.** A breakdown in which every figure is underlined stops telling anything apart ([signal-to-noise, NN/g][nn-snr]). The column-head rule ("a column whose figures share one state carries the mark in its head") could extend to breakdowns and receipts. **(inference)**
- **Colour roles.** I count 5 number states, 8 tag tones, 6 label families, 4 access steps, the cases' yellow, the fee colours and the chart colours. Each new hue weakens the others. Grey could carry kinds, with colour kept for what needs attention (caution, blocked, entered). **(inference)**
- **Weights.** At rest, one slider field uses three text sizes and three inks. Two of each would do. **(inference)**
- **Default versus on demand.** Show the essentials first and keep extras one step away ([NN/g 2006][nn-pd]). Reset figures, ranges and long explanations are extras.
- **Form or tool.** A stack of labelled boxes, underlines, resets and messages under them reads as a form. A tool shows the result first, puts a few controls beside it, and answers live. The site already answers live; removing the extras is what makes the controls look like a tool. **(inference)**

**Guideline affected.** This would mean changing `guidelines/design.md`'s line "A slider shows the value at each of its ends". The proposal: "A slider runs left to right, like the charts, and shows its value once, in its box." The founder decides it. Nothing in the il-finance rule base is involved.
