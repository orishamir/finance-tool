# Accumulating foreign funds: taxed only at sale?

Date: 2026-11-04. Issue #75. Rule changed: TXF-11 and open question 1 (narrowed), in `.claude/skills/il-finance/references/tax-securities-foreign.md`. Nothing in `data/`, code or the site was touched.

## Answer

Still open, and narrower than before. No published Tax Authority text says in words that an individual with a small stake in an accumulating foreign fund is taxed only when selling and on distributions. The new evidence comes from the fund managers' own documents, and all of it points to the sale-only reading. Several foreign ETF managers registered accumulating UCITS classes on TASE. Four of their Israeli prospectus annexes report a Tax Authority ruling given to the fund (1.7.2019, 24.1.2021, 9.2.2022), and a fifth cites the 2019 letter. Under that law and those rulings, each annex lists two taxed events: a sale, and a distribution "ככל שתתבצע". None of them taxes the income the fund keeps. This is institution-strength evidence: the rulings are not published, the annexes disclaim being an authoritative reading, and they cover funds registered on TASE, not funds bought at a foreign broker. Nothing found says the opposite. The one contrary sentence, circular 7/2003 s.4.4, ties its look-through from 2007 to a premise that Amendment 147 overtook (an inference; the circular was not found withdrawn).

## Searched and not found

- All 750 published taxation rulings, full text. A scripted search by category collected every row, then each file was downloaded. Phrases searched: קרן נאמנות זרה, קרנות נאמנות זרות, קרן חוץ, ETF, UCITS, קרן סל, צוברת, רווחים שלא חולקו, mutual fund, להשקעות משותפות, accumulat, PFIC, קרן זרה, השקעות הקרן, look through. 386 files have a text layer and none matched. 362 (mostly 2006 to 2008) have no Unicode text and no OCR tool is installed, so they were judged by title, and none has a title on a foreign fund. The fund rulings named above are not published.
- The gov.il Tax Authority policies list for 2002 to 2011, by title. No circular on funds or capital-market taxation came after 7/2003 and 22/2002. 7/2003 is still listed and is not marked withdrawn.
- A judgment, by a second Hebrew web search on free sources: none. Nevo and Takdin are still unreachable.
- An English professional publication: none. Barnea's hedge fund guide covers transparent partnerships only.
- Ruling 5257/26 (2026, an individual holding US private funds) is about a family company and foreign tax credit, not a fund holder's timing.

Each search is in `research/library/tried.jsonl`. The five annexes and form 1324 are filed in the library.
