## What the founder chose

The indices page ranks every house on its price list (the most its binding documents allow). What a person really pays can be lower: they negotiated, they are a student, they joined during an offer. The founder chose option א of `research/specs/2026-11-04-own-broker/mockups.html` (screenshot `mockups-1440.png`, the first section and the 390 px one): a panel that opens over the comparison, starts from one house's price list, and lets the person change only what they got. The row they add is ranked like any other.

Then, on 2026-11-04, three additions:

1. **Groups and joining offers stay out of the ranking**, as now (the sub-rows under each house). Each sub-row gets a link "חשבון בתנאים שלכם" that opens the same panel with the house's price list and that group's prices already in the person's column. At the top of the panel, a small "start from" choice: the price list, or any group or offer of that house. Prices filled from a group's document are sourced (grey underline, the group's document as source), never blue: nobody typed them. A value the person changes becomes blue (entered).
2. **A price for a time.** A group or offer price usually ends (Leumi's student price while a student, 21 to 35; its opening offer of no custody for a year). The panel has one field "until when", in years, filled where the document prints an end (Leumi's opening offer: one year) and empty otherwise (empty means the whole plan). After it, the row returns to the house's price list. The engine prices it period by period.
3. **The stacked cost bars are interactive and glide.** Pointing at a part (and tapping it on a phone) shows its name, amount, share and where its price comes from; when a field changes, every part's width and figure glide to the new value, and a part that falls to zero shrinks to nothing rather than vanishing. And every claim in the panel has its source: list prices link to the price list page, group prices to the group's document, computed figures carry the computed state and their formula.

**What customers paid on average** (the mockup's middle column): the exchange's terms forbid copying its figures, and the site's rule is to show that the figure exists and link to it, not the number, until the exchange consents (`docs/product.md` D22). So the column shows no number: one link per panel to the exchange's fee calculator (`https://market.tase.co.il/he/market_data/trading_fees`, the link the site already reads from rule BRK-37 in `site/app/lib/fees.server.ts`), with words that say the exchange publishes what this house's customers paid on average. No data job collects the averages. Leave a single obvious place in the code where the numbers would go if consent comes.

## Job 1: the price underneath (engine, data, saved state)

No visible page change in this job beyond what the tests need.

1. **Numbers on group prices.** Each group price in `data/fees/*@2026-11-04.json` has only words (`price_he`, "0.075% לרבעון"). Proposed: `rate_pct` (number), `per` ("trade" | "month" | "quarter" | "year"), `min` and `max` (in `currency`), `currency` ("ILS" | "USD"), `exempt: true` for "פטור", `for_months` where the price itself says how long ("פטור, לשנה" is 12). The validator (`packages/pipeline/src/validate.ts`) checks that every number in `value` appears in `price_he` or in the source's quote. Change no other field of any record. 132 group prices across 12 houses today.
2. **A price for a time (a new formula, F-101, in `docs/method.md` section 4 beside F-63 and F-14).** An entered tariff may carry a period: the first tariff applies from month 1 to month `u`, the second (the house's list tariff) after it. A trade in month `m` pays the price of month `m`; custody and fixed fees charged in month `m` pay that month's price (F-14's period phase as now); the sale at the horizon `T` pays the price at `T`; a minimum is the minimum of the tariff in force. Write the formula with a worked example from Leumi's record (student custody 0.075% a quarter, list 0.15% a quarter). Build it in the securities ledger (`packages/engine/src/ledgers/securities.ts`, the only one the indices page runs) and `EnteredTariff` in `packages/engine/src/providers/workbench.ts`; a tariff without a period runs exactly as today (every existing test keeps its numbers). Tests: the expected values come from a short independent script you write beside the test (not from the engine's own output), including `u` = 0, `u` past the horizon, a period ending between two custody charges, and a monthly plan. This is calculation work: careful, small, and the lead sends it to the finance reviewer afterwards.
3. **An own account built on a house.** `CustomBroker` in `site/app/lib/custom-entries.ts` today holds six flat fees. Extend it, keeping every saved entry valid with its meaning unchanged:
   - `base`: the house (its provider id, so a newer price list is followed), and, when started from one, the group entry (its name for display, and its `value`s copied in at creation with their source, so a later record change does not silently move the person's row);

   - `untilYears`: null for the whole plan, else the period of item 2 for the group's part.
   The tariff the engine runs is built in layers: the house's list tariff (the engine's own `buildTariff` at level `list`, so no second reading of a price list exists), then the group's values on the lines they name, then the person's values. A group value replaces only what it states (a rate without a minimum keeps the list's minimum: silence is not zero). Where the group price does not name funds (`names_fund: false`), the line is still filled, and the panel will say so beside it (job 2). A house that is no longer on the site: the row keeps only what the person typed and says so. Saved state (`site/app/lib/state.ts`, export and import): old entries load unchanged; a test proves an old entry gives the same figure as before; bump the version only if you must, with a migration and its test.
