## Over all the parts

**Colours that mean different things in different places**

- **Yellow:**
  - Israeli ETF (Parts 1 and 2);
  - "דמי משמרת" (Part 3 Y, Part 4 X, Part 7 Y);
  - the *hedged* fund (Part 6 X);
  - both "חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט"ח" (Part 5 Y, Part 8 X);
  - the "התעריפון" period (Part 7 Y).
- **Teal:**
  - account ticks (Part 2 X);
  - Israeli tracker fund (Part 1 Y, the "קרן מחקה" chip in Part 5 Y);
  - "without hedging" (Part 6);
  - "מחירי הקבוצה" (Part 7 Y).
- **Pink:**
  - Israeli tracker in Part 1 X and Part 2 Y, but **foreign** fund in Part 1 Y;
  - account ticks in Part 2 Y;
  - tax warnings in Parts 5 and 8.
  - Within version Y alone, the Israeli tracker is teal in Part 1 and pink in Part 2.
- **Green:** foreign fund on the Tel Aviv exchange (Part 1 Y), but "צוברת" (Parts 5 Y and 8 X).
- **Red:** a normal fee, "דמי ניהול חשבון" (Part 4 X), but a warning in Part 8.

Neither version is consistent throughout. X and Y even trade approaches: words on the bars are in X for Part 3 but in Y for Part 4, and coloured chips are in Y for Part 5 but in X for Part 8.

**Overall I'd rather use Y.** It wins Parts 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8. The main reasons are that its Part 7 numbers add up and that red is kept for real warnings. X wins only Parts 3 and 6.

**What I would still change**

1. **One colour, one meaning, on the whole page.**
   - The Israeli tracker should be the same colour everywhere.
   - Yellow should not mean "ETF", "custody fee", "hedged" and "price list" all at once.
2. **Write the words on the cost bars *and* show a legend under every bar chart**, including Part 3. Name the small pieces too, perhaps on hover.
3. **Pull apart the colours that are close:** teal from mint green in Part 1, and light pink from magenta in Parts 1 and 2. Make the marks bigger, since the Tel Aviv triangle and the ring legend are too small for me.
4. **Keep red only for real unknowns and warnings**, and give opposites like "חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט"ח" different colours.
5. **Explain or remove the diagonal slashes** in the Part 4 bars.
