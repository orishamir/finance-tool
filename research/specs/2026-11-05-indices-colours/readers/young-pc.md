- Hollow consistently means hedged (parts 1 and 6).

2. **Fee colours:** Don't make fees yellow or teal, since those already mean fund types. Use a separate palette for fees.
3. **Part 1:** Keep X's triangle for "קרן חוץ בבורסה בתל אביב", or make the teal and green much further apart.

6. **Part 2:** Use a neutral colour for the account strip.
7. **Part 7 numbers:** Check the "1,757" / "₪ 263" figures in X against Y's "3,100" / "₪ 302". They can't both be right.
