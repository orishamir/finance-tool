# Fresh readers on the colour table, round 1 (2026-11-05)

Three readers who knew nothing of the project (`research/audit/tools/fresh-reader.mjs`, brief `brief.json`) compared
eight parts of the indices page in two versions, X and Y, without being told which is today's and which is proposed.
Which letter was which changed from part to part (`key.json`). Reports: `young-pc.md`, `pension-pc.md`, `careful-pc.md`.

## What they saw

- **The dot plot's pinks (part 1, all three).** Rose (Israeli tracking fund) and magenta (foreign fund) read as one
  pink family, "they mean opposite things: Israeli versus foreign"; the hollow ring is pinkish too. The triangle that
  marks a foreign fund on the Tel Aviv exchange is lost inside the clusters. The palette check passes this pair
  (18.1 / 17.8); the eye does not. In today's colours teal and mint green were "close but separable".
- **The hollow ring has two meanings** (hedged, or tax not yet clear), so a reader cannot tell which a ring is.
- **The account strip's ticks (part 2, proposed)** are rose because the fund is the same tracking fund at every
  account; two readers read the rose as "account", and nothing on the strip says why.
- **Words on the fee bar win (parts 3 and 4, six of six).** "'דמי ניהול חשבון' written on the biggest segment tells me
  exactly why מיטב טרייד and אקסלנס come out worst." But the small parts carry no word, and all three asked that every
  part be named (one asked for a legend as well).
- **Red only for real warnings (part 8, three of three).** In today's chip row "מס דיבידנד בתוך הקרן", a known cost,
  "looks as alarming as" "מס בצבירה: לא ידוע", a real unknown.
- **Coloured chips mislead (part 5).** Opposites share a colour ("חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט״ח" both gold; "צוברת"
  and "מחלקת דיבידנד" both green), and the colours "suggest judgements that aren't there". Those who still preferred
  today's chips did so only for the red tax chip.
- **Hedging (part 6).** Two colours are easier inside the chart; hollow for hedged "matches the hollow ring in part 1, so
  the idea carries over". The tiny legend markers (a dot against a ring) were hard for the 61-year-old.
- **The own-price sheet (part 7) is confounded.** The proposed crop's figures do not add up (1,118 + 1,757 against a
  total of 4,278 ₪ and "263 ₪ פחות"; today's crop shows 1,101 + 3,100 = 4,239 ₪ and "302 ₪"): the two crops were not
  taken in the same state, and the proposed one leaves a part unnamed. Its result says nothing yet about the colours.
- **Today's yellow means five things** (Israeli ETF, custody fee, hedged, both currency chips, the price-list period),
  and all three noticed it. That is what the rule of one meaning per colour fixes.

## What it means for the table

The proposal holds for the fee bars, the chips and red. It fails, by eye, on the fund kinds in the dot plot and the
strips, which is exactly the founder's condition ("make sure the graph colors are still differentiable from one
another"). Round 2 revises those, splits the hollow ring, names every part of a fee bar without hover, redraws the
sheet from one state, and goes back to fresh readers before the founder sees it.
