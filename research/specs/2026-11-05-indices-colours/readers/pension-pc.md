## 1. "כל הזוגות של חשבון וקרן" (dot chart)

**What the marks mean**
- **X:** Light pink dot is "קרן מחקה ישראלית" and yellow dot is "קרן סל ישראלית". The magenta dot and the magenta triangle share one legend item, "קרן חוץ: שנקנית בחו"ל, או ▲ בבורסה בתל אביב", so I read the circle as bought abroad and the triangle as bought on the Tel Aviv exchange. A hollow pink ring is "מנוטרלת מט"ח, או קרן שהמס עליה עוד לא ברור". A white ring is "הבחירה שלכם". The dashed line is "הדרך ממנה לזוג הטוב ביותר".
- **Y:** Teal is "קרן מחקה ישראלית", yellow is "קרן סל ישראלית", green is "קרן חוץ בבורסה בתל אביב", magenta is "קרן שנקנית בחו"ל", and a hollow teal ring is the hedged or unclear-tax fund.
- **Unsure:** The hollow ring stands for two different things ("מנוטרלת מט"ח" or "המס... לא ברור"), so I can't tell which one a given ring is.

**Hard to tell apart:** In X, the light pink (Israeli mimicking fund), the magenta (foreign fund) and the hollow pink ring are all pinks. The tiny triangle is lost inside the clusters, for example in the לאומי row. In Y, teal and green are close but still separable.

**Easier: Y.** The four kinds of fund get four different hues, so I can see at once that the top of פסגות is teal (an Israeli mimicking fund).

## 2. Result card, "כמה כל בחירה משנה"

**What the marks mean**

- **Y:** There is a legend: yellow "קרן סל ישראלית", pink "קרן מחקה ישראלית", magenta "קרן חוץ". The account line is all pink because every account is holding the same מחקה fund.

**Hard to tell apart:** In Y, pink and magenta in the small legend squares. Also, at first the pink account ticks look like they mean "account" rather than "fund type".

**Easier: Y.** It has a legend, and it shows that the best fund (ילין, pink) is a different kind from most of the yellow ones.

## The whole page

**Yes, several colours mean different things in different parts:**
- **Yellow:** "קרן סל ישראלית" (part 1), "a fund" (part 2 X), hedged fund (part 6 X), "דמי משמרת" (parts 3 Y, 4 X, 7 Y), "התעריפון" (part 7 Y), and "מנוטרלת מט"ח" / "חשיפה לדולר" chips (parts 5 Y, 8 X).
- **Teal:** "an account" (part 2 X), "קרן מחקה ישראלית" (part 1 Y), unhedged (part 6), "מחירי הקבוצה" (part 7 Y), and also the selection, radio buttons and the "הוספה להשוואה" button.
- **Pink/magenta:** a מחקה fund (part 1 X, part 2 Y), a foreign fund (part 1), and the swap/holding chips (part 5 Y).
- **Green:** a foreign fund on the Tel Aviv exchange (part 1 Y) but "צוברת" (part 5 Y).
- **Red:** the account management fee (part 4 X) but a warning (part 8).

**Overall I'd rather use Y.** I preferred it in parts 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 and 8. Its approach is fee names written on the bars or in a legend, hollow = hedged, and red only for warnings.

**What I'd still change:**
1. Give each colour one meaning across the whole page. Keep yellow only for "קרן סל ישראלית", not for fees or for the tariff period.
2. Keep teal for the interface (selection and buttons) and don't use it for data.
3. Always write the fee name on the bar, as in part 4 Y, and also add a legend in parts 3 and 4.
4. Keep the chips grey, and colour only real warnings such as "מס בצבירה: לא ידוע".
5. Split the hollow ring's two meanings ("מנוטרלת מט"ח" versus "המס לא ברור").
6. Check why part 7 shows 4,278 / 263 ₪ in one version and 4,239 / 302 ₪ in the other.
