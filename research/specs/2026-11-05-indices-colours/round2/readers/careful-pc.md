## Part 1 – "כל הזוגות של חשבון וקרן" (dot chart, one row per account, one dot per fund)

**1. What each colour or mark means**

**X:**
- White filled dot = "קרן מחקה ישראלית".
- Yellow = "קרן סל ישראלית".
- Pink = "קרן חוץ".
- Hollow white ring = "מנוטרלת מט״ח: מוצגת ולא מדורגת".
- Pink dot with a small red lock = "המס עליה עוד לא ברור: מוצגת ולא מדורגת".
- ★ = "הזוג שמשאיר הכי הרבה".
- Teal ring = "הבחירה שלכם (כאן: לדוגמה)".
- Teal dashed line = "הדרך ממנה לזוג הטוב ביותר".

**Y:**
- Teal = Israeli index-tracking fund ("מחקה").
- Yellow = Israeli ETF ("סל").
- Mint green = "קרן חוץ בבורסה בתל אביב".
- Pink = "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל".
- Thin hollow teal ring = "מנוטרלת מט״ח, או קרן שהמס עליה עוד לא ברור".
- Big white ring = "הבחירה שלכם".
- There is no lock and no ★ in the legend.

**Z:** like Y, except:
- Pink = "קרן חוץ בבורסה בתל אביב".
- Orange = "קרן חוץ שנקנית בחו״ל".
- Orange with a lock = the tax is not yet clear.
- ★ is back in the legend.

**Where I was unsure:**
- **In X,** a foreign fund traded in Tel Aviv and one bought abroad are both pink. In the "לאומי" row, the pink dot near −6,000 and the pink dots near −15,000 look like the same kind of fund. Only the tiny lock separates them.
- **In Y,** the legend says the hollow ring covers both hedged funds and funds whose tax is unclear. Yet the foreign funds bought abroad are drawn as filled pink dots, not rings, so I could not tell which dots are "not ranked".

**2. Hard to tell apart**
- In Y, teal vs mint green: two cool, similar colours on dots this small.
- In X and Z, a filled dot vs a hollow ring of the same colour.
- In X, the lock is very small for my eyes.

**3. Easiest: Z.** Every kind of fund has its own colour, the lock and ★ are explained, and my example is the only large white ring.

## Part 5 – Fund list with tag chips

**1. What the colours mean.** There is no legend, so this is my guess. The colour seems to group a *topic*, not good versus bad:
- Pink/purple = how the fund holds the index: "עסקת החלף", "מחזיקה את המניות", "כמה דרכים יחד".
- Yellow = currency: "חשיפה לדולר", "מנוטרלת מט״ח".
- Teal = fund type: "קרן מחקה", "קרן סל".
- Grey = where it trades: "קרן חוץ בת״א", "נקנית בחו״ל".
- **X:** dividends are orange ("צוברת", "מחלקת דיבידנד"), and "מס גם על שינויי המטבע" is grey.
- **Y:** dividends are green, "מס גם על שינויי המטבע" is red, and there is a teal dot before "בלי נטרול מט״ח · 19 קרנות".

**2. Looked the same but are opposites**
- "חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט״ח" are the same yellow but mean opposite things.
- "צוברת" and "מחלקת דיבידנד" share a colour: orange in X, green in Y.
- In Y, green looks like "good", which I doubt is true of "מחלקת דיבידנד".

**3. Slightly easier: Y.** The red "מס גם על שינויי המטבע" catches my eye as a tax warning, which matters to me. The green on both dividend chips is misleading, though.

## Over all parts

**Colours that mean more than one thing:**
- **Yellow** is the worst. It means:
  - "קרן סל ישראלית" (part 1).
  - "דמי משמרת" (parts 3, 4, 7, 9).
  - The hedged fund (part 6).
  - Both "חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט״ח" (chips).
  - "התעריפון" (part 7 Z).
  - All the funds (part 2 X).
- **Teal** means:
  - A tracking fund (part 1 Y/Z).
  - "הבחירה שלכם" and the selected row (radio buttons, highlighted row border).
  - The unhedged funds (part 6).
  - "מחירי הקבוצה" (part 7).
  - Accounts or funds on the range lines (part 2).
- **Pink** means:
  - A foreign fund (part 1).
  - "המרת מטבע" (part 4 X) or "דמי ניהול חשבון" (part 4 Y).
  - How a fund holds the index (chips).
- **White** means:
  - A tracking fund (part 1 X, part 2 Z).
  - "הבחירה שלכם" (part 1 Y/Z).
  - "המרת מטבע" (part 4 Y).
- **Orange** means:
  - Buy/sell commissions.
  - A foreign fund bought abroad (part 1 Z).
  - Dividend chips (X).

**Where it misled me:**
- In part 6 X, I first read yellow as "Israeli ETF".
- In part 2 Y, the teal ticks made me think all 13 funds were tracking funds.
- In part 9 Z, I linked the yellow fund ticks with the yellow custody-fee bars next to them.

**Which I'd use:** No single letter is best everywhere; the versions swap strengths from part to part. For example, X has the labelled bars in part 3 but Y has them in part 4. My pick:

| Part | Version I'd use |
|---|---|
| 1, 2, 7 | Z |
| 3, 9 | X |
| 4, 5, 6, 8 | Y |

The common thread is that the version with **words and amounts written on the bars, and a legend in view**, always won. I'd rather read than match colours.

**What I'd still change:**
1. Give each colour one meaning across the whole page. Keep yellow for "דמי משמרת" only, and teal for "your choice" only.
2. Always write the label and the ₪ amount on or under each bar segment, never colour alone.
3. Make the dots, the lock and the legend text bigger and brighter. The small grey-on-black legends were the hardest thing for me to read.
4. Use red only for real warnings. Never colour "אין מס בצבירה" red, and don't make "מחלקת דיבידנד" green.
5. Rename "העמלות זו על זו" to something plain, like "עמלות אחרות".
