## Across the whole page

**Yes, several colours mean different things in different parts:**

- **Yellow:** Israeli ETF (Part 1), all funds (2X), custody fee (3, 4, 7), hedged fund (6), "חשיפה לדולר" *and* "מנוטרלת מט"ח" (5), the tariff period (7Z).
- **Teal:** tracker fund (1Y/Z), your choice (1X), not hedged (6), the group-price period (7), the "קרן מחקה" chip, and the highlight on the selected row.
- **Pink:** foreign fund (1X), bought abroad *or* tax unclear (1Y), foreign fund traded in Tel Aviv (1Z), account fee (4Y), currency conversion (4X), fund-structure tags (5).
- **Orange:** foreign fund bought abroad (1Z), trading fees (3, 4, 7), "צוברת" (5X, 8X).
- **White:** tracker (1X, 2Z), your choice (1Y/Z), currency conversion (4Y), the tariff (7X).

**Where it misled me:**

- In the card (2X) I first took the yellow fund ticks to be ETFs, but the top one is a tracker.
- In Part 6 I carried "yellow = ETF" over from Part 1 and briefly thought the hedged fund was an ETF. It is "הראל מחקה", a tracker.
- In Part 5 the matching yellow made me think "חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט"ח" were related, when they are opposites.

None of this changed my final choice, because the numbers and text are clear. But it made me double-check things I shouldn't have had to.

**Which version overall:** no single version is best everywhere. If I had to pick one, **Z**: its dot chart and card are the clearest at the top of the page, where I actually decide. But I'd take labelled bars from 3X and 4Y, the chip colours from Y, and the hollow hedged bar from 6Y.

**What I'd still change:**
1. Give each colour one meaning. Use one set of colours for fund types and a separate set for cost types (for example shades of grey or patterns for costs).
2. Use one colour only for "my selection" and use it nowhere else.
3. Use the same fund-type colours in the dot chart, the card strips and the chips. Right now a tracker is white in one place and teal in another.
4. Always put names and ₪ amounts on cost bars, in every list.
5. Don't give opposite tags ("חשיפה לדולר" and "מנוטרלת מט"ח") the same colour.
6. Show known costs and unknowns differently. A known tax should look different from "לא ידוע" by more than a tiny lock.
7. Explain "העמלות זו על זו".
8. Put the ★ in the legend of every version.
