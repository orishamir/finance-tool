## Part 1: "כל הזוגות של חשבון וקרן על S&P 500" (dot chart)

**1. What the marks mean**
- **X:** filled white means "קרן מחקה ישראלית" (Israeli index fund). Yellow means "קרן סל ישראלית" (Israeli ETF). Pink means "קרן חוץ" (foreign fund). A pink dot with a red lock means "המס עליה עוד לא ברור" (its tax is still unclear). A hollow white ring means "מנוטרלת מט"ח: מוצגת ולא מדורגת" (currency-hedged: shown but not ranked). ★ means "הזוג שמשאיר הכי הרבה" (the pair that leaves the most). A teal ring means "הבחירה שלכם (כאן: לדוגמה)" (your choice, here an example). A dashed teal line means "הדרך ממנה לזוג הטוב ביותר" (the path from it to the best pair).
- **Y:** teal is the Israeli index fund and yellow the Israeli ETF. Mint green means "קרן חוץ בבורסה בתל אביב" (foreign fund listed in Tel Aviv). Pink means "קרן שנקנית בחו"ל, או קרן שהמס עליה עוד לא ברור" (fund bought abroad, or fund whose tax is unclear). A thin teal ring is the hedged fund. A thick white ring is your choice.
- **Z:** teal is the index fund, yellow the ETF, and pink the "קרן חוץ בבורסה בתל אביב". Orange means "קרן חוץ שנקנית בחו"ל" (foreign fund bought abroad). Orange with a lock means the tax is unclear. A teal ring is hedged, the ★ is the best pair, and a white ring is your choice.
- **Where I was unsure:** in all three versions there is one lock per row, floating above two dots, so I couldn't tell which dot it belongs to.

**2. Hard to tell apart**
- **X:** the white index-fund dot and the white hollow hedged ring are the same colour for two different things. Pink also covers two different things: foreign funds bought in Tel Aviv and those bought abroad.
- **Y:** teal and mint green look almost the same in the crowded clusters. The legend has no ★, and the best pair at פסגות gets the same white ring as "הבחירה שלכם", so it looked as if I had already chosen it. Pink merges "bought abroad" with "tax unclear".
- **Z:** orange and yellow touch in the אקסלנס row, but they are still distinguishable.

**3. Easier version: Z.** It separates all five kinds of fund, and keeps the star and the lock apart, so I can see that the far-left losers are all orange abroad-bought funds and ignore them.

## Part 2: result card, "כמה כל בחירה משנה" (how much each choice changes)

**1. What the marks mean**
- **X:** there is no legend. The "החשבון" row (the account; "אותה קרן, ב־15 חשבונות", the same fund in 15 accounts) has teal ticks. The "הקרן" row (the fund) has yellow ticks.
- **Y:** the account ticks are white and the fund ticks are teal. There is no legend.
- **Z:** there is a legend: yellow is "קרן סל ישראלית", white is "קרן מחקה ישראלית" and pink is "קרן חוץ". The account row is white because, as it says, it uses "ילין לפידות מחקה: ▮ קרן מחקה ישראלית".

**2. Hard to tell apart**
- **X:** after Part 1 I read the all-yellow fund ticks as "all 13 are Israeli ETFs", which is wrong.
- **Y:** after Part 1 Y I read the all-teal fund ticks as "all are index funds", which is also wrong.
- **Z:** the colours are right within the card, but white for the index fund matches Part 1 X, not Part 1 Z, where the index fund is teal.

**3. Easier version: Z.** It shows that the best fund (86,492 ₪) is a white index fund and the cheaper end is yellow ETFs. That one fact tells me which kind of fund to look for.

## Part 3: account list, top

**1. What the marks mean**
- **X:** yellow means "דמי משמרת" (custody fees), e.g. "699 ₪". Orange means "עמלות קנייה ומכירה" (buy and sell commissions), e.g. "356 ₪". Grey means "העמלות זו על זו", e.g. "2 ₪". The bars also carry the words written on them.
- **Y:** same colours, but there are no words or amounts on the bars, and no legend appears in this crop.
- **Where I was unsure:** what "העמלות זו על זו" (fees on fees) means exactly. I guessed it is the compounding of fees on each other.

**2. Hard to tell apart:** in Y the grey sliver is barely visible. Yellow here means a fee, while a few screens earlier yellow meant "Israeli ETF".

**3. Easier version: X.** I want the shekel amounts, because "699 ₪ custody fee" is something I can compare with my pension statement, and a bar length is not.

## Over all the parts

**Colours that change meaning**
- **Yellow** means:
  - "Israeli ETF" (Part 1)
  - "all funds" (Part 2 X)
  - custody fees (Parts 3, 4 and 7)
  - the hedged fund (Part 6)
  - both "מנוטרלת מט"ח" and "חשיפה לדולר" (Part 5)
  - "התעריפון" (Part 7 Z)
- **Teal** means:
  - the index fund (Part 1 Y and Z)
  - a hedged ring (Part 1)
  - unhedged (Part 6)
  - your choice (Part 1 X)
  - group prices (Part 7)
  - the account ticks (Parts 2 and 9)
- **Pink** means:
  - a foreign fund (Part 1)
  - tax unclear (Part 1 Y)
  - the account management fee (Part 4 Y)
  - currency conversion (Part 4 X)
  - several unrelated chips
- **Orange** means:
  - a fund bought abroad (Part 1 Z)
  - buy and sell commissions
  - "צוברת" (Part 5 X)

**Where it actually misled me**
- **Part 2:** single-colour fund ticks (yellow in X, teal in Y) made me think all 13 funds were one type.
- **Part 6:** after Part 1 taught me "teal ring = hedged", I first read the teal bars as the hedged fund. They are the unhedged ones.
- **Part 4 X:** I treated red and orange as the same fee.

**Which version overall:** Z where there is one (Parts 1 and 2), then Y for Parts 4–6 and 8, and X for Parts 3 and 9. No single letter is best everywhere.

**What I would still change**
- Keep one palette only for fund type, the same in every part. Under Z's Part 1 legend, that would mean the index fund is always teal, never white.
- Show fees in neutral shades with words and shekel amounts on the bars.
- Show hedged against unhedged by shape (hollow or filled), never by a colour already used.
- Use red only for "tax unclear" or "extra cost", and attach the lock to its own dot.
- Never give opposite chips ("צוברת" and "מחלקת דיבידנד", or "מנוטרלת" and "חשיפה לדולר") the same colour.
