# Requests from the rules-scope builder (2026-10-26)

What the build of spec sections 3 and 4 leaves to the lead, the editor or the founder. No rule-base classification was changed, and none was found to be wrong; the items below are site, data-table and method decisions.

## For the lead

1. **The child-savings fee after 21** (closed by main's `followups`, 2026-10-26): F-54 runs at the legal cap (CS-18), and CS-30's 0.23% is two companies' own rate, named only on their pages.
2. **`IdRef` still prints the code of a company's term** where a page calls it directly with a rule id. Every rule chip the site draws goes through `RuleRef` or `RulesLine`, which show the company's name, so nothing on screen is affected today. Once `closed-questions` has landed, a one-line follow-up in `IdRef`'s rule branch (return `<RuleRef id={id} />`) would close it for good; I stayed out of `IdRef` as the brief asked.
3. **The switch playbook (`/switch/:wrapper`) cites companies' terms** (SW-45 to SW-55 and the forum notes SW-X01 to SW-X04; `site/app/lib/playbook.ts`). The spec's table does not list the playbook, and D30 names "the comparison" among product contexts but not the playbook. Today those chips name the company (הפניקס, מיטב טרייד and so on), and the card says it is that company's term. Decide whether the playbook is a product context: if it is, its steps need the same "תלוי בחברה" treatment as the guides.
4. **Companies the site has no page for** (fund managers, index providers, foreign brokers, the exchange, Fair, J.P. Morgan, the Securities Authority as a site owner, Reit 1, The Phoenix's insurer): their terms (51 records) are listed on `/companies` under "מה כתוב במסמכים של חברות שאין להן עמוד כאן", one fold per company, plus a fold of the four records that name no company (SW-34, SW-X02 to SW-X04). This extends the spec, which names company pages only for managing companies, banks and brokers. Foreign brokers have fee snapshots but no page (`hasPage` in `invest.ts`); giving them pages would move 13 records to them.
5. **The fee snapshots' protection lists still cite BRK-12 and BRK-13** (TASE's own description of its clearing house and nominee company) on every bank's and broker's page. The spec leaves price lists unchanged, so they stay; their chips now read "הבורסה לניירות ערך בתל אביב" and the card says it is TASE's own text.
6. **The not-rules allowance** (`notRules` in `packages/schema/src/scope.ts`, the scope settling requests §4) lets IRA-28 stay on the IRA guides and wrappers, REG-14 on the brokerage guide, SW-34 in the brokerage wrapper and the playbook, MKT-01 on the comparison, CAT-43 and MF-55 in the fees and fund pages. Each still shows its scope on its card. The list should shrink as each moves to `data/` or the open questions. Since the review of 2026-10-26 each entry names the product-context files where it may stand (IRA-28: the two IRA wrappers and guides; REG-14: the brokerage guide; SW-34: the brokerage wrapper), and no not-rule is listed in a company's section: the 13 that are not general sit in their own `/companies` section, "לא נכלל ככלל: פרסומים, ספירות ונתונים". Since the third round (2026-10-26) each names every file that cites it, data and site alike (MKT-01 the comparison, SW-X01 to SW-X04, SW-34, SW-37 and SW-53 the playbook), and the checks read every data file and every site source file.
7. **`scope` is required on every `data/rules` record.** A new record needs `npm run extract:sources -w @finance-tool/pipeline` before the validator passes (done for the six records of `closed-questions` after the rebase, and for the thirteen new ones below).
7a. **The new ids of the scope settling** (its requests §3): records were made for BANK-36, BANK-37, BANK-38, BRK-51, BRK-52 (general), ETF-68, ETF-69, KH-33, PEN-45, PEN-47, REG-32, SW-56 and TXK-28 (with Harel's 25% that TXK-26 lost), each from its block, in its neighbours' shape (`tools/new-records.py`). ## For the data tables (`data/sources/`)

8. **`ccpc.ie` has no `publishers.json` row**, so BRK-35's first source (Ireland's consumer and competition regulator) extracts as kind `other`. The validator accepts it as an official text of strength `primary`, but a row (kind `regulator_page`, a Hebrew publisher name) would label it properly. The same holds for the Supreme Court host (`supremedecisions.court.gov.il`, kind `other`; TXI-25) and the Civil Service Commission form (KH-03, kind `form`).
9. **TASE's own documents are labelled `exchange_rules` by host** (scope settling requests §7). The validator cannot tell a TASE brochure from a TASE rule, so a general record cited first from a TASE brochure would pass. The extraction now ranks a company's own data page (a `dataset` of strength `institution`) with the institutions, which put ETF-44 and ETF-45's exchange guidelines first; the brochures still need `documents.json` rows of kind `institution_doc`. Note for the `followups` builder, who owns the host rules: this branch changed `extract-sources.ts` in three places (the scope keys in `OWN_KEYS` and `writeKeys`, the scope read in `main`, and that one line of `rankOf`), so a rebase there may meet them.

Each is an `only_records` list in `data/sources/institutions.json`; a different reading is a one-line change there.

## From the second review round (2026-10-26)

13. **A general rule's pointers to general rules** still show as "נגזר מ־" chips where the Source line says "pointers" or "see" (BRK-35 to REG-27, SW-20 to PF-18, KH-14, KH-17 and TXH-19, and the like). The extraction cannot tell a derivation from a pointer; a word the rule base writes for each (for example "derived from:" against "see:") would let the card say which.

Companies none of whose documents is collected (Migdal, Clal, Menora Mivtachim, Slice, Globalnet) have no host, so no record is listed on their pages.
