# Requests from the rules-scope builder (2026-10-26)

What the build of spec sections 3 and 4 leaves to the lead, the editor or the founder. No rule-base classification was changed, and none was found to be wrong; the items below are site, data-table and method decisions.

## For the lead

1. **The child-savings fee after 21 still runs on CS-30 in the engine.** The guide now states CS-18's cap (1.05% of the balance and 4% of each deposit after 21) and says "דמי הניהול אחרי גיל עשרים ואחת תלויים בחברה" with links to Harel, Altshuler Shaham and Bank Yahav. The timeline under it still takes `book.rate("CS-30", "fee_after_21")` (F-54, `packages/engine/src/ledgers/child-savings.ts`), so its value sheet shows two companies' 0.23% as the product's rate. Moving F-54 to the cap labelled "עד", or to an entered rate, is the method change in the scope settling requests §1.
2. **`IdRef` still prints the code of a company's term** where a page calls it directly with a rule id. Every rule chip the site draws goes through `RuleRef` or `RulesLine`, which show the company's name, so nothing on screen is affected today. Once `closed-questions` has landed, a one-line follow-up in `IdRef`'s rule branch (return `<RuleRef id={id} />`) would close it for good; I stayed out of `IdRef` as the brief asked.
3. **The switch playbook (`/switch/:wrapper`) cites companies' terms** (SW-45 to SW-55 and the forum notes SW-X01 to SW-X04; `site/app/lib/playbook.ts`). The spec's table does not list the playbook, and D30 names "the comparison" among product contexts but not the playbook. Today those chips name the company (הפניקס, מיטב טרייד and so on), and the card says it is that company's term. Decide whether the playbook is a product context: if it is, its steps need the same "תלוי בחברה" treatment as the guides.
4. **Companies the site has no page for** (fund managers, index providers, foreign brokers, the exchange, Fair, J.P. Morgan, the Securities Authority as a site owner, Reit 1, The Phoenix's insurer): their terms (47 records) are listed on `/companies` under "מה כתוב במסמכים של חברות שאין להן עמוד כאן", one fold per company, plus a fold of the four records that name no company (SW-34, SW-X02 to SW-X04). This extends the spec, which names company pages only for managing companies, banks and brokers. Foreign brokers have fee snapshots but no page (`hasPage` in `invest.ts`); giving them pages would move 12 records to them.
5. **The fee snapshots' protection lists still cite BRK-12 and BRK-13** (TASE's own description of its clearing house and nominee company) on every bank's and broker's page. The spec leaves price lists unchanged, so they stay; their chips now read "הבורסה לניירות ערך בתל אביב" and the card says it is TASE's own text.
6. **The not-rules allowance** (`notRules` in `packages/schema/src/scope.ts`, the scope settling requests §4) lets IRA-28 stay on the IRA guides and wrappers, REG-14 on the brokerage guide, SW-34 in the brokerage wrapper and the playbook, MKT-01 on the comparison, CAT-43 and MF-55 in the fees and fund pages. Each still shows its scope on its card. The list should shrink as each moves to `data/` or the open questions.
7. **Rebase note.** `scope` is now required on every `data/rules` record. When `closed-questions` lands with its six new records, run `npm run extract:sources -w @finance-tool/pipeline` after the rebase: their blocks already carry `Scope: general` (scope settling §6), and the extraction writes the field.

## For the data tables (`data/sources/`)

8. **`ccpc.ie` has no `publishers.json` row**, so BRK-35's first source (Ireland's consumer and competition regulator) extracts as kind `other`. The validator accepts it as an official text of strength `primary`, but a row (kind `regulator_page`, a Hebrew publisher name) would label it properly. The same holds for the Supreme Court host (`supremedecisions.court.gov.il`, kind `other`; TXI-25) and the Civil Service Commission form (KH-03, kind `form`).
9. **TASE's own documents are labelled `exchange_rules` by host** (scope settling requests §7). The validator cannot tell a TASE brochure from a TASE rule, so a general record cited first from a TASE brochure would pass. The extraction now ranks a company's own data page (a `dataset` of strength `institution`) with the institutions, which put ETF-44 and ETF-45's exchange guidelines first; the brochures still need `documents.json` rows of kind `institution_doc`.

## For the editor (rule base and glossary)

10. **`scope_settle` is English working text**, so the rule card of an unsettled record says "אולי כך בכל החברות; לא מצאנו לזה מקור רשמי" but not what would settle it. A Hebrew line per unsettled record (12) would let the card say it.
11. **24 glossary terms cite companies' terms** in `rule_ids` (BRK-23, IRA-21, KH-09, BRK-36, KH-25, BRK-28 and others; the validator warns). The term card now shows only the general ones. `data/terms/` is generated from `research/glossary/terms/` by `gen-terms.ts`, so the fix belongs in the research files.
12. **The registry's readings of shared names**, my calls from each record's own sources: "IBI" in the ETF records is IBI's fund manager (אי.בי.אי קרנות נאמנות), elsewhere the broker; "Harel" and "Mor" in ETF-64 to ETF-66 (and ETF-68, TXS-26) are their fund managers; "Meitav" in MF-56 is Meitav's fund manager (it quotes Meitav's fund pages); "The Phoenix" in TXA-29 is the insurer (a Phoenix investment policy); "MTF" is Migdal's fund brand and "Tachlit" Meitav's (the fund records name the managers מגדל קרנות נאמנות and מיטב קרנות נאמנות). Each is an `only_records` list in `data/sources/institutions.json`; a different reading is a one-line change there.
