## 2. Rules for every visual (the founder's, 2026-10-25)

1. **One question per product.** One or two controls, two to four figures, one chart or drawing. It must make sense to someone who has never heard of the product.
2. **Name the holding.** A container (study fund, provident fund, pension) has no cost or result of its own without a named track or fund. Say on screen what it holds; the nearest-alternative contrast holds the same thing in another container.
3. **Exact vs range.** What is the same everywhere (tax mechanics, locks, exemptions) is exact. What depends on the institution (a bank's rate, a fund manager's fee) is a range across the collected institutions by default and the person's own number once they pick theirs. While only one institution is collected (today Leumi for deposits and the current account), say so on the visual ("לפי הפרסום של לאומי; בנקים אחרים עוד לא נאספו") and build the bank choice so more records slot in.
4. **Fills only when they mean a number.** A faded area only for a gap, a range or a stacked part, and the number is written on it. No decorative fill under a single line.
5. **Ceilings are not rates.** Where a figure is "up to" (25% tax, a fee cap), show what it depends on, from the rule base.
6. **Show the difference as a difference.** Two values with a bracket or bar between their ends and the difference on it; never a third box that reads like a third option (the founder misread one).
7. **Every number** goes through `Num` with its state (sourced, computed, entered). Figures come from the engine and `data/`, never from the sketches. A figure the site assumes (P-05's 3.5% `cash_rate` for money-market funds, makam and the bond, D20) is labelled as the site's assumption. Never fill a financial or tax rule from memory: read `.claude/skills/il-finance` and the rule records; a rule that's missing is named on the page as "עוד לא חושב" with the gap, not guessed.
8. **Design system.** Tokens only, logical properties, chart colours from `docs/design-system.md` (teal value, orange fees, rose tax, neutral contributions; blue and violet are never series colours; direct labels; time left to right). Controls are 44 px touch targets; a draggable marker also works with a tap on the axis and with the keyboard (arrow keys). Works at 390 px wide.
9. **The person's own values.** Start from the saved goal or the guide inputs when present (amount, monthly, years, age, employment), else P-05, and mark which.

## 6. The brokerage account: what can live inside it (added 2026-10-25; withdrawn the same day, not built)

**Withdrawn.** After the third sketch the founder decided the page gets no visual for now: "i think we shouldnt have anything there for now. its just trying to distill tons of information into a specific visualization but the truth is just more complex than that. so for now dont." The brokerage page keeps its prose and its old timeline, open, as before. The design below stays as a record of what was tried and why it was dropped; don't build it. What it taught: a container that can hold almost anything has no single question to draw, and three sketches each squeezed a different slice of it (broker cost, long-term index money, what can live inside) without being true to the whole.

**Why this one is different.** The founder rejected two earlier sketches: a broker-cost table ("thats not gonna make me switch banks") and a comparison against a provident fund on long-term index money ("why do you think he specifically thinks about long-term index money?"; I had projected his own case). A brokerage account is a container that can hold almost anything, and both its cost and its tax depend on what goes in ("כשמוכרים says 25% but it depends"). He approved this version on 2026-10-25 ("lets do the last visualization you suggested"). The sketch is in the session log; the build follows this section, not the sketch's wording (several of its tax lines were loose).

**The question.** What can I hold in a brokerage account, and for each holding, how do I buy it, what does it cost here, and how is it taxed?

**Controls (rule 1).**
1. A holding picker, chips, 44 px: קרן על מדד, מניות, מק״מ, קרן כספית, אג״ח ממשלתית, ניירות ערך בחו״ל. The default is the index fund. Each chip names its holding (rule 2): the index fund is P-05's `brokerage-account` fund (5139282) with an Israeli ETF on the same index as its exchange-traded twin (the cost row shows both instrument classes); the money-market fund is P-05's `money-market-fund-daily` fund; the makam and the bond follow P-05 (`makam`, and `government-bond` via GOV-11); shares are "a share you choose" (no record; class `tase_shares_bonds`); foreign securities use the foreign fund record in `data/funds/` (ie00b5bmr087) and class `foreign_securities`.
2. An amount, from the saved goal's amount now when there is one, else P-05's `amount_now`, marked which (rule 9).
3. Optional, under the cost row: "יש לכם כבר חשבון? בחרו את הבנק או בית ההשקעות" (a select over the collected providers). Picking one highlights it and turns the cost figures into that provider's own numbers (rule 3). Nothing is picked by default.

**The card, four rows.**
1. **איך קונים.** One or two sentences per holding, in the new holdings block of `content/guide/brokerage-account.tsx`, with `Term` links. Every fact comes from a rule record or the glossary (read `.claude/skills/il-finance`: mutual funds and money-market funds, ETFs and index funds, government bonds and makam, brokerage account). Where the rule base doesn't say it, leave the claim out; don't fill from memory.
2. **כמה עולה (range by provider, rule 3).** Two dot strips, each one horizontal axis in ₪, one dot per collected bank or broker, the lowest and highest labelled with the provider's name and the figure. The founder could not tell what the dots were in the sketch ("what are the green circles"), so each strip has: a caption "כל נקודה היא בנק או בית השקעות אחד"; dots coloured by the kind of provider (bank, Israeli broker, foreign broker: three clearly different hues from the chart palette, validated pairwise, no blue or violet), with a three-entry legend; an axis with ₪ ticks; dots at the same figure stacked up to four, then "+N"; every dot a 44 px target that names its provider and figure on tap, hover or keyboard focus, and the picked provider highlighted on both strips at once, with its two figures written under them. The founder asked for a colour per bank or broker (2026-10-25); with 17 providers that can't give clearly different hues (his own rule), so kind colours plus names were proposed, pending his answer:
   - "קנייה אחת של {amount}": the provider's buy commission for the holding's instrument class (engine `tradeCommission`, through the same tariff building the provider workbench uses); for foreign securities add the currency conversion line (`fxFee`) and say so on the strip.
   - "שנה של החזקה": twelve months of custody on the amount (engine `custodyFee`).
   Providers with no line for the class are not dots: one line under the strip counts them ("לעוד {n} אין שורה לסוג הזה בתעריפון, ולכן הם לא מחושבים"), per the "no line, no price" note on F-63, money-market units excepted (MF-26). Where BRK-17 applies (makam, money-market funds), one sentence says banks may not charge custody on them, and that brokers' tariffs read so far don't show such an exemption. For a fund, a third figure, not a strip: the fund's own yearly fee from its record ("ובתוך הקרן: …"). Every figure through `Num`, computed state.
3. **מס (exact, rule 5).** The mechanic for the holding, with each rate as the rule record's figure through `Num`, and the rule id beside it:
   - index fund: by the fund record's `tax_status.il_fund_tax_track`: exempt TXS-09 (real gain against CPI, withheld at sale), distributions TXS-10; taxable TXS-11 (the fund pays inside, nothing at sale, losses lost).
   - shares: TXS-01, dividends TXS-16.
   - makam: TXI-11 (held to redemption, the discount is interest at the fixed rate, withheld at redemption, no inflation deduction; TXI-01, TXI-03). Sale before redemption: TXI-12 (a capital gain up to the same rate, all of it treated as real).
   - money-market fund: TXS-12 (on the exempt track, the real-gain rate once, at redemption, on the gain above CPI; a loss offsets). On the same yield it leaves less tax than makam; the bar must show that, not copy makam's.
   - government bond: a switch inside the card, "צמודה למדד" (P-05's GOV-11, the default) and "שקלית" (GOV-13). Linked: TXI-14 (the linkage is exempt; real interest and a real gain on sale up to the maximum rate). Unlinked: TXI-03 and TXI-15 on the coupons, TXI-12 on a sale before maturity. The card also says the price can move before maturity (GOV-17) and links to the bond guide's rate visual.
   The founder clicked makam, the money-market fund and the bond in the second sketch and saw nothing change (only three of six holdings were filled). Each holding must differ where the rules differ: its buying sentence, its cost strip (no fees on a money-market fund with a distribution agreement, MF-26; regular custody on bonds), its tax bar and rule ids, its chips and its alternative.
   - foreign securities: TXF-03 and TXS-04 (the gain is measured in the currency, not against CPI), dividends TXF-08, a foreign broker means reporting yourself (TXF-22).
   **The split bar ("ממה בדיוק לוקחים מס").** One horizontal stacked bar of a sale of {amount} after P-05's horizon at P-05's return (A-01 for funds and shares; `cash_rate` for makam, the money-market fund and the bond, labelled as the site's assumption) and P-05's inflation (A-02). Segments, each with its number written on it (rule 4): what you paid (neutral), the part from CPI that isn't taxed (neutral, hatched), the real gain (teal), with the tax (rose) as a bracket over the part it is taken from. For makam and the unlinked bond there is no CPI segment: the tax bracket spans the whole nominal gain. That contrast is the point. For a taxable fund the bar shows no tax slice and says "המס שולם בתוך הקרן". For foreign securities the base depends on the exchange rate at sale, and the site holds no exchange-rate assumption: show the mechanic in words and no numbers ("לא חושב: תלוי בשער המטבע ביום המכירה"). Take the figures from each holding's own engine run (the wrapper run the guide timeline uses for brokerage-account, makam, money-market-fund-daily and government-bond) with the amount as a lump sum and no monthly deposit, so tax withheld along the way (each makam redemption, each bond coupon) is counted and the bar says "נוכה בדרך" or "נוכה פעם אחת, בפדיון". If a step has no formula, write one with a method note.
   **"מה משנה את זה".** Chips under the tax, only those that apply to the holding, each opening its rule card: losses (TXS-17, TXS-19), age 60 and over (TXS-23), high income (TXG-09, TXG-10), a substantial shareholder (shares: TXS-01, TXS-16), switching is a sale (TXS-21), a foreign broker (TXF-22), a taxable fund (TXS-11).
4. **לאן ממשיכים.** Index fund and foreign securities: "השקעה במדד" (/invest). Makam, money-market fund, government bond: that holding's guide page. Shares: none.

**Nearest alternative (D29).** No chart. One line per holding names where else the same holding can live and links there: the index fund in a provident fund for investing (switching tracks isn't a sale there, TXS-21); makam against a bank deposit; the money-market fund against the current account; foreign securities at an Israeli broker against a foreign broker (who withholds tax).

**Out of scope.** Ranking brokers (that is "השקעה במדד"), any recommendation, holdings with no rule base (options, corporate bonds beyond a sentence, crypto).

## 7. Current account: idle money loses value (2026-10-25, builder G3)

**Why.** The founder, on the current-account page: the expanded chart "is still very specific to leumi and one specific user's case. like im pretty sure almost 99% of people who have their cash idle in the bank will lose slowly". The eight collected banks' 447 publications (`held-deposits/`, `REPORT.md`) bear him out: Hapoalim, Mizrahi-Tefahot, Massad and Mercantile pay nothing on a current account; Discount (3%), First International (2%), Leumi (1%) and Yahav (0.75%) pay only with conditions, each on at most 10,000 ₪, and the conditions favour small balances (Discount stops above 30,000 ₪ of assets, First International above 50,000 ₪, Yahav when the monthly average reaches 10,000 ₪). He asked to make the interest "less central", and approved the sketch: "yes use this new visualization. but maybe include the table or a slight modification of it there".

**Data.**
1. Publish the seven held current-account records (`*-current-account.json` in `held-deposits/`) to `data/terms/deposits/`. The deposits and savings plans stay held (their pages don't take a range yet). Keep each record's notes on its doubts (REPORT.md 1 to 4): the no-interest banks as rate 0 with the printed sentence; Mercantile's printed 0.0; First International's 2% as published, with the note that its conditions PDF says the benefit ran to 31.03.2026; Yahav's `effective_to` 2026-12-31.
2. Add an optional `conditions_he` to the deposit-terms schema (`packages/schema/src/collections/deposit-terms.ts`, the exported JSON schema, `docs/data-schema.md`): a short plain-Hebrew summary of the conditions the bank prints for the rate, faithful to the printed text quoted in the record's notes, adding nothing that isn't printed. Fill it for all eight current-account records (Leumi's included).
3. `npm run validate` must pass.

**The rate most banks pay** (new formula F-85, engine function plus registry entry plus method note): the most common `rate_offered` among the published current-account records (ties go to the lower rate), with how many banks pay it out of how many were collected. Today 0%, 4 of 8. This is the page's default, labelled as such ("הריבית שרוב הבנקים משלמים: 0%, ב־4 מתוך 8 הבנקים שנאספו"), never one bank's rate.

**The visual (`current-account.tsx`).**
- A headline sentence: after N years the balance in the current account is worth X ₪ in today's money, and the loss; under it, that most banks pay no interest on a current account, so the money loses value at the pace of inflation.
- The chart as now (the account line, the daily money-market fund as the nearest alternative, the starting balance dashed, the gap labelled with its number), the account at the rate most banks pay. The value axis fits the data with some padding instead of starting at 0, so the gap is visible (at 0 the lines were flat in the founder's screenshot).
- The conditions switch and the Leumi terms paragraph go. In their place, a folded section (closed by default) titled along the lines of "חלק מהבנקים משלמים ריבית קטנה, בתנאים ועל עד 10,000 ₪ בלבד" (the 10,000 is the largest ceiling in the data). Inside it:
  - A table: bank, rate, who gets it (`conditions_he`), and the most it pays a year on the person's balance after tax: `rate × min(balance, ceiling) × (1 − TXI-03's rate)`, computed through `Num`. The banks that pay come first, highest rate first, then one row listing the banks that pay nothing. A record with `effective_to` shows "עד {date}"; the First International doubt is flagged with the caution state.
  - "הבנק שלכם": a picker over the collected banks. Picking a bank that pays redraws the account line at its rate and ceiling, assuming its conditions are met, and says so in one line; the headline follows. Nothing is picked by default.
- The line "לפי הפרסום של לאומי; בנקים אחרים עוד לא נאספו" goes from this page.

**The expanded timeline ("שחקו עם המספרים שלכם") on this page** runs by default at the rate most banks pay, not Leumi's 1% (the injection is in `guide-signature-bundle.server.ts`), with the hint saying so; an entered rate still overrides it. The ladder row for the current account on the daily money-market fund page uses the same default and says that some banks pay up to the highest rate, with conditions, on at most 10,000 ₪.

**Out of scope:** the goal comparison and the idle-cash verdict (still on P-05's Leumi record; the institution sweep will cover them), the deposit and savings-plan pages.
