# Requests from builder G2 to the lead, 2026-10-25

Things G2 needs changed in files it does not own. Each is numbered; G2 did not touch the files.

1. **`SignatureFrame` must allow a page with no alternative** (`site/app/components/guide-signature/index.tsx`). The three pension pages (`pension-comprehensive`, `pension-general`, `provident-for-annuity`) have no nearest alternative: pension is required for salaried people. Their entries in `guideSignature.pages` have `alt: ""` and `altId: ""` and an `altLine` that says so. Until the frame skips the lead-in when `altId` is empty, those pages show "הכי קרוב לזה, באותה החזקה: . {altLine} לעמוד של · להשוואה המלאה…" with a link to `/guide/`. Wanted: when `page.altId === ""`, render only `page.altLine` as a plain paragraph (no lead-in, no link to the alternative; keep the link to the full comparison or drop it, the lead's call). The type of `pages` in copy.ts already allows empty strings.
2. **P-05 has no track for the comprehensive pension** (`data/parameters/illustrative-scenario.json`, `instruments`). Only `pension-general` (2158) and `provident-for-annuity` (923) are named, so the comprehensive pension page can split the deposit and show the credit but has no fees and no balance ("עוד לא חושב", reason on the page). Suggested entry, the sibling of 2158: `"pension-comprehensive": { "track": 2155 }` (מיטב פנסיה מקיפה לבני 50 ומטה, 0.11% a year from the balance, 1.25% of deposits, Pensia-Net 2026-09). A site judgement, so the lead's and the founder's call; nothing in the visual changes once it is there.
3. **The study fund guide text is out of date** (`site/app/content/guide/keren-hishtalmut.tsx`, `afterTax`): the caution box says the early-withdrawal calculation "הוא מגיע בגרסה הבאה". F-47 is in the engine and the new visual computes it for an employee (a self-employed person's early withdrawal is still not computed, TXH-07 has no rule record). Suggest rewording to what is true.
4. **Design system** (`docs/design-system.md`): the pension visual draws a monthly deposit as one bar in three clearly different hues, teal (the person's own part, `--chart-value`), gold (the employer's, `--chart-alt`) and magenta (the severance component, `--chart-fee-4`), each with its number above it and a bracket under the person's part for the tax credit. The study fund and the provident fund use teal for the container and gold for the brokerage account, as G1 did. The magenta is borrowed from the fee-kind palette; the all-pairs colour validation was not re-run.
5. **A missing rule record**: TXH-07 (a self-employed person's early withdrawal is business income, deposits not deducted are excluded) is in `.claude/skills/il-finance/references/tax-hishtalmut.md` but has no JSON in `data/rules/`. TXH-06, TXH-12, TXH-14, TXH-16, TXH-20, TXH-21 and TXH-23 are also in the reference without a record (checked against `ls data/rules/txh-*`). The page names TXH-07 in plain text. Add the records and the engine can price that early withdrawal.
