# Accumulating foreign funds, and US funds in trust form

Date: 2026-10-31. Issues #75 and #147. Rules changed: TXF-11; new TXF-36, TXF-37; open questions 1, 2 (Q19), 11, 19 narrowed, 23 added, all in `.claude/skills/il-finance/references/tax-securities-foreign.md`. The `data/` records were not touched.

## Answers

**#75: is an individual taxed on a foreign accumulating fund's undistributed income before a sale?**
No official statement or ruling says either way. No provision taxing such a holder was found, and one more provision now points to no (s.75יז(1)). The sale-only answer remains an inference. The question is narrowed, not closed.

**#147: an official statement on a US fund in trust form, compared with an Irish one?**
Not found. No Israeli text compares them or distinguishes a fund by legal form. The nearest texts make no distinction, and that is an inference from silence for a fund bought at a foreign broker.

## What was inferred

These are my readings. No official text says any of them.

1. A listed foreign ETF is a "קרן להשקעות משותפות מחוץ לישראל". The term is not defined in the Ordinance and appears there once; the fund fits the words and the s.113א definition, which belongs to another law.
2. So the trust chapter cannot attribute such a fund's income to a holder, whatever the fund's legal form.
3. With s.75ב needing control, no statutory attribution rule is left for a retail holder, so income the fund keeps reaches the holder only through the sale price. Section 75יז says what does not apply; it does not say how the holder is taxed.
4. Circular 7/2003 s.4.4 is older than the amendment that left such funds out of the trust chapter, and no later text applies it. It was not found withdrawn, so it still stands as a contrary official sentence, weak and old.
5. A US fund in trust form bought at a foreign broker is taxed in Israel like any foreign security. The basis is s.88's "נייר ערך שהונפק מחוץ לישראל", which has no test of form, and the letter's "הסדר או תאגיד" for funds on TASE.
6. The US and Irish differences read so far are not Israeli classification: US withholding on the fund's own distributions (TXF-12), fund-level tax that is not creditable (TXF-16), and US estate tax (TXF-27).

## Should the site's treatment change?

**Accumulating funds: keep calculated, shown, not ranked.** The finish line was an official statement, a ruling or a provision that decides it. s.75יז(1) removes one route but does not decide the question, and the 2003 sentence was not found withdrawn. Whether a reading by implication is enough stays the founder's call under the site's rule on open rules.

**The tag is now slightly off, for the lead to weigh.** "מקור משני" suggests a secondary source is the best evidence. The reading now rests on primary text by implication, with one secondary source agreeing. This does not change the ranking. If the tag stays, the rule card can name s.75יז(1) as support.

**US funds (#147).** The tax question is answered as far as the sources allow: no official statement, and nothing found that treats trust form differently. My recommendation is that the Israeli side should not hold these funds back separately. Priced distributing US funds would carry a caution that the classification rests on a reading (TXF-37). What the distribution counts as for a bank's withholding on a trust unit stays open (open question 19). Any ranking still needs the independent reviewer the issue names.

**What would settle both.** A letter to the Tax Authority's capital-market department, which only the founder can send.
