Five general sources settle most of the batch. Each was opened and read on 2026-10-25:

1. **Income Tax Rules (Approval and Management of Provident Funds), 1964**, the National Insurance Institute's consolidated copy, file dated 18.8.2025. This is the "timetable set in the law" that Altshuler's form refers to.
   - **Rule 41יא(א)** sets the withdrawal payment: within 4 business days of the request, with a deferral to the 4th business day of the month. KH-16, KGL-08, and probably PEN-33 and CS-14 in other batches.
   - **Rule 19(ב2)** says the employee pays at least a third of the employer's amount (KH-05).
   - **Rule 19א(א)** gives a self-employed study fund only to someone with business income (KH-02, KH-08).
   - **Rule 37(א)** bars further deposits into an account after a withdrawal (KH-15).
   - **Rule 33ב** covers track switching in a study fund (KH-18).
   - **Rule 41יז1** sets late-payment interest (KGL-08).
   - **Rule 34(ז)(1)** limits when a fund may require the employer's approval (KH-10, KH-13).
2. **Income Tax Ordinance (Tax Authority text).**
   - s.9(16א)(ב) closes the account after a withdrawal, except a one-third study withdrawal (KH-15).
   - s.9(16א)(ג) defines the first payment date (KH-11).
   - s.3(ה) sets the employer limits (KH-04).
3. **CMA circular 2022-9-30 (investment tracks)**, in force from 1.1.2024.
   - s.7(ב): a kupat gemel lehashkaa's bylaws must set a default track (KGL-11, PF-24).
   - s.7(ג): a study fund's default must be **either the general track or the flexible index-tracking track** (KH-19). "The default is the general track" is therefore one of two lawful choices, not a rule.
4. **Ministry of Finance Chief Economist paper, October 2025.**
   - p.5, note 7 and p.20: the study-fund tax is 25% of the **real** gain (TXH-11).
   - p.5, note 10: deposits above the ceiling are allowed and taxed (KH-07).
   - p.5: the tax-benefited structure is 7.5% employer and 2.5% employee (KH-04).
5. **Bank of Israel proper-conduct directive 432**, s.3א and 8א. A bank must take an online request to move a securities portfolio to any financial body. It moves Israeli securities within 5 business days and foreign securities within 14 business days, and may not refuse unreasonably (SW-35). Altshuler Shaham Trade's "5 and 14 days" are these bank deadlines.

### TXK-07
- **Verdict: mixed.**
- **General part:**
  - Gains a person withdraws from a kupat gemel lehashkaa are interest income. The part equal to linkage differentials is "linkage income". A partial withdrawal is taken pro rata from principal, interest and linkage: Ordinance s.3(ה4)(3), p.21, "או שמשך יחיד מקופת גמל להשקעה, יראו אותם כהכנסה מריבית, ואולם רווחים שקיבל כאמור בסכום הפרשי הצמדה … יראו אותם כהכנסה מהפרשי הצמדה".
  - The rate is capped at 25% as the top slice: TXK-06, s.125ג(ב).
- **Unsettled part: the base is the real gain (the inflation part is untaxed), and the manager withholds 25%.**
  - **Searched:**
    - Tax Authority guide 2025: the 1301 explanations (section ג for provident funds, section ד for the study fund) have no kupat gemel lehashkaa item. Its table row 9(13), p.89, ends "הוראות אלה לא יחולו על חשבון בקופת גמל". That sentence reads either as "the s.9(13) exemption does not reach a provident account" or as "its conditions do not apply". Ambiguous; TXK open question 2.
    - Ordinance s.9(13), p.41, and s.9(18ב), p.45: a closed home-sale scheme.
    - The NII consolidated copy of the 2005 withholding regulations (btl.gov.il/Laws1/02_0103_100269.pdf, file dated 3.1.2018): reg. 3(א) shows 20% (15% for older deposits) for interest a provident fund pays. Out of date.
    - The Chief Economist paper covers only the study fund.
    - Web searches for an official statement found only managers and press.
  - **What would settle it:** the current withholding regulations (Nevo or Reshumot, reg. 3), a Tax Authority circular on Amendment 15 or on kupat gemel lehashkaa, or the Tax Authority's reading of the s.9(13) row.
- **Rule-base text:**
  - Add the s.3(ה4)(3) line: `[פקודת מס הכנסה [נוסח חדש], נוסח רשות המסים](…same URL…) p.21, s.3(ה4)(3) "או שמשך יחיד מקופת גמל להשקעה, יראו אותם כהכנסה מריבית" · checked 2026-10-25 · primary`.
  - The `real_base` claim and the withholding rate stay with Harel and Bizportal, marked `- Scope: unsettled (seen at Harel; settle: withholding regulations reg. 3, a Tax Authority circular)`.
  - The site should not present "25% of the real gain" as a general rule; see "Needs the founder".
