Settling pass, 2026-10-26: conventions for every fork (input for the forks; the lead's brief is settle-brief.md).

OWNERSHIP
- You edit ONLY the rule-base files named in your prompt and the data/rules/*.json records of their prefixes.
- Never edit: site/, packages/, docs/ (docs/method.md is the editor's, at the very end), README.md or coverage.md of the
  references, data/sources/*, other forks' files, requests.md or settled.md (the editor writes those from your partial).
- Never commit, push, stash, reset or check out files. The main checkout holds the lead's work.
- Do not run `npm run extract:sources` (it writes every data/rules record while other forks edit theirs). You may run
  `npm run extract:sources -w @finance-tool/pipeline -- --dry` (prints only) and `npm run validate` (read-only).

SOURCES
- Never fill a financial or tax fact from memory. Every change cites the source you read, with its locator, and the
  Source-line segment you touch says `checked 2026-10-26`.
- Read sources with tools/src.py and always set SRC_CACHE=/home/ori/dev/finance-tool/research/knowledge/2026-10-25-scope/tools/work/srccache
  (the review's 915 cached sources are there). src.py fetches gov.il HTML pages through research/audit/tools/govil-page.sh
  (the only allowed workaround); everything else by plain curl. A bot check, captcha, 403 shell or JS-only page is
  "blocked": list it and leave the item as it was. Never work around it.
- Keep shell commands simple: scripts in files under research/knowledge/2026-10-25-scope/tools/ named with your fork id
  (f1_*.py ...), large downloads under tools/work/, never /tmp.
- General sources first (CLAUDE.md, D30): never cite a company's document where a law, regulation or regulator says it.

TOOLS
- tools/rb.py show ID ...    the markdown block with file and line;  rb.py noscope;  rb.py ids PREFIX (next free id)
- tools/ref.py ID            the review's bundle (record JSON + references block as the reviewer saw it)
- tools/rj.py show ID ...    a data/rules record's title, title_he, text_he, strength, checked
- tools/rj.py apply P.json   patch fields of data/rules records (keeps format; refuses sources/derived_from and new keys)
- review.json entries: scope, institutions, general_source (quote, url, locator), settle, split, company_doc_needed,
  note, text_issue. Read every text_issue of your prefixes, not only review.md's table (the table is cut short).

THE SCOPE LINE (README "The Scope line", strict format, one line directly after `- Source:` and its continuation lines)
  - Scope: general
  - Scope: institution: <Name>[, <Name>...]
  - Scope: unsettled: <what would settle it, in English>[; seen at: <Name>[, <Name>...]]
- Add one to every data/rules record of your prefixes that has none (`rb.py noscope`). Start from review.json's verdict;
  depart from it only on the sources, and say why in your partial.
- Names: the names already used in Scope lines (Altshuler Shaham, Altshuler Shaham Trade, Harel, Mor, Meitav,
  Meitav Trade, The Phoenix, Infinity, Clal, Migdal, Migdal Mutual Funds, Menora Mivtachim, Analyst, Yalin Lapidot,
  IBI, MTF, Kesem, Tachlit, Psagot, Excellence, Fair, Yachad Rofim, Hebrew University provident fund, Bank Leumi,
  Bank Hapoalim, Discount Bank, Mizrahi Tefahot Bank, First International Bank, Bank Yahav, Mercantile Discount Bank,
  Bank Massad, Bank of Jerusalem, One Zero Bank, Interactive Israel, Interactive Brokers, Interactive Brokers Ireland,
  iShares, BlackRock, Reit 1), else data/sources/publishers.json's name. New ones decided: Tel Aviv Stock Exchange,
  S&P Dow Jones Indices, MSCI, Nasdaq, J.P. Morgan Asset Management, Israel Securities Authority (only where it acts
  as a site owner, MF-55).
- general: the record's Source line carries a general source FIRST that says the record's main statement. If the
  review quoted a general source the record does not cite, verify the quote yourself (src.py find) and add it first.
  A company document that only repeats a general rule leaves the Source line (README: "Company documents that only
  repeat the rule are dropped"). A company detail worth keeping stays only as a named example in Conditions
  ("Example: in Harel's bylaw s.8 ..."); its document may stay on the Source line, after the general source, only when
  the text names that company beside the detail.
- institution: the record's text names each company in the sentence that carries its term; where a general
  counterpart exists (a legal maximum), Conditions point to that rule.
- unsettled: Conditions say what was searched; `seen at:` lists where it was seen.

SPLITS (a record joining a general rule and a company's term)
- The general part keeps the old id. The company part takes the next free id of the prefix (`rb.py ids PREFIX`),
  as a new `### PREFIX-NN <title naming the company>` block placed directly after the parent, with its own Rule,
  Conditions (first sentence: "Split from PREFIX-MM on 2026-10-26 (scope settling)."), Figures if any, Source (the
  company's document), Scope, Dimensions, Likely to change. The parent's Conditions say where the part went
  ("<Company>'s <term> moved to PREFIX-NN on 2026-10-26.").
- Where the company sentence is already said by another record (the review names one: "it is CAT-43", "it belongs
  in ETF-58"), drop it from the parent and point there instead of making a new id.
- Do NOT create data/rules JSON for a new id; list it for requests.md.
- Search site/app, data/ (outside data/rules), packages/ and docs/method.md for the parent id (grep -rn "PARENT-ID").
  Report what cites it and whether that citation uses the part that moved (then it is a request for the lead, and a
  docs/method.md line for the editor).

DATA/RULES JSON
- When you change a record's meaning, or its Hebrew text is stale or wrong against its source, update title_he and
  text_he (and the English `title` to match the markdown heading), set "checked": "2026-10-26", and change "strength"
  when the record's evidence level changes (an institution record re-sourced to a regulator becomes "primary"; a
  record left only on a company document is "institution"). Never edit sources / sources_missing_reason /
  derived_from: the extraction writes them. A Scope line added alone needs no JSON change.
- Hebrew: docs/copy.md section 10. Plural address or impersonal; the site is "האתר"; spoken words, not form words
  (no ניתן, טרם, לרבות, במידה ו); maqaf ־ with no spaces between a prefix letter and a number, date, code or Latin
  word (ב־2026); gershayim ״ in abbreviations as the existing text_he does; one idea per sentence. Name the company in
  the sentence that carries its term ("אצל הראל: ..."). No doc ids in text_he unless the existing text uses them.

JUDGEMENT CALLS (the lead's provisional calls; apply them)
- Index rules: the S&P, MSCI and Nasdaq rulebooks are `institution` (the index provider); TASE's index rules stay general.
- The exchange as a company (its website terms, paid data products, Maya filter/download, nominee company, its own
  brochures and annual report): `institution: Tel Aviv Stock Exchange`. BRK-12 and BRK-29 stay unsettled until the
  rule text is read; try TASE's rulebook (and the clearing-house rules) first.
- "No rule exists" records: keep them; the record's own text (Conditions, and briefly text_he) says what search was made.
- Regulator texts that bind no one (yearly reports, voluntary outlines, drafts, plans, the chief economist's paper,
  an FAQ whose legal basis the record doubts, a published tax ruling): `general`, with a line in Conditions
  ("Not binding: <what it is>; it binds no one.") and a sentence in text_he such as "פרסום של הרשות, לא מחייב."
  (name the body: "פרסום של רשות ניירות ערך, לא מחייב.").
- Company clauses copied word for word from a regulator's standard bylaw: general once the standard bylaw is quoted.
  Cite it first; the company stays only for its own terms.
- Not rules (forum posts, press reports, return series, website terms, market counts): every data/rules record is
  listed on the rules page, so do not move any: list each (id, what it is, where it should go: data / a note / an
  open question) for requests.md. Give it the scope the definition gives it meanwhile.
- Partial counts (a figure seen in some companies' documents, no regulator text): unsettled, `seen at:` the companies.
