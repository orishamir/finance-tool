== THE DEFINITION (spec section 1, verbatim in substance) ==
Every rule record has exactly one scope, decided by what its sources say, never by where the record happens to be used.
- general: a law, a regulation, a regulator's circular, a standard bylaw (CMA "תקנון תקני/אחיד") or published regulator
  guidance, or an exchange rule says it, and it binds every institution of that kind. Cited from that general source
  first; a company document only as a named example, never as the source.
  Examples: payout within 4 business days (Income Tax Rules 1964, r.41יא); the provident fund's fee cap of 1.05% (fee
  regulations, r.2); track switching is not a tax event.
- institution: <names>: it is found only in one (or more named) institution's documents and no general source requires
  it: its forms and channels, its own figure inside a legal range, its own deadline shorter than the legal one, its
  track menu, its tariff. Examples: Altshuler Shaham's withdrawal form and bank-transfer-only channel; Harel's and
  Altshuler's 0.23% child-savings fee after 21; Mor's track menu.
- unsettled: <what would settle it>: it reads like a general rule, but only institution documents (or the press) support
  it, and a recorded search found no general source. Name the documents seen, what you searched, and what would settle it.
Not rule records (flag them in "note" if a record is one):
- a count over the collected institutions ("4 of 8 banks pay no interest"): belongs in data/ as a computed count;
- an example of a general rule (a company document showing how it applies the rule): the record is general, cites the
  general source, and may carry the company detail as a named example.
Edge cases, decided:
- a legal maximum with each company's own figure: two records, the maximum general, the figure institution;
- a company's wording that contradicts a general rule: the general rule is shown; the company clause, if kept, is
  institution and must say it departs from the rule;
- a mixed record (general part + company part) is split: the general part keeps the id, the company part gets a new id.
  Give the scope of the part that keeps the id, and describe the part to split off in "split";
- a third-party copy of a regulator's text (prisha.co.il hosting a CMA circular, olaw.org.il, he.wikisource, Capitax,
  Nevo) is a general source with the host named; the scope is general;
- a count over only some institutions ("in the three bylaws we read") is never general: unsettled or institution.
Practical readings (reviewer's guidance; say in "note" when you lean on one):
- Scope is about who the statement applies to. A regulator's dataset (Gemel-Net, Pension-Net, data.gov.il) that shows one
  company's track menu or one fund's figure makes the record institution: <that company>, even though the source kind is
  general. A statement about how the dataset itself is built, true for every row, is general.
- A company's own bylaw (one fund's "תקנון") is an institution document, unless the clause is copied from a CMA
  standard bylaw; then look for the standard bylaw (gov.il circular annexes, prisha.co.il) and, if found, it is general.
- Bank of Israel Proper Conduct of Banking Business directives (ניהול בנקאי תקין), banking (customer service) rules and
  BoI circulars bind every bank: general. A bank's own tariff, deposit terms or channel: institution.
- TASE rules, bylaws, directives and guidelines (תקנון, הנחיות) are exchange rules: general. A TASE corporate document
  (its periodic report, its website terms) is TASE's own document: decide on the content and explain.
- Index methodologies (S&P DJI, MSCI, Nasdaq) and foreign fund issuers' pages (iShares, Vanguard) are company documents.
- Foreign law or a foreign regulator (IRS, ESMA, SIPC, US statutes) binding everyone in that jurisdiction: general, note
  the jurisdiction.
- Press, forums, Wikipedia: never general; with no general source the record is unsettled (or institution if it is
  about one named company).
- A bill or a draft is not binding: if that is all, say so in "note" (scope as the definition gives).

== THE QUOTE RULE ==
No record is general on your word alone. For every general verdict, quote the general source: the exact sentence (Hebrew
as printed), its URL, and its locator (section, regulation, page). Read it yourself in the fetched source:
  python3 research/knowledge/2026-10-25-scope/tools/src.py find URL "quoted span"      (is it there? which page?)
  python3 research/knowledge/2026-10-25-scope/tools/src.py grep URL "regex" [--page N] [--ctx 200]
  python3 research/knowledge/2026-10-25-scope/tools/src.py text URL --page N [--max 6000]
The record's references block often already carries a quoted span; check it with "find" and take the sentence around
it. Mark "read": "fetched" when you found it in the fetched source; if the source cannot be fetched (blocked, login,
dead link), use "read": "recorded only: <why>" and still give the recorded quote. The reviewer re-checks every
fetched quote by script, so copy the span exactly as src.py prints it (a run of at least 8 Hebrew words is ideal).
