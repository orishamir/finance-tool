# Search over rules and data: design (2026-10-24)

Written 2026-10-24 by the search engineer for the knowledge-layer brief (`research/knowledge/README.md`): let a person type a rule id, a Hebrew phrase, a question in their own words, app wording or slang, or a number, and reach the rule or data record. Read-only study of `site/app/lib/search.ts`, `search.test.ts`, `routes/search-index.ts`, `components/search.tsx`, the `search` block of `copy.ts`, `docs/architecture.md` 3.5 and `docs/flows.md` 6.3, plus a census of `data/`. A design, not code; nothing under `site/` or `data/` was changed. The site was built once to measure the index (`site/build/` had no build).

## 0. Summary and the numbers

- **Today's index**: 2,070 entries, 550 KB raw, **44 KB gzipped** (GitHub Pages serves gzip). Kinds: 1,082 tracks, 644 terms, 149 guide names, 82 catalogue names, 59 companies, 27 banks and deposit names, 27 not-covered. About 264 bytes an entry; `line` text is repeated per entry.
- **The rule base**: 500 rules (26 id prefixes), every one with `title_he` and `text_he` (avg 417 chars, max 891, about 73 words), 214 with `figures` (501 numeric leaf values; units: 141 `pct`, 75 `nis`, 76 `count`, 35 `years`, 33 `days`, 34 `business_days`, 31 `pct_per_year`, 28 `months`, 17 `text`, …) and `figure_names_he`. 7,334 distinct Hebrew tokens in the texts, 4,271 after a naive prefix strip.
- **What adding rules costs** (measured on the real records, minified JSON, gzip -9):

  | Variant | Raw | Gzip |
  |---|---|---|
  | Rule titles only (id, title_he) | 73 KB | 13 KB |
  | Titles + full `text_he` | 452 KB | 102 KB |
  | **Light rule entry** (title, topic, strength, href, norm, numeric figures with units, figure names) | 182 KB | **27 KB** |
  | Texts as a unique-token bag (stop words removed) | 308 KB | 74 KB |
  | **Texts as raw `text_he`** keyed by id | 381 KB | **91 KB** |
  | Today's index + light rule entries | 732 KB | 71 KB |

- **Decision**: two files. `search-index.json` grows by the light entries (44 → about 75 KB gzipped with parameters, tariff groups and fund extras); `search-texts.json` (about 95 KB gzipped: rule texts, term meanings, parameter rationales) is fetched right after the first file resolves and only changes the results when it lands. First keystroke stays as fast as today; a phone on a slow network gets names and titles first and sentence matches a second later.

## 1. What exists and what it cannot do

`normalize` maps final letters, geresh/gershayim to ASCII quotes, maqaf to hyphen, strips niqqud, lower-cases Latin. `scoreEntry` ranks 0 exact, 1 prefix, 2 word start, 3 contained, 4 all words contained; `ids` match from the first digit (so "88" finds track 880). `kindRank` puts guide before term before not-covered, company, product, bank, track. One row per `href` (terms also by name), "נקרא גם" for other names of the same page. Groups of five with "הצגת כל התוצאות בקבוצה". Empty state: "עוד לא באתר" with a copy button, written for pasted product names.

Measured against today's built index:

| Query | Today | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `TXG-02`, `txg 2`, `P-03` | nothing | rule and parameter ids are not indexed |
| `מעבר מסלול` | nothing | only names are indexed; the rules PF-23, TXH-17, TXK-04 carry it in their titles |
| `מס על פיקדון`, `האם אפשר להחליף מסלול בלי מס` | nothing | no all-words pass over sentences, no question-word stripping |
| `25%` | 53 CMA tracks ("אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25%)") | the figure 25% of 28 tax rules is not indexed; the track names win by substring |
| `83,641` | nothing | the ceiling KGL-03 is a figure, not an id |
| `פקמ` (no gershayim) | nothing | the quote is normalised, never removed |
| `קרן כספית`, `פק״מ`, `גמל להשקעה`, `ניוד`, `דמי משמרת`, `טופס 867` | the guide page or a term, correctly | names and glossary variants already cover app wording well |

The rules page (`/rules`) already has its own filter over id, titles and text, and every rule card is an anchor (`/rules#TXG-02` opens its group and scrolls). The parameters page gives each parameter the anchor of its method id (`/parameters#P-03`). Deposit records are anchors on the bank page (`/companies/leumi#product-leumi-pakam-kavua-365`) and are already indexed by their 447, app, ad and abbreviation names (kind `bank`). Fund records are indexed by name, TASE number and ISIN (kind `track`, to `/workbench/funds/<exposure>`). Series have no page of their own; MKT-01 is a rule record (`etfs-and-index-funds.md`, secondary) shown inside the comparison without an anchor.

## 2. Index design

### 2.1 Entries per record kind

All entries keep today's shape (`kind, name, line, href, norm, ids?`) plus three optional fields: `figs` (numeric figures: `[value, unit]`), `alt` (extra normalised words that may match but are not shown, like the bank entries' `extra` today), and `key` (the record id, for the text file). Everything is computed at build in `routes/search-index.ts`; nothing is computed on the client but the query.

| Kind (new) | One entry per | `name` | `norm` and `alt` | `ids` | `figs` | `href` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rule` | rule (500) | `title_he` (fallback `ruleTitle()`) | norm of the title; `alt`: the Hebrew figure names, the Hebrew dimension names (`data/dimensions/*.he`), the topic name | lower-cased id `txg-02` plus the spaced and unpadded forms `txg 2`, `txg2` | every numeric leaf of `figures` as `[value, unit]`, de-duplicated by value and unit family | `/rules#<ID>` |
| `parameter` | parameter (8) | `parametersPage.names[method_id]` (P-08 has no Hebrew name yet: a copy gap to close) | norm of the name; `alt`: every field label from `parametersPage.fields` | `p-03`, `p 3`, `p3` | numeric leaf values | `/parameters#<P-id>` |
| `tariff` | provider × fee base (17 providers × up to 4 bases ≈ 60) | "<provider>: <base label>" e.g. "בנק לאומי: קנייה ומכירה" | norm of the name; `alt`: the other names of the base ("עמלות מסחר", "דמי משמרת" for custody) | — | the line rates (`[0.65, "pct_of_trade"]`) | `/companies/<id>#fees-<base>` (new anchors on the brokerage card, one per base group) |
| `track` (fund records, existing) | fund record (12) | as today | add `alt`: manager name, domicile word, "מחלקת"/"צוברת", legal form | as today | management fee `[0.07, "pct_per_year"]` | as today |
| `bank` (deposit records, existing) | each name of a deposit record (3 records) | as today | add `alt`: the wrapper's names and the type-447 word | — | `rate_offered`, `term_months` | as today |
| `series` | series (2) | `names.he[0]` | norm of the name; `alt`: publisher, "סדרה", "מדד" | — | — | the first `rule_ids` entry (`/rules#CS-10`); the CPI series has none, so it waits for a data page (see 7) |

MKT-01 needs nothing special: it is a rule (title "טווחי תשואה היסטוריים של מדד S&P 500 לפי אורך ההחזקה"), with eight `pct` figures and a secondary strength. The alias table gives it "תשואה היסטורית" and "S&P 500 בעבר".

### 2.2 The second file

`search-texts.json`: `{ "TXG-02": "<text_he>", …, "term:krn-kspyt": "<meaning>", "P-03": "<rationale_he>" }`, raw Hebrew, not normalised, so the client can show a snippet in the record's own words. The client normalises once on arrival (500 × 417 chars, a few milliseconds), builds a token set per record with the stems of 3.2, and keeps both. It is fetched by `loadIndex()` right after the index resolves; if `navigator.connection?.saveData` is true it is fetched only when the typed query has two or more content words. The current query re-runs when it lands (the hook already recomputes on state change).

Why not a token bag: it saves 17 KB gzipped but loses the snippet and phrase matching, and the snippet is what tells "מעבר מסלול בתוך הקרן אינו אירוע מס" apart from "מעבר מסלול: תוך ימי עסקים ספורים, בלי עמלה" in a list of five.

### 2.3 Matching a number inside the figures without matching every 2026

Only `figures[*].value` numerics enter `figs`. Dates (`checked`, `effective_from`, `tax_year`, `recheck_date`) are never indexed, and `unit: "text"` values (which hold dates such as "2025-04-01") are skipped. The two year-like values that remain are genuine counts (BRK-39 `2016`, CAT-42 `2009`) and only match an exact query.

The query parser recognises a **number query**: digits with optional thousands separators, a comma or dot decimal (`parseNumber` in `lib/format.ts`), an optional sign and an optional unit mark: `%`, `אחוז`, `₪`, `ש"ח`, `שח`, `שקל`, `ימים`, `שנים`, `חודשים`. Then:

| Query | Matches | Score |
|---|---|---|
| `25%`, `25 אחוז` | `figs` with value 25 and a `pct*` unit | 0 (exact figure) |
| `83,641`, `83641`, `83641 ₪` | `figs` with value 83641 and a `nis*` unit (with ₪) or any non-pct unit (without) | 0 |
| `0.3%`, `0,3%` | value 0.3, `pct*` | 0 |
| bare integer `n ≤ 100` (e.g. `25`, `15`, `3`) | `pct*` figures equal to n, **and** today's id-prefix rule for tracks | 1 for the figure, as today for ids |
| bare integer `n > 100` (e.g. `83641`, `2026`) | exact equality against every non-pct figure, and today's id-prefix rule | 1 |
| `21 ימים` | value 21, unit `days` or `business_days` | 0 |

A digit-only query never runs against the texts (so `25` does not hit "2025" in a sentence). A number query shows the figure's name in the row line (3.3), so twenty-eight "25%" rows are tellable apart; ordering among them is in 4.2.

## 3. Hebrew matching

### 3.1 Normalisation (extend `normalize`)

Keep everything it does. Add, on both sides:

- **Quotes inside a word are deleted**, not just unified: `פק״מ`, `פק"מ`, `פקמ` all become `פקמ`; `מק"ם` and `מק"מ` both become `מקמ` (finals are already mapped). The regex: a quote between two Hebrew letters. Geresh at a word end (`מס׳`) is deleted too.
- **Hyphens and maqaf become a space** in the token pass (`ת"א-125` → `תא 125`), stay as `-` in the whole-name pass (so `ת"א-125` still matches the exposure name exactly).
- **Latin and digits**: as today; rule ids are lower-cased on both sides.

The highlight in `search.tsx` assumes equal positions in raw and normalised names; with deletions it must map through an offsets array (build it in `normalize` once per name at render) or fall back to bolding the whole name when the mapping fails.

### 3.2 Prefixes, plurals, construct forms

**Strip on the query side, never on the names.** For each query word of four letters or more that starts with ו, ב, ל, מ, ה, ש, כ (and the two-letter runs וב, ול, ומ, וה, וש, שב, של, שה, שמ, כש, מה, לכ, בכ), add the stripped form as an extra variant. A word matches a name or token if any variant does; the stripped variant scores one tier lower than the whole word, so "הקרן" exact beats "קרן" via "ה". Names stay canonical; only the texts are tokenised with both the raw token and its stripped form (at load, on the client).

**A light stemmer for the token pass and for the all-words pass over names**, applied to both the query word and the token: for words of five letters or more, strip one suffix from `ות`, `יות`, `ים`, `יים`, `ה`, `ת`, `י` and keep a stem of three letters or more. Finals are already mapped, so `קרנות` → `קרנ` = `קרן`; `פיקדונות` → `פיקדונ` = `פיקדון`; `מסלולים` → `מסלול`; `קופת` → `קופ` = `קופה` → `קופ`; `תקרת` → `תקר` = `תקרה` → `תקר`. The stem match is the lowest tier; a whole-word match is never outranked by a stem match. No verb morphology: "להחליף" is handled by the alias table, not by the stemmer.

Why not strip prefixes from the index: "מסלול" would also index "סלול", "הון" would be lost (3 letters, below the floor anyway), and every name would need a second normalised form. Query-side variants add recall without touching the data.

### 3.3 Question-form queries

A query is treated as a question when it has three or more words, starts with a question word, or ends with `?`. Before matching, drop these words (a stop list, not a grammar): `האם, אפשר, איך, כמה, מה, מהו, מהי, מהם, למה, מתי, מי, איזה, איזו, צריך, אני, אנחנו, לי, לנו, שלי, שלנו, את, של, על, עם, זה, זו, רוצה, רוצים, יש, אין, אם, או, גם, כדאי, שווה, האתר`. Keep negation and tax words that carry meaning: `בלי, ללא, לא, פטור, פטורה, אסור, מותר`. Apply the phrase aliases of 3.4 first (so "להחליף מסלול" becomes "מעבר מסלול"), then run the remaining content words as an **all-words pass with stems over title + text** (tier 6 in 4.1), requiring every content word when there are two, and all but one when there are three or more (coverage ≥ 2/3). A single content word left after stripping ("כמה מס?") runs as a normal word query.

## 4. Ranking, labels and row lines

### 4.1 Score tiers (extend `scoreEntry`)

| Tier | Match | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | exact id (`txg-02`, `txg 2`, `p-03`); exact name or variant; exact figure **with** a unit mark | `TXG-02`, `קרן כספית`, `25%`, `83,641 ₪` |
| 1 | name starts with query; id prefix (`txg` lists the TXG rules in id order); bare number equals a figure | `מעבר מסלול` → PF-23, TXH-17, TXK-04 |
| 2 | a word of the name starts with the query; the query phrase in the title | `השתלמות` |
| 3 | contained in the name | `תלמות` |
| 4 | every query word in the name (whole words, then prefix-stripped, then stems) | `תקרת הפקדה קופת גמל להשקעה` → KGL-02 |
| 5 | the query phrase in the record's text (needs the texts file) | `אינו אירוע מס` → TXH-17 |
| 6 | all content words in title + text (coverage rule of 3.3) | `כמה מס על פיקדון` → TXI-03 |

Secondary order, as today: `kindRank`, then name length. New `kindRank`: guide 0, term 1, **rule 2, parameter 3**, notcovered 4, company 5, product 6, bank 7 (deposits and tariffs), **series 8**, track 9. A rule row therefore sits under a guide page or a term that matches equally well ("קרן כספית": the guide first, then the term, then MF-07), and above everything when it alone matches the person's words (a question, a rule id, a figure). A rule **outranks everything** when the query is an exact id, an exact figure with a unit mark, or a question form whose only tier ≤ 4 hits are rules.

### 4.2 Order inside the rules group

Tier first. Then, until the knowledge-layer synthesis defines a rule importance order (its "most known and important rules easiest to reach" ordering should become the tie-break here, as a `rank` field on the entry): strength (`primary`, `primary-mirror`, `institution`, `secondary`, `open`), then for figure hits a figure keyed `rate`/`ceiling`/`threshold` before any other key and fewer figures before more (the figure is the rule's point), then title length, then id. Under this fallback `25%` opens with TXF-02 and `15%` with TXI-03.

## 5. Zero results and "did you mean"

Classify the query before choosing the empty state:

| Query looks like | Empty state |
|---|---|
| **a name** (one or two words, no question word, or a pasted model code) | today's "עוד לא באתר" with the copy button and the not-covered link; unchanged |
| **a question** (3.3) | title "אין כלל שעונה על זה בניסוח הזה"; body "האתר מחפש בכותרות ובטקסט של 500 כללים. נסו מילה או שתיים מהשאלה, או עברו על הכללים לפי נושא."; then up to three **"אולי התכוונתם"** rows from a relaxed pass (any single content word, stems on, words weighted by rarity across the texts so "מסלול" counts more than "מס"); links "לכל הכללים" (`/rules#<topic>` of the best relaxed hit) and "לשאלות הפתוחות"; a quiet "להעתיק את השאלה" instead of the primary copy button, with the line "אפשר לשלוח אותה למי ששלח לכם את האתר" |
| **a number** | title "אין מספר כזה בכללים ובקטלוג"; then "קרוב לזה:" rows for figures within ±2% of the number (an old ceiling someone remembers lands near the new one) and ids that contain the digits; no copy button |
| **one word with a near miss** | before the empty state, a Damerau-Levenshtein distance-1 pass of words of five letters or more against the vocabulary of names and texts (about 7,000 tokens; cheap) gives "אולי: <word>?" as a tappable chip that rewrites the query |

The "לחפש רק חלק מהשם" link stays for names; for a question it becomes "לחפש רק את המילים העיקריות" and keeps the content words after stripping.
