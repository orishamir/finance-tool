## Summary

- A kupat gemel lehashkaa is a provident fund for paying a lump sum to a self-paying member or the member's beneficiaries. It was created by Amendment 15 to the Provident Funds Law in June 2016 (KGL-01). The first tracks opened on 2016-11-10 (KGL-22).
- One annual deposit ceiling applies per person across all of that person's gemel lehashkaa accounts at every manager. It is fixed in the law at NIS 70,000 and CPI-linked every 1 January. Five institution sources give NIS 83,641 for 2026. The official 2026 notice was not reached (KGL-02, KGL-03).
- The home-sale exception in the discovery report is not a current rule. The bylaws describe it as a window that closed on 31 Dec 2017, capped at NIS 2,500,000 (not 2,000,000), and the current law text marks section 22(א1) as repealed (KGL-05).
- Money can be withdrawn at any time, in full or in part. Payment is due within 4 business days (KGL-08). At early retirement age (60) the balance can move to an annuity provident fund, into a track for annuity recipients, for an annuity only (KGL-09). Transfers out go to another gemel lehashkaa. No route into a gemel lehashkaa from another product type was found (KGL-10).
- If no track is chosen, the member joins the manager's default track. In every bylaws document read, that is the "general" (כללי) track. The age-based default model does not apply to this product (KGL-11).
- Management fees may be charged on the balance (at most 1.05% a year) and on deposits (at most 4%), under the 2012 fee regulations. The text was read through a mirror (KGL-14). An offered discount must last at least two years (KGL-16).
- 12 managing companies offer it to the general public and 6 sector funds offer it to defined groups (CMA Gemel-Net, period 202609).

## Rules

### KGL-01 What it is and its legal basis
- Rule: A "קופת גמל להשקעה" is defined in section 1 of the Provident Funds Law as "קופת גמל המיועדת לתשלום סכום הון לעמית עצמאי או למוטביו". The definition was added by Amendment 15, passed on 30 May 2016 and published on 7 June 2016. The same amendment added "להשקעה" to the fund types in section 13(א), set the deposit ceiling in section 22(א) (KGL-02), allowed pledging in section 25(א)(1) (KGL-18), and amended section 27(ג). It also amended the Income Tax Ordinance (sections 1, 3(ה4)(3), 3(ה7)(2), 9(17א)(ד) and 9א(א)); those are for the tax file. Amendment 15 also added "קופת גמל להשקעה" as a "סוג מוצר פנסיוני" in the advice, marketing and clearing law, and as a form of pension saving in the law on dividing pension savings between separated spouses.
- Conditions / exceptions: The definition says "עמית עצמאי", meaning a member paying for themselves. The wording on Wikisource is "עמית יחיד המשלם כספים לקופת גמל בעד עצמו" (read through the mirror only). For employer deposits, see open question 5.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Sefer HaChukim 2554, 7.6.2016, p. 885–886: חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (תיקון מס' 15), התשע"ו-2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il//20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf), sections 1–9 · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. Context: the [Knesset bill P/3539/25 explanatory notes](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lst_2708087.pdf), p. 3, describe Amendment 15 as the law that "אפשר לחברות מנהלות להקים מוצר חיסכון שנקרא 'קופת גמל להשקעה'" · checked 2026-10-22 · primary (Knesset document, descriptive).
- Dimensions: `regulator`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: no.

### KGL-02 Annual deposit ceiling: one ceiling per person across all managers, CPI-linked
- Rule: The total a member may deposit "בכלל חשבונותיו בכל קופות הגמל להשקעה" may not exceed NIS 70,000 "בכל שנת כספים". The amount is updated every 1 January by the rise in the CPI known on that date. For the first update, the comparison is with the CPI known on 1 July 2016.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ceiling is per member (per ID number, as Altshuler words it), not per account and not per manager. A child's account counts against the child's own ceiling (KGL-21). For the expired home-sale window, see KGL-05.
- Figures: NIS 70,000 per tax year (שנת כספים), base CPI known on 1 July 2016. Indexed every 1 January. The base figure is fixed in law.
- Source: [Sefer HaChukim 2554, p. 885](https://fs.knesset.gov.il//20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf), Amendment 15 section 3 (adding to section 22(א)) · checked 2026-10-22 · primary. The current law text on [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)), section 22(א), still carries the 70,000 base · checked 2026-10-22 · secondary (mirror). The bylaws repeat the rule: [Altshuler Shaham "חיסכון פלוס" bylaws, March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clause 8.1 · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.
- Dimensions: `deposit-ceiling`, `real-vs-nominal`
- Likely to change: yes. A private bill to raise the ceiling to NIS 300,000 a year passed a preliminary reading (17 for, none against), as reported on 20 Mar 2024 by [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/consumerism/news/article/1005636) (secondary). Its current status is open question 12. The current law text still shows the 70,000 base.

### KGL-03 The 2026 ceiling figure
- Rule: For 2026 the ceiling is NIS 83,641 per person per calendar year. Altshuler gives NIS 83,641.09.

### KGL-04 When the ceiling is checked, and the deadline for counting a deposit in a year
- Rule: The managing company checks compliance with the ceiling when the money is deposited, not at the end of the tax year: "חברה מנהלת תבדוק עמידה בתקרת ההפקדה במועד הפקדת כספים לקופת גמל להשקעה ולא בתום שנת הכספים".
- Conditions / exceptions: The bylaws do not say how a deposit that breaches the ceiling across two managers is detected or returned (open question 4). Altshuler sets its own year-end cut-offs for 2026: a bank transfer must reach the company's account by 31.12.26 at 18:30, and a cheque must reach its offices by 28.12.2026 at 12:00. Later deposits count toward 2027.
- Figures: cut-off dates as above, Altshuler only, for 2026.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clause 8.2; the same clause is in [Infinity bylaws 1.7.2024](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf), 8.2, and [Yachad Rofim bylaws 25.7.2024](http://www.krn-rofim.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/%d7%aa%d7%a7%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%9f_%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a4%d7%aa_%d7%92%d7%9e%d7%9c_%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%a9%d7%a7%d7%a2%d7%94__25.7.2024-1.pdf), 8.2; cut-offs from the [Altshuler deposit page](https://www.as-invest.co.il/interstedin/saving_plus_deposit/) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.
- Dimensions: `deposit-ceiling`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: cut-off dates change yearly.

### KGL-05 Home-sale exception: a window that closed on 31 Dec 2017, not a current rule
- Rule: Notwithstanding the annual ceiling, a member could deposit "עד ליום י"ג בטבת התשע"ח (31 בדצמבר 2017)" an amount up to NIS 2,500,000 ("שני מליון וחמש מאות אלף") or the proceeds from selling dwellings, whichever was lower. This was subject to the conditions in section 22(א1) of the law, including presenting approval from the Director of the Tax Authority under section 22(א1)(4). The current law text marks section 22(א1) "(בוטל)", and the section's amendment tag cites בג"ץ 10042/16.
- Conditions / exceptions: This corrects the discovery report (`domain-landscape.md` 4.3), which gives the figure as NIS 2,000,000 and presents the exception as current. Two later bylaws still print the clause with the 2017 date: Altshuler (March 2025) and Infinity (1.7.2024). The Yachad Rofim bylaws (25.7.2024) leave it out. How and when 22(א1) ceased to apply is open question 2.
- Figures: NIS 2,500,000 or the sale proceeds, whichever is lower. Deposits allowed until 31 Dec 2017. Not in force for 2026 on the sources read.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clause 8.3, and [Infinity bylaws 1.7.2024](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf), clause 8.3 · institution; [Wikisource law text](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)), section 22(א1) "(בוטל)" and the section 22 tag "[תיקון: תשע״ו, תשע״ו־3, תשע״ו־5, תשע״ז־2, בג״ץ 10042/16]" · secondary (mirror) · all checked 2026-10-22.
- Dimensions: `deposit-ceiling`
- Likely to change: no (historical).

### KGL-06 Who may open one, and in whose name
- Rule: Anyone may open a gemel lehashkaa, at any age ("כל אדם רשאי לפתוח קופת גמל להשקעה, בכל גיל ובכל סכום", Altshuler). It can be opened in another person's name, including children, grandchildren or a spouse. Doing so "כמוה כמתן מתנה בלתי חוזרת" (an irrevocable gift), and the account is then managed by that person, except where the person is a minor (Meitav). For a minor, the manager pays withdrawals to the parent who is the minor's guardian: "בחשבונו של ילדו הקטין, אם הוא אפוטרופסו" (bylaws clause 11). The managing company may accept or refuse a joining request at its discretion, subject to law (clause 7.1), and may not close an account against the member's will except as in clause 33 (clause 7.2).
- Conditions / exceptions: No residency rule and no minimum age for acting alone were found (open question 6). The number of accounts per person is not limited in any source found; the ceiling is worded across "כלל חשבונותיו" (all their accounts).
- Figures: none.
- Source: [Altshuler deposit page](https://www.as-invest.co.il/interstedin/saving_plus_deposit/); [Meitav product page](https://www.meitav.co.il/provident_pension/provident_invest/); [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 7.1, 7.2, 11; the [Hebrew University fund joining form](https://gemel.huji.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/‏‏‏‏‏‏הצטרפות-קופת-גמל-להשקעה-מאי-2026.pdf), p. 3, has a "חתימת אפוטרופוס" (guardian signature) line · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.
- Dimensions: `eligibility`
- Proposed dimension: `ownership-gift` (money put in another person's account is theirs).
- Likely to change: the CMA 2026 plan lists a revision of the joining circular (KGL-23).

### KGL-07 How money goes in, and minimums
- Rule: A managing company may accept deposits only by credit card, bank transfer, standing order (direct-debit authorisation), or cheque. A member may deposit into, or move money to, any track listed in the bylaws annex.
- Conditions / exceptions: No regulatory minimum deposit was found. Altshuler requires a minimum of NIS 100 a month for a standing order and states no minimum for one-off deposits. Meitav's page states no minimum. Altshuler also offers deposits from its personal area online.
- Figures: Altshuler standing-order minimum NIS 100 a month (2026 page).
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 9 and 10 (same in Infinity and Yachad Rofim); [Altshuler deposit page](https://www.as-invest.co.il/interstedin/saving_plus_deposit/) "מותנות בהפקדה חודשית מינימלית של 100 ₪" · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.
- Dimensions: `minimum`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: yes, per manager.

### KGL-08 Withdrawal at any time, in full or in part
- Rule: The managing company pays the accumulated balance on a complete, signed request, within four business days of receiving it. If that date falls in the first three business days of a month, the company may defer payment to the fourth business day of that month. A member may ask to receive the money as consecutive monthly payments on a fixed day. Within 10 business days of payment, the company sends a report of the withdrawal. If payment is late, the company pays late-payment interest ("ריבית פיגורים"), funded from its management fees. If the account value fell during the delay, the company pays the higher of the two amounts.
- Conditions / exceptions: Partial withdrawal is recognised in clause 36.2.1 ("משיכה מלאה או חלקית"). Tax is withheld at payment under the regulatory arrangement (clause 15); see `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. No exit fee appears in the bylaws; the only amounts that may be taken from a member's account are management fees and direct expenses (clause 27.1).
- Figures: 4 business days; up to the 4th business day of the month when the date falls in the month's first 3 business days; report within 10 business days.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 11–15, 27.1, 36.2.1 (identical in [Infinity 1.7.2024](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf) and [Yachad Rofim 25.7.2024](http://www.krn-rofim.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/%d7%aa%d7%a7%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%9f_%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a4%d7%aa_%d7%92%d7%9e%d7%9c_%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%a9%d7%a7%d7%a2%d7%94__25.7.2024-1.pdf)) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution. The Meitav page also says "תוך 4 ימי עסקים" · institution.
- Dimensions: `liquidity-lockup`, `fee-exit`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: yes. The CMA 2026 plan (p. 20) includes a new circular setting uniform rules for withdrawing money from pension savings (KGL-23).

### KGL-09 Taking the money as an annuity: transfer to an annuity provident fund from early retirement age
- Rule: An annuity provident fund (קופת גמל לקצבה) may receive a transfer from a "קופת גמל להשקעה, ובלבד שהעמית הגיע לגיל הפרישה המוקדמת". Money transferred this way is credited "למסלול השקעה המיועד למקבלי קצבה בקופה המקבלת, בהתאם לבחירתו של העמית, לשם קבלת קצבה בלבד". The Retirement Age Law, section 5, sets early retirement age at "גיל 60 לגבר ולאישה".
- Conditions / exceptions: After the transfer the money is for an annuity only. Section 8 of the Retirement Age Law has a transition table for women born up to April 1955. For the tax treatment of the annuity, see `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. Meitav's page claims a full tax exemption; that claim is unverified here. For annuity-fund rules, see `pension-and-gemel-lekitzba.md`.
- Figures: age 60 (men and women), fixed in law.
- Source: [Transfer regulations 2008 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(העברת_כספים_בין_קופות_גמל)), regulations 2(א)(7) and 2(ב)(2); [Retirement Age Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_גיל_פרישה), section 5 · checked 2026-10-22 · secondary (mirror of law and regulation text; official text not reached).
- Dimensions: `liquidity-lockup`, `age-or-use-exemption`, `transfer-switch-process`
- Likely to change: the CMA 2026 plan (p. 20) includes a retirement circular.

### KGL-10 Transfers between funds
- Rule: Transfers are allowed "מקופת גמל להשקעה לקופת גמל להשקעה אחרת" (regulation 3(א)(11א)), and to an annuity fund at early retirement age (KGL-09). Other transfers need the Commissioner's approval and the managing company's consent (regulation 3(א)(12)). The bylaws let a member transfer all or part of the balance, under the transfer regulations and the Commissioner's instructions. A transfer is blocked when there is an attachment or pledge on the whole balance, an unpaid loan balance, the member has died, or the receiving fund is a closed fund. A transfer delayed beyond the deadline earns the member the difference in returns, payable by whichever manager caused the delay.
- Conditions / exceptions: No route into a gemel lehashkaa from a study fund or another provident fund was found in regulation 3(א) as read through the mirror (open question 9). The transfer timeline is 10 business days from a complete request, extendable by the Commissioner by up to 20 business days (regulation 5(א)); this is shared with other products and belongs in `provident-family-common.md`.
- Figures: 10 business days (+ up to 20 with Commissioner approval).
- Source: [Transfer regulations 2008 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(העברת_כספים_בין_קופות_גמל)), regulations 3(א)(11א), 3(א)(12), 5(א) · secondary (mirror); [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 16–19 · institution · checked 2026-10-22.
- Dimensions: `transfer-switch-process`, `liquidity-lockup`
- Likely to change: the CMA 2026 plan (p. 21) amends the transfer circular so that Israelis living abroad can be identified by a document other than an Israeli ID card.

### KGL-11 Default track when none is chosen
- Rule: A member who does not choose a track when joining is placed in the default track named in the bylaws annex ("מסלול השקעה ברירת מחדל"). In every gemel lehashkaa bylaws document read, the default is the manager's general track: Altshuler 7798 "חיסכון פלוס כללי (מסלול זה מהווה גם מסלול השקעה ברירת מחדל)", Infinity 9638, Yachad Rofim ("יצורף העמית למסלול השקעה כללי כברירת מחדל"), and the Hebrew University fund 12905. The Meitav Kids page also names a general track as the default. The CMA's age-based default model (three age bands: up to 50, 50 to 60, 60 and over) applies only to provident funds "שאינן קרנות השתלמות ואינן קופות גמל להשקעה". It does not apply here.
- Conditions / exceptions: The Meitav gemel lehashkaa bylaws were not found (open question 13). Its default track is inferred only from its Kids page and from the founder's "general, most common" label.
- Figures: none.
- Source: [CMA circular 2020-9-18 "מסלולי השקעה בקופות גמל - תיקון", 16 Nov 2020](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/investmentsoptions16112020/he/2020-9-18.pdf), annex sections 3 and 4(א) · primary; bylaws clause 7.4 and annex A of [Altshuler](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), [Infinity](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf) and [Yachad Rofim](http://www.krn-rofim.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/%d7%aa%d7%a7%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%9f_%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a4%d7%aa_%d7%92%d7%9e%d7%9c_%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%a9%d7%a7%d7%a2%d7%94__25.7.2024-1.pdf); [Hebrew University joining form](https://gemel.huji.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/‏‏‏‏‏‏הצטרפות-קופת-גמל-להשקעה-מאי-2026.pdf), p. 2; [Meitav Kids page](https://www.meitav.co.il/provident_pension/saving_kids/) · institution · checked 2026-10-22.
- Dimensions: `transfer-switch-process`, `eligibility`
- Likely to change: yes. The CMA 2026 plan (p. 20) revises the investment-tracks circular.

### KGL-12 What kinds of tracks a gemel lehashkaa may offer (regulator's categories)
- Rule: Under circular 2020-9-18, annex section 7, a managing company may run the following tracks in study funds, gemel lehashkaa and non-insurance-fund policies:
  - (א) "שני מסלולים כלליים ובלבד שאחד ממסלולי ההשקעה יהיה מסלול פאסיבי-כללי" (two general tracks, one of which is passive-general), as defined in the CMA track list;
  - (א1) further general tracks, closed to new members;
  - (ב) specialised tracks by asset class, by investment field, by investment instrument, or combined;
  - (ג) tracks for a defined target population;
  - (ד) other specialised tracks with the Commissioner's approval;
  - (ה) "לא יותר מעשרה מסלולי השקעה מתמחים" (no more than ten specialised tracks).

  Further rules in the same circular:
  - Section 8 bans track names that promise quality or risk, giving as examples "בטוחה, רווחית, סולידית, יציבה, איתנה, אגרסיבית".
  - Section 9 requires a track's name to match its investment policy as set out in the CMA track list (רשימת מסלולי ההשקעה, section 10).
  - Section 12(ג) requires at least 45 days' notice, and no more than three months, before a track's policy changes.
  - Section 14 allows a track to be closed only if it is empty, or through a merger.
- Conditions / exceptions: The ten-track limit in 7(ה) conflicts with the 202609 data: Meitav reports 16 gemel lehashkaa tracks, 15 of them not "general". The 2020 text has probably been amended or superseded (open question 10). The categories that managers actually use, as Gemel-Net's specialisation field shows in 202609, are: כללי, מתמחים בניהול אקטיבי (מניות, אשראי ואג"ח, כספי (שקלי), אג"ח ממשלות), עוקבי מדדים (עוקב מדד s&p 500, עוקב מדדי מניות, עוקב מדדים - גמיש, עוקב מדדי אג"ח), מתמחים באפיקי השקעה סחירים (מניות סחיר, אג"ח סחיר, משולב סחיר, אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר), הלכתי (הלכה יהודית, הלכה איסלאמית), and קיימות. The full per-manager list belongs in `tracks-catalogue-and-decoder.md`.
- Figures: up to 2 general tracks open to new members; up to 10 specialised tracks (2020 text); policy-change notice of 45 days to 3 months.
- Source: [CMA circular 2020-9-18](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/investmentsoptions16112020/he/2020-9-18.pdf), annex sections 7–14 · primary; [CMA Gemel-Net datastore, resource a30dcbea-a1d2-482c-ae29-8f781f5025fb, REPORT_PERIOD 202609, FUND_CLASSIFICATION "קופת גמל להשקעה"](https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=a30dcbea-a1d2-482c-ae29-8f781f5025fb) · primary · checked 2026-10-22.

### KGL-14 Fee bases and the regulated maximum (domain open question 8, for this product)
- Rule: A managing company may charge management fees from two bases:
  - the accumulated balance (יתרה צבורה), at most 1.05% a year ("מן היתרה הצבורה בחשבונו של כל עמית בשיעור שלא יעלה על 1.05% לשנה");
  - the payments transferred to it for each member, at most 4% ("מתוך התשלומים המועברים אליה בשל כל עמית – בשיעור שלא יעלה על 4%").

  Regulation 2 applies to every provident fund except those listed in regulation 2(ב). Gemel lehashkaa is not on that list. The listed exclusions include study funds, "קופת גמל בניהול אישי" and "קופת גמל ענפית"; regulation 4 caps those at 2% a year of the balance, and regulation 5 has sector funds charge actual expenses within the regulation 4 cap. The bylaws repeat both bases and point to "שיעור דמי הניהול המרבי הקבוע בהסדר התחיקתי".
- Conditions / exceptions: Each track counts as a separate fund for fee purposes (regulation 6). The fee regulations were read only through the Wikisource mirror (open question 3). Kol Zchut, which cites "סעיף 2(א)", returned 403. The self-directed variant falls under regulation 4; see `ira-self-directed.md`. Sector funds charge "לפי הוצאות שהוצאו בפועל" up to the regulatory maximum (Yachad Rofim clause 35).
- Figures: balance up to 1.05% a year; deposits up to 4% of each payment. Fixed in the regulations of 2012 (effective 1 January 2013; amended 2014 and 2022). Not indexed.
- Source: [Fee regulations: תקנות הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל) (דמי ניהול), התשע"ב-2012 (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(קופות_גמל)_(דמי_ניהול)), regulations 2(א)(1), 2(א)(2), 2(ב), 4, 5, 6 · secondary (mirror of regulation text); [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clause 35 · institution; [Yachad Rofim bylaws](http://www.krn-rofim.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/%d7%aa%d7%a7%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%9f_%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a4%d7%aa_%d7%92%d7%9e%d7%9c_%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%a9%d7%a7%d7%a2%d7%94__25.7.2024-1.pdf), clause 35 · institution · checked 2026-10-22.

### KGL-16 Fee discounts: minimum duration and when they can end early
- Rule: A managing company may offer a member fees below the maximum, "ובלבד שההצעה האמורה תהיה תקפה במהלך תקופה של לפחות שנתיים" from the day the lower fee starts. It may raise the fee before the discount period ends only in two cases, with at least three months' separate notice from when the discount was granted:
  - a full or partial withdrawal, or a full or partial transfer to another fund or to another track in the same fund;
  - a balance-linked fee mechanism set out in the agreement, or another variable the Commissioner approved, other than one based on membership length.

  For a fee rise, the member and the licensee who serves them get separate notice. The rise takes effect two months after the notice, or at the date the notice states if later, and no more than four months after it was sent.
- Conditions / exceptions: The regulatory source of the two-year minimum was not found; only the bylaws were read (open question 8).
- Figures: discount valid at least 2 years; at least 3 months' notice of the early-raise conditions; a rise takes effect 2 to 4 months after notice.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 36–37 (same in [Infinity 1.7.2024](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf)) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.

### KGL-17 Beneficiaries and death
- Rule: A member may name beneficiaries for the balance on death, through an original signed form, through a licensee, or digitally under the Commissioner's rules. Without shares stated, beneficiaries split equally. A later will that deals with the fund money counts as the beneficiary instruction if it is dated after the last instruction and was delivered before payout. Without beneficiaries, the money goes to heirs under a will-probate or inheritance order. The manager may pay a small balance without an order when all of the following hold:
  - the balance is at most NIS 5,000 when the request is made, indexed under the regulatory arrangement;
  - three years have passed since the death;
  - the claimants sign an indemnity;
  - the claimants are the spouse, parent or child;
  - no order appears in the inheritance registry.
- Conditions / exceptions: none beyond the above.
- Figures: NIS 5,000 (indexed, base date not stated); 3 years.
- Source: [Altshuler bylaws March 2025](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 22–26 (same in Infinity) · checked 2026-10-22 · institution.
- Dimensions: `eligibility`
- Proposed dimension: `beneficiaries-on-death`.
- Likely to change: the CMA 2026 plan (p. 20) updates the circular on locating members and beneficiaries.

### KGL-18 Pledging rights, loans and attachment
- Rule: Amendment 15 added gemel lehashkaa to section 25(א)(1), which allows a member's rights to be pledged. The bylaws say rights "ניתנות לשעבוד" and are otherwise not transferable to another person, except through enforcement of a pledge (treated as a withdrawal under section 23) or under the 2014 law on dividing pension savings between separated spouses. A transfer is blocked while an attachment (עיקול) or pledge covers the whole balance. Some funds lend to members. Infinity's bylaws allow loans:
  - total loans up to 10% of the fund's assets;
  - each loan up to 80% of the member's balance;
  - a term of no more than 7 years;
  - repayment starting no later than one year after the loan.

  An outstanding loan is deducted on withdrawal. The Yachad Rofim template note says the clause is included only where the fund offers loans.
- Conditions / exceptions: Whether, and how far, creditors can attach a member's gemel lehashkaa balance was not researched; see `regulators-and-protection.md`.
- Figures: Infinity loans as listed above.
- Source: [Sefer HaChukim 2554, p. 885](https://fs.knesset.gov.il//20/law/20_lsr_343169.pdf), Amendment 15 section 4 · primary; [Altshuler bylaws](https://www.as-invest.co.il/media/md1afb1l/אלטשולר-שחם-גמל-להשקעה-חיסכון-פלוס-תקנון-נקי-מרץ-2025.pdf), clauses 17.1, 28; [Infinity bylaws](https://infinity.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/תקנון-גמל-להשקעה-בתוקף-מיום-1.7.2024.pdf), clause 38; [Yachad Rofim bylaws](http://www.krn-rofim.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/%d7%aa%d7%a7%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%9f_%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a4%d7%aa_%d7%92%d7%9e%d7%9c_%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%a9%d7%a7%d7%a2%d7%94__25.7.2024-1.pdf), clause 36 footnote 2 · institution · checked 2026-10-22.

## Open questions

1. **Official 2026 ceiling (domain question 11): not closed.** **[2026-10-22 browser pass 3: Closed by KGL-24: the CBS calculator reproduces 83,641.09 from the law's formula; no separate notice found or needed.]**
   - Tried: Tax Authority and gov.il CMA pages are blocked by bot protection (`data-landscape.md` 1.8). The CBS CPI API (`api.cbs.gov.il/index/data/price` and `/calculator`) returned ECONNRESET, so the indexation could not be recomputed. Wikisource notes that amount updates are published in "י"פ תשפ"ה, 9555" for the law in general, which suggests the update appears in Yalkut HaPirsumim.
   - Where the answer probably is: the Yalkut HaPirsumim notice for 1 January 2026, or a Tax Authority or CMA "עדכון סכומים" notice. As a cross-check, the CBS CPI known on 1 Jan 2026 against the CPI known on 1 July 2016, times 70,000.
2. **Home-sale exception (section 22(א1)).**
   - Open: which law added it (the Wikisource tags point to תשע"ז־2), and whether it lapsed on its own date or was voided by בג"ץ 10042/16 (6 Aug 2017, the multi-apartment tax ruling).
   - Tried: Wikisource (marks it repealed) and bylaws (window to 31.12.2017). The ruling and the amending law were not opened; the search budget ran out.
   - Where the answer probably is: the official consolidated law text in the Knesset National Legislation Database, and the ruling on supremedecisions.court.gov.il (file `16100420_o23.pdf`, linked from Wikisource).
3. **Fee cap from the official text (domain question 8): closed for this product at mirror strength.**
   - Regulations 2(א)(1)–(2) were read only on Wikisource. Kol Zchut returned 403.
   - Where the answer probably is: the Reshumot ק"ת files (תשע"ב 1260; תשע"ד 977; תשפ"ב 1812), or Nevo as a mirror.
4. **Deposits over the ceiling across managers.** How is a breach that spans two managers detected, and what happens to the excess: refund, refund with returns, or tax? The bylaws only say each manager checks at deposit time.
   - Where the answer probably is: the CMA payments circular ("חוזר אופן הפקדת תשלומים", listed in the 2026 plan p. 18), the pension clearing house (מסלקה פנסיונית) rules, or the 2014 payments regulations. Those regulations' regulation 12 covers only employer over-deposits.
5. **Employer deposits.** The law's definition points to a self-paying member ("עמית עצמאי"). No source found says whether an employer may deposit into an employee's gemel lehashkaa, or how such a deposit would count.
   - Where the answer probably is: the payments circular, or the 2014 payments regulations in their official text.
6. **Residency and age.** Can a non-resident open or keep an account? From what age can a minor act without a guardian?
   - Found so far: the 2026 plan (p. 21) implies that Israelis living abroad hold provident accounts.
   - Where the answer probably is: the joining circular ("חוזר הצטרפות לפנסיה וגמל"), which is being revised (2026 plan p. 19).
7. **Minimum deposits** per manager. Only Altshuler was found (NIS 100 a month by standing order). Other managers' joining pages or forms have not been collected.
8. **Regulatory source of the two-year minimum for a fee discount**, and of the early-raise conditions. Only the bylaws were read. Probably a CMA circular on management fees or on disclosure of fee discounts.
9. **Transfers into a gemel lehashkaa from other products** (study fund, tagmulim, policies), and from a gemel lehashkaa to products other than an annuity fund.
   - The mirror's regulation 3(א) lists only gemel lehashkaa to gemel lehashkaa. The תשפ"ג amendment (ק"ת 2099) was not checked in its official text.
   - Where the answer probably is: the official transfer regulations and the CMA transfer circular.
10. **Whether circular 2020-9-18 is still the binding track rule.** Its limit of ten specialised tracks (s.7(ה)) conflicts with the 202609 data: Meitav and Mor each have about 15 specialised gemel lehashkaa tracks.
    - Where the answer probably is: the CMA consolidated circular ("החוזר המאוחד") or a later amendment to the investment-tracks circular, plus the CMA "רשימת מסלולי ההשקעה" page.
11. **Eligibility for five sector funds** (domain question 13, partly closed): Gal, Handasaim and Technicians, the Hebrew University staff fund, Ami, and Machog. Can family members join?
    - Tried: the Hebrew University joining form, which does not state eligibility. The search budget ran out before the other bylaws were found.
    - Where the answer probably is: each fund's bylaws, under the definition of "עמית".
12. **Status of the bill to raise the ceiling to NIS 300,000** (preliminary reading reported 20 Mar 2024; secondary source). Where the answer probably is: the Knesset bills database.
13. **Meitav's own gemel lehashkaa bylaws** were not found. The site footer links only to product pages. Needed to confirm Meitav's default track, loan clause and discount terms.
    - Lead for the founder's duplicate "general" track: Gemel-Net shows only one Meitav gemel lehashkaa "כללי" track (7978). The duplicate in the app may be the Meitav Kids entry point, or a general track listed for another product.

## Left out and why

- Tax at deposit, during and at exit, the annuity exemption, and the claims on Meitav's page about tax-free annuities after 60 and 25% tax on lump sums: these belong in `tax-gemel-lehashkaa-ira-child.md`. The Amendment 15 Income Tax Ordinance hooks are listed in KGL-01 for that file.
- The self-directed (IRA) form of gemel lehashkaa: belongs in `ira-self-directed.md`. Fee regulation 4 (2% a year of the balance) applies to "קופת גמל בניהול אישי".
- Transfer timelines, statement rules and fee-regulation structure shared by all provident products: these belong in `provident-family-common.md`. They are quoted here only where a saver needs them for this product.
- The per-manager track list, per-track fees and returns, and the Meitav app walk-through: these belong in `tracks-catalogue-and-decoder.md`. Only the categories and counts are given here.
- Fee ranges observed in Gemel-Net: left out because the dataset's units are undefined (open question 14), and the full 160-row listing was read through a summarising fetch that may have dropped rows. Counts were confirmed with filtered queries instead.
- Historical rules before 2016 (the old capital "קופת גמל לתגמולים", closed to new deposits in 2008, per the Knesset bill notes): these do not change the outcome for a saver opening a fund today.
- Kol Zchut, Hamichlol, igemel-net and other secondary explainers: not used, because the regulation text, the bylaws or the regulator's own data were reachable for the same points.
