## What it said along the way

**What I'd tell my friend:** I'd go with a Pesagot (פסגות) account and buy "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)". The page says you end up with about 86,500 ₪ after 10 years. I'm fairly sure, not totally. What decided it was the big card at the top that said "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול" with 86,492 ₪. Pesagot was also #1 at the top of the accounts list. Second was Leumi at 83,396 ₪, and you lose about 3,000 ₪ with them. One catch: that number uses the page's default amounts, 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month. My real 30,000 ₪ would end up higher, but I don't know how much.

**How it went:**
- **Top of the page:** The heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" was exactly my question, so I stayed. S&P 500 was already picked. The sliders were set to 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years and 7%. I left them alone, so every figure I saw is for those amounts.
- **The first big number:** "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪" with an arrow to 86,492 ₪. Honestly that felt low. 7% a year for 10 years should be about 70% more, so I expected more like 119,000. There's a "בכסף של היום" line and an "אינפלציה" button under it, so I guess they took off for prices going up. I didn't open it.
- **Next screens:** On the right is a ranked list of accounts: Pesagot, Leumi, then Hapoalim and other banks lower down. Pesagot isn't a bank, but I've heard the name, so OK. On the left is a list of funds. Mine was ticked as "הבחירה", +0.37%. The others show minus shekel amounts in red, like −447, −1,268 and −4,492 ₪. To me those look like "you lose money with these", so they scared me off.
- **Scrolled past:** the coloured bar of costs, the English iShares and Vanguard funds marked "אין מחיר כאן", the "עם נטרול מט״ח" block (one fund there said "בין +2,212 ₪ ל־−23,766 ₪", which scared me off), the "מה הכי משנה" chart and table, and the dollar-hedging boxes. I stopped around there.

**Words I didn't get:** נאמן 0.02%, 4D / 4A, צוברת, נטרול מט״ח, חשיפה לדולר, "מס גם על שינוי המטבע", מתנהלת מט״ח, דמי משמרת, "עוד לא נאסף".
