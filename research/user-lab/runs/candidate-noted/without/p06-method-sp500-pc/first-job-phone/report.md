## What it said along the way

Gonna open an account at פסגות and buy "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)". The page marked both of them "הזול". Fairly sure. The page says I'd have about **86,492 ₪** after 10 years. That's with the numbers that were already filled in: 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month, 7% a year, so 70,000 ₪ put in. I didn't change them. With my own 30,000 ₪ the result would be different, but I don't know what it would be.

**What decided it:** the big box right at the top, "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול 86,492 ₪". Next to it the next-best pair gives 86,449 ₪, only "‎−43 ₪" less. So I figured the cheap one is cheap and picking a different one hardly matters.

**How it went:**
- **Screen 1:** The heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן" was exactly what I came for. S&P 500 was already selected. I saw the sliders (סכום היום 10,000, הפקדה חודשית 500, 10 שנים, 7%). I'd have dragged סכום היום to 30,000, but this is screenshots, so it stayed where it was. Then the big numbers.
- **Screen 2:** On the right, the account list starts with "פסגות ★ הזול 0 ₪". I know פסגות, so good. Everyone else is in minus: לאומי ‎−3,096, הפועלים ‎−5,021. On the left the fund list starts with ילין לפידות, "הבחירה". There was a row of colourful tags; I didn't read them.
- **Screens 3–4:** The minuses just keep getting bigger, down to ‎−22,985 for מיטב טרייד. That freaked me out a bit about the banks. I skimmed the long list of funds and skipped the English ones with "אין מחיר כאן".
- **Screen 5:** There was a red "‎−23,766 ₪" with "בין… ל…", which scared me off that fund. Under it was a block of text, "למה חלק מהקרנות…", which I didn't read. Got bored here and stopped; I didn't look at screens 6–10.

**Words I didn't get:** דמי משמרת, נאמן, צוברת, מחלקת דיבידנד, מנוטרל מט"ח, "סל" vs "מחקה", 4D / 4A, "מס גם על שינוי המטבע", "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל".

**What bothered me:**
