## What it said along the way

So: open an account at פסגות and buy "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)". The page marks that pair "הזול ★" and puts it right at the top. It says I'd end up with about **86,492 ₪** after 10 years. I'm fairly sure, and Pesagot being a name I know is what settled it. One catch: that number is for the page's own amounts, 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month at 7%, not my 30,000. I only had screenshots so I couldn't type my amount into the box. With 30,000 it would come out higher, but I don't know by how much.

**What I looked at, in order**
- **Top of the page:** the big heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" with S&P 500 already picked. Then the boxes: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7%.
- **Two big cards:** 86,492 ₪ for "הבחירה שלכם · הזול", and 86,449 ₪ for the next pair, only 43 ₪ less. So the difference between them is tiny.
- **Right-hand column, list of accounts:** פסגות on top at 86,492. Below it לאומי at 83,396, then הבינלאומי, מסד, הפועלים and the rest going down. The bar under each one goes from small to long and red. I took it as "more fees", but that's a guess.
- **Left-hand column, list of funds:** I read the fund names and the shekel number at the far left of each row. Everything under the top one has a minus: −43, −213, −447 and so on, down to −4,492. Those minuses put me off the lower ones.
- **Near the bottom of that list:** "הראל מחקה S&P 500 (4A) מנוטרלת מט"ח" says "בין +2,212 ₪ ל־−23,766 ₪". That range scared me off straight away.
- **Last screen I looked at:** a chart with lines and a table saying more years or a bigger monthly amount gives you more. For example, 12 years gives 105,608. Then I stopped.
