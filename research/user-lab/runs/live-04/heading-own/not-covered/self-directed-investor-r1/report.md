## What it said along the way

I've read all ten screens. Here's what I'd tell a colleague, followed by the form.

**Bottom line:** The site tells you what it can't do, which is more than most sites manage. There are two separate lists. One is the stuff it doesn't compute *yet*: ETC, physical gold through funds, corporate bonds, Israel Bonds, foreign-currency deposits and dollar-linked deposits, structured products and deposits, life insurance and old executive-insurance policies, managed policies (מסלולי האירוע, "hosting"), REITs, digital portfolio management, Amendment 190, and turning a recognized pension into a lump sum (היוון). The other is what it *won't ever* do: loans, insurance beyond risk policies, stock picking/trading/derivatives, physical real estate, ranking by past returns, actually executing anything. I'm fairly sure about the lists themselves. I'm less sure what the cards are really worth, because the page lumps things together that aren't the same kind of thing.

**What decided it:** Every card has a red "לא מחושב" (not computed) tag. The bottom section "מה האתר לא משווה ולא עושה" (what the site doesn't compare and doesn't do) says outright "אלה לא חסרים זמניים" (these aren't temporary gaps). The footer says "אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (personal, non-commercial, no marketing links), and I saw no broker name anywhere, so the suspicion check passes.

**For my setup specifically:** "בחירת מניות, מסחר ונגזרים" (stock picking, trading and derivatives) is permanently out, so the part where I trade single stocks will never be covered. And "עמלת חליפין" (the currency-conversion fee, i.e. the rate when shekels become dollars) is marked not computed. That's exactly the thing I never check. So if I came looking for one number to hold up against my broker, it isn't here. I'd have closed the tab somewhere around the second screen if the task hadn't been this list.

**What bothered me:**
- **The heading promises "מוצרים" (products), but half the list isn't products.** That's the main thing. "LBMA Gold Price AM / PM" is a price benchmark. "חברת דירוג" (rating agency) and "נאמן" (trustee) are organisations. "הסדר חוב" (debt settlement) is an event. "עמלת חליפין" is a fee. "מענק" (bonus) is a feature of a deposit. "שיווק השקעות" (investment marketing) is a service. "היוון קצבה מוכרת" is an action. "תשלומים פטורים" (exempt payments) and "קצבה מוכרת" (recognized pension) are tax categories. If the heading is wrong about these, I can't tell how much else on the page to trust.
- **The "מה חוסם" (what's blocking) block is copy-paste.** Every expanded card ends with the same sentence, "כשייסגר: יופיע במדריך..." (when it's closed it will show up in the guide). And most say "עוד לא נרשמו סגירות" (no closures recorded yet). There's no timeline.
- **Some blockers have internal codes** like MF-28, TX2-01 and TXA-28, and I have no idea what they mean.
- **The red banner says "החוקים והכללים לכל המוצרים כאן עוד בבדיקה"** (the rules for every product here are still being checked). That covers everything, so it tells me nothing.
- **Some sources are flagged "לפי החברה"** (according to the company). I read that as the issuer's own word, unchecked.
- **There's no search or filter on the list itself,** and it's long.

**What I read:** headings, the red tags, the open "מה חוסם" sections and the bottom list. I skimmed the "מה ידוע" (what's known) definitions and the source links, and didn't read the explanatory paragraphs at all.
