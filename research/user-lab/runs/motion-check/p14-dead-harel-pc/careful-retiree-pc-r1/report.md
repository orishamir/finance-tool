## What it said along the way

I'd go with **"כספי (שקלי)"**, number 13254, though I'm only fairly sure. Before I sign anything I'd ask you, or ask at Harel.

It says **0%** for "מניות" and **0%** for "מט"ח", so there are no shares and no foreign money in it. "שקלי" means shekels, so it sounds closest to my deposit. It's also one of the few safe-looking ones that shows a number: **4.25%** next to "3 שנים". The fee is "0.57% מההיסכון", about the same as all the others.

What I looked at, in order:
- **Top of the page:** "הראל" is a name I trust. The line about "דמי הניהול" was good, because the fee is what I wanted to check. Then a lot of buttons. The one marked was "קופת גמל להשקעה 16", which is what I came for. Under that, more buttons with "S&P 500" and "עוקב מדד", and I didn't know what they meant.
- **The table:** I went down the right side, reading the names of the tracks.
  - I skipped "מניות", "משולב סחיר", "S&P 500" and the "עוקב מדד" ones. Shares are gambling to me.
  - The **24.04%** on "מניות" stuck in my head, but it's shares, so not for me.
  - I stopped at two tracks: "אג"ח סחיר", because I know bonds are safe, and "כספי (שקלי)". Both say 0% shares.
  - On "אג"ח סחיר" the return only says "פחות מ־3 שנים". I think that means it's too new to have a number, so there was nothing to compare. That's why I chose "כספי (שקלי)", the one with a number.
  - I didn't understand "עוקב מדדי אג"ח". I thought מדד was the price index on the news.
- **Below the table:** I glanced at "מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל". It's a list of folded sections, and I didn't open them. At the bottom there's a search box.
