2% a year inflation. Fine. Close and scroll to see who "הזול" is.

I tried it with 750 ₪ a month for 15 years and nothing at the start. The page says I'd have **179,889 ₪**, with the account at **פסגות ניירות ערך** and the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**. It marks that pair "הזול" (the cheapest). I'm fairly sure. What decided it: the cheapest pair is at Psagot, a name I know from ads, so I'd go with that. The first thing I'd do is open a securities account (חשבון ניירות ערך) at Psagot.

**The inflation assumption:** the "אינפלציה" button opens a box that says "אינפלציה בשנה 2%". The result line says "בכסף של היום", so I take 179,889 ₪ to be in today's money, after management fees and tax. It also assumes the stock market goes up 7% a year, which is the page's default. I put in 135,000 ₪ in total (750 × 180 months).

**How it went:**
- **Top of the page:** the heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" (where and how to invest in an index most cheaply) was exactly what I came for. S&P 500 was already selected. I changed "סכום היום" (amount today) to 0, "הפקדה חודשית" (monthly deposit) to 750 and "שנים" (years) to 15, and the big numbers updated.
- **Inflation box:** opened it, saw 2%, closed it.
- **Scrolling down:** I saw the "כמה זה עולה לכם ב־15 שנים" (what it costs you over 15 years) bars. The total is about 18,299 ₪, and most of it is tax (15,364). Then the Psagot account box and the Yalin Lapidot fund box.
