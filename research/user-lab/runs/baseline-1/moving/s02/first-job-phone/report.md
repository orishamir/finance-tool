## What it said along the way

I'd say a money-market fund (קרן כספית) is basically a bank-type place to park cash. It holds short, safe stuff like מק"מ and bank deposits, earns about 3.25% a year, and you can take money out any trading day. So yes, it fits money I might need soon, and it beats the 0% in my עו"ש. I'm fairly sure about that part. The cost is what I didn't get: the page never shows a fee number I could understand.

The thing that decided it was the box "כמה מהר הכסף חוזר, ומה הוא מרוויח עד אז?". It puts עו"ש at "0%" and קרן כספית יומית at "3.25%", with "ביום מסחר", meaning you get it back on a trading day. That clicked.

**What I looked at, in order:**
- **The heading "קרן כספית" and the line under it:** "סיכון נמוך", low risk. OK, this is the right page.
- **"מה זה":** I read the first line only, "נכסים קצרים ובטוחים יחסית" (short-term, fairly safe assets), and saw "אין תשואה מובטחת" (no guaranteed return) further down. I skimmed the rest; it was a wall of text.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה":** The first line, "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן חירום…" (money that needs to stay available, like an emergency fund), answered my question. I noticed "בלי נעילה", nothing locked, and stopped there.
- **The comparison box:** This was the real answer: עו"ש 0%, קרן כספית יומית 3.25%, פיקדון לשנה (a one-year deposit) 1.8% where the money is stuck for 12 months. The fund card had a green tag, "המוצג בעמוד". I took it as the page's pick, but I'm not sure that's what it means. I didn't open "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם" (play with your own numbers).
- **"שווה לדעת":** I only caught the line saying the return is after management fees but not guaranteed.
- **"כמה זה עולה" (what it costs):** I was hoping for a number. Instead, every row says "אין תקרה בחוק" (no legal cap) or "אין". No percent, no shekels. This is where I got annoyed. The only fee number I'd seen was "0.1%" back in the first section, and I'm not sure it's even a fee I pay.
- **"מיסוי" (tax):** I saw "25%" and that you pay only when you sell. I didn't really read it.
- **"סיכונים" (risks):** I saw "אין ביטוח" (no insurance) and moved on fast.
- **"מונחים" (glossary):** I scrolled past.

**What I skipped:** "מי מציע ומי מפקח" (who offers it, who supervises), "כסף נכנס ויוצא" (money in and out), "מה האתר לקח בחשבון" (what the site took into account), the glossary table, all the "הצגת החוקים והכללים" links, the MF/BRK/BANK tags and the footer.

**Words I didn't get, as written:** נאמן, מק"מ, דמי משמרת, שכר נאמן, השעה היעודה, מפיץ, הריאלי / הנומינלי, קרן כספית מתחדשת, קרן נאמנות פטורה / חייבת. The MF-11 style tags too. Also "123 מקור / חושב / הזנתה" under the heading; no idea what those mean.

**What bugged me:**
- The cost section has no actual number, so I still don't know what I'd pay in shekels.
- The text blocks are long and dense, which is hard going on a phone.
- I don't know which company to buy this from. No names I recognise; it just says to go through the bank or a בית השקעות (investment house).
- "אין ביטוח" scared me a bit.

I didn't give up, but I'd still need to ask my bank what the actual fee is.
