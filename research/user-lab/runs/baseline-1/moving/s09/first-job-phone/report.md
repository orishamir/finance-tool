## What it said along the way

**My answer:** You put money in the bank for a fixed time, a year for example, and the bank pays you a set interest rate. The example uses 1.8% a year. When the time is up you get your money back plus the interest, and they take 15% of the interest as tax. What you give up is getting at the money early. If you break the deposit you pay a fine, the page doesn't say how much, and you might lose the interest. A red box near the bottom also says there's no state guarantee if the bank goes bust. I'm fairly sure I get the basic idea. The thing that made me hesitate: the example shows "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪" but "נשאר לכם 68,270 ₪", so it looks like you get back less than you put in. I didn't really get why. So I wouldn't lock my 30,000 today. I'd first ask my bank what their fine is.
