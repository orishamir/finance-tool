## What it said along the way

Well, son, this is what I'll tell you. I didn't decide anything, and I won't move the money because of this site. I'll ask you first and then the bank. I'm fairly sure I understood the basics. The bank pays a fixed interest, I can't take the money out before the end without the bank agreeing, and there may be a fine. What worried me is that it says the money isn't insured if the bank collapses.

**What I looked at, in order**
1. **Top of the page.** The heading "פיקדון" and the line "כסף שמפקידים בבנק לתקופה מוסכמת, ובתמורה הבנק משלם עליו ריבית" were good, I know this. Then came a row of "123 מקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנתה" and I didn't know what it was for. Under it there's a short explanation of fixed or variable interest and what happens at the end, which I understood. It also says each bank must publish a table, but I didn't open that.
2. **The example box.** It uses 10,000 ₪, then 500 ₪ a month, for 10 years at 1.8%. The big number "68,270 ₪" stayed with me. Further down it says "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪". So I put in 70,000 and get back 68,270? That looked like a loss and it scared me. There's a small button, "בכסף של היום", which I think is to do with prices going up, but I wasn't sure. There are sliders there, which I didn't touch, and a chart on a black background.
3. **"למה משתמשים בזה".** It says a deposit is better than a "קרן כספית" because the interest is fixed when you deposit. I don't know what a קרן כספית is.
4. **"שווה לדעת".** It lists 15% tax on the interest of an unlinked deposit, and the line "שבירת פיקדון: הסכמת הבנק, הטופס והעלות". This is what I give up: if I want the money early, the bank has to agree and there's a cost. Then came "אין ביטוח פיקדונות". That made me stop and worry.
5. **"מי מציע ומי מפקח".** Bank Israel supervises the banks, which calmed me a little.
6. **"כמה זה עולה".** It only says "דמי יציאה… לפי התעריפון של הגורם המציע". It doesn't say how much.
7. **"מיסוי".** The bank takes the tax off by itself. There's a red box that says the site doesn't calculate the fine for breaking a deposit early, "שאלה פתוחה". Red, so I backed off.
8. **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן".** Again: if a bank goes bust, no law gives me my money back. There's only a proposal for 250,000 ₪, and only at small banks. Also, a deposit that isn't linked doesn't protect against prices going up.
9. **The table of words.** I was already tired here. I only went through it quickly, and found "קנס שבירה", which says it can eat the interest "ואף חלק מהסכום שהופקד".
