## What it said along the way

**What I'd tell my friend:** I didn't open anything. A deposit at a bank is simple enough. You give them the money for a fixed time, for example "12 חודשים", and they pay you "ריבית". The example uses Leumi at 1.8%. Until the end you can't take the money out, or you can only if you "break" it, and then the bank can fine you. The page also says "האתר לא מחשב אותה", so nobody tells you what the fine would be. What decided it for me was the big number: 68,270 ₪ after ten years, against "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪". So after 10 years I end up with less than I put in. I think that's because it's shown "בכסף של היום", and prices go up 2% a year while the interest is only 1.8%. On top of that there's "מס 986.20 ₪". So a deposit keeps the money safe-ish, but it doesn't really grow. I'm fairly sure about that.
