## What it said along the way

I'm keeping it, and I'm fairly sure. What decided it is the line at the top: the employer puts in up to **7.5%** of my salary and I put in **2.5%**. If I leave the money **6 years** after the first payment, the profits are tax-free. I can take it out earlier, it's not locked, but then I pay tax on it. The orange box said **"משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה"** (taking it out early is possible but costly), so I'm not touching it before 6 years.

One thing bugged me. The big bars came out at **17,370 ₪** for the keren against **16,955 ₪** for a securities account, which they called **"משאירה 415.84 ₪ יותר"**. After 10 years, that's all? I nearly thought it wasn't worth it. Then I caught a line saying the employer's money **"מחוץ להשוואה"**. So the bars are only my own money? I'm not sure I got that right. Showing the employer's money would have made the case straight away.
