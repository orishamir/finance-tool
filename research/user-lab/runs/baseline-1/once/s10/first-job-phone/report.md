**What I took from it:** The ones worth checking for my 30k are a חשבון ניירות ערך so I can buy that S&P 500 fund everyone at work talks about, the קרן השתלמות I already have through work, and maybe a קרן כספית for money I need to be able to get at. I'm only fairly sure. What made it click was the card "חשבון ניירות ערך – קונים ומוכרים מניות, קרנות" with the "מדד S&P 500" card right below it. The page never says which one to pick, and it has no numbers at all.

**How it went:**
- **Top of the page.** "מדריך – כל סוג של חיסכון והשקעה". That's what I came for, so I stayed.
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון".** I read the first lines. Where the money is held, and what it's invested in. OK, that kind of made sense.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל".** Three links: קרן השתלמות, קרן חדשה מקיפה, קרן כספית. None of them says "put your 30k here so it grows". I didn't tap any.
- **Cards I scrolled fast.** כסף שוטף וקצר: just the titles, all bank stuff I already know. חיסכון עם הטבת מס: קרן השתלמות, nice, I have one. "קופת גמל להשקעה" sounds like something for old people, so I skipped it.
- **Cards I skipped.** פרישה and לילדים aren't for me.
- **"לבחירה עצמית".** The heading was at the very bottom of the first screen and the cards were on the next one, so for a second I didn't get what they belonged to. Then I saw חשבון ניירות ערך, the one I picked.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע".** I was looking for S&P 500 and found it. The rest of the index cards (MSCI, נאסד"ק, ת"א) I only skimmed the titles.

**Words I didn't get:** מק"מ, אג"ח, "חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו", the "יומית" and "מתחדשת" tags on קרן כספית, "מסלול כללי", "קרן חדשה כללית" next to "מקיפה", "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים".
