Son, I didn't decide anything. I'll ask you, and then go to the clerk at the bank. I'm only half sure of what I understood.

**What I took from it.** The page is called "חשבון עובר ושב", so at least it was something I know. It says money in the עו"ש "לא תמיד מקבל ריבית". If more than "15,000.00 ₪" stays there for "רבעון שלם", the bank has to write to me about "חלופות". So the bank knows it isn't paying me and has to tell me. I don't remember getting a letter like that, and I'll ask. On cost it only says "דמי חשבון קבועים… לפי התעריפון של הגורם המציע". It never says how much the bank actually takes. I always thought a deposit was safe. Now I'm worried about the 400,000. Then came a dark red box with a lock, and I stopped there.
