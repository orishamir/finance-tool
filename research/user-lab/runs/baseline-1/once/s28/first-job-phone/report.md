## What it said along the way

**What I decided:** I'd go with פסגות as the account and the "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" fund. After 10 years it shows "86,492 ₪" left from the 70,000 ₪ I put in. I'm fairly sure, not 100%. The thing that decided it was the box at the top, "★ הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם", which says it's "+2,308 ₪ יותר" than the default. The page is basically recommending it, and פסגות is a name I know. I'd never heard of the default, "אינטראקטיב ישראל (מותג של MEXEM LTD)", so I'd hesitate on that one anyway.

**Where I looked, in order:**
- **Heading:** "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן". That's exactly what I came for, so I stayed. S&P 500 was already picked.
- **Sliders:** 10,000 today, 500 a month, 10 years, 7%. Those aren't my amounts (I have 30,000), but I left them. I wasn't going to fiddle with them on my phone.
- **Big numbers:** "70,000 ₪ הפקדתם", then "84,184 ₪ הבחירה שלכם", then "86,492 ₪" for the cheapest. The 70,000 is what stuck. Honestly it bugged me a bit: 7% a year for 10 years and I only end up with about 14–16k more than I put in? I expected more.
- **Bars under "כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים":** I saw 9,184 ₪ against 6,876 ₪, so the cheap option costs less. The pink 7,483 is the biggest chunk. I had to drop down to the legend to find out pink is "מס", and that back-and-forth was annoying.
- **The two long lists of funds and accounts:** I scrolled them fast. Every row has the same coloured tags ("נקנית בחו״ל", "חשיפה לדולר", "צוברת", "מס גם על שינוי המטבע"...). I kept re-reading them on each row just to find what's different, and it wore me out. "-533 ₪" and "-536 ₪" next to some funds looked like losing money, so I avoided those.
- **The rest:** I scrolled past "מה כתוב בפרסום, מול התעריפון", "מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם", "עם נטרול מט״ח או בלי" (charts, lots of minus signs, skipped) and "מתחילים מבחירה אחת…". In that last part I only caught "לעבור אל ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות" and "2,308 ₪", which matched what I'd already decided. Somewhere around the middle of screen 2 I'd probably have closed it if I hadn't already had my answer.
