# Picking an S&P 500 fund: what I took from the page, as a 24-year-old first-time investor

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot), shown as "פסגות ניירות ערך (פסגות טרייד)". There I'd buy the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, number 5139282, every month.

According to the big box, if I put in 10,000 now and 500 a month for 10 years, that's "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪". At the end I'd have **"86,492 ₪ … נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"**.

**How sure: about 70% on the account, about 50% on the fund.**
- **The account matters a lot.** In the account ranking, Psagot is first. Big names I'd probably have picked on my own, like אלטשולר טרייד, אקסלנס and מיטב טרייד, leave me about 17,000 ₪ less. That convinced me.
- **The fund barely matters.** The next fund is only "−43 ₪" behind. So I'm not worried about picking the "wrong" fund, but I also can't tell why I should prefer this one.
- **One thing bothers me.** Next to my fund it says "+0.37% · שנה אחת" ("one year"), while most others say "4 שנים" ("4 years"). So my pick may be a new fund, and I don't know if that matters.
- **I might qualify for a discount.** Several banks say "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים" ("not in the calculation: different price for young people"), and I'm 24. Maybe Leumi or Hapoalim would be cheaper for me, but the page doesn't say by how much.

- The title, "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן", and the S&P 500 button, which was already selected.

- The result box: "הפקדתם 70,000", "הבחירה שלכם · הזול 86,492", and "זוג הבא בתור 86,449, −43".
- The two cards, "חשבון ניירות ערך" (account) and "קרן" (fund), with "1,057 ₪" for 10 years and "דמי ניהול: 0% קבוע, 0% משתנה, 0.02% נאמן".
- The account ranking on the right (1 to 15).
- "מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם", the table part only.
- The final section, "מתחילים מבחירה אחת…", the account table ("חשבון אחר, אותה קרן").

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

- **Cost bar:** I looked only at the numbers on the coloured parts: pink 5,703 (tax), yellow 699 and orange 356. In the legend I matched pink to "מס" and yellow to "דמי משמרת". I ignored "העמלות זו על זו" and "המרת מטבע".
- **Fund list:**
  - Looked at: the fund name, "לעומת הבחירה" (e.g. "−213", "−1,685") and "מול המדד" ("+0.37%", "−0.53%").
  - Ignored: almost all the tags ("עסקה מחוץ", "צבירה", "חשיפה לדולר", "מס גם על שינוי המטבע", "כמה דרכים יחד").

- **Hedging charts:** I only looked at the white bar "+13.4" and the yellow bar "−21.8". I ignored the year-by-year table.
- **Waterfall:**
  - Looked at: the column labels "+4,972 חשבון אחר" and "+2,344 סוג קרן אחר", and the green and red bars in the tables below.

- Whether Psagot lets me set up an automatic monthly purchase, and how to actually open the account.
