# Psagot Trade: what it would cost me and what I could buy

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would not open the account yet. First I would phone Psagot and ask two questions:

- **Is there a custody fee or not?** Their advert says "פטור מלא מדמי ניהול ללא הגבלת זמן" (full exemption from management fees, with no time limit). Their own document says "דמי משמרת 0.12% גבייה רבעונית 0.03%". That means 0.12% a year, charged as 0.03% every three months. On 200,000 ₪ that is about 240 ₪ a year. "Free" and "240 shekels" can't both be true. The page marks this "המסמך אומר אחרת" (the document says otherwise), and I believe the document.
- **What does buying foreign shares really cost?** The page says "לא ידוע" (unknown).

I'm fairly sure about the main numbers, because each one has a link "בתעריפון" (in the price list) back to the real document:
- Buying or selling costs "0.07%" with a "מינימום לפעולה 2.00 ₪" (minimum 2 ₪ per trade). A 10,000 ₪ purchase costs 7 ₪.
- There is no monthly account fee: "דמי חשבון קבועים 0.00 ₪ לחודש".
- I need "מינימום לפתיחת חשבון: 10,000.00 ₪" (10,000 ₪ minimum to open an account). That's fine for me.

I'm less sure about the currency conversion cost, and not sure at all about foreign shares.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **"העמלות"** (the fees), especially the buy/sell fee, the custody fee and the account fee.
- **"מה אפשר לקנות כאן"** (what you can buy here).
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, ומה במסמכים"** (what the advert says and what the documents say). This was the most useful part, because it caught the advert contradicting the document.
- **"מחירים לקבוצות ומבצעים"** (prices for groups and special offers), for the 10,000 ₪ minimum and the regulator.

## 3. Block by block

- **Title, breadcrumb and top tabs ("מקור", "מדריך"…):** skimmed. They told me I was on the right company. I didn't click anything.
- **Search box on the left:** skipped. I'm not looking for another company.
- **"ללא תאריך פרסום, נאסף ב־04.11.2026"** (no publication date, collected on 04.11.2026): this worried me a little. Why doesn't the price list have a date? Still, it was collected two days ago, so it's probably current.
- **"למי המחירים כאן: לקוח רגיל, בלי הטבה לקבוצה"** (who these prices are for: an ordinary customer, no group discount): used it. Good, I'm an ordinary customer.
- **"11 שורות עמלה נאספו"** (11 fee lines were collected): confused me. I counted only 5 boxes below it. Where are the other 6?
- **Buy/sell fee box:** used it carefully and did the 7 ₪ sum above.
- **Custody fee "0.03% לרבעון"** (0.03% per quarter): used it. Only later did I see that this is 0.12% a year. The yearly figure should have been written right here.
- **Two "המרת מטבע" (currency conversion) boxes, one "1.5 אגורות לדולר" (1.5 agorot per dollar) and one "0.5%":** confused me. Which one applies to me? Both say "מרווח ההמרה: המסמכים לא קובעים לו תקרה" (the documents set no ceiling on the conversion margin). That sounds like they can charge as much as they like on the exchange rate, and that makes me nervous.
- **"דמי חשבון קבועים 0.00 ₪"** (fixed account fee 0.00 ₪): used it, and it reassured me.
- **"מה אפשר לקנות כאן":** used it. Mutual funds and ETFs are "אפשר" (allowed). Foreign securities are "לא ידוע" (unknown). I read the long grey paragraph about Europe and Asia twice. It says the price there "עשויה להשתנות" (may change) and that the customer has to ask. Not reassuring.
- **Advert vs. document comparison:** read every word. On mutual funds, the advert says "פטור מעמלת ק/מ" (no buy/sell fee). The document charges "0.07%… מינימום 2 ₪" on funds traded on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange. I'm not sure these are the same kind of fund, so this is another question for the phone call.
- **"מה מגן על הכסף"** (what protects the money): I saw it was folded up. I would open it, but from the screenshot I couldn't.
- **"חבר בורסה" / "רשות ניירות ערך"** (stock exchange member / Israel Securities Authority): skimmed. It's good that a regulator is named.
- **"הודעה על שינוי בעמלות · 1"** (notice of a fee change): this worried me. Are the prices about to change? I couldn't read the notice.
- **The two buttons at the bottom:** skipped. I don't need a comparison yet, and I don't understand what "חשבון בתנאים שלכם" (an account on your terms) means.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של פסגות" and "חשבון ניירות ערך · 2"** (what Psagot's documents say; securities account): skipped. It's folded up.
- **Footer:** skimmed. "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי" (a personal, non-commercial site) made me trust it a bit more.

## 4. What I looked at in the lists

- **Looked at:** the big underlined numbers (0.07%, 2.00 ₪, 0.03%, 0.00 ₪, 10,000 ₪), the small words beside them ("לרבעון" per quarter, "לחודש" per month, "מינימום" minimum), the green "אפשר" (allowed) and orange "לא ידוע" (unknown) labels, the orange "המסמך אומר אחרת" (the document says otherwise) labels, and the quoted sentences under "בפרסום" (in the advert) and "במסמך המחייב" (in the binding document).
- **Ignored:** the dates under each quote ("נראה ב־", seen on), the long section-number links ("חלק 4, סעיף 8", part 4, section 8), and the small "בתעריפון" link icons. I trusted that they were there and didn't follow them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- Why there are two different currency conversion prices.
- What "מרווח ההמרה" (the conversion margin) means for my money in practice.
- Whether "דמי ניהול" in the advert (management fees) and "דמי משמרת" in the document (custody fee) are the same thing. If they are, the advert is misleading.
- What "מק"מ" is. It appears in the custody fee line.
- What "11 שורות" (11 lines) refers to when I only see 5.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **A plain yearly total**, something like "if you hold 100,000 ₪ and trade 10 times a year, you pay X ₪."
- **Buying American shares:** can I, and at what fee?
- **Ordinary Israeli shares:** they aren't in "מה אפשר לקנות" at all. They only appear inside the fee lines.
- **What protects my money** if Psagot gets into trouble. That section was folded up.
- **The fee change notice:** what is changing, and when.
- **Closing the account:** whether there is a fee to leave or to transfer my holdings elsewhere.

## 7. If half the page had to go

I would remove these first: the search box, the top tabs, the two buttons at the bottom, the folded "מה כתוב במסמכים של פסגות" section, the dates and section numbers under each quote, and one of the two currency conversion boxes, once someone tells me which one is right.

I would keep the fees, what I can buy, and above all the advert-versus-document comparison. That part saved me from believing "free" when it isn't.
