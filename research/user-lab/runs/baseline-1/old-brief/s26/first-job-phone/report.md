# s26: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 41 s, $0.13

## 1. My answer

I'd probably open the account, but I'm only about 60% sure, and I'd have questions first.

What I took away:
- **Buying or selling costs 0.07% of what I buy, with a minimum of 2 shekels** ("0.07% … מינימום לפעולה ₪2.00"). If I put in 10,000 shekels, that's 7 shekels. That seems cheap.
- **No monthly account fee**: "דמי חשבון קבועים ₪0.00 לחודש". Good.
- **A holding fee of 0.03% every quarter** ("0.03% לרבעון"). It took me a moment, and the lower part of the page, to see this means 0.12% a year. On 10,000 shekels that's about 12 shekels a year.
- **I need 10,000 shekels to open the account** ("מינימום לפתיחת חשבון: ₪10,000.00"). This matters most to me. I'd have to check I have that much free.

Why I'm not more sure:
- I want the S&P 500. Under "מה אפשר לקנות כאן", foreign securities are marked **"לא ידוע"** (unknown), and the S&P 500 is American. I think an Israeli "קרן סל" (marked "אפשר", allowed) can follow the S&P 500, but I'm guessing. The page doesn't say.
- The ad says one thing and the contract says another (more in section 5). That makes me trust the ads less.

## 2. What I actually used

- The title, "פסגות ניירות ערך (פסגות טרייד)", to check I'm on the right company.
- "העמלות" (fees): the buy/sell line, the holding fee, and the monthly account fee.

- "מחירים לקבוצות ומבצעים" (group prices and deals), only for the 10,000-shekel minimum and the regulator line.

## 4. Labels, colours and numbers

- **What I looked at:** the big underlined numbers (0.07%, ₪2.00, 0.03%, ₪0.00, ₪10,000). I read those first and the small text after.
- **Coloured tags:** I used the green "אפשר" (allowed) and the orange "לא ידוע" (unknown) right away; the colours did their job. The orange "המסמך אומר אחרת" (the document says otherwise) also got my attention and told me to read that block.
- **The comparison block:** I read the right column ("בפרסום", in the ad) and the left column ("במסמך המחייב", in the binding document) side by side, but only the quotes in large text.
- **What I ignored:** every "בתעריפון ↗" link, the small source lines ("תעריפון עמלות פסגות טרייד, חלק 4, סעיף 8"), and the dates "נראה ב־04.11.2026" and "מ־09.08.2026".

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **Two currency conversion lines.** One says "1.5 אגורות לדולר", the other "0.5%". Which one would I pay? Both say "מרווח ההמרה: המסמכים לא קובעים לו תקרה", and I don't know what "מרווח" means here. "No ceiling" sounds bad.
- **"ניירות ערך זרים" marked "לא ידוע".** Does that mean I can't buy American stocks, or just that the site doesn't know the price? The text sounds like you can buy but the price is unclear.
- **The ad vs. the contract.** The ad says "פטור מלא מדמי ניהול ללא הגבלת זמן" (fully exempt from management fees, no time limit), but the document says 0.12% a year. Are "דמי ניהול" and "דמי משמרת" the same thing? If they are, the ad is just wrong. The bottom of that first comparison was also cut off between my two screens, so I may have missed part of it.
- **The buy/sell row.** The ad says "פטור מעמלת ק/מ בקרנות כספיות ואקטיביות" (no buy/sell fee on money-market and active funds), but I don't know what "כספיות" or "אקטיביות" funds are, or whether that matters for an S&P 500 fund.
- **Terms I don't know:** "מק"מ", "קרן כספית", "חבר בורסה", "דמי משמרת".

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **A straight answer to "can I buy the S&P 500 here, and how?"** That was my whole question.
- **A total yearly cost for someone like me**, for example "if you invest X a month, you'll pay about Y a year". I had to work it out myself. And if I invest 500 shekels a month, the 2-shekel minimum is actually 0.4% each time, not 0.07%. The page didn't point that out; I worked it out myself.
- **Whether the fund itself also charges something**, on top of Psagot's fees.
- **Whether 10,000 shekels is normal** or a lot compared to other places.
- **How to actually open the account.** There's no "open account" button.
- **Whether there's a fee to withdraw or to leave.**
