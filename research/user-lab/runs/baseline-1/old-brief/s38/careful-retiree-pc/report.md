# s38: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 54 s, $0.25

# My pick: option 3, "טבלת חברות מול סוגים"

I noticed the "מומלץ" badge on it. I know that's the designers' own opinion, so it didn't decide anything for me. I chose option 3 because of the coloured table in the middle, "כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה". It's the only place where I could see in one look which company charges me less for the same kind of saving. Below the table, option 3 has the same list as option 1, so I lose nothing by choosing it.

Option 2 ("קיבוץ לפי חברה") made me scroll through all 16 of Harel's tracks before I even reached Mor. That's too much reading just to compare two companies.

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

At 61 I don't want all my money in the American stock market. So I looked at the "כללי" (general) column and row rather than "עוקב מדד s&p 500".

- **Harel's general track:** "הראל גמל להשקעה כללי" charges "0.55%". It shows "13.50%" for 3 years and "8.26%" for 5 years, and it holds "6.7 מיליארד ₪". It's big, cheap, and it has a 5-year record.
- **Mor's general track:** "מור גמל להשקעה - כללי" charges more ("0.72%") and earned a little less ("12.82%" and "8.13%").

So I would lean toward Harel's general track. I'm only about 50–60% sure, though. I would phone them, and probably talk to someone, before moving any money. The page told me what things cost. It didn't tell me what's sensible for someone my age.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The sentence under the title. It says fees are "הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025" (the average actually charged in 2025) and that the data comes from "רשות שוק ההון" (the government regulator). That made me trust the numbers.
- The "במה הכסף מושקע" chips, mainly "כללי".
- The coloured table in option 3, especially the "כללי" and "עוקב מדד s&p 500" columns and the Harel, Mor, Menora and Altshuler rows.
- The list rows, for "ההחיסכון" (the fee), the 3- and 5-year returns, and the size ("נכסים").
- In option 2, Harel's and Mor's "כללי" rows. That's the only place I could see the returns for the general tracks. In options 1 and 3 the list only shows S&P 500, with a note that "שאר הקבוצות" (the other groups) come after it.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top title and the three option buttons:** I read them. They're there to choose a design, so it was fine.
- **"מקור · מדריכים · חוקים וכללים · שאלות…" tabs:** Skipped. I don't know what "מקור" means here.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר":** Skimmed. I didn't understand why there's also "קיבוץ לפי" further down. Isn't that the same thing?
- **Product chips ("תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים 182", "קרן השתלמות"…):** Skipped. I already know I want "קופת גמל להשקעה".
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link:** I noticed it and would click it later.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" chips:** Used. There are a lot of them, though (23 kinds). I don't know the difference between "מניות" and "עוקב מדדי מניות", or between "אג"ח סחיר" and "אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר".
- **"מיון לפי" (sort by):** I saw it was already sorted by "דמי ניהול מהחיסכון" and left it.
- **"חיפוש בשם המסלול" (search box):** Skipped.
- **Coloured table:** Confused me at first. I assumed orange meant "good". Then I read "כתום חזק יותר הוא דמי ניהול גבוהים יותר" (stronger orange means higher fees), and after that it worked. Mor's whole row is orange, so Mor is expensive almost everywhere. That was the most useful thing on the whole page.
- **Small grey line under the table ("סוגים עם פחות משבעה מסלולים…"):** Skimmed. Not important to me.
- **The long list:** Used, but only the first few rows of each group. I stopped reading once I'd seen the rows with "פחות מ־3 שנים".
- **Orange lines starting "בשרטוט בלבד":** Skipped. They're notes for the designers.
- **Bottom note, "התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך":** Read it, and I appreciated it. It's honest that past returns don't predict the future.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

**What I looked at:**
- Company name, in bold.
- "ההחיסכון", the fee taken from my savings.
- "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" (the 3- and 5-year returns).
- "נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026", the size. Bigger feels safer to me.
- In the table: the colours, the "כללי" and S&P columns, and the "נכסים בקופות גמל להשקעה" column.

**What I ignored:**
- The "קופה" number (13874 and so on). I don't know what it is for.
- The coloured circles.
- The "מניות / מט"ח" lines with their small cyan and yellow dots.
- "ההפקדות 0%". It's zero almost everywhere, so I stopped looking at it.
- The small clock icons.
- The purple dotted underlines under the percentages.
- The "<" arrows on the left.
- "2 מסלולים" in the table cells.
- The second grey line under each company name, which just repeats the track's name.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"חשיפה בסוף ספטמבר 2026" with "מניות 99%" and "מט"ח 97%":** What does foreign currency exposure mean for my money? Can I lose money because the dollar falls?
- **The circles:** I couldn't tell whether a full circle is good or bad.
- **"ההחיסכון" versus "ההפקדות":** Two fees? Which one do I actually pay?
- **"תשואה… לפני דמי ניהול":** So I have to subtract the fee myself to know what I really got?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים":** Is that a new track, or a missing number?
- **"עוד לא דווח" and the tag "חדש":** I'm not sure what these mean.
- **The underlined numbers:** Are they links? To what?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which track suits a 61-year-old,** and how much each one dropped in a bad year.
- **Real money:** "If I put in 100,000 ₪, how much is taken in fees each year, and roughly what's left after 5 years?"
- **Tax:** What happens when I take the money out, especially after age 60? I'm 61, so this matters to me most.
- **Fee negotiation:** Can I ask for a lower fee than this "average"?
- **Moving and access:** How do I transfer money in? Is there a minimum? Can I get it out quickly if I need it?
- **Returns after fees:** I wanted these shown directly.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The top tabs ("מקור, מדריכים, שאלות…") and the "לפי חברה / לפי מוצר" tabs.
2. The product chips for other products.
3. The "קופה" number, the circles, the "מט"ח" line, and "ההפקדות 0%".
4. The second line under each company name.
5. The repeated full product explanation above every section.

**Keep:** the coloured table, and in the list the fee, the 3- and 5-year returns, and the size.
