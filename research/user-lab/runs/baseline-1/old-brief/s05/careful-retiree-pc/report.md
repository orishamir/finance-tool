# What I made of the page, as a 61-year-old reading every word

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd draw up a short list and read only those cards. I wouldn't sign up for anything yet.

My short list:
- **Money I want safe and easy to reach:** "פיקדון", "קרן כספית" and "מק"מ". The words "סיכון נמוך" (low risk) and "הלוואה קצרה למדינה" (a short loan to the state) are what I want to hear at my age.
- **Money I could leave alone for a few years:** "קופת גמל להשקעה". I liked "אפשר למשוך בכל עת" (you can withdraw at any time). It means my money isn't locked up. The catch is "ובמשיכה משלמים מס על הרווח" (you pay tax on the profit when you withdraw).
- **"קרן השתלמות":** I'd check whether I already have one through work. The line "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס" (after a legal period you can withdraw tax-free) sounds good. But the page never says how long that period is.
- **Protection against prices rising:** "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה". It says the loan is "צמודות למדד המחירים לצרכן" (linked to the consumer price index), so my money keeps pace with prices. That matters to me.

How sure am I? I'm fairly sure about **which kinds of product to look into**. I'm **not at all sure what to do with my money**. The page has no interest rates, no fees, no numbers and nothing about my age. So "so what does this mean for my money?" stays unanswered on this page.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The top part, "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון" (two parts to every saving). It told me there are two questions: where the money is kept, and what it's invested in. That cleared things up for me.
- "מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start), mainly the line "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן כספית" (money that must stay available: a money-market fund).
- In "איפה הכסף מוחזק" (where the money is held), I used the cards under "כסף שוטף וקצר" (everyday and short-term money) and "חיסכון עם הטבת מס" (saving with a tax benefit).
- In "במה הכסף מושקע" (what the money is invested in), I used only "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה", "אג"ח ממשלתית לא צמודה", "מק"מ" and "מזומן שקלי".
- The footer line "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (a personal, non-commercial site with no marketing links). It made me trust the page more. So did "נתונים נכון ל־26.10.2026" (data correct as of 26.10.2026).

## 4. Lists and cards (there are no tables or charts)

- **What I looked at:** the bold heading on each card, then the first line of the description. I read the line "בשפה פשוטה:" (in plain language) every time. It's a good idea, but it only appears on a few cards, in "פרישה" and "לילדים". I wanted it on all of them.
- **The green links** in "מאיפה להתחיל": I understood they're clickable. I didn't click.
- **The small arrows** on the left of each card: I understood each card leads to more detail.
- **What I ignored:** the search icon in the corner, the sub-headings between groups of cards, and most of the end of each description. Once a description reached "קרן שעוקבת אחריו" (a fund that tracks it) and "חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו" (exposed to the dollar or neutralised from it), I stopped reading.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו": what does it mean for my money? Can I lose because of the dollar?
- "קופון" in the bond cards.
- "מסלול שאינו מתמחה ואינו מתחייב לטווח מניות" (a track that doesn't specialise and isn't committed to a range of shares). I read it twice and still don't know.
- "מק"מ" appears twice, once as a place the money is held and once as an investment. So which is it?
- "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים". Even the plain-language version, "קופת גמל לתגמולים", didn't help me.
- "בשוקים מפותחים ומתפתחים" (developed and emerging markets): which countries? Is it riskier?
- "בניהול אישי": who manages it, me or someone else?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Numbers.** How much interest a "פיקדון" pays today compared with a "קרן כספית". What the management fees (דמי ניהול) are.
- **Risk.** Can I lose money, and how much? A simple scale like "safe / medium / risky" on each card would be enough for me.
- **Anything about my age.** What should someone close to retirement do? How do I turn the money into a monthly pension? None of that is under "פרישה".
- The period "שקבע החוק" (set by law) for a "קרן השתלמות": how many years?
- How much tax is paid on the profit in a "קופת גמל להשקעה".
- Which of these are covered by a state guarantee.
- Whether I can lose money in a "קרן כספית".
