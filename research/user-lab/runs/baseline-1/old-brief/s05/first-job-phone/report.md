# What I took from this page, as a 24-year-old on a salary

## 1. My answer: what I'd do, and how sure I am

The page sorts things into three groups that fit my life:

- **Things my job should already give me.** "קרן חדשה מקיפה" (the pension fund) is called "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר", the base for every salaried saver. So I assume I already have one through work and should check that I do. "קרן השתלמות" is listed as for "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם", employees whose employer pays into one. I'd ask HR whether I get one, because "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס" (after a set period you can withdraw it tax-free) sounds like free money.
- **Emergency money.** "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין" (money that must stay available) points to "קרן כספית". That's where I'd keep a cushion instead of leaving it in "חשבון עובר ושב", which "לא תמיד מקבל ריבית" (doesn't always earn interest).
- **The S&P 500 I keep hearing about.** This page taught me that it isn't a product on its own. It's what goes *inside* a product. I'd put it in either a "קופת גמל להשקעה" or a "חשבון ניירות ערך".

**How sure am I?** About 70% on the first two. About 30% on the S&P 500 part, because I can't tell which of those two containers is better for me or what each one costs.

## 2. Which parts I actually used

## 4. Lists and cards: what I looked at and what I ignored

- **Looked at:** the bold card titles first, then the first line of the description. On the pension cards I read the "בשפה פשוטה:" line, which was the most useful line on those cards. In "מאיפה להתחיל" my eye went to the green link words, and I read the white text before them to see whether it applied to me.
- **Ignored:** the little ">" arrows (I didn't think about clicking), the section subtitles in small grey text, and the second half of long descriptions. I also skipped the repeated endings like "הכסף מושקע בשקלים דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו" (the money is invested in shekels through a fund that tracks it) once I'd seen them twice.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "מק"מ": it appears twice, once in each section, and I didn't get why.
- "אג"ח", and "צמודה" / "לא צמודה" (linked to which index?). Also "קופון", "פדיון" and "השווי הנקוב".
- "חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו" (exposed to the dollar or neutralised from it). Should I want that or not?
- "קרן חדשה מקיפה" vs "קרן חדשה כללית", and "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים".
- "קרן כספית מתחדשת" vs a plain "קרן כספית".
- "מסלול": I worked out from context that it's the investment choice inside a product, but the page never says so.
- "שווי השוק הסחיר" and "במשקל לפי שווי השוק".

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Fees.** What does each one cost me?
- **Numbers.** Roughly what return, and how much risk?
- **What the tax benefit actually is.** For "קופת גמל להשקעה" especially, and how long the "תקופה שקבע החוק" for קרן השתלמות lasts.
- **Which is better for S&P 500:** קופת גמל להשקעה or חשבון ניירות ערך?
- **How to check what I already have** through work, and whether I can choose the S&P 500 inside my pension.
- **An order of steps.** Something like "first an emergency fund, then this, then that", and how much to put where.
