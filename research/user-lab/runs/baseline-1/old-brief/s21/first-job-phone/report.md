# s21: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 42 s, $0.14

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd go with Harel's **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** track (number 13414), because that's the one thing on this page I recognised. It says **"0.56% מהחיסכון"** and **"0% מההפקדה"**. The returns are **"13.7% 3 שנים"** and **"11.92% 5 שנים"**. I'd then call Harel, or check my own account, and ask them to move my money into that track.

**I'm about 40% sure.** That's fairly low, for three reasons:

- **I don't know which product my savings are in.** The page opened on **"קופת גמל להשקעה"**, but my money through work is probably pension or **"קרן השתלמות"**. I never clicked those tabs, so I don't know if the S&P 500 option or the fee is the same there.
- **The "מניות" track looks better.** It shows **"24.04%"** over 3 years and **"14.64%"** over 5 years, for **"0.55%"**. That's almost double, and it tempted me. But the note under the table says past returns don't tell you what will happen next (**"היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"**). So I stuck with the name I know.
- **I can't tell whether 0.56% is cheap.** Nothing on this page compares it to other companies.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- **The intro under "הראל"**: the bit about fees being the average actually charged in 2025.
- **The product tabs under "כל המוצרים של הראל"**: I only noticed which one was selected.
- **The "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons**: I spotted **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** there.
- **The line "16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול מההיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"**.
- **The big table**: the track name, fee and return columns.
- **The two notes right under the table**: the ceiling line (**"תקרה בחוק"**) and the explanation of the returns.

## 3. Block by block

**Top menu: skipped.** I didn't need it. **"השוואה למטרה"** might have been useful, but I didn't know what it meant.

**Breadcrumb and "הראל" title: used.** They confirmed I was on the right company.

**Intro paragraph: used.** **"דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל"** told me the fee is what people actually paid. That made me wonder whether I'd pay the same.

**"כולל את החברות: הראל פנסיה וגמל..." line: skimmed.** It's company legal names, so I didn't care.

**"123 ממקור · 123 מחושב · 123 הזנתה" line: confused me.** I had no idea what it meant or why it says 123 three times, so I ignored it.

**Product tabs: confused me.** I didn't know which tab was "mine." **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"** means nothing to me. I'd heard of **"קרן השתלמות"** but never clicked it. **"קופת גמל להשקעה"** was already selected, so I just stayed there.

**"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link: noticed but didn't click.** I probably should have.

**"במה הכסף מושקע" buttons: skimmed.** I found **"עוקב מדד S&P 500 1"** there. Most of the others, like **"אג"ח סחיר"**, **"כספי (שקלי)"** and **"הלכה"**, I didn't understand or didn't care about.

**Summary line and "המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול": used.** It's helpful that the range is tiny (0.54% to 0.61%), so the fee hardly changes my choice.

**The table: used**, but only parts of it (see section 4).

**"תקרה בחוק: 1.05% מההיסכון, 4% מההפקדה" note: used.** It told me Harel charges about half the legal maximum, which felt reassuring.

**The note about extra costs ("עמלות מסחר, משמורות"): skimmed.** It made me a bit nervous that there are hidden costs on top. The **"PF-10"** tag means nothing to me.

**"קרנות מחקות", "מרכזית לפיצויים", "קרנות סל" (collapsed sections): skipped.** I don't know these words.

**"מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל" and its list: skipped.** It looks like fine print.

**"לא מצאתם שם?" search box: skipped.**

**Footer: skipped.** I did notice **"נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026"**, which was nice to know.

## 4. In the table

**What I looked at:**

- **"המסלול" (track name):** my main way of choosing.
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע, 2025":** I only read the **"מההיסכון"** number. **"0% מההפקדה"** is the same on every row, so I stopped reading it.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול":** I compared the 3-year and 5-year numbers. Most rows say **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**, which I took to mean "too new, no data." So I just skipped those rows.
- **"כסף מנוהל":** I glanced at it. **"2.3 מיליארד ₪"** for the S&P 500 track made it feel safe and popular.

**What I ignored:**

- **"קופה" numbers (15283, 13414…):** meaningless to me.
- **The ring chart and the "מניות" / "מט"ח" lines with teal and yellow dots:** I worked out that teal is stocks. **"מט"ח"** I only vaguely know as foreign currency. I didn't know why it matters, so I ignored the yellow and the ring. The dotted underlines under the percentages suggest I could click for more, but I didn't.
- **The grey tags like "אג"ח סחיר" and "הלכה יהודית":** ignored.
- **The ">" arrows on the left:** I didn't click them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"ההיסכון" vs **"ההפקדה"**:** I guessed it's "from what you've saved" vs "from each new deposit," but I'm not sure.
- **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"**, **"מרכזית לפיצויים"**, **"קרנות מחקות"**, **"קרנות סל"**, **"אג"ח"**: I don't know any of these.
- **"123 ממקור / מחושב / הזנתה":** no idea.
- **The **"S&P 500"** button says 1 but **"הכול"** says 16.** I didn't see why a track sits in some categories and not others.
- **Whether **"24.04%"** is really per year.** It sounds too good to be true.
- **Why returns are shown "before fees and tax" (**"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"**).** So what do *I* actually end up with?

- **Which product and track my money is in right now**, and what fee *I* personally pay.

- **What 0.56% means in shekels** for someone with my amount of savings.

- **Returns after fees.**
- **How to actually switch tracks.**
