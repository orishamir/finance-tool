# Harel's page, read as a 61-year-old saver

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I wouldn't change anything today.** First I'd find my own Harel statement and work out which product and which track my money is actually in. Then I'd come back to this page.

This is what the page told me about my money:
- **Fees are low and almost the same everywhere.** Every track costs between **0.54% and 0.61%** a year of what I have saved, and **"0% מההפקדה"**, so nothing comes off the money I pay in. The legal maximum is **"1.05% מהחיסכון, 4% מההפקדה"**. Harel charges about half of what it's allowed to, so fees aren't a reason for me to move.
- **The returns that are shown vary a lot.** **"מניות"** shows **24.04%** over 3 years and **14.64%** over 5. **"כספי (שקלי)"** shows **4.25%** and **3.08%**. **"כללי"** sits between them at **13.5%** and **8.26%**.
- **At 61, I don't want to be 94% in shares.** That's the **"מניות 94%"** line on the מניות track. If I were choosing, "כללי" looks like the middle road: **"מניות 49%"** and 6.7 billion shekels managed. That's a guess, not a decision.

**How sure am I? Not very, maybe 4 out of 10.** The page opened on **"קופת גמל להשקעה"**, and I'm not sure that's where my savings are. It could be pension (**"קרן חדשה מקיפה"**) or **"קרן השתלמות"**. The page also says **"ממוצע"**, so the fee shown is an average and my own fee may be different.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The intro under **"הראל"**. I used it to learn the fees are what was "**נגבה בפועל ב־2025**" (actually charged in 2025) and the data is "**לסוף ספטמבר 2026**" (to the end of September 2026).
- The product buttons under **"כל המוצרים של הראל"**. I looked at which one was lit up.
- The summary line **"16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול מההיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"**.
- The table: the track name, the fee, the return and the money-managed columns.
- The small print under the table: **"התקרה בחוק"** (the legal limit), **"בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות מהקופה הוצאות ישירות"** (extra direct costs come out of the fund on top of the fee), and the warning that returns are **"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"** (before fees and before tax).

## 4. Inside the table

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (track name). I read all of them.
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025"**. I read the **"מההיסכון"** number, the yearly fee on my savings. "מההפקדה" said 0% every time, so I stopped reading it after a few rows.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"** (average yearly return, before fees), the 3-year and 5-year numbers. Most rows just say **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years). That's 10 or so out of 16, so for most tracks I learned nothing about how they've done.
- **"כסף מנוהל, סוף ספטמבר 2026"** (money managed). I used it to see which tracks are big and settled: מניות 7.3 billion, כללי 6.7 billion, S&P 500 2.3 billion. The rest are small, like **"1.4 מיליון ₪"** on מניות סחיר.

**What I ignored or didn't get:**
- **"קופה"**, the numbers like 15283 with a little arrow. I guess it's a fund code, and I skipped it.
- **The small rings, and the "מניות" (green) and "מט״ח" (yellow) percentages.** I read "מניות" as how much is in shares, and that was useful. "מט״ח" I only half understand: foreign currency? Some rows have more מט"ח than shares, like משולב סחיר with **"מניות 46%"** and **"מט״ח 86%"**. How can that be more than 100% together? The rings themselves told me nothing extra.
- The little arrows on the column headings and the **"?"** circles. I didn't touch them.
- The small grey labels like **"עוקב מדדי אג״ח"** and **"הלכה יהודית"**. Skimmed.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "ממקור / מחושב / הזנה".
- "PF-10".
- "מט״ח", and why it can be bigger than the shares number.
- Whether "תשואה שנתית ממוצעת" over 3 years means **every year on average** or the total. The heading says yearly, but 24.04% a year sounds very high.
- What "סחיר" means in "מניות סחיר" compared with plain "מניות". They're two separate tracks, and I can't tell the difference.
- Which date counts: September 2026 or 03.11.2026.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which product and track I'm in, and what I personally pay.** The page shows averages, not my account.
- **In shekels, what does 0.56% cost me?** Something like "on 500,000 ₪ that's about 2,800 ₪ a year" would help me a lot.
- **How much the "הוצאות ישירות" (extra direct costs) are.** The page says they exist but not how big.
- **Returns after fees and tax.** That's what I actually get.
- **How Harel compares with other companies** for the same kind of track.
- **Anything for someone close to retirement:** which tracks are "safer", and what happens when I start drawing a pension.
- Returns for the many tracks marked "פחות מ־3 שנים". Even one year's figure would be something.

## 7. If half the page had to go, I'd remove first

1. The "123 ממקור / מחושב / הזנה" line.
2. The row of "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons. The table already shows the same things.
3. The rings in the table. Keep the percentages and drop the circles.
4. The "קופה" number column.
5. The three folded sections (קרנות מחקות, מרכזית לפיצויים, קרנות סל).
6. The "לא מצאתם שם?" search box.

**What I'd keep:** the fee summary line, the table's name, fee, return and money-managed columns, the legal limit, and the honest note about past returns.
