# s35: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 38 s, $0.13

**My pick: option ב, "כל חלק בעמודה משלו".**

Both options give the same numbers: 66,513 ₪ in the keren against 19,595 ₪ on my own. Option ב shows how they got there, one step at a time, like a bank statement: my money 18,000, then the employer's 54,000, then profit 8,487, then tax −13,974, leaving 66,513. I can check the sum in my head. In option א I had to follow thin lines from labels to coloured slices, and the top slice is hatched in two colours, so I wasn't sure which part was tax and which was profit. I know the designers marked א "מומלץ", but ב is the one I could follow.

**1. What I would do, and how sure I am**

I would join. I'm fairly sure: about 8 in 10.

What convinced me is that I put in 300 ₪ a month and the employer puts in 900 ₪ on top. Even if I take the money out early and pay the tax, I end up with 66,513 instead of 19,595. As the page puts it, "כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה 3.70 ₪ בקרן".

Two things hold me back from being certain:
- **My age.** I'm 61. After 6 years I'd be 67, and I'm not sure I'll still be working that long.
- **My own numbers.** The orange warning says "האחוזים האמיתיים כתובים בהסכם העבודה". So these figures are an example, and my employer may pay a different percentage.
