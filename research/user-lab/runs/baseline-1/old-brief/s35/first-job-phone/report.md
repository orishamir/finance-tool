# s35: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 32 s, $0.12

I'm 24, salaried, and my employer offers a keren hishtalmut. I prefer **option א ("עמודה לכל דרך")**.

## 1. My answer
I would join. I'm about 85% sure.

The numbers made it obvious. I put in "300 ₪ בחודש" and my employer adds "900 ₪ מהמעסיק". After 5 years the keren leaves me "66,513 ₪ נשאר בידיים". The same 300 a month in the other account ("חשבון ניירות ערך") leaves only "19,595 ₪". The line "כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה 3.70 ₪ בקרן ו-1.09 ₪ בחשבון ניירות ערך" basically decided it for me. Even if I take the money out early and pay "מס במשיכה ‎−13,974 ₪", I'm still far ahead.

The missing 15% is because I don't know what happens if I need the money before 5 years, or if I change jobs.

## 2. Parts I actually used
- The block under "א. עמודה לכל דרך": the "שכירים" tab, the year-5 chart, the "3.70 / 1.09" sentence, and the two tall bars with "נשאר בידיים".
- The repeat of the same chart one year later ("אותו ציור שנה אחת אחר כך"): at 6 years the tax goes to "0 ₪" and I'd have "98,738 ₪". That's a reason to wait the extra year.
- The example line in orange and grey, but only the part "שכר ברוטו 12,000 ₪". It told me the 300 and 900 come from a salary of 12,000, so mine would be different.
