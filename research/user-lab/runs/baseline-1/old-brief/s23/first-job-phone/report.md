# s23: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 30 s, $0.10

## 1. My answer

I'd go with **כלל** (Clal). Its S&P 500 keren hishtalmut has the lowest fee on the list: **"0.48% מהחיסכון"**. The line above the table backs this up: **"דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.48% עד 0.7%"**. So 0.48% is the cheapest and 0.7% (מור) is the most expensive.

I'm about 75% sure. What holds me back:
- **מנורה מבטחים** charges 0.49%, almost the same. Its row is cut between the two screenshots, so I only saw part of it.
- The fee is an average for 2025 (**"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**). I don't know if I would pay 0.48% myself.
- I have no idea what 0.48% versus 0.7% means in actual shekels for me.

- The table, but only the company name column and the **"דמי ניהול"** column.

- **"לפי מוצר / לפי חברה" tabs**: skimmed and left them alone.
- **Title "כל המסלולים בכל החברות" and the paragraph under it**: skimmed. I picked up that the fees are what was actually charged in 2025 and that the data comes from a government body (רשות שוק ההון). That made me trust it a bit more.
- **"123 ממקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנתה"**: confused me. Three numbers labelled 123 with words I don't get. Skipped it.
- **Product buttons**: used them. "קרן השתלמות" was already highlighted, which was good. I didn't click **"מה זה קרן השתלמות"**.
- **Search box "שם המסלול או החברה"**: skipped. I didn't need it.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" filters**: used them. "עוקב מדד S&P 500" was exactly what I wanted. I ignored the other filters (הלכה, אג"ח, and so on).
- **Summary line (0.48% to 0.7%, sorted expensive to cheap)**: very useful. It gave me the answer before I even looked at the table. It also told me the cheapest is at the **bottom**, which felt backwards. I had to scroll to the end to find it.
- **Table**: used two columns and ignored the rest (details below).
- **Notes under the table** (**"אין: בסוג הקופה הזה מותר לגבות דמי ניהול רק מהחיסכון, לא מההפקדות"**): I read it and half understood it.
- **Note about returns** (**"היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"**): read it. I took it to mean I shouldn't choose based on past returns, which was fine with me.
- **Footer**: skipped.
