# Looking for the cheapest S&P 500 keren hishtalmut

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I would phone **כלל** (Clal) first, then **מנורה מבטחים** (Menora) to compare. On this page Clal's S&P 500 track has the lowest fee: **"0.48% מהחיסכון"**. Menora is right behind at **0.49%**, and **הראל** and **מגדל** are both at **0.5%**.

How sure am I?
- **Fairly sure** Clal is the cheapest on this list. The line above the table confirms the range: **"דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.48% עד 0.7%"**.
- **Not sure** that 0.48% is what I would pay. The heading says **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**, so it's an average across everyone last year. My own fee could be higher or lower.

So what does this mean for my money? The top four are almost the same. The real gap is between them and the most expensive, **מור at 0.7%**. On 200,000 ₪ I worked it out like this:

| Company | Fee | Cost per year on 200,000 ₪ |
|---|---|---|
| כלל | 0.48% | about 960 ₪ |
| מור | 0.7% | about 1,400 ₪ |

That's roughly 440 ₪ a year difference, and it adds up over time. Between Clal and Menora it's about 20 ₪ a year, which is nothing.
