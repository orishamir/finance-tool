# Me, age 61, salary 30,000 a month: should I join the keren hishtalmut?

## 1. My answer and how sure I am

**I would join.** I'm about 70% sure.

What decided it was the big chart in "מה מייחד קרן השתלמות". If I join, I put in "750 ₪ בחודש מכם" and my employer puts in "2,250 ₪ מהמעסיק". For every shekel I put in, my employer adds three. I don't know of a savings plan that does better.

The chart starts at 5 years, which is the bad case. Even then, I put in 45,000 and "נשאר בידיים" shows 136,457. After the "−64,761" tax I still end up with about three times what I put in myself.

Why I'm not more sure:
- **My age.** The page says "למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים". I'm 61 and close to retirement age. I can't tell whether that means 3 years from when I join, or 3 years only after I've retired. This is the most important point for me, and the page skips over it.
- **Tax for someone over 60.** It says "מתחת לגיל 60 זה לפחות 31%". I'm over 60, so what rate do I pay? It never says.
- **My salary is over the limit.** The 750 and 2,250 are worked out on my full 30,000. But the tax-free part only counts "על שכר של עד 15,712 ₪". As I understand it, roughly half of what goes in is not tax-free. That's probably why the striped tax part is still there in year 12.

Before signing, I would ask HR two things: does the deposit cover my whole salary or only up to 15,712, and when can I take the money out at my age?
