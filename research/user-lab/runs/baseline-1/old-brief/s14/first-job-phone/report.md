# s14: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 62 s, $0.24

## 1. My answer

I would join. I'm about 90% sure, maybe more.

My answer comes from one line: **"קרן השתלמות במסלול מדד. ₪750 בחודש מכם, ₪2,250 מהמעסיק."** So I put in 750 a month and my employer adds 2,250. In the big chart, after 5 years I put in **"45,000 ₪"**, the employer added **"+135,000 ₪"**, and I end up with **"136,457 ₪"** under **"נשאר בידיים"**. That's even after the scary tax column. Turning that down would be silly.

The other thing I took from the page is not to touch the money before 6 years (**"מ־6 שנים: פטור עד תקרה"**). One more thing made me happier: the example plan is **"מיטב השתלמות S&P500"**. That's the S&P 500 everyone tells me to invest in.

The missing 10% is practical. I don't know how much smaller my monthly paycheck will be. I also don't know which company to pick or how to actually sign up.

## 2. What I actually used

- The calculator under **"מה מייחד קרן השתלמות"**: my salary box, the 12-year bar chart, and the big 5-column chart. This was 80% of the value for me.
- The orange warning box in **"מיסוי"** (**"משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה"**).
- The **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"** cards, mostly **"כשעוברות 6 שנים"**.
- The **"כמה זה עולה"** table, briefly.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top line with "123 ממקור / 123 מחושב / 123 הזנתה / מונח הקישו להסבר"**: confused me. I don't know what these little labels are for. I skipped them.
- **"מה זה"**: I read the first two paragraphs. **"המעסיק מפקיד לקרן כל חודש כסף בנוסף לשכר"** is the sentence that hooked me. Then I hit **"מס רווחי הון"**, **"גיל הפרישה"**, 7.5%, 2.5%, **"₪15,712"**, **"20,566"** and started skimming. Too many numbers before I'd seen what they mean for me.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: I read it. It's short and clear: **"זה בדרך כלל הצעד הראשון"**. That was reassuring.
- **Calculator**: used it heavily (details in section 4). I left the **"תשואה בשנה 7"** and **"דמי ניהול 0.54"** boxes alone because I wouldn't know what else to put there.
- **The text under the chart** (**"הגרף נמתח..."**, **"47% מס במקור"**, etc.): I skimmed it. I only noticed **"לא תחזית"**, which means it's a guess, fine.
- **"הכי קרוב לזה: חשבון ניירות ערך"**: I noticed it but didn't follow it. I'd like to know whether that's better or worse for me, but the page doesn't say.
- **"שווה לדעת"**: I skimmed the three lines. The codes **"TXH-09"**, **"KH-15"**, **"PF-18"** meant nothing to me.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"**: I read the paragraph. **"המעסיק לא יכול להתנות את ההפקדה בקרן מסוימת"** was useful because I get to choose. The two cards that say **"תלוי בחברה: הראל, אלטשולר שחם"** and **"תלוי בחברה: מיטב"** confused me. Depends on the company how? I didn't open **"הצגת החברות"**.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**: I used it. The 4 cards are big and easy to read. **"עד 4 ימי עסקים"** is nice to know.
- **"כמה זה עולה"**: I skimmed it. I took away "management fee up to 2%" and **"דמי יציאה: אין"**.
- **"מיסוי" cards**: I read the first one partly. There are arrows, so it seems to slide sideways, and I didn't bother. The paragraph under it repeats the 7.5%/15,712 numbers from the top.
- **Orange box**: I read it twice. **"47%"** scared me, then **"זה לא המס הסופי"** half calmed me down.
- **"הכלכלן הראשי במשרד האוצר ממליץ לצמצם את ההטבה"**: I read it and got a little worried that the benefit might go away. The line **"החוק לא השתנה"** helped.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: I skimmed it. **"מוחזקים בנאמנות, בנפרד מהרכוש של החברה"** sounds safe, so good.
- **"מונחים" table**: I skimmed it. I looked up **"ותק"** and **"הפקדה מוטבת"**.
- **Footer**: skipped.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"בין 19 המסלולים... ₪135,908 בדמי ניהול של 0.19% עד ₪137,668 בדמי ניהול של 0.7%"**: the higher fee gives more money? That seems backwards, and it made me trust the numbers a bit less.
- **"−64,761"** tax at year 5 next to **"47% מס במקור"** and **"מקבלים בחזרה את מה שנוכה מעבר למס בפועל"**: so how much tax do I really pay? I don't know.
- **"כסף שהמעסיק מפקיד מעל הגבול ממוסה במס הכנסה כבר באותו חודש"**: since I earn 30,000, which is above 15,712, does this hit my paycheck?
- Words: **"מסלול השקעה"**, **"מס רווחי הון"**, **"רווח ריאלי"**, **"משכורת קובעת"**, **"חברה מנהלת"**, **"נעילה"**, **"ניכוי"**.
- The **"123 ממקור / מחושב / הזנתה"** labels, and the codes like **"TXH-09"**.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
