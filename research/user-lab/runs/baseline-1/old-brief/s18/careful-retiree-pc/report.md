# I'm 61 and want the cheapest stock-index kupat gemel lehashkaa. Here's how the page went for me.

## 1. My answer

I'd call two companies and ask for these tracks:
- **הראל, "עוקב מדדי מניות", קופה 15040**: 0.56% of my savings per year, 0% of my deposits.
- **אלטשולר שחם, "עוקב מדדי מניות", קופה 14864**: also 0.56% and 0%.

**מנורה מבטחים (8683)** is right behind at 0.57%, but it also takes 0.02% of each deposit.

What it means for my money: if I put in 200,000 ₪, 0.56% is about 1,120 ₪ a year. The most expensive one here, מור at 0.67%, is about 1,340 ₪. So the gap is roughly 200 ₪ a year. That's real money, but it isn't huge.

**How sure am I?** About 70%. I trust that these two are the cheapest of the 11 tracks shown. I'm not sure I've seen everything, and I'm not sure I'd personally pay 0.56% (see 5 and 6).

- **Product buttons**: used.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע"**: skipped. I didn't see what difference it would make.
- **Search box**: skipped.
- **The "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons**: used, but they raised a doubt. I also saw **"עוקב מדד s&p 500 12"** and **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש 12"**. Isn't the S&P 500 a stock index too? I never looked at those, so I don't know if something cheaper is hiding there.
- **"החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה לפי הכסף שהן מנהלות…"**: read it. That's why I had to scan the whole list: it's sorted by company size, not by price.

- **"עוד לא דווח" note and אילון קופות גמל**: read it, then dropped אילון. It's marked "חדש", it manages only "1 מיליון ₪", and its fee is unknown. I won't put my savings there.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה".
- The spelling "ההיסכון". I assume it means החיסכון, but it made me stop and wonder.
- "מט"ח" in the ring. Foreign currency, I think, but what does it mean for my money?
- Why "פחות מ־3 שנים" appears at הראל when it manages 305 million. Is the track new?
- What the dotted underline under the numbers means. Can I click them?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Will I personally pay 0.56%?** These are averages for 2025. Can I negotiate lower, and could they raise it later?
- **Is the S&P 500 track cheaper?** It wasn't in my list.
- **A plain shekel example**, like "on 100,000 ₪ you'd pay X ₪ a year". I had to do the arithmetic myself.
- **A way to sort by cheapest fee.** The only order was company size.
- **How to switch or join**, and whether there's any cost or tax when moving money.

## 7. If half the page had to go

First to go: the "123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה" line, the grey company rows that repeat the fee "מ־0.56% עד 0.56%", the coloured rings, and the return column full of "פחות מ־3 שנים". After that, the "קיבוץ לפי" toggle and the search box. I'd keep the product buttons, the "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons, the fee columns, and the sentence explaining that fees are 2025 averages.
