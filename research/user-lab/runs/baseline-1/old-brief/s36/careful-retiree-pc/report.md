# s36: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 43 s, $0.14

# As myself, aged 61, salaried, reading slowly

**The option I'd rather see: B, "ב. אותו סיפור בשורות" (the sideways bars).** Each line puts the words and the shekel amount next to each other on the right, like a bank statement: "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪", "המס לקח −13,974 ₪", "נשאר בידיים 66,513 ₪". I read it top to bottom and never had to look back. In option A the amount is at the top of each bar and its label is at the bottom, so my eyes kept going up and down. I saw the green "מומלץ" (recommended) label on A. That's the designers' view and it didn't change mine.

## 1. My answer

I'd take the keren hishtalmut and try not to touch it for 6 years. I'm fairly sure, maybe 8 out of 10.

What convinced me is the line "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪". My employer puts in 900 ₪ a month, but only if the money goes into the keren. A plain securities account gets nothing from the employer. The tiny picture at the top shows my own 18,000 ₪ in a securities account turning into about 19,595 ₪. In the keren, even if I take it out early at year 5 and pay the tax, I'm left with 66,513 ₪. If I wait for year 6, "המס לקח 0 ₪" (the tax took nothing) and I have 98,738 ₪.

It's only 8 out of 10 because of the warning "האחוזים האמיתיים כתובים בהסכם העבודה": the real percentages are in my employment contract. If my employer doesn't pay in, none of this applies to me.

## 2. What I actually used

- **The small grey example text:** my own deposit is 2.5%, my employer's is 7.5%, the return is 7%, and "הכול בכסף של היום" (everything is in today's money).
- **The five-year statement**, in both versions.
- **The dashed box** "אם מחכים לשנה 6, בלי להפקיד עוד 83,978 ₪" (if you wait until year 6 without depositing more).
- **The second card in A** showing year 6 with "0 ₪" tax.
- **The tiny picture at the top**, squinting.

## 3. Block by block

- **Title and subtitle:** read them. "אפשר לגרור את השנים" means you can drag the years. I didn't try it.
- **"מה היה" (what was) thumbnail:** it confused me, and the writing is too small for my eyes. It's the only place that shows the securities account on its own, and that was my real question.
- **Grey example paragraph:** read every word. The orange warning was useful. The fund names and numbers, "(13245)", "(5139282)", "פייר פיננשיאל טכנולוג'יז", meant nothing to me.
- **Heading and intro for A:** skimmed. "תזוזה וסיפור" (movement and story) is designer talk.
- **"שכירים / עצמאים" switch (salaried / self-employed):** I left it on salaried and ignored it.
- **Little year bars 1 to 12:** I glanced at them. They showed me that the money grows and that after year 6 it's "פטור ממס" (tax-free). The bars themselves are too small.
- **Big statement in A:** used it, but with more effort than B.
- **"אם מושכים עכשיו ושמים את הכסף בחשבון ניירות ערך…"** (if you withdraw now and put it in a securities account): read it twice. It says waiting beats moving the money, by 15,141 ₪. Useful, but it isn't the comparison I came for.
- **"בין 19 המסלולים…"** (among the 19 tracks): skimmed it. The tracks differ by only about 750 ₪, so fees don't matter much here.
- **"הגובה נמתח בכל שנה…"** (the height stretches each year): didn't understand it, skipped it.
- **B:** used it. This was my favourite.

## 4. In the charts

- **What I looked at:** the white "נשאר בידיים" (left in hand) bar, the yellow employer bar, the striped pink tax and the dashed year-6 box. In the little bars, the dotted line at year 6 and the words "לפני 6 שנים: מס" (before 6 years: tax).
- **What I ignored:** the dark brown and dark grey inside the bars. I couldn't tell at first whether that was more money or just what was carried over. I also ignored the teal-on-yellow detail in the small bars.
- **The dotted underlines under some numbers:** I didn't know whether I was meant to click them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- Why the tax at year 5 (13,974 ₪) is bigger than all the return (8,487 ₪). Is the tax on the employer's money too? Nothing says so.
- The fund numbers in brackets, and the name "S&P500".
- "ברצועה" (in the strip). Which strip?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- One plain sentence: "keren: X ₪, securities account: Y ₪", in big letters.
- Does my age matter? I'm 61. Can I take the money out at 67 or earlier with no tax?
- What if the market falls instead of rising 7%?
- What happens if my salary is above the ceiling ("התקרה")? The page mentions it but never gives the number.
- How do I check whether my employer pays in, and how do I open one?

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. The tiny "מה היה" picture, but first move the securities-account numbers into large text.
2. Option A, keeping B.
3. The 19-tracks line and "הגובה נמתח…".
4. The fund names and numbers in the example paragraph.

I'd keep the little year bars, though. The line at year 6 is the one thing on the page I'll actually remember.
