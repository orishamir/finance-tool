## 1. My answer

I would open a keren hishtalmut. I'm about 85% sure.

What decided it for me is the yellow bar, **"המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪"**. I put in 18,000 ₪ over 5 years and my employer added 54,000 ₪. A plain securities account doesn't give me that. Even when I take the money out early and pay **"המס לקח −13,974 ₪"**, I still have **"נשאר בידיים 66,513 ₪"**. If I wait until year 6, I get **98,738 ₪** and **"המס כבר 0 ₪"**.

I'm not 100% sure because I'm assuming the employer money only comes if I have the keren. The page doesn't say that clearly. I also never saw what the same 18,000 ₪ would become in a securities account, at least not in anything I could read.

**Which chart I'd pick:** option **ב. אותו סיפור בשורות** (the rows), even though option א has the **"מומלץ"** badge. In the rows, each label and its number sit right next to the bar, so I read it from top to bottom like a receipt. In the columns I kept looking back and forth between the number on top and the label at the bottom. It's not a strong preference, though. Both told me the same thing.
