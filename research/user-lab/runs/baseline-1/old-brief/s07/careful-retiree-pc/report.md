# A fixed bank deposit: how I read the page, aged 61 and careful with my money

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I would **not lock my money into a fixed deposit yet.** First I would go to my bank and ask three questions: what rate they pay me, what it costs to take the money out early, and whether there are dates when I can leave without paying.

What changed my mind was the box **"מה קורה לכסף לאורך הזמן"** (what happens to the money over time). Its example puts in 10,000 ₪, adds 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, at **"בריבית של 1.8%"** (at 1.8% interest). It says I put in **"ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪"** (your deposits) and end with **"נשאר לכם 68,270 ₪"** (what's left for you). At first I thought it was a mistake, because the bank pays me interest. Then I saw the button **"בכסף של היום"** (in today's money) and the inflation box set to 2%. So for my money, at 1.8% interest with 2% inflation, I end up a bit poorer in what the money can buy. On top of that I pay **"מס 986.20 ₪"** (tax).

Two more things worried me:
- **"אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות: אם בנק קורס, אין חוק שמחזיר לכם את הכסף"** (Israel has no deposit insurance; if a bank collapses, no law gives you your money back). I didn't know that.
- The red box: **"שבירת פיקדון לפני הזמן: האתר לא מחשב אותה"** (the site doesn't calculate breaking a deposit early). That is exactly what I'm afraid of giving up, and the page can't tell me what it costs.

**How sure am I?** About 60% sure that I shouldn't rush. I'm not sure what the better alternative is. The page mentions **"קרן כספית"** (money market fund) and **"מק"מ"**, but it doesn't compare them for me.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- The top line under **"פיקדון"**: "כסף שמפקידים בבנק לתקופה מוסכמת, ובתמורה הבנק משלם עליו ריבית" (money you leave in the bank for an agreed period, and the bank pays you interest on it). That's clear.
- **"מה זה"**: the first and third paragraphs.
- The calculator, **"מה קורה לכסף לאורך הזמן"**, with the boxes next to it and **"תנאי המוצג בדוגמה"** (the terms in the example).
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"** (why people use this).
- **"שווה לדעת"** (worth knowing): the three lines.
- **"מיסוי"** (taxation): the three cards and the red box.

- A few lines of **"מונחים"** (terms): **"קנס שבירה"** (early-withdrawal penalty), **"תחנת יציאה"** (exit date), **"חידוש אוטומטי"** (automatic renewal).

## 3. Block by block

**Top of page**

- **Title and first line**: used. It's simple.
- **The strip "123 מקור 123 מחושב 123 הדגמה… לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"** (source / calculated / example, click a number to see where it comes from): confused me. I read it twice and still don't really know what to do with it. I didn't click anything.
- **Right-hand list "בעמוד הזה"** (on this page): glanced at it, didn't use it.
- **The "הצגת החוקים והכללים" links** (show the laws and rules) on the left of every heading: skipped all of them. I want the bottom line, not the law.

**"מה זה" (what it is)**
- **First paragraph**: used. It explains "קבועה" (fixed rate) and "משתנה" (variable rate), and that at the end the money comes back or is **"מתחדש אוטומטית"** (renewed automatically). The automatic renewal made me nervous: I'd want to know about it before I'm locked in again.
- **Second paragraph**, about "8 סוגי פיקדון מקובלים" (8 standard deposit types): skimmed. It didn't tell me which one suits me.
- **Third paragraph**: used. It says non-linked means I get back what I put in plus interest **"גם אם המחירים עלו"** (even if prices went up). That's the important warning.
- **"איזו ריבית היא העוגן תלוי בבנק"** (which rate is the anchor depends on the bank): confused me. What is an "עוגן" (anchor)?

**The calculator**

- **"תנאי המוצג בדוגמה"**: used. It told me these are Leumi's terms: 12 months, **"לא צמוד"** (not linked to prices).
- **The big number and the three totals**: used heavily.
- **"הצגה כטבלה"** (show as table): didn't open it.

**Middle sections**
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: used. "כסף שאתם יודעים מתי תצטרכו" (money you know when you'll need) is the right question for me, and the comparison with a money market fund helped a bit.
- **The "מזומן שקלי" chip** (shekel cash): skipped. It didn't mean anything to me.
- **"שווה לדעת"**: used the words, ignored the codes (TXI-03, BANK-06, BANK-23). They look like a filing system.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"** (who offers it and who supervises): skimmed. It's useful that I can open a deposit at another bank since **01.12.2025**, but **"חשבון במערכת סגורה"** (an account in a closed system) is new to me.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"** (money in and out): read it. It only says **"בסוף התקופה שנקבעה בהסכם"** (at the end of the period set in the agreement). That's thin. I didn't open the detail.
- **"כמה זה עולה"** (what it costs): read it, and it disappointed me. **"דמי יציאה… לפי התעריפון של הגורם המציע"** (exit fees, according to the provider's price list) means "go ask the bank".

**"מיסוי" (taxation)**
- Used the three cards. 15% of the interest is plain enough.
- The paragraph about exemption for low income: read it carefully, because I may qualify. The phrase **"פטור למעוט הכנסה"** (exemption for people with little income) made me look up the term.
- The paragraph about losses on securities: skipped. It doesn't concern me.
- The red box: read it twice.

**"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן" (risks)**: read every word.

**"מה האתר לקח בחשבון" (what the site took into account)**: skipped. It's collapsed and sounds technical.

**"מונחים" (terms)**: skimmed and used a few rows. It's long.

**Footer**: noticed **"אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"** (a personal site, not commercial, no marketing links). That reassured me a little.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **How much do I actually lose if I need the money early?** A number, or at least a typical range. This is the main thing I give up, and the page doesn't calculate it.
- **What rates the banks pay today**, side by side, for 3 months, 1 year and 3 years. The page only has Leumi's 1.8%.
- **A plain comparison with a money market fund or מק"מ**, for the same amount over the same time.
- **Whether a price-linked deposit would protect me from inflation**, and what it pays.
- **How safe the big banks really are**, given there's no deposit insurance, and whether I should split my money between banks.
- **Whether I can stop the automatic renewal**, and how.
- **What "דמי יציאה" (exit fees) actually costs** in shekels.
