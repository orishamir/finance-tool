# A fixed bank deposit (פיקדון), as I read the page

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I think I now get the basic idea. A deposit is **"כסף שמפקידים בבנק לתקופה מוסכמת, ובתמורה הבנק משלם עליו ריבית"** (money you leave in the bank for an agreed time, and the bank pays you interest). What I give up is access to the money. If I take it out early, that's **"שבירת פיקדון"** (breaking the deposit), and the bank may charge a fine.

What would I actually do? I'd probably put **part** of my idle money into a 12-month deposit that is **"לא צמוד"** (not linked to anything), the one the page uses as its example. I'd only use money I'm sure I won't need for a year, or pick one with **"תחנות יציאה"** (exit points). I'd keep the rest available.

I'm hesitant for one reason, which is the calculator. I put in 70,000 ₪ over 10 years, and it says I'd have **68,270 ₪ "בכסף של היום"** (in today's money). If I read that right, with 1.8% interest and 2% inflation I end up **poorer** than what I put in. That made me wonder whether a deposit is even worth it compared with the S&P 500 everyone talks about. The page doesn't help me compare.

- How sure am I that I understand how a deposit works? About 70%.
- How sure am I that it's the right move for me? About 40%.
