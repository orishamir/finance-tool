# s37: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 40 s, $0.20

I'd pick **option C** (the one titled "ג. כמו א׳, ועוד תשואה עבר וגודל"). It's the same as A, but it also shows what each track earned in past years and how much money it holds. As someone with no finance background, those were the two things I most wanted to know.

**1. My answer**

I'd put my savings in an **S&P 500 track**, inside the "עוקב מדד s&p 500" group. That's the thing everyone tells me to do, and it was easy to find.

For the company, I'd probably pick **הראל גמל להשקעה עוקב מדד s&p 500**. It charges "0.56% מהחיסכון", earned "13.70%" over 3 years and "11.92%" over 5 years, and holds "2.3 מיליארד ₪". It's not the cheapest, but it has a 5-year record and it's big, so it feels safe. My second choice would be **מנורה** at "0.52%". It's the cheapest, but it shows "פחות מ־5 שנים", so it has no 5-year history.

- **About choosing an S&P track:** fairly sure, around 75%.
- **About which company:** only about 50% sure. The fee gaps are tiny (0.52% to 0.71%) and the returns are almost the same, so the choice felt a bit random.

**2. Parts I actually used**

- The intro bullet at the top saying "דמי הניהול... נעים רק בין 0.50% ל־0.73%". It told me the fees are all similar, which calmed me down.
- In option C:
  - the "במה הכסף מושקע" chips (I left it on "הכול" and scrolled to the S&P group);
  - the grey group header "עוקב מדד s&p 500 · 12 מסלולים · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.71%";
  - the table rows, especially the fee, return and size columns;
  - the note at the bottom: "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס".
- In option A, the same S&P group plus the "מניות" group, for comparison.

**3. Block by block**

- **Top title and three bullets:** I read them. The fee-range bullet helped. The first bullet ("משותף לשלוש...") meant nothing to me, so I skimmed it.
- **Tabs "מקור / מדריכים / חוקים וכללים / שאלות / חברות ומסלולים / השקעה במדד":** skipped. "השקעה במדד" caught my eye, though. Maybe that's what I actually need.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר":** skipped.
- **Product chips (תגמולים, קרן השתלמות, קופת גמל להשקעה, …):** confusing. I don't know how these differ. I just trusted that "קופת גמל להשקעה" was already selected. I saw the "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link and wished it explained more right there.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" chips:** I skimmed them. I recognised "s&p 500", "מניות" and "כללי". "הלכה יהודית", "אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר", "כספי (שקלי)" and "קיימות" I ignored.
- **"מיון לפי" buttons:** noticed, didn't touch. The default "דמי ניהול מהחיסכון" seemed fine.
- **"חיפוש בשם המסלול":** skipped.
- **Option A's grouped table:** used, until the long "מניות" and "עוקב מדדי מניות" groups. I didn't understand the difference between them and the S&P group, so I skimmed those.
- **The orange "ציור בלבד, כדי לקצר…" note:** skipped. It looked like a designer's note.
- **Option B (one mixed list):** this one confused me. The first row is "עוקב מדדים - גמיש" with "55%" stocks, then an S&P track, then "מניות סחיר", then "אשראי ואג"ח". The little tag under each name helps, but I had to read every tag. I'd never choose that page.
- **Option C:** used the most. The return and size columns were what made me decide.
- **Bottom note in C:** read it. It's good that it warns me, but it made me unsure whether I can trust the returns.

**4. Tables: what I looked at and what I ignored**

- **Looked at:**
  - the track name;
  - "מהחיסכון" percent (the main fee);
  - "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" returns;
  - "מיליון ₪ / מיליארד ₪" size;
  - the "חדש" tag (I avoided those);
  - "עוד לא דווח" (it put me off).
- **Glanced at:** "מההפקדות". It's almost always 0%, so I stopped looking.
- **Ignored:**
  - the "קופה" number column (13874, 14865…), which is meaningless to me;
  - the ‹ arrows;
  - the small clock icons;
  - the purple dotted underlines.
- **The rings and the "מניות / מט"ח" percentages:** I noticed that a full yellow-and-teal ring means "99%/99%" and a thin ring means low. I understood the S&P ones are "full". But I didn't really know what to do with that information.

**5. What I didn't understand**

- "מט"ח". I know it means foreign currency, but not why I should care that the fund is "97%" or "26%" in it.
- Why there are two fees, "מהחיסכון" and "מההפקדות".
- "101%" stocks (מיטב מניות). How is that more than 100?
- The difference between "מניות", "עוקב מדדי מניות" and "עוקב מדד s&p 500".
- "פחות מ־3 שנים" in the return column. Does it mean the track is new, or that the return is low?
- The clock icons and the dotted underlines.
- What a "קופת גמל להשקעה" even is, compared with a "קרן השתלמות".

**6. What I wanted and couldn't find**

- What the fee costs me in shekels. For example, how much I'd pay a year on ₪50,000.
- Whether I can take my money out whenever I want, and what the tax is.
- The returns after fees.
- Is there a minimum deposit, and how do I actually open one? Is there a button or link?
- Whether S&P is riskier than "מניות" or "כללי", in plain words.
- Returns for the other groups. C only showed returns for S&P, so I couldn't compare.

**7. If half the page had to go**

1. **Option B, all of it.** It's the hardest one to use.
2. Most of the "במה הכסף מושקע" chips. Keep הכול, S&P 500, מניות and כללי.
3. The "קופה" number column.
4. The top tabs row and "לפי חברה / לפי מוצר".
5. The "מניות / מט"ח" rings, unless someone explains them.

I'd keep the fee column, the past returns, the fund size and the bottom warning.
