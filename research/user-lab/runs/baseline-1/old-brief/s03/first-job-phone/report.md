# s03: first-job-phone (old brief)

phone; 51 s, $0.26

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd keep about one or two months of expenses in my current account and move the rest out. I'm about **80% sure** that leaving it there loses me money. I'm only about **30% sure** what to move it into.

What convinced me was the calculator **"כמה עולה להשאיר כסף בעו"ש?"** ("how much does it cost to leave money in the current account?"). It says 10,000 ₪ left for 10 years is **"שווים 8,203 ₪ בכסף של היום: 1,797 ₪ פחות"** (worth 8,203 ₪ in today's money, 1,797 ₪ less). The next line explains why: **"רוב הבנקים לא משלמים ריבית על עו"ש"** (most banks don't pay interest on current accounts). Then it says a **"קרן כספית יומית"** would give 10,779 ₪, so **"המחיר של להשאיר"** (the price of leaving it) is 2,576 ₪. That's a real number about money like mine, and it made me want to act.

I'm not confident about the alternative for three reasons:

- I don't know what a "קרן כספית" is or how to get one.
- The page says the 3.25% is **"הנחה של האתר, לא הבטחה ולא תחזית"** (the site's assumption, not a promise or a forecast).
- There's another option I can't judge: **"4 מתוך 8 הבנקים... משלמים ריבית... עד 10,000 ₪ בלבד; הכי הרבה: 3%, אצל דיסקונט"** (4 of 8 banks pay interest, only up to 10,000 ₪; the most is 3%, at Discount). Maybe I should just ask my own bank first.

My next step would be the link **"לעמוד של קרן כספית יומית"** (to the daily money fund page).
