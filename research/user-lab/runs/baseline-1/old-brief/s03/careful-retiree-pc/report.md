# What happens to my savings if they stay in the current account

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I wouldn't leave all my savings sitting in the current account. I'd keep what I need for the next month or two there. Then I'd phone my bank and ask whether it pays interest on my balance. After that I'd look into the money-market fund (קרן כספית) the page keeps comparing against. I wouldn't move anything until I understood that fund better.

The line that decided it for me was in the calculator:
> "אחרי 10 שנים, 10,000 ₪ בעו״ש שווים 8,203 ₪ בכסף של היום: 1,797 ₪ פחות."

So what does this mean for my money? If I've understood it, money sitting there loses roughly a fifth of what it can buy over ten years. That's because the page says *"רוב הבנקים לא משלמים ריבית על עו״ש"*. My balance is bigger than 10,000, so I'd expect to lose more in shekels, but the same share.

**How sure am I?**
- **Fairly sure** that leaving it there loses value.
- **Not sure at all** what the better option is. The fund's 3.25% is described as *"הנחה של האתר, לא הבטחה ולא תחזית"*. The 2% price rise is also just *"הנחה של האתר"*. Both numbers are guesses.

## 2. The parts I actually used
- The calculator box *"כמה עולה להשאיר כסף בעו״ש?"*: the sentence, the three totals (8,203 / 10,779 / 2,576) and the notes underneath.
- The line in *"שווה לדעת"*: *"4 מתוך 8 הבנקים שנאספו משלמים ריבית על יתרה בעו״ש, כולם בתנאים ועל עד 10,000 ₪ בלבד; הכי הרבה: 3%, אצל דיסקונט"*.
- The 15,000 ₪ notice. It's in *"מה זה"* and again in *"שווה לדעת"*.
- *"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"*, the part about no deposit insurance.
- *"כסף נכנס ויוצא"*: I can withdraw any time, and moving banks is free and tax-free.

## 3. Going down the page

- **Title and grey line under it** (*"כסף שיושב בו לא תמיד מקבל ריבית"*): Read it. It's the right question for me.

- **"מה זה"**:
  - First paragraph: read it.
  - The 15,000 ₪ notice: useful. My balance is above that, so the bank should be writing to me, and I don't think it has.
  - The third paragraph about *"המסלול הבסיסי… המורחב… BANK-17… 01.10.2026… 01.07.2027… BANK-35"*: lost me. Too many dates and codes, and I couldn't tell whether my fees are going up or down.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: Skimmed. It told me the account is for money I need soon, which is fair. *"מזומן שקלי"* in the bubble meant nothing to me.
- **The calculator**: Used it the most. I'd type my own balance into *"יתרה בעו״ש"*. I left the years at 10.
- **The collapsed box "חלק מהבנקים כן משלמים ריבית"**: I wanted it, but it was closed. The *"שווה לדעת"* line gave me the gist.
- **"הכי קרוב לזה… קרן כספית יומית"** and its links: Read it. I didn't follow the links.
- **Second calculator, "מה קורה לכסף לאורך הזמן"**: Skimmed, then dropped it. It assumes I add *"500 ₪ בחודש"*, and I don't. The 62,681 against 70,000 just told me the same story again.
- **"שווה לדעת"**: Used. The BANK-20 and BANK-23 tags meant nothing to me.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"**: Skimmed. It was reassuring that Bank of Israel supervises the banks.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**: Read it. Knowing I can switch banks online for free matters.

- **Red box** (*"הפטור למעוטי הכנסה… לא ידוע"*): Read it and worried, because I'm not sure where I stand.
- **Paragraph on "הפסד ממכירת ניירות ערך"**: Skipped. I don't sell securities.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: Read every word. *"אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות"* and *"זו סמכות שבשיקול דעת, לא זכות שלכם"* frightened me a little.

- **"מונחים"**: Skimmed. A bar with *"מקור"* and buttons sat on top of some of the text.
- **Footer**: Only noticed *"נתונים נכון ל־26.10.2026"*.

## 5. What I didn't understand
- What a *"קרן כספית"* actually is, and whether it's as safe as a bank account.
- *"מסלול בסיסי / מורחב"*, and what the 7 ₪ and 10 ₪ caps mean for my monthly bill.
- The codes: *BANK-17, BANK-35, TXI OQ11, TXI-17*.
- *"ריבית צמודה למדד: 25% מהריבית הריאלית"*.
- *"הפיקדון האחרון נחשב כאילו נפדה ביום המשיכה"*.
- What *"מנהל מורשה"* would mean for me if a bank failed.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
- **My age:** Is there a tax break for someone my age? The page says it's an open question and isn't calculated.
- **My bank:** Does it pay interest, and on what conditions? What exactly are Discount's conditions for the 3%?
- **Above 10,000 ₪:** What happens to the part of my balance over that, even at a bank that pays interest?
- **The money-market fund:** Is it insured? Can it lose money? What does it cost in fees, how is it taxed, and how do I actually buy one?
- **Fees in shekels:** What I really pay each month to keep the account.
- **Safety:** Is my money safer at a big bank than a small one, since there's no deposit insurance?

## 7. If half the page had to go, I'd cut first
1. The second calculator. It's built around monthly deposits I don't make.
2. The securities-loss paragraph.
3. The *"מונחים"* list and the footer links.
4. The *"123 מקור/מחושב/הזנתם"* legend and all the code tags.
5. *"מה האתר לקח בחשבון"*. Though if they answered the age question, I'd want that kept.

I'd keep the first calculator, the 4-of-8-banks line, the 15,000 ₪ notice and the no-insurance warning.
