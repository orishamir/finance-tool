# How I used the "מדריך" (guide) page, as a 24-year-old salaried employee

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd do three things in this order:

1. **Check my payslip or ask HR about a קרן השתלמות.** The "מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start) box says "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם: קרן השתלמות" (salaried people whose employer deposits for them: a study fund). The card says that after a period "שקבע החוק" (set by law) I can withdraw "בפטור ממס" (tax-free). Free money from my employer with no tax sounds like the first thing to check.
2. **Make sure I already have a pension.** The page calls "קרן חדשה מקיפה" "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר" (the base for every salaried saver). I think my employer already deposits into one, but I don't know which fund it is or whether I chose it.
3. **Then the S&P 500.** I found "מדד S&P 500" at the bottom of the page. I learned it's an index (a list of companies), not something I buy directly. I'd have to pick a "place" (קרן השתלמות, קופת גמל להשקעה or חשבון ניירות ערך) and put it in there. That's what the "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון" (two parts of every saving) box taught me.

**How sure:** fairly sure about steps 1 and 2, maybe 70%, because the page clearly tells salaried people to start there. For step 3 I'm about 30%. I don't know which "place" to use for the S&P 500, and the page doesn't compare them.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון" (two parts of every saving).** This was the most useful part. It explained that "איפה הכסף מוחזק" (where the money is held) and "במה הכסף מושקע" (what it's invested in) are separate questions.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start).** These three green lines are basically my answer.
- **Cards under "חיסכון עם הטבת מס" (saving with a tax benefit):** קרן השתלמות and קופת גמל להשקעה.
- **"פרישה" (retirement):** only the first card, קרן חדשה מקיפה.
- **"לבחירה עצמית" (your own choice):** the "חשבון ניירות ערך" (securities account) card.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע":** the S&P 500 card, and the קרן כספית card, because the start box mentioned it.

## 4. Lists and labels I looked at vs ignored

There are no tables or charts on the page, only grids of cards.

**What I looked at:**
- The bold card titles.
- The small section headings (כסף שוטף וקצר, פרישה, לילדים…).
- The green links in "מאיפה להתחיל".
- The bold "בשפה פשוטה:" (in plain language) line on the retirement cards. I hoped it would help, but it mostly just repeated the title.

**What I ignored or didn't get:**
- The little arrows on each card. I assume they mean "click for more".
- The two grey buttons "יומית" (daily) and "מתחדשת" (renewing) on the קרן כספית card. They're the only buttons like that on the page and I have no idea what they are.
- All the Tel Aviv index cards (ת"א-35, ת"א-90, ת"א-125).
- The two MSCI cards. I couldn't tell how "MSCI World" differs from "MSCI AC World", and the cards have walls of text.
- The second half of most card descriptions.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **Words I don't know:** "מק"מ", "אג"ח", "קרן נאמנות", "ברוקר", "תגמולים", "פיצויים", "תקרה שנתית" (from the קופת גמל להשקעה card).
- **"עם חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו"** (with exposure to the dollar or neutralized from it). Is that good or bad for me?
- **"קרן שעוקבת אחריו"** (a fund that tracks it). So who runs that fund, and where do I buy it?
- **"במשקל לפי שווי השוק"** (weighted by market value) and **"שווי החזקות הציבור"** (value of public holdings).
- **"מסלול כללי"** (general track): "לפי שיקול דעת של ועדת ההשקעות" (at the investment committee's discretion). Is that the default I'm probably in right now?
- **Two kinds of קרן השתלמות:** a regular one and "בניהול אישי" (self-managed). Which one do I have?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **How long a קרן השתלמות is locked.** The page only says "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק" (after a period set by law). How many years?
- **Fees.** Nothing on the page tells me what each option costs.
- **Returns and risk.** Roughly what has the S&P 500 made compared with a קרן כספית? How much could I lose?
- **For the S&P 500 specifically:** should I use a קופת גמל להשקעה or a חשבון ניירות ערך? What's the practical difference in tax and in being able to take the money out?
- **Order of priority.** Something like: "first emergency money, then השתלמות, then…", and how much money each step needs.
- **Minimum amounts**, and whether I can put in a small amount every month.
