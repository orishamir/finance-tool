## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd move my money at Harel into the track called **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"**, in whatever product I actually have. If it's my pension (I'm salaried, so I'm guessing that's most of it), that's **קרן חדשה מקיפה → "עוקב מדד S&P 500"**, fund number 13415. If I have a **קרן השתלמות**, I'd switch that to the S&P 500 track there too (13502).

I'm about 50% sure. I'm fairly confident S&P 500 is what I want, because that's the advice I'd already heard. The page shows it at roughly **"13.7%"** a year over 3 years and **"11.9%"** over 5, and I understood that. I'm not sure which product I actually have. I also can't tell whether the pension S&P track is really S&P 500, because it says only **"מניות 73%"** while the others say 99%. And I still don't know how to switch.

I skipped **"חיסכון לכל ילד"** because I don't have kids. **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"** and **"קרן חדשה כללית"** sound almost the same, and I couldn't tell which one is my pension.

## 4. What I looked at in the tables

- **Looked at:** the track name (**"המסלול"**), the **"מהחיסכון"** fee number, both return lines (**"3 שנים"**, **"5 שנים"**), and the **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** entries, which I took to mean the track is too new.
- **Glanced at:** **"כסף מנוהל"**. Bigger amounts felt safer, so **"33.1 מיליארד ₪"** on the pension S&P track reassured me.
- **Ignored:** the **"קופה"** numbers. I also ignored the half-circle charts, though I did notice that teal = **"מניות"** and yellow = **"מט"ח"**, and that a full yellow-and-teal ring means S&P-type tracks. I ignored the dotted underlines and the sort arrows in the column headers.
- **Tags** like **"הלכה יהודית"** and **"50-60"**: read them, but they weren't for me.
- **Coloured tags in the funds table** (**"חוזקת את המניות"**, **"צוברת"**, **"חשיפה לדולר"**, **"מס גם על שינויי המטבע"**): ignored them. Too much.

- **"מהחיסכון"** vs **"מההפקדה"**. In the pension it's **"0.16% מהחיסכון"** but **"1.47% מההפקדה"**. Is that expensive or cheap? Which one matters more for me?
- **"מט"ח"**, **"אג"ח"**, **"כספי (שקלי)"**, **"משולב סחיר"**, **"קיימות"**
- Why the pension **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** has only **"מניות 73%"**
- Why **"מניות"** shows **"21.45%"** over 3 years when S&P 500 shows **"13.71%"**. Should I pick that instead?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול"**: so what do I actually get after fees?
- **"עוד לא דווח"**
- **"4A" / "4D"** and **"קבועים / משתנים / נאמן"**
- **"גילאי 50 ומטה"**: is that a default I'm already in without knowing?
