# Me, 61, reading the Harel page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would not move any money today.** First I would phone Harel or dig out my annual statement and find two things: which track I'm in, and its fund number. On this page that is the number under "קופה" (for example "9829"). Then I'd find that row and check it.

What I took away:

- **My pension is the thing I'd ask about.** In "קרן חדשה מקיפה" every track charges "0.16% מהחיסכון" but also "1.47% מההפקדות". That second number looks big. Nobody tells me what it means in shekels, for example on ₪3,000 a month.
- **At 61 I want a track for my age.** In the pension I'd look at "גילאי 60 ומעלה" (number 9829): about 31% in shares, with "9.49%" over 3 years and "6.63%" over 5 years. In the savings fund I'd look at "גמל מסלול לגילאי 60 ומעלה" (9745). I'd be afraid of the 98–99% shares tracks like "עוקב מדד S&P 500", even though their returns look nicer.
- **I'd be suspicious of the high-return tracks.** The page says itself that the returns are "לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס" and "לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך". So the 21% figures are not what I'd get.

**How sure:** about 4 out of 10. I learned what Harel offers and roughly what it charges. But the page shows "ממוצע 2025", the average Harel actually charged in 2025. It doesn't show what *I* pay, and I don't know which product my money sits in.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**Looked at:**

- **"המסלול"**, the track name, and the small grey labels like "הלכה יהודי" and "50 עד".
- **The fee column**, both lines: "מהחיסכון" and "מההפקדות". Some words in it confused me:
  - "אין" (none) at first looked like "no information". The footnote then explained it means *not allowed to charge*.
  - "עוד לא דווח" (not reported yet) with the little warning mark.
- **The returns**: "3 שנים" and "5 שנים". There are many rows of "פחות מ־3 שנים", so those tracks are new and have no history. I ignored them.
- **The numbers in the shares and foreign-currency column**, beside "מניות" (shares) and "מט"ח" (foreign currency).

**Ignored:**

- **The coloured rings.** I read the numbers instead. The teal and yellow dots helped a little as a key.
- **"קופה"** (fund number), except to match it to my statement.
- **"כסף מנוהל"** (money managed). I only glanced at it to see that a track isn't tiny. Seven billion or 900 million means nothing for my money.
- **The little arrows at the end of each row and the sort arrows.** I didn't know what they'd open.
- **The dotted lines under the numbers.** They seem clickable, but I wasn't going to click everything.
- **The bar heading each product section** ("16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול… מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"). I read the fee range once and then ignored it.

## 5. What I did not understand

- "123 מקבוץ / 123 מחושב".
- Which is worse for me: "1.47% מההפקדות" or "0.16% מהחיסכון". Why does pension charge both?
- Whether "ממוצע 2025" fees are what I pay, or whether my own deal could be different.
- "מקיפה" versus "כללית".
- "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים" and "מרכזית לפיצויים".
- In the index-tracking and exchange-traded funds:
  - what "קבוע", "משתנה" and "נאמן" add up to;
  - what "4A" and "4D" mean;
  - what "מנוטרלת מט"ח" means.
- The "PF-06" and "PF-10" codes.
- The "הוצאות ישירות" (direct costs) that come out of the fund but aren't shown. How much are they?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **The fees in shekels.** For example: "on ₪500,000 you pay X a year."
- **Returns after fees.** That's what actually reaches me.
- **How to tell which product and track I'm in, and how to switch** and whether switching costs anything.
- **The best fee I could ask for**, not just the average.
- **A comparison with other companies.** Is 1.47% normal? There's a "השוואה למטרה" (compare by goal) link at the top, but nothing here pointed me to it.
- **What happens to the money at retirement** in each product: a monthly pension or a lump sum.
