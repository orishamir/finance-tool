## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd pick an S&P 500 track, because that's the one thing I already knew to look for. The page has a filter button for it, **"עוקב מדד s&p 500 12"**, so 12 companies offer one. Going by the lowest fees with at least 5 years of history, my shortlist is:

| Company | Fee on savings ("מהחיסכון") | Fee on deposits ("מההפקדה") | 3-year return | 5-year return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **כלל** ("עוקב מדד s&p 500") | 0.47% | 0.01% | 13.39% | 12.01% |
| **מגדל** ("ולפיצויים עוקב מדד s&p500") | 0.48% | "עוד לא דווח" | 13.6% | 12.09% |
| **הראל** ("גמל - עוקב מדד s&p 500") | 0.5% | 0.01% | 13.71% | 11.86% |
| **הפניקס** ("גמל עוקב מדד s&p500") | 0.56% | 0% | 13.34% | 12.33% |

I'd probably go with **כלל**. It's the cheapest one that shows both fees, and its returns are about the same as the others. All the S&P tracks look almost identical, so fees seem to be the only real difference. The note at the bottom backs this up: past returns don't predict the future, so the list isn't sorted by them.

**How sure: about 6/10.** I'm fairly sure about "S&P 500 plus a low fee." I'm not sure this product is even the right kind of account for my salary pension, as I explain in point 6.
