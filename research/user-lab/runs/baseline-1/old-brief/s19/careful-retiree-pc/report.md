# Choosing a track for my retirement savings: what I made of the page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'm 61, so I want something careful and cheap. I would put my money in a **"לבני 60 ומעלה"** track (the "for people aged 60 and over" track) at one of the big companies, choosing mainly on the fee. I'd make a shortlist and phone the company before moving anything.

I searched each company's block by eye for that track and found 12 of them, which matches the "לבני 60 ומעלה 12" button. Here are the ones I wrote down:

| Company | Fee from savings / from deposits | Shares | 3 yrs / 5 yrs | Money managed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| מנורה מבטחים | 0.45% / 0.13% | 31% | 9.34% / 5.39% | ₪3.5 billion |
| מגדל | 0.49% / 0% | 31% | 9.42% / 5.45% | ₪3.2 billion |
| מיטב | 0.56% / 0.05% | 29% | 9.9% / 6.21% | ₪17 billion |
| כלל | 0.56% / 0.09% | 33% | 9.56% / 5.47% | ₪11.6 billion |
| הראל | 0.56% / 0.25% | 33% | 8.08% / 4.79% | ₪13.2 billion |
| מור | 0.66% / 0% | 34% | 8.67% / 6.08% | ₪10.3 billion |

All of these have roughly the same share of shares (about 30%), which is the level of risk I want. So the fee is what separates them. My first choices would be **מגדל** (0.49% and nothing taken from deposits) or **מיטב** (0.56%, and much bigger).

**What it means for my money:** if I have, say, ₪500,000 saved, a fee difference of 0.1% is about ₪500 a year. That's real money, but it isn't enough to panic over.

**How sure am I?** About 6 out of 10. I'm fairly sure about the *kind* of track. I'm much less sure about the company, because these fees are averages and I don't know what fee I would actually be charged.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- The introduction under **"כל המסלולים בכל החברות"** (all tracks at all companies). It told me the fees are "הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025", the average actually charged in 2025.
- The yellow warning box: "נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026", meaning the regulator's figures haven't been updated since September 2026.
- The highlighted product button **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים 182"** and the line under it: "182 מסלולים של 15 חברות · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.15% עד 2%" (182 tracks at 15 companies, fees from 0.15% to 2%).
- The **"לבני 60 ומעלה 12"** button in the **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is invested in) row.
- The big table, company by company, looking only at the "לבני 60 ומעלה" rows.
- The notes at the very bottom. These were important:
  - "אין: בסוג הקופה הזה מותר לגבות דמי ניהול רק מהחיסכון, לא מההפקדות" (none: this kind of fund may only take fees from savings, not from deposits)
  - "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך" (past returns, before fees and before tax; they don't say what will happen next)

## 3. Going down the page, block by block

- **Top menu** (מדריך, חוקים וכללים, השוואה למטרה…): skipped. I'm already where I need to be.
- **"לפי מוצר / לפי חברה" tabs** (by product / by company): skimmed. I stayed on "by product".
- **Title and introduction:** read every word. "חשיפה" (exposure) put me off a bit, but the important part was that the fee is a 2025 average.
- **"123 מקור, 123 מחושב, 123 הזנה"** (source, calculated, entered): confused me. I don't know why I'd want to know where a number came from. Skipped.
- **Yellow warning:** read it, and it worried me a little. The bottom of the page says "נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026" (data as of 3 November 2026), so which date is right?
- **Product buttons:** used the one already selected. Ignored קרן השתלמות, חיסכון לכל ילד and the rest.
- **Search box:** skipped. I don't know the track names.
- **The "what the money is invested in" buttons:** used "לבני 60 ומעלה" (60 and over). Skimmed "כללי" (general). Several confused me: "מבטיח תשואה" (guaranteed return? that sounds lovely, so why is everything next to it "לא דווח", not reported?), "הלכה יהודית" (Jewish religious law), "אג"ח ממשלות" (government bonds).
- **The sentence about the order** ("החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה... ובתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול", companies from largest to smallest, and within each company by fee): read it. It helped me understand why the cheapest track wasn't at the top.
- **The table:** used it, but only the over-60 rows. I scrolled past dozens of S&P 500, shares and IRA rows.
- **Grey company bars** (e.g. "מיטב 20 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 80 מיליארד ₪", 20 tracks, ₪80 billion managed): used them. Seeing how big a company is reassured me.
- **Bottom notes:** read every word.
- **Footer:** saw "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (a personal, non-commercial site with no marketing links). That made me trust the page a bit more.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

- **"המסלול" (track name):** looked. I also looked at the small grey labels "50-60", "עד 50" (up to 50) and "כללי".
- **"קופה" (fund) number with the little arrow** (1486, 551…): ignored. I don't know what it is.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025" (management fee, 2025 average):** the column I used most. I looked at both lines, "ההחיסכון" (from savings) and "ההפקדה" (from deposits). I ignored the little ▲ sorting arrows.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח" (share of shares and foreign currency):** I read the "מניות" (shares) number. I ignored "מט"ח" (foreign currency) because I don't know what it means for me. I also ignored the turquoise and yellow rings. The numbers were enough.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול" (average annual return before fees):** I looked at it, but carefully, because the page itself says not to rely on it. I ignored rows saying "פחות מ־3 שנים" (less than 3 years).
- **"כסף מנוהל" (money managed):** looked at it, to avoid tiny or brand-new tracks with ₪0 or ₪2 million in them.
- **"חדש" (new) labels and the orange "עוד לא דווח" (not yet reported):** I steered away from those rows.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"ההחיסכון" vs "ההפקדה"** (from savings vs from deposits). I worked it out roughly, but I'm not sure which matters more for me at 61, now that I'm hardly depositing anything.
- **Shares at 101%, 102% or 103%.** How can it be more than 100%?
- **"מט"ח"**, and whether yellow is good or bad.
- **Infinity's "לבני 60 ומעלה" with a deposit fee of "−0.21%".** A minus fee? Do they pay me? There's a warning icon and no explanation.
- **"בניהול אישי: כל חוסך מחליט במה להשקיע"** (self-managed: every saver decides what to invest in). Is that for people like me? It sounds dangerous.
- **"מבטיח תשואה"** (guaranteed return). It's tempting, but it's always "לא דווח" and I don't know whether I can even join.
- **Why some names start with "ולפיצויים..."** In the מגדל section it looks as if the beginning of the name is cut off.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **What fee I would get.** These are averages. Can I negotiate? What does a newcomer pay?
- **What happens to my money if I move** from my current company to another. Does it cost anything? Is it taxed? How long does it take?
- **Return after fees.** Do I just subtract the fee myself?
- **A bad year.** How much did the "לבני 60 ומעלה" tracks lose in their worst year? That matters more to me than the 3-year average.
- **When I can withdraw, and whether I get a lump sum or a monthly pension.**
- **Whether I'll be moved to a different track automatically** as I get older.
- **A simple summary of the over-60 tracks only, side by side.** I had to build that table myself on paper.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. All the S&P 500, "עוקב מדדים" (index tracker), shares and IRA rows. They're not for a 61-year-old.
2. The brand-new tracks with "עוד לא דווח" all over them and ₪0 managed.
3. The "123 מקור / מחושב / הזנה" line.
4. The coloured rings. The numbers next to them are enough.
5. The "מט"ח" line.

I would keep the introduction, the warning, the "לבני 60 ומעלה" button, the fee column, the money managed and the notes at the bottom. Those are what actually helped me.
