# s39: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 61 s, $0.27

## 1. My answer

**I would pick Option 3, "טבלת חברות מול סוגים".** I'm fairly sure, about 7 out of 10.

My reason is simple. I want to know which company is cheap, and Option 3 answers that in one screen. Under the heading **"כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה"** each company gets one line and each type of investment gets one column. The note says **"כתום חזק יותר הוא דמי ניהול גבוהים יותר"**, so stronger orange means higher fees. Reading across the lines:

- **הראל** (Harel) is dark almost the whole way across, around 0.54%–0.61%. It's also the biggest, at **"19.1 מיליארד ₪"**.
- **מור** (Mor) is orange almost the whole way across, around 0.67%–0.73%.

So in one look I knew Mor costs more everywhere and Harel costs less. Under the grid, Option 3 still has the full list (**"כל המסלולים"**) with returns, so I lose nothing.

What I would actually do with my money: I'd shortlist Harel, and maybe מנורה מבטחים (Menora Mivtachim) for the S&P 500 track, where it's cheapest at **"0.52%"**. Then I'd phone them before moving a shekel. I would not decide from this page alone.

I noticed the designers put a **"מומלץ"** ("recommended") badge on Option 3. That made me slightly suspicious, but I chose it for my own reasons.

Why not the others:
- **Option 1 ("החברה בראש השורה")** works if I already know I want an S&P 500 track. The heading line **"דמי ניהול מההיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.71%"** was useful. But it only compares companies within one type at a time.
- **Option 2 ("קיבוץ לפי חברה")** made me scroll through 16 Harel lines and 15 Mor lines just to see two companies. The note **"בשרטוט בלבד, כדי לקצר"** says ten more companies would follow. That's far too long for me.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"קופה" and its number.** Is it an ID? Do I need it when I call?
- **"מההפקדה" versus "מההיסכון".** Fee on deposits versus fee on savings. Menora shows **"0.01%"** and אינפיניטי (Infinity) **"0.05%"** on deposits. Is that something I'd actually pay?
- **"חשיפה", "מט"ח" and the rings.** Is 99% stocks dangerous at my age?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים".** I think it means the track is too new to have a 3-year figure, but I'm guessing.
- **"עוד לא דווח"** for איילון (Ayalon). Does that mean they hide their fees?
- **The types "גמיש", "קיימות" and "הלכה יהודי".** What is "גמיש" (flexible)? Is "קיימות" (sustainability) green investing?
- **Why returns are shown "לפני דמי ניהול".** So what did I actually earn? Do I have to subtract the fee myself?
- **The dotted underlines** under the numbers. Can I click them?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Fees in shekels.** What does 0.56% versus 0.73% mean on, say, 300,000 ₪? I think it's about 500 ₪ a year, but the page should tell me.
- **Returns after fees**, and the last 1 year.
- **Whether the fee is negotiable.** "Average charged" suggests some people pay less.
- **Tax and withdrawal rules at my age.** What happens at 60? Can I take the money out, and with what tax?
- **How to move my money**, how long it takes, and whether there's a cost.
- **A safer, low-stock option for someone 61**, and which type that is.
- **Service, and how reliable each company is.**
