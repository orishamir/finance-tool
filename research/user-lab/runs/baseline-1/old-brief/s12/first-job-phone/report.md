# s12: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 49 s, $0.22

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would say yes to the keren hishtalmut at work. I'd put in my 2.5% and let my employer put in their 7.5%. I'd treat it as money I don't touch for 6 years.

What convinced me were two lines near the top. The first is under "מה זה": "6 שנים אחרי התשלום הראשון אפשר למשוך את הכסף, והרווחים... פטורים ממס". The second is "הטבת המס חלה על הפקדות של עד 7.5% מהשכר מהמעסיק ועד 2.5% מכם". On my salary of about ₪12,000 that's about ₪900 a month from my employer for ₪300 from me. Later the page says "הכסף של המעסיק לא עומד לבחירה ולכן הוא מחוץ להשוואה", so I get that money only if I join.

**How sure:** about 80% that I should join. I'm much less sure what to pick inside it. I don't know which company, or whether to choose "S&P 500 מדד" or "מסלול כללי".

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **My actual number.** With my employer's 7.5% included, roughly how much would I have after 6 years? The page deliberately leaves that money out, but that's the number I care about most.
- **Can I say no, or is it automatic?** It says no law forces employers to offer it, but not whether I can opt out once it's offered.
- **What happens if I change jobs** before 6 years: does the clock keep running?
- **Which track to pick at 24**: S&P 500 or כללי. The page lists them but doesn't help me choose.
- **After 6 years, should I take the money out or leave it?** I gather taking it out restarts the clock, but not whether leaving it is smart.
