# What I made of this page, as a 61-year-old who wants to put money in the S&P 500 every month

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I left the example as it was: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, for 10 years, at 7% a year. That is 70,000 ₪ of my own money in total.

- **What I'd get back:** the page says the pair it picked for me, "אינטראקטיב ישראל" with "iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating)", leaves me **84,184 ₪**. That is after fees and tax, "בכסף של היום".
- **The cheapest pair:** "★ הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם" is **86,492 ₪**, which is "+2,308 ₪ יותר אם עוברים לזוג הזה". Lower down I learn this means Psagot with "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)".
- **Total costs:** 9,184 ₪ with my pair and 6,876 ₪ with Psagot.

**What I'd do:** I would lean towards Psagot with the Yelin Lapidot fund, but I would not sign up straight from this page. First I would phone Psagot to confirm the fees and that I can set up a monthly standing order.

**How sure I am:** about 6 out of 10. My doubts:
- The difference is about 230 ₪ a year. That is real money, but not a reason to rush.
- The Yelin fund shows "בשנה · שנה אחת", so it has only one year of history.
- Lower down it is written "(D4)" and higher up "(4D)". I'm not sure it's the same fund.
- The page itself says that for 6 firms, "מה שכתוב בפרסום על עמלה לא תואם את מה שכתוב במסמכים". Psagot is one of them, with "3 טענות".
- My default choice, Interactive Israel, has a label "לא בפיקוח רשות ני"ע". That scared me away from it more than the 2,308 ₪ did.

**The most useful thing I learned:** staying invested 2 more years adds 18,436 ₪, and 100 ₪ more a month adds 14,242 ₪. Both are much more than the 2,308 ₪ I'd save by picking the cheapest firm.

## 3. Block by block

1. **Title and index buttons:** used. Clear.
2. **Sliders:** used. The strip below them confused me a little: "בכסף של היום · שער הדולר 3.16 ₪ · מחושב משער הייצוג של הבנק המרכזי האירופי". Why does the European central bank matter to me? And what does the "אינפלציה" button do? Is the 7% before inflation or after?

6. **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500" (long list with coloured labels):** mostly confused me. There are lots of labels, and each fund shows "אין מחיר כאן" or "עוד לא נאסף". I only looked for the Yelin fund, and saw "+0.37% בשנה · שנה אחת".

11. **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל":** skimmed. Too much for me. I took two things from it. One is the sentence "הנטרול לא מגן מירידה של השוק". The other is that by their example, the fund without hedging leaves 7,152 ₪ more.
