# Using the page as a 24-year-old who has heard "invest in the S&P 500"

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and put 500 ₪ a month into the fund called **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**. I took that from the box at the top, **"★ הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם"**. It says I'd end up with **86,492 ₪**, which is **"+2,308 ₪ יותר אם עוברים לזול הזה"**. The section near the bottom says the same thing: **"לעבור אל ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות"**.

The money part I understood. If I put in **70,000 ₪** over 10 years, I'd have about 84,000–86,000 ₪ left "בכסף של היום" (in today's money). That assumes the 7% a year the page filled in for me.

**How sure: about 60%.** Three things bother me:
- In the list of accounts on the right, פסגות shows **"אין נתון"** (no data). Yet the page also calls it the cheapest. I don't understand how both can be true.
- The "פרסום מול התעריפון" box says Psagot's ad claims "פטור מלא מדמי ניהול" (no management fees at all). The fee list says **"דמי משמרת 0.12% גבייה רבעונית 0.03%"**. So I'm not sure I'd really pay what the page shows.
- I'm 24, so I'd want to see 30 years, not 10. I'd move the "שנים" slider, but I don't know if the winner would stay the same.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

- **Cost bars:** I read the totals (9,184 / 6,876) and the big pink part (7,483 / 5,703). I took the pink to be the tax. I ignored the small green, orange and yellow pieces and their legend.
- **Account list:** I looked only at the big number and the bank name. I ignored the small number under it (1,298, 2,749…), the little coloured strips, and the "לא בחשבון" text.
- **Fund list:** I looked at the fund names. I didn't understand the column **"מול המדד"** (against the index; +0.37%, −0.43%). I ignored the tags (צוברת, חשיפה לדולר, מס גם על שינוי המטבע…). I saw "אין מחיר כאן" (no price here) on almost every Israeli fund and didn't know why.
- **"מה הכי משנה" table:** I read only the **"ההפרש"** (difference) column. I ignored the yellow, orange, pink and blue lines.
- **Hedging charts:** I ignored them completely.
- **Last tables:** I read "נשאר בסוף" (left at the end) and the red **"פחות"** (less) bars only to confirm that the others are worse.
