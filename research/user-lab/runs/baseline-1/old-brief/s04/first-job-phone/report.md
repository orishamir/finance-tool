# s04: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 36 s, $0.16

I'm writing this as the 24-year-old. One note first: screen-04 is just a thin black strip with nothing in it. Screen-01 is the home page, and screens 02–03 are the "מדריך" (guide) page I ended up on.

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

The thing I've heard of is "invest in the S&P 500," so that's what I wanted explained. On the home page I clicked the first box, **"להבין סוג של חיסכון"** ("understand a type of saving"), because its subtitle **"מה זה, כמה זה עולה ואיך זה ממוסה"** ("what it is, what it costs, how it's taxed") is exactly what I wanted. That took me to the guide. I scrolled until I found the card **"מדד S&P 500"** and I would click it to read more.

I'm **fairly sure (about 70%) that I found the right thing**. I'm **not at all sure what to actually do next**. The guide told me that S&P 500 is only "what the money is invested in" and that I also need a separate "where the money is held." I don't know which one of those to pick for myself.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- Home page: the box **"להבין סוג של חיסכון"**.
- Guide: the intro block **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** ("two parts in every saving").
- Guide: **"מאיפה להתחיל"** ("where to start").
- Guide: the section **"במה הכסף מושקע"** ("what the money is invested in"), and only the **"מדד S&P 500"** card in it.

## 3. Block by block

**Home page (screen-01)**
- **Top menu**: I skimmed it. **"השקעה במדד"** ("investing in an index") caught my eye and I almost clicked it. It might have been the better door for me.
- **"מקור / להבין חיסכון והשקעה בישראל, עם מקור לכל מספר"**: I read it. It told me what the site is and nothing more.
- **"מה השאלה שלכם?"** form (chips like דירה / חתונה / פרישה, then "יש לכם היום ₪", "ובכל חודש ₪", "כמה שנים"): I skipped it. I don't have a goal yet, I just wanted an explanation. "בלי מטרה מסוימת" ("no specific goal") was the closest chip to me, but I didn't want to type in numbers before I understand anything.
- **Search box "מה ראיתם באפליקציה, בפרסומת או בדוח?"** ("what did you see in an app, an ad or a report?"): I noticed it. Typing "S&P 500" there would probably have been faster. I didn't, because I wasn't sure it would understand English.
- **"להבין סוג של חיסכון"**: I used it (see above).
- **"מה חברה מציעה לי"**, **"כסף שיושב בעו"ש"**, **"מה כבר יש לכם"**: skimmed and skipped. None of them is my question.
- **"שאלות שכולם שואלים"** (tax on stock market profit, etc.): skimmed. "כמה מס משלמים על רווח בבורסה" ("how much tax on stock market profit") is interesting for later.
- Small links and footer: skipped.

**Guide page (screens 02–03)**
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"**: I read it carefully. It was the most useful thing on the page, and also where I got confused. "איפה הכסף מוחזק" ("where the money is held") versus "במה הכסף מושקע" ("what it's invested in") was news to me. I had thought S&P 500 *is* the saving.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל"**: I read it. It gives three short lines with a link each: **"קרן השתלמות"**, **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"**, **"קרן כספית"**. Neither "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם" ("employees whose employer deposits for them") nor "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר" ("the basis of every salaried saver") tells me how they relate to S&P 500.
- **"איפה הכסף מוחזק"**, all of its subsections (כסף שוטף וקצר, חיסכון עם הטבת מס, פרישה, לילדים, לבחירה עצמית): I skimmed the headings and skipped most of the cards. There are too many, and the names all sound alike to me (קרן כספית, קרן כספית מתחדשת, קופת גמל להשקעה, קרן השתלמות בניהול אישי...). The one I paused on was **"חשבון ניירות ערך"** ("securities account"): "שבו קונים, מוכרים ומחזיקים מניות, קרנות ואגרות חוב" ("where you buy, sell and hold shares, funds and bonds"). That sounds like where you'd "buy the S&P," but I'm guessing.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע"**: I scanned it looking for "S&P" and found **"מדד S&P 500"**. I read that card and skipped the rest (MSCI, נאסד"ק, ת"א-35, אג"ח...).
- **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** ("what the site doesn't cover yet"): skipped.

## 4. Lists, tables, charts

There are no tables or charts, only grids of cards. On the cards I read the **bold title** and sometimes the first line of grey text. I used the **small grey grouping headings** (כסף שוטף וקצר, פרישה, לילדים) to decide what to skip. I noticed the **"בשפה פשוטה:"** ("in simple words") line on the retirement cards and liked it, but most cards don't have one. I ignored the **arrows (›)**. The **turquoise links** in "מאיפה להתחיל" stood out as the clickable things.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- In the S&P card, **"במשקל לפי שווי השוק הסחיר"** ("weighted by tradable market value"): no idea what that means.
- **"הכסף מושקע בדולרים דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו"** ("money is invested in dollars through a fund that tracks it"): which fund? Do I buy it myself? Is dollars a good thing or a risk?
- **"חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו"** ("dollar exposure or neutralised", on other cards): lost me.
- **"מק"מ"**, **"אג"ח"**, **"צמודה"**, **"ניהול אישי"**, **"מסלול"**: words I don't know.
- I don't understand how "where it's held" and "what it's invested in" connect. Can I put S&P 500 inside a קרן השתלמות? Inside a חשבון ניירות ערך? Both?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- A plain answer to **"I want to invest in the S&P 500, how do I actually do it?"**
- **Is it risky? Can I lose money?** Nothing on this page says so.
- **How much does it cost and how is it taxed?** The home box promised "כמה זה עולה ואיך זה ממוסה," but the guide page shows none of that. It may be inside the card, which I didn't get to see.
- **How much should I start with?** Can I put in a little each month?
- Which "where it's held" option suits **someone like me** (24, salaried, no specific goal)?

## 7. If half the page had to go

**Guide page:** first to go are most of the **"איפה הכסף מוחזק"** cards: the children's section, the retirement section, קרן כספית מתחדשת, מק"מ (which also appears twice), and the "ניהול אישי" ones. Next are most of the **index cards** I don't need (MSCI AC World vs MSCI World, ת"א-90, the two government bond cards) and "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה."

**Home page:** the **"מה השאלה שלכם?" form** goes before anything else.

What I'd keep: "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון," "מאיפה להתחיל," and the S&P 500 card, plus a single line connecting them.
