# s04: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 30 s, $0.15

# What I did on "מקור" and what I took away

I've heard about **קרן השתלמות** (a tax-advantaged savings fund) for years from friends and my brother-in-law. I came here to find out what it actually is.

## 1. My answer, and how sure I am

On the home page I clicked the first box on the left, **"להבין סוג של חיסכון – מה זה, כמה זה עולה ואיך זה ממוסה"** ("understand a type of saving – what it is, what it costs, how it's taxed"). It was the only one that matched why I came. It took me to a page called **"מדריך"** ("guide").

There I found two short lines about my fund:
- Under **"מאיפה להתחיל"** ("where to start"): **"שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם: קרן השתלמות"** ("employees whose employer pays in: קרן השתלמות").
- On its card: **"חיסכון שהמעסיק ואתם מפקידים אליו מהשכר, או עצמאים בעצמם; אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס"** ("savings that you and your employer pay into from your salary, or self-employed people pay in themselves; after a period set by law you can withdraw it tax-free").

What I'd do: I'd click into that card to read more, and I'd ask HR whether I have one. I'm fairly sure I now know roughly what it is: my employer and I put money in, and after a while I can take it out without tax. I'm **not** sure what it means for my money. "תקופה שקבע החוק" ("a period set by law") doesn't say how long. At 61 that's the first thing I need to know. The page also promised to tell me "כמה זה עולה" ("what it costs"), and nowhere on this page did I see a cost.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **Home page:** the box **"להבין סוג של חיסכון"**.
- **Guide page:** the opening line under **"מדריך"**, the block **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** ("two parts of every saving"), **"מאיפה להתחיל"**, and the **"קרן השתלמות"** card under **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"** ("saving with a tax benefit").

## 3. Block by block

**Home page (screen 1)**
- **Top menu.** I skimmed it. "השקעה במדד" ("investing in an index") and "פרמטרים" ("parameters") mean nothing to me.
- **"מקור – להבין חיסכון והשקעה בישראל, עם מקור לכל מספר"** ("understanding saving and investing in Israel, with a source for every number"). I read it. "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" ("a personal, non-commercial site, no marketing links") reassured me. "הכול נשמר במכשיר שלכם" ("everything stays on your device") I didn't fully understand, but it sounded safe.
- **"מה השאלה שלכם?" form** ("what's your question?"). It confused me and I left it. It wants "יש לכם היום ₪" ("how much you have today"), "ובכל חודש" ("and each month") and "כמה שנים" ("how many years"). I didn't want to type my money into something before I understood it. I saw "קרן השתלמות" among the buttons under "יש לכם כבר…" ("you already have…"), but I didn't know what pressing it would do.
- **"מה כבר יש לכם"** ("what you already have"). I skipped it, and "הזינו מהדוח השנתי" ("enter from your annual statement") put me off. **"כסף שיושב בעו"ש"** ("money sitting in a current account") I only skimmed.
- **The search box: "מה ראיתם באפליקציה…"** ("what did you see in the app…"). I skipped it. I didn't see anything in an app.
- **"שאלות שכולם שואלים"** ("questions everyone asks"). I skimmed it. "עוברים מסלול בתוך הקופה: משלמים מס או עמלה?" ("switching track inside the fund: do you pay tax or a fee?") caught my eye, but "מסלול" ("track") is jargon to me.

**Guide page (screens 2–3)**
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון".** I read it twice. "איפה הכסף מוחזק" ("where the money is held") versus "במה הכסף מושקע" ("what the money is invested in") made some sense. But "מזומן, אג"ח, מדד מניות" ("cash, bonds, a stock index") lost me.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל".** This was the most useful part. Three lines, and mine was one of them.
- **"כסף שוטף וקצר"** ("everyday and short-term money"). I skimmed it. "פיקדון" (bank deposit) and "תכנית חיסכון" (savings plan) I know from the bank. The rest I passed over.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס".** I used it, for my card.
- **"פרישה"** ("retirement"). I skimmed it, because at my age it matters. But "קרן חדשה מקיפה" and "קרן חדשה כללית" (two kinds of pension fund) look the same to me, and I couldn't tell which one I have.
- **"לילדים"** ("for children"). I skipped it, since my children are grown.
- **"לבחירה עצמית"** ("your own choice"). I skimmed it. "בניהול אישי" ("personally managed") sounds like something for someone more expert than me.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע".** I skipped it and it confused me. S&P 500, MSCI World, "נאסד"ק 100", "ת"א-125". It's a wall of English names and numbers. "חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו" ("exposed to the dollar or hedged against it") is exactly the kind of language I distrust.
- **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** ("what the site doesn't cover yet"). I didn't open it. Still, it's honest that they say so.

## 4. Lists and charts

There were no tables or charts, only cards. On each card I read the bold title and the first line, and the "בשפה פשוטה:" ("in plain words:") line where there was one. I wish every card had one. I ignored the little arrows and the English index names. The turquoise colour told me what was clickable, and that's all it meant to me.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "תקופה שקבע החוק" ("a period set by law"): how many years?
- "מסלול" ("track") and "קופה" ("fund"), which appear everywhere.
- "אג"ח צמודה / לא צמודה" (bonds that are linked or not linked to the index), "קופון", "קרן שעוקבת אחריו" ("a fund that tracks it").
- The difference between "קרן חדשה מקיפה" and "קרן חדשה כללית".
- What the "מה הכי מתאים" button ("what suits best") on the home page would do with my numbers.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- How much a קרן השתלמות costs me in fees. The home page promised "כמה זה עולה" and I didn't see it.
- Whether it's still worth it at 61, and when I can withdraw.
- How I find out whether I already have one and where it is.
- What happens to the money if I stop working.

## 7. If half the page had to go

On the guide page, **"במה הכסף מושקע"** goes first: all the indexes and bonds. After that, **"לבחירה עצמית"** and **"לילדים"**. I'd keep "מאיפה להתחיל" and the cards with "בשפה פשוטה". On the home page, the money form could go further down. It came before I understood anything.
