pc; 44 s, $0.16

# Speaking as a 61-year-old salaried worker

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would open the keren hishtalmut**, as long as my employer really pays into it. I'm about 80% sure.

The page convinced me that most of the gain is the employer's money, not some investment trick. The table in option א says it plainly:

- **"הפקדת המעסיק"**: ₪54,000 in the fund, **"אין"** in the securities account.
- After 5 years, **"נשאר בידיים"** is ₪66,513 in the fund against ₪19,595 in the securities account.
- That's with tax already taken off: **"מס במשיכה −13,974 ₪"**.

So even if I take the money out early and pay tax, I'm ahead. If I wait 6 years it's ₪98,738 against ₪23,973.

The other 20% is doubt because I'm 61:

- Six years takes me to 67. I don't know whether retirement changes the rules, and the page doesn't say.
- Everything assumes **"תשואה 7% בשנה"** (7% a year). At my age a bad year hurts more.
- The **"+1,721 ₪"** card warned me that without the employer's money, the fund is barely better: **"אחרי 5 שנים הקרן דווקא 394 ₪ פחות"** (after 5 years the fund actually has ₪394 less). So everything depends on the employer.

**The option I'd rather see: א, "מגדלי מטבעות"** (the coin towers), together with its table. The designers marked it **"מומלץ"** (recommended). I noticed that, but I chose it because the table answered my question in shekels, row by row.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The three cards at the top, especially **"הכסף של המעסיק"** (the employer's money) and **"הכסף שלכם לבד, בלי המעסיק"** (your money alone, without the employer).
- The small grey example line: **"שכר ברוטו 12,000 ₪ בחודש… אתם 2.5% והמעסיק 7.5%"** (gross salary ₪12,000 a month, you pay 2.5%, the employer pays 7.5%).
- In option א: the two big totals, the table under them, and the second copy for year 6.
- The summary numbers under option ג: **"אחרי 5 שנים, נשאר בידיים 66,513"**, **"מתוך זה הפקדתם 18,000"** and **"אותו כסף בחשבון ניירות ערך 19,595"**.

## 3. Block by block

- **Title and subtitle.** I skimmed them. **"כל הציורים חיים: אפשר לגרור את השנים"** says you can drag the years, but in the pictures I had, nothing moved.
- **Card "הכסף של המעסיק +900 ₪ בחודש".** Used it. **"על כל שקל שלכם נכנסים עוד 3 שקלים"** (for every shekel of yours, three more go in) is the sentence that sold me.
- **Card "הקפיצה בשנה 6 +32,225 ₪"** (the jump in year 6). Used it. It told me waiting one more year is worth about ₪32,000, and I would wait.
- **Card "+1,721 ₪".** I used it, but read it twice. I first thought it meant I gain only ₪1,721, then understood it's the case where the employer pays nothing.
- **The grey example and warning text.** Read every word, because it holds the assumptions. The fund names went past me: **"מיטב השתלמות עוקב מדד S&P500 (13245)"** and **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**.
- **א, the coin towers.**
  - The bar strip of years 1–12: I skimmed it. I understood the break at **"מ-6 שנים: פטור ממס"** (from 6 years: tax-free).
  - The coins: I looked, but I didn't count them.
  - The table: used it fully.
  - The second copy, **"אותו ציור שנה אחת אחר כך"** (the same picture one year later): used it for 98,738.
- **ב, "מדרגות"** (the steps). Confused me.
  - **"אותו כסף עובר לקרן +527"** (the same money moves to the fund): why only 527?
  - **"הכסף של המעסיק +60,365"**: the table said 54,000.
  - I suppose growth is mixed in, but nothing explains it.
- **ג, "כל השנים בעמודות"** (all the years in columns). I skimmed the bars. I used the three numbers under the chart, which repeat what I already knew.

## 4. Inside the charts and tables

- **Table in א.** I read every row, in both columns:
  - **"ההפקדה שלכם"** (your deposit)
  - **"הפקדת המעסיק"** (the employer's deposit)
  - **"רווח אחרי דמי ניהול"** (profit after management fees)
  - **"מס במשיכה"** (tax on withdrawal)
  - **"נשאר בידיים"** (what's left in your hands)

  The bottom row mattered most.
- **Colours.** Yellow is the employer, grey is me, the pink stripes are tax. Once I knew the colours, the year-6 picture showed me the stripes disappear in the fund. I paid no attention to green versus grey inside the coins.
- **The footnote under the table.** I glanced at **"66,280 ₪ בדמי ניהול של 0.7% עד 67,028 ₪ בדמי ניהול של 0.19%"**. Fees change the result by under ₪1,000, so I stopped worrying about which fund.
- **Chart ג.**
  - The white line (the securities account): I noticed it is low.
  - The y-axis numbers and the year columns after 6: ignored.
  - **"שנים עד המשיכה"** (years until withdrawal): read it.
- **Waterfall ב.** I read the five top numbers but couldn't make them add up in my head.
- **"עצמאים" tab** (self-employed). Ignored it, I'm salaried.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה 3.70 ₪ בקרן"** (every shekel you deposited becomes ₪3.70 in the fund). It sounds like a fantastic return, but it's mostly the employer's money. It felt a little like a sales line.
- Why the tax in the fund (13,974) is so much bigger than in the securities account (594). Is the employer's money taxed like salary?
- **"עד תקרה בחוק"** (up to a ceiling set by law): what is the ceiling?
- The +527 and +60,365 in ב.
- **"פייר פיננשיאל טכנולוג'יז בע"מ"** and the fund codes in brackets.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- What happens at my age. Can I withdraw at retirement before 6 years, without tax?
- What happens if the market falls in the year I need the money.
- Whether I can stop, or move the money, if I change jobs.
- The legal ceiling in shekels.
- How to check what my own employer pays.

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. **ב "מדרגות" goes first.** It confused me.
2. Then the big column chart **ג**. Its useful numbers already appear elsewhere.
3. Then the year-1-to-12 bar strips inside א.

I'd keep the three top cards, the example and warning line, and the coins with their table, for both year 5 and year 6.
