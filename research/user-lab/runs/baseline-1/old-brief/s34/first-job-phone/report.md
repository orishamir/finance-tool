# s34: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 46 s, $0.16

**My pick: Option A, "א. מגדלי מטבעות".** I didn't pick it for the coins. I picked it for the one sentence under the bars and the small table under the coins. The designers marked it "מומלץ", but I'd have chosen it anyway.

## 1. My answer
I'd open a keren hishtalmut and not put that money in a plain securities account. I'm about 85% sure.

What decided it was "כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה ₪3.70 בקרן ו-₪1.09 בחשבון ניירות ערך". Even if I take the money out early at year 5 and pay tax, I'd have 66,513 against 19,595. That gap is too big for me to argue with.

I'm not 100% sure because of "⚠ הנחה: אתם 2.5% והמעסיק 7.5%… האחוזים האמיתיים כתובים בהסכם העבודה". I don't know what my employer actually puts in, so I'd have to check my contract first. The whole answer depends on the "+900 ₪ בחודש" from the employer.

## 2. What I actually used
- The first card on the right, "הכסף של המעסיק", showing "+900 ₪ בחודש". This was the main reason.
- The sentence "כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה…" under the bars in A.
- The two big numbers in A: "66,513 ₪ נשאר בידיים" against "19,595 ₪".
- The table under A, mainly the "הפקדת המעסיק" row ("54,000" against "אין") and the "נשאר בידיים" row.
- The orange warning line in the fine print.

## 3. Block by block
- **Title and subtitle:** skimmed. "שרטוט לבחירה" told me nothing.
- **Card "הכסף של המעסיק" (+900):** used. It's clear: my 300 becomes 1,200.
- **Card "הקפיצה בשנה 6" (+32,225):** used. It told me waiting until year 6 is worth a lot.
- **Card "הכסף שלכם לבד, בלי המעסיק" (+1,721):** confused me. It says "אחרי 5 שנים הקרן דווקא ₪394 פחות". Then in B, "אותו כסף עובר לקרן" shows "+527". Is it 394 less or 527 more? I gave up on it.
- **Small grey fine print:** skimmed. I only caught "7% בשנה", "S&P500" (which I recognise) and the orange warning. I skipped "דמי ניהול 0.54%", "מחקה", "4D" and the long number codes.
- **"שכירים / עצמאים" toggle:** I'm salaried and it was already on, so I left it alone.
- **A, small bars for years 1–12:** I took away only one thing: before 6 means tax, from 6 means no tax ("לפני 6 שנים: מס" / "מ-6 שנים: פטור ממס"). I didn't try dragging the years.
- **A, coin towers:** I looked at which pile is bigger and read the big numbers. I didn't count coins. "מטבע אחד הוא ₪1,000… מטבע מקווקו הולך למס" helped me get the striped ones.
- **A, table:** used. This is where I actually checked the numbers.
- **The line under the table about "19 המסלולים":** skimmed. What I took from it is that fees change the result only a little (66,280 to 67,028), so I stopped worrying about them.
- **A again for year 6 (98,738 against 23,973):** skimmed. It's the same picture with bigger numbers. Useful, but I'd already decided.
- **B, "מדרגות":** partly confused me. The row "19,595 / +527 / +60,365 / −13,974 / 66,513" runs left to right. I read Hebrew from the right, so I hit the end result first and the steps backwards. Once I got it, "הכסף של המעסיק" was clearly the big yellow step.
- **C, "כל השנים בעמודות":** skimmed. It's a pretty picture but too much going on. I only noticed the white line staying far below the bars.

## 4. Charts and tables
- **A table:** I read the "קרן השתלמות" and "חשבון ניירות ערך" columns, and the rows "הפקדת המעסיק" and "נשאר בידיים". I glanced at "רווח אחרי דמי ניהול". I looked at "מס במשיכה" only to see it was negative and fairly big in the fund (−13,974), but still worth it.
- **Colours:** Yellow (employer) caught my eye most. I understood that grey is my own money and pink stripes are tax. I barely noticed teal (profit).
- **Small year bars:** I only noticed the dashed line between 5 and 6 and the pink and grey labels. I ignored the bar heights.
- **B:** I looked at the yellow block and the final teal block. I ignored the y-axis (50,000, 100,000 and so on).
- **C:** I looked at the legend, the white line ("אותם 300 ₪ בחודש בחשבון ניירות ערך") and the three numbers at the bottom. I ignored the axis numbers and the hatched tops.
