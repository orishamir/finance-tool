# s31: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 46 s, $0.17

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd open an account at **פסגות** ("פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ (פסגות טרייד)"). It's #1 with the star, "★ הזול", and the page says it charges "1,058 ₪" over 10 years. **אינטראקטיב ישראל** is close at "1,303 ₪", and everyone else costs a lot more, up to about 26,000 ₪.

I'm only about 60% sure. I'm fairly confident about where to open the account. I'm not sure about the "how" part, because the page told me straight out: "האתר לא אסף קרנות על test". It also says "בלי רשומת קרן האתר לא מנחש דמי ניהול". So the full cost, including the fund's own fee, is missing. I don't know which fund to buy, and I don't know if the order would change once that fee is in.

I'm also 24, and לאומי, הפועלים and others say "מחיר אחר לצעירים", marked "לא בחישוב". One of them might be cheaper for me, and I can't tell.

- The title "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן", and the index buttons where my "test" is selected.
- The four boxes: סכום היום 10,000, הפקדה חודשית 500, שנים 10, תשואה בשנה 7%. I left them alone.
- The box "כמה נשאר בסוף תלוי בקרן".
- The two cards: "חשבון ניירות ערך – פסגות" on the right and "קרן – עוד לא נבחרה קרן" on the left.
- The ranked list "כל החשבונות", mostly the top 5.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** (מדריך, חברות ומסלולים…): skipped.
- **Title and subtitle**: used. "בוחרים חשבון ניירות ערך וקרן" told me I need to pick two things.
- **Index buttons**: used, since my "test" is selected. The little "זר" badge next to "test" and "אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ" confused me. I don't know what a "קרן חוץ" is or whether I should want one. I ignored "מדד שהוספתם, שמור במכשיר הזה · שינוי התשואה".
- **Sliders**: glanced at them and left the defaults. I didn't press "אינפלציה". I skimmed the line about "שער הדולר 3.16 ₪ … הבנק המרכזי האירופי" and didn't care.
- **"כמה נשאר בסוף תלוי בקרן"**: read it. It explained why there's no final number, and I got that the list is ranked only by "מה שהם גובים". I didn't press "+ הוספת קרן שלא ברשימה" because I don't know any fund to add. That's exactly my problem.
- **Fund card (left)**: read it and got stuck. "את דמי הניהול של קרן אפשר למצוא במאיה" means nothing to me, because I don't know what מאיה is. "בקשו שנאסוף" sounded like the useful option, but I didn't know what happens if I press it.
- **Account card (right)**: used. פסגות and 1,058 ₪. I skipped "התעריפון ↗" and "קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו", since I haven't gotten any price.
- **The paragraph above the list** ("קנייה חודשית מחושבת כהוראת קבע: 3 מתוך 15…"): tried to read it and gave up halfway. Too dense.
- **The list**: used the top rows and skimmed the rest. Below row 10 the numbers are so much higher that I stopped caring.
- **"+ הוספת חשבון בתנאים שקיבלתם"**: skipped.
- **Colour legend**: read it once to understand the bars.
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, מול התעריפון"**: skimmed. I got the idea that ads don't match the real price list, and the פסגות example ("פטור מלא מדמי ניהול ללא הגבלת זמן") made me slightly worried about my #1 pick. I didn't click the "3 טענות" links.
- **Small print at the bottom**: skimmed. "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־4 מקומות" made me a bit less sure of the ranking. "קרנות בחו"ל … מחכות למחיר היחידה של הקרן, שעוד לא נאסף" sounded like something is still missing from the numbers. "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד": fine.
- **Footer**: skipped.

## 4. The list and bars

- **What I looked at:** the rank number, the bank name, the big ₪ number on the left, and the star "★ הזול". I also read the grey "לא בחישוב" notes, because of the "צעירים" price.
- **The bars:** I looked at their length and at the two big colours. Orange is "עמלות קנייה ומכירה" and yellow is "דמי משמרת". I noticed the red "דמי ניהול חשבון" takes over at אלטשולר, מיטב and אקסלנס, and that Interactive Brokers has pink for "המרת מטבע".
- **What I ignored:** the grey "העמלות זו על זו", the "// הפס נחתך" note, the radio circles on the right, the small arrows (˅), and the dotted underline under the numbers. I didn't hover or click any of them.
- **Labels I noticed but didn't understand:** "קרן מחקה", "קרן סל", "בחו"ל" and "מחיר להוראת קבע".

## 5. What I didn't understand

- What a "קרן מחקה" is, what a "קרן סל" is, and how each differs from "בחו"ל". Which one am I supposed to buy?
- "דמי משמרת" (I'm paying someone to "guard" something?), and "העמלות זו על זו".
- "קרן חוץ" and the "זר" badge.
- "מאיה".
- Whether 1,058 ₪ is the total over 10 years or per year. It's measured against which amount: my 10,000, or everything I put in?
- Why "פסגות" says "קרן מחקה" and the star sits next to it. Is that the only kind of fund I can buy there?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **The actual fund to buy for "test", and its fee.** The page openly says it doesn't have this.
- **My own price as a 24-year-old.** "צעירים" up to what age? Would לאומי or הפועלים beat פסגות for me?
- **The final amount I'd end up with.** It says it will show this only once I add a fund.
- **How to actually open the account,** or a simple "step 1, step 2".

## 7. If half the page had to go

Going first: the "מה כתוב בפרסום, מול התעריפון" block and its list of "טענות", then the small print at the bottom. After that, the inflation and dollar-rate line, the dense paragraph above the list, and rows 11–17. Nobody picks those anyway.

I'd keep the title, my index buttons, the two cards, and the top of the list with the star.
