# Finding the cheapest way to invest in my own index, "test"

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I wouldn't open an account yet. If I had to pick today, Pesagot (פסגות) looks cheapest, at **"₪1,058"** in account fees over 10 years. Interactive Israel (אינטראקטיב ישראל) is a close second at ₪1,303. I'm only about 40% sure, for three reasons:

- **The fund isn't counted.** The page tells me so near the top: **"האתר לא אסף קרנות על test"**. On the right of that section it says **"עוד לא נבחרה קרן · בלי רשומת קרן האתר לא מנחש דמי ניהול"**. So the ₪1,058 is only what the bank or investment house charges. The fund's own yearly fee still has to be added, and that could change the order.
- **Pesagot's advert and its price list disagree.** The section at the bottom says the advert promises **"פטור מלא מדמי ניהול ללא הגבלת זמן"**, but the price list shows **"דמי משמרת 0.12% גבייה רבעונית 0.03%"**. The page used the price list, which I prefer. It still makes me want to phone them and get the price in writing.
- **"test" is a foreign index.** The page says **"אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ"**. A note at the bottom says foreign funds priced per share **"מחכות למחיר היחידה של הקרן, שעוד לא נאסף"**. If the cheapest route is a fund abroad, Interactive Brokers' ₪2,759 may not be the full story.

My next step would be to click **"חיפוש במאיה"** (the stock exchange's search) and find funds that track my index. I'd add them with **"+ הוספת קרן שלא ברשימה"** and look at the ranking again.

## 4. The ranking and its colours

- **What I looked at:** the rank number, the name, the small type (**"קרן מחקה"** = tracker fund, **"בחו"ל"** = abroad), the shekel total, and the **"★ הזול"** (cheapest) star next to Pesagot.
- **Colours I followed:**
  - orange = **"עמלות קנייה ומכירה"** (fees to buy and sell)
  - yellow = **"דמי משמרת"** (custody fee)
  - red = **"דמי ניהול חשבון"** (account fee)
  - pink = **"המרת מטבע"** (currency exchange), which only Interactive Brokers has
- **Colours I didn't follow:** the grey one, labelled **"העמלות זו על"**. I don't know what it means.
- **Bars I didn't understand:** the slanted cut marks. The key says **"הפס נחתך: הסכום ארוך מהשטח"**, but I'd rather just see the number.
- **What I ignored:** the radio buttons on the left, the arrows that open the details, and the dotted underline under the amounts.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- What **"מחיר להוראת קבע"** means under some of the names.
- What **"סגור למצטרפים"** means. I take it to mean closed to new customers.
- Whether the ₪1,058 is the fees I pay, or money I lose from my final amount including lost growth.
- Why the European bank's dollar rate is used.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **The final total:** what I'd actually have after 10 years, after all fees, in one number.
- **Fund costs for my index:** what a typical tracker fund or a foreign fund charges.
- **Safety:** whether my money is protected if one of these firms gets into trouble.
- **Tax:** how buying through a fund abroad affects my tax.
- **Phone or branch service:** whether the cheap firms offer it or are app-only. That matters to me.
