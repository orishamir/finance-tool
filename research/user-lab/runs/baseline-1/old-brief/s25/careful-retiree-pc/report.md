# Me, 61, savings with Harel, going through this page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would not move any money today. First I'd take out my annual statement and find my fund number. Then I'd look for that number in the **"קופה"** column and see what my own track charges and how it has done.

From the page I got a few things:

- **Harel has a lot of tracks.** The page says "96 מסלולים ב־7 מוצרים" (96 tracks in 7 products), and "כל המוצרים 105" (all products, 105) once the index funds are added.
- **Pension fees look the same across tracks.** In the pension fund ("קרן חדשה מקיפה") every track shows "0.16% מהחיסכון" (from savings) and "1.47% מההפקדה" (from deposits). So switching tracks inside the pension fund wouldn't change my fees.
- **There's an age-based track for my age.** It's "גילאי 60 ומעלה" (age 60 and up): 31% in shares, 9.49% a year over 3 years, 6.63% over 5 years. This is probably where I am or should be. The share-heavy tracks did much better, for example "מניות" (shares) at 17.06% and 11.46%. At 61 I'm not sure I want that much risk, and the page doesn't help me decide.

**How sure am I:** I'm fairly sure the page lists everything Harel offers. On the fees I'm only medium sure, because they are averages and not necessarily what I pay. On the returns I'm not very sure, because they are before fees and before tax. I can't tell what I would actually have earned.
