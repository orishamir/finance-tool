pc; 60 s, $0.33

# Comparing kupot gemel lehashkaa: what I took from the page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'm 61, so I don't want all my money in shares. I looked at the "general" tracks, **כללי**, and at the bond tracks with a small share part, **אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)**.

I wrote down every **כללי** track by hand: fee, 3-year return, 5-year return.

| Company | Fee from savings | 3 years | 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| הראל (8211) | 0.55% | 13.5% | 8.26% |
| אינפיניטי (9638) | 0.56% | 12.65% | 8.06% |
| מנורה (8675) | 0.59% | 12.9% | 7.78% |
| מיטב (7978) | 0.61% | 13.22% | 8.02% |
| מור (12538) | 0.72% | 12.82% | 8.13% |

The rest were close: 0.62% to 0.68% fees and 6.4% to 8% over 5 years. Alternative's (**אלטשולר שחם**) general track was the weakest, at 6.43% over 5 years.

**What I'd do:** I'd call Harel (**הראל**) about the general track, number 8211. It has the lowest fee of all the general tracks, the best 5-year return, and it's a big company with **6.7 מיליארד ₪** in the track. Big matters to me; I don't want something tiny. Infinity (**אינפיניטי**) looks just as good on paper, but the whole company has **910 מיליון ₪**, and I don't know it.

On my own savings, 0.55% versus Mor's (**מור**) 0.72% is 0.17% a year. On 400,000 ₪ that's about 680 ₪ a year. That's real money, but it's not huge.

**How sure:** About 60%. I'm sure Harel's general track is cheap and did well. I'm not sure it's right for my age; it's about half in shares (**מניות 49%**). Maybe the safer **עד 25% מניות** track is better for me. The page doesn't tell me which one suits me, and I'd still phone before signing anything.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The title **"כל המסלולים בכל החברות"** and the short paragraph under it.
- The track-type buttons: I looked for **כללי 12** and **אשראי ואג"ח 23**.
- The sentence **"החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה... בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר"**. That one helped a lot.
- The grey company bars, for example **"הראל 16 מסלולים · נכסים 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"**.
- In the table: the track name, the fee **מהחיסכון**, the 3- and 5-year returns, and the assets.
- The note at the very bottom about past returns.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu (מדריך, חוקים וכללים...)**: skipped it. I came for the table.
- **Tabs "לפי חברה / לפי מוצר"**: skimmed. "By product" was already chosen, so fine.
- **Intro paragraph**: used it. **"דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025"** helped: real fees, not advertised ones. I wasn't sure who **"רשות שוק ההון"** is, but it sounds official, which reassured me.
- **"123 מקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנתה"**: confused me. 123 of what? I didn't click.
- **Orange box "נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026"**: worried me at first. Then I saw it's only November, so one month old. If that's all it means, why warn me in orange?
- **Product buttons**: used one. **קופת גמל להשקעה 143** was already lit up. I ignored the others, and didn't open **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה"** because I already know.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע"**: skimmed. I left it on company, though maybe the other option would have put all the general tracks side by side for me.
- **Search box**: skipped it.
- **Track-type buttons**: used them to see what types exist. I didn't understand **עוקב מדדים - גמיש** or **הלכה איסלאמית**.
- **The sorting sentence**: used it, very useful.
- **Main table, about 140 rows**: used it, but it's long. I scrolled through 7 screens hunting for the word **כללי** in each company.
- **Grey company bars**: used them for size and the fee range.
- **Tags like "הלכה יהודית", "חדש", "עוקב מדדי אג"ח"**: skimmed. **חדש** told me to stay away, which is useful.
- **Ayalon (איילון קופות גמל), all "עוד לא דווח"**: skipped it. It's new, tiny, and has no numbers.
- **The ">" arrows on the left**: didn't click.
- **Note under the table about "עוד לא דווח"**: understood it, but didn't need it.
- **The past-returns note**: used it, and wished it were at the top. **"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"** changes how I read every number above it.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 7"**: didn't click. I don't know what's in it.
- **Footer, "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"**: I read it, and it made me trust the page more, since nobody is selling me something.

## 4. Columns, colours and labels

**What I looked at:**
- **המסלול** (track name).
- **דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025, מהחיסכון**: the main thing I compared.
- **תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול**: both lines, 3 and 5 years. I trusted 5 years more.
- **נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026**: to see which tracks are big and established.
- The grey company bars.

**What I glanced at or ignored:**
- **מההפקדה**: almost always **0%**, so I stopped looking. It's mostly 0.01% to 0.05% where it isn't, which is tiny.
- **קופה**, the number column: ignored it. Maybe I need it when I call.
- **The round teal and yellow charts**: I worked out that teal is **מניות** and yellow is **מט"ח**, but I read the numbers next to them, not the circles.
- **The dotted underlines under the numbers**: I didn't know they were something to click.
- **The small arrows next to column titles**: I didn't notice they were for sorting until later.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** in the returns column. I guessed it means the track is too young to have a 3-year figure. A plain "track is too new" would have been clearer.
- **"מניות 101%"**. How can more than 100% of my money be in shares?
- **מט"ח**: I know it's foreign currency, but **"מט"ח 99%"** on an S&P track: is that a risk for me or not?
- **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש"**: flexible how?
- **"123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה"**.
- Why the same company has two tracks with the same name: two **מניות** at Harel (8522 and 15286), two **כספי (שקלי)** at Mor. Which one would I be in?
- Whether the fees I'd be charged are these, or whether I can bargain them down.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **What does this mean for my money?** Something like: on 100,000 ₪, this track costs X ₪ a year.
- **Returns after fees.** I had to subtract the fee myself.
- **Tax**: how much I'd pay when I take the money out, and whether the age-60 annuity option applies to me.
- **Risk in plain words**: how much did each track drop in the worst year? At 61 that matters more to me than the best average.
- **Which track suits someone my age.**
- **How to take money out**: how fast, and with what penalty.
- **Minimum deposit**, and how to actually open one.
- **Whether I can move between companies later** without paying tax.
- **The general tracks side by side in one short list**, instead of scrolling through 12 companies.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The **"123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה"** line.
2. The round coloured charts. The numbers beside them are enough.
3. The **מההפקדה** line, since it's almost always 0%.
4. The **קופה** number column, or at least move it to the details.
5. Ayalon's tracks with no data, and anything marked **חדש** with **"עוד לא דווח"**. Hide them unless I ask.
6. The other product buttons (**קרן השתלמות**, **חיסכון לכל ילד**...) when I've already chosen.
7. The orange warning, or turn it into a small line.

**What I'd keep:** the type buttons, fees, 5-year return, assets, the company bars, and the sentence explaining the order. I'd move the **"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"** note up to the top.
