# Comparing kupat gemel lehashkaa tracks: my notes as a 24-year-old first-timer

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd pick an **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** track, because that's the "invest in the S&P 500" advice I keep hearing. Every company has one, and they look almost the same. The returns over 3 years are all about 13.3% to 13.9%, and their ring charts are full ("מניות 99%"). So the only real difference I could see was the fee under "דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון".

These are the cheapest S&P 500 tracks I found:

| Company | Track no. | Fee (savings) | Fee (deposit) | 3 years | 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| מנורה מבטחים | 13874 | 0.52% | 0.01% | 13.79% | "פחות מ־5 שנים" |
| אלטשולר שחם | 14865 | 0.54% | 0% | "פחות מ־3 שנים" | none |
| הראל | 13414 | 0.56% | 0% | 13.7% | 11.92% |
| אנליסט | 13854 | 0.56% | 0% | 13.44% | none |
| מגדל | 13563 | 0.57% | "עוד לא דווח" | 13.65% | 12.07% |

**I'd probably go with Harel 13414.** It's nearly the cheapest, it has 5 years of history, and it's the biggest company. Menora is 0.04% cheaper, but I don't understand the extra 0.01% "מההפקדה".

**How sure am I? About 60%.** I'm fairly sure an S&P 500 track is the right kind for me. I'm not sure the fee is the only thing that matters, or that 0.5% is a good price. I have nothing to compare it to.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The intro under **"כל המסלולים בכל החברות"**, which told me what the numbers mean and their dates.
- The product buttons, where **"קופת גמל להשקעה 143"** was already selected.
- The **"במה הכסף מושקע"** chips. Seeing **"עוקב מדד s&p 500 12"** told me every company has one. In real life I'd click it so only those 12 show.
- The sorting note: "בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר".
- The table: the track names, the fee column and the 3/5-year returns.
- The note at the bottom: "התשואה… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס… ולכן אין מיון לפיה".

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** ("מדריך", "חוקים וכללים", "השוואה למטרה" and the rest): skipped. "השוואה למטרה" sounded tempting, but I stayed on this page.
- **"לפי מוצר / לפי חברה" tabs:** skimmed them and didn't touch them.
- **Title and intro:** used. "דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025" was useful.
- **"123 מקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנה לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא":** confused me. I didn't get what "הזנה" means or why I'd care.
- **Orange box, "נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026":** read it. It made me a bit worried, but the data is only a month or two old, so I went on.
- **Product buttons:** used, just to confirm I was on the right product. I don't know what "קרן השתלמות" or "קרן חדשה מקיפה" are, and I didn't need to.
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link:** noticed it and would probably click it later.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע":** I'd guess "במה הכסף מושקע" puts all the S&P 500 tracks together. That would have saved me a lot of scrolling.
- **Search box:** skipped.
- **Filter chips:** used the S&P 500 one. I didn't understand most of the others: "אג"ח", "סחיר", "גמיש", "קיימות", "הלכה יהודית/איסלאמית", "כספי (שקלי)".
- **The table, roughly 140 rows over 7 screens:** I scrolled the whole thing, but I only really read the S&P 500 rows and the grey company header rows. I skimmed the rest.
- **Grey company rows** (e.g. "הראל 16 מסלולים · נכסים 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"): skimmed. They helped me see that big companies don't charge much more.
- **Bottom notes:** read them. I was relieved to learn that "עוד לא דווח" just means the data is missing.
- **Footer** ("אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"): read it, and it made me trust the page a bit more.

## 4. Inside the table

- **Columns I looked at:**
  - The track name.
  - "דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון". This was my main column.
  - "תשואה שנתית ממוצעת" for 3 years ("3 שנים") and 5 years ("5 שנים").
- **Columns I mostly ignored:**
  - "קופה", which is just a number to me.
  - "מההפקדה", because it's almost always "0%".
  - "נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026". I used it a little: "2.3 מיליארד ₪" felt safer than "2 מיליון ₪".
  - The ">" arrows. I never clicked one.
- **Ring chart and colours:** I only used the general idea. Teal "מניות" and yellow "מט"ח", and a full ring means "all in". I didn't really understand the yellow part.
- **Grey labels:** I noticed the "חדש" tags and the little grey labels like "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" and "הלכה יהודית", and ignored them.
- **Orange "!" with "עוד לא דווח":** noticed. Together with "פחות מ־3 שנים", it made me skip the newer tracks, like all of איילון's.
- **Dotted underlines:** I guess they're clickable. I didn't try.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"**, and why an S&P 500 track shows "מט"ח 99%".
- **"אג"ח"**, "סחיר", "גמיש", "משולב".
- **"מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדה"**: why are there two fees, and which one hurts more?
- **"מניות 101%"**: how is that more than 100%?
- **The Ayalon S&P 500 track** (15977) shows "מניות 34%" and "מט"ח 82%". Isn't an S&P 500 track supposed to be all stocks?
- **"עוקב מדדי מניות" vs "עוקב מדד S&P 500" vs "מניות"**: what's the difference?
- **"לפני דמי הניהול"**: so do I subtract about 0.55% from the 13.7% myself?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- Is 0.5% a good fee? Can I ask a company for a lower one?
- How do I actually open one, and is there a minimum amount or a monthly deposit?
- When can I take the money out, and what tax do I pay?
- A simple example, like "if I put in ₪1,000 a month for 10 years…".
- Can I move later to another company or another track?
- A one-click way to see only the S&P 500 tracks, sorted by fee across all companies, instead of grouped by company.

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. **Remove first:** most of the rows. All the bond, money-market and "הלכה" tracks could go behind the filter.
2. Then the "123 מקור/מחושב/הזנה" line.
3. Then the "קופה" number column, and "מההפקדה" when it's 0%.
4. Then the "נכסים" column.
5. **Keep:** the track name, the fee from savings, the 3/5-year returns, and the S&P 500 filter.
