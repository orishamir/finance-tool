# s11: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 57 s, $0.23

## 1. My answer

I'd join, and I'd make sure my employer puts in the full amount. The sentence that convinced me is near the top, under "למה משתמשים בזה":

> "לשכירים עם מעסיק שמפקיד זה כמעט תמיד הצעד הראשון: הפקדת המעסיק קיימת רק כאן"

As I understand it, my employer adds up to 7.5% of my salary and I add 2.5%. That's money I only get if I'm in the fund. Later the calculator box says outright that it leaves the employer's money out:

> "הכסף של המעסיק לא עומד לבחירה ולכן הוא מחוץ להשוואה"

So the comparison shows the keren winning by only ₪415.84, and even that leaves out the employer's part. That made me more convinced, not less.

**When I can take the money out:** 6 years after the first deposit, with no tax. It's only 3 years if I've reached retirement age or use it for studies ("שמושך את הכסף להשתלמות"). I can take it out earlier, but the orange warning says that's expensive ("משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה"), and there's a 47% figure in it that scared me.

**Investing:** I'd pick the S&P 500 track, because I saw "מדד S&P 500" in the list of things you can invest in.

**How sure I am:**
- About 85% sure that joining is right.
- About 40% sure which company to choose. The page says fees go "עד 2% בשנה" depending on the company and names "אלטשולר שחם, הראל, הפניקס". It doesn't tell me which one is cheap.

## 4. Tables, charts and labels

**Slider:** I looked at the numbers 0 to 12, the striped grey part, the teal part and the two labels above them. I didn't drag it.

**Bars:** I used the teal bar for the keren, the yellow bar for the securities account, and the difference line. I didn't understand the small empty checkbox under the bars.

**Costs/tax mini-table:**
- I looked at the "עלויות" and "מס" rows.
- I noticed the keren has much higher costs (₪2,402 against ₪466) but much less tax (₪2,922 against ₪5,787).
- I ignored the dotted underlines under the numbers.

**"כמה זה עולה":**
- I read the "סוג" and "תקרה" columns: "עד 2% בשנה", "אין תקרה כוללת" (no overall cap, which sounded bad), and exit fees "אין".
- I ignored the "חוקים וכללים" column with the PF-06 / KH-22 tags.

**"מונחים":**
- I read the "מונח" and "מה זה" columns.
- I ignored the "איפה מופיע" tags ("תלוש שכר", "טופס", "תקנון"). Actually "תלוש שכר" was a bit useful: it tells me I'll see this on my payslip.

**Colours:** I used teal = keren and yellow = securities. I didn't understand why some words have dotted or dashed underlines.

- "הרווח הריאלי". I looked it up in the glossary and it's still fuzzy.

- The orange box: "לשכיר, כמה זה עולה מופיע למעלה, ב'מה מייחד'". I went back up and couldn't find which number that was.
- "איחוד רק כשנזיל".
