# Keren hishtalmut, read by me: 61, salaried, careful

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would join, and I would put in the full 2.5% myself so my employer puts in its full 7.5%.** I'm fairly sure about this, about 8 out of 10.

These lines decided it:

- **"המעסיק יכול להפקיד עד 7.5% מהמשכורת הקובעת בלי שזה ייחשב לכם הכנסה"** (my employer can put in up to 7.5% and it doesn't count as my income). On my 12,000 a month, the page says that's up to **"900 ₪"** a month. That's money I don't get any other way.
- **"הרווחים... פטורים ממס רק אם מושכים אחרי 6 שנים"** (the profits are tax-free only if I take the money out after 6 years).
- **"למי שהגיע לגיל פרישה... רק 3 שנים"** (for someone who has reached retirement age, it's only 3 years). At 61 this is the line that matters most to me.

Why I'm not fully sure:

- I don't know if I count as "reached retirement age" yet. The page doesn't tell me what that age is.
- If the 6-year rule applies to me, the money is locked until I'm 67.
- The orange warning box scared me a bit: **"משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה"** (early withdrawal is possible but expensive). It mentions **"47%"** for high earners. I'm not one, but it's still tax on the employer's part.
