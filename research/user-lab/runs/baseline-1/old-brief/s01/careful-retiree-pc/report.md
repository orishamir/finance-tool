# How the page worked for me, a 61-year-old with savings in my current account

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd keep a few months of spending money in the current account. I'd move the rest into a daily money-market fund or a bank deposit. Before that, I'd ring my bank with two questions: what interest do I get on my balance, and what do they charge me every month?

The page convinced me the current account is mainly for money I need right away. Two lines did it:
- "כסף שיושב בו לא תמיד מקבל ריבית" (money sitting in it doesn't always get interest)
- "מול קרן כספית יומית העו"ש עדיף רק כשצריך את הכסף באותו יום" (compared with a daily money-market fund, the current account is better only when you need the money that same day)

Also: "כסף שיושב בעו"ש בלי ריבית לא גדל" (money sitting in a current account without interest doesn't grow). Prices go up 2% a year, so my money is quietly shrinking.

**How sure:** About 75% sure the direction is right. Only about 30% sure which product to pick. The page names the alternatives but never shows me side by side what each would give me.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The title and the line under it
- "מה זה" (What it is), especially the 15,000 shekel line
- The calculator, "מה קורה לכסף לאורך הזמן" (what happens to the money over time), with its settings box "ההנחות של הגרף" (the graph's assumptions)
- The top of the section "למה משתמשים בזה" (why people use this), where the money-market fund is mentioned
- "שווה לדעת" (worth knowing)
- "כסף נכנס ויוצא" (money in and out)
- "כמה זה עולה" (what it costs)
- "מיסוי" (tax), plus the red box under it
- "סיכונים ומה לא מוגן" (risks and what isn't protected)
- "מה האתר לקח בחשבון" (what the site took into account), mainly the locked item about age exemptions

## 3. Going down the page, block by block

- **Title and subtitle: used.** "כסף שיושב בו לא תמיד מקבל ריבית" was the first useful warning.
- **The little row "123 מקמן / 123 חושב / 123 הזנה": confused me.** I don't know what מקמן means. I gathered the colours show where a number comes from, but I didn't care.
- **"מה זה": used.** It says a bank that pays interest on a balance in credit must show its rate, and one that doesn't pay must say so. It also says that if more than 15,000 ₪ stays in for a quarter, the bank has to write to me. So the bank owes me that letter. I'd check whether I got one. The part about "המסלול הבסיסי" (the basic package: 1 action with a clerk and 10 online actions a month) and "המורחב" (the extended one: 10 and 50) was useful, but it gives no prices.
- **The calculator: used it, and it bothered me.** More on this in section 4.
- **"הצגה כטבלה" (show as table): skipped.** It was closed, and the summary under the graph was enough.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה": used what I could see.** Its heading falls between my first two screenshots, so I only saw the end of it. The line comparing it with a money-market fund is the most useful sentence on the page for me. Under it, "במה אפשר להשקיע בתוכו: מזומן שקלי" (what you can invest in inside it: shekel cash) I skimmed, since it just means plain cash.
- **"שווה לדעת": used the text, ignored the codes.** "אין ביטוח פיקדונות" (no deposit insurance) got my attention. The labels BANK-20, BANK-23 and BANK-15 mean nothing to me.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח" (who offers it and who supervises): skimmed.** It's all banks, supervised by the Supervisor of Banks. Fine.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא": used.** "בכל עת, את כל הכסף או חלק ממנו" (any time, all or part of the money) is reassuring. So is "מעבר לבנק אחר... בלי עלות ובלי מס" (moving to another bank, at no cost and no tax).
- **"כמה זה עולה": used it, and it let me down.** It says "דמי חשבון קבועים... לפי התעריפון של הגורם המציע": a fixed account fee, set by the bank's own price list. So it doesn't tell me how much. I saw the link "כמה העמלות האלה עולות לאורך זמן" (what these fees cost over time) but didn't follow it.
- **"מיסוי": used.**
  - "ריבית לא צמודה: 15% מהריבית": 15% tax on interest that isn't linked to prices.
  - "ריבית צמודה למדד: 25% מהריבית הריאלית": 25% tax on the real interest when it is linked to the price index.
  - The tax is taken by the bank, so I don't have to do anything.
- **The red box: read it twice.** It says it's unknown whether the exemption for low-income people applies to current-account interest, and "האתר לא מחיל אותו כאן" (the site doesn't apply it here). It worried me and didn't help.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן": used, and it frightened me a little.** There's no deposit insurance. Government help is "שבשיקול דעת, לא זכות שלכם" (at their discretion, not your right). The 250,000 ₪ protection is "רק הצעה" (only a proposal), and current accounts aren't included anyway.
- **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון": skimmed, but one item stopped me.** "פטורים לפי גיל ושימוש" (exemptions by age and use) is exactly my situation at 61, and the site says "האתר לא מחשב את זה" (the site doesn't calculate this). The codes TXI OQ11 and BANK OQ9 I ignored.
- **"מונחים" (terms), the glossary table: skimmed.** I looked at the "מונח" (term) and "מה זה" (what it is) columns. I ignored "איפה מופיע" (where it appears) and its little tags.
- **The side menu "בעמוד הזה" (on this page) and the footer: skimmed.** I used the menu to see what was coming. I noticed "נתונים נכון ל־25.10.2026" (data correct as of 25.10.2026) and "בלי קישורי שיווק" (no marketing links). That made me trust it a bit more.

## 4. In the chart and the lists, what I looked at and what I ignored

**The calculator:**
- **Looked at:** the big number "74,044 ₪", and the four lines under "שנה 10" (year 10): נשאר לכם (left for you) 74,044, מס (tax) 2,005, דמי ניהול (management fees) 0, ההפקדות שלכם (your deposits) 62,681.
- **What puzzled me about the deposits:** I'd be putting in 10,000 plus 500 a month for 10 years. That's 70,000. Why does it say I put in 62,681? I think it's because the button "בכסף של היום" (in today's money) was selected, but nobody said so next to the number.
- **The settings box:** I saw "תשואה בשנה 5%" (5% return a year) and "אינפלציה 2%" (2% inflation). That's where I lost trust. Who pays me 5% in a current account? The page itself says many banks pay nothing. If I typed 0% there, I'd expect my money to shrink in today's terms. The page should start with that picture.
- **The graph:** I looked at the teal area (נשאר לכם) and the white line (ההפקדות שלכם). I couldn't see the pink tax line or the orange fees line; they're too thin. I ignored the year numbers along the bottom (2, 4, 6...).
- **Ignored:** "בשקלים של העתיד" (in future shekels), the "חושב" (calculated) label, "הצמדה: לא צמוד" (indexation: not linked), and the age field. The age field says "מסמן בגרף גילים שבהם החוקים והכללים משתנים" (marks ages on the graph where the rules change), but it doesn't say what changes.

**"כמה זה עולה":** I read all four columns: סוג (type), על מה נגבה (what it's charged for), תקרה (ceiling), חוקים וכללים (rules). The ceiling column just sends me to the bank's price list, and the rules column is empty.

**Tax cards:** I read all three cards. I didn't use the little arrows between them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "מקמן", and that row of 123s at the top.
- Why the calculator assumes a 5% return for an account that often pays nothing.
- Why "ההפקדות שלכם" is 62,681 and not 70,000.
- The codes: BANK-20, TXI OQ11, BANK OQ9.
- "הטבה בנקאית" (bank benefit): is that something I should ask my bank for?
- Whether the low-income tax exemption in the red box applies to me.
- What "גיל" in the calculator actually changes.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **How much my bank charges me each month, in shekels.** Even a typical range would do.
- **What interest the banks actually pay on a current account today.** Even a range.
- **Side-by-side figures.** For 100,000 ₪ over a year: current account, versus money-market fund, versus a fixed deposit. What would I end up with in each, after tax and fees?
- **Whether I get any tax relief at 61, or when I retire.** The site admits it doesn't work this out.
- **How much to keep in the current account and how much to move.**
- **Whether a money-market fund is safe**, since it isn't covered by deposit insurance either.
