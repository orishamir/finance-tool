# s01: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 50 s, $0.20

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd keep one month of expenses plus my next credit card bill in the current account and move the rest somewhere that pays something. These two lines convinced me:

- **"כסף שצריך להיות זמין ברגע זה: ההוצאות של החודש והכרית הקרובה. כסף שנשאר בעו"ש רבעון שלם מעבר לסף שבחוק כבר שייך במקום אחר"**
- **"כסף שיושב בעו"ש בלי ריבית לא גדל"**

The 2% inflation box made it click: if my bank pays me nothing, I'm losing about 2% a year.

My first step would be to ask my bank whether it pays **"ריבית על יתרת זכות"** at all, and what alternatives it has to offer me. The page says that if I keep more than ₪15,000 there for a full quarter, the bank has to tell me about them. After that I'd look into a **"קרן כספית"**, because the page mentions it as the other option.

- **That my idle money is costing me something:** about 80% sure.
- **Where exactly to move it:** about 30% sure. The page names "פיקדונות" and "קרן כספית" but never explains them or compares them, and it says nothing about the S&P 500.
- **What my account actually costs me in shekels:** I still don't know. The cost table just says **"לפי התעריפון של הגורם המציע"**.

- **Calculator ("ההנחות של הגרף"):** used, but it confused me. It starts at 5% return, and my current account doesn't pay 5%. The small print says "התשואה ההתחלתית היא דוגמה, לא תחזית", but the big ₪74,044 still made it look like my account grows. I'd want to put 0 there. ## 4. Chart and tables

**The chart:**
- **Looked at:** the big ₪74,044, the teal "נשאר לכם" area, and the white "ההפקדות שלכם" line. The gap between teal and white is what I tried to read as "how much I gained". I also looked at the Y-axis labels (20–80 אלף) and the X-axis (היום … 10).
- **Barely saw:** the pink "מס" line. It's a thin sliver at the top.
- **Ignored:** the orange "דמי ניהול". It's ₪0 and invisible. I also ignored the purple "מחושב" badge.
- **The toggle:** I saw "בכסף של היום / בשקלים של העתיד" and left it on the default. I wasn't sure why ₪62,681 of deposits isn't the ₪70,000 I'd actually put in, until I guessed it's adjusted for inflation.

**The "שנה 10" rows:** I read all four. "מס ₪2,005" confused me, because tax on what, if my account pays no interest?

**The cost table (כמה זה עולה):** I read "סוג", "על מה נגבה" and "תקרה". I ignored "חוקים וכללים", which was empty.

**The מונחים table:** I read "מונח" and "מה זה" and ignored "איפה מופיע".
