# Me, trying the page with my iShares fund

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd open an account at **פסגות** and buy **ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)**. The page marks it "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול". It says I'd end up with **86,492 ₪** after 10 years, in today's money, after fees and tax.

I'd drop my own fund, the iShares S&P 500 Swap. I'm about 70% sure of that. I came to see how my fund compares, and I only got half an answer:

- In the main fund list it says "אין מחיר כאן" and "עוד לא נאסף". It gets no number and no rank.
- I found one number for it near the bottom, under "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" in the table "סוג קרן אחר, אותו חשבון". It shows **59,196 ₪**, which is "פחות 19,980 ₪". That's about 20,000 less, which scared me off.
- But that number seems to be for בנק ירושלים, not פסגות. There's also a little lock that says "נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג". So I'm not sure I can trust it.
- Even without the big result, the Israeli fund's fee looks lower: "דמי ניהול: 0% קבוע, 0% משתנה, 0.02% נאמן", against the 0.05% I typed in.

I'd still want to know whether my fund is cheaper through a foreign broker like Interactive Brokers. The page says "אין נתון" for that.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- The title "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" and the S&P 500 button.
- The four inputs: סכום היום, הפקדה חודשית, שנים, תשואה בשנה. I left them at 10,000 / 500 / 10 / 7%.
- The result box: "הבחירה שלכם 86,492 ₪", "הזוג הבא בתור 86,449 ₪" (only "−43 ₪" different), and "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪".
- The fund list "כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500", to find my fund's row.
- The account list "כל החשבונות" on the right, for the ranking of banks.
- The table near the bottom, "עכשיו: ... ירושלים", where I found my fund's 59,196 ₪.

## 3. Going down the page

- **Title, index buttons and S&P 500 description:** Used. I liked "אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ", because that's my case.
- **Sliders and the exchange-rate line ("שער הדולר 3.16 ₪"):** I used the sliders. I skimmed the dollar line.
- **Result box (86,492 vs 86,449):** Used. This was the clearest part of the page.
- **Bar "כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים":** Skimmed. I saw that the total cost is about 6,876 ₪ and that the pink part (מס, the tax) is the biggest. I didn't try to understand the small pieces.
- **Fund list (left):** Used, but mostly scrolled. It's very long, and most rows are the same Israeli funds with different numbers. My row was halfway down and only had "עריכה" and "הזנתי 0.05%". It gave me nothing to compare.
- **Account list (right, פסגות, לאומי, וואן זירו...):** Skimmed the names and the big numbers. Many rows say "לא בחישוב" with conditions like "מחיר אחר לצעירים, לחיילים...". I'm 24, so I wondered whether one of those cheaper deals is meant for me. I didn't open them.
- **Interactive Brokers / אינטראקטיב ישראל ("אין נתון"):** Read it. This is exactly where my fund would be bought, and there's no number. That was frustrating.
- **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות":** Read it. It explained "בתעריפון אין שורה לניירות ערך זרים". OK, but it doesn't help me decide.
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, מול התעריפון":** Skimmed. It's interesting that ads lie, but it isn't my question.
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם" (chart and table):** Skimmed. I took away one thing: more years means more money ("19,116 ₪ יותר" if I add 20% more years). I didn't look at the chart lines.
- **"עם נטרול מט״ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל":** Skipped most of it. Confused. Two bar charts and a table full of percentages about the dollar. I don't know what נטרול מט״ח is, or whether I should care.
- **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת, ורואים מה משפר אותה" (staircase chart):** Confused. It starts from "MTF סל" at "ירושלים", which I never chose. Then it climbs to 86,492. I got the idea of "switch account, then switch fund", but not why it starts there.
- **The table under it with "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל":** Used. It's the only place my fund got a number.
- **Small print at the bottom and the "חוקים וכללים" buttons:** Skipped.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

- **Result box:** I looked at the big ₪ numbers and "הזול". I ignored the "שנים" link and the arrow.
- **Cost bar:** I looked at the total on the left (6,876 ₪) and the big pink chunk. I ignored the small green, orange and yellow pieces and most of the legend (דמי משמרת, עמלת קנייה ומכירה, המרת מטבע...).
- **Fund list:** I looked at the fund names and the left column "לעומת הבחירה" (like "−213 ₪" and "−4,492 ₪"). I ignored the "מול המדד" column (+0.37% and so on) and nearly all the coloured tags. Exception: on my own fund I noticed "נקנית בחו״ל", "צוברת", "חשיפה לדולר", and the red "מס רק במכירה". I also noticed my fund's name says "Swap" but it doesn't get the "עסקת החלף" tag the Israeli swap funds get. I didn't know what to make of that.
- **Account list:** I looked at the names and the big numbers. I ignored the small numbers under them (1,057 ₪, 4,541 ₪...) and the coloured bars.
- **"מה הכי משנה" table:** Only the "ההפרש" column.
- **Currency charts:** I looked at the yellow vs. light-blue bars for a second and gave up.
- **Bottom tables:** Only the "נשאר בסוף" column and the red/green "פחות/יותר". I ignored the "יותר כסף | פחות כסף" bars.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "4D" and "4A" in the fund names.
- "צוברת", "עסקת החלף", "נאמן", "משמרת".
- "מנוטרלת מט״ח", "חשיפה לדולר", and that whole currency section.
- "W-8BEN", "מס עיזבון אמריקאי", "חלוקת דיבידנד".
- "מס רק במכירה" is red on my fund. Is red bad? It sounds good to me.
- "נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג". What's open about it? Is the 59,196 real?
- Why the staircase starts from "MTF סל" at "ירושלים".
- "יש ספק אם נמכרת שם: וואן זירו, Interactive Brokers". Can I buy my fund there or not?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **My fund's final number in פסגות, or anywhere I can actually buy it.** That was the whole reason I added it.
- If I buy it through Interactive Brokers, what's left after 10 years compared with 86,492?
- **Why** my fund came out 20,000 lower in ירושלים. Is it fees, or tax?
- One plain sentence like "your fund vs. the cheapest: X ₪ less, because Y".
- Whether, at 24, any of the "לא בחישוב" young-person deals apply to me.
