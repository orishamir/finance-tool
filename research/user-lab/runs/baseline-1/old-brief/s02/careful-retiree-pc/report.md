# A 61-year-old saver reads the money-market fund page

## 1. My answer: what I'd do, and how sure I am

I would put some of my "might need it soon" money into a daily money-market fund (קרן כספית יומית) through my bank. I would keep a smaller amount in my current account for anything I might need today.

What decided it for me was the comparison box near the top:
- Current account: **"עו״ש … 0%"** (zero).
- Daily money fund: **"קרן כספית יומית … 3.25% לפי ריבית בנק ישראל"**, with **"ביום מסחר – הוראת פדיון בכל יום מסחר"**. That means I can ask for my money back any trading day.
- One-year deposit: **"פיקדון לשנה … 1.8%"**, and the money is locked until the end.

So what does this mean for my money? On money I may need within months, the fund pays something, and I'm not locked in. A deposit locks me in for less.

**How sure am I: about 65–70%.** Three things hold me back:
- **The return isn't guaranteed.** The page says **"אין תשואה מובטחת"** and **"גם לפיקדונות בבנק אין ביטוח"**.
- **I don't know my real return after costs.** The 3.25% is **"לפני מס ועלויות"**. The fee table says **"אין תקרה בחוק"** for the management fee, but the page never gives me a typical number.
- **I can't get the money the same minute.** It's "ביום מסחר", not "מיד". If I truly need cash today, the current account is still the place.

Before I put any money in, I'd ask my bank for the fund's actual management fee (דמי ניהול).

## 4. Lists, tables and charts: what I looked at and what I ignored

**The comparison box (the bars):**
- I looked at the four names on the right (עו״ש, קרן כספית יומית, קרן כספית מתחדשת, פיקדון לשנה), the percentages on the left (0%, 3.25%, 3.25%, 1.8%), and the small grey line under each bar saying when I get my money ("מיד – בכל רגע", "ביום מסחר", "במועד קבוע", "בסוף התקופה, בלי שבירה, תקופה: 12 חודשים").
- I noticed the tabs at the top ("מיד / ביום מסחר / במועד קבוע / בסוף התקופה"), with "ביום מסחר" ticked. I understood that as "I need the money within a trading day."
- I read the summary line: "הריבית הגבוהה ביותר שמחזירה את הכסף בזמן: קרן כספית יומית, 3.25% בשנה". That was the bottom line I wanted.
- I read "4 מתוך 8 הבנקים… ויש בנקים שמשלמים עד 3% (דיסקונט), בתנאים ועל עד 10,000 ₪ בלבד". Interesting, but small print.
- **Colours:** I noticed the fund's bar is turquoise and the other two are mustard, and I guessed turquoise means "this is the one this page is about". The green label "המוצג בעמוד" confirmed it. I didn't know what the little yellow dot on the current-account line meant, and I assumed it was "almost zero".
- **What I ignored:** the lengths of the bars. I read the numbers instead.

**The fee table in "כמה זה עולה":**
- I read the "סוג" column and the "על מה נגבה" column.
- In "תקרה" I saw "אין תקרה בחוק" over and over, which made me uneasy.
- I ignored the whole "חוקים וכללים" column (MF-16, BRK-17…). It means nothing to me.

**The glossary table "מונחים":**
- I read "מונח" and "מה זה".
- I ignored "איפה מופיע" (אפליקציה, דוח שנתי, תעריפון…).

**The tax cards:** I read all three titles. I looked at "25% מהרווח הריאלי" and skipped the small top labels.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- The top line **"123 מקור · 123 חושב · 123 הזנה"**, and **"מונח הקישו להסבר"**.
- The small codes everywhere: **"MF-11", "MF OQ3", "TXS-24", "BANK-18"**. Are they laws? Footnotes? I don't know.
- **"הצגת החוקים והכללים"** on the left of every heading. I didn't know whether I needed it.
- The paragraph with **"המסלקה"** and **"ניירות ערך זרים לא יעלו על 10%"**. What does this mean for me in practice? When does the money reach my account, the next day or later?
- **"השעה היעודה"**. I understood the idea from the glossary, but what time is it? Not stated.
- **"קרן נאמנות פטורה" versus "חייבת"**, and **"הרווח הנומינלי" / "הריאלי"**. I followed it only halfway.
- **Is the 3.25% what I actually get?** The text says the return moves with the Bank of Israel rate **"פחות העלויות"**. Yet the chart shows exactly the Bank of Israel rate, "לפני מס ועלויות". So my real number is lower, but by how much?
- **"עמלת הפצה של עד 0.1%"**. Is that taken from me or not? I think it's paid by the fund manager, but it isn't clear whether it comes out of my money in the end.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **A typical management fee in numbers**, e.g. "most funds charge between X% and Y%". The page says the Israel Securities Authority (רשות ניירות ערך) publishes an average but doesn't say what it is.
- **A worked example in shekels.** If I put in 50,000 ₪ for six months, about how much do I get back after fees and tax?
- **Exactly how many days until the money is in my current account** after I ask for it.
- **Has a fund like this ever lost money, and by how much?** The page says "השווי יכול גם לרדת" but gives no sense of how big a drop could be.
- **How to choose between funds**, and whether my own bank's offer is fine.
- What the closed **"שחקו עם המספרים שלכם"** box does. Maybe that's where my example is.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון"**. It's about the site, not about me.
2. **All the law codes** (MF-…, BRK-…, TXS-…) and the **"חוקים וכללים"** column in the fee table.
3. **The line "123 מקור / חושב / הזנה"** at the top.
4. **The "איפה מופיע" column** in the glossary, and most of the glossary itself. Keeping the hover explanations on the words is enough.
5. **The paragraph about the 10% rule, the clearing house and the Friday question.**
6. **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"**, cut down to a single sentence.

**What I'd keep at all costs:** the sentence under the title, "למה משתמשים בזה", the comparison box, the first row of the fee table (preferably with a real number), the tax cards, and the first paragraph of the risks section.
