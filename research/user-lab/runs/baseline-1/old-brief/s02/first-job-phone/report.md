# What I took from the money-market fund page, as a 24-year-old checking where to park cash

## 1. My answer and how sure I am

I'd move the money I might need in the next few months out of my checking account (עו"ש) and into a daily money-market fund (**קרן כספית יומית**). I'd buy it through my bank.

What convinced me:
- The opening line: **"קרן נאמנות שמשקיעה רק בנכסים קצרי טווח עם סיכון נמוך שהלווה לא יחזיר"**. I read that as "safe, short-term stuff."
- The bars comparing where I could keep the money. Checking pays **0%**, the money-market fund **3.25%**, and a 12-month deposit **1.8%**. The fund also lets me take money out **"ביום מסחר"** (on any trading day) and **"בלי נעילה"** (nothing locked).
- **"הפסד בקרן ניתן לקיזוז"** and **"בקרן כספית פטורה"**. I only half understood these, but they sounded like the tax is fair.

How sure: about 6.5 out of 10. I'm sure it beats leaving the money in checking. I'm not sure what I'd actually earn after fees and tax, how fast the money reaches my account, or which fund to pick.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- The title and the grey sentence under it.
- **"מה זה"**, mostly the first and third paragraphs.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**, especially the first paragraph: **"כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן חירום..."**. That describes me exactly.
- The comparison box **"כמה מהר הכסף חוזר, ומה הוא מרוויח עד אז?"**. This was the most useful part.
- The cards in **"מיסוי"** (tax).
- The first paragraph of **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"** (risks).

## 3. Going down the page block by block

- **Top menu bar:** skipped. None of it meant anything to me yet.
- **Title and grey sentence:** used. It's the clearest explanation on the page.
- **"123 מקור / 123 חושב / 123 הזנתה / מונח הקישו להסבר":** confused me. I didn't know what "123" was doing there or what "הזנתה" means. I ignored it.
- **"מה זה", first paragraph:** used. **"אין תשואה מובטחת"** (no guaranteed return) stuck with me. So did the 90-day average life of what the fund holds, which made it feel short and safe. **"נאמן"** (trustee) went over my head.
- **"מה זה", second paragraph (buying and selling):** I skimmed it and got lost. **"השעה היעודה"** (the order cut-off time), **"מפיץ"** (the seller), and then **"MF-11"** and **"MF OQ3"**. Then **"שאלה פתוחה"**: the site itself doesn't know whether you can trade on Friday. That made me a bit nervous.
- **"מה זה", third paragraph (costs):** read it, partly understood. I got that there's a yearly fee taken out of the price and that I don't see it as a separate charge. **"0.1%"** is the most the bank gets. But there's no number for the fee itself. It only says it appears **"בקישור שבכברטיס של המספר שלמטה"**, and I didn't know which number that meant.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה":** used, especially the comparison with a bank deposit. The line **"הפסד בקרן ניתן לקיזוז; הפסד בפיקדון לא"** (a loss in the fund can offset tax, a loss in a deposit can't) I didn't really get. The buttons at the bottom (**"אג"ח ממשלתית לא צמודה"**, **"מק"מ"**, **"מזומן שקלי"**) I skipped.
- **"מה מייחד את קרן כספית יומית" with the bars:** used heavily. It's the only place with real numbers I could compare.
- **"שחקו עם המספרים שלכם"** (play with your own numbers): it was closed. I'd have liked to open it and put in my own amount.
- **"שווה לדעת":** skimmed. I only took in **"התשואה הצפויה: אחרי דמי ניהול, לפני מס, לא מובטחת"**. That sentence confused me, because the box above said the 3.25% is **"לפני מס ועלויות"** (before tax and costs). So which is it?
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח":** skimmed. It's supervised, and the bank has to show the fund next to deposits without pushing one. That was enough for me.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא":** a quick look. **"בכל יום מסחר, במחיר של אותו יום"** is good, but it doesn't say when the money actually lands in my account.
- **"כמה זה עולה" table:** looked at it and was disappointed (details in point 4).
- **"מיסוי":** used the three cards. I understood: no tax benefit when I put money in, nothing while it sits there, and 25% when I sell. The paragraph under the cards, about nominal vs. real gain and the 2 out of 73 funds, I skimmed and gave up on.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן":** used the first paragraph. The money isn't owned by the company running the fund. Then **"אין ערבות של המדינה... וגם לפיקדונות בבנק אין ביטוח"**: so a deposit isn't protected either. The second paragraph I skimmed. I caught **"להשעות פדיונות לכמה ימי מסחר"**, which is a little scary for money I might need, but it says that's only in rare cases.
- **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון":** skipped.
- **"מונחים" (terms table):** skipped. Maybe I'd go back to it for **"נאמן"** or **"מפיץ"**.
- **Footer:** skipped.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

**The bar chart (the most important one):**
- **What I looked at:** the four product names, the percentages on the left (0%, 3.25%, 3.25%, 1.8%), and the bar lengths. The bold words **"מיד" / "ביום מסחר" / "במועד קבוע" / "בסוף התקופה"** showed me how fast the money comes back. I also noticed the green **"המוצג בעמוד"** tag and the summary line **"הריבית הגבוהה ביותר שמחזירה את הכסף בזמן"**.
- **Colours:** I took the turquoise to mean "this is the one you're asking about" and the yellow-brown to mean "the others." I didn't think about it beyond that.
- **The tabs at the top (מיד / ביום מסחר / במועד קבוע / בסוף התקופה):** I saw "ביום מסחר" was selected but didn't click the others or understand what they would change.
- **What I ignored:** the small grey tags **"לפי ריבית בנק ישראל"** and **"ריבית שפורסמה"**, and **"4 מתוך 8 הבנקים"** (it's about checking accounts, which I'm leaving anyway). Same for **"תקופה: 12 חודשים"**.

**The cost table:**
- **What I looked at:** the **"סוג"** column (I saw there are six kinds of fees) and the **"תקרה"** column. "אין תקרה בחוק" (no legal cap) on almost every row actually scared me a bit.
- **What I ignored:** the whole **"חוקים וכללים"** column with **"MF-16", "BRK-17", "BANK-18"**. Those mean nothing to me.
- **What's missing:** a column with an actual percentage.

**"שווה לדעת" list:** I read only the text and ignored the codes on the left.

**Terms table:** I didn't really go into it. I ignored the **"איפה מופיע"** column with **"אפליקציה", "דוח שנתי"**.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- What **"123 מקור / חושב / הזנתה"** means.
- All the codes: **MF-11, TXS-24, BANK-18**, and so on.
- **"נאמן"** (trustee) and **"מנהל הקרן"** (fund manager): who is who?
- **"מהרווח הריאלי"** vs. **"הרווח הנומינלי"**: the difference between "real" and "nominal" gain.
- **"פטורה" / "חייבת"**: why a fund is "exempt" and what it means for me if it's "liable."
- **"קיזוז"** (offsetting a loss against tax).
- Whether 3.25% is before or after management fees. One place says before costs, another says after management fees.
- **"חשבון ניירות ערך"** (a securities account): do I already have one at my bank, or do I need to open one?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **How many days until the money reaches my checking account after I sell.** For money I might need soon, this is the most important question. All I found was **"לפי התקנות"** (according to the regulations).
- **What the management fee actually is, as a number** (for example "around 0.2%").
- **A simple example:** "If you put in 10,000 ₪ for six months, you'll be left with about X after fees and tax." Maybe that's inside "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם", but it was closed.
- **How to choose between funds**, or whether they're all basically the same.
- **Whether there's a minimum amount.**
- **A step-by-step for actually buying one** in my bank's app.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The terms table and **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון"**.
2. The **"חוקים וכללים"** column and all the codes.
3. The **"123 מקור..."** line under the title.
4. The second paragraph of "מה זה" (cut-off times, distributor, Friday trading).
5. **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"**.
6. The long paragraphs under the tax cards.

I'd keep the title, "למה משתמשים בזה", the bar comparison, the tax cards and the risks. I'd also want "כמה זה עולה" to stay, but with real numbers in it.
