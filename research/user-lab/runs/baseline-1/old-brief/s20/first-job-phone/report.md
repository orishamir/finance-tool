# Me, 24, looking at the Harel page

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd go with **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** (track number 13414), because it's the "invest in the S&P 500" thing I've heard about, and the page says it actually exists at Harel. It costs **0.56%** a year (just 0.02% more than the cheapest track, **0.54%**). The returns look good: **13.7%** a year over 3 years and **11.92%** over 5.

I'm only about 50% sure, for two reasons:

- **This might not be my money.** The page opened on the **"קופת גמל להשקעה"** tab. I'm salaried, so I think my Harel money is really a pension (**"קרן חדשה מקיפה"**?) or a **"קרן השתלמות"**. I don't know which one, and I didn't see those tabs' tables. So my choice may apply to a product I don't even have.
- **The "מניות" row did better.** It shows **24.04%** and **14.64%**, beating the S&P 500 track on both, for 0.55%. That pulled me in. Then I read the note under the table, **"היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"** (it doesn't say what will happen next), so I didn't fully trust the number. I'm still not sure if it's simply better.
