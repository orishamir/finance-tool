# Looking at Harel's page, as a 61-year-old saver

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would not move any money today.** I'm about 70% sure that's the right call. I'm much less sure what I'd do after that.

The first thing I noticed is that the page had already chosen a product for me: the highlighted tab is **"קופת גמל להשקעה 16"**. I don't know if that's where my savings are. They could be in **"קרן השתלמות"**, a pension fund (**"קרן חדשה מקיפה"**), or the old **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"**. So my first step would be my annual statement, to see which product and track I'm in. Then I'd click that tab.

What I learned from the table:
- **Fees barely differ.** The line **"16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ-0.54% עד 0.61%"** told me the cheapest and dearest tracks are 0.07% apart. On ₪500,000 that's about ₪350 a year. I wouldn't switch tracks for that.
- **Harel could charge me much more.** The bottom line **"התקרה בחוק: 1.05% מהחיסכון, 4% מההפקדות"** says the legal maximum is about double what they charge here. If my own statement shows more than about 0.6%, I'd phone Harel and ask them to lower it. That's the one concrete thing I would do.
- **Returns differ hugely, but so does risk.** **"מניות" 24.04% (3 שנים)** looks wonderful. But at 61 I don't want **94%** of my money in stocks. **"כספי (שקלי)" 4.25%** is safe but dull. **"כללי" 13.5% / 8.26%** with **49%** in stocks looks like the middle road. I'd only move toward it after talking to someone, not because of one table.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- The intro under **"הראל"**, especially **"דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב-2025... נכונים לסוף ספטמבר 2026"**. It told me how old each number is.
- The product tabs under **"כל המוצרים של הראל"**.
- The summary line **"16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול..."** and **"המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר."**
- The big table, all 16 rows.
- The note under the table: legal cap, extra costs, and the warning about past returns.

## 3. Block by block, top to bottom

- **Top menu (מקור, מדריך, חוקים וכללים...): skimmed.** I didn't need it.
- **"הראל" and the dates paragraph: used.** Useful. It warned me the fees are last year's and the returns run to September.
- **"כולל את החברות: הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ · הראל קרנות נאמנות בע"מ": skimmed.** That's fine; it's Harel.
- **"123 ממקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנה לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא": confused me.** Three identical "123"s with different words. I didn't understand it and moved on.
- **Product tabs: used, but uneasy.** I don't know the difference between **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"** and **"קרן חדשה כללית"**, or what **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"** is.
- **Links "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" / "כל המסלולים... בכל החברות": noticed, didn't click.** The second one may be worth it later to compare Harel with other companies.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" buttons: skimmed.** I could see the same rows in the table below, so I didn't filter.
- **The table: used heavily.**
- **Notes under the table: used.** See the next section.
- **"קרנות מחקות 5 קרנות", "מרכזית לפיצויים 7 מסלולים", "קרנות סל 4 קרנות": skipped.** I don't know what they are, and they're closed anyway.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל": skimmed the heading, didn't open anything.** The line **"תנאים שכתובים במסמכים של הראל ולא בחוק"** made me think it could matter: the small print. But seven closed rows with no hint of what's inside didn't tempt me. **"קרן השתלמות 8"** stood out, because eight conditions sounds like a lot.
- **"לא מצאתם שם?" search box: skipped.**
- **Footer, "נתונים נכונים ל־03.11.2026" and "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק": read.** It reassured me a little that nobody is selling me something.

## 4. What I looked at in the table

**Columns I used:**
- **"המסלול" (track name):** read every one.
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025":** I read the top number (**"0.54%"** etc.). Every bottom number is **"0% מההפקדות"**, so after two rows I stopped reading those. My understanding is that they don't take a cut when I deposit, which is good.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול":** used heavily. I read **"3 שנים"** and **"5 שנים"**.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח":** I only looked at the **"מניות"** number, the green dot. That's my risk number.

**Things I ignored:**
- **The orange "מט"ח" number:** I don't know what it means. I guess foreign currency.
- **The little rings:** they're pictures of the same two percentages, so I didn't need them.
- **"קופה" numbers (15283, 8211...):** meaningless to me, though maybe I'll find mine on my statement.
- **"כסף מנוהל":** glanced at it. **"7.3 מיליארד"** in **"מניות"** versus **"10 מיליון"** in **"קיימות"** told me which tracks most people are in, and that reassured me a bit.
- **The dotted underlines, the small arrows, the "?" circles:** ignored.

**The worrying label:** In **9 of the 16 rows**, the return just says **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**. Nine tracks with no track record at all. I'd stay away from those.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **"123 ממקור / מחושב / הזנה"**.
- **"מט"ח"**, and why **"עוקב מדדי אג"ח"** shows **0%** stocks but **96%** מט"ח. Is that risky or not?
- **"סחיר"** (in "מניות סחיר", "משולב סחיר"), and how **"מניות סחיר"** differs from plain **"מניות"**.
- **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש"**: flexible how?
- **"PF-10"** next to the note about extra costs.
- **"בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות מהקופה הוצאות ישירות... שלא מופיעות כאן"**. So there are more costs, but how much?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול ולפני מס"**. So what do I actually get in hand? About 0.55% less, then tax, but what tax?
- **"מרכזית לפיצויים"**, **"קרנות מחקות"**, **"קרנות סל"**.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which track I'm in now**, and what I'm actually paying.
- **Returns after fees**, in shekels. For example, "₪100,000 became ₪X".
- **How badly each track fell in a bad year.** At 61 that matters more to me than the average.
- **Last year's return**, not just 3 and 5 years.
- **How to switch tracks**, whether it costs anything, and whether it triggers tax.
- **What happens when I retire or withdraw.**
- **Whether these 2025 fees are what I'll pay in 2026.**
- **A phone number** at Harel to negotiate fees.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The **"123 ממקור / מחושב / הזנה"** line.
2. The three closed sections **"קרנות מחקות", "מרכזית לפיצויים", "קרנות סל"**.
3. The **"לא מצאתם שם?"** search box.
4. The rings in the table, and the **"0% מההפקדות"** line repeated 16 times.
5. The **"במה הכסף מושקע"** buttons, since the table already shows everything.

**Keep:** the table, the dates paragraph, the legal fee cap, and the warning that past returns don't predict the future. Those are the parts that told me something about my money.
