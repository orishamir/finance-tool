# Speaking as myself: a careful 61-year-old looking at one company's index funds

## 1. My answer

**I'd pick the second option (screen-02).** I'm fairly sure, about 8 out of 10.

Both options show the same funds, numbers and fees. The only difference is that the second one adds a short sentence under each index name, under the heading "המדד ומה יש בו" ("the index and what's in it"). In the first option, "ת"א-125" and "MSCI AC World" are just labels to me. In the second, I'm told in plain Hebrew what I'd actually own:
- "מאה עשרים וחמש החברות בעלות השווי הגבוה ביותר בבורסה בתל אביב" (the 125 most valuable companies on the Tel Aviv exchange)
- "חמש מאות החברות הגדולות בארצות הברית" (the 500 biggest US companies)

That is exactly what I came to find out: what each fund follows. I'm not fully sure because I still couldn't work out what each fund **costs me in total**, and the second option doesn't help with that either.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The title "קרנות סל" ("index funds", 4 of them) and the sentence under it
- The "שם הקרן" (fund name) column
- The "המדד ומה יש בו" column (in the second option)
- The "דמי ניהול לפי הדוח של הקרן" (management fees according to the fund's report) column
- Some of the small grey tags under each fund name

## 3. Block by block

- **Title "קרנות סל" with "4 קרנות":** Used it. It's clear, and four funds is a manageable number.
- **The sentence "רק הקרנות שהאתר אסף עליהן דוח, לא כל הקרנות של החברה" ("only the funds the site collected a report on, not all of the company's funds"):** I read it twice. It's honest, and I appreciate that. But it worries me: which funds are missing, and might one of them be cheaper? It doesn't say.
- **"שם הקרן" column:** Used it, but "(4A)" and "(4D)" mean nothing to me. Is that a grade? A risk level? I skipped past it, a little annoyed.
- **The grey tags under the names:**
  - "צוברת" (accumulating, with the piggy bank): I think it means the fund keeps the dividends instead of paying them to me. I'm guessing, though.
  - "מחזיקה את המניות" (holds the shares): It sounds reassuring, as if the fund really owns the shares.
  - "עסקת החלף" (swap): This one confused me and frankly worried me. If the S&P and World funds don't hold the shares, what do they hold? Is my money riskier there?
  - "חשיפה לדולר" (exposure to the dollar): I understand it. My money goes up and down with the dollar.
  - "מס גם על שינויי המטבע" (tax also on currency changes): This is the most important tag for my money, and it's tucked away in small grey text. Do I pay tax even when the dollar moves and I haven't really earned anything? I'd want that spelled out.
- **"מספר נייר" (security number):** Skimmed it. I'd copy it if I phoned my bank to buy, but I wasn't reading it.
- **"בדף הקרן במאיה" (the fund's page on Maya):** Skipped it. I don't know what "מאיה" is, and the little arrow icon tells me it would take me off this site. I'm not clicking something I don't know.
- **"המדד" / "המדד ומה יש בו":** In the first option I skipped it, because it was just repeating the name. In the second option it's the column I used most.
- **The fees column:** Used it the most, and it confused me the most (see below).

## 4. Inside the table

**What I looked at:**
- The fee numbers next to "קבוע" (fixed), "משתנה" (variable) and "נאמן" (trustee)
- The descriptions under the index names in the second option
- The tags "מחזיקה את המניות", "עסקת החלף" and "מס גם על שינויי המטבע"

**What I noticed in the fees:**
- The Tel Aviv 90 fund is "קבוע 0.1%". The S&P 500 and World funds are "קבוע 0.8%". That's eight times more, and it's the one thing on the page that jumped out about my money.
- The "משתנה" figure for S&P is "0.3%". Variable how? Could it be more next year?

**What I noticed in the colours and labels:**
- The fee numbers are underlined. I wondered whether I'm supposed to click them, but I didn't try.
- The turquoise index names have a little "<" arrow. I didn't know where it would take me, so I left it alone.

**What I ignored:**
- The security numbers
- The "4A"/"4D" codes
- The Maya links
- The little icons inside the tags

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **How the three fees fit together.** Do I add "קבוע" + "משתנה" + "נאמן"? Does that make the S&P fund about 1.1% a year? Nowhere does it say "this is what you pay in total".
- **What "משתנה" means in practice.** Can it change, and who decides?
- **What "נאמן" is, and why I pay it.**
- **What "(4A)" and "(4D)" mean.**
- **"עסקת החלף":** what it actually means for the safety of my money.
- **What "מאיה" is.**
- **Why a description says "שאינן במדד ת"א-35"** ("that aren't in the TA-35 index"). I'd have to know a second index just to understand the first.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **One plain number per fund:** the total yearly cost. Better still, what it comes to in shekels, for example "on 100,000 ₪ you pay about X ₪ a year".
- **How the fund actually did** compared with its index, over a few years.
- **What the missing funds are,** the ones the site has no report on.
- **A one-line explanation of the tax tag:** what it means for me when I sell.
- **Whether there are buying or selling costs** on top of the management fees.

## 7. If half the page had to go

I'd remove these first:
1. The "בדף הקרן במאיה" links
2. The "(4A)/(4D)" codes in the names
3. The security numbers (they could sit somewhere less prominent)
4. The "צוברת" and "חשיפה לדולר" tags

I'd keep:
- The fees
- The "what's in the index" descriptions
- The tags about swaps and tax on currency changes, because those are about my money
