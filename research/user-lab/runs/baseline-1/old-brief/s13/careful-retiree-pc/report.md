# s13: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 55 s, $0.24

I'm 61, I'm salaried, and my employer offers this. I read all five screens slowly. I'll answer as myself.

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would join.** I'm about 85% sure.

What decided it for me was the chart's example. On a 12,000 ₪ salary, I put in 300 ₪ a month and my employer puts in 900 ₪. After 5 years:

- "הפקדתם 18,000 ₪" – I put in 18,000.
- "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪" – my employer added 54,000.
- "נשאר בידיים 66,513 ₪" – I end up with 66,513.

That's even if I take it out early and pay the tax. My money goes in once and roughly comes back almost four times over. I don't need more convincing than that.

I'm not 100% sure because of my age. One line says: "למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים" (for people who have reached retirement age, 3 years is enough). That sounds like it could help me. But the page never says what retirement age is, so I don't know if I'll wait 3 years or 6. I also don't know which investment track someone my age should pick.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: especially the second paragraph about taking money out.
- The orange warning box in **"מיסוי"**.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **What age is retirement age?** That decides whether I wait 3 years or 6.
- **If I leave my job or retire before the 6 years are up**, is the employer's money still mine? Can I leave it there until it matures?
- **After 6 years, do I have to take the money out**, or can it keep growing tax-free?
- **Which track suits someone who's 61.** There's a list of names (מסלול כללי, אג"ח, S&P 500) but not a word about who each one is for.
- How the 300 ₪ a month will show on my payslip, and how much my take-home pay will actually drop.
- What happens to the money if, God forbid, I die. Does it go to my heirs?
