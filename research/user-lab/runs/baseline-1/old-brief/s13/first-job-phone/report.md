# Keren hishtalmut: what I'd do after reading this page

## 1. My answer

I'd say yes to the keren hishtalmut and leave the money alone for 6 years. I'm about 85% sure it's worth having. I'm much less sure which company or track (the investment option inside the fund) to pick.

Three things convinced me:
- The opening line, **"חיסכון שהמעסיק ואתם מפקידים אליו מהשכר… אחרי תקופה שבעב החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס"**. So it's savings that both my boss and I pay into, and after a while I can take it out without tax.
- **"אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון"**. That answers when I can take the money out: 6 years.
- The big bar chart. It uses a 12,000 ₪ salary, which is close to mine. I put in **18,000 ₪**, the employer adds **+54,000 ₪**, and even if I take it out early at year 5, **"נשאר בידיים" 66,513 ₪**. My boss puts in three times what I do, so even pulling out early leaves me far ahead. That's what made it "worth it" for me.

I'm not sure about which company or track to choose, and what happens if I change jobs.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- **"מה זה"**: the first few paragraphs and the bullet list about withdrawing before 6 years.
- **"מה מייחד קרן השתלמות"**: the calculator with the two charts.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**: the four boxes, especially **"כשעוברות 6 שנים"** and **"עד 4 ימי עסקים"**.
- **"כמה זה עולה"**: only the first row, **"דמי ניהול… עד 2% בשנה"**.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: the second paragraph, which surprised me. If I take money out, the account closes and new deposits start over with **"ותק שמתחיל מחדש"**. That's important and I'd have missed it.
- The orange warning box in **"מיסוי"**, partly.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which fund or track to pick.** I saw S&P 500 mentioned and **"מיטב S&P500"** in the small print, but nothing tells me "for someone like you, pick this".
- **What happens if I leave my job.** Is the money still mine? Does the 6-year count continue at a new job?
- **Can I keep paying in myself after leaving?**
- **Can I use it for an apartment, or borrow against it** without withdrawing?
- **How to check what my employer actually deposits**, and whether I can ask for the full 7.5%.
- **What I physically do to open one.** Who do I call, and what do I tell HR?
