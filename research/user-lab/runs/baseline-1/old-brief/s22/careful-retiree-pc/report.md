# Looking for the cheapest S&P 500 keren hishtalmut

## 1. My answer

I would phone **כלל** (Clal) first and ask about their S&P 500 track, fund number **13342**. On this page it has the lowest fee of all: **"0.48% מהחיסכון"**. **מנורה מבטחים** (Menora) is just behind at **0.49%**, and **הראל** and **מגדל** are both at **0.5%**.

I'm fairly sure, maybe 75%, that Clal is the cheapest. Here is what holds me back:
- The fee is **"ממוצע 2025"**, an average of what they actually charged last year. It is not a promise of what they would charge me.
- Clal, Menora, Harel and Migdal are only 0.02% apart. On ₪300,000 that's about ₪60 a year, which isn't worth worrying over. The difference between the dearest and the cheapest is real money, though. Going from 0.7% to 0.48% saves roughly ₪660 a year on ₪300,000.

Before I signed anything, I'd ask Clal on the phone: "What fee will you give **me**, in writing?"

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The product buttons near the top, where **"קרן השתלמות 154"** was already highlighted.
- The **"במה הכסף מושקע"** row, where **"עוקב מדד S&P 500 12"** was highlighted.
- The summary sentence under it: **"12 מסלולים של 12 חברות... דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.48% עד 0.7%"**. This one sentence gave me nearly my whole answer.
- The note **"המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהיקר לזול"**. Without it I would have read the list the wrong way round. I'm used to the cheapest being at the top.
- The table, mainly the company name and the fee column.
- The two footnotes under the table, which explained "אין" and "עוד לא דווח".

- **Product buttons**: I used them to confirm I was in קרן השתלמות.
- **"מה זה קרן השתלמות" link**: I skipped it because I already know.
- **Search box "שם המסלול או החברה"**: I skipped it. I didn't need it.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" buttons**: I used them. I noticed a separate **"עוקב מדדי מניות 11"** button and wondered if it's the same thing. I decided to stick with the S&P one.
- **Summary sentence and sort note**: used, and the most useful part of the page.
- **The table**: used, but only two columns of it (see below).
- **Footnotes**: I read them. **"אין: בסוג הקופה הזה מותר לגבות דמי ניהול רק מהחיסכון, לא מההפקדות"** settled a question I had. The little **"PF-06"** tag meant nothing to me.

- **Footer**: I noticed **"נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026"**, so the figures are recent. Good. **"אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"** made me trust it a little more.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**What I looked at:**
- **"החברה"**: the company name.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025 מהחיסכון"**: the fee, and the main thing I came for. I saw a small ▼ arrow next to the header but didn't touch it.
- **"מההפקדות: אין"**: I looked at it once and understood it after reading the footnote.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"**: I glanced at the 3-year and 5-year figures. They are all around 13% and 12%, which makes sense if everyone is following the same index. A few say **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**, meaning the track is new.
- **Ayalon's row**: it has **"עוד לא דווח"** with an orange warning mark, plus a **"חדש"** label. I ruled it out.

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"**: the fund numbers (9451, 14849...). They're useful only if I phone someone.
- **"כסף מנוהל"**: how much money each fund manages. I don't care whether it holds ₪44 million or ₪10 billion.
- **"מסלולים"** and the company-wide total under each name.
- The **turquoise and yellow rings** and the **"מניות / מט"ח"** percentages. I didn't understand them, apart from noticing that Ayalon's ring wasn't closed (**40% מניות, 54% מט"ח**). That seemed odd for something that's meant to follow the S&P.
- The dotted underlines under the numbers. I didn't click on any of them.
- The **little arrows "<"** on the left of each row. I didn't open any of them.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **"So what does this mean for my money?"** I wanted it in shekels: on my balance, how much would I pay each year at each company? I had to work it out myself.
- Whether I can **negotiate a lower fee**, and whether the 2025 average is what a new customer like me would actually pay.
- How to **move** my existing keren to another company, and whether that costs anything or creates a tax problem.
- Whether 0.48% includes everything, or whether there are other costs on top, like the cost of the index fund itself.
