# s32: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 34 s, $0.19

I looked at all three pictures. They show the same table of four funds (קרנות סל) drawn three ways, so I'm treating them as options 1, 2 and 3, in file order.

## 1. My answer

**I'd pick option 3 (screen-03).** I'm fairly sure, about 7 out of 10.

It's the calmest of the three. It has one column on the left headed "חשיפה מרבית למט"ח", and each fund has one number under it, for example "מט"ח עד 10%". That tells me how much of my money could be riding on foreign currency. I can follow that.

Options 1 and 2 also show "מניות עד 120%". That number worried me, and nothing on the page explains it. I couldn't see how a fund holds more than all of my money in shares. Option 2 also drops the small note at the bottom that explains what the ceiling means. That was the one note that calmed me down.

As for the funds themselves: if I were choosing today, I'd look hardest at "אי.בי.אי. סל (4A) ת"א-90". Its fixed fee is "0.1% קבוע", the lowest of the four. It's labelled "מחזיקה את המניות", which sounds like it really owns the shares rather than doing something clever. I wouldn't buy anything from this page alone, though.

## 2. What I actually used

- The line under the title: "רק הקרנות שהאתר אסף עליהן דוח, לא כל הקרנות של החברה". It told me this isn't the full list.
- Fund names ("שם הקרן") and the small tags under them.
- "במה הכסף מושקע", the index each fund follows.
- "דמי ניהול לפי הדוח של הקרן", the fees, which I read most carefully.

## 3. Block by block

- **Title "קרנות סל 4 קרנות":** Skimmed it. Fine.
- **Grey line under the title:** I read and used it. I liked being told honestly that it's not every fund.
- **"שם הקרן":** Used it. I don't know what "(4A)" or "(4D)" mean. The tags helped a little:
  - "מחזיקה את המניות" is clear and reassuring.
  - "עסקת החלף" means nothing to me. It sounds like a deal I'm not part of.
  - "חשיפה לדולר" I understand.
  - "מס גם על שינויי המטבע" got my attention. Tax on currency changes is about my money, and I'd want to know more.
- **"מספר נייר" and "בדף הקרן במאיה":** I skipped the number, though I'd copy it down for the bank. I might click the Maya link later.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע":** Used it. "עוקבת אחרי מדד ת"א-125" is plain enough.
- **Fees:** Used it, and it confused me (see 5).
- **Exposure column:** In option 1 it confused me. In option 2 it's grey small print ("פרופיל 4A: מניות עד 120%, מט"ח עד 10%"), which I nearly skipped. In option 3 I understood it.
- **Bottom notes:** I used both.
  - "כל הקרנות כאן לא מחלקות: הדיבידנד והריבית מושקעים בקרן מחדש": good. I won't get payouts in my account.
  - "החשיפה המרבית היא התקרה שהקרן מצהירה עליה... לא מה שהיא מחזיקה היום": helpful, and it's missing from option 2.

- **"נאמן":** A trustee of what?
- **"מניות עד 120%" and "מט"ח עד 120%":** How can it be over 100%?
- **4A / 4D:** I don't know what they mean.
- **"עסקת החלף":** I don't know what this is.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
