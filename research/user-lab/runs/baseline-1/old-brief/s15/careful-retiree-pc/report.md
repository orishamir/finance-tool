# Looking for a kupat gemel lehashkaa: what I took from the page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

At 61 I don't want all my savings riding on the stock market. I also don't want it sitting in cash and earning almost nothing. So I would look at the middle-of-the-road track, **"כללי"** (general), which every company offers. My second choice would be the more cautious **"אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"** (bonds with up to a quarter in shares).

For "כללי", I went down the whole list company by company and noted the fee and the 5-year return:

**My answer:** I'd lean towards **הראל כללי, fund 8211**. It has about the cheapest fee, the best 5-year number, and it's one of the biggest, so it's not some fly-by-night fund. I'm **about 60% sure**, and I would not sign anything before phoning them.

The reason I'm only 60% sure: the page says the fee is **"דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025"**, the average actually charged in 2025. That isn't necessarily what they'd charge *me* as a new customer. And the returns are **"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"**, before fees and before tax. So what I'd actually get is less than 8.26%.

If I chose the safer bond track, the cheap ones are:
- אינפיניטי 9639: 0.54%, 5.38% over 5 years
- הראל 8521: 0.58%, 4.73%
- מיטב 7980: 0.61%, 5.49%

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- **The opening paragraph** under "כל המסלולים בכל החברות". It told me what the numbers mean and how old they are.
- **The orange warning:** "נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026". Fine, the numbers are a little stale.
- **The product button "קופת גמל להשקעה 143"**, which was already selected. Good, that's my product.
- **The sentence about the order:** "החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה... בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר" (biggest company first, cheapest fee first within each company). Once I read that, the list made sense.
- **The grey company header bars**, e.g. "הראל 16 מסלולים · נכסים 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". These gave me each company's fee range at a glance, and I used them a lot.
- **The table rows themselves:** track name, fee, and the 3- and 5-year returns.
- **The note at the very bottom:** "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך, ולכן אין מיון לפיה" (past returns don't tell you the future, so the list isn't sorted by them). Honest, and I appreciated it.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** ("מדריך", "חוקים וכללים", "שאלות שכולם שואלים"...): skipped. I'm only meant to look at this page, though "שאלות שכולם שואלים" (questions everyone asks) did tempt me.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר" tabs** (by company / by product): skimmed. "By product" was already chosen, which suited me.
- **Title and explanation:** used and read twice, because it says when the data is from.
- **"123 מקומי 123 מחושב 123 הזנתה, לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"** (local / calculated / entered): **confused me.** Three ways of writing "123" with no explanation in words. I didn't know if I should trust one kind more than another.
- **Orange warning:** used.
- **Product buttons** (תגמולים, קרן השתלמות, קרן חדש מקיפה...): skimmed. They're other products, not mine.
- **"143 מסלולים של 12 חברות. מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה"** (143 tracks from 12 companies, plus a "what is this?" link): used the count. I'd have clicked the link if I could.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע"** (group by company / by what the money is invested in): skimmed. I only understood it after looking at the buttons underneath. I think "במה הכסף מושקע" would have made my comparison much easier, because it would put all the "כללי" tracks side by side.
- **Search box "שם המסלול או החברה":** skipped.
- **Buttons under "במה הכסף מושקע"** ("כללי 12", "מניות 12", "אשראי ואג"ח 23", "הלכה יהודית 9"...): looked at them and understood they filter the list. Pressing "כללי 12" would have saved me scrolling through eight screens. Some names confused me, see section 5.
- **The order sentence:** used.
- **The big table:** used heavily, but only some columns (section 4).
- **Rows marked "חדש" (new) where everything says "עוד לא דווח" or "פחות מ־3 שנים":** skipped. With no history I wouldn't put my money there. This includes the whole of איילון at the end.
- **Bottom notes** about "עוד לא דווח" and past returns: used.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 7" button:** skipped. I didn't know what it would show.
- **Footer** ("אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק", "נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026"): read. It reassured me that nobody is trying to sell me something.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**Looked at:**
- **"המסלול" (track name)**, and the small grey labels under some names, like "הלכה יהודית" and "אג"ח סחיר".
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025", the "מהחיסכון" line** (fee taken from the savings). This was my main number.
- **The "מההפקדות" line** (fee on deposits): glanced at it. It's almost always 0%, but I did notice 0.16% at הפניקס משולב סחיר 15214 and wondered why.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול"** (average yearly return, before fees): mainly the 5-year line ("5 שנים"), less the 3-year line. I noticed many tracks just say "פחות מ־3 שנים" / "פחות מ־5 שנים" (less than 3 / 5 years old). I took those as too young to judge.
- **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"** (size): used to see how big and established a track is.
- **The grey company bars.**

**Ignored or half-used:**
- **"קופה" (fund number):** ignored, apart from writing down 8211 to give the company when I phone.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"** (exposure to shares and to foreign currency): I read the "מניות" percentage only, to check how much is in shares. I ignored "מט"ח" (foreign currency) because I don't really know what it means for me.
- **The coloured rings (teal and yellow):** ignored. The numbers next to them already say it.
- **The little orange clock icons** next to almost every number: ignored. I didn't know what they meant.
- **The dotted purple underlines** under the percentages: ignored.
- **The little ">" arrows** on the left of every row: ignored. Maybe they open more details, but nothing says so.
- **The sort arrows** in the column headings: didn't touch them.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **"123 מקומי / מחושב / הזנתה"**: no idea.
- **"מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדות"**: I guessed one is a fee on all my money and the other on new money I put in. The page never says so.
- **"101%" shares** (מיטב "מניות" 7860, מגדל "מניות סחיר" 15456): how can more than everything be in shares? It worried me.
- **The difference between "מניות", "מניות סחיר" and "עוקב מדדי מניות"**, and between "משולב סחיר" and "כללי". What does "סחיר" (tradable) mean for my money?
- **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש"**, a "flexible" index tracker. Flexible how?
- **"עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet) on a fee. If no fee is reported, what will they actually charge me? The note says to "click on the value", but I'd rather it just told me.
- **Why the חשיפה (exposure) of the same kind of track differs so much.** "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" is a bond tracker, but הראל 15285 shows "מניות 0%, מט"ח 96%" while others show different numbers.

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **The fee I would actually pay** as a new customer, and whether I can bargain it down.
- **Returns after fees**, or simply "if I put in 100,000 ₪, roughly what would it be after 5 years?" That's my real question.
- **Tax, and what happens at 60 and over.** I've heard there is a benefit if you take it as a monthly pension. That's the whole reason someone my age considers this product, and it isn't mentioned here.
- **How safe each track is**: bad years, how far it fell in a crash. One average number hides that.
- **Can I take money out whenever I want, and is there a penalty?**
- **Can I move between tracks or companies later?**
- **The minimum deposit.**
- **Returns over 10 years.**

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The coloured rings and the clock icons.** They add nothing for me.
2. **The "123 מקומי / מחושב / הזנתה" line.**
3. **The rows marked "חדש" that have no fees and no returns**, such as almost all of איילון. Put them at the bottom or hide them.
4. **The other product buttons** (תגמולים, קרן השתלמות...). I came for one product.
5. **The "מט"ח" line** in the exposure column.
6. **The fund number column.** It could go inside the details.

**What must stay:** the explanation at the top, the warning about the date, the company bars with their fee range, the fee and 5-year return columns, the size column, and the honest note at the bottom about past returns.
