# Picking an S&P 500 track in a kupat gemel lehashkaa, as a first-timer

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd go with an S&P 500 track. That's the only thing I've ever heard I should do. My first pick would be **מנורה מבטחים "עוקב מדד S&P500"**, and my backup would be **הראל "עוקב מדד s&p 500"**.

I searched the long list for every row with "S&P" in the name and found 12, one per company. The return numbers for the 3 years ("3 שנים") are almost the same for all of them, roughly 13.3% to 13.9%. So the only real difference I could see was the fee:
- **Menora** is the cheapest at **0.52% "מהחיסכון"**, plus 0.01% "מההפקדה". It has no 5-year number.
- **Altshuler Shaham** is 0.54%, but its returns say "פחות מ־3 שנים", so it has no history yet.
- **Harel** is 0.56% with 0% "מההפקדה". It has a 5-year number (11.92%) and is big (2.3 מיליארד ₪), which made me feel safer.

The note at the bottom pushed me toward choosing by fee: "היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך, ולכן אין מיון לפיה" (past returns don't tell you what will happen next, so the list isn't sorted by them).

**How sure:** about 6 out of 10. I'm fairly sure the S&P choice and the "cheapest fee" logic are right. I'm not sure whether 0.52% vs 0.56% even matters, or whether I'd actually get that fee if I signed up.

## 2. Parts I actually used
- The product buttons near the top, with **"קופת גמל להשקעה 143"** already highlighted. That told me I was in the right place.
- The row of buttons under **"במה הכסף מושקע"**, especially **"עוקב מדד s&p 500 · 12"**. That showed me there are 12 S&P options. I'd click it for real. In the screenshots I hunted for the rows by hand instead.
- The sentence about the sorting order: "החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה… בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול… מהזול ליקר" (companies go from biggest to smallest, and inside each company from cheapest fee to most expensive).
- In the table: the track name, the fee, and the 3-year and 5-year returns.
- The note under the table about returns.

## 3. Block by block
- **Top menu** ("מדריך", "שאלות שכולם שואלים", "השוואה למטרה"…): skipped. "שאלות שכולם שואלים" (questions everyone asks) looked tempting for later.
- **"לפי מוצר / לפי חברה" tabs:** skimmed and left them as they were.
- **Title "כל המסלולים בכל החברות" and the intro text:** read it. I half understood "דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025" (the fee is the average actually charged in 2025). I didn't get why I should care about "רשות שוק ההון".
- **"123 מקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנתה":** confused me. I have no idea what that is, and I skipped it.
- **Orange warning "נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026":** read it, got a little worried, then moved on. I don't know whether it changes anything for me.
- **Product buttons:** used them, as above. I ignored the others, like "קרן השתלמות" and "חיסכון לכל ילד".
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link:** noticed it. I'd probably click it, since I don't fully know what I'm opening.
- **"קיבוץ לפי" (group by) and "חיפוש" (search):** skipped. I could have typed "S&P" into the search box, but I didn't think of it.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" buttons:** used the S&P one. "אג"ח", "גמיש", "הלכה איסלאמית", "כספי (שקלי)" meant almost nothing to me.
- **The big table, company after company (pages and pages):** skimmed it, stopping only at S&P rows. It is very long.
- **Bottom note about returns:** read it and it helped.
- **"עוד לא דווח: השדה ריק בנתוני הרשות…":** read it. It explained the "עוד לא דווח" (not reported yet) cells.
- **Footer:** skipped.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored
**Looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (the track name): this is how I found the S&P rows.
- **The company name on the grey separator lines** ("הראל", "מור", "מנורה מבטחים"…): to know which company each S&P row belonged to.
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025", the top line "מהחיסכון":** the main number I compared.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת", the "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" numbers:** only to check that the S&P ones are all similar.

**Glanced at:**
- **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"** (assets): only to feel that a company is big and safe.
- **The small grey tags** like "חדש" (new): this told me to avoid the brand-new ones.

**Ignored or didn't understand:**
- **"קופה" numbers** (15283, 13414…): meaningless to me.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"** with the teal and yellow dots and the ring charts: ignored.
- **The second fee line "מההפקדה":** I didn't know how it's different from "מהחיסכון", so I mostly ignored it.
- **The clock icons, the dotted underlines and the "›" arrows:** ignored.
- **The grey-line summaries** like "16 מסלולים · נכסים 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול… מ־0.54% עד 0.61%": ignored.

## 5. What I didn't understand
- **"מט"ח"** and **"חשיפה"**: what does "99% מט"ח" mean, and is it good or bad?
- **"מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדה"**: two kinds of fees? Which one do I actually pay?
- **The rings:** no idea.
- **"101%" מניות:** how can it be more than 100%?
- **Returns of "פחות מ־3 שנים":** at first I thought it meant the return was less than something. Later I guessed it means the track is younger than 3 years.
- **"עוד לא דווח"** under the fee for Migdal and Ayalon: can I even trust those?
- **"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"**: so my real return is lower, but by how much?
- **"123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה"**.
- **Different names for the same idea:** "עוקב מדד S&P 500" vs "s&p500" vs "S&P500". Are they the same thing?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
- In shekels, what does 0.52% vs 0.56% cost me per year on, say, ₪10,000?
- Is this fee what *I* would pay, or can I bargain it down? It says "ממוצע" (average).
- What's the minimum to start, and can I deposit monthly?
- How do I actually open one? There's no button or link to the company.
- When can I take the money out, and what's the tax?
- A simple "best S&P option for a beginner" summary, or the S&P rows side by side without scrolling through 143 lines.
