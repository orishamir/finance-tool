# s09: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 46 s, $0.20

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I wouldn't put all my idle money into a deposit right now. I'd probably put only the part I know I won't touch for a year into a short deposit (12 months, like the example), and only if it has a way out partway through. I'm only about 50–60% sure, mostly because I still don't know what interest my own bank would give me.

The thing that changed my mind was the calculator. I put in ₪70,000 over 10 years (`ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪`) and the big number came out ₪68,270 `בכסף של היום`. That's less than I put in. The interest of 1.8% is lower than the 2% `אינפלציה בשנה`, so my money is slowly losing value even though the bank pays me. Before this I thought a deposit was safe money that grows.

Here's what I learned about what I give up by locking the money away:
- I can't take it out before the end (`משיכה: בסוף התקופה שנקבעה בהסכם`).
- Taking it out early is called `שבירת פיקדון`, and it can cost a fine that "eats the interest and even part of the amount deposited" (`מוחקת את הריבית ואף חלק מהסכום שהופקד`).
- Some deposits have fixed dates when I can leave for free (`תחנות יציאה`).

I also didn't know that `אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות`, meaning if the bank collapses, no law gives me my money back. That scared me a bit.
