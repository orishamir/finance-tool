# Me, aged 61, trying to choose a kupat gemel lehashkaa from this page

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I would shortlist **הראל – כללי (קופה 8211)**. It's my first choice, but I'm not ready to sign.

I don't want all my money in shares at my age, so I left out the "מניות" and "S&P 500" tracks. Their 3-year numbers like 24.04% are tempting, but "מניות 100%" scares me. The "כספי (שקלי)" tracks are safe but earn only about 3%, which barely keeps up with prices. "כללי" looked like the middle road: about half in shares (the ring says "מניות 49%").

I compared every "כללי" line company by company:
- **הראל כללי** has the lowest fee of the general tracks, "0.55% מהחיסכון". It also has the best 5-year number, "5 שנים 8.26%", and a big pot, "6.7 מיליארד ₪". Big, cheap, and not worse than the others.
- **אינפיניטי כללי** (0.56%, 8.06%) is close, but it's a small company ("910 מיליון ₪" for the whole company). I'm more comfortable with a big one.
- **מור כללי** has the same 5-year number (8.13%), but costs "0.72%", the most expensive. Crossed off.

**How sure:** about 60%. I'm sure on fees: 0.55% versus 0.72% is about 170 ₪ a year on 100,000 ₪, and I worked that out myself because the page doesn't. I'm not sure about the rest. The returns are "לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס", and the page itself says they don't predict anything. Before deciding I'd call הראל and maybe a second company, and ask what fee *I* would actually pay.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **"מקור / מחושב / הזנתה"**.
- **"מהחיסכון" versus "מההפקדה"**. I guessed: one is a yearly charge on everything I have, the other a cut of each deposit. The page never says.
- **"מניות 101%"** (מיטב מניות, מגדל מניות סחיר). How can more than all the money be in shares?
- **Why 3 years beats 5 years** in almost every row, e.g. "13.5%" against "8.26%". Was there a bad year in between? Which one should I believe?
- **"סחיר", "גמיש", "עוקב מדדים", "משולב"**, and what "כללי" actually holds.
- **The warning saying "not updated since September"** next to a footer saying the data is correct to 03.11.2026.
- **What the ring shows** that the two numbers next to it don't.

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **What this means in shekels for me.** If I put in, say, 200,000 ₪, what do I pay a year, and what might I roughly have in 5 years after fees?
- **Return after fees.** The page shows returns before fees, so I had to do the subtraction in my head.
- **Can I negotiate the fee?** These are averages "שנגבה בפועל". Will I get 0.55% or something higher?
- **Tax at my age.** It says "לפני מס", but what tax will I pay when I take the money out? I've heard something about after 60 or an annuity, but nothing here.
- **How quickly I can get my money out**, and whether there's a penalty.
- **Which track suits someone of 61.** Even a simple "less risky / more risky" label would help.
- **How bad the worst year was** for each track. That matters more to me than the average.
- **How much I'm allowed to deposit** each year, and how to actually open one.
- **One table of only the "כללי" tracks side by side.** I had to build it in my head across eight screens.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The rings.** The numbers beside them already say it.
2. **The "קופה" number column.** I could get it after clicking on a track.
3. **The "123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה" line.**
4. **The "מההפקדה" line**, since it's nearly always 0%. Show it only when it isn't zero.
5. **Brand-new tracks with no fees and no history** (most of איילון, the "חדש" rows). Put them behind a button.
6. **The other product buttons and the "לפי חברה / לפי מוצר" tabs.**

**Keep at all costs:** the fee, the 5-year return, the assets, the company header rows, the honest note about returns at the bottom, and the "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link.
