# s17: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 60 s, $0.33

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd put my money in an S&P 500 track (`עוקב מדד S&P 500`), and right now I lean towards **מנורה מבטחים, `עוקב מדד S&P500`**. It has the lowest yearly fee of all the S&P 500 tracks I found: `0.52% מהחיסכון`. Its return is `13.79%` a year over 3 years. My backup is **הראל** (`0.56%`, `13.7%` over 3 years, `11.92%` over 5 years). It's the biggest company and that track holds `2.3 מיליארד ₪`, which makes me feel safer.

All the S&P 500 tracks that have been around for a few years show nearly the same return, about 13.3% to 13.9% over 3 years. The note at the bottom says the return is "`לפני דמי הניהול`" (before fees). So the funds really only differ on the fee, and I picked the cheapest one.

**How sure:** about 6 out of 10. I'm sure about "S&P 500" and fairly sure about "pick the cheapest fee." I'm not sure whether 0.52% versus 0.56% even matters. I also don't know if I'd actually get that fee, since the header calls it a `ממוצע` (average) from 2025. And Menora has a tiny `0.01% מההפקדה` that I don't understand.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The title and subtitle `כל המסלולים בכל החברות`, to check I was in the right place.
- The product button `קופת גמל להשקעה 143`. It was already selected, which reassured me.
- The filter button `עוקב מדד s&p 500 12`. It told me there are 12 of these. I then went through the list hunting for those rows.
- The table rows named `עוקב מדד S&P 500`, the grey company header bars (`הראל`, `מור`, `מנורה מבטחים`…), and the fee and return columns.
- The small note under the table: `התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס... ולכן אין מיון לפיה`. This note changed how I picked.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** (`מדריך`, `חוקים וכללים`, `השוואה למטרה`…): skipped. `השוואה למטרה` made me curious, but I stayed here.
- **`לפי מוצר / לפי חברה` tabs:** skimmed and left as they were.
- **Title and subtitle:** used. "Fees are what was actually charged in 2025" was helpful.
- **`123 מקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנתה`:** confused me. I have no idea what this is, and "click a number to see where it comes from" didn't make me want to.
- **Orange warning `נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026`:** I read it and got a bit worried. Is the data old? The bottom of the page says `נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026`, so I'm not sure which date to believe.
- **Product buttons** (`תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים`, `קרן השתלמות`…): skimmed. Mine was already on. I don't know what the others are.
- **`143 מסלולים של 12 חברות. מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה`:** read it. I'd probably click that link later.
- **`קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע`, and the search box:** I noticed them. If I came back, I'd try `במה הכסף מושקע` to get all the S&P tracks together.
- **Category buttons** (`הכל`, `מניות`, `כללי`, `הלכה יהודית`, `אג"ח סחיר`, `הלכה איסלאמית`, `קיימות`…): I only cared about `עוקב מדד s&p 500`. Most of the rest are words I don't know.
- **Sorting sentence** (`החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה... בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול`): read it. It explained why I had to jump between companies to find the S&P rows.
- **The big table:** used, but only the S&P 500 rows. I scrolled past around 130 other rows.
- **Company header bars** (e.g. `16 מסלולים · נכסים 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%`): skimmed them to see who's big and roughly how cheap each company is.
- **The `אילון קופות גמל` block:** skipped. Everything there is `עוד לא דווח` and `חדש`, with `0 מיליון ₪` in some tracks, so I'd avoid it.
- **`עוד לא דווח` explanation, and the return note under the table:** used both. The return note was the most useful sentence on the page.
- **`חוקים וכללים · 7` and the footer:** skipped.

## 4. What I looked at in the table

**Looked at:**
- **Track name**, for example `עוקב מדד S&P500`. I noticed it's written `s&p 500`, `S&P500`, `s&p500` in different rows, so I had to read carefully.
- **The first fee line, `X% מהחיסכון`**: the main thing I compared.
- **Returns, `3 שנים` / `5 שנים`**: compared these too. `פחות מ־3 שנים` made me drop Altshuler (`0.54%`) and Yelin (`0.69%`), because they have no history to show.
- **Assets, e.g. `2.3 מיליארד ₪` / `30 מיליון ₪`**: used this as a feeling of "is this popular or tiny".
- **The `חדש` tag and `עוד לא דווח` with the orange `!`**: treated them as warning signs.

**Ignored or barely looked at:**
- **The `קופה` number** (15283, 13414…): meaningless to me.
- **The `מההפקדה` line**: it's almost always `0%`, so I ignored it after a while.
- **The donut circles and the turquoise/yellow dots**: no idea what they're for.
- **`מניות` / `מט"ח` percentages**: I saw `99% / 99%` on the S&P rows and figured that's normal for S&P.
- **The dotted underlines and the `>` arrows**: I didn't click them.
- **The sort arrows in the header** (`ההחיסכון ▲`, `ההפקדה`): didn't touch them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- `מהחיסכון` vs `מההפקדה`: is it two separate fees I pay both of? Is 0.52% a year a lot?
- `מט"ח`, and why an S&P track shows `מט"ח 99%` while another shows `0%`.
- `מניות 101%`: how can it be more than 100%?
- The difference between `עוקב מדד S&P 500`, `עוקב מדד מניות`, `מניות`, and `מניות סחיר`. Which is "the S&P 500" I heard about?
- `אג"ח`, `גמיש`, `סחיר`, `כספי (שקלי)`, `שריעה`.
- `123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה`.
- Why the warning says the data hasn't been updated since September when the footer says it's correct for 03.11.2026.
- `לפני מס`: how much tax will I pay, and when?

## 6. What I wanted but couldn't find

- One short list of only the S&P 500 tracks, cheapest first, across all companies (maybe `במה הכסף מושקע` does this; I didn't try).
- Will I get this fee, or can I ask for a lower one? An average from 2025 isn't a price quote.
- The return after fees, i.e. what I'd actually earn.
- The minimum deposit, whether I can take money out whenever I want, and how the tax works.
- How to open one: a link or a phone number for the company.
- Is this better than just buying an S&P 500 fund myself?
- Whether it matters which company I choose if they all track the same index.

2. The donut circles and the `קופה` numbers.
3. The other product buttons (`קרן השתלמות`, `חיסכון לכל ילד`…), since I came for one product.

5. The `מההפקדה` line when it's `0%`.

I'd keep the track name, the yearly fee, the 3- and 5-year returns, the asset size, and the note saying returns are before fees.
