## Rewrites

**s03**
- Old: On your phone, you are reading about current accounts because your savings sit in one. You opened the section 'שחקו עם המספרים שלכם' to see what happens to your own money if it stays there.
- New: On your phone, you are reading about current accounts because your savings sit in one, and you want to see what would happen to your own money if it stays there.
- Why: it named the section the problem is in.

**s04**
- Old: You came to the site's home page to start learning about saving. You pressed the link 'להבין סוג של חיסכון' and it opened this page; you want to find the kind of saving that fits you.
- New: You came to the site's home page to start learning about saving. You have heard of one kind of saving and want the site to explain it to you.
- Why: it named the element the problem is in and told the visitor where that element leads, which is the spot being graded.

**s14**
- Old: You are salaried and your employer offers a keren hishtalmut. You entered your monthly salary of 30,000 to see how much money it would leave you and when you could take it out.
- New: You are salaried and your employer offers a keren hishtalmut. You entered your monthly salary of 30,000 to see what joining would mean for you in money.
- Why: "when you could take it out" steered attention to the sentences of the graphic where two of the graded problems sit; the salary stays because it sets up the screen.

**s18**
- Old: You want a kupat gemel lehashkaa that invests in stock indices. You chose that kind of track and sorted the list by management fee to find the cheapest one.
- New: You want a kupat gemel lehashkaa that invests in stock indices, and you want to find the one with the lowest management fee.
- Why: it named the control the problem is in and the action that reveals it.

**s21**
- Old: Your savings are with Harel and you want to see everything Harel offers, what each track costs and how it has done.
- New: Your savings are with Harel and you want to see what Harel can offer you, what it costs and how it has done.
- Why: "everything" invited a completeness check, which is what the graded problem is about. The same old wording stays on s20, s24 and s25, where it points at nothing graded.

**s27**
- Old: You want to invest regularly in an S&P 500 fund for the long term at the lowest cost. You pressed the label 'מס רק במכירה' on a foreign fund to learn what it means for you.
- New: You want to invest regularly in an S&P 500 fund for the long term at the lowest cost, and you are working out which fund to pick and what it would mean for you.
- Why: it named the element all three graded problems sit in and sent the visitor to read it.

**s28**
- Old: You want to invest regularly in an S&P 500 fund for the long term at the lowest cost. You chose the account 'אינטראקטיב ישראל' to see what investing through it would leave you.
- New: You want to invest regularly in an S&P 500 fund for the long term at the lowest cost, and you want to see how much your money would come to after costs.
- Why: it named the item whose card holds the problem, steering attention to that card.

**s31**
- Old: You want to invest in an index the page does not list. You added your own index, named it 'test', saved it, and now want to see where and how to invest in it most cheaply.
- New: You want to invest in an index the page does not list, so you added your own and called it 'test'. Now you want to see where and how to invest in it most cheaply.
- Why: it echoed the wording of the line the problem sits in. Adding the index and its name stay, because the visitor needs them to read the screen.

**s35**
- Old: Designers drew options for a chart on the keren hishtalmut page. Imagine you are salaried and deciding whether a keren hishtalmut is worth it over a plain securities account, and pick the option you would rather see.
- New: Designers drew options for a chart on the keren hishtalmut page. Imagine you are salaried, your employer offers a keren hishtalmut, and you want to know whether joining is worth it. Pick the option you would rather see.
- Why: it framed the decision around the very part of the drawings the graded problems are about, so the visitor would take that part as given. s34 and s36 keep the old wording, which points at nothing graded there.

**s37**
- Old: Designers drew options for the page that lists every kupat gemel lehashkaa track across all companies. Imagine you want to choose a track for your savings, and pick the option you would rather use.
- New: Designers drew options for the page that lists every kupat gemel lehashkaa track. Imagine you want to choose a track for your savings, and pick the option you would rather use.
- Why: "across all companies" hinted at the topic of the graded problem.

**s38**
- Old: Designers drew options for how companies appear on the page listing every kupat gemel lehashkaa track. Imagine you want to compare companies to choose where to save, and pick the option you would rather use.
- New: Designers drew options for the page that lists every kupat gemel lehashkaa track. Imagine you want to choose where to save, and pick the option you would rather use.
- Why: it pointed at the part of the drawings the problem is in. s39 keeps the old wording, which points at nothing graded there.
