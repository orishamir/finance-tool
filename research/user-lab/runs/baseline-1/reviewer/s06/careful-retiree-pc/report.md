- מאיפה להתחיל: The 'where to start' suggestions are only for employees. There is nothing for a retired person with deposits.

## What it said along the way

Thinking aloud, at the screen:

- Everything is black, with grey writing on it. I put the glasses on and lean in.
- The top says "מדריך" (guide) and "כל סוג של חיסכון והשקעה שהאתר מכסה" (every kind of saving and investing the site covers). Fine, a guide.
- "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון" (two parts to every saving): "איפה הכסף מוחזק" (where the money is kept), and the examples include "פיקדון". That's something I know. Then "מדד מניות". What does the price index have to do with shares? I skip it.
- "מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start): "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם" (employees whose employer pays in). That's not me, I'm retired. "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן כספית" (money that has to stay available). I don't know that word.
- "כסף שוטף וקצר" (everyday, short-term money): עובר ושב, פיקדון, תכנית חיסכון. These I know, and the explanations say what I already knew: "הבנק משלם עליו ריבית" (the bank pays interest on it). Good.
- "קרן כספית – קרן נאמנות…" A קרן נאמנות is what fell on me back then. It does say "סיכון נמוך" (low risk), though. Hmm.
- "מק"מ – הלוואה קצרה למדינה, עד שנה, שבנק ישראל מוכר" (a short loan to the state, up to a year, sold by the Bank of Israel). The state and Bank of Israel, that sounds safe. I'll write it down.
- "חיסכון עם הטבת מס" (saving with a tax benefit): קרן השתלמות is through an employer. I skip the pension part, that's behind me.
- "לילדים – חיסכון לכל ילד" (for children – savings for every child). For the grandchildren! But it's the National Insurance that pays in, so that's not my money.
- Second screen: "איגרות חוב ממשלתיות – המדינה מחזירה את הסכום במועד שנקבע, ולרוב משלמת גם ריבית" (government bonds: the state returns the sum on the set date and usually pays interest too). That's what I know as safe. I'll write it down.
- Then "במה הכסף מושקע" (what the money is invested in): MSCI, S&P, נאסד"ק, ת"א-35… English letters and a lot of "מדד". That's shares, not for me. My eyes are tired.
- "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה / לא צמודה" (index-linked / not linked). I read it, but I'm not sure what "צמודה למדד" means here.
- At the bottom, in small print: "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי" (a personal, non-commercial site). Who is behind it? It's not a bank.
- The third screen is just black, there's nothing on it.
