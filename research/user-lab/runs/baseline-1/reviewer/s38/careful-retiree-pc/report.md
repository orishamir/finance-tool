- All the screens: Black background with grey writing. Many of the small labels are pale and hard to read even when enlarged.
- Screen 1, the first list: The first option opens with S&P 500 and shares. Someone looking for safe saving leaves at the very first screen.
- The lists in options 1 and 2: Too many numbers on every line: two fees, two percentages, a circle, returns and assets. It makes me anxious.
- Screen 4, option 3: The colours in the table are close browns, and I couldn't tell which means expensive and which cheap.
- Screens 3–4: The safe tracks (כספי, אג"ח) are at the bottom of each company, after the shares, and I almost didn't get to them.
- The track names and the column headings: English and words like מט"ח and חשיפה, with no explanation.
- Screen 1 at the top: It isn't clear that the three buttons at the top are three separate drawings further down the page.
