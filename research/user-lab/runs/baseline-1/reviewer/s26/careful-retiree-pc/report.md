## What it said along the way

Psagot is a name I know, so I'll give it that much. But I'm not opening anything yet. I'll ask you first, or go and ask at the bank. I'm fairly sure about the prices I read, much less sure what they would mean for us. Two things decided it. There's nothing on this page about a deposit or interest. And what Psagot writes in its advertisements is not what its own papers say.

**What I read, in order:**

1. **The heading.** "פסגות ניירות ערך (פסגות טרייד)". Psagot, I've heard of them, so I kept reading. The page is black with grey writing, and that alone made it hard.
2. **"העמלות" (the fees):**
   - Buying and selling "מניות ואיגרות חוב, קרן סל" costs **0.07%**, at least **2.00 ₪** each time.
   - "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך" is **0.03% "לרבעון"**. I read it as 0.03% every three months. At first the word "פיקדון" made me think of my own deposits, but I don't think it's the same thing.
   - Currency exchange appears twice: "1.5 אגורות לדולר" and also "0.5%". Under each it says "המסמכים לא קובעים לו תקרה". If I understand that, there's no upper limit, and that I don't like.
   - "דמי חשבון קבועים 0.00 ₪ לחודש". That one is nice.
3. **"מה אפשר לקנות כאן" (what you can buy here).**
   - "יחידות של קרן נאמנות" – "אפשר". I know what a קרן נאמנות is, and that's exactly what fell on me years ago.
   - "קרן סל" – "אפשר". I don't know what that is.
   - "ניירות ערך זרים" – "לא ידוע", on orange. There's a long paragraph under it in small writing. I read the start and gave up.
   - I looked for "פיקדון" or "ריבית" and found neither. So nothing here is as safe as my deposits.
4. **"מה כתוב בפרסום, ומה במסמכים" (the ads against the documents).** This is what worried me most:
   - The ad says "פטור מלא מדמי ניהול ללא הגבלת זמן", no management fees at all, for as long as you like. But the documents say "דמי משמרת 0.12% גבייה רבעונית 0.03%".
   - The ad says "ללא עמלות המרה", no exchange fees. The documents say 1.5 agorot per dollar.
   - The ad says no fee on mutual funds. The documents say 0.07% with a minimum of 2 ₪.
   - Each one has a little orange label, "המסמך אומר אחרת", meaning the document says otherwise. If they write one thing in the ad and another in the papers, why should I trust them?
5. **The rest.** You need at least **10,000 ₪** to open an account, and the supervisor is "רשות ניירות ערך", which sounds official. That's where I stopped.
