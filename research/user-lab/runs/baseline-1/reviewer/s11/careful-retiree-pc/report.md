## Answer

It seems worth having: my employer adds up to 7.5% of my salary and I put in 2.5%, and after 6 years from the first payment I can take it all out without tax (3 years if I've reached retirement age). But I didn't understand what the money is invested in, so I'd ask the bank which option is the safe one, and about the fee of up to 2% a year.

Confidence: fairly sure; gave up: false

## What it said along the way

The way I'd tell it to my son:

"I think it's worth it. Your employer puts in up to 7.5% of your salary and you put in 2.5%, so it's mostly their money. After 6 years you can take it all out without tax. I'm fairly sure of that much, because the page says it clearly at the top and again further down. What I didn't understand is what the money is actually put into. Before signing anything I'd ask the clerk at the bank, or you, which option in the fund is the safe one."

**What I looked at, in order**

1. **Screen 1.** The big heading "קרן השתלמות" and the sentence under it, "חיסכון שהמעסיק ואתם מפקידים אליו מהשכר… אחרי תקופה שקבעה החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס". That's in plain Hebrew and it's what I came for.
   - Under "מה זה" I read "עד 7.5% … ועד 2.5% מכם" and "6 שנים אחרי התשלום הראשון אפשר למשוך הכול בפטור ממס".
   - Under "למה משתמשים בזה" I read "הפקדות המעסיק קיימות רק כאן". So it's money you'd lose if you didn't open one.
   - Then the box "מה הנעילה של קרן ההשתלמות קונה לכם?" starts with "מדד S&P 500" in English letters, and green and yellow dots. I didn't follow it after that. There was a slider bar with numbers 0 to 12, which I didn't touch.
2. **Screen 2.** There were lots of numbers. The first big one I saw was "17,370 ₪". There's a table with "עלויות" and "מס", and then a minus sign: "‎-342.02 ₪". That one frightened me. I skimmed this part.
   - What I did hold on to: "הנעילה היא 6 שנים… למי שהגיע לגיל פרישה… רק 3 שנים", and further down, in large letters, "מתי הכסף פנוי — כשעוברות 6 שנים", and "עד 4 ימי עסקים" to get the money.
   - "מי מציע ומי מפקח" says it's under the official regulator, "בפיקוח רשות שוק ההון". That calms me.
3. **Screen 3.** Under "כמה זה עולה": "דמי ניהול… עד 2% בשנה" from all the money saved. That's not nothing, and I'd ask the bank about it.
   - The tax cards, then the orange warning box: "משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה". In the same box: "47%".
   - Under "סיכונים": "השווי עולה ויורד עם ההשקעות". So it's not a deposit. It goes up and down.
4. **Screen 4.** This one was just the list of words, "מונחים". I glanced at it and stopped there. I was tired.
