## What it said along the way

**What I took from it:** A פיקדון means you leave money in the bank for a set time, from one day to a few years, and the bank pays you ריבית. The example uses 1.8% a year, and they take 15% tax on the interest. What you give up is getting at the money. You can only take it out at the end of the period, or on certain dates if the deposit has "תחנת יציאה". If you "break" it early there's a fine, and it can eat your interest and sometimes part of what you put in. The page doesn't say how big that fine is. So I'm not putting my 30,000 there yet. I'm not very sure about any of this.

**The one thing that decided it:** The big number. 68,270 ₪ was the first figure I saw, and under it "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000". So it looked like I put in 70 and get back 68 after 10 years, which means I lose money in a deposit. There was a "בכסף של היום" button that was already selected. Maybe that's why the number is lower, but nothing next to the number said so, and I didn't press "בשקלים של העתיד".
