**My answer:** Harel has loads of stuff: tax-free savings (קרן השתלמות), pension, a savings plan for kids, and the one the page opens on, קופת גמל להשקעה, with 16 tracks. The fees are all about the same, around half a percent (0.54% to 0.61%), so fees won't decide it. For how they've done, "מניות" is way ahead at 24.04% a year over 3 years, and "עוקב מדד S&P 500" is the one my friends talk about, at 13.7%. I'd pick מניות, or S&P if I wanted the safe-sounding one.
