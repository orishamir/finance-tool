## What it said along the way

Here's my answer: it's worth having. It's a fund where the employer puts in money on top of my salary and I put in a bit too, and after 6 years I can take it all out tax-free. I'm fairly sure. What decided it was the bar chart showing the employer adding "+54,000 ₪" against my "18,000 ₪".

**What I looked at, in order**
- **Top:** the big heading "קרן השתלמות" and the line under it, which says the employer and I both put money in and after a set time I can take it out tax-free. That's exactly what I came for, so I stayed.
- **"מה זה":** "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון". So it's 6 years. There's also something about 3 years if you reach retirement age or for studies, which doesn't apply to me. Taking it out before 6 years means paying tax, and I saw "31%".
- **"למה משתמשים בזה":** I read only the first line: "הפקדת המעסיק מגיעה רק לכאן". The rest was bubbles like "S&P 500 מדד" and "מסלול כללי" that I didn't touch.
- **The bar chart:** the top row has "לפני 6 שנים: מס" on one side and "מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס" on the other. The big chart below is set to 5 years: "הפקדתם 18,000 ₪", "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪", "+8,487 ₪", "המס לקח −13,974 ₪", "נשאר בידיים 66,513 ₪". That's when it clicked: the employer puts in three times what I do. At 5 years tax takes about 14,000, so I just wait the 6 years.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא":** a card says "מתי הכסף פנוי: כשעוברות 6 שנים" and another says "עד 4 ימי עסקים". That confirmed the 6 years.
