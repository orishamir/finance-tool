## Answer

From what I understood, a keren hishtalmut is savings that the employer and I both pay into from the salary, and after 6 years from the first deposit I can take it out without income tax or capital gains tax. If I take it out earlier I pay tax. It sounds worth having because the employer adds money, but I'll ask the bank clerk, and you, whether I can put it somewhere safe, because the example on the site was in something that goes up and down and had a red minus.

Confidence: fairly sure; gave up: false
