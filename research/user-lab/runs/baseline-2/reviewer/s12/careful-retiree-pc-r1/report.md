I've decided to take it. My employer puts in money on top of my salary, and the page says the money is free of tax after 6 years. I'm fairly sure about that much. I'm not sure what it gets invested in, so I'll ask the clerk or you before I choose anything.

**What I looked at, in order**
- **Screen 1:** the title "קרן השתלמות" and the line under it. That's the part that told me what it is: "חיסכון שהמעסיק ואתם מפקידים אליו מהשכר". After "6 שנים אחרי התשלום הראשון" you can take the money out without tax. For someone at retirement age, or taking it out for a course ("לצורך השתלמות"), it's 3 years. You can take it out earlier: "הכסף לא נעול, אבל המשיכה ממוסה". The employer puts in up to "7.5%" and I put in up to "2.5%".
- **Screen 2:** a row of buttons with "S&P 500" and "אג"ח ממשלתית". I only know the government bonds. Then a box headed "מה הנעילה של קרן השתלמות קונה לכם?" with long lines full of English letters. I read it two or three times and moved on.
- **Screen 3:** two coloured bars, "17,370 ₪" against "16,955 ₪". The 17,370 stayed with me. Under them was a table of "עלויות" and "מס", which I didn't read. There was a "−342.02" with a minus sign. That worried me, because it sounded like it could come out less, and I don't know less than what.
- **Screen 4:** "שווה לדעת" said again: "מתי אפשר למשוך מקרן השתלמות בלי מס – 6 שנים". It also said the employer can't make you join a particular fund.
- **Screen 5:** big clear boxes. "מתי הכסף פנוי – כשעוברות 6 שנים", and the money reaches you within "עד 4 ימי עסקים". This was the easiest screen to read.
- **Screen 6:** a table of costs, then "מיסוי". It says "אין מס על הרווחים" while the money stays in, and it's tax-free when you take it out after the waiting period. Above a certain amount you pay "25%".
