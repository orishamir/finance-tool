## Answer

A פיקדון means you lock your money in the bank for a set time and they pay you interest. The example says 1.8% a year, and if you take the money out early the bank can fine you, sometimes taking all the interest and even some of what you put in. Going by the page's own example it looks like you end up with less than you put in, so I'm not running to open one. I don't really get why that happens.
