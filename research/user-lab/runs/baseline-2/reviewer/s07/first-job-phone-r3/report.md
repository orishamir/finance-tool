## Answer

A פיקדון means the bank holds your money for a set time and pays interest, and you only get it back at the end or on an exit date. Breaking it early costs a fine the page doesn't put a number on, and it can eat the interest and even part of what you put in. The example also seemed to show me ending with 68,270 ₪ after putting in 70,000 ₪, so I'm not keen.

## What it said along the way

**What I'd tell my friend:** A פיקדון means you give the bank your money for a set time and it pays you ריבית. You can only take the money out at the end, or on set dates they call "תחנת יציאה". If you break it early there's a fine, and it can wipe out the interest and even some of the money you put in. The page didn't say how big that fine is. I'm fairly sure I got the basics. What decided it was the red box: "שבירת פיקדון לפני הזמן: האתר לא מחשב אותה". So only money I definitely won't touch goes in there. Also, the example with 1.8% interest showed "נשאר לכם 68,270 ₪" against "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪". That looks to me like I end up with less than I put in, which kind of killed my enthusiasm.
