## What it said along the way

I think it's worth having, and I'll ask you and the payroll office before I sign anything.

So, how it went. As far as I understood: my employer puts in up to 7.5% of my salary and I put in up to 2.5%. After 6 years I can take it all out without paying tax. If I take it out sooner I can, but "אבל יקר", it's expensive, because it gets taxed. That much I'm fairly sure of. Whether it's worth it, I think yes. It's money the employer adds that I don't get any other way. The page says "כמעט תמיד הצעד הראשון", that it's almost always the first step. What I don't know is what they'd actually do with the money. That's why I'm asking you.

**What I looked at, in order:**
1. **The top: "קרן השתלמות", "מה זה" and "למה משתמשים בזה".** The opening line said "חיסכון", savings, so I kept reading. That's where I got the 7.5%, the 2.5% and the 6 years. Right under it were round buttons that said "S&P 500" and "מדד", and that made me nervous.
2. **"מה מייחד".** A dark box full of English letters, fund names and something you drag along a line. I didn't touch it. I did read the line that said "הרווחים פטורים ממס רק אם מושכים אחרי 6 שנים".
3. **The next screen.** All numbers with dotted lines under them: 2,402.23 ₪, 2,922.17 ₪, and further down "−342.02 ₪". The minus sign stopped me cold. I also saw "6 שנים ... למי שהגיע לגיל פרישה ... רק 3 שנים", which I think means that if you've reached retirement age it's only 3 years. Mostly I backed away from that screen.
4. **"שווה לדעת" and "כסף נכנס ויוצא".** These were clear and big: "מתי הכסף פנוי – כשעוברות 6 שנים". It also said the money arrives within 4 working days.
5. **"כמה זה עולה".** Fees of "עד 2% בשנה" on the money that has built up, and "דמי יציאה – אין", no charge for leaving. I didn't know Altshuler or Harel took that much.
6. **"מיסוי".** The orange box: "משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה". There was also "47%", which scared me.

After that I stopped. I only saw the headings "סיכונים ומה לא מוגן" and "מה האתר לקח בחשבון". The first one did say "אין ערבות של המדינה", meaning the state doesn't guarantee it, and that bothered me.

**What I didn't understand:** S&P 500, מדד (on the news it's the price index, so what is it here?), מסלול השקעה, מסלול כללי, אג"ח צמודה / לא צמודה, חשבון ניירות ערך, הפקדה מוטבת, משכורת קובעת, "הרווח הריאלי", ותק, נאמנות, and the codes like TXH-09, KH-15 and PF-18. I also don't know the name of the company that runs the site, "מקור". Is it official?

**What I couldn't read:** small grey lines like "ההכנסה: דוגמה של האתר" and "גררו את הסמן...". Also the gray-green links on the left, "הצגת החוקים והכללים". The whole page is black with pale letters, which was hard on my eyes even with my glasses.

**Where I got tired or worried:** on the third screen, with all those numbers and the minus. If not for the clear headings further down, I would have closed it there.
