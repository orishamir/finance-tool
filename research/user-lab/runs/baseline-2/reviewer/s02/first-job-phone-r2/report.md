What I still don't know is what it costs. The "כמה זה עולה" table doesn't have one actual number in it. It just keeps saying "אין תקרה בחוק" (no legal cap). So I'd want to see the fee in shekels before I buy. To buy it I'd go to my bank's app or "חשבון ניירות ערך" (securities account) and enter the fund's number. I didn't find which fund or which number.

**Where I looked, in order:**
1. **Top of the page.** Heading "קרן כספית" and the subtitle: low risk, "לא יחזיר" (no guarantee). I read the first lines of "מה זה". You buy through the bank and get the money "ביום המסחר הבא" (next trading day).
2. **"למה משתמשים בזה".** "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן חירום" (money that has to stay available, like an emergency fund). That's exactly my question. There was something about tax, 15% vs 25%, which I didn't really follow.
3. **Comparison bars.** 0% / 3.25% / 3.25% / 1.8%. This is where it clicked.
4. **"שווה לדעת".** A line saying "התשואה הצפויה... לא מובטחת" (the expected return isn't guaranteed). I scrolled past "מי מציע ומי מפקח". From "כסף נכנס ויוצא" I only took "בכל יום מסחר".
5. **"כמה זה עולה".** A table full of fee names (דמי ניהול, עמלת קנייה, הוספה…) with no amounts. Annoying.
6. **"מיסוי" and "סיכונים ומה לא מוגן".** I only caught "אין ערבות של המדינה" (no state guarantee) and "אין ביטוח" (no insurance). That's a bit scary.
