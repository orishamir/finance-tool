## What it said along the way

Found it, I think. On the home page there's a box called "להבין סוג של חיסכון" with the line "מה זה, כמה זה עולה ואיך זה ממוסה", which was exactly what I wanted. It took me to a "מדריך" page where everything is in cards, and way down there's "מדד S&P 500", the thing everyone at work goes on about. The short line says it's 500 big American companies and the money goes in "בדולרים דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו". I'm fairly sure, but I only read the short card, not the full page behind it.

What made it click was the bit near the top, "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון". "איפה הכסף מוחזק" is like a קרן השתלמות or a "חשבון ניירות ערך". "במה הכסף מושקע" is the S&P part. So the S&P isn't somewhere you put money; it's what the money sits in. I didn't know that.
