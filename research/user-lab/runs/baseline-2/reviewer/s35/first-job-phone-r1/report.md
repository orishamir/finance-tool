## What it said along the way

**My pick: option א, the one marked "מומלץ".** I'm fairly sure. What decided it was the second picture in א, at year 6: under the keren it says "מס במשיכה 0 ₪" and a big **98,738 ₪**, and next to it the other account has only 23,973 ₪. That's when it clicked for me: you wait 6 years and you pay no tax. It also says "כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה 4.57 ₪ בקרן", and the employer's yellow block is huge. So yes, joining is worth it.

**What I looked at, in order:**
- **Screen 1:** the heading "קרן השתלמות: בלי כדורים ובלי טבלה" and a small preview with 66,513 ₪ against 19,595 ₪. That 66,513 is the number that stayed in my head.
- **Screen 2 (option א, year 5):** the header "מושכים את הכסף אחרי 5 שנים". On the right: "הפקדות שלכם 18,000", "הפקדות המעסיק 54,000", then the scary red "−13,974 ₪ מס במשיכה", and at the bottom "נשאר בידיים 66,513 ₪". On the left, the "חשבון ניירות ערך" comes to 19,595 ₪.
- **Screen 3:** the same picture at year 6, where the tax drops to 0. That's the one that convinced me.
- **Screen 4 (option ב, "כל חלק בעמודה משלו"):** the steps go up and down. I started reading on the right and hit "נשאר בידיים 66,513" first, then the drops. It's the same story, but it took me longer to follow. On the small account the numbers have no ₪ sign. And there's no year-6 picture like in א, so the tax-free part didn't land for me there.
- **Screen 5** was empty.

**What I skipped:** the orange/grey example text under the first screen (I only caught "12,000 ₪ ברוטו" and the ⚠). I also skipped the tiny lines under the charts about tracks and fees, the "שכירים / עצמאים" toggle and the 1–12 bars at the top. I only noticed that before 6 there's "מס" and after it says "פטור ממס".

**Where I hesitated:** on the red minus with the striped pink at year 5. For a second it put me off. I also wasn't sure the 18,000 is just my 300 ₪ a month, and I'm not sure where the 300/900 comes from. My salary isn't 12,000 gross.

**Words I didn't get:** "חשבון ניירות ערך" (sort of, the stock thing?) and "רווח אחרי דמי ניהול", which I understood as profit after fees but I'm not sure.

I wouldn't have closed the page. The big numbers kept me going.
