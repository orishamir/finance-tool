I did get what I came for:
- **How it works:** you leave the money in the bank for a set time, anything from a day to a few years. The bank pays interest. The interest can be "קבועה", "משתנה", or tied to the "מדד". At the end the money goes back to your עו"ש, or it starts again automatically, depending on what you picked.
- **What it pays:** the example uses "1.8%". Tax is "15%" of the interest, and it comes off automatically.
- **What you give up:** you can't touch the money until the end. If you break it early there's a "קנס" (fine), and the page says "האתר לא מחשב אותה", so they don't work it out for you. It also says "אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות": no law gives you the money back if the bank goes under. That one scared me a bit.
