**What I took from it:**
- You leave the money in the bank for a set time and they pay you interest on it. The rate is either fixed or it moves.
- The example used 1.8% a year, at Leumi.
- The bank takes off 15% of the interest as tax by itself.
- The catch is that you only get the money back at the end ("בסוף התקופה שנקבעה בהסכם").
- If you take it out early ("שבירת פיקדון") you need the bank's OK and you pay a fine. The fine can wipe out the interest and even some of the money you put in.
- They never say how big that fine is in shekels. Exit fees just say "לפי התעריפון של הגורם המציע", meaning it depends on the bank's own price list.
- "אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות" freaked me out. It says that if a bank goes bust, no law gives you your money back.
