## What it said along the way

So basically: you put money in the bank for a set time and they pay you interest, around 1.8% a year in their example. The catch is it's locked until the end. If you break it early there's a penalty, and it can wipe out the interest and even some of what you put in. I'm fairly sure I got the idea. The thing that stuck with me was their example: 70,000 ₪ in over 10 years, and "נשאר לכם 68,270 ₪". So with prices going up 2% and interest at 1.8%, I'd end up with less than I put in. I'm not opening one today.
