# s32: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 38 s, $0.19

I noticed straight away that the three pictures aren't one long page. They show the same table of four funds drawn three different ways, so I compared them as three choices.

## 1. My answer
**I'd pick the third picture (screen-03).** I'm fairly sure, about 7 out of 10.

All three give the same funds and the same fees. The only difference is the last column, on the left:
- **First picture:** says "מניות עד 120%" and "מט"ח עד 10%". The "120%" in stocks frightened me. How can more than all my money be in stocks? That sounds like borrowing and risk.
- **Second picture:** puts the same numbers in small grey text, "פרופיל 4A: מניות עד 120%, מט"ח עד 10%". "פרופיל 4A" means nothing to me. This picture also drops the bottom line that explains what the ceiling is.
- **Third picture:** keeps only "חשיפה מרבית למט"ח", meaning how much can be in foreign currency. That is the part I care about: how much of my money moves with the dollar. It also keeps the explanation at the bottom.

So with the third picture I get less to worry about and nothing I need is missing.

## 2. What I actually used
- The heading "קרנות סל · 4 קרנות" and the grey sentence under it
- **"שם הקרן"** (fund name)
- **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is in): "עוקבת אחרי מדד ת"א-125" and so on
- **"דמי ניהול לפי הדוח של הקרן"** (fees). This is the column I looked at most.
- **"חשיפה מרבית למט"ח"** (third picture only)
- The two lines at the bottom
- **"מספר נייר"**, which I'd write down to give my bank

## 3. Going down the page, block by block
- **"קרנות סל 4 קרנות" with the little arrow:** skimmed it. Fine.
- **"רק הקרנות שהאתר אסף עליהן דוח, לא כל הקרנות של החברה":** read it twice. It's honest, but it worried me. So there are other funds I'm not seeing? Maybe a cheaper one?
- **Fund name, e.g. "אי.בי.אי. סל (4A) ת"א 125":** used it. But the "(4A)" and "(4D)" codes mean nothing to me.
- **The little grey tags:**
  - "מחזיקה את המניות" (holds the shares): reassuring, I liked it.
  - "חשיפה לדולר" (exposed to the dollar): understood it.
  - "מס גם על שינויי המטבע" (tax also on currency changes): **this is about my money**, so I read it carefully. I'd want to know how much tax.
  - "עסקת החלף": confused me. I don't know what it is, and it sounds like something I wouldn't trust.
- **"מספר נייר" and "בדף הקרן במאיה":** I used the number. I skipped the Maya link because I didn't know what "מאיה" is or where it would take me.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע":** used it. "עוקבת אחרי מדד S&P 500" is clear enough. "MSCI AC World" is not. The coloured text and the "<" made me think it's clickable, but I didn't know what would open.
- **Fees:** used them the most. See section 4.
- **Exposure column:** in the first picture it confused and frightened me. In the third I understood it.
- **Bottom lines:**
  - "כל הקרנות כאן לא מחלקות: הדיבידנד והריבית מושקעים בקרן מחדש" means I won't get payments into my account and the money stays in the fund. Good to know, I read it.
  - "החשיפה המרבית היא התקרה שהקרן מצהירה עליה בשם שלה... לא מה שהיא מחזיקה היום" means this is a limit, not what the fund holds today. That calmed me down a little about the 120%, but I only found it at the very end.

## 4. Inside the table
- **What I looked at:**
  - In the fees, the three lines "קבוע" (fixed), "משתנים" (variable) and "נאמן" (trustee).
  - I added them up in my head. Ta-125 comes to about 0.46%. Ta-90 is about 0.21%. S&P 500 is about 1.1%. MSCI is about 0.97%.
  - So the Tel Aviv funds are much cheaper. The 0.8% "קבוע" on the foreign funds stood out.
  - In the third picture's exposure column, the difference between 10% and 120% foreign currency.
- **What I ignored:** the icons inside the tags, the underlines under the numbers (are they links?), and the colours. I took the turquoise to mean "you can click".

1. The "עסקת החלף" tags. They frighten me and tell me nothing.
2. The stock exposure line, "מניות עד 120%". It only scares me.
3. The "בדף הקרן במאיה" link, or at least a plainer name for it.

I'd keep the fees, what the money is in, the fund number, "מס גם על שינויי המטבע", and the line about dividends.
