# Me, 24, looking at Harel's page

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd lean toward the **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** track in Harel's **"קופת גמל להשקעה"**. It's the thing everyone told me to buy, the fee is **0.56%**, and it shows **13.7%** a year over 3 years and **11.92%** over 5 years.

The page also made me hesitate. The row called **"מניות"** has a slightly lower fee (**0.55%**) and much higher numbers: **24.04%** over 3 years and **14.64%** over 5 years. It also has the most money in it (**7.3 מיליארד ₪**). So now I'm not sure the S&P 500 is the best choice even inside Harel.

I'm also not sure this is even the right product. My savings at Harel come through my job. I don't know if that's **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"**, **"קרן השתלמות"** or this **"קופת גמל להשקעה"**, which the page opened on by default.

**How sure: about 4 out of 10.** I'd want to ask someone or check my own Harel account before moving anything.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The title **"הראל"** and the sentence right under it, which says the fees are what was really charged in 2025 and the returns are up to September 2026.
- The row of product buttons under **"כל המוצרים של הראל"**. This is how I saw everything Harel offers.
- The **"במה הכסף מושקע"** buttons, but only to spot that there's a **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** track.
- The line **"16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול ממוצע: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"**.
- The big table, mainly the track name, the fee and the return columns.
- The two notes under the table: the legal limit (**"התקרה בחוק: 1.05% מההיסכון, 4% מההפקדה"**) and the warning about past returns.

## 4. Table: what I looked at and what I ignored

- **"המסלול"** (name): looked at it. That's how I found the S&P 500 and "מניות" rows.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**: looked at the top number (**"מההיסכון"**). I ignored the bottom number (**"מההפקדה"**), which is **0%** everywhere. Since every fee is between 0.54% and 0.61%, this column didn't help me choose.
- **"קופה"** (numbers like **15283, 13414**): ignored. I don't know what they're for. The little arrow icon next to them suggests a link, but I didn't know why I'd click it.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"**: half-used. I understood "מניות" (stocks). I didn't get "מט"ח". The teal/yellow colours and the ring drawing I ignored. I just noticed the S&P 500 row is full rings with 99%/99%.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**: this is the column I looked at most. A lot of rows just say **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** with no number. I guess those tracks are new, so I couldn't compare them.
- **"כסף מנוהל"**: glanced at it. A big number (**7.3 מיליארד, 2.3 מיליארד**) made the track feel safer and more popular. I didn't otherwise use it.
- **The ">" arrows on the left** of each row: didn't click them.
- **The sort arrows and ⓘ icons in the headers**: ignored.
- The screenshots skip some rows between **"קיימות"** and **"הלכה"**, so I didn't see every one of the 16 tracks.
