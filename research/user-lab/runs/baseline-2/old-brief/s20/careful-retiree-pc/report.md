# Reading the Harel page as myself (61, savings with Harel)

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

For now I would **move nothing**. I'd write down a few numbers and phone Harel, or my agent, before deciding anything. I'm fairly sure that's right, but I'm not sure about much else.

Here's why:
- **The fees are almost the same everywhere.** Under the line "16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול מההחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%", every track charges between about half a percent and 0.6% of my savings a year, and "0% מההפקדות" on deposits. The difference between the cheapest and dearest is 0.07%. On 500,000 ₪ that's about 350 ₪ a year. So fees shouldn't decide anything for me.
- **Most tracks have no history.** In the returns column most rows say only "פחות מ־3 שנים / פחות מ־5 שנים". Only a few show real numbers:
  - "מניות": 24.04% (3 years), 14.64% (5 years)
  - "עוקב מדד S&P 500": 13.7%, 11.92%
  - "הלכה": 11.42%, 5.73%
  - "כספי (שקלי)": 4.25%, 3.08%
  - "קיימות": 1.78% (3 years)
- **The page warns against trusting those numbers.** It says "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך". So the returns are before fees and tax, and they don't predict the future.
- **The best returns come from the riskiest tracks.** "מניות" shows 94% in shares. At 61 I'm not jumping into the track with the biggest number when I may need the money in a few years.
- **This may not even be my product.** The page opened on "קופת גמל להשקעה". I'm not sure my money is in that and not in "קרן השתלמות" or "פוליסת חיסכון". So I don't yet know if this table is about *my* money at all.

## 4. Inside the table

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"**: the track names, plus the small grey labels like "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" and "הלכה יהודי".
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**: only the top number, "מההחיסכון". The second number, "מההפקדות", was 0% every time, so after a few rows I stopped checking it.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"**: I read the "מניות" percentage on every row, because that tells me how risky the track is.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**: the 3-year and 5-year numbers.
- **"כסף מנוהל"**: I glanced at it. 7.3 billion ₪ in "מניות" feels safer than "13 מיליון ₪" in "עוקב מדדי אג"ח".

**What I ignored or didn't understand:**
- **The "קופה" column of numbers (15283, 8522...).** I don't know what they're for. Is that my account number? And what does the little icon next to them do?
- **The coloured rings.** Teal and yellow. I gathered teal is shares and yellow is foreign currency from the dots, but the rings added nothing the percentages didn't already say.
- **The arrows at the far left of each row.** I didn't click them.
- **The "?" icons and the sort arrows in the headers.**
- **I didn't see every row.** The screenshots skip some, for example "כללי". One row near the bottom is cut off.

**Two things looked odd to me:**
- "קיימות" has 93% in shares but only 1.78% a year.
- "אג"ח סחיר" shows 0% shares and 0% foreign currency, but "פחות מ־3 שנים", so I can't judge it.

- **"PF-10".**
- **"הוצאות ישירות (עמלות מסחר, משמורת ועוד)".** How much is that in shekels?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Which track my money is in right now, and what fee *I* pay.** I may have a personal discount, and the table shows only an average.
- **How bad each track got in a bad year.** At my age the big question is how much I could lose next year, not just the average return.
- **The real return after fees and tax.** I'd also like it in shekels, for example "100,000 ₪ became...".
- **Something for my age.** For example: "if you need the money within 5 years, these tracks are calmer".
- **How Harel compares with other companies on the same track.** There's a link, but I didn't go there.
- **Whether moving between tracks costs anything or has tax consequences.**
