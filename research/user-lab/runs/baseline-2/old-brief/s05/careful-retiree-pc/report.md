# Reading the guide page as a careful 61-year-old

## 1. My answer: what I'd do and how sure I am

I'd look at three things, in this order:

1. **My pension and קרן השתלמות.** I'm 61, so retirement is close, and the "פרישה" section is about my money. I'd ring my fund to find out which kind I have, and when I can take the השתלמות money out "בפטור ממס" (tax-free).
2. **Money I can't risk: קרן כספית, פיקדון or מק"מ.** The cards say a קרן כספית invests "רק בנכסים קצרי טווח עם סיכון נמוך". That sounds like what I want for money I may need soon.
3. **קופת גמל להשקעה, maybe.** "אפשר למשוך בכל עת" (you can withdraw at any time) appeals to me. But the card also says "במשיכה משלמים מס על הרווח" (you pay tax on the profit when you withdraw), so I'd need to know whether my age changes that.

I would **not** go near the index cards (S&P 500, נאסד"ק 100 and the rest). Nothing on the page tells me how risky they are for someone my age.

**How sure am I?** Not very, maybe 4 out of 10. The page told me what exists. It didn't tell me what each one costs, how safe it is, or which ones suit someone close to retirement.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** (top of the page). The idea that every saving has two parts, "איפה הכסף מוחזק" (where the money is held) and "במה הכסף מושקע" (what it's invested in), finally made sense to me.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל"** (the three green links).
- **"כסף שוטף וקצר"**, mainly the קרן כספית and מק"מ cards.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"**: the קרן השתלמות and קופת גמל להשקעה cards.
- **"פרישה"**: all three cards, especially the "בשפה פשוטה" (in plain language) lines.
- **The footer line** "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (a personal site, not commercial, no marketing links). That made me trust the page more. So did "נתונים נכון ל-26.10.2026" (data correct as of 26.10.2026), because at least I know the information is recent.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** (מקור, מדריך, שאלות שכולם שואלים, חברות ומסלולים, השוואה למטרה, השקעה במדד): **skimmed.** "שאלות שכולם שואלים" (questions everyone asks) tempts me. I stayed on this page as asked.
- **"מדריך" title and intro** ("לכל אחד אותו מבנה, כדי שיהיה קל להשוות", every product has the same layout so they're easy to compare): **read it.** It was a fair promise. In practice the cards are too short to compare properly.
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון": used, the most useful block.** "הוא קובע מי מחזיק את הכסף, מה המס ומתי אפשר למשוך" (it decides who holds the money, what the tax is and when you can withdraw) is exactly what I care about.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל": used, but it isn't aimed at me.** "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם" (employees whose employer pays in for them) and "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר" (the basis for every salaried saver) are for working people. There was nothing for someone near retirement.
- **"איפה הכסף מוחזק" → "כסף שוטף וקצר":** the top row (חשבון עובר ושב, פיקדון, תכנית חיסכון) was cut off between my screens, and I saw only the titles. **Used** קרן כספית and מק"מ. **Skimmed** קרן כספית מתחדשת; I don't see how it differs from a regular one.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס": used.** But "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק" (after a period set by law) annoyed me. What period? Say the number.
- **"פרישה": used, and the most important block for me.** "קרן פנסיה שחוסכת לקצבה חודשית בפרישה" (a pension fund that saves toward a monthly payment in retirement) was clear. "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים" **confused me**: "כדי לקבל ממנה קצבה, מעבירים את הכסף לקרן פנסיה או לחברת ביטוח" (to get a monthly payment from it, you move the money to a pension fund or an insurance company). Do I need to do that now, at 61?
- **"לילדים": skipped.** My children are grown up. (The cards were also cut off in my screenshots.)
- **"לבחירה עצמית"** (do it yourself): **skimmed.** "חשבון ניירות ערך... אצל ברוקר בחו"ל" (a securities account... with a broker abroad) is not for me. "בניהול אישי" (self-managed) means I'd pick the investments myself, which I'm not going to do.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע": partly confused me.** "מסלול כללי" (general track) said "ואינו מתחייב לטווח מניות" (and doesn't commit to a range for shares). I read it twice and still don't know whether that means more risk or less. The אג"ח cards (צמודה / לא צמודה) are where I got lost.
- **Index cards** (MSCI AC World, MSCI World, נאסד"ק 100, S&P 500, ת"א-35, ת"א-90, ת"א-125): **skimmed, then skipped.** They all say the same thing: "הכסף מושקע... דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו" (the money is invested... through a fund that follows the index).
- **מק"מ shown a second time, and מזומן שקלי:** **confused me.** Why is מק"מ in both halves of the page? Is it a place to hold money or a thing to invest in?
- **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** (what the site doesn't cover yet): **noticed it, didn't open it.** I'd like it if the page told me straight away what's missing.
- **Footer links: skipped.**
- **Screens 4 and 5** were almost the same, so I read that part once.

## 4. Lists, labels and colours

There are no tables or charts, only cards.

- **Looked at:** the bold card titles, the descriptions, and especially the **"בשפה פשוטה:"** lines in the פרישה and לילדים cards. Those helped most, and I wished every card had one.
- **Looked at:** the green link words in "מאיפה להתחיל" (קרן השתלמות, קרן חדשה מקיפה, קרן כספית).
- **Ignored:** the little arrows on each card, the search icon, the round "back to top" button, and the grey sub-lines under the big headings.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה"** (index-linked government bonds): "צמודות למדד המחירים לצרכן" (linked to the consumer price index), "קופון קבוע" (fixed coupon), "הקרן חוזרת בפדיון" (the principal comes back at redemption). Too much jargon at once.
- **"קופון", "פדיון", "תשואת המק"מ"** (coupon, redemption, the מק"מ yield): no explanation for any of them.
- **The מק"מ card:** "נמכר במכרז חודשי במחיר נמוך מהשווי הנקוב" (sold at a monthly auction below face value) is meaningless to me. Can I buy it, or only banks?
- **"שווי שוק" and "במשקל לפי שווי השוק הסחיר"** (market value, weighted by tradable market value).
- **"MSCI AC World" vs "MSCI World":** the only difference I could see is "בשווקים מתפתחים" (in developing markets). So what?
- **"קרן חדשה מקיפה" vs "קרן חדשה כללית":** I couldn't tell which one I have, or whether that matters.
- **"עד תקרה שנתית"** (up to an annual ceiling): what's the ceiling?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Fees.** There is no mention of דמי ניהול (management fees) on any card. So what does it cost me?
- **Risk.** Which ones can lose money, and how much? Nothing like "safe / medium / risky".
- **Guarantees.** Is my money protected if the bank or fund company fails?
- **My age.** What's different at 60 and over? When can I draw my pension, and what tax applies?
- **Actual numbers:** how many years for the השתלמות, and what the "תקרה" (ceiling) is.
- **Interest or returns**, even a rough range for a פיקדון or a קרן כספית today.
- **A clear "if you're close to retirement, read this" path.**

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. **The seven index cards first.** They are nearly identical and mean nothing to me.
2. **"לבחירה עצמית".**
3. **"לילדים"** (useful for others, not for me).
4. **The second מק"מ card and the אג"ח cards,** unless they're rewritten in plain language.

I'd keep the "שני חלקים" explanation, "מאיפה להתחיל", the פרישה section, the tax-benefit cards, and the safe short-term options.

**A note on the screenshots:** they had small gaps. I couldn't see the descriptions for חשבון עובר ושב, פיקדון and תכנית חיסכון, the end of the children's cards, or the text of the MSCI and נאסד"ק cards.
