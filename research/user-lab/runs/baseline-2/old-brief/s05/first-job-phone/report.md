# s05: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 39 s, $0.16

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd look into three things, in this order:

1. **"קרן השתלמות"** (keren hishtalmut). The page puts it first under "מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start), for "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם" (employees whose employer pays into one). I'm salaried, so this applies to me. The card says that after a set period "אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס" (you can withdraw tax-free). Free money from my employer plus no tax sounds like the best deal on the page. My first step is to ask HR whether I have one.
2. **My pension, "קרן חדשה מקיפה"**. The page calls it "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר" (the basis for every salaried saver). I think I already have one through work, but I've never looked at it.
3. **The S&P 500 thing I keep hearing about.** I found the "מדד S&P 500" card. It says the money is "מושקע בדולרים דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו" (invested in dollars through a fund that tracks it). For buying it myself, I'd probably need a **"חשבון ניירות ערך"** (a brokerage account) "בבנק, בבית השקעות או אצל ברוקר בחו"ל" (at a bank, an investment house, or a broker abroad). There's also **"קופת גמל להשקעה"**, which looks like a place where I could put my own money and still take it out "בכל עת" (any time).

**How sure am I?** I'm fairly sure about the keren hishtalmut and pension being the places to start. I'm not sure how to actually get into the S&P 500. The page lists the index, the brokerage account and the investment gemel, but it never connects them for me. Can I pick the S&P 500 inside my keren hishtalmut? Do I have to open a brokerage account? I can't tell. I'm maybe 60% confident overall.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The intro box **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** (two parts of every saving). It helped a lot: first, where the money sits, and second, what it's invested in. Once I read that, I understood why the S&P 500 isn't in the same list as keren hishtalmut.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל"** (where to start). This was the most useful part by far.
- The **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"** (savings with a tax benefit) cards: keren hishtalmut and kupat gemel lehashkaa.
- The **"פרישה"** (retirement) cards, but only the first one.
- In **"לבחירה עצמית"** (self-chosen), only the "חשבון ניירות ערך" card.
- In **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is invested in), the "מסלול כללי" (general track) and "מדד S&P 500" cards.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** (מקור, מדריך, שאלות שכולם שואלים, חברות ומסלולים, השוואה למטרה, השקעה במדד). I skimmed it. "השקעה במדד" (investing in an index) caught my eye as the place to go next for S&P 500. I didn't click anything.
- **"מדריך" title and subtitle.** Skimmed. It told me this is a list of all the options.
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון".** I used it. It's the most important idea on the page for me. The small line "בכל עמוד של מוצר יש שורה עם מה שאפשר להשקיע בתוכו" (every product page has a line about what you can invest in) told me I'd have to click into a product to see whether it can hold the S&P 500.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל".** Used it, and it's the best part. My only wish is that it were longer.
- **"איפה הכסף מוחזק" → "כסף שוטף וקצר"** (where the money is held, everyday/short-term money). Skimmed it. "קרן כספית" (money market fund) is for money that has to stay available. That's not my question right now. The first row of cards ("חשבון עובר ושב", "פיקדון", "תכנית חיסכון") was mostly cut off between my screenshots; I only saw the word "ריבית" (interest).

- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס".** Used it, and read it closely.
- **"פרישה".** Read the first card. I skimmed "קרן חדשה כללית" and "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים". Having three pension types confused me, and I don't know which one I have.
- **"לילדים".** Skipped it. I don't have kids.

- **"במה הכסף מושקע".** I read "מסלול כללי" and the S&P 500 card. I skipped both "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה / לא צמודה" cards. They're too technical: "קופון", "צמודות למדד המחירים לצרכן" (linked to the consumer price index).

- **"מזומן שקלי"** (shekel cash). Skipped it.
- **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** (what the site doesn't cover yet). Skimmed it. Fine to know.
- **Footer.** Skimmed it. "נתונים נכון ל־26.10.2026" (data correct as of 26.10.2026) made me trust it a bit more, and so did "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (personal site, not commercial, no affiliate links).

## 4. Lists and cards: what I looked at

There are no tables or charts. It's all cards.

- **What I looked at:** the bold card titles, the grey section labels ("חיסכון עם הטבת מס", "פרישה", "לילדים", "לבחירה עצמית"), and the line **"בשפה פשוטה:"** (in simple language) on the retirement and kids cards. That line helped, and I wish every card had it.
- **What I ignored:** the "‹" arrows (I know they're links, but I didn't click), and most of the long grey description text on cards I'd already decided weren't for me.
- **Colours:** I only noticed the turquoise links in "מאיפה להתחיל" and in the footer. Nothing else stood out by colour.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Fees and returns.** There isn't a single number on the page: no percentages, no "this one usually earns X% and costs Y%". I know what a percentage is, and that's exactly what I wanted to compare.
- **Which "place" can hold which "thing".** Can I put the S&P 500 inside my keren hishtalmut, or inside a kupat gemel lehashkaa? The page says that's on each product page, but I wanted to see it here.
- **How much to put where.** Something like: first the hishtalmut, then an emergency fund, then the rest into...
- **Risk.** How likely am I to lose money with each one?
- How do I find out what I already have through work?
