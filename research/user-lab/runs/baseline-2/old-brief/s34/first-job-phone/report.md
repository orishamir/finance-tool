# s34: first-job-phone (old brief)

I'd pick **Option A ("א. מגדלי מטבעות")**. The cards at the very top had already convinced me, though. A was just the drawing that made it feel real.

## 1. My answer
I'd open a keren hishtalmut. I'm about 90% sure.

What did it was **"על כל שקל שלכם נכנסים עוד 3 שקלים מהמעסיק"** (for every shekel of mine, the employer adds 3) and **"כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה 3.70 ₪ בקרן ו-1.09 ₪ בחשבון ניירות ערך"** (each shekel I put in becomes 3.70 in the fund and 1.09 in a securities account). That isn't a close call.

The missing 10% is the orange warning: **"האחוזים האמיתיים כתובים בהסכם העבודה"** (the real percentages are in your employment contract). I don't know if my employer actually gives me 7.5%. I'd have to check before I'd be fully sure.

## 2. What I actually used
- The **three cards at the top**, mostly "הכסף של המעסיק +900 ₪ בחודש" (the employer's money).
- In option A, the **sentence above the coins** ("כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה…"), the two big numbers **66,513 ₪ vs 19,595 ₪**, and the **table under the coins**.
- The **year-6 version** (98,738 vs 23,973), to see what waiting one more year gets me.

## 3. Block by block
- **Title and subtitle:** skimmed. "שרטוט לבחירה" (a draft to choose from) told me nothing.
- **Three cards:** used. The 900 card is the reason I'd sign up. The 32,225 card ("הקפיצה בשנה 6", the jump in year 6) also mattered: I learned not to pull the money out at year 5. The first card ("+1,721 ₪… זה כל מה שהגרפיקה היום מראה") confused me. Why is the fund only 1,721 better? Then I realised it means without the employer, and I stopped caring about it.
- **Small grey/orange assumptions paragraph:** skipped, except for the orange warning. "מסלול ׳מיטב השתלמות עוקב מדד S&P500׳", "קרן ׳ילין לפידות מחקה'", "(4D)" and "טכנולוג'יז בע"מ" were all noise to me.
- **Option A (coins):** used. The bar strip with years 1–12 helped. I clicked 5, then 6, and saw "לפני 6 שנים: מס" (before 6 years: tax) vs "מ-6 שנים: פטור ממס" (from 6 years: tax-free). The coin piles are nice, but I didn't count coins. I just saw that the left pile is huge and the right one is a thin line.
- **The "אותו ציור שנה אחת אחר כך" line and the year-6 version:** used, for the jump.
- **Option B (steps/waterfall):** skimmed. The order made sense to me: the same money in a securities account, then the yellow employer block, then the tax, then what's left. But I nearly missed the "+527" step because it's a thin green line. It gave me nothing new after A.
- **Option C (stacked bars per year):** skimmed. It looks impressive, but there are many colours and the white line took me a moment. The page itself says it: **"קשה יותר להרגיש את היחס מול חשבון ניירות ערך"** (harder to feel the comparison with a securities account). That matched my experience.
- **The "עצמאים" tab** (self-employed): skipped, it's not me.

## 4. Charts and tables
- **Option A table:** I read the rows "ההפקדות שלכם" (my deposits), "הפקדות המעסיק" (the employer's deposits) and "נשאר בידיים" (left in my hands). "הפקדות המעסיק: 54,000 vs אין" (54,000 vs none) was the strongest line on the page. I looked at "מס במשיכה −13,974" (tax on withdrawal) and got scared until I saw that it disappears at year 6. I ignored "רווח אחרי דמי ניהול" (profit after management fees).
- **Coin colours:** yellow = employer, grey = me. That one I got. Teal and the pink stripes I only understood after reading the table.
- **The small note under the table** ("בין 19 המסלולים… 0.7%… 0.19%"): ignored.
- **Option C:** I looked at yellow vs grey and the three numbers at the bottom. I ignored the teal tops, and only understood the white line (the securities account) from the legend.

## 5. What I didn't understand
- "דמי ניהול" (management fees). I guess it's a fee, but how much is it costing me?
- "מסלול" (track), "מחקה" (index tracker), "תקרה בחוק" (legal ceiling).
- Why the tax at year 5 is so big (13,974) when the securities account only pays 594.
- "מטבע אחד הוא 1,000 ₪, בעיגול" (one coin is 1,000 ₪, rounded). Fine, but I never used it.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find
- What happens if I **change jobs** before 6 years? Does the money stay? Does the clock reset?
- Can I take money out **in an emergency**, and what exactly do I lose?
- How do I actually **open** one, and is it through my employer or by myself?
- How do I find out what percentage **my** employer gives?
- Can I have **both** the fund and a securities account?
