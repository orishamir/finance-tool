# ת"א-125 for a 24-year-old: what I'd do after using the page

## 1. My answer

I'd open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and set up a monthly buy of the fund **"אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125"**, number 5112628.

That's the pairing the page marks with a star, **"הבחירה שלכם · הזול ★"** (your choice · the cheapest). With the default numbers (10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7%) it says I'd end with **86,045 ₪** "in today's money". The next best pairing is **"תכלית סל (40) ת"א 125"** at 85,015 ₪, which is only **"‎−1,030 ₪"** less over ten years.

**How sure am I:** about 70% on Psagot plus that fund. I'm not sure enough to act today, for two reasons:
- **The young-person discounts.** Several accounts say **"לא בחישוב · מחיר אחר לצעירים"** ("not included in the calculation · different price for young people"). I'm 24, so a bank like **לאומי**, ranked 2nd anyway, might end up cheaper for me. The page doesn't tell me how much cheaper.
- **The type of fund.** I don't really know what **"קרן מחקה"** is compared with **"קרן סל"**. The page says the cheapest one is a **"קרן מחקה"**, and I'm not sure whether that changes how I'd buy it.
