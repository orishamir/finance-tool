# s22: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 50 s, $0.14

I'm going to read this the way I'd read a bank statement: slowly, and asking each time what it means for my money.

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would look closer at **כלל** (Clal), fund number 13342. It charges **0.48% ההחיסכון** (0.48% of my savings each year), which is the lowest fee on the page that has an actual number. **מנורה מבטחים** (Menora) is right behind it at 0.49%, and the difference is too small to matter.

For my money, if I had 200,000 ₪ in the fund:
- Clal's 0.48% would cost me about 960 ₪ a year.
- **מור** (Mor) at the top, at 0.7%, would cost about 1,400 ₪ a year.

That's about 440 ₪ a year between the most and least expensive. Clal versus Menora is about 20 ₪ a year.

I'm about 65% sure. I wouldn't move anything until I phoned Clal and asked "is this the fee I would actually pay?" Three things hold me back:
- The fee is described as **"ממוצע 2025"** (an average from 2025). An average isn't a promise.
- One company, **אילון**, shows **"עוד לא דווח"** (not yet reported). It might be cheaper and I can't tell.
- The page says there are 12 tracks, but I only counted 11 companies. Maybe one was hidden while I scrolled, and that bothers me.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The heading **"כל המסלולים בכל החברות"** (all tracks at all companies) and the two sentences under it, which say the fee is what was actually charged in 2025.
- The **מוצר** (product) buttons, where **קרן השתלמות 154** was already lit up.
- The **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is invested in) buttons, where **עוקב מדד S&P 500 12** was lit up. That's exactly what I wanted.
- The summary line **"12 מסלולים של 12 חברות... דמי ניהול מההחיסכון: מ־0.48% עד 0.7%"** (12 tracks from 12 companies, fees from 0.48% to 0.7%). This was the most useful sentence on the page.
- The line **"המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהיקר לזול"** (sorted by fee, most expensive first). Without it I would have assumed the top row was the cheapest.
- In the table: the company name column and the **דמי ניהול** (management fee) column.
- The note under the table explaining **"אין"** (none) and **"עוד לא דווח"** (not yet reported).
