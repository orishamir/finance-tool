# Me, 61, savings with Harel, reading the Harel page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would not move any money because of this page. First I'd find out which Harel product and track I'm actually in.** I'm fairly sure that's the right first step, but I'm not sure of much beyond that.

The page opened on **"קופת גמל להשקעה"** (an investment savings fund). I'm not sure that's what I have. At my age my money may be in **"קרן השתלמות"** (a study fund) or **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"** (a pension fund), which are separate buttons. So everything in the table may be about someone else's money.

What I took from it:

- **Fees barely differ between Harel's own tracks.** The line **"דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"** (management fees on savings: from 0.54% to 0.61%) tells me that. I did the sum: on 500,000 shekels that's about 2,700 against 3,050 a year, roughly 350 shekels apart. That's not a reason to switch tracks.
- **There is a legal maximum.** The line **"התקרה בחוק: 1.05% מהחיסכון, 4% מההפקדות"** (legal ceiling: 1.05% of savings, 4% of deposits) is what I'd check my own statement against. If I'm paying close to 1.05%, I'd phone Harel.
- **Most tracks have no track record.** For most of them the return just says **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years). Only a few have real numbers:
  - **"מניות"** (stocks): 24.04% over 3 years
  - **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** (S&P 500 index tracker): 13.7%
  - **"הלכה"** (Jewish-law track): 11.42%
  - **"כספי (שקלי)"** (cash, shekel): 4.25%
  - **"קיימות"** (sustainability): 1.78%
- **The high numbers come with risk I don't want.** The stocks track shows **94%** under **"מניות"** (stocks). At 61 I don't want almost all my money in shares, so the high number scares me as much as it tempts me.

## 3. Block by block, top to bottom

- **Top menu (מדריך, חוקים וכללים, and so on):** skipped. I didn't come to browse.
- **"הראל" title and the grey sentence:** used. I liked being told the dates and that the figures come from the regulator, **"רשות שוק ההון"** (the Capital Market Authority). That makes me trust it more.
- **"כולל את החברות: הראל פנסיה וגמל… הראל קרנות נאמנות"** (includes the companies…): skimmed. Fine.
- **"123 ממקור · 123 מחושב · 123 הזנתה"** (from source / calculated / "you entered"): confused me. "הזנתה" means "you entered", but I didn't enter anything. I left it alone.
- **Product buttons (6 of them):** used, and worried. Nothing tells me which one is mine.
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" / "כל המסלולים… בכל החברות"** (what is this fund / all its tracks at every company): noticed them. I'd click both later, especially the second, because I want to compare Harel with other companies.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" filter buttons (14 of them):** skimmed and gave up. Almost all of them say "1". The words **"סחיר", "עוקב מדדים - גמיש", "משולב"** mean nothing to me.
- **The table:** used. Details in section 4.
- **The two notes under the table:** used both. The second one, **"בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות מהקופה הוצאות ישירות… שלא מופיעות כאן"**, worried me. It says more costs come out of the fund on top of the fees and aren't shown here. How much?
- **Folded sections "קרנות מחקות", "מרכזיות לפיצויים", "קרנות סל"** (index funds, severance funds, ETFs): skipped. They aren't mine.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל"** (what Harel's documents say) and its folded list: I read the explanation, **"תנאים שכתובים במסמכים של הראל ולא בחוק"** (terms in Harel's documents rather than in the law). That sounds important, the small print. But I didn't open the sections, so I don't know what's in them. **"קרן השתלמות · 8"** caught my eye.
- **"לא מצאתם שם?" search box** (didn't find a name?): skipped.
- **Footer:** I noticed **"אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"** (personal, not commercial, no marketing links). It reassured me a little.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

- **"המסלול" (track name):** read it, but half the names are jargon.
- **"קופה" (the numbers like 15283, 8522):** ignored. Maybe they match a number on my statement, but nothing says so.
- **Fees, "דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025":** looked closely. The top figure, **"מהחיסכון"** (from savings), is what matters to me. The bottom one, **"מההפקדות 0%"** (from deposits), is 0% in every row, so I stopped reading it.
- **Exposure, "חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח" (the coloured rings):** I read the green **"מניות"** (stocks) percentage as "how risky". I didn't understand the yellow **"מט"ח"** (foreign currency) or what the rings add. The dotted underlines under the numbers puzzled me.
- **Returns, "תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול":** looked at the **"3 שנים"** line more than the **"5 שנים"** line. The endless **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** didn't help.
- **"כסף מנוהל" (money managed):** glanced at it. **"7.3 מיליארד ₪"** in stocks against **"10 מיליון ₪"** in sustainability tells me where most people are. That's a bit comforting, but I don't know if it should matter.
- **The ">" arrows on the left:** ignored. I didn't know they open something.
- **Rows:** I didn't see every one. Somewhere between **"קיימות"** and **"הלכה"** I scrolled past a few rows without reading them properly.

- **"מט"ח"** and why it matters to me
- The coloured rings
- **"סחיר", "עוקב מדדים - גמיש", "משולב סחיר", "אשראי ואג"ח"**

- **"ממקור / מחושב / הזנתה"**
- **"לפני דמי ניהול ולפני מס"** (before fees and before tax). So what do I actually keep? 24% minus what?

- What's sensible at 61, a few years from retirement
- If I switch tracks or companies: does it cost anything, is there tax, does it take long?
- What the "direct expenses" add up to
- My pension and study fund, not just this investment fund
- Can I ask Harel for a lower fee than the 2025 average?
