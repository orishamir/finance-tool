## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I was tempted by the plain **"מניות"** track instead. It made **24.04%** a year over 3 years and **14.64%** over 5, costs about the same (**0.55%**), and is even bigger (**7.3 מיליארד ₪**). I'm only about **40% sure**. The main reason is that I don't know whether my existing Harel savings are in this product at all. They might be in "קרן השתלמות" or "תגמולים". The page opened on קופת גמל להשקעה, and I just went along with it.
