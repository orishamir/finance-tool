# s39: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 45 s, $0.24

## 1. My answer

I'd pick **option 1, "החברה בראש השורה"** (company name at the top of each row). I'm fairly sure, maybe 65–70%.

I came in thinking "S&P 500". In option 1 there's a block headed **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"** that lists all 12 companies one under another, cheapest fee first. The first line answered my question straight away: **מנורה מבטחים, 0.52% "מהחיסכון"**, with 13.79% over 3 years. Further down I can see **מור at 0.71%** with about the same return. If I had to act today I'd look into Menora or Altshuler Shaham (0.54%).

Option 3 was tempting (the designers marked it "מומלץ"), and its coloured table was the fastest way to see that **מור** is the expensive one. But it shows me 12 investment types I don't care about. The list I actually used sits underneath it anyway, so it's the same as option 1 with extra stuff on top. I didn't want option 2, "קיבוץ לפי חברה": I had to scroll through all 16 Harel tracks before I even reached another company's S&P line.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- In options 1 and 3, the **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"** block. Its grey header line, **"דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.71%"**, told me the whole range in one sentence.
- The **company name** (bold) and the **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"** column.
- A glance at **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"**.
- In option 3, only the **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"** column of the coloured table.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top title and sentence** "143 מסלולים של 12 חברות… כל מספר כאן אמיתי": I skimmed it. It was reassuring.
- **The three option buttons and מחשב/טלפון**: I used these to switch.
- **The grey explanation sentences** between the options ("כמו ג׳, אבל כל שורה…"): I skimmed them. They're written for whoever is choosing a design, not for me.
- **Menu row "מקור / מדריכים / חוקים וכללים / שאלות…"**: I skipped it, and it shows up again in every option.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר"**: I skipped it. I didn't get how it's different from the option buttons.
- **"מוצר" chips** (תגמולים, קרן השתלמות…): I skimmed them. "קופת גמל להשקעה" was already selected, and I left it alone.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" chips**: this confused me. There are 18 of them: "אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר", "עוקב מדדים – גמיש", "כספי (שקלי)"… The only one I recognised was "s&p 500", so I'd click that and ignore the rest.
- **"מיון לפי"**: I left it on "דמי ניהול מהחיסכון", which seemed right.
- **"חיפוש בשם המסלול"**: I skipped it.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע"** (option 2): this confused me. It changed the whole order, and I wasn't sure which one I was supposed to want.
- **The track table**: I used it, but only the S&P block. In option 2 I skimmed Harel's and Mor's other tracks and gave up.
- **Orange note "בשרטוט בלבד…"**: I skipped it. It looks like a note between designers.
- **Small print "התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"**: I read it, and it mattered. It means the 13% isn't what I'd actually get.
- **Option 3's coloured table "כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה"**: I used one column and skipped the rest.

## 4. Inside the tables

**What I looked at:**
- Bold company names.
- The **"…% מהחיסכון"** number.
- The **"3 שנים"** return.
- The S&P column and the orange rows in the coloured table. The text says "כתום חזק יותר הוא דמי ניהול גבוהים יותר", so I could see מור lit up across the whole row.

**What I ignored or half-understood:**
- **"קופה"** numbers like 13874 and 14865: no idea what they are for.
- **"…% מההפקדה"**: almost always 0%, so I stopped looking at it.
- **"חשיפה" with the rings, "מניות 99%" and "מט"ח 97%"**: I know מט"ח means foreign currency, but not why I should care. All the S&P rings looked the same, so I ignored them.
- **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר"** (674 מיליון, 2.3 מיליארד): I wasn't sure if bigger is better.
- The second line under each company, "12 מסלולים · 3.8 מיליארד ₪ בקופות…".
- The small **"›"** arrows on the left.
- The tags like "מניות סחיר".
- Every heatmap column except S&P.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- Why there are two fees, "מהחיסכון" and "מההפקדה", and which one actually costs me more.
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**: does that mean the track is new, or that I'd lose something?
- The difference between "מניות" and "מניות סחיר", and what "גמיש" and "הלכה יהודית" mean.
- What the dashes (—) and **"חדש"** in the coloured table mean, and **"עוד לא דווח"** for Ayalon.
- What a "קופת גמל להשקעה" even is compared to the "קרן השתלמות" I probably already have through work. There's a link, "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה", but it's small.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- What the fee difference means in shekels. Is 0.52% vs 0.71% worth 50 ₪ a year or 5,000?
- The return **after** fees. That's what I'd actually get.
- Whether I can take the money out whenever I want, and if there's tax.
- The minimum amount and how to sign up.
- Whether the fee can be negotiated. People say it can, and if so the 0.52% vs 0.71% gap matters less.

## 7. If half had to go

1. **Option 2 (grouping by company)** goes first. For my question it buries what I need.
2. Option 3's heatmap columns for every type except the one I picked.
3. The **"קופה"** number column and the exposure rings.
4. The repeated menu rows and the designer notes.
5. Most of the 18 "במה הכסף מושקע" chips. Show the common ones and hide the rest.

I'd keep the S&P block, the company name, the fee, the return, and the "תשואת עבר… לפני דמי ניהול" warning.
