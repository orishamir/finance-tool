# s39: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 46 s, $0.25

I read all ten screenshots. The page shows three drawings of the same list: **"1. החברה בראש השורה"** (company at the top of each row), **"2. קיבוץ לפי חברה"** (grouped by company) and **"3. טבלת חברות מול סוגים"** (companies against investment types). The third has the designers' **"מומלץ"** (recommended) badge.

## 1. My answer

I'd pick **option 3, "טבלת חברות מול סוגים"**, and use the list under it, **"כל המסלולים"**, to check returns. I'm about 70% sure.

I didn't pick it because of the "מומלץ" badge. I picked it because it was the only part where one row per company let me see who is cheap and who is expensive. The note says **"כתום חזק יותר הוא דמי ניהול גבוהים יותר"** (stronger orange means higher fees). That's plain enough even for me. The **מור** row is orange almost all the way across, at 0.67%–0.73%. The **הראל** row is dark, at 0.54%–0.61%.

So what does that mean for my money? If I had 200,000 ₪, the gap between 0.52% (מנורה) and 0.71% (מור) in the S&P 500 track is about 380 ₪ a year, every year. That I understand.

What I'd actually do: shortlist הראל, מנורה and אלטשולר for an S&P 500 track. Then I'd phone them. I wouldn't move money based on this page alone.

## 2. Parts I actually used
- The top line: **"143 מסלולים של 12 חברות… מנתוני רשות שוק ההון"**. It told me the numbers come from the official regulator, the Capital Market Authority, so I trusted the page a bit more.
- The **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"** button, to compare like with like.
- The grid **"כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה"** in option 3.
- In the list, the fee column **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"** and the return column **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**.
- The note at the bottom of each list: **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר… היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"** (past returns don't say what will happen next). I read it twice. It's honest, and it's the reason I lean on fees and not on returns.

## 3. Block by block
- **Title and the line about "מספר אמיתי; אף מספר לא הומצא"** (every number is real, none made up): read it. It reassured me.
- **"משותף לשלוש, ומה שהנתונים מראים"** (shared by all three, and what the data shows): looks like a link. I didn't know what it would open, so I skipped it.
- **The three option buttons and "מחשב / טלפון"** (computer / phone): used the option buttons. I ignored computer/phone.
- **The bar "מקור · מדריכים · חוקים וכללים · שאלות · חברות ומסלולים · השקעה במדד"** (source, guides, rules, questions, companies and tracks, index investing): this confused me. It appears three times on one page, and I kept thinking I had scrolled back to the top.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר"** (by company / by product): skipped. I wasn't sure what clicking would change.
- **The product row ("תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים 182", "קרן השתלמות 154"…)**: I checked that **"קופת גמל להשקעה"** was selected and left the rest alone.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is invested in), the row of 19 buttons: skimmed it. I recognised **"מניות"** (shares), **"כללי"** (general) and **"s&p 500"**. **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש"**, **"אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר"** and **"מט"ח"** read as jargon to me.
- **"מיון לפי"** (sort by): noticed it, didn't need it.
- **"חיפוש בשם המסלול"** (search by track name): skipped.
- **Option 1 list (S&P 500, one row per company)**: used it. Easy, because every row is the same kind of track.
- **Option 2 (grouped by company: all 16 of הראל's tracks, then all 15 of מור's)**: skimmed, then gave up. It's very long, and comparing הראל with מור meant scrolling back and forth. I don't need 16 tracks from one company.
- **Option 3 grid**: used it most.
- **Note under the grid, "סוגים עם פחות משבעה מסלולים מופיעים רק ברשימה"** (types with fewer than seven tracks appear only in the list): fine.
- **Orange notes starting "✎ בשרטוט בלבד"** (in the drawing only): I gathered they were notes from the designers, not for me. Skipped.

## 4. Tables and columns
**Looked at:**
- **"מתוך החיסכון"** fee (fee taken from savings): the main number.
- The 3-year and 5-year returns ("3 שנים", "5 שנים"). In the S&P track they're all about 13.3%–13.9%, so nearly the same everywhere. For me that pointed back to fees.
- **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"** (assets at end of September 2026): glanced at it to see whether a fund is big or tiny. 2 million ₪ for **איילון**, marked **"חדש"** (new), made me wary.
- The orange shading in the grid.

**Ignored:**
- The **"קופה"** numbers (13874, 14865…). Meaningless to me.
- **"מתוך ההפקדות 0%"** (fee from deposits). Almost always 0%, so I stopped looking.
- The coloured rings, and the green **"מניות"** and yellow **"מט"ח"** dots with percentages under **"חשיפה"** (exposure). I couldn't tell what 99% מט"ח does to me.
- The little clock icons and the dotted underlines. I didn't know if they were clickable.

## 5. What I didn't understand
- **"חשיפה"** with **"מט"ח 99%"**: does that mean my money moves with the dollar? Is that good or bad for me?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years): the track is too young to have a 3-year return? I guessed. Nobody told me.
- **"עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet), **"חדש"** and **"—"** in the grid: what's the difference between them?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול"** (before fees): so what did people actually get? I had to do the subtraction myself.
- **"ממוצע 2025"** (2025 average): average of what? Will I pay 0.56%, or can I get less?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
- Return **after** fees: what actually reaches my pocket.
- An example in shekels, like "on 100,000 ₪ over 10 years, the difference between companies is X ₪."
- How much a track lost in a bad year. At 61, that matters more to me than the average.
- Whether I can bargain the fee down.
- How to move money between companies, and whether moving costs anything or affects tax.
- What happens at 60 and over: taking a monthly pension from it, and the tax rules.
- Customer service. Will someone answer the phone?

## 7. If half the page had to go
1. The repeated bar **"מקור · מדריכים · חוקים וכללים…"** and the repeated **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר"**: keep one at the top.
2. **Option 2**, the long grouped list.
3. The rings and the **"מניות / מט"ח"** exposure percentages, or else explain them in plain words.
4. The **"קופה"** number column and **"מתוך ההפקדות"**.
5. Half of the **"במה הכסף מושקע"** buttons, the ones with fewer than three tracks.

I'd keep the grid, the fee and return columns, and the **"תשואת עבר"** warning.
