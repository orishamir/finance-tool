# What I took from the page, as a 61-year-old saver

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd buy an S&P 500 fund every month for 10 years through one of the cheap accounts near the top of the right-hand list. I would not use a big bank.

I kept the page's starting numbers: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year. That's 70,000 ₪ of my own money (**"הפקדתם 70,000 ₪"**).

- **The page's default pick:** the **"iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating)"** fund at **"אינטראקטיב ישראל"**. It leaves me **"84,184 ₪"**, and the page says that's in today's money, after fees and tax.
- **The cheapest pair:** **"הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם 86,492 ₪"**, which is **"+2,308 ₪ יותר"**. Further down I worked out that this means the Israeli fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500"** at **"פסגות"**.

**How sure am I?** I'm very sure the bank matters more than the fund. The same fund leaves me 84,184 ₪ at Interactive Israel but only **"51,730 ₪"** at **"מיטב טרייד"**, which is **"32,454 ₪ פחות"**. That decided it for me.

I'm only about half sure which cheap account to pick, for two reasons:
- Interactive Israel has a red-orange badge saying **"לא בפיקוח רשות ני״ע"**. At my age that scares me more than an extra 2,308 ₪ is worth.
- For Psagot, the cheapest pair, the account card says **"אין נתון"** ("no data"), and the page also writes **"יש ספק אם נכתב שם: פסגות, אי.בי.אי"** ("it's unclear whether that's what they wrote").

So my likely plan is to call Psagot and ask for the Yelin Lapidot S&P 500 tracker fund at the terms the page quotes. If they agree, I'd go with that. If not, I'd choose Interactive Brokers (**"82,848 ₪"**) over Interactive Israel, but only after asking someone whether "not regulated" puts my money at risk.

- The top title, **"איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן"**, and the **S&P 500** button.
- The four boxes: **"סכום היום"**, **"הפקדה חודשית"**, **"שנים"** and **"תשואה בשנה"**.
- The three result cards: **"הפקדתם"**, **"הבחירה שלכם"** and **"הזוג הזול ביותר"**.
- The right-hand column, **"כל החשבונות"**, with the big number for each bank.
- The table near the bottom, **"חשבון אחר, אותו קרן"**. This showed the same thing as a clean list with **"פחות"** ("less") amounts, and it was the part I trusted most.
- The short line **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"**: if I stay 20% longer, I end up with **"18,436 ₪ יותר"**.

## 3. Going down the page, block by block

- **Top menu (מקור, מדריך…):** skipped.
- **Title and the index buttons:** used. I noticed **"האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"** ("the site doesn't recommend an index"). Fine, I already know I want the S&P 500.
- **The paragraph under "מדד S&P 500":** skimmed. **"הכסף מושקע בדולרים"** ("the money is invested in dollars") registered with me.
- **The four input boxes and sliders:** used. I didn't touch **"אינפלציה"**.
- **The three result cards:** used. The line **"אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל"** ("if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel") made me uneasy. What if it does move?
- **The coloured cost bar and "עלות אצלכם, לעומת הזוג השני":** half used. I understood **"החשבון 241 ₪ יותר"**, **"הקרן 288 ₪ יותר"** and **"המס 1,779 ₪ יותר"**. Most of the gap is tax, which surprised me, and nothing explains why.
- **The left list, "כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500" (19 funds plus one hedged fund):** this confused me, and I gave up partway. Every row has four to six coloured badges, and most say **"אין מחיר כאן"** ("no price here").
- **The right list, "כל החשבונות":** used. The big shekel number and the bank name were what I needed.
- **The grey "אין נתון" cards (אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד, פסגות):** I read them and wished I hadn't. Long text about **"0.275% משווי העסקה + עלות ברוקר"** made Psagot, the supposed winner, look shaky.
- **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר…":** skimmed.
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, מול התעריפון":** skimmed. The useful point was **"הרבה פעמים אפשר לקבל מחיר טוב יותר במשא ומתן"** ("you can often get a better price by negotiating"). Worth knowing before I call the bank.
- **"מה הכי משנה…", the chart and its table:** I used the sentence and the table but skipped the chart.
- **"עם נטרול מט״ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל":** I tried and got lost. I took away one thing: the hedged fund lost **"−21.8% ב־2022"**. The conclusion, **"השורה התחתונה"** ("the bottom line"), says the hedge **"לא מגן מירידה של השוק"** ("doesn't protect against a market drop"). So my answer is no hedging, and I skipped the rest.
- **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת…" (the big grey bar chart):** skimmed. It's one grey bar labelled **"עכשיו 84,184"**. The line under it, **"עלות הקרן 403.62, מס 7,483"**, was useful.
- **The two tables "חשבון אחר, אותו קרן" and "קרן אחרת…":** used. These were the clearest part of the page.
- **The footnotes at the bottom:** I read **"התשואה שבחישוב: תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"** ("the return used is an example, not a forecast"). Good that they say it.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Is my money safe if a broker that isn't supervised by the Israel Securities Authority (Interactive Israel) goes bust?**
- Can I set up a fixed 500 ₪ that buys automatically every month, or do I have to log in and buy each time?
- What happens to the account if I die? How do my heirs get it, and is there US estate tax on foreign funds?
- How exactly is tax charged when I take the money out at 71?
- Is 7% a sensible number to assume, or is it optimistic?
- One plain sentence saying "for your inputs, do X at Y", with a phone number or a link to open the account.
