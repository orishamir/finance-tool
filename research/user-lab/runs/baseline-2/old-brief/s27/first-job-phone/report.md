# Picking an S&P 500 fund on this page, as a 24-year-old first-time investor

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd open an account at **Psagot** and put money every month into **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" (5139282)**. The page put the two together as "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול". With 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, it says I'd end with **86,492 ₪** after fees and tax, in today's money. I'd put in 70,000 ₪, so that's about 16,500 ₪ more.

**How sure I am:** about 65%. I'm fairly sure about the fund. Its fees look like almost nothing ("דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים 0.02% נאמן"), and it's first in the list. I'm less sure about the account:
- The next pair is only "−43 ₪" behind. Over 10 years that's basically the same.
- Leumi, Hapoalim, Mizrahi and Discount all say "מחיר אחר לצעירים", and those prices are marked "לא בחישוב". I'm young, so one of them might be cheaper for me than Psagot. The page doesn't tell me.

Before opening anything I'd ask Leumi or Hapoalim what their youth price is.
