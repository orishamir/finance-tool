# s04: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 51 s, $0.21

I'm 24, on a salary, and the only kind of saving I'd heard of is "put it in the S&P 500," so that's what I went looking for.

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

On the home page I clicked **"להבין סוג של חיסכון"** ("understand a kind of saving"), which took me to the **מדריך** (guide) page. I scrolled until I found the **"מדד S&P 500"** card. It says it's "מדד של חמש מאות החברות הגדולות בארצות הברית… הכסף מושקע בדולרים דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו" (an index of the 500 biggest US companies; the money goes in, in dollars, through a fund that follows it). That's roughly what I'd heard, so it was nice to see it confirmed.

The most useful thing was the box at the top, **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** ("two parts in every saving"). It says every saving has a "where the money is kept" part and a "what it's invested in" part. Reading it, I realised the S&P 500 is the "what" part. I still need a "where", like **קרן השתלמות** (a savings fund at work), **קופת גמל להשקעה** (a savings fund you open yourself) or **חשבון ניירות ערך** (a stock-market account).

Next I'd click the S&P 500 card to read more, then probably go back and look at **קרן השתלמות**. The **"מאיפה להתחיל"** ("where to start") box says it's for "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם" (salaried people whose employer pays into it), and that's me, I think.

How sure am I? Not very, maybe 3 out of 10. I understand what the S&P 500 is now. I don't know where to put it, how much it costs, or whether it's a good idea for me. I wouldn't put money in today.

- On the guide page:
  - **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"**

  - the **"מדד S&P 500"** card
  - the **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"** cards (saving with a tax benefit: קרן השתלמות and קופת גמל להשקעה)

## 3. Block by block

**Home page**
- **Top menu.** I skimmed it. I noticed **"השקעה במדד"** ("investing in an index") and thought that might be exactly my thing. I went into the guide instead.
- **"מקור — להבין חיסכון והשקעה בישראל"** ("understand saving and investing in Israel"). Read it. It told me I was in the right place.
- **"מה השאלה שלכם?"** ("what's your question?") form, with buttons like דירה (apartment), חתונה (wedding), כרית ביטחון (safety cushion), "בלי מטרה מסוימת" (no particular goal), plus boxes for how much I have, how much a month, and how many years. I skimmed it and skipped it. I came to learn, not to type numbers in yet. Also, "קרן השתלמות נזילה" (a fund you can now take money out of) and "פיקדון שמסתיים" (a bank deposit that's ending) meant nothing to me.
- **Search box, "מה ראיתם באפליקציה, בפרסומת או בדוח?"** ("what did you see in an app, an ad or a statement?"). I noticed it. I could have typed "S&P", but the cards seemed easier.
- **The four cards on the left.** I used the first one. I skipped "מה חברה מציעה לי" (what a company offers me) and "מה כבר יש לכם" (what you already have). I briefly read "כסף שיושב בעו"ש" (money sitting in your current account) because I do have that.
- **"שאלות שכולם שואלים"** (common questions). I skimmed it. "כמה מס משלמים על רווח בבורסה?" (how much tax on stock-market profit?) caught my eye, but I didn't click.

**Guide page**
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון".** Used it. It was the most helpful thing on the site.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל".** Used it. It confused me a bit, though. It says "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר: קרן חדשה מקיפה" ("the basis for every salaried saver: a comprehensive new fund"). Do I already have that through work? Do I need to do something?
- **"כסף שוטף וקצר"** (everyday and short-term money): עובר ושב, פיקדון, תכנית חיסכון, קרן כספית. I skimmed the titles. It's not what I came for.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס".** Read it. "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס" (after a period set by law you can withdraw tax-free) sounds good. I didn't understand what "תקרה שנתית" (a yearly ceiling) means for me.
- **"פרישה"** (retirement). I skimmed it. The "בשפה פשוטה" ("in simple words") lines helped. "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים" lost me completely.
- **"לילדים"** (for children). Skipped it. I don't have kids.
- **"לבחירה עצמית"** (choose it yourself). I read the חשבון ניירות ערך card because it mentions a broker. I skipped "בניהול אישי" (self-managed).
- **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is invested in). I scrolled fast past מסלול כללי (the general track) and the government-bond cards until I hit **S&P 500**. I skimmed the Tel Aviv indices, MSCI and Nasdaq, but I don't know why I'd pick them over S&P.
- **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** (what the site doesn't cover yet). Skipped it.
- **Footer.** Ignored it.

## 4. Lists and cards

There were no tables or charts, just grids of cards, three in a row. I read the bold white titles and searched for anything I recognised. I only read the grey description when the title meant something to me.

The **"בשפה פשוטה:"** lines were the best part, and I wished every card had one. The green links in "מאיפה להתחיל" looked clickable, so I paid attention to them. I ignored the little arrows and the section subtitles in small grey text.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **The word "מדד".** In "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה… צמודות למדד המחירים" (government bonds linked to the price index), the word "מדד" means something different from "מדד S&P 500" (the stock index). That confused me.
- **Words in the investment cards:** "אג"ח" (bonds), "מק"מ" (short-term government loans), "קופון" (coupon), "פדיון" (redemption), "סחיר" (tradeable), "במשקל לפי שווי השוק" (weighted by market value).
- **Words in the guide:** "מסלול" (track), "נזילה" (available to withdraw), "תקרה שנתית" (yearly ceiling), "תגמולים" and "פיצויים" (the pension and severance parts of a pension fund).
- **"הכסף מושקע בדולרים"** (the money is invested in dollars). Is that a risk for me, since I earn shekels?
- **"קרן שעוקבת אחריו"** (a fund that follows it). Who runs that fund, and how do I pick one?
- **מק"מ appears twice**, in two different sections. Is it saving or investing?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- Roughly how much the S&P 500 has made per year, and how much I could lose.
- How much it costs (fees).
- The actual steps to start, and the minimum amount.
- For someone like me: is S&P 500 inside a קרן השתלמות better than in my own stock-market account?
- Whether I should first keep a "כרית ביטחון" (safety cushion), and how big it should be.
- Whether I already have some of these through my job, and how to check.

## 7. If half the page had to go

First, from the home page: the **"מה השאלה שלכם?"** form and the **"שאלות שכולם שואלים"** box. They're not useful to someone who's just trying to learn.

Then, from the guide:
- the bond cards (אג"ח צמודה / לא צמודה, איגרות חוב)
- the extra indices (ת"א-35/90/125, MSCI, Nasdaq)
- the "בניהול אישי" cards
- the duplicate מק"מ card
- "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"

I'd keep "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון", "מאיפה להתחיל", the S&P 500 card, the tax-benefit cards and the "בשפה פשוטה" lines.
