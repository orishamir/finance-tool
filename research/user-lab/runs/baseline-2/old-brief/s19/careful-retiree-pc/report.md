# Comparing the תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים tracks, as a 61-year-old saver

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd keep my money in a track made for my age, **"לבני 60 ומעלה"** (for people 60 and over), at one of the big companies, and pick the one with the lowest fee. I would not move into the exciting share tracks.

Here are the 60-plus tracks I found and noted down:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Menora, "תגמולים ופיצויים מסלול לבני 60 ומעלה" | 0.45% / 0.13% | 9.34% / 5.39% | 31% / 14% | 3.5 billion |
| Harel, "גמל מסלול לגילאי 60 ומעלה" | 0.56% / 0.25% | 8.08% / 4.79% | 33% / 14% | 13.2 billion |

| Altshuler Shaham | 0.61% / 0.17% | 8.01% / 4.41% | 28% / 19% | 8.6 billion |
| Mor | 0.66% / 0% | 8.67% / 6.08% | 34% / 15% | 10.3 billion |

The returns are close to each other, so for me the fee is what separates them. **Menora at 0.45%** looks best on paper. **Harel** is bigger, if size makes me feel safer.

**How sure: about 50%.** I'm fairly sure about the *type* of track (60-plus, roughly a third in shares). I'm not sure about the *company*. The fee shown is "ממוצע 2025", an average, and not what I'd actually be charged.

What tempted me and what I decided:
- The S&P 500 and share tracks, like "גמל עוקב מדד S&P500" at 13.89% over 3 years and "גמל משולב סחיר" at 28.61%. At 61, with 99–100% in shares, I said no.
- **"ביטוחן מבטיח תשואה 4.5%"** and **"5.5%"** (guaranteed-return tracks). They really caught my eye. But every number on them says "לא דווח" (not reported), and nothing tells me if I'm even allowed to join.

- **The big table**, mainly the rows with "לבני 60 ומעלה".
- **The grey company header bars**, e.g. "מור 14 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 51.3 מיליארד" (number of tracks and money managed).
- **The note at the bottom**: "התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך" (past return, before fees and tax, doesn't predict the future). That's honest, and I appreciated it.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**Looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (track name) and the small grey tags, especially "50-60" and "עד 50", which helped me spot the age tracks.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"** (management fee, 2025 average), both lines: "מהחיסכון" (from savings) and "מההפקדות" (from deposits). From savings matters more to me, since I have a lot saved and not many deposits left.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול"** (average yearly return before fees), "3 שנים" and "5 שנים". I noticed the 5-year figure is always much lower.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"**, only the **"מניות"** (shares) number, as a measure of risk.
- **The grey company bars**, for company size.

**Ignored or half-ignored:**
- **"קופה"** (the turquoise number like 9793 with an arrow icon). I don't know what it's for.
- **The ring charts with teal and yellow.** I worked out teal = shares and yellow = foreign currency, but the numbers next to them already say it.
- **"כסף מנוהל"** (money managed) per track. Glanced at it only to avoid tiny tracks like "0 מיליון ₪".
- **The sort arrows ⇅.** Didn't touch them.
- **The dotted purple underlines** on the numbers. I didn't know what clicking would do.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **"מניות 101%"**, **"מט"ח 103%"**. How can more than 100% of my money be in shares? Is that borrowing? Dangerous?
- **"מההפקדות -0.21%"** (Infinity's 60-plus track), a negative fee with a warning sign. Are they paying me?
- **"מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדות"**: the page never says, in money, which one hurts me more.
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years) in the return column. I guess it means a new track, but it looks like an error.
- **"לא דווח"** (not reported) vs **"עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet). Is there a difference?
- **"אין"** (none) in the fee column, and the "PF-06" footnote.
- **"123 מקור / מחושב / הזנה"** (source / calculated / entered).
- **"מבטיח תשואה"** (guaranteed return). Guaranteed by whom, and can I join?
- **"אשראי ואג"ח", "אג"ח ממשלות", "משולב סחיר", "כללי"**: jargon. "כללי" (general) appears both as a filter that shows all 182 tracks and as a tag on single tracks.
- The **first company** in the table: the screen jumped and I never saw its name. Same with the company around "ולפיצויים מסלול לבני 60 ומעלה" (0.49%).

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **"So what in shekels?"** For example: on ₪800,000, 0.45% vs 0.66% = how much a year?
- **The fee I would actually pay.** Can I bargain? Is the average just a starting point?
- **Return after fees.** Everything is before fees and before tax.
- **What happens at 67**, when I want to take the money out. Does the track matter then?
- **How to move my money**, and whether it costs anything. ("העברה למכשיר אחר" in the footer was the only hint.)
- **A plain explanation of the 60-plus tracks**: what's inside them, and do they get safer automatically as I age?
- **Bad years.** How much did each track lose in its worst year? Shares % alone doesn't tell me how much I could lose.
- **Service, and how reliable the companies are.**
- **One list of just the 60-plus tracks** across all companies, in fee order. I had to collect them myself over 18 screens.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **All the "חדש / עוד לא דווח" rows**, like the whole Ayalon block. They tell me nothing yet.
2. **The "בניהול אישי / IRA" rows.** Not for someone like me.
3. **The ring charts.** The numbers next to them say the same thing.
4. **The "קופה" number column.**
5. **The "123 מקור / מחושב / הזנה" line.**
6. **The other product buttons** (קרן השתלמות, חיסכון לכל ילד…) on this page.
7. **The tiny tracks** with "0 מיליון ₪" or "2 מיליון ₪".

I would keep: the fees, the 3- and 5-year returns, the shares %, the age filter, and that honest note about past returns.
