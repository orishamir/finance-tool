# What I made of the page, as a 61-year-old trying to sort out my own money

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I would look into three things, and nothing else for now:

- **The money I might need in the next year or two.** I'd compare **פיקדון** with **קרן כספית**. The קרן כספית card says "סיכון נמוך" (low risk), and the third line under "מאיפה להתחיל" points the same way: "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן כספית" (money that needs to stay available: a money-market fund). For me, that means somewhere safe that still earns a little.
- **Retirement money.** I'd check what I already have under **פרישה**, meaning my pension fund (קרן פנסיה מקיפה / כללית). I'm 61, so this matters most, but the page doesn't tell me what to do with it at my age.
- **Spare money I won't touch for a while.** I'd look at **קופת גמל להשקעה**, because of "אפשר למשוך בכל עת" (you can withdraw at any time). I'd also look at **קרן השתלמות**, if I have one, because of "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס" (after a period set by law you can withdraw tax-free).

**How sure am I?** I'm fairly sure these are the right *things to look at*. I'm not at all sure which one is *right for me*. The page explains what each product is, but it gives no figures: nothing on returns, fees or risk level. So I'd call it about 3 out of 10 on actually deciding.

## 2. Which parts I actually used

- **The top block "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** (two parts in every saving). This was the most useful bit. It gave me the idea that the account ("איפה הכסף מוחזק", where the money is kept) is separate from what the money is invested in ("במה הכסף מושקע"). I hadn't understood that before. "הוא קובע מה המס ומתי אפשר למשוך" (it decides the tax and when you can withdraw) is exactly the "what does it mean for my money" answer I want.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל"** (where to start), especially the קרן כספית line.
- **The section headings "חיסכון עם הטבת מס"** (savings with a tax benefit) **and "פרישה"** (retirement), and their cards.
- **The קרן כספית and מזומן שקלי cards** under "במה הכסף מושקע".
- **The footer line "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"** (a personal, non-commercial site with no marketing links). That made me trust the page a bit more.

## 4. Lists, labels and colours

- **I looked at:** the bold card titles, the "בשפה פשוטה:" (in plain language) lines (very welcome, and I read every one), and the section headings on the right.
- **The green links** in "מאיפה להתחיל": I noticed them and understood they were clickable.
- **The two little buttons "יומית" (daily) and "מתחדשת" (renewing)** under קרן כספית: I looked at them and didn't understand them. Are these two kinds of קרן כספית? Is one better? Nothing explains them.
- **The little arrows on each card:** I understood they open more detail.
- **Ignored:** the search box at the top and most of the top menu. I did notice "השוואה למטרה" (comparison by goal), and that sounds like what I actually need.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **The abbreviations.** "מק"מ" is explained on its card, but in the top block it's just letters. Same with "אג"ח".
- **"עם חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו"** (with exposure to the dollar, or neutralised from it). So what does that mean for my money? Is it more risk or less?
- **The retirement section.** "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים" (contributions and personal severance) is a mouthful. And what's the real difference between "קרן חדשה מקיפה" and "קרן חדשה כללית" for someone who is already 61?
- **"לפי שיקול דעת ועדת ההשקעות של החברה המנהלת"** (at the discretion of the managing company's investment committee). That means someone else decides, but who are they, and what do they charge?
- **"MSCI AC World" next to "MSCI World".** I couldn't tell the difference without reading both cards twice.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Anything about my age.** What changes at 60 or 67? Can I take money out, and what tax do I pay then?
- **Fees (דמי ניהול).** Not one word about them on the whole page.
- **Risk.** Which of these can lose money, and roughly how much? Only the קרן כספית card mentions "סיכון".
- **Returns.** Even a rough "this usually earns about X" would help.
- **How long the money is locked up.** קרן השתלמות says "תקופה שקבע החוק" (a period set by law). How long is that?
- **Whether there's a deposit guarantee** if the bank or fund gets into trouble.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **Most of the "מדד" cards.** One card saying "funds that follow an index (Israel, America, the world)" would do me.
2. **The "לילדים" section.**
3. **"לבחירה עצמית".**

I'd keep the top block, "מאיפה להתחיל", the tax-benefit section, the retirement section and the safe-money cards.
