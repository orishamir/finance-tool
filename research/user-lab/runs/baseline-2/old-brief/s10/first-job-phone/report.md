# What I took from the "מדריך" (guide) page, as a 24-year-old employee

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd look into four things, in this order:

1. **Ask my employer or payslip whether I get a קרן השתלמות (study fund).** The page puts it first under "מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start): "שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם" (employees whose employer pays into one). The card also says "אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בפטור ממס" (after a period set by law you can withdraw tax-free). Free money from my employer plus no tax sounds like the best deal on the page.
2. **Check that my pension is a "קרן חדשה מקיפה" (new comprehensive pension fund).** The page calls it "הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר" (the basis for every salaried saver). I think I already have a pension through work, but I don't know which kind.
3. **Put my emergency money in a "קרן כספית" (money market fund),** because the page says it's for "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין" (money that needs to stay available).
4. **Check "קופת גמל להשקעה" (investment savings fund) for the S&P 500.** The "two parts" idea at the top made it click that the S&P 500 isn't a place to keep money. It's what you invest in, inside some product. So I could choose an S&P 500 option inside a קופת גמל להשקעה or a קרן השתלמות.

**How sure I am:**
- About **which things to look into**: fairly sure, around 70%.
- About **what to actually do with my money**: not sure at all, maybe 20%. The page gives no amounts, fees, returns or time periods, so I can't compare anything.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** (two parts in every saving). This was the most useful part. "איפה הכסף מוחזק" (where the money is held) vs "במה הכסף מושקע" (what it's invested in) finally explained why "invest in the S&P 500" didn't tell me where to go.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל"** (where to start). Three lines, and I trusted them as my to-do list.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"** (tax-benefit savings): both cards.
- **"פרישה"** (retirement): only the קרן חדשה מקיפה card.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע"**: only the S&P 500, MSCI World and קרן כספית cards.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** ("שאלות שכולם שואלים", "השוואה למטרה", "השקעה במדד"): I skimmed it. "השקעה במדד" (investing in an index) caught my eye as the next thing I'd click.
- **Title "מדריך" and the subtitle**: skimmed. "לכל אחד אותו מבנה, כדי שיהיה קל להשוות" (each one has the same layout so it's easy to compare) was fine but didn't do anything for me.
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"**: used and read twice. The small gray line "בכל עמוד של מוצר יש שורה עם מה שאפשר להשקיע בתוכו" (every product page has a row showing what you can invest in it) I barely noticed.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל"**: used. This is the part I'd come back to.
- **"איפה הכסף מוחזק" → "כסף שוטף וקצר"** (everyday and short-term money): in my screenshots the tops of these cards, including their titles, were cut off. I only saw endings like "ובתמורה הבנק משלם עליו ריבית" (and in return the bank pays interest on it). I guessed it was a current account and bank deposits, then moved on.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"**: used, both cards.
- **"פרישה"**: partly used, partly confusing.
  - I read the מקיפה card.
  - "קרן חדשה כללית" (new general pension fund) lost me at "ההפקדות שמעל הסכום המרבי" (deposits above the maximum amount). I don't know what that maximum is, and I'm probably nowhere near it.
  - "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים" (contributions and personal severance) confused me. What is severance pay doing in a savings fund?
- **"לילדים"** (for children): skipped. I don't have kids.
- **"לבחירה עצמית"** (self-managed): this was also cut off at the top. I saw the endings about "ברוקר בחו"ל" (a broker abroad) and "אתם, ולא מנהל התיקים, בוחרים את הניירות" (you, not the portfolio manager, pick the securities). It sounded like it's for people who know what they're doing, which isn't me yet.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע"**: skimmed. I hunted for S&P 500. I read קרן כספית, מזומן שקלי (shekel cash) and MSCI World.
  - מק"מ, אגרות חוב (government bonds) and מסלול כללי (general track): skimmed without really understanding.
- **Tel Aviv indexes (ת"א-35, ת"א-90, ת"א-125)**: skipped.
- **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** (what the site doesn't cover yet): skipped.
- **Footer**: I noticed "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (a personal, non-commercial site with no marketing links). That made me trust the page more. "נתונים נכון ל-30.10.2026" (data as of 30.10.2026) was good to know.

## 4. Inside the cards

- **What I looked at:** the bold title first, then the gray description. The line "בשפה פשוטה:" (in plain language) caught my eye, but it didn't help, because it only reworded the title ("קרן חדשה מקיפה" became "קרן פנסיה מקיפה חדשה").
- **What I noticed:** the green links in "מאיפה להתחיל". They looked like the important clicks.
- **What I ignored:** the little arrows on the cards.
- **What confused me:** the two grey buttons "יומית" (daily) and "מתחדשת" (renewing) on the קרן כספית card. I had no idea what choosing one would mean.
- **What I compared:** MSCI World vs MSCI AC World. I spotted the difference only by reading "עשרים ושלוש מדינות" (23 countries) vs "ארבעים ושבע מדינות" (47 countries).

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **Investment words:**
  - "מק"מ": the description "הלוואה קצרה למדינה" (a short loan to the state) helped a bit, but I still don't get why I'd want it.
  - "אג"ח" (bonds), "קרן נאמנות" (mutual fund), "קרן שעוקבת אחריו" (a fund that tracks it).
  - "עם חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו" (with exposure to the dollar or neutralized from it). This one is the S&P 500 thing I care about, and I didn't understand it at all.
- **Pension words:** "קצבה" (monthly pension payout), "תגמולים" (contributions), "פיצויים" (severance).
- **Who the people are:** "מנהל תיקים" (portfolio manager), "בית השקעות" (investment house).
- **Vague amounts and times:**
  - "תקרה שנתית" (yearly ceiling): how much is it?
  - "תקופה שקבע החוק" (a period set by law): how long is it?
- **Tax:** "ובמשיכה משלמים מס על הרווח" (and when you withdraw you pay tax on the profit). Is that better or worse than buying the S&P 500 myself?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- Some sense of **how much to put where**, and in what order once I've done the basics.
- **Fees**, which I've heard matter. And any idea of risk or returns. Is the S&P 500 risky for someone my age?
- **S&P 500 inside a קופת גמל vs buying it myself through a broker**: which is better for me?
- **How long the קרן השתלמות locks my money**, in actual years.
- **How to check what I already have** through work.

2. "לילדים"
3. "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"
4. "קרן חדשה כללית" and "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"
5. Nasdaq 100 and MSCI AC World
6. "לבחירה עצמית"

I'd keep "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון", "מאיפה להתחיל", the tax-benefit cards, the קרן כספית card and the S&P 500 card.
