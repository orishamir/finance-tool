# s02: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 52 s, $0.25

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd put my emergency money, the money I might need in the next few months, into a **קרן כספית יומית**, a daily money-market fund. I'd move it from my checking account (עו"ש), which pays basically nothing. I'm about 70% sure.

What convinced me:
- The chart line **"הריבית הגבוהה ביותר שמחזירה את הכסף בזמן: קרן כספית יומית, 3.25% בשנה"** ("the highest rate that still returns the money on time"). That's 3.25% against 0% in עו"ש and 1.8% for a one-year deposit.
- **"פודים בכל יום מסחר, בלי נעילה"**: I can sell any trading day and nothing is locked.
- On tax, **"בקרן כספית פטורה 25% רק מהרווח שמעבר לעליית המדד"**. Most of these funds are tax-exempt, and I only pay 25% on the gain above inflation. A bank deposit pays 15% on all the interest.

Why I'm not more sure:
- I never found out what the fee actually is in numbers.
- I don't know if the 3.25% is before or after that fee. The chart only says **"ריבית שנתית לפני מס ועלויות"** (before tax and costs), so I think it's before.
- I don't know which fund to pick or how to actually buy one.
- **"אין תשואה מובטחת"** and **"אין ערבות של המדינה"** made me a bit nervous: there's no guaranteed return and no state guarantee. But **"סיכון נמוך"** (low risk) calmed me down.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The **title and the grey line under it**: "קרן נאמנות שמשקיעה רק בנכסים קצרי טווח עם סיכון נמוך…" This told me what the thing is in one sentence.
- **למה משתמשים בזה** (why people use this). The first paragraph matched my exact situation: "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן חירום". The paragraph comparing it to a bank deposit was also useful.
- The **chart "מתי אתם צריכים את הכסף?"** ("when do you need the money?"). This was the most useful part of the page.
- **כסף נכנס ויוצא** (money in and out): "בכל יום מסחר, במחיר של אותו יום" (any trading day, at that day's price).
- **מיסוי** (tax): the 25%-of-real-gain part.
- **סיכונים ומה לא מוגן** (risks and what isn't protected): the first two paragraphs.
