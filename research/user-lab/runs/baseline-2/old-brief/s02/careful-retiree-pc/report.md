# My answers after reading the money-market fund page (קרן כספית)

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd put the money I might need in the next few months into a fund you can cash out on any trading day, which the page calls a **"קרן כספית יומית"**. I'd keep a few days' worth in my current account, because the money doesn't arrive the moment I ask for it. Before I bought, I'd ask my bank or broker two things: what the yearly fee is for this particular fund, and whether they charge any custody fees.

I'm fairly sure about the idea, maybe 70%. These lines convinced me:

- **"פודים בכל יום מסחר, בלי נעילה"**: I can cash out on any trading day and nothing is locked up.
- **"כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן חירום, כסף שמחכה להחלטה, או סכום שיש לו שימוש בחודשים הקרובים"**: money that has to stay available, like an emergency fund or money I'll use in the coming months. That describes my money exactly.
- The comparison box: my current account pays **0%**, a one-year deposit pays **1.8%**, and the fund shows **3.25%**.

I'm much less sure how much I'd actually earn. The 3.25% is labelled **"לפי ריבית בנק ישראל"** (it's the Bank of Israel rate) and **"ריבית שנתית לפני מס ועלויות"** (before tax and costs). The page also says plainly **"אין תשואה מובטחת"**: no return is guaranteed. So 3.25% isn't what I'd get. I'd get 3.25% minus a fee the page never puts a number on, and then tax.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- The title and the grey sentence under it, which told me what this is.
- **"מה זה"**, the first paragraph and the cost paragraph.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**, all of it. This was the most useful section for me.
- The comparison box **"מתי אתם צריכים את הכסף?"** and the line under it: **"הריבית הגבוהה ביותר שמחזירה את הכסף בזמן: קרן כספית יומית, 3.25% בשנה"**.
- **"שווה לדעת"**, especially **"אין דמי משמרת על מק"מ וקרן כספית בבנק"** (no custody fee on Treasury bills or money-market funds at a bank).
- **"כמה זה עולה"**: only the left-hand text columns.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: what happens if the fund manager goes under, and that nothing is insured.
- Some of **"מיסוי"**: the 25% tax on real gains versus 15% on bank deposits.

## 3. Going down the page block by block

- **Top menu (מדריך, שאלות שכולם שואלים…)**: Skipped. I came for this one page.
- **Title and grey summary**: Used. The phrase **"סיכון נמוך שהלווה לא יחזיר"** confused me for a moment. I had to reread it to see it means low risk that the borrower won't pay back.
- **The row of "123 מקור / 123 חושב / 123 הזנה" and "לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"**: Skipped. I didn't understand what they were for, and they looked like something for the people who built the site.
- **"מה זה", first paragraph**: Used. "Bank of Israel rate, minus the costs" is the sentence that stayed with me.
- **"מה זה", second paragraph on buying and selling**: Confused me. Here is the sentence: **"בקרן שלפי מדיניות ההשקעות שלה ניירות ערך זרים לא יעלו על 10% מנכסיה, ביום שבו המפיץ חייב להעביר את ההוראה למסלקה, ובקרן שיכולה להחזיק יותר, ביום המסחר הבא"**. It tries to say when the money from a sale reaches my account, and I still don't know. Is it the same day or the next day? How would I know which kind of fund I have? There's also **"האם אפשר לקנות ולפדות יחידות גם ביום שישי עוד לא אומת. שאלה פתוחה"**, which says nobody has checked yet whether you can buy and sell on Fridays. I appreciate the honesty, but it bothered me.
- **"מה זה", cost paragraph**: Used. **"שיורד מהמחיר כל יום, ולא רואים אותו כחיוב נפרד"** tells me the fee comes off the price every day and never shows up as a separate charge. That's important, and a little worrying. I'll never see a bill.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: Used, every word. Two lines I liked: **"הפסד בקרן ניתן לקיזוז; הפסד בפיקדון לא"** (a loss in the fund can be set against gains, a loss on a deposit can't), and that a deposit can be better **"כשברור שלא תצטרכו את הכסף עד הפירעון"**, when you're sure you won't need the money before it matures.
- **The grey buttons "אג"ח ממשלתית לא צמודה / מק"מ / מזומן שקלי"**: Skimmed. They show what the fund may invest in. Fine.
- **The comparison box**: Used. More on this in question 4.
- **"שחקו עם המספרים שלכם"**: It was closed and I didn't open it. Maybe it would have answered my question about how much I'd earn, but nothing told me it would.
- **"שווה לדעת"**: Used the words. Ignored the small codes like **"TXS-24"**.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"**: Skimmed. It's reassuring that the Israel Securities Authority supervises these funds, and that a bank has to show them **"בלי להעדיף אף מוצר"**, without favouring any product.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**: I only saw the card **"בכל יום מסחר, במחיר של אותו יום"** (every trading day, at that day's price). Good, short, clear.
- **"כמה זה עולה" (the cost table)**: Used it, and it disappointed me. More in question 4.
- **"מיסוי"**: Partly used. **"אין מס בזמן ההחזקה"** (no tax while you hold it) is clear. **"2 קרנות כספיות מתוך 73 הן קרן נאמנות חייבת"** confused me. It means 2 of the 73 funds pay the tax themselves, so I'd want to avoid those. How would I tell which ones they are?
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: Used, slowly. **"אין ערבות של המדינה לשווי הקרן, וגם לפיקדונות בבנק אין ביטוח"**: the state doesn't guarantee the fund's value, and bank deposits aren't insured either. That was sobering, but at least it's honest.
- **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון"**: Skipped. It's about the site checking itself.
- **"מונחים" (glossary)**: Skimmed. I looked up one or two words I had stumbled on earlier.
- **Footer**: I noticed **"נתונים נכון ל־26.10.2026"**, so the figures date from 26 October, a few days ago. Good to know the numbers are recent.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

**The comparison box (מיד / ביום מסחר / במועד קבוע / בסוף התקופה)**

- I looked at the tab that was already selected, **"ביום מסחר"** (on a trading day). I didn't click the other tabs.
- I read the name of each line and the percentage on the left: 0%, 3.25%, 3.25%, 1.8%.
- I noticed the green label **"המוצג בעמוד"** (shown on this page) next to the daily fund.
- I read the small grey lines under each bar, such as **"הוראת פדיון בכל יום מסחר"** (you can give a sell order on any trading day).
- I read the note about the current account: **"יש בנקים שמשלמים עד 3% (דיסקונט), בתנאים ועל עד 10,000 ₪ בלבד"**. Some banks pay up to 3% (Discount), with conditions and on 10,000 shekels at most.
- I ignored the colours of the bars: turquoise, mustard and the yellow dot. I didn't know what the colours meant. The two 3.25% bars are the same length, so the bars told me nothing the numbers didn't.
- I also didn't understand why the "renewing" fund (קרן כספית מתחדשת) looks greyed out.

**The cost table**

- I read **"סוג"** (type) and **"על מה נגבה"** (what it's charged on).
- The **"תקרה"** (ceiling) column says **"אין תקרה בחוק"**, no legal limit, on almost every line. That scared me a little.
- I ignored the **"חוקים וכללים"** column entirely. **"MF-16, BRK-17, BANK-18"** mean nothing to me.
- The line under the table, **"הסכומים בפועל נקבעים אצל הגורם המציע"**, says the real amounts are set by whoever sells the fund. So the "how much does it cost" section has no amount in it.

**The glossary table**

- I read **"מונח"** (term) and **"מה זה"** (what it means).
- I glanced at the **"איפה מופיע"** column, the little tags like **"אפליקציה"**, **"דוח שנתי"**, **"תעריפון"**. They're useful for knowing where I'd find the fee: in the bank's price list.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- When exactly the money reaches my account after I sell (the 10% foreign securities sentence).
- **"מסלקה"** (clearing house) and **"השעה היעודה"** (the cut-off hour). The glossary explained the hour, but not what time it actually is.
- **"רווח ריאלי"** versus **"רווח נומינלי"** (real versus nominal gain), and the sentence **"ולכן האתר מחשב את זה במקום לקבוע מראש"**, about the site calculating this rather than fixing it in advance. What is it calculating, and where?
- What **"123 מקור / חושב / הזנה"** at the top means.
- What the colours in the comparison box mean.
- **"במקרים חריגים אפשר להשעות פדיונות לכמה ימי מסחר"**: in unusual cases they can suspend selling for a few trading days. When has that happened? How often?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **The actual fee as a number.** For example, "typical fees are around X% a year". The page mentions that the Israel Securities Authority publishes an average for this kind of fund, but I couldn't see the number itself.
- **What 3.25% minus fees minus tax means in shekels.** If I put 50,000 ₪ in for six months, how much would I have at the end?
- **Has a money-market fund ever lost money in Israel?** How much, and for how long?
- **How to pick one fund out of the 73**, and how to spot the 2 that pay tax themselves.
- **Can I buy one at my own bank**, or do I need a brokerage account?
- **Is there a minimum amount** to invest?
- **Do I need to report anything to the tax office myself**, or is the tax taken off automatically when I sell?

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The code labels (MF-16, BRK-17 and so on) and the "הצגת החוקים והכללים" (show the laws and rules) links on the left.
2. The "123 מקור / חושב / הזנה" line at the top.
3. **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון"**.
4. Most of the glossary. Keep five or six words at most.
5. The **"חוקים וכללים"** column in the cost table.
6. The long sentence about the 10% foreign securities. I'd replace it with one plain line, like "the money arrives the same day or the next trading day."

I'd keep **"למה משתמשים בזה"**, the comparison box, **"שווה לדעת"** and the risks section. Those answered my real question: "what does this mean for my money?" The cost section stays too, but only if it gets an actual number.

I should also say that the screenshots didn't cover every part of the page. I didn't see the start of the tax section, part of the risks section, or the closed "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם" box. One of those may answer something I listed above.
