# Keren hishtalmut: what I took from the page

## 1. My answer

**I'd join, put in my share every month, and not touch the money for 6 years.** I'm about 80% sure that's right.

What convinced me was the big bar chart in "מה מייחד קרן השתלמות". It uses an example where I put in 300 ₪ a month and the employer puts in 900 ₪. After 5 years:

- I put in "18,000 ₪"
- "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪"
- the investments earned "+8,487 ₪"
- tax took "−13,974 ₪"
- I'd be left with "66,513 ₪"

So even if I take the money out early and pay tax, I end up with more than three times what I put in. If I wait, the top bar chart shows the pink striped tax part gone from year 6: "מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס".

The answer to "when can I take it out" was right at the top: "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון". I also saw that "לימודים מספיקות 3 שנים" (3 years are enough if it's for studies).

I'm not 100% sure for two reasons. First, I don't know whether my own salary is really 300/900 like the example. Second, I saw that the Finance Ministry's chief economist wants to cut the tax benefit ("הכלכלן הראשי במשרד האוצר ממליץ לצמצם את ההטבה"). The page says the law hasn't changed, but it still worried me a little.

## 4. Charts and tables

**Top bar chart (years 1–12):**
- **Looked at:** the year numbers on the bottom, the circled "5", and the dotted line with "לפני 6 שנים: מס" on the left and "מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס" on the right. I noticed the pink striped tops stop after year 5 and that the bars grow.
- **Colours:** I guessed grey = me, yellow = employer, teal = profit, only by matching them to the big chart. Nothing in this chart says so directly.
- **Ignored:** how tall each bar is exactly, since there are no numbers on it.

**Big bar chart:**
- **Looked at:** all five numbers on top and the labels underneath ("הפקדתם", "המעסיק הוסיף", "התשואה הוסיפה", "המס לקח", "נשאר בידיים"). The white bar "נשאר בידיים" is the one I cared about.
- **Ignored:** the dark brown bars inside, which I didn't understand, the small grey "אחרי דמי ניהול", and the dotted lines between the bars.

**"כמה זה עולה" table:**
- **Looked at:** the "סוג" and "תקרה" columns: "עד 2% בשנה" for management fees and "אין" for "דמי יציאה" (exit fees). That was enough.
- **Ignored:** "הוצאות ישירות" (didn't understand it), the blue company names, and the green tags.

**"מונחים" table:**
- **Used:** the "מונח" and "מה זה" columns.
- **Ignored:** the whole "איפה מופיע" column (תלוש שכר / דוח שנתי / טופס…).

## 6. What I wanted but couldn't find

- **My own numbers.** How much goes in at my salary? "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם" was closed, so I didn't get them.
- **What happens if I change jobs** before 6 years. Does the time I've built up (ותק) keep counting? Does the new employer continue the same fund?
- **Do I have to take the money out after 6 years,** or can I leave it to keep growing?
- **Which track to pick** if I'm 24, and whether the S&P 500 one is a good default.
- **Which company to choose,** or at least what to look for. The only thing I got was "low fees", from the 0.19% vs 0.7% line.
- **Can I ask for it** if my employer doesn't offer it by default, and how much to ask for.
- **What the economist's proposal would mean** for someone joining now.
