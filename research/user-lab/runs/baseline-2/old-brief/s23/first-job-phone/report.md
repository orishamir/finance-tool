## 1. My answer

I'd go with **כלל (Clal)**. Its S&P 500 track has the lowest fee on the page: **0.48% "מההיסכון"**, fund number **13342**. I'm about 75% sure.

- I'm confident on one point: the page says **"המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול מההיסכון, מהיקר לזול"** (sorted from most expensive to cheapest). The line above it says the fees run **"מ־0.48% עד 0.7%"**. So the last row should be the cheapest, and Clal is the last row that has a number.
- I'm less sure for three reasons:
  - The bottom row, **אילון קופות גמל**, says **"עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet) and has a **"חדש"** tag. It could turn out cheaper, but I can't tell.
  - The heading says the fee is **"ממוצע 2025"**, an average from last year. I don't know if I would actually pay 0.48%.
  - The gaps are tiny. **מנורה מבטחים** is 0.49%, and **הראל** and **מגדל** are 0.5%. If Clal didn't work out, I'd just pick Menora.

- **"מקור / מחושב / הזנחה"** near the top.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"**. What is מט"ח, and how can something be 101%?
- Why Ayalon's S&P 500 track shows only **40% / 54%** when it's supposed to follow the S&P 500.
- The top says 12 tracks, but I only counted 11 rows while scrolling. I may have missed one between Mor and Altshuler. Since the list is sorted from expensive to cheap, a row there wouldn't be the cheapest anyway.
