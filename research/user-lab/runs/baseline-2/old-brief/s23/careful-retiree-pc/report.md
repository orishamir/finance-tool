# Me, at 61, looking for the cheapest S&P 500 keren hishtalmut

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I would go with **כלל** (Clal). It has the lowest fee on the page, **0.48%**. Right behind it is **מנורה מבטחות** (Menora) at **0.49%**. **הראל** (Harel) and **מגדל** (Migdal) are at **0.5%**.

I nearly picked the wrong one. The first row is **מור** (Mor) at **0.7%**, and I took it for the "best" because it was on top. Then I read the small line "המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, **מהיקר לזול**", which means "sorted by fee, most expensive to cheapest." So the cheapest is at the bottom of the list, not the top.

**What it means for my money:** the gap between cheapest and most expensive is 0.7% − 0.48% = 0.22% a year. On 200,000 ₪ that is about 440 ₪ a year. Between Clal and Menora (0.01%) it is about 20 ₪ a year, which is nothing. So really "any of the bottom four" is fine. I'd choose Clal because it is cheapest and also has a full 5-year record (**11.99%**). Menora and Migdal show "פחות מ־5 שנים" (less than 5 years).

I would **not** choose **איילון קופות גמל** (Ayalon). Its fee says "עוד לא דווח" (not reported yet), it is marked "חדש" (new), and its stock exposure is only **40%**.

**How sure am I:** fairly sure Clal is the cheapest *on this page*. I'm less sure that 0.48% is what *I* would pay, because it says "ממוצע 2025" (average for 2025), not a promised price.
