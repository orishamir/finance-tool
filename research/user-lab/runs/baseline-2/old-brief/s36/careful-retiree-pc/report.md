# Speaking as the 61-year-old salaried reader

I read all four screens, top to bottom. Two charts are on offer. **Option A, "א. הערימה גדלה"**, has standing columns and carries the designers' "מומלץ" (recommended) badge. **Option B, "ב. אותו סיפור בשורות"**, shows the same numbers as lying-down rows. **I would pick B.** In B the name and the amount sit together on the right, like lines on a bank statement: "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪", "המס לקח −13,974 ₪", "נשאר בידיים 66,513 ₪". I read down it once and I'm done. In A the labels are split over two lines under the columns ("המעסיק / הוסיף"), and I kept going from the number at the top to the word at the bottom. The "מומלץ" badge made no difference to me, because I don't know who is recommending it or why.

## 1. My answer

**I would take the keren hishtalmut and not a plain securities account. I'm about 85% sure.**

What decides it for me is the line "המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪". I put in 300 ₪ a month and my employer puts in 900 ₪. I checked the numbers myself: 2.5% and 7.5% of 12,000 ₪. Even in the worst case, where I pull the money out early and "המס לקח −13,974 ₪", I still have 66,513 ₪ from my own 18,000 ₪. If I wait until year 6, the tax is "0 ₪" and I have 98,738 ₪.

I'm not 100% sure because of my age and because the page never puts the securities account side by side with the fund (see points 5 and 6).

## 2. What I actually used

- **The assumptions paragraph** that starts "הדוגמה: שכר ברוטו 12,000 ₪". I used it to check that the example is someone like me.
- **Option A at year 5 and at year 6.** These are the waterfall columns and the sentence "אם מושכים עכשיו ושמים את הכסף בחשבון ניירות ערך עד שנה 6: 68,837 ₪, כלומר 15,141 ₪ פחות מאשר לחכות."
- **Option B's rows.** This is the one I'd keep.
- **The small "before" picture at the top.** I squinted at it because it had the sentence I actually wanted: "כל שקל שהפקדתם הפך ל-3.70 ₪ בקרן ו-1.09 ₪ בחשבון ניירות ערך." That one line answers my question better than anything else on the page.

## 3. Block by block

- **The title "קרן השתלמות: הסיפור של הכסף, שלב אחרי שלב"**: read it. It was fine.
- **The subtitle, "בלי המשפט על כל שקל, ובלי חשבון ניירות ערך לצד הקרן"**: this confused me. It says the securities account was removed, but comparing against a securities account is exactly why I came. I didn't understand "אפשר לגרור את השנים" until later. Drag what?
- **"מה היה: חלקים שמרחפים…" with the small old picture**: half-confused. "חלקים שמרחפים" (floating parts) means nothing to me. But the tiny picture had the fund and the account side by side (19,595 ₪ against 66,513 ₪), and that was useful even though it was hard to read.
- **The assumptions paragraph**: I read every word. The orange warning, "⚠ הנחה: אתם 2.5% והמעסיק 7.5%", was helpful because it told me to check my own payslip. Then came "(13245)", "(5139282)", "4D", "פייר פיננשיאל טכנולוגיז בע"מ" and "מבין 14 שנאספו". I skipped those. They're code numbers to me.
- **Option A at year 5**: used it. I worked out the colours, and then the numbers made sense.
- **"אותו ציור שנה אחת אחר כך…", option A at year 6**: used it. Seeing "המס לקח 0 ₪" was the most reassuring moment on the page.
- **"הגובה נמתח בכל שנה, והגדילה משנה לשנה נראית ברצועה."**: confused me. See point 5.
- **Option B**: used it and liked it best.

## 4. Inside the charts

**Looked at:**
- **Grey** = my deposit, "הפקדתם". **Bright yellow** = the employer. **Green-blue** = the return. **Pink stripes** = tax. **White** = "נשאר בידיים", what's left in my hands.
- **The dashed box**, "אם מחכים לשנה 6, בלי להפקיד עוד 83,978 ₪". This was important to me: waiting one year without depositing anything more is worth about 17,000 ₪.
- **The labels above the top strip**: "לפני 6 שנים: מס" in red and "מ-6 שנים: פטור ממס". This was clear.
- **The circled year number** (5 or 6), so I knew which year I was looking at.

**Ignored or unsure about:**
- **The dark brown and dark grey blocks** under the bright colours. I took them to be "the same money from before" and stopped looking at them.
- **The small grey words "אחרי דמי ניהול"** under the return. I noticed them but didn't stop.
- **Years 7 to 12 in the top strip.** I just saw "it keeps growing".
- **In the year-6 chart, the dark green box above "המס לקח 0 ₪".** If the tax is zero, why is there a box there at all? It made me doubt the number for a moment.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"הגובה נמתח בכל שנה"** (the height stretches every year). Does that mean I can't compare the size of the columns from year to year? Then what am I supposed to compare?
- **Why the tax is so big**, 13,974 ₪, when the return is only 8,487 ₪. Is it taxing the employer's money too? The page doesn't say what is being taxed.
- **"בין 19 המסלולים שנאספו… 66,280 ₪ בדמי ניהול של 0.7% עד 67,028 ₪ בדמי ניהול של 0.19%"**. I think it means "the fees barely matter here", but the page doesn't say that in so many words.
- **"מסלול מדד", "S&P500", "מחקה"**. These are jargon to me.
- **"מומלץ"**: recommended by whom?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **The plain comparison I came for.** If I skip the keren and put my own 300 ₪ a month into a securities account, what do I have after 5 and 6 years? It was only in the small old picture, and the new page says on purpose that it's gone.
- **What happens to the employer's 900 ₪ if I don't join.** Do I get it in my salary? Do I lose it?
- **Anything about age or retirement.** I'm 61. Can I take the money out tax-free before 6 years once I retire? So what does this mean for *my* money?
- **Whether there is a ceiling** on how much salary counts.
- **The fact that the amounts are "in today's money".** I only worked that out from "אינפלציה 2%" in the small print.

1. **First:** the "מה היה: חלקים שמרחפים…" line. Keep the one sentence "כל שקל שהפקדתם הפך ל-3.70 ₪ בקרן ו-1.09 ₪ בחשבון ניירות ערך" and make it big.
2. **Second:** the fund and account codes and company names in the assumptions paragraph.
3. **Third:** one of the two chart options. I'd keep B.
4. **Fourth:** the line "הגובה נמתח…".
5. **Never remove:** the 12,000 ₪ / 2.5% / 7.5% assumptions, the tax line, "נשאר בידיים", and the "אם מחכים לשנה 6" box.
