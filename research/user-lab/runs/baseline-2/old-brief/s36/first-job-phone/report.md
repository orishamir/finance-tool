# s36: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 48 s, $0.15

I'd pick **option B (ב. אותו סיפור בשורות)**, even though the designers marked A as **מומלץ**. The rows put each label right next to its number, so it reads top to bottom like a payslip: **הפקדתם 18,000 ₪**, **המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪**, and so on. In A, I had to look back and forth between the tall bars and the labels under them.

### 1. My answer and how sure I am
I'd open a keren hishtalmut, or keep the one I have, and not take the money out before year 6. I'm about 80% sure.

What decided it for me is **המעסיק הוסיף +54,000 ₪** next to **הפקדתם 18,000 ₪**. I put in 300 a month and my boss puts in 900. My own S&P 500 account never gets that extra 900. The year 6 chart settled the timing: **המס לקח 0 ₪** and **נשאר בידיים 98,738 ₪**, compared with 66,513 ₪ if I take it out at year 5.

The missing 20% is because of the note **"האחוזים האמיתיים כתובים בהסכם העבודה"**. I don't know if my job even offers this, or at what percentages.
