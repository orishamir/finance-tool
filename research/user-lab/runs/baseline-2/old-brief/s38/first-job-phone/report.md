# I'd use option 1 ("החברה בראש השורה")

I'd pick option 1. When it opens, a group headed "עוקב מדד s&p 500" is already at the top, with the cheapest fund first. That's exactly what I came for. Option 3 has the same list, but you only reach it after a big coloured grid that I didn't need. Option 2 sorts by company first, so I'd have to dig through all 16 of Harel's tracks before I got to the S&P one.

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd open the S&P 500 track at **מנורה מבטחים**. It has the lowest yearly fee, "0.52% מהחיסכון", and its "13.79% 3 שנים" is about the best return in that group.

I'm about 60% sure. Two things hold me back:
- Menora also charges "0.01% מההפקדה" while almost everyone else charges "0%". I can't tell whether 0.52% + 0.01% costs me more than Altshuler's "0.54% / 0%".
- If I got cold feet, I'd go with **הראל**. It's only a little more expensive (0.56%), and "19.1 מיליארד ₪" makes it feel big and safe.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- The product chips row, to check that "קופת גמל להשקעה 143" was selected.
- The "במה הכסף מושקע" chips, to see that "עוקב מדד s&p 500" exists (12 tracks).
- The "מיון לפי" buttons. The default, "דמי ניהול מהחיסכון", was the right one for me.
- The table under the "עוקב מדד s&p 500" header: its 12 rows, and the summary line "דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.71%".

## 3. Block by block

- **Title and the "143 מסלולים של 12 חברות" line:** skimmed. It's nice to know it's "כל מספר כאן אמיתי".
- **Option tabs (1/2/3, "מומלץ") and the "מחשב / טלפון" switch:** this is how I picked between the options. Not part of the actual page.
- **"מקור · מדריכים · חוקים וכללים · שאלות…" navigation:** skipped.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר":** skipped. I didn't know what I'd gain by switching.
- **Product chips ("תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים", "קרן השתלמות", "חיסכון לכל ילד"…):** skimmed. A bit confusing, because I don't know how these differ from what I chose. I'd click "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה".
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" chips:** used the S&P one and skipped the rest. "אג"ח", "מט"ח", "גמיש" and "הלכה יהודית" mean nothing to me as investments.
- **"מיון לפי" and "חיפוש בשם המסלול":** used the sort and ignored the search.
- **S&P table:** used. This is the whole page for me.
- **The other groups (מניות, כללי, אג"ח…):** skipped.
- **Small grey note "התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס":** read it, and it worried me a bit (see question 5).
- **Option 3's grid, "כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה":** I looked only at the "עוקב מדד s&p 500" column to confirm that Menora (0.52%) is the cheapest. Everything else was noise, and I didn't know what the colours were trying to tell me at first.
- **Orange "בשרטוט בלבד…" notes:** skipped. They're designer notes.

## 4. Inside the table

**What I looked at:**
- **Company name:** in bold, plus the track's long name.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025":** the main thing I compared, especially "מהחיסכון". I glanced at "מההפקדה" and noticed that Menora (0.01%) and Infinity (0.05%) aren't zero.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת":** I compared the 3-year and 5-year numbers. Seeing "פחות מ־3 שנים" made me trust Altshuler, Analyst, Infinity and Yelin Lapidot less.
- **"נכסים":** a size check. Ayalon's "2 מיליון ₪" and its "חדש" tag put me off.

**What I ignored:**
- **The "קופה" number column (13874, 14865…):** meaningless to me.
- **The circles and the "מניות / מט"ח" percentages:** I understood that 99% מניות means "all in stocks", which was fine. I didn't understand מט"ח, and I didn't look at the colours.
- **The little clock icons:** I ignored them, and I never figured out what they mean.
- **The dotted underlines on numbers:** I guessed they lead somewhere, but I didn't try them.
- **The "›" arrows at the end of each row:** ignored.
- **The heatmap colours in option 3:** I worked out "darker = more expensive" from the text, but I only used one column.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח" next to a percentage:** why does it matter that my money is 99% in foreign currency?
- **"דמי ניהול מההפקדה" vs "מהחיסכון":** what's the difference, and which one costs me more?
- **"לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס":** so how much would I really get?
- **"עוד לא דווח" and "חדש":** I didn't know whether these are a bad sign.
- **"אג"ח", "גמיש", "משולב סחיר":** I don't know what any of these mean.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- How much I'd actually pay in shekels a year, for example if I saved 1,000 ₪ a month.
- Whether the fee is negotiable. I've heard people say that.
- How to actually join, whether there's a minimum, and whether I can take the money out early.
- Why I'd use this instead of just buying the S&P 500 through a bank or an app.
- Since the S&P tracks all look nearly the same, whether the company I pick even matters.
- A plain "cheapest overall" answer that combines both fees.

## 7. If half the page had to go

First I'd cut the grid "כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה". Then:
- the "קופה" number column
- the "מקור / מדריכים…" navigation and the "לפי חברה / לפי מוצר" tabs
- every investment group except the one I picked
- the circle charts

I'd keep the "במה הכסף מושקע" filter, the fee column, the return column and the size column.
