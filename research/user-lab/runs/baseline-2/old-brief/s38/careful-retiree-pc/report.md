# Choosing where to save: which page I'd use

**The option I'd pick:** No. 3, **"טבלת חברות מול סוגים"** (the companies-against-types table, the one marked "מומלץ"). I didn't pick it because of that mark. I picked it because it has the same list as option 1 underneath, plus one table on top that answers my real question: *which company keeps less of my money?* Option 1 only compares companies inside one kind of track. Option 2 (**"קיבוץ לפי חברה"**) says itself it's "פחות טוב להשוואה" (less good for comparing), and that's true. I scrolled through a long list of Harel tracks and then Mor tracks, and none of it helped me choose.

## 1. My answer

I'd lean towards **Harel's general track, "הראל גמל להשקעה כללי"** (fund number 8211):
- The fee is **0.55%** a year, compared with **0.72%** for the same kind of track at Mor. If I had ₪100,000 in it, that's about ₪550 a year instead of ₪720. I had to work that out myself.
- It's about half in shares (**"מניות 49%"**). At 61 I don't want **99%** shares, which is what all the **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"** tracks have, even though they show the best returns (**13.79%** and so on).
- Harel's row in the big table is dark nearly all the way across, so it's cheap in most types, and it's the biggest company (**"19.1 מיליארד ₪"**).

**How sure am I?** Maybe 5 out of 10. I'm fairly sure about the fees. I'm not sure the general track is right for someone my age, and I'd phone someone before moving any money.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The light-blue button **"קופת גמל להשקעה 143"**, so I knew I was in the right product.
- The colour table **"כל החברות מול כל סוגי ההשקעה"**, especially the **"כללי"** and **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"** columns.
- The list **"כל המסלולים"** under it: the fee column, the share/foreign-currency figures and the 3- and 5-year returns.
- The small print under the table: **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס"** (returns are past returns, before fees and before tax).

## 4. The tables: what I looked at and what I ignored

**The colour table**
- I looked at the **"חברה"** column, the percentages, and the explanation **"כתום חזק יותר הוא דמי ניהול גבוהים יותר"** (stronger orange means higher fees). Once I'd read that, it worked: Mor's row is bright orange, so Mor is expensive.
- I ignored **"נכסים בקופות גמל להשקעה"** (money in the funds) and the tiny **"2 מסלולים"** (2 tracks) under some figures.
- I took **"—"** to mean the company doesn't offer that type. **"חדש"** (new) puzzled me: new, so there's no fee yet?

**The long list**
- I looked at **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"** (average fee in 2025).
- I looked at **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"** (average yearly return) and its **"3 שנים / 5 שנים"** (3 years / 5 years).
- I looked at the **"מניות"** percentage.
- I ignored the rings, the little clock icons, the purple versus white underlines, **"קופה"** (the fund number), and **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"** (assets at end of September 2026).

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"** next to **"מניות"**. My guess is foreign currency. Is 97% of that good or bad for me?
- There are two fees: **"מהחיסכון"** (from savings) and **"מההפקדות"** (from deposits). Mostly the second one is **"0%"**, but Menora shows **"0.01%"** and Infinity **"0.05%"**. Do I pay both?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years). I worked out that the track is too young to have a figure, but it took me a while.
- **"עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet), which Ayalon shows instead of a fee. Is that a warning?
- **"שנגבו בפועל"** (actually charged). Is that what *I* would pay, or can I bargain for less?
- What the rings and the clock icons mean.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **What does this mean for my money, in shekels?** For example, "on ₪100,000 you pay ₪550 a year here and ₪720 there".
- Returns *after* fees. The page says they're before fees and leaves the sums to me.
- Which kinds of tracks are sensible at my age, and how risky each one is in plain words.
- When I can take the money out, how it's taxed, and what changes at 60.
- How to move my money and what it costs.
- Whether the company answers the phone. Service matters to me.

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. Option 2 (grouped by company) goes first.
2. Then the rings, the clock icons, the **"נכסים"** column, the **"קופה"** numbers and the repeated menus.
3. Then the rarely used chips (**"אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר 1"**, **"הלכה איסלאמית 1"**, **"קיימון 2"**).

I'd keep the colour table, the fee and return columns, and that small print about returns being before fees.
