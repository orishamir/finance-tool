# s35: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 52 s, $0.17

**Which I'd rather see: Option A ("א. עמודה לכל דרך").** I noticed the designers marked it "מומלץ", but that's not why I picked it. It has one short line that tells me what this costs me: "300 ₪ בחודש מכם, 900 ₪ מהמעסיק". Option B leaves that line out.

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd join. My reasoning, in my own words: 300 shekels a month comes off my pay, and my employer adds 900 that I wouldn't get otherwise. After 5 years that's "66,513 ₪" in the fund against "19,595 ₪" if I'd invested the same 300 myself. The sentence that settled it was "כל שקל שהפקדתם נהיה ₪ 3.70 בקרן ו-₪ 1.09 בחשבון ניירות ערך". I understood that sentence on the first read.

I'm about 80% sure. The rest is doubt about my age. The page says that before 6 years "מס", and from 6 years "פטור ממס". At 61, six years takes me to 67. If I need the money sooner, the page shows tax of "−13,974 ₪" at year 5, more than all the profit. I'd still come out far ahead, but I'd like to know my real situation.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- The title and the line under it, "רצועת השנים נשארת, עכשיו בצבעים…".
- In Option A: the 5-year picture, the toggle set to "שכירים", the "כל שקל…" sentence, and the two big totals marked "נשאר בידיים".
- The second copy, "אותו ציור שנה אחת אחר כך", for year 6. The tax there is "0 ₪" and the total is "98,738 ₪". That jump of about 32,000 shekels in one year told me to wait the full 6 years.

- The line "בין 14 התעריפים שנאספו: 10,764 ₪ … עד 19,595 ₪". So the 19,595 shown is the best case? Then the account could be half that.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **My age:** can I take the money without tax when I retire, even before 6 years? At 61 this matters most to me.
- **Leaving my job:** what happens if I change jobs or stop working before 6 years?
- **Not joining:** do I get the 900 in my salary instead, or is it simply lost?
- **My paycheck:** how much less will actually reach my bank each month?
- **Bad years:** what if the market falls? The page assumes 7% every year.
- **My own numbers:** a place to put in my real salary instead of 12,000.
