# s30: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 52 s, $0.27

I'm 24, I have a salary, and I came here to see how "my" fund (the iShares S&P 500 Swap, yearly fee 0.05%) compares with the funds on the list. I left the sliders as they were: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7%.

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd drop my fund and go with what the page puts on top: an account at **פסגות** (Psagot) with the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, which ends at **86,492 ₪**.

- **About not buying my fund: about 70% sure.** In the main list, my fund's row only says **"עוד לא נאסף"** (not collected yet) and **"אין מחיר כאן"** (no price here). It gets no final number. The only number I found for it was further down, in the table **"סוג קרן אחר, אותו חשבון"** (another fund type, same account): **59,196 ₪**, marked **"פחות 19,980 ₪"** (19,980 less) with a red bar. That is almost 20,000 ₪ less than the Israeli funds, which scared me off. I also noticed my fund costs 0.05% a year, and the Yelin fund shows **0.02%**.
- **About Psagot + Yelin being right: about 60% sure.** I don't fully trust it. The lower part of the page starts from a different place (**"עכשיו: MTF סל (S&P 500 (4D)), ירושלים"**, 79,176 ₪) and I don't know why. The 20,000 gap might also come from the bank, not from my fund. I can't tell.
