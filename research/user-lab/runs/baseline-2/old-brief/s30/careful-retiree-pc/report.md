# s30: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 65 s, $0.29

**1. What I would do, and how sure I am**

I would not buy my iShares Swap fund yet. If I had to put money in this month, I'd lean toward the page's top pick: **פסגות** (Psagot) as the account with **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**. The page says that leaves me **86,492 ₪** after 10 years. The next pair is only **"‑43 ₪"** behind, so I see the top few choices as roughly equal.

On "don't buy my fund yet" I'm fairly sure, maybe 7 out of 10. On "go with Psagot and Yelin" I'm only about 5 out of 10, because I came here to check my own fund and I never got a clear answer about it.

Three things worried me about my fund:
- **The main list doesn't price it.** My fund shows only **"עוד לא נאסף"** and **"אין מחיר כאן"**. It does show the 0.05% fee I typed in (**"הזנתם"**), so it did take my input.
- **One table near the bottom prices it very badly.** Under **"סוג קרן אחר, אותו חשבון"**, the row **"קרן שנקנית בחו"ל – iShares S&P 500 Swap UCITS ETF USD (Acc)"** leaves me **59,196 ₪**. That's **"פחות 19,980 ₪"**, in red, with a little lock. The other iShares fund bought abroad, "Core", sits in the row just above it and comes out **"עוד 1,995 ₪"**. I don't see why two similar foreign funds would be about 22,000 ₪ apart. The lock's explanation, **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג"**, sounds like "there's a tax question nobody has settled". To me that means "we're not sure what tax you'll pay". With my retirement money, that alone stops me.
- **The ID code may be wrong.** The code under my fund is **IE00B5BMR087**. The very next line, iShares **Core**, has exactly the same code. I'm not an expert, but two different funds shouldn't share one ID. It makes me wonder whether the page is really looking at the fund I mean.

**2. The parts I actually used**

- The four boxes at the top: **"סכום היום"** (amount today), **"הפקדה חודשית"** (monthly deposit), **"שנים"** (years) and **"תשואה בשנה"** (yearly return). I wanted to check that the numbers were mine.
- The result box with **"הבחירה שלכם · הזול"** showing 86,492 ₪, and **"הזוג הבא בתור"** (the next pair) showing 86,449 ₪.
- The fund list **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500"**, mainly my own fund's row.
- The account list on the right, **"כל החשבונות"**: the big numbers and the **"לא בחישוב"** (not in the calculation) notes.
- The text **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות"** (why some funds have no price at Psagot).
- The tables near the bottom starting **"עכשיו: …"**. That's where I finally found my fund with a number.
- The final notes, especially **"קרנות בחו"ל שהתעריפון שלהן נקבע לפי מספר המניות מחכות למחיר היחידה של הקרן, שעוד לא נאסף"**. In plain words: some foreign funds can't be priced yet because the site doesn't have their unit price.

**3. Block by block**

- **Top menu and title "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן"**: read it. Clear, and it's what I want to know.
- **Index buttons (S&P 500 selected)**: glanced to confirm S&P 500 was picked. Fine.
- **"מדד S&P 500" description**: read it. It says the money is held in dollars, and that **"אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ"** (you can also hold it through a foreign fund). Good, that's my case.
- **The four sliders**: used them. I left them as they were.
- **"אינפלציה" line with the dollar rate 3.16**: skimmed. I understood that everything is in today's money, which I like.
- **Result box "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪ → 86,492 ₪"**: used it. It's the clearest thing on the page: I put in 70,000 and get 86,492.
- **Coloured cost bar ("עמלות קנייה ומכירה, דמי משמרת, דמי ניהול חשבון…")**: skimmed. Too many colours. I took only the three totals below it, **"החשבון 1 ₪ יותר"**, **"הקרן 58 ₪ פחות"** and **"המס 14 ₪ יותר"**, and those are tiny amounts.
- **Psagot card "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,057 ₪"**: used it. A cost I can understand.
- **Fund list**: used it for my fund and skimmed the rest. The coloured tags (**"צוברת", "חשיפה לדולר", "מס רק במכירה", "כמה דרכים יחד"**) were too many to read on every row. I read them only for my fund.
- **Account list on the right**: used the top few. The long **"לא בחישוב"** notes about soldiers and students don't apply to me at 61, so I skipped them.
- **Interactive Brokers box ("אין נתון")**: read it, because that's the kind of foreign broker I'd use for my fund. It gives 82,848 ₪ for the Core fund, not mine. Half useful.
- **Text block under the fund list**: read it. The line saying the Israeli law on fee caps doesn't apply to foreign funds was useful.
- **"ההשוואה כאן היא לפי התעריפונים…" with the list of complaints (טענות)**: read it. It made me trust the advertised prices less, which is fair. I didn't click the complaints.
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם" (line chart and table)**: skimmed. The message that the number of years matters most (**"19,116 ₪ יותר"**) is common sense, and it doesn't help me choose a fund.
- **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל" (currency hedging) and its two bar charts and table**: skimmed, and it confused me a bit. My fund is unhedged (**"חשיפה לדולר"**). I took one thing from it: the dollar can swing a lot (**"‑12.5% עד +13.4%"** in a single year). But **"זה בסיס קטן: 4 שנים"** (it's only 4 years of data) told me not to lean on it.
- **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת…" step chart (79,176 → 86,492)**: confused me. It starts from **"עכשיו"** (now) as MTF at **ירושלים** (Jerusalem). I never told the page I hold anything there. I don't hold that.
- **The "עכשיו" tables**: used them, because that's where my fund finally shows up, with the big red minus.
- **Footer notes**: read them all, as I always do.

**4. Lists, tables and charts: what I looked at and what I ignored**

- **Fund list**: I looked at the fund name, the code, the **"לעומת הבחירה"** (compared with your choice) column in shekels, and the tags on my fund. I ignored the **"מול המדד"** (versus the index) column, with figures like +0.37% and "שנה אחת". I didn't know if bigger was better.
- **Account list**: I looked at the big top number, for example 86,492. I ignored the smaller number under it (1,057), the orange and yellow bars, and the circled numbers 1 to 15.
- **Cost colour bar**: ignored the colours. I read only the three totals.
- **Line chart, "מה הכי משנה"**: looked at the coloured labels on the right (שנים, הפקדה חודשית, …) and the "ההפרש" (difference) column. Ignored the axis marks "50% פחות … 50% יותר".
- **Currency charts**: on the right I looked only at the white bar for 2025 (‑12.5). On the left I compared the teal bars (no hedge) with the yellow ones (hedge) for 2022. I ignored the grey bars for older years.
- **Hedge table**: read only the **"4 השנים יחד"** (all 4 years) row. Ignored the rest.
- **Step chart**: looked at the numbers on top (+4,972, +2,344). Ignored the wavy line inside the grey bar.
- **"עכשיו" tables**: looked at **"נשאר בסוף"** (left at the end) and **"לעומת עכשיו"** (compared with now), plus the green and red bars. I noticed the lock icon on my fund and looked up its meaning in the legend.

**5. What I didn't understand**

- Why my fund comes out **19,980 ₪ worse** when the similar Core fund comes out better. What exactly is the **"כלל מס פתוח"** (open tax rule)?
- Why my fund and Core show the same code, **IE00B5BMR087**.
- Why the page thinks I'm starting from MTF at Jerusalem ("עכשיו").
- What **"4D"** and **"4A"** mean in the fund names.
- What **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"** (tax also on currency change) means for me in shekels, compared with **"מס רק במכירה"** (tax only when you sell).
- **"נאמן 0.02%"**: who is the trustee, and do I pay that on top?
- The **"מול המדד"** column: is +0.37% good or bad?

**6. What I wanted and couldn't find**

- One clear line saying: "Your iShares Swap fund, bought abroad at 0.05%, leaves you X ₪ after 10 years with broker Y," next to the winner.
- Which Israeli account can actually buy my fund, and what it costs there.
- A plain explanation of the tax on a swap fund held from Israel. Is it really worse, or is it just uncertain?
- Whether my 0.05% fee was even used in the 59,196 ₪ figure.
- Something about safety: what happens to my money if the foreign broker or the fund company goes bust?

**7. If half the page had to go, what goes first**

1. The **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי"** section, with both bar charts and the table.
2. The **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"** line chart.
3. The coloured cost bar and its legend at the top.
4. The long **"לא בחישוב"** notes about soldiers and students.
5. The **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת"** step chart, which assumes something I never told it.

I would keep the four boxes at the top, the result box, the fund and account lists, and the explanation of why some funds have no price. Above all, I'd want my own fund shown clearly next to the winner, with the reason it's better or worse.
