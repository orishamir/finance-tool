# Keren hishtalmut: what I took from the page

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd say yes to my employer and open it. I'm about **80% sure** it's worth having.

The page convinced me with these lines:
- From "מה זה": the employer puts in up to 7.5% of my salary and I put in up to 2.5% (*"מעסיק עד 7.5% מהשכר ועד 2.5% מכם"*).
- From the calculator area: *"הכסף של המעסיק לא עומד לבחירה ולכן הוא מחוץ להשוואה"*.

On my 12,000 ₪ salary, I put in 300 ₪ a month and my employer adds 900 ₪ that I wouldn't get otherwise. That's the main reason.

On when I can take the money out, I'm fairly sure:
- After **6 years** from the first deposit, tax-free (*"6 שנים אחרי התשלום הראשון אפשר למשוך הכול בפטור ממס"*).
- Only **3 years** if I'm retiring or using it for studies (*"למי שמושך את הכסף להשתלמות, היא רק 3 שנים"*).
- Before that I can still take it, but it's expensive. The orange box says *"משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה"*, with tax up to **47%** on the employer's part. That scared me a bit. It tells me not to put money there that I might need in the next couple of years.

The other 20%: at first the calculator made it look tiny, only *"415.84 ₪ יותר"* after 10 years. I almost thought "why bother." Only later did I understand that the comparison leaves out the employer's money. I also don't know how to choose a fund, so I'm not sure about the next step.
