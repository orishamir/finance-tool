# Keren hishtalmut, read by a 61-year-old salaried worker

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would say yes to the keren hishtalmut and ask my employer to put in the full amount.** I'm about 80% sure.

What convinced me was the first explanation under "מה זה". My employer adds up to 7.5% on top of my salary, and I add up to 2.5%. The line that mattered most was:

> "המעסיק יכול להפקיד עד 7.5% מהמשכורת הקבועה בלי שזה ייחשב לכם הכנסה"

So that's money my employer pays in, and I don't pay income tax on it now. Then under "למה משתמשים בזה":

> "לשכירים עם מעסיק שמפקיד זה כמעט תמיד הצעד הראשון"

And in the same box:

> "הכסף של המעסיק לא עומד לבחירה ולכן הוא מחוץ להשוואה"

In other words, if I don't join, I don't get that money any other way. That settled it for me.

The other 20% is my age. The rule says the money is locked for 6 years:

> "6 שנים אחרי התשלום הראשון אפשר למשוך הכול בפטור ממס"

At 61, six years takes me to 67. But in the box under "מה מייחד" I found this:

> "למי שהגיע לגיל פרישה... היא רק 3 שנים"

That probably matters a lot for me. The page doesn't say what "גיל פרישה" means in years, though, or whether the 3 years start from when I reach that age or from the first payment.

- **"מה זה"**. This was the main explanation.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**. Only the first paragraph.
- **"מה מייחד קרן השתלמות"**. Only the paragraphs of words under the calculator, not the calculator itself.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**. The cards "כשעוברות 6 שנים" and "עד 4 ימי עסקים". The second one told me that once I ask, the money arrives within 4 business days.
- **"כמה זה עולה"**. The fees table.
- **"מיסוי"**. The orange warning box.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**.

## 3. Going down the page, block by block

- **Top menu (מדריך, חוקים וכללים, ...).** Skipped it.
- **Title and grey sub-line.** Used. Clear.
- **"123 מקור 123 חושב 123 הזנתי..." line.** Confused me. I don't know what these numbers are or why I'd click them, so I ignored it.
- **"מה זה".** Used, and I read it twice. "לפני כן משיכה אפשרית, אבל ממוסה" was reassuring: the money isn't completely trapped.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה".** Used the paragraph. The line "המחיר לעומת קופת גמל להשקעה" lost me, because I don't know what a קופת גמל להשקעה is. Then come the buttons: "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים", "קרן חדשה מקיפה", "S&P 500 מדד", "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה"... Skipped them. Too much jargon, and I don't know which one I'm supposed to pick.
- **"מה מייחד" and the calculator.** Here I started reading carefully and then got lost. It compares my fund with something called "חשבון ניירות ערך", which I don't have and didn't ask about. It mentions "(13245)" and "(5139282)", "יילין לפידות" and "פייר פיננשיאל טכנולוגיז". These are product numbers and company names that mean nothing to me. - **The year slider (0 to 12).** I looked at it. The striped part, "נעול: משיכה ממוסה", runs until 6, and the green part, "אפשר למשוך בפטור", starts after that. That picture I understood. The page doesn't show where I'd be at 61.

- **Paragraphs under the calculator.** Used about half. "300 ₪ ... הפקדה מוטבת" confused me: 300 ₪ out of 12,000 ₪ is 2.5%, so is that only my own part? The 15,712 ₪ ceiling and the 25% tax on profits I did take in.
- **"ההפרש תלוי גם במי שבוחרים" (bank vs fund manager ranges).** Skimmed it. A spread from "‎-342.02 ₪" to "2,106 ₪" just tells me the choice of company matters. It doesn't tell me which company to choose.
- **"הכי קרוב לזה... חשבון ניירות ערך" link.** Skipped it.
- **"שווה לדעת" (TXH-09, KH-15, PF-18).** Skimmed. The codes mean nothing to me. The third line made me pause: "אין העברה חלקית, ואיחוד רק כשנזיל". I don't know what "נזיל" means here.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח".** Used. Good to know the employer chooses the company: "המעסיק לא יכול להתנות את ההפקדה בקרן מסוימת". I think that means I may choose the company myself. I'm not 100% sure.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא" (cards).** Used. These were the clearest part of the page.
- **"כמה זה עולה" table.** Used. See section 4.
- **"מיסוי" cards and orange box.** Used. The orange box was frightening in a useful way: "הפקדות המעסיק ממוסות כשכר... עד 47%". So if I take the money out early, I could lose almost half.
- **"באוצר דנים בצמצום ההטבה".** Read it, and it worries me. Will the rules change before my 6 years are up?
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן".** Used. "אין ערבות של המדינה לשווי הקרן" is important to me. "הנכסים מוחזקים בנאמנות, בנפרד מהרכוש של החברה המנהלת" reassured me.
- **"מה האתר לקח בחשבון".** Only the heading reached my screen, so I didn't read it.
- **"מונחים" (glossary).** Used a few lines, "ותק" and "מועד התשלום הראשון", to understand the 6 years. The tags on the left ("אפליקציה", "טופס", "תקנון", "דוח שנתי") I ignored.
- **Footer.** Skipped it. "נתונים נכון ל־01.11.2026" was good to see, because it means the numbers are recent.
- **The links "הצגת החוקים והכללים" on the left.** Never clicked them.

## 5. What I did not understand

- "גיל פרישה" and the 3 years. Does this apply to me at 61?
- "הפקדה מוטבת" next to "300 ₪" in the example. Is that my part, or the whole thing?
- "קופת גמל להשקעה", "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים", "קרן חדשה מקיפה". Are these things I need to choose?
- "נזיל", "איחוד", "העברה חלקית".
- "הרווח הריאלי". I got the gist from the glossary ("פחות החלק שנובע מעליית המדד"), but I still couldn't work out what 25% of that would be in shekels.
- Why the calculator compares me with a "חשבון ניירות ערך" at all.
- The "123 מקור / חושב / הזנתי" line at the top.

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **An example with the employer's 7.5%.** For example: "at 12,000 ₪ salary, the employer puts in 900 ₪ and you put in 300 ₪, and after 6 years you have about X ₪." That is the number I actually care about, and it isn't there.
- **A clear answer for someone my age.** If I start at 61, when exactly can I take everything tax-free?
- **What happens if I stop working**, retire or change jobs before 6 years. Do I lose the employer's money?
- **Whether the 2.5% comes out of my net salary**, and how much less I'd take home each month.
- **Which investment track to choose** when I'm close to retirement and can't afford a big loss.
- **What a normal management fee is in practice.** "עד 2%" is the maximum, not what people actually pay.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The comparison with "חשבון ניירות ערך": the bank/fund-manager ranges, the product numbers and company names. It answers a question I didn't ask.
2. The buttons with investment tracks and related products ("כדאי להכיר גם", "במה אפשר להשקיע בתוכו").
3. The "123 מקור..." line and all the codes (TXH-09, KH-22, PF-01...).
4. The "איפה מופיע" column in the glossary.

I would keep "מה זה", the "כסף נכנס ויוצא" cards, the fees table, the orange tax warning and "סיכונים". Those are what actually told me what this means for my money.
