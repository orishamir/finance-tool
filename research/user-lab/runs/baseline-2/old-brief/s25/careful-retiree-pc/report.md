# Me, 61, savings with Harel, going through the Harel page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would not move any money today.** First I would phone Harel, or log into my account, and find out three things:
- which product my money is actually in (pension, קרן השתלמות, or a קופת גמל)
- which track I'm in
- what fee I'm really paying

Then I'd come back to this page and compare.

What I learned: at Harel the fees differ more between products than between tracks inside one product. For a pension, the number that worried me was **"1.47% ההפקדות"** in קרן חדשה מקיפה. That is 1.47% taken off every deposit, next to only **"0.16% החיסכון"** taken off the savings. In the קרן חדשה כללית it is **0.15% / 1.14%**.

In money terms, as I understand it:
- On 300,000 ₪ of savings, 0.16% comes to about 480 ₪ a year.
- If I put in about 2,000 ₪ a month, 1.47% of that is about 350 ₪ a year.

So at my age, with a big balance and not many years of deposits left, the charge on savings matters more. If my pension is in the age track, I'd look at **"גילאי 60 ומעלה"**. In the כללית fund (9856) that track shows **"8.9%" over 3 years and "4.87%" over 5 years**. That's far lower than the share tracks, but I assume it's lower because it's safer. The page didn't tell me that. I'm guessing.

**How sure: about 4 out of 10.** I trust the numbers. The page says they come from **"מנתוני רשות שוק ההון"**, so they're from the regulator. But I still don't know which row is *mine*, and the page couldn't tell me that.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The top paragraph under **"הראל"**, about where the numbers come from and how old they are.
- The row of buttons under **"כל המוצרים של הראל"**, to see what products exist and how many tracks each has.
- The grey header line above each product, e.g. **"קרן השתלמות 16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 37.4 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.41% עד 0.67%"**. This was the most useful single line on the page.
- The big table, mainly the pension sections (**"קרן חדשה מקיפה"**, **"קרן חדשה כללית"**) and **"קרן השתלמות"**.
- The two notes under the table: the one about **"הוצאות ישירות"** and the one starting **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר..."**.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu (מקור, מדריך, חוקים וכללים…).** Skipped. I came for Harel.
- **Title and first paragraph.** Used, and read every word. **"דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025; החשיפה, התשואה והכסף המנוהל נכונים לסוף ספטמבר 2026"** told me the fees are last year's and the rest is recent. Good, though mixing two dates made me stop and reread.
- **"כולל את החברות: הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ · הראל קרנות נאמנות בע"מ".** Skimmed. Fine.
- **"123 מקור 123 מחושב לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא."** Confused me. I didn't understand what "123" means here, or the difference between "מקור" and "מחושב".
- **The product buttons (כל המוצרים 105, קרן השתלמות 16…).** Used them to see the list. Then the next line says **"96 מסלולים ב־7 מוצרים"**, but the button says 105 and I count 10 buttons. Which is it? That made me a bit suspicious.
- **"בתוך כל מוצר, המסלולים מסודרים לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר."** Used it. Good to know cheapest is on top.
- **The main table.** Used it, but only some columns (see 4). It is very long. By the second screen I was tired.
- **Footnotes under the table.** Used them. **"אין: בסוג הקופה הזה מותר לגבות דמי ניהול רק מהחיסכון"** finally explained why I kept seeing "אין". I wish it had been at the top. The codes **"PF-06"** and **"PF-10"** mean nothing to me, so I ignored them. The note **"בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות מהקופה הוצאות ישירות... שלא מופיעות כאן"** worried me: so the fee shown is *not* the full cost?
- **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס."** Used it, and it's important. It means the return I'd actually get is lower than what's shown.
- **"קרנות מחקות" and "קרנות סל".** Skimmed, then skipped. That isn't where my savings are. The coloured tags (**"צוברת"**, **"חשיפה לדולר"**, **"מס גם על שינויי המטבע"**, **"כמה דרכים יחד"**) and the three fees **"קבועים / משתנים / נאמן"** were jargon to me.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל".** Skimmed the headings. It sounded important (**"תנאים שכתובים במסמכים של הראל ולא בחוק"**), but everything was closed and I didn't know what I'd find. I would open **"פנסיה וקופת גמל לקצבה · 2"** next time.
- **"לא מצאתם שם?" search box.** Skipped.
- **Footer.** Read **"נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026"** and **"אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"**. That made me trust the page a bit more.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (the track name). Yes. That's how I find myself, e.g. **"גילאי 60 ומעלה"** or **"כללי"**.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**, both lines: **"מהחיסכון"** and **"מההפקדות"**. These were the most important numbers for me. I noticed the fees inside one product barely change (pension is 0.16% on every track), so choosing a track doesn't change the fee. The product does.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול"**, the "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" figures. I looked at these, but **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** appears on maybe half the rows, so there was nothing to compare. I also noticed the S&P 500 track in pension מקיפה shows **12.13% / 11.03%**, while the same track elsewhere shows **13.67%–13.71%**. Same index, different result, and the page doesn't say why. That bothered me.

**What I glanced at:**
- **"כסף מנוהל"**, as a comfort check. A track with **"13.2 מיליארד ₪"** feels safer than one with **"7 מיליון ₪"**. Otherwise I ignored it.

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"** (the numbers like 15283, 9827). I don't know what they're for, or what the little icon next to them does.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"** and the round coloured charts. I worked out that teal means **"מניות"** and yellow means **"מט"ח"**. But the little rings meant nothing to me, so I just read the two percentages. Once I understood "99% מניות", I knew to stay away at 61.
- The dotted underlines on numbers. I didn't know they were clickable or why.
- The sorting arrows in the headers.
- The small grey tags under names, like **"הלכה יהודית"**, **"עוקב מדדי אג"ח"**, **"50-60"**. I mostly ignored them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"קופת גמל להשקעה"** vs **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"** vs **"מרכזית לפיצויים"**: what's the difference, and which one is mine?
- **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"** vs **"קרן חדשה כללית"**: why do both exist, and which do I have?
- **"מט"ח"** exposure: is it good or bad for me?
- **"עוד לא דווח"** (on one תגמולים row): does it mean zero, or unknown?
- Why the same S&P 500 track has different returns in different products.
- **"123 מקור / 123 מחושב"**, **"PF-06"**, **"PF-10"**.
- In the funds: **"צוברת"**, **"מנוטרלת מט"ח"**, **"מס גם על שינויי המטבע"**, **"נאמן"**.
- Why 105 in the button, but **"96 מסלולים ב־7 מוצרים"** in the text.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which product and track am I in?** Obviously the page can't know. But it could say "check your annual report for this number" and point to the **"קופה"** number. Then I'd know what that column is for.
- **The fee in shekels.** "What does 0.16% plus 1.47% cost me a year on X ₪?"
- **Is Harel cheap or expensive compared with other companies?** The page is only Harel. I saw **"השוואה למטרה"** in the menu but didn't go there.
- **Can I negotiate the fee?** The 1.47% looks like a ceiling, not a rule.
- **Returns after fees.** Everything is before fees and before tax.
- **What's sensible at 61, close to retirement?** Some guidance on risk by age.
- **What the "הוצאות ישירות" add up to.** The page says they exist but not how much.

## 7. If half the page had to go

In this order:
1. **"קרנות מחקות" and "קרנות סל"**, with their colourful tags. These aren't my savings, and they're the most jargon-heavy part.
2. **The round charts.** The percentages next to them are enough.
3. **The "קופה" number column**, unless someone tells me what to do with it.
4. **The "חיסכון לכל ילד" section.** Not relevant to me.
5. **The rows that say "פחות מ־3 שנים" twice.** No return to compare, so they're just length. They could be folded away.

**What must stay:** the top paragraph, the grey summary line for each product (fee range and total money), the two fee numbers, the 3-year and 5-year returns, and the two explanation notes under the table, which I'd move up to the top.
