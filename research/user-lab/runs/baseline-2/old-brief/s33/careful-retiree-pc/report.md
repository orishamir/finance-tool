# s33: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 31 s, $0.13

I looked at both pictures. They aren't the top and bottom of one page. They're two versions of the same box, **"קרנות סל · 4 קרנות"**, listing the same four funds with the same numbers. The second one adds a short Hebrew sentence under each index name. Neither picture is marked as recommended, so I judged them only on what I saw.

## 1. My answer
**I'd pick the second one (screen-02).** I'm about 80% sure.

The difference is the column **"המדד ומה יש בו"** ("the index and what's in it"). In the first version I see **"ת"א-125"** or **"MSCI AC World"** and I have to guess. In the second it tells me plainly, for example **"חמש מאות החברות הגדולות בארצות הברית"** ("the 500 largest companies in the United States"). Now I know what my money would sit in.

I'm not fully sure for two reasons. The fees are just as confusing in both versions. And in the second one the S&P and MSCI rows are taller, because the little grey tags spill onto a second line.

From the page, if I were choosing a fund, I'd lean toward **ת"א-90** or **ת"א-125**. Their fees look much lower: **"0.1% קבוע"** against **"0.8% קבוע"** for the foreign ones. But I wouldn't buy anything until I clicked **"בדף הקרן במאיה"** and saw what I pay in total.

## 2. The parts I used
- The line under the heading: **"רק הקרנות שהאתר אסף עליהן דוח, לא כל הקרנות של החברה"** ("only the funds the site collected a report on, not all of the company's funds").
- The **"שם הקרן"** (fund name) column.
- **"המדד"** / **"המדד ומה יש בו"** (the index it follows).
- **"דמי ניהול לפי הדוח של הקרן"** (management fees according to the fund's report).
- The grey tags under each name, read but only half understood.

## 3. Block by block
- **Heading "קרנות סל 4 קרנות":** Used it. Fine: four funds.
- **The sentence under the heading:** Read it carefully. It's useful because it warns me the list may be incomplete. But it makes me wonder which funds are missing and whether a cheaper one is among them.
- **Column "שם הקרן":** Used it. The names all repeat **"אי.בי.אי. סל"**, so I only read the end. **"(4A)"** and **"(4D)"** mean nothing to me.
- **Grey tags:** Read them and was half confused.
  - **"צוברת"**: I think it means they don't pay out dividends and keep them in the fund. I'd want that confirmed.
  - **"מחזיקה את המניות"** ("holds the shares") sounds reassuring.
  - **"עסקת החלף"**: I don't know what a swap deal is. It sounds like the foreign funds don't really own the shares, and that worries me. Is it riskier?
  - **"חשיפה לדולר"**: OK, the dollar affects me.
  - **"מס גם על שינויי המטבע"** ("tax also on currency changes"): I read it twice. It sounds like I'd pay tax even if I only made money because the dollar moved. That matters for my money, but I don't know how much.
- **Column "מספר נייר"** (security number): Skimmed it. It's for my bank or broker, and I'd copy it from here when buying.
- **Link "בדף הקרן במאיה":** I'd probably click it to check things. I'm not sure what "מאיה" (Maya) is. I think it's the stock exchange site, but the page doesn't say.
- **Column "המדד" / "המדד ומה יש בו":** Used it. In version 1 it's just names. In version 2 the explanation is what won me over. **"תשעים המניות בעלות שווי השוק הגבוה ביותר בבורסה בתל אביב שאינן במדד ת"א-35"** ("the 90 largest stocks in Tel Aviv that aren't in the TA-35") was surprising and useful. I would have assumed 90 meant the top 90.
- **Column "דמי ניהול לפי הדוח של הקרן":** Used it most, and it confused me most. More on that below.

## 4. In the table
- **What I looked at:** fund name, the index explanation, and the three fee lines **"קבוע"** (fixed), **"משתנה"** (variable) and **"נאמן"** (trustee).
- **What I ignored:** the security number, and mostly the tags on the foreign funds once they got long.
- **Colours:** The turquoise index names have a little arrow **"<"**, so I understood I could click them. The fee numbers are underlined, so maybe they can be clicked too, or maybe there's an explanation behind them. I'm not sure.

## 5. What I didn't understand
- **"קבוע", "משתנה", "נאמן":** Do I add the three together? Is **0.35% + 0.1% + 0.008%** what I pay each year on the TA-125 fund? And what makes "משתנה" change? It could go higher.
- **"(4A)" / "(4D)"**.
- **"עסקת החלף"**.
- **"מס גם על שינויי המטבע"**: how much does it actually cost me?
- **"מאיה"**.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
- **One total yearly cost per fund**, ideally in shekels, for example "on ₪100,000 you'd pay about ₪X a year."
- How each fund has done over the last few years.
- How big the fund is, so I know it's not some tiny thing.
- Which of the company's funds are missing from this list, and why.
- A sentence explaining the difference between holding the shares and a swap deal, in terms of my risk.

## 7. If half the page had to go
Remove **"מספר נייר"** first; I can find it when I buy. Next, shorten or drop the tags I don't understand, like **"(4A)/(4D)"** and **"עסקת החלף"**, unless they come with a one-line explanation. Keep the index explanation and the fees at all costs. Those two are what tell me what I'm buying and what it costs me.
