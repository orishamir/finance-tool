# s37: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 50 s, $0.24

I'd pick **option ג (C), "כמו א׳, ועוד תשואה עבר וגודל"**, even though the designers marked א (A) as "מומלץ". It's the only one that shows what each track has actually earned and how big it is.

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I'd look at the S&P 500 group (**"עוקב מדד s&p 500"**). All 12 tracks follow the same index, so the cheapest solid one makes sense. In option C:

- **Cheapest:** **מנורה מבטחים** charges **"0.52% מהחיסכון"** and has **"13.79%"** for 3 years. But it has no 5-year figure and it also charges **"0.01% מההפקדות"**.
- **Cheap with no history:** **אלטשולר שחם** charges **0.54%**, but both return lines say **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**, so it has no track record yet.
- **Large with a long record:** **הראל** (**0.56%**, **"13.70%"** for 3 years, **"11.92%"** for 5 years, **"2.3 מיליארד ₪"**) and **הפניקס** (**0.58%**, **"12.33%"** over 5 years, **"3.4 מיליארד ₪"**).

I'd lean towards הראל or הפניקס: low fee, long record, a big fund.

**How sure: not very, maybe 4 out of 10.** The returns of all these tracks are within about half a percent of each other, so the choice of company hardly matters. What matters is whether, at 61, I should put my savings in **"מניות 99%"**. Nothing on the page helps me with that. Before moving any money I would call the fund or an adviser.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- In option C: the filter row **"במה הכסף מושקע"**, the S&P 500 group header (**"דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.71%"**), the table columns, and the small print at the bottom: **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"**.
- In option A: the **"מניות"** group, to see how different it is from the S&P group.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top title and three bullet points ("משותף לשלוש", "מה שהנתונים מראים", "בשתי הקרנות החדשות"):** I skimmed them. They talk to the designers, not to me. One line was useful: **"20 מסלולים חדשים עוד לא דיווחו דמי ניהול"**.
- **Heading "א. קבוצה לכל סוג השקעה" with the "מומלץ" tag:** I read it. Grouping by what the money is invested in is sensible.
- **Tabs "מקום / מדריכים / חוקים וכללים / שאלות / חברות ומסלולים / השקעה במדד":** I skipped them. "מקום" means nothing to me.
- **"לפי מוצר / לפי חברה" and the product buttons ("קופת גמל להשקעה 143" and the rest):** I glanced at them to confirm I'm in the right product.
- **Investment-type buttons ("הכול 143", "עוקב מדד s&p 500 12", "אשראי ואג"ח 23" and so on):** I used them, but several confused me (see 5).
- **"מיון לפי" sort buttons and "חיפוש בשם המסלול":** I skimmed them. Sorting by fee is enough for me.
- **Table in option A:** I used the S&P and "מניות" groups. The orange line at the bottom (**"ביצוע בלבד, כדי לקצר: אחרי שלוש הקבוצות האלה באות עוד 13"**) told me most groups aren't even shown. That was frustrating, because the bond groups are the ones I'd want at my age.
- **"ב. רשימה אחת מהזול ליקר":** I skimmed it and gave up. It mixes S&P, bonds, "כספי (שקלי)" and "משולב סחיר" in one list sorted by fee. Comparing a bond track's fee with a stock track's fee tells me nothing about my money. The only useful thing was the sentence above it: the fee gap is small and the gap in what you hold is large.
- **"ג. כמו א׳, ועוד תשואה עבר וגודל":** I used it the most.

## 4. Tables: what I looked at and what I ignored

**Looked at:**
- **Track name:** **"שם המסלול, כמו שהחברה כותבת אותו"**.
- **Fees:** **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**, mainly the **"מהחיסכון"** line.
- **Exposure:** **"חשיפה בסוף ספטמבר 2026"**, both the **מניות** line (teal dot) and the **מט"ח** line (yellow dot). In the "מניות" group, מט"ח was only about **"25%"**, against **"99%"** in the S&P group, which caught my eye.
- **Returns (option C only):** **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**, the 3-year and 5-year lines.
- **Fund size (option C only):** **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"**.
- **Tags:** **"חדש"** and **"עוד לא דווח"**. I'd avoid those tracks.

**Ignored:**
- The **"קופה"** column (13874, 14865…). It's just a code to me.
- The ring charts. The numbers next to them say the same thing.
- The little clock icons and the dotted purple underlines. I didn't know what they meant, so I left them alone.
- The **"מההפקדות"** line once I saw it's almost always **"0%"**.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **"מט"ח 97%":** does this mean my money rises and falls with the dollar? Is that good or bad for me?
- **"מניות 101%":** how can more than 100% be in shares?
- **The difference between groups:** "מניות", "מניות סחיר" and "עוקב מדדי מניות" all look the same to me. I also don't know what **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש"**, **"משולב סחיר"** or **"קיימות"** mean.
- **"מההפקדות":** do I pay a fee every time I put money in?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול ולפני מס":** so how much do I actually keep? Is it roughly 13.79% minus 0.52%, minus tax? Which tax?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים":** is a track like that riskier, or just new?

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **Risk:** how far each track fell in a bad year. That's what scares me at 61.
- **Guidance by age:** which kind of track suits someone near retirement.
- **Returns for the other groups:** past returns for bonds and the general (**"כללי"**) track. Option C shows only the S&P group.
- **Practical rules:** whether I can switch tracks later at no cost, and the tax rules when I withdraw.
- **The full list:** all the groups and tracks, which the orange notes cut off.
- **Joining:** how to actually sign up.

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. **Option ב** first. One mixed list, sorted by fee alone, doesn't help me choose.
2. **The opening bullets** that explain the three options.
3. **The "קופה" number column.**
4. **The ring charts.** The percentages already say it.
5. **The repeated tabs and product buttons** above each option.

I'd keep option C, with all the groups filled in.
