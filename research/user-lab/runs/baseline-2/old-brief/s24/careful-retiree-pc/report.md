# Speaking as myself: 61, savings at Harel

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would not move any money today.** First I would find my last yearly statement from Harel and get the **fund number (קופה)** and the name of my track. Then I would come back and look for that exact line on this page.

The page showed me everything Harel offers, but it never told me which line is mine. The fee shown is "**דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025**", an average. That is not necessarily what I pay.

There were three things I would follow up on:
- **Tracks for my age.** In the general pension fund, "**גילאי 60 ומעלה**" (fund 9856) costs 0.15% of savings and 1.14% of deposits. It made **8.9%** a year over 3 years and **4.87%** over 5. In the comprehensive pension fund, the same age line was cut off between two screens, so I never saw its numbers. The provident-fund version, "**גמל מסלול לגילאי 60 ומעלה**" (fund 9745, 13.2 billion), shows 8.08% over 3 years, but its 5-year figure was also cut off.
- **The same S&P 500 track appears in almost every product** with nearly the same result, about **13.7%** over 3 years and **11.9%** over 5. Only the fee changes, from 0.15% to 0.56% of savings. So for my money, the wrapper seems to matter more than the track.
- **"קופה לפנסיה תקציבית"** costs **1.33%**, far more than anything else. If I were in it, I would want to know why.

**How sure am I?** Not very, maybe 4 out of 10. I trust the numbers. What I can't tell is which of them apply to me.

## 4. Columns, colours and labels

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"**, the track name.
- **"דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון / מההפקדות"**, the fee from savings and the fee from deposits. I read both numbers. In pension, "מההפקדות" (1.14% to 1.47%) is much bigger than "מהחיסכון" (0.15% to 0.16%). I don't know which one hurts me more.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**, the average yearly return. I noticed "**לפני דמי ניהול**": it's before fees, so I get less than this.
- The words "**פחות מ־3 שנים**" (less than 3 years). There are a lot of them, so many tracks have no result at all.
- **"כסף מנוהל"**, the money managed. Big means many people trust the track, which comforts me a little.

**What I ignored:**
- The rings. I read the numbers "מניות" (shares) and "מט"ח" (foreign currency) next to them and ignored the drawing and the teal and yellow colours.
- The **"קופה"** number in green with the little arrow icon. I only need it to match my statement.
- The small grey tags "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" and "הלכה יהודית".
- The "?" icons and the little sort arrows.
- The codes "**PF-06**" and "**PF-10**". They mean nothing to me.
- The coloured badges in the index-fund section: "צוברת", "חשיפה לדולר", "מס גם על שינויי המטבע".

## 5. What I did not understand

- **Two fees.** Do I pay both "מהחיסכון" and "מההפקדות", or one or the other?
- **"מניות 99% / מט"ח 99%".** How can a track be 99% shares and also 99% foreign currency? They don't add up the way I expect.
- **"בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות מהקופה הוצאות ישירות... לא כשורה בחשבון".** It says direct expenses are taken from the fund on top of the fees, but don't appear as a line on my statement. So the fee isn't the whole cost. How much more is there?
- **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש", "קיימות", "כספי (שקלי)", "קמ"פ".** I don't know what these track names mean.
- **Index-fund fees "קבועים / משתנים / נאמן".** I don't know what fixed, variable and trustee fees mean.
- **"105" against "96 ... ב־7 מוצרים"**, and the two different dates.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which track I am in, and what I actually pay.**
- **A full-year result in shekels.** For example: "100,000 ₪ here cost X ₪ a year and grew to Y ₪."
- **Returns after fees.**
- **Last year's result**, and whether a track ever lost money. At 61, a drop is what scares me.
- **How Harel compares with other companies** on the same kind of track.
- **What happens if I move**, and whether it costs anything.
- **How risky each track is, in plain words.**
