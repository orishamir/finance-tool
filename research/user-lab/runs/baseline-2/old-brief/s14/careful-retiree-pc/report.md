# What joining a keren hishtalmut would mean for me, at a salary of 30,000

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

**I would join**, and I'm about 75% sure.

The line under the big chart gave me the answer in money: **"קרן השתלמות במסלול מדד. ‏750 ₪ בחודש מכם, ‏2,250 ₪ מהמעסיק."** So I put in 750 a month and my employer puts in 2,250. That's three shekels from them for every one of mine. I can't think of anything else that pays me like that.

I picked year 5 on the chart, which is before the 6 years are up. The bars read:
- **45,000 ₪**: my own money
- **+135,000 ₪**: my employer's money
- **+21,218 ₪**: profit
- **−64,761 ₪**: tax, because I took it out early
- **136,457 ₪**: what I end up with

So even if I take the money out too early and lose almost 65,000 to tax, I still get about three times what I put in. That's what convinced me.

Why I'm not more sure:
- **My age.** I'm 61 and nothing on the page asked how old I am. The text says **"למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים"**, so after retirement age 3 years are enough. But the chart still shows **"מ־6 שנים: פטור עד התקרה"**. Which one applies to me? If I join at 61, do I wait 6 years or 3?
- **The ceiling.** My salary is 30,000, but the tax break only covers salary up to **"15,712 ₪ בחודש"**. The page says employer money above that **"מוסה במס הכנסה כבר באותו חודש, כמו משכורת"**, so it's taxed every month like salary. Roughly half of my employer's 2,250 is above that line. I couldn't tell whether the 136,457 already includes that tax, or whether I'll see less on my payslip every month.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The intro under **"מה זה"**, especially the 6-year rule and the list of what you pay if you take the money out early.
- The calculator under **"מה מייחד קרן השתלמות"**: the 12 small bars, the year-5 marker, the 750 / 2,250 line, and the big bar chart.
- The small-print notes under the chart, especially **"הסכומים בכסף של היום, אחרי מס ועלויות"**. That told me the numbers are in today's money and are already after tax and fees.
- **"כמה זה עולה"**, the fees table.
- **"מיסוי"**, mainly the orange warning box.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** (**"מדריך"**, **"חוקים וכללים"** and so on): skipped. I came to see my own numbers.
- **The line with the coloured "123"s** (**"123 ממקור 123 מחושב 123 הזנתי"**): confused me. I take it that "הזנתי" means a number I typed in, but I didn't know what to do with this line.
- **"מה זה"**: used it and read every word. It's clear. The bullet **"מתחת לגיל 60 זה לפחות 31%"** bothered me, though. I'm over 60, so what rate do I pay? It doesn't say.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: read it. **"המחיר, לעומת קופת גמל להשקעה"** went over my head.
- **The "כדאי להכיר גם" and "במה אפשר להשקיע בתוכו" buttons** (S&P 500, אג"ח and so on): skipped. Too technical.
- **The calculator**: this was the heart of it. The boxes at the top were partly cut off in my screenshots, so I never saw my 30,000 written anywhere. I worked it out from the 750 and 2,250. I left **"תשואה בשנה 7%"** and **"דמי ניהול בשנה 0.54%"** as they were, because I wouldn't know what to change them to.
- **The notes under the chart**: read them. **"בין 19 המסלולים... ‏135,908 ₪... עד 137,668 ₪"** told me that picking a different fund changes the result by less than 2,000. That was reassuring: the choice of company isn't a big deal.
- **"הכי קרוב לזה... חשבון ניירות ערך"**: skimmed it. I don't need it.
- **"שווה לדעת"**: read the three headings. The last one was useful: **"אפשר להעביר קרן השתלמות לחברה אחרת"**, so I can move the fund to another company later. I ignored the codes like **"TXH-09"**.
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"**: skimmed it. The line **"המעסיק לא יכול להתנות את ההפקדה בקרן מסוימת"** is good to know: my employer can't make me use a particular fund.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**: read the cards. **"עד 4 ימי עסקים"** (I get the money within 4 business days) is nice to know.
- **"כמה זה עולה"**: read it. **"עד 2% בשנה"** looks like a lot until you remember the chart used 0.54%.
- **"מיסוי"**: read it, but slowly. The three cards repeat the intro.
- **The orange box**: read it twice. **"לשכירים הסכום מופיע למעלה"** told me the chart already counts the early-withdrawal tax, which is good. It also mentions 47% tax taken at source and a refund through a tax return. That sounds like paperwork at my age.
- **The chief economist line** (**"הכלכלן הראשי... ממליץ לצמצם את ההטבה"**): it made me a little nervous. Then it says the law hasn't changed, so I moved on.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: read it. **"המדינה לא ערבה לשווי הקרן"** is honest: the state doesn't guarantee the value. My money is kept separate from the company's own money, and that's what I wanted to know.
- **"מונחים"** (the glossary): skimmed it. It helped with **"משכורת קובעת"**.
- **Footer**: skipped it. I did notice **"נתונים נכון ל־02.11.2026"**, so the figures are recent.

- **Small bar chart (years 1–12):** I looked at the year numbers along the bottom, the dotted line in the middle, and the two labels: **"לפני 6 שנים: מס"** in pink and **"מ־6 שנים: פטור עד התקרה"**. The bars have no numbers on them, so I only noticed that they grow. I could see the pink striped piece and guessed it was tax. I didn't notice that the striped piece stays on after year 6.

The second green bar has a darker brown lower part and I don't know what that is. The pink bar has two kinds of stripes (light and dark) and I don't know why. - **Glossary table:** I read **"מונח"** and **"מה זה"**. I ignored the **"איפה מופיע"** column with its small tags (**"תלוש שכר"**, **"טופס"**, **"גמל נט"**).
