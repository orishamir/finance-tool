# s03: first-job-phone (old brief)

phone; 41 s, $0.25

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd keep about a month or two of spending in my current account (עו"ש) and move the rest of my savings out. The page points me to a "קרן כספית" (a money market fund) as the closest thing that still lets me get my money quickly. I'd also check whether my bank is one of the few that pay interest on the balance.

I'm about 75% sure that leaving it all there loses me money. The page made that very clear: "אחרי 10 שנים, ₪10,000 בעו"ש שווים ₪8,203 בכסף של היום. ₪1,797 פחות." It hit me harder than I expected.

I'm only about 40% sure about the next step. I don't know what a קרן כספית really is, how to buy one, or if it's safe. The page also says its 3.25% is "הנחה של האתר, לא הבטחה ולא תחזית", so I'm not sure what I'd actually get.
