# Reading about current accounts as a 61-year-old with savings in one

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I wouldn't leave all my savings sitting in the current account. I'd keep enough there for this month's bills and something near them, and phone my bank to ask two things. First, do they pay any interest on my balance at all? Second, what is this "קרן כספית יומית" (daily money market fund), and can they show it next to my deposits, as the page says they must?

I'm **fairly sure the problem is real** and **not sure at all about the fix**. I'd say about 70% sure I should move some of the money, and maybe 30% sure where it should go.

The line that convinced me was in the calculator: "אחרי 10 שנים, 10,000 ₪ בעו״ש שווים 8,203 ₪ בכסף של היום: 1,797 ₪ פחות." In other words, after 10 years my 10,000 would buy only what 8,203 buys today. If I multiply that by what I actually have, that's real money.

What held me back: the page tells me twice, in its own words, that the fund's return is a guess: "זו הנחה של האתר, לא הבטחה ולא תחזית" (the site's assumption, not a promise and not a forecast). It also says "אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות" (Israel has no deposit insurance). Nobody tells me whether the fund is safer or riskier than the bank. I won't move my savings on a guess.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **The opening line under the title "חשבון עובר ושב"**: "כסף שיושב בו לא תמיד מקבל ריבית" (money sitting in it doesn't always earn interest). That's my situation in one sentence.
- **"מה זה"**: the part about the bank having to write to me if more than 15,000 ₪ sits there for a whole quarter. I've never noticed a letter like that and now I want to check.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: "כסף שנשאר בעו״ש רבעון שלם מעבר לסף שבחוק כבר שייך במקום אחר" (money that stays above the legal limit for a full quarter belongs somewhere else). That's blunt, and I liked that.
- **"מה מייחד את חשבון עובר ושב"**: the calculator box "כמה עולה להשאיר כסף בעו״ש?" (how much does it cost to leave money in the current account?) and the 8,203 / 1,797 sentence.
- **The "4 מתוך 8 הבנקים" line**: only 4 of 8 banks pay any interest, only "בתנאים ועל עד 10,000 ₪ בלבד" (with conditions, and only on up to 10,000), at most 3% at Discount. So even the good banks only cover a small amount.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: no deposit insurance, and a government guarantee is "סמכות שבשיקול דעת, לא זכות שלכם" (something the government may decide to do, not something you're entitled to). That frightened me a little.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"**: "משיכה – בכל עת" (you can take it out at any time). Good, nothing is locked.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- Why two different numbers for what I lose: 1,797 or 2,576.
- "בכסף של היום" (in today's money), and the 2% a year the site assumes prices rise. I got the idea in the end, but only on the second reading.
- What a "קרן כספית יומית" actually is. Who holds my money there? Can it go down?
- The codes "BANK-35", "BANK-17", "BANK-15", "TXI OQ11" and "TXI-17".
- "יחידות בקרן נפדות רק ביום מסחר" (units in the fund are only redeemed on a trading day). So I can't get my money on a Friday?
- "ריבית על יתרת זכות" (interest on a credit balance). I worked out that it means interest on money I have in the account, but it took a while.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Does my own bank pay interest, and how much?** The page names Discount and says "לטבלה" (to the table), but I didn't see the table.
- **Is a money market fund safe for someone my age?** Could I lose money in it? It says nothing about the fund's own risks, only the bank's.
- **How do I actually buy one, and what does it cost?** "כמה זה עולה" gave me no numbers.
- **The age exemption.** It's locked and unanswered.
- **The fee changes in 2026–2027.** The text was cut off, and it may change what I pay.
- **What about 3 or 5 years?** I'd have wanted that without having to work the slider myself.
