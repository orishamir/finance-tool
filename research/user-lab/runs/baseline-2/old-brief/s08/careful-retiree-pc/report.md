# What I made of this page, as a 61-year-old reading it for my own money

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

At my age, the money question is: what is safe, what saves me tax, and what happens when I stop working. This page drew me a map. It did not tell me what to do.

What I would look into, in this order:

1. **My pension and the retirement section, "פרישה".** I would check what kind of fund I already have. I would also read "תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים", because it says something that matters at my age: "כדי לקבל ממנה קצבה, מעבירים את הכסף לקרן פנסיה או לחברת ביטוח". In plain English, a provident fund doesn't pay a monthly pension by itself; you move the money first. I didn't know that.
2. **Money I need to keep safe and reachable.** I'd look at "קרן כספית" (a money-market fund), "מק"מ" (short-term government loans) and a bank deposit, "פיקדון". The page says a money-market fund is for "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין" (money that has to stay available). It also says "מק"מ" is "הלוואה קצרה למדינה, עד שנה". Those two I understood.
3. **The tax-benefit savings, "חיסכון עם הטבת מס".** These are "קרן השתלמות" (a tax-free savings fund) and "קופת גמל להשקעה" (an investment savings fund). I would only look at these if I'm still working or have spare money to put away.

**How sure am I?** About the map, fairly sure: I now know the main kinds exist and roughly what each one is. About what to do with my own money, not sure at all. The page gives no numbers, no fees, nothing about risk and nothing about my age. I would click into the cards and the "שאלות שכולם שואלים" page (common questions) before I did anything.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- **The title and opening line:** "מדריך – כל סוג של חיסכון והשקעה שהאתר מכסה". This told me the page is a guide to every kind of saving and investing the site covers. Good, that's what I came for.
- **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון" (two parts to every saving).** This was the most useful bit. There is "איפה הכסף מוחזק" (where the money is kept) and "במה הכסף מושקע" (what the money is invested in). Once I read that, I understood why a "קרן השתלמות" and a "מדד" are listed separately.
- **"מאיפה להתחיל" (where to start).** These are three short lines. Only the last one, "כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן כספית", fit me.
- **"פרישה" (retirement).** I read all three cards.
- **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס" (savings with a tax benefit).** I read both cards.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע", top two rows only.** That's "מזומן שקלי" (cash in shekels), "קרן כספית", "מק"מ", "איגרות חוב ממשלתיות" (government bonds) and "מסלול כללי" (the general track).
- **The footer.** "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" means it's a personal site, not selling anything, with no marketing links. That made me trust it more. "נתונים נכון ל־30.10.2026" told me the information is recent.

## 4. Lists, tables and labels

There were no tables or charts, only boxes.

- **What I looked at:** the bold name at the top of each box, the "בשפה פשוטה:" line (sometimes helpful, sometimes not) and the first line of the description.
- **The light-green links** in "מאיפה להתחיל" ("קרן השתלמות", "קרן חדשה מקיפה", "קרן כספית"). I noticed them because they're coloured.
- **The two little buttons, "יומית" and "מתחדשת",** on the "קרן כספית" box. I looked at them and didn't understand them. Daily what? Renewing what?
- **What I ignored:** the small arrows on each box, the round button at the bottom corner and the grey subtitles under the big headings. The grey text on black was too faint for me.

## 5. What I did not understand

- "יומית" / "מתחדשת" on the money-market fund.
- "קרן חדשה כללית… ההפקדות שמעל הסכום המרבי שמותר להפקיד בכל חודש".
- "מסלול שאינו מתמחה ואינו מתחייב לטווח מניות". Translated: a track that "doesn't specialise and doesn't commit to a range of shares". So how much of my money is in shares, then?
- "עם חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו", which means "with exposure to the dollar or neutralised from it". So what does this mean for my money if the dollar falls?
- "במשקל לפי שווי השוק", "weighted by market value".
- The difference between "MSCI World" and "MSCI AC World", and between ת"א-35 and ת"א-90.
- "אישית לפיצויים", the "personal severance" fund. Is that my severance pay? Can I touch it?

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **Something for someone near retirement.** "מאיפה להתחיל" speaks to salaried workers, not to someone who is 61.
- **Risk.** Which of these can lose money, and how much?
- **Fees.** Nothing at all about what each one costs me.
- **Access.** When can I take the money out, and what does it cost to do so? It says "תקופה שקבע החוק" (a period set by law) but doesn't give the period.
- **Tax at my age.** Is there anything special about withdrawing after 60?
- **What I may already have.** How do I find out which of these I'm already in?
- **The names** of the first three boxes, which I couldn't see.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The seven index cards ("מדד …"). They're too many and too alike, and they're not for me.
2. "לבחירה עצמית".
3. "לילדים".

**Keep:** "שני חלקים בכל חיסכון", "פרישה", "חיסכון עם הטבת מס", the safe-money boxes ("קרן כספית", "מק"מ", "פיקדון") and the "not commercial" line at the bottom.
