# s08: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 54 s, $0.17

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I'd start with the three lines under **"מאיפה להתחיל"** ("where to start"). They were the only part of the page that spoke to someone like me:

- **"שכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם: קרן השתלמות"** ("employees whose employer pays into one: keren hishtalmut"). I'd check my payslip or ask HR whether my employer pays into one for me. The card further down says that after a period set by law I can take the money out **"בפטור ממס"** (tax-free). That sounds like the best deal on the page.
- **"הבסיס של כל חוסך שכיר: קרן חדשה מקיפה"** ("the basis for every salaried saver: new comprehensive pension fund"). I think I already have a pension through work. I'd check whether it's this type.
- **"כסף שצריך להישאר זמין: קרן כספית"** ("money that has to stay available: money market fund"). This would be for my emergency money instead of leaving it in the bank. The current account card says money sitting there **"לא תמיד מקבל ריבית"** (doesn't always earn interest), and that bothered me.

For the S&P 500 I'd click the **"מדד S&P 500"** card. Then I'd try to work out whether to buy it in a **"קופת גמל להשקעה"** (investment savings fund) or a securities account. The page didn't tell me which.

**How sure:** I'm fairly sure about checking the keren hishtalmut and the pension, because the page tells me to start there. I'm not sure about anything beyond that. The page lists everything that exists, but it has no numbers to compare them with: no fees, no tax amounts, no lock-up periods, no past returns.

## 3. Block by block

1. **Top menu** (מקור, מדריך, שאלות שכולם שואלים, חברות ומסלולים, השוואה למטרה, השקעה במדד). I skimmed it. **"שאלות שכולם שואלים"** ("questions everyone asks") and **"השקעה במדד"** ("investing in an index") made me want to click away, because those sound like exactly my questions.
2. **"מדריך" title and subtitle.** I read it. It told me this page is a catalogue.
3. **"שני חלקים בכל חיסכון"** ("two parts of every saving"). I read it twice. It's the idea that "where the money sits" (**"איפה הכסף מוחזק"**) is separate from "what it's invested in" (**"במה הכסף מושקע"**). That was new to me and helpful. Until now I thought "keren hishtalmut" and "S&P 500" were the same kind of thing. But the second line lists **"מק"מ"** and **"אג"ח"** before explaining them, so it lost me a little. I didn't notice the small grey line "every product page has a line with what you can invest in it" until later. It matters, though, because that's how I'd find out where I can buy the S&P 500.
4. **"מאיפה להתחיל"** ("where to start"). I used it. It was the most useful part of the page.
5. **"איפה הכסף מוחזק" → "כסף שוטף וקצר"** ("where the money is held" → "everyday and short-term money"). I skimmed it. These seemed to be the current account, a bank deposit and a savings plan. I know these already, so I scrolled past. (The screenshots cut off most of these card titles, so I'm guessing from the text.)
6. **"חיסכון עם הטבת מס"** ("saving with a tax benefit"). I used it. I read both cards carefully, because "tax benefit" is what I'm after.
7. **"פרישה"** ("retirement"). This part confused me. I read **"קרן חדשה מקיפה"** but couldn't see how it differs from **"קרן חדשה כללית"**. **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"** ("contributions and personal severance") meant nothing to me.
8. **"לילדים"** ("for children"). I skipped it because I don't have kids.
9. **"לבחירה עצמית"** ("for self-selection"). I skimmed it and was half-confused. I got that I can open a securities account **"אצל ברוקר בחו"ל"** (with a broker abroad), or choose the investments myself inside a keren hishtalmut. I couldn't tell which one is for someone like me.
10. **"במה הכסף מושקע"** ("what the money is invested in"). I skimmed it. I looked for S&P 500 and קרן כספית and read the cards around them. I read **"מסלול כללי"** ("general track") because I think my pension might be in one.
11. **The rest of the index cards** (MSCI, נאסד"ק, ת"א-125/35/90). I skimmed the titles and skipped the text. They all read almost the same.
12. **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** ("what the site doesn't cover yet"). I skipped it.
13. **Footer.** I glanced at it. **"לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"** ("not commercial, no affiliate links") made me trust the page more, and **"נתונים נכון ל־30.10.2026"** ("data as of 30.10.2026") told me it's up to date.

There are no tables or charts, only cards.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מק"מ"**, **"אג"ח"**: these were abbreviations I didn't know until I reached their cards.
- **"ההפקדות שמעל הסכום המרבי"** ("deposits above the maximum amount"), in קרן חדשה כללית. What maximum? Does it apply to me?
- **"תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"** ("contributions and personal severance"): I didn't understand the whole card.
- **"חשיפה לדולר או מנוטרלת ממנו"** ("dollar exposure or hedged against it"). Which one is better?
- The S&P 500 card says the money is invested **"בדולרים"** ("in dollars"), but the MSCI World card says **"בשקלים"** ("in shekels"). Why are they different, and does it matter to me?
- **"במשקל לפי שווי השוק"** ("weighted by market value") and **"החזקות הציבור ועם תקרה למניה"** ("public holdings, with a cap per share").
- **"אחרי תקופה שקבע החוק"** ("after a period set by law"). How long is that?
- The chips **"יומית / מתחדשת"** on קרן כספית.
