# s15: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 50 s, $0.33

I'm going in as a 24-year-old who has heard "put it in the S&P 500" and knows nothing else.

## 1. My answer

I'd pick an S&P 500 track. That's the only thing I came in knowing, and the page has a button for it: **"עוקב מדד s&p 500 12"** (S&P 500 tracker, 12 tracks). Scrolling the full list, the S&P rows I could compare were:

- **מנורה מבטחים (Menora), "עוקב מדד S&P500"**: **0.52% מהחיסכון**, the lowest fee of the S&P rows I saw. 13.79% a year over 3 years. **"פחות מ־5 שנים"** (less than 5 years), so no 5-year number. ₪674 million in it.
- **כלל (Clal), "עוקב מדד s&p 500"**: 0.59%. **13.39%** over 3 years and **12.01%** over 5 years. ₪847 million in it, so it's been around and is big.
- The S&P row at the very top of the table: 0.56%, 13.7% / 11.92%, ₪2.3 billion (**"2.3 מיליארד"**). **I couldn't tell which company this is.** The company name row seems to sit under the table header that stays at the top of the screen.
- **מור (Mor)**: 0.71%. **אנליסט (Analyst)**: 0.56%, but less than 5 years. **אלטשולר שחם (Altshuler Shaham)**: 0.54%, but **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**, so no track record at all.

I'd probably go with **Menora** for the lowest fee, or **Clal** because it has a 5-year record. I'm about **5 out of 10** sure. The fees differ by tiny amounts like 0.52% vs 0.59%, and I don't know if that matters. The bottom of the page also says past returns don't tell me what happens next.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The product buttons near the top, with **"קופת גמל להשקעה 143"** already selected.
- The track-type buttons under **"במה הכסף מושקע"** (what the money is invested in), especially **"עוקב מדד s&p 500"**.
- The big table, but only the S&P 500 rows. In those rows I used the fee, the return and the amount of money in the track.
- The grey company rows inside the table, e.g. **"מנורה מבטחים · 12 מסלולים · נכסים 3.8 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.67%"**. They tell me which company the rows below belong to.
- The sentence under the table: **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר… היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"** ("this is past return… it doesn't say what will happen next").

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu (מדריך, חוקים וכללים, השוואה למטרה…)**: skipped. "השוואה למטרה" (compare by goal) sounded interesting, but I stayed on this page.
- **"לפי חברה / לפי מוצר" tabs** (by company / by product): skimmed. I was already on "by product".
- **Title "כל המסלולים בכל החברות"** (all tracks at all companies) and the paragraph under it: read. "Fees are the average charged in 2025" made sense. **"מתנוני רשות שוק ההון"** (from the Capital Market Authority's data) told me it's official data, which is good.
- **"123 מקומם / 123 מחושב / 123 הזנתה — לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"** (a legend: click a number to see where it comes from): confused me. I didn't get what the three "123"s mean, so I ignored it.
- **Orange warning "נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026"** (data not updated since September 2026): read it. I wasn't sure whether it should worry me. It's November, so is the data old?
- **Product buttons** (תגמולים, קרן השתלמות, קרן חדש…): skimmed. I left it on קופת גמל להשקעה. I also clicked **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה"** in my head (what is this product?). I'd have wanted that.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע"** (group by company / by what it's invested in): unsure what it does. I left it on company.
- **Search box "שם המסלול או החברה"** (track or company name): skipped.
- **Track-type buttons**: used, but only "S&P 500". I didn't know what most of the others are: **"אג"ח ממשלתי סחיר"**, **"הלכה איסלאמית"**, **"גמיש"**, **"כספי (שקלי)"**.
- **The big table**: I used the S&P rows and scrolled past the rest. It's very long, around 15 screens.
- **Grey company rows**: useful, but when I scrolled I sometimes lost track of which company I was looking at.
- **Bottom note "עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet) and the past-return sentence: read. They should have been at the top.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 7"** (rules, 7) and the footer: skipped.

## 4. Columns, colours and labels

- **Looked at:**
  - **"המסלול"**, the track name. Most important to me.
  - **"דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון"**, the fee taken from my savings. The top number only.
  - **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"**, average yearly return. The **"3 שנים"** (3 years) and **"5 שנים"** (5 years) numbers.
  - **"נכסים"**, how much money is in the track. It made me trust the big ones more.
- **Glanced at, didn't use:**
  - The ring charts and the **"מניות"** (shares) / **"מט"ח"** (foreign currency) percentages with the teal and yellow dots. A full yellow-and-teal ring seemed to mean "S&P", but I didn't really get it. **"101%"** shares confused me: how is it more than 100%?
  - **"ההפקדה"**, the fee on deposits. Almost always 0%, so I stopped looking.
- **Ignored:**
  - **"קופה"**, the numbers like 15286. Meaningless to me.
  - The little clock icons and the dotted underlines.
  - The **"<"** arrows on the left.
  - The labels **"חדש"** (new), **"הלכה יהודית"** (Jewish law) and **"עוקב מדדי אג"ח"** (bond index tracker).

## 5. What I didn't understand

- The difference between **"מהחיסכון"** (from savings) and **"ההפקדה"** (from deposits).
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"**, exposure to shares and foreign currency. Is high good or bad?
- **"אג"ח"** (bonds), **"גמיש"** (flexible), **"משולב סחיר"** (mixed tradable), **"כספי (שקלי)"** (money market, shekel), **"כללי"** (general).
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years). Does it mean the track is new or the data is missing?
- **"עוד לא דווח"** (not reported yet).
- Why there are two S&P tracks at the same company. Menora seems to have two, and Clal has several similar-looking ones.
- Whether **"לפני דמי ניהול"** (before management fees) means I'd actually get less than the number shown.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- Which company is the **first group** in the table. I never saw its name.
- What 0.52% vs 0.59% means in shekels for someone like me, e.g. on ₪500 a month.
- How to actually open one. Minimum amount? Can I deposit monthly?
- Can I take the money out, when, and what about tax?
- Is the fee negotiable?
- A simple "best S&P track" ranking. The page says it won't sort by return, but even a sort by fee within S&P would have helped.

## 7. If half the page had to go

1. The ring charts and the share / foreign-currency column.
2. The **"קופה"** (fund number) column.
3. The **"ההפקדה"** (deposit fee) line, which is almost always 0%.
4. The "123 מקומם / מחושב / הזנתה" legend.
5. The other product buttons, which aren't what I came for.

I'd keep the track-type buttons, the track name, the fee from savings, the 3- and 5-year returns, the money in the track, and the company name rows.
