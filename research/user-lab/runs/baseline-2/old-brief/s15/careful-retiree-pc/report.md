# What I made of the tracks page, as a 61-year-old about to open a kupat gemel lehashkaa

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would not open anything today. I would make a short list and phone two companies.

For someone my age I would only look at two kinds of track. The first is **"כללי"** (general). The second, more cautious, is **"אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"** (bonds, with at most a quarter in shares). I left out **"מניות"**, **"S&P 500"** and anything that shows **"100%"** next to **"מניות"**. Those tracks show the biggest numbers, like **"26.39%"** and **"25.86%"** over 3 years, but I can't afford a big fall at 61.

My short list from the "כללי" rows:
- **Fund 8211, the first group on the page:** fee **"0.55%"**, 5-year return **"8.26%"**, **"6.7 מיליארד ₪"** in it. It has the lowest fee and the best 5-year number among the big ones. But I could not see which company it belongs to (see point 5).
- **Fund 7978, which seemed to be under "מיטב":** fee **"0.61%"**, 5-year return **"8.02%"**, **"3.9 מיליארד ₪"**.
- **Fund 12538 under "מור":** a similar return, **"8.13%"**, but a fee of **"0.72%"**. I'd drop it for that.

**So what does this mean for my money?** On 200,000 ₪, a fee of 0.55% instead of 0.72% saves me about 340 ₪ a year, every year. That is real money. The 5-year returns of the big "כללי" tracks are all within about half a percent of each other, so the fee is what separates them for me.

**How sure I am:**
- Fairly sure, maybe 70%, that "כללי" or "עד 25% מניות" is the right kind of track for me.
- Only about 40% sure which company. I couldn't name the top choice, and the returns are shown before fees.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- The title **"כל המסלולים בכל החברות"** and the paragraph under it.
- The yellow warning box **"נתוני הרשות לא עודכנו מאז ספטמבר 2026"**.
- The grey company header rows, for example **"מור · 15 מסלולים · נכסים 14.4 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.67% עד 0.73%"**.
- In the big table: the track name, the fee, the 3- and 5-year returns, and the amount of money in the track.
- The note at the very bottom, under the table, about past returns.

## 3. Going down the page, block by block

- **Top menu** (**"מדריך"**, **"חוקים וכללים"**, **"שאלות שכולם שואלים"**…): skipped. I came here to compare tracks.
- **Tabs "לפי מוצר / לפי חברה"**: skimmed. "לפי מוצר" was already chosen, and that was fine.
- **The intro paragraph**: read every word. It told me the fees are the average actually charged in 2025, and the other numbers are correct to the end of September 2026. That's useful, but it also means the fees are almost a year older than the returns.
- **"123 מקור · 123 מחושב · 123 הזנתה · לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"**: confused me. I don't know what "הזנתה" means here, and I didn't want to click on numbers to find out.
- **The yellow warning**: read it, and it worried me a little. Not updated since September? But the bottom of the page says **"נתונים נכון ל־03.11.2026"**. Which one is right?
- **The product buttons** (**"קופת גמל להשקעה 143"** and the others): used them only to check that my product was chosen.
- **"קיבוץ לפי: חברה / במה הכסף מושקע"**: skimmed.
- **Search box**: skipped.
- **The "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons** (**"כללי 12"**, **"אשראי ואג"ח 23"**…): I should have pressed **"כללי 12"**, and it would have saved me a lot of scrolling. I only realised this afterwards. Several labels confused me (see point 5).
- **The line "החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה בנכסים…"**: read it, useful. Now I know the order isn't a ranking.
- **The big table**: used it a lot, but it is very long. I scrolled through about 15 screens.
- **The "איילון קופות גמל" group at the end**: skipped. Every row says **"חדש"** and **"עוד לא דווח"**, and some show **"0 מיליון ₪"**. There's nothing to compare there.
- **The bottom note**: read it, and it was very important to me (see point 4).
- **Footer**: skimmed. **"אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"** made me trust the page a bit more.

## 4. What I looked at in the table, and what I ignored

**Columns I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (the track name).
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**, but only the **"מהחיסכון"** line. The **"מההפקדות"** line is almost always **"0%"**, so I stopped looking at it.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**. I trusted the **"5 שנים"** figure more than the **"3 שנים"** one.
- **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"**. I read a big number like "מיליארד" as a sign that many people trust the track.

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"**, the fund number. I'll only need it when I phone the company.
- The little round charts. I could see green means **"מניות"** and yellow means **"מט"ח"**, but the two rings meant nothing to me. **"101%"** shares made no sense to me at all.
- The small clock icons next to every number.
- The dotted lines under the numbers.
- The arrow **"›"** at the end of each row.

**The phrase that mattered most to me** was **"לפני דמי ניהול"**. The returns don't take off the fee. So I have to subtract it myself: 8.26% minus 0.55% is roughly 7.7% for me.

The bottom note confirmed this: **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר… לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"**. That is honest, and I appreciated it.

**Rows I passed over:** many show **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** and **"פחות מ־5 שנים"**. They are too new to have a history, so I couldn't judge them.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **Which company the first group is.** The group with fund numbers 8211, 8522 and 8521 had no visible name. Its heading disappeared under the column titles that stay at the top of the screen. The same thing happened to a few other groups in the middle of the page, so I couldn't always tell whose rows I was reading.
- **"סחיר"** (as in **"מניות סחיר"**, **"אג"ח סחיר"**), **"גמיש"**, **"קיימי"**, **"משולב"**. Nobody explains these words.
- **"מט"ח"**: I know it means foreign currency. But is **"99%"** of it good or bad for me?
- **"עוד לא דווח"**: the bottom explains it as **"השדה ריק בנתוני הרשות"**. But does a new track charge me nothing, or a lot? I don't know.
- **The difference between "S&P 500" and "עוקב מדד s&p 500"**, and why the same name appears in two forms.

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **What happens to my money if the market drops.** For example, how much did each track lose in its worst year?
- **The return after fees**, already worked out for me.
- **Tax:** what changes for me at 60 and over if I take the money out as a monthly pension (קצבה) or as a lump sum.
- **Whether I can switch tracks later**, and whether that costs anything.
- **Whether the fee can be negotiated.** The 2025 average suggests some people pay less than others.
- **How to actually open one**, and whether there is a minimum amount.
- **A short line under each investment type** saying in simple words how risky it is.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The tracks with no history at all: the whole **"איילון קופות גמל"** block and every **"חדש"** row.
2. The round charts and the **"מנייות / מט"ח"** percentages next to them.
3. The **"123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה"** line.
4. The **"מההפקדות"** fee line, since it's almost always 0%.
5. The **"3 שנים"** return, if only one return can stay. I'd keep the 5-year one.

I would keep the intro paragraph, the company summary rows, the fee, the 5-year return, the money in each track, and the bottom note about past returns.
