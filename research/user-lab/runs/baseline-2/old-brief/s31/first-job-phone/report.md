# s31: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 41 s, $0.18

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd probably open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot). It's #1 in the list "כל החשבונות" (all accounts) at **‏1,058 ₪**, with a green dot already on it and a little "★ הזול" ("the cheapest") tag. The next one, אינטראקטיב ישראל, is 1,303 ₪, and after that the numbers climb to over 26,000 ₪, so the gap felt big enough to trust.

But I would **not** actually buy anything yet. The page told me straight away that it doesn't know any funds for my index: "האתר לא אסף קרנות על test" (the site didn't collect funds on test). The left box says "אין עדיין קרנות ברשימה" (no funds in the list yet). So I know where to open the account, but not what to buy inside it. The page also says the fund's own fee (דמי ניהול) isn't counted until I add the fund myself.

How sure: about 60% on the account, about 0% on what to buy. I'd have to go to "מאיה" (Maya), which I've never heard of, find a fund, come back and type it in.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- The title "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן" (where and how is cheapest to invest in an index over time). It told me I was on the right page.
- The row of index buttons, where I saw my **test** button was selected.
- The four sliders: 10,000 ₪ today, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% return. I left them as they were.
- The box "כמה נשאר בסוף תלוי בקרן" (how much is left at the end depends on the fund).
- The left box "כל הקרנות על test" with "לחיפוש במאיה" (search in Maya) and "בקשו שנאסוף" (ask us to collect it).
- The ranked list "כל החשבונות", mainly the top five rows.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** (מקור, מדריך, חברות ומסלולים, השקעה במדד, חוקים וכללים): skimmed it. I was already on the right tab.
- **Title and the line under it**: used it. "בוחרים חשבון ניירות ערך וקרן" told me there are two things to choose, an account and a fund.
- **"המדד" and the index buttons**: used them. I saw test highlighted, with a pencil and a bin next to it. I ignored "הבחירה שלכם; האתר לא ממליץ על מדד" (your choice; the site doesn't recommend an index).
- **"test" heading with the small "זר" tag**: confused me. I don't know what "זר" (foreign?) means for my own index. "אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ" (you can also hold it through a foreign fund) didn't help either, because I don't know what a קרן חוץ is or whether I want one. I also skipped "שינוי התשובה" (change the answer) because I didn't know which answer it meant.
- **Sliders**: used them, but only to read them. I didn't change anything because I don't know if 7% is a sensible number. I skipped the "אינפלציה" button and the euro/dollar line.
- **"כמה נשאר בסוף תלוי בקרן" box**: used it. It was the most honest part of the page, and the moment I realised I'd get a half answer. I didn't press "+ הוספת קרן שלא ברשימה" (add a fund not in the list) because I don't have a fund to add.
- **Two cards, "חשבון ניירות ערך" and "קרן"**: I only saw their tops. The "קרן" card said something like "עוד לא נבחרה קרן" (no fund chosen yet).
- **"כל הקרנות על test" (left)**: used it. "לחיפוש במאיה" means nothing to me, but at least it's a next step.
- **"כל החשבונות" ranked list**: used the top. I read the first sentence "מסודרים לפי מה שכל חשבון גובה" (sorted by what each account charges) and skimmed the rest of that paragraph. "קנייה חודשית מחושבת כהוראת קבע" (monthly purchase calculated as a standing order) and "סכום חד־פעמי" were too dense to follow.
- **The grey "לא בחישוב" rows** under many banks: skimmed them. "מחיר אחר לצעירים... לסטודנטים" (different price for young people, students) got my attention because I'm 24. Does that count as young? Some banks might be cheaper for me than shown, and I couldn't tell which.
- **Rows 6 to 17**: skimmed them. Once the numbers went past 5,000 ₪ I stopped caring.
- **"+ הוספת חשבון בתנאים שקיבלתם"** (add an account with terms you got): skipped it. I haven't been offered anything.
- **Colour legend under the list**: I used it a little (more in section 4).
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, מול התעריפון"** (what the ad says vs. the price list): I read the first lines. It was interesting that Psagot's ad says "פטור מלא מדמי ניהול" (fully exempt from management fees) and the real price list says something else. It made me a bit less sure about Psagot. I skipped the list of "טענות" (claims) per company.
- **Small print at the bottom**: skimmed it. "התשואה שבחישוב: תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" (the return is an example, not a forecast) I understood. "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־4 מקומות" (at 0% or 10% the order changes in 4 places) worried me slightly, but Psagot was so far ahead that I let it go.
- **Footer**: skipped it.

## 4. Lists and colours

- **What I looked at**: the bank name, the big number on the left (e.g. "1,058 ₪") and the rank number. I read "מה החשבון גובה" (what the account charges) as total fees over my 10 years, but I'm guessing.
- **What I partly looked at**: the small grey line under each name, like "קרן מחקה" (tracking fund?) and "מחיר להוראת קבע" (standing-order price). They meant nothing to me.
- **The coloured bars**: I only noticed that some are long and some are short. From the legend:
  - orange is "עמלות קנייה ומכירה" (buying and selling fees)
  - yellow is "דמי משמרת" (I don't know this word)
  - red is "דמי ניהול חשבון" (account management fee)
  - pink is "המרת מטבע" (currency conversion)
  - grey is "העמלות זו על זו" (fees on fees?)
  - "//" means "הפס נחתך" (the bar is cut)

  I noticed that the expensive ones at the bottom have a lot of red. I didn't compare colours row by row.
- **What I ignored**: the round radio buttons on the right, the small arrows that open the "לא בחישוב" notes, and the dotted underline on the numbers. I didn't realise I could tap them.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Which actual fund to buy for my index.** That was the main thing I came for, and the page has none.
- **The final amount I'd end up with.** It says that depends on the fund, so I didn't get a final shekel number.
- **Whether I count as "צעיר" (young).** If I do, would Mizrahi or Leumi become cheaper than Psagot?
- **Is Psagot's "★ הזול" still true** after reading that its ad and its price list don't match?
- **How to actually open the account**, or what step to take next.
