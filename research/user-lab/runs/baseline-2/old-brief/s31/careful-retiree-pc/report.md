# Looking for the cheapest way to invest in my own index, "test"

## 1. My answer: what I'd do, and how sure I am

**I wouldn't put money in yet.** The page gave me half the answer.

- **The half it gave me: which account is cheapest.** It ranks the banks and investment houses by what the account charges. Top of the list is **פסגות, ₪1,058**, marked **"קרן מחקה ★ הזול"** (index fund, "the cheapest"). Second is **אינטראקטיב ישראל, ₪1,303**, marked "בחו"ל" (abroad). Then **Interactive Brokers at ₪2,759**. At the bottom, **אקסלנס** and **מיטב טרייד** charge about **₪26,800**. That's 25 times more. That gap is what got my attention.
- **The missing half: which fund to buy.** The page told me plainly: **"האתר לא אסף קרנות על test"** (the site has no funds for "test"). It says **"עוד לא נבחרה קרן"** (no fund chosen yet) and **"אין עדיין קרנות ברשימה"** (no funds in the list yet). So I don't know what the fund itself charges each year, and that could matter as much as the account fee.

**My next step:** find a fund that tracks my index. The page says **"את דמי הניהול של קרן אפשר למצוא במאיה"**, so I'd look its fee up on Maya. Then I'd add it with **"+ הוספת קרן שלא ברשימה"** and see whether פסגות still comes out cheapest.

**How sure am I?** Fairly sure פסגות or אינטראקטיב ישראל will be among the cheapest. Not sure enough to sign anything. Three things hold me back:
- The page says the order changes in 4 places if the return is 0% or 10% instead of my 7%.
- פסגות, the "cheapest", has **3 claims** where the advert doesn't match the official price list.
- I don't know the fund's fee.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **Is ₪1,058 for the whole 10 years or per year?** The column says "מה החשבון גובה" but doesn't say over what period. For my money, that's the first thing I need to know.
- **What does "זר" next to "test" mean?** I guess "foreign", but did the site decide that, or did I? I only gave it a name. How does it know what my index is?
- **"העמלות זו על זו"** (fees on fees): no idea.
- **"קרנות בחו"ל שהתעריפון שלהן נקבע לפי מספר המניות מחכות למחיר היחידה של הקרן, שעוד לא נאסף"**: foreign funds priced by the number of shares are waiting for a unit price that hasn't been collected yet. My index is "זר", so does this make the Interactive figures wrong for me?
- **Is inflation counted or not?**
- **"קרן מחקה" versus "בחו"ל":** they're two different ways of holding the index. Which one is better for me (tax, risk)? It isn't explained.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **The bottom line:** how much money I'd **have** after 10 years, not just what I'd pay. The page promises this once a fund is added.
- **Which actual funds** track my index, and their annual fees. The page sends me to Maya, so I'd have to do this myself.
- **The full text of פסגות's 3 claims** right next to its row, without scrolling away. If the cheapest one is advertising something untrue, I want that up front.
- **Which 4 places change** at 0% or 10%. It says **"פירוט"** (details), but I wasn't confident to click it.
- **Whether the cheap ones are safe and easy to use:** opening the account, closing it, getting my money out.
