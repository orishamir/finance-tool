# Psagot Trade: what it would charge me and what I can buy (as a 24-year-old first-timer)

## 1. My answer

I'd probably open the account and buy an Israeli **"קרן סל"** (I think that's a fund you buy like a share). Before putting money in, I'd confirm one that tracks the S&P 500 exists there. I'm about **60% sure**.

What I think it would cost me:
- **Buying or selling:** "0.07%" with "מינימום לפעולה ₪2.00". If I buy ₪1,000, 0.07% is only 70 agorot, so I'd pay the ₪2 minimum. Small purchases cost me relatively more.
- **Holding the shares:** "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך 0.03% לרבעון". If I understand it, ₪10,000 sitting there costs about ₪3 a quarter, roughly ₪12 a year. That's fine.
- **Monthly account fee:** "₪0.00 לחודש". Good.
- **To open:** "מינימום לפתיחת חשבון: ₪10,000". I didn't expect this. I'd have to save that much first.

I can't buy US shares directly. Under "ניירות ערך זרים" the label says "לא ידוע", and the text says even Psagot's own document doesn't say which European exchanges you can buy on. So if "investing in the S&P 500" means buying something American, I don't know if I can do it here. That's why I'm only 60% sure.

## 2. What I actually used
- **"העמלות"** (the fees): the buying and selling fee, the holding fee, the monthly fee and the currency conversion.
- **"מה אפשר לקנות כאן"** (what you can buy here): the green "אפשר" and yellow "לא ידוע" labels.
- **"מחירים לקבוצות ומבצעים"** (group prices and deals): only for the ₪10,000 minimum.
- **"למי המחירים כאן: לקוח רגיל, בלי הטבה לקבוצה"** (prices are for a regular customer, no group discount). Good, that's me.
