# Psagot Trade: what I worked out from the page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would **not open the account yet**. First I would phone Psagot and get the fees in writing. I'm about 70% sure I understand the basic costs. I'm much less sure what I'd really pay, because the page tells me twice that **"המסמך אומר אחרת"** (the document says something else). Their advertising and their own price list disagree.

What I took away for my money:
- **To buy or sell:** **"0.07%"** with **"מינימום לפעולה 2.00 ₪"**. A 10,000 ₪ purchase costs me 7 ₪. A small 1,000 ₪ purchase still costs 2 ₪. That seems fine.
- **To hold my shares (custody):** **"0.03% לרבעון"**. That's per quarter, which I make 0.12% a year. On 200,000 ₪ that's about 240 ₪ a year, every year, even if I never trade.
- **Monthly account fee:** **"0.00 ₪ לחודש"**. Good.
- **To open the account:** **"מינימום לפתיחת חשבון: 10,000.00 ₪"**. I have that.
- **Currency exchange:** **"1.5 אגורות לדולר"**, then another line saying **"0.5%"** and **"מרווח ההמרה: המסמכים לא קובעים לו תקרה"**. That last part says the documents set no cap on the exchange spread. It worries me.

What I can buy: **"אפשר"** (allowed) next to **"יחידות של קרן נאמנות"** (mutual funds) and **"קרן סל"** (ETFs). For **"ניירות ערך זרים"** (foreign securities) it says **"לא ידוע"** (unknown).

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The title and the line under it: **"בית השקעות, חבר בורסה שאינו בנק"** (an investment house and stock exchange member that isn't a bank).
- **"התעריפון"** (the price list), especially **"העמלות"** (the fees), line by line.
- **"מה אפשר לקנות כאן"** (what you can buy here).
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, ומה במסמכים"** (what the ads say versus what the documents say). This was the most useful part.
- **"מחירים לקבוצות ומבצעים"**, for the minimum deposit and the regulator line **"מפוקח: רשות ניירות ערך"** (supervised by the Israel Securities Authority).

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu (מקור, מדריך, חברות ומסלולים…):** Skipped it.
- **"לא מצאתם שם?" search box on the left:** Skipped it. I already had the right company.
- **"123 ממקור · לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא":** Confused me. Is 123 a number of sources or a page number? I left it alone.
- **"ללא תאריך פרסום, נאסף ב־04.11.2026":** Read it, and it bothered me. The price list has *no publication date*, so how do I know it's current?
- **"למי המחירים כאן: לקוח רגיל, בלי הטבה לקבוצה":** Used it. These are prices for an ordinary customer like me, which is good.
- **"11 שורות עמלה נאספו":** Confused me. It says 11 fee lines were collected, but I counted only five or so on the page. Where are the rest?
- **Buy/sell fee, custody fee, account fee:** Used all three carefully.
- **Currency exchange (two separate blocks):** Read both and was confused. One says 1.5 agorot per dollar and the other says 0.5%. Which one do I pay? Maybe both?
- **"מה אפשר לקנות כאן":** Used it.
- **Long paragraph under "ניירות ערך זרים":** Read it twice. As I understand it, Psagot's papers don't say which European exchanges they offer, and the fee on Asian and European shares *"עשויה להשתנות"* (may change). The site could only work out a range of **"בין 0.48% ל־1.5%"**. That's a big range.
- **"מה כתוב בפרסום, ומה במסמכים":** Used it, and this is where my trust dropped:
  - Their ad says currency exchange is **"ללא עמלות המרה"** (no conversion fees). The binding document says **"1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל"**.
  - Their ad says mutual funds are **"פטור מעמלת ק/מ"** (exempt from the buy/sell fee). The binding document says **"0.07% משווי העיסקה, מינימום 2 ₪"**.
  - The **"דמי משמרת"** (custody fee) row also says **"המסמך אומר אחרת"**, but it was cut off between my screenshots. I never saw what the disagreement is about, and custody is the fee I care about most.
- **Dates "נראה ב־04.11.2026" and "מ־09.08.2026":** Noticed them. The ad was seen recently, and the document is from August.
- **"מחירים לקבוצות ומבצעים":** Used the minimum deposit and regulator lines. I skipped **"מה מגן על הכסף"** (what protects the money) because it's closed and I didn't open it. I'm sorry now, because that's important to me.
- **"הודעה על שינוי בעמלות · חוקים וכללים 1":** Didn't understand it. Did they announce a fee change? Nothing says what changed.
- **Two buttons, the turquoise "להשוואה מול שאר הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות" and "חשבון בתנאים שלכם…":** Noticed them and would click the comparison one next. The second one I don't understand.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של פסגות" with "חשבון ניירות ערך · 2":** Skimmed it. It's closed and I didn't open it.
- **Footer:** Skipped it, except **"בלי קישורי שיווק"** (no marketing links), which reassured me a little.

## 4. Lists, labels and colours

- **I looked at:** the green **"אפשר"** (allowed), the orange **"לא ידוע"** (unknown) and the orange **"המסמך אומר אחרת"** (the document says otherwise). The colours worked: green felt safe, orange made me stop. I also read the underlined numbers (0.07%, 2.00 ₪, 0.03%, 0.00 ₪, 10,000 ₪), the small words after them (**"לרבעון"**, **"לחודש"**) and the two columns **"בפרסום"** (in the ad) and **"במסמך המחייב"** (in the binding document).
- **I ignored:** all the little turquoise **"בתעריפון"** and **"תעריפון עמלות פסגות טרייד"** links, the section numbers like **"חלק 5 – מטבע חוץ, סעיף 1"**, and the **"חבר בורסה"** tag.
- **The grey lists after each fee:** I only half read these, for example **"מניות ואיגרות חוב, קרן סל"** next to the buy/sell fee. They tell me which products the fee applies to, but they're small and easy to miss.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- Whether I pay **1.5 agorot per dollar, 0.5%, or both** when I change shekels to dollars.
- **"מרווח ההמרה"** (the exchange spread). I think it's a hidden cost inside the exchange rate. If there's no cap, how much could it be?
- **"123 ממקור"**.
- **"11 שורות עמלה"** when I could see only about five.
- What the custody disagreement actually says. It was cut off.
- **"חשבון בתנאים שלכם"** (an account on your terms).
- Whether **"0.03% לרבעון"** is charged on my whole portfolio, and whether there's a minimum per quarter.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Ordinary Israeli shares and government bonds** aren't listed under "what you can buy". Their fee lines mention **"מניות ואיגרות חוב"** (shares and bonds), so I assume yes, but I want it said plainly.
- **American shares.** That's the main reason I'd want foreign securities, and the answer is "unknown".
- **Any minimum on the custody fee**, and **fees for closing the account or moving my shares** to another firm.
- **Dividend handling or tax-related fees.**
- **Which price wins** when the ad and the document disagree. I assume the document, but I'd like that stated.
- **A simple yearly total**, something like "with 100,000 ₪ and 4 trades a year you'd pay about X ₪".

## 7. If half the page had to go

First to go: the search box on the left, the "123 ממקור" line, all the small "בתעריפון" links and section numbers, and the closed "חשבון ניירות ערך · 2" block at the bottom. After that, the long paragraph about European shares could shrink to one line: "Foreign shares: fee unclear, between 0.48% and 1.5%."

**Never cut:** the five fee numbers, "מה אפשר לקנות כאן", and the "ad versus document" comparison. That comparison is what stopped me from signing up today.
