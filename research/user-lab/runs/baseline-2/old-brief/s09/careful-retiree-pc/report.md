# What I made of the fixed-deposit page

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would not lock all of my idle money away yet. If I did anything, it would be a short deposit of one year or less, and only with money I'm certain I won't need. First I would call or visit my own bank to get their real rate and their exact penalty for taking the money out early.

I'm not very sure about this, maybe 4 out of 10. The page taught me how a deposit works, but it also gave me two worrying facts and didn't answer them.

**The first worrying fact is the calculator.** It assumes 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 1.8% interest and 2% inflation, over 10 years. I'd have paid in 70,000 ₪ (**"ההפקדות שלכם 70,000"**). At the end I'd have **"נשאר לכם 68,270"** "in today's money", after **"מס 986.20"**. So by my reading, I'd put in 70,000 and get back less than that in buying power, and I'd still pay tax. When I worked that out, my reaction was: then why bother? The page never says this in plain words. I had to work it out myself.

**The second worrying fact is about safety.** The page says **"אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות: אם בנק קורס, אין חוק שמחזיר לכם את הכסף"**. That means there's no deposit insurance, and no law that gives my money back if the bank collapses. I had assumed money in a bank was protected. It's the sentence that bothered me most, and the page doesn't say how likely that is or what I should do about it.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **"מה זה"** at the top. This is the only place that explained the basics: fixed or variable interest, money returned or renewed at the end, and indexed versus non-indexed.
- **The calculator, "מה קורה לכסף לאורך הזמן"**: the box with the big number, the inputs beside it, and the three-line summary under the chart.
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"**: it told me a deposit is for money when you know the date you'll need it.
- **"מיסוי"**, the tax section: the three cards and the red warning box.
- The opening of **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**, about risks.
- A few rows of the **"מונחים"** glossary, to look up words I didn't know.

"הקרן מוגנת כשהמדד יורד הוא תנאי של המוצר... לא משהו שהחוק קובע" was useful too. It means protection of my original sum when prices fall is only a condition of the product, not the law. **"מיסוי" (tax)**

The low-income exemption, "עד 69,840 ₪ ... עד 10,920 ₪ בשנה מהריבית", could matter to me after I retire, so I noted it.

**The red box: "שבירת פיקדון לפני הזמן: האתר לא מחשב אותה"**
Used it. It says the site doesn't calculate what happens if I break the deposit early, and calls the tax question "שאלה פתוחה", an open question. I appreciate the honesty. But breaking early is exactly the thing I'm afraid of.

**"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן" (risks and what isn't protected)**

**"מונחים" (glossary)**

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Do I actually come out ahead?** The page should simply say it: at 1.8%, with prices rising 2%, you lose buying power.
- **What the indexed deposit pays.** If the non-indexed one loses to inflation, what does the price-linked one give? I saw no rate for it.
- **The real penalty for breaking early.** Not just "לפי תנאי הבנק" (according to the bank's terms), but an example in shekels.
- **The real cost.** The page gives no figure for the exit fee.
- **How risky my bank is,** given there's no insurance, and whether I should split my money between banks.
- **A lump-sum example.** I have money sitting idle. I'm not adding 500 a month, and I'd have liked an example that matches that.
- **Today's rates at a few banks, side by side.**
- **How a deposit compares** with just leaving the money where it is, or with the money-market fund the page mentions.
- **The example product's terms (Leumi, 12 months).** They were cut off, so I never saw them.

## 7. If half the page had to go

I'd remove these first:

1. The glossary table. It's long, it seemed to repeat, and the "איפה מופיע" column is useless to me.
2. All the code tags.
3. The key line at the top.
4. "שווה לדעת", which is just titles with nothing behind them.
5. "מה האתר לקח בחשבון".
6. "כסף נכנס ויוצא", which says one obvious thing.
7. The repeated "הצגת החוקים והכללים" links.

I'd keep:

- "מה זה"
- the calculator, especially that three-line summary
- the tax cards
- the red warning about breaking early
- the risks section

The most important line on the whole page for me is **"אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות"**, that there's no deposit insurance in Israel.
