# My notes on the "כל המסלולים בכל החברות" page, as a 61-year-old thinking about a kupat gemel lehashkaa

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I wouldn't open anything yet. If I had to choose today, I'd pick a **"כללי"** (general) track at one of the big companies. These tracks hold about half shares and half bonds (around "מניות 49%–53%"). At my age I don't want 100% shares. I also don't want "כספי (שקלי)", which made only about "3 שנים 4.33%". The middle way looks right to me.

These are the כללי tracks I wrote down. Every one of them returned roughly 12–13.6% a year over 3 years and 7.6–8.3% a year over 5 years:

| Company | Fee from savings | 3 years | 5 years | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Infinity | 0.56% | 12.65% | 8.06% | small (174 million) |
| Menora | 0.59% | 12.9% | 7.78% | 1.6 billion |
| Meitav | 0.61% | 13.22% | 8.02% | 3.9 billion |
| Analyst | 0.62% | 12.72% | 8.13% | 4.3 billion |
| Altshuler Shaham | 0.63% | 12.02% | 6.43% | 3.9 billion |
| Clal | 0.65% | 13.62% | 7.96% | 2.1 billion |
| Mor | 0.72% | 12.82% | 8.13% | 6 billion |

The first כללי on the page had the best mix: "0.55%", "13.5%", "8.26%", "6.7 מיליארד". **But I could not see which company it belongs to.** The company name for that first group never showed in my screenshots. I'm not putting money somewhere without a name on it.

How sure am I? About 50%. I'm fairly sure "כללי" is the right kind of track for me. I'm not sure about the company, because the results are so close that the page doesn't really pick one for me. On fees, 0.55% versus 0.72% on 100,000 ₪ is about 170 ₪ a year. That matters, but it isn't huge. I'd still phone two companies before deciding.

- **The chips under "במה הכסף מושקע"**. I looked for "כללי 12".
- **The sentence above the table**: "החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה בנכסים... בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר". It told me how to read the list.

- **The note at the bottom**: "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך". I read it twice.

## 4. What I looked at and ignored in the table

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"**: yes, but only to find "כללי" and to avoid "מניות".
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025"**: yes, only the number next to "מהחיסכון". The "מההפקדה" line is almost always "0%", so I stopped reading it.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"**: yes, both the "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" lines. Many rows just say "פחות מ־3 שנים", and I mentally crossed those off. I'm not giving my money to a track with no history.
- **"נכסים בסוף ספטמבר 2026"**: I glanced at it. I preferred billions over a few million. "0 מיליון ₪" scared me off.
- **The grey company header lines**: I read all of them.

**What I half-looked at:**
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"**: only the "מניות" percentage. I ignored "מט"ח".
- **The teal and yellow rings**: I ignored them. I couldn't tell what the circle meant beyond the two numbers next to it.

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"**: the numbers like 15286 and 8211. They mean nothing to me.
- **The little "<" arrows** on the left of each row.
- **The sort arrows** in the column headers.
- **The tags "חדש", "הלכה יהודית", "אג"ח סחיר"**.

## 5. What I did not understand

- The first group of tracks has no company name. Whose are they?
- Why "מניות 101%"? How can you have more than 100%?
- What "מט"ח" exposure means for my money. Is it a risk or a good thing?
- "דמי ניהול... מההפקדה" versus "מהחיסכון": which one do I actually pay?
- "עוקב מדדים - גמיש", "עוקב מדד s&p 500", "משולב סחיר", "קיימות". What are these in plain words?
- The dotted underlines under almost every number, and the "123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה" badges.
- "עוד לא דווח": not reported by whom, and why?
- Why the page won't sort by return ("ולכן אין מיון לפיה") but will sort by fees.

## 6. What I wanted to know and could not find

- **What I actually get after fees and after tax.** The returns are "לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס". So what is my real number?
- **The tax rules** when I withdraw, and whether there's something special after 60 (I've heard about an annuity option).
- **How bad a bad year was.** I'd like the worst single year for each track, not just averages.
- **Last year's return.** At my age I care about recent behaviour too.
- **A fee in shekels**, for example "on 100,000 ₪ you pay X a year".
- **How to open one and how to take money out**, including how long withdrawal takes and whether I can move between companies.
- **Whether I can negotiate the fee.** The page shows an average. Will they give me that, or more?

1. The whole **איילון** group and every other "חדש" / "עוד לא דווח" row. They're useless for comparing.
2. The **"קופה"** number column.
3. The **coloured rings**. The two percentages are enough.
4. The **"123 מקור / מחושב / הזנתה"** line.
5. The **chips for other products** (קרן השתלמות, תגמולים, חיסכון לכל ילד…). I'm already on the one I want.
6. The **"מההפקדה"** fee line, since it's nearly always 0%.

What must stay: the company name lines with their fee ranges, the "כללי"-type filter, the fee and return columns, and that bottom warning about past returns.
