# Looking for the cheapest stock-index kupat gemel lehashkaa: what I found

## 1. My answer, and how sure I am

I would look first at **Altshuler Shaham, track no. 14864, "עוקב מדדי מניות"**. Its fee from savings is **0.56%** and its fee from deposits is **0%**. That's the lowest number I could see.

In money terms: on 100,000 ₪, 0.56% is about 560 ₪ a year. Mor's 0.67% would be about 670 ₪, so the difference is roughly 110 ₪ a year.

**I'm only about 60% sure**, for three reasons:
- **I counted 9 tracks but the page says 11.** It says "11 מסלולים של 11 חברות". I saw Mor, Analyst, Meitav, Altshuler, Phoenix, Migdal, Menora, Infinity and Ayalon. Two are missing, and one of them might be cheaper.
- **Menora is almost the same price.** It charges 0.57% from savings plus 0.02% from deposits. A 0.01% difference doesn't feel like enough to decide on.
- **I didn't check the "עוקב מדד s&p 500 12" button.** That's also a stock index, and I don't know if those tracks are cheaper.

Before I sign anything, I'd phone Altshuler and ask what fee *I* would pay. The page says the number is "הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025", meaning the average people actually paid in 2025. That isn't a promise to me.

## 4. What I looked at in the table

**Looked at:**
- **Company name** in bold: מור, אלטשולר שחם, and so on.
- **The grey line under each company.** It shows "כסף מנוהל" (money managed). 10.6 billion ₪ told me Altshuler is big, which comforts me. It also shows "דמי ניהול מההיסכון: מ־0.56% עד 0.56%", which means "from 0.56% to 0.56%". "From X to X" read oddly, but I understood it.
- **"מההיסכון" and "מההפקדות"** (from savings / from deposits). I compared these two numbers across every company. That's how I spotted Menora's extra 0.02%.
- **Track number** (14864) in green. I noted it so I can give it on the phone.
- **"עוד לא דווח" (not reported yet) for Ayalon.** I ruled it out because I won't pick something without knowing its fee. The "חדש" (new) label put me off as well.

**Ignored or barely glanced at:**
- **The yellow and turquoise rings and the "מניות 99% / מט"ח 99%" figures.** I only noticed they're all high.
- **The return column.** For most companies it says "פחות מ־3 שנים" (less than 3 years), so there's nothing to compare. Meitav shows 25.86% over 3 years, but the column says "לפני דמי ניהול" (before fees), so it isn't what I'd actually get.
- **"כסף מנוהל" (money managed) per track,** for example "504 מיליון ₪". It doesn't matter for my question.
- **The small arrows ‹ on the left and the dotted underlines.**
