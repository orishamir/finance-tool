# Keren hishtalmut: what I'd do after reading this page

## 1. My answer
**I'd join, and I'd ask for the fund's "S&P 500" option.** I'm about 85% sure joining is right. I'm much less sure about which company or option to pick.

The page convinced me with three things:
- **The employer adds money.** "הטבת המס חלה על הפקדות של עד 7.5% מהשכר מהמעסיק ועד 2.5% מכם" (the tax benefit applies to up to 7.5% of salary from the employer and up to 2.5% from you). So if I put in 2.5%, my employer puts in up to 7.5%. On my 12,000 ₪ that's about 900 ₪ a month I wouldn't get otherwise. Later the page says it straight: "הכסף של המעסיק לא עומד לבחירה ולכן הוא מחוץ להשוואה" (the employer's money isn't optional, so it's left out of the comparison). That line settled it for me.
- **It's tax-free after 6 years.** "6 שנים אחרי התשלום הראשון אפשר למשוך את הכסף, והרווחים... פטורים ממס" (6 years after the first payment you can withdraw the money, and the profits are tax-free).
- **The money isn't locked.** "אפשר למשוך גם קודם: הכסף לא נעול, אבל המשיכה ממוסה" (you can also withdraw earlier; the money isn't locked, but the withdrawal is taxed). That makes it feel less scary at 24.

I noticed S&P 500 in the list of things I can invest in ("במה אפשר להשקיע בתוכו… מדד S&P 500"). That's the one thing I'd heard of, so I'd choose it.

## 2. Parts I actually used
- **"מה זה"** (what it is): the three paragraphs at the top. This is where I got most of my answer.
- **"במה אפשר להשקיע בתוכו"** (what you can invest in): the row of buttons. I only used the S&P 500 one.
- **"מה מייחד קרן השתלמות"** (what's special about it): the calculator box. I used the two coloured bars, the "ההפרש" (difference) line and the text under them.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא"** (money in and out): the four cards.
- **The orange warning box** about early withdrawal.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"** (risks and what isn't protected).
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח"** (who offers it and who supervises): just the paragraph about employees.

## 3. Block by block
- **Title and grey subtitle:** Used. They give a one-line definition, which was a good start.
- **The row "123 מקור · 123 חושב · 123 הזנתה · מונח":** Confused me. I didn't understand what these numbers and words were explaining, so I ignored them.
- **"מה זה":** Used and read carefully. "למי שמושך לצורך השתלמות, התקופה היא 3 שנים" (for someone withdrawing for professional training, the period is 3 years) got my attention, but I don't know what counts as "השתלמות" (training).
- **"למה משתמשים בזה"** (why people use it): Skimmed. "לשכירים שהמעסיק מפקיד להם זה בדרך כלל הצעד הראשון" (for employees whose employer pays in, this is usually the first step) told me "yes, someone like me."
- **"כדאי להכיר גם: קרן חדשה מקיפה, תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים"** (also worth knowing: these other products): Skipped. These are words I don't know.
- **"במה אפשר להשקיע בתוכו":** Skimmed. I only spotted S&P 500. I don't know what "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה" is.
- **The calculator ("מה הנעילה של קרן ההשתלמות קונה לכם?", what does the keren's lock-in get you?):** Used, but it confused me. At first I thought I'd end up with only 17,370 ₪ after putting in 10,000 plus 500 every month for 10 years. Then I saw "הרווח נטו: מה שנשאר אחרי עלויות ומס, פחות מה שהפקדתם" (net profit: what's left after costs and tax, minus what you put in). So it's the profit only. The difference "קרן ההשתלמות משאירה ₪415.84 יותר" (the keren leaves 415.84 ₪ more) looked tiny and almost made me think it wasn't worth it. Then I understood the employer's money isn't even included. I never touched the inputs or the "שינוי הסכומים" (change the amounts) button.
- **Text under the calculator:** Used part of it. I read the lines about 300 ₪ counting toward the benefit and 200 ₪ not counting. "ההפרש תלוי גם במי שבוחרים" (the difference also depends on who you choose) gave the range "בין ₪−342.02 לבין ₪2,106". A minus means the keren could come out worse? That confused me.
- **"הכי קרוב לזה… חשבון ניירות ערך"** (the closest alternative is a regular investment account) and **"שחקו עם המספרים שלכם"** (play with your own numbers): Skipped. I didn't open them.
- **"שווה לדעת"** (worth knowing): Skimmed. The 6 years repeated, which was reassuring. "הפקדה אחרי משיכה הולכת לחשבון חדש" (a deposit after a withdrawal goes into a new account) didn't mean much to me yet. I ignored the codes "TXH-09", "KH-15" and "PF-18".
- **"מי מציע ומי מפקח":** Used one sentence: "שכירים מצטרפים דרך המעסיק ובוחרים את הקרן בעצמם; המעסיק לא יכול להתנות" (employees join through the employer and choose the fund themselves; the employer can't make it conditional). Good to know I choose. "לא נמצא חוק שמחייב כל מעסיק להפקיד" (no law was found requiring every employer to contribute) made me think I should ask HR.
- **Company cards ("תלוי בחברה: הראל, אלטשולר שחם…", depends on the company):** Skipped. The names mean nothing to me.
- **"כסף נכנס ויוצא":** Used. "כשעוברות 6 שנים" (once 6 years have passed), "עד 4 ימי עסקים" (up to 4 business days) and switching "בלי מס" (without tax) were clear and short.
- **"כמה זה עולה"** (what it costs) table: Skimmed. I only caught "דמי יציאה: אין" (exit fees: none), which is good. "אלה התקרות שבחוק" (these are the legal maximums) told me it isn't the real price, so I moved on.
- **"מיסוי"** (tax) cards: Skimmed. The middle card, "אין מס על הרווחים כל עוד הכסף בפנים" (no tax on profits while the money stays in), was clear. The first card, "שכירים: אין ניכוי על ההפקדה שלכם" (employees: no deduction on your deposit), lost me.
- **Orange warning:** Read it, and it scared me a bit. "משיכה לפני הזמן אפשרית, אבל יקרה" (early withdrawal is possible but expensive), plus "31%" and "47%". I understood "don't take it out early". I didn't understand the tax-bracket part.
- **"הכלכלן הראשי… המליץ לצמצם את ההטבה"** (the Finance Ministry's chief economist recommended cutting the benefit): Read it and felt slightly worried. "החוק לא השתנה" (the law hasn't changed) calmed me.
- **"סיכונים":** Used. "הנכסים מוחזקים בנאמנות, בנפרד מרכוש של החברה המנהלת" (the assets are held in trust, separate from the managing company's property) was reassuring. "משיכה סוגרת את החשבון… ותק שמתחיל מחדש" (withdrawing closes the account, and seniority starts over) was important to me.
- **"מונחים"** (glossary) table: I looked up "ותק" (seniority), "הפקדה מוטבת" (the deposit that gets the tax benefit) and "משכורת קובעת" (the salary the benefit is calculated on). Otherwise I skipped it.
- **Footer:** Skipped.
