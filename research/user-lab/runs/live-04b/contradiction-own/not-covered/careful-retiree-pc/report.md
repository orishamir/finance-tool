## What it said along the way

I'll tell my son what I found: the site itself says it doesn't calculate a long list of things, many of them close to my own money. That includes structured deposits, foreign-currency deposits, dollar-linked deposits, savings policies and corporate bonds. I'm fairly sure of this, but I didn't get to the end of the list. **What decided it:** a red banner at the top says "החוקים והכללים לכל המוצרים כאן עוד בבדיקה. עד שייסגרו, האתר לא מחשב אותם." Every card has the same red tag, "לא מחושב". Red makes me back away, so I won't rely on this site for now.

**What I looked at, in order (6 screens):**
1. The heading "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה" was exactly what I came for. The first cards were "ETC" and "LBMA Gold Price", English letters that mean nothing to me. Then "אג"ח קונצרניות", with an open question about bond losses and tax, and "אגרת בונדס".
2. "ארנק מט"ח דיגיטלי", "ביטוח חיים משתתף ברווחים", "ביטוח מנהלים" (open question: "איך ממוסות הכנסות ההשקעה"), "היוון קצבה מוכרת".
3. "הסדר חוב" and "השקעה בזהב דרך קרנות סל". Further down were "חברת דירוג" and "חשבון מטבע חוץ".
4. "מוצר מובנה", "מנוטרלת מט"ח", "מענק", "מסלולי האירוע".
5. Two cards had their titles above the screen. One was about a licensed company that buys and sells "לפי שיקול דעתה" in my own bank account. The other was about a company that watches over bond holders. Then "עמלת חליפין" and "ניירות ערך מסחריים".
6. This screen worried me most: "פיקדון במטבע חוץ", which has an open question about tax on the interest, plus "פוליסת חיסכון", "צמוד דולר" and "פיקדון מובנה".

After that I was tired, and I didn't read screens 7 to 10. I didn't see a plain shekel deposit anywhere, so I don't know whether the site covers deposits like mine.
