# not-covered: self-directed-investor

## Answer

The site gives no number at all for about thirty items, all tagged "לא מחושב". For me the ones that matter are ETC and gold through a fund, currency-hedged funds (מנוטרלת מט"ח) and above all the currency conversion fee (עמלת חליפין), which is the cost I've never checked. It also doesn't compute corporate bonds, structured products and deposits, dollar-linked and foreign-currency deposits, managed or digital portfolios, insurance-type savings (ביטוח מנהלים, משתתף ברווחים, קרן י') and the pension tax items (היוון, קצבה מוכרת, תיקון 190, תשלומים פטורים). By design it never covers loans, picking stocks, trading, derivatives or ranking by past returns. So I can use it to read up on these, but not to decide anything about my US ETFs or FX costs.

Confidence: fairly sure; gave up: false
