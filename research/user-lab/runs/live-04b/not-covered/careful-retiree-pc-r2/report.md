## What it said along the way

I'll tell you straight. I didn't decide anything, and I wouldn't rely on this site for my money yet. It has a page called "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה" ("what the site doesn't cover yet"). At the top, in a red box, it says it isn't calculating these products yet: "החוקים והכללים לכל המוצרים כאן עוד בבדיקה. עד שייסגרו, האתר לא מחשב אותם" ("the laws and rules for all the products here are still being checked; until they are settled, the site doesn't calculate them"). Every card on that page has a red "לא מחושב" ("not calculated") label.

**What the site can't compare or explain yet (the ones I saw in six screens):**
- **Things close to what I have:** פיקדון מובנה (structured deposit), פיקדון במטבע חוץ (foreign-currency deposit), צמוד דולר (dollar-linked deposit), and the מענק (bonus) a bank pays on a structured deposit. There is also מוצר מובנה (structured product), which the card says can be a deposit.
- **Insurance savings:** ביטוח מנהלים (managers' insurance), פוליסת חיסכון (savings policy), ביטוח חיים משתתף ברווחים (with-profits life insurance), מסלולי האירוח (hosted investment tracks).
- **Bonds and debt:** אג"ח קונצרניות (corporate bonds), אגרת בונדס (Israel Bonds), הסדר חוב (debt settlement), חברת דירוג (rating company), ניירות ערך מסחריים (commercial paper).
- **Gold:** LBMA Gold Price, and gold through funds. I have no idea what LBMA is.
- **Others:** ETC, ארנק מט"ח דיגיטלי (digital foreign-currency wallet), היוון קצבה מוכרת (turning a pension allowance into one lump sum), חשבון מטבע חוץ (foreign-currency account), מנוטרלת מט"ח (currency-neutralised), עמלת חליפין (exchange fee).

**What worried me:** The deposit cards matter most to me, and on some of them a question is still open. On the foreign-currency deposit, the open question is how a bank deducts tax on the interest, which is about my own interest. On the savings policy, it is whether a rate that isn't linked can count as the return. So exactly the things nearest to my deposits are the ones it can't answer yet.

Then there's the savings plan the clerk gave me, whose name I can't remember. If it's one of these structured or dollar-linked deposits, this site can't tell me whether it's good or how much the bank takes. I'll ask you, or go into the branch.

I'm fairly sure about the list. I'm not sure what comes after screen six, because I stopped there.
