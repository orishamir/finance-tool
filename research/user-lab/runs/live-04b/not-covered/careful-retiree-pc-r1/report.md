## Answer

This site says outright that it can't yet work out or explain quite a lot of things. The ones that matter to me are the deposits: the "פיקדון מובנה" (מוצר מובנה), the foreign-currency deposit (פיקדון במטבע חוץ), the deposit tied to the dollar (צמוד דולר) and the savings policy (פוליסת חיסכון). The rest of the list, as far as I got: corporate bonds (אג"ח קונצרניות), Israel bonds (אגרת בונדס), gold and ETC, ביטוח מנהלים, insurance with profit sharing, taking a pension as one lump sum (היוון קצבה), dollar hedging (מנוטרלת מט"ח), a digital currency wallet, and investment management (מסלולי ניהול, מנהל תיקים). So I won't rely on it for my deposits. I'll ask you, and then the bank, and only then decide.

Confidence: fairly sure; gave up: false
