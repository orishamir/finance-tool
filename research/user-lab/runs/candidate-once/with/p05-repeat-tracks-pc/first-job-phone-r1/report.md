## What would bother this person, most first

- The whole table: 143 tracks that look alike, with no recommendation and no shortcut. Very tiring.
- Every row in the table: Every row repeats the same labels (מניות/מט"ח, מהחיסכון/מההפקדות, 3 שנים/5 שנים), so I re-read them on every row just to find what's different.
- Returns column: Lots of rows say 'פחות מ־3 שנים' instead of a number, so there's nothing to compare them by.
- Exposure column: The colours in the ring are only explained with small dots, and the percentages sit beside it, so I had to look back and forth.
- Fees column: Fees are only shown as percents, nothing in shekels, so it's hard to tell if the difference between 0.56% and 0.65% matters.
- Whole page: Nothing tells me what to do next or how to open one.
