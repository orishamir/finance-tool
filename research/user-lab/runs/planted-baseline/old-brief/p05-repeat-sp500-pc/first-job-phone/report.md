I'd open an account with **Psagot (פסגות)** and every month buy the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, number 5139282. With the page's numbers that gives me **about 86,500 ₪ after 10 years**, compared with 70,000 ₪ I'd put in. I'm fairly sure about the account and the fund, maybe 70%. I'm much less sure about the final number.

## 1. My answer

- **Where:** Psagot. It was #1 in "כל החשבונות" and was marked "★ הזול". The page says it costs about "1,057 ₪" over 10 years. That's the smallest amount I saw, and it has no "לא בחישוב" warning attached.
- **What to buy:** the Yelin Lapidot fund above. The page pre-picked it ("הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול"). Its fee is "0.02%", which looks tiny. In the fund list it's the top row, marked "הבחירה".
- **How much:** "86,492 ₪". The page says that's what "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום" ("what's left for you in 10 years, after fees and tax, in today's money"). I left the sliders as they were: 10,000 ₪ to start, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year. I don't have 10,000 ₪ to start with for sure, so that part is a guess.
- **How sure:** I trust the ranking. The top choices are only a few hundred to a few thousand shekels apart, so even if I'm a bit off it won't hurt me much. I trust the 86,492 much less. Near the bottom the page says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" ("an example return, not a forecast"), and the 7% is just a number someone put in. There's also "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" ("if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel"), and I have no idea if it will. So my honest answer is "somewhere around 80–90 thousand, maybe."
