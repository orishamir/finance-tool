# Choosing an investment savings fund (kupat gemel lehashkaa) from my friend's screenshots

## 1. My answer

**I would choose Harel (הראל), the track called "אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)", fund number 8521.** My second choice is Harel's "כללי" track, number 8211.

My reasons:
- **The yearly fee.** The page's last line says returns are past returns and "היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך, ולכן אין מיון לפיה". So I compared fees. Harel's fees run "מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". Its 8521 track charges "0.58% מהחיסכון" and "0% מההפקדות". On 100,000 ₪ that comes to about 580 ₪ a year. Mor's similar track (12955) charges 0.72%, about 720 ₪. The difference is small, but it comes out of my pocket every year.
- **Size.** Harel manages "19.1 מיליארד ₪" across its tracks, and this track alone holds "431 מיליון ₪". I would rather not be one of the first people in something new.
- **My age.** "עד 25% מניות" means at most a quarter of my money is in shares. I am 61, and I can't afford to lose half my savings in a bad year.
- **A track record.** The returns are "8.6%" over 3 years and "4.73%" over 5 years. They aren't spectacular, but at least the track has a history.

**How sure am I? About 50/50.** I'm sure I want a big company, a low fee and a cautious track. I'm not sure about the 8521 track itself, because its top line is cut off at the top of screen 3. I can't fully read its share percentage. I would phone Harel before signing anything.

## 2. The parts of the page I actually used

- **The table's company header rows.** For example, "הראל 16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". These were the most useful lines on the page.
- **The "דמי ניהול" column**, mainly the number "מהחיסכון".
- **The "כסף מנוהל" column**, to see how big each track is.
- **The track names**, to find the cautious ones.
- **The note at the very bottom** saying past returns don't predict the future. It changed how I read the returns column.

- **Product buttons** ("תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים 182", "קופת גמל להשקעה 143"…): I only checked that "קופת גמל להשקעה" was the highlighted one. Good, that's the one I want.
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה"**: I wanted to click it but couldn't.
- **The search box** ("שם המסלול או החברה"): Useless to me in a picture.
- **The "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons** ("הכל 143", "כללי 12", "מניות 12"…): I skimmed them. "הלכה יהודית" and "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" meant nothing to me.
- **"143 מסלולים של 12 חברות" and the sorting explanation**: I used it. It told me the companies are ordered by size and the tracks inside each company by fee. That helped.
- **"123 מקור / 123 מחושב. לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"**: This confused me. I don't know what difference it makes to me whether a number is "source" or "calculated".
- **The long table (screens 1–15)**: I used it, but it tired me. It's 15 screens long. Some screens start in the middle of a company, so I couldn't tell whose rows I was looking at. Screens 7, 8, 10, 11 and 14 have no company name on them at all.
- **The "חדש" rows with "עוד לא דווח" (mostly screens 14–15)**: I skipped them. No fee and no returns means nothing for me to judge.
- **The bottom note and the footer** ("אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק"): I read them. The footer reassured me a little that nobody is being paid to push me somewhere.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח".** Shares I understand. Why does foreign currency matter, and is 99% foreign currency good or bad for me? Also, how can "101% מניות" (Meitav's מניות track) be more than 100%?
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, ברוטו".** If "ברוטו" means before fees, what did I actually get? Am I supposed to subtract 0.6% myself?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים".** Does this mean the track is too young to have a 3-year figure? I think so, but I'm guessing.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025".** An average of what? Will I pay this, or can the company charge me more?
- **Track names** like "עוקב מדדים - גמיש", "S&P 500" and "משולב סחיר".
- **Why the same company runs two tracks with the same name.** For example, Mor has "מניות סחיר" and "מניות".

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Money in shekels.** For example: on 100,000 ₪, how much would I pay in fees each year?
- **The worst year.** How much did a cautious track lose in a bad year? Being 61, this matters more to me than the average.
- **Tax and withdrawals.** Can I take the money out whenever I want, and what tax would I pay? I've heard something about turning it into a monthly payment after 60. None of that is here.
- **Whether I can bargain for a lower fee**, and what the maximum fee is.
- **The company's service.** How easy is it to reach someone, and how fast do they pay out?
- **Four of the companies' names.** The page says 12 companies, and I only saw 8 names: הראל, מור, אנליסט, מיטב, ילין לפידות, מגדל, מנורה מבטחים, אינפיניטי. That may be my friend's screenshots and not the page.
- **A plain "if you're cautious, look here" pointer.**
