# What I made of the page about my current account (חשבון עובר ושב)

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

**What it costs me.** The page says every bank has to offer a cheap basic package, "המסלול הבסיסי". It covers up to 1 action at the teller ("פקיד") and up to 10 actions online or by app ("בערוץ ישיר") a month, for at most "₪10.00 בחודש". There is a bigger package, "המסלול המורחב", with 10 teller and 50 online actions. Anything over the package is charged "לפי התעריפון המלא", the bank's full price list.

There is also a new cap on debit card fees of "₪7.00" a month from "01.10.2026", so it should already apply to me. From "01.07.2027" the packages go away, and the fee depends on how many actions I make:
- up to ₪5 a month for up to two actions
- up to ₪10 a month for three to a hundred actions
- ₪1 for each action after that

**What this means for my money:** my account should cost me no more than about ₪10 a month plus up to ₪7 for the card, about ₪200 a year, as long as I'm on the basic package and stay inside its limits. So I would phone the bank and ask: "Which package am I on, and how many actions a month do I really use?" If I'm not on the basic one, I'd ask to move.

**How much to keep in it.** The page says most banks pay nothing on the money in the account. Its example shows that ₪10,000 left there for 10 years is worth "₪8,203" in today's money, which is "₪1,797 פחות". So I would keep only what I need for my monthly bills plus a cushion, maybe one or two months of spending. I'd think about moving the rest to the thing the page compares the account to, "קרן כספית יומית" (a money market fund you can take money out of daily).

**How sure am I?**
- On the fees, fairly sure, around 70%. The page gives clear caps. It doesn't tell me *my* bank's price or which package I'm on.
- On how much to keep, only about 50%. The page never actually says how much to keep. I made up "one or two months" myself.
- The only figure it gives is ₪15,000: if more than that sits in the account for a whole quarter ("במשך רבעון שלם"), the law makes the bank send me a letter offering other options. I don't know if that's meant as advice or is just when the letter comes.
- Before I move anything, I'd want someone to explain whether a קרן כספית is safer or less safe than the bank. The risks section scared me a bit and didn't answer that.
