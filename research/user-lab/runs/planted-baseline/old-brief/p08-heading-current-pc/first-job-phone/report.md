## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

**What it costs me.** If I make 10 or fewer actions a month, the bank should charge me at most about 10 shekels a month. The page says every bank has to offer "המסלול הבסיסי" (the basic plan) and that it is "מוגבל בצו: עד ₪10.00 בחודש" (capped by an order at ₪10 a month). It covers "עד 1 פעולה בפקיד ועד 10 פעולות בערוץ ישיר" (one action with a teller and ten direct ones). Two more things I took away:
- There's a new cap of ₪7 a month on "דמי כרטיס חיוב מיידי". I think that's my debit card, but I'm not sure.
- From 01.07.2027 the plans go away. Instead it will be ₪5 for up to two actions, ₪10 for 3 to 100 actions, and ₪1 for each action over 100.

So I'd open my bank app and check which plan I'm on. If it's the "מורחב" (extended) plan and I don't use it, I'd ask to switch to the basic one.

**What it makes me.** Probably nothing. The page says "רוב הבנקים משלמים בעו"ש: 0%" (most banks pay 0% on a current account). The calculator showed me that ₪10,000 left in the account for 10 years is worth ₪8,203 in today's money, so "₪1,797 פחות" (₪1,797 less). In a "קרן כספית יומית" (some kind of daily money fund) the same money would be ₪10,779. "המחיר של להשאיר" (the price of leaving it there) is ₪2,576. That got my attention.

**How much to keep in it.** The page only says "כסף שעשוי לצאת היום נשאר בעו"ש" (money that might go out today stays in the account) and "כסף שלא צריכים באותו יום לא חייב לשבת שם" (money you don't need that day doesn't have to sit there). So I'd keep about one month of rent, card bills and expenses, plus a bit extra. I'd look into moving the rest to that fund.

**How sure I am.**
- About the fee: fairly sure, maybe 70%.
- About keeping less in the account: fairly sure it's the right direction.
- About exactly how much to keep, and about actually putting money into a "קרן כספית": not sure at all. I don't really know what it is, how to buy one, or whether it's safe.
