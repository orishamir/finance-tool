## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Join the fund and leave the money alone for **at least 6 years** from my first deposit. Then I'd take it out with no tax. I'd also look at the "מדד S&P 500" option at the top of the calculator, because it's the only investment name on the page I recognise.

**Roughly how much:** The calculator was set to a salary of 12,000 ₪ a month, with 2.5% from me and 7.5% from my employer. On that setting, after 5 years I'd have put in **18,000 ₪** and get back about **66,000 ₪**. That's from "נשאר בידיים... 66,280 עד 66,601 ₪", and only if I ask for a tax refund. If I don't ask, I'd only get **50,719 ₪**, because "הקרן מעבירה 29,768 ₪ למס הכנסה". After 6 years it's tax-free, and the bars to the right of the dotted line are taller. But I never found a shekel number for 6 years.

**When:** At 6 years ("אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה... בלי מס"). It's 3 years if the money is for studies, or for someone near retirement, which isn't me.

**How sure:** I'm fairly sure about the 6-year rule, since the page repeats it many times. I'm only roughly sure about the amount. The 12,000 salary isn't mine, the 7% return is "הנחות של האתר, לא תחזית", and the one clear number is for 5 years, not 6.
