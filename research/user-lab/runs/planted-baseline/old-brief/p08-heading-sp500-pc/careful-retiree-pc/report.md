# How I used the page, as a 61-year-old saving for ten years

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would open a securities account at **Psagot** ("פסגות", listed under "חשבון ניירות ערך"). I would buy **Yelin Lapidot's S&P 500 tracker fund, number 5139282** ("ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)") and set up a fixed monthly purchase.

**What this means for my money:** the page already had 10,000 ₪ today, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years and 7% a year filled in.
- I would put in **70,000 ₪** ("הפקדתם 70,000 ₪").
- I would end up with about **86,492 ₪**, measured in today's money, after fees and tax ("נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום").
- That's roughly 16,500 ₪ more than I put in.

**How sure I am:**
- **About the account and fund: fairly sure.** The second-best pair was only 43 ₪ behind over ten years ("−43 ₪ לעומת הבחירה שלכם"). The fees panel said the account costs "1 ₪ יותר", the fund costs "58 ₪ פחות" and the tax is "14 ₪ יותר". So between those two it barely matters. Altshuler Shaham's fund at Psagot would be just as good. Leumi, the first real bank on the list, comes to 83,396 ₪. That's about 3,000 ₪ less, which is the price of staying with a bank I know.
- **About the 86,492: not sure at all.** The number holds only "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל", and only at a 7% return, which the page itself calls "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". The 86,492 is a sample figure, not a promise.
- **One thing that worried me:** this fund has only one year of track record ("שנה אחת"). Most of the others have four ("4 שנים").

## 3. Block by block, top to bottom

- **Title and index buttons: used.** I clicked S&P 500 in my head. "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד" was fine with me, I had already chosen. The little "זר" tag beside the heading confused me. Foreign? So what?
- **"מדד של חמש מאות החברות הגדולות בארצות הברית… הכסף מושקע בדולרים": read it.** "In dollars" stuck with me and worried me all the way down.
- **The four boxes and sliders: used.** They already matched my plan except the 10,000 ₪ starting sum, which I accepted. I skipped the "אינפלציה" button and the line "שער הדולר 3.17 ₪ · מחושב משערי הייחוס של הבנק המרכזי האירופי". Why does a European bank set my shekel figure?
- **Three result cards: used most of all.** This is where my answer came from.
- **Coloured fee bar and legend (top of screen 2): skimmed.** I only read the three totals: החשבון / הקרן / המס.
- **Selected fund card (Yelin, fees "0% קבועים 0% משתנים 0.02% נאמן"): used.** I took it to mean almost no management fee. I don't know what "נאמן" means.
- **Account list "כל החשבונות": used.** I read the name, "נשאר", and the small grey number under it (for Psagot, 1,057 ₪).
  - I read the "לא בחישוב" notes carefully. Hapoalim has a different price "לגמלאים… לנשים מגיל 60 וגברים מגיל 65", and Massad has one "לגמלאים". That made me wonder whether a bank could come out cheaper for someone my age.
  - I read the paragraph "3 מתוך 15 הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות שברשימה מפרסמים לה מחיר נמוך יותר" twice. I got the gist.
  - I skimmed below Discount, because the numbers only got worse. I skipped the Interactive rows.
- **Fund list: used the top 3–4 rows.** I skimmed the rest down to I.B.I. (−4,492 ₪).
- **Foreign funds (iShares, Vanguard, "אין מחיר כאן", "עוד לא נאסף"): skipped.** No price means no use to me. "מס עיזבון אמריקאי" and "צריך טופס W-8BEN" frightened me off entirely.
- **Hedged fund row: used.** It says "בין +2,212 ₪ ל־−23,766 ₪… לפי הדולר, מירידה של 3% בשנה ועד עלייה של 3% בשנה". I read it as: hedging gains me at most about 2,000 ₪ and could cost me about 24,000 ₪. So I skip hedging.
- **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות": skimmed.** It was a fair explanation, but I didn't need it.
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם": used the table, skimmed the four-line chart.** Two more years of saving is worth "19,116 ₪ יותר". 600 ₪ instead of 500 a month is worth "14,474 ₪ יותר". That was useful: saving longer and more matters more than which bank.
- **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל": read the cards and "השורה התחתונה", skimmed the rest.**
  - "הנטרול לא מגן מירידה של השוק" was the most useful sentence on the page for me.
  - "זה בסיס קטן: 4 שנים" made me trust it more, because they admit it.
- **The two bar charts (dollar per year, funds per year): skimmed.** I noticed "ב־15 מתוך 25 שנים הדולר ירד" and moved on.
- **Year-by-year table: skimmed.** I looked only at "4 השנים יחד": +50.4% to +56.8% without hedging against +35.9% with it.
- **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת, ורואים מה משפר אותה ובכמה" (the steps chart): confused me.**
  - It starts from "עכשיו: S&P 500 (4D) של MTF, ירושלים. 79,176 ₪". I don't have anything at Bank Jerusalem, so why is that "now"?
  - In the table under it, the "+" and "−" signs (for example "+444" under מס, "+41" under עלות החשבון) didn't tell me whether that was good or bad for me.
- **"חשבון אחר, אותה קרן" / "סוג קרן אחר" / "קרן אחרת" tables: skimmed.** Green "עוד" and red "פחות" were clear. Otherwise it repeated what I'd already seen.
- **Footnotes: read them all.** One alarmed me: "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות". Does my choice stay first at 0%? It doesn't say.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- "(4D)" and "(4A)" after the fund names.
- "עסקת החלף", and "מס גם על שינוי המטבע". Am I taxed on the dollar going up even if the index doesn't?
- "שכר נאמן 0.02%", and why "0% קבועים 0% משתנים" is even possible. Who's paying them?
- "דמי משמרת", and "העמלות זו על זו".
- Why the shekel number depends on "הבנק המרכזי האירופי".
- The steps chart: why it starts at Bank Jerusalem, and what "+41" and "+444" mean.
- "נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג".
- The "זר" tag.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **A real range for my own number.** If the dollar drops 3% a year, how much would I actually have, not just compared with the hedged fund? And how much at 0% return?
- **Do I qualify for the senior price?** Hapoalim and Massad have one ("לגמלאים", "לנשים מגיל 60…"). Would it beat Psagot? It's "לא בחישוב".
- **Is Psagot safe?** It's not a bank. If they go under, what happens to my fund?
- **How to actually do it:** open the account, set up the monthly order ("הוראת קבע"), how long it takes, and whether I can do it by phone and not only in an app.
- **Taking money out:** what happens if I need some at 66, how the tax at withdrawal works, and whether 86,492 is before or after that final tax. The page says "אחרי… מס", but I wanted that spelled out.
- **Whether something other than a securities account suits someone my age better**, for example a קופת גמל להשקעה.
- **Whether the ranking holds at 0% or 10% return.** The page only says "הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות", and "פירוט" didn't show me those places.
