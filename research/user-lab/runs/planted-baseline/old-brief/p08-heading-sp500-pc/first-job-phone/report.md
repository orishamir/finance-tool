# Using the S&P 500 page as a first-time investor

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot). Buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, number 5139282, every month.

**How much I'd end up with:** I left the page's default numbers in place: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year. They're close enough to my situation. I'd put in **70,000 ₪** and end up with about **86,492 ₪**. The page says this is *"נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"* (after fees and tax, in today's money) and *"אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל"* (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel).

**How sure I am:**
- **Fairly sure about the fund.** The top few are almost the same. The next pair is only *"‎−43 ₪ לעומת הבחירה שלכם"*, so picking the "wrong" one of the top three hardly matters.
- **Less sure about Psagot.** The difference between banks is bigger, about 5,000 ₪ between Psagot and Jerusalem. But I don't know how to open an account there, or whether they'll actually give me that price. The line *"קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו"* (got a different price? enter it) makes me think the price can vary.
- **Not sure about the 86,492 itself.** The page says the 7% is *"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"*, an example and not a prediction. A gain of only about 16,000 ₪ on 70,000 ₪ over ten years felt low. I guess that's because of "today's money" and tax.

- The **account list on the right** (*כל החשבונות*), mainly the ranking and the *נשאר* number.
- The **fund list on the left** (*כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500*), mainly the top few rows and the green/red money on the far left.
- The **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם" table**, for one point: adding years or money matters far more than which bank I pick.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **Fund labels:** *4D* versus *4A*, *קרן מחקה* versus *קרן סל*, *צוברת* versus *מחלקת דיבידנד*, *חשיפה לדולר*, *מנוטרלת מט"ח*, *נקנית בחו"ל*, *קרן חוץ בת"א*, *כמה דרכים יחד*.
- **Fees:** *דמי משמרת*, *נאמן*, *דמי ניהול קבועים / משתנים*, *"העמלות זו על זו"*.
- **The *מול המדד* percentages**, like *"+0.37% · בשנה · שנה אחת"*: is plus good?
- **The foreign-fund warnings:** *W-8BEN*, *מס עיזבון אמריקאי*, *"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"*, *"מס דיבידנד בתוך הקרן"*.
- **What the אינפלציה button does**, and why the European Central Bank appears at all.
- **Why the staircase chart starts at Jerusalem with MTF** and calls it *"עכשיו"*.
- **The dotted underlines under the numbers.** I assume they explain something, but I didn't try them.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **How to open an account at Psagot** and how long it takes. Can I do it online?
- **How to set up the automatic monthly buy.** Some banks have *"מחיר להוראת קבע"*; does Psagot even allow a standing order?
- **Whether I'd get a cheaper "young person" price** at another bank, and whether that would beat Psagot. The page shows those offers but says *"לא בחישוב"*.
- **Whether 7% is a realistic number to type in.**
- **Before tax and inflation:** roughly how much would I see in the account in actual shekels?
- **Whether Psagot and this fund are safe.** What happens if Psagot closes?
- **When I pay the tax:** every year, or only when I sell?
