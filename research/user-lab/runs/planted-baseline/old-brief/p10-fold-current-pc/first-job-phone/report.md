# p10-fold-current-pc: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 27 s, $0.13

## 1. My answer

I'll keep roughly one month's money in the current account (עו"ש): rent, bills, card payments and whatever comes up before the next salary. Anything above that I'll move out. The page points to a **קרן כספית יומית** (daily money market fund) as the closest thing that's just as available. I'm fairly sure about the "don't leave a pile sitting there" part, maybe 7/10. I'm not sure what the fund is or how to buy one.

On what the account costs me, I came away only half-answered. The page showed me the hidden cost: money sitting there loses value. It never told me what fees my bank charges. So on that question I'm maybe 3/10.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- **"כמה כסף להחזיק בעו"ש"** told me what the account is for: "המשכורת, ההוצאות של החודש והתשלומים הקרובים". It also mentioned the 15,000 ₪ line.
- **"מה מייחד חשבון עובר ושב"**, the calculator box. This is what convinced me. I read the sentence "אחרי 10 שנים, 10,000 ₪ בעו"ש שווים 8,203 ₪ בכסף של היום: 1,797 ₪ פחות." I also read the three numbers under the chart.
- The line just under the box: "הכי קרוב לזה, באותה החזקה: קרן כספית יומית".
- The first sentence of **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: "אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות: אם בנק קורס, אין חוק שמחזיר לכם את הכסף".

## 4. Chart and table

- **Chart:** I looked at the yellow line (בקרן כספית) going up and the light line (בעו"ש) going down. I looked at the shaded brown area labelled "המחיר להשאיר 2,576 ₪". I ignored the dashed line ("הסכום שהתחלת בו") and the left axis labels (7–12 אלף). I didn't try clicking the chart.
- **Three numbers under it:** 8,203 / 10,779 / 2,576. I looked at all three. That's where it clicked for me.
- **Glossary table:** I only read the "מונח" and "מה זה" columns. I ignored the "איפה מופיע" tags (אפליקציה, תעריפון, דוח שנתי), though "תעריפון" might actually be where my fees are?
