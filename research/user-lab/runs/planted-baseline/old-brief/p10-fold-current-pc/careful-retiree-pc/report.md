# What I made of the page about my current account (חשבון עובר ושב)

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I'd keep about one month of what goes out of the account in there: salary in, and enough for the bills and payments coming up soon. I'd ask my bank whether it pays any interest on the rest. Anything above that I'd think about moving somewhere that earns something, but I wouldn't move it on the strength of this page alone.

The sentence that convinced me was **"רוב הבנקים לא משלמים ריבית על עו"ש, ולכן הכסף מאבד מערכו בקצב עליית המחירים."** So for my money: cash that just sits there shrinks. The example says **10,000 ₪** left for **10 שנים** is worth only **8,203 ₪** in today's money, so I'd be **1,797 ₪** worse off.

How sure I am:
- **That I shouldn't leave a big pile in the account: fairly sure, maybe 8 out of 10.** It's simple arithmetic and it matches what I already suspected.
- **What the account actually costs me in fees: not sure at all.** The page never gave me a shekel figure for monthly fees. The only "cost" it shows is the money lost to rising prices.
- **Moving money to a "קרן כספית": not sure.** The page itself says its 3.25% is **"הנחה של האתר, לא הבטחה ולא תחזית"**. I respect that it says so, but it also means the 10,779 ₪ figure is a guess.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **The top heading and the line under it:** "כסף שיושב בו לא תמיד מקבל ריבית". That told me what the page was about.
- **"כמה כסף להחזיק בעו"ש"**, especially: "העו"ש הוא המקום לכסף שצריך להיות זמין באותו יום". It also had the **15,000 ₪** line: if more than that stays in for a quarter, the bank has to write to me with alternatives. That gave me a number I can relate to.
- **"מה מייחד חשבון עובר ושב"**: the box with the 10,000 ₪ / 10 years sentence, the three totals under the chart, and the small print under them.
- **The list under "שווה לדעת"**, mainly the line that only **"4 מתוך 8 הבנקים"** pay interest, and only up to **10,000 ₪**, best **3%** at Discount.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**: "אין בישראל ביטוח פיקדונות" frightened me, and I read it twice.
- **The footer:** "נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026" and "אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק". I want to know who is talking to me and how fresh the numbers are, so that reassured me a little.

## 3. Block by block

1. **Menu across the top (מדריך, חברות ומסלולים, השקעה במדד, חוקים וכללים):** Skipped. It isn't about my account.
2. **Menu on the right side ("בעמוד הזה"):** Skimmed it as a table of contents. Useful for seeing what's coming.
3. **Title and the line under it:** Used.
4. **The small line "123 מקור · 123 מחושב · מונח – לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא":** Confused me. Some numbers have different underlines and I don't know why I should care. I only had pictures, so I couldn't click anything.
5. **"מה זה":** Read it, but it tells me what a current account is, and I know that. I did note "ריבית שכן משולמת הבנק מנכה מס", so tax comes off any interest. The green link **"עמלות ומחירים"** looked like exactly what I wanted, but I couldn't see where it leads.
6. **The little badges "חוקים וכללים · 5" (and 3, 4, 6):** Skipped. No idea what they are.
7. **"כמה כסף להחזיק בעו"ש":** Used, the most useful words on the page. The sentence about "יחידות של קרן כספית נפדות רק ביום מסחר" half lost me.
8. **The calculator box ("כמה עולה להשאיר כסף בעו"ש?"):** Used the sentences and the three numbers. The slider and the input boxes are no use to me in a picture, and 10,000 ₪ isn't necessarily my amount.
9. **The chart:** Looked at it, but the sentence above it had already told me the story. See section 4.
10. **Two small paragraphs under the totals (2% a year, 3.25%):** Read carefully. They told me the numbers are assumptions.
11. **Folded bar "חלק מהבנקים כן משלמים ריבית, בתנאים ועל סכום קטן בלבד":** Wanted to open it but couldn't, so I only had the title.
12. **"הכי קרוב לזה... קרן כספית יומית" with a link:** Skimmed. It sends me to another page.
13. **Folded bar "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם":** Confused me. Wasn't the box above already where I put in my numbers? Why is there a second one?
14. **"שווה לדעת" list:** Read it. The codes **BANK-20, BANK-23, BANK-15** mean nothing to me and I ignored them. "מק"מ" is never explained. The link **"לטבלה"** sounds like the list of banks I need, but I couldn't follow it. Also, the next picture starts at a new section, so I may have missed a few lines of this list.
15. **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן":** Used, read every word. The law bits ("שר האוצר ... לבחון תוך 12 חודשים", "250,000 ₪ ... רק הצעה") made it clear there is no protection today. The last paragraph matters to me: if the bank gives me a better deal, it has to warn me **21 ימים** before it ends.
16. **"מונחים" table:** Skimmed it. Mostly it repeats things I'd already read. The one useful column was "איפה מופיע" (see section 4).
17. **"הצגת כל המונחים של העמוד" / "הצגת המונחים הכלליים":** Skipped.
18. **Footer links (מסמכים, פרמטרים, מה האתר עוד לא מכסה, העברה למכשיר אחר):** I skipped them, though "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה" made me wonder whether fees are on that list.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

**The chart:**
- I looked at the **yellow line "בקרן כספית"** going up and the **light blue line "בעו"ש"** going down.
- I looked at the **brown shaded area with the label "המחיר להשאיר 2,576 ₪"** and the years along the bottom, **"היום" to 10**.
- I ignored the **dashed line "הסכום שהתחלת בו"** at first, then realised it's just where I started.
- I noticed the scale on the left starts at **"7 אלף ₪"**, not at zero. That makes the drop look bigger than it is, so I trusted the written numbers more than the picture.
- The line "לחיצה על הגרף נועלת שנה" is useless to me in a screenshot.

**The three totals under the chart:**
- I used all three: **8,203 ₪** (current account after 10 years), **10,779 ₪** (fund) and **2,576 ₪** (the price of leaving it).
- The coloured dots next to them matched the chart, which helped.
- The numbers have dotted underlines, which I took to mean "calculated". I'm not sure.

**The "שווה לדעת" list:** I read the text. I ignored the grey codes on the left (BANK-20 and so on).

**The "מונחים" table:**
- I read the "מונח" column only for **"מסלול בסיסי"** and **"מסלול מורחב"**. Those are the words I need to say to my bank.
- I read "מה זה" for those two rows.
- I looked at the **"איפה מופיע"** tags, **"אפליקציה", "תעריפון", "דוח שנתי"**. They actually helped, because they tell me where to look up my own bank's numbers.
- I ignored the rows that repeat the top of the page ("חשבון עובר ושב", "יתרת זכות בחשבון").

## 5. What I didn't understand

- The **"123 מקור / 123 מחושב / מונח"** line and the different underlines.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 5"**: is it a button, a count, a warning?
- **"BANK-20" and the other codes.**
- **"מק"מ"**, never spelled out.
- **"נפדות רק ביום מסחר"**: if I need the money on a Friday, can I get it?
- **"קרן כספית"**: what it actually is, whether it costs anything, and whether it's safer or riskier than the bank. The page assumes I know.
- **"הטבה"**, the word is underlined. I think it means a temporary discount, but I'm not certain.
- Why there's a **second calculator** ("שחקו עם המספרים שלכם") under the first one.
- Whether **"בכסף של היום"** is the money I'd actually have, or something else. I think it means what it will buy, but I had to read it three times.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **What my account costs me each month in fees, in shekels.** That was my first question, and the page doesn't answer it. There's only the link "עמלות ומחירים", which I couldn't open.
- **What the "מסלול בסיסי" costs**, and whether I'd save by switching to it.
- **Which 4 banks pay interest, and whether mine is one of them.** That's behind "לטבלה".
- **A rule of thumb for my own case**, like "one month of expenses" or "two months". The page says "כסף שעשוי לצאת היום" but never puts a number on how much is sensible.
- **How to move money to a קרן כספית, what it costs, and how quickly I get it back.**
- **What happens to my money if the bank fails.** The page says "אין חוק שמחזיר לכם את הכסף", but nothing about what to do about that, for example whether to split money between banks.
- **Whether the tax on interest** changes the sums.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The "מונחים" table and the two "הצגת..." links.** They mostly repeat what's above.
2. **The "123 מקור / מחושב" line, the "חוקים וכללים" badges and the BANK codes.** They only confused me.
3. **The folded "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם" bar.**
4. **The chart itself.** The sentence "אחרי 10 שנים, 10,000 ₪ בעו"ש שווים 8,203 ₪" and the three numbers below it say it more clearly.
5. **"מה זה".** I know what a current account is.

What must stay: **"כמה כסף להחזיק בעו"ש"** with the 15,000 ₪ line, the plain sentences and three totals in the calculator box, the small print saying the numbers are assumptions, the "4 מתוך 8 הבנקים" line, and **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"**. If something could be added in the space saved, it would be the actual fees in shekels.
