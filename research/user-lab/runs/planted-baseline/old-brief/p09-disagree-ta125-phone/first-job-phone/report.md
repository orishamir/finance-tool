# What I'd do with the page my friend sent

## 1. My answer

I'd open an account at **פסגות** and buy the fund **"אי.בי.איי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125"**, number **5112628**. The page marks this pair with a star (**"הבחירה שלכם ★ · הזול"**), and the step chart says no other choice leaves more (**"אחרי המדרגות האלה אין בחירה אחרת שמשאירה יותר"**).

I left the page's starting numbers as they were: ₪10,000 now and ₪500 a month. That's **"הפקדתם ₪70,000"**, and I'd end up with about **₪86,000** (₪86,045), **"אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"**. I understand that only works if the market really gives 7% a year, and the page says that 7% is just an example (**"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"**).

**How sure am I:** about 70% on the account and the fund, less on the amount. Three things hold me back:
- The big box says **"נשאר לכם בעוד 15 שנים"**, but everywhere else it says 10 years (120 months). I'm not sure which is right.
- I'm 24, and the page says the banks' young-people discounts are **"לא בחישוב"** (not counted). My own bank might be cheaper for me than it looks.
- The fund's result vs the index is based on only **"שנה אחת"** (one year).
