# Study fund page: what I took from it, as a 24-year-old on my phone

## 1. What I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd join the fund, put it in the S&P 500 option, and not touch it for 6 years.

- **When I can take it out:** The top of the page answers this: "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס". I'm about 90% sure on this part.
- **How much I'd have:** The calculator example is someone who puts in "300 ₪ בחודש" while the employer adds "900 ₪". That's about my salary, if I've worked out right that 300 is 2.5% of 12,000. If they take the money out after 5 years, about "66,5..." stays with them. The number was cut off behind the round up-arrow button. I'm only about 50% sure of this, for three reasons:
  - That number is for leaving early, after 5 years. Leaving early is the thing I'm not going to do.
  - I couldn't find a number for 6 years. The bars only get taller.
  - The example isn't my salary, and I can't type into a screenshot.
- **The scary part:** The orange box says "47% מהכסף שחייב במס הקרן מעבירה ישירות למס הכנסה". That convinced me not to take anything out early.

- **"מה זה"**: I used it. It was clear: the employer adds money, I add money, and after 6 years it's tax-free. "לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים" doesn't matter to me at 24.

  - The "שכירים / עצמאים" switch was clear: I'm שכיר.
  - The four boxes made sense except "דמי ניהול" (0.54%). I guessed it's a fee.
