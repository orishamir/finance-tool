## 1. My answer

I'd open a securities account (**"חשבון ניירות ערך"**) at **Psagot (פסגות)**. Every month I'd buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, number **5139282**.

With the page's starting numbers (10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year), it says I'd end up with about **86,492 ₪**: **"נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"** (what's left after fees and tax, in today's money). I didn't change the sliders because 500 a month sounded about right. I'm not sure I have 10,000 to start with, though.

How sure am I?
- **Which account and fund: fairly sure, maybe 7 out of 10.** Psagot plus this fund is at the top, marked **"★ הזול"** (the cheapest). The runner-up is only **"‎−43 ₪"** worse, so even if I pick slightly wrong, it barely matters.
- **The 86,492 ₪: not sure at all.** The 7% is just an example (**"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"**, an example return, not a forecast). There's also a condition, **"אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל"** (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel), so the real result could be quite different.
- **One thing that nags me:** I'm 24, and several banks say **"מחיר אחר לצעירים"** (a different price for young people). Those prices are marked **"לא בחישוב"** (not included). So Leumi or Mizrahi might actually be cheaper for me, and I can't tell.
