I'd open an account at **Psagot (פסגות)** and buy the **IBI tracker fund on TA‑125, fund number 5112628**. The page says I'd end up with about **₪86,000**, though I'm only about 70% sure of the choice, and the number is just an example.

## 1. My answer

- **Where:** Psagot. It's #1 in the list and marked "★ הזול" (the cheap one).
- **Which fund:** "אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125", number 5112628. It sits at the top of "כל הקרנות על מדד ת"א-125" and is marked "הבחירה".
- **How much:** "86,045 ₪ נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום". That's from the "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪" I put in.

**How sure I am:**
- **Psagot:** fairly sure. It's top of the list and also wins in the steps chart lower down ("הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪").
- **The fund:** fairly sure. The second‑best pair is only "‑1,030 ₪" behind, so it's not a huge deal anyway.
- **The final number:** not sure at all. It rests on "תשואה בשנה 7%", and the page itself says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". Also, I didn't type in my own amounts. I left 10,000 now and 500 a month.
- **What could change the answer:** I'm 24, and several banks show "לא בחישוב · מחיר אחר לצעירים". So Leumi, Discount or Hapoalim might actually be cheaper *for me*, and the page doesn't tell me.
