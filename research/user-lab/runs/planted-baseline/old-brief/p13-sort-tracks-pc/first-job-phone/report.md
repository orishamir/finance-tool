# Choosing an investment savings fund from the page my friend sent

## 1. My answer

I'd open the **הראל** fund with the track **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** (קופה 13414). I'm about 60% sure about the company. I'm more sure about the track, around 80%.

**Why this track:** "S&P 500" is the only thing in the list I'd heard of before. On the page these tracks have some of the best numbers, about 13–14% a year over 3 years and about 12% over 5 years.

**Why הראל:**
- Most companies have an S&P 500 track, and their returns are almost the same (13.3%–13.9%). So I decided the thing to compare was the fee, **"דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון"**.
- הראל charges 0.56%, which is one of the lowest.
- It shows both a 3-year and a 5-year return (13.7% and 11.92%).
- It's big: **"2.3 מיליארד ₪"**. I took that to mean lots of people trust it.

**Runner-up:** **מנורה מבטחים** charges 0.52%, which is cheaper. But its 5-year number was cut off between screenshots, so I went with the one I could see completely. If the 0.04% difference matters, maybe I should pick מנורה. I can't tell if it does.

- **"מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדה"**: two different fees? Which one actually costs me more?

- **"עוד לא דווח"**: does a new track with no fee shown mean it's risky?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול"**: so is my real return the number minus the fee, about 13.7% − 0.56%?
