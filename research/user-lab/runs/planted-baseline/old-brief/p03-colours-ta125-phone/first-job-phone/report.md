I'm 24, reading this on my phone from the screenshots my friend sent.

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd open an account at **פסגות** and buy **"אי.בי.אי. מחקה ת"א 125 (4A)"**, number **5112628**. I'd put in ₪500 every month.

I didn't change the starting numbers: ₪10,000 now, ₪500 a month, 7% a year, 10 years. With those, the page says I'd put in **₪70,000** in total and end up with **₪86,045**: "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום".

I'm fairly sure this is the cheapest pair, maybe 7 out of 10. The star and "הזול" on both the account and the fund made the choice easy.

I'm much less sure about the ₪86,045 itself. The page says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית", and I have no idea whether 7% is realistic. Also, I'm 24, and several banks show "מחיר אחר לצעירים", but those prices are marked "לא בחישוב". My own bank might be cheaper for me than the page shows.
