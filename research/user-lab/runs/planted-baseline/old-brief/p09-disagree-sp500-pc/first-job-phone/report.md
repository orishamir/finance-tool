pc; 75 s, $0.31

## 1. My answer

I'd open a securities account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, fund number **5139282**, every month. That's the box at the top marked **"הבחירה שלכם · הזול ★"** ("your choice · the cheapest").

With the page's sample numbers (10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year), I'd put in **70,000 ₪** and end up with about **86,500 ₪**, which the page says is **"בכסף של היום, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס"** (in today's money, after fees and tax). I couldn't type in my own amounts because these are screenshots, so I used the defaults.

**How sure am I?**
- **Pretty sure about Psagot as the place.** It's first in the "כל החשבונות" list and costs **1,057 ₪** over 10 years, while Leumi costs 4,541 ₪ and some others over 20,000 ₪. The gap is big and easy to see.
- **Fairly sure about the fund, but it hardly matters.** The next option is only **"‎−43 ₪"** worse ("הזוג הבא בתור", the next pair in line). One thing bothers me: the fund line says **"שנה אחת"** (one year), so it only has one year of history.
- **Not sure about the final amount.** The bottom of the page says **"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"** (a sample return, not a forecast). The big number also says **"אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל"** (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel). So it's really "around 86k if things go normally."
- **The final number is printed two ways.** The top card says **86,942 ₪**. The account list, the step chart and the table all say **86,492 ₪**, and 86,492 − 43 = 86,449 matches the "next pair" box. So I think 86,492 is right, but it made me trust the page a bit less.

## 3. Block by block

1. **Top menu** (מקור, מדריך, חברות ומסלולים…): skipped.
2. **Title, subtitle and index buttons:** used. The title is exactly my question. I noticed **"האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"** (the site doesn't recommend an index) and was fine with that since I already want the S&P 500.
3. **S&P 500 description:** skimmed. **"אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ"** (you can also hold it through a foreign fund) meant nothing to me.
4. **The four input boxes:** used. They matched my plan. The **אינפלציה** (inflation) button and the line about the euro central bank exchange rate were skimmed and confused me.
5. **The three result boxes:** used the most. The word **"זוג"** (pair) confused me at first. I later understood it means an account plus a fund together.
6. **"כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים, בכל אחד מהזוגות"** (what it costs you over 10 years, for each pair): mostly cut off in my screenshots. I saw the coloured bar and the line **"עלות אצלכם, לעומת הזוג השני"** (your cost compared with the other pair): account 1 ₪ more, fund 58 ₪ less, tax 14 ₪ more. Those tiny differences told me the two options are basically equal, so I moved on.
7. **Fund card (ילין לפידות):** used the name and number. I didn't understand **"נאמן"** or **"קרן מחקה"**.
8. **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500"** (all the S&P 500 funds), with filter buttons and a long list: I looked at the first two or three rows and the **לעומת הבחירה** (compared with your choice) column, then skimmed the rest. The coloured tags confused me (**צוברת, עסקת החלף, חשיפה לדולר, כמה דרכים יחד, מס גם על שינוי המטבע**). Below the funds bought in Tel Aviv, the foreign ones (iShares, Vanguard, **us-voo**, **W-8BEN**) all say **"אין מחיר כאן"** (no price here), so I skipped them.
9. **"עם נטרול מט"ח · קרן אחת"** (with currency hedging, one fund), the Harel 4A: I saw **‎−2.44%** a year and a range of **+2,212 to −23,766 ₪**. That scared me off, so I skipped it.
10. **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות"** (why some funds have no price at Psagot): skimmed. I half-understood the explanation of **"מול המדד"** (against the index).
11. **"כל החשבונות"** (all accounts, right side): used. The **"לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים, לחיילים…"** notes (not in the calculation: different price for young people, soldiers…) made me stop, because I'm 24. The two rows marked **"אין נתון"** (no data), Interactive Israel and Interactive Brokers, I skipped.
12. **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"** (what changes what you're left with the most): used the table, not the chart. **"אם תגדילו כל מספר ב־20%, הכי ישנה מספר השנים"** (if you raise each number by 20%, the number of years changes it most) was a nice, clear takeaway: staying longer matters most.
13. **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל"** (with or without currency hedging: what actually happened), with three cards, two charts and a year-by-year table: skimmed fast, and parts were cut off in my screenshots. I only kept **"השורה התחתונה: הנטרול לא מגן מירידה של השוק"** (the bottom line: hedging doesn't protect against a market drop) and **"הקרן עם הנטרול תשאיר יותר; אחרת, הקרן בלי נטרול"** (if the dollar ends up much weaker over the years, the hedged fund will leave more; otherwise, the unhedged one). My conclusion: go unhedged and stop thinking about it.
14. **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת, ורואים מה משפר אותה ובכמה"** (start from one choice and see what improves it, and by how much): skimmed, and it confused me. Why does it start from **ירושלים** with an MTF fund (79,176 ₪)? I never chose that. The three steps (+4,972 for a better account, +2,344 for a better fund) did show me that **the account matters more than the fund**. That was useful.
15. **"עכשיו: … ירושלים. נשארים בסוף 79,176"** (now: … Jerusalem. You're left with 79,176) and the lists below it: skimmed. It felt like a repeat of what I already knew.
16. **Bottom notes:** read **"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"**, **"הערכים הם בכסף של היום"** (the amounts are in today's money) and **"האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"**. Skipped the rest.

- **Account list:** I looked at the bank name, the big **נשאר** number and the small cost number under it, plus the grey **"לא בחישוב"** notes. I ignored the colours inside the orange/yellow/red bars. I only noticed that the bars get longer further down.
- **Fund list:** I looked at the fund name and the **לעומת הבחירה** column (−43, −213, −447…). I glanced at **מול המדד** (+0.37% "בשנה · שנה אחת") without really understanding it. I ignored all the coloured tags, the filter buttons and the ISIN codes.
- **Cost bar near the top:** I only saw that the pink part, **מס** (tax), is by far the biggest. That was a surprise: tax costs me more than all the fees.
- **Sensitivity chart:** I ignored the lines and used the table: the **המספר** (which number), **אחרי השינוי** (after the change) and **ההפרש** (the difference) columns. Yellow **שנים** (years) at +19,116 was the one that mattered.
- **Hedging charts:** I noticed the grey/white dollar bars go up and down and the yellow bar at **‎−21.8** in 2022. Everything else I ignored.
- **Step chart:** I looked at the labels **79,176 → +4,972 → +2,344** and the colour key at the bottom (teal = **חשבון אחר**, other account; pink = **סוג קרן אחר**, other fund type).
- **The "now" lists:** I read the green/red bars as just "more/less," and looked at **עוד/פחות** (more/less) next to them.
