# What I'd do after going through the page my friend sent

## 1. My answer

I'd open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy the fund **"אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125"**, number 5112628. The page tags this pair "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול", meaning "your choice, the cheapest". It's #1 in the account list and #1 in the fund list.

**How much I'd end up with:** the page says **86,045 ₪** after 10 years, out of the **70,000 ₪** I'd put in. It says "אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום", which I read as after fees and tax, in today's money. That's with the page's own numbers: 10,000 ₪ to start, 500 ₪ a month, 7% a year. I don't have 10,000 ₪ sitting around, so on the real page I'd change the "סכום היום" (amount today) box and my number would come out lower.

**How sure I am:**
- **Fairly sure (about 7/10) about the choice.** Every list puts Psagot plus that fund on top, and the box "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪, אי.בי.אי. מחקה ת"א 125, פסגות" ("the most across all price lists") says it directly.
- **Not sure at all about the 86,045.** The page itself says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית": the 7% is an example, not a forecast.
- **What worries me:** Leumi, Mizrahi and others have "מחיר אחר לצעירים" (a different price for young people). The page says those deals are "לא בחישוב", not counted. I'm young, so one of those banks might actually beat Psagot for me, and I can't tell.
