## 1. My answer

I'd open the **S&P 500 track at הפניקס**. On the page it's "עוקב מדד S&P500": the fee is 0.58% "מהחיסכון", 0% "מההפקדות", it made 13.31% a year over the last 3 years and 12.33% over 5 years, and it has 3.4 billion shekels in it.

Why I picked it:
- **The track.** "S&P 500" is the one thing I already knew I wanted, and it's here by name.
- **The company.** I lined up every S&P 500 track I could find. The fees are all close (0.52% to 0.71%). Of the ones with a 5-year number, הפניקס has the highest (12.33%) and the most money in it, so it felt like the safe crowd choice.
- **Backup:** הראל. Its S&P 500 track is a bit cheaper (0.56%) but returned a little less over 5 years (11.92%).
- **Cheapest I skipped:** מנורה מבטחים at 0.52%. It shows "פחות מ־5 שנים", so I couldn't see how it did over 5 years.

How sure I am:
- **S&P 500 as the track: about 6/10.** The "מניות" tracks show much higher numbers, like "24.04%" over 3 years at הראל and "26.39%" at אנליסט. That shook me, because nothing tells me why they did so much better. I stuck with what I'd heard of.
- **הפניקס as the company: about 5/10.** The S&P tracks look almost the same, and I picked mostly on small differences.

## 4. What I looked at inside each card

**Looked at:**
- The **track name** first.
- **"0.xx% מהחיסכון"**, the big fee number.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת – 3 שנים / 5 שנים"**. These were the most important numbers for me.
- **"כסף מנוהל"**, a little, as a sign of how popular a track is.

**Glanced at:**
- **"מההפקדות"**. It was 0% almost everywhere, so I stopped checking it.
- **"מניות" %**. It helped me notice that S&P is about 99–100% stocks.

**Ignored:**
- **"מט"ח" %**. I know מט"ח means foreign currency, but not why it matters to me. The S&P cards show "מניות 99%" and "מט"ח 99%" side by side, which adds up to almost 200%, and that confused me. "מניות 101%" confused me even more.
- **The round donut charts** with teal and yellow rings. I couldn't tell what they showed that the two numbers next to them didn't.
- **"קופה 15283"** and the other fund numbers with the little arrow icon. They meant nothing to me.
- **Dotted purple underlines** under the numbers. I guessed they're tappable, but couldn't tap.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדות".** Why are there two fees, and which one actually costs me more?
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים" / "פחות מ־5 שנים" in place of a number.** I figured out that the track is too young, but at first it looked like an error.
- **"עוד לא דווח" with the warning icon**, and the "חדש" label. Are those tracks risky, or just new?
- **Why S&P 500 tracks that follow the same index show different returns at different companies** (for example 11.47% vs 12.33% over 5 years).
- **Why plain "מניות" tracks show 20–26% over 3 years while S&P shows about 13%.**
- **The track names "עוקב מדדים – גמיש", "משולב סחיר" and "אשראי ואג"ח".**
- **Whether the returns shown are before or after the management fee.**

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **Fees in shekels**, e.g. "on 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, you'd pay X".
- **The worst year**, i.e. how much a track lost when markets fell. I only see averages.
- **The minimum deposit**, and whether I can set up a monthly standing order.
- **How to actually open it.** There's no button, and I don't know if I can ask for a lower fee than the one listed.
- **Whether the companies differ in anything besides the numbers**, like app quality, service, or how easy it is to withdraw.
- **A plain hint like "young, long-term saver? people usually pick…"**

I'd keep the intro, the company headers, and for each track just the name, the fee, and the 3- and 5-year returns.
