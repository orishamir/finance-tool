pc; 37 s, $0.13

## 1. My answer

I'd pick **עוקב מדד S&P 500** (track number 13414). I'm about 70% sure.

- **It's the one I've heard of.** "Invest in the S&P 500" is the only advice I know, and it's right there in the name.
- **The fee is the same as most others.** Its "דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון" is **0.56%**. Almost every row is 0.54–0.61%, so the fee doesn't really separate them.
- **It has a track record and lots of money in it.** It shows **13.7%** for "3 שנים" and **11.92%** for "5 שנים", and **2.3 מיליארד ₪** under "כסף מנוהל". Many rows only say "פחות מ־3 שנים", so there's nothing to judge them by.

What keeps me from being fully sure is the **מניות** row (8522). It looks better on every number I understand: a lower fee (**0.55%**), higher returns (**24.04%** over 3 years, **14.64%** over 5 years) and more money (**7.3 מיליארד ₪**). But the note under the table says the return "לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך", so I didn't let the bigger number decide for me. I don't understand how מניות differs from S&P 500, so I stuck with the name I know. I might ask a friend about מניות before signing.
