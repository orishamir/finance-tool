# Harel's investment savings fund page, as I read it

## 1. My answer

I'd pick **"כללי"** (General). I'm only about 5 out of 10 sure, and I wouldn't sign anything until I'd spoken to someone at Harel.

Here is why:
- I'm 61, so I don't want everything in shares. The **"מניות"** (Shares) line looks great, **24.04%** over 3 years, but its circle shows **94%** in shares. If the market fell I'd lose sleep.
- I also don't want the **"כספי (שקלי)"** (cash) line. It shows **4.25%** over 3 years and **3.08%** over 5 years, which is safe but barely beats prices going up.
- "כללי" sounds like a mix, and it has a real 5-year record: **8.26%** a year. To me that sounds steady.
- Many of the other lines say **"פחות מ־3 שנים"** (less than 3 years). That means no track record, and I don't put my savings into something with no history. That rules out "משולב סחיר", "עוקב מדדי מניות", "אג"ח סחיר" and both "עוקב מדדים" lines.
- **"עוקב מדדים - אג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"** (index tracker, bonds with up to 25% shares) actually sounds closest to what I want. But it's new, so no.

Why I'm not more sure: the "כללי" row was cut off at the top of my second picture. I could see **"8.26%"** for 5 years and **"מט"ח 19%"** (19% in foreign currency). I couldn't see its 3-year return or how much of it is in shares. And I never saw two of the 16 tracks at all, "מניות סחיר" and "קיימות". They fell between my pictures.

## 2. What I actually used

- The sentence under "הראל" at the top. It told me the fees are what was **"נגבה בפועל ב־2025"** (actually charged in 2025) and the figures are correct to **"סוף ספטמבר 2026"** (end of September 2026). Good, it's recent.
- The line **"16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"** (16 tracks, fees on savings from 0.54% to 0.61%). This told me the fees are all about the same, so fees won't decide it for me.
- The big table: track name, fees, the share/foreign-currency figures, and the returns.
- The notes under the table. The most important was **"התקרה בחוק: 1.05% מהחיסכון, 4% מההפקדות"** (the legal maximum is 1.05% of savings and 4% of deposits). So Harel charges about half the maximum, and **0%** on what I put in. Good.
- **"התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך"** (past returns, before fees and before tax, and they don't say what will happen next). Fair enough. It means the real figure in my pocket is lower.

## 4. In the table

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (the track name): always.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"** (average fees for 2025). I looked at **"מהחיסכון"** (on savings), saw they're all 0.54% to 0.61%, and stopped. **"מההפקדות 0%"** (on deposits) was reassuring.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"** (share and foreign-currency exposure). I read the **numbers**, **"מניות"** (shares) and **"מט"ח"** (foreign currency). This mattered a lot: 99% shares on the S&P 500 line, 0% on cash. I only half used the coloured rings. Green is shares and yellow is foreign currency, but I read the numbers, not the rings.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"** (average yearly return). Both the 3-year and 5-year lines.
- **"כסף מנוהל"** (money managed). I glanced at it. The "מניות" track's **7.3 billion** and the S&P 500 track's **2.3 billion** tell me a lot of people trust them. **13 million** on "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" seems small. I don't know whether that matters.

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"** (the numbers like 15283, 8522), plus the little arrow icon next to them.
- The small grey labels under some names, such as "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" and "הלכה יהודית" (Jewish law).
- The arrows **">"** on the left.
- The **"?"** circles. I probably should have clicked them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"**: I know it means foreign currency. But is 19% in foreign currency good or bad for me? Is it a risk?
- **"עוקב מדד"** (index tracker) and **"גמיש"** (flexible). Flexible how?
- **"סחיר"** (traded). Traded versus what?
- **"אשראי ואג"ח"** (credit and bonds) versus **"אג"ח סחיר"** (traded bonds). What's the difference?
- **"הלכה"**: I assume it follows Jewish law. Fine, but not for me.
- **"קופה 15283"**: is that a number I need when I sign up?
- **"123 מחושב"** and **"PF-10"**.
- Why do so many tracks have no returns at all? Are they brand new?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **So what does this mean for my money?** If I put in 100,000 shekels, what might I have in 5 years, after fees and tax?
- **How bad can it get?** What was the worst year for each track? That matters more to me at 61 than the average.
- **Can I take the money out whenever I want?** Is there a penalty? I believe there's something special at age 60, but the page doesn't say.
- **Tax**: how much, and when? It's hidden in a closed section at the bottom.
- **How much the "direct costs" actually are**, on top of the 0.54%.
- **Can I move between tracks later**, and does it cost anything?
- **How to actually open one.** There's no link or phone number for Harel.
- The **share percentage for "כללי"**, and the two rows I never saw ("מניות סחיר", "קיימות").

## 7. If half the page had to go

First to go:
1. The **"כל המוצרים של הראל"** buttons (pension, study fund, insurance for every child...). I'm not looking for those.
2. The **"במה הכסף מושקע"** buttons. They all say "1", so they just duplicate the table.
3. The **"מה כתוב במסמכים"** sections for products that aren't mine. I'd keep only the investment fund and tax ones.
4. **"לא מצאתם שם?"**
5. The **"123 מקור / 123 מחושב"** line.
6. The **"קופה"** column and the little coloured rings. The numbers next to them are enough.

**Keep:** the table (name, fees, shares percentage, returns), the line with the legal maximum, and the warning that returns are before fees and tax.
