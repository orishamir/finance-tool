# Using my friend's page, as me (24, salaried, first time investing)

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** open a brokerage account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy every month the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, fund number **5139282**.

**How much I'd end up with:** the page says **86,492 ₪**, *"נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"* ("what's left for you in 10 years, after management fees and tax, in today's money"). That's if I start with 10,000 ₪ and add 500 ₪ a month, so I'd have put in about 70,000 ₪ in total. Those were the numbers already filled in on the page. My real numbers would be different, but the order of the options probably wouldn't change.

**How sure am I:**
- **Fairly sure about Psagot plus the Yelin Lapidot fund**, maybe 7 out of 10. The page puts it first everywhere: the big card, the ranked list, and the waterfall chart that ends at *"הכי הרבה בכל המיגוונים 86,492"* ("the most of all the combinations").
- **Not sure at all about the 86,492 figure.** The page itself says *"התשואה שבחישוב היא תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"* ("the return in the calculation is an example, not a forecast"). The 7% is something I left as it was.
- **The gaps between options are tiny.** The second-best pair is only *"‎−43 ₪ לעומת הבחירה שלכם"* ("−43 ₪ compared with your choice") over ten years. So I wouldn't worry much about which of the top few I pick.
