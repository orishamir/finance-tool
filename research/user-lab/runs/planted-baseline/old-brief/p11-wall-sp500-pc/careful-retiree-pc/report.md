# What I made of the page

## 1. My answer

I would open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, fund number **5139282**. I'd put in 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ a month for ten years. That's 70,000 ₪ of my own money.

The page says I'd end up with about **86,492 ₪** — "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום", and only "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל".

**How sure I am:**
- **The account: fairly sure.** The table "חשבון אחר, אותו קרן" shows the account matters a lot. The same fund ends at 84,148 ₪ at Psagot but 61,638 ₪ at מיטב טרייד. That's over 20,000 ₪ of my money just for picking the wrong place.
- **The fund: only somewhat sure.** The runner-up, אלטשולר שחם 5139100, comes out just "‎−43 ₪" behind over ten years. That's a tie as far as I'm concerned.
- **The 86,492 ₪: not sure at all.** It depends on 7% a year, which the page itself calls "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". It also assumes the dollar doesn't move. The dollar section shows it moving between "‎−12.5% עד ‎+13.4%" in a single year. So I'd say "somewhere around 70–100 thousand, maybe less if things go badly".

One thing I'd check before signing anything: under הפועלים it says "מחיר אחר ... לנשים מגיל 60 וגברים מגיל 65". At 61 that may apply to me, and it's "לא בחישוב". The bank might come out cheaper for me than the page shows.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- What "(4D)" and "(4A)" mean.
- "עסקת החלף", "נאמן", "צוברת" vs "מחלקת דיבידנד", "W-8BEN", "מס עיזבון אמריקאי", and "עמלות זו על זו".
- "בכסף של היום": does 86,492 ₪ mean what that money would buy today? I think so, but it isn't said plainly.
- "סכום חד־פעמי ומכירה מחושבים במחיר הרגיל".
- Why more tax counts as fine ("כי נשאר לכם יותר רווח").
- The ‎+0.37% / ‎+0.65% "שנה אחת / 4 שנים" column. Harel has a better percentage than Kesem but the same ‎−447 ₪.
- What the dotted underlines on the numbers do.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **How much could I lose?** At 61, if the market drops 30% the year I turn 70, what's left? The page shows one number, not a bad case.
- Is 7% sensible for the S&P 500? Why is that the default?
- Does my age get me a better price? The Hapoalim note hints yes, but it isn't in the sums.
- How do I actually open the Psagot account and set up the monthly purchase? Is there a minimum?
- What happens if I need some of the money early?
- Is my money safe if Psagot or the fund company goes under?
- The tax: is it 25%, and is it paid only when I sell?
- "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות": so would Psagot still be first? It doesn't say.
