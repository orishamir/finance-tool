# Me (24, salaried) using the study fund page

## 1. My answer

I'd join. I'd put the money in the S&P 500 track instead of leaving it on whatever the fund picks for me, and I'd plan not to touch it for 6 years.

What I took from the page:
- **The deal:** my employer puts in 7.5% of my salary and I put in 2.5% (`"עד 7.5% מהשכר מהמעסיק ועד 2.5% מהשכר שלכם"`). For every shekel I put in, my employer adds three. That alone convinced me.
- **When I can take it:** `"אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון"`. So about 6 years from my first month at this job, or 3 years if it's for studies.
- **How much:** the calculator was already set to 12,000 ₪ a month, which is close enough to my salary. If I take the money out after 5 years, the result is `"66,513 ₪ נשאר בידיים"`. Of that, I put in 18,000 and my employer put in 54,000. Then it adds `+8,487` from investing and takes off `−13,974` tax.
- **At 6 years:** the bars from 6 onwards say `"מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס"`, and the bars keep getting taller. I didn't see an actual number for year 6. I'm guessing something around 90,000–100,000 ₪ "in today's money", but that's only from looking at the bar heights.

**How sure I am:**
- Joining and waiting 6 years: about 85% sure. It's free money from my employer.
- The exact amount: about 50% sure. The number I saw is for 5 years, it's based on a made-up 7% return, and the page itself says `"הנחות של האתר, לא תחזית"`.

## 2. Parts I actually used

- **The opening section `"מה זה"`**, mostly the paragraph about 6 years and 3 years, and the 7.5% / 2.5% split.
- **The S&P 500 chip** in the `"במה אפשר להשקיע"` row. It's the only option there I recognised.
- **The calculator `"כמה כסף נשאר לכם, ומתי?"`**: the `שכירים` button, my salary and the two percentages, the year bars 1–12, and the big bar chart ending in `"נשאר בידיים"`.
- **The line under the calculator** about the S&P 500 example: whichever company you pick, you end up with `"66,601 ₪ עד 66,280 ₪"`. That told me the company barely matters.
- **The first item under `"שווה לדעת"`**: if you don't choose a track, you get the default one. That's why I'd choose S&P 500 myself.
- **The orange warning box** about 47%. It scared me off taking the money out early.
