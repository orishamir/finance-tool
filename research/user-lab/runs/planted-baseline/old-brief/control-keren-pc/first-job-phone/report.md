# What I took from the page about the study fund

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Join the fund through my employer and pick the S&P 500 option. I saw "מדד S&P 500" as one of the choices above the calculator. I wouldn't touch the money for at least 6 years.

**Roughly how much:** The calculator's example salary is ₪12,000 a month, about what I earn, so I left it. With it:
- I put in 2.5% a month. I worked that out myself as about ₪300; the page never says it. Over 5 years that's "18,000 ₪" from me.
- The employer adds 7.5%, three times what I put in.
- After 5 years, everything together was worth about ₪80,000.

**When I can take it out:**
- If I take it out at 5 years: "לחשבון נכנסים קודם 50,719 ₪, והקרן מעבירה 29,768 ₪ למס הכנסה". Of that, I can get "15,794 ₪" back if I ask for a tax refund.
- After 6 years: "מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס", so no tax.
- The note about 3 years is only if I use it for studies, or if I'm close to retirement age.

**How sure:**
- About the 6-year rule: very sure. The page says it many times.
- About the amounts: medium. Roughly ₪65–80k after 5–6 years "in today's money", but I'm not sure which number is what actually reaches my bank.
