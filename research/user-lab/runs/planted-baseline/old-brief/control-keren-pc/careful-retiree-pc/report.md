# Study fund (keren hishtalmut): what I took from the page, as a careful 61-year-old

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

**I would join.** If my contract includes it, the employer adds 7.5% of my salary on top. My share is only 2.5%. In the big bar chart the yellow bar ("המעסיק הוסיף", the employer added) is about three times the size of the grey bar ("הפקדתם", you deposited). Saying no would mean turning down free money.

**Roughly how much:** I have no salary figure from the employer yet, so I used the page's example of ₪12,000 a month. Over 5 years:
- I put in **₪18,000** (the only number printed on the bar chart).
- After 5 years the page says "לחשבון נכנסים קודם ₪50,719, והקרן מעבירה ₪29,768 למס הכנסה" (first ₪50,719 reaches your bank account, and the fund sends ₪29,768 to the tax office). So about ₪80,500 was in the fund, and over a third of it went to tax. That shocked me.
- "אם מבקשים החזר ממס הכנסה, ₪15,794 מהם חוזרים" (if you ask the tax office for a refund, ₪15,794 of it comes back). So I end up with about **₪66,000 in today's money**, and only if I remember to file for the refund.

**When I can take it out:** I'm not sure about this, and it's the part that matters most to me. The page says "אחרי 6 שנים... אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס" (after 6 years you can take the money out with no tax). But it also says "למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים" (for someone who has reached retirement age, 3 years are enough). At 61, which rule is mine? That depends on when I reach retirement age, and the page never says what that age is. So it's 64 if I'm already at retirement age by then, otherwise 67. Until I know, I'd plan not to touch the money before 6 years.

**How sure:** About 80% sure that joining is right. About 50% sure about the date. On the amounts I'm only sure of the rough size, because the example isn't my salary.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **"מה זה"** (what is it), at the top: the 7.5% / 2.5% split, the 6-year rule, the 3-year rule, and the ₪15,712 ceiling.
- **"מה קורה לכסף בקרן"** (what happens to the money in the fund): the calculator, the 12 small bars, the big bar chart, and the text under it.
- **"מיסוי"** (tax): the "משיכה לפני 6 שנים" list (withdrawing before 6 years) and the yellow-orange warning box.
- **"משיכה ומעבר"** (withdrawal and transfer): only the card that says money arrives "עד 4 ימי עסקים" (within 4 business days).

- **"גיל הפרישה"** (retirement age): what age is it, for me?

- **The "123 מקור / מחושב" (source / calculated) markers, the KH-19-style codes, and the "חוקים וכללים · 2" (laws and rules · 2) badges.**
- **"מסלול כללי" vs. "אג"ח ממשלתית צמודה" vs. "מדד S&P 500"** (general track vs. index-linked government bonds vs. the S&P 500 index): which is safer for someone my age?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **The 6-year number.** The page shows 5 years (with tax) in detail but not 6 (no tax), and 6 is the one I'd actually choose.
