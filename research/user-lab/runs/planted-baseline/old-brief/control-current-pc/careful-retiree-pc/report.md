# What I made of the page about my current account (חשבון עובר ושב)

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**What the account costs me.** The page says every bank must offer a cheap package, "המסלול הבסיסי". It includes one teller action and up to 10 online actions a month, for "עד ₪10.00 בחודש". Anything over the package is charged "לפי התעריפון המלא", meaning at full price. So my first step is to ring the bank or look in the app and check that I'm on the basic or extended package, not paying per action. I'd also note two dates:
- From 01.10.2026, immediate-debit card fees are capped at "₪7.00 בחודש". That has already started.
- From 01.07.2027 the packages end. Instead there's a cap by how many actions I do: ₪5 for up to two actions, ₪10 for up to a hundred, then ₪1 for each action over a hundred. That's fine for someone like me.

**How much to keep in it.** The only rule I could find is "כסף שעשוי לצאת היום נשאר בעו"ש, כסף שלא צריכים באותו יום לא חייב לשבת שם". In plain terms: keep what might go out, and anything else doesn't have to sit there. The example frightened me. ₪10,000 left for 10 years is "₪8,203 בכסף של היום: ₪1,797 פחות", and the "קרן כספית יומית" (a daily money fund) would have made ₪2,576 more. So I'd keep about one or two months of bills plus a cushion, and move the rest. First I'd ask my bank whether it's one of the "4 מתוך 8" that pay interest on the balance.

**How sure I am:** fairly sure about the fees, maybe 70%. About how much to keep, only about 50%. The page never gives me a number. The ₪15,000 it mentions is when the bank has to write to me, not advice.

## 2. Parts I actually used
- The top section, "מה זה": the paragraphs about fees and packages.
- The start of "כמה כסף להחזיק בעו"ש": the line about money going out today, and the ₪15,000 notice.
- The calculator box, "כמה עולה להשאיר כסף בעו"ש?", especially the three big numbers under the chart.
- The rows in "שווה לדעת" about which banks pay interest (Discount, up to 3%).
- "סיכונים ומה לא מוגן": there is no deposit insurance.

## 3. Block by block
- **Menu across the top** (מקור, מדריך, חברות ומסלולים…): skipped. I didn't need to go anywhere else.
- **Title and the one-line summary under it:** used. It's clear: a current account doesn't always pay interest.
- **The little legend "123 מקור · 123 מחושב · מונח הקישו להסבר"** (source, calculated, tap a term for an explanation): it confused me. I could see the underlines come in different colours, but I couldn't tell which numbers were facts and which the site worked out itself.
- **"מה זה"** (what it is): used and read twice. It was the most useful part for costs. The little tags "BANK-17", "BANK-35" and "חוקים וכללים · 5" meant nothing to me, so I ignored them. The small orange circles beside ₪5.00, ₪10.00 and ₪1.00 worried me. Is something wrong with those numbers? Nothing said.
- **"כמה כסף להחזיק בעו"ש"** (how much to keep in it): used, but my screenshots jump. I never saw how this section begins, and it's the very section I needed. The button "במה אפשר להשקיע: מזומן שקלי" (what you can invest in: shekel cash) I didn't understand, so I skipped it.
- **The calculator box:** used. The sentence on top ("אחרי 10 שנים, ₪10,000 בעו"ש שווים ₪8,203") is the clearest thing on the page. I didn't move the slider; 10 years and ₪10,000 is close enough to my case. The fold-out "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם" (play with your own numbers) I left closed, and the other one, "חלק מהבנקים כן משלמים ריבית…", I didn't open either.
- **"הכי קרוב לזה… קרן כספית יומית"** (the closest thing: a daily money fund): read it. Useful, it tells me where the extra money could go.
- **"שווה לדעת"** (worth knowing): skimmed. The interest row was useful. "מוצרי שוק הכסף: פיקדונות, קרנות כספיות ומק"מ יחד" (money-market products together) is jargon, so I skipped it. Part of this section is also missing between my screenshots.
- **"סיכונים ומה לא מוגן"** (risks and what isn't protected): read it carefully, and it made me nervous. "אם בנק קורס, אין חוק שמחזיר לכם את הכסף" means if a bank collapses, no law gives me my money back. A real reason not to leave a big pile there.
- **"מונחים" table** (glossary): skimmed. Nothing new.
- **Footer:** I only noticed "נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026", so the figures are a day old. Good.

## 4. Charts and tables
- **Chart:** I looked at the legend ("בעו"ש" in the pale line, "בקרן כספית" in yellow, "המחיר של להשאיר" as the brown shaded area) and the left-hand scale (7 to 12 thousand). Mostly I used the three numbers under the chart: 8,203 / 10,779 / 2,576. I ignored the year marks (היום, 2, 4…10) and the dashed line "הסכום שהתחלתם בו" (the amount you started with). I read the note that the vertical scale doesn't start at zero, so the gap looks bigger than it is. The small print says 2% inflation and 3.25% for the fund are "הנחה של האתר, לא הבטחה ולא תחזית", the site's own guess. I took that seriously.
- **Glossary table:** I read the "מונח" (term) and "מה זה" (what it is) columns. I ignored the "איפה מופיע" (where it appears) column and its little labels (אפליקציה, דוח שנתי, תעריפון…).

## 5. What I didn't understand
- What the orange circles beside the 2027 prices mean.
- What the codes "BANK-17", "BANK-35" and so on are.
- "רוב הבנקים משלמים בעו"ש 0%: 4 מתוך 8". Four out of eight is half, not most. That made me trust the numbers a bit less.
- "המסלול המורחב": I understood up to 10 to 50 actions, but not its price.
- "מזומן שקלי" and "מק"מ" (Treasury bills).
- The "חוק משנת 2026" (the 2026 law) and the private bill guarantee of up to ₪250,000. I understood the main thing: my current-account balance isn't covered either way.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find
- **A simple answer to how much to keep.** One month's spending? Three? The page never says.
- **The price of the extended package**, and how to check which package I'm on.
- **My own bank by name.** Does it pay interest on the balance, and on what conditions? Only Discount is named, and the full table is behind the link "לטבלה".
- **Whether a daily money fund is safe** for someone my age, and how quickly I can get the money out.
- **What fee the money fund itself charges.**

## 7. If half the page had to go
First to go: the "123 מקור / מחושב" legend, the codes and the "חוקים וכללים" (rules and laws) tags, the "איפה מופיע" column, the slider with the "שחקו עם המספרים" fold-out, and the "מוצרי שוק הכסף" row. Next I'd cut the glossary table. The definitions repeat what I'd already read above. I would keep "מה זה", the calculator's top sentence with its three numbers, and the risks section.
