pc; 48 s, $0.17

I read the page the way I would at home, after work, coming from my friend's screenshots.

## 1. My answer

**What the account costs me:** at most **10 ₪ a month**, if I'm on the cheap plan. That comes from "המחיר של המסלול הבסיסי מוגבל בצו: עד 10.00 ₪ בחודש". The cheap plan covers "1 פעולה בפקיד ועד 10 פעולות בערוץ ישיר בחודש". I think that means one action at the bank counter and 10 in the app. There's also a bigger plan, but I couldn't work out what it costs (see point 5). The rules are changing too: the card fee will be capped at 7 ₪ from 01.10.2026, and from 01.07.2027 the plans go away. After that it's "עד 5.00 ₪" for up to two actions a month, "עד 10.00 ₪" for 3 to 100 actions, plus 1 ₪ for each action over 100. So whatever happens, I shouldn't be paying much more than 10 ₪ a month.

**How much to keep in it:** the page says "כסף שעשוי לצאת היום נשאר בעו״ש" and "כסף שלא צריכים באותו יום לא חייב לשבת שם". The calculator showed that 10,000 ₪ sitting there for 10 years is worth only "8,203 ₪ בכסף של היום", which is "1,797 ₪ פחות". In something called "קרן כספית יומית" it would be 10,779 ₪. My plan:
- Keep about one month of spending in the current account.
- Move the rest to a "קרן כספית יומית".
- Ask my bank which plan I'm on, and switch to the basic plan if I'm paying more.

**How sure I am:**
- **Fairly sure** about the 10 ₪ cap.
- **Fairly sure** that big amounts shouldn't just sit there.
- **Not sure** how much is "enough" to keep. "One month of spending" is my own guess; the page doesn't give a number.
- **Not sure** how to actually put money into a קרן כספית, or whether it's safe.
