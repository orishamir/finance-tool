# Picking an S&P 500 savings fund from the screenshots, as a 24-year-old beginner

## 1. My answer

I'd go with **Menora Mivtachim (מנורה מבטחים), the track called "עוקב מדד S&P500"**. It's on screen 12.

- **Why:** People keep telling me "invest in the S&P 500," and this page has a filter chip called "עוקב מדד S&P 500 · 12", so I went looking for that kind of track at each company. Of all the S&P 500 tracks I found, Menora's has the lowest number in the "החיסכון" (savings) line: **0.52%**. The others are 0.54%–0.71%. Its 3-year return, **13.79%**, is about the same as everyone else's S&P 500 track (13.3%–13.9%), so the cheapest one looked like the obvious choice.
- **Runner-up:** Harel (הראל), "עוקב מדד S&P 500" on screen 2, at **0.56%**. It's the only S&P 500 track where I could see a 5-year number (11.92%), and it holds the most money, "2.3 מיליארד ₪". To me that means lots of people trust it.
- **How sure:** About 60%. The gap between 0.52% and 0.56% is tiny. On 10,000 ₪ it's about 4 ₪ a year, if I'm reading it right. Menora also has a small "0.01% ההפקדה" (deposit) fee and Harel has 0%, and I don't know whether that matters. A friend could easily talk me into Harel.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- **The intro line at the top** (under "כל המסלולים בכל החברות"). It told me the fees are what was "נגבה בפועל ב־2025" (actually charged in 2025).
- **The "מוצר" (product) buttons**, just to check that "קופת גמל להשקעה 143" was selected.
- **The "במה הכסף מושקע" (what the money is invested in) chips**, where I spotted "עוקב מדד S&P 500 · 12".
- **The company header rows**, e.g. "מנורה מבטחים · 12 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 3.8 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.52% עד 0.67%". Those told me which company I was looking at.
- **In each row:** the track name, the "מהחיסכון" fee, and the 3-year return.
- **The note at the bottom:** "התשואה היא תשואת עבר… היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך, ולכן אין מיון לפיה" (past returns don't tell you what will happen, so we don't sort by them). That's what pushed me to choose on fees and not on returns.
