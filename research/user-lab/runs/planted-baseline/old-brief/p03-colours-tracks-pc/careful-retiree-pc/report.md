# Choosing a kupat gemel lehashkaa from my friend's screenshots

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**My choice: Harel, track "כללי", fund number 8211.**

I'm about **60% sure**. I'd phone Harel before I sign anything.

Here is why:
- **It costs the least.** It charges "0.55% מהחיסכון" (0.55% of my savings each year) and "0% מההפקדות" (nothing on what I deposit). For every 100,000 ₪ I put in, I'd pay about 550 ₪ a year. More's "כללי" track charges 0.72%, which is about 720 ₪.
- **It did as well as or better than the others in the past.** It shows "13.5% 3 שנים" and "8.26% 5 שנים". That's the best 5-year figure of all the "כללי" tracks I found. Analyst had 8.13%, More 8.13%, Infinity 8.06%, Meitav 8.02%, Migdal 7.96%, Menora 7.78% and Yalin Lapidot 7.61%. The difference is small, though.
- **It's big and has been around a long time.** It manages "6.7 מיליארד ₪". Harel is at the top of the list as the biggest company, with "כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪".
- **It's middle of the road.** About half is in shares: "מניות 49%". At 61, I don't want the all-shares tracks ("מניות", "עוקב מדד S&P 500"), even though they show 20–26% a year over 3 years. They look tempting, which is exactly why I don't trust myself with them.

**Why only 60%:** I'm not sure "כללי" is right for someone my age. I could have taken the safer Harel "אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)" track, fund 8521. It charges 0.58% and shows 8.6% over 3 years and 4.73% over 5 years. The page never told me which of the two suits someone who is 61.

## 2. Which parts of the page I actually used

- **The top heading "כל המסלולים בכל החברות"** and the two lines under it. They told me the fees are from 2025 and the other numbers are from the end of September 2026.
- **The product button "קופת גמל להשקעה 143"**, which was already highlighted.
- **The line "החברות מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה לפי הכסף המנוהל; בתוך כל חברה לפי דמי הניהול מהחיסכון, מהזול ליקר".** This line was the key to the whole table.
- **The grey company header rows**, for example "הראל · 16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". These were the most useful part of the page.
- **The rows named "כללי"** in each company. I went down the page comparing just those.
- **The note at the very bottom:** "התשואה היא תשואת עבר כמו שהרשות מפרסמת אותה: לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך". This was important, and I wish it had been at the top.

- **Top menu ("מקור", "מדריך", "חברות ומסלולים", "השקעה במדד", "חוקים וכללים"): skipped.** I didn't know what "מקור" was. "מדריך" (guide) might have helped, but it wasn't in the screenshots.

- **The heading and its two lines: used.** They were clear enough.
- **"123 מקור 123 מחושב · לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא": confused me.** 123 of what? I couldn't tell what it meant for my money, so I moved on.
- **The product buttons ("תגמולים ואישית לפיצויים 182", "קרן השתלמות 154" and so on): skimmed.** I only checked that mine was the highlighted one.
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה": I wanted to click it** but couldn't, because these are screenshots.
- **The search box "שם המסלול או החברה": skipped.**
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" (what the money is invested in) with 16 buttons: skimmed, partly confused.** "כללי 12" was useful because it told me there are 12 general tracks to compare. I didn't know what "עוקב מדדים - גמיש", "הלכה יהודית", "קיימין" or "הלכה איסלאמית" meant.
- **The summary line "143 מסלולים של 12 חברות · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.5% עד 0.73%": used.** It told me the cheapest and dearest are not that far apart.
- **The big table (screens 1–15): used, but only parts of it.** It's very long, and I scrolled through 14 screens of it.
- **The last company's rows, where nearly everything says "חדש", "עוד לא דווח" and "פחות מ־3 שנים": skipped.** There's nothing to compare there. I also never saw that company's name, because its header row wasn't in the screenshots.
- **"עוד לא דווח: השדה ריק בנתוני הרשות": used it to understand the orange marks,** but only after I'd already worried about them for several screens.
- **The note about past returns: used.** See above.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 7" and the footer links ("פרמטרים", "מסמכים", "העברה למכשיר אחר"): skipped.**
- **"נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026": glanced at.** It's reassuring that the data is recent.

## 4. The table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (the track name). I mostly searched for "כללי" and "אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)".
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025", only the top line "מהחיסכון"** (the fee on my savings).
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"** (average yearly return, before fees). I used both the 3-year and 5-year numbers, but trusted the 5-year one more.
- **"כסף מנוהל"** (money managed), as a rough sign of how solid the track is.
- **In "חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח", only the "מניות" number,** to see how much of my money would be in shares.
- **The grey company rows,** for size and fee range.

**What I ignored:**
- **The circles (the turquoise rings).** I couldn't tell what a fuller or emptier ring meant, so the percentages beside them did the job.
- **The "מט"ח" line.** I think it's foreign currency, but I don't know whether 19% is good or bad for me.
- **"מההפקדות"** (the fee on deposits). It's almost always 0%, so I stopped reading it.
- **The "קופה" numbers with the little arrow icon.** I only wrote down 8211.
- **The arrows "<" on the left of each row.** I couldn't click them.
- **The dotted underlines under numbers.** They're probably clickable, but I couldn't click.
- **The small tags "הלכה יהודית" and "חדש".**
- **The sort arrows in the column headers.**

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"**, and why it sits in the same column as shares.
- **"מניות 101%"** (Meitav "מניות", and Harel's "מניות סחור"). How can more than 100% of my money be in shares? Is that borrowing? It worried me.
- **The difference between "מניות" and "מניות סחיר", and between "אג"ח" and "אג"ח סחיר".** "סחיר" was never explained.
- **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש".** Flexible how?
- **Why the 3-year return is so much higher than the 5-year one** (13.5% against 8.26%). Did something bad happen 4 or 5 years ago? Could it happen again?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול ולפני מס"** (before fees and before tax). So what do I actually get? Roughly 8.26% minus 0.55%, and then tax on top? The page never says what lands in my pocket.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025".** If it's an average, will I pay the same 0.55%, or could they charge me more?
- **"123 מקור / 123 מחושב".**
- **Why the companies aren't in fee order.** More is second on the list but more expensive than Harel, Analyst or Infinity. It took me a while to realise the order is by size.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which track suits a 61-year-old,** or how much risk is sensible at my age.
- **How much the track lost in its worst year.** That matters more to me than the average.
- **Tax.** What happens when I take the money out? I've heard something about a tax benefit after 60 if you take it as a monthly pension, but there's not a word about it here.
- **Whether I can take money out whenever I want, and how quickly.**
- **Whether I can switch tracks or companies later, and whether that costs anything.**
- **How much I'm allowed to deposit each year.**
- **Whether I can bargain the fee down,** and whether 0.55% is the most I'd ever pay.
- **Service.** Will anyone answer the phone, and are there complaints?
- **The fees in shekels** on my actual amount, rather than percentages.
- **The name of the last company,** the one with all the "חדש" rows, and the companies whose header rows fell between screenshots.
- **What "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" says.**

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The tracks marked "חדש", "עוד לא דווח" and "פחות מ־3 שנים".** That's a whole company and a good part of the others, and there's nothing in them I can compare.
2. **The rings.** The numbers next to them already say it.
3. **The "מההפקדות" line.** It's almost always 0%.
4. **The "123 מקור 123 מחושב" line.**
5. **Most of the 16 "במה הכסף מושקע" buttons.** I'd keep "כללי", "מניות", "אשראי ואג"ח" and "הלכה".
6. **The other product buttons at the top** ("קרן השתלמות", "קרן פנסיה" and so on). I came for one product.

The note saying past returns are before fees and tax, and don't predict the future, should **move to the top, not go**.
