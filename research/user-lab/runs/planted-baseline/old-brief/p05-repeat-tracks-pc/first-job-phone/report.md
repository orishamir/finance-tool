# p05-repeat-tracks-pc: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 65 s, $0.39

## 1. What I'd do and how sure I am

I'd open the **הראל, "עוקב מדד S&P 500"** fund (Harel's S&P 500 tracker, fund number 13414).

- It's the "S&P 500" thing I've heard I should invest in, so I looked for that phrase in the track names first.
- The fee is low: **"0.56% מההיסכון"** (0.56% of my savings each year) and **"0% מההפקדות"** (nothing taken from my deposits). Most other S&P 500 rows cost about the same or more. Mor charges 0.71%.
- It has real history: **"13.7%" for 3 years and "11.92%" for 5 years**. Some cheaper ones only say "פחות מ־5 שנים" (less than 5 years).
- It manages **"2.3 מיליארד ₪"** (2.3 billion shekels), and Harel is the first company on the page. Big felt safe to me.

My runner-up is **מנורה מבטחים "עוקב מדד S&P500"**, the cheapest one I saw at **0.52%**. It also takes **0.01%** from deposits, and it only has a 3-year return (13.79%). Phoenix (0.58%, 3.4 billion, 12.33% over 5 years) was close too.

How sure: about 6 out of 10. I'm fairly sure I want an S&P 500 track. I'm not sure the company matters. The S&P rows all look almost the same, and I can't tell if 0.52% vs 0.56% is a big deal or a few shekels.

- The big table: the track name, **"דמי ניהול"** (management fee), **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"** (average yearly return) and **"כסף מנוהל"** (money managed).
- Each company's grey header line, for example **"הראל 16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מההיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%"** (16 tracks, money managed, fee range).

## 4. The table: what I looked at and what I ignored

- **"המסלול" (track name) and the company under it:** Looked at, first thing in every row.
- **"קופה" number (13414 etc.) with the little arrow icon:** Ignored. I don't know what I'd do with it.
- **"דמי ניהול" top line, "ההיסכון":** Looked at closely. It was my main comparison.
- **"דמי ניהול" bottom line, "ההפקדות":** Glanced. It's almost always "0%", so I stopped looking, except where Menora had "0.01%" and Infinity "0.05%".
- **The orange "!" with "עוד לא דווח":** Noticed it, and treated those rows as "skip". Migdal's S&P row has it on the deposit fee only, which made me a bit unsure about Migdal.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח" (exposure to stocks and to foreign currency), with the teal/yellow ring:** Mostly ignored. For S&P rows I checked it said about 99–100% stocks, which made sense. I don't know why foreign currency matters to me, or why Harel's "מניות" (stocks) track has only 25% foreign currency while the S&P has 99%. The ring itself told me nothing the numbers didn't.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת לפני דמי ניהול" (yearly return, before fees):** Looked at closely, both the "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" lines. My eye jumped to the biggest numbers: Altshuler's "עוקב מדדים - גמיש" at **26.39% / 15.37%**, Meitav's "עוקב מדדי מניות" at **25.86% / 17.25%**, Harel "מניות" at **24.04% / 14.64%**. Those beat the S&P, and I almost switched. I stayed with the S&P because I only understand that one, and because of the bottom note.
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים" / "פחות מ־5 שנים" (less than 3 / 5 years):** Treated as "too new, no proof".
- **"כסף מנוהל" (money managed):** Looked at as a "how popular / safe" sign: billions good, "0 מיליון ₪" scary.
- **Tags ("חדש", "הלכה יהודית", "עוקב מדדי אג"ח", "אג"ח סחיר"):** Ignored, except "חדש" as a warning.
- **Dotted underlines and "?" icons:** Noticed. I couldn't click them in a screenshot.
- **Sort arrows in the header:** Noticed the little ▲ next to "ההיסכון". I assume that's the fee sort.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"** (foreign currency). I know the word but not why it's on this page or whether high or low is good.
- **"אג"ח", "אשראי ואג"ח", "אג"ח סחיר", "אג"ח ממשלתי"** (bonds, credit and bonds, tradable bonds, government bonds). I couldn't tell how these differ.
- **"גמיש"** (flexible). Flexible how?
- **"עוקב מדדים"** (tracks indexes) vs **"מניות"** (stocks). Is one a robot copying an index and the other a person choosing stocks?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול"** (before fees). So what do I actually get? Do I subtract 0.56% myself?
- **"ההיסכון" vs "ההפקדות"** (savings vs deposits). I kind of guessed: a % of everything I have each year vs a % of each new deposit. I'm not sure.
- **"123 מקור / 123 מחושב"** (123 source / 123 calculated).
- **Why the same "S&P 500" has a different return at each company** (13.31% vs 13.79%) if they all copy the same index.
- **"101%" stocks** in Migdal's "מניות סחיר". How do you have more than 100%?
