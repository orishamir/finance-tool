**1. What I'd do, and how sure I am**

I'd join. The employer puts in 7.5% of my salary and I put in 2.5%. In the example, that's ₪900 a month from them and ₪300 from me. I'm quite sure joining is right, because most of the money isn't mine.

I'd plan to leave it **6 years**, which takes me to 67. The page says "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון". The orange box frightened me off taking it out early: "במשיכה לפני הזמן, 47% מהכסף שחייב במס הקרן מעבירה ישירות למס הכנסה".

On the actual amount I'm only about 50% sure. The page's example is ₪300 + ₪900 a month, which means a ₪12,000 salary, and that isn't my salary. In that example, if you take it out after 5 years:
- the fund sends **₪50,719** to your bank account first, "והקרן מעבירה ₪29,768 למס הכנסה";
- you only get **₪15,794** of that back if you ask the tax office for it ("אם מבקשים החזר");
- so in the end you're left with about ₪66,500.

The bars for 6 years and more are taller and say "פטור ממס", but they have no numbers on them. So for my real case I'd guess "roughly ₪80–90 thousand at that salary", but that's my guess, not the page's figure.
