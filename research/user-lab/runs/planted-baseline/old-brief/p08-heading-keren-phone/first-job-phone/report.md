# What I took from the page about the keren hishtalmut

## 1. My answer

I'd join it. The calculator uses 300 ₪ a month from me and 900 ₪ from the employer (**"₪300 בחודש מכם, ₪900 מהמעסיק"**). Most of the money comes from the employer, so saying no would feel like turning down free money.

**When I can take it out:** the page says **"אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון"**, meaning after 6 years I can take it all with no tax. Before that I can still take it, but I pay tax. If it's for studies, 3 years can be enough (**"גם על כסף שמושכים כדי לשלם על לימודים מספיקות 3 שנים"**). I didn't know about the studies option.

**How much money:** for 5 years the calculator says **"נשאר בידיים"** about **66,5…**. The rest of the number is hidden behind the up‑arrow button. The text under it gives **₪66,601 עד ₪66,280**, so about 66,000 ₪. I only get that much if I ask the tax office for money back. Otherwise the account gets **₪50,719** and **₪29,768** goes to tax. If I wait past 6 years, the bars get taller and the pink tax part disappears (**"מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס"**). I couldn't see the exact amount for 6 or 10 years in the screenshots.

**How sure I am:**
- **Fairly sure (around 80%)** about the "6 years and no tax" rule and that I should join.
- **Not very sure** about the amount. The numbers come from the site's guesses (**"הנחות של האתר, לא תחזית"**), and I don't know if 2.5% / 7.5% are my real percentages. I'd need to check my contract.

- The top summary under **"קרן השתלמות"**: "save through work, after a set period you can take it out without tax."
- The **"מה זה"** section: the employer adds money, I add some, 6 years, 3 years for studies.
- The calculator under **"מה קורה לכסף בקרן"**: the bar chart for years 1–12, the five bars below it, and the paragraph that starts **"משיכה אחרי 5 שנים"**.
