# What I made of the study-fund page (as myself, 61, on my phone)

## 1. My answer

**I would join, and I'm fairly sure about that.** The calculator's example says "300 ₪ בחודש מכם, 900 ₪ מהמעסיק". Put simply, for every shekel I put in, my employer adds three. I don't need an expert to tell me to take that.

**When I could take the money out:**
- The main rule is "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון". For me that means about age 67.
- Then the next sentence: "למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים." That one matters most to me. If I reach retirement age soon, I might wait only 3 years, until about 64.
- The page never says what retirement age is, so I don't know whether it's 3 years or 6 years for me. I'm only about 50% sure on this.

**How much money:**
- The only real number I could follow was for 5 years: about **66,500 ₪**.
- But I'd only get "50,719 ₪" at first, because "הקרן מעבירה 29,768 ₪ למס הכנסה", and "15,794 ₪" comes back only "אם מבקשים החזר".
- The bars after year 6 are taller, but no number is written on them. My guess is 80–90 thousand after 6 years, but that's only my guess from bar heights.
- **What it means for my money:** roughly 65–90 thousand shekels, available somewhere between age 64 and 67. Don't touch it early.
