# p02-percent-keren-phone: first-job-phone (old brief)

phone; 72 s, $0.35

Here's how I got on with it, answering as myself, a 24-year-old who just got this job.

## 1. My answer

I'd join and leave the money alone for **at least 6 years**. The line I held onto from the very top was "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס". There's also a note that if I need the money for studies, "מספיקות 3 שנים".

**How much:** The page worked out an example of "₪ 300 בחודש מכם, ₪ 900 מהמעסיק". If I took the money out after 5 years, the bar marked "נשאר בידיים" said about **66,5..** (the round "up" button covered the last digits). The tax bar took "−13,974". On the 1–12 chart, years 6 and up are under "מ־6 שנים: פטור ממס" and the bars keep growing. By year 12 the bar looks about three times as tall as year 5. So in 6 years I think I'd have roughly 80–100 thousand shekels. The page said "כל הסכומים בכסף של היום", so that's in today's money. I never saw the exact year‑6 number, because the marker was sitting on 5.

**How sure:** I'm pretty sure about "6 years, or else tax". On the amount I'm only about 50/50. I don't know if my employer really pays 7.5% and I pay 2.5%. And the "7%" is "הנחות של האתר, לא תחזית".
