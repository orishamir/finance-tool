# What I made of my friend's page, as a 61-year-old who wants to know what it means for my money

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Open a securities account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", fund number 5139282**. That's the fund without currency hedging, the one marked **"בלי נטרול מט"ח"**.

**How much I'd end up with:** About **86,492 ₪**. That assumes I put in 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, which is 70,000 ₪ of my own money in total. The page says the figure is **"אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"** (after fees and tax, in today's money). It also says **"אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל"** (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel).

**How sure I am:**
- **Which bank and fund:** fairly sure. Everything I read points the same way.
- **The 86,492 ₪ figure:** not sure at all. It depends on a 7% yearly return, and at the bottom the page itself says **"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"** (an example return, not a forecast). It also depends on the dollar staying put, and the dollar chart shows it moved as much as **"−12.5%"** in a single year.
- **Whether my choice is clearly the best:** I'm only half sure. The runner-up pair is just **"−43 ₪"** worse over ten years, so it's practically a tie. Worse, at the bottom it says **"בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות"** (at a 0% or 10% return, the order changes in 6 places). So "the cheapest" depends on a guess.

Before doing anything, I'd phone Psagot and ask what they would really charge me.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **The 7%: is it before or after inflation?** It says **"בכסף של היום"** (in today's money), and there's a separate **"אינפלציה"** button. So is 86,492 ₪ what it buys today, or not? This is the most important question for my money.
- **"(4D)" and "(4A)"** at the end of the fund names. I have no idea.
- **"עסקת החלף"** (a swap) on my chosen fund. Is it risky? Is it a bad thing?
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"** (tax on the currency change too). Will I pay tax even if the fund didn't really earn?
- **"צוברת"** against **"מחלקת דיבידנד"** (accumulating vs paying out dividends).
- **"נאמן 0.02%"**: what a trustee is, and why it's paid separately.
- **"עכשיו: … ירושלים"**: why does the page think I'm "now" at Bank Jerusalem with an MTF fund? I'm not anywhere yet.
- **The table with +41 ₪, −3,164 ₪ and +779 ₪:** a plus on a cost? A minus on a fund cost? I couldn't work it out.
- **"העמלות זו על זו"** (fees on top of each other), the grey colour in the legend.
- **"הפסד נתחתך: הסכום ארוך מהשטח"**: I didn't understand this one at all.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **I'm 61.** Hapoalim says it has a different price **"לנשים מגיל 60 וגברים מגיל 65"** (for women from 60 and men from 65), but it's **"לא בחישוב"** (not in the calculation). Would that make Hapoalim cheaper for me? Do the other banks have something for older people?
- **What if the market falls just when I need the money, at 71?** The page only talks about a good average year.
- **Is a securities account even the right place for me?** What about a **קופת גמל להשקעה** (investment provident fund) or my existing pension? The page only compares bank accounts.
- **How do I actually do it?** Opening the account, setting up the monthly purchase, and whether it's a **"הוראת קבע"** (standing order). Leumi mentions **"מחיר להוראת קבע"**, a price for standing orders.
- **Is my money safe if Psagot or the fund manager gets into trouble?**
- **What happens if I need the money after 5 years?**
- **How much is that 86,492 ₪ before tax**, and how much tax will I really pay?
