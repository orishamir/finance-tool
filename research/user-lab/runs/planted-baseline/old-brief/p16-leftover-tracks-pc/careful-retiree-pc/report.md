# Choosing a kupat gemel lehashkaa from my friend's screenshots

## 1. My answer

**I would go with Harel (הראל), in the "כללי" track, fund number 8211.** I'm about 60% sure of the company and the track. I'm less sure that "כללי" is the right amount of risk for someone my age.

Why:
- **It costs the least of all the "כללי" tracks.** The fee is "0.55% מהחיסכון" and "0% מההפקדות". The others run from 0.56% to 0.72%. If I put in 100,000 ₪, I worked out that 0.55% is about 550 ₪ a year. Mor's "כללי" at 0.72% would be about 720 ₪. That's real money every year, for the same kind of track.
- **Its past returns were the best, or near the best, of the "כללי" tracks.** It shows "13.5% 3 שנים" and "8.26% 5 שנים". The other "כללי" rows are mostly 12–13% and 7.6–8.1%.
- **Harel is the biggest company on the page, and this fund is big.** The header says "16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪", and this fund alone manages "6.7 מיליארד ₪". A big, old fund makes me less nervous than one marked "חדש".

What makes me hesitate: "כללי" is about half in stocks ("49% מניות"). At 61 I don't want to lose a big piece in a bad year. If my nerve fails, my second choice is Harel's "אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)" (fund 8521, 0.58%). It's safer, but it earned less: "4.73%" over 5 years against "8.26%". The page doesn't tell me which one suits my age, so I'd still want to ask someone.

I would not pick a stock-only track like "מניות" or "עוקב מדד S&P 500". Their returns look exciting ("24.04%" over 3 years), but they are 94–99% stocks, and that's too much for me now.

- **"סחיר"** ("מניות סחיר", "משולב סחיר"). How is "משולב סחיר" different from "כללי"?
- **"עוקב מדדים - גמיש"** (flexible index tracking). Flexible how?
- **"קיימי 2".** I have no idea what that is.
- **"פחות מ־3 שנים" in the return column.** I think it means the fund is too young to have a record, but it looks like a return figure.
- **"123 מקור 123 מחושב" and "TODO".** Both unclear, as above.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **What should someone aged 61 choose?** Even a simple line like "the closer you are to needing the money, the less in stocks" would help.
- **How bad was the worst year for each track?** That's my real fear, and there's nothing about it.
- **What's left for me after fees and tax?** In plain shekels, for example on 100,000 ₪.
- **The tax rules.** I've heard that after 60 there's a tax advantage if you take the money as a monthly pension (annuity). Is that true, and does it matter which company I choose?
- **Can I bargain on the fee?** The page shows an average, so some people must pay less.
- **How do I open the account?** What's the minimum, and is there a limit on how much I can deposit each year?
- **How do I withdraw, and how long does it take?**
- **What is service like at each company?** Do they answer the phone?
- **How does this compare with a bank deposit (pikadon)?** The page says nothing about that.
- **Which company owns the rows on screens 7, 8, 11 and 14–15?**
