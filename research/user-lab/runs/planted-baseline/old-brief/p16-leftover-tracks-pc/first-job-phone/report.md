## 1. My answer

I'd open the **Menora Mivtachim (מנורה מבטחים) fund, track "עוקב מדד S&P500"** (fund number 13874). I'm about 60% sure.

My reasons:
- I came in wanting the S&P 500, and the page has a track called "עוקב מדד S&P 500" (S&P 500 tracker) at almost every company. I only looked at those rows.
- All the S&P rows show nearly the same 3-year return, roughly "13.3%" to "13.9%". So I figured it's the same thing at every company, and the only real difference is the fee.
- Menora's row has the lowest fee I saw: "0.52% מהחיסכון" (from savings). Its 3-year return, "13.79%", is also near the top.

My backup is **Harel (הראל), "עוקב מדד S&P 500"** (13414). It charges "0.56%", shows a 5-year number ("11.92%"), and manages "2.3 מיליארד ₪" (2.3 billion shekels). Menora's row says "פחות מ־5 שנים" (under 5 years) for the 5-year figure, which makes me slightly nervous. There's also a row (14865) at "0.54%", but I couldn't see which company it belongs to.

I'm not more sure for two reasons. Menora also takes "0.01% מההפקדה" (from the deposit), which I don't really understand. And I don't know whether a 0.04% difference matters at all.
