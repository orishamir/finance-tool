# What I made of the study fund page

## 1. My answer: what I would do, and how sure I am

I would join the fund my new employer offers. I would plan **not to touch the money for 3 years**, not 6.

The sentence that decided it for me was in the "מה זה" ("what is it") part: **"אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס הכנסה ובלי מס רווחי הון. למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים."** That means after 6 years from the first deposit you can take the money out without income tax or capital gains tax, and if you have reached retirement age, 3 years are enough. I'm 61, so I'd reach retirement age within those years. For me that means around age 64–67 I could take it all out and pay no tax.

On **when**, I'm moderately sure, maybe 6 out of 10. The page never says what "גיל הפרישה" (retirement age) actually is. I also can't tell whether the 3 years only start counting once I reach that age.

On **how much**, I'm not sure at all, maybe 2 out of 10. The page never gave me a figure in shekels for someone like me. My best guess is "about 10% of my salary each month, mine and my employer's together, plus or minus whatever the investments do." That guess rests on a line that was half cut off at the top of screen 2, something about "7.5%" and "2.5%". I'd ask payroll for the real percentages, which the page says are **"כתוב בהסכם העבודה"** ("written in the employment agreement").

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **The title and the line under it:** "חיסכון... שאחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בלי מס" ("savings you can withdraw tax-free after a period set by law"). That told me what this thing is.
- **"מה זה" ("what is it"):** the 6 years / 3 years paragraph, and that the employer puts money in "בנוסף לשכר הברוטו" (on top of the gross salary).
- **"משיכה לפני 6 שנים: מה משלמים שכירים"** ("withdrawing before 6 years: what employees pay"). Here I learned that **"למי שמתחת לגיל 60 זה לפחות 31%; מגיל 60 המדרגות מתחילות נמוך יותר"** (under 60 it's at least 31%, and from 60 the brackets start lower). That's good news for me if I ever need the money early.
- **"משיכה ומעבר" ("withdrawal and transfer"):** **"עד 4 ימי עסקים"**. Once I ask for the money, it reaches me within 4 business days.
- **"כמה זה עולה" ("how much it costs"):** "עד 2% בשנה" (up to 2% a year), but in practice "0.34% עד 0.74%". So the fees are small, and **"דמי יציאה... לא גובים"**: no exit fee.
- **"סיכונים ומה מוגן" ("risks and what's protected"):** **"הפסד בהשקעות הוא שלכם... והמדינה לא ערבה לו"**. Any investment loss is mine and the state doesn't guarantee it. At my age that matters a lot.
