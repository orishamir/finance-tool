phone; 53 s, $0.31

## 1. My answer

I'd open an account at **פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ** and put the money every month into **אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125**, the one with the number **5112628**.

I kept the numbers the page started with: 10,000 ₪ now, then 500 ₪ a month for 120 months. That's 70,000 ₪ of my own money (**"הפקדתם 70,000 ₪"**). The page says I'd end up with about **86,045 ₪**, **"אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"**. So I'd gain roughly 16,000 ₪.

How sure am I?
- **About where and what to buy: fairly sure, maybe 7 out of 10.** The big card at the top (**"הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול"**) said this, and further down **"הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪"** gave the same answer. That made me trust it. The next-best option is only **"1,030 ₪ − לעומת הבחירה שלכם"** worse, so even if I'm a bit off it doesn't matter much.
- **About the 86,045: not very sure.** The 7% is only an example (**"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"**), so the real amount could be quite different.
- **One thing that could change my mind.** I'm 24, and many banks show **"לא בחישוב — מחיר אחר לצעירים"**. Maybe Leumi or Discount is cheaper for someone my age, and the page didn't work that out for me.
