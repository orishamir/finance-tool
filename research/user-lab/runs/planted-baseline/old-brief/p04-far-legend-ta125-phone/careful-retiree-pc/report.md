## 1. My answer: what I'd do, and how sure I am

**What I'd do:** I'd open a securities account with **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy **"אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125"**, fund number **5112628**. I'd put in 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ every month.

**What I'd end up with:** the page says **86,045 ₪** after 10 years, after fees and tax, "בכסף של היום" (in today's money). I would have put in 70,000 ₪ of my own. So the gain is about 16,000 ₪, and only if the market really grows 7% a year. The page says that 7% is "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית", an example and not a forecast.

**How sure:**
- **Fairly sure about the account.** Psagot was clearly cheapest. It costs 1,049 ₪ over 10 years, while my kind of bank costs 4,500–10,500 ₪. That gap is big enough that I believe it.
- **Less sure about the fund.** It wins by only about 1,000 ₪ over 10 years, roughly 100 ₪ a year. Beside it I read "שנה · שנה אחת", which I take to mean it only has one year of history. A fund with a one-year record makes me nervous. If it turned out to be new or risky, I'd happily take **"תכלית סל (40) ת"א 125"** and give up the 1,030 ₪.
- I'd still phone Psagot before sending any money.
