# What I made of the study fund page (keren hishtalmut), as a 61-year-old

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**I would join the fund and leave the money alone until I'm about 65 to 67.** I'm fairly sure about joining. I'm much less sure about the exact amount.

Why I'd join: for every 1 shekel I put in, the employer puts in 3. The page's example uses 2.5% from me and 7.5% from the employer: "קרן השתלמות במסלול מדד. 300 ₪ בחודש מכם, 900 ₪ מהמעסיק". I can't turn that down.

**How much:** a rough rule I worked out for myself is that 10% of my salary goes in each month. After 6 years, that's about 7 months' salary, plus a bit of growth. The page's example after 5 years ends at "66,5…" in the white column "נשאר בידיים". That's with 300 + 900 a month, and it's already after tax. If I wait the full 6 years there's no tax. From the height of the bars I'm guessing around 90,000 to 100,000 shekels, but that's only my guess. I couldn't type my own salary into the screenshots, so these aren't my numbers.

**When I can take it out:** "אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך… בלי מס". And "למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים". I start at 61, so 6 years takes me to 67. If I've reached retirement age by then, the 3-year rule may let me out sooner, but the page never says what retirement age is. So, roughly 65 to 67.

**What would stop me taking it early:** "לפני 6 שנים: מס". The warning box says "מהכסף שחייב במס הקרן מעבירה 47% ישירות למס הכנסה". I only get part of that back "רק אם מבקש החזר ממס הכנסה". I'm not chasing the tax office for my own money.

## 2. Which parts I actually used

- The top: the title and the grey line under it, "…שאחרי תקופה שקבע החוק אפשר למשוך בלי מס".
- **"מה זה"**: especially the 6 years / 3 years paragraph.
- **"מה קורה לכסף בקרן"**: the calculator with the bar charts, and the text right under it.
- The rules about taking money out before 6 years in the tax part. One line there spoke to me directly: "למי שמתחת לגיל 60 זה לפחות 31%; מגיל 60 המדרגות מתחילות נמוך יותר". I'm over 60, so that's good for me.
- The orange warning box with the 47%.
- **"סיכונים ומה מוגן"**: "הפסד בהשקעות הוא שלכם… והמדינה לא ערבה". At my age that matters a lot.

## 4. Charts: what I looked at and what I ignored

**The 1–12 years chart:**
- **Looked at:** the year numbers along the bottom, the circle on "5", and the dashed line splitting "לפני 6 שנים: מס" (pink) from "מ-6 שנים: פטור ממס". I also compared how tall the bars are.
- **Ignored or didn't understand:** which colour is which. There's no explanation on that chart, so I only worked it out from the chart below. I didn't see the pink stripes on the small bars until I looked twice. I also couldn't drag the marker, because they're screenshots.

**The 5-column chart:**
- **Looked at:** the labels underneath and the numbers on top: "+54,000" (employer), "+8,487" (return), "−13,974" (tax, the pink striped one), and "66,5…" (white, "נשאר בידיים"). Only the white column truly interested me.
- **Ignored or missing:** the number for "הפקדתם" got cut off, so I worked it out myself (300 × 60 = 18,000). The "66,5…" was hidden under the round up-arrow button. I didn't look at the grey stepping that links the columns.

**Underlined numbers (dotted and dashed):** I ignored them. I didn't know if they were links.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **The biggest one:** the chart says "מושכים את הכסף אחרי 5 שנים", but the text says "משיכה אחרי 6 שנים: … הקרן מעבירה 29,768 ₪ למס הכנסה". So is there tax after 6 years or not? Everything else says that after 6 there's none. I assume it means 5 years, but it made me doubt the numbers.
- "למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים": 3 years from when? And do I need to already be at retirement age when I start, or when I take the money out?
- "תשואה בשנה 7" and "דמי ניהול 0.54": are these good or bad? I have no idea.
- "על הרווח הזה: עד 25% מהחלק שמעל האינפלציה". I don't understand this.
- "הפקדה מוטבת", "ותק", "מסלול מדד": jargon.
- What "חוקים וכללים · 3" is supposed to give me.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **One sentence for someone my age:** "if you start at 61, you can take the money out at age __". It isn't there.
- What retirement age actually is. Is it the same for men and women?
- **My number with my salary.** The salary box was cut off, so I don't know what salary the example uses.
- Whether a 61-year-old should pick a safer track than the S&P 500 example. The page says the loss is mine but doesn't say what's appropriate at my age.
- What happens if I leave this employer after 2 to 3 years. Does the money stay, and does it keep growing?
- What happens to the money if I die. Who inherits it?
- The number for year 12, or at least year 6, written out. I had to guess from the bar height.
