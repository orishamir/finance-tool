## 1. My answer

I'd join. The thing that sold me is in the calculator: I put in `300 ₪ בחודש` and the employer puts in `900 ₪`, so they add three times what I do. I'd pick the S&P 500 option, because that's the one name I recognised under `במה אפשר להשקיע`, and I'd leave the money alone for 6 years.

**How much:** the page's example takes out the money after 5 years. Roughly 80,000 ₪ builds up. If I take it out early, `לחשבון נכנסים קודם 50,719 ₪`, and if I ask for a tax refund I end up with about `66,5..` ₪ (the number was cut off). After 6 years there's no tax. The bars there look taller, but I never saw a number for them, so my guess is "a bit over 80,000".

**When:** `אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה`, or after 3 years if it's for studies.

**How sure:**
- Joining and waiting 6 years: fairly sure.
- The amounts: not sure. They're for an example salary (I think 12,000 ₪, it was cut off at the top), not mine. And the screenshots don't let me move the slider to year 6.
