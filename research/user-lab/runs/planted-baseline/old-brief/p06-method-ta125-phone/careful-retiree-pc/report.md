# What I'd do with my money, after going through all 13 screenshots

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Open a securities account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy the fund **"אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125"**, number **5112628**. I'd put in 10,000 ₪ at the start and 500 ₪ every month. Those are the page's example amounts, not ones I chose, and I couldn't change them in screenshots.

**How much I'd end up with:** The page says **"86,045 ₪ נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"**, which means 86,045 ₪ after fees and tax, in today's money. I'd have paid in **70,000 ₪** ("הסכום ההתחלתי ועוד הפקדה חודשית, 120 חודשים"), so that's about 16,000 ₪ more than I put in.

**How sure I am:**
- **Which account and fund: fairly sure, about 75%.** The page says it plainly: **"הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪, אי.בי.אי. מחקה ת"א 125 (4A), פסגות"** and **"אחרי המדרגות האלה אין בחירה אחרת שמשאירה יותר"**. Nothing else leaves more.
- **The 86,045 ₪ figure: not sure at all.** The page itself says **"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"**: the 7% is an example, not a forecast. The figure only holds if the market really earns 7% a year.
- **The gaps are small.** The second-best pair is only **"1,030 ₪ לעומת הבחירה שלכם"** behind. A normal bank like הפועלים would leave 81,052 ₪, about 5,000 ₪ less over 10 years. That's worth having, but it isn't life-changing.
