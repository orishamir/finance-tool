# What I'd do with my S&P 500 money, from the screenshots my friend sent

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** I'd open a securities account at **Psagot** (פסגות). Every month I'd buy the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, number **5139282**.

**How much I'd end up with:** I put in my own numbers: 10,000 ₪ to start (סכום היום), 500 ₪ a month (הפקדה חודשית), 10 years, 7%. Over the 10 years I'd pay in **70,000 ₪**. The page says I'd keep about **86,492 ₪**: "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום". So I'd be about 16,500 ₪ ahead in today's money.

**How sure I am:**
- **Fairly sure about the choice.** It's the cheapest pair on the page. The fund shows "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים" and only "0.02% נאמן". The Psagot account would cost me "1,057 ₪" over 10 years. At my own bank it could cost 5,000 to 10,000 ₪.
- **Not sure about the 86,492.** The same box says "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל", and the dollar always moves. The 7% is "תשואה לדוגמה", only an example. Lower down, the hedged fund shows a range of "בין +2,212 ₪ ל־‎−23,766 ₪". So the real result could be well above or well below 86,000.
- **The top choices are nearly a tie.** The next pair is only "‎−43 ₪" behind. I'd stop worrying about which fund and just pick the cheap account.
- **One worry:** this fund's record is short. It says "בשנה · שנה אחת", one year only, while others show "4 שנים".

1. **Top menu** (מקור, מדריך, חברות ומסלולים…): skipped.
2. **Title and index buttons:** used. I clicked S&P 500. I liked "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד", because nobody is pushing me.
3. **The S&P 500 description:** read it. "הכסף מושקע בדולרים" made me nervous straight away.
4. **The four input boxes:** used, and they were easy.
5. **The grey line with the "אינפלציה" button:** confused me. Why would "שער הייחוס של הבנק המרכזי האירופי" matter to an Israeli putting shekels into an American index?
6. **The three result boxes:** used. This was the most useful part of the page.
7. **"כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים, בכל אחד מהזוגות":** I only saw the bottom of this coloured bar chart. The one line I took from it was "החשבון 1 ₪ יותר, הקרן 58 ₪ פחות, המס 14 ₪ יותר". It told me the top two are basically equal.

9. **Fund list "כל הקרנות":** used the first five or so rows and skimmed the rest. I ignored the coloured filter buttons ("עסקת החלף 5", "חשיפה לדולר 19"…).
10. **Foreign funds (iShares, Vanguard, us-ivv, us-voo):** skimmed, then gave up. They all say "אין מחיר כאן" and "עוד לא נאסף". "מס עיזבון אמריקאי" and "צריך טופס W-8BEN" frightened me. At my age, an American estate tax is not something I want to discover later. I'd stay away from these.
11. **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות":** skimmed. It was too technical.
12. **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם", chart and table:** used the table and skipped the chart. The table gave me a useful lesson: two more years add 19,116 ₪, and 100 ₪ more a month adds 14,474 ₪. Time and the monthly amount matter more than choosing between funds.
13. **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קורה בפועל":** read the headline numbers ("‎−12.5% עד +13.4%", "‎−21.8% ב־2022") and the "השורה התחתונה" paragraph. I skipped the two bar charts and the yearly table. My conclusion: hedging is not protection, and it's a gamble either way.
14. **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת, ורואים מה משפר אותה ובכמה":** confused me. It starts from "עכשיו: S&P 500 (4D) של MTF, ירושלים, 79,176 ₪". I don't hold MTF and I've never heard of Jerusalem Bank. Why is that "now"? The table also has **two columns both called "פסגות"**, with numbers like "‎−5,416 ₪" and "0 ₪". I couldn't follow it.
15. **The two "change one thing" lists** (חשבון אחר, אותו קרן / סוג קרן אחר…): skimmed. They only confirmed Psagot is at the top.
16. **Small print at the bottom:** read it. "התשואה שבחישוב: תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" is honest. But "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות" worried me: does the ranking change if the market does badly?

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"(4D)" and "(4A)"** in every fund name.
- **"עסקת החלף", "צוברת", "קרן מחקה" vs "קרן סל", "כמה דרכים יחד", "נאמן 0.02%"**: is the trustee fee a charge to me?
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"**: do I pay tax even when the dollar moves and I made no real profit?
- **"נטרול מט"ח"**: I worked out roughly what it is from the section, but the word itself means nothing to me.
- **Whether the 7% is before or after inflation**, given that the results are "בכסף של היום".
- **Why the "עכשיו" box starts from MTF at Jerusalem Bank.**
- **The two "פסגות" columns** in the step table.
- **"קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו"**: different from what?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Is Psagot safe?** If Psagot goes bust, is my money protected? This matters most to me.
- **How do I actually do it?** How do I open the account, and can I set up an automatic 500 ₪ every month?
- **What does the monthly purchase cost?** Is there a fee on each 500 ₪ buy?
- **What does Hapoalim really charge someone my age?** That price is "לא בחישוב".
- **What happens to the money if I die, and what about the American estate tax?**
- **How much could I lose in a bad decade?** I'm 61 and will be 71. I'd like a "bad case" number for my money, not only the 7% example.
- **Can I take money out early** if I need it, and what does that cost?
