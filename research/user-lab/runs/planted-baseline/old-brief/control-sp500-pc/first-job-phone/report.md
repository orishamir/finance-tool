# control-sp500-pc: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 65 s, $0.28

## 1. My answer

I'd open a securities account (חשבון ניירות ערך) at **פסגות** and buy **ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)**, fund number **5139282**, every month.

The page used example numbers: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year. I didn't change them. With those numbers I'd put in **70,000 ₪** in total and end up with about **86,492 ₪**, which the page calls "בכסף של היום". As I understand it, that's after all fees and tax, if "הדולר לא זז מול השקל".

**How sure am I?**
- **The account (פסגות):** fairly sure. It's #1 in the list, the box says "הזול ★", and the bottom section agrees.
- **The fund:** fairly sure too, but the next pair is only "−43 ₪" worse over 10 years. So the fund choice barely seems to matter.
- **The final amount:** not very sure. The page itself says the 7% is "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". The amount also assumes the dollar doesn't change, and the dollar's own chart moves a lot from year to year.
- **What bothers me most:** I'm 24, and many banks say "מחיר אחר לצעירים" but are marked "לא בחישוב". So the cheapest place for someone my age may not be the one shown. Before signing up I'd check what my own bank offers young people.
