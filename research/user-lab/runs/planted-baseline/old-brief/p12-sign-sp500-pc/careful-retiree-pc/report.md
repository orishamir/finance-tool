# What I made of the page my friend sent

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Open a securities account at **Psagot (פסגות)**. Buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, fund number **5139282**. Put in 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ a month for 10 years.

**How much I'd end up with:** about **86,492 ₪**. I put in 70,000 ₪ in total (*"הפקדתם 70,000 ₪"*). The page says the 86,492 is *"אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום"*, so it's after fees and tax, in today's money. That's roughly 16,500 ₪ more than I put in, measured in today's prices.

**How sure I am:**
- **Fairly sure about Psagot.** It came out top, and the account costs only *"1,057 ₪"* over 10 years. At Leumi it's 4,541 ₪, and at the bottom of the list it's over 26,000 ₪. That gap is real money.
- **Only half sure about the fund.** The next one, Altshuler Shaham 5139100, ends just *"‎−43 ₪"* lower. Forty-three shekels over ten years is nothing, so it's a tie. My fund also shows *"שנה אחת"* (one year) of history, while others show *"4 שנים"*. It looks new, which makes me a bit nervous.
- **Not sure at all about the 86,492.** I typed nothing in; the page started with 7% a year. At the bottom it says *"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"*: an example, not a forecast. In 2022 the index fell 18.5% in dollars, so I treat 86,492 as "if things go averagely".
