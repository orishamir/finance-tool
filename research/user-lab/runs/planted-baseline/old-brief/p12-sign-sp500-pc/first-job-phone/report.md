# What I'd do with my monthly S&P 500 money, using the page my friend sent

## 1. My answer

**I'd open an account at Psagot (פסגות) and buy the "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" fund, number 5139282.** The page marks this pair with a star: "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול" (your choice, the cheapest).

**How much I'd end up with:** I left the page's starting numbers as they were: "סכום היום" ₪10,000, "הפקדה חודשית" ₪500, "שנים" 10, "תשואה בשנה" 7%. With those, I'd put in **₪70,000** and end up with about **₪86,492**. The page says that's after fees and tax and "בכסף של היום" (in today's money). It also says it only holds "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel).

**How sure am I?**
- **Fairly sure about the choice.** The first box and the "מנקודת ההתחלה ועד הכי הרבה" chart near the bottom both point to the same pair.
- **Not sure about the final number.** The 7% is the page's guess, not a promise. The bottom of the page says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" (example return, not a forecast).
- **Two things could change my choice.** First, the next pair in line is only "‎−43 ₪" worse over 10 years, which is nothing. Second, I'm 24, and under Leumi (לאומי) it says "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים" (not in the calculation: a different price for young people). Maybe Leumi is cheaper for me, and the page didn't count it. I'd check that before opening anything.
