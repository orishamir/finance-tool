I would **open an account at Psagot ("פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ")** and **buy the fund "אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125", number 5112628**.

**So what does this mean for my money?** The page already had 10,000 ₪ as the starting amount and 500 ₪ a month filled in. I left them as they were. I'm counting that as roughly 16,000 ₪ of real gain.

**How sure am I?**
- **About which account and fund: fairly sure, maybe 7 out of 10.** Both lists put them at the top, and the "steps" chart lower down agrees: "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪, אי.בי.אי. מחקה... פסגות".
- **About the 86,045 ₪: not sure at all.** The page itself says the 7% is "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". So it's only an example.
- **The winning margin is small.** The next pair gives 85,015 ₪, only "‎-1.2%". Leumi comes in at 82,968 ₪. If my own bank is easier for me, I'm only giving up a few thousand shekels over ten years. That's worth knowing, but it isn't scary.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts

- **Account list:** I looked at the **name**, the **big number "נשאר"** and the **small number under it** (the account's cost). I mostly ignored the **orange, yellow and red bars**. The colour key was further up the page and I'd already forgotten it.
- **I didn't know what the purple dotted underlines under every number mean.** Do I tap them? I didn't.
- **Fund list:** I looked at the **name**, the **number** and the **‎-0.04%**. I ignored the pink and grey labels ("מחזיקה את המניות", "קרן סל", "כמה דרכים יחד"). On my phone they were also cut off.
- **Lines graph:** I looked at the **colour names on the right** ("שנים", "הפקדה חודשית"). I ignored the axes, "50% פחות" and "50% יותר". **The table under it was easier for me.**
- **Steps chart:** I looked at **the numbers above the bars** (+5,022 ₪, +1,113 ₪). I ignored the colours and the zigzag line.

## 5. What I did not understand

- **"(4A)"**, **"MTF"**, **"ETF"**, and the difference between **"קרן מחקה"** and **"קרן סל"**.
- **"נאמן 0.025%"**: who is this trustee, and why do I pay them?
- **"העמלות זו על זו"** and **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג"**.
- **"לעומת הבחירה ‎-1,030 ₪"** next to a fund that's ‎-0.28% a year: what is that compared with?
- **"קרנות בחו"ל שהתעריפון שלהן נקבע לפי מספר המניות מחולק למחיר יחידה"**: this went completely over my head.
- **"יש ספק אם נמכרת שם: וואן זירו"**: can I buy it there or not?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Can I set up an automatic monthly purchase at Psagot?** For Leumi it says "מחיר להוראת קבע", but for Psagot it says nothing. For me that's the whole point.
- **How do I open the account, and how safe is my money there?** What if Psagot gets into trouble? Is Psagot a bank or not?
- **What happens if I need the money before the ten years are up?** And how is the tax actually paid: do I have to report it myself?
- **What happened in bad years in the past?** What if the market falls just before I'm 71?
- **What does it cost to move money from my current bank to Psagot?**
