# What I'd do with my savings after reading the page my friend sent

## 1. My answer

I'd open the **הראל** fund in the **"כללי"** track (general). It's fund number **8211**, the card at the top of screen 3.

How sure am I? About 60%. I wouldn't sign anything until I've phoned them. Here's my reasoning:

- **Harel is the biggest company and among the cheapest.** The company heading says "16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪", with yearly fees "מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". No other company manages more money. Only two companies have a lower bottom fee, and theirs are only slightly lower.
- **Its "כללי" track did best of all the general tracks I could find.** It shows "13.5%" for 3 years and "8.26%" for 5 years. The other general tracks were around 12–13.6% and 6.4–8.1%. It also holds "6.7 מיליארד ₪", so plenty of people trust it.
- **I can't see its own fee.** The card was cut between screenshots 2 and 3. Harel's tracks are sorted cheapest first, and this one sits between a 0.54% track and a 0.55% track, so I'm guessing it's about 0.54–0.55%. I'd still have to ask.
- **What the fee means for my money:** on 100,000 ₪, 0.55% is about 550 ₪ a year. מור's general track charges "0.72%", which is about 720 ₪. That's roughly 170 ₪ a year I keep, every year.
- **My worry:** I'm 61, and "כללי" doesn't say how much is in shares. The other companies' general tracks have about half: "מניות 49%", "52%", "53%". If I need this money in the next few years, I'd choose Harel's safer track instead: **"אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"**. It costs "0.58%" and returned "8.6%" over 3 years and about "4.73%" over 5 years (that number was half cut off).

## 4. What I looked at inside each card, and what I ignored

**What I looked at:**
- **Fee, "X% מהחיסכון".** Always. This is the one I understand: it's taken from my money every year.
- **"מניות" with the green dot.** Yes. It's my risk gauge: more shares means a bigger fall in a bad year.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת — 3 שנים / 5 שנים".** Yes, the 5-year one more. I'm wary of the very high numbers like "26.39%" or "25.86%". That's shares in a good stretch, not something I can count on.
- **"כסף מנוהל".** Yes, as a sign of how many people trust the track. The S&P tracks have "99%" in both green and yellow, which already tells me they're all shares.

**What I ignored, or skimmed:**
- **"0% מההפקדות".** Glanced, then ignored. It's 0% almost everywhere. Even "0.16%" (הפניקס, משולב סחיר) is only 160 ₪ on 100,000 ₪, and I'm putting in a lump sum.
- **"מט"ח" with the yellow dot.** Ignored. And it confused me: "99% מניות" plus "99% מט"ח" adds up to nearly 200%. How?
- **The ring chart next to the numbers.** Ignored. Teal and yellow rings in a little circle tell me nothing that the two numbers don't.
- **"קופה 15283" with the arrow.** Ignored. I suppose it's a fund ID I'd need when I phone them.
- **The grey tags ("עוקב מדדי אג"ח", "אג"ח סחיר").** Ignored.
