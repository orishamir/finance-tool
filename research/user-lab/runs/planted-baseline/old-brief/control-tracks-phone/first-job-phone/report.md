### 1. My answer

I'd go with **הפניקס, the track "עוקב מדד S&P500"** (קופה 13250). I'm about 6 out of 10 sure.

- I came in wanting "the S&P 500", and the page has a track with that name at almost every company. So I only compared those tracks.
- Phoenix's has the best "5 שנים" number I found, **12.33%**. Its fee is **0.58%**, which is close to the cheapest. It also has the most money in it, **"כסף מנוהל 3.4 מיליארד ₪"**, which makes me feel it's a safe, popular choice.
- The runners-up were Harel (0.56%, 11.92% over 5 years), Clal (0.59%, 12.01%), Migdal (12.07%) and Meitav (0.6%, 11.94%).
- Altshuler Shaham has the cheapest fee, **0.54%**, but it only says **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**. I read that as "too new to show a result", so I passed.

Why only 6/10: all the S&P tracks look almost the same to me. I can't tell if 0.58% versus 0.56% matters in real shekels. I'm also not sure whether a 5-year past number tells me anything about the next 20 years.

- A minimum deposit, and how to actually open one. Can I do it online, from the company's site?
- Can I take the money out? When? Is there tax? Why choose this over just buying the S&P myself?
