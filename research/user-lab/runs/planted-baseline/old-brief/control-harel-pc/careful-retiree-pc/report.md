# control-harel-pc: careful-retiree-pc (old brief)

pc; 46 s, $0.15

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would pick **"אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"**, fund number **8521**. That's bonds and loans, with at most a quarter of the money in shares.

My reasons:
- I'm 61. I can't afford to watch half my savings drop in a bad year and then wait ten years for it to come back. "עד 25% מניות" (up to 25% in shares) is a limit I can understand. Most of the money is in the safer things.
- It's one of the few tracks that actually shows a history: **8.6%** a year over 3 years and about **4.7%** over 5 years. Most tracks just say "פחות מ־3 שנים" (less than 3 years), so they're too new to judge.
- It manages **431 מיליון ₪** (431 million shekels). That's big enough that I'm not the guinea pig.
- The fee, **0.58% מההיסכון** (0.58% of my savings), is about the same as all the others. Everything sits between 0.54% and 0.61%, and **0% מההפקדה** (0% of my deposits) everywhere. So the fee didn't decide anything for me.

I was tempted by **"כללי"** (general), fund 8211. It showed **13.5%** a year over 3 years and holds **6.7 מיליארד ₪** (6.7 billion shekels). But it has **49% מניות** (49% in shares). Half in the stock market is more than I'm comfortable with at my age. I also saw **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** at 13.7%. It's 99% shares and 99% "מט"ח" (foreign currency), so that's not for me.

**How sure: maybe 60%.** I wouldn't sign anything from this page alone. I'd phone Harel or ask my bank's advisor first. I also didn't see every track (see point 6).

## 2. The parts I actually used

- The line at the top: "דמי הניהול הם הממוצע שנגבה בפועל ב־2025" (the fees are the average actually charged in 2025).
- The row of buttons, with **"קופת גמל להשקעה 16"** already selected.
- The sentence "16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול ההיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%" (16 tracks, fees on savings from 0.54% to 0.61%).
- The big table. I used it most.
- The note under the table: "התקרה בחוק: 1.05% מההיסכון, 4% מההפקדה". That's the legal maximum for fees, so I can see Harel charges about half of what's allowed.
- The sentence "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך" (past returns don't tell you what will happen next). It's fair, and it made me lean on the returns a little less.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu ("מקור", "מדריך", "השקעה במדד", "חוקים וכללים")**: skipped. I came for Harel, not for the site.
- **Title "הראל" and the fees sentence**: used. I wanted to know whether the numbers were current. "סוף ספטמבר 2026" (end of September 2026) is recent enough.
- **"כולל את החברות: הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ · הראל קרנות נאמנות בע"מ"** (includes these Harel companies): skimmed. Fine, it's all Harel.
- **"123 מקורו 123 מחושב — לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"** (roughly "123 original, 123 calculated, click a number to see where it comes from"): confused me. I didn't know why there were two 123s or what "מחושב" (calculated) means for me. Is a calculated number less reliable?
- **Product buttons ("קרן השתלמות", "חיסכון לכל ילד", etc.)**: skimmed. Mine was already chosen.
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה"** (what is an investment fund) **and "כל המסלולים ... בכל החברות"** (all tracks at all companies): noticed both, didn't click. Comparing with Migdal or Altshuler would be sensible, but I wanted to finish with Harel first.
- **The table**: used, carefully, row by row.
- **"בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות מהקופה הוצאות ישירות... לא כשורה בחשבון"** (on top of the fees, direct costs such as trading fees come out of the fund, not shown here): used, and it worried me. So I pay *more* than the 0.58%, and they don't say how much? There's also a little "PF-10" tag next to it that meant nothing to me.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל"** (what Harel's documents say), the closed list of sections: mostly skipped. The only one I cared about was "קופת גמל להשקעה · 1", and it was closed. The pension, child-savings and other product sections weren't relevant.
- **"לא מצאתם שם?"** (didn't find the name?) search box: skipped. I wasn't looking for a specific name.
- **Footer**: I noticed "נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026" (data correct as of 5 Nov 2026) and "לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (non-commercial, no marketing links). The second one made me trust it a bit more.

## 4. In the table

**Columns I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"** (track name): this was the main thing.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"** (average fees in 2025): looked once, then stopped, because they're all nearly the same. The 0% on deposits is good to know.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח"** (exposure to shares and foreign currency): I used the **מניות** (shares) number a lot. I mostly ignored the **מט"ח** (foreign currency) line because I wasn't sure what it means for me.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול"** (average yearly return, before fees): used. I noticed "לפני דמי ניהול" (before fees), so in my pocket it's about half a percent less, and before tax as well.
- **"כסף מנוהל"** (money managed): used as a "how established is this" signal.

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"** (fund number, in green): ignored, except to write down 8521.
- **The ring charts**: ignored. The teal and yellow circles didn't tell me anything the numbers next to them didn't already say.
- **The small "?" icons and little arrows in the headers**: ignored.
- **The dotted underlines**: I saw them, but I didn't know they were something to hover over.
- **The grey tag "עוקב מדדי אג"ח"** (bond-index tracker) under one track name: noticed it, but I wasn't sure why only that row had one.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"**: is 86% foreign currency good or bad for me? Does it mean that if the dollar falls, I lose?
- **"עוקב מדדים"** (index tracker) versus **"סחיר"** (tradable). Why are there both "אג"ח סחיר" and "עוקב מדדי אג"ח"? What's the difference for my money?
- **"גמיש"** (flexible): flexible how? Who decides how much goes into shares?
- **"קיימות"** (sustainability), **"הלכה יהודית"** (Jewish law): I could guess, but how do they behave?
- **"123 מקורו / 123 מחושב"** and **"PF-10"**.
- Why "אשראי ואג"ח" (credit and bonds) has 5% in shares when the name says nothing about shares.

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **The tracks I didn't see.** The page says 16, but I counted only 14. Between the screens, rows were cut off, including whatever came before "הלכה יהודית".
- **How much the track dropped in a bad year.** That's what really matters at 61.
- **Tax.** What happens if I take the money out? I've heard that after 60 you can take it as a monthly pension without tax. Is that true here?
- **Whether 0.58% is fixed for me,** or just an average I might not get. Can I bargain it down?
- **How much the "הוצאות ישירות"** (direct costs) **come to.**
- **Minimum deposit, how to join, how fast I can get my money out,** and whether I can switch tracks later at no cost.
- **Any example in shekels,** like "100,000 ₪ in this track for 5 years became…". That's what would actually answer "what does this mean for my money?".

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. The ring charts. The numbers are enough.
2. The "מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל" list for other products (pension, child savings, ETFs, and so on).
3. The other product buttons.
4. The "לא מצאתם שם?" search box.
5. The "123 מקורו / 123 מחושב" line.

What must stay: the track names, % in shares, returns, fees, money managed, and the warnings about past returns and extra costs.
