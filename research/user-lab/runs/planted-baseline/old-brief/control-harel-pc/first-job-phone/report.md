# Choosing a Harel track, as a 24-year-old first-time investor

## 1. My answer

I'd pick **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"**, fund number 13414.

**Why:**
- It's the S&P 500, the one thing I've actually heard I should invest in.
- It's one of the few rows that shows real numbers: **"13.7% 3 שנים"** and **"11.92% 5 שנים"**. Almost every other row just says **"פחות מ־3 שנים"**.
- It's big (**"2.3 מיליארד ₪"**), which makes it feel safe and popular.
- Its fee, **"0.56% מהחיסכון"**, is about the same as everyone else's.

**How sure:** about 70%.

**What makes me hesitate:**
- **"כללי"** is cut off at the bottom of the first screen, but I could see **"13.5%"** for 3 years and **"6.7 מיליארד ₪"**. That's almost the same return, it's even bigger, and it has only 49% in stocks. It might be the smarter choice and I can't tell.
- I also don't know how **"עוקב מדדי מניות"** is different from the S&P one.
- I'd still need to find out how to actually open the account.

## 2. What I actually used

- **The tab "קופת גמל להשקעה 16"**, which was already selected. It told me I was in the right place.
- **The table under "כל המוצרים של הראל"**, mainly:
  - the track name column ("המסלול")
  - "תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"
  - "כסף מנוהל"
  - "דמי ניהול" a little
- **The line above the table:** "16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול ההיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". It told me the fees barely differ, so I could stop worrying about them.

## 4. Inside the table

**What I looked at:**
- **Track names.** I scanned for words I know: "S&P 500", "מניות", "כללי".
- **Returns column.** I looked for actual percentages. The rows saying "פחות מ־3 שנים" I mostly ignored, because to me they're blank.
- **"כסף מנוהל".** I used this as a popularity signal: "מיליארד" against "מיליון".
- **"דמי ניהול".** I only checked the top number, "מהחיסכון". All of them are 0.54–0.58%, so it didn't help me choose.

**What I ignored:**
- **"מההפקדה 0%".** It's 0% in every row.
- **The "קופה" number** (15283, 13414…).
- **The rings and the coloured dots.** I can roughly see that a full ring means more stocks. The teal dot means "מניות", which I understand. The yellow dot means "מט"ח", which I don't, so I didn't use the yellow part.
- **The "?" icons and the little sort arrows.** I never touched them.
- **The "עוקב מדדי אג"ח" tag and the highlighted (grey) row.** I didn't know why that row is highlighted.

- **Can I take my money out whenever I want, and is there tax if I do?** I assume this is in "מס:…" but it's closed.
- **How much is the minimum deposit,** and can I deposit monthly?
- **How do I actually open it with Harel?** There's no link or button.
- **What 0.56% means in shekels,** for example on ₪500 a month.
- **How risky each track is,** in plain words.
- **Why choose "כללי" over S&P,** or "עוקב מדדי מניות" over S&P.
