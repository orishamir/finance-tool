# What I took from the page, as a 61-year-old putting money into the S&P 500 every month

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Open an account at **Psagot (פסגות ניירות ערך)** and buy the fund **ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)**, number **5139282**.

**How much I'd end up with:** The big box at the top says **86,492 ₪**. That assumes the site's example: 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month for 10 years. I'd have put in **70,000 ₪**. The box says: "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום". It also says "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל". I only had screenshots, so I couldn't type in my own amounts. That figure is for the site's example, not for my real savings.

**How sure I am:**
- **The account:** fairly sure. Psagot comes out about 3,100 ₪ ahead of the next one, Leumi (לאומי, 83,396 ₪). It is also far ahead of the worst ones, around 63,500 ₪. Over ten years that's real money.
- **The fund:** not very sure. The runner-up, אלטשולר שחם מחקה, is only "‎−43 ₪" behind over ten years, which is nothing. The fund I picked shows "שנה אחת" next to its record, so it has only one year of history. Harel's fund (הראל מחקה, 5129275) has "4 שנים" of history and costs only "‎−447 ₪" more over ten years. A careful person might well pay 447 ₪ for that.
- **The final amount:** not sure at all. It depends on the 7% return, which the page itself calls "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". It also depends on the dollar staying put. Elsewhere the page says the dollar moved between "‎−12.5%" and "‎+13.4%" against the shekel in a single year.

**What the page taught me about my money:** Which fund I pick matters by tens of shekels. The account can matter by thousands. What matters most is how long I stay in and how much I put in each month. The table near the middle says staying 12 years instead of 10 adds **19,116 ₪**, and putting in 600 ₪ instead of 500 ₪ adds **14,474 ₪**.
