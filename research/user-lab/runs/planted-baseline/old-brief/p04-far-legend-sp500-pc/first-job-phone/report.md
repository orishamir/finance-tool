# Using the page as myself (24, salaried, putting money in every month)

## 1. My answer

**What I'd do:** Open an account with **פסגות** and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" (number 5139282)** every month.

**How much I'd end up with:** The calculator at the top was already filled in with "סכום היום 10,000", "הפקדה חודשית 500", "שנים 10" and "תשואה בשנה 7%". With those numbers, the big box says **"86,492 ₪ — נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום."** I would have put in "70,000 ₪" in total.

**How sure I am:**
- **The choice: fairly sure, about 75%.** The page puts "★ הזול" (the cheapest) next to it. The next option, "הזוג הבא בתור", ends with only 43 ₪ less ("‎−43 ₪ לעומת הבחירה שלכם"). That tells me the top few choices are basically the same, and that makes me relaxed about choosing.
- **The amount: not sure at all.** The 7% is only "לדוגמה" (an example). The small print says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". The result also assumes "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל". So 86k is a rough picture, not a promise.

- **Noticed but didn't follow up:** the grey "לא בחישוב" lines. "לאומי – מחיר אחר לצעירים" caught my eye because I'm 24.
