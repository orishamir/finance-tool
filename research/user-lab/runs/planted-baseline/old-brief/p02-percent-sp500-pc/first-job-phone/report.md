# p02-percent-sp500-pc: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 71 s, $0.29

## 1. My answer

I'd open an account at **Psagot (פסגות)** and every month buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282**.

The page put this pair at the top with **"הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול"** ("your choice · the cheapest"). It says I'd end up with about **86,492 ₪**. That's "בכסף של היום" (in today's money), after fees and tax, and only "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel). I'd have put in **70,000 ₪**.

That number isn't really my plan, though. The page started me with **"סכום היום 10,000"** (10,000 ₪ up front) plus 500 ₪ a month, and I only had screenshots, so I couldn't change it. I was only thinking about the monthly part.

How sure I am:
- **Psagot as the account:** fairly sure. It's clearly cheapest on its list. The next one, Leumi, is about 3,000 ₪ worse.
- **The exact fund:** only medium. The next pair is **86,449**, just 43 ₪ less, and a few others are close too. A note at the bottom says that with 0% or 10% growth **"הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות"** (the order changes in 6 places), so the ranking isn't fixed.
- **The 86,000 ₪:** not sure at all. It assumes 7% a year (**"תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"**, an example return, not a forecast) and a steady dollar. I'd take it as "roughly 80–90 thousand, if things go normally."

## 2. Parts I actually used

- The title, **"איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן"** ("where and how to invest in an index most cheaply, over time"), and the **S&P 500** button, which was already selected.
- The four boxes: **סכום היום / הפקדה חודשית / שנים / תשואה בשנה** (amount today / monthly deposit / years / yearly return).
- The big result card: **הפקדתם 70,000 → הבחירה שלכם 86,492 → הזוג הבא בתור 86,449** (you deposited 70,000 → your choice 86,492 → next pair in line 86,449).
- The right-hand list **"כל החשבונות"** (all the accounts).
- The top of the left-hand fund list, **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500"** (all the S&P 500 funds), mainly the first two or three rows.
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"** (what changes your result the most), mostly the sentence about years.

- **The four sliders:** used them, at least to read what was set. I noticed 10 years and 500 ₪ were already filled in.
- **The "אינפלציה" (inflation) button and the "שער הדולר 3.17" (dollar rate 3.17) line:** skimmed. All I took from it was "numbers are in today's money."
