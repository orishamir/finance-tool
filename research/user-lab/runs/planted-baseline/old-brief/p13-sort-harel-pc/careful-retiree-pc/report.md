# What I'd do with Harel's investment savings fund

## 1. My answer

I would pick **"אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"**, fund number 8521. This is the track that holds mostly bonds and loans, with no more than a quarter in shares. I'm about **60% sure**. I'd want to talk to someone before I actually move money.

Here's why. I'm 61, and I don't want to see my savings drop by a third in a bad year. The track says "עד 25% מניות" ("up to 25% shares"), and it shows 18% today. It also has a 3-year record: "8.6%" a year on average. Lots of people already trust it with their money: "431 מיליון ₪" (431 million shekels). Most of the other calm-looking tracks only say "פחות מ־3 שנים" ("less than 3 years"), so they have no record I can judge.

I was tempted by **"כללי"** (General). It shows "13.5%" over 3 years and "8.26%" over 5, and holds "6.7 מיליארד ₪" (6.7 billion shekels). But it has "49%" in shares. Half my money in the stock market feels like too much at my age.

I'm only 60% sure for two reasons:
- I couldn't see this track's 5-year number. The row was cut off between my first two screenshots.
- I couldn't see all 16 tracks. I counted about 13–14, so a row or two may have been hidden between screens.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **The table**, mostly. I used the track name, the 3- and 5-year returns, the shares percentage, and the money managed.
- **The line above the table**: "דמי ניהול ההחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%" (management fees on savings from 0.54% to 0.61%). It told me fees are about the same everywhere, so I stopped worrying about them.
- **The note under the table**: "התשואה היא תשואה עבר... היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך" (the return is past return and doesn't say what will happen next). That's fair, and it made me cautious.
- **The legal cap line**: "תקרה בחוק: 1.05% מהחיסכון, 4% מההפקדות" (legal maximum: 1.05% of savings, 4% of deposits). It told me Harel charges about half the legal maximum. Good.

## 4. In the table

**What I looked at:**
- **Track name ("המסלול").** The words "מניות" (shares), "אג"ח" (bonds) and "עד 25% מניות" (up to 25% shares) mattered most.
- **Return ("תשואה שנתית ממוצעת").** I read the 3- and 5-year numbers. "פחות מ־3 שנים" (less than 3 years) meant "no record, skip it."
- **Shares ("מניות") percentage.** This was my risk gauge: 0% calm, 49% medium, 94–99% scary.
- **Money managed ("כסף מנוהל").** I used it as a trust signal: millions versus billions.

**What I barely used:**
- **Fees column.** Everything was 0.54–0.58% of savings and "0% מההפקדות" (0% of deposits), so it didn't separate the tracks.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח" (foreign currency).** The S&P 500 track shows 99% shares *and* 99% foreign currency. Is that extra risk? Does my money go up and down with the dollar?
- **"מניות סחיר" (traded shares).** It holds "1.4 מיליארד ₪" (1.4 billion) but has "פחות מ־3 שנים" (less than 3 years) of returns. How can such a big track have no record?
- **"סחיר" (traded) versus plain tracks.** What's the difference between "אג"ח סחיר" (traded bonds) and "אשראי ואג"ח" (loans and bonds)? Which is safer?
- **"PF-10" and "הוצאות ישירות... שלא מופיעות כאן"** (direct expenses... not shown here). So am I paying more than 0.58%? How much more?
- **The dotted underlines** under some numbers. Are they clickable, or a warning?
- **"לפני דמי ניהול"** (before management fees). So what do I actually get after fees? Should I subtract about 0.57% myself?

## 6. What I wanted and couldn't find

- **So what does this mean for my money?** If I put in 200,000 ₪, what might I have in 5 years, and how much could I lose in a bad year?
- **The worst year for each track.** For example, how far did it fall in 2022?
- **Tax.** I've heard that after 60 you can take it as a monthly pension without tax. Is that true? What happens if I take a lump sum? This is probably in the closed "מס" (tax) section, but it should be up front.
- **Rules.** Can I take money out whenever I want? Can I switch tracks later for free? Is there a yearly deposit limit?
- **Next steps.** How do I actually open one with Harel?
- **A plain "who is this track for"** line, like "for people near retirement."
