# Choosing a Harel investment savings fund track

## 1. My answer

I'd pick **"עוקב מדד S&P 500"** (S&P 500 index tracker, fund number 13414). I'm about 70% sure.

- **I recognised the name.** It's the "invest in the S&P 500" advice I've heard, and it's the only line on the page whose name meant something to me.
- **It has a real history.** It shows "3 שנים 13.7%" and "5 שנים 11.92%". Most of the other tracks just say "פחות מ־3 שנים" (less than 3 years), so I can't tell how they did.
- **It's big.** "2.3 מיליארד ₪", so lots of people already trust it.
- **The fee is about the same as everything else.** "0.56% מהחיסכון" and "0% מההפקדות". All the fees are between 0.54% and 0.61%, so the fee didn't help me choose.

**What stops me being more sure:** "מניות" (Stocks) shows higher numbers, "24.04%" for 3 years and "14.64%" for 5 years, with an almost identical fee of 0.55%. I nearly switched to it. Then I read the line under the table: "היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך" (it doesn't say what will happen next). So I stayed with the thing I'd actually heard of. I still don't know if that's smart or just familiar.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- The intro under "הראל", only the first line, to understand what the numbers mean (fees from 2025, data to end of September 2026).
- The table under "כל המוצרים של הראל". I used the track name, the fee, the return and the money-managed columns.
- The note right under the table about past returns.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu (מקור, מדריך, השקעה במדד…):** skipped. "השקעה במדד" (investing in an index) caught my eye, but I stayed on this page.
- **"הראל" heading and intro:** used the first sentence. I skipped "כולל את החברות: הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ · הראל קרנות נאמנות", which is company names I don't care about.
- **"123 מקורר 123 מחושב… לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא":** confused me. I didn't get why there are two kinds of 123.
- **Product buttons (כל המוצרים 105, קופת גמל להשקעה 16…):** I saw that my product was already highlighted, so I left them alone.
- **Links "מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" and "כל המסלולים… בכל החברות":** skipped. The second one tempted me, but the task is Harel only.
- **"16 מסלולים · דמי ניהול החיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%":** useful. It told me fees barely differ, so I stopped worrying about them.
- **The table:** this was the main thing. I read it row by row, but I couldn't see every row. My screenshots skip a few between pages, so I may have missed a track or two.
- **Bottom of the table, "התקרה בחוק: 1.05%… 4%…" and "בנוסף לדמי הניהול יורדות… הוצאות ישירות… PF-10":** skimmed. The legal maximum is nice to know. The "extra direct expenses you don't see here" part worried me a bit, and I had no idea what "PF-10" is.
- **Note about returns being before fees and before tax:** read it, and it changed how much I trusted the big numbers.
- **"מה כתוב במסמכים של הראל" accordions:** skipped. They were all closed. "מס: קופת גמל להשקעה…" (tax) is the only one I'd have opened.
- **"לא מצאתם שם?" search box:** skipped, because I wasn't looking for anything specific.
- **Footer:** skipped, apart from noticing "נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026", which tells me the data is recent.

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול" (track name):** the main thing I read.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת" (average yearly return):** the 3-year and 5-year numbers. Most rows just say "פחות מ־3 שנים", and I ignored those rows.
- **"דמי ניהול… מהחיסכון" (fee on savings):** glanced at it, then ignored it once I saw it's always about 0.55%.

**What I ignored or didn't really understand:**

- **"קופה" numbers like 15287:** ignored. I didn't know what they're for.
- **The small tags like "אג"ח סחיר" under some names:** ignored.
- **The ring charts:** I worked out that teal means "מניות" (stocks) and yellow means "מט"ח" (foreign currency). Mostly I just noticed that S&P 500 is a full circle, which I took to mean "all stocks, all abroad". I didn't look at the rest of the rings.
