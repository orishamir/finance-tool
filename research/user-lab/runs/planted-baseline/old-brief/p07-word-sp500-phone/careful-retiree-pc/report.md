**How sure am I:**
- **Pesagot:** fairly sure. It's first in the list, and the account costs about 1,057 ₪ over ten years. Leumi is about 4,541 ₪ and Altshuler Trade about 23,790 ₪. That gap is real money.
- **The fund:** only somewhat sure. The runner-up is just "‎−43 ₪" behind over ten years, so it hardly matters. What worries me is that Yelin's track record says "שנה · שנה אחת", only one year. Kesem and Harel have four years but cost me about 447 ₪ more. At my age I might take the one with the longer record and pay the 447 ₪.
- **The final figure:** not very sure. It depends entirely on the 7%.
