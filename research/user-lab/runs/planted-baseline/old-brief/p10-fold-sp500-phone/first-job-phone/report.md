# p10-fold-sp500-phone: first-job-phone (old brief)

phone; 71 s, $0.44

I'm 24 and checked this on my phone. Here's how it went.

## 1. My answer

I'd open an investing account at **Pesagot** ("פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ"). Every month I'd buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**. The page already had numbers close to mine filled in: "סכום היום 10,000 ₪", "הפקדה חודשית 500 ₪", "תשואה בשנה 7%", 10 years. With those it says I'd end up with about **86,492 ₪**, after fees and tax, "בכסף של היום". I'd be putting in about 70,000 ₪ myself (10,000 now plus 500 × 120 months), so roughly 16,000 ₪ would be gain.

**How sure am I?** About 65%.
- I'm fairly sure about the account. Pesagot is #1 and marked "★ הזול", and the cost box gives it the lowest cost.
- I'm less sure about the fund, because I don't really know what "4D" means.
- I'm also bothered that the page's "עכשיו" (now) is a different account and fund that I never picked.
- The 86,492 ₪ only holds if the market really gives 7% a year. The page says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית", so it's an example, not a promise.

## 2. What I actually used

- The top inputs: "סכום היום", "הפקדה חודשית", "תשואה בשנה", with S&P 500 selected.
- The list of accounts, "כל החשבונות" (#1 Pesagot 86,492 ₪, #2 Leumi 83,396 ₪, and so on).
- The fund list, "כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500", which showed which fund is "הבחירה" (the pick).
- The summary line further down: "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות". That one line gave me my answer.

- **Young-person prices.** I'm 24, and many banks say "מחיר אחר לצעירים", but it's marked "לא בחישוב" (not counted). Would Leumi or Mizrahi beat Pesagot for me? The page doesn't say.

- **The tax.** A one-line explanation of the tax, since it's the biggest cost.
