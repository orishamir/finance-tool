# I'm 61 and want to put money into the S&P 500 every month for ten years. Here's how the page went for me.

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

**What I would do:** I'd open an account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and set up a monthly standing order into the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**.

**How much I'd end up with:** I couldn't move the sliders on screenshots, so I kept the page's example: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, and 7% a year. That's 70,000 ₪ of my own money over ten years. The page says I'd end up with **86,492 ₪**. It also says this is "אחרי מס, בכסף של היום" (after tax, in today's money). I think that means the number has already been adjusted for prices going up, but I'm not certain. So I'd be about 16,500 ₪ ahead after ten years. That's less than I'd hoped, but it's what the page says.

**How sure:**
- **Fairly sure about what to avoid.** Some places leave me much less:
  - Altshuler Trade (66,183 ₪), Excellence (63,678 ₪) and Meitav Trade (63,507 ₪).
  - A fund held abroad: "קרן שנקנית בחו"ל … 20,100 ₪ פחות" (20,100 ₪ less).
  - The fund with currency protection ("נטרול מט"ח"): "9,460 ₪ יותר" (9,460 ₪ more) if I avoid it.
- **Only somewhat sure that Psagot plus Yelin is the best choice.**
  - The top places are close. Psagot gives 86,492 ₪ and Leumi gives 83,396 ₪, about 3,000 ₪ apart over ten years.
  - Funds inside Psagot are even closer. The next fund is just "43 ₪" less.
  - The page warns: "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" (an example return, not a forecast).
  - It also says "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות". In plain words, if returns come out at 0% or 10%, six places in the ranking change. So the order isn't fixed.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- **The top section** "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן", with the S&P 500 button and the three boxes "סכום היום", "הפקדה חודשית" and "תשואה בשנה".
- **The list "כל החשבונות"**, the ranking of banks and brokers by "נשאר" (what's left).
- **The fund card** for "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)". The line "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים" (no management fees) reassured me.
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"**, the small table under the line chart.
- **The final sentence** "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות … פסגות". It confirmed my choice.
- **The small print** at the bottom: "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" and "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד".

## 3. Going down the page block by block

1. **Title and index buttons** ("MSCI World", "ת"א-125" and the rest). **Used** the title. **Skipped** the other indexes because S&P 500 was already chosen. I didn't understand the little "זר" tag (foreign).
2. **The three input boxes.** **Used** them. This is my money, so I read them carefully. The "אינפלציה" button **confused me**. Is the 7% before or after inflation? I couldn't tell.
3. **"לדוגמה של האתר · בכסף של היום · שער הדולר 3.17 ₪"** (the site's example, today's money, dollar at 3.17 ₪). **Skimmed** it. I noted the date, 05.11.2026.
4. **"הציגו כמה יישאר לכם"** (show how much you'll keep). It's a button, so I couldn't press it on a screenshot. **Skipped.**
5. **"כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים, בכל אחד מהזוגות"** (what it costs you over 10 years, for each pair). **Partly used.** Total costs were 6,876 ₪, and **5,703 ₪ of that is "מס" (tax)**. That surprised me: most of the "cost" is tax, not the bank. The word "זוגות" (pairs) puzzled me until I worked out it means an account plus a fund.
6. **"1 ₪ יותר / הקרן 58 ₪ פחות / המס 14 ₪ יותר"** (1 ₪ more / the fund 58 ₪ less / the tax 14 ₪ more). **Confused me.** I couldn't see what it was being compared to. More of what? Tax going up being shown as something good ("כי נשאר לכם יותר רווח", because you keep more profit) is strange to read.
7. **The Psagot card** ("מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,057 ₪", what the account charges over 10 years). **Used** it. 1,057 ₪ over ten years is a number I understand.
8. **The note above the list**, "קנייה חודשית מחושבת כהוראת קבע…" (monthly purchases are calculated as a standing order). **Used** it, and I read it twice. I understood that I need a standing order to get these prices.
9. **The list of 15 banks.** **Used** it, mainly the top half. I read the "לא בחישוב" (not included in the calculation) notes. One of them concerns me: Hapoalim has "מחיר אחר … לנשים מגיל 60 וגברים מגיל 65" (a different price for women from 60 and men from 65). If I'm a woman of 61, Hapoalim might be cheaper for me, and the page didn't count it. I'd phone them.
10. **"אינטראקטיב ישראל" and "Interactive Brokers"** with "אין נתון" (no data). **Skimmed** them. Foreign brokers aren't for me.
11. **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500"** (all the funds on the S&P 500). **Skimmed** it. I saw that Yelin is "הבחירה" (the choice) and the others leave less. Tags like "עסקת החלף" (a swap deal) and "כמה דרכים יחד" (several ways together) meant nothing to me.
12. **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות"** (why some funds have no price at Psagot). **Skimmed.** It was too technical.
13. **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"** (what changes most what you'll keep). **Used** it, and this was the most useful part. Two more years ("12 שנים") gives "19,116 ₪ יותר" (19,116 ₪ more). Adding 100 ₪ a month gives 14,474 ₪ more. So the bank matters less than how long I stay in and how much I put in.
14. **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל"** (with or without currency protection: what actually happened), down to "השורה התחתונה" (the bottom line). **Skimmed most of it**, then read the bottom line: "הנטרול לא מגן מירידה של השוק" (the protection doesn't shield you from a market drop). Good, that tells me to skip it.
15. **The dollar bar chart and the 2022–2025 table.** **Confused me.** In the table the numbers were printed on top of each other, things like "−2±8.2%", and I couldn't read them.
16. **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת"** (start from one choice), the staircase chart starting at 79,176 ₪. **Confused me.** It starts from "MTF סל … ירושלים". I never chose Jerusalem, so why is that my "עכשיו" (now)?
17. **The lists "חשבון אחר, אותה קרן" / "סוג קרן אחר" / "קרן אחרת מאותו סוג"** (another account, same fund / another kind of fund / another fund of the same kind). **Skimmed.** I only took the "20,100 ₪ פחות" for buying abroad. Something didn't add up: One Zero is ranked 3rd in the list above, but here it says "יש ספק אם נמכרת שם: … וואן זירו" (it's doubtful whether it's sold there). That shook my trust a little.
18. **The small print and footer.** **Used** the warnings and the date. **Skipped** "חוקים וכללים · 6" (rules and laws), "פרמטרים" and the other links.

## 4. Lists, tables and charts: what I looked at and what I ignored

- **Bank list:** I looked at the bank name, the big number on the left (what's left), the "★ הזול" star (cheapest), the "מחיר להוראת קבע" line (standing-order price) and the "לא בחישוב" notes. I ignored the smaller number under it (4,541 ₪ and so on, which I guess is costs), the coloured bars and the round selection buttons.
- **The cost colours:** I only looked at **pink = "מס"** (tax) and **yellow = "דמי משמרת"** (custody fees), because they're big and have numbers written on them. I ignored orange, red, green and purple. I didn't understand grey, "העמלות זו על זו" (fees on top of fees?).
- **"מה הכי משנה" table:** I used the columns "בתוכנית שלכם" (in your plan), "אחרי השינוי" (after the change) and "ההפרש" (the difference). I ignored the line chart and its four colours. The table was enough.
- **Fund list:** I looked at the name, the shekel difference (−43, −447) and "הבחירה" (the choice). I ignored the fund numbers (5139282), the percentages "+0.37% … בשנה · שנה אחת" (per year, one year) and the tags.
- **Dollar chart and fund chart (teal versus yellow bars):** I glanced at 2022, the yellow −21.8% bar, and understood that the protected fund fell more. I ignored the rest.
- **Staircase chart:** I only looked at the grey bar (79,176 ₪) and the final number. I ignored the "K" axis.
- **The dotted purple underlines** under almost every number: I didn't know if I should tap them, so I ignored them.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- Is the 7% before or after inflation, and what does the "אינפלציה" button do?
- What "4D" and "4A" mean, and what "MTF", "ETF", "UCITS" and "Accumulating" mean.
- "נאמן 0.02%" (trustee 0.02%): is that a fee I pay?
- "עסקת החלף" (swap deal), "כמה דרכים יחד" (several ways together), "נקודות" (points), "מט"ח" (foreign currency).
- Why tax counts as a "cost". It isn't the bank's fault.
- Why the staircase starts from Jerusalem MTF.
- Why One Zero is 3rd in one place and "יש ספק" (doubtful) in another.
- "העמלות זו על זו" (fees on top of fees).

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Is my money safe at Psagot?** It's a broker, not a bank. What happens if it goes under?
- **How do I actually open an account and set up the standing order?** How long does it take, and can I do it at a branch rather than in an app?
- **What if I need the money at 66 or 68?** Can I take it out, and what does that cost?
- **The age discount**, "לנשים מגיל 60" (for women from 60): why isn't it in the calculation, when it fits me?
- **What if the market falls just before I need the money?** At my age that's my real worry, and the page doesn't speak to it.
- **The total I paid in, next to the final amount.** I had to work out the 70,000 ₪ myself.
