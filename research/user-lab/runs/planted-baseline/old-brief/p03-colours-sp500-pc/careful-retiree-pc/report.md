# What I'd do with my S&P 500 money, after reading my friend's page

## 1. My answer

I would open an account at **Psagot (פסגות)** and set up a standing order of 500 ₪ a month into **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282**. I'd also put in 10,000 ₪ at the start.

The page's own example says that after 10 years I'd have about **86,492 ₪**. The big box says "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום". I'd put in 70,000 ₪ myself (the box "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪"), so the gain is about 16,500 ₪ in today's money.

**How sure am I?**
- **About where and which fund: fairly sure, but not attached to it.** The runner-up is only "‎−43 ₪" worse over ten years, which is nothing. My bank, Leumi, comes out at 83,396 ₪, about 3,000 ₪ less. That's real money, but not so much that I'd panic if moving my money to a new firm felt like a hassle.
- **About the 86,492 ₪: not sure at all.** It depends on the 7% a year that was already filled in. Near the bottom the page says "התשואה שבחישוב: תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית" (the return is an example, not a forecast). So it's a picture, not a promise.
- **One thing bothers me.** The winning fund shows "שנה אחת" next to its +0.37%, so it has only one year of history. The funds below it have "4 שנים".

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **The four boxes at the top:** "סכום היום", "הפקדה חודשית", "שנים", "תשואה בשנה". I checked they matched my plan: 10,000, 500, 10 years. I left 7% alone because I don't know what else to put.
- **The result boxes:** "הפקדתם", "הבחירה שלכם · ★ הזול", "הזוג הבא בתור". These told me what I wanted to know.
- **The account list on the right, "כל החשבונות"**, especially the grey notes beginning "לא בחישוב".
- **The fund card at the top left**, which shows the fund name and "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים 0.02% נאמן".
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"**, the table with years, monthly deposit and so on.
- **"השורה התחתונה"** in the currency-hedging section. I needed someone to just tell me.

## 3. Going down the page, block by block

- **The heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן".** Read it. It's exactly my question, which was a good start.
- **The index buttons (MSCI World, נאסד"ק 100, ת"א-125…).** I checked S&P 500 was picked and ignored the rest. "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד" was fine; I already know which one I want.
- **The short S&P 500 explanation.** Read it. "הכסף מושקע בדולרים" worried me a little, because I live in shekels. I didn't get "אפשר להחזיק אותו גם דרך קרן חוץ" at that point.
- **The four sliders.** Used. Simple enough.
- **The "אינפלציה" button and the grey line "שער הדולר 3.17 ₪ · מחושב משערי הייחוס של הבנק המרכזי האירופי".** Confused me. Why a European bank for the shekel? I didn't press the button because I was afraid it would change everything.
- **The three result boxes.** Used, and the most useful part.
- **"כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים, בכל אחד מהזוגות" (the coloured cost bars).** Skimmed. I only looked at the line "העלות אצלכם, לעומת הזוג השני": the account costs "1 ₪ יותר", the fund "58 ₪ פחות", tax "14 ₪ יותר כי נשאר לכם יותר רווח". These are tiny amounts, so I stopped worrying.
- **The fund card on the left.** Used it for the name and fees. I didn't know what "נאמן" (trustee) is or why it costs 0.02%.
- **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500" with the filter buttons ("סנן לפי תווית").** Mostly skipped. There are 12 buttons, like "עסקה חליפית 5" and "מחזיקה את המניות 6", and I don't know what most of them mean.
- **The long fund list.** I looked at the top three and the numbers on the far left. Below "‎−447 ₪" I only skimmed. When I reached the foreign funds with "אין מחיר כאן" and "עוד לא נאסף", I stopped.
- **The account list on the right.** Used it, carefully. I read Psagot, Leumi, Hapoalim, then just watched the numbers drop.
- **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות".** Skimmed. Too much text about "שכר נאמן" and "לא נאסף".
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם".** Used. The message got through: staying in longer (12 years gives "19,116 ₪ יותר") and putting in a bit more each month (600 ₪ gives 14,474 ₪ more) matter more than anything else. At 61 that's food for thought.
- **The line chart above that table.** Skipped. The table said it more clearly.
- **"עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי: מה קרה בפועל".** Partly read, partly confused. The three cards were dense. I took away that hedging costs "2.1 עד 3.3 נקודות בשנה" and that in 2022 it didn't protect ("‎−21.8% ב־2022"). That's enough for me to say no to it.
- **The two bar charts (the dollar by year, the funds by year).** Skimmed. I looked at 2022 and 2025 and nothing else.
- **The table by year.** Only the last row, "4 השנים יחד": unhedged "+50.4% עד +56.8%" against hedged "+35.9%". That settled it.
- **"השורה התחתונה".** Read every word. Good.
- **The sources paragraph ("מקורות:…").** Skipped.
- **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת, ורואים מה משפר אותה ובכמה" (the staircase chart).** Confused me. It says "עכשיו 79,176 ₪", but I have nothing "now". Why does it start from some fund at Bank Yerushalayim? I thought maybe it was a mistake.
- **The small table below it (עלות החשבון / עלות הקרן / מס).** Confused me. Why is tax "+444" and "+779" with a plus sign at Psagot and 4,481 at Yerushalayim? Does a plus mean I pay more, or get money back?
- **"עכשיו: MTF סל S&P 500 (4D), ירושלים" with the three small tables.** Skimmed. I noticed just one thing: "קרן שנקנית בחו"ל 59,076 ₪, פחות 20,100 ₪". So buying abroad is a bad idea for me, which is good to know.
- **The notes at the bottom and the footer.** Read them. "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות" scared me a bit. Does the winner change too? Also, "נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026" is yesterday, which is good.

- **Account list:** the bank name, the big number ("נשאר"), and the grey "לא בחישוב" notes. I ignored the coloured bars and the small number under the big one (1,057, 4,541…). Later I understood that small number is the cost, but the bar colours meant nothing to me. The legend at the bottom ("עמלות קנייה ומכירה, דמי משמרת, דמי ניהול חשבון, המרת מטבע") was too small and too far away.
- **Fund list:** the fund name, then the left column "לעומת הבחירה" (‎−43, ‎−213, ‎−447…). I glanced at "מול המדד" (+0.37%) and noticed "שנה אחת" against "4 שנים". I ignored all the coloured tags ("צוברת", "חשיפה לדולר", "מס גם על שינוי המטבע" and so on). There are too many, and they look like warning signs without telling me whether they're good or bad. The pink "מס עיזבון אמריקאי" and "צריך טופס W-8BEN" did jump out at me.
- **The "what changes things" table:** all of it, especially the columns "אחרי השינוי" and "ההפרש". I ignored the coloured dots (yellow, orange, purple, light blue) because the row names were enough.
- **The hedging table:** only the "4 השנים יחד" row and the "2022" row. I ignored the column "עם נטרול מול בלי נטרול, בנקודות".
- **The dollar chart:** only the white bars, "+13.4" and "‎−12.5". I didn't understand why they're white and the rest grey. Later I read "העמודות הבהירות: השנים שיש עליהן דוחות של הקרנות".

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"עסקה חליפית"** (a swap deal). It's written on my winning fund. What is it, and is it risky for my money?
- **"(4D)" and "(4A)"** in the fund names.
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע".** Will I pay tax even if the dollar just goes up and the index doesn't?
- **"נאמן 0.02%".** Who is that?
- **"קרן מחקה" versus "קרן סל".** What's the difference for me?
- **"מס עיזבון אמריקאי".** I'm 61, so this matters to my heirs. Nobody explains it.
- **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג".**
- **The plus and minus signs on tax in the staircase table.**
- **Why the staircase chart starts from "עכשיו" at Yerushalayim.**
- **"בכסף של היום".** Is the 7% already after inflation, or does the page subtract inflation from it? This changes everything.
- **The "אינפלציה" button.** What happens if I press it?

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Anything for my age.** All the discounts are for "צעירים, חיילים משוחררים, סטודנטים". The only thing close is at Hapoalim: "לנשים מגיל 60 וגברים מגיל 65 (סוג למצטרפים)", and it's "לא בחישוב". How much would it save?
- **What happens if I need the money in, say, year 5.** At my age, that's my main question. Is there a penalty? How long does it take to get it out?
- **How safe Psagot is.** If it goes bust, is my money protected? It isn't my bank, and the page doesn't calm me about that.
- **How to actually open the account and set up the standing order.** The page tells me what, not how.
- **A middle number.** What do I end up with at 4% or 5%, without moving the slider and guessing?
- **Whether the winning fund, with only one year of history, could change completely in a few years.**

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The staircase chart and the whole "עכשיו: … ירושלים" block.** It starts from a place I'm not at, and only confused me.
2. **The foreign funds with "אין מחיר כאן".** They give no number, so they're no use to me.
3. **The 12 filter buttons and most of the coloured tags.**
4. **The two bar charts in the hedging section.** I'd keep only "השורה התחתונה" and the 4-years-together row.
5. **The line chart above the "what changes things" table.** The table is enough.

**What I'd keep:** the sliders, the three result boxes, the account list with the "לא בחישוב" notes, the top of the fund list, and the "what changes things" table.
