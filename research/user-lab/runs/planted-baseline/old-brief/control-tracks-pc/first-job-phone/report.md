## 1. What I'd do

I'd open the **מנורה מבטחים** "עוקב מדד S&P500" track (fund number **13874**). I'm about 65% sure about the track and only 50% sure about the company.

- **Why this track:** I went in wanting "the S&P 500", and the page has tracks with exactly that name. That part I'm fairly sure of.
- **Why Menora:** Among all the S&P 500 rows I saw, it had the lowest "מהחיסכון" fee: **0.52%**. Most others were 0.56%–0.62%, and Mor's was 0.71%. On ₪10,000 that's only a few shekels a year, but it adds up over decades.
- **Is it big enough:** It manages "674 מיליון ₪", which sounds big enough to trust. Its "3 שנים 13.79%" is about the same as everyone else's S&P 500 track.
- **What bothers me:** It's the only one with a fee "מההפקדות 0.01%". I think that's tiny, but I'm not sure what it means.
- **My backup:** **הראל** S&P 500 (**13414**) at 0.56%. Harel is the biggest company at the top ("כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪"), and that track alone has 2.3 billion. I don't know if "biggest" matters, so I'm torn.

## 4. Inside the table

- **"המסלול" (track name):** used it to find S&P 500. I also noticed "S&P500" and "S&P 500" are written two ways, and a few rows had both "חדש" and "הלכה יהודית" tags.
- **"קופה" (the number with the arrow icon):** ignored it. I only wrote down my choice's number in case I need it later.
- **"דמי ניהול ממוצע 2025": "מהחיסכון" vs "מההפקדות":** used both, but mostly "מהחיסכון". Almost all the "מההפקדות" values were 0%, so I stopped looking. The little "▲" next to "מהחיסכון" suggested it's sorted by that inside each company, which helped.
- **"חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח" with the rings:**
  - I understood "מניות". I sort of know "מט"ח" is foreign currency.
  - I learned that the green and yellow rings that are almost fully closed mean S&P 500, since those rows show about 99%/99%.
  - "101%" (Meitav מניות, Mor מניות) confused me. How can it be more than 100?
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת, לפני דמי ניהול" (3 שנים / 5 שנים):** looked at it, but every S&P track was around 13% over 3 years, so it didn't decide anything. The many "פחות מ־3 שנים" entries meant those tracks are new.
- **"כסף מנוהל":** used it as a "is this safe or tiny" check.
- **Ignored:** the "?" icons (couldn't click them), the sort arrows, the "<" arrows at the right end of each row, and the dotted underlines.
