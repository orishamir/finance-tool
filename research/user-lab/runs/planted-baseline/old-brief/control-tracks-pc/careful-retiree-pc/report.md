# Choosing a kupat gemel lehashkaa from my friend's screenshots

## 1. What I would do, and how sure I am

I would open the **Harel "כללי" track (fund number 8211)**. I'm only moderately sure, about 6 out of 10.

Why this one:
- **It's among the cheapest on the page.** The fee is "0.55% מהחיסכון" and "0% מההפקדות". On 200,000 shekels that's about 1,100 shekels a year. The page says fees run "מ־0.5% עד 0.73%", so this is near the bottom. The worst one would cost me about 1,460, so the gap is a few hundred shekels a year. That matters, but it isn't huge.
- **Harel is listed first and is the biggest.** The page says companies are sorted by money managed ("החברה מסודרות מהגדולה לקטנה לפי הכסף המנוהל"). Harel shows "כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪", and this track alone holds "6.7 מיליארד ₪". Lots of people trust it with their money. That comforts me, even though it proves nothing.
- **It's the middle of the road.** "כללי" means "general", which sounds neither reckless nor timid. The page shows "מניות 49%", so I read it as roughly half in shares. At 61 I don't want the all-shares tracks ("מניות", or "עוקב מדד S&P 500" with "99%") even though they show bigger numbers like "24.04%". I'm not sure I can wait out a bad year.
- **Its results were not worse than other companies' "כללי" tracks.** Harel showed "13.5%" for 3 years and "8.26%" for 5 years. Menora's showed "12.9%" and "7.78%" for a higher fee of "0.59%". Analyst's showed "12.72%" and "8.13%" for "0.62%". The page warns that this is past performance, so I treat it as "not worse", not "better".

Why I'm only 6 out of 10:
- If I'll need the money within a few years, I'd lean towards Harel's **"אשראי ואג"ח עם מניות (עד 25% מניות)"** (8521, "0.58%"). Its shares are capped at 25%. The page can't tell me which of the two suits me.
- I didn't see every row. The screenshots jump, so I never saw the names of some companies.

## 2. Parts of the page I actually used

- **The opening text** under "כל המסלולים בכל החברות", including the line about how companies are ordered.
- **The line above the table**: "143 מסלולים של 12 חברות · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.5% עד 0.73%". This told me the whole range of fees in one sentence.
- **The grey company header rows**, such as "הראל 16 מסלולים · כסף מנוהל 19.1 מיליארד ₪ · דמי ניהול מהחיסכון: מ־0.54% עד 0.61%". I used these to compare companies quickly.
- **The "כללי" rows in each company.** I hunted for these and compared them.
- **The note at the very bottom**: "התשואה היא תשואת עבר... לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס. היא לא אומרת מה יקרה בהמשך". This was important and it changed how I read the returns.

## 3. Block by block

- **Top menu** ("מקור", "מדריך", "חברות ומסלולים", "השקעה במדד", "חוקים וכללים"): skipped. With screenshots I couldn't click anything, and I didn't know what "מקור" was.
- **The tabs "לפי חברה" / "לפי מוצר"**: skimmed. I understood I was looking by product.
- **Title and first paragraph**: used. "נכונים לסוף ספטמבר 2026" told me the numbers are recent.
- **"123 מקורו 123 מחושב · לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא"**: confused me. I didn't know what "מקורו" versus "מחושב" meant for my money, so I ignored it.
- **The product buttons** ("קופת גמל להשקעה 143", "קרן השתלמות 154" and so on): I only checked that "קופת גמל להשקעה" was the one highlighted. Good.
- **"מה זה קופת גמל להשקעה" link**: I would have liked to click it, but I couldn't.
- **Search box** ("שם המסלול או החברה"): skipped.
- **"במה הכסף מושקע" buttons** ("כללי 12", "מניות 12", "הלכה יהודית 9" and others): I read them and they told me "כללי" exists at 12 companies. If this were live, I would press "כללי 12" first and save myself 15 screens of scrolling.
- **The big table**: used, but only some of it (see section 4). Scrolling through 143 rows was tiring.
- **The rows marked "חדש" with "עוד לא דווח"**: skipped. No history and no fee figure means no thanks.
- **Bottom note on returns**: used, as above.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 7" and the footer**: skimmed. "אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" reassured me a little that nobody is being paid to push a fund.

## 4. In the table: what I looked at and what I ignored

**What I looked at:**
- **"המסלול"**, meaning the track name. I mostly looked for "כללי" and the "עד 25% מניות" ones.
- **"דמי ניהול, ממוצע 2025"**, but only the **"מהחיסכון"** line. The "מההפקדות" line was almost always "0%", so I stopped reading it.
- **"תשואה שנתית ממוצעת"**: the "3 שנים" and "5 שנים" figures. I trusted the 5-year one more.
- **"כסף מנוהל"**, just to see whether a track is big or tiny. Some of the new ones show "0 מיליון ₪", which put me off.
- **The grey company header lines.**

**What I ignored:**
- **"קופה"**, the fund number with the little arrow icon. I'd write it down only for the one I pick, to give the company.
- **The teal and yellow rings and "חשיפה למניות ולמט"ח".** I read the "מניות" percentage. I didn't understand the yellow "מט"ח" one or what the ring was drawing (see section 5).
- **The dotted underlines under every number.**
- **The little arrows (⇅) and "?" icons in the headers.**
- **The tags "הלכה יהודית" and "אג"ח סחיר".**
- **All the "פחות מ־3 שנים" / "פחות מ־5 שנים" cells.** To me that means "too young to judge".

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"מט"ח"**: I guess it means foreign currency. So what does it mean for my money if it's "99%"? Is that riskier?
- **"מניות 101%"** (Meitav "מניות"). How can you have more than 100%?
- **"מהחיסכון" versus "מההפקדות"**: I think one is charged on what I've built up and the other on what I put in, but I'm guessing.
- **"עוד לא דווח"**: the bottom says "השדה ריק בנתוני הרשות". Which authority, and why is it empty?
- **"גמיש"** in "עוקב מדדים - גמיש". Flexible how?
- **"סחיר"**, as in "אג"ח סחיר" and "משולב סחיר".
- **Whether "13.5%" for 3 years is per year or in total.** The header says "שנתית ממוצעת", so per year, but that sounds too good to me.
- **"123 מקורו / 123 מחושב"**.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **Which track suits someone my age**, or someone who needs the money in 3 years versus 10.
- **What I'd actually get after fees and tax.** The page says the returns are "לפני דמי הניהול ולפני מס", so what about after?
- **The rules at 60+.** I've heard you can take this money as a monthly pension with a tax benefit. Nothing about that here.
- **How easily I can take money out**, how long it takes, and whether there are penalties.
- **How bad the worst year was** for each track. That matters more to me than the average.
- **Whether I can switch track or company later**, and whether that costs anything.
- **Whether the fee is negotiable.** People say you can bargain it down.
- **The names of a few companies.** The screenshots cut off before their header rows.

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The rings and the "מט"ח" line.** Keep "מניות X%" as a plain number.
2. **All the "חדש" / "עוד לא דווח" tracks**, or at least hide them at the bottom.
3. **The "מההפקדות" line**, since it's nearly always 0%.
4. **"123 מקורו / 123 מחושב"** and the dotted underlines.
5. **The search box and the top menu**, which I didn't use.

I'd keep the company header lines, the "כללי" and "עד 25% מניות" rows, the fee from savings, the 5-year return and the warning at the bottom. For someone like me, that's the useful half.
