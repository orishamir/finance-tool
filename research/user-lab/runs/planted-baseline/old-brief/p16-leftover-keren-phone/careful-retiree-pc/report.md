# What I made of the study fund page

## 1. My answer: what I'd do, and how sure I am

I'd join. My employer puts in money on top of my salary. The first block says **"המעסיק מפקיד לקרן כל חודש כסף בנוסף לשכר הברוטו"**: the employer deposits money into the fund every month, on top of my gross salary. In the calculator that's 7.5% from them and 2.5% from me. That's free money and I wouldn't turn it down.

**When can I take it out?** The page says **"אחרי 6 שנים מההפקדה הראשונה אפשר למשוך את הכסף בלי מס"**: after 6 years from the first deposit I can withdraw without tax. That would make me about 67. Right after that it says **"למי שהגיע לגיל הפרישה מספיקות 3 שנים"**: for someone who has reached retirement age, 3 years are enough. That sentence matters most to me at 61, but the page never says what "retirement age" is, or whether I'd count. So it's either about 3 years or 6. I don't know which, and I'd phone the fund or HR before relying on 3.

**How much?** The page's own example is 300 ₪ a month from me and 900 ₪ from the employer, which I think means a salary of about 12,000. Taken out after 5 years, that comes to:
- **"נשאר בידיים"** (left in your hands): about **66,500 ₪**, but only if I ask the tax office for a refund.
- Without the refund I'd first get only **50,719 ₪**. The text says 29,768 ₪ goes to income tax, and only 15,794 ₪ of that comes back if I claim it.

For 6 years, with no tax, the page didn't give me a number. Judging from the bar heights, it's maybe somewhere around 90,000 ₪, but that's my own guess.

**How sure am I?**
- Joining: fairly sure.
- The amounts: only roughly, because it's an example with an assumed 7% return, not a promise.
- When I can take it out: not sure at all.

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **Retirement age.** **"גיל הפרישה"** (retirement age): what age is it, for me? Do I count?
- **Two different tax numbers.** The bar says the tax took **13,974**, but the text says **29,768** went to tax and **15,794** comes back. How much do I lose if I don't file for a refund? From the text it looks like about 66,500 vs. 50,700, but I'm not sure I've got that right.
- **The ceiling.** I'm not sure what **"15,712 ₪"** means for my salary, or what **"20,566 ₪ בשנה"** refers to.
- **The badges.** **"חוקים וכללים · 10"** (laws and rules), "KH-15" and "PF-18". No idea.
- **The three colours** in the 12-bar chart.
- **The before-60 line.** **"…למי שמתקרב לגיל 60… 31%"** (for someone approaching 60… 31%) was cut off right where it got to my age.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **When can I take it out at my age?** A plain sentence like "If you're 61 now, you can take it out at ___" was what I wanted.
- **The 6-year total.** The actual number for 6 years in my hands, tax-free, not just the 5-year example.
- **My own salary.** What happens with my real salary instead of 12,000.
- **Death.** What happens to the money if I die before I take it out. Does my family get it?
- **Changing jobs.** What happens if this job ends after 2 or 3 years. Does the employer's part stay mine?
- **Monthly payments.** Can I take it as a monthly payment instead of all at once?
