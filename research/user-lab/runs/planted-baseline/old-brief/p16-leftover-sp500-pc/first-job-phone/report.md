# p16-leftover-sp500-pc: first-job-phone (old brief)

pc; 60 s, $0.27

## 1. My answer

I'd open a trading account at **פסגות** (Psagot) and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, the fund numbered **5139282**. I'd put in 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ every month for 10 years. The page says I'd end up with about **86,492 ₪**. I'd pay in 70,000 ₪ in total, so that's about 16,000 ₪ more.

How sure am I?
- **Where to hold it and which fund:** fairly sure, maybe 7 out of 10. Psagot and that fund are marked "★ הזול" (the cheapest) all over the page. But the next choice down, "הזוג הבא בתור", is only "−43 ₪" behind over ten years. So I don't think it matters much among the top few.
- **How much I'd end up with:** not sure at all. The 86,492 depends on the "7%" in "תשואה בשנה", and I don't know if 7% is realistic. I just left it as it was.
- **One thing holding me back:** I'm 24, and many banks say "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים" (a different price for young people, not included). A bank might actually be cheaper for me, and the page doesn't tell me.

- **"מדד S&P 500" explanation:** read it. "הכסף מושקע בדולרים" made me a bit nervous, but I moved on.
