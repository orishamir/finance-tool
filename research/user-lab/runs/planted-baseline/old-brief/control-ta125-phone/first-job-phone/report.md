## 1. My answer

I'd open an account at **פסגות** and buy the fund **"אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125"**, number **5112628**. I'd put in 500 ₪ every month.

The page says I'd end up with about **86,045 ₪** after 10 years. It says that's "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום". I'd have put in 70,000 ₪ of my own money.

I didn't change any of the numbers at the top. 10,000 ₪ to start, 500 a month and 7% were already filled in. Honestly, I'm not sure I have 10,000 ₪ to put in today, so my real result would be lower.

**How sure am I?**
- **Account and fund: fairly sure, about 7/10.** Both have a star and "הזול", and the lower part of the page says it again: "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪, אי.בי.אי. מחקה ת"א 125, פסגות".
- **The 86,045 ₪ figure: not very sure.** The page itself says the 7% is "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית". So I read it as "roughly this much if things go normally".
- **One real doubt:** I'm 24. Some banks say "מחיר אחר לצעירים" and it's "לא בחישוב". Maybe לאומי or הבינלאומי would be cheaper for me, and the page doesn't tell me.
