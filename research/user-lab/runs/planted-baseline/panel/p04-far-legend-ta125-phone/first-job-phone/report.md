**How it went**
- **Top of the page:** The heading said "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" (where and how to invest in an index cheapest). The index buttons already had "ת"א-125" picked, which is exactly what I wanted. The page had 10,000 ₪ today, 500 ₪ a month and 7% a year filled in. I left them as they were.
- **The big number:** The first one I saw was "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪" (you deposited). Then the starred box said "הזול 86,045 ₪", with the next pair at 85,015 ₪. There was a coloured bar with "5,555" on it and I didn't know what the colours meant.
- **The bank list:** פסגות was #1 with 86,045, then לאומי, וואן זירו, הבינלאומי and so on. The ones further down, like אקסלנס and מיטב טרייד, drop to about 63,000 ₪ and have a big red chunk in their bar. I didn't open any of the "לא בחישוב" (not in the calculation) folds.

**Where I got stuck**

- **The minus signs scared me.** My chosen fund shows "−0.04%" in green, and the others show "−0.37%", "−0.54%". For a second I thought the fund was losing money. The note explaining it ("מול המדד...") is small grey text and I didn't read it.
