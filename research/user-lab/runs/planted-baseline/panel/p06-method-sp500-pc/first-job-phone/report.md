## What it said along the way

**What I'd do:** open an account at פסגות and buy "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", 500 ₪ a month. The page says I'd have about **86,492 ₪** after 10 years. I'm fairly sure, but not fully. What decided it is that the box marked "הבחירה שלכם · הזול" put those two together. I know פסגות from ads, so that was enough for me.

**One catch:** that 86,492 is for the page's own numbers, 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 a month at 7%. It isn't for my 30,000. I didn't change the boxes, so my real number would be higher, and I don't know by how much.

**How it went:**
- **First screen:** I saw the heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד", that S&P 500 was already chosen, the boxes (10,000 / 500 / 10 years / 7%) and the big numbers. "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪" turned into 86,492 ₪, and next to it "הזוג הבא בתור" showed 86,449, only "−43 ₪" less. So the difference hardly matters.
- **Second screen:** At the top was a coloured bar with a legend I didn't read, plus "1 ₪ יותר", "58 ₪ פחות" and "14 ₪ יותר". I don't know what these are compared to. On the right was the list of banks. פסגות came first, "הזול", with "1,057 ₪" over 10 years. לאומי was "−3,096". On the left, the fund was ילין לפידות 5139282, with "דמי ניהול 0%". I didn't get what "נאמן 0.02%" means.
- **Screens 3–5:** I scrolled with my thumb. Lots of funds and lots of banks with red minus numbers, getting bigger: אלטשולר and מיטב over −20,000. That put me off them. Then came a few English funds (iShares, Vanguard), and one with "בין +2,212 ל־−23,766", which scared me. I stopped at the bottom of screen 5, at "מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם". I didn't look at screens 6–10.

**Words I didn't get:** נאמן, צוברת, מחלקת דיבידנד, מנוטרלת מט"ח, ממונף ("4D"/"4A"), חשיפה לדולר, "סל", W-8BEN, "לא בחישוב", "אין שנה מלאה".

**What I'd change:** Put the bank name and the fund name right next to the big 86,492 on the first screen, so I don't have to scroll to find out what "הזול" is. The 1,057 ₪ under פסגות and the 0 ₪ next to it confused me: is it a cost or not? There are too many tags on every fund. The two lists are so long I almost got lost.
