## What it said along the way

I'd go with Pesagot as the account and the IBI ת"א-125 tracker fund, "אי.בי.אי. מחקה ת"א 125 (4A)". By the page's sums I'd end up with about **86,045 ₪** after 10 years. That's not for my own amounts though. It's for the page's example of 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month, which is 70,000 ₪ put in. It's after fees and tax, in today's money, and it assumes 7% a year. I'm fairly sure. What decided it was that the page itself says "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,045 ₪, אי.בי.אי. מחקה ת"א 125 (4A), פסגות", and that same pair is starred "הזול" at the top.

**How it went:**
- **First screen:** the heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" was exactly what I wanted. ת"א-125 was already picked. The amounts showed 10,000 ₪ and 500 ₪, not my 30,000. These are only screenshots, so I couldn't move the sliders, and I went with the page's numbers.
- **Result box:** "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪", then Pesagot, "הזול", 86,045 ₪. Next to it was another pair at 85,015 ₪, "−1.2%". That 86k is the number that stuck.
- **Fee bar and legend:** I glanced at it. "דמי משמרת" and "העמלות זו על זו" mean nothing to me.
- **List of accounts:** Pesagot first at 1,049 ₪ in costs. Leumi is second at 82,968 ₪, with 4,506 ₪ in costs. Leumi is a name I trust more, but 3,000 ₪ less is real money. Pesagot I've at least heard of. The bottom ones, אלטשולר טרייד, אקסלנס and מיטב טרייד, are around 63–65k with big red bars, so no way.
- **Fund card:** "דמי ניהול 0.1%" sounds like nothing. I skipped "נאמן 0.025%".
- **List of funds:** every one has a minus, from "−0.04%" up to "−0.54%", and "−1,030 ₪" and so on. That freaked me out a bit until I saw that the one I picked had the smallest minus.
- **Further down:** I skimmed the "what changes it most" chart and saw "שנים" with "18,905 ₪ יותר". The steps chart and the line "הכי הרבה..." confirmed my pick, so I stopped there.

**What I skipped:** the long grey explanation above the account list, the "לא בחישוב" notes and the folds under them, the note under the fund list ("מול המדד..."), and screens 12–13.

**Words I didn't get:** דמי משמרת, נאמן, העמלות זו על זו, "(4A)", מחקה versus סל (aren't they the same?), "קבועים / משתנים", "−0.04% בשנה · שנה אחת", "נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג".

**What I'd change:**
- The minus signs on every fund look like losses.
- The example amounts don't match mine, and that's easy to miss.
- I'd tell you up front how you actually open an account at Pesagot.
- Three legends in different colours is too much.
