## What it said along the way

I'm going with a Pesagot (פסגות) account and the Yelin Lapidot S&P 500 tracker fund (ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500, 4D, number 5139282). The page says I'd have about 86,500 ₪ after ten years, but that's for 10,000 ₪ up front plus 500 a month. With my 30,000 it should be more. I'm fairly sure about the account and the fund, and less sure about the amount.

**Where I looked, in order:**
- **Top of the page:** the heading "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד" looked like exactly what I came for. S&P 500 was already selected. The amount boxes showed 10,000 now, 500 a month, 10 years and 7%. The big result was 86,492 ₪ "נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס", against 70,000 ₪ put in.
- **Fund list (left):** Yelin Lapidot is first and marked "הבחירה". The rest are just more S&P funds.
- **Account list (right):** Pesagot 86,492, Leumi 83,396, Mizrahi lower down. A few at the bottom drop to about 63,000.
- **Where I stopped:** the "מה הכי משנה" chart and table. "שנים 12 → 19,116 ₪ יותר" was the only line that stuck: staying longer gets you more.

**What I skipped:** the coloured cost bar and its legend, the long grey account notes ("לא בחישוב…"), the foreign ETF rows ("אין מחיר כאן"), the "עם נטרול מט"ח" block, the paragraph explaining the list, and everything below the table.
