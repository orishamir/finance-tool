# Predictions of the founder's choices

Before the founder sees a choice, the lead writes here which option it expects him to pick and why, and commits it.
After he decides, his choice and the match are added in the same row. The running hit rate is the measure of whether
the lead's judgement can be trusted with larger features (founder, 2026-11-05: "we need to achieve this").

A miss gets a lesson in `lessons.md` beside this file: what the lead weighed wrongly.
