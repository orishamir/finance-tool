# Other sites

The founder's reactions to sites that have nothing to do with this one (his idea, 2026-11-05: "send me to some
random sites … and i tell you my problem or what i like about them"). They show what bothers him and what he likes
on screens no one here built, so they carry no trace of this project's decisions.

Each folder holds his words exactly as he wrote them (`words.md`), the keys the grader reads (`case.json`: one
neutral sentence per problem he named, and the things he liked, which a reviewer should not flag), the page as a
person saw it the same night (`screens/`, kept out of git because the pages are not ours), and the reviewer's
blind run on the same page.

These are a set of their own. They never enter the held-out score in `metric.md`, which counts only problems on this
site; they test whether the reviewer flags what he flags on a page it has never seen, and their reasons go to
`lessons.md` like any other flag. A reason that recurs here and on this site weighs more than one seen once.

The form listed 15 sites; he answered the first two on 2026-11-06 and may answer more later.
