# What I'd do with this page: 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month into the S&P 500 for 10 years

## 1. My decision

I'd open an account at **Psagot** (פסגות) and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**, fund number **5139282**.

The first block says it plainly: "אחד משני הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם" (one of the two cheapest combinations for your plan), with **86,492 ₪** left after 10 years. Its fees are low, and these fee terms I recognise from my pension: "דמי ניהול: 0.02%", "קבוע 0%", "משתנה 0%". Psagot is also #1 in the accounts list, at "1,057 ₪" for the account over 10 years. The banks start at #2, "לאומי", with 83,396 ₪.

**How sure I am:** about 70% on the fund and maybe 60% on Psagot.

- **The fund:** the other half of the "two cheapest" is "אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" at the same broker, and it is only "‎−43 ₪" behind. The page says "ההפרש קטן מכדי לקבוע איזה זוג זול יותר, ולכן שני הזוגות שקולים" (the difference is too small to say which is cheaper, so treat them as equal). So I'm not worried about choosing wrong between those two.
- **Psagot:** I'm less sure because I know nothing about them, and I'd rather not open an account at a company I've never heard of. My own bank's cost isn't shown on the page as a comparison, though I may have missed it.

## 2. What I used to decide

- **The four boxes at the top:** amount today, monthly deposit, years, and yearly return (סכום היום / הפקדה חודשית / שנים / תשואה בשנה). I checked that they matched my plan: 10,000 / 500 / 10.
- **The result block right under them.** It holds the "70,000 ₪" I'd deposit, the highlighted "אחד משני הזוגות הזולים ביותר" box, and the "הזוג הבא בתור" (next pair) box next to it. This block basically made the decision for me.
- **The bottom box of the Psagot card**, with its fee line for the fund.
- **"כל החשבונות" (all accounts), on the right.** I only looked at the top 5 or so, to see where my kind of bank lands.
- **"כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500" (all S&P 500 funds), on the left.** I only looked at the top 2 or 3, to confirm the fund really is first.
