# What I decided after reading my friend's screenshots

I read all seven pictures. In plain words, this is what I'd do, and where the page helped me and where it lost me.

## 1. My decision

**Where I'd buy:** Psagot (פסגות) for the account.
**Which fund:** "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", fund number 5139282.

**How sure I am about the account:** fairly sure. Psagot is at the top of the account list. On my numbers I'd end with **86,492 ₪**, and the next account, Leumi, gives **83,396 ₪**. That gap of about 3,000 ₪ is real money to me. The page says Psagot's own charges over 10 years come to **1,057 ₪**. Leumi's are 4,541 ₪, and the small trading accounts at the bottom (אלטשולר טרייד, אקסלנס, מיטב טרייד) are over 23,000 ₪. That surprised me.

**How sure I am about the fund:** not very, and the page tells me so itself. Next to the second-best pair it says:

> "ההפרש קטן מכדי לקבוע איזה זוג זול יותר, ולכן שני הזוגות שקולים."

So Yelin Lapidot and Altshuler Shaham (אלטשולר שחם, 86,449 ₪) are a coin toss, 43 ₪ apart over ten years. I'd take Yelin because it's on top, but Altshuler would be just as good. Both have only "שנה אחת" (one year) of history, which makes me a little uneasy.

**What I would not do:** buy the dollar-hedged fund (נטרול מט"ח). The page says it could leave me anywhere from **+2,212 ₪ to −23,766 ₪** compared with my pick. I don't want that kind of gamble.

## 2. The parts I actually used

- **The four boxes at the top** (סכום היום, הפקדה חודשית, שנים, תשואה בשנה). They were already set to my 10,000 / 500 / 10 years.
- **The big result box.** It shows "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪", the winning pair at 86,492 ₪, and the runner-up with the "שקולים" sentence. This box gave me 80% of my decision.
- **The coloured bar under "כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים"**, which shows where the money goes. That's how I learned most of my cost is **tax (5,703 ₪)**, not the bank. So what does that mean for my money? Wherever I go, the tax man takes the most.
- **The two small cards** for Psagot and Yelin Lapidot. The fund's fee is "0.02% נאמן", with 0% קבוע and 0% משתנה.
- **"כל החשבונות" (the account list)**, to see that Psagot really is cheapest and by how much.
- **The "עם נטרול מט"ח או בלי" section**, mostly the plain sentence at the end. It says that if the dollar doesn't move, I'll be left with more without the hedge.

5. **"אינפלציה 2% בשנה" button and the grey line about today's money.** Skimmed, and partly confused. Are 86,492 ₪ today's shekels or future shekels? I think today's ("בכסף של היום"), but I'm not certain.

11. **"כל החשבונות" (account list).** Used. See question 4.

## 4. In the two lists, what I looked at and what I ignored

**Account list ("כל החשבונות"):**
- **Looked at:** the bank name, the big number (what I'd be left with), and the small number under it (what the account costs me over 10 years).
- **Looked at:** the grey labels "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים, לחיילים…". None of them apply to me at 61, so I ignored them after reading.
- **Looked at:** "מחיר להוראת קבע" under Leumi, Hapoalim (הבינלאומי) and Mizrahi. Psagot doesn't have that label, and that worried me. See question 5.
- **Looked at:** "אין נתון" for אינטראקטיב ישראל and Interactive Brokers. Fine, they're not for me anyway.
- **Ignored:** the coloured bars (orange, yellow, red). I didn't try to work out which colour was which.
- **Ignored:** the explanation paragraph at the top of the list. It was too long.

**Fund list ("כל הקרנות"):**
- **Looked at:** the fund name, and the right-hand number "לעומת הבחירה" (−43 ₪, −213 ₪, −447 ₪…). That told me what I'd lose by choosing a different fund.
- **Looked at, half-understood:** "מול המדד +0.37% בשנה · שנה אחת". I understood it after reading the note below the list. I noticed Yelin and Altshuler have just one year of history, while Kesem (קסם) and Harel (הראל) have 4 years.
- **Looked at:** "עוד לא נאסף" and "אין מחיר כאן" on the foreign funds. Fine, I skip those.
- **Ignored:** almost all the coloured tags (קרן מחקה, צוברת, חשיפה לדולר, כמה דרכים יותר, מס גם על שינוי במטבע, עסקה חוץ, נקנית בחו"ל, צריך טופס W-8BEN). There were too many, and I don't know what most of them mean for my money.
- **Ignored:** the fund numbers (5139282 etc.), until the moment I'd actually have to buy.
- **Ignored:** the filter buttons at the top ("סנן לפי תווית").

## 5. What I didn't understand

- **"(4D)" and "(4A)".** What are they? The page never says.
- **"0.02% נאמן".** What is a "נאמן" (trustee) fee, and is 0.02% all I pay for the fund? Is there a hidden fee?
- **"עמלות זו על זו", "דמי משמרת", "המרת מטבע".** These appear in the legend with no explanation.
- **"מס גם על שינוי במטבע".** It sounds bad, but how much does it cost me?
- **"כמה דרכים יותר" and "חשיפה לדולר".** No idea.
- **Psagot and "הוראת קבע" (standing order).** Leumi's price is marked "מחיר להוראת קבע" and Psagot's isn't. Does the Psagot price assume I buy by hand every month? Can I set up an automatic 500 ₪ a month there at all?
- **Why the cheaper pair pays more tax** ("מס 14 ₪ יותר"). I sort of get it, but it took effort.
- **Which date is right.** I saw both 04.11.2026 and 05.11.2026.

## 6. What I wanted to know and couldn't find

- **How to actually open an account with Psagot and set up the 500 ₪ monthly purchase.** Is it online only? Do I need to go to a branch?
- **Whether my money is safe if Psagot or the fund company has trouble.** For a 61-year-old, that matters more to me than 43 ₪.
- **What happens if I need the money at 65 or 68.** Can I take some out, and what tax do I pay then?
- **What a bad case looks like.** The page shows 10% and 20% less return, but never what happens if the market crashes the year before I need the money.
- **What the tax rate actually is** (I assume 25%), and whether there's any way to lower it.
- **Who runs this site.** "אתר אישי" (a personal site), but whose?

## 7. If half the page had to go, what goes first

1. **The long "נקודת התחלה לדוגמה" lists at the bottom.** They repeat the account and fund lists.
2. **The staircase chart** ("מתחילים מבחירה אחת…"). Nice, but I'd already decided.
3. **Most of the hedging section's charts and the year-by-year table.** Keep only the one plain sentence: "הקרן עם הנטרול תשאיר יותר; אחרת, הקרן בלי נטרול".
4. **The "מה הכי משנה" chart.** Keep only the one line about years.
5. **The coloured tags in the fund list.** Keep just one or two that change my money, and explain them.

I'd keep the top: my four numbers, the result box with "שקולים", the cost bar, and the two lists with just the names and the "what's left" numbers.
