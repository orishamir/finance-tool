**1. My decision**

I'd open an account at **פסגות** and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"**. If that fund turned out to be a hassle, I'd take **"אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D)"** instead.

I'm about 70% sure. The box near the top told me what I needed to know: "אחד משני הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתיעות שלכם" with **86,492 ₪**, and right next to it "‎₪43− לעומת הזוג הראשון… ההפרש קטן מכדי לקבוע איזה זוג זול יותר, ולכן שני הזוגות שקולים". That made it simple: Psagot, and either of the two funds.

Two things keep me from being fully sure:
- I've never heard of פסגות. My salary goes into a regular bank.
- Under לאומי it says "לא בחשבון: מחיר אחר לצעירים". I'm 24, so Leumi might actually be cheaper for me, and the page doesn't say.

**2. What I actually used**

- The headline "איפה ואיך הכי זול להשקיע במדד, לאורך זמן", to confirm the page was for me.
- The index buttons, to check S&P 500 was selected.
- The four sliders. I checked they were already on 10,000 / 500 / 10 years.
- "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪", so I could compare it with ~86,000.
- **The two cards with the cheapest pairs.** This is where I made my decision.
- The top of the "כל החשבונות" list, to see that Psagot is #1 and how far behind the banks are.
- Quickly, the top of the "כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500" list, to see the Yelin Lapidot fund marked "הבחירה" and Altshuler at "‎−43 ₪".

**3. Block by block**

- **Header, index buttons, the S&P 500 text:** Used. "הכסף מושקע בדולרים דרך קרן שעוקבת אחריו" was clear enough.
- **Sliders and "אינפלציה 2% בשנה":** I used the sliders. I didn't understand the 7% one and left it alone. The line "בכסף של היום · שער הדולר 3.16 ₪" I skimmed.
- **The two cheapest pairs:** Used. This was the most useful part of the page.
- **"כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים", the coloured bars:** Skimmed. 6,876 against 6,919 shows they're about the same. I didn't need the legend ("דמי משמרת", "עמלות זו על זו", "המרת מטבע"…). The lines "החשבון ₪1 יותר / הקרן ₪58 פחות / המס ₪14 יותר" just confused me. Why would one cost less but leave me less money?
- **Psagot card ("מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: ₪1,057"):** Skimmed. It was good to see the number is small.
- **"כל החשבונות" list:** Used the top few rows, then skimmed the rest. I stopped at "אין נתון" for Interactive.
- **Fund card (5139282, "דמי ניהול 0% … נאמן 0.02%"):** Skimmed. 0% sounds good, but I don't know what a "נאמן" is.
- **"כל הקרנות" list:** Skimmed the top. The "עם נטרול מט״ח" fund with "בין ₪2,212+ ל־₪23,766−" scared me, so I avoided it.
- **"למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות":** Skipped. It's a wall of text.
- **"מה הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם" (chart and table):** Skimmed. I took away one thing: years and the monthly deposit matter most ("שנים … ‎₪19,116 יותר"). It's nice but didn't help me choose.
- **"עם נטרול מט״ח או בלי" through "השורה התחתונה":** Skipped most of it. Long, full of percentages, and the table had overlapping numbers I couldn't read. I gathered only that "בלי נטרול" is the normal choice.
- **"מתחילים מבחירה אחת" (step chart from Jerusalem MTF):** Confused me. Why start from Jerusalem? The lists under it ("חשבון אחר, אותו קרן", "סוג קרן אחר", "קרן אחרת מאותו סוג") felt like a repeat with different numbers (84,148 instead of 86,492?), which made me less sure.
- **Footer notes ("התשואה שבחישוב: תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית", "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"):** Skimmed. They're reassuring.

**4. In the lists**

- **Accounts:**
  - I looked at the account name, the big "נשאר" number, the rank number, and the grey label "לא בחשבון: מחיר אחר לצעירים…".
  - I glanced at "מחיר להוראת קבע" without really understanding it.
  - I ignored the small coloured bar and the small number under it (4,541 ₪ and so on). I wasn't sure whether that's a cost or what.
  - I ignored the drop-down arrows.
- **Funds:**
  - I looked at the name, the "לעומת הבחירה" number (−43, −213…), and the yellow/green "בלי נטרול / עם נטרול" headers.
  - I ignored the fund number (5139282), the "קרן מחקה / קרן סל / עסקת החלף" tags, and "+0.37% בשנה · שנה אחת". I didn't know if plus means good.

- "נטרול מט״ח"
- "הוראת קבע"
- "דמי משמרת"
- "עמלות זו על זו"
- "נאמן"
- "דמי ניהול משתנים"
- "UCITS ETF … Accumulating"
- "פער מהמדד … נקודות"
- "אין נתון"
- Why the same Psagot choice shows 86,492 in one place and 84,148 in another.
- Whether "בכסף של היום" means I'll actually get more than 86k.

**6. What I wanted and couldn't find**

- What פסגות actually is, whether it's safe, and how I open an account there. Is it on my phone? Is there a minimum?
- Whether "מחיר לצעירים" at Leumi or Hapoalim applies at 24, and whether it would beat Psagot.
- How I set up the 500 ₪ each month.
- What happens if I need the money out early.
- How tax works when I sell.

**7. If half the page had to go, I'd cut these first**

- The whole currency-hedging section: the boxes, both bar charts, and the year table.
- The "מתחילים מבחירה אחת" step chart and its three lists.
- The "למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר" text.
- The repeated colour legends.
- The "מה הכי משנה" chart. I'd keep only the one sentence about years mattering most.

I'd keep the sliders, the two-pair cards, and the top of both lists.
