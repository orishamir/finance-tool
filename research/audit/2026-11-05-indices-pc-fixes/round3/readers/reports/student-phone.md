## 1. My decision

**Account: פסגות (Psagot). Fund: "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" (Yelin Lapidot).** The Altshuler Shaham fund, "אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", would be just as good.

**Expected result:** about **86,500 ₪ after 10 years**, from 70,000 ₪ that I put in (10,000 now plus 500 a month). That number is in today's money, after fees and tax. It only holds if the market really gives 7% a year and "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel). The dollar part alone could swing it by a few thousand.

**How sure am I?**
- **Account: fairly sure.** Psagot is first in the account list with "★ הזול" (the cheap one). Its fees come to 1,057 ₪ over 10 years. The banks are at 4,500 ₪ and up.
- **Fund: I don't really care which.** The page says the top funds are a tie.
- **Not sure about one thing:** many banks show "לא בחישוב · מחיר אחר לצעירים... לסטודנטים" (not in the calculation: different price for young people, students). I'm a student, so a bank could be cheaper for me, and the page doesn't say by how much.

**What I used:** the two result boxes at the top, the cost bars under them, the account list with its "נשאר" (what's left) column, and the fund list. I skimmed the hedging section and the steps chart. They told me to avoid the hedged fund and the foreign account, and nothing more.
