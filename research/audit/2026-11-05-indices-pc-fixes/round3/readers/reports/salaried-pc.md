## 1. My decision

**Account: Psagot (פסגות). Fund: Yelin Lapidot's S&P 500 tracker, "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282.** If I'd picked Altshuler Shaham's equivalent fund (5139100) instead, I'd be just as happy.

**How much I'd expect:** I put in 10,000 ₪ now and 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, which is 70,000 ₪ in total. The page says about **86,492 ₪** is left at the end, "אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום" (after fees and tax, in today's money). That assumes the index returns 7% a year "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel).

**How sure I am:**
- **The pair:** fairly sure. Psagot is ranked #1 with "הזול ★" (the cheapest). The two Israeli tracker funds are within 43 ₪ of each other over 10 years, and the page itself calls them equal.
- **The amount:** not sure at all. 7% is just an example. The page also shows the dollar moving 12–13% in a single year, and says one percentage point a year is worth 3,400–4,400 ₪ by the end.

**Two things I'd check before opening anything:**
- Several banks show "לא בחישוב · מחיר אחר לצעירים" (not in the calculation; different price for young people). I'm 29 and don't know whether I count as young.
- My own bank isn't on the list.

**Parts of the page I used:**
- The top result boxes.
- The coloured cost bars and the "החשבון / הקרן / המס" comparison under them.
- The account list (Psagot #1, 1,057 ₪ of account costs over 10 years).
- The fund list.
- The step chart at the bottom. It showed that switching account was worth about 5,000 ₪ and switching fund about 2,300 ₪.
