I looked at all seven screenshots. I don't have set A, so I can't answer item 6.

**1. The account form as it opens: "בחרו את הבנק או בית ההשקעות. מתחילים מהתעריפון שלו ומשנים רק את המחירים שקיבלתם."**
I pick my bank from the "על בסיס" dropdown. The form fills in that bank's official prices, and I only change the ones where I got a better deal. For me that might be the buy and sell commission. Anything I don't touch stays at the official price.
- **What's unclear:** "המחירים שקיבלתם" could mean only prices I negotiated. It could also mean every price I actually pay, from my statement. My friend chose "not on the list", so I never saw what the form looks like after choosing a real bank. I'm assuming the fields come pre-filled.

**2. After choosing: "החשבון נשמר רק במכשיר הזה… שדה ריק הוא מחיר לא ידוע… עמלה או מינימום שלא גובים מכם: כתבו 0."**
- The account stays on this computer only, so it won't be on my phone.
- It gets compared like the other accounts.
- An empty field means "I don't know", and then the account gets no rank. If I'm sure there's no charge, I type 0.
- The fields my friend saw:
  - "עמלת קנייה ומכירה": אחוז 0.08, "מינימום לפעולה" left empty
  - "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך": "אחוז לשנה" 0.1, "מינימום לחודש" left empty
  - "המרת מטבע": 0.2
  - "דמי חשבון קבועים": 0 ₪ a month

**What I trip on:**
- My statement uses "דמי משמרת". The page's colour legend also says "דמי משמרת" and "דמי ניהול חשבון". The form says "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך" and "דמי חשבון קבועים". I'm guessing these are the same charges, but I'm not sure.
- "מינימום לפעולה": I think it's the smallest commission per trade, in shekels.
- "עמלה או מינימום שלא גובים מכם": I'm not sure I'd know for certain that there is no minimum. If I'm unsure, should I leave it empty?

**3. The fund form: "הקרן נשמרת רק במכשיר הזה, ברשימה של המדד הזה, ומתומחרת כמו כל קרן מהסוג שלה. שדה ריק הוא דמי ניהול לא ידועים…"**
The fund is saved on this computer and shows up only in the S&P 500 list. I think "מהסוג שלה" means the type I chose, here "קרן מחקה ישראלית". Its cost is then worked out the same way as other funds of that type. An empty fee field means the fee is unknown and no cost is shown. A fee the fund doesn't charge gets 0.

**What I trip on:**
- **"דמי ניהול משתנים":** I don't know what this is. My statement just shows one "דמי ניהול". I don't know where to find the "קבועים" and "משתנים" parts separately, or whether my fund has a variable fee at all.
- **"שכר נאמן":** Does this count as one of the "דמי ניהול" the sentence talks about?
- **Units:** The account form said "אחוז לשנה", but the fund fields only show "%". I assume it's a yearly percentage.
- **The note inside the form:** "אי אפשר לחשב… השלימו אותם בעריכה" appears in the preview while I'm still filling in the form. That made me wonder if I had to save first and then edit.

**4. The saved account row: "חסרים מחירים, ולכן החשבון לא מדורג. השלימו אותם בעריכה." and "לא הזנתם, ולכן לא ידוע: …"**
"הבנק שלי" has no rank number and no shekel amount, just a dash and "אין נתון". The row shows what was typed: 0.08%, 0.1%, 0.2% and 0 ₪. It also lists what's missing: "מינימום לפעולה בעמלת קנייה ומכירה ומינימום לחודש בדמי ניהול פיקדון ני"ע". I fill those in with the "עריכה" link and then it gets ranked. That's clear to me.
- **What could confuse:** The row sits in the middle of the list, between "אינטראקטיב ישראל" and "Interactive Brokers". At a glance it could look like it has a place in the ranking. It doesn't.

**5. The saved fund row: "אי אפשר לחשב: חלק מדמי הניהול של הקרן לא הוזנו…" and "לא הזנתם, ולכן לא ידוע: דמי ניהול משתנים."**
"המחקה שלי" shows "אין מחיר כאן" and can't be compared until I enter the variable fee, or 0 if there isn't one. It shows what I did enter: 0.1% "דמי ניהול קבועים" and 0.02% "שכר נאמן". That part is clear.

**What I trip on:**
- It also says "עוד לא נאסף". Collected by whom? I typed this fund in myself, so I don't know what is still waiting to be collected.
- The preview in the form had a "קרן ישראלית" tag. The saved row doesn't show it.

**6.** I don't have set A, so I can't say what those dates mean.

**What I'd actually do if my bank gave me a lower commission**
1. Open "+ הוספת חשבון בתנאים שקיבלתם".
2. Choose my bank under "על בסיס", if it's on the list.
3. Change only the "עמלת קנייה ומכירה" percentage to my deal.
4. Save, and compare its rank with the others.

**What I wouldn't know how to fill in:**
- Does my deal have its own minimum per trade, and is that in shekels or dollars?
- Is my lower commission only for Israeli trades or also for US ones? The form has just one commission field.
- Is "דמי משמרת" on my statement the same as "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך" here?
- My bank's deal might end on a certain date. I don't see anywhere to put that.

For a fund I'd type in myself, I wouldn't know where to find its "דמי ניהול משתנים", or whether 0 is the honest answer.
