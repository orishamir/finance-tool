I went through all eight screenshots. Here is what I took from each line.

**1. The first form as it opens: "בחרו את הבנק או בית ההשקעות. מתחילים מהתעריפון שלו ומשנים רק את המחירים שקיבלתם."**

What it tells me: I pick my bank from the box marked "על בסיס" (it says "בחרו"). The form then starts from that bank's official price list, and I only change the prices the bank gave me that are different. So I don't have to type everything.

What's unclear:
- I don't really know what a "בית השקעות" is. I'm guessing it's some kind of non-bank company.
- "על בסיס" is a strange label for a dropdown, but the sentence above it explains it.
- My friend picked "not in the list", so I never saw what the form looks like after picking a real bank. I'm guessing the boxes come pre-filled.

**2. The first form after choosing: "החשבון נשמר רק במכשיר הזה… שדה ריק הוא מחיר לא ידוע, והחשבון לא ידורג עד שתמלאו אותו. עמלה או מינימום שלא גובים מכם: כתבו 0."**

What it tells me:
- My account stays only on my phone.
- If I leave a box empty, the site treats that price as unknown and won't put my account in the ranking.
- If my bank doesn't charge something, I should type 0, not leave it blank.

So I must fill every box. My friend left "מינימום לפעולה" and "מינימום לחודש" empty, and those are exactly the boxes I'd also leave empty without thinking.

What's unclear:
- "מינימום": the minimum of what? I think it means the smallest fee they'll charge on each buy, or each month, even if the percent works out lower. That's a guess.
- "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך" means nothing to me. I get "דמי ניהול" (management fee), but not "פיקדון ניירות ערך".
- "מושווה כמו כל חשבון אחר" sounds like it gets compared right away, but the next sentence says it won't be ranked while a box is empty.

**3. The second form: "הקרן נשמרת רק במכשיר הזה, ברשימה של המדד הזה, ומתומחרת כמו כל קרן מהסוג שלה. שדה ריק הוא דמי ניהול לא ידועים, והקרן לא תתומחר עד שתמלאו אותו. דמי ניהול שהקרן לא גובה: כתבו 0."**

What it tells me: same idea as the account form. The fund is saved on my phone, in the S&P 500 list. If any fee box is empty, no price is calculated for it. If there's no such fee, I type 0.

What's unclear:
- "מהסוג שלה": I think that means the "סוג הקרן" box (my friend picked "קרן מחקה ישראלית").
- I don't know the difference between "דמי ניהול קבועים" and "דמי ניהול משתנים", or where I'd find the second one.
- "שכר נאמן": no idea what that is.
- Lower on the form, picking "לא ידוע" for dividends says "הקרן תופיע בלי מחיר". So an empty fee box isn't the only thing that leaves the fund without a price, and the top text doesn't mention that.
- Good: the bottom of the form already warned "אי אפשר לחשב…" before saving.

**4. The saved account row: "חסרים מחירים, ולכן החשבון לא מדורג. השלימו אותם בעריכה." and "לא הזנתם, ולכן לא ידוע: …"**

What it tells me: "הבנק שלי" saved, but it has no total amount and no place in the ranking. It shows what my friend typed (0.08%, 0.1%, 0.2%, 0 ₪) and says exactly what's missing: "מינימום לפעולה בעמלת קנייה ומכירה ומינימום לחודש בדמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך". There's an "עריכה" button to fix it. That's clear.

What's unclear: the orange label on top says "אין נתון", as if there's no data at all, while most of it was filled in. "חסרים מחירים" says it better.

**5. The saved fund row: "אי אפשר לחשב: חלק מדמי הניהול של הקרן לא הוזנו. השלימו אותם בעריכה." and "לא הזנתם, ולכן לא ידוע: דמי ניהול משתנים."**

What it tells me: "המחקה שלי" has no number. The left side says "אין מחיר כאן" because the "דמי ניהול משתנים" box was empty. I should go to "עריכה" and type a number, or 0. Clear.

What's unclear: under it, it says "עוד לא נאסף". I don't know what wasn't collected yet. The real iShares fund has the same label, so I can't tell if it's about my missing fee or something else.

**6. Set A**

- **"התעריפונים נאספו בין 04.11.2026 ל־05.11.2026"**: the banks' price lists were taken two days ago and yesterday, so they're fresh.
- **"נאסף ב־…" next to "התעריפון"**: I didn't find this line on any of my screenshots, so I can't say what it says.
- **"הנתון האחרון באתר נבדק ב־05.11.2026"** (bottom of the page): I read it as "the site's data was last checked yesterday".
  - It could also mean "the newest piece of data was checked then", which isn't the same thing.
  - I'm not sure "נבדק" (checked) means the same as "נאסף" (collected).

**If my bank gave me a lower commission than its price list:**

1. Tap "+ הוספת חשבון בתנאים שקיבלתם".
2. In "על בסיס", pick my actual bank (for example לאומי) instead of "not in the list", so everything starts from its price list.
3. Change only "עמלת קנייה ומכירה – אחוז" to my number.
4. Leave every other box as it is, and make sure none are empty (0 where they don't charge).
5. Save with "שמירה במכשיר".

What I wouldn't know how to fill in:
- **Which number was lowered.** If the bank says "you pay X", I can't tell whether that's the "אחוז" or the "מינימום לפעולה", or whether the minimum changed too.
- **The safekeeping fee.** Whether my deal also changed "דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך" (I don't know what that is) or "דמי חשבון קבועים".
- **My student price.** Leumi's row already says "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר… לסטודנטים". If I got the student price, I'd need to type it myself, and I don't know all its parts.
- **The fund form.** I couldn't fill "דמי ניהול משתנים" or "שכר נאמן" without knowing where to look them up.
- **Using another phone.** I'd have to remember it's saved only on this phone, though the bottom of the page has "העברה למכשיר אחר".
