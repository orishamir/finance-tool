I already have a securities account at my bank, so the question for me was whether moving is worth it and which fund to buy. These are my reactions after going through all 18 screenshots.

---

## 1. My decision

**Account:** Psagot. **Fund:** "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282. I'd be just as happy with "אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", 5139100.

**How much at the end:** about **86,500 ₪**. That assumes the page's example: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, 70,000 ₪ put in, 7% a year. The page says this is "בכסף של היום, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס", and "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל".

**How sure:**
- **Psagot as the account:** fairly sure. Its row says "★ הזול", with 1,057 ₪ of account costs over 10 years. The bank rows (Leumi, Hapoalim, Discount, Mercantile and the rest) are mostly 2,000–5,000 ₪ worse at the end, with account costs of roughly 4,500–10,600 ₪. My bank is in there somewhere, and on these numbers moving looks worth it.
- **Which of the top two funds:** a coin toss. They are 43 ₪ apart over 10 years.
- **The 86,500 figure itself:** not sure at all. The 7% is "לדוגמה של האתר", and the dollar alone moved between "−12.5%" and "+13.4%" in a single year. That's far bigger than any fee difference here.
- **The ranking:** a note at the very bottom shook me a bit: "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות". I couldn't see which places change.

**What I used:**
- The two result boxes at the top.
- The bars under "כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים". For Psagot + Yelin they show 6,876 ₪ in total, of which 5,703 is tax.
- The ranked account list on the right, the fund list on the left, and the fund card showing "דמי ניהול 0% קבוע 0% משתנה 0.02% נאמן".

## 2. Line by line

### a. "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר, ולכן הם שקולים."

**What it tells me:** the top three account-and-fund pairs cost practically the same, so I shouldn't agonise over which one to take. The next sentence names the third pair: "הזוג השלישי: פסגות עם iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating) 1159250."

So the three pairs are:
1. Psagot + Yelin Lapidot 5139282, 86,492 ₪
2. Psagot + Altshuler Shaham 5139100, 86,449 ₪ ("−43 ₪ לעומת הזוג שמימין")
3. Psagot + iShares Accumulating 1159250

**Could I find the third one?** Yes. It's the third row in the fund list, marked "−213 ₪", with "קרן חוץ בת״א".

**What tripped me up:**
- **"קטנים מכדי"**: 213 ₪ counts as "too small to tell", but the next fund at −447 ₪ apparently doesn't. I don't know where the line is.
- **"שקולים"**: if they're equal, why does Psagot's row still say "★ הזול", and why is only one of them "בחירת האתר"?
- **The same fund name twice:** the list has two rows called "iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating)". One is 1159250 and the other is IE00B5BMR087. I had to work out that the second is the same fund bought abroad, and that it has no price at Psagot.

### b. "לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר" and "בחירת האתר"

**What it tells me:** the column shows how many shekels more or less I'd end up with in each fund than in the fund the site picked. For example "−447 ₪" means 447 ₪ less. "בחירת האתר" marks the Yelin Lapidot row as that picked fund.

**What tripped me up:**
- **"בחר"**: is that a recommendation, or just "the cheapest one, shown by default"? The top of the page says "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד", so "the site chose" feels a bit like a recommendation sneaking in.
- **Which account the comparison assumes:** I assumed every row is compared inside Psagot. The "אין מחיר כאן" on the abroad funds supports that, but the page doesn't say it outright.
- **The column changes name:** after I tapped something else, the header became "לעומת הבחירה".

### c. Set C: "גם הקרן הוחלפה." and "חזרה לפסגות ולקרן הקודמת"

**What happened:** I tapped Interactive Brokers, so that became my account. The page also swapped my fund from Yelin Lapidot to the iShares Accumulating fund bought abroad (IE00B5BMR087). The end amount dropped to 82,842 ₪, and the other box says "+3,650 ₪ יותר" if I go back.

**Why the fund changed:** I'm guessing it's because of the earlier Interactive Brokers text: "אין קרן ישראלית ואין מחיר בשקלים". The row itself only says "גם הקרן הוחלפה". It doesn't say why, or which fund it switched to. I had to look at the fund list to see that.

**What the link would do:** I'd expect it to put me back on Psagot with Yelin Lapidot.

**What tripped me up:**
- **"הקודמת"**: the previous fund means Yelin, I think.
- **The ranking under this choice:** "אינטראקטיב ישראל" sits above Interactive Brokers here, at 84,181 ₪ with the same fund. So even if I wanted to go abroad, IB wasn't the best option.

### d. Set D: "החשבון עבר לאינטראקטיב ישראל" and "חזרה לפסגות ולקרן הקודמת"

**What happened:** I picked the iShares fund bought abroad. Psagot has no price for it ("אין מחיר כאן / בחירה בה מעבירה את החשבון לאינטראקטיב ישראל"), so the page moved my account to Interactive Israel. It's the top account for that fund, at 84,181 ₪.

**What the link would do:** I'd expect it to undo both changes, back to Psagot and Yelin. This one is clearer than in (c), because I chose the fund myself and "הקרן הקודמת" is obviously Yelin.

**What tripped me up:**
- **"אינטראקטיב ישראל" vs "Interactive Brokers":** at first glance I wasn't sure these were two different companies.

### e. Set D: the box "הבחירה שלכם"

It shows:
- **Account:** "אינטראקטיב ישראל"
- **Fund:** "IE00B5BMR087 iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accu…"
- **End amount:** 84,181 ₪

The box next to it says Psagot + Yelin would give "+2,311 ₪ יותר". The bars below explain the gap: account costs 244 ₪ more, the fund 288 ₪ more, and tax 1,779 ₪ more.

The fund name is cut off at "Accu…". I only knew it was "Accumulating" from the line underneath.

### f. Sets D and G: Psagot's grey row

**The collapsed row (set D)** shows "אין נתון", then two lines quoting Psagot's price list: "עמלת ני"ע באסיה ובאירופה עשויה להשתנות בהתאם לסוג המניה ואופי הפעילות…". The text is cut off at "על מנ...".

**What it tells me:** Psagot doesn't publish a fixed price for buying in Europe, so the site can't price this fund there.

**The line under it** says "בחירה בחשבון הזה מעבירה גם לקרן ילין לפידות… ונשארים 86,492 ₪". So if I pick Psagot, I also go back to the Yelin fund.

**The expanded row (set G), after "ההסבר המלא",** adds two things:
- "אף מסמך של פסגות לא מונה את הבורסות באירופה שאפשר לקנות בהן". So I don't even know whether Psagot can buy this fund at all.
- The dollar-conversion fee, "1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל", can be read as anything between 0.48% and 1.5%, "ולכן האתר לא מחשב לה מחיר".

**The two links:**
- "ההסבר המלא" opens the full explanation.
- "הסבר מקוצר" folds it back up.
- I assume neither one changes my selection.

**What tripped me up:**
- **"1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל":** I'd have read it as 1.5 agorot per dollar converted, which is about half a percent. I'm glad the page flagged that it could be read another way.
- **"אין נתון":** on a grey row, I wasn't sure at first whether it meant "Psagot is bad" or just "we don't know".

## 3. Other things that stopped me or that I couldn't find

- **"4D" vs "4A":** I don't know what these codes mean. I gathered that 4A is the currency-hedged one ("מנוטרלת מט״ח"), but nothing I saw explains 4D.
- **The tags:** "עסקת החלף", "כמה דרכים יחד" and "מס גם על שינוי המטבע" weren't explained in what I saw.
- **"+0.37% בשנה · שנה אחת":** Yelin's record against the index is based on only one year, and the third pair says "עוד לא נאסף". I don't know if that tracking difference is already counted in the 86,492.
- **Moving from my bank:** what does it cost to transfer my existing holdings, or to sell them and pay tax? How do I open a Psagot account? I couldn't find either.
- **My own deal:** the "לא בחישוב" chips show special prices for soldiers, students and so on. None apply to me at 41. But "קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו" is useful, because I may have a negotiated rate at my bank.
- **The waterfall example:** it starts from "ירושלים" + MTF at 79,176 ₪. I didn't understand why that was the starting point; it just says "לדוגמה".
- **What matters more than fees:** "מה הכי משנה" showed that 20% more years adds 19,116 ₪ and more monthly saving adds 14,474 ₪. That puts the 43–213 ₪ differences between funds in perspective. Saving more and for longer matters far more than which of the top funds I pick.
