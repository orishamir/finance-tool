# Which account and fund I'd pick, based on my friend's screenshots

## 1. My decision

**Account:** Psagot (פסגות). **Fund:** Yelin Lapidot S&P 500 (4D) tracker fund, number 5139282 ("ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)").

**How much I'd expect:** I left the page's starting numbers as they were: 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year. That's 70,000 ₪ put in. The page says I'd end up with about **86,500 ₪**, "בכסף של היום", after fees and tax. I read that as today's money, adjusted for inflation.

**How sure I am:**
- **The pick: fairly sure.** Psagot is ranked #1 with "★ הזול". Its account fee is shown as about 1,057 ₪ over 10 years. Most other accounts cost 4,500 to 26,000 ₪.
- **The fund: not sure, and it barely matters.** The page says the top three pairs are effectively tied. The Altshuler Shaham fund (5139100) is only 43 ₪ behind over 10 years. If Psagot didn't have Yelin, I'd take Altshuler without a second thought.
- **The amount: not sure at all.** The 7% is only an example. The "what changes the result most" table shows that 8.4% instead of 7% gives about 92,400 ₪. The year-by-year charts show swings like −21.8% in a single year.

**What I used:** the top box with my plan and the two cheapest pairs, the cost bars ("כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים"), the account list on the right, and the fund list on the left. Further down there's the hedged vs. unhedged section and the "starting point" step chart. I read them but they didn't change my choice.

## 2. Line by line

**a. "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר, ולכן הם שקולים." and "הזוג השלישי:"**

The three cheapest account-and-fund combinations come out so close that the site won't name a winner. The second sentence names the third pair: Psagot with "iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating) 1159250".

- **The three pairs:** Psagot + Yelin (86,492), Psagot + Altshuler (86,449, shown as "−43"), and Psagot + iShares 1159250.
- **Could I find the third one?** Yes. It's the third row in the fund list, with "−213 ₪". Its final amount isn't shown anywhere, though. I worked out roughly 86,279 myself.
- **Where I tripped:**
  - "שקולים": I'm not sure where the cutoff is. −213 counts as "equal", but the next fund (Kesem KTF) at −447 apparently doesn't.
  - The iShares row says "עוד לא נאסף" where the other funds have a "vs. the index" figure, so I know less about that fund.
  - It's a fund "bought abroad" that trades on the Tel Aviv exchange. That was new to me and I wasn't sure what it means for me.

**b. "לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר" and "בחירת האתר"**

The column header means how much more or less I'd end up with in each fund than in the site's pick, staying with the same account (Psagot). "בחירת האתר" marks that pick: Yelin, the cheapest one. Everything else is a minus number.

- **Where I tripped:** "האתר בחר" sounds like a recommendation. Yet the page says twice "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד". My reading is that it's choosing the cheapest fund, not advising me, but the wording made me hesitate.
- Rows that say "אין מחיר כאן" have no number at all, so I can't compare them.

**c. Set C: "גם הקרן הוחלפה." and "חזרה לפסגות ולקרן הקודמת"**

I picked Interactive Brokers. It can't hold the Israeli Yelin fund, so the site also switched the fund on its own, to iShares IE00B5BMR087 (Accumulating). The result dropped to 82,842 ₪, which is 3,650 ₪ less than the cheapest pair.

I think the link puts me back on Psagot + Yelin. "הקרן הקודמת" probably means Yelin, since that's what was selected before I tapped.

- **Where I tripped:**
  - In set A, the Interactive Brokers row had already warned me it would switch me to "iShares … (Accumulating)". There are two Accumulating iShares funds in the list, though: 1159250 on Tel Aviv and IE00B5BMR087 abroad. Before tapping, I couldn't tell which one it meant.
  - Interactive Israel shows 84,181 for the same fund, higher than Interactive Brokers. So I'd picked the weaker of the two foreign options without realising it.

**d. Set D: "החשבון עבר לאינטראקטיב ישראל" and "חזרה לפסגות ולקרן הקודמת"**

This is the reverse of (c). I picked the foreign fund, Psagot apparently can't price buying it, and the site moved my account to Interactive Israel. The result is 84,181 ₪, which is 2,311 ₪ less than Psagot + Yelin.

The link should put me back on Psagot + Yelin.

- **Where I tripped:** the link has the same wording in (c) and (d) but undoes a different thing each time. In (c) it reverses a fund change; in (d) it reverses an account change. I'm assuming it always means "Psagot + Yelin", but I can't be certain.

**e. Set D: "הבחירה שלכם"**

It shows Interactive Israel ("אינטראקטיב ישראל") with "IE00B5BMR087 iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accu…" and 84,181 ₪. The fund name is cut off, but the ISIN tells me it's the Accumulating version.

**f. Set D and set G: Psagot's grey row**

The row says "אין נתון", meaning there's no figure. As I understood the two lines:

1. **Psagot's fee for European securities isn't final.** The quote reads: "בתעריפון המחיר על ניירות ערך באירופה לא סופי: 'עמלת ני"ע באסיה ובאירופה עשויה להשתנות…'". So the site couldn't work out what buying this foreign fund at Psagot would cost.
2. **Choosing Psagot puts me back on Yelin.** The second line says "בחירה בחשבון הזה מעבירה גם לקרן ילין לפידות … ונשארים 86,492".

The two links:
- **"ההסבר המלא"** expands the first line (set G). It adds that no Psagot document lists which European exchanges I can buy on. It also says the currency-conversion fee, "1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל", can be read "ביותר מדרך אחת, בין 0.48% ל־1.5%". That's why there's no price.
- **"הסבר מקוצר"** folds it back to the short version.

Where I tripped:
- **"1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל":** I get how 1.5 agorot per dollar comes to about 0.48% at a rate of 3.17. I don't get how it could also mean 1.5%.
- **"על מנ…":** the short version cuts off mid-word, so I had to open the full explanation to understand it at all.
- **"אין נתון" vs. "אין מחיר כאן":** the account list uses one and the fund list uses the other. I couldn't tell if they mean different things.

## 3. Other things that stopped me

- **Young-customer prices.** Several banks are marked "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים". I'm 29, so these might apply to me. Leumi, Discount, Mizrahi and others might be cheaper for me than shown, and I couldn't find out how much.
- **Jargon I didn't understand.** I didn't know what "4D" vs. "4A", "מחקה" vs. "סל", "עסקת החלף", "צבירה" or "W-8BEN" mean. I assume "מס גם על שינוי המטבע" means I pay tax on dollar-rate gains, but I'm not sure.
- **Hedged vs. unhedged.** I understood the general idea of the "מנוטרלת מט"ח" section but couldn't decide if it applies to me. The page itself says "עוד אין לאתר מקור".
- **The odd starting example.** The step chart starts from "MTF סל S&P 500 (4D)" at Jerusalem, marked "לדוגמה", and I don't know why that combination.
- **Interactive Brokers in the bottom table.** It says "יש ספק אם נמכרת שם: … Interactive Brokers", yet higher up the page gives it a full price.
- **What the 1,057 ₪ fee covers.** I couldn't find out what's included, or whether there are extra costs when I eventually sell.
- **How to open the account.** Nothing on the page explains how to actually open an account at Psagot.
