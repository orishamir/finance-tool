## 1. My decision

**Account:** Psagot (פסגות). **Fund:** "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282.

**What I'd end up with:** about **86,500 ₪** after 10 years. That's 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month, so 70,000 ₪ put in. The page says this is "בכסף של היום", after fees and tax. I understand that as "in what money buys today", which is nice. But it assumes the 7% a year that I left as it was ("תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית"). So the 86,492 is an example, not a promise.

**How sure am I?**
- **That Psagot plus this fund is the cheapest choice:** fairly sure, maybe 75%. The page ranks Psagot first ("★ הזול") and puts this fund first with "בחירת האתר".
- **That I'll really get 86,500:** not sure at all. The graph shows the years and the return change the result much more than the choice of account.
- **One real doubt:** I'm a student. Lots of banks have a grey tag "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר ... לסטודנטים" (Leumi, Beinleumi, Mizrahi, Discount, Mercantile, Hapoalim, Massad). So a bank might be cheaper *for me*, and the page didn't count it.

**Parts I used:**
- the two boxes at the top
- the account list (Psagot 86,492 ₪, then Leumi 83,396 ₪, and so on)
- the fund list, where Yalin has the filled circle
- the bottom line "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות"

I skipped most of the dollar-hedging ("נטרול מט"ח") section. What I took from it: my fund is "בלי נטרול", so if the dollar falls against the shekel, I lose some.
