## 1. My decision

**Account:** Psagot (פסגות). **Fund:** ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), number 5139282.

This is the pair the page picks when it opens. In the account list Psagot is number 1 with "★ הזול". In the fund list the Yelin fund is the row marked "בחירת האתר".

**How much at the end:** about **86,500 ₪**, from the 70,000 ₪ I'd put in. That's 10,000 ₪ now plus 500 ₪ a month for 10 years, using the page's example return of 7% a year. The page says this is "בכסף של היום", after management fees and tax. The cost bar says the whole thing costs me about 6,900 ₪ over 10 years, and most of that (5,703) is tax, not fees.

**How sure am I:**
- **Fairly sure** about the pair. The page says the top three pairs are basically tied. Any of them is fine, and Psagot is in all three.
- **Not sure** about 86,500. It's built on 7% a year, which is just an example. The "מה הכי משנה" section shows the number of years and the return move the result far more than the account does.
- **The bigger question for me:** should I leave my bank? Most banks in the list end up 3,000–5,000 ₪ lower (Leumi 83,396, Discount 79,901), and their account costs are 4,500–8,300 ₪ against Psagot's 1,057. The page doesn't say what moving to a new account involves, and I'd want to know that before acting.

**What I used:** the two result boxes at the top, the cost bars ("כמה זה עולה לכם ב־10 שנים"), the account list on the right with the cost under each name, and the fund list. I skipped most of the currency-hedging (נטרול מט"ח) section.
