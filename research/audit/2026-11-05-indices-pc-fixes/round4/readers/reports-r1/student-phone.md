## 1. My decision

**Account: Psagot (פסגות). Fund: "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)".**

This is the pair the page picked for me, and the numbers back it up. The box at the top says I'd have **"86,492 ₪"** after 10 years, "אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום". I'd be putting in 70,000 ₪ in total (10,000 now plus 500 a month for 120 months), so I'd come out about 16,500 ₪ ahead in today's money.

**How sure am I?** I'm fairly sure about **the account**. In the account list Psagot charges **"1,057 ₪"** over 10 years, and the next one, Leumi, charges 4,541 ₪. Psagot also ends 3,000+ ₪ ahead of every other account.

I'm less sure about **the fund**. The page itself says the top three pairs are basically a tie.

I'm not sure at all about **the 86,492** figure itself. It assumes a 7% return that I didn't choose. Near the bottom the page says "תשואה לדוגמה, לא תחזית", so it's an example, not a forecast.

One thing nags me. Almost every bank has a "לא בחישוב" note like "מחיר אחר לצעירים, לחיילים... לסטודנטים". I *am* a student, so a bank could be cheaper for me, and the page doesn't count that.

**Parts I used:** the two result boxes at the top, the account list (Psagot at #1 with "★ הזול"), and the "כמה זה עולה לכם" bars. Those bars showed me that most of the cost is tax (5,703 of 6,876), not fees. I also used the step chart at the bottom ("הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות... פסגות").

The section on dollar protection ("נטרול מט"ח") told me that the fund with protection (Harel 4A) would have left me about 9,460 ₪ less. That's why I'm happy with a fund without it.
