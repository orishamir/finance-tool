## 1. My decision

**Account:** Psagot (פסגות). **Fund:** "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282.

**How much I'd end up with:** about **86,500 ₪** after 10 years. I'd put in 70,000 ₪ in total (10,000 now plus 500 a month for 120 months). The page says the 86,492 ₪ is what's left "אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום", meaning after fees and tax and in today's money, "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" (if the dollar doesn't move against the shekel). It also assumes 7% a year, which the page calls "לדוגמה" (just an example). So I treat 86,500 as a rough picture, not a promise. If my savings or monthly amount are different, I'd type in my own numbers first.

**How sure am I:**
- **About Psagot:** fairly sure. It's number 1 in the account list, marked "★ הזול" (the cheap one). It costs about 1,057 ₪ over 10 years, while the next bank, Leumi, costs about 4,541 ₪.
- **About the fund:** less sure. The page itself says the top three pairs are "שקולים" (equivalent). So Altshuler Shaham (אלטשולר שחם, 86,449 ₪) would be about as good.

**Parts of the page I used:**
- the top inputs (sum today, monthly deposit, years, return)
- the two result boxes
- the cost bar chart: total cost 6,876 ₪, of which 5,703 is tax
- the account list
- the fund list

I also looked at sets C and D. They show that moving to a fund bought abroad leaves me with *less*: 82,842 ₪ with Interactive Brokers and 84,181 ₪ with Interactive Israel. That made me more confident about staying in Israel with Psagot.

## 2. Line by line

**a. "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר, ולכן הם שקולים."**

- **What it tells me:** the top three account-and-fund pairs are so close in cost that I shouldn't fuss over which one I pick.
- **The three pairs:**
  1. Psagot with Yelin Lapidot (big box, 86,492 ₪)
  2. Psagot with Altshuler Shaham (small box, 86,449 ₪, "‎−43 ₪")
  3. "הזוג השלישי": Psagot with iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating) 1159250
- **Could I find the third one?** Yes. It's the third row in the fund list, tagged "קרן חוץ בת"א", with "‎−213 ₪".
- **What trips me up:**
  - **The difference size.** Is a 213 ₪ gap really "too small to decide"? The second box showed a gap of only 43.
  - **The fund name.** The third pair has exactly the same name as the fund bought abroad, IE00B5BMR087, further down the list. Only the number, 1159250, tells them apart. At first I thought the sentence meant the overseas one.

**b. "לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר" and "בחירת האתר"**

- **What it tells me:** each fund's number is how many shekels more or less I'd end up with compared to the fund the site picked, Yelin Lapidot. The filled-circle row is that picked fund.
- **What's unclear:**
  - **"בחר" (chose).** Does it mean the site recommends this fund, or just that it's the cheapest by the site's sums? At the bottom the site says "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד" (the site doesn't recommend an index), but nothing similar is said about funds.
  - **Which account.** The column header doesn't say which account the comparison is in. I assumed Psagot because of the "בחשבון פסגות" label above the list.

**c. Set C: "גם הקרן הוחלפה." and "חזרה לפסגות ולקרן הקודמת"**

- **What happened:** I tapped Interactive Brokers, and the site also swapped my fund to the iShares Accumulating fund bought abroad. Apparently IB has no Israeli fund. The account row says this ("אין קרן ישראלית ואין מחיר בשקלים"). The result drops to 82,842 ₪, about 3,650 ₪ less than the Psagot pair.
- **What's unclear:**
  - "גם הקרן הוחלפה" (the fund was also replaced) doesn't say *which* fund it was replaced with. I had to look at the fund list to see.
  - **What the link does.** I think it puts back Psagot plus Yelin Lapidot. But "הקודמת" (the previous one) only says "previous". I can't be sure it means Yelin Lapidot, or whether it resets anything else. Nobody tapped it, so I don't actually know.

**d. Set D: "החשבון עבר לאינטראקטיב ישראל" and the same link**

- **What happened:** I picked the foreign fund, and the site moved my account from Psagot to Interactive Israel without asking. Psagot apparently can't be priced for that fund. To be fair, the fund list in set A had already warned me on that row: "בחירה בה מעבירה את החשבון לאינטראקטיב ישראל" (choosing it moves the account to Interactive Israel).
- **What the link does:** I'd assume it puts back Psagot and the fund I had before, Yelin Lapidot. Same uncertainty about "הקודמת" as in c, and again I didn't see it tapped.

**e. Set D, the "הבחירה שלכם" box**

- **What it shows:** Interactive Israel (אינטראקטיב ישראל), the fund "iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating)", and 84,181 ₪. Next to it, the Psagot pair would leave "+2,311 ₪ יותר".
- **What's unclear:**
  - **No fund number in the box.** From the box alone I couldn't tell whether it's the abroad IE00B5BMR087 or the Tel Aviv 1159250. I only know it's the abroad one because of what I tapped.
  - **"שלכם" (yours) feels wrong.** I never chose Interactive Israel; the site did.

**f. Psagot's grey row (sets D and G)**

- **What it tells me:**
  - Psagot shows "אין נתון" (no data) and a dash instead of an amount.
  - The reason: Psagot's price list says the fee for European securities "עשויה להשתנות" (may change). No Psagot document lists which European exchanges you can buy on. The currency conversion fee, "1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל", can be read as anything between 0.48% and 1.5%. So the site doesn't price it.
  - The last line says choosing Psagot also switches me back to Yelin Lapidot, leaving 86,492 ₪.
- **The two links:** "ההסבר המלא" (the full explanation) opens the whole paragraph. "הסבר מקוצר" (short explanation) folds it back into the short version. Neither one changes my choice.
- **What's unclear:**
  - I still don't know whether Psagot *can't* buy this fund at all, or just can't be *priced* for it. "אין נתון" could mean either.
  - "1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל" itself confused me, and the site admits it can be read more than one way.

## 3. Other things that stopped me

- **"לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים"** (not in the calculation: a different price for young people) appears on Leumi, Discount, Mizrahi and others. I'm 29. Do I count as "young"? Would one of those banks then beat Psagot? I couldn't find out.
- **Psagot's own list price.** Is Psagot's price fixed, or would I need to negotiate it? There's "קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו" (got a different price? enter it), but nothing saying whether Psagot's price really is the price I'd get.
- **Unexplained terms:** "4D" vs "4A" (I guessed: without and with currency hedging), "עסקת החלף", "צוברת", "מס גם על שינוי המטבע", "נשען על כלל מס פתוח", "W-8BEN", and the difference between "קרן מחקה" and "קרן סל". I don't know what most of these mean for me.
- **The yearly track records are short.** "+0.37% בשנה · שנה אחת" is a record of only one year. The tax is by far the biggest cost (5,703 of 6,876 ₪), and I can't do anything about it.
- **The dollar.** The hedging section says the result can swing a lot with the dollar, and the hedged fund's range runs from "+2,212" to "‎−23,766 ₪". That scared me a bit, but I understood that the unhedged funds are the default.
- **What I couldn't find:** how to actually open a Psagot account, how long it takes, and whether there's a minimum amount.
