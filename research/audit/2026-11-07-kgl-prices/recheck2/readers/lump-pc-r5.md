# lump-pc-r5

Who: You are 45, on a computer. You sold a car and have 60,000 ₪ you want to put in once, with nothing added later, and leave for about 10 years. You know what a bank deposit is and have heard of "דמי ניהול", but you have never opened a provident fund for investing.

I read all nine screenshots.

**1. My decision**

I haven't decided yet. If I had to open one tomorrow I'd go with **Harel (הראל פנסיה וגמל)**, but I'm only about 40% sure. The only thing pointing me there is the folded box "תנאי המוצר בדוגמה" (screenshot 09). It says "דמי הניהול בדוגמה הם של הראל פנסיה וגמל" and "מבין 12 החברות… במסמכים של החברה הזו כתובים דמי הניהול הנמוכים ביותר". So Harel only looks cheapest *on paper*. The page also says "בהסכם אישי חברה אחרת יכולה לגבות פחות", and the actual prices are on each company's own page ("מופיע בעמוד של החברה"), which I don't have. The list of companies ("הצגת החברות") was never opened, so I don't even know who the other 11 are.

The parts I used: the fee table "כמה זה עולה" and the paragraph under it, the terms box, the deposit limit card (my 60,000 is under the "83,641.00 ₪" limit, so that's fine), the tax cards, and the "סיכונים" section. The calculator didn't help much because its example is 10,000 ₪ plus 500 ₪ a month, not one payment of 60,000.

Before I decide I still need to find out:
- Which investment track to pick (מסלול כללי, S&P 500 and so on) and what each one charges.
- What fee I would actually get offered.
- Above all, whether a single deposit ends the discount early (see point 4).

**2. What I'll pay in fees**

As far as the page tells me:
- **On the 60,000 I put in:** 0% during the discount period ("דמי ניהול מהפקדה בתקופת ההנחה 0%"). Without a discount it can be "עד 4%", which is up to 2,400 ₪ taken straight off the top.
- **Every year on my balance:** 0.7% during the discount, which in the example lasts "5 שנים". After that it goes up to "עד 1.05%".

I did a rough sum assuming 7% a year. Years 1–5 come to about 450–575 ₪ a year. Years 6–10 come to about 900–1,150 ₪ a year. That's roughly **7,700 ₪ over 10 years**.

What the page doesn't tell me:
- The real rate I'd get.
- Whether the discount lasts 5 years or 6 months for someone like me.
- The extra costs called "הוצאות ישירות", where it says "לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת". So there's a cost with no upper limit and no number.

At least there's no exit fee: "דמי יציאה – לא גובים".

**3. Unclear, repeated or oddly written**

- "הדוגמה מניחה שההנחה נמשכת את כל תקופת ההנחה" just says the discount lasts for the discount period. It tells me nothing.
- "לכן אצלה נשאר הכי הרבה" can be read two ways: the most stays with *me*, or the most stays with *the company*. I had to think about it.
- The terms box has the label "דמי ניהול מחיסכון אחרי ההנחה, עד" with an awkward comma before "עד". That makes 1.05% look like a fixed rate rather than a maximum.
- Stray full stops: "פרטים . בתום ההנחה", "שנוכה יותר מדי TXK-26 ." and "( פרטים )." with spaces in the wrong places.
- "83,641.00 ₪" has pointless agorot on it. "נבדק ב07.11.2026" is missing a space or hyphen.
- The line "בכמה המחירים עולים בשנה, בממוצע." hangs on its own under the return box. It seems to explain the inflation field, but it reads like a broken sentence.
- The key to the page says both "הקישו להסבר" and "לחצו על מספר", two different words for "click".
- "Switching tracks or moving to another fund is not taxed" is said about four times: in "למה משתמשים בזה", in "שווה לדעת", on the "מעבר בין מסלולים" card and in "מיסוי".
- Two of the three items under "שווה לדעת" are bare headlines with a code and no explanation, for example "משיכה חד פעמית: הרווחים נחשבים הכנסה מריבית".

**4. What I distrust or want explained**

- **The discount may not apply to me at all.** The discount ends in some cases, "למשל חצי שנה אחרי שמפסיקים להפקיד". Then: "כשמפקידים פעם אחת ולא מפקידים שוב, לא ברור אם זה נחשב הפסקת הפקדות, וזו שאלה פתוחה". One deposit and nothing more is exactly my plan. So my 0.7% might become 1.05% after six months, and the 5-year example may not apply to me.
- **"Cheapest" ends at the maximum.** The cheapest company in the example ends up at the legal maximum ("דמי הניהול עולים עד התקרה"). So "cheapest" only means cheapest for 5 years.
- **The tax rules seem to contradict each other.** One card says "עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי", while the text says the company "מנכה במקור 25% מהרווח, בכל גיל". Then "שווה לדעת" says profits count as "הכנסה מריבית". And "האתר מציג גם חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי" is never explained. Which of these applies to me at 55?
- **The rules may change soon.** The orange box mentions a July 2026 Treasury recommendation: "מעל התקרה ההטבות של היום היו מסתיימות". That matters a lot for a 10-year decision.
- **Open questions about losses and retirement.** It's unclear whether I can offset a loss ("קיזוז הפסד… שאלה פתוחה"). The pension option isn't calculated either ("את הקצבה האתר עוד לא מחשב").
- **This isn't a bank deposit.** "המדינה לא ערבה לשווי הקופה". My money can go down.
