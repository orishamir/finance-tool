## 1. My decision

I'm not deciding yet. If I had to pick today it would be **הראל פנסיה וגמל**, but I'm only about 35% sure, and I'd call a few companies before signing anything.

I only know Harel's name from the folded box (`תנאי המוצר בדוגמה`): "דמי הניהול בדוגמה הם של הראל פנסיה וגמל". The page picked Harel because "במסמכים של החברה הזו כתובים דמי הניהול הנמוכים ביותר". The fee section also says "אצל 9 מתוך 12 החברות המנהלות שנאספו, במסמכים כתובה רק התקרה שבתקנות". So Harel is cheapest only among the 3 companies that published a discount. The page also says "מה שגובים בפועל נקבע בהסכם אישי" and "בהסכם אישי חברה אחרת יכולה לגבות פחות". That means the real price is negotiated, and my 50,000 ₪ might get me a better deal somewhere else.

The list of companies (`הצגת החברות`, `כל החברות`) was folded, so I never saw it.

Parts I used:
- the fee table (`כמה זה עולה`)
- the terms box
- `כסף נכנס ויוצא`: the yearly cap of 83,641 ₪ is fine for my 62,000 in year one, and I can take money out "בכל עת"
- `מיסוי`

## 2. What I'd pay in fees

**At first (the discount period, "לפחות 5 שנים"):**
- 0.7% a year of my balance ("דמי ניהול מחיסכון בתקופת ההנחה 0.7%"). On 50,000 ₪ that's about 350 ₪ in year one. By year five my balance should be around 140,000 ₪, so roughly 1,000 ₪ a year.
- 0% on deposits.

**Later:** the company "מותר להעלות את דמי הניהול עד התקרה", and the example assumes they will ("דמי הניהול עולים בסופה עד התקרה"). Those ceilings are:
- 1.05% a year of my balance, about 1,450 ₪ on 140,000 ₪
- "עד 4% מכל הפקדה", which would be up to 480 ₪ a year on my 1,000 ₪ monthly deposits

**What's missing:**
- The terms box is cut off right after "דמי ניהול מחיסכון אחרי … 1.05%". I can't see what the deposit fee becomes after the discount.
- Direct expenses (`הוצאות ישירות`) come on top: "נוספות על דמי הניהול", and "לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת". I have no idea how much they are.
- The calculator shows only the default 10,000 ₪ plus 500 ₪ a month (fees of 5,303 ₪ over 10 years), not my own numbers. I'd have to type in 50,000 ₪ and 1,000 ₪ myself.

## 4. What I distrust or would want explained

- **"Cheapest" from a narrow pool:** Harel is called the lowest out of only 3 companies with published numbers.
- **Fragile discount:** the discount can end early ("חצי שנה אחרי שמפסיקים להפקיד, או כשמושכים כסף"). Whether a single deposit followed by no more deposits counts as stopping is "לא ברור". That matters to me if I ever pause my monthly 1,000 ₪.
- **Rule changes coming:** "צוות של משרד האוצר ממליץ ביולי 2026... תקרה אחת של 200,000 ₪". It's not law yet, but the product could change under me.
- **Open tax questions:** loss offset ("קיזוז הפסד... שאלה פתוחה") and pension conversion ("את הקצבה האתר עוד לא מחשב") are unanswered.
- **A name under "not commercial":** the footer says "לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק", and I believe it, but only one company is named and it's hidden in a folded box. I'd want to see the full company list before trusting that choice.
