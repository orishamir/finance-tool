## 1. My decision

I wouldn't open it yet, and I can't choose a company from this page. I'm fairly sure the product suits me, but I have no idea which company to use.

- **Why it suits me:** "אפשר למשוך בכל עת" (I can take money out anytime), "אין נעילה" (it isn't locked), and the yearly limit is "83,641.00 ₪ לשנה", so my one-time 50,000 ₪ fits.
- **Why I can't pick a company:** the company list was folded ("הצגת החברות") and my friend didn't open it. The only company named is in the example box: "דמי הניהול בדוגמה הם של הראל פנסיה וגמל". The page says it picked that company because it has the highest fee limits, "כתובים דמי הניהול המנוכים ביותר". So it's a worst case, not a recommendation.
- **What I used:** the top summary, the "כמה זה עולה" (what it costs) section, the tax section, the warning boxes and the folded terms box (screenshots 12–13).

## 2. What I'd pay in fees

As far as the page tells me, in the example company (Harel):

- **First years:** "0.7% בשנה" on my whole balance. On 50,000 ₪ that's about 350 ₪ a year, more as the money grows. There's "0%" on deposits during the discount period, which is "לפחות 5 שנים" (at least 5 years).
- **After the discount ends:** up to "1.05%" a year on the balance (about 525 ₪ or more a year), plus up to "4% מכל הפקדה" on deposits. The 4% shouldn't touch me if I never deposit again. But if a company charged 4% on my first deposit, that's 2,000 ₪ gone on day one.
- **Other companies:** "אצל 9 מתוך 12" companies the limits are the same. It doesn't say what the other 3 charge, or what anyone really charges: "מה שגובים בפועל נקבע בהסכם אישי" (the real fee is set in a personal deal).

What's missing:

- **Extra costs:** "הוצאות ישירות" (costs of buying and selling the investments) come on top, and the page says "לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת" (no limit found). So my total cost is unknown.
- **Exit fees:** the "דמי יציאה" (exit fees) section is cut off at the bottom of screenshot 6. I don't know if they exist.
- **The calculator doesn't match me:** it uses 10,000 ₪ plus 500 ₪ a month, not 50,000 ₪ once. In that example the management fees ("דמי ניהול 5,303 ₪") are bigger than the tax ("4,501 ₪"), so fees really matter.

## 3. Unclear, repeated or odd

- **Some of the page seems to be missing between screenshots.** They don't always overlap. Parts of the cost section, the tax section and the section starting "כסף נכנס ויוצא" (money in and out) look skipped, so I may have missed things.
- **The discount length contradicts itself.** "תקופת ההנחה, לפחות 5 שנים" (at least 5 years) sits next to "היא יכולה להיגמר מוקדם יותר: חצי שנה אחרי שמפסיקים להפקיד" (it can end half a year after you stop depositing).
- **The calculator's assumption reads two ways:** "הדוגמה מניחה שההנחה נמשכת כל התקופה, ושדמי הניהול עולים בסופה עד התקרה". Does the 10-year example use 0.7% for all 10 years, or only for the first 5?
- **Odd punctuation:** "( פרטים ). בתום ההנחה" has spaces inside the brackets and a full stop right after them. It appears twice.
- **Repeated text:** the same discount paragraph appears on the page (screenshot 7) and again in the terms box. The top summary and the "מה זה" (what is it) section say almost the same thing.
- **Confusing labels:** "123 ממקור 123 חשוב מונח הקישו להסבר" near the top is hard to understand. "מקדמה" (advance payment) appears with no context.
- **Two tax rates:** "עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי" (up to 25% of the real profit), but also "האתר מציג גם חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי". Which one would I actually pay?

## 4. What I distrust or want explained

- **The most important one for me:** "כשמפקידים פעם אחת ולא מפקידים שוב, לא ברור אם זה נחשב הפסקת הפקדות". That's exactly my plan. If it counts as stopping, my discount could end after 6 months, and I'd pay 1.05% instead of 0.7% almost from the start. I'd ask the company in writing.
- **The fee can go up:** "בתום ההנחה מותר לחברה להעלות את דמי הניהול עד התקרה".
- **Unknown limits:** "לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת" on the extra costs. On using a loss to lower tax ("קיזוז הפסד"), the page says it's "שאלה פתוחה" (an open question).
