I read all six screenshots in order. I'm answering as the 38-year-old with ₪40,000.

## (1) What the pages say about fees, now and later

The product is a קופת גמל להשקעה, an investment savings fund. It charges two kinds of management fee:

- **A yearly fee on everything saved.** The legal maximum is 1.05% a year (`עד 1.05% בשנה`).
- **A fee on each deposit.** The legal maximum is 4% of each deposit (`עד 4% מכל הפקדה`).

On top of that come "direct expenses", such as trading costs, which have no overall cap (`לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת`). There are no exit fees (`דמי יציאה… לא גובים`).

**Now:** The paragraph under the fee table says most of the 12 companies only state the maximum in their documents, and the real rate is agreed with you personally. Where lower rates are written, the most common is 0.7% a year and 0% on deposits, as a time-limited discount.

**Later:** The discount must last at least 5 years. After that, the company may raise the fee up to the maximum. It can end sooner in some cases: half a year after you stop depositing, or if you withdraw money or move it to another fund or track.

**The cost page** uses an example of ₪10,000 now plus ₪500 a month for 10 years at 7%. It assumes 0.7% a year for 5 years, then 1.05%, and 0% on deposits. On that basis, fees cost about ₪5,223 in today's money. You end with ₪82,303 instead of ₪87,526. It tags the yearly fee "worth negotiating" and the deposit fee "not worth the trouble".

## (2) Things I had to read twice or found misleading

1. **Four different "total fee" numbers.**
   - Guide page table: `דמי ניהול ₪5,303`.
   - Cost page headline: `₪5,223`.
   - Cost page cards: `3,902 ₪` + `1,293 ₪` + `השפעה משולבת 27.81 ₪`.
   - Cost page chart: `5,203 ₪` + `1,324 ₪` = `סך העמלות 6,564 ₪`, plus `השפעה משולבת 37.08 ₪`.

   The small note under the chart explains that the chart is before tax, so it ends ₪1,341 higher than the headline. But the same label, `דמי ניהול מחיסכון`, shows 3,902 on the card and 5,203 in the chart, right beside each other. The guide page's 5,303 doesn't match anything on the cost page.

2. **`השפעה משולבת`** ("combined effect"). The only explanation is `הסכום של כל השורות והשורה הזו הוא העלות הכוללת` (the rows plus this row are the total cost). It never says what the effect actually is.

3. **`למרות העמלה, לפי המספרים בעמוד המוצר הזה במקום 2 מבין כל האפשרויות`** (cost page, under the title). Place 2 out of which options, ranked by what? No list or ranking appears anywhere.

4. **`לפחות 5 שנים מהיום שמתחילים לגבות אותה`** (guide, paragraph under the fee table). "At least 5 years from the day they start *charging* it" (the discount). I had to reread it: does the clock start when I join, or at some other point?

5. **`המוצע שהרשות פרסמה לשנת 2025`** (both cards on the cost page). I think this should be `הממוצע` ("the average"). I stumbled on it twice.

6. **`תקופת ההחזקה … תעבור מ־10 שנים ל־8.17 שנים`** (cost page, "what would change the conclusion"). 8.17 years is a false level of precision. It's also odd that holding for a shorter time turns the advice into "not worth the trouble".

7. **`דמי ניהול מהפקדה, מחצי עד כפול: … 0% (אחרי ההנחה עד 2%) עד 0% (אחרי ההנחה עד 4%)`** (top of the second cost screenshot). Half of 0% and double 0% are both 0%, so the "range checked" is no range at all. And why would the after-discount rate be 2% in one case?

8. **The `PF-39`, `TXK-04`, `KGL-02` tags.** They're never explained.

## (3) Missing or contradictory

- **The cards say there's no average, then show one.** Both cards say `אין ממוצע להשוואה` (no average to compare with). The next line gives one anyway: `0.53%` for the yearly fee and `0.08%` for the deposit fee.

  This matters for me. The example assumes 0.7% rising to 1.05%, which is well above the published 0.53% average. The deposit assumption of 0% is below the 0.08% average.

- **The "negotiate" label doesn't follow the stated rule.** The legend says `שווה להתמקח מ־1,000 ₪; שווה להשוות מ־2,500 ₪` (worth negotiating from ₪1,000, worth comparing from ₪2,500). Yet:
  - The deposit fee costs `1,293 ₪`, which is over ₪1,000, but it's labelled `לא שווה את הטרחה` (not worth the trouble). The reason given is that there's no average to compare with, which is not the same as "not worth the trouble".
  - The yearly-fee badge is labelled "worth negotiating", but its explanation uses the ₪2,500 *comparing* threshold: `עולה 2,500 ₪ או יותר`.

- **My actual case is left open.** I want to put in ₪40,000 once. The guide itself says `כשמפקידים פעם אחת ולא מפקידים שוב, לא ברור אם זה נחשב הפסקת הפקדות, וזו שאלה פתוחה` (if you deposit once and never again, it's unclear whether that counts as stopping deposits, an open question). If it does count, my discount could end after half a year rather than 5. The calculator still assumes the full 5 years and doesn't warn about this.

- **"No exit fees", but withdrawals still cost you.** The table says `דמי יציאה… לא גובים`. But the paragraph below says withdrawing money, or moving it to another fund or track, can end the discount early. That isn't an exit fee, but it's a cost of taking money out.

- **The cost page names a company the guide never mentions.** The banner reads `לפי עמוד המדריך: קופת גמל להשקעה, לפי הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ`. The guide page is generic and never names a company. I couldn't tell whether 0.7%→1.05% is that company's real rate or a general assumption. Also, the footer says the site is non-commercial (`לא מסחרי`).

- **Things I expected and didn't find:**
  - Any actual company rates. The guide says they're on each company's page, which isn't here.
  - Any estimate of direct expenses, which have no cap. As far as I can tell, they're left out of the ₪5,223.
  - What a 4% deposit fee would do to a lump sum. For me that would be up to ₪1,600 on day one.
  - The fee rates behind the guide page's ₪82,303. They're hidden in a collapsed section, `תנאי המוצר בדוגמה`.
