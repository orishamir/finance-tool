**1. My decision**

I wouldn't open it with anyone yet. This page doesn't show the fees for each company. It says so under the cost table: "אלה התקרות שבחוק. כמה כל חברה גובה בפועל, בכל מסלול, מופיע בעמוד של החברה." So what I'd still need is each company's page, and above all an actual fee offer for my 50,000 ₪.

The only company the page names is "הראל פנסיה וגמל", in the example box. That doesn't make it the cheapest for me: 9 of the 12 companies don't publish their real rate (more in part 2). I'm only about 20% sure Harel would be the best pick.

**2. The small grey sentences in the cost table**

They're the same for both fees; only the number changes. In my words: 9 of the 12 companies they looked at only write the legal maximum in their documents ("המסמכים כותבים רק את התקרה שבחוק"). What you actually pay is set in a personal deal ("נקבע בהסכם אישי"). The other 3 write a lower rate. The lowest is 0.7% a year on savings, or 0% on deposits, but only "בתקופת ההנחה" (during a discount period). After the discount ends they're allowed to raise it up to the maximum.

What's unclear or odd:
- "והנמוך שבהם": I read "them" as only the 3 companies that publish a rate. So "the lowest" really means lowest of 3, not of 12.
- The discount period has no length here. I only learn "5 שנים, לפחות" from the example box.
- "מותר להעלות את השיעור עד התקרה" can be read two ways: "they may raise it" or "it will go up". Same with the "עד" (up to) in front of 1.05% and 4%.
- On the page, "בתקופת" and "ההנחה." are split across two lines, so the full stop sits alone at the start of a line. I had to reread it.
- The exact same paragraph appears twice. It looks copy-pasted, not written for each fee.

**3. The example**

- **Which company:** "הראל פנסיה וגמל".
- **Why that one:** of the 12 companies, the fees written in its documents were the lowest ("הנמוכים ביותר"), so the example leaves the most money in the account.
- **Its fees over time:** 0.7% a year on savings and 0% on deposits for at least 5 years. After that, up to 1.05% a year and up to 4% of each deposit.
- **What the example assumes:** "שההנחה נגמרת בדיוק בתום התקופה הזו ושדמי הניהול עולים אז עד התקרה". So in the 10-year chart, years 6–10 are charged at the maximum.

That's a fair worst case, but it's odd to pick a company for being cheapest and then charge it the maximum for half the time. The page itself admits "בהסכם אישי חברה אחרת יכולה לגבות פחות."

**4. What I distrust or want explained**

- **Fees in the chart:** "דמי ניהול 5,303 ₪" over 10 years, on 70,000 ₪ of deposits. A lot of that is the 4% on deposits after year 5. With a negotiated deal it could be much less, so the chart may scare people more than it should.
- **Two tax numbers:** "עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי" and then "האתר מציג גם חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי". Which one applies to me? It isn't explained.
- **Tax taken anyway:** "מנכה במקור 25% מהרווח, בכל גיל", even though from age 60 the tax "יכול להיות נמוך יותר". You get it back only after the annual report ("בדוח השנתי"). I'd want to know how much hassle that is.
- **Questions the page leaves open:**
  - offsetting losses: "שאלה פתוחה"
  - moving to an annuity fund at 60: "האתר עוד לא מחשב"
  - a July 2026 recommendation, "המלצה, לא חוק", that may change the product
- **The return figure:** "7%" is an example, "לא תחזית". The chart shows 82,303 ₪ "בכסף של היום" but 100,327 ₪ in future shekels. Those are easy to mix up.
- **Who runs the site:** the footer says "אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" and "נתונים נכון ל־07.11.2026". That's reassuring, but I don't know who's behind it.
