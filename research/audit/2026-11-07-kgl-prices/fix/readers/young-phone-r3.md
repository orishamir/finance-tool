**1. My decision**

I wouldn't open it anywhere yet. I'm about 30% sure of any one company. The page never says which company is cheapest for someone like me. Two things stopped me:

- **The real fees are negotiated.** The page says "ומה שגובים בפועל נקבע בהסכם אישי" (what they actually charge is set in a personal agreement). So the fees on the page are only ceilings.
- **Each company has its own page.** It says "כמה כל חברה גובה בפועל, בכל מסלול, מופיע בעמוד של החברה" (what each company actually charges is on that company's page). The company list was folded shut ("הצגת החברות"), so I never saw it.

My next steps:
- Ask 2–3 companies for a written fee quote. I'd start with הראל, since the page says its published fees are the lowest.
- Ask each one how long the discount lasts and what the fee becomes afterwards.
- Ask about extra costs, which the page calls "הוצאות ישירות".

Parts I used: "מה זה", the example calculator, "כמה זה עולה", "מיסוי", "כסף נכנס ויוצא" and the example's terms sheet. My 50,000 ₪ is under the yearly limit of "83,641.00 ₪ לשנה, לכל אדם בכל הקופות יחד" (per year, per person, across all funds together), so that's fine.

**2. The small grey sentences under the fees**

Both fees (the yearly fee on the money saved, and the fee on each deposit) have nearly the same sentence. It says:
- Of the 12 companies they collected, 9 only print the legal maximum. That's "עד 1.05% בשנה" (up to 1.05% a year) on the money saved, and "עד 4% מכל הפקדה" (up to 4% of every deposit) on deposits.
- The other 3 print a lower rate. The lowest is 0.7% (or 0% for deposits), but only "בתקופת הנחה" (during a discount period).
- "בתום ההנחה מותר להעלות את השיעור עד התקרה": when the discount ends, they're allowed to raise the fee up to the maximum.

What's unclear or reads oddly:
- **"הנמוך שבהם"** (the lowest of them) could mean the lowest of those 3 companies or of all 12. I think it means the 3, but it reads two ways.
- **0% looks free but isn't.** A 0% deposit fee sounds great until you notice it's only for the discount period.
- **No discount length here.** The grey text never says how long the discount lasts. I only found "לפחות 5 שנים" (at least 5 years) in the terms box.
- **Uncapped extra costs.** "הוצאות ישירות" (direct costs, like buying and selling the investments) come out of the fund on top of the fee, with "לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת" (no overall cap was found). So I can't know my total cost.
- **Typos.** I didn't spot a misplaced number or full stop in those sentences. The terms box does have a typo: "במוצע" should be "בממוצע" (on average). The inflation number that should follow it is missing from that screenshot.

**3. The example's company**

It's **הראל פנסיה וגמל**. Why: "הם הנמוכים ביותר, ולכן נשאר אצלה הכי הרבה בתוכנית לדוגמה" (its published fees are the lowest, so the most money stays with you in the example).

Its fees in the example:
- First 5 years: 0.7% a year on the savings and 0% on deposits.
- From year 6: up to 1.05% a year and 4% of every deposit.

The box says "הדוגמה מניחה שההנחה נגמרת בדיוק בתום התקופה הזה ושדמי הניהול עולים אז עד התקרה" (the example assumes the discount ends exactly at the end of this period and the fees then rise to the maximum). So the second half of the 10-year chart uses the most expensive fees allowed. The box also admits "בהסכם אישי חברה אחרת יכולה לגבות פחות" (with a personal agreement, another company could charge less). So "cheapest on paper" doesn't mean cheapest for me.

**4. What I distrust or want explained**

- **Tax rate.** It says "עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי" (up to 25% of the profit above inflation), and then "האתר מציג גם חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי" (the site also shows an alternative: 15% of the total profit). Which one applies to me? Who chooses?
- **Tax in the chart.** "החברה המנהלת מנכה במקור 25%… בכל גיל" (the company takes 25% off at source, at any age), but "25% הוא תקרה, לא שיעור קבוע" (25% is a maximum, not a fixed rate). So is the 4,501 ₪ tax in the chart actually right for me?
- **Today's money vs future money.** The headline 82,303 ₪ is in today's money, while my bank statement would show 100,327 ₪. Are the "70,000 ₪" deposits and the fee and tax lines in the same kind of shekels? It isn't said.
- **The rules may change.** "המלצה, לא חוק" (a recommendation, not a law): a July 2026 Treasury proposal could change the tax benefits.
- **Open questions.** Moving the money to a pension fund at 60 and offsetting losses against other profits are both marked "שאלה פתוחה" (an open question). I'd want those answered before I commit.
- **The example isn't me.** It uses "10,000 ₪ ועוד 500 ₪ בחודש" (10,000 ₪ plus 500 ₪ a month) at an assumed 7% return. I'd change it to 50,000 ₪ up front with nothing monthly. The page itself notes "התשואה היא דוגמה, לא תחזית" (the return is an example, not a forecast).
