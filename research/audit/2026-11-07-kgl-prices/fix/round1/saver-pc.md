1. **My decision:** I wouldn't open one yet. I'm not confident in any company from this page alone. The only company it names is **"הראל פנסיה וגמל"** (Harel), and only because it's the one used in the example. That isn't a recommendation. The page says itself: **"אלה התקרות שבחוק. כמה כל חברה גובה בפועל, בכל מסלול, מופיע בעמוד של החברה"**, meaning the table shows legal maximums and the real prices are on each company's own page. So my next step is to open those company pages, or call two or three companies, and ask two things. What management fee would I pay on 50,000 ₪? How long does that discount last, and what does it go up to afterwards? My 50,000 ₪ is under the yearly deposit limit (**"83,641.00 ₪ לשנה"**), so that's fine. The parts I used: the cost table, the "תנאי המוצר בדוגמה" box, the tax section, and the **"בכל עת"** line about withdrawing at any time.

2. **The grey sentences in "כמה זה עולה":**
   - **Fee on the savings** (**"עד 1.05% בשנה"**): the site collected the documents of 12 companies, and the printed rates run from 0.7% to 1.05%. But **"9 מהן מדפיסות רק את התקרה שבחוק"**: 9 of the 12 print only the legal maximum. So the "range" really comes from 3 companies, and for the other 9 I don't know the real price. Then **"שיעור נמוך מהתקרה הוא הנחה, ובתום ההנחה מותר להעלות אותו עד התקרה"**, meaning anything below the maximum is a discount that can be raised to the maximum when it ends. This reads two ways. Is every low rate temporary by definition, or only some of them? And the sentence opens with "the rates are between 0.7% and 1.05%", which sounds like real prices, when most of them are just the maximum.
   - **Fee on each deposit** (**"עד 4% מכל הפקדה"**): the same idea, from 0% to 4%, and again 9 companies print only the maximum. Oddly, this one doesn't repeat the warning that a discount can end. Is a 0% deposit fee also temporary?
   - **"הוצאות ישירות"** (direct expenses: trading costs and the like, charged on top of the management fee): **"לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת"**, meaning the site found no overall limit on them. That worries me.
   - **"דמי יציאה"**: no exit fee is charged.
   - I didn't see a number or full stop clearly in the wrong place. The full stop after **"לפי החברה."** makes "9 מהן" start a new sentence, which reads a bit abruptly, but it's not wrong.

3. **The example:** it uses Harel, **"התנאים והתעריף בדוגמה הם של הראל פנסיה וגמל"**. The reason given: of the 12 companies, Harel's fees on savings and on deposits **"הם הנמוכים ביותר, ולכן נשאר אצלה הכי הרבה בתוכנית לדוגמה"**, so it's the company where you'd keep the most in the example. Its fees over time:
   - During a discount period of at least 5 years (**"תקופת ההנחה, לפחות 5 שנים"**): 0.7% a year on savings and 0% on deposits.
   - After that: up to 1.05% a year and up to 4% on each deposit. These are exactly the legal maximums.
   
   The example says **"הדוגמה מניחה שכך יקרה"**, so it assumes the fees really do rise. That means the "cheapest" company ends up at the maximum after 5 years in the example. The chart runs 10 years and shows 5,303 ₪ in fees. I can't tell whether the switch happens in exactly year 5. "לפחות" (at least) suggests it could come later.

4. **What I distrust or want explained:**
   - "Cheapest of 12" is shaky when 9 of the 12 only print the maximum. Harel looks cheapest partly because it published a discount and the others didn't.
   - At the top: **"123 מקור · 123 מחושב"**, meaning 123 sources and 123 calculated figures. Both are exactly 123, which looks like a placeholder number, not a real count.
   - Tax: **"החברה המנהלת מנכה במקור 25% מהרווח"** (25% deducted from the profit) sits next to **"עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי"** (up to 25% of the real profit) and **"חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי"** (an alternative of 15% of the nominal profit). Real or nominal profit, 25% or 15%? It's confusing, and the page says itself that the final tax is settled in the annual tax return.
   - **"ההמלצה, לא חוק... היו מסתיימות"**: a Treasury committee recommendation, not a law, about a 200,000 ₪ cap. The tenses are muddled, and I don't know if it affects me.
   - **"קיזוז הפסד... שאלה פתוחה"**: whether a loss can be offset against other profits is an open question. So is the annuity part at age 60. The site admits these are open.
   - The chart's 82,303 ₪ in today's money versus 100,327 ₪ "in future shekels". It's explained, but it's easy to quote the wrong one.
