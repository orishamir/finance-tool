1. **My decision:** I'm not picking a company yet. If someone forced me today I'd say Harel (הראל פנסיה וגמל), but I'm only about 30% sure. That's only because it's the company in the example, and the page says it was picked as the cheapest: "אצל החברה הזו דמי הניהול מחיסכון ומהפקדה הם הנמוכים ביותר". But the page itself says the real prices are somewhere else: "כמה כל חברה גובה בפועל, בכל מסלול, מופיע בעמוד של החברה." The list of companies ("הצגת החברות") is folded shut in the screenshots, so I never saw any company's actual fees. Before I choose, I'd need to find out what each company charges me, how long its discount lasts, and what it charges after that. I used:
   - the "what it costs" table (כמה זה עולה)
   - the example chart and the numbers box behind it
   - the "who offers it" section (מי מציע ומי מפקח)
   - the tax section (מיסוי)

2. **The grey sentences:** For the yearly fee on my savings, they say the documents the site collected from 12 companies show 0.7% to 1.05%. 9 of the 12 only print the legal maximum. A lower rate is a discount, and when it ends the company may raise it to the maximum. The fee on each deposit gets the same kind of sentence, with 0% to 4%. So really, almost everyone just says "up to the max", and only 3 companies show anything lower.

   What's unclear or odd:
   - **The "9" after the full stop.** "...לפי החברה. 9 מהן..." starts a sentence with a bare number right after a full stop. On my phone I first read it as "1.05% לפי החברה.9", like a decimal.
   - **The same 9 twice.** Both fees say exactly "9 מהן". Is it the same 9 companies, or a copy-paste?
   - **The range mixes two things.** The 0.7% at the bottom is a temporary discount, but it sits in the same range as the permanent 1.05% maximum.
   - **"מדפיסות רק את התקרה" can be read two ways.** Does it mean they actually charge the maximum, or just that they don't tell you?
   - **Direct costs have no number.** The "הוצאות ישירות" row (trading costs, on top of the fees) gets cut off in my screenshot, and I'm not sure the example even counts it.

3. **The example:** It's Harel. The site says it chose Harel because Harel's fees are the lowest of the 12, so the most money is left over. Harel charges 0.7% a year and 0% on deposits, but only during a discount period of "לפחות 5 שנים". After that, the example assumes the fees jump to the maximums, 1.05% a year and 4% of every deposit: "הדוגמה מניחה שכך יקרה". The chart runs for 10 years, so the second half is at the maximum. That means the "cheapest" company ends up priced like the 9 that only print the maximum. So I don't know if it's really the best choice over time.

4. **What I distrust or want explained:**
   - **Tax.** The tax section says "עד 25%" and "25% הוא תקרה, לא שיעור קבוע". Then it says the company takes 25% off the profit "בכל גיל". So do I get the extra back myself later, through a tax return?
   - **Deposit limit.** The page gives "₪83,641 לשנה" as the yearly deposit limit. A yellow box then talks about a treasury recommendation for a ₪200,000 limit, "לא חוק". That's fine for me with ₪50k, but it's confusing.
   - **Open questions the site admits.** "שאלה פתוחה" appears twice: moving the money to a pension at 60 ("האתר עוד לא מחשב"), and using losses to cut tax ("לא נמצא מקור").
   - **Two kinds of shekels.** The chart shows "₪100,327 בשקלים של העתיד" but "82,303 ₪" in today's money. Are the fees (₪5,303) and tax (₪4,501) in today's money too, while my deposits (₪70,000) are plain shekels? The yearly price-rise rate it assumed isn't shown anywhere I could see.
   - **7% a year** is just an example ("דוגמה, לא תחזית"), but it drives the whole chart.
   - **Missing bits of the page.** The screenshots don't overlap in two places: after "שנה 10" (between shots 2 and 3), and between the fee table and "דמי יציאה" (shots 6 and 7). I may have missed part of the year-10 breakdown and at least one fee row.
