**1. My decision**

I wouldn't open it anywhere yet. If you made me pick today, I'd lean towards **הראל פנסיה וגמל**, but I'm only about 30% sure.

- The page never tells me what each company actually charges. It says: "כמה כל חברה גובה בפועל, בכל מסלול, מופיע בעמוד של החברה" (what each company really charges is on the company's own page). The company list ("הצגת החברות") is closed in the screenshots, so I never saw it.
- Harel is in the example only because its documents show the lowest numbers. The page itself warns: "בהסכם אישי חברה אחרת יכולה לגבות פחות" (in a personal deal, another company could charge less).

What I'd still need to find out:
- What fee each company would actually give me, in writing.
- How long that discount lasts, and what the fee becomes afterwards.
- Whether they'd take the deposit fee on my 50,000. At "עד 4%" that could be 2,000 ₪ the day I put the money in.

Parts I used: the fee table "כמה זה עולה", the example and its terms box, the tax section "מיסוי", and the basic facts box. From the box: I can take money out any time ("בכל עת"), I get it within "עד 4 ימי עסקים" (up to 4 business days), and the yearly limit of "83,641.00 ₪" is fine for my 50,000.

**2. The grey sentences under the fees**

My reading: they collected the papers of 12 companies. The yearly fee on my savings is somewhere between 0.7% and 1.05%, and the fee on each deposit is between 0% and 4%. But 9 of the 12 only write the legal maximum. What you really pay is agreed one on one. Any price below the maximum counts as a discount, and when the discount ends they're allowed to raise it back to the maximum.

What's unclear or odd:
- **The range is misleading.** If 9 of 12 only list the maximum, then the "0.7%–1.05%" really comes from just 3 companies. The rest just say "1.05%". I first read it as "most companies are somewhere in between", and they aren't.
- **"שיעור נמוך מהתקרה הוא הנחה"** (a rate below the ceiling is a discount). This could mean every low price is temporary by definition, or only some deals. I can't tell which, and it's scary either way.
- **"עמית"** in "בהסכם עם כל עמית" (in an agreement with each member) is never explained. I'm guessing it means me, the customer.
- **The two grey texts are word-for-word the same**, just with different numbers. It looks copy-pasted.
- **"…ומכירה של ההשקעות, מהקופה"** (under extra costs). The comma before "מהקופה" reads strangely. I think it means those costs come out of the fund itself, but it could be read as part of the list.
- **"לא נמצאה תקרה כוללת"** (no overall ceiling found). Does that mean there isn't one, or that they didn't find one? It's not the same thing.

**3. The example's company**

It's **הראל פנסיה וגמל**. Why: "דמי הניהול שכתובים במסמכים של החברה הזו… הם הנמוכים ביותר" (its written fees are the lowest of the 12), so the website picked it.

What happens over time:
- For "תקופת ההנחה, לפחות 5 שנים" (a discount period of at least 5 years) it charges 0.7% a year and 0% on deposits.
- After that it can go up to 1.05% a year and 4% on each deposit.

The example assumes the discount ends exactly at 5 years and the fees jump straight to the maximum ("נגמרת בדיוק בתום התקופה… עולים אז עד התקרה"). That's the worst case, which I suppose is fair, but it's a guess. "At least 5 years" could also mean longer.

**4. Things I distrust or want explained**

- **The tax looks contradictory.** It says "עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי" (up to 25% of the profit above inflation). Then it says the company takes "25% מהרווח" off at source, "ולא מנכה כלום מהסכום שהופקד ומההצמדה עליו" (and nothing from what you deposited or its adjustment for prices). And then it adds "האתר מציג גם חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי" (the site also shows an option: 15% of the profit before inflation). So is it 25% or 15%? Which one did the chart use?
- **Open questions the page admits.** On cancelling losses against gains ("קיזוז הפסד"), it says: "לא נמצא מקור… שאלה פתוחה" (no source found… open question). On moving the money to a pension fund at 60, it says: "האתר עוד לא מחשב" (the site doesn't calculate this yet).
- **The tax-shield box.** "המלצה, לא חוק" (a recommendation, not a law). It could change the rules later, but it isn't real yet.
- **The example doesn't fit me.** It uses 10,000 ₪ plus 500 ₪ a month, not my 50,000 at once. Also 82,303 ₪ "בכסף של היום" (in today's money) next to 100,327 ₪ "בשקלים של העתיד" (in future shekels) took me a second to understand.
- **The fees in the example.** 5,303 ₪ in fees against 70,000 ₪ deposited over 10 years is a lot. Most of it probably comes from the 4% deposit fee after year 5, which makes the deposit fee the big thing to negotiate.
- **The top line "123 ממקור 123 מחושב".** It probably means 123 numbers come from a source and 123 are calculated. It's cryptic.
