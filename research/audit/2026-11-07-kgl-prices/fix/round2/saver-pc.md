I'm answering as the 38-year-old saver, after reading all 12 screenshots.

**1. My decision**

I wouldn't open it anywhere yet. If I had to pick today I'd lean towards הראל פנסיה וגמל, but I'm only about 30% sure, and the page itself tells me not to rely on that.

- **Why הראל:** the side box says its documented fees are "הנמוכים ביותר" (the lowest) of the 12 companies. During the discount that's 0.7% a year on my savings and 0% on deposits.
- **Why I'm not sure:** the cost table says "אצל 9 מהן המסמכים כותבים רק את התקרה שבחוק, ומה שגובים בפועל נקבע בהסכם עם כל עמית". So 9 of the 12 companies only publish the legal maximum, and the real fee is whatever I negotiate. The "lowest" claim compares published numbers, not what I would actually pay. The box even says "בהסכם אישי חברה אחרת יכולה לגבות פחות" (with a personal deal, another company could charge less).
- **What I still need to find out:** real quotes from two or three companies, and whether I can lock the fee in for good rather than for "5 שנים, לפחות" (5 years at least). I'd also check each company's own page, since the table footnote says "כמה כל חברה גובה בפועל, בכל מסלול, מופיע בעמוד של החברה" (what each company really charges, per investment option, is on the company's page). I'd also need to choose an investment option, such as "מדד S&P 500" or "מסלול כללי".
- **Parts I used:** the fee table under "כמה זה עולה", the "תנאי המוצר בדוגמה" box, the chart, and the yearly limit "83,641.00 ₪ לשנה" (my 50,000 fits). I also used the tax and withdrawal sections: "בכל עת" (any time) and "עד 4 ימי עסקים" (up to 4 business days).

**2. The grey sentences under each fee**

- **Fee on savings:** the 12 companies' documents give between 0.7% and 1.05% a year. 9 of them only state the legal ceiling, and the real fee is agreed with each customer. Anything below the ceiling counts as a "discount", and when the discount ends they're allowed to raise it to the ceiling.
- **Fee on deposits:** same wording, with a range of "0% לבין 4%".

What's unclear or odd:
- "בין 0.7% לבין 1.05%": if 9 of the 12 publish only 1.05%, then that "range" really comes from just 3 companies. That isn't obvious.
- "שיעור נמוך מהתקרה הוא הנחה" can be read two ways. Is any fee below the ceiling a temporary discount, even a company's normal price? Then every low fee could be raised one day.
- "עמית" (member) is jargon the page never explains. I guessed it means me, the customer.
- The two sentences are nearly copy-paste, so it's easy to miss that one fee is "בשנה" (on my whole balance every year) and the other is "מכל הפקדה" (on each deposit).
- I didn't spot a number or full stop that's actually in the wrong place. The line breaks before "שיעור" and "התקרה." look like plain text wrapping.

**3. The company behind the example**

- **Which:** "התנאים והתעריף בדוגמה הם של הראל פנסיה וגמל" (the example's terms and rates are Harel's).
- **Why that one:** the site says its documented fees are the lowest, "ולכן נשאר אצלה הכי הרבה בתוכנית לדוגמה" (so the most money is left in the sample plan).
- **What happens over time:** 0.7% a year on savings and 0% on deposits for a discount period of "5 שנים, לפחות". The example then assumes "שההנחה נגמרת בדיוק בתום התקופה הזו ושדמי הניהול עולים אז עד התקרה". So from year 6 it's 1.05% a year on savings and 4% of every deposit.

The odd part is that the "cheapest" company ends up charging the legal maximum after 5 years. In the 10-year chart, half the period is at the ceiling. That's how the chart gets "דמי ניהול 5,303 ₪".

**4. What I distrust or want explained**

- **The 82,303 figure:** "נשאר לכם 82,303 ₪" is in today's money ("בכסף של היום"), but "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪" looks like plain shekels. Comparing the two makes the gain look smaller than the 100,327 the account would actually show. I'm also not sure which kind of money the tax and fee figures are in.
- **The example doesn't match me:** it uses 10,000 ₪ plus 500 a month, not my 50,000. There are pencil icons, so I'd change the numbers.
- **The tax wording:** "עד 25% מהרווח הריאלי" (up to 25% of the real profit) sits next to "החברה המנהלת מנכה במקור 25% מהרווח" (the company deducts 25% of the profit at source). The second one doesn't say real or nominal. Then there's "האתר מציג גם חלופה: 15% מהרווח הנומינלי" (the site also shows an alternative: 15% of the nominal profit), and I don't know when that applies.
- **Possible future change:** "המלצה, לא חוק" points to a Treasury recommendation for a 200,000 ₪ cap. It could change the rules later.
- **Things the site admits it doesn't know:** "קיזוז הפסד... שאלה פתוחה" (offsetting a loss against other profit is an open question). Also whether money moved at 60 into "קופת גמל לקצבה" (a pension-type fund) can still come out as a lump sum: "שאלה פתוחה".
- **The 5-year discount:** "תקופת ההנחה, לפחות 5 שנים" is presented as a legal minimum, but I'd want to see that in the actual contract.
- **The source itself:** the footer says "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (a personal, non-commercial site with no marketing links), with data correct to 07.11.2026. I take that as a good sign about bias, but it means one person's research, not official advice.
