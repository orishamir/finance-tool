Who: You are 27 and have 25,000 shekels saved for a master's degree that may start in six months or in a year. You are deciding between a one-year bank deposit and leaving the money in your current account. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance.

**1. Can I take the money out after 6 months, and at which banks?**
Only sometimes. The site collected 28 deposits from the rate tables of 4 banks, which it doesn't name. Of those 28:
- **18 can't be broken.** The money comes back only at the end of the term.
- **4 of those 18 have "exit points" (תחנות יציאה).** These are set dates when you can take the money out without breaking the deposit and without a penalty. The page doesn't say when these dates fall, so I can't tell whether one lands at 6 months. It also doesn't say which bank offers them.
- **10 can be broken early with a penalty.** All 10 are at Bank Discount.

**2. What would breaking it early cost me?**
The page gives no amount in shekels. At Discount the penalty is set only on the day you break the deposit, based on interest rates that change every day, so there's no amount known in advance. Breaking "can" wipe out the interest and even part of the money you deposited.

**3. The line 'הקנס הוא תנאי של כל בנק: בנק דיסקונט'**
That exact sentence isn't on the page. The closest line is "תנאי השבירה בעמוד הבנק: בנק דיסקונט", which is a link telling me the breaking terms are on the bank's own page. Only Discount is linked, which fits with all 10 breakable deposits being at Discount.

**4. The red box in the tax section, in my own words**
If breaking the deposit eats into the money I put in, nobody knows how tax is handled; no source the site uses covers it. The only known fact is that the bank takes tax off the interest it actually pays out.

**5. Are the numbers (28, 18, 4, 10) about every deposit in Israel?**
No. They only cover the 28 deposits the site collected from 4 banks' tables. The page also lists more banks in Israel than that, and it doesn't say which 4 were surveyed.

**6. What confused me**
- **"ו־4 מתוך ה־18 אפשר למשוך גם בתחנות יציאה"**: These deposits supposedly "can't be broken", yet you can still withdraw from them. The word "גם" (also) is confusing: also in addition to what? The end of the term? When exactly are these exit points?
- **"4 בנקים"**: Which banks? It matters, because I'd need to know where to go.
- **"לפי ריביות שמתעדכנות כל יום"**: Which rates? The deposit's own rate, or the bank's current rate? I can't use this to estimate the penalty.
- **"שבירה יכולה למחוק את הריבית ואף חלק מהסכום"**: The word "יכולה" (can) doesn't tell me how likely this is or how much it would be.

**7. Is anything missing that I need to decide?**
Yes:
- an actual penalty amount or a worked example;
- whether any exit point falls at 6 months, and at which bank;
- which banks were surveyed;
- what the tax would be if I break the deposit;
- what my current account would earn, to compare against. The page doesn't cover leaving the money in a current account at all.

Since the master's might start in 6 months, the only option the page shows I can count on without a possible loss is a deposit with an exit point at the right time. The page doesn't tell me where to find one.
