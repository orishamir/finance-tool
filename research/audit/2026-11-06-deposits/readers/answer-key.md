# Answer key (written before the readers ran)

1. Can I get my money out before the end? Most of the deposits the site collected (18 of 28, at 4 banks) cannot be broken: the money comes back only at the end, and in 4 of them also at exit points. 10, all at Discount, can be broken with a penalty.
2. How much does breaking cost? No shekel figure: at the bank that allows it, the penalty is set only on the day of the break, from rates updated daily, so no amount is known in advance. It can take all the interest and part of the money deposited.
3. "הקנס הוא תנאי של כל בנק: בנק דיסקונט" means: each bank sets its own penalty; the link leads to Discount's terms.
4. The red box: no source says how tax works when a break takes part of the money deposited; the bank withholds tax on the interest it pays. It is an open question, not a statement that breaking is impossible.
5. The counts are of what the site collected, not of every deposit in the market.
