**(1) Can I take the money out after 6 months? At which banks?**
Sometimes. The page looked at 28 deposits from the rate tables of 4 banks:
- **18 can't be broken.** The money comes back only at the end of the term. 4 of these 18 have "exit points" (תחנות יציאה), which the glossary defines as fixed dates during the deposit when you can withdraw without a penalty. The page doesn't say when those dates are, so I can't tell if one falls at 6 months.
- **10 can be broken with a penalty, and all 10 are Discount's.**
- The only bank named in this paragraph is Discount. It doesn't say which bank has the 4 deposits with exit points.
- The page's own example deposit (12 months, not linked to the index) is one that **can't** be broken.

**(2) What would it cost?**
The page gives no amount in shekels. It says the penalty is set only on the day you break the deposit, based on rates that change daily, so there's no known amount in advance. Breaking can wipe out all the interest and even part of the money you put in.

**(3) "הקנס הוא תנאי של כל בנק: בנק דיסקונט"**
The penalty is set by each bank's own terms, not by the site or by law. "בנק דיסקונט" is a link to the one bank whose terms the site collected. It does **not** mean all banks have a penalty or that the rules are the same everywhere.

**(4) The red box in the tax section**
"המס כששבירה פוגעת בסכום שהופקד: שאלה פתוחה." In my words: if breaking the deposit eats into the money you put in, the site doesn't know how that is taxed. It's an open question.

**(5) Do the numbers (28, 18, 4, 10) cover every deposit in Israel?**
No, they're much narrower. They cover only the 28 deposits the site took from the rate tables of 4 banks. They aren't all deposits in Israel, and the page doesn't say they're all the deposits at those 4 banks.
