# phone-parent

Who: You are 58, reading on your phone. Your bank offered you a one-year deposit for 100,000 shekels you may need if your daughter's wedding moves earlier. You read Hebrew natively and know little about banking terms.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-2.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-3.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-4.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-5.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-6.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-7.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-8.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-9.png, research/audit/2026-11-06-deposits/readers/shots-phone/page-10.png

I read all 10 screenshots. Some text falls between screenshots or sits under the floating "up" button, and I note where.

**1. Can I take the money out after 6 months? At which banks?**
It depends on the deposit. The page looked at 28 deposits from 4 banks:
- **18 can't be broken.** The money comes back only at the end of the period.
- **4 have "exit points"** (תחנות יציאה). The glossary defines these as set dates when you can withdraw without breaking the deposit or harming its terms.
- **10 can be broken with a penalty, and all 10 are Discount's.**

The page doesn't say which banks the 18 and the 4 belong to. It doesn't say whether any exit point falls at 6 months. A line in "שווה לדעת" reads "שבירת פיקדון: הסכמת הבנק, הטופס והעלות". That suggests breaking a deposit needs the bank's agreement, but the page doesn't explain this.

**2. How much would it cost?**
The page gives no amount. It says the site doesn't show the penalty in shekels, because the bank sets it only on the day you break the deposit, based on interest rates that change. Breaking can wipe out the interest **and even part of the money you put in.**

**3. "הקנס הוא תנאי של כל בנק: בנק דיסקונט"**
Each bank sets its own penalty, and Discount is the only bank linked. It's unclear whether Discount is just an example or the only bank whose terms the site has. Either way, it tells you nothing about other banks' penalties.

**4. The red box in the tax section**
I can't tell you. Only its top edge (end of screenshot 6) and bottom edge (top of screenshot 7) appear. Its contents aren't in any screenshot.

**5. Do 28, 18, 4 and 10 cover every deposit in Israel?**
No. They cover only the 28 deposits the site collected from the rate tables of 4 banks. The page lists 9 banks elsewhere and doesn't name the 4.

**6. Confusing parts**
- **"בתנאים שנאספו"** (in the conditions that were collected): which conditions, from which banks?
- **"לפי ריביות שמתעדכנות … ולכן"** (based on rates that update … and therefore): the floating button covers part of this sentence, so I couldn't read it all.
- **"ואף חלק מהסכום שהופקד"** (and even part of the amount deposited): this is frightening, and no example or limit is given.
- **"BANK-06"**: a code that means nothing to me.
- **"הסכמת הבנק"** (the bank's agreement): does this mean the bank could refuse to let me take my own money?

**7. What's missing for my decision**
- Whether the deposit my bank offered me can be broken, or has an exit point at about 6 months.
- Even a rough example of the penalty on 100,000 ₪.
- Which banks the 4 deposits with exit points belong to.
- Whether the bank can refuse an early withdrawal.
- What the red box says.

Before signing, ask the bank in writing: "Can I withdraw after 6 months, is there an exit point, and what would the penalty be on 100,000 ₪?"
