## The founder's plan: 6,000 NIS once, then 400 NIS a month into one ETF for 20 years

Broker charges only (the fund's own costs, tax and the final sale are not included). Shekels to dollars at the ECB reference rates of 23.10.2026 (USD 1.1193 and ILS 3.4939 per euro, so 3.1215 NIS per dollar; GBP 0.83728 per euro). That makes 400 NIS = USD 128.14 and 6,000 NIS = USD 1,922.15. Share counts stay under the per-share minimum thresholds (250 shares at Interactive Israel, 200 at IBKR Fixed), so the minimum is what each buy costs.

| Route | First buy | Each monthly buy | 241 buys over 20 years | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interactive Israel, US ETF | USD 2.50 | USD 2.50 (the 2% cap is USD 2.56, so it does not bite); 1.95% of 400 NIS | USD 602.50, about 1,881 NIS | 0 in the recurring plan; otherwise 10 NIS a conversion, 2,410 NIS over 241 |
| Interactive Israel, CSPX on the LSE | USD 2.88 (0.15%) | USD 2.50, or USD 3.34 if the minimum is 2.5 GBP | USD 602.88, or USD 805.44 on the GBP reading | same as above |
| IBKR Fixed, US ETF | USD 1.00 | USD 1.00 (1% cap is USD 1.28); 0.78% of 400 NIS | USD 241, about 752 NIS | auto 0.03%: about 31 NIS on 102,000 NIS; or spot USD 2.00 minimum a conversion, USD 482 |
| IBKR Tiered, US ETF | USD 0.35 plus third-party fees | USD 0.35 plus third-party fees | USD 84.35 plus third-party fees | same as above |
| IBKR Fixed, CSPX on the LSE | USD 4.00 | USD 4.00; 3.12% of 400 NIS | USD 964, about 3,009 NIS | same as above |
| IBKR Tiered, CSPX on the LSE | USD 1.70 plus LSE fees | USD 1.70 plus LSE fees | USD 409.70 plus LSE fees | same as above |

What this says: at 400 NIS a month the minimum per trade decides the cost. The cheapest route printed is IBKR Tiered on a US ETF, then IBKR Fixed on a US ETF; for CSPX, Interactive Israel's minimum beats IBKR Fixed, and IBKR Tiered sits between them before LSE fees. None of the two charges custody, so the cost does not grow with the balance. The figures above are illustrations of the price lists, not site output: the site's engine cannot compute them yet (schema needs 1 to 5).

Doubts that move these numbers:
- IBKR's US footnote on fractional shares says both "the same commission rates as whole share trades" and "the greater of 1% of the trade value or USD 0.01" for each fractional trade. If a monthly 400 NIS buys a fraction of a share and the second sentence applies, the Fixed buy costs USD 1.28, not USD 1.00.
- Interactive Israel's cap of 2% on US trades: the tariff does not say what happens when the cap is below the minimum (IBKR says the cap wins). At 400 NIS it does not matter (USD 2.56 against 2.50); below about 390 NIS a month it would.
- Interactive Israel's European minimum for a USD line on the LSE: "מינימום 2.5 בהתאם למטבע העסקה" points to USD, "תיגבה במטבע הראשי של הבורסה" points to GBP. The snapshot records USD 2.5 and states the conflict.
- Whether a USD buy from a shekel balance at IBKR runs through auto conversion (0.03%) or needs a spot conversion (USD 2.00 minimum) depends on the account's settings; not stated as a default.

## Schema needs (the schema was not changed)

1. **`provider.regulator` has only BoI, ISA, CMA.** Neither broker is supervised by any of them (Interactive Israel: CySEC, and it says it is not supervised by the ISA; IBKR: SEC/CFTC/FINRA for IB LLC, Central Bank of Ireland for IB Ireland). Both snapshots carry "ISA" because the schema forces a value; the built company pages therefore print "מפוקח: רשות ניירות ערך", which is wrong. **Fix before these pages are published**: a foreign-regulator value (or a free regulator name) in `packages/schema` and `site/app/copy.ts`.
2. **`provider.class` has no `foreign_broker`** (data-schema §2 lists it; the Zod enum has `broker`). Both are `broker`.
3. **No per-share unit** (USD per share): both brokers' US commissions are recorded with `rate: null` and the per-share figure in notes, as in `psagot-buy-sell-foreign-us`. The engine marks the line "not comparable".
4. **A cap as a percentage of trade value** (2% at Interactive Israel, 1% at IBKR) cannot be a `max`: the engine reads a trade max in shekels. Recorded in notes, `max: null`.
5. **Trade minimums in a foreign currency.** The engine reads a trade `min` in shekels only (`nis()` in `packages/engine/src/providers/workbench.ts`); the LSE lines carry a non-null rate with a USD minimum. Today the foreign trade lines are all banded and the engine stops at "ambiguous", but if a person names a plan band (for example "IBKR Pro - Fixed: בורסת לונדון") the engine throws a non-ambiguity error and the site returns an engine error for the whole run. The existing Psagot Europe line (min EUR 15) has the same exposure.
6. **No GBP unit** (Interactive Israel's 2.5 GBP minimum on GBP trades).
7. **No basis-point unit**: IBKR's spot conversion "0.20 basis point * Trade Value" is `rate: null` with the minimum USD 2.00 recorded.
8. **Plans with no standard line.** IBKR's Fixed and Tiered are both plans the client picks; they are recorded as bands, so there is no `band: null` trade line and the engine needs the person to name one. Tiered volume schedules are in notes only.
9. **No base for a cash-withdrawal fee** (a free count per month, then a fee per withdrawal). Not recorded as lines; in BRK-40 and BRK-42.
10. **No snapshot-level notes field.** The TASE-for-institutions-only fact and Interactive Israel's "personal offer within 30 days" sentence went into a line's notes.
11. **No per-class availability field.** "Not available at this broker" can only be shown by the absence of a line (Interactive Israel's TASE lines are left out for that reason, with the figures in notes); IBKR's TASE lines exist although eligibility for Israelis is unconfirmed.
12. **No line type for third-party fees** (SEC, FINRA CAT, NSCC/DTC, exchange fees); in notes. BRK already proposes `fee-third-party`.
13. **`applies_to` cannot name an exchange** (US against LSE are both `foreign_securities`), so the two foreign trade lines per broker are told apart by `band`.
14. **No shares-only TASE class** (`tase_shares_bonds` includes bonds); IBKR's TASE lines name `etf_units` only.

## Hand-offs outside my files

- `data/sources/publishers.json` has no row for `interactivebrokers.com`; the rule sources of BRK-42 to BRK-45 show the host as publisher and no `publisher_he` (the validator warns).
- Site company page for IBKR (`/companies/interactive-brokers`): it lists the two TASE lines without their plan names (Fixed, Tiered) and the FX minimum "2" without its currency; the null-rate US lines are not shown. Seen in the built HTML, not changed.
- The comparison's own pick (F-80) is unaffected: every foreign-broker trade line is banded, so neither broker is priced unless the person names a plan, and the check's broker tests still pass. The zero-fee lines (Interactive Israel's account fee and both brokers' custody and conversion lines) name only the classes each broker prices, because a probe of the engine's `buildTariff` showed that a zero line alone made a broker "compared" at no cost for a class it has no trade line for (a TASE fund at Interactive Israel). With that, Interactive Israel is "no applicable line" for every TASE and Israeli-fund class and "ambiguous" for foreign securities; IBKR is "ambiguous" for TASE ETFs and foreign securities and "no applicable line" for Israeli funds.
