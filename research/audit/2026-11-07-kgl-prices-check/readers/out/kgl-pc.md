# kgl-pc

Who: You are 41 and have about 60,000 shekels in a kupat gemel lehashkaa (a provident fund for investing) that you opened a couple of years ago. You want to know whether it is worth asking the fund for a lower fee. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers/shots-kgl-1440/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers/shots-kgl-1440/page-2.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers/shots-kgl-1440/page-3.png

I read all three screenshots.

**1. Would I call?** Probably not, and I'm not sure. Two things on the page point opposite ways:
- Both fee cards have an orange "שווה להתקשר" badge. The rule behind it is: "שווה להתמקח מ־1,000 ₪, כשיש ממוצע נמוך מהמחיר שלכם". The management fee costs 3,902 ₪, and the average (0.62%) is lower than the fee after the discount (1.05%), so the badge appears.
- But the page says I'd gain only "כ־108.03 ₪ ב־10 שנים". Calling for about 11 ₪ a year doesn't seem worth it.

The ranking line also made me doubt it: "המוצר הזה במקום 2 מבין כל האפשרויות". If my product is already second best, why call? In the end, the 108 ₪ figure decided it for me.

**2. How much it's worth:** About 108 ₪ over 10 years, if the management fee drops from 0.7% to the average 0.62%. That is with the page's default inputs: 10,000 ₪ balance, 500 ₪ a month, 7% return. It is not my 60,000 ₪. The page never says the figure for my balance, and I won't guess it.

**3. When or why it could change:** The lower fee only lasts 5 years. After that the calculation assumes it goes back up to 1.05%, so the saving is small. The page also says the answer flips to "not worth the bother" if:
- I hold the money for less than about 8.9 years instead of 10;
- the deposit fee after the discount is 3.09% instead of 4%;
- the management fee is 0.42% now instead of 0.7% (and 0.62% instead of 1.05% after the discount).

Changing the return (0–10%) or inflation (0–5%) doesn't change the answer.

**4. Things that seemed to say two different things:**
- "שווה להתקשר" next to "שווה 108.03 ₪ ב־10 שנים". The badge comes from the 1,000 ₪ rule, but what I'd actually gain is about a tenth of that.
- The deposit fee card also says "שווה להתקשר". But my deposit fee is 0% and the average is "0% (חציון 0%)", so there seems to be nothing to ask for.
- Different numbers for the same fee: management fee "3,902 ₪" in the card but "5,203 ₪" in the graph. Combined effect "27.81 ₪" in one place and "37.08 ₪" in another. The note "הגרף מראה את ההפרש לפני מס" explains part of this, but not why the management fee gap is so big.
- The top bar says "לפי הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ", but the average is based on "מיטב גמל להשקעה כללי של מיטב גמל ופנסיה בע"מ". So which fund is this about?

**5. Wording that confused me or I read twice:**
- "המוצר הזה במקום 2 מבין כל האפשרויות": second out of what, and is 1 the best?
- "מאוחז זמן שווה להתמקח": I couldn't parse it. It looks like a typo.
- "במוצע": a typo for "בממוצע"?
- "מה הגיד בשיחה": a typo for "מה להגיד"?
- "השפעה משולבת של העמלות על זו": "על זו" of what?
- "מחצי עד כפול" as a heading, while the numbers inside (4% → 3.09%) are neither half nor double.
- "הייתם משאירים אצלכם": I had to stop to work out that it means money I'd save.
- "זו הערכה בלבד: היא לא משנה את הדירוג בהשוואה": what ranking?
- "האתר לא משתמש בו במסקנה" (about the 0.53% average): then why show it?

**6. What's missing to decide:**
- The result for my own 60,000 ₪ balance and how much I actually deposit each month.
- Whether 0.7% is really my fee, and whether my discount actually ends after 5 years.
- Which fund this is (Harel or Meitav).
- Whether the fund is likely to agree, and what to say: "מה להגיד בשיחה" was collapsed.
