# kh-pc

Who: You are 38, self-employed, and have a keren hishtalmut (a study fund) that you deposit into every month. You want to know whether it is worth asking the fund for a lower fee. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers/shots-kh-1440/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers/shots-kh-1440/page-2.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers/shots-kh-1440/page-3.png

I read all three screenshots. Hebrew quotes are copied from the page; I explain them in English.

**(1) Would I call?** Probably yes, but I wouldn't expect much from it. The orange label "שווה להתמקח" ("worth haggling") on the management-fee card decided it. The rule under it says haggling is worth it when the fee costs "1,000 ₪ או יותר, ויש ממוצע נמוך מהמחיר שלכם" (1,000 ₪ or more, and there is an average lower than your price). My fee is 0.6%, the regulator's average is 0.51%, and the cost is 3,911 ₪. One problem: the page is built on one specific fund, "מיטב השתלמות כללי". I don't know if that's my fund or my fee, so I'd first have to check my own fee and change the inputs.

**(2) How much is it worth?** About **187 ₪ (186.97) over 10 years**, if the fee drops from 0.6% to 0.51%. That uses the page's example numbers: 10,000 ₪ today, 500 ₪ a month, 7% a year.

**(3) When or why could that amount change?**
- The page assumes the lower fee lasts only 5 years and then goes back to 0.6% ("נשמרת 5 שנים, ואחר כך חוזרת ל־0.6%"). It doesn't say why 5 years.
- The amount moves if I change the sliders (amount today, monthly deposit, years, return).
- The "what would change the conclusion" box says the advice flips only if my fee were already 0.51%. Changing the return (0–10%), inflation (0–5%) or holding period (8–12 years) doesn't flip it.

**(4) Things that seemed to say two different things**
- The label "שווה להתמקח" sits next to a gain of only "186.97 ₪ ב־10 שנים". The label is based on the total cost (3,911), not on what I'd save. The page even says "זו הערכה בלבד: היא לא משנה את הדירוג בהשוואה" (this is only an estimate; it doesn't change the ranking).
- "שווה להימנע מ־2,500 ₪" (worth avoiding from 2,500 ₪). My cost is 3,911, so should I avoid this fund? But the page also says "המוצר הזה במקום ראשון מבין כל האפשרויות" (this product is in first place among all options).
- The card says 3,911 is "אחרי מס" (after tax). The year-10 table says "לפני מס" (before tax) and shows the same 3,911, with "השפעה משולבת 0 ₪" (combined effect 0 ₪).
- The average is 0.51%, but the median is "חציון 0.6%", which is exactly my fee. By the median my fee is normal. There's also a 0.53% average for all funds that "האתר לא משתמש בו במסקנה" (the site doesn't use in the conclusion).
- The page suggests lowering the fee to 0.51%. Then it says the conclusion flips if the fee goes "מ־0.6% ל־0.51%: אז לא שווה את הטרחה" (from 0.6% to 0.51%: then it's not worth the trouble). It took me a while to understand that this means "if you were already paying 0.51%".

**(5) Words or sentences I didn't understand or read twice**
- "המסקנה על דמי ניהול מחיסכון מתהפכת אם דמי ניהול מחיסכון, מחצי עד כפול יעבור…": the grammar is broken, and I don't know what "מחצי עד כפול" (from half to double) refers to.
- "שווה להימנע מ־2,500 ₪, כשאפשרות אחרת בהשוואה לא גובה את העמלה": avoid what, exactly?
- "במקום ראשון… גם אחרי העמלות": first place in what ranking, compared with what?
- "השפעה משולבת" (combined effect): not explained.
- "PF-39", "123 מקור / 123 מחושב" and "חוקים וכללים · 2" / "· 8": I don't know what these codes and counts mean.
- "בכסף של היום" (in today's money) and the 2% inflation figure: inflation isn't one of the inputs.

- How likely the fund is to agree.
- What to actually say on the call. "מה להגיד בשיחה" (what to say on the call) is collapsed, so I couldn't see it.
