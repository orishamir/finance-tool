# kh-pc

Who: You are 38, self-employed, and have a keren hishtalmut (a study fund) that you deposit into every month. You want to know whether it is worth asking the fund for a lower fee. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p7/shots-kh-1440/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p7/shots-kh-1440/page-2.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p7/shots-kh-1440/page-3.png

**(1) Would I call?** Probably not, though the page never says so outright. It says a lower fee is "worth checking" (שווה להתמקח) only if it saves **₪1,000 or more**. For my fund it shows a saving of **₪186.97**, well under that line. What settled it for me was the line under the card: "שווה להתמקח כשהורדת העמלה לממוצע שווה ‎₪1,000 או יותר". The orange label "יקר יחסית לחלופות בלי עמלה" made me think for a moment that I should do something. As far as I can tell, though, it's about moving to products with no fee, not about asking for a discount.

**(2) How much is it worth?** About **₪186.97 over 10 years**, if the fee drops from 0.6% to the 0.51% average. The calculation assumes the lower fee lasts only **5 years** and then goes back up to 0.6%. These numbers come from the site's example (balance ₪10,000, ₪500 a month, 10 years, 7% return), not from my own fund, unless I change the inputs. The fee as a whole costs ₪3,911 over 10 years: ₪108,613 without fees against ₪104,702 with them.

**(3) When could it change?** The page gives three things that could change the result:
- How many years I keep the money (8 years instead of 10 changes the conclusion).
- A different fee to begin with (0.38% instead of 0.6%).
- A very different return (0.81% instead of 7%).

Inflation anywhere from 0% to 5% doesn't change it. But all three conditions are about the "relatively expensive" label. None of them says when the ₪186.97 would get past ₪1,000.

**(4) Places that seemed to say two different things:**
- "לפי המספרים בעמוד, המוצר הזה במקום ראשון מבין כל האפשרויות, גם אחרי העמלות". Yet the same page also says "יקר יחסית לחלופות בלי עמלה" and "שווה להימנע כשהעמלה עולה ‎₪2,500 או יותר ואפשרות אחרת בהשוואה לא גובה אותה". Is it first place, or something to avoid?
- "שווה ‎₪186.97 ב־10 שנים" sits right next to "בחישוב, עמלה נמוכה יותר נשמרת 5 שנים". So is the saving over 10 years or over 5?
- Two different averages: 0.51% (the site's own, from 19 tracks) and 0.53% (the published one, "האתר לא משתמש בו במסקנה").

**(5) Things I didn't understand or read twice:**
- "זו הערכה בלבד: היא לא משנה את הדירוג בהשוואה". Which ranking? Why would a fee discount not count?
- "ב־8 שנים במקום 10 שנים, המסקנה... עוברת מ'יקר יחסית...' ל'לא שווה את הטרחה'". It seems to say that a shorter period makes the fee matter less, but the sentence is hard to parse.
- "ב־0.81% במקום 7%". A 0.81% return seemed strange and unexplained.
- "השפעה משולבת: הפער עד הקו" and "השפעה משולבת ‎₪0". I didn't understand either one.
- "סך העמלות לפני מס". Tax isn't mentioned anywhere else on the page.
- "PF-39", the "מחושב" badge, "123 ממקור / 123 מחושב", "פרמטר של האתר". All of these are unexplained labels.
- "אין עמלה כזו בפיקדון… קרן כספית". Are those real alternatives to a keren hishtalmut? The page doesn't mention the tax benefit I'd lose.

**(6) What's missing:**

- Anything specific to being self-employed, such as the deposit ceiling or the tax benefit.
- How hard it is to get a discount.
- What to actually say on the call. That section ("מה להגיד בשיחה") is collapsed, so I couldn't read it.
