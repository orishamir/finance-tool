# kgl-pc

Who: You are 41 and have about 60,000 shekels in a kupat gemel lehashkaa (a provident fund for investing) that you opened a couple of years ago. You want to know whether it is worth asking the fund for a lower fee. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p7/shots-kgl-1440/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p7/shots-kgl-1440/page-2.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p7/shots-kgl-1440/page-3.png

**1. Would I call?** Probably not. The page never says "call" or "don't call" in plain words. It does set a rule: "שווה להתמקח כשהורדת העמלה לממוצע שווה 1,000 ₪ או יותר". It then says lowering my fee to the average (0.62%) is worth only "כ־108.03 ₪ ב־10 שנים". That is far below 1,000 ₪, so by the page's own rule, asking isn't worth it. The second line on the page, "המוצר הזה במקום 2 מבין כל האפשרויות", also told me my fund isn't bad.

**2. How much is it worth?** About 108 ₪ over 10 years. But that is for the page's example: 10,000 ₪ balance, 500 ₪ a month, 7% return. I have 60,000 ₪. The page says "המספרים לדוגמה של האתר, עד שתשנו אותם", and on a screenshot I can't change them. So I don't know my own number.

**3. When could it change?** Two reasons:
- In the calculation, a lower fee only lasts 5 years. After that it goes back to 1.05% ("נשמרת 5 שנים, ואחר כך חוזרת ל־1.05%"). That is part of why the saving is so small.
- The "מה היה משנה את המסקנה" box says the answer flips if I hold for only 8.17 years instead of 10. It also flips if the fee is 0.44% instead of 0.7%. Return (0–10%), inflation (0–5%) and the deposit fee (2–4%) don't flip it.

It never says *which* conclusion flips. Both flips end with "אז לא שווה את הטרחה". That reads as if the current answer is "worth it", which clashes with the 108 ₪.

**4. Things that seemed to say two different things:**
- On the same fee card, there's a badge "שווה להחליף" with "העלות 2,500 ₪ או יותר", next to "שווה 108.03 ₪ ב־10 שנים" and "שווה להתמקח… 1,000 ₪ או יותר". So is acting worth it or not?
- "שווה להימנע כשהעמלה עולה 2,500 ₪ או יותר" sounds like an instruction to leave. It sits right next to the negotiation advice.
- The fee cost is 3,902 ₪ on the card ("אחרי מס") but 5,203 ₪ in the chart ("לפני מס"). The total is 5,223 at the top but 6,564 in the chart.
- "השפעה משולבת" is 27.81 ₪ in one place and 37.08 ₪ in another.
- There are two averages: 0.62% ("ממוצע… 11 מסלולים") and 0.53% ("לכל קופות הגמל יחד… האתר לא משתמש בו במסקנה").

**5. Words I didn't understand or read twice:**
- "השפעה משולבת": the explanation ("הסכום של כל השורות והשורה הזו היא העלות הכוללת") didn't help me.
- "123 מקור / 123 מחושב", "PF-39", "פרמטר של האתר": codes I don't know.
- "לפי מסלול 7978": I wasn't sure if this is a track number or a count of tracks.
- "שווה להחליף": switch what, to where?
- "דמי ניהול מהפקדה 0%… לא שווה עכשיו": why mention it at all?
- "תקופת ההחזקה ב־8.17 שנים": a strangely exact number.
- "לפי עמוד המדריך: קופת גמל להשקעה, לפי הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ": is this my fund, or just an example?

**6. What's missing:**
- My own numbers: balance, fee, which company, how many years. The page only shows the example.
- What to say on the call: "מה להגיד בשיחה" is collapsed.
- Whether the 1,000 ₪ threshold should change for a bigger balance.
- What to do once the 5-year discount ends.

**7. Things that looked like links but didn't lead anywhere I could see:**
- Every dotted-underlined number (108.03, 5,223, 0.62%, 1.05%…).
- The "מחושב", "מקור" and "PF-39" labels.
- "חוקים וכללים · 2 / · 14".
- "מה להגיד בשיחה" and "הצגה כטבלה": the content is hidden.
- "לכל הפרמטרים" and "פרמטרים": they suggest the thresholds are explained somewhere else.
