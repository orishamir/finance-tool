# kh-pc

Who: You are 38, self-employed, and have a keren hishtalmut (a study fund) that you deposit into every month. You want to know whether it is worth asking the fund for a lower fee. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p9/shots-kh-1440/page-0.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p9/shots-kh-1440/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p9/shots-kh-1440/page-2.png

**1. Would I call?** No, not with the numbers on this page. The fee card is labelled **"לא שווה את הטרחה"**. The line under it decided it for me: "הורדת העמלה לממוצע שווה פחות מ־1,000 ₪. שווה להתמקח כשהיא מגיעה ל־1,000 ₪: כשסכום היום 170,000 ₪ או יותר." So my answer depends on my balance. If my fund holds 170,000 ₪ or more, the page says it is worth asking. The page's example uses 10,000 ₪, and I'd need to enter my real balance.

**2. How much is it worth?** About **186.97 ₪ over 10 years, after tax**, if the fee drops from 0.6% to the 0.51% average. That figure assumes the lower fee lasts only 5 years and then goes back to 0.6%. It uses the site's example inputs (10,000 ₪ balance, 500 ₪ a month, 7% return), not mine.

**3. When would the amount change?** It grows with a bigger balance. At about 170,000 ₪ it crosses the 1,000 ₪ line where the page says it's worth asking. The answer would also flip if my fee were 1.1% instead of 0.6%. The page says the conclusion stays the same for any return from 0% to 10%, inflation from 0% to 5%, and holding for 8 to 12 years.

**4. Things that seemed to say two different things**
- "העמלות בקרן השתלמות יעלו לכם 3,911 ₪" sits right next to "186.97 ₪". The big number makes the fee feel expensive, yet asking is worth only about 187 ₪. I had to work out that one is the whole fee and the other is only the reduction.
- "המוצר הזה במקום הראשון מבין כל האפשרויות, גם אחרי העמלות" doesn't fit with my fee (0.6%) being above the average (0.51%). Is my fund good or expensive?
- There are two averages: "0.51%" (19 tracks) and "0.53%… האתר לא משתמש בו במסקנה". Which one do I quote on the phone?
- The calculation runs over 10 years, but the lower fee is "נשמרת 5 שנים". So is the 186.97 ₪ really a 10-year figure?

**5. Words I didn't understand or read twice**
- "מוצג גבוה יותר במקום כשהעמלה עולה 2,500 ₪ או יותר": I couldn't tell what is shown higher, or where.
- "העמלות זו על זו: הפער עד הקו", with the value "0 ₪": I didn't understand what this is, or why it's zero.
- "בחישוב, עמלה נמוכה יותר נשמרת 5 שנים, ואחר כך חוזרת ל־0.6%": why 5 years? Is that what funds actually do?
- "זו הערכה בלבד: היא לא משנה את הדירוג בהשוואה": which ranking?
- "הטווחים שנבדקו הם פרמטר של האתר" / "פרמטר של האתר": the wording is jargon to me.
- "123 ממקור / 123 מחושב": it took me a moment to see this explains the dotted underlines.
- "בכסף של היום": fine once I thought about it, but I read it twice.

- What counts as a realistic fee to ask for.
