# kgl-pc

Who: You are 41 and have about 60,000 shekels in a kupat gemel lehashkaa (a provident fund for investing) that you opened a couple of years ago. You want to know whether it is worth asking the fund for a lower fee. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p9/shots-kgl-1440/page-0.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p9/shots-kgl-1440/page-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-kgl-prices-check/readers-p9/shots-kgl-1440/page-2.png

I read all three screenshots. Screenshot 2 ends at the heading "מה היה משנה את המסקנה" and screenshot 3 starts straight in a table, so any text that came under that heading wasn't captured.

**1. Would I call and ask for a lower fee?**
On these numbers, probably not, or at least not urgently. Three things on the page decided it:
- The saving from asking is small: "אם יורידו ל־0.62% … שווה 108.03 ₪ ב־10 שנים, אחרי מס".
- The page's own rule of thumb: "שווה להתמקח כשהודעת העמלה לממוצע שווה 1,000 ₪ או יותר". 108 ₪ is far below 1,000 ₪.
- The label on the fee card is "מוצר אחר במקום גבוה יותר", and it points to "חשבון ניירות ערך". So the page seems to be nudging me to look at a different product, not to haggle.

Big caveat: every number on the page uses its default inputs (10,000 ₪ today, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7%), not my 60,000 ₪. The screenshots don't show the result for my balance.

**2. How much is asking worth, and over what period?**
108.03 ₪ over 10 years, after tax, in today's money. That assumes the fee drops from 0.7% to 0.62% (the average) and that the lower fee lasts only 5 years.

**3. When or why could that amount change?**
- The calculation assumes the lower fee lasts 5 years and then goes back up to 1.05%.
- Separately, my own discount (0.7%) is only for the "first 5 years". After that the fee can rise to 1.05%, and the page says "אז כדאי לבדוק שוב".
- At the bottom, the page says the conclusion would flip to "לא שווה את הטרחה" in two cases: if I hold for 8 years instead of 10, or if my fee is 0.4% (0.6% after the discount) instead of 0.7% (1.05% after).
- Return, inflation and the deposit fee don't change the conclusion within the ranges it tested.

**4. Things that seemed to say two different things**
- The fee card says "העמלה עולה לפחות 2,500 ₪ … כדאי לבדוק שווה" (look into it), while the saving from asking is only "108.03 ₪", below the "1,000 ₪" threshold.
- "למרות העמלה … המוצר הזה במקום 2 מבין כל האפשרויות" sounds positive, but the badge says "מוצר אחר במקום גבוה יותר".
- Two different averages: "ממוצע הרשות … 0.62%, … מ־11 מסלולים" and "הממוצע שפורסם לשנת 2025 לכל קופות הגמל יחד: 0.53%". The deposit fee has the same problem (0% vs 0.08%).
- Two "העמלות זו על זו" figures: 37.08 ₪ (chart) and 27.81 ₪ (below the cards).
- Different totals for the same fee: 5,203 ₪ vs 3,902 ₪ for the custody fee, and 6,564 ₪ vs 5,223 ₪ overall. The page does explain the 1,341 ₪ gap as before vs after tax and exit costs, but I still read it twice.
- The company: the top says "לפי הראל פנסיה וגמל בע"מ", but further down it says "מסלול מספר 7978, מיטב גמל להשקעה כללי של מיטב גמל ופנסיה בע"מ". I couldn't tell which company it is.
