One thing matters before anything else: **the page is set to 10 years (`שנים 10`), not my 20.** Every shekel figure below is a 10-year result: `₪ 86,492`, `−43 ₪`, `₪ 1,057` and so on. The footer also says the order changes with the return assumption (`בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות`). So I can't take the ranking as final for 20 years.

## 1. Which fund I'd pick

**`ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282` in a `פסגות` account.** The lines that got me there:
- The top card: `אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם` … `₪ 86,492` … `נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים, אחרי דמי ניהול ומס, בכסף של היום`.
- The fund card: `דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים 0.02% נאמן`.
- The account list: Psagot is #1 with `★ הזול` and `₪ 1,057` under `מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים`. The next account, One Zero (`וואן זירו`), takes `₪ 4,544`.
- The step chart: start at MTF in Jerusalem (`ירושלים`) with `78,038`, switch account `+6,110`, switch fund `+2,344`, end at `86,492`.

It is effectively tied with `אלטשולר שחם מחקה 5139100`, which is just `−43 ₪` behind. Both funds have only one year of results (`ב־2025`), which is thin for a 20-year commitment. The `0%` management fee can also be raised later. If I wanted a fund with a 10-year record and no tax while it accumulates (`אין מס בצבירה`), I'd look at `iShares … 1159250` (`−213 ₪`).

I don't want the hedged fund. Its own row reads `−2.44% בשנה · 4 שנים`, and the page says it leaves `₪ 9,460` less than the Yelin fund (`9,460 ₪ יותר מהראל … (עם נטרול)`).

## 2. What I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **`עמלות החשבון זז על`** (grey legend item, values `2` and `3`): I can't parse it. My guess is some fixed account fee.
- **`(4D)` / `(4A)`**: I had to scroll to the bottom of the fund list to learn these are exposure profiles. 4D can be fully foreign-currency; 4A is almost no foreign currency, like a hedged fund.
- **`כמה דרכים יחד`**: my guess is mixed replication (holding shares plus swaps or futures). It's never explained.
- **`מס גם על שינוי המטבע`**: I took it as "you're taxed on the shekel gain, including the currency move". Yet the `iShares 1159250` row doesn't carry this tag, and nothing says why.
- **`מול מדד נטו`** column: it mixes a single year (`+0.37% ב־2025`) with 4- and 10-year averages (`בשנה · 4 שנים`, `בשנה · 10 שנים`) in one column. That's apples and oranges.
- **`לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר`**: so the site does pick a fund, even though it says `האתר לא ממליץ על מדד`.
- **`נשען על כלל פתוח`** and **`מס בצבירה: לא ידוע`**: I only understood these from the legend far below (`נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג`).
- **`לא מושווה`**, **`אין מחיר כאן`** and **`בחירה בה מעבירה את החשבון לאינטראקטיב ישראל`**: confusing. Picking a fund changes my account?
- **The small `זר` badge** next to `מדד S&P 500`: I guessed it means "foreign".
- **`F-92`** in the panel: a meaningless code to me.
- **The paragraph about Europe** in the "why some funds have no price at Psagot" note (`למה חלק מהקרנות בלי מחיר בפסגות`): it contains `אף מסקן` (a typo, probably `מסמך`), and `"1.5 אגורות עבור כל דולר/שקל"… בין 0.48% ל־1.5%` is hard to follow.
- **The step chart's breakdown table**: in the step columns, `−6,659` / `−3,164` mean costs went down (good), and `+549` / `+779` mean tax went up (bad). But in the bars above, `+` means good. The signs flip meaning.
- **`הפרש של נקודת אחוז אחת … שווה … 3,400 ₪ עד 4,400 ₪`**: I had to guess that this is per year, compounded over 10 years.

- `אלטשולר שחם מחקה 5139100` (`−43 ₪`)

This matches the line `ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר, ולכן הם שקולים`.

## 5. The opened panel

- **The figure:** `+0.37% ב־2025`, tagged `מחושב`, labelled `מול מדד נטו`.
- **What a net index is:** one that adds back company dividends after the tax withheld in the company's home country.
- **What the panel says about the method:**
  - Returns are in shekels and come from the fund's own reports.
  - The fund's return is after all its costs.
  - When working out the shekel cost, lag beyond the management fee is added to the cost, but beating the index is not counted as a gain.
- **The formula:** `חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד לפי הדוחות שלה` (`F-92`).
- **The inputs:** `תשואת הקרן ב־2025 2.98%` minus `תשואת S&P 500 (SPTR500N) ב־2025 2.61%`. 2.98 − 2.61 = 0.37 ✓.

Two caveats:
- It's a single year.
- SPTR500N is a dollar index, and `2.61%` only makes sense once converted to shekels (17.4% in dollars × dollar −12.5% ≈ 2.7%). The panel doesn't say that next to the number.

## 7. What looked wrong or untrustworthy

1. **The horizon is 10 years, not 20,** and the page admits the order is sensitive to assumptions.
2. **Invesco `−128`** is shown as almost the cheapest but excluded from both the "equivalent" set and the ranking. The only explanation is a legend far down the page.
3. **The tax tags contradict each other.** `iShares 1159250` gets `אין מס בצבירה`, but Invesco, also an Irish accumulating ETF on the Tel Aviv exchange, gets `מס בצבירה: לא ידוע`.
4. **Interactive Israel** (`אינטראקטיב ישראל`) is marked `אין נתון`, yet the same box gives a result: `ונשארים 84,172 ₪`.
5. **Excellence vs. Meitav Trade** in the account list:
   - Excellence (`אקסלנס`) costs more (`26,827`) yet leaves more (`63,678`).
   - Meitav Trade (`מיטב טרייד`) costs `26,817` and leaves `63,507`.
   - The step-chart tables show the same reversal (`61,931` vs `61,638`).
   - Lower cost should mean more money left.
6. **The `2025` row of the year table:** `+13.5%` should be 13.4 (15.3 − 1.9). It's minor, but sloppy.
7. **The table counts `8 קרנות` for 2022–2024,** although the 10-year iShares and Vanguard funds clearly existed then. The page doesn't say why they were left out.
8. **`קרנות בלי נטרול נשארו קרובות למדד שלהן: −0.9 עד +1.4 נקודות`** doesn't match the per-fund figures in the list, which run from −0.53% to +0.65%.
9. **The top pick and its twin are both one-year-old funds,** one swap-based (`עסקת החלף`), with a `0%` fee that can change. The page ranks them first on 10-year cost without flagging that risk.
