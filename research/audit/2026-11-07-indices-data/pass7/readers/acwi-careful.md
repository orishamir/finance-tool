## 1. Which fund I'd pick

**iShares MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF USD (Acc), Tel Aviv number 1159235**, held at Psagot (פסגות), if Psagot will open an account for me. These lines got me there:

- **Top card:** "★ הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם — פסגות, 1159235 iShares MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF USD (Acc), 85,728 ₪". The card next to it, "הזוג הבא בתור", shows MTF 1201003 at 84,870 ₪, which is "‎-858 ₪ לעומת הזוג הזול ביותר".
- **Fund details:** "דמי ניהול: 0.2% קבועים · אין משתנים · אין שכר נאמן". In the list: "‎-0.06% בשנה · 10 שנים" against the index. That is the longest track record and the smallest gap of any fund that has a price.
- **Its tags:** "אין מס בצבירה" (no tax while the money stays invested). The Israeli funds carry "מס גם על שינוי המטבע" (tax also on currency moves).
- **The dollar-hedged funds:** the hedging box says "בכל שנה שיש עליה דוח היא השיגה פחות ממנו", and "2.5 עד 3.6 נקודות בשנה" behind the index. Over 20 years that rules out the hedged (4A) funds for me.
- **The account matters more than the fund:** the waterfall shows "+5,995 ₪" for switching account and "+3,154 ₪" for switching fund.

**Caveats before I commit:**
- The page is set to **10 years**, not my 20. A footnote warns "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־5 מקומות", so the order can change. I would have to rerun it at 20.

## 3. "בחירת האתר" in the selected row

It sits under the column heading "לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר". It says that this row is the zero point, and every shekel figure in the other rows is measured against it:

- MTF: ‎-858 ₪
- IBI (אי.בי.אי.): ‎-3,154 ₪
- Tachlit (תכלית) 4D: ‎-4,566 ₪
- The two hedged funds (Kesem/קסם 4A and Tachlit 4A): the ranges +1,295/‎-24,327 and +1,193/‎-24,429.

It does not cover the two rows marked "אין מחיר כאן": the foreign-listed IE00B6R52259 and the US ACWI.

Two problems:
- The page never says that these differences are **at Psagot**. At other banks they differ. At First International (הבינלאומי) and Masad (מסד), IBI actually beats iShares: 77,931 vs 76,849 and 77,847 vs 77,271.
- "The site's choice" contradicts the disclaimer "האתר לא ממליץ", and also the card label "הזוג הזול ביותר". Is it a recommendation or just a ranking?

## 5. The opened panel

- **The figure:** "‎-0.06% בשנה · 10 שנים", tagged "מחושב" (calculated by the site), labelled "מול מדד נטו".
- **What "net index" means here:** the index counts dividends after the tax withheld in each company's country. Returns are in dollars, taken from the fund manager's documents. The fund's return is after all its costs. The average includes years when the fund was ahead of the index.
- **In the shekel cost:** "פיגור מעבר לדמי הניהול נוסף על עלות, והקדמה על המדד לא נספרת כרווח" (a lag beyond the management fee is added as a cost; beating the index is not counted as profit).
- **Formula:** "חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד לפי הדוחות שלה", code F-92.
- **Inputs shown:** "תשואת הקרן ב־2016: 7.82%". The index figure ("MSCI All Country World Index 2016 (Net)") is hidden behind the "יש עוד לטמל" button.

So I cannot check even one year.

It is also ambiguous whether the 0.2% fee sits inside the ‎-0.06% or on top of it. If the fund only lagged by 0.06% a year after costs, then charging the full 0.2% (1,148 ₪) as "fund cost" overstates its real drag. The page's own rule ("הקדמה … לא נספרת") produces that.

## 7. What looks wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

1. **The headline depends on the account, and the list doesn't say so.** Psagot+iShares wins, but at First International and Masad the IBI fund beats iShares. The fund list's ‎-3,154 is silently "at Psagot".
2. **"אין מחיר כאן" yet a price elsewhere.** The IE00B6R52259 row has no price, but "סוג קרן אחר, אותו חשבון" prices the same fund bought abroad at 57,371 (‎-19,208) without naming the account. Interactive Israel gives 83,622 and IB 82,277 for the same fund, and IB is marked "אין נתון" while still showing that number.
3. **Hedged funds:** the list gives ranges, but the lower table shows single figures (70,027 / 69,932) with no stated dollar assumption.
4. **Weak data treated as solid:** MTF has "שנתיים" of data, IBI and Tachlit 4 years, all on the same footing as 10 years.
5. **The panel is unverifiable:** the index figure is hidden behind the typo "יש עוד לטמל", and "F-92" means nothing.
6. **"בחירת האתר" vs "האתר לא ממליץ".**
7. **The waterfall chart's y-axis starts at 50,000** (a zigzag on the first bar only), which makes the steps look bigger than they are.
8. **The dollar rate** "3.20 ₪" and the yearly shekel/dollar moves are cross-rates from the European Central Bank's euro rates ("חישוב של האתר"), not the Bank of Israel's official rate.
9. **Unexplained parts:** "עמלות החשבון זו על זו", and the "מוצג ולא מדורג" lock rule.
10. **Wrong horizon by default:** everything is shown for 10 years, and the site itself admits the ranking shifts with assumptions.
