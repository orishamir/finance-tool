I read all 13 screenshots. One limit applies to every number below: the page shows a **10-year plan** (`שנים 10`, 10,000 ₪ to start, 500 ₪ a month, 7% a year). My plan is 20 years. None of the screenshots shows 20-year figures, so I'm judging on the 10-year numbers.

## 1. Which fund I'd pick

**iShares MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF USD (Acc), number 1159235, bought through Pesagot (פסגות).**

These lines got me there:
- The top card, `הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם`: this fund at Pesagot leaves `85,728 ₪`. The next pair, `MSCI AC World (4D) של MTF 1201003` at Pesagot, leaves `84,870 ₪`, shown as `‎-858 ₪ לעומת הזוג הזול ביותר`.
- The fund card: `דמי ניהול: 0.2% קבועים`, `אין שכר נאמן`, `מנהלת הקרן: BlackRock`. In the list it shows `‎-0.06%`, `בשנה · 10 שנים`. That is ten years of history. MTF's `‎-0.31%` covers only `שנתיים` (two years), and IBI and Tachlit (`‎-0.76%` / `‎-0.86%`) cover 4 years.
- In the account list, Pesagot comes first with `85,728 ₪` and `1,043 ₪` (`מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים`). Second is Yahav (יהב) at `80,268 ₪` / `7,032 ₪`, about 5,500 ₪ less.
- The step chart, which goes from `76,579 ₪` to `+5,995` (switch account) and then `+3,154` (switch fund), ending at `85,728`.
- Why not a dollar-hedged fund: they show `‎-2.93%` / `‎-2.91%` a year against the index. The page also says I'd keep `10,219 ₪` more with the unhedged iShares fund than with Kesem (קסם) 4A, if the dollar stays flat.

## 2. Things I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **`עמלות זו על זו`** (grey, the tiny "2" under each bar): word for word, "fees on each other." My guess is fees charged on top of other fees, such as VAT or a minimum top-up. Nothing on the page explains it.
- **`הזוג`** / **`★ הזול`**: "the pair" means a fund plus an account. It took me a moment to see that the ranking is of pairs, not funds.
- **`סכום היום`**: I first read "today's amount" as a current value. It's the starting lump sum.
- **`תשואה בשנה 7%`**: I assumed it was after inflation. The small print says `התשואה בשנה היא לפני אינפלציה`, and the results are `בכסף של היום`.
- **`מול מדד נטו`** (column heading): I had to work out that it's the fund's average yearly gap from the net index. The `בשנה · שנתיים` underneath means the average covers only two years.
- **`לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר`** (left column heading): see question 3.
- **`מחושב`** badge and **`F-92`**: I take `מחושב` to mean the site calculated the figure itself rather than copying a published one. `F-92` looks like an internal formula code and means nothing to me.
- **`(4D)` / `(4A)`**: unclear until I found the footnote. 4A means "almost no foreign-currency exposure" (hedged), and 4D means it can be fully in foreign currency.
- **`עסקת החלף`** (swap, meaning it doesn't hold the shares), **`כמה דרכים יחד`** ("several ways together", which I guess is a mix of holding shares and swaps), and **`מחזיקה את המניות`** (holds the actual shares): these are labels I had to interpret.
- **`לא בחישוב`** pills next to other prices, for example `מחיר אחר לצעירים בגילאי 14 עד 20`: I read them as "this cheaper price exists but wasn't used in the calculation."
- **`// הפס נחתך: הסכום ארוך מהשטח`**: the slashes in the Excellence (אקסלנס) and Meitav (מיטב) bars mean the bar was cut short. At first they looked like a separate cost.
- **`אין נתון`** on Interactive Brokers, sitting right above a figure (`ונשארים 82,277 ₪`): I couldn't tell whether there is data or not.
- **`יש ספק אם ... נמכרת שם`** ("it's doubtful whether it's sold there"): this is a guess by the site, not a fact.
- The sentence that starts `תשאר 10,219 ₪ יותר מ־קסם ... בפסגות iShares ...` is scrambled by right-to-left ordering. I had to rebuild it as: "with iShares you keep 10,219 ₪ more than with Kesem."

## 3. The words at the far left of the selected row

The selected row shows **`בחירת האתר`** ("the site's choice") where the other rows have a shekel figure. It marks this fund as the baseline for the column headed **`לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר`**. The figures in the other rows are differences from it:
- MTF: `‎-858 ₪`
- IBI 1149772: `‎-3,154 ₪`
- Tachlit 1144237: `‎-4,566 ₪`
- The two hedged funds: ranges, `בין +1,295 ₪ ל־‎-24,327 ₪` and `בין +1,193 ₪ ל־‎-24,429 ₪`

The two buy-abroad rows (IE00B6R52259 and the US ACWI) show `אין מחיר כאן` instead of a figure.

The numbers check out. 85,728 − 858 = 84,870, which matches the top card. 85,728 − 3,154 = 82,574, which matches the step chart.

The wording is a problem, though. "בחירת האתר" reads like a recommendation, while the page says `האתר לא ממליץ על מדד`.

## 4. The `כמה זה עולה לכם` chart

The figures add up:
- **Best pair:** 5,449 + 1,148 + 352 + 689 + 2 = **7,640** ✓
- **Next pair:** 5,164 + 2,306 + 348 + 678 + 2 = **8,498** ✓
- The best pair's account parts (352 + 689 + 2 = 1,043) match `1,043 ₪` in the account list ✓
- In the step-chart table, the account, fund and tax rows end at 1,043, 1,148 and 5,448. The 5,448 is 1 ₪ off the bar's 5,449, which is just rounding.
- Remaining plus costs comes to 93,368 for every pair I checked, so the totals are internally consistent.

What doesn't check out:
- **The MTF fund cost of 2,306 is exactly twice iShares's 1,148.** MTF's fee isn't on the page, and its tracking gap (‑0.31%) is about 1.5 times iShares's 0.2% fee, not twice. I can't verify 2,306.
- **Tax (`מס רווחי הון`, the magenta part) is the largest "cost" in the winning pair.** That's because the pair makes more profit. It makes the cheaper pair look more expensive in that segment.

## 5. The opened panel

- The top figure is **`‎-0.06%`, `בשנה · 10 שנים`**, labelled **`מחושב`** and **`מול מדד נטו`**: the fund's average yearly gap from the net index over 10 years.
- **How it was worked out:**
  - The net index counts dividends after the withholding tax taken in each company's own country (`אחרי המס על דיבידנדים שמנוכה מהם במדינה של החברה`).
  - Returns are in dollars, taken from the fund manager's documents (`ממסמכים של מנהל הקרן`).
  - The fund's return is after all its costs, and the average includes years when the fund beat the index.
  - Formula: `חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד, לפי הדוחות שלה`, code `F-92`.
  - Inputs shown: `תשואת הקרן ב־2016: 7.82%`, then `תשואת MSCI All Country World Index`, which is cut off at `יש עוד למטה` ("more below"). I only saw one input value.
- **The important line:** `בחישוב העלות בשקלים, פיגור מעבר לדמי הניהול נוסף לעלות, והקדמה על המדד לא נספרת כרווח`. Lagging beyond the fee is added to cost, but beating the index is not counted as gain. So the shekel cost uses the 0.2% fee, not the ‑0.06%. The percentage column and the shekel column therefore measure different things.

## 6. The part-by-part line and the group counts

**The part-by-part line agrees with the bars:**
- `החשבון 15 ₪ יותר`: (352 + 689 + 2) − (348 + 678 + 2) = 15 ✓
- `הקרן 1,158 ₪ פחות`: 2,306 − 1,148 = 1,158 ✓
- `מס רווחי הון 285 ₪ יותר`: 5,449 − 5,164 = 285 ✓
- Net: −15 + 1,158 − 285 = 858, which equals the `‑858` gap ✓

**The group headings:**
- `בלי נטרול · 5 קרנות, אחת מהן בשתי שורות`: 6 rows, one of which (IE00B6R52259) is the same fund as 1159235. That makes 5 ✓
- `עם נטרול הדולר · שתי קרנות`: 2 rows ✓
- The filter counts (`קרן סל 5`, `עסקת החלף 3`, `צוברת 6`, `כמה דרכים יחד 2`, `נקנית בחו"ל 2`, etc.) work only if the tags on a group heading count for every row in that group. They also count by fund in some cases and by row in others. With that allowance they agree.

**The account count does not agree.** The text says `3 מתוך 15 הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות`, but the list has 17 entries: 14 numbered, plus One Zero (וואן זירו), Interactive Brokers and Interactive Israel (אינטראקטיב ישראל). The page also never says which 3 have the cheaper standing-order price.

## 7. What looked wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

- **The page doesn't show my horizon.** Everything is 10 years, and I can't see 20-year figures.
- **Every headline number assumes the dollar stays flat for 10 years** (`אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל`). Yet the same page shows the dollar moving `‑12.5% עד +13.4%` in a single year, and says each percentage point a year is worth `3,300 עד 4,400 ₪`. That one assumption is worth far more than the 858 ₪ between first and second place.
- **The fund comparison mixes track-record lengths:** 10 years for iShares, 2 for MTF, 4 for IBI and Tachlit, 5 for the US ACWI. The ‑858 ₪ for MTF rests on two years of data.
- **Interactive Brokers says `אין נתון` and then gives `ונשארים 82,277 ₪`.** Interactive Israel says `לא אפשרי שם` and then gives `83,622 ₪`. In both cases the figure is for a different listing of the fund (the one bought abroad).
- **Buying abroad gives wildly different results.** At Interactive Israel it's `83,622 ₪`. In the Jerusalem (ירושלים) example it's `57,371 ₪` (`פחות 19,208`). No reason is given.
- **The hedging cost range doesn't match the table.** The text says `2.5 עד 3.6 נקודות בשנה`, but in 2025 the gap for one fund is 21.5 − 19.1 = 2.4.
- **2022 doesn't add up.** Index ‑18.4% in dollars with the dollar +13.4% gives about ‑7.5% in shekels. The unhedged funds show ‑8.7% to ‑8.9%, a gap of about 1.3 points in one year, which is far more than their stated ‑0.06% to ‑0.86% a year. The other years reconcile to within a point.
- **Pesagot's currency conversion:** `1.5 אגורות` at the page's own rate of `3.20 ₪` is 0.47%, not `0.48%`. It's minor, but it's their own number.
- **Investment-house account fees look implausible.** `23,564` to `26,555 ₪` over 10 years for Altshuler (אלטשולר), Excellence and Meitav is around 200 ₪ a month on a small account. The page doesn't say where these come from.
- **The account ranking flips by fund without explanation.** Beinleumi (הבינלאומי) is 10th for iShares but 3rd for the IBI fund.
- **The text about the shekel column contradicts itself.** It says the column is the difference in what's left `ולא הפער מהמדד` ("not the gap from the index"), and the next sentence adds index lag to the cost.
- **"בחירת האתר" and the star `הזוג הזול ביותר` contradict the disclaimer** `האתר לא ממליץ על מדד`.
- **Prices are snapshots,** `נאסף ב־04.11.2026`. A 1,043 ₪ advantage depends on that price list still being in force. Several cheaper prices are marked `לא בחישוב` and aren't explained.
