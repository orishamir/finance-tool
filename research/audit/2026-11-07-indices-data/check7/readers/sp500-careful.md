I went through the page as that saver, with 20 years of monthly deposits through an Israeli account in mind.

## 1. Which fund I'd pick, and why

**I'd pick ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), fund number 5139282, held in a פסגות account.** This is provisional: the page is set to 10 years, not my 20, and I'd need to re-run it at 20 first.

These lines brought me there:
- **The top card:** "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם – פסגות – ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282 – ₪86,492". The runner-up card says "אלטשולר שחם… ₪86,449 / ₪43− לעומת הזוג שמימין".
- **The fund's fees:** "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים, 0% משתנים, 0.02% נאמן".
- **The accounts list:** פסגות is ranked 1 with ₪86,492, and "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: ₪1,057". The next account, וואן זירו, is ₪83,141.
- **The step chart:** "הכי הרבה במחירונים ₪86,492" and "אחרי המדרגות האלה אין בחירה אחרת שמשאירה יותר".

What holds me back:
- **Short track record.** This fund's gap from the index is "ב־2025" only, one year. The iShares fund 1159250 shows "+0.24% בשנה · 10 שנים".
- **A 0% fee can rise** over 20 years. The page says nothing about that.
- **The ranking depends on the inputs.** The footnote says "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות", so the 10-year result may not hold at 20.

## 2. Lines I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess at

- **"זר"** (small badge next to "מדד S&P 500"): I guessed "foreign index". It isn't explained.
- **"העמלות זו על זו"** (grey item in the chart legend): I guessed it means fees on fees, i.e. growth lost on money paid out as fees.
- **"עלות בגוף העליון, לעומת הזוג שמתחתנות"**: "שמתחתנות" means "who are getting married". It must be a typo for "שמתחתיו" (the pair below it).
- **"מול מדד נטו"** column, and "+0.37%": I had to guess whether plus means the fund beat the index. The panel later confirmed it does.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"**: did the site choose this fund, or did I? The radio button looks like my choice. The step chart then says "עוד לא בחרתם קרן וחשבון".
- **"אחת מ־3 הקרנות השקולות"**: "equivalent" by what threshold? See section 7.
- **"כמה דרכים יחד"** (filter tag): I guessed it means a mix of ways to replicate the index (shares, futures, swaps). It isn't explained.
- **"נשען על כלל פתוח" / "מס בצבירה: לא ידוע"** on the Invesco row: the row doesn't explain these. Only the step-chart legend says "נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג".
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"**: I guessed tax on the shekel gain that comes from the dollar's move.
- **"F-92"** (formula code) and **"SPTR500N"**: opaque codes, never explained.
- **"★ הוזל"** under פסגות: price cut when, and is it already in the calculation?
- **"לא בחישוב / מחיר אחר ל…"**: I took it as other price tracks that aren't counted.
- **"אין שנה מלאה"** and **"לא מושווה"** in the gap column: I guessed "too new" and "not compared because it pays dividends".
- **"לא נכרכת שם"**: a typo for "נמכרת".
- **"החברה כותבת… ולא כותבת שלא קרן נסחרת בבורסה"** (אינטראקטיב ישראל): garbled, I couldn't parse it.
- **"שער הדולר ₪3.20 · מחושב משערי הייצוג של הבנק המרכזי האירופי"**: an ECB rate for dollar/shekel is odd. It's a cross rate the site works out itself.
- **Signs in the step-chart table:** costs have no sign in the first column. After that, "−6,659" on a cost is good while "+6,110" on the bar is also good. The opposite sign conventions made me stop and work it out.

## 3. The words at the far left of the selected row

The selected row shows "אחת מ־3 הקרנות השקולות" instead of a shekel figure. It is the reference row, so it has no difference against itself. The words mean it is one of three funds the site treats as equally cheap.

The other two rows carry the same label under their figures:
- אלטשולר שחם מחקה 5139100, at "₪43−"
- iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating) 1159250, at "₪213−"

The same iShares fund appears again in the foreign-bought rows (IE00B5BMR087), and that row does not carry the label.

## 4. The "כמה זה עולה לכם" bars

**The figures add up.**
- **Top bar (Yelin):** 5,703 + 116 + 356 + 699 + 2 = 6,876, matching the "₪6,876" beside it.
- **Bottom bar (Altshuler):** 5,689 + 174 + 355 + 698 + 3 = 6,919, matching "₪6,919".
- **Segment widths** are proportional to the figures.
- **Cross-checks:** 699 + 356 + 2 = 1,057, the same as "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: ₪1,057". In the step-chart table, 7,675 − 6,659 + 41 = 1,057, 3,280 − 3,164 = 116, and 4,375 + 549 + 779 = 5,703.

**Two oddities.**
- About 83% of each bar is "מס רווחי הון", which no choice of mine changes. It swamps the costs I can actually compare.
- The heading says "בכל אחד מהזוגות", and the page claims three equivalent pairs, but only two bars are shown. The iShares pair is missing.

## 5. The opened panel

**The figure:** "+0.37% ב־2025", labelled "מול מדד נטו", with a "מחושב" tag.

**How it was worked out:**
- "נוסחה: חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד לפי הדוחות שלה (F-92)".
- The inputs: "תשואת הקרן ב־2025: 2.98%" and "תשואת S&P 500 (SPTR500N) ב־2025: 2.61%". 2.98 − 2.61 = 0.37.
- The net index counts dividends after the tax withheld in the company's country. Returns are in shekels, taken from the reports, and the fund's return is after all its costs.
- Key line: "פיגור מעבר לדמי הניהול נוסף לעלות, והקדמה על המדד לא נספרת כרווח". So this fund's +0.37% earns it nothing in the ₪86,492, while a fund that lags is penalised.

It is one year of data. The panel also covers the page and is cut off at "יש עוד למטה".

## 6. Does the comparison line, and do the group counts, agree?

**The comparison line agrees with the bars.**
- "החשבון ₪1 יותר": 1,057 vs 1,056.
- "הקרן ₪58 פחות": 116 vs 174.
- "מס רווחי הון ₪14 יותר": 5,703 vs 5,689.
- Net: −1 + 58 − 14 = 43, which matches "₪43−" and 86,492 − 86,449.
- One surprise: both pairs are in פסגות, yet the account costs differ by ₪1. Presumably that's custody charged on a slightly bigger balance, but the page doesn't say.

**The group counts agree with the rows.**
- "בלי נטרול מט"ח · 19 קרנות, אחת מהן בשתי שורות": I counted 20 rows, with the iShares fund appearing twice (1159250 and IE00B5BMR087). That matches.
- "עם נטרול מט"ח · קרן אחת": one row, הראל 4A 5127527. That matches.
- The filter-tag counts also match when you count funds rather than rows: קרן מחקה 5, קרן סל 8, צוברת 16, עסקת החלף 6, כמה דרכים יחד 8, נקנית בחו"ל 6, חלוקת דיבידנד 4.

## 7. Things that looked wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

1. **Invesco is cheaper than one of the "3 equivalent" funds but left out.** Invesco 1183441 is at "₪128−", better than iShares at "₪213−". Yet it sits at the bottom of the list, out of sort order, and isn't called equivalent. The reason ("מוצג ולא מדורג") is only in a legend elsewhere on the page.
2. **"Equivalent" has no stated threshold.** ₪213 counts as "שקולות" but ₪447 doesn't.
3. **"אין נתון" next to a number.** אינטראקטיב ישראל is marked "אין נתון", ranked "—", and then says "ונשארים ₪84,172". That would make it second, above וואן זירו's ₪83,141. IBKR likewise shows ₪82,820.
4. **"You haven't chosen" vs. a selected fund.** The step chart says "עוד לא בחרתם קרן וחשבון" and starts from MTF at ירושלים, while Yelin and פסגות are shown as selected.
5. **The list compares unlike periods.** The gap column mixes one-year figures ("ב־2025") with 4- and 10-year averages. The two "cheapest" funds have only one year of history.
6. **Asymmetric treatment of the gap.** Yelin's lead isn't credited, while הראל 4A is charged its full "−2.44%". That's conservative, but the list shows "+0.37%" as if it mattered.
7. **The 4A range doesn't match the stated rule of thumb.** The page says one percentage point a year is worth "₪3,400 עד ₪4,400". From the flat-dollar case (₪9,460 less), "−23,766" implies about ₪4,770 per point and "+2,212" about ₪3,890. The first is outside the stated range, and nothing explains why.
8. **One account doesn't reconcile.** For most accounts, the shortfall against פסגות is about 9–11% smaller than the extra fees, which fits less profit meaning less tax. וואן זירו's is only about 4% smaller. I can't explain it, though fixed monthly fees might.
9. **The defaults don't fit my goal.** The page defaults to 10 years, every headline figure is for 10 years, and the ranking is admitted to change with the inputs.
10. **Sloppy text in a money tool.** "שמתחתנות", "נכרכת", the garbled אינטראקטיב sentence, and the unexplained "F-92", "SPTR500N" and "זר" all lower my trust.

The rest checked out: the sensitivity table (all four rows imply the base of ₪86,492), the step chart (78,038 + 6,110 + 2,344 = 86,492), the year-by-year table, and "ב־15 מתוך 25 שנים הדולר ירד".
