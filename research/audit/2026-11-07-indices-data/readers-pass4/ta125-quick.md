I'd pick **אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125**, fund number **5112628**. It's the closest to the index of the six funds, and the page also ranks it cheapest when bought through Psagot. Several labels I could only guess at, and a few figures on the page contradict each other.

## 1. Which fund I'd pick, and why

These lines brought me there:
- **The list of funds.** Its column **"מול מדד ברוטו"** shows **"-0.02% בשנה · 3 שנים"** for this fund. The other five show **-0.28% to -0.33%**. Since my goal is "follows the index most closely", this is the line that decides it.
- **The top box.** **"★ הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם"** says Psagot with this fund leaves **86,045 ₪**. The next best pair, **"MTF סל (4A) ת"א 125"** at Psagot, leaves 85,119 ₪, which is **"-925 ₪"** less.
- **The fees line:** **"דמי ניהול: 0.1% קבוע 0% משתנה 0.025% נאמן"**.

I'd still have to check that my own broker sells this fund. The page assumes Psagot.

## 2. Things I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **"(4A)"** and **"(40)"** in the fund names: no idea. Maybe a tax or dividend code.
- **"קרן מחקה"** vs **"קרן סל"**: I don't know the practical difference. Can I buy and sell a "מחקה" during the day like the others?
- **"מחזיקות את המניות"** vs **"כמה דרכים יחד"** (on Tachlit): I guessed that "כמה דרכים יחד" means the fund doesn't simply own the shares, and that this is riskier. Nothing explains it.
- **"מול מדד ברוטו"**: I took this to mean "how far behind the index". I didn't know what "ברוטו" adds until I found the small print, which says dividends are counted before tax.
- **"דמי ניהול משתנה"** and **"נאמן"**: I don't know what they are. I just added them up.
- **"דמי משמרת"**, **"עמלות זו על זו"**, **"דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך"**: I don't know what these are. I just treated them all as costs.
- **"מחושב"** (a badge) and **"נוסחה: חישוב של האתר F-92"**: is this number the fund's own, or the site's guess? And "F-92" means nothing to me.
- **"בכסף של היום"** and **"אינפלציה 2% בשנה"**: I guessed this is adjusted for prices going up.
- **"לא בחישוב"** next to the discount lines on each bank: I guessed this means the discount wasn't counted.
- **"הפס נחתך: הסכום ארוך מהשטח"**: I guessed the bar was too long to fit.
- **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג"**: I couldn't understand this.
- **"יש ספק אם נמכרת שם"**: I couldn't tell whether that means the fund or the account.
- The underlined dotted numbers everywhere: I assumed they're clickable, but nothing says so.
- The footnote **"בשנה המלאה שנמלה הקרן פרסם"** reads garbled to me.

## 3. The line just above the list

The line directly above the first fund is the column headings: **"קרן" | "מול מדד ברוטו" | "לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"**. Above that sits the filter row **"סננו לפי תוויות: קרן מחקה 1 · מחזיקות את המניות 5 · קרן סל 5 · כמה דרכים יחד 1"**.

It matters a lot:
- **"מול מדד ברוטו"** is exactly my criterion: how much the fund lags the index each year, after its costs.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** is a different thing: how many shekels less I'd end up with compared to the site's pick. For the first fund it just says **"בחירת האתר"**.

So I should choose by the middle column. The right-hand column also mixes in tax and fees. In this case both columns point to the same fund.

## 4. The opened panel

- **The figure at the top** is **"-0.02% בשנה"** with the label **"מול מדד ברוטו"**. It means the fund trailed the index by 0.02% a year.
- **The words right after it** say the figure is **"בשנה"** (per year) and carries the badge **"מחושב"**, meaning the site calculated it. The list said **"3 שנים"**, and the panel shows 2023, 2024 and 2025. So it's an average over those three years, not one year.
- **The year rows:**

| Year | Fund | Index | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 50.92% | 50.96% | -0.04 |
| 2024 | 28.58% | 28.61% | -0.03 |
| 2023 | 4.09% | 4.07% | +0.02 (fund ahead) |

The three gaps average to about -0.02.

## 5. What looked wrong, contradictory, or untrustworthy

- **One Zero (ווואן זירו) is both ranked and doubted.** It's ranked **#2 at 82,709 ₪**. At the bottom the page says **"יש ספק אם נמכרת שם: וואן זירו, Interactive Brokers"**. Why rank a bank if you doubt you can buy the fund there?
- **The site's neutrality.** It says **"האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"**, yet it labels a fund **"בחירת האתר"** and stars a **"הזוג הזול ביותר"**.
- **The closeness figure is the site's own calculation** (**"מחושב"**, "F-92"), not something published by the fund. It's averaged over only 3 years, and one of those years shows the fund *beating* the index. That feels shaky.
- **2025 return of ~51%** for the TA-125 looks suspiciously high. I'd want to check it elsewhere.
- **Small sums that don't match:**
  - Psagot's account cost is **"1,049 ₪"** in the bank list, but the bar pieces add to **693 + 354 = 1,047**.
  - The first bar totals **7,323 ₪**, but its pieces add to **7,321**.
  - The next-best pair's account bars add to **681 + 349 = 1,030** against 1,047, a gap of 17, while the text says **"16 ₪ יותר"**.
- **Confusing signs in the step-by-step table.** It shows **"−6,754"** next to **"+577"** and **"+17"**, and minus means "cheaper" in one row while plus means "more tax" in another. I couldn't tell good from bad at a glance.
- **"+17" account cost in the same account.** In the "switch fund" step the account cost goes up by **"+17"** even though the account didn't change. This isn't explained.
- **Dates.** Prices were **"נאסף ב־04.11.2026"**, but the footer says **"הנתון האחרון באתר נבדק ב־07.11.2026"**. Which one applies to the fees?
