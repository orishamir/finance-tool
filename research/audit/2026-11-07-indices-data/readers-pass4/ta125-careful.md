# ta125-careful

Who: You are 41, live in Israel, read Hebrew natively, and invest a little each month through your bank's online brokerage. You have decided to buy a fund that tracks the Tel Aviv 125 index (ת"א 125) and want to know which of these funds follows the index most closely. You are careful and read the small print.

I read all 11 screenshots.

## 1. Which fund I'd pick

**אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125, fund number 5112628.**

These lines brought me there:
- **The tracking column in the fund list.** Its heading is "מול מדד ברוטו". This fund shows "‎-0.02% בשנה · 3 שנים". All five others show between ‎-0.28% and ‎-0.33%. That makes it more than ten times closer to the index than the next fund.
- **The definition under the list:** "מול המדד: בכמה הקרן, אחרי כל העלויות שלה, הקדימה (+) או פיגרה (−) אחרי המדד שלה". So the column measures exactly what I care about, after the fund's costs.
- **The panel I opened**, which shows the yearly figures behind the number. The fund and the index are within 0.04 points of each other every year: 2025 50.92% vs 50.96%, 2024 28.58% vs 28.61%, 2023 4.09% vs 4.07%.
- **The fee line:** "דמי ניהול: 0.1% קבועים · 0% משתנים · 0.025% נאמן".

The site also marks this fund "בחירת האתר" and "הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם ★". That ranking is about cost, though, not tracking. Here the two happen to agree.

Two things I'd watch:
- **Two IBI funds are in the list.** "אי.בי.אי. סל (4A) ת"א 125" (number 1148808) is near the bottom at ‎-0.33%. I'd buy using the number 5112628, not the name.
- **The account the site paired with the fund is פסגות.** That isn't my bank. The tracking figure doesn't depend on the account, but the shekel results on the page do.

## 2. Lines I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess at

- **"(4A)" and, for תכלית, "(40)" in the fund names.** I guess it's a regulatory exposure code (share exposure / currency exposure). The page never explains it.
- **"מדד ברוטו".** The panel explains it: the index counted with dividends before dividend tax. On the list itself I had to guess.
- **The fund-type tags:**
  - "קרן מחקה" = index mutual fund, and "קרן סל" = ETF. I know this, but the page doesn't say what the difference means for me, such as once-a-day pricing versus trading on the exchange.
  - "מחזיקות את המניות": I assume it means the fund actually holds the shares.
  - "כמה דרכים יחד" (on תכלית): I don't know what it means. A mix of holding shares and derivatives?
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר".** At first I read it as "how much worse it tracks". It's actually shekels lost over 10 years against the site's pick (‎-925 ₪ and so on), which is cost, not tracking.
- **"העמלות זו על זו" in the cost legend.** I guess it means fees charged on top of fees, or lost compounding. It's unclear.
- **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג" (with a lock icon).** I don't understand which tax rule is "open", or which item is shown but not ranked.
- **"נוסחה: חישוב של האתר F-92".** F-92 means nothing to me.
- **"הפס נחתך: הסכום ארוך מהשטח" and the "//" marks on bars.** I eventually worked out these are bars cut off for space.
- **Plus and minus signs in the waterfall table.** "עלות החשבון ‎-6,754" (minus is good), "מס ‎+577" (plus is bad), and the step "‎+6,177" (plus is good). The signs mean opposite things in neighbouring rows.
- **"בשנה המלאה שנמדד הקרן פרסם" in the definition under the list.** The sentence is garbled, and I had to guess "the last full year the fund published".
- **Text hidden under the top bar.** The top of page 4 cuts off the line above "פיגור מעבר לדמי הניהול נכלל בעלות…", because it sits under the sticky menu.

## 3. The line just above the list

The line directly above the first fund is the column header row: "קרן | מול מדד ברוטו | לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר".

- **It matters a lot.** "מול מדד ברוטו" is the only tracking measure on the page. "לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר" is a shekel cost against the site's own pick. If I had sorted by the shekel column, I'd have ranked MTF second (‎-925 ₪). By tracking, תכלית is second (‎-0.28%) and MTF is joint third (‎-0.29%).
- **The two lines above the header matter less:**
  - "משותף לכל הקרנות ברשימה: צוברת" means all the funds reinvest dividends rather than paying them out. That's good to know, but it doesn't separate one fund from another.
  - "סננו לפי תוויות: קרן מחקה 1 · מחזיקות את המניות 5 · קרן סל 5 · כמה דרכים יחד 1" is just filters.

## 4. The opened panel

- **The figure at the top, "‎-0.02%",** means the fund lagged the gross TA-125 by 0.02% a year on average, after all its costs.
- **The words right after it** are "בשנה" (per year, so an annual average, not a total), then the badge "מחושב" (calculated by the site, not reported by the fund), then "מול מדד ברוטו".
- **The header doesn't say which period it covers.** I had to work it out:
  - The list row says "3 שנים".
  - The values in the panel are the calendar years 2023, 2024 and 2025, taken from the fund's annual reports ("מהדוחות השנתיים של הקרן").
  - So 2026 so far is not included. As of November 2026 the figure is almost a year stale, and three years is a short record.
  - The average does check out: (‎-0.04 − 0.03 + 0.02) / 3 ≈ ‎-0.02.
  - The values list may continue below the visible area. I can't tell.

## 5. What looked wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

- **The fund beats its own fees.** It charges 0.125% a year (0.1% plus 0.025% trustee), yet lags the gross index by only 0.02–0.04%. In 2023 it even beat it (4.09 vs 4.07). That's possible, for example through securities lending, but the page doesn't explain it.
- **The panel's index line is unlabelled.** It says only "תשואת ת"א 125", not "ברוטו", so I can't confirm those index figures really are the gross version.
- **Outperformance is ignored.** "הקדמה על המדד לא נספרת כרווח" means the 2023 lead is thrown away. I can't tell whether the ‎-0.02% includes it or zeroes it.
- **The shekel ranking doesn't follow the tracking ranking.** תכלית tracks better (‎-0.28%) than MTF and הראל (‎-0.29%), yet costs more (‎-1,030 vs ‎-925 and ‎-982 ₪). Either the shekel figure uses something other than the tracking gap, or the numbers are inconsistent. Nothing on the page explains it.
- **ואן זירו is contradictory.** It's ranked #2 for my chosen fund (82,709 ₪). Yet the bottom table says "יש ספק אם נמכרת שם: ואן זירו, Interactive Brokers", and ואן זירו is missing from that table. Oddly, the doubt attaches to an exchange-traded ETF (קסם), while the non-traded mutual fund is ranked there.
- **The page contradicts itself on fund types.** At the top it says "קרנות ישראליות שמחזיקות את המניות", but תכלית is tagged "כמה דרכים יחד", not "מחזיקות את המניות".
- **The ranking isn't stable.** "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־2 מקומות": the order of the accounts depends on the assumed 7% return.
- **"האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"** sits next to a starred "בחירת האתר". It's technically about the index, not the fund, but it reads as a recommendation.
- **A boilerplate footnote that doesn't apply.** "קרנות בחו"ל שהתעריפון שלהן נקבע לפי מספר המניות מחכות למחיר היחידה…" is irrelevant on a page that says there is no foreign fund for this index.
- **Small rounding gaps in the cost bars:**
  - 693 + 354 + 719 + 5,555 = 7,321, shown as 7,323.
  - 681 + 349 + 1,968 + 5,247 = 8,245, shown as 8,249.
  - Not alarming, but the totals don't add up exactly.
- **The site defaults to an account that isn't mine.** It picks פסגות, and my own bank is hidden behind the dropdown "לא באחד מהבנקים האלה". Every shekel figure assumes an account I don't have.
- **The cost comparison starts from an arbitrary point.** The waterfall begins from "לדוגמה" (קסם at ירושלים), which inflates the "‎+6,177" and "‎+988" gains.
