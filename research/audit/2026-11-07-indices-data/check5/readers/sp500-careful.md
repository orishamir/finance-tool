I went through all 13 screenshots in order. The page runs its numbers on its default plan: ₪10,000 now, ₪500 a month, **10 years**, 7% a year, 2% inflation. My plan is 20 years. Nothing on the page shows 20-year results, so everything below is from the 10-year run.

## 1. Which fund I'd pick

**"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282", held at "פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ"**, as a monthly standing order. That is a narrow win, close to a tie with Altshuler. The lines that got me there:
- The middle card, "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם / פסגות / ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282 / ₪86,492", next to "אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139100 ₪86,449 / ₪-43 לעומת הזוג שמימין".
- The fund card: "דמי ניהול: 0% קבוע 0% משתנה 0.02% נאמן".
- The account list: "פסגות ★ הזול 86,492 ₪ / 1,057 ₪", against the next one, "וואן זירו 83,141 / 4,544". The step in "מנקודת התחלה ועד הכי הרבה" shows that changing account (+6,110) matters more than changing fund (+2,344).
- I'd go without currency hedging, because of "בכמה הראל מחקה S&P 500 (4A) … השיגה פחות מהמדד שלה … 2.1 עד 3.3 נקודות בשנה" and "תשאיר 9,460 ₪ יותר מ־הראל … מנוטרלת מט"ח".

Reservations: Yelin has only one year of history ("+0.37% ב־2025"). It uses a swap ("עסקת החלף"). Its lead over Altshuler is ₪43 over ten years. Before deciding I'd set "שנים" to 20, because the footer warns "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות".

## 2. Lines I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess at

- **"(4D)" / "(4A)"**: never explained. I worked out from the page that 4A means hedged and 4D means not hedged.
- **"זר"**, the small badge next to "מדד S&P 500": I guessed it means "foreign".
- **"מול מדד נטו"** (column heading): I first read +0.37% as a return. It is actually the fund's gap against the net-dividend index. I didn't know whether plus was good or bad until I opened the panel.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** (column heading): this clashes with "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד". I took it to mean "compared with the top card's fund".
- **"אחת מ־3 השקולות"**: see question 3.
- **"בשנה · 10 שנים" / "בשנה · 4 שנים" / "ב־2025" / "אין שנה מלאה" / "לא מושווה"**: one column mixes a yearly average over 10 years, an average over 4 years, a single year, and no figure at all. They can't be compared row against row.
- **"כמה דרכים יחד"** (tag): no idea. Maybe a mix of physical holdings and swaps?
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"** (on every Israeli fund): I guessed that tax is charged on gains that come only from the shekel–dollar rate.
- **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח"** and **"מס בצבירה: לא ידוע"**: I only understood these through the legend in the step chart, much further down: "נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג".
- **"אין מחיר כאן / בחירה בה מעבירה את החשבון לאינטראקטיב ישראל"**: unclear whether choosing that fund switches my broker for me.
- **"עמלות זו על זו"** (legend): I guessed it means fees layered on top of fees, i.e. fund-of-funds fees.
- **"לא בחישוב"**: I took it as "a discount for this group exists but isn't in the figure".
- **"הפקדתם ₪70,000"** next to "בכסף של היום": deposits are shown in nominal shekels and the result in today's money. Nothing says whether the ₪500 a month rises with inflation.
- **"F-92"** and **"SPTR500N"** in the panel: an unexplained formula code and an index ticker.
- **The signs in the step table**: "עלות החשבון −6,659", "+41", "מס +549". Here minus means less cost (good), but the steps above it are "+6,110" and "+2,344", where plus is good. I had to work out the direction.
- **"הצעד הכי גדול שנשאר, עוד לא נלקח"** (legend): it marks nothing visible in the chart.

## 3. The words at the far left of the selected row

It says **"אחת מ־3 השקולות"**: "one of the 3 equal ones", meaning too close to rank. The rows themselves don't mark the other two. I found them only through the line under the cards: "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע… הזוג השלישי: פסגות עם iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating) 1159250". So the other two are:
- **Altshuler 5139100** (₪−43)
- **iShares 1159250** (₪−213)

Problems with this:
- Calling ₪213 a tie while ₪447 (Kesem, Harel) is not looks like an arbitrary cutoff.
- **Invesco 1183441, at ₪−128, is cheaper than iShares**, yet it is left out of the three. It also sits out of order, below ₪−4,492. The only reason is its lock tag.

## 4. The chart "כמה זה עולה לכם"

The labelled parts **don't add up to the totals**:
- Yelin: 5,703 + 356 + 699 = 6,758, against a total of **6,876**.
- Altshuler: 5,689 + 356 + 698 = 6,743, against **6,919**.

The gap is the small green slice with no figure on it, the fund's fees ("דמי ניהול הקרן"). It works out to about ₪118 and ₪176. With those filled in, everything holds together:
- 1,055 vs 1,054 gives "החשבון ₪1 יותר".
- 118 vs 176 gives "הקרן ₪58 פחות".
- 5,703 vs 5,689 gives "המס ₪14 יותר".
- 6,919 − 6,876 = 43, which matches ₪−43 and 86,492 vs 86,449.

Smaller faults:
- The account fees in the bar add up to 1,055. Elsewhere the account cost is "1,057". The step table implies the fund cost is 116 (3,280 − 3,164), not 118.
- About 83% of each bar is tax, which is the same for every choice. That squashes the parts that actually differ.
- "המרת מטבע" and "מס" are nearly the same pink. Grey and "המרת מטבע" don't appear in the bars at all, so I couldn't tell if they are zero or just too thin to see.

## 5. The opened panel

- The top figure is **"+0.37% ב־2025 · מחושב / מול מדד נטו"**: the fund against the net-dividend index.
- What it counts: "מדד נטו סופר את הדיבידנדים של החברות אחרי המס…; תשואת הקרן היא אחרי כל העלויות שלה".
- Formula: "חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד, לפי הדוחות שלה", tagged "F-92".
- The inputs: "תשואת הקרן ב־2025: 2.98%" minus "תשואת S&P 500 (SPTR500N) ב־2025: 2.61%" = 0.37.
- It adds that a lag beyond fees counts as a cost, but being ahead of the index does not count as profit.

What it leaves out:
- The panel **never says these are shekel returns**. A 2.61% return for the S&P 500 in 2025 looks wrong until you connect it with the dollar falling 12.5% that year, which appears elsewhere on the page.
- It doesn't explain how a fund with fees beat its index. My guess is withholding tax, but the panel doesn't say.

## 6. Things that looked wrong, contradictory, or untrustworthy

- **The fund count**: the header says "בלי נטרול מט"ח · 19 קרנות", but I count **20 rows** under it. The filter counts (5 + 8 + 2 + 6 = 21, including the one hedged fund) also give 20. My guess is that iShares 1159250 and IE00B5BMR087 are the same fund counted once, but the page doesn't say so.
- **IVV has no "מחזיקה את המניות" tag** while VOO does, so the tagging looks inconsistent.
- **Interactive Israel**: it shows "אין נתון" and says it serves institutional clients only, yet right underneath gives "ונשארים 84,172 ₪". Interactive Brokers likewise shows "אין נתון" and then "82,820".
- **The site picks a fund while saying it doesn't recommend**: "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד" sits next to "הקרן שהאתר בחר" and "בחרו את הזוג הזה". At the same time it calls the top three tied.
- **The "cheapest" fund has one year of history**, while it is compared with funds that have 4–10 years.
- **The ranking depends on assumptions**: the page itself admits the order changes in 6 places at 0% or 10% return. And it was calculated for 10 years, not my 20.
- **Missing and conflicting figures**: account cost is 1,057 in one place and 1,055 in another, fund cost 116 in one and 118 in another, and the green slices have no figures at all.
- **Invesco** is excluded from the ranking. The only explanation is a legend line far below the list.
