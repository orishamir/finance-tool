I'd pick **Yelin Lapidot S&P 500 tracker (4D), fund number 5139282, held in a Psagot account**. But the page backs that pick with less than it seems to, and it never showed me a 20-year result.

## 1. My pick and the lines that got me there

- The top card says **"אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם – פסגות – ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282 – 86,492 ₪"**. I took this as "one of the 3 cheapest pairs for your plan".
- In the account list, **"פסגות ★ הזול"** is ranked #1, and **"מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,057 ₪"** is far below the next one (Ve'Ahn Zero at 4,544).
- In the fund list this row is selected, and it has the best **"מול מדד נטו"** figure among the cheap funds: **"+0.37% ב־2025"**.
- The runner-up is only **"86,449 ₪ … −43 ₪ לעומת הזוג שמימין"** (Altshuler Shaham). The page itself says the gap is meaningless: **"ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר, ולכן הם שקולים"**.

Two caveats for a 20-year plan:
- The page is set to **"שנים 10"**. Every figure I saw is for 10 years, not my 20.
- Yelin's tracking figure covers only **one year** (**"ב־2025"**). Others have **"בשנה · 10 שנים"** or **"בשנה · 4 שנים"**. Altshuler's figure (**"+0.32% ב־2025"**) is also one year only. In practice it's a coin toss between Yelin and Altshuler.

- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** (column heading): "compared with the fund the site chose". That reads as a recommendation, while the page says **"האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"**.
- **"זר"** (badge next to "מדד S&P 500"): I guessed "foreign".

- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"** (tag): I guessed it means tax is charged on currency gains too. It appears on almost every Israeli fund, so it doesn't help me choose.
- **"כמה דרכים יחד"**, **"מחזיק את המניות"**, **"נשען על כלל פתוח"** (tags): I guessed "holds the shares in several ways", "holds the actual shares" and "depends on an unsettled tax rule". I'm not sure of any of them.
- **"מס בצבירה: לא ידוע"** (tag): I guessed "tax while you accumulate: unknown".
- **"אין שנה מלאה"** and **"לא מושווה"**: I guessed "no full year of history" and "not compared".
- **"העמלות זו על זו"** (cost-chart legend): I guessed "fees compounding on fees". No bar showed it.

## 3. The words at the far left of the selected row

I had to find that in a small grey line under the top cards. The other two are:

Calling a ₪213 gap a "tie" while Kesem KTF at −447 isn't one feels arbitrary. Also, the top card says "one of 3" but only two cards are shown.

## 5. The opened panel

- **The figure:** **"+0.37% ב־2025 – מחושב – מול מדד נטו"**. That is, the fund's 2025 gap against the net index, calculated by the site.
- **What "net index" means:** an index that subtracts the tax on dividends, after US withholding. It's in shekels, before fees. The fund's return is its published annual return in shekels, after its costs. So the fund's management fee counts against it, and the fund's lower dividend tax counts in its favour.
- **How it was worked out:** **"חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד, לפי הדוחות שלה"**, then **"תשואת הקרן ב־2025: 2.98%"** minus **"תשואת S&P 500 (SPTR500N) ב־2025: 2.61%"**. 2.98 − 2.61 = 0.37, which checks out.
- **Problem:** it doesn't say the 2.61% is in shekels. I know the S&P rose about 17% in dollars in 2025, so 2.61% looks wrong at first. You only understand it once you read the dollar table further down (+17.4% in dollars, dollar −12.5% against the shekel).
- It is one year of data.

## 6. What looks wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

- **The default is 10 years.** My 20-year horizon is nowhere on the page unless I move the slider, and the ranking might change. The page itself says **"בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות"**.
- **The ranking ignores the "מול מדד נטו" column.** Harel 5129275 shows **"+0.65% בשנה · 4 שנים"**, better than Yelin's +0.37%, yet it is ranked lower at −447. The footnote says the order is **"לפי עלויות"** (by costs). So why put the tracking figure next to it, and why does the hedge text use **"הפער יישאר כמו בדוחות"** (the gap stays as in the reports)?
- **The top pick rests on one year of history.** Funds with 10-year records sit below it.
- **"האתר לא ממליץ"** (the site doesn't recommend) sits next to **"הקרן שהאתר בחר"** (the fund the site chose), a ★ **"הזול"** badge, and a pre-selected radio button.
- **"3 השקולות"** (3 equivalent) is applied to a ₪213 gap without explanation.
- **Account cost** is 1,057 in one place and 1,055 in another.
- **The waterfall chart's y-axis starts at 50,000** with a break mark, which makes the differences look bigger than they are.
- **Mixed signs.** In the waterfall table, **"−6,659"** and **"+41"** are changes in cost, while the bars above show **"+6,110"** and **"+2,344"** as gains. The signs mean opposite things in the same chart.
- **Tax numbers I can't reconcile.** The MTF start point pays **4,375** tax against Yelin's **5,703**. That's ₪1,328 less tax on roughly ₪9,800 less gain, about 13.6%, not 25%. Maybe it's real vs nominal gains, but it isn't explained.
- **The hedged fund** shows **"−2.44% בשנה"** "מול מדד נטו". That mixes the hedge effect into the tracking figure, so it can't be compared with the other rows.
- **Interactive Israel** is marked **"אין נתון"** (no data) and isn't ranked, yet it shows a result: **"84,172 ₪"**.
- **Unexplained internal codes** (F-92, SPTR500N, 4D/4A) and roughly 15 kinds of tag. For someone skimming, the list is noise until you've read the fine print at the bottom.
