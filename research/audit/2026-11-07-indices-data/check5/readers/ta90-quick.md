## 3. The words at the far left of the selected row

The selected row says **"אחת מ־2 השקעות"** ("one of 2 investments"). I read it as "this is one of the two cheapest". The words don't name the other one. I worked out that it's **"אי.בי.אי. סל ת"א־90 (4A) 1209444"**, the next row, because:
- the top cards name that same pair;
- that row shows only "‎-423 ₪".

The wording "השקעות" is odd. I expected something like "הזולות".

## 4. The chart "כמה זה עולה לכם"

Adding up the parts of each bar:
- **פסגות + הראל:** 5,562 + 691 + 354 + 693 = **7,300**, but the total beside it says **7,302**.
- **פסגות + אי.בי.אי:** 5,421 + 1,261 + 352 + 688 = **7,722**, but the total says **7,725**.

Both are off by 2–3 ₪. That's small, but it shouldn't happen on a page about precise costs. Similarly, the account card says "1,049 ₪", while the account parts in the bar add up to 354 + 693 = 1,047.

The differences between the two bars do match the summary underneath:
- account: 7 ₪ more;
- fund: 1,261 − 691 = 570, shown as "571 ₪ פחות";
- tax: 141 ₪ more.

The gap between the two totals (423) also matches the −423 in the fund list.

Two more problems with this chart:
- **The biggest part of the "cost" is tax (מס, pink, about 5,500).** The cheaper pair "costs" more tax "כי נשאר לכם יותר רווח". Calling tax a cost makes the bars mostly meaningless for comparing.
- Both bars carry the same label, "אחד מהזוגות הזולים ביותר". So the labels don't tell them apart.

## 5. The opened panel

The figure at the top is "+0.02% בשנה · 3 שנים", labelled "מול מדד ברוטו".

What it says the figure is:
- The gross index counts the companies' dividends before tax.
- The returns are in shekels and come from the fund's annual reports.
- The fund's return is after all its costs.
- The average also includes years when the fund beat the index.
- In the cost calculation, lagging the index beyond the management fee is added as a cost, but beating the index isn't counted as a gain.

How it says it was worked out: "נוסחה: חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד, לפי הדוחות שלה" (F-92), using these figures:
- 2025: fund 46.53% vs index 46.56%
- 2024: fund 30.83% vs index 30.86%
- the list is cut off after that

## 6. What looked wrong, contradictory, or untrustworthy

- **The panel contradicts its own headline.** In both years I can see, the fund was 0.03% *behind* the index, yet the headline says *+0.02%*, meaning ahead. The third year is cut off, so I can't check it. As shown, it looks wrong.
- **The default is a 10-year plan** on a page titled "לאורך זמן". Every comparison is for a horizon I don't have.
- **Mixed messages about the top two:** "אחד משני הזוגות הזולים ביותר" vs "שני הזוגות שקולים", even though one is 423 ₪ ahead. So is it cheaper or not?
- **Tax is counted as a "cost"**, and the cheaper option shows more of it.
- **Small rounding mismatches:** 7,300 vs 7,302, 7,722 vs 7,725, 1,047 vs 1,049.
- **The fund rows aren't comparable:** the אי.בי.אי fund has only "ב־2025" data, while the others have 3 years.
- **Better tracking still ranks worse:** תכלית shows "‎-0.56%", better than קסם's "‎-0.62%", yet it ranks worse (‎-2,489 vs ‎-2,464). The footnote sort of explains this, but it looks contradictory at a glance.
- **The account ranked #2 may not sell the fund.** וואן זירו is #2, but further down: "יש ספק אם קסם ETF ת"א 90 נמכרת שם: וואן זירו, Interactive Brokers". That's about a different fund, and it makes me doubt the list.
- **Typos:** "חבר" for "בחר" in a column heading, and "שמתמחת".
- **The "starting point" in the step chart is arbitrary:** "נקודת התחלה לדוגמה: קסם ETF… ירושלים". The "+6,070" and "+2,464" are gains relative to a choice I never made.
- **The ת"א 90 returns look suspiciously high:** 46.56% in 2025 and 30.86% in 2024. I can't verify them, and nothing on the page backs them up besides "לפי הדוחות".
- **Account prices aren't independently checked:** they were collected on 04.11.2026 and "המחיר… כתוב בשורה של הבנק". I'd have to trust them.
