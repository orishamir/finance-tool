## 1. Which fund I'd pick

I'd pick **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282"**, bought through **"פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ"**.

These lines brought me there:
- The top card says "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם" and shows "86,492 ₪".
- It is the highlighted row with the radio button selected in the fund list, and פסגות ranks #1 in the bank list with "★ הזול".
- The waterfall section ends on it: "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים 86,492 ₪".

Two problems with that answer:
- **The page answers the wrong plan.** Its defaults are "שנים 10" and "סכום היום 10,000 ₪". My plan is 20 years of monthly deposits, so every number on the page is for someone else until I move the sliders. The screenshots never show a 20-year result.
- **The pick has almost no history.** It only shows "+0.37% ב־2025", one year. The iShares fund 1159250 shows "+0.24% בשנה · 10 שנים" and is called equivalent ("ההפרשים... קטנים מכדי לקבוע... ולכן הם שקולים"). For a 20-year hold, I'm not sure the site's pick is better than the one with a 10-year record.

## 2. Lines I misread, couldn't understand, or guessed at

- **"(4D)" / "(4A)"**: never explained. I guessed 4D means no currency hedge and 4A means hedged, but only from the section headings.
- **"מול מדד נטו"** (column heading): I first took it as the fund's return. The panel later shows it is the fund's gap from the index. Nothing on the row says whether plus is good.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"**: this means "relative to the fund the site chose". That reads like a recommendation, which clashes with "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד".
- **"אחת מ־3 השקולות"**: I took it to mean "one of the 3 equivalent funds". I only understood that after reading the small line about the three cheapest pairs.
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע"** (tag on every Israeli fund): I guessed it means tax is charged on the shekel gain, including gains that come only from the dollar rising. It isn't explained.
- **"קרן מחקה" vs "קרן סל"**: the difference is never explained.
- **"העמלות זו על זו"**: I guessed it means fees charged on fees. It isn't explained.
- **"דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים 0.02% נאמן"**: I guessed "נאמן" is a trustee fee. Not explained.
- **"לא בחישוב"** chips in the bank list: I took these as youth, student or soldier discounts that are left out of the calculation. They don't apply to me at 31.
- **"מס בצבירה: לא ידוע"** and **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח"**: I guessed the tax treatment is unclear. I couldn't tell what that means for me.
- **"F-92"** in the panel: an unexplained code.
- **"קניה חודשית מחושבת כהוראת קבע: 3 מתוך 15 הבנקים"**: unclear whether the other 12 banks were priced with a worse method.
- **The waterfall table** ("עלות החשבון", "עלות הקרן", "מס"): the first column holds absolute costs (7,675 / 3,280 / 4,375), and the next columns hold changes (−6,659, 0, +549). On top of that, "−6,659" sits under a bar marked "+6,110". It took me a while to see that minus means less cost, which means more money.

- the fund 1159250

- 7 more Israeli-listed funds

The gap is explained by two rows that refer to each other:

So 20 rows make 19 funds, which adds up. But you only find that out by reading small grey text in two rows far apart, and the heading doesn't mention it. They also don't look alike: one has a shekel figure (−213) and the other has "אין מחיר כאן".

The hedged section, "עם נטרול מט"ח · קרן אחת", shows one row, which matches.

## 6. The opened panel

- **The figure:** "+0.37% ב־2025", tagged "מחושב", with the label "מול מדד נטו".
- **What it says the figure is:** the fund's yearly return after all its costs, compared with a net-dividend index measured in shekels before tax. A shortfall is extra management cost, and an advantage over the index is not a mistake.
- **How it says it was worked out:** "נוסחה: חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד לפי הדוחות שלה" (the fund's gap from the index, from its own reports), "F-92". It lists "תשואת הקרן ב־2025 2.98%" and "תשואת S&P 500 (SPTR500N) ב־2025 2.61%". 2.98 − 2.61 = 0.37 ✓.
- **Is there more below?** Yes, it looks like it. At the bottom there's a faded, cut-off row of grey text and a "⌄" chevron, so the panel scrolls.

One snag: "2.61%" for the S&P 500 in 2025 only makes sense in shekels (the page elsewhere says the index rose 17.4% in dollars). The panel's label doesn't say that.

## 7. What looked wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

- **Defaults ignore a monthly saver's typical plan.** It assumes 10 years and a 10,000 ₪ lump sum. The sensitivity table even admits that years matter most ("הכי ישנה מספר השנים").
- **The recommended fund has a 1-year track record**, yet it's presented as the winner over a fund with a 10-year record.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** contradicts "האתר לא ממליץ".
- **Invesco's −128 ₪** beats the #3 "equivalent" fund but is buried at the bottom of the list with no explanation on its row.
- **Interactive Israel says "אין נתון"** (no data), then right below gives "ונשארים 84,172 ₪". Interactive Brokers does the same with "82,820 ₪".
- **The heading count needs hidden text to reconcile**: 19 in the heading, 20 rows visible.
- **The waterfall chart is hard to trust:**
  - Its y-axis is cut at 50,000 (there's a zigzag break), which exaggerates the bar differences.
  - It starts from an arbitrary "לדוגמה" fund (MTF סל at ירושלים) that I never chose.
  - It mixes absolute amounts and changes in the same rows.
- **Page 12 is a large empty dark box**, as if something failed to load.
- **The account-vs-fund split** in the "1 ₪ / 58 ₪" line doesn't match the legend's grouping.
- **"2025: +13.5%"** in the hedged-vs-unhedged column should be 15.3 − 1.9 = +13.4. It's a small error, but it's on a page selling precision.
- **The ranking is fragile, by the page's own admission:** "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות" (at a 0% or 10% return, the order changes in 6 places).
- **Many terms are never explained**: 4D/4A, נאמן, F-92, העמלות זו על זו, and the tax-rule tags.
