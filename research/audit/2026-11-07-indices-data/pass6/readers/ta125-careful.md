**One thing to know up front:** the page is set to `שנים 10`, `סכום היום 10,000 ₪` and `הפקדה חודשית 500 ₪`. ## 1. Which fund I'd pick

**`אי.בי.אי. מחקה (4A) ת"א 125` (5112628), bought through `פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ`.** The lines that brought me there:
- The card `★ הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם`: Psagot with this fund, `86,045 ₪`. The next pair, `MTF סל (4A) ת"א 125` at Psagot, is `85,231 ₪`, which the page shows as `814 ₪−`.
- The fund's cost line: `דמי ניהול: 0.1% קבוע, 0% משתנה, 0.025% נאמן`. That is about 0.125% a year in total, against roughly 0.3% for the ETFs.
- In the fund list it shows `0.02%−` under `מול מדד ברוטו`, while every ETF shows between −0.28% and −0.33%.
- The cost chart: the fund's part is `719` here against `1,818` for the next pair. The comparison line says `הקרן 1,099 ₪ פחות`.
- Psagot heads the account list (`★ הזול`) at `1,049 ₪` for 10 years. Most banks are at 4,500 ₪ or more.

Before I committed I would check three things:
- Psagot's own price list for custody fees on a mutual fund. The chart charges `693` of `דמי משמרת` on it, and in Israel mutual funds are often exempt from custody fees.
- That `0.1% קבוע` really is fixed. The manager can raise it.
- That the dropdown `הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו"ש: לא באחד מהבנקים האלה` fits me. Bank rows include current-account fees, and that is part of why Psagot comes out cheapest.

## 2. What I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **`כמה דרכים יחד`** (the tag on Tachlit): my guess is that the fund tracks the index by several methods, perhaps shares plus derivatives. It isn't explained.
- **`מחזיקות את המניות`, `קרן מחקה`, `קרן סל`:** I understood them, but a beginner wouldn't.
- **`העמלות זו על זו`:** I guessed "fees charged on fees" or the growth lost on money paid out as fees. It's only `2` ₪ at Psagot, but in the Excellence row the grey piece looks large. I can't tell what it is.
- **`מול מדד ברוטו`:** at first I thought this was the fund's return. It is the gap from the index, measured against an index that counts dividends before tax.
- **`לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר`:** the column heading doesn't say which account the comparison uses. I inferred Psagot from `בחשבון פסגות` above it.
- **`בחירת האתר`:** this sits beside the claim `האתר לא ממליץ על מדד`. The site doesn't recommend an index, but it does pick a fund.

- **`הפקדתם 70,000 ₪`:** see section 7. The figures only work if the monthly deposit rises with inflation.
- **`לא בחישוב`:** I worked out that this means "a different price exists that the calculation leaves out". As a label it is cryptic.

## 6. The opened panel
- **The figure at the top:** `0.02%−`, labelled `שנה · 3 שנים`, with the badge `מחושב` and the caption `מול מדד ברוטו`.
- **How it says it was worked out:**
  - The gross index counts dividends before dividend tax. Returns are in shekels and come from the fund's annual reports.
  - The fund's return is after all its costs. The 3-year average is how far the fund beat or lagged the index.
  - In the shekel cost, any lag beyond the management fee is added as a cost, and beating the index isn't counted.
  - Formula: `חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד לפי הדוחות שלה`, `F-92`.
- **Inputs shown under `ערכים שנכנסו לחישוב`:**

  - `תשואת ת"א 125 ב־2025 50.96%` (a gap of −0.04% for that year)
  - `תשואת הקרן ב־2024 28.58%`

- **Is there more below?** Yes. A `⌄` chevron at the bottom and the cut-off row show the panel scrolls. The 2024 index return and all of the third year are hidden, so I can't check the −0.02% from what's on screen.

## 7. What looked wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy
- **The deposits seem to rise with inflation, and the page doesn't say so.** All the cost breakdowns add back to the same gross of about 93,370 ₪ (86,045 + 7,323, and 78,951 + 14,417). I recomputed: with 7% nominal growth, 2% inflation and a flat 500 ₪ a month, the zero-cost, zero-tax result is only about 86,700 ₪ in today's money. You get about 93,200 ₪ only if the 500 ₪ rises with inflation. For someone saving a fixed nominal sum, every figure here is too high.
- **The headline numbers don't fit my plan.** They're for 10 years with a 10,000 ₪ lump sum. Fixed account fees hurt small balances more, and the site admits the order changes under other assumptions.
- **The percentage and shekel columns in the fund list contradict each other** (section 4), with no explanation.
- **Custody fee on a mutual fund** (`693`): this needs checking against Psagot's price list.
- **"15" versus 17 rows:** `3 מתוך 15 הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות` against 17 account rows. Two are unranked, which I worked out myself; the page doesn't say so.
- **Sell-side availability is unclear.** `יש ספק אם הראל סל ... נמכרת שם: וואן זירו, Interactive Brokers`, yet Vaan Zirou appears with a full figure for the mutual fund.
- **The track record is short.** Three years of tracking data back the site's pick, and the figure that decides it (−0.02%) can't be checked from the panel as shown.
- **The tariffs are only days old.** They were collected between 04.11 and 07.11.2026, and Meitav's price is a promotion ending `30.11.2026`. The ranking could change within weeks.
- **Screenshots 09 and 10 are identical,** so I may have missed something between the end of the step table and the footer.
