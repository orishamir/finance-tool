I'd go with **ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), 5139282**, held in a **פסגות** trading account. Most of the page holds together arithmetically, but several things on it are confusing or don't add up, and it never calculates my actual plan: 20 years of monthly deposits.

## 1. Which fund, and why

**Pick: ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282, in a פסגות account.** It's a tentative pick.

The lines that brought me there:
- The top card: "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם – פסגות – ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282 – 86,492 ₪".
- The second card shows אלטשולר at "86,449 ₪ … ‎−43 ₪ לעומת הזוג שמימין".
- The fund card: "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים, 0% משתנים, 0.02% נאמן".
- The account list: "פסגות 86,492 ★ הזול" and "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,057 ₪". The next account, ווואן זירו at 83,141, is about 3,350 ₪ worse.
- The bottom summary: "אחרי המדרגות האלה אין בחירה אחרת שמשאירה יותר".

Why I'm not fully sold:
- The page itself says "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר". So ילין, אלטשולר (‎−43) and iShares 1159250 (‎−213) are a tie.
- ילין's tracking figure is "+0.37% ב־2025", a single year. iShares 1159250 has "+0.24% בשנה · 10 שנים". For a 20-year commitment, ten years of history counts for more than ‎213 ₪.
- Every figure assumes "שנים 10", 500 ₪ a month, 10,000 ₪ today. I'd have to set the slider to 20 and check that the order still holds. The footnote says "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות", so the ranking is fragile.

## 2. Lines I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **"(4D)" / "(4A)"**: I guessed 4D means no currency hedge and 4A means hedged. That's only inferred from "4A … מנוטרלת מט"ח". The codes are never explained.
- **"זר"** (the badge next to "מדד S&P 500"): I guessed "foreign index".
- **"מול מדד נטו"** (column heading): at first I read it as "versus the index, net of fees". Only the panel explains that it means a net-dividend index ("מדד נטו סופר את הדיבידנדים … אחרי המס").
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** (column heading): the comparison is against "the site's pick", yet the page also says "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד". "Chose" and "doesn't recommend" sit uneasily together.
- **The "מול מדד נטו" figures**: some are "ב־2025" and some are "בשנה · 10 שנים" or "בשנה · 4 שנים". These are different measures in one column, and I nearly compared +0.37% with +0.24% directly.
- **"אחת מ־3 השקולות"**: I guessed "one of the 3 equivalent ones".
- **"העמלות זו על זו"** (grey legend item): I guessed "fees charged on fees". At first glance I read it as "זז על זה".
- **"// הפס נחתך: הסכום ארוך מהשטח"**: I guessed this means a bar is cut off because the amount doesn't fit. I couldn't find which bar it applies to on the visible page.
- **"נשען על כלל פתוח"** (lock tag on the Invesco 1183441 row): only the legend much further down explains it: "נשען על כלל פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג".
- **"כמה דרכים יחד", "עסקת החלף", "מחזיקה את המניות"**: I guessed these describe how each fund replicates the index (mixed methods / swap / holds the shares).
- **"F-92"** (in the panel): an unexplained code. I guessed it's a formula ID.
- **"מסלול ONE, 49 ₪ לחודש"** (ווואן זירו row): I couldn't tell whether this would make that account cheaper or more expensive for me.
- **"לא בחישוב"**: I took it as "a different price exists but wasn't used". It appears on nearly every account row.
- **The chart under "הכי משנה את מה שיישאר לכם"**: the x-axis "השינוי בכל מספר" running from "50% פחות" to "50% יותר" took a moment. The "שנים" line steps up in jumps, which is presumably because years are whole numbers.

## 7. What looked wrong, contradictory, or untrustworthy

- **The calculation can't match my plan.** It's built on 10 years with 10,000 ₪ up front. My goal is 20 years of monthly deposits, and the page warns that the order changes under other assumptions. The headline answer is not my answer.
- **"אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל" is baked into every shekel figure.** That neutralises the very tag "מס גם על שינוי המטבע" that sits on every Israeli fund, including my pick. Over 20 years that tag could matter, and the ranking ignores it.
- **The "מול מדד נטו" column mixes a single year (2025) with 4- and 10-year averages.** The top pick's track record is one year.
- **The list is not in order.** Invesco ‎−128 sits below ‎−4,492, and the only explanation is a lock tag plus a legend many screens away.
- **אינטראקטיב ישראל says "אין נתון" and then "ונשארים 84,172 ₪".** That would beat every numbered account except פסגות, yet it's unranked. Interactive Brokers does the same: "אין נתון", then 82,820 ₪.
- **ווואן זירו is ranked #2 with a figure**, but the later section says "יש ספק אם S&P 500 (4D) של MTF נמכרת שם: Interactive Brokers, וואן זירו".
- **The hedged fund's range "+2,212 ₪ ל־‎−23,766 ₪" is very lopsided** for a symmetric ±3% dollar move. The two numbers being so far apart only makes sense if hedging costs about 2–3 points a year, which the page states elsewhere ("2.1 עד 3.3 נקודות בשנה"). The row itself doesn't say so.
- **The 2025 difference is shown as "+13.5%"**, but 15.3 − 1.9 = 13.4. The 2025 row is also based on "11 קרנות" while the other years use 8.
- **The fund cost depends on the account and vice versa.** In the stepped chart, פסגות account costs go up 41 ₪ when you switch to ילין, and nothing explains why.
- **"אין מחיר כאן" rests on one quoted clause from the פסגות fee schedule.** The site says the currency-conversion fee can be read as anything "בין 0.48% ל־1.5%". The site admits its own reading is ambiguous, so I'd phone פסגות before trusting any of the foreign-fund comparisons.
- **The page has rendering problems.** Screenshot 12 is a large empty box with nothing in it, and the "הפס נחתך" legend refers to a cut bar I couldn't find. The page also shows three different dates: the fee schedules range from 04.11.2026 to 07.11.2026, the exchange rate is from 06.11.2026, and the footer says 07.11.2026.
