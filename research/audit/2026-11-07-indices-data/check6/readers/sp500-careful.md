# sp500-careful

Who: You are 45, live in Israel, read Hebrew natively, and want to save every month for the next 20 years in a fund that tracks the S&P 500, through an Israeli trading account. You compare numbers carefully before you decide.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-01.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-02.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-03.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-04.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-05.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-06.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-07.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-08.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-09.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-10.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440/page-11.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-indices-data/check6/shots/indices_sp500-1440-sheet.png

I read all 12 screenshots: pages 1–11 top to bottom, then sheet 12 with the panel open. Short version: I would pick **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139282" at "פסגות"**. But the page treats it as tied with the Altshuler fund, and it only shows a 10-year plan, not my 20 years.

## 1. Which fund, and why

**Pick:** "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" (5139282), held in a "פסגות ניירות ערך בע"מ" account.

These lines brought me there:
- **Top card:** "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם … ₪86,492 נשאר לכם בעוד 10 שנים".
- **Account list on the right:** "פסגות ★ הזול 86,492 ₪". The account costs "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,057 ₪", against 4,544 to 11,520 at the other banks. The cheaper account is the biggest single gain on the page.
- **Fund details:** "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים, 0% משתנים, 0.02% נאמן".
- **Step chart:** "חשבון אחר +6,110" then "סוג קרן אחר +2,344". This tells me the account choice matters about 2.5 times more than the fund choice.

Two things hold me back:
- **It is basically a tie.** "אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139100" is only "−43 ₪" over 10 years. That is noise. The page itself puts both in "אחת מ־3 השקולות".
- **Neither fund has a track record.** Both show a tracking figure for one year only ("+0.37% ב־2025" and "+0.32% ב־2025"). For a 20-year commitment, Altshuler is no better here, so I would go with the lower fee and watch the 2026 report.

I also can't trust the order for my plan. The inputs are "שנים 10", "סכום היום 10,000", "הפקדה חודשית 500". The footnote "בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־6 מקומות" says the order is sensitive to assumptions, and I see nothing for 20 years.

## 2. Lines I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **"עמלות זו על זו" (grey part of the bars).** I can't tell what it means; "fees on top of each other"? I first took it as a fund cost because it sits next to the green fund-fee part. Only by doing the sums did I find the legend files it under "החשבון:", not "הקרן:". The legend runs right to left with group labels ("החשבון:", "הקרן:", "המדינה:"), so the grouping is easy to misread.
- **Colours: "המרת מטבע" (pink) and "מס" (crimson).** They are almost the same colour. I had to guess that the big 5,703 part is tax.
- **"(4D)" and "(4A)".** These codes are never explained. I guessed 4D means unhedged and 4A means hedged, from the hedged-fund section.
- **"מול מדד נטו".** I guessed "tracking versus the index after dividend withholding tax". The panel confirmed it.
- **"אחת מ־3 השקולות".** "One of the 3 equivalent ones". Equivalent by what rule? See question 3.
- **"כמה דרכים יחד".** No idea. My guess is that the fund replicates the index in several ways at once.
- **The lock tag on Invesco: "נעשן/נעול על כלל פתוח".** I could not parse it. My guess is that it is locked or only available at one broker.
- **"מס בצבירה: לא ידוע".** I guessed that nobody knows whether gains inside the fund are taxed.
- **"מס גם על שינוי המטבע".** I guessed that Israeli tax applies to currency gains as well as real gains.
- **"F-92" in the panel.** A meaningless code to me.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר" (column heading).** Does the site choose a fund, even though it says "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד"? It seems to mean the selected row.
- **Row ends: "אין מחיר כאן / בחירה בה מעבירה את החשבון לאינטראקטיב ישראל".** Clicking a fund changes my account? That is surprising.
- **"התשואה בשנה היא לפני אינפלציה…".** I first read this as "7% nominal". On that basis my own estimate was about 79–80k after costs, in today's money, not 86,492. The site's own figures (86,492 plus 6,876 costs makes 93,368 gross) only come out if the 500 ₪ monthly deposit rises with the 2% inflation each year. I worked that out myself; the page doesn't say it next to the "הפקדה חודשית" input. Also, the "₪70,000 הפקדתם" in the header is then in today's money, not what I would actually pay in.
- **Mixed time spans in the "מול מדד נטו" column.** Some rows say "ב־2025" (one year), others "בשנה · 4 שנים" or "בשנה · 10 שנים". At first I compared them as if they were the same measure.

## 3. The words at the far left of the selected row

The selected row shows **"אחת מ־3 השקולות"** where the others show a shekel figure. It means this fund is one of three the site treats as equivalent in cost. The same label sits under the shekel figures of:
- **"אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D) 5139100": "−43 ₪"**
- **"iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF USD (Accumulating) 1159250": "−213 ₪"**

Problems:
- No threshold is stated. Why is a fund that is −213 "equivalent" while "קסם KTF" at −447 is not?
- The top cards say "3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר" but only two pairs are shown. The cost chart also has only two bars. The iShares pair is missing from both.

## 4. The "כמה זה עולה לכם" chart

The figures add up:
- **Psagot + Yelin:** 699 + 356 + 2 + 116 + 5,703 = **6,876** ✓
- **Psagot + Altshuler:** 698 + 356 + 3 + 173 + 5,689 = **6,919** ✓
- **Account part** (custody 699 + buy/sell 356 + grey 2) = 1,057, which matches "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,057 ₪". It only matches if the grey part counts as an account cost.
- **Fund fee 116** matches the step chart's "עלות הקרן 3,280 − 3,164 = 116" ✓
- **Tax 5,703** matches the step chart's 4,375 + 549 + 779 ✓
- **The 43 ₪ gap** between bars matches the "−43" on the Altshuler row and 86,492 − 86,449 ✓

The problem is the title: "בכל אחד מהזוגות" says every pair is shown, but there are only two bars for "3" pairs. The small parts (2, 3, 116, 173) are labelled outside the bar and hard to match to their colours.

## 5. The opened panel

- **Top figure:** "+0.37% ב־2025", tagged "מחושב", labelled "מול מדד נטו".
- **How it was worked out:** "מדד נטו סופר את הדיבידנדים של החברות אחרי המס על דיבידנדים שנוכה מהם במדינה של החברה. התשואות בשקלים, מהדוחות השנתיים של הקרן. תשואת הקרן היא אחרי כל העלויות שלה." In plain terms: the fund's 2025 shekel return from its annual report, after all its costs, minus the net-dividend index return.
- **Cost rule:** "בחישוב העלות בשקלים, פיגור מעבר לדמי הניהול נוסף לעלות, והקדמה על המדד לא נספרת כרווח". A fund that beats the index gets no credit for it in the cost figures.
- **Formula line:** "נוסחה: חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד, לפי הדוחות שלה", "F-92".
- **Inputs:** under "ערכים שנכנסו לחישוב" the only visible value is "תשואת הקרן ב־2025 … 2.98%". The rest is cut off, so I can't check the index figure it was compared against.

As a rough check, 17.4% in dollars with the dollar down 12.5% gives about +2.7% in shekels. So +0.37% is plausible.

## 6. The comparison line under the chart, and the group counts

**The comparison line:** "החשבון בלי הפרש · הקרן 57 ₪ פחות · המס 14 ₪ יותר כי נשאר לכם יותר רווח".
- Account: 1,057 vs 1,057, so no difference ✓ (trivially; it is the same account).
- Fund: 173 − 116 = 57 ✓
- Tax: 5,703 − 5,689 = 14 ✓
- Net: 57 − 14 = 43 ✓

It all agrees, once you know the grey part belongs to the account. If you assume it belongs to the fund, it looks like 58, not 57.

**The group heading:** "בלי נטרול מט"ח · 19 קרנות, אחת מהן בשתי שורות". I counted about 20 rows. iShares appears twice: 1159250 in Tel Aviv, and IE00B5BMR087 abroad, marked "אותה קרן כמו…". That gives 19 funds ✓, though one row at the top of screenshot 4 is cut off and there may be a gap between screenshots 4 and 5.

**The hedged group:** "עם נטרול מט"ח · קרן אחת" has 1 row (Harel 4A) ✓.

**The filter chips** count rows, not funds, and they include the hedged fund:
- "קרן מחקה 5" = Yelin, Altshuler, KSM KTF, Harel 5129275, Harel 4A
- "נקנית בחו"ל 6", "מחלקת דיבידנד 4", "קרן חוץ בת"א 2" and "צוברת 17" all match the rows.

These counts work on a different basis from the "19 קרנות" heading, and nothing says so.

## 7. Things that look wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

- **"3" cheapest pairs, but 2 shown.** Both the cards and the chart show two. The third (iShares at Psagot, −213) is missing.
- **"השקולות" is undefined.** A 213 ₪ gap is called "equivalent" and a 447 ₪ gap is not, with no rule given.
- **The "מול מדד נטו" column mixes time spans.** Yelin and Altshuler show one year ("ב־2025"); most others show "בשנה · 4 שנים". Ranking them side by side is apples to oranges. Harel 5129275, at +0.65% a year over 4 years, has a better record than the "chosen" fund, but the cost rule never credits a lead.
- **The defaults don't match my plan.** The plan is 10 years and the page says nothing about 20. It admits the order changes "ב־6 מקומות" under other returns.
- **Hidden assumption about deposits.** The numbers only reconcile if deposits rise with inflation, and that isn't stated next to the input.
- **One table figure is off.** In the hedged-vs-unhedged table, 2025 reads "+13.5% עד +12.3%", but 15.3 − 1.9 = 13.4.
- **"אין נתון" alongside a figure.** Interactive Israel and Interactive Brokers say "אין נתון", yet show "84,172 ₪" and "82,820 ₪" for another fund in the same box.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** reads like a recommendation, while the page says "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד".
- **The step chart starts from an arbitrary point.** It begins at "ירושלים / MTF" because "עוד לא בחרתם קרן וחשבון… מאמצע הרשימה". So the "+6,110" and "+2,344" gains depend on where it chose to start.
- **Psagot's foreign-trading fees can't be priced.** The site says so itself: "אפשר לקרוא ביותר מדרך אחת, בין 0.48% ל־1.5%, ולכן האתר לא מחשב לה מחיר". That doesn't affect an Israeli fund, but it shows Psagot's fee schedule is vague.
- **The first-choice fund's "+0.37%" is a one-year number** for a new fund. The panel that explains it is cut off and shows only one of its inputs.
