I read all 12 screenshots. Short version: I'd buy **iShares MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF USD (Acc), TASE number 1159235, through a Pesagot (פסגות) account**. The arithmetic on the page mostly checks out. The problems are in labelling, in comparing tracking figures over different periods, and in a few places where the page says one thing and shows another. Also, everything on the page is worked out for **10 years**, not my 20.

## 1. My pick and why

**iShares MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF USD (Acc) 1159235, held at Pesagot.** These lines brought me there:
- The top card: "★ הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם", "פסגות", "1159235 iShares MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF USD (Acc)", "85,728 ₪". The next pair is "פסגות 1201003 MSCI AC World (4D) MTF סל 84,870 ₪ / ‎−858 ₪ לעומת הזוג הזול ביותר".
- The fund card: "דמי ניהול: 0.2% קבועים · אין משתנים · אין שכר נאמן" and "קרן חוץ בת"א", managed by BlackRock (iShares V plc).
- The fund's tags: "צוברת", "אין מס בצבירה", "מחזיקה את המניות". Its tracking figure "מול מדד נטו" is "‎−0.06% בשנה · 10 שנים". The rivals' figures are ‎−0.31% (שנתיים), ‎−0.76% and ‎−0.86% (4 שנים).
- The account list: "פסגות 85,728 ₪", with "מה החשבון גובה ב־10 שנים: 1,043 ₪". The next account, יהב, charges 7,032 ₪.
- What ruled out the US-listed ACWI: "מס עיזבון אמריקאי", "צריך טופס W-8BEN" and "מס על כל חלוקה". Over 20 years, US estate-tax exposure alone disqualifies it.
- What ruled out the currency-hedged funds: they cost about 2.9% a year ("‎−2.93%", "‎−2.91%"), and the box "הנטרול עולה: 2.5 עד 3.6 נקודות בשנה" says the same.

Before deciding I would move the "שנים" slider to 20. Every figure on the page is for 10 years.

## 2. Things I misread, couldn't understand, or had to guess

- **"(4D)" and "(4A)"** in fund names are never explained. I guessed 4D means not currency-hedged and 4A means hedged, because the 4A funds carry "מנוטרלת דולר".
- **The small badge "זר"** next to the heading "מדד MSCI AC World": unclear. I guessed it means "foreign".
- **"מול מדד נטו"**: I took this to mean the fund's yearly return gap against the index with dividends counted after withholding tax. The panel confirmed that, but only after a click.
- **"מחושב"** tag and **"F-92"** in the panel: a formula ID with no explanation.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** (column heading): "compared with the fund the site chose". This sits oddly next to "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד".
- **"עמלות זו על זו"**: I guessed "fees charged on top of fees". It's the grey legend item, and I had to guess that it's what the tiny grey "3" and "2" under the bars mean.
- **"המרת מטבע"** vs **"מס"**: two nearly identical pink/crimson legend dots. I only worked out that the big bar segment is tax by checking that 5,449 − 5,164 = 285 matches "המס 285 ₪ יותר".
- **"לא בחישוב"** next to the bank rows: I read it as "this special price (young people, students, soldiers) was not used in the calculation".
- **"נשען על כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג"** with a lock icon: "relies on an unsettled tax rule: shown but not ranked". I couldn't find which row it applies to.
- **"עסקת החלף"** (swap), **"כמה דרכים יחד"** (several methods together), **"מחזיקה את המניות"** (holds the shares): the first two are guesses at how the fund tracks the index.
- **"קנייה חודשית מחושבת כהוראת קבע: 3 מתוך 15…"**: I read it as "monthly buys are priced as a standing order, and only 3 of 15 firms publish a cheaper price for that".
- **"◄ בתשואה של 0% או של 10% הסדר משתנה ב־5 מקומות. פירוט"**: I understood it (the ranking changes in 5 places at 0% or 10% return), but the detail stays collapsed.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 3"** and **"· 6"**: unexplained counters.

## 4. The "כמה זה עולה לכם" bars

The parts add up exactly:
- **Pesagot + iShares:** 5,449 + 1,147 + 352 + 689 + 3 (grey) = **7,640** ✓
- **Pesagot + MTF:** 5,164 + 2,306 + 348 + 678 + 2 (grey) = **8,498** ✓
- 8,498 − 7,640 = 858, which matches "‎−858 ₪" ✓

What's wrong with it:
- The biggest "cost" by far is **tax** (5,449), and it's labelled "כמה זה עולה לכם" alongside the fees. That blurs the comparison.
- The grey 3 and 2 sit outside the bar as tiny labels, so they're easy to miss.
- The account parts (352 + 689 = 1,041) don't exactly match the account card's "1,043 ₪", though that's just rounding.

## 5. The opened panel

- **What the figure is:** "‎−0.06% בשנה · 10 שנים", tagged "מחושב", labelled "מול מדד נטו".
- **What the index is:** the index counts dividends after the tax the company's country withholds. Returns are in dollars, taken from the fund manager's reports.
- **What the fund's return is:** the return after all its costs. The average over the years includes years when the fund beat the index.
- **How it feeds the shekel costs:** "פיגור מעבר לדמי הניהול נוסף לעלות, והקדמה על המדד לא נספרת כרווח". Any lag beyond the management fee is added as an extra cost, and beating the index is not counted as a gain.
- **"נוסחה: חישוב הפער של הקרן מהמדד לפי הדוחות שלה · F-92"**

What's odd: the fund charges 0.2% a year yet trails the index by only 0.06%. So it beat the net index after fees. The panel never says why. My guess is that the Irish fund pays less dividend withholding tax than the index assumes, but the panel doesn't say so.

- Fund: 1,150 − 2,308 = **1,158**, not 1,159.

**The group headings:**

## 7. What looked wrong, contradictory or untrustworthy

- **Tracking figures over different periods are ranked side by side.** The winner's ‎−0.06% is over 10 years; the runner-up MTF's ‎−0.31% is over only 2 years ("שנתיים"); the others are over 4. The runner-up's position rests on 2 years of data.
- **The hedged funds' ranking doesn't follow their tracking figures.** Kesem trails by ‎−2.93% and Tachlit by ‎−2.91%, yet Kesem comes out ahead in money: 70,027 vs 69,932, and +1,295 vs +1,193. Kesem's period is cut off on screen, so I can't tell why.
- **"15 הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות"** — but the list shows 14 numbered firms plus 3 unnumbered ones (וואן זירו, Interactive Brokers, אינטראקטיב ישראל), which is 17.
- **Interactive Brokers** is marked "אין נתון" (no data), yet the page still gives a precise "82,277 ₪" for it.
- **"בכסף של היום"** next to "הפקדתם 70,000 ₪": the result only works if the monthly deposits are assumed to rise with inflation. The page never says so. If I deposit a flat 500 ₪, the real result would be lower.
- **"לעומת הקרן שהאתר בחר"** contradicts the page's "we don't recommend" stance. The site does pick a fund.
- **"הסדר משתנה ב־5 מקומות"** at 0% or 10% return: the account ranking is not stable, and the detail stays collapsed.
- **Everything is fixed at 10 years** with "אם הדולר לא זז מול השקל". For a 20-year saver the whole page has to be recalculated.
- **The account assumption is buried.** It assumes I can open a Pesagot account without a bank current account ("לא באחד מהבנקים האלה"), and the explanation sits in fine print.
- **The dotted-underlined figures** hide their explanations behind clicks, and some codes are never explained (F-92, the small "זר" badge).
