# pension-pc

Who: You are 41, an employee, and your employer deposits to a comprehensive pension fund (קרן פנסיה מקיפה) for you every month. You want to know what its fees cost you. You read Hebrew natively and do not work in finance. You are reading on a computer.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-fee-dead-ends/readers/shots-1440/01-first.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-fee-dead-ends/readers/shots-1440/02-panel.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-fee-dead-ends/readers/shots-1440/03-panel-filled.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-fee-dead-ends/readers/shots-1440/04-result-1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-fee-dead-ends/readers/shots-1440/05-result-2.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-fee-dead-ends/readers/shots-1440/06-result-3.png

**1. What the page needed first, and was it clear where to give it?**
Yes, mostly. An orange box at the top said: "כדי לחשב צריך עוד כמה פרטים: גיל, סוג ההעסקה ודמי הביטוח בקרן הפנסיה. אפשר להזין אותם ב'הנחות נוספות'." The button "הנחות נוספות" is right under the sliders. It is small and grey, so it doesn't stand out, but the box names it so I found it. The four sliders (סכום היום 10,000, הפקדה חודשית 500, 10 שנים, 7%) are only the site's examples: "המספרים לדוגמה של האתר, עד שתשנו אותם". At first glance they look like real data.

**2. What the fees cost, by the page, and over what period**
**₪2,841 over 10 years, "בכסף של היום"**:
- Without fees I'd have ₪122,087; with fees ₪119,246.
- Management fee on savings (0.22%) is ₪1,617.
- Management fee on deposits (1%) is ₪1,208.
- "השפעה משולבת" adds ₪16.38.

But this uses the example balance and deposit, not mine. It runs only 10 years, while I'm 41 with about 26 years to retirement. It is also "לפני מס ביציאה".

**3. Required vs. not required: was it clear?**
Mostly yes. סוג ההעסקה, גיל and דמי הביטוח are marked "(חובה)", and each has a reason under it ("בלי גיל אין חישוב…"). Nothing is marked "not required" in words. I had to work it out from the missing "(חובה)" plus the line "שדה שלא הוזנתם הוא ערך התחלתי, פרמטר של האתר". There is an odd empty gap under the inflation field. After it, "בכמה המחירים עולים בשנה, בממוצע." floats alone, like the label of a field that isn't there.

**4. Fields I wouldn't know how to fill**
- **"דמי הביטוח בקרן הפנסיה, כאחוז מההפקדות"**: I don't know this number. I'd need my fund's annual report (דוח שנתי) or my personal area on the fund's website. The page doesn't say where to find it.
- **"סכום היום"**: I'd need my report too.
- **"הפקדה חודשית"**: unclear whether this means employer plus employee plus severance, or only part of it. My deposit is a percentage of my salary, not a fixed shekel amount.

**5. Words and sentences that confused me or that I read twice**
- "לפי החברה: המספר מבוסס על מסמך של החברה": which company? I never chose one. After the calculation it says "לפי מיטב גמל ופנסיה בע"מ", which may not be my fund.
- "123 מקור · 123 מחושב": I don't know what the "123" means.
- "לפני מס ביציאה: שאלה פתוחה": what open question?
- "PEN-OQ7", "PF-39": codes with no explanation.
- "לא שווה את הטרחה": what isn't worth the bother? The line right after it says "אין ממוצע להשוואה, ולכן אי אפשר לומר שכדאי להתמקח".
- "אין ממוצע להשוואה" followed by "הממוצע שהרשות פרסמה לשנת 2025… 0.15%": first it says there is no average, then it gives one. Contradictory.
- "למוצר הזה לא נבחר מסלול מייצג, ולכן אין סיווג שממנו מחשבים את ממוצע הרשות": jargon.
- "הספים: שווה להתמקח מ־1,000 ₪; שווה להימנע מ־2,500 ₪": my total is 2,841. So should I avoid this fund? Avoid what? Yet each fee row says "לא שווה את הטרחה".
- "העמלה הגדולה מתחלפת אם דמי ניהול מחיסכון, מחצי עד כפול תעבור מ־0.22% ל־0.16%: אז דמי ניהול מהפקדה.": a broken sentence, I read it three times.
- "לא מתהפכת בטווח שנבדק": what doesn't flip?
- "השפעה משולבת של העמלות זו על זו" and, on the chart, "השפעה משולבת: הפער עד הקו": unclear.
- "אם יורידו ל־ [%]": who would lower it, and what does the field do?
- "פרמטר של האתר": repeated many times without explanation.

**6. Is anything missing?**
- **The insurance fee I was required to enter (0.3%) doesn't appear anywhere in the result.** There is no row for it.
- There is no place to enter my actual fees. The page used the company's 0.22% and 1%, but my real rates may be different.
- There is no place to choose my own fund.
- The period should run to retirement, not 10 years.
- The deposit can't be entered as a percentage of salary.
- It doesn't say what I should actually do next.

**7. Things that looked like links or explanations but weren't explained**
I only have screenshots, so I can't tell what happens on a click. These look clickable or meant to explain something, but nothing on screen explains them:
- The dotted-underlined numbers (2,841, 122,087, 0.22% and others), even though the page says "לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא".
- The badges "PEN-OQ7", "PF-39", "מחושב" and "לא שווה את הטרחה".
- The chips "חוקים וכללים · 2" and "· 19".
