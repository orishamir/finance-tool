I read all nine screenshots. Some of them overlap, so the glossary rows at the bottom show up twice; I count that as overlap in the screenshots, not a problem with the page.

**(1) Would I lock the 20,000 ₪ in a one-year deposit?**
Probably not, or at least not all of it, unless I'm sure I won't touch it for a year. The page doesn't recommend either way. Three things decided it for me:
- **The money can be stuck.** At 3 of the 4 banks the site collected, a one-year deposit can't be broken at all. At the fourth, Discount, breaking costs a penalty that isn't known in advance.
- **Low return.** The example deposit pays 1.74% a year, not indexed (Hapoalim's terms, dated 26.10.2026). The calculator assumes 2% inflation. Tax on unindexed interest is 15%. My own sum: 20,000 × 1.74% = 348 ₪, minus about 52 ₪ tax, leaves about 296 ₪. That is less than 2% inflation, so in real terms I'd lose a little.
- **No deposit insurance.** In Israel no law returns your money if the bank collapses. A guarantee of up to 250,000 ₪ is only a private bill.

**(2) If I need the money after six months**
- **At 3 of the 4 banks:** I can't get it. The money comes back only at the end of the term. Some deposits also release it at an "exit point" (תחנת יציאה), but the page doesn't say which ones or when.
- **At Discount:** I can break it, but the bank has to approve. In the first year I can only break the whole deposit, not part of it, and I pay a penalty.

**(3) What breaking early would cost**
The page doesn't give a number. The penalty is set only on the day I break, using Discount's "borrower interest table", which changes daily. Under the bank's terms it can wipe out all the interest and even part of the money I put in.

**(4) The early-breaking part, in my words, bank by bank**
- **Sentence 1** ("ב־3 מתוך 4 הבנקים…"): at three of the four banks you can't break any of the one-year deposits collected. This is about 3 banks the page doesn't name. I can only work out that Hapoalim is one of them, because the example box says the example comes from the non-breakable deposits and uses Hapoalim's terms.
- **Sentence 2** ("…ובחלק מהפיקדונות גם בתחנת יציאה"): same 3 banks. Some of their deposits also pay out at an exit point.
- **Sentence 3:** at Discount you can break every one-year deposit, with a penalty.
- **Next paragraph (Discount only):** you need the bank's approval, you can break only the whole deposit in the first year, the penalty is set on the day by the borrower-rate table, and it can erase the interest and part of the principal.

**(5) The red box in the tax part**
No source says what happens with income tax or capital-gains tax if breaking the deposit eats into the money I deposited. The only known fact is that the bank deducts tax on the interest it actually pays. It's an open question.

**(6) What confused me or seemed contradictory**
- **"ב־3 מתוך 4 הבנקים"**: which 4? At the top the page lists 9 banks as examples, and only Discount is named here.
- **"אי אפשר לשבור אף פיקדון… ובחלק מהפיקדונות גם בתחנת יציאה"**: so can I get the money out early or not? Which deposits have an exit point, and when?
- **"בבנק דיסקונט אפשר לשבור את כל הפיקדונות"** against **"שבירה צריכה אישור של הבנק"**: if it needs approval, it isn't really "possible".
- **"בשנה הראשונה של פיקדון לשנה או יותר"**: a one-year deposit has only a first year, so this just means "only the whole deposit".
- **"טבלת ריבית לווים"**: never explained, and not in the glossary.
- **"123 מקור · 123 מחושב · מונח הקישו להסבר"**: I didn't understand this legend.

- **"פיקדון צמוד פריים ממוסה כפיקדון לא צמוד"**: the term "צמוד פריים" is never introduced. Earlier, prime is the anchor for a variable rate.
- **Tax rates**: "שווה לדעת" gives only 15%, but the tax section adds 25% of the real interest for indexed deposits. "ריבית ריאלית" isn't defined.
- **"המס כבר שולם בכל פדיון… הפיקדון האחרון נחשב כאילו נפדה ביום המשיכה"**: hard to follow.

- **The calculator**: it shows 10,000 ₪ plus 500 ₪ a month for 10 years. The example product is a 12-month deposit, so I'm not sure monthly additions even apply.
- **"הפיקדון האמצעי"**: middle by what, the interest rate?
- **"גיל… מסמן גילים שבהם החוק והכללים משתנים"**: what changes by age?
