I read all 12 screenshots. 11 and 12 are the same screen. Some text was missing for me: it was hidden behind the round "^" button, or cut off between screenshots.

**1. Would I lock the money for a year?**
No, not based on this page. I wouldn't lock all 20,000 ₪ unless I were sure I won't touch it for a year. Three things decided it:
- The early-breaking paragraph: in 3 of 4 banks you can't break a one-year deposit at all.
- At Discount you can break it, but the fine is unknown and "יכולה למחוק את הריבית ואף חלק מהסכום שהופקד" (it can wipe out the interest and even part of what you put in).
- The risks part says "בישראל אין חוק כזה" about deposit insurance (a state guarantee on bank deposits). There is only a bill, and it covers only small banks.

The page also never tells me what interest a one-year deposit pays today. That leaves me nothing to weigh against those risks.

**2. If I need the money after six months**
- In 3 of the 4 banks, I can't get it: "הכסף חוזר רק בסוף התקופה" (the money comes back only at the end of the period).
- Some deposits have a "תחנת יציאה" (exit point): a fixed date when you can take the money out with no fine. The page doesn't say when those dates are, so I don't know if one falls at six months.
- At Discount I can break it, but only with the bank's approval. In the first year I'd have to break all of it, not part.

**3. What breaking early would cost**
The page doesn't give a number. It says the fine is set only on the day you break, by the bank's "טבלת ריבית לווים", which changes every day. It could cost all the interest and part of the 20,000 itself.

**4. The breaking-early part in my own words**
- **Sentence 1:** Of the 4 banks the site checked, 3 don't let you break a one-year deposit at all. You get the money at the end, or at an exit point if your deposit has one. It doesn't say which 3 banks these are.
- **Sentence 2:** At Bank Discount every one-year deposit can be broken, with a fine.
- **The rest:** All about Discount. You need the bank's OK. In year one you break all of it or nothing. The fine is only known on the day you break. It can take your interest and some of your money.

The page doesn't name the fourth bank either. Earlier it lists 9 banks, so I don't know which 4 "4 הבנקים" means.

**5. The red box**
Nobody knows how tax works if breaking the deposit eats into the money you put in. No source says. The only known fact is that the bank takes income tax from the interest it actually pays.

- **"ב־3 מתוך 4 הבנקים":** which banks? It doesn't match the list of 9.
- **The calculator:** "נשאר לכם 68,074 ₪" is less than "ההפקדות שלכם 70,000 ₪". Did I lose money? The note about today's money explains it a little, but it was confusing. The example is also 10 years with monthly deposits, which isn't my case.
- **"TXI-03", "BANK-06", "BANK-23", "TXI-17", "TXI-04":** codes with no meaning to me.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 7"** and similar: unclear what these are.
- **"פרסומת"** (advertisement) tag on glossary entries: this contradicts the footer, which says the site is "לא מסחרי" (not commercial).
- **The prime rate entry:** "ריבית בנק ישראל בתוספת 1.5" versus "הצו... לא כותב מספר". Is there a number or not?
- **The tax exemption:** "למי שהכנסה שלו ושל בן או בת הזוג עד 69,840 ₪". The start of that section was cut off, so I don't know if it applies to me.
