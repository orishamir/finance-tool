Everything below comes only from the 17 screenshots. The page lists 8 deposits from בנק מזרחי טפחות, all for 12 months.

**(1) Which deposits let me take money out before the year ends?**
- **189 ליצ'י קבועה שנה**: the bank says I can take out all or part of the money "בתחנות יציאה כל שלושה חודשים" (every 3 months). Minimum ₪10,000.
- **256 רימונים‑חודשי פריים לשנה**: all or part, every month. Minimum ₪2,000.
- **287 רימונים‑יומי פריים לשנה**: all or part, on any day after the first week. Minimum ₪2,000.
- The other five say "תחנות יציאה: אין", so the money stays locked for the full year.

**(2) Labels and condition lines under the names**
- **165 and 261 (דובדבן)**: a small badge "ריבית במהלך התקופה" and the line "הריבית משולמת בכל חודש". I understand this to mean the interest is paid to me every month. Both need at least ₪30,000, so neither fits my ₪20,000.
- **189, 256, 287**: the line says when I can take money out (see answer 1).
- Every deposit also has a "נקרא גם" line. It just gives other names for the same deposit.

**(3) Interest rates I could not tell**
- **189**: the rate says "לא ידוע". The page explains: "הבנק מפרסם ארבעה שיעורי ריבית, אחד לכל תקופה של שלושה חודשים, ולא שיעור אחד לשנה". The four rates themselves are not shown.
- **165**: on my phone, the floating "back to top" arrow covered the rate number, and the next screenshot starts below it. So I never saw it.
- **231, 256, 261, 287**: these show a percentage, but they are "ריבית משתנה: פריים פחות X". The page never says what the prime rate is, so I can't tell what the rate will be over the year.

**(4) My choice**
**131 פומלית‑קבועה שנה**. Its interest is fixed at 1.55% a year before tax, which is the highest known rate I can get with ₪20,000. It is not linked to anything, and the minimum is ₪1,000. The downside is that I can't touch the money for a year. If I might need the money, I'd take 256 instead: 1%, with monthly withdrawals. I'd skip 287, which pays only 0.1%. I'd also skip 331, which is "צמוד למדד" (linked to the price index), because I don't understand what that means for me.

**(5) Words and sentences that confused me**
- **"שבירה: אין אפשרות שבירה"** appears even on 189, 256 and 287, which do let me take money out. To me that is a contradiction: the page never explains how "שבירה" differs from "תחנות יציאה".
- **"תחנות יציאה: יש"** (287): this just says "yes". The real meaning ("any day after the first week") is only in the line under the name.
- **"סוג לפי הוראה 447"**: I have no idea what this is.
- **"נומינלי שנתי", "לפני מס"**: these are jargon. How much tax is taken off?
- **"ריבית לא צמודה; השיעור בעמוד המדריך"**: the tax rate is on some other page, not here.
- **"פריים פחות 3.55 נקודות אחוז"**: the page never says what the prime rate is.
- **"הצמדה" / "צמוד למדד"**: on 331 the word was partly covered by the arrow button. I don't understand what this means for my money.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 1/2/3/4"**, **"הבנק כותב:"** (collapsed) and **"BANK-13"**: I didn't know what these were or whether I had to open them.
- **"הריבית המוצעת היא המינימום שהבנק מפרסם... לא הצעה אליכם"**: so is 1.55% what I would actually get, or not?
- **Dates**: the page says "נכון ל‑06.11.2026", but 131 and 231 say "26.10.2026". The footer says "07.11.2026". Which rates are up to date?

**(6) What's missing**
- The prime rate, so I could work out the variable rates.
- The tax rate on interest.
- What the price index is doing, so I could judge 331.
- The four rates of 189, and the rate of 165, which was covered on my screen.
- What happens to the money at the end of the year, since "חידוש אוטומטי: לא".
- Whether I can negotiate a better rate than the published minimum.
- A plain explanation of breaking a deposit versus exit points.
