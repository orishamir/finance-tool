# makam-guide

Who: You are 41, you read Hebrew fluently, you have a bank deposit and some money in a provident fund, and you have heard of makam but never bought one.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-check/reader-shots/guide_makam-1440-part0.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-check/reader-shots/guide_makam-1440-part1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-check/reader-shots/guide_makam-1440-part2.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-check/reader-shots/guide_makam-1440-part3.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-check/reader-shots/guide_makam-1440-part4.png

I read all five screenshots, top to bottom. Here is my reaction as a 41-year-old with 60,000 ₪ I won't need for two or three years.

## 1. What the page says it can and cannot help with

**What it helps with:** It explains what a makam is and compares it with things I already know.
- **What it is.** The opening line under the title calls it "הלוואה קצרה למדינה, עד שנה, שבנק ישראל מוכר לציבור". You buy below 100, get 100 at redemption, and the difference is your profit. There's no interest and it isn't linked to the index ("לא משלם ריבית ולא צמוד למדד").
- **Compared with a bank deposit** ("מול פיקדון בבנק", in "למה משתמשים בזה"). There's no lock-in and no early-exit penalty ("אין נעילה ואין קנס שבירה"), and no custody fee ("דמי ניהול פיקדון ניירות ערך"). But you pay buy and sell commissions.
- **Compared with a money market fund** ("מול קרן כספית"). The makam is better if you hold to redemption and the commission is small relative to the amount.
- **A calculator** ("מה קורה אם מוכרים לפני הפדיון?"). It shows what's left if you sell early, after commission and 15% tax.
- **Where to buy and who issues it** ("איפה קונים ומי מנפיק"). Through a bank or brokerage. The minimum order in continuous trading is 10,000 ₪ face value. The last trading day is 3 trading days before redemption.
- **Tax** ("מיסוי"). 15%, deducted at source. I don't need to file an annual return because of it, up to the limits shown (717,000 ₪ interest and 2,810,000 ₪ in sale proceeds a year).

**What it cannot help with, some of it said outright:**
- **My time frame.** Everything is built around one year or less. The chart's x-axis stops at 12 months. Nothing deals with 2–3 years except a vague line in the tax cards (see below). I'd have to roll over several times, and the page doesn't really walk me through that.
- **The real current return.** The 3.25% in the calculator is the site's own assumption: "התשואה היא הנחה של האתר, לא ריבית שפורסמה". The 10,000 ₪ amount and the 6-month holding period are also just an example: "הסכום והזמן: דוגמה של האתר".
- **Open questions it admits it hasn't answered**, each marked "שאלה פתוחה":
  - How a private person's order reaches the monthly auction, and at what minimum and commission: "עוד לא מתועד".
  - The trading unit size outside continuous trading.
  - How the sale price splits between discount and capital gain: "עוד לא נמצא".
  - The tax rate over age 60.
- **Anything about my own situation.** It doesn't mention provident funds, other kinds of bonds, or say what I personally should do. The footer links to "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה", which suggests there is a list of gaps. I didn't open it.

## 2. What's confusing or contradictory

- **The tax on the calculator doesn't add up.** It shows profit ("רווח עד עכשיו") of 161.10 ₪ and tax ("מס על רווח הון (15%)") of 23.24 ₪. But 15% of 161.10 is about 24.17. I couldn't work out what the 23.24 is 15% of. It's small money, but it's the one place I checked the math and it didn't match.
- **"עד 15%" vs "15%".** In the tax section (top of screenshot 4) it says "מי שמוכר לפני הפדיון משלם **עד** 15%". "Up to" 15%, so when is it less? Meanwhile the "שווה לדעת" list (TXI-12), the calculator and the tax cards all just say 15%.
- **Real vs nominal profit.** The third tax card says tax comes "מהרווח הריאלי או מהרווח הנומינלי, לפי סוג הנכס" and then "15% מהרווח הנומינלי". The text below says "הרווח כולו נחשב רווח הון ריאלי". I think it means that since there's no inflation adjustment, nominal and real come out the same. But as a reader I had to stop and work that out.
- **Does it roll over by itself?** The middle tax card ("כל עוד הכסף בפנים") says "וסדרה חדשה נקנית מהסכום נטו", meaning a new series is bought with the net amount. That sounds like automatic reinvestment, which matters a lot for my 2–3 years. Nothing else on the page says a makam renews by itself, so I don't know if this is a feature or a description of what I'd have to do myself.
- **Which commissions exist.** "למה משתמשים בזה" mentions a buy commission and a sell commission "למי שלא מחכה לפדיון", which suggests no fee if you wait for redemption. But "שווה לדעת" (BANK-18) says "עמלות קנייה, מכירה **ופדיון**". The calculator's footnote also mentions "עמלת הפדיון". So there *is* a fee at redemption too.
- **A comparison that isn't apples to apples.** Under the calculator it says a one-year deposit pays "1.55%–2.25% בשנה". That's real collected rates set against a 3.25% makam return the site assumed. The makam looks better, but partly because of an assumption.
- **The glossary entry for "אג"ח ממשלתית קצרה".** Its definition, "איגרת חוב של המדינה לתקופה של עד שנה, בלי ריבית: קונים בפחות מהסכום שחוזר בסוף", is basically the makam definition. Earlier the page says it's a *separate* security from the Finance Ministry. Which is which?
- **Small things:**
  - Codes like "TXI-01", "TXI-12", "BANK-18" in "שווה לדעת" mean nothing to me.
  - The "חוקים וכללים · 6" chips appear everywhere without explanation.
  - The red box says "לפי מדרגות נוכות", which I think is a typo for "נמוכות".
  - The "איפה מופיע" column in the glossary ("אפליקציה", "דוח שנתי", "טופס מס") was clear enough once I read the header.

**What did not bother me:**
- The legend at the top ("123 מקור", "123 מחושב", "לחצו על מספר כדי לראות מאיפה הוא") was clear and useful.
- The over-60 box doesn't apply to me, and that's fine.
- The Hebrew is plain and the sections follow a sensible order.

## 3. Trust

**Things that make me trust it more:**
- It openly labels its own assumptions ("הנחה של האתר") and gaps ("שאלה פתוחה", "עוד לא נמצא").
- The orange box admits a conflicting source ("מקור אחד סותר"): a line in a Tax Authority guide gives a different rate. It explains that it follows the Income Tax Ordinance because the guide itself says the law wins. That's honest.
- It names the broker behind the commission example: "לפי התעריפון של פסגות", picked as the one that leaves the most out of 14 collected. It also says "אצל האחרים העמלה שונה".
- The footer: "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" and "הנתון האחרון באתר נבדק ב־07.11.2026", which is today.

**Things that make me trust it less:**
- "אתר אישי" means I don't know who wrote it or what their qualifications are.
- The tax figure I couldn't reproduce.
- The flattering comparison of an assumed return against real deposit rates.
- The fact that basic practical questions are still open, like how I'd actually buy at auction and for what fee.

Overall: I'd trust it as an honest explainer, not as the basis for the decision itself.

## 4. What I'd do next

1. **Rethink whether a makam fits at all.** It's a one-year product, and my money sits for 2–3 years. I'd need to roll over 2–3 times, paying fees each time, without knowing future rates. I'd want to find out separately about government bonds with 2–3 years left, or a money market fund, which the page says can hold makam with up to 13 months left.
2. **Get the real current makam yield** from the Bank of Israel or the stock exchange, instead of the site's 3.25%.
3. **Ask my own bank for its fee schedule**: buy, sell, redemption, and any minimum fee. Also ask whether it rolls over automatically or I have to do it.
4. **Open "שחקו עם המספרים שלכם"** and put in 60,000, to see how much the fees matter at my amount. Then read "מה האתר עוד לא מכסה".
5. **Ask my bank whether tax is deducted at source on my account**, so I'm covered by the page's point that I don't need an annual return.

I'd use this site to understand what I'm asking about, then make the decision with real numbers from my bank or broker.
