## 2. What's confusing or contradictory

- **The site never says what it does calculate.** "לא מחושב" means not calculated, but calculated *what*? Tax? Net return? Fees? I can't tell from this page.
- **"Explain" or "calculate"?** The subtitle says the site can't "להסביר עד הסוף" (fully explain) these products. The banner says it "לא מחשב" (doesn't calculate) them. Those are different promises.
- **Manager's insurance seems to contradict the permanent list.** The bottom list says "ביטוח, חוץ מפוליסות חיסכון" (no insurance except savings policies) is permanently out. Yet "ביטוח מנהלים", which is an insurance product, appears in the top-left card as a temporary gap. In the same way, "בחירת מניות" (picking stocks) is permanently out, but individual corporate bonds are only temporarily out. Maybe there's a logic to it, but it isn't explained.
- **"תיקון 190" isn't a product.** It's on the last card (top of the third screenshot), while the subtitle calls everything here "מוצרים" (products).
- **Internal jargon on every card:**
  - "מצב הכיסוי" (coverage status)
  - "חסום: שאלה פתוחה אחת" (blocked: one open question)
  - "מה חסום" / "מה נסגר" (what's blocked / what's closed)
  - "עוד לא נרשמו סגירות" (no closures recorded yet)

  It reads like the author's to-do list. Every single card says exactly "one open question", which feels formulaic.
- **Wording that changes from card to card in "מה נסגר":**
  - Most entries say "נסגר ב־25.10.2026: [question]" (closed on [date]: [question]).
  - The savings-policy card says "השאלה צומצמה ב־25.10.2026" (the question was *narrowed*). What does narrowed mean? Is it still blocking?
  - The portfolio-management card (second screenshot, right) says "שאלה נסגרה ב־24.10.2026, לפי חוקים וכללים · 2" (a question was closed, according to laws and rules · 2) without saying *which* question was closed.
- **"חוקים וכללים · 6" pills.** I assume this is the number of laws or rules behind each product, but it isn't labelled.
- **Cut-off text.** The savings-policy source line ends in "...ינואר" and the structured-deposit source ends in "נוסח מש...", so I can't read the full citation.
- **What didn't bother me:** The Hebrew itself is clear. The short "מה ידוע" (what's known) definitions are actually good plain explanations. For example, the corporate bond card says "המדינה אינה ערבה לו" (the state doesn't guarantee it). The dark layout, the dates and the two-column grid were fine.

## 3. Trust: less or more?

**More:**
- It openly lists what it can't do, and says why.
- It cites primary sources down to the page number, for example "עמודים 40 ו־52 בדפוס" (pages 40 and 52 in print), and tags them "מקור ראשי" (primary source).
- Everything is dated and recent. The footer says "הנתון האחרון באתר נבדק ב־07.11.2026" (latest data checked 07.11.2026).
- The footer says "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (personal, non-commercial, no marketing links).
- It deliberately refuses to rank by past returns.
- No login, and data stays on my device.

**Less:**
- "אתר אישי" means a personal site, and I can't see who runs it or what their qualifications are.
- This page has no "this isn't investment advice" note.
- The bright red banner and the blocked-question language make it feel unfinished.

Overall, the honesty raises my trust in what it *says*. It lowers my expectation of how much it can *do* for me.

## 4. What I'd do next

1. Go to "מדריך" (guide) and "השקעה במדד" (index investing) in the top menu to find out what the site actually calculates. In particular, I'd check whether it covers shekel bank deposits, money-market funds and government bonds, since those fit a 2–3 year horizon.
2. Open "מסמכים" (documents) in the footer to look for who's behind the site and how it works.
3. I wouldn't use it for any product marked "לא מחושב". If I'm considering one of those, like a structured deposit, I'd read the cited primary source myself or ask my bank or a licensed investment adviser.
4. I'd treat the site as a way to understand rules and taxes, not as the thing that makes the decision. I'd only rely on it if the pages it does cover clearly match the products I'm considering.
