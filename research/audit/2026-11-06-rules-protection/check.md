## Must fix

1. **"A bank enters insolvency proceedings only through the Governor" is not what s.8יב says.** s.8יב(ב) stops a *creditor's or shareholder's* application (and one under Insolvency Law s.329(א)) from reaching the court before it is submitted to the Governor, and lets it go ahead on his written consent **or after 90 days**, whichever is earlier. The Insolvency Law s.6 also lets the corporation itself and the Attorney General apply, and s.8יב as read does not name either. Appears in the Summary (line 13), REG-33 conditions (line 383) and `bank-current-account-deposits.md` open question 8. Fix: "a creditor's or shareholder's application against a bank goes to the Governor first, and to court after his consent or 90 days (s.8יב(ב))"; REG-06's body is already right.
2. **Single shares, REIT row: "A shareholder's claim is deferred, last (read)."** REG-33 marks the same statement as derived ("Read from these sections, not stated in them"), so the table's "(read)" contradicts the record. The basis is also off: s.237(2) ranks *debts* owed to members because they are members (for example a declared dividend); the equity itself is not a debt and is paid only from what remains after all debts, which goes to the members (s.242(2), p.368, file p.72). Fix: cite s.242(2) for the residual and s.237(2) for debts to members, and mark the row derived.
3. **The IRA derivation skips s.57 and does not hold for an insurer's IRA.** `ira-self-directed.md` open question 11 and the table's provident-fund row say an IRA is a provident fund "by definition (IRA-01), so its assets are held by the managing company as trustee". But s.57(א) says most of the law does **not** apply to an IRA; for one approved under s.13(א) it keeps ss.2 to 6, 27 to 30 and 39 to 42 (s.57(א)(1), gazette 2005 p.21), which is the actual reason s.3(א) applies. For an IRA that is an insurance fund approved under s.13(ב), s.57(א)(2) keeps only s.3(ב)-(ה), not s.3(א) or s.27, so its assets are not held in trust under s.3(א) and follow the insurer's row (REG-22). Fix: cite s.57(א)(1) for the managing-company IRA, add the insurance-fund IRA to the savings-policy row or say it is not covered, and note that s.57 was read as enacted.

## Should fix

1. **"Priority debts are a closed list" (REG-33, bank open question 8).** s.234(א) lists them but does not say no other law may grant priority, and other laws were not searched; the law's transitional s.374 also keeps the old Companies Ordinance s.354 priorities for floating charges registered before commencement. Say "the s.234 list names no deposit; other laws were not searched", and give the unread amendments to ss.216 to 237 an open-question number instead of a note.
2. **Unsecured bond = general debt (REG-33, `corporate-bonds.md` open question 12, table).** The Insolvency Law read has no provision on contractual subordination, and a bond whose terms subordinate it (for example a subordinated note) would not rank as a plain general debt. Add "unless its terms subordinate it; not researched".
3. **How a bylaw makes a trust (REG-34, REG-35).** Trust Law s.2 recognises a trust created by law, by contract with a trustee or by an endowment deed. The clearing-house bylaws and TASE rules say the member "ישמש נאמן", but which s.2 route makes that a Trust Law trust with s.3(ב) effect is not stated. Mark it derived in REG-34's list ("by law or by a binding rule").
4. **REG-35 leaves out exception (ג).** Part B ch.ב s.1 also excludes "ניירות ערך אחרים כפי שיקבע מעת לעת על ידי הדירקטוריון"; board decisions were not read. Add it.
5. **UCITS: "(EU law, read)" in the table.** Art.22(8) obliges member states ("Member States shall ensure"); REG-36 itself says Irish and Luxembourg law were not read. The outcome for a holder is therefore derived. Say so in the table and the Summary.
6. **Summary bullet 5 lacks "derived".** It applies Trust Law s.3(ב) to provident funds, mutual funds and clearing members, and the UCITS rule, with no derived marker; REG-34 and REG-35 mark the same steps derived.
7. **Copies still cited on lines this branch rewrote.** INS-21's source line keeps Wikisource for s.56, but the 2005 gazette's s.56(א) already lists s.30, which is all INS-21 uses it for: drop the copy. REG-22 concludes s.54(ב)-(ג) is unchanged partly from the mirror's amendment tags, and still cites the mirror for ss.68 and 70א; under the founder's rule (official publications only, a copy only to find the official text), the current wording should be an open question rather than a source.
8. **Foreign gold ETC row is general but rests on one issuer's factsheet (RG-20).** Name the issuer in the row or say it is one issuer's terms (CLAUDE.md, "a company's term is never general").
9. **Library index metadata.** `research/library/index.jsonl` gives the Trust Law as ספר החוקים 942 (the gazette and REG-34 say 941) and the Insolvency Law as 2699 (the gazette says 2708).
