# Indices page on a PC: a user-perspective review (2026-11-05)

The founder asked how the indices page (`/indices/`, "השקעה במדד") feels to a real
PC user, what should be better, and how to improve it, from a UI and UX point of view.

## Method

- Live site after commit bf68937 (deployed 16:42 UTC, the advertising block already gone).
- The lead walked the page first at 1440x900 (landing state, then S&P 500).
- Six independent reviewers then walked it in a real browser, each with one persona or
  lens: a first-time visitor with a concrete goal, a shopper who clicks every control and
  measures timing and movement, a sceptical reader checking trust and words, a visual
  designer (1280, 1440, 1920 wide), an information architect (page map and order), and a
  chart specialist. None saw the lead's notes.
- Two verifiers merged duplicates and tried to refute each finding on the live page.
  A completeness critic then tried the states nobody had tried (extreme plans, an added
  index, own accounts, return visits, a 960 px window, first load) and ranked the changes.
- Result: 103 raw findings, merged to 72 (51 confirmed, 19 partly, 2 by design, none
  refuted), plus 10 found by the critic. The lead re-checked the strongest by hand: the tie
  rule (`data/parameters/tie-threshold.json`, not applied on this page), the name scrambling
  in `kit/stairs-chart.tsx` (`iso()` isolates every digit run, so "S&P 500 (4D)" turns into
  "(D4) 500 S&P"), and the fund names stored wrong in `data/funds/` (13 records, listed below).
- Out of scope, by the founder's rules: accessibility, phone layouts, correctness of the
  figures, any question about who the reader is, recommending an index.

## How it felt

The page is powerful, honest and fast. The sliders answer in 15 to 30 ms. A pick lives in
the address, so a reload or a shared link keeps it. Every fee opens its document, page and
date. Label chips explain themselves, and the hedging section uses real yearly figures. Once
a reader picks their own bank, the top card becomes exactly the comparison they need:
"הבחירה שלכם 83,396 ₪" against "★ הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית שלכם 86,492 ₪, ‎+3,096 ₪ יותר אם עוברים לזוג הזול".

Before that, the first screen does not say what the answer is. It calls the site's cheapest
pair "הבחירה שלכם" although nothing was chosen, and it names neither the account nor the
fund. It compares that pair with a runner-up 43 ₪ away (the same house with another fund).
So the first impression is that the choice hardly matters. On the default S&P 500 plan
(10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7% a year) the account alone moves what is left by
about 23,000 ₪: 86,492 ₪ at Psagot against 63,507 ₪ at Meitav Trade, for the same fund.
A reader learns this only from two long lists. Further down, the step chart calls a fourth
pair "עכשיו" (MTF at Jerusalem bank, 79,176 ₪), and that pair also changes when a slider moves.
The page is about 8,800 px tall, close to ten screens, with no way to jump between sections.
Its clearest "why" (the steps) sits about 6,500 px down.

## What works (keep)

- Instant response: sliders 15 to 30 ms, sensitivity chips 3 to 7 ms, an index switch about 160 ms.
- The plan survives an index switch and a reload; the pick is in the address and opens on a fresh device.
- A clicked row stays where it was when the list re-sorts.
- Picking one's own bank turns the top card into the right comparison, with a one-tap way back.
- Source sheets on fund fees (Maya report, page, date), label chips with a sentence and rules, provenance on the dollar rate.
- Unpriced accounts (Interactive Brokers, Interactive Israel) say why in plain words.
- The sensitivity chart: names at the line ends, left to right, a draggable line that rewrites the headline.
- The entered-account dialog on the account card ("מתחילים מהתעריפון … מה שלא שיניתם נשאר כמו בתעריפון").

## What to improve, in order

### A. Defects the existing rules already decide (no decision needed)

1. **Fund names are scrambled** (A12, C1, B3). Two causes.
   (a) `kit/stairs-chart.tsx` `iso()` wraps every digit run in its own isolate, so names with
   digits reverse: "ילין לפידות מחקה (D4) 500 S&P" ([03-stairs-label.png](2026-11-05-indices-pc-review/03-stairs-label.png)).
   Isolate whole Latin runs, or do not run names through `iso()` at all.
   (b) Thirteen records in `data/funds/` have a malformed first Hebrew name, shown everywhere
   the fund appears: unpaired or misplaced brackets, or a stray "י" after the bracket.
   They should be corrected from the fund's own listing name, not retyped from memory:

   | 1143734 | תכלית סל (4A)י NASDAQ 100 מנוטרלת מט"ח |
   | 1146331 | קסם 4A) ETF) ת"א 90 |
   | 1146356 | קסם 4A) ETF) ת"א 125 |
   | 1146570 | קסם 4A) ETF) ת"א 35 |
   | 1149772 | אי.בי.אי. סל (4D)י MSCI AC World |
   | 1150333 | MTF סל (S&P 500 (4D |
   | 1165810 | מור סל (4D)י S&P 500 |
   | 1165844 | מור סל (4A)י NASDAQ 100 מנוטרלת מט"ח |
   | 1201003 | MTF סל (MSCI AC World (4D |
   | 5122569 | MTF מחקה (MSCI World (4D |
   | 5129283 | הראל מחקה (4D)יNASDAQ 100 |
   | 5139696 | קסם KTF י(4D)י MSCI World מנוטרלת דולר |
   | 5140165 | MTF מחקה (MSCI World (4D מנוטרלת דולר |

   A data check that fails on unpaired brackets or a letter glued to a bracket would keep it fixed.

2. **The page calls a pair "yours" and "now" before anything is chosen, and never names it**
   (A1, C12, C2, B1, F1, E1; [01-top-card.png](2026-11-05-indices-pc-review/01-top-card.png),
   [02-stairs-now.png](2026-11-05-indices-pc-review/02-stairs-now.png)). Before a pick, the top
   tile should use the wording the page already has after a pick ("הזוג הזול ביותר לתוכנית
   שלכם"). Each tile should name its account and fund on one fixed line ("פסגות · ילין לפידות
   מחקה (4D) S&P 500"), and both cost-bar rows should say more than "פסגות". The steps should start from the
   reader's pick, or call their start an example ("נקודת התחלה לדוגמה"), never "עכשיו".
   Text and a fixed name line; no new graphic.

3. **The page ignores the site's own tie rule** (G1, A2, D6). The site counts two results within
   0.5% of each other as a tie (`data/parameters/tie-threshold.json`), and the comparison page
   already applies it. Here a 43 ₪ (0.05%) winner gets the star, and equal figures rank one
   above the other: Hapoalim and Yahav both show 81,471 ₪ at ranks 6 and 7. Tied rows should
   share the rank and the existing "שקולות" wording, and the top tile should say the runner-up ties.

4. **A pick silently changes the other half of the pair** (F4;
   [06-silent-account-switch.png](2026-11-05-indices-pc-review/06-silent-account-switch.png)).
   Choosing a fund that the current account cannot price moves the account to another house.
   Nothing near the clicked row says so, and the account card is 1,500 px above. Say it on the
   spot with a way back, or ask first. Picking in a list also resets the steps (D5).

5. **An own account with blank lines counts them as zero and takes the star** (G2;
   [05-own-account-star.png](2026-11-05-indices-pc-review/05-own-account-star.png)).
   "הוספת חשבון בתנאים שקיבלתם" with only a 0.08% commission becomes "★ הזול" and takes over
   the top card. This breaks the founder's rule that silence is not zero. The card's own dialog
   already does it right, starting from a house's tariff. The list's dialog should do the same.
   A house not in the list should be a grey row that is never starred.

6. **The reason for "אין מחיר כאן" is wrong for Psagot** (F2). The note says Psagot's tariff has
   no line for foreign securities. The Psagot page one click away lists those lines. The data
   record's real reason is that two lines could apply. Each row should state its true reason (see also issue #187).

7. **Things move or clip when a number changes**, against the "nothing moves" rule.
   - An error line pushes the result down (B4).
   - Everything below the tiles shifts 23 px between a foreign and an Israeli index (G8).
   - Large plans cut leading digits on the axis and in the account cells (G5).
   - Gliding figures show agorot, then snap to whole shekels (G9).
   - The steps section changes height by 4 px (D14).

8. **Words.**
   - "1 שנים" (B12).
   - Internal ids "us-ivv" and "us-voo" shown as identifiers (B16).
   - An English research note and a scrambled citation in a fund-fee source sheet (B13).
   - Bare rule ids and "המנוע" in reader text (C20).
   - With no monthly deposit, monthly and standing-order wording still shows (G3).
   - The inflation value (2%) is hidden behind a button (A14, C18).
   - The 7% is never said to be before inflation, fees and tax (F8).
   - No column head over each account's cost figure and bar; the 960 px layout already has "מה החשבון גובה" (A3, C16).
   - The footer's "נתונים נכון ל־05.11.2026" is the build date, not the date the prices were collected (F7).

### B. Layout changes (no new graphic, but they change the page; worth a look first)

9. **The account rows are three lines tall because of the group-price sentence** (C3, A9). On
   Tel Aviv indices the fund column ends about 1,800 px before the account column, leaving half
   the screen black ([04-ta125-hole.png](2026-11-05-indices-pc-review/04-ta125-hole.png)).
   A one-line chip that opens the sentence would close most of the hole and shorten the page.

10. **Order and length** (D3, D7, C6, C22, G7). The page measures 8,789 px:
    - lists 3,167 px
    - sensitivity 765 px
    - hedging 1,499 px
    - steps 1,635 px

    The proposal:
    - Put the steps right after the lists: they are the clearest "why".
    - Open the hedging section with its conclusion, keep its prose at reading width (it runs to
      1,511 px, up to 178 characters a line), and fold its detail.
    - Add a row of jump links under the result card.

    The reviewers estimate the page would come to 5,300 to 5,800 px.

### C. Need a mockup or a decision first

11. **The headline should carry the choice that moves the result most** (A2, D6, C4, C10, E3).
    Today the top card compares two near-twins, and its cost bars differ by 43 ₪ out of about
    6,900, mostly tax. The 23,000 ₪ account spread, the founder's own lesson from Doron's case,
    never reaches the first screen. Proposal: the range over every priced account for the same
    fund, as a difference, in the top card. This is a major visual, so it needs a mockup first.

12. **One colour, one meaning, across the page's charts** (E2, A16, C10). Gold is the custody fee
    in the cost bars, "years" in the sensitivity chart, the hedged fund in the hedging chart, and
    "another fund" in the steps. Teal and magenta are reused the same way. Proposal: one colour
    vocabulary for account, fund, tax and plan inputs, used by every chart. This needs a mockup.

13. **Comparing indices** (A6). "Does the whole world cost more?" takes three clicks and memory:
    S&P 500 costs 18,360 ₪, MSCI AC World 20,611 ₪ and MSCI World 25,089 ₪ over 15 years on one
    reviewer's plan. A cost difference beside each chip states costs, not a recommendation. The
    founder decides whether it fits "the site recommends no index".

14. **Remembering the last index and pick** (G4). The nav tab and a return visit land on bare
    chips, and a picked house is forgotten. The founder decides.

15. **First load** (G10). "טוען…" over an empty screen for 1.4 to 2.6 s while 1.4 MB of
    compressed bundles load, then the title jumps 9 px. Pre-render the default view. Overlaps #116.

Polish worth a later sweep (all in the appendix):
- Chart text at 10 to 11 px, below the 13 px minimum (C13).
- The sensitivity chips run opposite to the chart axis (C11).
- The "+" between the cards looks like a button (A15, C17).
- Field labels sit closer to the neighbouring field than to their own (C5).
- Single words left alone on a line (C7).
- The landing state is a row of chips over empty space (A7, C19).
- A label filter's count disagrees with the list (B21, C21).
