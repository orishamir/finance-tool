# R-taxregs: official texts for tax legislation other than the Income Tax Ordinance (2026-10-25)

Scope: income-tax regulations, orders and rules, the VAT Law and its orders, the 2017-2018 Economic Efficiency Law (chapter on the self-employed), and the Tax Authority / Ministry of Finance documents that the rule base cites through copies (Wikisource, Nevo, capitax.co.il). The Ordinance itself is in `maps/ito.json`. I edited no rule or data file; the map is `maps/taxregs.json` (45 entries) and the shared-table requests are `requests/R-taxregs.json` (25 rows).

## How the official texts were found

- **The Tax Authority does not publish these regulations.** Google `site:gov.il` searches on each full title found only circulars, rulings and guides that cite them. (Google began returning captchas after about 20 queries. I did not try to get past them, so later checks were done by direct URL.)
- **National Insurance hosts current consolidated texts** of six of them under `btl.gov.il/Laws1/02_0103_1xxxxx.pdf`. I found them by scanning IDs 100000-100420 (137 files exist). Each text lists its amendments, and in every case the list ends at the same last amendment Wikisource shows. That makes them up to date, unlike National Insurance's 2013 copy of the Ordinance. They are: 100022 (provident-fund rules 1964, to ק"ת 10716 of 12.7.2023), 100048 (study-fund accounts 1993), 100097 (study-fund withholding 1997), 100223 (capital-gain computation 2002), 100228 (securities withholding 2002) and 100269 (interest/dividend withholding 2005). They are government-hosted, so they are not copies, and their text layer is clean.
- **Gazette (קובץ התקנות) PDFs** come from the Reshumot file service. The gov.il collector page (`gazette-official?FolderType=3&BookletNum=N`) shows the link; I read the links in Chrome, 44 booklets in all. I checked each downloaded PDF's cover for its booklet number, and all matched. Booklet numbers came from the olaw links in Wikisource's publication boxes. Every quote in the map was checked against the text of its file page by script, comparing Hebrew letters and digits. Some old scans have OCR noise, so I chose quote spans whose text layer is clean, and wrote quotes with normal punctuation.
- **Laws (ספר החוקים)** come from fs.knesset.gov.il, using the Knesset OData list of amending laws.

## Mismatches and notes for the family agents (no rule changed)

- **TXS-10**: the quoted sentence "רווחים שחילק מנהל קרן נאמנות פטורה ... חייבים במס בשיעור של 25%" is not in reg. 10(ב) of the 2005 regulations. That regulation is about withholding: "מנהל קרן נאמנות פטורה המשלם רווחים לבעל יחידה שהוא יחיד, ינכה מהם מס בשיעור של 25%".
- **TXP-24** cites reg. 34 for "a provident fund intended to pay an annuity". That wording is the reg. 1 definition "קופת גמל לקצבה" – קופת גמל שנועדה לתשלום קצבה (BTL p.4).
- **TXP-10/TXP-11** cite reg. 19 for השיעור להפקדה, סכום התקרה, תקרת הפיצויים and 32,000. Those terms are not in the regulations; they are Ordinance s.3(ה3) terms. Only the 7.5% cap is in reg. 19(א)(1)(ג)(1) (BTL p.7).
- **TXH-15**: the 25% withholding in reg. 2(ב) applies only to taxable interest on payments made to the fund from 1.1.2012 (ק"ת 7080, s.2). Earlier payments were withheld at 20%.
- **TXS-14**: the 2003 inflationary-amount regulations define their fund by Ordinance s.105יא and "mixed funds", which belong to the pre-2006 law. Whether they still operate was not checked.
- **TXG-17**: the filing-exemption regulations were amended on 16.9.2024 (reg. 3(א) now says "יחיד תושב ישראל"; 3(ג),(ד) repealed; applies to new and veteran returning residents from 1.1.2026), on 26.11.2024 (micro-business income added) and on 22.12.2025. The rule's ceilings come from the Tax Authority guide, which is official and already cited.
- **TX2-17**: since 31.3.2026 para. (2) of the VAT "מוסד כספי" definition ends "ולמעט בנק חברתי". The rule's paraphrase predates this; it does not matter for portfolio managers.
- **TXF-25**: the rule says gov.il returned 403, but the circular is on gov.il (`professional-directives-180226-1`) and downloads with curl. The rule's title says "ההצהרה"; the circular says "הצהרה".
- **TXH-13/TXH-23**: the 2024 memorandum is on the government legislation site. I found no law that enacted it: Ordinance Amendment 273 (ס"ח 3262, 25.7.2024) is a different measure (reserve soldiers' deposits).
- **Wikisource errors met on the way**: the partial-linkage amendment is linked to booklet 6337, but it is in 6377 (p.568). The 2004 rewrite of the filing-exemption regulations (ק"ת 6317א, 31.5.2004, printed inside the 6317 PDF) is missing from Wikisource's list, which shows only its erratum (6325).
- **Economic Plan Law 2026 PDF**: R-other requested 25_lsr_12224370.pdf as its official_url. Knesset OData gives 25_lsr_12846788.pdf (64 pages, gazette pp.350-413) as the "פרסום ברשומות" document. The lead should choose one.
