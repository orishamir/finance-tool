# FA-tax3: securities and interest tax files (2026-10-25)

Scope: the Source lines of the rule blocks with a record in data/rules in `tax-securities-israeli.md` (TXS, 23 rules),
`tax-securities-foreign.md` (TXF, 19 rules) and `tax-interest-deposits-bonds.md` (TXI, 18 rules), and the figure
sources of those records. Every page below is the PDF file page, read with pdftotext on a copy downloaded today.

Documents used throughout:

- Income Tax Ordinance, Tax Authority consolidated PDF (gov.il, 312 pages, up to Amendment 262). Every section page
  was found on the page itself; the map `maps/ito.json` was right except s.91(ג), which is on p.152 (map: p.151).
- Tax Authority guide "דע זכויותיך וחובותיך" 2025 (file pages; the lead's +2 conversion was right in every citation
  checked).
- National Insurance consolidated texts of the securities withholding regulations 2002 (02_0103_100228), the
  interest and dividend withholding regulations 2005 (02_0103_100269) and the capital-gain computation regulations
  2002 (02_0103_100223).
- Migdal Mutual Funds main prospectus: a newer edition exists, dated 30.1.2026 (Maya report 1719487, the prospectus
  the manager's Maya page links today); its tax chapter 25 is on file pp.72-74 (2025 edition: pp.76-78), same
  numbering and same wording, except that 25.2.3 is now headed "שינוי מסלול המס".

Findings for the lead (no rule text changed):

1. **The consolidated Ordinance PDF is wrong at s.125ב1(ב)**: it reads "חייבים במס בשיעור של 20%" (p.203), but
   Amendment 187 (ספר החוקים 2324, 6.12.2011, gazette p.45 = file p.5, item (11)) replaced "20%" with "25%". The
   TXS-10 figure (25%) now rests on the gazette. Any other family that cites the consolidated PDF for a rate that
   Amendment 187 raised should check the page shows the new rate (s.125ב and 125ג(ב) on pp.203-204 do).
2. TXS-10's quoted sentence ("רווחים שחילק מנהל קרן נאמנות פטורה ... חייבים במס בשיעור של 25%") is s.125ב1(ב) of the
   Ordinance, as amended in 2011; reg. 10(ב) of the 2005 regulations only sets the 25% withholding.
3. TXS-13 rests on a sentence of the 2025 Migdal prospectus ("כל הקרנות המחקות, לרבות קרנות הסל ... הינן קרנות פטורות
   לצורך מס"). The 2026 edition no longer has it (each fund's status is in the fund tables only). The citation stays
   on the 2025 edition, which the rule names; whether to re-base the rule on the 2026 tables or on Maya's tax-status
   field (TXS-24) is a decision for the lead.
4. TXS-14: the 2003 inflationary-amount regulations define their fund through s.105יא and "קרן נאמנות מעורבת", which
   belong to the pre-2006 Ordinance. Migdal's 2026 prospectus (ch.25.4, p.73) still describes them as applying. Not
   decided here.
5. Rule text that the sources no longer match (rule text is not mine to change): TXS-05 quotes the guide as "אם נוכה
   המס כחוק" (the page: "אם נוכה מלוא המס כחוק"); TXS-19 says "The guide (p.39)" (now file p.41); TXS-01 says "S5
   p.38" for the substantial-shareholder definition (file p.40).
6. Not moved to a newer edition: the Yelin Lapidot new-fund prospectus (26.11.2024) and the Migdal (00) כספית שקלית
   part A of 2014. Both funds now sit in their managers' main-prospectus tables; the table rows could not be matched
   to the fund reliably from the PDF text, so the fund-specific prospectuses stay (both open and still say
   "מסלול המס: פטורה").

Figures that differ: none.
