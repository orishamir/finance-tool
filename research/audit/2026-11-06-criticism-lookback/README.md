# Criticism look-back, step 1: every criticism collected (2026-11-06)

The founder asked on 2026-11-06 that every criticism he gives be learned, not only fixed (CLAUDE.md ground rule 7: by the user lab's reviewer, by a general rule in the docs builders build from, and by a check where the rule can be named), so this look-back gathers every criticism he has given since the project began and groups them into general rules that name no page; later steps check each rule against the reviewer, the docs and the checks. Sources read: every message he typed in every session from 2026-10-22 to 2026-11-06 (877 entries, 672 once repeats are removed; kept outside the repository because they hold private matters, and nothing from them is quoted here except his short criticisms of the site, its drawings, its words, its charts and how Claude works); Claude's notes on his tastes; the user lab's lessons, its live flags, its benchmark of 123 problems he flagged (`research/user-lab/bench/cases.jsonl`) and his words on two other sites (`research/user-lab/elsewhere/`); the decisions in `docs/product.md` section 1 whose reason is his complaint; the founder quotes in `docs/design-system.md`, `docs/copy.md` and the design specs under `research/specs/`; the flag log `research/process-flags.md` (still empty); and all 190 GitHub issues, open and closed, of which about two dozen are in his own words. Questions with no complaint, one-off corrections of a number, requests for new features and anything private were left out; where he later changed his mind the later word is kept and the change is noted in the rule's `reversed` field (5 rules). The result, `criticisms.json`, holds 285 instances of criticism in 72 general rules; 58 rules were raised on two or more different dates. By kind: visual 15 rules (48 instances), words 10 (60), lists 9 (29), layout 7 (40), controls 9 (32), pages 8 (25), and how Claude works with him 14 (51).
