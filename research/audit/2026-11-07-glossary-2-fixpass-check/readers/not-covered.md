# not-covered

Who: You are 38, you read Hebrew fluently, you keep most of your savings in a bank deposit, and you have heard of makam but never bought one.

Shots: research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-fixpass-check/reader-shots/not-covered-1440-part0.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-fixpass-check/reader-shots/not-covered-1440-part1.png, research/audit/2026-11-07-glossary-2-fixpass-check/reader-shots/not-covered-1440-part2.png

I read all three screenshots. The site is called **"מקור"** ("Source"). This page is **"מה האתר עוד לא מכסה"** ("What the site doesn't cover yet").

## 1. What the page tells me, and what the site can and can't do

**What it says.** The subtitle reads "אפשרויות חיסכון והשקעה וכללי מס שהאתר מכיר אבל עוד לא מחשב, ולמה": savings options and tax rules the site knows about but doesn't calculate yet, and why. A red banner adds that it won't calculate them "עד שתהיה תשובה לכל שאלה על החוקים והכללים שלהם, ועד שיהיו להם נתונים עם מקור". That means not until every question about their rules is answered and there is sourced data.

**Nine items, each marked "לא מחושב" (not calculated):**
- savings policy (פוליסת חיסכון)
- manager's insurance (ביטוח מנהלים)
- structured deposit (פיקדון מובנה)
- corporate bonds (אג"ח קונצרנית)
- portfolio management (ניהול תיקי השקעות)
- foreign-currency deposit (פיקדון במטבע חוץ)
- real-estate fund / REIT (קרן להשקעות במקרקעין)
- gold via ETFs (השקעה בזהב דרך קרנות סל)
- the tax rule Amendment 190 (תיקון 190)

**Each card shows:**
- other names for the product
- a short definition ("מה ידוע", what's known)
- a citation to a law or regulator document, with a page number and a "מקור ראשי" (primary source) tag
- the one open question still blocking the calculation
- the questions already answered, with dates

**Things the site will never do.** Under the heading "מה האתר לא משווה ולא עושה" at the bottom of page 3:
- loans and mortgages
- insurance other than the two above
- stock picking, trading, derivatives
- physical real estate
- ranking by past returns
- automatic allocation of money between goals
- buying or transferring anything ("אין כאן קנייה, העברה או הגשת בקשה")
- user accounts: no login, and your data stays on your device

**What this means for me.** **Makam (מק"מ) doesn't appear anywhere on this page.** It isn't on the "not yet" list or the "never" list. The same goes for a plain shekel bank deposit; only the structured and foreign-currency kinds are listed. That hints the site might cover both, and the menu has sections called "חברות ומסלולים", "השקעה במדד" and "מדריך". But this page can't confirm that. I learned what the site *doesn't* do, not whether it handles my actual question. I also know I can't buy makam through it.

## 2. Confusing or hard-to-follow parts

- **Sentences that stop at "לפי" ("according to").**
  - Savings-policy card (top right, page 1): "לחלק מהשאלה נמצאה תשובה ב־25.10.2026, לפי" ends there, and only a "חוקים וכללים · 1" chip follows.
  - Foreign-currency deposit card (top left, page 2): the same thing.
  - Portfolio-management card (top right, page 2): "...כולל מע"מ על דמי הניהול, לפי" breaks at the line end, and the chip sits on the next line.

  I think the chip is meant to finish the sentence, but it reads as cut off.
- **The "חוקים וכללים · N" (laws and rules) counts.** I tried adding up the chips under each card's answered questions:
  - Savings policy: the card says **5**, and the chips add up to 1+1+1+2 = 5. ✔
  - Foreign-currency deposit: the card says **6**, but the chips add up to 1+2 = 3. ✘
  - Amendment 190: the card says **5**, but the chips add up to 1+1+1 = 3. ✘
  - Manager's insurance: the card says **6**, but it has no answered questions at all.

  So the number is probably "rules the site knows," not a sum, but nothing explains it.
- **Dates with no label.** Each card has a date next to "מקור ראשי", for example 22.10.2026 or 07.11.2026. On the Amendment 190 card the cited circular is from 2013 but the date says 22.10.2026, so it must be a "last checked" date. It isn't labeled as one.
- **"שאלה אחת עוד בלי תשובה" ("one question still without an answer") is on every single card.** That's possible, but nine out of nine made me wonder whether it's a template rather than a real count.
- **What "מחשב" ("calculates") means is never said.** Return? Tax? Fees? Comparison? This page assumes I already know.
- **Not confusing, though it looks it at first.** "Insurance other than savings policies and manager's insurance" on the "never" list matches those two being on the "not yet" list. Physical real estate being "never" while REITs are "not yet" is also consistent. The two-column right-to-left layout and the open/close sections were easy enough to follow.

## 3. Trust

**Makes me trust it more:**
- It's upfront about its limits and refuses to calculate without sources.
- It cites specific documents with page numbers. Examples: the 1995 investment advice law, page 3; the Tax Authority's "דע זכויותיך" 2025, pages 40 and 52.
- The footer says "אתר אישי, לא מסחרי, בלי קישורי שיווק" (personal, non-commercial, no affiliate links), and nothing is stored on their side.
- "הנתון האחרון באתר נבדק ב־07.11.2026": the data was checked today.

**Makes me trust it less:**
- "Personal site" means I don't know who runs it or what their qualifications are. This page names no person.
- The dangling "לפי" sentences, the unexplained counts and the unlabeled dates feel a little unfinished.
- None of this is serious, but it's an anonymous hobby project, not a regulator.

## 4. What I'd do next

1. Use the search icon (top left) or the "מדריך" (guide) page to look for **מק"מ**. I want to know whether the site actually covers makam and compares it with a regular bank deposit over a few months, including tax and fees.
2. If it does, treat it as a starting point and check what it cites myself. I'd confirm current makam yields and how to buy them at my bank or broker, including any custody or purchase fees. Then I'd compare that with the rate my bank offers on a deposit of the same length.
3. If makam isn't covered, this page has already shown me the site's limits, and I'd go straight to the Bank of Israel and my bank.
