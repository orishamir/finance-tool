TXK-21 records this; TXK-09 drops it. Shown on the site as proposed ("ממליץ להגביל את הפטור לחיסכון של עד 200,000 ש"ח לאדם"), it tells someone who already holds a large fund the opposite of what the report recommends for them. Fix: add the clause (cap for new and future savings; an existing fund above the cap keeps the age-60 annuity exemption on all of it, p.16, s.12.4.2) to TXK-09 and to the proposed Hebrew.
