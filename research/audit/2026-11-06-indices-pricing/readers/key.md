# Answer key (written before the readers ran)

What a correct reading of each line is, for scoring the readers' answers.

a. The box: a choice of the bank where the person already has a current account. Its purpose: a securities account at a bank needs a
   current account there, which costs a monthly fee; the person who already has one pays it anyway, so that bank's row should not count it.
   A reader with a bank account would choose their bank; a reader with none (or whose bank is not on the list) leaves "אף אחד מהם".
   A miss: reading it as "your securities account", "your broker", or as a filter that hides banks.
b. With no bank chosen, every bank's row includes the bank's monthly current-account fee ("דמי ניהול העו״ש"), because investing at a bank
   needs a current account there; an investment house needs none, so its rows do not include it. A miss: thinking the fee is added to
   investment houses too, or that the site charges it, or that it is a one-time fee.
c. Leumi's row no longer counts the current-account fee (the person pays it anyway); other banks' rows still do. In the list, Leumi's
   figure rises (83,396 ₪ against 82,723 ₪ before in the PC set; its charges bar lost its red part) and it moves from third to second,
   above One Zero. A miss: thinking Leumi got a discount or a special price, or that other banks also changed.
d. The fold says what a price for a fund bought abroad leaves out. First point: buying abroad also involves a gap in the exchange rate
   (spread); most banks and houses do not limit it in their price lists, so it is not in the price; only IBI, Altshuler Trade and Meitav
   Trade print a ceiling on it. A miss: reading that the conversion fee itself is left out (it is included), or that the three named
   houses are more expensive, or that the spread is charged by the site.
e. Fees charged by others abroad (a broker, a custodian, an exchange or an authority) to the bank or house are passed on to the customer,
   mostly with no ceiling, and are not in the price; so a fund bought abroad can cost more than the figure shown. A miss: thinking this
   applies to Israeli funds, or that it is a fee the bank itself sets.

## Round 2 (after the wording fixes; written before round 2 ran)

a. "לא באחד מהבנקים האלה" now covers both "no current account" and "my bank is not listed"; same correct reading as above.
d. First point: beyond the conversion fee (which is in the price), the bank or house converts at a rate it sets, which can be worse than
   the market rate; most do not cap that gap, so it is not in the calculation; IBI, Altshuler Trade and Meitav Trade print a cap.
e. Second point: bodies abroad (a broker, the body that holds the securities, the exchange, authorities) charge the bank or house; most
   pass them on with no ceiling and they are not in the calculation, so a fund bought abroad can cost more than calculated.
Legend: red in a bank's bar is the account's own management fee and the current account's fee together ("דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך
   ועו״ש"); in set B Leumi's red part is gone because its current account is no longer counted.
