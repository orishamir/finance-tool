## 2. Line by line

**a. "השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם את דמי ניהול העו״ש שלו, למי שיפתח שם חשבון עו״ש כדי להשקיע. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**
- **Whose figures include the fee:** the banks' figures (Leumi, Hapoalim, Discount and so on) include their monthly current-account fee.
- **Whose don't:** investment houses like Psagot don't, because they don't make you open a current account with them.

The page doesn't tell me to do anything outright. It only hints that I should use the box above it. When I did (set B), the line changed to "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי תשלמו עליו בכל מקרה", and Leumi's cost dropped from 5,283 to 4,541 ₪.

What trips me up:
- **"למי שיפתח שם"** sounds as if I'd be opening a new current account. I already have one, so before set B the Leumi row was charging me for something I already pay. The line should say "choose your bank above" plainly.
- **Who counts as a "בנק" and who as a "בית השקעות"?** I can't tell for "וואן זירו" or "אי.בי.אי". Is Wallen Ziro a bank, and is a current-account fee inside its 4,544 ₪?

**b. The two red items in the colour keys.**
- Top chart: red is "דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך", the securities account fee only.
- Account list: red is "דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך ועו״ש", the securities account fee plus the current-account fee.

The difference makes sense once I connect it to line (a). The top chart shows only Psagot pairs, which have no current-account fee, and I don't even see red in those two bars. The account list includes banks, whose red includes the current-account fee.

Still, the same red means two different things on the same page, and the page doesn't say why. Also, after I chose Leumi, does Leumi's red still contain any current-account fee? Logically it shouldn't, but the colour key doesn't change.

**c. "מעבר לעמלת המרת המטבע, הבנק או בית ההשקעות ממירים את השקלים לדולרים בשער שקובעים, שיכול להיות גרוע משער השוק…"** The point: when they convert my shekels to dollars, they can use a worse rate than the market rate.
- **IBI, Altshuler Trade and Meitav Trade** (the companies named at the end): their price lists state a maximum gap, so their figures **do** count the gap, at that maximum ("בגובה התקרה").
- **Everyone else:** their price lists state no limit, so the gap is **left out** of their figures. They look cheaper than they really are for a fund bought abroad.

This only matters if I buy a fund abroad. My Yalin fund is bought in shekels in Tel Aviv, so I think it doesn't affect me.

It's not quite clear whether the 9,934, 23,790 and 26,817 ₪ shown for those three right now include the gap, since I've selected an Israeli fund. I assume they don't, but the text doesn't say "only when you choose a foreign fund".

**d. "גם גופים בחו״ל גובים עמלות…"** What's left out: fees charged by others abroad, such as the broker, the custodian, the exchange or the authorities. Most banks and investment houses pass these fees on to me. They're left out even when the price list states a maximum ("גם כשהתעריפון כותב להן תקרה"). So a fund bought abroad can cost more than shown.

"יוצא מן הכלל" says Wallen Ziro states a fixed charge on each dividend for the custodian, and the calculation does include that.

What trips me up:
- **"סכום קבוע" and then "בגובה התקרה".** Is it a fixed amount or a maximum? I can read it either way.
- **Why the exception?** The sentence before says these fees aren't counted even when a maximum is written. Then Wallen Ziro's is counted. I don't understand why it's treated differently.
- **Does it matter for me?** For an accumulating fund that pays no dividends, I'd guess this charge never applies, but the page doesn't say.

**e. Set D, the Leumi cost box.**

*The row "דמי ניהול חשבון עו״ש בחודש".*
- **What it is:** the 5.50 ₪ a month current-account fee the page added to Leumi's 5,283 ₪.
- **Where 5.50 comes from:** I'm not sure. The other values carry the tag "פרמטר של האתר"; this one has no tag. The rule listed right under it is "התקרות החדשות לעמלות העו״ש" (BANK-35). So I can't tell whether 5.50 ₪ is Leumi's own price list or a legal maximum. I'd check my own Leumi statement.
- **Mislabelled defaults:** my plan numbers (10,000, 500 and so on) are tagged "פרמטר של האתר". I'd call them my inputs; they're only the site's defaults.

*The amber box "מיולי 2027 הסכום כאן יכול להיות גבוה מדי…"*
- **What it says:** from July 2027, an order from the Bank of Israel governor caps current-account fees by how many actions you make a month. For an account with up to two actions a month, the cap is lower than the fee counted here.
- **Why the page doesn't adjust:** nobody knows yet how an "action" will be counted, so the page keeps today's fee for all 10 years.
- **What it means for Leumi's figure:** 5,283 ₪ may be too high.
- **What it means for me:** nothing. Once I choose Leumi, the current-account fee isn't counted at all. Nothing to do, unless I want to watch my own statement after July 2027.
- **Wording:** "גבוה מדי" took me a second. It means the page may *overstate* Leumi's cost.

*"רשומות · צו הבנקאות (שירות ללקוח) (פיקוח על…"* I believe "רשומות" is the state's official gazette, where laws and orders are published. The source is the Banking (Customer Service) order, but the title is cut off with "…", so I can't read the full name or date.
