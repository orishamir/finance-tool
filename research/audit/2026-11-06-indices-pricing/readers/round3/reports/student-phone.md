## 1. My decision

**Account: Pesagot (פסגות ניירות ערך בע״מ). Fund: "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282.** I'm fairly sure about this, maybe 75%.

**Why:**
- The account list "כל החשבונות" puts Pesagot first. After 10 years I'd have 86,492 ₪, and the account would cost 1,057 ₪.
- The next account, One Zero (וואן זירו), leaves 83,141 ₪, which is about 3,350 ₪ less.
- Even when my friend picked Leumi as the bank (set B), Leumi only moved up to second place with 83,396 ₪. Pesagot still wins by about 3,100 ₪.
- For the fund, the top card says Yelin and Altshuler are basically tied, only "‎−43 ₪ לעומת הזוג שמימין". The page itself says "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר". So I'm not worried about which of the two I pick.
- The step chart near the bottom agrees. It says "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות".

**What makes me less sure:**
- **I'm a student, and student prices aren't counted.** Almost every bank row has a grey "לא בחישוב" tag saying "מחיר אחר לצעירים… לסטודנטים". That's me. If my bank gives students a big discount, the 3,000 ₪ gap could shrink.
- **I don't understand the fund's tags.** Yelin is tagged "עסקת החלף", which I don't understand. Its track record shows only "שנה אחת", while Harel and Kesem show "4 שנים".

## 2. The lines

**a. "השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם את דמי ניהול העו״ש שלו, למי שיפתח שם חשבון עו״ש כדי להשקיע. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**
- **What it means:** the banks' figures include the monthly fee for a current account, as if I opened a new one there just to invest. Investment houses like Pesagot, Altshuler Trade and Meitav Trade don't need a current account, so that fee isn't in their figures.
- **What to do:** the page doesn't say it directly. I guess the hint is to pick my own bank in the box above. In set B, once Leumi was picked, the line changed to "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי משלמים עליו בכל מקרה", and that made sense.
- **What's unclear:**
  - "למי שיפתח" — what if I already have an account at that bank? The box answers this, but only if I notice the box.
  - It doesn't say whether One Zero counts as a bank here. Its bar has no red at all.

**b. Red in the two colour keys**
- In the chart near the top, red is "דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך": only the fee for the investing account.
- In the key under the account list, red is "דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך ועו״ש": that fee plus the current-account fee lumped together.
- **Does the difference make sense?** Mostly. The top chart compared two Pesagot pairs, and Pesagot has no current account. In set E, where the top pairs were One Zero and Leumi, the top key also says "ועו״ש".
- **What bothers me:**
  - The same red means slightly different things in two places.
  - In the list I can't tell how much of a red bar is the current-account fee and how much is the investing-account fee.

**c. "מעבר לעמלת המרת המטבע, הבנק או בית ההשקעות ממירים את השקלים לדולרים בשער שהם קובעים…"**
- **What it means:** besides the fee for changing shekels to dollars, the bank sets its own exchange rate, which can be worse than the market rate. Most price lists put no limit on that gap, so it's left out of their figures.
- **For IBI, Altshuler Trade and Meitav Trade** (named at the end), the price list does give a limit. Their figures include the gap at that maximum ("בגובה התקרה").
- **The catch:** those three look worse partly because they're the honest ones. Everyone else's figure may be too low.
- **Does it matter for me?** I think it's only about a fund bought abroad, not the Israeli fund I'm choosing. I'm not 100% sure.
- **What's unclear:** "עמלת המרת המטבע" versus "שער שהם קובעים". I'm not sure I understand how those two are different costs.

**d. "גם גופים בחו״ל גובים עמלות…"**
- **What's left out:** fees charged by bodies abroad, like the broker ("ברוקר"), the body that holds the securities, the stock exchange and authorities. Banks pass these fees on to the customer. They're left out even when the price list gives a limit ("גם כשהתעריפון כותב להן תקרה"). So a fund bought abroad can cost more than shown.
- **The "יוצא מן הכלל" part:** One Zero is the exception. It charges a fee on every "דיבידנד" for the holding body, and the figure counts that at its maximum.
- **What I trip on:**
  - I don't know what "דיבידנד" means.
  - The fund is called "(Accumulating)", which I also don't understand. So I can't tell whether this fee would ever hit me.
  - Point c includes a cost at its limit for three companies, but here costs with a known limit are left out. That seems inconsistent.

**e. Set D**
- **The row "דמי ניהול חשבון עו״ש בחודש 5.50 ₪":** this is the monthly current-account fee at Leumi, built into Leumi's 5,283 ₪.
  - Every other row (amount, deposit, years, return, inflation) has a "פרמטר של האתר" tag. This row has none, so I can't see where 5.50 comes from.
  - The closest source is "התקרות החדשות לעמלות העו״ש… BANK-35". That's confusing: is 5.50 today's fee or the new upper limit?
- **The amber box "מיולי 2027 הסכום כאן יכול להיות גבוה מדי…":**
  - From July 2027 a Bank of Israel order limits this fee by the number of actions per month.
  - For accounts with up to two actions a month ("עד שתי פעולות בחודש"), the limit is lower than the fee used here.
  - It isn't known yet how an action will be counted, so the figure keeps today's fee for all 10 years.
  - **For me:** Leumi's 5,283 ₪ may be too high, so Leumi could be a bit cheaper than shown.
  - **Should I do anything?** The box doesn't say. If Leumi is my salary bank, the fee isn't counted anyway. If I opened a Leumi account only to invest, with one standing order a month, I might fall under the lower limit.
  - I can't tell what "פעולה" includes, and the page admits it doesn't know either.
- **The line "רשומות · צו הבנקאות (שירות ללקוח) (פיקוח על שירות נ…":**
  - I think "רשומות" is the official government publication where laws and orders appear. So the source is the official Banking (Customer Service) order.
  - The name is cut off at "נ…", so I can't read the full title or a date.

**f. "מיליארד" in set E**
- **What the figures are:** 1.86 billion ₪ for One Zero, Leumi and Benleumi, and 1.85 billion for Discount through Mizrahi. Then Pesagot 1.78, Altshuler 1.75, IBI 1.6, Meitav 1.48, Interactive 1.81, and Excellence's cost of 1.57 billion. All of these are thousands of millions of shekels.
- **What confused me:**
  - **Ties hide real gaps.** One Zero and Leumi both show 1.86 billion, yet the top card says Leumi is "‎−3,509,878 ₪" behind. Rounding hides millions.
  - **The ranks don't match the figures.** One Zero, Leumi, Benleumi, Discount, Mercantile and Yahav are all ranked "1", although Discount, Mercantile and Yahav show 1.85 and the first three show 1.86. Then Poalim is "7".
  - **Mixed formats.** "1.6" has one decimal place while the rest have two.
  - **Excellence looks backwards.** It's the only row where what's left is written in full, "758,833,521 ₪". That's smaller than its cost, which is written as "1.57 מיליארד".
  - **The table at the bottom is garbled.** The "סכום היום" row has numbers printed on top of each other: "295,664,70560,000,0005…". I can't read it.
  - **Two funds show the same figure.** Kesem and Harel both show exactly "‎−71,042,305 ₪".
