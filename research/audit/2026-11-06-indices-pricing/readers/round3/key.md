# Round 3: the answer key, written before any reader ran (2026-11-06)

Two lines changed after the finance review and the independent check of the build:

1. Under the box "הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו״ש", with no bank chosen:
   "השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם את דמי ניהול העו״ש שלו, למי שיפתח שם חשבון עו״ש כדי להשקיע. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."
   Meant: each bank's figure includes that bank's monthly current-account fee, as for a person who would open a current
   account there in order to invest; an investment house's figure has no such fee, because it does not ask for one.
   The page no longer states, as a fact about every bank, that a securities account needs a current account there.
   A miss: the reader thinks the page tells them to open a current account; thinks the houses' figures include the fee;
   thinks the fee is counted only for people who already have an account there; cannot say what "למי שיפתח שם" adds.

2. In the fold "מה לא נכנס למחיר של קרן שנקנית בחו״ל", the first point now ends:
   "תקרה לפער כתובה רק אצל אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד ומיטב טרייד, ואצלם הפער נכנס לחישוב לפי התקרה."
   Meant: at most houses the gap between their own exchange rate and the market's has no limit in the price list and
   is not in the figures; at the three houses that print a limit, the figures count the gap at that limit, the most
   they may take. A miss: the reader thinks the three houses' gap is left out; thinks the other houses' figures
   include the gap; reads "לפי התקרה" as a discount or as "up to"; thinks the three are cheaper because of the limit.
