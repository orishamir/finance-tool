I'm writing this as a 23-year-old student with 10,000 ₪ saved who can add 500 ₪ a month.

## 1. My decision

I'd go with **Pesagot (פסגות) as the account** and the fund **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282**. I'm about 70% sure.

What I used:
- **The big green box near the top** with my numbers (10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7%). It says "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם" ("one of the 3 cheapest pairs for your plan"): Pesagot plus Yelin leaves me **86,492 ₪**. The next pair, Pesagot plus Altshuler, leaves "‎−43 ₪". The page itself says "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע" ("the differences between the 3 cheapest pairs are too small to decide"). So the fund choice barely matters. Any of those three is fine.
- **The list "כל החשבונות"** (all the accounts). Pesagot is number 1 with the star "הזול" ("the cheapest"). It takes 1,057 ₪ over 10 years. Most banks take 4,500 to 27,000 ₪.
- **The step chart further down.** It also ends with "הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות" ("the most across all the price lists").

Why only 70%:
- I'm a student, and almost every bank row has a "לא בחישוב" tag ("not in the calculation") with "מחיר אחר ... לסטודנטים" ("a different price for students"). My real price at a bank could be lower than what's shown, and I don't know by how much.
- I don't know what opening a Pesagot account involves when my salary stays in my bank.
- The middle of the page, about "נטרול מט״ח", went mostly over my head. I just stayed with the "בלי נטרול" funds, which is what the page picked by default.

## 2. The lines

**a. "הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו״ש" set to "לא באחד מהבנקים האלה"**
- **What it's for:** I tell it where my current account (עו״ש) is, and it fixes the costs for that bank.
- **Would I change it?** Yes, to my own bank, because my salary already goes into a bank account. "לא באחד מהבנקים האלה" ("not at one of these banks") is wrong for almost everyone, so it's an odd thing to start with.
- **Unclear:** nothing tells me the list changes when I pick a bank. I only learned that from comparing set A and set B.

**b. "חשבון ניירות ערך בבנק צריך חשבון עו״ש באותו בנק, ולכן השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם את דמי ניהול העו״ש שלו. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**
- **What it means:** if I invest through a bank, I need a current account at that same bank, so each bank's cost includes the current account's fees. An investment house (בית השקעות), like Pesagot, doesn't need a current account with it.
- **For me:** banks look more expensive in the default view partly because of fees I may already be paying.
- **Unclear:** "דמי ניהול העו״ש". Is that the monthly fee I already pay on my salary account? If an investment house doesn't need a current account, how does my money get in? Do I transfer it from my bank, and does that cost something? The page doesn't say.

**c. "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי משלמים עליו בכל מקרה. השורות של שאר הבנקים סופרות אותו."**
- **What it means:** I already pay for my Leumi current account anyway, so that fee isn't counted against Leumi. The other banks still have it counted.
- **What changed between A and B:**
  - Leumi went from **82,723 ₪ (cost 5,283 ₪) at #3** to **83,396 ₪ (cost 4,541 ₪) at #2**.
  - Leumi and Wan Ziro (וואן זירו) swapped places.
  - The small red part disappeared from Leumi's bar.
  - Pesagot is still #1, about 3,100 ₪ ahead, so for me nothing changes.
- **Unclear:** "סופרות אותו" ("count it") is fair for the other banks. But Pesagot doesn't need a current account, so I'd still pay Leumi for mine anyway. Is that left out of Pesagot's 1,057 ₪? I think so, but the line only talks about banks, so I'm not sure.

**d. "מה לא נכנס למחיר של קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" and "מעבר לעמלת המרת המטבע, הבנק או בית ההשקעות ממירים את השקלים לדולרים בשער שהם קובעים, שיכול להיות גרוע משער השוק…"**
- **What it means:** the folded line lists costs left out for funds bought abroad. The first point says that besides the fee for changing shekels to dollars, the bank picks its own exchange rate, which can be worse than the real one. That gap isn't counted. Only "אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד ומיטב טרייד" write down a cap on it.
- **For me:** the real cost of a foreign fund is higher than shown.
- **Unclear:**
  - "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" ("a fund bought abroad"). Does that include the iShares fund, which the page also calls "קרן חוץ בת״א" ("a foreign fund in Tel Aviv")? Or does it not matter for my Israeli Yelin fund at all? I'm guessing it doesn't matter for my choice, but the page doesn't say.
  - "תקרה לפער" ("a cap on the gap"). I worked out that it's a limit on how bad their rate can be, but "הפער" ("the gap") alone took me a second read.

**e. "גם גופים בחו״ל גובים עמלות מהבנק או מבית ההשקעות: ברוקר, הגוף ששומר את ניירות הערך, הבורסה או רשויות…"**
- **What it means:** companies abroad charge the bank or investment house fees, and most pass them on to me without a cap. They aren't counted, so a fund bought abroad "יכולה לעלות יותר ממה שמחושב כאן" ("can cost more than what's calculated here").
- **Unclear:** I don't know what a "ברוקר" (broker) is. "הגוף ששומר את ניירות הערך" ("the body that keeps the securities") is also strange to me: someone keeps my shares? "רשויות" ("authorities") is vague too. Which ones, and how much? There's no number at all.

**The red in the bars:** the key says "דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך ועו״ש", the monthly fee for keeping the securities account and the current account. That's why Leumi's red vanished when I picked Leumi.
- **Unclear:** it's one colour for two different fees. I can't tell how much of the red is the account itself and how much is the current account.
- I also don't know what the yellow "דמי משמרת" means. I don't know the word משמרת in this sense, and it's a big part of most bars.

## 3. Other things that confused or bothered me

- **Student prices left out.** "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר ... לסטודנטים" appears on Leumi, Discount, Mizrahi, Mercantile and others. I'm exactly that person, and the page ignores it. Pesagot's lead might shrink, and there's no button like "I'm a student".
- **Wan Ziro (וואן זירו) and Interactive Brokers** have no proper price ("אין נתון", no data). Further down it says "יש ספק אם נמכרת שם" ("doubtful whether it's sold there"). I can't judge them.
- **Grey in some bars and "הפס נחתך"** ("the bar is cut"). I didn't understand "העמלות זו על זו" ("the fees on each other") in the key.
- **The fund names "(4D)" and "(4A)"** are never explained. I only worked out from the hedging section that 4A means "מנוטרלת מט״ח" (hedged against currency changes).
- **"מחיר להוראת קבע"** ("price for a standing order") shows on some banks. I understood that the monthly 500 ₪ counts as a standing order, but not what I'd have to set up to get that price.
- **What I expected and didn't find:**
  - How to actually open the account, and whether Pesagot has a minimum.
  - Whether moving money from my bank to Pesagot costs anything.
  - What happens if I want the money out early.
- **The page says it doesn't recommend.** "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד" ("the site doesn't recommend an index"), yet it puts "הזול" ("the cheapest") with a star on Pesagot. I get the difference, but the ranking still feels like a recommendation.
