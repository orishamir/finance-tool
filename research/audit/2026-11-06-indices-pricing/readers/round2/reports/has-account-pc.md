# has-account-pc

Who: You are 41, on a computer. You already have a current account and a securities account at your bank and pay their fees; you know words like "דמי ניהול" and "עמלת קנייה" from your statements, but you have never compared accounts or funds.

I've gone through all nine screenshots. I'm writing as the 41-year-old with a bank current account (עו״ש) and securities account.

## 1. My decision

I'd use **פסגות** (Psagot) as the account and the fund **ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)**, number 5139282. I'm fairly sure, maybe 70%.

What I used to decide:
- **The top card, "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם".** With 10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month for 10 years and 7% a year, this pair leaves 86,492 ₪. It's the top row in both lists.
- **The fund card.** "דמי ניהול: 0% קבוע, 0% משתנה, 0.02% נאמן" is far lower than anything I've seen on my statements.
- **The account list.** Psagot costs "1,057 ₪" over 10 years. Leumi costs 4,541 ₪ even after choosing Leumi in set B. The others cost more.

Why I'm not more sure:
- **The runner-up is a near tie.** "אלטשולר שחם מחקה S&P 500 (4D)" is only "−43 ₪" behind over 10 years. That's nothing, so the fund choice is really a coin toss between those two.
- **Switching costs effort.** Moving from my bank to Psagot gains about 3,100 ₪ over 10 years (83,396 vs 86,492) if my bank is Leumi. That means opening a new account, which the page doesn't count. It's probably still worth it, though not by a huge margin.
- **My real fees may be lower.** I may have a better price than the published tariff. The account card says "קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו", so I'd enter my real fees from my statements before deciding.
- **The page doesn't know my bank yet.** It shows "לא באחד מהבנקים האלה" until I choose. My friend picked Leumi. I'd pick my own bank first.

## 2. The lines

**a. "הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו״ש" / "לא באחד מהבנקים האלה"**
This is where I tell the page which bank already has my current account. Yes, I'd change it to my bank. Otherwise the page charges my bank's row for a current account I'm already paying for anyway, so my own bank looks worse than it really is.

What's unclear:
- **"האלה" ("these").** It isn't obvious which banks the list covers until I open it.
- **More than one bank.** It doesn't say what to do if I have current accounts at two banks.

**b. "חשבון ניירות ערך בבנק צריך חשבון עו״ש באותו בנק, ולכן השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם דמי ניהול עו״ש שלו. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**
A bank only gives you a securities account if you also keep a current account there. So each bank's cost includes its current-account fee. Investment houses like Psagot don't need one, so their rows don't include it. That's clear.

What's unclear: "דמי ניהול עו״ש שלו" doesn't say which current-account price is used. Many people have a discounted or special track.

**c. "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי משלמים עליו בכל מקרה. השורות של שאר הבנקים סופרות אותו."**
Since I already pay for a Leumi current account, it's no longer counted against Leumi.

What changed between set A and set B:
- Leumi's 10-year cost dropped from 5,283 ₪ to 4,541 ₪.
- Its result rose from 82,723 ₪ to 83,396 ₪.
- It moved from 3rd to 2nd place, above וואן זירו (Van Zir).
- The red part of its bar disappeared.
- Nothing else in the list seemed to change.

What's unclear: "בכל מקרה" ("in any case") assumes I'll keep my current account no matter what. That's true for me, but the page should say it's assuming that.

**d. "מה לא נכנס למחיר של קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" → "מעבר לעמלת המרת המטבע…"**
On top of the currency-conversion fee, the bank changes my shekels to dollars at a rate it sets itself, which can be worse than the market rate. That hidden gap isn't counted. Only "אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד ומיטב טרייד" publish a cap on it.

This matters only for funds bought abroad, like the Vanguard and iShares Ireland funds. It doesn't affect my choice, which is an Israeli fund.

What's unclear: the sentence reads "רוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות מגבילים את הפער הזה בתעריפון, ולכן הוא לא נכנס לחישוב". As I read it, it says most of them *do* limit the gap. That contradicts the next sentence, which says only three firms publish a cap. I think a "לא" ("not") is missing, but I'm not certain how it's meant.

**e. "גם גופים בחו״ל גובים עמלות: ברוקר, הגוף ששומר את ניירות הערך, הבורסה או רשויות…"**
Foreign brokers, custodians, exchanges and authorities also take fees. Most banks pass these on to me "בלי תקרה" (with no cap), and they aren't in the numbers. So a fund bought abroad can cost more than the page shows. This is again a reason not to buy abroad.

What's unclear: "רשויות" ("authorities"). Does that mean taxes or something else? I don't know.

**Red in the bars:** the colour key labels red "דמי ניהול חשבון ניירות ערך ועו״ש". So red is the monthly account fee, and for a bank it also includes the current-account fee. When Leumi was chosen, its red disappeared completely. That suggests Leumi's red was all current-account fee, which would mean Leumi charges no securities-account management fee. I can't tell that from the bar, though. I'd want the two parts shown separately.

## 3. Other things that confused or bothered me

- **Discounts left out.** Many banks have notes like "לא בחישוב: מחיר אחר לצעירים, לחיילים משוחררים, לסטודנטים, ללקוחות טוטאל דיגיטל…". I don't know if I qualify for any of them, and they could change the order.
- **No fee rates in the account list.** The bars show shekels only. They don't show the actual custody fee percentage or the per-trade fee I could check against my statement.
- **The bars have no numbers.** I have to guess how many shekels each coloured piece is.
- **The comparison example starts at the wrong bank.** The step-by-step comparison begins at "ירושלים" with "MTF סל", which the page says was picked "מאמצע הרשימות" (from the middle of the lists). I'd expect it to start from my own bank once I've chosen it.
- **Unexplained terms.** "(4D)" and "(4A)" aren't explained anywhere I could see. The same goes for the tags "מס גם על שינוי המטבע" (on every Israeli fund) and "קרן מחקה" vs "קרן סל".
- **"אין נתון" rows.** אינטראקטיב ישראל and Interactive Brokers sit at the bottom with no price. I can't tell if they're cheap or expensive.
- **Things I expected and didn't find:** how hard it is to open a new account, and what happens to the investments I already hold at my bank. The page also doesn't say whether my current-account fee changes if I stop using my bank's securities account.
