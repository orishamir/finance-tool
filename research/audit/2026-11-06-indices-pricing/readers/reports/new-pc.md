# new-pc

Who: You are 33, on a computer. You have savings in a bank deposit and want to start investing in the S&P 500. You have never opened a securities account and do not know which bank or investment house to choose.

## 1. My decision

**I'd open a securities account at פסגות (Psagot) and buy "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", fund number 5139282.** I'm fairly sure about the account and less sure about the fund. Two things would make me more confident: entering my real numbers in the inputs at the top, and setting the bank box to my own bank.

What I based it on:
- **The top summary.** With the default inputs (10,000 ₪ now, 500 ₪ a month, 10 years, 7%), "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם" is פסגות + ילין לפידות, leaving **86,492 ₪**.
- **The account list.** פסגות is #1 with the star "★ הזול". It charges only 1,057 ₪ over 10 years. Every other account leaves at least about 3,000 ₪ less.
- **The fund list "כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500 בחשבון פסגות".** ילין לפידות is "בחירת האתר". It's only 43 ₪ ahead of אלטשולר שחם מחקה (5139100). The page says so itself: "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר, ולכן הם שקולים". So in practice the fund is a toss-up between those two (and the iShares 1159250).
- **The step-by-step section at the bottom.** It confirms that picking the right **account** matters much more (+6,110 ₪) than picking the right fund (+2,344 ₪).

What holds me back on the fund: its fees, "0% קבוע, 0% משתנה, 0.02% נאמן", look almost too good, and "מול המדד +0.37%" is based on just "שנה אחת". The page doesn't say whether those fees could go up later.

## 2. The lines

**a. "הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו״ש", set to "אף אחד מהם"**
This tells the page which bank I already have a checking account with, so it doesn't charge me that bank's checking fee again. I keep savings in a bank deposit, so I definitely have a bank account. **I'd change it to my own bank.** Leaving "אף אחד מהם" makes my own bank look more expensive than it really is for me.

What's unclear: "אף אחד מהם" can be read two ways. It could mean "I have no checking account anywhere", or "my bank isn't on this list". It seems odd as the default when almost everyone has a bank.

**b. "חשבון ניירות ערך בבנק צריך חשבון עו״ש באותו בנק, ולכן השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם את דמי ניהול העו״ש שלו. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**
If I open securities at a bank, I also need a checking account at that bank. So each bank's row includes the checking-account fee, while an investment house doesn't need one. That partly explains why investment houses like פסגות come out ahead.

What I trip on:
- "**כל בנק**": does this include my own bank, where I already pay that fee anyway? Box (a) seems to handle that, but this line doesn't say so.
- **The rows aren't labelled "bank" or "investment house".** I know לאומי is a bank and guess פסגות and מיטב טרייד are investment houses, but I'm not sure about ואן זיו, אקסלנס or אלטשולר טרייד.
- The legend colour "**דמי ניהול חשבון**" is ambiguous. Is it the checking fee, the securities-account fee, or both? There is no separate "checking" colour.

**c. Set B: "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי משלמים עליו בכל מקרה. השורות של שאר הבנקים סופרות אותו."**
Since I'd pay Leumi's checking fee regardless, it isn't added to Leumi's row. That's fair and clear.

What changed between A and B:
- לאומי went from **#3, 82,723 ₪ (cost 5,283)** to **#2, 83,396 ₪ (cost 4,541)**. It moved up past ואן זיו (83,141).
- The red part of Leumi's bar disappeared. That's how I worked out that red means the checking fee.
- פסגות stayed #1 at 86,492, and the funds didn't change.

So even as a Leumi customer, פסגות still leaves about 3,100 ₪ more after 10 years.

One small doubt: "בכל מקרה" assumes I'd keep my Leumi checking account no matter what. That's true for me, but not necessarily for everyone.

**d. "מה לא נכנס למחיר של קרן שנקנית בחו״ל", and in set C: "בקרן שנקנית בחו״ל יש גם פער בשער ההמרה. רוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות לא מגבילים אותו בתעריפון, ולכן הוא לא נכנס למחיר. תקרה לפער כתובה רק אצל אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד ומיטב טרייד."**
The folded line is a warning that funds bought abroad cost more than the page shows. The first point says that when I convert shekels to dollars, the bank gives me a worse rate than the real one. Most places don't cap that spread, so the page can't include it. Only three firms publish a cap.

What's unclear:
- "**פער בשער ההמרה**": the gap between which two rates?
- "**גם**": in addition to what? The legend already has "המרת מטבע" as a cost. Is this extra, on top of that?
- For אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד and מיטב טרייד, does "תקרה לפער כתובה" mean their cap *is* included in their numbers, or only that it exists?
- Which funds count as "נקנית בחו״ל"? I assume the ones tagged "נקנית בחו״ל" (Vanguard, IVV, VOO, iShares IE…), not "קרן חוץ בת״א".

For me this doesn't change much, because I'm choosing an Israeli fund.

**e. "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל יכולה לעלות יותר מהמספר שבשורה: גוף משמרת, ברוקר, בורסה או רשות בחו״ל גובים עמלות מהבנק או מבית ההשקעות, ורוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות מעבירים אותן ללקוח בלי תקרה. העמלות האלה לא נכנסות למחיר."**
Foreign intermediaries charge my bank, and the bank passes those charges on to me with no limit. So the real cost of a foreign fund is unknown, and higher than shown. That pushes me further toward an Israeli fund.

What I trip on:
- "**המספר שבשורה**": which row, the account or the fund? The foreign funds' rows say "אין מחיר כאן" and have no number.
- I don't really know what "**גוף משמרת**" or "**רשות בחו״ל**" are. Is the second a tax authority? I'd only be guessing.

## 3. Other things that confused or bothered me

- **A contradiction.** The fund list says "אין מחיר כאן" for the foreign iShares, but the bottom section gives "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" a number: 57,957 ₪, "פחות 20,081". Where did that come from?
- **"לא בחישוב" discounts** (Leumi "לצעירים, לחיילים…", מיטב טרייד "למצטרפים חדשים עד 30.11.2026", ואן זיו "ONE, 49 ₪ לחודש"). They could change the ranking, but nobody tells me the age limit for "צעירים". Am I, at 33, young?
- **"מחיר להוראת קבע"** appears under some banks but not under פסגות. Does פסגות also charge a standing-order price for monthly buys?
- **Tags I don't understand**: "עסקת החלף" on my chosen fund, "מס גם על שינויי המטבע", and what "(4D)" means. Is a swap-based fund riskier?
- **אינטראקטיב ישראל and Interactive Brokers** show "אין נתון", so I can't compare them.
- **Why is the bottom section's "נקודת התחלה לדוגמה" MTF סל at ירושלים?** It looks random. And does it change after choosing Leumi? Set B doesn't show that part.
- **Things I expected and didn't find**: how to actually open a פסגות account (online? minimum deposit?), whether the fund's very low fees are guaranteed, and whether the fund ranking would be different inside a different account (the list says "בחשבון פסגות").
