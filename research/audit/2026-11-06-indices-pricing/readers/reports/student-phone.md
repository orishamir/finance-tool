## 1. My decision

I'd open the account at **פסגות** (the row that says "פסגות ניירות ערך בע״מ") and buy **"ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282**.

I'm fairly sure, maybe 7 out of 10. These are the parts I used:

- **The two top cards.** "אחד מ־3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר לתוכנית שלכם" puts פסגות + ילין לפידות at 86,492 ₪ after 10 years.
- **The account list "כל החשבונות".** פסגות is #1 with "★ הזול". It costs 1,057 ₪ over 10 years, while the next ones cost about 4,500 ₪.
- **The fund card.** It shows "דמי ניהול: 0% קבועים 0% משתנים" and "נאמן 0.02%". I don't know what נאמן is, but 0.02% looks tiny.
- **The fund list.** ילין לפידות is marked "בחירת האתר" and is cheapest in its group.
- **"הכי הרבה בכל המחירונים: 86,492 ₪, ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D), פסגות"** near the bottom, which confirms the same pair.

What keeps me from being sure:

- The page says "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר". The second-best pair (פסגות + אלטשולר שחם) is only 43 ₪ behind, so the fund choice barely matters.
- I'm a student, and almost every bank has a "לא בחישוב / מחיר אחר ... לסטודנטים" tag (more on this in part 3).
- The long "עם נטרול מט״ח או בלי" section says the dollar could help or hurt me. I don't fully understand it, so I'm sticking with the unhedged fund the page picked.

## 2. The lines

**a. "הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו״ש" with "אף אחד מהם"**

- **What it means:** You tell the page where you already have a current account. The page then stops charging you for opening a new current account at that bank.
- **Would I change it?** Yes. My salary goes into a current account, so "אף אחד מהם" is wrong for me. I'd choose my real bank. My friend chose לאומי, but that only makes sense if לאומי is actually my bank.
- **Would it change my decision?** Probably not. Even in set B, פסגות stays first.
- **Unclear:** Nothing really. The box doesn't say why it matters until you read the line under it.

**b. "חשבון ניירות ערך בבנק צריך חשבון עו״ש באותו בנק, ולכן השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם את דמי ניהול העו״ש שלו. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**

- **What it means:** If I invest through a bank, I need a current account at that same bank. That account has its own monthly fee, and the page adds it to that bank's cost. An "investment house" doesn't need one.
- **What I trip on:**
  - **"בית השקעות":** The list never says which rows are banks and which are investment houses. I guess פסגות, אלטשולר טרייד, מיטב טרייד and אקסלנס are investment houses, but I'm guessing.
  - **"דמי ניהול העו״ש":** Does that mean the bank's regular price? Many banks give students a free current account, so this fee might not apply to me.
  - **"דמי ניהול חשבון":** The colour key at the bottom has this label. I can't tell if it means the current-account fee or the investing account's own fee.

**c. "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי משלמים עליו בכל מקרה. השורות של שאר הבנקים סופרות אותו."**

- **What it means:** I already pay for my current account at לאומי, so investing there adds no new current-account cost. That makes לאומי cheaper for me than it looked before.
- **What changed from A to B:**
  - **The לאומי row:** It went from 82,723 ₪ to 83,396 ₪ left, and its cost went from 5,283 ₪ to 4,541 ₪.
  - **Its place in the list:** It moved from 3rd to 2nd, just above וואן זירו (83,141 ₪ / 4,544 ₪).
  - **Its bar:** The red part at the start of לאומי's colour bar disappeared.
  - **Everything else:** פסגות is still 1st with the same numbers, and the other rows I can see look unchanged.
- **Unclear:**
  - The cost dropped by 742 ₪ but the amount left grew by only 673 ₪. I don't know why those don't match.
  - **"משלמים עליו בכל מקרה":** That's only true if I really pay a fee. If my student account is free, the "before" number was already wrong for me.

**d. "מה לא נכנס למחיר של קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" and "בקרן שנקנית בחו״ל יש גם פער בשער ההמרה. רוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות לא מגבילים אותו בתעריפון, ולכן הוא לא נכנס למחיר. תקרה לפער כתובה רק אצל אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד ומיטב טרייד."**

- **What it means:** If you buy a fund abroad, your shekels get changed into dollars, and the bank takes a cut on the rate. Most places don't publish a maximum for that cut, so the page couldn't include it. Only three places publish one.
- **Does it affect me?** I don't think so. My fund (ילין לפידות) is an Israeli fund, so I don't think I'd be buying abroad at all.
- **What I trip on:**
  - **"פער בשער ההמרה":** I only half get it. Elsewhere the page mentions "בין 0.48% ל־1.5%", which helped a little.
  - **"קרן שנקנית בחו״ל":** Does that include the iShares fund tagged "קרן חוץ בת״א"? It's a foreign fund, but it's bought in Tel Aviv. I can read it both ways.
  - **"תקרה":** For the three places with a cap, does their number include the cut or not? It doesn't say.
  - **"לא נכנס למחיר":** Which number is "the price"? The amount left or the cost?

**e. "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל יכולה לעלות יותר מהמספר שבשורה: ברוקר, גוף משמרת, בורסה או רשות בחו״ל גובים עמלות מהבנק או מבית ההשקעות, ורוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות מעבירים אותן ללקוח בלי תקרה. העמלות האלה לא נכנסות למחיר."**

- **What it means:** When you buy abroad, foreign companies charge the bank extra fees. The bank passes them on to you with no limit, so the real cost could be higher than what the row shows. In practice: if I buy abroad, the page's number is too optimistic.
- **What I trip on:**
  - **"ברוקר" and "גוף משמרת":** I don't know what these are.
  - **"רשות":** Some kind of government body? I'm not sure.
  - **"המספר שבשורה":** Again, I don't know which number in the row it means.
  - **Who it applies to:** It's unclear whether this matters for the list I'm looking at, which is for an Israeli fund. Further down, the iShares fund "bought abroad" drops to 57,957 ₪, so I'd just avoid that option.

## 3. Other things that confused or bothered me

- **The student prices aren't counted.** I'm a student, and many rows say "לא בחישוב – מחיר אחר ... לסטודנטים": לאומי, הבינלאומי, מסד, הפועלים, דיסקונט, מרכנתיל, מזרחי טפחות. The page also says "המחיר בכל שורה הוא של לקוח רגיל", so the ranking may be wrong for me. I wanted a "I'm a student" switch.
- **"מחיר להוראת קבע".** This appears under some banks but not under פסגות. The page says only "3 מתוך 15" publish a lower price for monthly buying. So is פסגות's number for my 500 ₪ a month at full price? Not clear.
- **The colour bars.** The key explaining them is far below the list, so on my phone I couldn't tell what each colour was while looking at the rows.
- **Unexplained words:** "4D", "4A", "מחקה", "נאמן", "נטרול מט״ח", "דמי משמרת", "כלל מס פתוח: מוצג ולא מדורג". I don't know what most of these mean.
- **"וואן זירו ... ONE, 49 ₪ לחודש".** Is that a cheaper or a more expensive plan? It doesn't say.
- **אינטראקטיב ישראל and Interactive Brokers** show "אין נתון" but still show an amount left for a different fund. That was confusing.
- **What I expected but didn't find:**
  - How to actually open the פסגות account.
  - Whether there's a minimum amount.
  - How easily I can take money out.
  - Whether my money is safe if the company has problems.
  - Whether פסגות is the same as Pepper ("ללקוחות פפר" appears under לאומי).
