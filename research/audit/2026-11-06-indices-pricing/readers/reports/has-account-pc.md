# has-account-pc

Who: You are 41, on a computer. You already have a current account and a securities account at your bank and pay their fees; you know words like "דמי ניהול" and "עמלת קנייה" from your statements, but you have never compared accounts or funds.

I'm assuming I'm a regular customer. I'm 41, so none of the "צעירים / חיילים / סטודנטים" discounts apply to me.

## 1. My decision

**Account: פסגות (Pesagot). Fund: "ילין לפידות מחקה S&P 500 (4D)", number 5139282.**

**How sure am I?** Fairly sure about the fund and less sure about the account.

**The fund:** It is top of the list at "86,492 ₪" left after 10 years. Its fees read "דמי ניהול: קבוע 0% · משתנה 0% · נאמן 0.02%". The runner-up, "אלטשולר שחם מחקה", is only "‎-43 ₪" behind over 10 years, so I don't care much which of the two I pick. The page itself says "ההפרשים בין 3 הזוגות הזולים ביותר קטנים מכדי לקבוע איזה מהם זול יותר".

**The account:** Pesagot only wins once my own bank is set correctly in the bank box. Using my friend's Leumi view (set B), Leumi comes second at "83,396 ₪". That's about 3,100 ₪ less than Pesagot over 10 years, or roughly 25 ₪ a month. That's real money, but I'd need to open a new account and move money there. I'd also want to click "קיבלתם מחיר אחר? הזינו אותו" or "הוספת חשבון בתנאים שקיבלתם" and enter what my bank actually charges me. I may already pay less than the published price.

**What I used:**
- the account list on the right ("כל החשבונות")
- the fund list on the left ("כל הקרנות על מדד S&P 500")
- the bank box
- the step chart lower down, which shows that switching account matters far more (+6,110 ₪) than switching fund (+2,344 ₪)
- the "סוג קרן אחר" table, which shows a fund bought abroad leaves "57,957 ₪", much worse

I mostly skimmed the currency-hedging section ("עם נטרול מט״ח או בלי"). It's about a different kind of fund than the one I'm choosing.

## 2. Line by line

**a. "הבנק שבו כבר יש לכם חשבון עו״ש" / "אף אחד מהם"**
- **What it's for:** You tell the page where you already have a current account, so that bank's price doesn't include fees you're paying anyway.
- **Would I change it?** Yes, to my own bank. If I leave "אף אחד מהם", my bank looks more expensive than it really is for me.
- **What's unclear:** Only the heading explains the box. Nothing near "אף אחד מהם" says the bank rows get more expensive while it's selected. If I hadn't read the line underneath, I'd have left it as is.

**b. "חשבון ניירות ערך בבנק צריך חשבון עו״ש באותו בנק, ולכן השורה של כל בנק סופרת גם דמי ניהול עו״ש שלו. בית השקעות לא דורש עו״ש אצלו."**
- **What it tells me:** Each bank's number includes the current-account fee, but the investment houses (Pesagot, Altshuler and so on) don't need one. So with the box on "none", the banks are partly priced on a fee that has nothing to do with investing.
- **What's unclear:** The colour legend says "דמי ניהול חשבון". I can't tell whether that means the current-account fee ("דמי ניהול עו״ש") or the securities account's own management fee. On my statements those are two different charges.
- **Also unclear:** It doesn't say how Pesagot gets my money without a bank account. I assume a transfer from my current account, but is there a fee for that?

**c. "השורה של לאומי לא סופרת את העו״ש, כי משלמים עליו בכל מקרה. השורות של שאר הבנקים סופרות אותו."**
- **What it tells me:** Since I pay the Leumi current-account fee anyway, the page drops it from Leumi's price. That's fair.
- **What changed between A and B:** Leumi moved from 3rd to 2nd, ahead of וואן זירו. Its number went from "82,723 ₪" to "83,396 ₪", and its 10-year cost from "5,283 ₪" to "4,541 ₪". The red part of its bar disappeared. Pesagot stayed first at "86,492 ₪", and no other row changed.
- **What's unclear:** The drop is only about 742 ₪ over 10 years, around 6 ₪ a month. That seems low for a current-account fee, which makes me wonder again whether the red part really is the current-account fee.
- **Also unclear:** The other banks still include the fee. Fair enough, but the line doesn't say so for the investment houses. Do Pesagot's 1,057 ₪ cover everything?

**d. "מה לא נכנס למחיר של קרן שנקנית בחו״ל" / "בקרן שנקנית בחו״ל יש גם פער בשער ההמרה. רוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות לא מגבילים אותו בתעריפון, ולכן הוא לא נכנס למחיר. תקרה כתובה רק אצל אי.בי.אי, אלטשולר טרייד ומיטב טרייד."**
- **What it tells me:** If I buy a fund on a foreign exchange, I also lose money converting shekels to dollars. Most places don't publish a maximum for this, so the page can't include it. The real cost of a foreign fund is therefore higher than shown.
- **Does it matter for me?** No. My fund is Israeli, so I don't think this applies to my choice.
- **What's unclear:**
  - "פער בשער ההמרה" – a gap compared to which rate?
  - "תקרה כתובה" – those three places have a written cap, but the page doesn't say how much it is, or whether the cap *is* included in their prices.
  - The heading was folded shut and sat above the bank box. It's easy to think it's about the whole account list and not only about foreign funds.

**e. "קרן שנקנית בחו״ל יכולה לעלות יותר מהמספר שבשורה: ברוקר, גוף משמרת, בורסה או רשות בחו״ל גובים עמלות מהבנק או מבית ההשקעות, ורוב הבנקים ובתי ההשקעות מעבירים אותן ללקוח בלי תקרה. העמלות האלה לא נכנסות למחיר."**
- **What it tells me:** There are also foreign middlemen who charge my bank, and my bank passes those charges on to me with no limit. So the number on a foreign fund is a minimum, not the real price.
- **Does it matter for me?** It's one more reason to stick with an Israeli fund.
- **What's unclear:**
  - I don't know what a "גוף משמרת" is. I guess it's some kind of custodian, but I'm not sure.
  - "בלי תקרה" sounds alarming, but there's no idea of a typical amount.
  - "מהמספר שבשורה" – which row? The fund row or the account row?

## 3. Other things that confused or bothered me

- **The coloured cost bars have no numbers.** The colour legend (orange עמלת קנייה ומכירה, yellow דמי משמרת, red דמי ניהול חשבון) sits far away in the top summary box.
- **Jargon on the fund tags is never explained:** "4D", "4A", "צוברת", "עסקת החלף", "כמה דרכים יחד", "מס גם על שינויי המטבע", "דיבידנד בתוך הקרן".
- **The fund appears to beat its index.** "מול המדד +0.37% · בשנה · שנה אחת" is based on one year only, and I don't understand how a fund can do better than the index it copies.
- **"0% management fee" sounds too good.** I'd want to know where the fund manager makes money.
- **"בחירת האתר" contradicts the page.** That label sits on the top fund, while the bottom of the page says "האתר לא ממליץ על מדד". They aren't strictly the same claim, but it feels mixed.
- **Things I expected and didn't find:**
  - how to open a Pesagot account, and whether it has a minimum
  - whether moving money from my current account costs anything
  - what happens to anything I already hold at my bank, and what moving it would cost
  - the actual fee percentages per account, without having to open the "התעריפון" link
- **It's not obvious the fund list depends on the account.** Above the fund list there's a "בחשבון פסגות" label (the funds are priced for a Pesagot account). If I switch to Leumi, do the fund rankings change?
- **The top summary numbers confused me.** "החשבון 1 ₪ יותר / הקרן 58 ₪ פחות / המס 14 ₪ יותר" is next to a total difference of "‎-43". It took me a while to see that it compares the top two pairs.
- **"אינטראקטיב ישראל" and "Interactive Brokers" just say "אין נתון".** That's fine, but they still sit in the ranked list.
