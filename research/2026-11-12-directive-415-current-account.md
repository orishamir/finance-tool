# Does directive 415 bar the monthly current-account minimum on an account kept only for investing? (2026-11-12)

Why: the indices page adds a bank's monthly current-account minimum to the cost of a securities account at that bank (F-102 in `docs/method.md`, D64 in `docs/product.md`). A reviewer read the Bank of Israel's proper-conduct directive 415 as possibly barring that fee. The rule is BRK-61 (rewritten today); what three banks' own documents say is BRK-66. Nothing in the method or in a fee record was changed: the founder decides.

## The answer

**Not settled by the text.**

- **What the directive bars.** Section 2(ג) bars "דמי ניהול חשבון עובר ושב", a current-account management fee, on a "חשבון תמורה". That is a current account kept only for the customer's other accounts, with no actions not directly connected to those accounts.
- **Why the text does not settle it.** The fee the site counts is the monthly minimum, "עמלת מינימום". In the Governor's fee tariff today that minimum is a note to the clerk-action price, not a management fee. The tariff's management-fee item for a current account is printed as deleted. The directive (last updated January 2008) says nothing either way about the minimum.
- **What else the text leaves open.** It does not define "other accounts" and does not name a securities deposit. It does not say whether money moved in to pay for purchases is an action "directly connected" to the other accounts.
- **What the text does give the customer.** An existing account counts as a "חשבון תמורה" only once the customer draws the bank's attention to it ("אם היפנה הלקוח את תשומת לב התאגיד הבנקאי לכך"). An account opened only for other accounts counts from the start.
- **Section 2(ה)** does not bar anything. It limits a fee on an inactive deposit account ("חשבון פיקדון ללא תנועה") to that account's credit balance. It does not define such an account and does not say it reaches a current account. An account that receives a monthly deposit is not inactive in the ordinary sense of the words, but the directive gives no definition. F-102 cites s.2(ה) for its cap (the minimum never above what is held). That is the method's choice; the directive does not state it for a current account.
- **What banks' own documents add.** Each speaks only for its own bank.
  - First International and Massad exempt a "חשבון צל/תמורה" from their action fees and minimum. They define it as an account opened only to repay a loan or to transfer to a savings plan, deposits, provident funds "וכד'". Securities are not named, so a securities deposit is neither in nor out by those words.
  - Massad itself calls its minimum line "השלמה למינימום דמי ניהול", a top-up to a minimum management fee.
  - Discount calls the current account that serves a securities deposit "חשבון התמורה של פיקדון ניירות הערך". That is its own contract's word, not the directive's definition.
  - No other bank's price list in the library says anything about such an account.
- **One clear bar exists, in another case.** After a move between banks through the online switching service, the old bank may keep a "חשבון תמורה" for what was left behind, securities that could not move included. It may charge no fees at all on that account (directive 448, s.3; SW-29). That covers someone who moved banks, not someone who opens an account to invest.

1. **Directive 415, "נהלים בנושא עמלות", version 6 (1/08)**, https://www.boi.org.il/media/n2dlnqyf/415.pdf (library f2301515371e486a; the live file fetched today is byte for byte the same, md5 a0522030…). Two pages; read in full.
   - s.1(א): "תאגיד בנקאי" – "לרבות חברת כרטיסי אשראי".
   - s.1(ב): banks charge fees by their own tariffs and the 2008 fee rules.
   - s.2, heading "אי גביית עמלות במקרים מסוימים". Subsections (א), (ב), (ו) and (ח) are each printed "בוטל".
   - s.2(ג), heading "עמלה בעד ניהול חשבון תמורה (1/02)": "תאגיד בנקאי המנהל עבור לקוח חשבון תמורה ולא נרשמו פעולות בחשבון התמורה שאינן קשורות קשר ישיר למטרת ניהול חשבונות אחרים, לא יחייב את הלקוח בדמי ניהול חשבון עובר ושב, בשל ניהול חשבון התמורה. לעניין סעיף קטן זה, "חשבון תמורה" - חשבון עובר ושב המנוהל מלכתחילה רק לצורך חשבונות אחרים של הלקוח וחשבון עובר ושב שבדיעבד מנוהל רק לצורך כאמור אם היפנה הלקוח את תשומת לב התאגיד הבנקאי לכך." (p.1)

   - s.2(ז)(1): a fee charged in advance for a set period, "כגון עמלת הקצאת אשראי רבעונית, דמי ניהול רבעוניים, עמלת ערבות, עמלת דמי שכירת כספת לשנה", is refunded "באופן יחסי על בסיס יומי" when the service stops early. s.2(ז)(2): reasonable minimum fees payable despite an early stop "אם ניתן לכך אישור המפקח מראש". (p.2)
   - ss.3 ("עמלת דמי ניהול") and 4: "בוטל". (p.2)
   - Updates table: versions 1 (27/4/93) to 6 (28/1/08, circular 2224). Nothing later. (p.2)
