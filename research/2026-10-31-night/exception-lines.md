# "מתי זה לא חל": which rules need the line (2026-10-31)

Issue: https://github.com/orishamir/finance-tool/issues/78. Scope, by the founder's decision: only where an exception could catch an ordinary saver (employee, freelancer, someone drawing money early).

Method: a first pass by the lead, reading the first 210 characters of the conditions line of each of the 205 general rules with a figure (11 say none). The writer reads each line in full, checks whether the rule's Hebrew already says it (TXS-01 and TXH-01 do, in the running text) and may add or drop a rule. Rules about one named company (35) and unsettled rules (6) are left out: the first are that company's terms, the second say their own status.
