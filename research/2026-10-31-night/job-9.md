## New rules, in the product order given

All are `Scope: general`. Each needs a site record in `data/rules/`, with Hebrew review, which I did not write.

### Index funds

- **ETF-70.** The Securities Authority's proposal of 15.10.2025: a tracking fund on a foreign index that uses derivatives and is not currency-neutralised would hold liquid assets in the index's currency, at least 10% of its exposure. Phased at 5%, 7.5% and 10% over three, six and nine months, with 30 days to restore. For a saver: such a fund would hold more idle cash, and the Authority itself notes possible harm to the return. A proposal; whether it became final is not checked.

### Money-market funds and funds generally

- **MF-57.** Since the amendment of 7.11.2021 the "(!)" exposure sign also counts bonds of foreign companies with no tie to Israel, unless rated ilAA or above. A proposal of 4.8.2025 is pending as far as read. For a saver: a fund with no "(!)" can still hold high-grade foreign credit, and the sign does not mean what it meant before 2021. The directive's own text is unread; the sources are the Authority's change table and its draft.

### Securities account (bank-held)

The Bank of Israel's directives bind banks. Non-bank exchange members' equivalents were not read, so these do not speak for them.

- **BRK-53** (directive 414). Half-yearly notice of your own securities fees against the weighted average charged to similar customers; a table on the website within 30 days; nine bands. For a saver: a bank tells you, in writing, how your rate compares, which is the opening for bargaining.
- **BRK-54** (directive 460). A return calculator and a quarterly statement, with time-weighted return before tax, before and after fees. For a saver: an official after-fees figure to compare with the alternatives.
- **BRK-55** (directive 415). No current-account fee on an account kept only to serve a securities account ("חשבון תמורה"); prepaid fees come back pro rata; a dormant-deposit fee is capped at the balance; no fee for basic information. For a saver: fewer reasons to pay for an account only used for investing.
- **BRK-56** (directive 437). Fees and tax on foreign securities can be paid in shekels unless you chose otherwise. For a saver: no forced conversion or foreign-currency debit for them.
- **BRK-57** (Banking Ordinance s.13א, directive 434). The survivor clause lets the other holder keep operating, moves no ownership and is not a will. For a saver: a joint account is not an inheritance plan.

### Study fund and provident fund for investing (shared file)

- **PF-42** (circular 2017-9-8). A spouse, parent or child can withdraw a deceased member's balance up to NIS 8,000 without a succession order after three years, with an indemnity and a registrar check. The figure is indexed each 1 January; today's figure is not checked. For a saver: a small balance need not cost a succession order.
- **PF-43** (circular 2022-9-1 with the consolidated service circular). A human answer within five minutes (10% of calls may exceed it), a record of a call within five business days, an answer in the same channel, debt notice by post and digitally, a charter on the website; a direct route at age 67 and a hold on withdrawal if exploitation is suspected. For a saver: named service levels to hold a manager to.
- **PF-44** (circular 2024-9-4). When employer deposits stop, the manager tells the employee: 30 days from the employer's report, or 90 days from the 15th of the due month when no report comes. Free monthly updates on money received, and an online table against the employer's report. For a saver: a study fund member learns of missing deposits, and can check them.

### Bank deposit

- **BANK-39** (directive 407). No investing or saving on silence; an increase in a plan needs a prior signed agreement. For a saver: money does not move on your silence.
- **BANK-40** (directive 421). A renewing deposit keeps the same margin over the base rate. For a saver: renewal cannot quietly worsen the margin.
- **BANK-41** (directive 440). The daily-redeemable deposit ("פיקדון פדיום") compounds daily and states its adjusted annual rate. For a saver: its rate can be compared with other deposits.
- **BANK-42** (directive 438). A bank may end a savings plan to set off a debt only on five conditions. For a saver: a small overdraft does not simply break a plan.
- **BANK-43** (directives 443, 436). When a deposit counts as dormant, how the bank must look for you, the Administrator General, and the search platform. For a saver: how to find forgotten money.
- **BANK-44** (directive 420). Notices in the app replace post only if you asked, and you can go back. For a saver: a change notice should not vanish into an app you never asked for.
- **BANK-45** (directive 432). A free yearly report within seven business days, including the value on early withdrawal; the balance returned in 14 business days free; closing within 5; a bank's own closing needs reasons and 30 days. For a saver: leaving is free and on a clock.
