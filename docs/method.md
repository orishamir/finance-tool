# Method: how every number on the site is computed

Written 2026-10-22 by the financial modeler from `docs/product.md` (D1 to D13), `docs/roadmap.md`, and the `il-finance` rule base (`.claude/skills/il-finance/references/`, 803 rules, checked 2026-10-22). Every rule is cited by its ID; no rule is stated from memory. Where the rule base is open, the formula is marked **blocked** and the engine must not compute it (D10).

Status: **in force.** On 2026-10-23 the founder asked Claude to settle every open item ("do whatever you think fits"); Claude did, and the founder has not reviewed this document line by line. F-83 and gaps G-28 to G-30 added 2026-10-23 for product D17 ("no blind spots"). Basis decisions B1 to B3 were chosen by the founder on 2026-10-22 (section 1). The golden scenarios in `tests/golden/scenarios.md` exercise every formula below; the expected results are computed by `tests/golden/expected_calc.py` and worked in `tests/golden/workings.md`.

How to read it:

- `A-nn` is an assumption: a visible input with a default and a source. Every assumption is listed in section 2. No formula uses a constant that is not an `A-nn`, a rule figure read from `data/rules/`, or a site parameter read from `data/parameters/` (`P-nn`).
- `F-nn` is a formula. The engine implements formulas by number; the finance-reviewer checks the engine against them.
- `G-nn` is a gap: something the method cannot compute yet, with the open question that blocks it (section 12).
- Rule IDs (`TXS-09`, `PF-02`, ...) point into `il-finance`. The strength shown is the rule's strength as recorded there; `institution` strength is flagged, not treated as primary (coverage.md F1).

## 1. The comparison basis

Chosen by the founder on 2026-10-22 after the alternatives below were put to him.

**B1. Liquidation value at the horizon, at equal net outlay and equal gross return, in real terms.** Every eligible wrapper receives the same shekels out of the person's pocket on the same dates (the amount now and the monthly contribution, section 3). The exposure earns the same gross return in every wrapper (D9 wrapper mode). The engine applies each wrapper's deposit-side tax effect, its fees, the drag inside the instrument, tax during the holding period and tax at exit, as if everything were withdrawn at the end of the horizon. The result is the real value of that withdrawal (F-40). Ranking and ties use that one number.

Alternatives considered and rejected: ranking on the annualised net real return (IRR), which handles irregular contributions but hides shekel differences and makes the tie threshold hard to state; and a two-figure basis (value if held, value if withdrawn), more honest for lock-ups but with two ranking keys. The chosen basis understates wrappers whose benefit is deferral past the horizon; the output says so wherever a wrapper's lawful exit is later than the horizon (B3).

**B2. Only the person's own money ranks.** Employer-funded deposits (hishtalmut, pension, provident fund for annuity) are computed by the same engine and shown as a separate line, "only available in this wrapper", with their value at the horizon (F-52). They do not enter the ranking, because no other wrapper can receive them.

**B3. Wrappers still locked at the horizon are listed, not ranked.** A wrapper whose lawful exit (section 8) is after the horizon appears with "locked until <date>" and its value at its own first exit date, computed by the same engine with the horizon moved to that date. Contributions stop at the goal's horizon; the balance grows until that date (product D16). It enters the ranking only if the person opts into early withdrawal, in which case the early-withdrawal tax rule applies (F-47, F-48). The same treatment applies to a wrapper whose exit tax is blocked by an open rule (G-list): it is listed with its pre-tax value and the reason, and it is not ranked.

### 2.4 From a wrapper record and P-05 to a run's inputs

Added 2026-10-23. `runInputs` in the engine (`packages/engine/src/inputs.ts`) builds the inputs of one run from three things: a wrapper record (`docs/data-schema.md` §3.3), the parameters in `data/parameters/`, and the instrument terms that neither of them holds. It is the step behind F-67 and behind every comparison that starts from wrapper records (F-83). Values pass through unchanged. The only conversions are percent to a fraction (`5` becomes `0.05`) and years to months. Anything missing or unexpected, or that the engine cannot compute yet, stops the build with the field named. It never falls back to a default or drops a field. A run the method cannot evaluate yet stops with its gap, as F-83 "not evaluated": monthly deposits into a renewable money-market fund stop with G-30.

**Wrapper or security record.** It is checked against its schema (`packages/schema`: `wrapperRecord`, or `securityRecord` when it carries `held_in`, D6 as amended 2026-10-26); a field the schema does not name is an error at every level. A security runs its own ledger (makam F-24, a government bond F-25, a money-market fund F-08 with F-23) inside the account it is held in: the account's tariff lines for its class are its instrument terms, and the member's kind (`memberKind`) goes with them (F-14).

## 3. Time, money and the deflator

**F-01 Time grid.** Months `m = 0, 1, …, T`, `T = 12 × horizon in years`. Dates are calendar months; the tax year is the calendar year (TXG-01). Age-based rules use the person's age on the first day of the month.

**F-02 Monthly inflation.** `π_m = (1 + π)^(1/12) − 1` (A-02).

**F-03 CPI index path.** `I_0 = 1`; `I_m = I_{m−1} × (1 + π_m)`. Where a sourced CPI history exists (`data/series/cbs-cpi`, the CBS general index), past months use actual index values rescaled so that `I_0 = 1`. Tax rules use "the index last published before the day in question" (TXG-12, TXG-14); with a monthly grid the engine uses `I_m` for an event in month `m` and records this one-to-two-month simplification as a convention of the method, not a reading of the rule.

**F-04 Exchange-rate path.** `X_0 = 1`; `X_m = X_{m−1} × (1 + f)^(1/12)` (A-03). For an instrument denominated in or linked to a foreign currency held by an individual, `X` replaces `I` as the tax index (TXS-04, TXF-03; for a TASE foreign fund only if the 90% test holds, TXF-04; for an Israeli foreign-investment fund, its principal currency, TXS-14). **A hedged fund (2026-10-29).** A shekel fund on a foreign exposure whose record says it neutralises the exposure's currency (`hedged: true`) is not moved by A-03: its return is A-01 alone. It hedges that one currency against the shekel; an index priced in several currencies (`other_currencies` on the exposure, ETF-48) keeps the others, against the exposure's currency, inside A-01.

**F-84 Exchange rate (cross rate) (D26, added 2026-10-25).** NIS per one unit of currency `c`: `S_c = E_ILS / E_c`, where `E_x` is the ECB's euro reference rate for `x` (units of `x` per one euro) on the same reference day; `S_EUR = E_ILS`, `S_ILS = 1`. Source: `data/series/ecb-fx.json` (ECB eurofxref-daily, terms read 2026-10-25: free use with the ECB cited; the site states that it computes a cross rate from them), state "computed" with sourced inputs. It is a reference rate for the ECB's reference day, not a rate anyone trades at, and it is stale after the series' `stale_after_days` (D3). It is the start rate `S_0` of every foreign-currency run (F-63, F-65 and the index page, D28); the person never types one. The rate's future path stays F-04 with A-03 (default 0). A currency the ECB does not quote cannot be priced, and the run says so. Example: 2026-10-23, `S_USD = 3.4939 / 1.1193 = 3.1215`, `S_JPY = 3.4939 / 175.51 = 0.01991` (shown as 1.99 per 100 yen).

**F-91 A way's cost by who takes it (added 2026-10-28, founder: the indices page's cost bars did not add up to the gap in what is left; option A of the mockup).** Every figure is a value at the horizon in today's shekels (F-06 at `m = T`). **F-92 A fund's gap to its index, from its reports (added 2026-10-29, founder: "the tracking difference should be a field either way").** A fund record's `tracking_record` holds, for each full calendar year its report prints, the fund's return `u_y` and its tracking asset's `t_y` in percent (a part year is left out). **F-05 Contributions.** `C_0` = amount now; for `m ≥ 1`, `C_m = C × I_m` if A-04 is "constant real", else `C_m = C`. A wrapper's deposit ceiling (section 8) caps `C_m`; the excess is reported and routed to the next goal in the person's order (D12), never silently dropped.

## 4. The ledger (one run per wrapper, provider and instrument)

The ledger is the single engine behind 3.1, 3.2, 3.4, 3.5 and 3.6. State at the end of month `m`:

- `B_m` market value of the holding (nominal shekels);
- `P_m` nominal tax basis, the sum of the amounts invested in units still held;
- `K_m` indexed-basis accumulator, `Σ basis_i / J_i` over lots `i` still held, where `J` is the tax index of the instrument (`I` or `X`, F-03, F-04). The adjusted basis at any later month `t` is `K_m × J_t` (TXG-12: adjusted cost = cost × index at sale ÷ index at purchase). Lots leave in first-in-first-out order (TXS-06);
- `Φ_m` cumulative fees paid, by fee type; `Θ_m` cumulative tax paid during the holding period;
- `E_m` cumulative employer money (B2) tracked as its own sub-ledger.

**F-08 Month step.** For `m = 1 … T`:

1. Deposit-side tax effect: gross deposit `D_m` from the person's net outlay `C_m` (F-30 to F-32; `D_m = C_m` where no benefit applies).
2. Entry costs: the cash left after the deposit fee and the FX fee, `A_m = D_m × (1 − φ_dep) − fee_fx(D_m)`, pays for the trade and its commission: `A_m = V_m + fee_trade(V_m)`, where the commission is a percentage of the trade value `V_m` (F-12, BRK-16). An exchange purchase is at the ask, so the NAV value bought is `N_m = V_m / (1 + A-08)` (F-13). Lot `m` opens with `basis = V_m`, the price paid including the half-spread (TXG-12), and `J = J_m`. Commissions and FX fees are not added to the basis (G-20). Where there is no trade (provident family), `N_m = V_m = A_m`.
3. Growth: `B'_m = (B_{m−1} + N_m) × (1 + g_m − δ_m)`, where `g_m` is from A-01 and `δ_m = (d + A-07 + A-09 + A-11) / 12` (F-10).
4. Distributions and tax during: F-20 to F-26 for the instrument type; net amounts reinvested open a new lot.
5. Holding costs: `B_m = B'_m − φ_bal/12 × B'_m − fee_fixed_m − fee_custody_m` (F-14 to F-16). On a securities holding, a custody or account fee is paid by removing units at NAV, oldest lot first. The units removed take their share of `P` and `K` with them, so earlier years' fees do not reduce the taxable gain (TXS-06). The removal is not treated as a taxable sale; this is a simplification of the method.
6. Fee schedule changes: F-16 updates `φ_bal`, `φ_dep` when a discount period ends.

**F-09 Exit at T (liquidation, B1).** The holding is sold at the bid: `G_T = B_T × (1 − A-08)`. The commission and FX fee are paid out of it: `R_T = G_T − fee_exit(G_T) − fee_fx(G_T − fee_exit(G_T))`. For tax the consideration is `G_T`; sale costs are not deducted (G-20). `gain = G_T − P_T − E_y`, where `E_y` is the management and custody fees paid in the exit tax year, deducted once (TXS-06). Then `infl = K_T × J_T − P_T`, `real = gain − infl`, tax at exit `τ_exit` from section 7, and `V_T = R_T − τ_exit`, shown as `V_T / I_T` (F-06). For securities, `real` is the sum over lots of `units_i × price_T × (1 − A-08) − basis_i × J_T / J_i`, less `E_y`. **A falling index (2026-10-26).** That holds for a lot whose index rose (`J_T ≥ J_i`). For a lot whose index fell (CPI deflation, or the currency of a foreign-currency security weakening against the shekel), the inflationary amount is zero, not negative, so the lot's real gain is its nominal gain, `units_i × price_T × (1 − A-08) − basis_i`; and when that is a loss, the part of it equal to the fall, `basis_i × (1 − J_T / J_i)`, is not allowable, so the lot contributes `min(0, units_i × price_T × (1 − A-08) − basis_i × J_T / J_i)` (TXS-02 and TXF-03: circular 10/2025, appendix, examples 3 and 4, and the Supreme Court in ע"א 3555/15 מוזס, para 26: a negative inflationary amount, "בין אם מקורו בשינוי ערך מדד או בשינוי שער חליפין", is not part of the tax base). So the taxable gain never exceeds the nominal gain. The same per-lot rule applies to a CPI-linked bond's sale (F-25, F-40). The engine: `indexedCost` in `ledgers/lots.ts`, used by the securities and bond ledgers; `detail.fallDisallowed` is the part of a loss the fall made not allowable. Rising-index runs are unchanged.

D3 decided by the founder on 2026-10-22 after the independent check (`tests/golden/workings.md`). The fee and basis wording of F-08 steps 2 and 5, F-09, F-12 and G-20 was corrected to the rule base. The earlier text had three effects: every year's custody fees reduced the gain, with the exit year counted twice; sale costs were deducted; the half-spread was left out of the basis.

**F-10 Gross growth less drag.** `δ` collects what the instrument itself costs regardless of wrapper: the fund's fixed management fee, trustee fee and variable fee (MF-16, MF-20, ETF-13, ETF-14), tracking difference beyond fees (A-07), and fund-level foreign withholding (A-11). For a renewable money-market fund the expected yield is already net of fund fees (MF-37), so `δ = 0` and `g` is the expected yield. For a daily money-market fund `g` is the BoI policy rate (A-20, the ISA's description of what these funds track, MF-34, labelled "proxy") and `δ` the fund's fees.

### 4.1 Fee types and their bases

Every fee is a record in `data/` (see `docs/data-schema.md`) with a base, a rate, a minimum, a maximum, a period and the security types it applies to. The engine evaluates fees only by these formulas.

**F-11 Deposit fee** (provident family): `φ_dep × D_m`, capped by the regulated maximum for the wrapper: 4% for provident funds and kupat gemel lehashkaa (PF-02, PF-03, KGL-14), 6% for a new comprehensive pension fund (PF-04, PEN-21), 4% for a new general pension fund (PF-05), none for hishtalmut and IRA (PF-06, KH-22).

**F-12 Trade commission** (brokerage): per order, `max(min, min(pct × amount, max))`, where `amount` is the trade value (on a buy, cash = trade value + commission), from the tariff line for the security type (TASE securities, makam, foreign securities; BRK-16), one order per contribution and one at exit (A-28). Money-market fund units at a bank under a distribution agreement: 0 (MF-26). Makam held to redemption: one purchase order, no sale order. **Per share, dollar bounds and a cap (2026-10-25, index page).** A foreign broker prints its commission per share (`usd_per_share`: Interactive Israel 0.01 a share, Interactive Brokers 0.005 a share) with a minimum and a cap: `fee = min(cap × amount, max(min, min(pct × amount + c × n, max)))`, where `n = amount / S_c / P` is the share count of the order, `P` the unit price in the fund's currency (the fund record's `share_price`, moved with the NAV and the exchange rate), `S_c` the shekels per unit of that currency at the trade, and `c` the per-share fee in shekels (the printed dollars times the rate of the dollar). The cap is a share of the trade value (`cap`, unit `pct_of_trade`) and wins over the minimum, as at Interactive Brokers (its note: the cap applies when it is below the minimum). A minimum or maximum printed in dollars (`usd_per_trade`; Altshuler Trade's `usd`) or pounds (`gbp_per_trade`) is converted to shekels at the run's rate for that currency (the run input `fx`, F-84); the converted bound is fixed for the run. Example: Interactive Israel, an order of 400 NIS with the dollar at 3.70: 0.108 USD of units at 500 USD a unit is 0.216 of a unit, the per-share fee 0.008 NIS, the minimum 2.5 USD = 9.25 NIS, the cap 2% × 400 = 8 NIS, so the order pays 8 NIS. Interactive's tariff rounds fractional US shares up to a whole share for the fee; the engine does not (a difference only below the minimum). `test/foreign-broker.test.ts`. **Standing orders (2026-10-31, D41).** A monthly purchase is priced on the tariff's standing-order line for that security type and channel (band `הוראת קבע`), where the tariff prints one (Leumi, Massad, First International); a lump sum, a reinvested distribution and a sale pay the regular line, and a tariff that prints no standing-order price pays the regular line on every order. In the materiality run the standing-order rate moves in proportion to the regular one.

**F-13 Exchange costs.** Half-spread `A-08 × amount` on each exchange buy and sell (ETF-06), as part of the price: it is in the basis on a buy and lowers the consideration on a sale (F-08, F-09); creation or redemption fee 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3% by category when the order goes to the manager rather than the exchange (ETF-05); FX conversion `fee_fx = pct_fx × amount` (plus any spread the tariff states, BRK-23, BRK-24) on each contribution and at exit when the instrument currency is not the shekel. **FX bounds (2026-10-24).** An FX line carries a minimum and a maximum like the other commissions: `fee_fx = max(min, min(pct_fx × amount, max))` per conversion, each order its own conversion (A-28), and a bound set in USD (Hapoalim's line is 0.19% with a minimum of $6.70 and a maximum of $2,700, `data/fees/hapoalim@2026-10-23.json`) applies to the converted amount in the trade currency, so the NIS bound is the USD bound times the rate of the trade; a bare percentage stays a percentage with no bounds. With 100 NIS an order, 0.19% is 0.19 NIS and the minimum $6.70 × 3.70 = 24.79 NIS is paid instead (review of R3 and R4, `test/review-r34.test.ts`).

**F-14 Custody fee** (brokerage): per period from the tariff, `max(min, min(pct × B, max))` on the holdings it applies to; never on makam or money-market funds at a bank (BRK-17, BANK-18); at non-bank members per their tariff: no rule found limits a non-bank member's custody fee on makam or money-market units, since the bank ban binds banks only and the distribution-fee regulations leave out an investment marketer, which most non-bank members are (BRK-17, BRK-52, MF-26; changed 2026-10-26: BRK OQ11 closed, the line said it was open), so the engine uses the tariff as published. A member that is not an investment marketer and holds a distribution agreement for the fund falls under reg. 2(ד) of those regulations, which bars any fee on its money-market units (MF-26); which members hold one is not known (BRK-52). **Makam (2026-10-26, D6).** Makam is a security held in the securities account, so the account's member decides its custody: the makam run takes the tariff's custody line for the `makam` class and `memberKind`, charges nothing at a bank (BRK-17, BANK-18) and the line as published at a non-bank member (BRK-52), paid by selling an equal slice of each series held at the month's price (the slice's own gain is not taxed apart, as the fund and bond ledgers pay theirs by removing units), with the exit year's custody taken off a sale gain once (TXS-06). Until that date the makam run read no custody line at all, which was right only at a bank. **Bank or not (2026-10-26).** The securities run takes `memberKind` (`bank`, the default, or `non-bank`): a money-market unit pays no custody at a bank whatever the tariff says, and pays a non-bank member's custody line as published. The provider workbench (F-63) sets it from the snapshot's provider class, and so do the fund-variant runs (F-65) and the switching-cost runs (F-66) that price a provider's tariff: only `bank` is a bank, a `broker` or `foreign_broker` is `non-bank` (`memberKindOf`, review 2026-10-26). The page shows that line with a note: no rule limiting it was found; a member with a distribution agreement for that fund may charge nothing on it (reg. 2(ד), MF-26); and the regulators' joint report of June 2026 (p.11) reports a blanket waiver at non-bank members of custody fees proper ("דמי משמרת") only, while a handling fee on the securities deposit ("דמי טיפול בפיקדון") is usually a fixed sum, often refunded or not charged. Two collected non-bank lines (Excellence's and Altshuler Trade's, "דמי ניהול/טיפול") are of the handling kind. Both are practice, not a term (BRK-52, read in a copy of the report; changed 2026-10-26, finance review: the note said the report waives the custody line whatever its kind).

**F-15 Balance fee** (provident family and IRA): `φ_bal / 12 × B'_m`, capped: 1.05% provident and kupat gemel lehashkaa (PF-02), 0.5% comprehensive pension (PF-04), 2% hishtalmut and IRA (PF-06, KH-22), 0.3% lost-contact accounts (PF-08). Direct investment expenses (A-18) are added to `φ_bal` as a separate line (PF-10).

**F-16 Fee change over time.** A discounted fee reverts to the list fee at the end of the discount period (A-19), no earlier than 5 years after it started unless a PF-14 trigger occurs. The engine also applies a fee rise six months after contributions stop, where the provider record says so (PF-14 case 1). Mutual-fund fee increases take effect on 1 January only (MF-17, ETF-16): a known increase is applied at the next 1 January.

**F-17 Fixed fees.** Monthly account or system fees at non-bank members (BRK-24) are subtracted as `fee_fixed_m`. **Securities in the account (2026-10-26, D6).** An account fee is the account's, on whatever it holds: the makam and bond runs charge it too (`tariff.monthlyFee`), paid like their custody, by selling a slice of what is held (makam) or units oldest first (a bond); the exit year's fixed fees come off the sale gain once, as custody does (TXS-06).

**F-18 Fee for interest and dividend handling** (some non-bank tariffs, BRK-24): per distribution event, from the tariff.

## 5. Tax during the holding period, by instrument

All rates are read from `data/rules/` by rule ID; the figures below are quoted for readability and are the 2026 figures recorded in the rule base.

**F-20 Distributions from a fund or share.**
- Israeli exempt fund (including money-market funds, tracking funds and ETFs): 25% of the distribution, withheld (TXS-10). Net reinvested as a new lot.
- Foreign-domiciled fund or TASE foreign fund held through an Israeli bank or member: Israel 25% (TXF-08, withheld by the intermediary, TXF-10, TXF-05). Foreign tax withheld on the distribution is credited up to the Israeli tax on it (TXF-17, TXF-18): `τ_IL = max(0, 0.25 × dist − credit)`, `credit = min(w_foreign × dist, 0.25 × dist)`. For a US fund held with a W-8BEN, `w_foreign = 25%` (TXF-12, TXF-13), so `τ_IL = 0` and the total is 25%. Without a W-8BEN, `w_foreign = 30%`; the excess 5% is not credited (TXF-18) and is lost. For an Irish fund's own distributions, Ireland deducts nothing from an Israeli resident: no exit tax on units held in a recognised clearing system, such as an ETF's, or paid to a holder who gave a non-resident declaration (TXF-28); the engine uses 0 and cites TXF-28 (changed 2026-10-26, open-questions audit: until then the 0 was flagged as G-05).
- At a foreign broker nothing is withheld in Israel; the same tax is paid by advance by 30 April of the next year (TXF-21). The engine charges it in the month of the distribution (timing difference ignored, stated in the output).
- Distributions from a TASE foreign fund are stated by the Tax Authority not to be offsettable against securities losses (TXF-07); losses are not modelled (G-07), so this has no effect.
- Taxable fund (TXS-11): the holder pays nothing on distributions or sale; the fund pays at individual rates on its income. The engine models only a shekel money-market taxable fund, as 15% of nominal interest at fund level (TXS-12 condition (b), derived; circular 7/2003 s.2.1.1 states the principle that a taxable fund bears an individual's rates, TXS-11), as an extra `δ`; any other taxable fund is blocked (G-08).

**F-21 Bank deposit, laddered.** Each contribution opens its own deposit of term `n` (A-15) at the rate in force (A-21 or A-26; a 447 rate is nominal annual before tax, BANK-13). At maturity: `interest = principal × ((1 + r)^(n/12) − 1)`. Tax withheld by the bank (TXI-07, TXI-08):
- unlinked or partly linked: `0.15 × interest` (TXI-03, TXI-04). Prime-linked deposits fall here: the statutory index is the CPI or an exchange rate, and the Tax Authority's guide counts a deposit as unlinked unless fully index-linked (TXI-04; changed 2026-10-26: TXI OQ2 closed, so the prime case is no longer labelled "reading");
- fully CPI-linked: linkage exempt, `0.25 × real interest` where the quoted rate is the real rate and linkage is added at `I` (TXI-05, TXI-02, BANK-26; the quoting as the banks read show it, BANK-36).
The net amount, principal plus after-tax interest, rolls into a new deposit at the then rate; the tax basis resets. The low-income deduction (TXI-20) and the 1948-cohort reliefs (TXI-21, TXI-22) apply only on inputs that meet their conditions; the engine applies TXI-20 as `interest_taxable = max(0, interest − min(deduction, interest))` when the couple's income is under the favoured ceiling, from `data/rules/`. Breaking a deposit before maturity is not modelled (G-16).

**F-22 Savings plan.** A savings plan is the F-21 ladder with the plan's own rate, term and exit points; no rule distinguishes it for tax (TXI-07, BANK-28).

**F-23 Money-market fund.** Exempt fund (TXS-12, TXS-24): no tax during; distributions per F-20; exit per F-40. Daily fund: `g = A-20`, `δ = fund fees` (F-10). Renewable fund: `g = A-24`, `δ = 0`, liquidity only on fixed dates (MF-35); if the horizon ends between fixed dates the exit is at the last fixed date before it. Two taxable money-market funds exist (TXS-24); for them F-20's taxable-fund case applies.

**F-24 Makam, rolled yearly.** Price per 100 face value `p = 100 / (1 + y)^(N/365)` with `y = A-22` and `N` days to redemption (GOV-06). At redemption the discount `100 − p` is interest taxed at 15% and withheld at 15% (TXI-01, TXI-11, TXI-09). The net proceeds buy the next series (12 series, one issued a month, GOV-22, GOV-23). A sale before redemption is a capital gain at up to 15% with the whole gain treated as real (TXI-12); the engine sells at the price implied by the unchanged yield when the horizon falls inside a series. The 2025 guide's "20% at realisation" line conflicts with the statute's 15% (TXI OQ3); the engine uses 15% (primary, s.125C(c)) and flags the conflict (G-09).

**F-25 Government bond.** Coupon `c` on face value, once a year for CPI-linked series (GOV-11); for fixed-rate series the frequency comes from the series data; GOV-13 now sources twelve-month interest periods for the type auctioned today, "אג"ח ממשלתית" (ק"ת 6457 regs. 1 and 5(ג); changed 2026-10-26, requests 6); unlinked (fixed or floating): `0.15 × coupon` withheld (TXI-15, TXI-09); CPI-linked: principal and coupon scale with `I` (GOV-11, GOV-12, no floor), linkage exempt, `0.25 × real coupon` (TXI-14). If the CPI falls, the reduced coupon is still taxed in full (TXI-25: interest and linkage are separate incomes, the Supreme Court); the negative linkage is not offset, as the district court held, and the Supreme Court left open whether any route allows the offset (TXI-25, para 23; wording corrected 2026-10-26). The bond's sale at the horizon follows F-09's falling-index rule (TXS-02). Net coupons reinvested in the same bond as a new lot. At the horizon the bond is sold at par plus A-27: unlinked gain taxed at up to 15% on the whole gain (TXI-12); linked gain at up to 25% real (TXI-14). Accrued interest bought between coupon dates is not modelled (G-10). **(2026-10-25) Price after a change in rates.** For the guide page's "if rates move" drawing, `bondPrice(c, y, years, perYear)` gives the price per 100 face of a bond that pays `c` a year in `perYear` equal coupons and redeems at 100 after `years`: `P = Σ_{k=1..n} (100 c / f) / (1 + i)^k + 100 / (1 + i)^n`, `f = perYear`, `i = (1 + y)^(1/f) − 1`, `n = years × f`; at `y = c` the price is 100. The coupon is the site's assumption (`cash_rate`, D20) and `perYear` comes from the series rule (GOV-11: one coupon a year). For a CPI-linked series the index multiplies price and face alike, so the percentage change is the same. Before fees and tax; the visual sets it against a makam of the ledger's term priced by F-24 at the same yield.

**F-26 Current-account credit balance.** Monthly interest `B × g` at A-25, taxed 15% at crediting (TXI-08 reaches current-account interest). **(2026-10-25)** The bank pays the rate on the balance up to a ceiling (`balanceCeiling`, the deposit-terms record's `amount_range.max`, BANK-09; Leumi 10,000 NIS): interest is `min(B, ceiling) × g`, taxed 15% as before. The ceiling belongs to the published rate: a rate the person enters applies to the whole balance. Only the current account reads it; a deposit's amount range is a condition of the offer, not a cap on the ladder. **(2026-10-25, the current-account page)** The page's table gives, per bank, the most it pays in a year on the person's balance `B`: `r × min(B, ceiling) × (1 − t)`, where `t` is the withholding on the interest (TXI-08, TXI-03: 15%). The balance is flat and the interest is not compounded; the figure is the ceiling of what the arrangement can pay, and only to a customer who meets the bank's conditions. `currentAccountYearlyInterest`, `packages/engine/test/account-interest.test.ts`.

**F-27 Provident family (kupat gemel lehashkaa, hishtalmut, pension, provident fund for annuity, child savings, IRA).** No tax during (TXK-03, TXH-18, TXP-13, TXK-17). Foreign tax withheld inside the fund is a cost inside the fund (TXK-18), carried in A-18 or A-11. Track switches and transfers between funds of the same kind are not tax events (TXK-04, TXH-17, TXH-19, PF-23).

**F-28 National Insurance and health contributions.** National Insurance is zero on everything the engine models: interest and dividends at special rates are exempt (TXG-27), and capital gains are outside the base (TXG-28). The deemed interest on a kupat gemel lehashkaa lump sum is income taxable under s.125C(b) or (c), which the National Insurance Law leaves out of the base (s.350(a)(6)), so neither contribution applies (TXK-27; changed 2026-10-26: until then it was open, TXK OQ9, and flagged G-11). A recognized annuity from kupat gemel lehashkaa money before retirement age is still open (TXK OQ9), and the engine does not model it. Health contributions (מס בריאות) are charged on the same income as National Insurance (TXG-34), so they are zero on the same items. Under product D14 (2026-10-22) the site does not show them. Interest taxed at marginal rates under s.125C(d) is outside both exemptions; an example is study-fund gains withdrawn early (F-47). The engine does not model NI or health on it (G-27).

## 6. Deposit-side tax effects (equal net outlay, B1)

The person's outlay `C_m` is net money. Where a wrapper returns part of a deposit as tax relief, the engine finds the gross deposit `D_m` whose net cost is `C_m`:

**F-30 Equal net outlay.** `D_m = C_m + benefit(D_m)`, solved exactly (benefit is piecewise linear in `D`, so the solution is found by testing each piece). The year's relief is spread evenly over the months of that year that have a deposit (2026-10-23); whether payroll grants the credit monthly is open (TXP OQ6), and a self-employed deduction comes through the return, a timing difference stated.

**F-31 Pension and provident fund for annuity, employee who is not a beneficiary member.** Credit `= 0.35 × credited`, `credited = min(paid, max(floor, 0.07 × E))`, `E = min(eligible income, 116,400 NIS a year)` (TXP-01, TXP-02; floor 2,268). The floor applies only to money paid (TXP-02's guide quote; the official text is still to be re-read against s.45A(d), README step 6; 2026-10-23). Existing pension deposits through work (A-12) use the cap first: the credit for new money is `credited(existing + new) − credited(existing)`. Marginal rate `τ_m` for deductions comes from the 2026 personal-effort brackets on the person's salary (TXG-02). Figures are frozen for 2025 to 2027 (TXP-27, TXG-06) and read from `data/rules/` by tax year.

**F-32 Beneficiary member and self-employed.** If deposits for the year, employer money included, reach 16% of the average wage (26,436 NIS in 2026, TXP-03), the credit follows TXP-04 and the deduction TXP-06 (11% first tier, 7% + 4% second tier, each at `τ_m`). A person who is not a beneficiary member and deposits without employer deposits gets the TXP-05 deduction: for business income (a self-employed person), limb (1), `7% × E` with `E = min(income, 164,400)` plus `4% × E` on the part of the deposit above `12% × E`, and the credit on the rest at 5% of `E` (TXP-02's no-work-income rate); for work income, limb (2), 5% of the work income that is not insured, up to the cap (2026-10-23). For a beneficiary member, one year's deposits split as in the guide's worked example (TXP-31). First tier: `11% × income for a self-employed member`. Second tier: on the base `deposits − beneficiary threshold`, deduct `min(base, 7% × AI) + min(4% × AI, max(0, base − 12% × AI))`, where AI is the additional income. What is left of the deposits goes to the s.45A(e) credit (TXP-04). The extra 0.5% credit (TXP-07) and the age-50 enlargement (TXP-08, 2026 amounts not found) are blocked (G-13).

**F-33 Everything else.** A self-employed person's study-fund deposit earns the TXH-05 deduction, 4.5% of income up to its ceiling, at the person's brackets (TXG-02) through F-30; the employee's study-fund deposit is not deducted. A self-employed person who is also an employee (TXH-06) is not modelled (2026-10-23). Otherwise `D_m = C_m`: kupat gemel lehashkaa (TXK-01), hishtalmut employee deposit (TXH-04), brokerage, deposits, funds, bonds, child savings (TXK-19).

## 7. Tax at exit (liquidation at T)

**F-40 Securities and fund units (brokerage wrapper, all instruments held as securities).**
- Real-basis instruments: shares, exempt-fund units including money-market funds, tracking funds and ETFs, TASE foreign funds, foreign-domiciled funds, CPI-linked bonds: `τ_exit = ρ × max(0, real)`, `ρ = 25%` (TXS-01, TXS-09, TXS-13, TXF-02, TXI-14), withheld by the bank or member (TXS-05, TXF-05) or paid by advance at a foreign broker (TXF-22). The index `J` is CPI, or the exchange rate for a foreign-currency security (F-04).
- Nominal-basis instruments: unlinked bonds, makam sold before redemption, commercial paper: `τ_exit = 0.15 × max(0, gain)` (TXS-03, TXI-12).
- `real < 0`: no tax. The loss is not carried anywhere (G-07).
- A falling index (2026-10-26): `real` comes from F-09's per-lot rule, so a lot bought at a higher CPI or exchange rate than at the sale is taxed on its nominal gain, never more, and the part of its nominal loss equal to the fall is not allowable (TXS-02, TXF-03).
- The inflationary amount accrued after 1993 is exempt (TXG-13, TXS-02); nothing in scope predates 1994.

**F-41 Deposits, savings plans and current accounts.** Tax was charged at each maturity or crediting (F-21, F-26). At `T` the last open deposit is treated as matured on the exit day: interest to date taxed under F-21.

**F-42 Age 60 and over at exit.** For "maximum rate" items (real capital gains, exempt-fund gains, linked interest) the person's brackets apply first (TXG-04, TXG-07, TXG-08, TXI-19, TXS-23): `τ_exit` = the sum, over the slice from `Y` to `Y + real`, of `min(bracket rate, ρ)` for each part of the slice, with the bracket rates from the 2026 reduced schedule 10/14/20/31/35/47% (TXG-04, TXG-02) and `Y` is A-13. Withholding stays at the flat rate (TXS-23); the difference is recovered through a return or form 0135 (TXG-25) when A-14 is on, and is otherwise lost. Fixed-rate items (15% unlinked interest, unlinked bonds) are not reduced (TXG-08 case (c); TXI OQ9 noted). Under 60: no change (TXG-08 case (a)). **(2026-10-26)** The same brackets apply at the kupat gemel lehashkaa exit (F-44); its withholding is 20% (TXK-26), not the flat rate, so its settlement in the return is F-44's.

**F-43 Surtax.** With `Z = Y + real` (inflationary amounts excluded, TXG-09): `surtax = 0.03 × max(0, Z − 721,560) − 0.03 × max(0, Y − 721,560)` plus, on capital-source income `Cap = real + other capital income`, `0.02 × max(0, Cap − 721,560)` (TXG-10). Settled through the return (TXG-11). Applied in every wrapper's exit tax and to taxable distributions in the year they fall.

**F-44 Kupat gemel lehashkaa, lump sum at any age.** Gains withdrawn are interest income at up to 25% (TXK-05, TXK-06, primary). The base is the real gain by law: the part equal to linkage on the money is linkage income, exempt under s.9(13), whose provident-account exclusion Amendment 190 deleted from 1.1.2012, and the 2016 bill's notes say "25% of the real gain" (TXK-07, primary since the 2026-10-25 tax-bases research; the manager withholds: the regulation says 20% (TXK-26), and Harel says it withholds 25%, which is unsettled (TXK-28)). The engine computes `τ_exit = 0.25 × max(0, real)`; the base carries no label since that date. A sensitivity line still shows the 15%-of-nominal alternative (TXK-06 second limb, G-02); whether to keep it now that the base is the law's is open for the founder. A partial withdrawal is pro rata (TXK-13). The 60+ reduction (F-42) applies to this deemed interest too: s.125C(b) is a maximum rate, so the reduced brackets s.121(b)(1) gives to all taxable income of a person aged 60 or over apply first, and since the gains are the top slice it helps only where other income leaves those brackets unused (TXK-06; changed 2026-10-26, open-questions audit, TXK OQ12 closed: the note said it was open). **Applied since 2026-10-26:** the engine computes the slice from `Y` to `Y + real` at `min(bracket rate, 25%)` with the 2026 reduced schedule (TXG-04, TXG-07), as F-42 does, and `detail.fullRateTax` keeps the flat 25% beside it. **Withholding and settlement (changed 2026-10-26, finance review).** The manager withholds 20% whatever the person's age (reg. 3(א) of the 2005 withholding regulations, TXK-26; Harel says it withholds 25%, its own statement and not settled, TXK-28). Withholding is an advance: the final tax, at the reduced brackets from 60, is settled against the 20% in the annual return, so money comes back only on a part of the slice whose bracket is below 20%, and more is due on a part whose rate is above it (TXK-26; under 60 the rest up to 25% is due the same way). The run cites TXK-26 and flags it (`TXK-26`). G-21 is not cited at this exit: its evidence is a bank's or TASE member's withholding. With the refund not claimed (A-14 off) the engine keeps the flat 25% and flags the relief it leaves out (flag `TXK-06`); that is the only case the flag still marks. That figure is an upper bound: with a 20% withholding, a part whose bracket is below 20% costs the 20% withheld, not 25%, so the A-14-off tax would be `max(20%, min(bracket rate, 25%))` a part. Correcting it moves golden S51's A-14-off figure, so it is left for the lead (2026-10-26); the site never turns A-14 off. TXS-23 is not cited here: it is the securities withholding regulations'. **A falling CPI (2026-10-26).** s.3(ה4)(3) deems the gains withdrawn to be interest and carves out only "רווחים ... בסכום הפרשי הצמדה" as linkage income (TXK-05, TXK-07); when the CPI fell over a deposit's life there is no such gain, so the engine counts that deposit at its amount and the taxable base never exceeds the gains withdrawn. No text says this for a fall in so many words, so a run where it bites carries the label "reading" on TXK-07. The study fund's excess (F-46, TXH-11) follows the same rule and label.

**F-45 Kupat gemel lehashkaa, annuity route from 60.** Transfer to a provident fund for annuity from age 60, annuity fully exempt (TXK-08 to TXK-10, TXK-12). The engine shows this route on the guide page as text and as "value transferred at 60" (the gross balance); the annuity itself is blocked (conversion factors, PEN OQ7; commutation TXK OQ4; G-03).

**F-46 Keren hishtalmut, lawful exit.** After 6 years from the first payment date (3 at retirement age or for study), everything from benefited deposits is exempt (TXH-09, TXH-10). Benefited own deposit: up to 2.5% of determining salary, determining salary capped at 15,712 NIS a month (TXH-08, TXH-02); benefited self-employed deposit 20,566 NIS a year (TXH-08). Gains on deposits above the benefited amount are taxed at withdrawal at 25% of the real gain (TXH-11; the real base is the Chief Economist paper's, primary since the 2026-10-25 scope pass). When the CPI fell over an excess deposit's life, that deposit counts at its amount, as in F-44, labelled "reading" on TXH-11 (2026-10-26). `τ_exit = 0` on the benefited part. The 6-year clock runs from the first payment date (KH-11); joining a fund that already exists keeps the clock (A-12).

**F-47 Keren hishtalmut, early withdrawal (only if the person opts in, B3).** Employer benefited deposits are taxed as work income at `τ_m` when received (TXH-13, TXH-01); gains at the s.121 rates for income not from personal effort, which start at 31% under 60 (TXH-13, s.125C(d)(5), TXG-03), stacked on the salary plus the employer deposits withdrawn (TXG-07) and at the TXG-04 rates at 60 or over (2026-10-23); the fund withholds 47% (TXH-15) and the difference is recovered through a return (A-14). Own deposits are not taxed again (they came from net salary, TXH-04).

**F-48 Pension and provident fund for annuity, early withdrawal (only if the person opts in).** Non-compliant withdrawal: `max(0.35, τ_m) × amount` (TXP-18), where `amount` is the whole sum paid: reg. 3(1) of the 1962 regulations taxes the member "על אותם כספים", and s.87(ד) applies the ordinary tax where it is higher (changed 2026-10-26, open-questions audit, TXP OQ3 closed). The engine does not compute it yet, so it is still **blocked** and the wrapper stays "listed, not ranked" even when the person opts in, until F-48 is built. The run's reason is "F-48" (not built), and no gap is flagged (changed 2026-10-26, closed-questions review: it returned G-06, which is closed). Lawful lump sums (severance component, small balances) follow PEN-29, PEN-30 and are listed, not modelled.

**F-49 Pension and provident fund for annuity at or after retirement.** The lawful exit is an annuity (PEN-27), partly exempt (TXP-14, 57.5% of 9,430 NIS a month in 2026, rising to 67% from 2028), with the offset formula (TXP-15) and commutation rules (TXP-17, PEN-28). The annuity value needs conversion factors that are not collected (PEN OQ7). **Blocked** (G-03): the wrapper is listed with its gross balance at the horizon and the text of the exemption rule; it is not ranked.

**F-50 Child savings.** Deposits, the parental addition and grants are not income (TXK-19). Gains are taxed on withdrawal (TXK-20). No official rate exists, but a secondary source gives the rates (TXK-23), so the engine computes them labelled "secondary source". Provident route: 25% of the real gain. Bank route, CPI-linked deposit: 25% of the real gain. Bank route, unlinked deposit: 15% of the nominal gain. Withheld at withdrawal. Deposits, the parental addition and grants are principal. **A falling CPI (2026-10-26).** The provident route is a kupat gemel lehashkaa (TXK-22), so F-44's rule applies: a deposit or grant made at a higher CPI than at the withdrawal counts at its amount, labelled "reading" on TXK-07. The CPI-linked bank deposit keeps the plain indexation, its interest taxed in full even when the linkage is negative. That is a reading, not a holding: the Supreme Court held it for a deposit linked to a foreign currency (it dismissed the appeal and left open whether the linkage loss can be offset), and applying it to a CPI-linked deposit is an inference from the same reasoning (TXI-25). So a run where a deposit's CPI fell carries the label "reading" on TXI-25, as the other inferred steps do (changed 2026-10-26, finance review: the line stated it as the Supreme Court's holding).

**F-51 IRA.** Tax follows the wrapper it was approved as (TXK-16, TXK-17): an IRA hishtalmut uses F-46 and F-47; an IRA provident fund for annuity uses F-48 and F-49 (blocked). Fees: the manager's balance fee within the 2% cap (PF-06, IRA-17) plus the brokerage tariff inside the account (IRA-21, IRA-22), both through F-12 to F-15.

**F-66 Switching cost.** Brokerage: moving from fund A to fund B at month `s` is a sale (TXS-21): tax per F-40 on A's lots at `s`, the net proceeds open B's lot. Provident: a track switch or a transfer to the same kind of fund costs nothing (TXK-04, TXH-17, TXH-19, PF-19, PF-23); a hishtalmut moves whole, never in part (PF-18, KH-17). Cost of switching `= V_T(no switch) − V_T(switch)`. The brokerage switch (2026-10-23) runs A to month `s` and sells there (F-09, F-40), then B from month `s` with the net proceeds, on the same calendar (tax years, custody quarters) and with later contributions raised by the index of month `s`; a hishtalmut moved in part is "not allowed" and a transfer between two kinds of fund is "not evaluated", because no rule says it is free. **A loss at the switch (2026-10-24):** when A's sale at month `s` is a real loss (G-07), the loss is a capital loss carried into leg B and offsets B's real gain at exit before tax, never beyond that gain: in the same tax year under TXS-17 (the institution nets inside itself, TXS-19), in a later year as a carried loss under TXS-18, which needs a return filed for the year of the loss and is flagged. ## 11. Ranking, ties and sensitivity

**F-80 Ranking and ties.** Rank eligible wrappers (section 8, B3) by `V_T^{real}`. Options `i` and `j` are a tie when `|V_i − V_j| / max(V_i, V_j) < θ` (P-01). Every option within θ of the top is in the tie set, compared pairwise with the top, not transitively (2026-10-23). The "X is best because Y" line names the formula that produces the largest difference between the top two (deposit-side relief, fee drag, tax during and at exit (2026-10-24: the engine's tax component sums the tax paid during the holding and the tax at exit), or lock-up), found by running F-61-style decompositions on both. An entered fee level for the representative instrument (D4) replaces its fee in F-80 and F-81 as an entered value (2026-10-24): the track's balance fee, or the fund's management line with its variable and trustee lines at zero; the tariff and the fund's other costs stay. A provider the person chose for the brokerage wrapper replaces the representative tariff in F-80 and F-81; the tariff lines stay sourced figures, the choice is the person's (2026-10-24). **Representative tariff of the brokerage wrapper (2026-10-24, Doron's case).** When the person chose no provider, the wrapper runs in F-80 and F-81 on the collected list tariff that gives this goal's run the highest real value, found by running the wrapper once on every tariff the providers workbench ranks (F-63's snapshots) before F-81's moves; the card names that provider as the site's pick, the picker marks it, and a saved comparison's re-run (F-82) makes the same pick. The fund inside the account already followed this rule (the cheapest record collected). P-05's tariff entry still serves the guide page's illustrative run and the fee page. The provident-family wrappers keep their one collected list fee (D4) until a second list price is collected. **Securities in the account (2026-10-26, D6).** Makam and a government bond are held in the securities account, so the same pick prices them: every collected Israeli bank and member tariff with a trade line for the security's class (`makam`, or `tase_bonds` with `tase_shares_bonds`), its commission, custody and monthly-fee lines (F-12, F-14, F-17), with `memberKind` from the provider's class; a foreign broker is left out, since it does not sell either on TASE. Each security takes its own pick, independently of the account's: one account for everything is a reading left to the founder (roadmap). Exposure mode prices them on the same pick (review 2026-10-26). The money-market funds keep P-05's bank case: at a bank with a distribution agreement the holder pays nothing to buy, hold or redeem them (MF-26, BANK-18), which no tariff undercuts. Why: on a goal of 67,000 NIS for eight years at 7 %, Leumi's list tariff (0.15 % custody a quarter, 0.4 % a trade) left the account at 85,507 NIS in today's money against 85,870 in a kupat gemel lehashkaa at Meitav's 0.8 % list fee, a tie the page showed with the gemel first; One Zero's published tariff (0 % custody, 0.3 % a trade) left 89,759 and Psagot Trade's 89,357, so the choice the page hid behind a link was worth ten times the choice it put in the headline. **The sentence under the headline** adds, when the deciding formula is the tax component between a provident-family option and a securities option that paid custody or account fees, that the tax gap is the fee's doing: a balance fee comes out of the balance and lowers the taxable gain, custody fees paid by selling units do not except in the exit year (F-08 step 5, F-09, TXS-06); both pay the same rate on the real gain. **The securities account over the indices (D27, D28, 2026-10-25).** The comparison names no index for the securities account until the person chooses one. Before a choice its value is the lowest of the plan's cheapest ways over the known indices (the conservative reading of D10, provisional and told to the founder), and the card shows the range; after a choice it is that index's cheapest way (F-63 on the fund's own instrument class), equal to the index page's value for the same plan. The site's tariff pick for the account reads only providers whose tariff has a buy/sell line for the fund's class (F-63, "No line, no price"): since then, on Doron's goal (67,000 ₪, 8 years, 7%) the pick is Psagot at 89,357 ₪, not One Zero at 89,759 ₪ (which had been priced as if the tracking fund were an ETF).

**F-81 Sensitivity panel.** For each assumption `a` in P-02 with bounds `[a_lo, a_hi]`: move `a` from its current value towards each bound by bisection, re-running F-80, until the top option changes or the bound is reached; report the smallest change that flips the result ("flips if `a` moves from `x` to `y`") or "no flip within bounds". A move that only breaks a tie is reported as that ("the tie breaks if … then X first alone"), and a fee row names the option and the fee it moves, never "the fund" (2026-10-24, Doron's case). Shared by 3.2, 3.4, 3.5 and 3.6. **On the fee page (2026-10-24).** The result that can flip is not the top option but the page's conclusion: the signature of the F-62 verdict of every fee plus which fee is the largest. `topOf` re-runs F-61 and F-62 with one assumption moved (A-01, A-02, the horizon, and each percentage fee from half to double, one at a time, P-02) and returns that signature; a flip is reported as the fee whose verdict changed and the new verdict, or the new largest fee, else "does not flip within the range tested". The comparison that decides "removes the fee" is held as it is. The in-fund cost axis is searched down to P-02's A-06 floor (0 %), not only to half the record's cost, and its flip point is reported in fee terms (the cost below which the answer changes) beside the lowest cost among the fund records collected for the exposure (2026-10-24; no parameter changed).

**F-82 Revisit (3.9).** A saved comparison stores every `A-nn`, `P-nn`, the rule figures and data versions used (by file and `checked` date). On revisit the engine re-runs with current versions, lists the entries whose values differ, and reports whether F-80's top option and tie set are unchanged. Engine (2026-10-24): the saved record keeps the inputs, every A-nn (A-01, A-02 and each candidate's cash rate), every P-nn, each rule figure read (rule, figure, value, `checked`), each data file (path, `checked`) and the result (top option, tie set, each ranked value). A rule figure or parameter differs when its value differs, a data file when its `checked` date differs or it is gone, an assumption when its value or state differs; a re-check with the same value is not a difference. The re-run uses the saved goal with the current wrapper records, terms, parameters and rules. The tie set is compared as a set.

**F-83 Candidate set and coverage line (product D17).** Every comparison starts from its full candidate set and accounts for each candidate exactly once. An option whose minimum-amount or other eligibility dimension is an open question in its wrapper record is listed, not ranked (outcome 4), with the reason "minimum amount: open question {code}" (2026-10-24). **Accepted fall below the fit table (2026-10-24):** when the fall the person accepts (A-29) is below the fall P-04 judges the option's own classification to take (`max_fall_pct` of its row), the option is "listed, not ranked" (review fix 2026-10-24: not every option that rests on the entered return A-01 is equity). The row is the one for the representative track's regulator classification for a provident or pension wrapper (a general track, such as P-05's 880, 2158 and 923, is judged at the "כללי" row, 20 %), the equity row for a fund-based wrapper whose fund holds an `equity_index` exposure, and the `equity_index` exposure's equity row in exposure mode; an option with no classification is not demoted. The option is "listed, not ranked" with that reason, unless the person entered a forecast fall for it that passes F-71 (the forecast does not exceed the accepted fall). No accepted fall entered: no change. P-04 stays a site parameter; D11 is unchanged (the site still holds no forecast fall).

- **Candidate sets.** 3.2 wrapper mode and its presets: every tier-1 product record, the wrappers and the securities held in them that a person chooses between (the product IDs in data-schema §2; D2, D6 as amended 2026-10-26: makam, a government bond and the money-market funds are securities held in the securities account, each a candidate of its own, so the coverage count is unchanged), and every tier-2 instrument on the "not covered yet" list (D2). 3.2 exposure mode: every exposure record. 3.4 providers: every provider in the provider list for the wrapper (F-63). 3.4 tracks and the catalogue fit mark: every track for the wrapper in the latest CMA period, at every managing company (F-64, F-70). 3.4 fund variants: as F-65. The tracks, providers and fund variants inside a wrapper are not candidates of 3.2; its coverage line links to 3.4 for them.
- **Outcome.** Each candidate gets one outcome, with its reason as a code (rule ID, G-ID, constraint or missing data item). When more than one applies, the first in this order is used:
  1. **not eligible**: a rule bars this person or this money (section 8; a track open only to a sector or employer, CAT-10);
  2. **excluded by the person's constraint**: section 8's constraint filters, or the loss filter (F-71);
  3. **not evaluated**: the run lacks an input (no tariff, no product terms, no fund record), the instrument is tier 2 (G-24), or the method has no run for it yet (a gap whose effect says "not evaluated", for example G-28 or G-30). A gap that blocks one rule inside a run that otherwise works, such as an exit tax, is not this outcome but B3 (item 4);
  4. **listed, not ranked**: B3 (locked at the horizon, or a rule in its run blocked by a gap), D15, or a ceiling that keeps the wrapper from taking the same money (F-05);
  5. **ranked**: F-80. In the idle-cash preset (F-60) this reads "compared against the current account", and the current account is the baseline.
- **Output.** `considered = N`, the count per outcome, and every candidate that is not ranked with its outcome and reason. A group the site knows exists but does not hold one by one (G-29) is one line with the count held. Invariant: every candidate appears exactly once and the counts sum to `N`.
- **Tests.** A golden scenario may restrict the candidate set to exercise a formula (S39 compares four wrappers); the site never restricts it. S40 and S41 test the full coverage line.
- **Track workbench outcomes (F-64).** The workbench ranks nothing, so its outcomes are: shown with a mark (fits, does not fit), shown without a rule (the classification is not in P-04), not eligible (a track open only to a sector or employer, CAT-10; it comes first, as above), and not evaluated (no saved goal, or no classification in the data); each track has exactly one, the counts equal the rows, and the page's coverage line names them (2026-10-24, chosen, revisable). **Engine (2026-10-24):** `trackWorkbench` gives each row exactly one outcome in that order. A track is not eligible when its `target_population` is not empty and not the CMA's "כלל האוכלוסיה" (the values in the data are the sector and employer ones, CAT-10); it is not evaluated when no goal was given or both its specialization and sub-specialization are empty; it is shown without a rule when P-04 has no row for it; otherwise it is shown with the mark. The coverage line carries the four counts and the finer split (fits, does not fit, no rule, not eligible, no goal, no classification), and both add up to the tracks considered.

## 13. Conventions for the engine and the tests

- Compute in full precision; round only for display. Display rule (2026-10-24): whole shekels from 1,000 ₪ for computed, entered and parameter amounts; two decimals for tariffs, fee rates and table cells, and within 0.50 ₪ of a P-03 threshold; percentages to two decimals.
- The golden tests compare against the independent check (`tests/golden/expected_calc.py`, worked in `tests/golden/workings.md`) within P-06.
- Every figure the engine reads carries its tax year or effective date; a run in tax year `y` reads the figure for `y`, and refuses to run with a figure whose `effective_to` has passed, showing "figure not updated" (D3).
- A rule of strength `open` never enters a formula. A rule of strength `secondary` or `institution` enters and labels the number. The dimension checklist is derived from the rules used in the run, not hand-marked (product.md 3.3).
- Every comparison test checks F-83's invariant: each candidate appears exactly once, with one outcome.
- `tests/golden/scenarios.md` is the test file named by the founder. **Answered 2026-10-23:** the name is `tests/golden/`; `docs/product.md` and `docs/roadmap.md` were changed to match.
